inner product of characters and jordan-hölder

Proposition

Let $V,W$ be a finite dimensional representations of a finite group $G$, where $W$ is a simple representation. Then

\begin{align*} \langle \chi_{V}, \chi_{W}\rangle =& \mathrm{dim} \mathrm{Hom}_{G}(W,V) \\ \cong& \bigoplus n_i W_i \end{align*}

which is the number of times $W$ appears in the Jordan-Hölder series at $V$

Proof
