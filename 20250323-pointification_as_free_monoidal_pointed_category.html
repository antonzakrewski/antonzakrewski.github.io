pointification as free monoidal pointed category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be presentable infinity categories

Then the basification functor as symmetric monoidal functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_* \end{align*}

induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}, \otimes}(\mathcal{C}_*, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}, \otimes}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}
