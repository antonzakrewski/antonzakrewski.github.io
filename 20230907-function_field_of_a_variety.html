function field of a variety

Definition / Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $X$ a variety. Then the function field $K(X)$ is defined as set of equivalence classes of regular functions $\langle U, f\rangle$ with $U \subseteq X$ and $f: U \rightarrow K$ where

\begin{align*} \langle U,f\rangle \sim \langle U', g\rangle :\Leftrightarrow f_{\vert U \cap U'} = g_{\vert U \cap U'} \end{align*}

and addition and multiplication is defined as

\begin{align*} \langle U,f\rangle + \langle U',g\rangle =& \langle U \cap U', f + g \rangle \\ \langle U,f\rangle \cdot \langle U',g\rangle =& \langle U \cap U', f \cdot g \rangle \end{align*}

Let $\langle U, f\rangle$ with $f \neq 0$. Then $V \coloneqq U \setminus Z(f)$ is open with $0 \not\in f_{\vert V}[V]$. Hence $\langle V, \frac{1}{f}\rangle$ is welldefined with

\begin{align*} \langle V, \frac{1}{f}\rangle \cdot \langle U, f\rangle =& \langle V, 1 \rangle \end{align*}
