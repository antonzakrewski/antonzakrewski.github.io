trivial conjugation action and abelian group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group TFAE:

  1. the conjugation action is trivial
  2. $G$ is abelian group

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Suppose it is not trivial. Then there exists an $g \in G$ with $g \cdot h \cdot g^{-1} \neq h$ for some $h \in G$ or equivalently

\begin{align*} g \cdot h \neq h \cdot g \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)

Sei $G$ eine Gruppe und seien $a, g \in G$.

\begin{align*} f_{a}(g) =& a^{-1}ga \\ =& a^{-1}ag \\ =& g \end{align*}
