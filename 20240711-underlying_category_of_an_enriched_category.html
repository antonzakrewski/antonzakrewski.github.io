underlying category of an enriched category

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a monoidal category and $\mathcal{C}$ a \(\mathcal{V}\)- enriched category Then the underlying category is defined via

  1. the same objects $\mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$
  2. $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}^{\mathrm{Set}}(x,y) \coloneqq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(\mathds{1}, \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}^{\mathcal{V}}(x,y))$
