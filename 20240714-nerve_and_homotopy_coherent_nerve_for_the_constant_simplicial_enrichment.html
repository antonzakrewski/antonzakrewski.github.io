nerve and homotopy coherent nerve for the constant simplicial enrichment

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $c\mathcal{C}$ the constant simplicial enrichment. Then the nerve and the homotopy coherent nerve are canonically isomorphic,

\begin{align*} \mathcal{N}(\mathcal{C}) \simeq \mathcal{N}(c \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

Proof
