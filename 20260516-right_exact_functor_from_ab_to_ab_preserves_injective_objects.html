right exact functor from LModA to Ab preserves injective objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{LMod}_A \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab}$ be a right exact functor for an Integral domain $A$. Then $\mathcal{F}$ preserves injective objects.

Proof

follows as $\mathcal{F}$ is additive and hence preserves multiplicatiion by $p$ and right exact, hence preserves epimorphisms
