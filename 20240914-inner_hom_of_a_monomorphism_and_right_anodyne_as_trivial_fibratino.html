inner hom of a monomorphism and right anodyne as trivial fibration

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ an right fibration $\iota: A \rightarrow B$ be a right anodyne map Then the map

\begin{align*} \langle f,\iota\rangle: X^B \rightarrow X^A \times_{Y^A} Y^B \end{align*}

is a trivial fibration

Proof

follows analogously to

  • inner hom of a monomorphism and anodyne as trivial fibration

using:

  • right anodyne map as monomorphism
  • inner hom of a monomorphism and right fibration
