functor of quasi-coherent objects

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a very large category with all limits and $k$ a commutative ring. Then the functor of quasi-coherent objects

\begin{align*} \mathrm{QC}: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}) \times \mathcal{P}(\mathrm{CAlg}_{k}^{\mathrm{op}}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}

is defined as right kan extension

Remark

  • categorical issues when left kan extending n-categorical yoneda into m-categories
