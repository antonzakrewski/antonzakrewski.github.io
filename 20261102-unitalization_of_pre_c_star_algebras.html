unitalization of pre c star algebras

Proposition

the unitalization functor of star algebras restricts to pre C star algebras

\begin{align*} C_{\mathrm{pre}}^*-\mathrm{Alg}^*{nu} \simeq C_{\mathrm{pre}}^*-\mathrm{Alg}_{/\mathbb{C}} \end{align*}

Proof

see:

  • maximal norm of the unitalization

a)

for a map $p: B \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$, the unitalization of the kernel is a pre-C^* Algebra. Hence the kernel must have been a pre c^* algebra, since the unitalization detects pre c star algebras.
