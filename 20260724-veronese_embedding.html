veronese embedding

Proposition

Let $k$ be a commutative ring, $M$ a \(k\)-module and $d \in \mathbb{N}$. Then the verones embedding of degree $d$ is the map of algebraic k-functors

\begin{align*} \rho_d: \mathbb{P}(M) \rightarrow \mathbb{P}(\mathrm{Sym}_k^d(M)) \end{align*}

sending a quotient line $M \otimes_{k} R \twoheadrightarrow L$ to the \(d\)-th symmetric power $\mathrm{Sym}_k^d(M) \otimes_{k} R \twoheadrightarrow \mathrm{Sym}_R^d(L) = L^{\otimes d}$.

Proof
