generated homogeneous ideal and homogeneous summands

Proposition

Let $M$ be a monoid and $R = \bigoplus_{m \in m} R_m$ a graded ring and $T \subseteq R$ a subset Then the generated homogeneous ideal is the generated Ideal of homogeneous summands, i.e. generated by the elements $t_m$ for $t \in T$ and $t = (t_m)$

Proof

It suffices to show that $t_m \in \mathrm{hom}(T)$. This follows from homogeneous ideal as additive prime ideal
