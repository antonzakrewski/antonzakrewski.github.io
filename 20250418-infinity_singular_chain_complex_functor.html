infinity singular chain complex functor

Definition

The infinity singular chain complex functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{C}_*: \mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}(\mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

is defined as unique colimit preserving infinity functor with

\begin{align*} \mathrm{C}_*(*) = \mathbb{Z}[0] \end{align*}
