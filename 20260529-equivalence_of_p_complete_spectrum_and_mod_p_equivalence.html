equivalence of p-complete spectrum and mod-p equivalence

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a map of p-complete spectra. TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an equivalence,
  2. $f/p: X/p \rightarrow Y/p$ is an equivalence.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

special case

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $F \rightarrow X \xrightarrow{f} Y$ be the fiber. Then p-completion of spectra is exact, so $F/p \rightarrow X/p \rightarrow Y/p$ is again a fiber sequence, where $F/p$ vanishes, since $f/p$ is an equivalence. But $F$ is also p-complete (cf. p-complete spectra closed under limits), so $F$ also had to vanish before (cf.p-complete spectrum and trivial mod p implies trivial spectrum). Hence $f$ is an equivalence.
