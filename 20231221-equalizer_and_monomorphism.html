equalizer and monomorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category

an equalizer

Then

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathrm{eq} \rightarrow A \end{align*}

is a monomorphism

Proof

Suppose there exists an object $C \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and morphisms $f,g$ such that

\begin{align*} \iota \circ f = \iota \circ g \end{align*}

Then we get a commuting diagram

Hence especially $C$ with $\iota \circ f$ is a fork

and therefore by universal property, there exists a unique mediating morphism

Since both $f,g$ make the diagram commute, we conclude, that by uniqueness

\begin{align*} f = g \end{align*}

and thus $\iota$ is a monomorphism
