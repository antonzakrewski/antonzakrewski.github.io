finitely generated algebra over a noetherian ring as finitely presented algebra

Proposition

Let $A$ be a noetherian ring, $B$ a commutative A-Algebra

TFAE:

  1. $B$ is a finitely generated A-Algebra
  2. $B$ is a finitely presented commutative A-Algebra

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from

  • finitely generated A-Algebra over a noetherian ring is noetherian

hence $A[X_1,...,X_n]$ is noetherian and each ideal is finitely generated

2) $\implies$ 1)

special case
