grouplike functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be preadditive categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ an additive functor Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be grouplike, if it factors through

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_\infty}(\mathcal{B}) \subseteq \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_\infty}(\mathcal{B}) \simeq \mathcal{B} \end{align*}
