alternative description of the inclusion of presentably monoidal categories into StRing

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{1Pr}) \rightarrow \mathrm{StRing}$ be the fully faithful inclusion of presentably monoidal categories into StRing. Then under the anti-equivalence of PrL and PrR, this functor identifies as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{1Pr}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{StRing} \\ \mathcal{C} \mapsto& (\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Mod}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathrm{1Pr}), ...) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • anti-equivalence of PrL and PrR
  • decategorification is given by End
