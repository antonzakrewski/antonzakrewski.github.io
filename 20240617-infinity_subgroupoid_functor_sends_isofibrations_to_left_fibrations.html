Let $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an <inner fibration> / isofibration> between infinity categories Then the induced morphism

\begin{align*} f^{\cong}: \mathcal{C}^{\cong} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}^{\cong} \end{align*}

under the maximal infinity-subgroupoid functor and the subcategory inclusion is a left fibration
