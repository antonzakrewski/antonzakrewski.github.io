free infinity sifted cocompletion

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category admitting finite coproducts Then the free sifted infinity cocompletion

\begin{align*} \mathcal{P}_{\Sigma}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is defined generalized presheaf construction for the infinity sifted categories,
