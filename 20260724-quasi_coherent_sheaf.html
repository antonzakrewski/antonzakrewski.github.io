quasi-coherent sheaf

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{O}_X)$ be a ringed space and $M$ an $\mathcal{O}_{X}$ module in $\mathrm{Sh}(X)$. Then $M$ is a quasi-coherent sheaf on $(X, \mathcal{O}_{X})$ if it admits a presentation locally on $X$, i.e., if for every $x \in X$, there exists an open neighborhood $U$ of $x$ and an exact sequence of the form

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O}_{U}^{(J)} \rightarrow \mathcal{O}_{U}^{(I)} \rightarrow M_{\vert U} \end{align*}

where $I$ and $J$ are sets.
