Proof

objectwise

Given $X \in \mathrm{sSet}$ construct the chain complex $C_*(X)$ with

\begin{align*} C_n(X) = \mathbb{Z}[X_n] \end{align*}

with the differential

\begin{align*} \partial_n \coloneqq \sum_{i=0}^{n} (-1)^i d_i^{n}: \mathbb{Z}[X_n] \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}[X_{n-1}] \end{align*}

which is a chain complex, as seen in singular homology functor.

By definition we have

\begin{align*} C_*(\mathrm{Sing}(X)) = C_*^{\mathrm{sing}}(X) \end{align*}

Then applying the homology functor gives the desired result
