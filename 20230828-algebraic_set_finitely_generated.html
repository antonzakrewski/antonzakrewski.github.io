algebraic set finitely generated

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $V(S)$ an algebraic set. Then there exist finitely many polynomials $f_1,...,f_k \in K[X_1,...,X_n]$ such that

\begin{align*} V(S) = \bigcap_{i=1}^k V(f_i) \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of

  1. Hilbert's basis theorem
  2. vanishing set of a union of ringelements
