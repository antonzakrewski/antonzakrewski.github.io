subcategory of compact objects is stable subcategory

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category and $\kappa$ a regular cardinal. Then the full subcategory $\mathcal{C}^{\omega}$ of $\kappa$ compact objects is stable subcategory.

Proof

follows from:

  • compact objects closed under cofiber sequences
  • compact object closed under finite limits in a stable category
