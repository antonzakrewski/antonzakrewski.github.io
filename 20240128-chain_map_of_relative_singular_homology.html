chain map of relative singular homology

Proposition

Let $(X,A), (Y,B)$ be pairs of spaces and $f: (X,A) \rightarrow (Y,B)$ a map of pairs. Then there exists a natural induced chain map

\begin{align*} f': C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X)/ C^{\mathrm{sing}}(A) \rightarrow C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y)/ C^{\mathrm{sing}}(B) \end{align*}

where $C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X)/C^{\mathrm{sing}}(A)$ is the relative singular chain complex
