right 1-kan extension

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be functors

Then a right kan extension

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ran}_{\mathcal{F}}(\mathcal{G}): \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

is a functor with a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \eta: \mathrm{Ran}_{\mathcal{F}}(\mathcal{G}) \circ \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{G} \end{align*}

such that for any functor $\mathcal{H}: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ with a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \mu: \mathcal{HF} \rightarrow \mathcal{G} \end{align*}

there exists a unique natural transformation $\epsilon: \mathcal{H} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ran}_{\mathcal{F}}(\mathcal{G})$
