zero locus and product

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space. Suppose $\mathfrak{a}, \mathfrak{b} \subseteq K[X_1,...,X_n]$ are ideals. Then the zero locus turns a product of ideals into a union

\begin{align*} \mathcal{V}(\mathfrak{a} \cdot \mathfrak{b}) = \mathcal{V}(\mathfrak{a}) \cup \mathcal{V}(\mathfrak{b}) \end{align*}

Proof
