group action on the power set - to watch

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group and $G \times X \rightarrow X$ a group action. Then the map

\begin{align*} g \times \mathcal{P}(X) \rightarrow& \mathcal{P}(X) \\ g \cdot A \mapsto& \{g \cdot a \vert a \in A\} \end{align*}
