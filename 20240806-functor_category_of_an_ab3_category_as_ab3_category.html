functor category of an Ab3 category as Ab3 category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an Ab3 category, $\mathcal{I}$ an indexing category. Then the functor category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{I}, \mathcal{A}) \end{align*}

is also an Ab3 category

Proof

follows from:

  • colimit of functors for a cocomplete codomain
