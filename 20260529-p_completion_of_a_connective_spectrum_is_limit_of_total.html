p-completion of a connective spectrum is limit of total complex with Fp

Proposition

Let $X$ be a bounded below spectrum, then there exists a canonical equivalence

\begin{align*} X_{p}^{\wedge} \rightarrow \lim_{\Delta} (\mathbb{F}_p^{\otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \bullet + 1} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} X) \end{align*}

Proof

a)

We first proceed to show that $\lim_{\Delta} (X \otimes \mathrm{coBar}(\mathbb{F}_p))$ is p-complete. Recall that any spectrum of the form $X/p$ is $p$-complete (cf. p-completion mod p) . This shows that $X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{F}_p$ is $p$-complete for any spectrum $X$, since $X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{F}_p$ is equivalent to $(X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z})/p$. Hence the diagram $X \otimes \mathrm{coBar}(\mathbb{F}_p)$ consists of $p$-complete spectra. Now p-complete spectra are closed under limits, which shows that $\lim_{\Delta} (X \otimes \mathrm{coBar}(\mathbb{F}_p))$ is $p$-complete. Now the universal property of $p$-completion induces a canonical map $X_p^{\wedge} \rightarrow \lim_{\Delta} (X \otimes \mathrm{coBar}(\mathbb{F}_p))$ and it remains to show that this map is an equivalence.

b)

using equivalence of p-complete spectrum and mod-p equivalence and the fact that p-completion of spectra is exact and $\lim_{\Delta} (\mathbb{F}_p^{\otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \bullet + 1} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} -)$ (todo) are exact it suffices to show that

\begin{align*} (X_{p}^{\wedge})/p \simeq X/p \rightarrow \lim_{\Delta} (\mathbb{F}_p^{\otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \bullet + 1} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} X/p) \end{align*}

is an equivalence:

this is precisely descend along a map between connective ring spectra of a bounded below spectrum for the map $\varphi: \mathbb{S} \rightarrow \mathbb{F}_p$:

  1. $X$ is bounded below
  2. $\pi_0(\varphi): \pi_0(\mathbb{S}) \rightarrow \pi_0(\mathbb{F}_p)$ is surjective and mod p an isomorphism
  3. X/p^n is bounded p-torsion spectrum
