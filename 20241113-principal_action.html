principal g-action

Definition

Let $G$ be a topological group action, $X$ a topological space Then a group action is said to be principal, if there exists an open cover $U_i$ of the orbital collapse

\begin{align*} p: E \rightarrow E/G \end{align*}

and G-equivariant homeomorphisms

\begin{align*} U_i \times G \rightarrow p^{-1}[U_i] \end{align*}

which are compatible with the projections to $U_i$,
