units in the trivial square zero extension

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $M$ an \(R\)-module and $R \oplus M$ the trivial square-zero extension and $(r,m) \in R \oplus M$ an element. TFAE:

  1. $(r,m)$ is a unit
  2. $r$ is a unit

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from the ring-homomorphism $R \oplus M \twoheadrightarrow R$.

2) $\implies$ 1)

First observe that $(1,0)$ is the neutral element of multiplication. Then follows from the inverse $(r^{-1}, -r^{-2}m)$ where $(r,m) \cdot (r^{-1}, -r^{-2}m) = (1, r^{-1} m - r r^{-2} m) = (1,0)$.d
