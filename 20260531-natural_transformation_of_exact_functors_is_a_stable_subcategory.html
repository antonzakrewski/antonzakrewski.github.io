objects with natural transformation as equivalence of exact functors is a stable subcategory

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be stable categories and $\mathcal{F}_1, \mathcal{F}_2: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be exact functor. Let $\eta: \mathcal{F}_1 \Rightarrow \mathcal{F}_2$ be a natural transformation Then the full subcategory

\begin{align*} \{X \in \mathcal{C} \vert \eta(X) \text{ is an equivalence} \} \end{align*}

is a stable subcategory

Proof

observe that $\eta(0): \mathcal{F}_1(0) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}_2(0)$ is an equivalence since $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(0,0) \simeq 0$. moerover $\mathcal{F}$ preserves finite limits and finite colimits, so we may apply natural transformation between I-shaped colimit preserving functors in spectra and equivalence (and the dual version) to conclude that the subcategory $\{X \in \mathcal{C} \vert \eta(X) \text{ is an equivalence} \}$ is closed under finite colimits and finite limits, hence a stable subcategory
