left exact functor preserves n-truncated objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories which admit finite infinity limits and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a left exact functor Then $\mathcal{F}$ preserves n-truncated object

Proof

corollary of:

  • n-truncated object and terminal morphism
  • left exact functor preserves n-truncated morphisms
  • and the terminal object is preserves as finite limit
