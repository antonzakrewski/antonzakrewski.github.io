open functor from algebraic functors to locales

Definition

The open functor from the category of algebraic functors to partially ordered sets is defined as the left kan extension

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CRing}, \mathrm{Set}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Loc} \end{align*}

of the zariski locale functor $\mathrm{CRing}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Loc}$
