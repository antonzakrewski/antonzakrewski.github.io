category of finitely generated abelian groups is not equivalent to opposite category

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{FinGenAb}$ be the finitely generated abelian groups. Then $\mathrm{FinGenAb}$ is not equivalent to $\mathrm{FinGenAb}^{\mathrm{op}}$.

Proof

Observe that $\mathrm{End}(\mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}, \mathrm{End}(\mathbb{Z}/n) \cong \mathbb{Z}/n$ and $\mathrm{End}(M_1 \oplus M_2 ... \oplus M_n)$ is not a single summand. Hence any equivalence $\mathrm{FinGenAb} \rightarrow \mathrm{FinGenAb}$ must map $\mathbb{Z} \mapsto \mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{Z}/n \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}/n$. But $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Z}/n) = \mathbb{Z}/n \not\cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\mathbb{Z}/n, \mathbb{Z})\cong 0$.
