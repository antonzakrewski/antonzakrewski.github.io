complete filtration and right derived functor

Proposition

Let $R = R_0$ be a ring and $\mathrm{GrRMod}$ the category of graded modules and $M$ a graded module. Let $F_s$ be a filtration. TFAE:

  1. $F_s$ is a complete filtration
  2. the right derived functor of the $\mathrm{lim}_{s} F_s$ is given by zero.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
