inclusion from the domain to the mapping cylinder as cofibration

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be topological spaces, $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a continuous map and $\mathrm{Cyl}(f)$ the mapping cylinder. then the map

\begin{align*} \iota_0: X \rightarrow \mathrm{Cyl}(X) = X \times [0,1] \coprod Y/\sim x \mapsto& (x,0) \end{align*}

is a cofibration
