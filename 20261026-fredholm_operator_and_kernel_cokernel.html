fredholm operator and kernel / cokernel

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be banach spaces and $T: X \rightarrow Y$ be a bounded operator. TFAE:

  1. there exists an operator $S: Y \rightarrow X$ such that $\mathrm{id}_{X} - ST$ and $\mathrm{id}_{Y} - TS$ are compact operators
  2. the dimension of the kernel and cokernel are each finite.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)

choose closed decompositions of $X$ and $Y$ as $X = X_0 \oplus \mathrm{ker}(T)$ and $Y = \mathrm{im}(T) \oplus Y_0$. This is possible in the $X$ case by topological decomposition for a finite dimensional subspace in a banach space and in the $Y$ case since any $T_0$ which is algebraically the sum $Y = \mathrm{im}(T) \oplus Y_0$ is finite dimensional, hence closed.
