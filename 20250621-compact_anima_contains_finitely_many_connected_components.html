compact anima contains finitely many connected components

Proposition

Let $X$ be a compact anima. Then $\pi_0(X)$ is finite.

Proof

using compact anima as retract of finite anima, retracts are preserved functors, so apply $\pi_0(-)$ Hence this reduces to finite anima, where this follows as pi_0 preserves colimits and finite colimits of finite sets are again finite.
