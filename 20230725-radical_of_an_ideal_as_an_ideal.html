radical of an ideal as an ideal

Proposition

Let $A$ be commutative ring and $\mathfrak{a}$ an ideal and $\sqrt{\mathfrak{a}}$ the radical. Then $\sqrt{\mathfrak{a}}$ is an ideal

Proof

Let $a,b \in \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}}$, then there exist $n,m \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $a^n,b^m \in \mathfrak{a}$

additive inverse

Therefore we conclude

\begin{align*} (-a)^n = \pm a^n \in \mathfrak{a} \end{align*}

additive closed

it follows from

\begin{align*} (a + b)^{n+m} \in \mathfrak{a} \end{align*}

ausschreiben

multiplicative

Let $r \in A$, then

\begin{align*} (r \cdot a)^n = r^n \cdot a^n \in \mathfrak{a} \end{align*}
