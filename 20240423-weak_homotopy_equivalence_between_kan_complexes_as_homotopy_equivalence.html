weak homotopy equivalence between Kan complexes as homotopy equivalence

Proposition

Let $K,L$ be Kan complexes and $f: K \rightarrow L$ a morphism of simplicial sets.

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a weak equivalence
  2. $f$ is a simplicial homotopy equivalence

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

By assumption

\begin{align*} f^{*}: \pi_0(L,K) \rightarrow \pi_0(K,K) \end{align*}

is a bijection.

Hence there exists a map $g$ unique up to homotopy such that

\begin{align*} g \circ f \simeq \mathrm{id}_K \end{align*}

Furthermore

\begin{align*} f g f \simeq f \end{align*}

so the injectivity of $f^*$ implies that $fg \simeq \mathrm{id}_L$
