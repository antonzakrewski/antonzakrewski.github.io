Grothendieck pretopology

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. A Grothendieck pretopology $\pi$ on $\mathcal{C}$ is a collection of families of morphisms $\{\varphi_I: U_i \rightarrow U\}_{i \in I}$, where each family has a fixed codomain satisfying

  1. for any isomorphism $\varphi: U \rightarrow V$ (or it satisfies to assume that the identity is a covering family)
  2. if $U_i \rightarrow X$ is a $\pi$ covering family and $f: Y \rightarrow X$ is any map, then the pullbacks $(U_i \times_{X} Y \rightarrow Y)$ is a $\pi$-covering family
  3. if $(U_i \rightarrow X)_{i \in I}$ and $(V_{i,j} \rightarrow U_i)_{j \in J}$ are \(\pi\)-covering families, then $(V_{i,j} \rightarrow X)_{i,j}$ is a $\pi$ covering family.
