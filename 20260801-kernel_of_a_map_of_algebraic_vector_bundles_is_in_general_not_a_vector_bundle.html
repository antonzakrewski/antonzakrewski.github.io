kernel of a map of algebraic vector bundles is in general not a vector bundle

Proposition

Let $\varphi: V \rightarrow W$ be a map between vector bundles. Then the kernel is in general not a vector bundle.

Proof

consider $R = \mathbb{Z}/4 \mathbb{Z}$ and $\cdot 2: \mathbb{Z}/4 \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}/4 \mathbb{Z}$, where the kernel is $\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}$, which is not a direct summand.
