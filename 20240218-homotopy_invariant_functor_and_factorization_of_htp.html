homotopy invariant functor and homotopic maps

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Top} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a functor.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is homotopy equivalent as in factorization through Category hTop
  2. for homotopic maps $f \sim g$ it follows, that
\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(f) \sim \mathcal{F}(g) \end{align*}

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let

Let $f \sim g$ be homotopic maps, then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(f) =& \mathcal{F}' \circ \pi(f) \\ =& \mathcal{F}'(\pi(g)) \\ =& \mathcal{F}(g) \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}': \mathrm{hTop} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ X \mapsto& \mathcal{F}(X) \\ [f] \mapsto& \mathcal{F}(f) \end{align*}

Then since $\pi$ is object-injective and homotopic maps get mapped to the same morphism, this functor is welldefined Functorality follows from

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}'([f] \circ [g]) =& \mathcal{F}(f \circ g) \\ =& \mathcal{F}(f) \circ \mathcal{F}(g) \\ =& \mathcal{F}'([f]) \circ \mathcal{F}'([g]) \\ \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}'([\mathrm{id}]) =& \mathcal{F}(\mathrm{id}) \\ =& \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{F}()} \end{align*}
