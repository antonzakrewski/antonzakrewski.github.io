colimit of a point in Anima

Proposition

Let $X$ be a simplicial set. Then the colimit

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{X} * \simeq \vert X \vert \end{align*}

where $\vert - \vert$ is the total dwyer-kan localization

Proof

follows from:

  • Lurie theorem about colimits in Anima
  • where the unstraightening functor of left fibrations sends
\begin{align*} X \rightarrow * \end{align*}

to

\begin{align*} \mathrm{id}: X \rightarrow X \end{align*}
