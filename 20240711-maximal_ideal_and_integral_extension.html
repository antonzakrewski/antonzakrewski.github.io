maximal ideal and integral extension

Proposition

Let $A \hookrightarrow B$ be an integral extension, $\mathfrak{q} \in \mathrm{Spec}(B)$ a prime ideal and $\mathfrak{p}$ the contraction

TFAE:

  1. $\mathfrak{p} \subseteq A$ is a maximal ideal
  2. $\mathfrak{q} \subseteq B$ is maximal

Proof

by integral rings and quotients it follows that

\begin{align*} A/\mathfrak{p} \hookrightarrow B/\mathfrak{q} \end{align*}

is an integral extension.

Then $B/\mathfrak{q}$ is an Integral domain, as $\mathfrak{q}$ is prime.

Then by integral rings and field it follows that $A/\mathfrak{p}$ is a field if and only if $B/\mathfrak{q}$ is a field.

Hence $\mathfrak{p}$ is maximal iff $\mathfrak{q}$ is maximal

(cf. maximal ideal of a commutative ring and quotient ring as field, prime ideal and quotient ring as integral domain)
