Proposition

Let $p > 2$ be a prime number and $G$ a group of group order $\vert G \vert = 2p$. Then either

\begin{align*} G \cong& \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} /(2p\mathbb{Z}) \\ D_{p} \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

here $D_p$ is the Diedergruppe
