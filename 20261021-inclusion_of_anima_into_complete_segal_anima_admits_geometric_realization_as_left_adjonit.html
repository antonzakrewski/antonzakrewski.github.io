inclusion of anima into complete segal anima admits geometric realization as left adjoint

Proposition

The inclusion $\mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathrm{cSAn}$ admits a left adjoint, which is taking the colimit or also called geometric realization.

Proof

Observe that the inclusion is given by the constant diagram functor, restricted to $\mathrm{csAn}$. But this functor $\mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathcal{P}(\Delta^{\mathrm{op}})$ has a left adjoint which is given by taking colimits or left kan extension. Hence this adjunctions restricts to full subcategories
