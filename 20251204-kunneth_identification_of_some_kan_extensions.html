kunneth identification of some kan extensions

Proposition

Let

be a pullback in category of Anima.

Equipp $\mathrm{Fun}(-,\mathrm{Mod}(k))$ with the pointwise monoidal structure.

Let $\underline{k}: W \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}(k)$ denote the constant functor.

Then consider the map $\varphi$

\begin{align*} \varphi: g_*g^*(\underline{k}) \otimes f_* f^*(\underline{k}) \rightarrow h_*h^*(\underline{k}) \end{align*}

corresponding to

\begin{align*} b^*g^* g_*g^*(\underline{k}) \otimes_{k} a^*f^*f_*f^*(\underline{k}) \xrightarrow{b^*(\epsilon)g^* \otimes a^*(\epsilon)g^*} b^*g^*(\underline{k}) \otimes_{k} a^*f^*(\underline{k}) \xrightarrow{\cdot} \underline{k} \end{align*}

which is the composition of the counit and multiplication of $k\otimes_{k} k \rightarrow k$ (since $b^*g^*(\underline{-})$ is strong monoidal for the monoidal structures)

Then under the right kan extension formula $\varphi$ identifies pointwise with the <kunneth map>

Proof

Note that the right kan extension formula for anima gives $g_*g^*(\underline{k}) \simeq \mathrm{map}(\mathrm{fib}_{-}(g), k), f_*f^*(\underline{k}) \simeq \mathrm{map}(\mathrm{fib}_{-}(f), k)$. Then the counit corresponds under the adjunction to the identity - similar to the evaluation morphism for a closed monoidal category .
