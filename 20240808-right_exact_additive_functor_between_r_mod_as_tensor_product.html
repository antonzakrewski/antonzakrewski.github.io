right exact functor between R-Mod as tensor product

Proposition

Let \(A,B\) be rings and $\mathrm{AMod}, \mathrm{BMod}$ the categories of modules and $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{AMod} \rightarrow \mathrm{BMod}$ a right exact functor. Then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(-) \cong \mathcal{F}(A) \otimes_{A} - \end{align*}

for the \(B,A\)-bimodule $\mathcal{F}(A)$

Proof

Note that $A$ is a right \(A\)-module (cf.ring as bimodule) Hence by additive functor from RMod induces module structure for an R,R bimodule it follows that $\mathcal{F}(A)$ is a \(B,A\)-bimodule

Furthermore a right exact functor from R-Mod is determined by free modules, hence it suffices to show that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(\bigoplus A) \cong \bigoplus \mathcal{F}(A) \end{align*}

Note that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(-) \cong \mathcal{F}(A) \otimes_{A} - \end{align*}

holds for each free module
