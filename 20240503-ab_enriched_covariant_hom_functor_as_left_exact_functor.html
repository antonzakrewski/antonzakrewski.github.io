covariant ab-hom-functor as left-exact functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{A})$ an object. Then the covariant ab-hom functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(A,-): \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab} \end{align*}

is left exact
