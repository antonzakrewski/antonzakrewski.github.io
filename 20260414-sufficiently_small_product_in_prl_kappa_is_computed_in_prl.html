sufficiently small product in PrL kappa is computed in PrL

Proposition

Let $I$ be a $\kappa$ small set and $\mathcal{C}_i$ be kappa presentable categories Then

\begin{align*} \prod_{i \in I}^{\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\kappa}} \mathcal{C}_i \xrightarrow{\simeq} \prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{C}_i \end{align*}

are canonically equivalent

Proof
