noetherian semi local ring as finite dimensional ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a noetherian semi-local ring. Then $A$ is finite dimensional

Proof

follows from the height theorem, as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{p}) \leq \mathrm{max}(\mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{m}_i)) \end{align*}

over finitely many maximal ideals $\mathfrak{m}_i$ and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{m}) \leq r \end{align*}

for $\mathfrak{m} = (a_1,...,a_r)$, as $\mathfrak{m}$ is a finitely generated ideal since $A$ is noetherian (cf. noetherian ring and finite generation of prime ideals)
