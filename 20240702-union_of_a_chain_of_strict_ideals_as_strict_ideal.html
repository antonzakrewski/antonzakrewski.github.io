union of a chain of strict ideals as strict ideal

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{p}_1 \subseteq ... $ a chain of strict ideals Then the union is also a strict ideal

Proof

Suppose it is not strict. Then $1 \in \bigcup \mathfrak{a}_i$ and hence there exists an $n$ such that $1 \in \mathfrak{a}_n$, contradiction
