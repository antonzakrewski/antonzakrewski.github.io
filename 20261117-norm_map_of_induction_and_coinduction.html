norm map of induction and coinduction

Proposition

Let $W$ be an $H$ representation for $H \subseteq G$. Then natural map - the norm map - $\mathrm{Ind}_{H}^G(V) \rightarrow \mathrm{Coind}_{H}^{G}(W)$.

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Nm}(g \otimes w)(x) = \begin{cases} (xg)(w) & \mbox{if } xg \in H \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

We have to prove first that this map $\mathrm{Nm}$ is well-defined:

  1. $\mathrm{Nm}(g h \otimes w) = \mathrm{Nm}(g \otimes hw)$
  2. $\mathrm{Nm}(g'g \otimes w) =& g' \mathrm{Nm}(g \otimes w)$:
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Nm}(g'g \otimes w)(x) =& \begin{cases} x g g'w & \mbox{if } xg'g \in H \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \\ g' \mathrm{Nm}(g \otimes w)(x) =& g' \begin{cases} xgw & \mbox{if } xg \in H \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \\ =& \begin{cases} xgg'w & \mbox{if } xgg' \in H \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
