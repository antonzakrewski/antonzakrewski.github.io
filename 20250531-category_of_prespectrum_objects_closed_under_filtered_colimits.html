category of prespectrum objects closed under sufficiently filtered colimits in the functor category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category. Then the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathrm{PSp}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathbb{N}\times \mathbb{N}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is accessible

Proof

corollary of:

  • kappa filtered colimit of terminal objects in a presentable category
  • pointwise calculation of colimits
