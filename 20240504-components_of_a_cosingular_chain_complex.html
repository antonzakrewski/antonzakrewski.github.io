components of a cosingular chain complex

Proposition

Let $M$ be an abelian group, $(X,A)$ a pair of spaces. Then the cosingular chain complex is given by

  1. in degree qn$
\begin{align*} C_{\mathrm{sing}}(X,A;M)_n = \mathrm{Hom}(C^{\mathrm{sing}}_{-n}(X,A),M) \end{align*}
  1. precomposition of the $\partial$ as differential
