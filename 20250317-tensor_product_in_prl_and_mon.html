tensor product in PrL and Mon

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be a presentable infinity categories Then there exist canonical (?) equivalences

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \otimes \mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{D}) \simeq& \mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{C} \otimes \mathcal{D}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{C}) \otimes \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

category PrL as closed symmetric monoidal category

Proof

corollary of:

  • tensor product of PrL as Fun^R
  • adjunction of limit preserving functors and cartesian commutative monoids

where

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \otimes \mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{D}) \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{R}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{D})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Mon}(\mathrm{Fun}^R(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{D}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{C} \otimes \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

and the second isomorphism follows from the symmetry of the monoidal structure category PrL as closed symmetric monoidal category
