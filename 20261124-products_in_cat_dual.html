Products in Cat dual

Proposition

There exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{C}_i \cong& \mathrm{ker}^{\mathrm{dual}}(\mathrm{Ind}(\prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{C}_{i}^{\kappa}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\prod_{i \in I} \mathrm{Cal}_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{cont}}(\mathcal{C}_i))) \\ \cong& \mathrm{ker}(\mathrm{Ind}(\prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{C}_{i}^{\kappa}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\prod_{i \in I} \mathrm{Cal}_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{cont}}(\mathcal{C}_i))) \\ \end{align*}

Proof
