Segal map out of gamma

Definition

Let $\mathrm{Fin}$ be the category Fin (resp. infinity category Fin) Then the segal map is defined as partial map

\begin{align*} e_i: \{1,...,n\} \rightarrow& \{1\} j \mapsto& \begin{cases} 1 & \mbox{if } i = j \\ \mathrm{undef} & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
