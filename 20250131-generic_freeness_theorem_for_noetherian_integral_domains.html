generic freeness theorem for a noetherian integral domain

Proposition

Let $A$ be a noetherian ring Integral domain and $M$ a finitely generated module. Then there exists an $\alpha \in A$ such that the localization

\begin{align*} M_{\alpha} \otimes_{A} Q(A) \end{align*}

is a free module

Proof

Consider

\begin{align*} M \otimes_{A} Q(A) \end{align*}

it is a vector space, hence admits a basis $\frac{m_i}{\alpha_i}$ for $i \in \{1,...,n\}$. w.l.o.g. we may assume that $\alpha_i = 1$ after multiplication with $\alpha_i$.

Then consider

\begin{align*} \varphi: A^n \rightarrow M \\ e_i \mapsto m_i \end{align*}

it becomes an isomorphism after localization at $(0)$.

By homomorphism between noetherian modules and localization it eventually becomes an isomorphism for some $\alpha$ (note that $A^n$ is noetherian module (cf. finitely generated module over a noetherian ring as noetherian module) By localization of a free module as free module we conclude that $M_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is eventually free.
