Ind preserves locally small categories

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal and $\mathcal{C}$ a locally small category . Then $\mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C})$ is also a locally small caetgory.

Proof

Let $x,y \in \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C})$, then both $x$ and $y$ are small, $\kappa$-filtered colimits of representables (cf. Ind Kappa arises freely by adding kappa filtered colimits). Now use the hom formula (todo).
