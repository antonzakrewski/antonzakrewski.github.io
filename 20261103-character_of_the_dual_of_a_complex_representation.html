character of the dual of a complex representation

Proposition

Let $V$ be a finite dimensional, $\mathbb{C}$ valued representationo of a finite group $G$. Then $\chi_{V^*}(g) = \overline{\chi_{V}(g)}$

Proof

Fix eigenvalues $\lambda_i$ of $p_V(g)$. Then

\begin{align*} \chi_{V}(g) = \sum \lambda_i \end{align*}

for root of unities $\lambda_i$. Then

\begin{align*} \chi_{V^*}(g) =& \sum \lambda_i^{-1} \\ =& \overline{\sum \lambda_i} \\ =& \overline{\chi_V}(g)} \end{align*}
