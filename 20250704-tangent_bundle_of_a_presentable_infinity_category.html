tangent bundle of a presentable infinity category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category Then a tangent bundle to $\mathcal{C}$ is a stable envelope $T_{\mathcal{C}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{C})$ for the presentable fibration

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ev}_1: \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}
