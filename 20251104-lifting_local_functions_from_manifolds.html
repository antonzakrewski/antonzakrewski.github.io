Proposition

Let $M,N$ be smooth manifolds, $U \subseteq M$ an open neighbourhood Let $f: U \rightarrow N$ be a smooth map.

Then there does not in general exist a smooth map $F: M \rightarrow N$ with

\begin{align*} F_{\vert U} = f \end{align*}
