trivial fibration as joyal equivalence and isofibration

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $p: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a infinity functor TFAE:

  1. $p$ is a trivial fibration
  2. $p$ is a joyal equivalence and isofibration

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

  • trivial fibration as joyal equivalence
  • trivial fibration as isofibration

2) $\implies$ 1)

  • isofibration and joyal equivalence as trivial fibration
