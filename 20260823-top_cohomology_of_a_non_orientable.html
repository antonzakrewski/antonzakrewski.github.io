top cohomology of a non orientable, closed manifold

Proposition

Let $M$ be an n-dimensional, connected, non orientable closed manifold. Then

\begin{align*} H^n(M, \mathbb{Z}) = \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

Proof

Throughout we use the universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology to deduce this statement from computations on homology: Here we have computed that $H_n(M, \mathbb{Z}) \cong 0$ (cf. n homology of a non orientable closed connected manifold) and $H_{n-1}(M, \mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}^{k} \oplus \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}$ (cf. n-1 homology of a closed, connected non orientable manifold) imply that

\begin{align*} H^n(M, \mathbb{Z}) \cong& \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathbb{Z}}^1(\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Z}) \oplus \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(0, \mathbb{Z}) \\ \cong& \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}
