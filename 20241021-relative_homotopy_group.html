relative homotopy group

Definition

Let $(X,A)$ be a pair of spaces.

Then for $n \geq 2$ the relative homotopy group is defined as continuous maps up to relative homotopy

\begin{align*} [(D^n, S^{n-1},*), (X,A,a)] \end{align*}
