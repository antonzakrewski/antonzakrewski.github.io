product and homotopic maps

Proposition

Let $X_1,X_2,Y_1,Y_2$ be topological spaces and $f,f': X_1 \rightarrow X_2$ and $g,g': Y_1 \rightarrow Y_2$ be homotopic relative to $A \subseteq X_1, B \subseteq Y_1$ with preferred Homotopies $H_X,H_Y$ Then for the product topology and the maps

\begin{align*} (f \times g), (f' \times g'): X_1 \times Y_1 \rightarrow X_2 \times Y_2 \end{align*}

it follows that

\begin{align*} f \times g \sim f' \times g' && \mathrm{rel} A \times B \end{align*}

with a preferred Homotopy

\begin{align*} (H_X \times H_Y) \end{align*}

Proof

By assumption, there exist homotopies $H_f,H_g$ from $f$ ($g$) to $f'$ ($g'$). Thus, take

\begin{align*} H_{f \times g}: [0,1] \times (X_1 \times Y_1) \rightarrow& (X_2 \times Y_2) \\ (t,x,y) \mapsto& (H_f(t,x), H_g(t,y)) \end{align*}

it is continuous as composition
