category Fin partial equivalent to Fin*

Proposition

The category Fin* and category Fin partial are equivalent via the functor

\begin{align*} ()_+: \mathrm{Fin}^{\partial} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Fin}_* \\ <n>^{\circ} \mapsto& <n> \\ (f^{\circ}: <n>^{\circ} \rightarrow <m>^{\circ}) \mapsto& f: <n> \mapsto <m> \end{align*}

where the elements without assignment under $f^{\circ}$ are mapped to $\{*\}$

Proof
