equivalence of gap and functors out of J

Proposition

Let $J = J_0 \sqcup \{- \infty\}$ be a linearly ordered set containing an initial object. Let $\iota: J_0 \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Ar}(J)$ be the inclusion via

\begin{align*} i \mapsto (-\infty, i) \end{align*}

Then the restriction along $\iota$ induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Gap}(J, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(J, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

for a pointed category

Proof

fully faithful

essentially surjective

Let $\mathfrak{J} = \{(i,j) \in J \times J_0 : (i = -\infty) \lor (i = j)\}$ be the union of the inclusion and the constant functor.

  2. HTT 4.3.2.13 the functor admits a left kan extension
