k-algebra modulo Rad is a product of matrix algebras

Proposition

Let $A$ be a finite dimensional K-Algebra Then

\begin{align*} A/\mathrm{Rad}(A) \cong \bigopuls_{V_i} \mathrm{Mat}_{n_i}(D_i^{\mathrm{op}}) \end{align*}

where $V_i$ is an enumeration of isomorphism classes of simple representations and $D_i = \mathrm{End}_{A}(V_i), n_i = \mathrm{dim}_{D_i} V_i$

Proof
