injective abelian groups are closed under colimits

Proposition

the subcategory of injective abelian groups are closed under arbitrary colimits.

Proof

we use Baer's criterion, so we show that the colimit is still divisible. So let $\mathrm{colim} A_i$ be a colimit and $p$ a prime. We need to show that that $\cdot p: \mathrm{colim} A_i \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} A_i$ is surjective. Recall that $\mathrm{colim}$ is right exact (cf. colimit functor as right exact functor), so in particular also additive. Hence $\mathrm{colim}(\cdot p) = \cdot p$ and

But the colimit functor preserves epimorphisms, so $\cdot p: \mathrm{colim} A_i \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} A_i$ is surjective.
