intersection of an integral domain with a prime ideal of the polynomial ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be an Integral domain, $R[X_1,...,X_n]$ the polynomial ring in $n$ variables and $\mathfrak{p} \subseteq R[X_1,...,X_n]$ a prime ideal. Then

\begin{align*} R \cap \mathfrak{p} \subseteq R \end{align*}

is a prime ideal in $R$
