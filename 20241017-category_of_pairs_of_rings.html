category of ideal pairs of rings

Definition

The category of ideal pairs of rings is defined as category containing

  1. pairs $(R, \mathfrak{a})$ where $R$ is a ring, $\mathfrak{a}$ an ideal
  2. morphisms consist of ring-homomorphism $\varphi: R \rightarrow S$ such that $\varphi[\mathfrak{a}] \subseteq \mathfrak{b}$
