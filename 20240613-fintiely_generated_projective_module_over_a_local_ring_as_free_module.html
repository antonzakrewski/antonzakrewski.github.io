finitely generated projective module over a local ring as free module

Proposition

Let $R$ be a local ring and $P$ a finitely generated projective module Then $P$ is a free module

Proof

Let $k = R/\mathfrak{m}$ be the residue field. Let $p_1,...,p_n$ be fibres of a basis of $k \otimes_R P$.

Then $p_1,...,p_n$ generate $P$ (cf. finitely generated Module over a local ring and fibres of a vector space basis as generators), hence

is an exact sequence.

This sequence splits, hence

is also exact (cf. additive functor preserves split exact sequences)

By construction $k \otimes_R R^n \rightarrow k \otimes_R P$ is an isomorphism, hence $k \otimes_R \mathrm{ker} = 0$. Hence $\mathrm{ker} = 0$, as the empty set generates $k \otimes_R \mathrm{ker}$ and $\mathrm{ker}$ is also generated by the empty set by finitely generated Module over a local ring and fibres of a vector space basis as generators
