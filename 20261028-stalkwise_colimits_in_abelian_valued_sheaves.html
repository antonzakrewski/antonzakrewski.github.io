stalkwise colimits in sheaves on a topological space

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable 1-category and $X$ a topological space. Let $I \rightarrow \mathrm{Shv}}(X,\mathcal{C})$ be a diagram of sheaves

Let $I^{\triangleright}$ be a cocone such that this cocone becomes - for every stalk - a colimit diagram. Then $I^{\triangleright}$ is already a colimit cone.

Proof

Use that

  • conservative functor reflects preserved colimits
  • stalkwise characterization of isomorphisms in sheaves of sets
  • stalk functor preserves colimits
