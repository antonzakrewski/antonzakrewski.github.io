coequalizer in Set

Proposition

Given the category set, sets $A,B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathrm{Set})$ and maps $\varphi, \psi: A \rightarrow B$, the coequalizer is the set

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Coeq}(\varphi, \psi) \coloneqq B / \sim \end{align*}

for the smallest equivalence relation such that $\pi \circ f(a) = \pi \circ g(a)$ with the usual projection $\pi$ as morphism 1

see: proof

Proof

equivalence relation

\begin{align*} b_1 \sim b_2 :\Leftrightarrow \begin{cases} b_1 = b_2 \\ \exists x_1,...,x_n : \varphi(x_i) = \psi(x_{i+1}), \varphi(x_1) = b_1, \varphi(x_n) = b_2 \\ \exists x_1,x_2 : \varphi(x_1) = \varphi(x_2), \psi(x_1) = b_1, \psi(x_2) = b_2 \\ \exists x_1,x_2 : \psi(x_1) = \psi(x_2), \varphi(x_1) = b_1, \varphi(x_2) = b_2 \end{cases} \end{align*}

note that this is reflexive ($b_1 = b_2$), transitive (existence of a zigzag) and symmetric (swapping $x_1,x_2$)

cofork

furthermore, by construction this is a cofork, as

\begin{align*} \pi \circ \varphi (x) = \pi \circ \psi(x) \end{align*}

by the second clause for $x_i = x$ and $i = 1$

universal property

Suppose

Then the map

\begin{align*} f': \mathrm{Coeq} \rightarrow& C \\ [b] \mapsto& f(b) \end{align*}

is the mediating morphism.

independent of representatives

Suppose $[b] = [b']$, then one of the following cases applies

1

$b = b'$, hence $f(b) = f(b')$

2

there exists a zigzag $x_1,...,x_n$, such that $\varphi(x_1) = b, \varphi(x_n) = b'$. Thus by $f \circ \varphi = f \circ \psi$, we conclude that

3 + 4

there exist $x_1,x_2$ such that $\varphi(x_1) = \varphi(x_2)$ and $\psi(x_1) = b, \psi(x_2) = b'$ Since $f \circ \varphi = f \circ \psi$ we get

\begin{align*} f(b) =& f \circ \psi(x_1) \\ =& f \circ \varphi(x_1) \\ =& f \circ \varphi(x_2) \\ =& f \circ \psi(x_2) \\ =& f(b') \end{align*}

unique

note that $[b] \mapsto f(b)$ is forced by $f'(\pi(b)) = f(b)$
