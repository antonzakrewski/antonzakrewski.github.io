global section functor for affine schemes

Definition

Given the category of affine schemes and category CRing the global section functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \Gamma: \mathfrak{Aff} \rightarrow& \mathrm{CRing} \mathcal{O}_X \mapsto& \mathcal{O}_X(X) (f^*, f^#) : \mathcal{O}_X \rightarrow \mathcal{O}_X') \mapsto& f^#(Y): \mathcal{O}_Y(Y) \rightarrow f_*\mathcal{O}_X(X) \end{align*}
