monomorphism and Hom

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $f: x \rightarrow y$ a morphism TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a monomorphism,
  2. the morphism $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(z,x) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(z,y)$ is a monomorphism for each object $z \in \mathcal{C}$.

Proof

  • monomorphisms in functor categories
  • yoneda embedding preserves monomorphisms
