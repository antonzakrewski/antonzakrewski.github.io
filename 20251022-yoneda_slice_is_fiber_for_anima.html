yoneda slice resp. coslice is fiber for anima

Proposition

Let $E \rightarrow B$ be a map of anima and $b\in B$ Then the slice category $E_{/b}$ resp. the coslice category $E_{b/}$ coincides with the fiber $E_b$

Proof

slice

follows from $\vert \Delta^1 \vert \simeq *$ and hence we get $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(\Delta^1, E) \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(\vert \Delta^1 \vert, E) \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(*, E) \simeq E$ resp.

via some

  • square with parallel equivalences as infinity pullback
  • glueing lemma for infinity pullbacks
  • window lemma for infinity pullbacks

coslice

follows from

  • opposite category functor is identity on Anima
