degreewise split exact sequence of chain maps and differential

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes and

\begin{align*} \iota: C_* \rightarrow E_* \end{align*}

a degreewise split mono. Then $E_*$ is given by 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
C_n \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & C_n \oplus D_n \arrow[]{d}{\begin{pNiceMatrix} d & \star \\ 0 & d' \end{pNiceMatrix} }\\
C_{n-1} \arrow[]{r}{}& C_{n-1} \oplus D_{n-1} \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

for $D_n \in \mathcal{A}$ and $d' \circ d' = 0$

Proof
