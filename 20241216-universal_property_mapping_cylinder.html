universal property mapping cylinder

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a continuous map and $Z$ a topological space. Then maps out of the mapping cylinder $\mathrm{Cyl}(f)$ are precisely given by

  1. a map $g: Y \rightarrow Z$
  2. a homotopy $X \times [0,1] \rightarrow Z$ to $g \circ f$
