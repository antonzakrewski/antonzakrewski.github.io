induced map for the completion of a noetherian ring as flat morphism

Proposition

Let $A$ be a noetherian ring, $\mathfrak{a}$ an ideal and $\widehat{A}$ the completion with respect to $\mathfrak{a}$

\begin{align*} \varphi: A \rightarrow \widehat{A} \end{align*}

the induced map into the completion of a ring.

Then $\varphi$ is a flat ring morphism

Proof
