stalk of the localization presheaf

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $\mathrm{local}(A)$ the localization presheaf. Then for a point $\mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ stalk is given by the ring localization the prime ideal

\begin{align*} A_{\mathfrak{p}} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from the universal property of a ring localization.

A morphism $\mathrm{colim} (A \setminus \bigcap_{\mathfrak{p} \in U_i} \mathfrak{p})^{-1} A \rightarrow R$ is given by morphisms $(A \setminus \bigcup \mathfrak{p})^{-1} \rightarrow S$ such that $\varphi[(A \setminus \bigcup \mathfrak{p})] \in S^{\times}$
