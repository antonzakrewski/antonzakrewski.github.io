associated bundle

Definition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a principal g-bundle and $F$ a topological space equipped with a left topological group action. Then the associated bundle is defined as

\begin{align*} E \times_G F \rightarrow B \end{align*}

where is the orbital collapse

\begin{align*} E \times_G F = E \times F / \sim \end{align*}

under the Group Action

\begin{align*} G \times (E \times F) \rightarrow& E \times F \\ (g,) \end{align*}
