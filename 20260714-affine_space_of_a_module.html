affine space of a module

Definition

Let $k$ be a commutative ring and $M$ a \(k\)-module, then the affine space of $M$ is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathbb{A}[M]: \mathrm{CAlg}_k \rightarrow& \mathrm{Set} \\ R \mapsto& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Mod}_k}(M, R) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Mod}_R}(M \otimes_{k} R, R) \\ \end{align*}
