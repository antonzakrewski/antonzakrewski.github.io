\begin{align*} \Psi_{p}: \tau_{\leq 1}(E) \rightarrow& \mathrm{hTop}_* e \mapsto& (E_{p(e)},e) \\ (\gamma: e \leadsto e') \mapsto& \Psi_p(\gamma) \end{align*}

where $\Psi_{p}(\gamma)$ is given by choosing a lift
