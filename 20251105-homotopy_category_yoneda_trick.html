homotopy category yoneda trick for anima

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a map of anima. TFAE:

  1. $f: X \rightarrow Y$ is an equivalence
  2. for all test anima $T$ the map $\mathrm{map}(T,f): \mathrm{map}(T,X) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}(T,Y)$ is a $\pi_0$ equivalence

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

postcomposition with equivalence

2) $\implies$ 1)
