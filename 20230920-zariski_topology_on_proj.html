Zariski topology on Proj

Definition

Let $M$ be a monoid, $R = \bigoplus_{m \in m} R_m$ a graded ring and $\mathrm{Proj}$ the Proj. Then the Zariski topology on $\mathrm{Proj}$ is defined by the closed sets

\begin{align*} V(\mathfrak{a}) = \{\mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm{Proj} \vert \mathfrak{a} \subseteq \mathfrak{p}\} \end{align*}

where $\mathfrak{a}$ is a homogeneous ideal

Beweis

  • union of closed sets in proj
  • intersection of closed sets in proj
