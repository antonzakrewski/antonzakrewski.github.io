compact closed unit ball and finite dimensional normed space

Proposition

Let $X$ be a normed vector space. TFAE:

  1. $\overline{B_{1}(0)} = \{x \in X : \| x \| \leq 1\}$ is compact
  2. $\mathrm{dim}(X)$ is finite

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

use:

  • Äquivalenz aller Normen auf endlichen reellen Vektorraum
  • Satz von Heine-Borel

2) $\implies$ 1)

Pick an $x_1$ in $X$ with $\| x_1 \| = 1$. Let $U_1 = \mathrm{span}(x_1) \subsetneq X$ be the closed subspace (cf. finite dimensional subspace of a normed space is closed and complete subspace )spanned by $x_1$. Then riesz lemma applied for $\lambda = \frac{1}{2}$ yields the existence of an $x_2 \in X \setminus U_1$ such that $\| x_2 \| = 1$ and $\| x_1 - x_2 \| \geq \mathrm{dist}(U_1,x_2) \geq \frac{1}{2}$. Now we may repeat this for $U_2 = \mathrm{span}(x_1,x_2)$ and get a sequence $x_n$ with $\| x_i - x_j \| \geq \frac{1}{2}$, hence not Cauchy. Therfore, $\overline{B_{1}(0)}$ cannot be sequentially compact or equivalently (since $X$ is a metric space) compact
