tensor product preserves colimits

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod. Then the tensor functor

\begin{align*} -\otimes_R - : \mathrm{R-Mod} \times \mathrm{Mod-R} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ab} \end{align*}

preserves colimits

Proof

corollary of:

  • tensor functor as left adjoint
  • left adjoint preserves colimits
