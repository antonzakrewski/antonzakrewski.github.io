formal derivation of a power series

Definition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $A [ [X]]$ the power series ring. Then the formal derivation is defined as

\begin{align*} \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}: A[ [X] ] \rightarrow& A [ [ X] ] \\ (a_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mapsto& ((n+1) \cdot a_{n+1}) \end{align*}
