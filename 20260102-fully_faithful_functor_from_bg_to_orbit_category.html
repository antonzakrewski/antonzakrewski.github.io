fully faithful functor from BG to orbit category

Proposition

Let $G$ be a (discrete) group Then there exists a fully faithful

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathrm{BG} \rightarrow \mathrm{Orb}_G \end{align*}

which evaluates as $\iota(*) = G/1 \cong G$
