kappa compact spectra and kappa small homotopy groups

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be an uncountable regular cardinal. The subcategory of kappa Spectra is equivalently characterized as full subcategory of the category of Spectra

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sp}^{\kappa} = \{X \in \mathrm{Sp} \vert \vert \pi_*(X) \vert < \kappa \} \end{align*}

Proof

Let $\mathcal{D} = \{X \in \mathrm{Sp} \vert \vert \pi_*(X) \vert < \kappa \}$. Observe that $\mathcal{D}$ is closed under

  1. $\kappa$ small coproducts as $\pi_*(-)$ is an homotopy functor is an additive
  2. cofibers as kappa small abelian groups is a serre class

hence $\mathcal{D}$ is closed under $\kappa$ small colimits and contains the sphere spectrum (cf. subcategory of a stable category closed under kappa small coproducts is closed under kappa small colimits, weak serre finiteness theorem of homotopy groups of spheres). Therefore $\mathrm{Sp}^{\kappa} \subseteq \mathcal{D}$.
