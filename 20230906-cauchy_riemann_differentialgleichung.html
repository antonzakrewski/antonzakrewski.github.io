Cauchy-Riemann equation

Satz

Sei $f: U \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ eine Funktion für $U \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ offen.

TFAE:

  1. $f$ ist holomorph
  2. für $f$ aufgefasst als Funktion $g(x,y) \coloneqq f(x + \mathrm{i} y)$ gilt
\begin{align*} \frac{\partial g}{\partial x} = - \mathrm{i} \frac{\partial g}{\partial y} \end{align*}

Beweis

1) $\implies$ 2)

Suppose $f$ is holomorphic. Then in particular

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_{h \rightarrow 0} \frac{f(z_0 + h) - f(z_0) - f'(z_0) h}{h} = 0 \end{align*}

and we conclude that

2) $\implies$ 1)

Identify $F = (u,v)^T$ for $u,v: \mathbb{R}^2 \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$. Then since $u,v$ are differentiable we get for $H = (H_1,H_2)^T$

\begin{align*} u(x_0 + H_1,y_0 + H_2) - u(x_0,y_0) = \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} H_1 + \frac{\partial u}{y} H_2 + \vert H \vert \psi(H) \\ v(x_0 + H_1,y_0 + H_2) - v(x_0,y_0) = \frac{\partial v}{\partial x} H_1 + \frac{\partial u}{y} H_2 + \vert H \vert \psi(H) \\ \end{align*}

for $\psi_1(H),\psi_2(H) \rightarrow 0$ as $H \rightarrow 0$. Let $h \coloneqq H_1 + \mathrm{i} H_2$. Then

\begin{align*} f(z_0 + h) - f(z) = \left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + \mathrm{i} \frac{\partial v}{\partial x}\right) H_1 + \left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} + \mathrm{i} \frac{\partial v}{\partial y}\right) H_2 + \vert h \vert (\psi_1(H) + \mathrm{i} \psi_2(H)) \end{align*}

which evaluates as

\begin{align*} \left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + \mathrm{i} \frac{\partial v}{\partial x}\right) H_1 + \left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} + \mathrm{i} \frac{\partial v}{\partial y}\right) H_2 + \vert h \vert (\psi_1(H) + \mathrm{i} \psi_2(H)) =& \left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} - \mathrm{i} \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right) (H_1 + \mathrm{i} H_2) + \vert h \vert \psi(h) \end{align*}

for $\psi(h) \coloneqq (\psi_1(H) + \mathrm{i} \psi_2(H))$ where $\psi(h) \rightarrow 0$ as $h \rightarrow 0$

Hence $f$ is given as linear function with vanishing remanider
