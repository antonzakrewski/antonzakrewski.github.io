1-sfp objects in Grp

Proposition

The 1-sfp objects in category Group are precisely the finitely generated free groups

Proof

1-sfp implies finite free

We will show that any 1-sfp object is a retract of a finitely generated free group, hence itself finitely generated and free (cf. subgroup of a free group as free group)

Let $G$ be 1-sfp, in particular compact, hence in particular finitely presented. Thus consider a generating set $S$ together with relations $R$.

This induces a reflexive coequalizer

(cf. coequalizer and reflexive coequalizer in a category with coproducts)

hence by 1-sfp object as sifted colimit and retract we may conclude that $G$ is a retract of $F_S \copord F_G$ or $F_S$, in particular a subgroup of $F_S$

finite free implies 1-sfp

follows from: forgetful functor from Grp to Set preserves sifted colimits and finite coproducts of 1-sfp objects as sfp object where

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Set}(-) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Grp}}(\mathbb{Z}, -) \end{align*}
