boxtensor of morphisms

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $f: X \rightarrow Y, g: W \rightarrow Z \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$ be morphisms and Then

\begin{align*} g \boxtimes f: W \times Y \coprod_{W \times X} Z \times X \rightarrow Z \times X \end{align*}
