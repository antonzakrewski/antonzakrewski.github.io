Zariski pretopology

Definition / Proposition

The zariski pretopology on $\mathrm{CAlg}^{\mathrm{op}}$ is the pretopology whose covers are the families $(R \rightarrow R_{f_i})$ such that $(f_i)$ generate the unit ideal. Observe that in $\mathrm{CAlg}^{\mathrm{op}}$, the morphisms are given by $R_{f_i} \rightarrow R$

Proof

isomorphisms are coverings

clear

composition of coverings

observe that in $\mathrm{CAlg}^{\mathrm{op}}$, we have $R_{f_i} \otimes_{R} R_{f_j} \cong R_{f_i f_j}$ and that $(f_i f_j)_{i,j}$ generates the unit ideal if and only if $f_i$ generates the unit ideal (by the trick $1 \cdot 1 = 1$.)

pullbacks

let $(R_{f_i} \rightarrow R$ be a covering in $\mathrm{CAlg}^{\mathrm{op}}$ and $\varphi:S \rightarrow R$ a mophsim. Then $\varphi(f_i)$ generates the unit ideal, i.e. $S_{\varphi(f_i)} \cong S \otimes_{R} R_{f_i} \rightarrow S$ is a covering.
