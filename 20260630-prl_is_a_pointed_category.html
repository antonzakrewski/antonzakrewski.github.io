PrL is a pointed category

Proposition

the category PrL is a pointed category.

Proof

follows as the terminal category is presentable and

  1. any the terminal functor $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow *$ admits a right adjoint, which maps $* \mapsto *$ to the terminal object
  2. $\mathrm{Fun}^{L}(*, \mathcal{C}) \simeq \{\emptyset\} \simeq *$ as left adjoints preserve colimits
