fundamental theorem of topological manifolds

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{tEuc}$ be the category tEuc and $\mathrm{tMfd}$ be category of topological manifolds. Then the inclusion $\mathrm{tEuc} \rightarrow \mathrm{tMfd}$ induces an equivalence upon idempotent completion.

In particular $\mathrm{tEuc}^{\natural}$ contains $\mathrm{tMfd}$ as full subcategory and for any idempotent complete category $\mathcal{C}$ the inclusion induces an equivalence on functor categories

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{tEuc}, \mathcal{C}) \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{tMfd}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
