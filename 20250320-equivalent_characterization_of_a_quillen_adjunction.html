equivalent characterization of a quillen adjunction

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}_{\mathcal{C}}, \mathrm{Cof}_{\mathcal{C}}, \mathrm{Fib}_{\mathcal{C}}), (\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{W}_{\mathcal{D}}, \mathrm{Cof}_{\mathcal{D}}, \mathrm{Fib}_{\mathcal{D}})$ be model categories and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L} \dashv \mathcal{R} \end{align*}

an adjunction

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{L}$ preserves cofibrations and trivial cofibrations
  2. $\mathcal{R}$ preserves fibrations and trivial fibrations
  3. $\mathcal{L}$ preserves cofibrations and $\mathcal{R}$ preserves fibrations
  4. $\mathcal{L}$ preserves trivial cofibration and $\mathcal{R}$ preserves trivial fibration

Proof

  • bijection lifting problem solutions for an adjunction
  • where (trivial) fibrations / cofibraitons are characterized by lifting properties
