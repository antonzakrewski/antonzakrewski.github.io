homotopy groups of a mapping spectrum of abelian groups

Proposition

Let $A,B$ be abelian groups, then the map $\tilde{\mathbb{S}[K(A,n)]} \rightarrow \Sigma^n A$ is n-connective (index ?), i.e.

\begin{align*} \pi_{-i}(\mathrm{map}(A,B)) \rightarrow H^{i+n}(K(A,n);B) \end{align*}

is an

  1. isomorphism for $i < n$
  2. monomorphism for $i =n$

Proof

consider the counit

\begin{align*} \tilde{\mathbb{S}}[K(A,n)] \rightarrow \Sigma^n A \end{align*}

of the infinite loop space adjunction.

First we note that it induces the identity on $\pi_n(-)$ (todo). Then using low stable homotopy groups of an Eilenberg-MacLane space we get that the map

\begin{align*} \tilde{\mathbb{S}}[K(A,n)] \rightarrow \Sigma^n A \end{align*}

is $2n+1$ connected, so in particular the cofiber $\mathrm{cofib}$ is $2n+1$ connected.

Now we may use the connected / coconnected adjunction to get that

\begin{align*} \pi_{-i} \mathrm{map}(\mathrm{cofib},B) \end{align*}

is given by $0$ for $i \leq n$

Hence the resulting claim follows from the long exact sequence homotopy groups for a spectrum
