tensor product compatible with colimits

Definition

Let $X$ be a collection of categories and $\mathcal{C}$ a monoidal category Then $\mathcal{C}$ is said to be compatible with \(X\)-shaped colimits if

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ admits \(X\)-shaped colimits
  2. $- \otimes -$ preserves \(X\)-shaped colimits in both variables.
