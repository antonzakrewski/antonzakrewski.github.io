higher homotopy groups of Hom of abelian groups in the heart

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category and $x,y \in \mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}$. Then for $* > 0$

\begin{align*} \pi_*(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y)) = 0 \end{align*}

Proof

follows as $\mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}$ is a (1,1)-Category
