factorization algebra

Definition

let $(\mathcal{C}, \otimes)$ be a symmetric monoidal infinity category and $\mathcal{U}^{\otimes}$ be the operad of a family of subsets for $\mathcal{U} \subseteq \mathrm{Op}(X)$ for a partially ordered subset of a hausdorff topological space. A prefactorization algebra $\mathcal{A}: \mathcal{U}^{\otimes} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is said to be a factorization algebra, if

  1. it is multiplicative, i.e. for all $U_1,...,U_n \in \mathcal{U}$ pairwise disjoint with $U_1 \coprod ... \coprod U_n \in \mathcal{U}$ the map
\begin{align*} \mathcal{A}(U_1) \otimes ... \otimes \mathcal{A}(U_n) \rightarrow \mathcal{A}(U_1 \coprod ... \coprod U_n) \end{align*}
