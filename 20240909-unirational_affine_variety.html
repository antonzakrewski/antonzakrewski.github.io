unirational affine variety

Definition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be an affine variety. Then $X$ is said to be unirational, if for the function field there exists an injective morphism

\begin{align*} k(X) \rightarrow k(X_1,...,X_n) \end{align*}

i.e. a dominant rational map between affine varieties

\begin{align*} \mathbb{A}_K^n \rightarrow X \end{align*}
