universal property of a quotient field

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring, $K$ a field, $\varphi: R \rightarrow K$ a ringmonomorphism and $Q(R)$ the quotient ring. Then there exists a unique homomorphism $\psi: Q(R) \rightarrow K$ such that following diagram commutes

Proof

existence

Let

\begin{align*} \psi: Q(R) \rightarrow& K \frac{r}{s} \mapsto& \varphi(r) \cdot \varphi(s)^{-1} \end{align*}

welldefined

Let $\frac{r_1}{s_1} = \frac{r_2}{s_2}$, then

\begin{align*} r_1 s_2 = r_2 s_1 \end{align*}

Hence

\begin{align*} \varphi(r_1) \varphi(s_2) =& \varphi(r_2) \varphi(s_1) && \vert \cdot \varphi(s_1)^{-1}\varphi(s_2)^{-1} \\ \varphi(r_1) \varphi(s_1)^{-1} =& \varphi(r_2) \varphi(s_2)^{-1} \end{align*}

ring-homomorphism

additive
\begin{align*} \psi(\frac{r_1}{s_1} + \frac{r_2}{s_2}) =& \psi(\frac{r_1 s_2 + r_2s_1}{s_1s_2}) \\ =& \varphi(r_1s_2 + r_2s_1) \varphi(s_1)^{-1}\varphi(s_2)^{-1} \\ =& \varphi(r_1)\varphi(s_2) \varphi(s_1)^{-1}\varphi(s_2)^{-1} + \varphi(r_2)\varphi(s_1) \varphi(s_1)^{-1}\varphi(s_2)^{-1} \\ =& \varphi(r_1)\varphi(s_1)^{-1} + \varphi(r_2)\varphi(s_2)^{-1} \end{align*}
multiplicative
\begin{align*} \psi( \frac{r_1}{s_1} \cdot \frac{r_2}{s_2}) =& \psi( \frac{r_1r_2}{s_1s_2}) \\ =& \varphi(r_1r_2) \varphi(s_1s_2)^{-1} \\ =& \varphi(r_1) \varphi(s_1)^{-1} \varphi(r_2) \varphi(s_2)^{-1} \\ =& \psi(\frac{r_1}{s_1}) \psi(\frac{r_2}{s_2}) \end{align*}

commutes

By assumption,

\begin{align*} \psi(\iota(r)) =& \psi(\frac{r}{1}) \\ =& \psi(r) \cdot \psi(1)^{-1} \\ =& \varphi(r) \end{align*}

uniqueness

Since $\psi(\frac{r}{1}) = \varphi(r)$ is induced, we conclude

\begin{align*} 1 =& \psi(1) \\ =& \psi(\frac{1}{s} \cdot \frac{s}{1}) \\ =& \psi(\frac{1}{s}) \cdot \psi(\frac{s}{1}) \\ =& \varphi(s)^{-1} \cdot \varphi(s) \end{align*}

Therefore, $\psi$ as constructed is the unique homomorphism
