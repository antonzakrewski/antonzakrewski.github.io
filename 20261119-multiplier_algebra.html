multiplier C^* algebra

Definition

Let $A \in C^*-\mathrm{Alg}^{\mathrm{nu}}$ be a (possibly nonunital) C star algebra. Then let

\begin{align*} \mathcal{E} = \{A \rightarrow B \vert \text{ideal inclusions}, \text{morphism, } B \rightarrow B' \(ext{ are unital}\} \end{align*}

Then the multiplier algebra $M(A)$ is the terminal object in this category, if it exists.
