Proposition

Then there exists a fully faithful inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}} \end{align*}

given by

\begin{align*} (u: Y \rightarrow X) \mapsto (X,Y,u,v) \end{align*}

where we define $v$ as composition

\begin{align*} \tilde{j}(X) \rightarrow j(X) = \lim_{\substack{Z\subset X,\\ Z\in\mathcal{B}, X/Z\in\mathcal{B}}} Z \xrightarrow{\mathrm{pr}_{Y}} Y \end{align*}

Furthermore the essential image of $\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}}$ consists of all elements where $B \hookrightarrow A$ is a monomorphism
