inclusion of the bousfield class admits a right adjoint

Proposition

Let $E$ be a spectrum, then the inclusion $\langle E\rangle \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}$ admits a right adjoint

Proof

follows from:

  • bousfield class of a kappa compact spectrum is kappa compactly generated
  • infinity left adjoint functor theorem for presentable categories / infinity right adjoint functor theorem for presentable categories
