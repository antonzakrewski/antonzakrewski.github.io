H-representable functor

Definition

Let

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

be an infinity functor

Then it is \(H\)-representable, if after applying the infinity homotopy category functor the functor

\begin{align*} h \mathcal{F}: h \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow h \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

is 1-enriched representable over the homotopy category of anima as closed monoidal category
