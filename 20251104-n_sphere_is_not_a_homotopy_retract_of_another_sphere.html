n-sphere is not a homotopy retract of a m-sphere

Proposition

Let $S^n,S^m$ be the n- resp. m-sphere. TFAE:

  1. $n = m$
  2. $S^n$ is a topological retract of $S^m$
  3. $S^n$ is a homotopy retract of $S^m$

2) $\implies$ 3)
