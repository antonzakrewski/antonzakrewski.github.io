cotangent bundle of a smooth manifold admits canonical symplectic form

Proposition

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold. Then the dual tangent bundle admits a canonical symplectic structure, where the symplectic form si given by

\begin{align*} \omgea = \sum_{i} d q^i \wedge d p_i \end{align*}

where $q^i,p_i$ are local coordinates on $T^M$
