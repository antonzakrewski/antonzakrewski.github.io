characterization of dualizable stable categories

Proposition (Lurie, Efimov)

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable presentable category.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ is a dualizable stable category (dualizable object with respect to the lurie tensor product in $\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\mathrm{st}}$)
  2. the colimit functor $\mathrm{colim}: \mathrm{Ind}_{\omega}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ admits a left adjoint, i.e. it is compactly assembled category
  3. there exists a <localization/ epi> $\mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ in $\mathrm{Pr}^{L, \mathrm{st}}_{\omega}$ such that $C \cong \mathrm{fib}(\varphi)$,
  4. $0 \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ has the left lifting property with respect to to <localizations> in $\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\mathrm{st}}$.
  5. $\mathcal{C}$ is a retract of a compactly generated category
  6. the functor $\mathcal{C} \otimes -: \mathrm{Pr}^{L, \mathrm{st}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Pr}^{L, \mathrm{st}}$ preserves fully faithful functors (flatness, efimov)
  7. $\mathcal{C}$ satisfies Ab6 (clausen-scholze)
