characterization of dualizable stable categories

Proposition (Lurie, Efimov)

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable presentable category.

TFAE:

  1. there exists a <localization> $\mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ in $\mathrm{Pr}^{L, \mathrm{st}}_{\omega}$ such that $C \cong \mathrm{fib}(\varphi)$,
  2. $\mathcal{C}$ is a dualizable stable category (dualizable object with respect to the lurie tensor product)
  3. the colimit functor $\mathrm{colim}: \mathrm{Ind}_{\omega}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ admits a left adjoint, i.e. it is compactly assembled category
  4. $\mathcal{C}$ is a retract of a compactly generated category
  5. the functor $\mathcal{C} \otimes -: \mathrm{Pr}^{L, \mathrm{st}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Pr}^{L, \mathrm{st}}$ preserves fully faithful functors (flatness, efimov)
  6. $\mathcal{C}$ satisfies Ab6 (clausen-scholze)
