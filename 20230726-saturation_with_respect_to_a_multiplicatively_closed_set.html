saturation with respect to a multiplicatively closed set

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $S \subseteq R$ a multiplicatively closed set and $A \subseteq R$ a subset. Then the saturation of $A$ with respect to $S$ is defined as

\begin{align*} A^S = \{a \in R \vert \exists s \in S : a \cdot s \in A\} \end{align*}
