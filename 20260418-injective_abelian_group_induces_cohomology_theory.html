injective abelian group induces cohomology theory

Proposition

Let $A$ be an injective abelian group. Then the functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\pi_n(-),A): \mathrm{Sp} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab} X \mapsto \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\pi_n{X}, A) \end{align*}

forms a cohomology theory.

Proof

follows as $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(-, A)$ is an exact functor
