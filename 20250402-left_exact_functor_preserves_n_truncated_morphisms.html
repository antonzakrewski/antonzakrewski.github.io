left exact functor preserves n-truncated morphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories which admit finite infinity limits and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a left exact functor Then $\mathcal{F}$ preserves n-truncated morphisms

Proof

Proof by induction

induction basis

follows from

  • -2 truncated morphism as equivalence
  • equivalence in an infinity category under functors

induction step

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a morphism. Then $f$ is \(n\)-truncated if and only if $\Delta^f: X \rightarrow X \times_Y X$ is \(n-1\)-truncated (cf. n-truncated morphism and diagonal).

By induction $\mathcal{F}(\Delta^f)$ is \(n-1\)-truncated.

But as $\mathcal{F}$ preserves finite limits, we get that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(\Delta^f) \simeq \Delta^{\mathcal{F}(f)} \end{align*}

and furthermore again by n-truncated morphism and diagonal that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(f) \end{align*}

is \(n-1+1\)- resp. \(n\)-truncated
