3 times 3 lemma for fibers

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a finitely complete pointed category and

a diagram, where

  1. all three rows are fiber sequences
  2. $A_2 \rightarrow B_2 \rightarrow C_2, A_3 \rightarrow B_3 \rightarrow C_3$ are fiber sequences.

Then so is $A_1 \rightarrow B_1 \rightarrow C_1$.
