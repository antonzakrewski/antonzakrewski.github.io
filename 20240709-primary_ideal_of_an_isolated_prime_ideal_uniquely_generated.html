primary ideal of an isolated prime ideal uniquely generated

Proposition

Proof

Let $\mathfrak{p}_i$ be an isolated prim, then $\mathfrak{q}_i$ is uniquely determined

\begin{align*} A \rightarrow A_{\mathfrak{p}_i} \end{align*}

with

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a} \mapsto \mathfrak{a}^{e} \mapsto \mathfrak{a}^{e c} = \mathfrak{q}_i \end{align*}
