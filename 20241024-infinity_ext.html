infinity Ext of a stable infinity category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category, $X,Y \in \mathcal{C}_0$. Then the ext group is defined as abelian group

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{C}}^n(X,Y) \coloneqq \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X[-n], Y)) \end{align*}

Proof

  • additive infinity category and homotopy category
  • stable infinity category as additive infinity category
