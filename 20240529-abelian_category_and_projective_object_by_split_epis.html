abelian category and projective object by split epis

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $P \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{A})$

TFAE:

  1. $P$ is a projective object
  2. each epimorphism $f: A \twoheadrightarrow P$ is split epi

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

corollary of epimorphism onto a projective splits

2) $\implies$ 1)
