category of finite spectra is sequential colimit

Proposition

The category of finite spectra is a sequential colimit

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sp}^{\omega} \simeq \mathrm{colim}_{\mathbb{Z}} ( ... \mathrm{An}_*^{\omega} \xrightarrow{\Sigma} \mathrm{An}_*^{\omega} \xrightarrow{\Sigma} ...) \end{align*}

Proof

Recall that spectra is the colimit

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sp} \simeq \mathrm{colim}_{\mathbb{Z}} ( ... \mathrm{An}_* \xrightarrow{\Sigma} \mathrm{An}_* \xrightarrow{\Sigma} ...) \end{align*}

in category PrL. Now the diagram restricts to PrL omega (as $\mathrm{An}_*$ is $\omega$ presentable and kappa small colimits of kappa compact objects are kappa compact) and the forgetful functor from PrL kappa to PrL preserves colimits, so in particular we may compute the colimit in $\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\omega}$.

Now gabriel-ulmer duality shows that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sp}^{\omega} \simeq \mathrm{colim}_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathrm{An}_*^{\omega} \xrightarrow{\Sigma} \mathrm{An}_*^{\omega} \xrightarrow{\Sigma} ...) \end{align*}

is the colimit in category Rex kappa, as equivalences of categories preserve colimits.

But the forgetful functor from Rex_kappa preserves kappa filtered colimits, so we get that $\mathrm{Sp}^{\omega}$ is the colimit in $\mathrm{Cat}$.
