Proposition

Let $U \subseteq X$ be an open subspace of a topological space $X$ and $\mathcal{F}$ an abelian sheaf. Then there exists a short exact sequence

Proof

observe that we have a sequence of sheaves after sheafification, which does not change stalks.

Then stalk of the extension of zero of Ab valued sheaves and stalks direct image sheaf of the inclusion of an closed subset imply that this is stalkwise given by the

\begin{align*} 0 \rightarrow \mathcal{F}_{z} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{id}} \mathcal{F}_z \\ \mathcal{F}_u \xrightarrow{\mathrm{id}} \mathcal{F}_{u} \rightarrow 0 \end{align*}

for $z \in Z$ and $u \in U$. These are evidently exact sequences, hence the sequence above is exact on sheaves.
