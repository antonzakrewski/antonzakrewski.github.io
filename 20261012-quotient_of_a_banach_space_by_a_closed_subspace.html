quotient of a banach space by a closed subspace

Proposition

Let $X$ be a banach space and $A \subseteq X$ a closed subspace. Then the quotient space $X/A$ admits a banach space structure

Proof

a) normed

  • quotient of a normed vector space is a normed vector space

complete

Let $[x_n] \in X/A$ be cauchy, then choose $y_n \in X$ with $\| [ x_n ] \| + \frac{1}{n} > \| y_n \|$ and $y_n \in [x_n]$. In particular, $y_n$ is a cauchy sequence in $X$ and converges to some $y$ in $X$. Furthermore, continuity of $X \rightarrow X/A$ implies that $[x_n] \rightarrow [y]$, i.e., $X/A$ is complete.
