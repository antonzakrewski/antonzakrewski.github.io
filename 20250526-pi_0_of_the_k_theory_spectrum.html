pi_0 of the direct sum k-theory spectrum

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $K(R)$ the algebraic k-theory spectrum Then for the k-theory spectrum of a ring $K(R)$

\begin{align*} \pi_0(K(R)) \simeq K_0(R) \end{align*}

coincide with the classical diagram

Proof

consider the diagram (a priori non commuting) 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{CMon}(\mathrm{An}) \dar{\tau_{\leq 0}} \rar{(-)^{\mathrm{grp}}} & \mathrm{CGrp}(\mathrm{An}) \dar{\tau_{\leq 0}} \\
\mathrm{CMon}(\mathrm{Set) \rar{(-)^{\mathrm{grp}}} & \mathrm{CGrp}(\mathrm{Set}) \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

which consists of left adjoints

We have to show that this commutes

Then by commuting diagram of adjoints and dualizing adjoint it suffices to show that 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{CMon}(\mathrm{An}) & \mathrm{CGrp}(\mathrm{An}) \lar{\iota}  \\
\mathrm{CMon}(\mathrm{Set) \uar{\iota} & \lar{\iota} \mathrm{CGrp}(\mathrm{Set}) \uar{\iota}\\
\end{tikzcd}
which consists of the inclusions of [[id:9e204c6a-f799-4623-ae56-89de8ef5a212][full subcategories]] (cf.[[id:062c1e6a-2c37-4b56-b0dc-f28505d978b8][inclusion of set to anima induces inclusion of groups / monoids]])
