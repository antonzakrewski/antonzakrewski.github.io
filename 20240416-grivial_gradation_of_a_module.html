grivial gradation of a module

Proposition / Definition

Let $R$ be a ring and $M$ an \(R\)-module. Then for the trivial gradation of $R$ there exists the trivial gradation of $M$ as graded module

\begin{align*} M = \bigoplus M_m \end{align*}

for

\begin{align*} M_m = \begin{cases} M & \mbox{if } m = 0 \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
