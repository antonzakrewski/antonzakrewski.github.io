right derived fuctor as half exact functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ a left exact functor. Suppose the right derived functor $\{\mathcal{RF}_n\}$ exists, then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{RF}_n \end{align*}

is half exact
