localization and intersection

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod Suppose $M$ is an \(R\)-module, $N_1,N_2 \subseteq M$ submodules Then

\begin{align*} S^{-1}(N_1 \cap N_2) = S^{-1}(N_1) \cap S^{-1}(N_2) \subseteq S^{-1}(N_i) \end{align*}

where $\cap$ denotes the intersection of modules

Proof

$S^{-1}(N_1 \cap N_2) \subseteq S^{-1}(N_1) \cap S^{-1}(N_2)$

Let $\frac{n}{s} \in S^{-1}(N_1 \cap N_2)$, then $n \in N_1,N_2$ showing that $\frac{n}{s} \in S^{-1}(N_i)$

$S^{-1}(N_1 \cap N_2) \supseteq S^{-1}(N_1) \cap S^{-1}(N_2)$

Let $\frac{n}{s} \in S^{-1}(N_1) \cap S^{-1}(N_2)$, then $n \in N_1,n \in N_2$.
