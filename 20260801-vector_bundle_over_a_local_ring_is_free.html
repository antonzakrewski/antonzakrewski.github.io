vector bundle over a local ring is free

Proposition

Let $M$ be an vector bundle over a local ring $R$. Then $M$ is free module.

Proof

Let $M/\mathfrak{m}$ be the vector space over $R/\mathfrak{p}$, then choose a basis $m_1,...,m_n \in M/\mathfrak{m}$ and lift it to a map $R^n \rightarrow M$. Then functor from Proj(R) to Proj(R/J) is a conservative functor for an ideal of the jacobson radical shows that $R^n \rightarrow M$ is an isomorphism
