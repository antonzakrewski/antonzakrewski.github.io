mean projection of a G-representation of a finite group

Proposition

Let $X$ be a representation of a finite group $G$ in $\mathrm{char}(k) \not\mid \vert G \vert$. Then the mean projection is defined as

\begin{align*} P: X \rightarrow X \\ x \mapsto \frac{1}{\vert G \vert}\sum_{g \in G} g x \end{align*}

is a morphism of G-representations.

Proof

remark: it the element

\begin{align*} \frac{1}{\vert G \vert} \in k[G] \end{align*}

which gives an element in the center of $k[G]$ hence in the center of a module category as center of the ring, i.e. a natural transformation
