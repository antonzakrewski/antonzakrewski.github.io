stable infinity category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category Then $\mathcal{C}$ is said to be a stable infinity category, if it is

  1. pointed infinity category
  2. every morphsim admits a fiber and cofiber
  3. a triangle is a fiber sequence if and only if it is a cofiber sequence

Note

  1. resembles existence of kernels / cokernels
  2. resembles the image coimage isomorphism
