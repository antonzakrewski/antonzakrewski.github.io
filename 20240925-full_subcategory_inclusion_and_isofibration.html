full quasi subcategory inclusion and isofibration

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathcal{C}' \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a full sub quasi category. Suppose $\mathcal{C}'_0$ is closed under equivalences, i.e. if $x,y \in \mathcal{C}_0$ are objects with $x \in \mathcal{C}'_0$, then

\begin{align*} y \in \mathcal{C}'_0 \end{align*}

Then the inclusion is an isofibration
