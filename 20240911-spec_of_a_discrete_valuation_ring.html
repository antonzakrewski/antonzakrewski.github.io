Spec of a discrete valuation ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be a discrete valuation ring. Then $\mathrm{Spec}(R) = \{0, \mathfrak{m}\}$

Proof

Let $\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime ideal. Then $(0) \subseteq \mathfrak{p} \subseteq \mathfrak{m}$

by dimension of a PID we conclude that either $(0) = \mathfrak{p}$ or $\mathfrak{m} = (0)$.
