isometric embedding from complete to dense space is surjective

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be metric spaces, $A \subseteq Y$ dense and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ an isometric embedding. Suppose $X$ is a complete metric space and $f[X]$ is dense Then $f$ is surjective

Proof

Let $y \in Y$, then there exists a convergent sequence $y_n \rightarrow y$. Observe that $f: X \rightarrow f[X]$ is an isometry, i.e. there exist unique preimages $x_n$. It follows that $x_n$ is a Cauchy sequence, as $f(x_n) = y_n$ is a cauchy sequence and $f$ is an isometric embedding. In particular, $x_n$ converges to some (unique) $x$. Moreover, $f$ is sequentially continiuous, hence $f(x) = \mathrm{lim} f(x_n) = y$.

Thus, $f$ is a surjection.
