irreducible component of a hausdorff space

Proposition

Let $X$ be a hausdorff topological space Then the irreducible components are precisely the singletons

Proof

Let $A \subseteq X$ be a nonempty set with $x,y \in A$. Then by assumption there exists disjoint opens $O_x,O_y$.

Then $A \setminus O_x, A \setminus O_y$ suffice as closed sets.
