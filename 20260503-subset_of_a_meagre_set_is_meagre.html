subset of a meager set is meager

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space and $A$ a meager subset. Then any subset $B \subseteq A$ is also meager.

Proof

Let $A = \bigcup_{n} A_n$ for nowhere dense subsets $A_n$. Then $B = \bigcup_{n} A_n \cap B$ where $A_n \cap B$ is also nowhere dense (cf. subset of a nowhere dense set is nowhere dense)
