weakly initial phantom map in spectra

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum, then there exists a spectrum $\tilde{X}$ and a $X \rightarrow \tilde{X}$ such that every phantom map $X \rightarrow Y$ factors through $\tilde{X}$, albeit not uniquely.
