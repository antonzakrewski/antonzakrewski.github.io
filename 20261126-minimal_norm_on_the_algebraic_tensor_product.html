minimal norm on the algebraic tensor product

Definition

Let $A,B$ be C star algebras. Then the minimal norm on $A \otimes^{\mathrm{alg}} B$ is defined as

\begin{align*} \| x \|_{\mathrm{min}} = \mathrm{sup}_{\pi: A \rightarrow B(H), \sigma: B \rightarrow B(H)} \| (\pi \otimes \sigma)(x) \|_{\mathbb{B}(H \otimes K)} \end{align*}
