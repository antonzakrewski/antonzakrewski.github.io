object in C_A,B is quotient of category of pairs

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{B}, \mathcal{A}$ be abelian full subcategories where $\mathcal{B}$ is closed under quotient objects and subobjects Then each object in C_A,B is a quotient of an object in the essential image of the category of pairs (cf. inclusion of category of pairs into C_A,B).

Proof

Let $B \rightarrow A$ be any object. Then let $A' = \mathrm{im}(B \rightarrow A)$.
