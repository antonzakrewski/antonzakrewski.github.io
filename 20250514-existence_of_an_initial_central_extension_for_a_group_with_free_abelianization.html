existence of an initial central extension for a group with free abelianization

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group where the Abelianization is a free abelian group. Then there exists an initial central extension

Proof

using the central extension and group cohomology we may describe central extension

in terms of group cohomology $x \in H^2(\mathrm{B}G,A)$.

Here we use first homology functor as abelianization of the first homotopy functor to get

\begin{align*} H_1(\mathrm{BG},\mathbb{Z}) \cong& G^{\mathrm{ab}} \\ \cong& \mathbb{Z}^{\kappa} \end{align*}

and hence by universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology + covariant ext for a fixed projective module

\begin{align*} H^2(\mathrm{B}G, A) = \mathrm{Hom}(H_2(\mathrm{B}G; \mathbb{Z}), A) \end{align*}

Let $A = H_2(\mathrm{B}G, \mathbb{Z})$, then under this isomorphism we get an element

\begin{align*} x \in H^2(\mathrm{B}G, H_2(\mathrm{B}G,\mathbb{Z})) \cong \mathrm{Hom}(H_2(\mathrm{B}G,A), H_2(\mathrm{B}G,A)) \end{align*}

which corresponds to the identity.

It follows, that this is universal:
