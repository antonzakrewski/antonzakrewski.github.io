bifunctor and pair of morphisms induces morphism to the pullback

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ a functor. Suppose that $\mathcal{E}$ admits (enough) pullbacks and $f: C_1 \rightarrow C_2, g: D_1 \rightarrow D_2$ be morphisms.

Then there exists a canonical map

\begin{align*} \widehat{\mathcal{F}(f,g)}: \mathcal{F}(C_1,D_1) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(C_1,D_2) \prod_{\mathcal{F}(C_2,D_2)} \mathcal{F}(C_2,D_1) \end{align*}
