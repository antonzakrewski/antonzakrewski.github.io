full image of a functor isomorphic to coimage

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor. Then there exists a canonical isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}': \mathrm{coim}(\mathcal{F}) \rightarrow \mathrm{im}(\mathcal{F}) \end{align*}

where

  1. $\mathrm{im}(\mathcal{F})$ is the full image
  2. $\mathrm{coim}(\mathcal{F})$ is the coimage of a functor

Proof

follows from isomorphism in category cat as surjective, fully faithful functor, the functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}': \mathrm{coim}(\mathcal{F}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{im}(\mathcal{F}) \\ [A] \mapsto& \mathcal{F}(A) \\ [f] \mapsto& \mathcal{F}(f) \end{align*}

suffices
