composition as surjection and never injective

Proposition

Let

be maps between sets

Assume that

  1. $g \circ f$ is surjective
  2. $f$ is not surjective.

Then $g$ is not injective

Proof

By assumption there exists an element $b \in B$ with $b \not\in \mathrm{im}(f)$ since $f$ is not surjective. Then consider $g(b) \in C$. We may find an element $a \in A$ with $(g \circ f)(a) = g(b)$. But $f(a) \in \mathrm{im}(f)$ or $f(a) \neq b$ with $g(f(a)) = g(b)$ Hence $g$ is not injective.
