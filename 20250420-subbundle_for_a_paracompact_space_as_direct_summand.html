subbundle for a paracompact space as direct summand

Proposition

Let $X$ be a paracompact space, $E$ a topological vector bundle and $E'$ a subbundle of a topological vector bundle. Then $E'$ is a direct summand, i.e. there exists a subbundle $\tilde{E}$ such that

\begin{align*} E' \oplus \tilde{E} = E \end{align*}

Proof
