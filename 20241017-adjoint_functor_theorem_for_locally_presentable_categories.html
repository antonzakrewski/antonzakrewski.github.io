left adjoint functor theorem for presentable categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable category, $\mathcal{D}$ a (sufficiently ?) cocomplete category and $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{L}$ is a left adjoint
  2. $\mathcal{L}$ preserving colimits

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

left adjoint preserves colimits

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from:

  • left adjoint functor objectwise representable
  • representability of a functor from a presentable category

where for an $d \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{D})$ we have

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(\mathrm{colim}(-)), d) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathrm{colim}\mathcal{L}(-), d) \\ \cong& \mathrm{lim} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(-), d) \end{align*}

hence the functor dualizes colimits to limits and therefore is representable
