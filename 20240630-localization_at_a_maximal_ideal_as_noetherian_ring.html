localization at a maximal ideal as noetherian ring and finitely many maximal ideals as noetherian ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring such that

  1. each localization at a maximal ideal $A_{\mathfrak{m}}$ in noetherian ring
  2. each nonzero element $a \in A \setminus \{0\}$ is contained in only finitely many maximal ideals

Then $A$ is noetherian
