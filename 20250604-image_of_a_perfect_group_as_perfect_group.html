image of a perfect group as perfect group

Proposition

Let $\varphi: G \rightarrow H$ be a group-homomorphism where $G$ is a perfect group. Then $\mathrm{im}(\varphi)$ is also perfect.

Proof

using perfect group and commutator:

Suppose $h \in \mathrm{im}(\varphi)$ is an element. We need to show $h \in [\mathrm{im}(\varphi), \mathrm{im}(\varphi)]$. So choose a preimage $g \in \varphi^{-1}[\mathrm{im}(\varphi)]$. By assumption there exist $g_1,g_2$ such that

\begin{align*} g = [g_1,g_2] \end{align*}

But application of $\varphi$ then shows that

\begin{align*} h =& \varphi(g) \\ =& [\varphi(g_1), \varphi(g_2)] \end{align*}

\begin{align*} g = [g_1,g_2] \end{align*}
