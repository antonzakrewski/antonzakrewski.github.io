specht module and order

Proposition

Let $\alpha_{\lambda}, \beta_{\lambda}$ be the young projectors. Then there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathbb{C}[S_n] \alpha_{\lambda} \beta_{\lambda} \cong \mathbb{C}[S_n] \beta_{\lambda} \alpha_{\lambda} \end{align*}

Proof

Recall that $(\alpha_{\lambda} \beta_{\lambda})^2 = c \cdot (\alpha_{\lambda} \beta_{\lambda})$ for some $c \in \mathbb{C}$ (even rational). Now consider the maps

\begin{align*} f = \cdot \alpha_{\lambda}: \mathbb{C}[S_n] \rightarrow& \mathbb{C}[S_n] \\ g = \cdot \beta_{\lambda}: \mathbb{C}[S_n] \rightarrow& \mathbb{C}[S_n] \end{align*}

It follows that these maps restrict as

\begin{align*} f: \mathbb{C}[S_n] \alpha_{\lambda} \beta_{\lambda} \rightarrow& \mathbb{C}[S_n] \beta_{\lambda} \alpha_{\lambda} \\ x \alpha_{\lambda} \beta_{\lambda} \mapsto& (x \alpha_{\lambda}) \beta_{\lambda} \alpha_{\lambda} \\ g: \mathbb{C}[S_n] \beta_{\lambda} \alpha_{\lambda} \rightarrow& \mathbb{C}[S_n] \alpha_{\lambda} \beta_{\lambda} \\ x \beta_{\lambda} \alpha_{\lambda} \mapsto& (x \beta_{\lambda}) \alpha_{\lambda} \beta_{\lambda} \\ \end{align*}

so in particular we get an automorphism

\begin{align*} g \circ f: \mathbb{C}[S_n] \alpha_{\lambda} \beta_{\lambda} \rightarrow \mathbb{C}[S_n] \alpha_{\lambda} \beta_{\lambda} \end{align*}

which is scaling by the $c$ above.

Moreover, we claim that $ \beta_{\lambda} \alpha_{\lambda}$ is also - up to scalar - an idempotent: This follows, as it is the image of the $\alpha_{\lambda} \beta_{\lambda}$ under the sign automorphism of the S_n group ring.

Hence $f \circ g$ is also given by multiplying with a nonzero scalar.
