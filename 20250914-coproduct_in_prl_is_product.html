coproduct in PrL is product

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_i$ be a collection of presentable categories. Then the coproduct in category PrL is given by the product in Cat infinity.

Proof

We need to show that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Pr^L}}(\prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{C}_i, -) \simeq& \prod_{i \in I} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Pr^L}}( \mathcal{C}_i, -) \end{align*}

where the first $\prod$ is taken in $\mathrm{Cat}$.

Here we use the anti-equivalence of PrL and PrR, to get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Pr^L}}(\prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{C}_i, -) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Pr^R}}(-,\prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{C}_i) \\ \simeq& \prod_{i \in I} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Pr^R}}(-, \mathcal{C}_i) \\ \simeq& \prod_{i \in I} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Pr^L}}(\mathcal{C}_i, -) \\ \end{align*}
