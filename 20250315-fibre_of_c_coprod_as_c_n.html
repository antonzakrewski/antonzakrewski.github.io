fibre of C^coprod as C^n

Proposition

Let $C$ be a simplicial set, $C^{\coprod}$ the C^coprod for a simplicial set. Then the fibre $C^{\coprod}_{ \langle n\rangle}$ is canonically isomorphic to $\mathcal{C}^n$

Proof

Note that the fibre is given by the pullback

Hence consider

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{/N(\mathrm{Fin}_*)}(\Delta^0, C^{\coprod}) \end{align*}

with $\Delta^0 \rightarrow C^{\coprod}$ which is precisely the fibre.

Then using the universal property of $C^{\coprod}$ for $K = \Delta^0$ we get a bijection

\begin{align*} C^{\coprod}_{ \langle n\rangle} \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{N(\mathrm{Fin}_*)}(\Delta^0, C^{\coprod}) \\ \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^0 \times_{N(\mathrm{Fin}_*)} N(\Gamma^*), \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} \Delta^0 \times_{N(\mathrm{Fin}_*)} N(\Gamma^*) \cong \coprod_{i \in \{1,...,n\}} \Delta^0 \end{align*}

is given by the disjoint union of the copies $( \langle n\rangle, i)$ for $i \in \{1,...,n\}$ (and higher simplices, which map to identities - hence no other nondegenerate simplices)

Therefore we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^0 \times_{N(\mathrm{Fin}_*)} N(\Gamma^*), \mathcal{C}) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\coprod_{i \in \{1,...,n\}} \Delta^0, \mathcal{C}) \\ \cong& \prod_{i \in \{1,...,n\}} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^0, \mathcal{C}) \\ \cong& \mathcal{C}^n \end{align*}
