mycielski-graph construction

Definition

Let $G = ([n],E)$ be a graph. Then the mycielski graph $\mathrm{Myc}(G)$ is defined as graph containing

  1. $V$ and $u_1,...,u_n, w$ as vertices, where$u_i,w \not\in V$ - i.e. n+1 additional vertices
  2. the edges $E'$ are given by a) the edges in $E$ b) edges $u_i,w$ for arbitrary $i$ c) for an edge $1,2$ edges $u_1,2$ and $1,u_2$
