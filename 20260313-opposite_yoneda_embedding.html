opposite yoneda embedding

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $よ_{\mathcal{C}}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An})$ be the yoneda embedding. Then the opposite yoneda embedding is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \overline{よ} \coloneqq よ_{\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}}^{\mathrm{op}}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{A})^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}
