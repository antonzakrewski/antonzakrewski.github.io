riesz lemma

Proposition

Let $X$ be a normed vector space and $U \subsetneq X$ a closed subspace Then for all $\lambda \in (0,1)$, there exists an $x_{\lambda} \in X \setminus U$ such that

  1. $\| x_{\lambda} \| = 1$
  2. $\| x_{\lambda} - u \| \geq \lambda$

Proof

Let $x \in X \setminus U$, where $X \setminus U$ is open. Then there exists an $\varepsilon_x > 0$ such that $B_{\varepsilon_x}(x) \subseteq X \setminus U$ and hence $d \coloneqq \mathrm{dist}(x,U) \geq \varepsilon_x$ . Now fix an $\lambda \in (0,1)$, then there exists an $u_{\lambda} \in U$ such that

\begin{align*} d = \mathrm{inf}_{u \in U} \| x - u \| \leq \| x - u_{\lambda} \| \leq \frac{d}{\lambda} \end{align*}

since $d$ is some infimum. Hence let $y = \frac{1}{\| x - u_{\lambda} \|} \geq \frac{\lambda}{d}$. we define $x_{\lambda} = y (x - u_{\lambda} \in X \setminus U$ (recall that complements of linear subspaces are closed under multiplication with nonzero elemenst).

then

  1. $\| x_{\lambda} \| = y \cdot \| x - u_{\lambda} \| = 1$ follows by defintiion of $y$

2)

\begin{align*} \| x_{\lambda} - u \| =& \| y \cdot (x - u_{\\lambda}) - u \| \\ =& \| y x - (u + yu_{\lambda}) \| \\ =& y \cdot \| x - (u_\lambda - \frac{u}{\lambda}) \| \\ \geq y d \\ \geq \lambda \end{align*}

since $u_{\lambda} - \frac{u}{y}$ lies in $U$.
