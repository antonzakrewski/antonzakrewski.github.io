extension of an ideal

Definition

Let $A,B$ be commutative rings and $f: A \rightarrow B$ be a ring-homomorphism. Suppose $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ is an ideal, then the extension $\mathfrak{a}^f$ is defined as generated Ideal

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a}^{e} = \mathfrak{a}^{f} = \langle f[\mathfrak{a}] \rangle \end{align*}
