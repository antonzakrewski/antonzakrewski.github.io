restriction functor is symmetric monoidal for sheaves of modules

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{O}_{X}$ be a ringed space and $\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathcal{O}_{X})$ the category of sheaves of modules. Then for any $U \subseteq O$, the restriction functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mod}_{\mathcal{O}_{X}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Mod}_{(\mathcal{O}_X)_{\vert U}} \end{align*}

is a symmetric monoidal functor.

Proof

follows from

  • restriction of sheaves to an open subset is a sheaf
  • pointwise formula of the pointwise definition of the tensor product of sheaves of modules.
