basis of a topological space induces dense subcategory

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space and $\mathcal{B} \subseteq \mathrm{Open}(X)$ basis. Then $\mathcal{B}$ is a dense subcategory for the canonical grothendieck topology.

Proof

Let $O$ be an object, then the self indexing trick shows that there exists a cover of $O$ using only elemenst from $\mathcal{B}$.
