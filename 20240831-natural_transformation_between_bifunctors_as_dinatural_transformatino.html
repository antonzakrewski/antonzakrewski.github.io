natural transformation between bifunctors as dinatural transformation

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ functors. Then a natural transformation $\eta: \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ defines a dinatural transformation via

\begin{align*} \alpha_{c}: \mathcal{F}(c,c) \rightarrow \mathcal{G}(c,c) \end{align*}
