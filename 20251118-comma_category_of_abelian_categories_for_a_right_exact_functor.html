comma category of abelian categories for a right exact functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be abelian categories and

a cospan where $\mathcal{F}$ is right exact and $\mathcal{G}$ an exact functor .

Then the comma category $\mathcal{F} / \mathcal{G}$ is also abelian and the functors

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F} / \mathcal{G} \rightarrow& \mathcal{A} \\ \mathcal{F} / \mathcal{G} \rightarrow& \mathcal{B} \\ \end{align*}

are exact

Proof
