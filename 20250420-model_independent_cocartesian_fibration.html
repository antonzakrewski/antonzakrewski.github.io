cocartesian fibration

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $p: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an infinity functor Then $p$ is said to be a cartesian fibration, if each morphism $f \in \mathcal{D}$ admits a p-cocartesian morphism
