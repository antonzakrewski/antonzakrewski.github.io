loop space of RP^n does not split as group into connected compontents

Proposition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, then the loop space $\Omega \mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^n$ does not split as group

\begin{align*} \Omega \mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^n \not\simeq C_2 \times \Omega S^n \end{align*}

(it only splits as anima)

Proof
