limits in a limit category

Proposition

Let $I: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}$ be a diagram and $\mathcal{K}$ a collection of categories. Suppose that each category $\mathcal{C}_i$ admits $\mathcal{K}$ indexed limits and that each functor in the diagram $I$ preserves these limits.

Then the limit category also admits $\mathcal{K}$ shaped limits and moreover these limits are computed pointwise, that is for each $i$ the functor

\begin{align*} \lim \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_i \end{align*}

preserves limits.

Proof

follows from Hom in the limit category
