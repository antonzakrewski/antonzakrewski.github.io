two out of three for isomorphisms (concrete)

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathrm{Iso}$ the class of isomorphisms. Then $\mathrm{Iso}$ satisfies two out of three

Proof

Let $f: A \rightarrow B, g: B \rightarrow C$ and $g \circ f: A \rightarrow C$ be isos. Then each case follows from the composition of isomorphisms as isomorphism

$g,f$

for $g \circ f$

$g, g \circ f$

for

\begin{align*} (g^{-1}) \circ (g \circ f) \end{align*}

$f, g \circ f$

for

\begin{align*} (g \circ f) \circ f^{-1} \end{align*}
