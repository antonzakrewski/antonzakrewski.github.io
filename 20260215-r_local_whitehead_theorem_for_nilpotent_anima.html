R-local whitehead theorem for nilpotent anima

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a morphism of nilpotent anima and $R = \mathbb{Z}[p^{-1} \vert p \in P] \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ a subring TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an equivalence
  2. $f$ is an E-local equivalence for a spectrum for $E \in \{M(\mathbb{Z}/p \mathbb{Z}, 1) \vert p \in P\} \cup \{R\}$
