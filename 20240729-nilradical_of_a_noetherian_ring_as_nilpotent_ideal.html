nilradical of a noetherian ring as nilpotent ideal

Proposition

Let $A$ be a noetherian ring, $\mathcal{N}_A$ the nilradical. Then $\mathcal{N}_A$ is a nilpotent ideal

Proof

Note that $\mathcal{N}_A = (n_1,...,n_n)$ is a finitely generated ideal for nilpotent elements $n_i$. Then for sufficiently large $m$ it follows that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{N}_A^m =& (\prod n_{\sigma{j}}^m) \\ =& 0 \end{align*}
