supersolvable group

Definition

Let $G$ be a group. Then $G$ is said to be supersolvable, if there exists a normal series

\begin{align*} \{e\} = G_1 \trianglelefteq G_2 ... \trianglelefteq G_n = G \end{align*}

such that $G_{i+1}/G_i$ is a cyclic group and $G_} \trianglefteq G$ is normal in $G$

in other words, $G$ is built by extensions of cyclic groups.
