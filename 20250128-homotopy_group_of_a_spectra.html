homotopy group functor of spectra

Definition

Let $\mathrm{Sp} = \mathrm{Fun}_{*}^{\mathrm{exc}}(\mathrm{Fin}_*,\mathrm{An})$ be the category of Spectra. Then the homotopy groups for a spectrum $X$ are defined as

\begin{align*} \pi_{n}(X) =& \begin{cases} \pi_0(\Omega^n X_0) & \mbox{if } n \geq 0 \\ \pi_0(X_n) & \mbox{if } n < 0 \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Note

We also could have defined

\begin{align*} \pi_n(-) = \pi_0(\mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}[n],-)) \end{align*}
