set theoretic group action with g-equivariant multiplacition defines group homomorphism

Proposition

Let $G,H$ be groups, and $\varphi: G \rightarrow \mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{Set}}(H}$ a group action, such that right multiplication is G-equivariant map

\begin{align*} g \cdot_{\varphi} (h_1 \cdot_h \cdot h_2) =& (g \cdot_{\varphi) h_1) \cdot_{h} h_2 \end{align*}

Then this group action defines a group homomorphism

\begin{align*} \psi: G \rightarrow H g \mapsto& g \cdot_{\varphi} e_H \end{align*}

Proof

Let $g,g' \in G$. Then

\begin{align*} \psi(g \cdot g') =& (g \cdot_G g') \cdot_{\varphi} e_H \\ =& g \cdot_{\varphi} (g' \cdot_{\varphi} e_H) \\ =& g \cdot_{\varphi} \psi(g') \\ =& g \cdot_{\varphi} (e_H \cdot_H \psi(g')) \\ =& (g \cdot_{\varphi} e_H) \cdot_H \psi(g') \\ =& \psi(g) \cdot_H \psi(g') \end{align*}
