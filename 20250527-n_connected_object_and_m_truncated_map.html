n-connected object and m-truncated map

Proposition

Let $X$ be a pointed anima

TFAE:

  1. $X$ is an n-truncated anima
  2. for each m-truncated morphism $f: Y \rightarrow Z$ between pointed anima the map
\begin{align*} f_*: \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,Y) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,Z) \end{align*}

is \((m-n-1)\) truncated

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)

note that:

  • conservative left exact functor reflects n-truncated morphisms & left exact functor preserves n-truncated morphisms

using:

  • n-truncated morphism and diagonal

the map

\begin{align*} f_*: \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,Y) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,Z) \end{align*}

is \(k+1\) truncated if and only if the map

\begin{align*} \Delta_{(f)_}*: \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,Y) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,Y) \times_{\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,Z)} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,Y) \end{align*}

is \(k\) truncated.

But as mapping space functor preserves limits this is equivalent to

\begin{align*} (\Delta_{f})_*: \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,Y) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,Z) \end{align*}

being \(k\) truncated.

Here $\Delta_f: Y \rightarrow Y \times_{Z} Y$ is the (pseudo-) diagonal.

Hence by induction it suffices to show that $f_*$ is an equivalence when $f$ is \(n-1\)- truncated (replacing $f$ with $\Delta_f$ and so on).
