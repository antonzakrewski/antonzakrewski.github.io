colimit of the identity functor as terminal object

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ admits a terminal object
  2. the identity functor $\mathrm{id}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ admits a colimit

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)

using triangle identities: We get a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{C}} \Rightarrow \mathrm{const}(c) \end{align*}

(which is the unit/counit of the left kan extension).

Then we take this as unit 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{C} \arrow[bend left]{rr}{}& \{c\} \arrow[bend left]{ll}{}\\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

and the counit is then determined, since it is some functor etc. into $\{c\}$
