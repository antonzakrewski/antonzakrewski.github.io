geometric realization to spaces preserves finite products

Proposition

The geometric realization

\begin{align*} \vert \cdot \vert: \mathrm{sSet} \rightarrow \mathrm{Space} \end{align*}

preserves finite products where $\mathrm{Space}$ is a category of convenient spaces

Proof

corollary of

\begin{align*} | \Delta^n \times \Delta^m | \cong \Delta^n_{\mathrm{top}} \times \Delta^m_{\mathrm{top}} \end{align*}
