closed subfunctor of a quasi-compact functor is quasi-compact

Proposition

Let $U \hookrightarrow X$ be a closed subfunctor of a quasi-compact algebraic functor Then $U$ is also quasi-compact.

Proof

Let $\coprod_{i \in I} Y_i \rightarrow X$ be a zariski-local epimorphism from a finite amount of affine schemes $Y_i$. Then we may consider the pullback

where $(\coprod_{i \in I} Y_i) \times_{X} U \simeq \coprod_{i \in I} (Y_i \times_{X} U)$ are isomorphic (cf. colimits are universal in a 1-presheaf category).

Now $\coprod_{i \in I} (Y_i \times X U) \rightarrow U$ is a Zariski-local epimorphism (cf. zariski-local epimorphisms are closed under pullbacks) so it remains to show that $(Y_i \times_{X} U)$ is affine. But it is a closed subfunctor of an affine scheme, therefore again affine.
