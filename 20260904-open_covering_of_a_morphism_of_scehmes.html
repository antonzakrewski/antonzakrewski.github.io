open covering of a morphism of schemes

Proposition

Let $g: Y \rightarrow X$ be a morphism of schemes. Then there exists a commutative diagram 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\coprod \mathrm{Spec}(S_j) \rar{j} \dar \rar & Y \dar
\coprod \mathrm{Spec}(R_i) \rar{j} & X
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

where the horizontal morphisms are affine open coverings

Proof

choose open covering $\coprod \mathrm{Spec}(R_i) \rightarrow X, \coprod \mathrm{Spec}(S_j') \rightarrow Y$. Then the pullback $\mathrm{Spec}(R_i) \times_{X} Y \subseteq Y$ is also an open covering (cf. pullback of an open cover of algebraic functors, + counit of zariski sheafification is a zariski local epimorphism). Moreover, $(\mathrm{Spec}(R_i) \times_{X} Y) \times_{Y} \coprod \mathrm{Spec}(S_j')$ is still an open covering (cf. intersection of open coverings of algebraic functors) and of the form $D(F) \subseteq \mathrm{Spec}(S_j)$. Hence use the standard zariski local epimorphism into a nonvanishing locus of an affine scheme
