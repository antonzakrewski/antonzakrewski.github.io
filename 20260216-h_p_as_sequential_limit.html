H_p as sequential limit

Proposition

Let $A$ be an abelian group Then $H_p(A) = \mathrm{lim} A_n$ where $A_n = \mathrm{ker}(A \rightarrow A/p^n)$ and the maps are given

\begin{align*} A_n \xrightarrow{ \cdot p} A_{n-1} \end{align*}

Proof

Note that $\mathbb{Z}[p^{-1}] \cong \mathrm{colim}_{n} (\mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\cdot p} \mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\cdot p} ...)$ (cf. localization of a module as filtered colimit of modules). Then the contravariant hom-functor dualizes small colimits to limits and $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(-,-)$ is an additive functor, so precomposition with $\cdot p$ induces the $\cdot p$ map
