indiscrete group functor

Definition

The discrete group functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dis}: \mathrm{Group} \rightarrow& \mathrm{TopGroup} G \mapsto& G \end{align*}

see:

  • group object in Set as group
  • functor preserving limits preserves group objects
  • forgetful functor from Top to Set as left adjoint
