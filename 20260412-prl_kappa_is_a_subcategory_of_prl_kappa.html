PrL kappa is a subcategory of PrL kappa'

Proposition

Let $\kappa < \kappa'$ be regular cardinals. Then $\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\kappa}$ is a subcategory of $\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\kappa'}$ (cf. PrL kappa)

Proof

Observe that the objects coincide (cf. jumping kappa for presentable categories). So it remains to show that any left adjoint, which preserves $\kappa$ compact objects also prerves $\kappa'$ compact objects.
