connective module spectra over a connective ring spectrum as closure under colimits

Proposition

Let $R$ be a connective E_1-ring spectrum, $\mathrm{LMod}_{R}$ the category of module spectra. Then the subcategory $\mathrm{LMod}_{R}^{\geq}$ is the smallest full subcategory which contains $R$ and is closed under colimits

Proof
