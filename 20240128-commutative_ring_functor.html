commutativication ring functor

Definition

Given category Ring and category CRing, the commutative ring functor is defined

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Comm}: \mathrm{Ring} \rightarrow& \mathrm{CRing} \\ R \mapsto& \mathrm{Comm}(R) (f: R \rightarrow S) \mapsto& \mathrm{Comm}(f): \mathrm{Comm}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{Comm}(S) \end{align*}

where $f'$ is the induced ring-homomorphism by the fundamental theorem of ring homomorphism

Proof

Note that

\begin{align*} f(xy - yx) = f(x)f(y) - f(y)f(x) \end{align*}

hence $\left(xy - yx \right) \subseteq \mathrm{ker}(f)$
