Proposition

The forgetful functor from category of lie groups to category of topological groups is fully faithful

Proof

Let $\varphi: G \rightarrow H$ be a continuous group-homomorphism between lie groups. We want to show that it is smooth

Consider the Graph

\begin{align*} \Gamma(\varphi) = \{(g,\varphi(g)) \in G \times H \vert g \in G\} \end{align*}
