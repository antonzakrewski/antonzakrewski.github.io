equalizer of modules

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $M,N$ be modules and $f,g: M \rightarrow N$ a module-homomorphism. Then the equalizer is the submodule

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ker}(f - g) = \{m \in M \vert f(m) = g(m)\} \end{align*}

Proof

kernel construction

Note that for $m \in M$

\begin{align*} (f - g)(m) = 0 \Leftrightarrow f(m) = g(m) \end{align*}

hence the sets agree see: kernel

submodule

follows from

  1. Menge der Modulhomomorphismen als abelsche Gruppe, hence $f - g$ is a module homomorphism
  2. Kernel of a module homomorphism as submodule

universal property

Suppose there exists a module homomorphism $h: L \rightarrow M$ making this a fork

Then we define

\begin{align*} i: L \rightarrow \mathrm{ker}(f - g) \\ l \mapsto& h(l) \end{align*}

by restriction of a module homomorphism to the image as module homomorphism it remains to show, that this map is welldefined: For $l \in L$, it follows that

\begin{align*} f(h(l)) = g(h(l)) \end{align*}

hence

\begin{align*} (f - g)(h(l)) = 0 \end{align*}

or $h(l) \in \mathrm{ker}(f - g)$
