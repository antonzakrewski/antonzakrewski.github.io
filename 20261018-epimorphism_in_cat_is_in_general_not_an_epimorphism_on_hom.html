epimorphism in cat is in general not an epimorphism on Hom

Proposition

An epimorphism in category Cat is in general not an epimorphism on Hom.

Proof

a)

consider

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ab} \rightarrow \mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb{Q}} \end{align*}

where $0 = \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{Z})$ does not surject onto $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Grp}}(\mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{Q})$.

b)

consider $B \mathbb{N} \rightarrow B \mathbb{Z}$ which is mirrored by the epimorphism $\Delta^1 \rightarrow \Delta^0$

here $\Delta^1 \rightarrow \Delta^0$ is an epimorphism because it identifies as fully faithful inclusion $(-)^{\simeq} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ar}(-)^{\simeq}$ after applying yoneda (cf. epimorphism fully faithful precomposition on Hom).

Here we unfortunately use ancient methods from simplicial homotopy theory to identify 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
S^0 \rar \dar &  * \dar \\
\Delta^1 \dar \rar & B \mathbb{N} \dar \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
