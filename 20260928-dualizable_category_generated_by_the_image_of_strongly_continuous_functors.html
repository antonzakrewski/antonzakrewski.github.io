presentable stable category generated by the image of strongly continuous functors is dualizable

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a presentable stable category and $\mathcal{F}_i: \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a family of strongly continuous functors, where $\mathcal{C}_i$ is a dualizable category Suppose $\mathcal{D}$ is generated by the essential images of $\mathcal{F}_i$ by colimits. Then $\mathcal{D}$ is dualizable.

Proof

Recall that $\prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{C}_i$ is the coproduct in PrL kappa, hence there exists a functor $\prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$. Now $\prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{C}_i$ is also dualizable (cf. product of dualizable categories is dualizable). Furthermore, the functor $\prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is also strongly continuous (cf. inclusion of PrLL stable into PrL stable preserves colimits)

Hence it suffices to show this for $I$ a singleton. Then the right adjoint $\mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is conservative (cf. image generating a presentable category under colimits and conservative adjoint) and preserves limits and colimits, since $\mathcal{F}$ is strongly continouus.

Now $\mathcal{C}$ satisfies Ab6 -- since it is dualizable -- and therefore so does $\mathcal{D}$. Hence $\mathcal{D}$ is also dualizable (cf. dualizable stable category and Ab6).
