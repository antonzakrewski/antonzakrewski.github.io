category of derived almost modules

Definition

$A$ is a unital associative ring, $I \subseteq A$ a two sided ideal such that $I^2 = I$. And $I$ is flat as either left or right module.

Then the category of derived almost modules $\mathcal{D}(A,I)^{a})$ is defined as fiber of the infinite extension of scalars functor in PrL stable

\begin{align*} (A/I) \otimes_{A}^L (A/I) \simeq A/I \end{align*}

using $I^2 = I$ and flatness
