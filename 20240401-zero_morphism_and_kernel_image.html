zero morphism and kernel / image

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and

a diagram such that

\begin{align*} g \circ f = 0 \end{align*}

is the zero morphism

Then there exists a canonical monomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{im}(f) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{ker}(g) \end{align*}

Proof
