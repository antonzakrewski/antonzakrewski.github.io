submodule of a finitely presented module is in general not finitely generated/presented

Proposition

Let $M$ be a $\kappa$ finitely presented A-module and $N \subseteq M$ a submodule Then $N$ is in general not $\kappa$ finitely presented.

Proof

Let $\kappa$ be arbitrary and $S$ a set with $\kappa < \vert S \vert$. Then consider $A = \mathbb{Z}[x_s \vert s \in S]$ be the polynomial ring, $M = A$ and $N = (x_s \vert s \in S)$ be the generated submodule.

It is not $\kappa$ finitely generated module: any $\kappa$ small subset of polynomials $\{f_n\}$ can only contain a $\kappa$ small amount of $x_s$, so in particular, there exists an $x_l$ with $x_l \not\in ( \{f_n\})$.
