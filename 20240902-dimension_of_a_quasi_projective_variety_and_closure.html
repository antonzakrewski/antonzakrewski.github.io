dimension of a quasi-projective variety and closure

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ a quasi-projective variety Thne the dimension is

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(X) = \mathrm{dim}(\overline{X}) \end{align*}

where $\overline{X}$ is the closure

Proof
