opposite linear order endofunctor of the simplex category

Definition

Let $\Delta$ be the simplex category Then the opposite order functor is defined as endofunctor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{rev}: \Delta \rightarrow& \Delta \\ [m] \mapsto& [m] \\ (\alpha: [m] \rightarrow [n]) \mapsto& (i \mapsto n - \alpha(m - i)) \end{align*}
