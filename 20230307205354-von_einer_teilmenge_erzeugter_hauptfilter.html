generated setfilter

Definition / Proposition

Let $X$ be a set and $\mathcal{S} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)$ a system of sets such that a finite intersection $\bigcap_{i=1}^n S_i \neq \emptyset$ is nonempty. Then the generated setfilter is defined as smallest - with respect to inclusion - setfilter $\mathcal{F}$ such that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{S} \subseteq \mathcal{F} \end{align*}
