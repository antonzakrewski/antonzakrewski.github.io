subcategory of dualizable objects is a thick stable subcateogry

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stably symmetric monoidal infinity category. Then the subcategory of dualizable objects is a thick stable subcategory.

Proof

follows from:

  • subcategory of dualizable objects of a stable category is stable
  • dualizable object in a closed monoidal category closed under retracts
