maximal norm estimate for a banach star algebra with C-star identity

Proposition

Let $A$ be a banach star algebra satisfying the $C^*$-identity $\| a^* a \| = \| a\|^2$. Then for any representation of a nonunital star algebra $\rho: A \rightarrow B(H)$, we have $\| \rho(a) \| \leq \| a \|_{B}$. So in particular $\| a \|_{A, \mathrm{max}} \leq \| a \|_{A}$ and $\| a \|_{A} = \| a \|_{A, \mathrm{max}}$
