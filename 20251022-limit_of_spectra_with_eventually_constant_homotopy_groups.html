sequential limit of spectra with eventually constant homotopy groups

Proposition

Let $... \rightarrow X_{k-1} \rightarrow X_{k} \rightarrow X_{k+1} \rightarrow ... $ be an \(\mathbb{Z}\)-indexed diagram in the category of Spectra Assume that for each $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ the diagram $... \rightarrow \pi_n(X_{k-1}) \rightarrow \pi_n(X_{k}) \rightarrow \pi_n(X_{k+1}) \rightarrow ...$ becomes eventually constant.

Then the canonical map of the sequential limit

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_{k} X_k \rightarrow X_k \end{align*}

is an isomorphism on $\pi_n$ for sufficiently small $k$

Proof

follows from

  • lim-lim1 vanishes for surjective maps
  • lim-lim1 sequence for homotopy objects in a stable category
