ideal relative to a subset

Definition

Let \(A\) be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ an ideal and $S \subseteq A$ a subset Then the \(\mathfrak{a}\)-ideal relative to $\alpha$ is defined as ideal

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a}_{S} \coloneqq \{x \in A \vert \forall s \in S : x \cdot s \in \mathfrak{a}\} \end{align*}

Proof

Let $\alpha, \beta \in \mathfrak{a}_S$.

multiplicative closure

Then

\begin{align*} s \cdot (\alpha \cdot \beta \cdot s) =& (s \cdot \alpha) \cdot (s \cdot \beta) \in A \end{align*}

additive abelian subgroup

Follows from the distributivity, as

\begin{align*} \alpha \cdot s + \beta \cdot s =& (\alpha + \beta)(s) \end{align*}

and multiplication

\begin{align*} -1 \cdot s \cdot \alpha \in \mathfrak{a} \end{align*}
