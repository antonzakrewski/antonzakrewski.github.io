Achims PhD theorem for the slope

Proposition

Let $X \in \mathrm{Syn}_{\mathrm{MU}}^{\omega}$ be a compact object in the category of synthetic spectra for MU. Then the homotopy groups $\pi_{n,w}(X)$ admit a minimal vanishing line, whose slope is of the form

\begin{align*} \mathrm{slope}(X) = \frac{1}{(p^i n 1) p^{j+1} -1} \end{align*}

for some prime $p$, $i \geq 1$, and $j \geq 0$.

Furthermore, $X$ admits a synthetic self map $\nu: \Sigma^{n,w}: X \rightarrow X$ "parallel to the vanishing line" for $n > 0$ such that the cofiber has a vanishing line of strictly lower slope.
