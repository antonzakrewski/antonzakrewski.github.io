duality of the infinity slice

Proposition

Let $X$ be a simplicial set, $\varphi: Y \rightarrow X$ a morphism of simplicial sets

Then there exists a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} (X_{/ \varphi})^{\mathrm{op}} \cong X_{\varphi^{\mathrm{op}}/} \end{align*}

of the

  1. right simplicial set of a morphism
  2. left simplicial set of a morphism
