monoid algebra functor adjunction

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, then the forgetful functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Alg}_{R} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Mon} \\ R \mapsto& R \end{align*}

admits a left adjoint, given by $M \mapsto R[M]$ the monoid algebra.

Proof

we use pointwise construction of left adjoints where the monoid algebra is a representing object.
