retract of a projective object as projective object

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $P$ a projective object and $R$ a retract. Then $R$ is also a projective object

Proof

Let $f: A \twoheadrightarrow B$ be an epimorphism and $g: R \rightarrow B$ an arbitrary morphism.

Then as $P$ is projective, there exists a lift

which satisfies

\begin{align*} f \circ \tilde{f} = g \circ \pi \end{align*}

Thus consider

This diargam commutes, as

\begin{align*} g =& g \circ \pi \circ \iota \\ =& f \circ \tilde{f} \circ \iota \end{align*}
