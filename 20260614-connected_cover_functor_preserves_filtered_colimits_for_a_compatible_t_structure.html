connected cover functor preserves filtered colimits for a compatible t-structure

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category equipped with a t-structure compatible with filtered colimits. Then the connected cover functor $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{\leq 0}$ preserves filtered colimits

Proof

follows from:

  • fiber functor of colimit preserving functors preserves colimits in a stable category
  • where $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{\leq 0}$ preserves filtered colimits by assumption
