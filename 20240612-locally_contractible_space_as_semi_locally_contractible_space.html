locally contractible space as semi locally contractible space_

Proposition

let $X$ be a locally contractible topological space. Then $X$ is semi locally contractible.

Proof

Let $x \in X$ be a point and $U$ a neighbourhood Then choose a contractible neighbourhood $V \subseteq U$ with a nullhomotopy

\begin{align*} H: V \times [0,1] \rightarrow V \end{align*}

Then postcomposition with the inclusion gives a nullhomotopy of

\begin{align*} \iota: V \rightarrow U \end{align*}
