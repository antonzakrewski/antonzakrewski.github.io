ind of an abelian category as left exact functors into Ab

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{A}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{lex}}(\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Ab}) \end{align*}

Proof

we may use:

  • product preserving functor out of an additive category lifts to commutative groups to identify
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{A}) \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Ab}) \end{align*}

$\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{A}) \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Ab})$

Note that the

  • $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(A,-)$ is left exact, since it preserves limits (computed underlying in Set).
  • filtered colimit of left exact functors in an ab5 category

$\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Ab}) \subseteq \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{A})$
