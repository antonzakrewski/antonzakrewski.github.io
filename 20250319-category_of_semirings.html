category of O-semirings

Definition

Let $\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}$ be an infinity operad, $\mathcal{C}$ be a closed symmetric monoidal infinity category. Then the infinity category $\mathrm{Rig}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{C})$ is defined as category of o-algebras

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Mon}^{\otimes}(\mathcal{C})) \end{align*}

with respect to the <monoidal structure>
