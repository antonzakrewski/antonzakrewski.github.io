tensor product of abelian groups

Definition

Let $A,B$ be abelian groups. Then the tensor product is defined

\begin{align*} A \otimes B := A \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} B \end{align*}

as underlying groups of the \(\mathbb{Z}\)-modules

Note that this satisfies the expected universal property of a tensor product, since forgetful functor from ZMod to Ab as isomorphism (wonky)
