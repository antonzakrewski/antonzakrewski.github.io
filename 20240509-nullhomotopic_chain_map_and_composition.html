nullhomotopic chain map and composition

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes and $A_*,B_*,C_*,D_*$ chain complexes and $g: A_* \rightarrow B_*, h: C_* \rightarrow D_*$ Suppose $f: B_* \rightarrow C_*$ is a nullhomotopic chain map, then so is $h \circ f \circ g$

Proof
