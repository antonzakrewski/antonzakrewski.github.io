associativity of the composition of morphisms

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,B), g \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(B,C), h \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(C,D)$ for objects $A,B,C,D \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ Then the composition of morphisms is associative, i.e.

\begin{align*} h \circ (g \circ f) =& (h \circ g) \circ f \end{align*}
