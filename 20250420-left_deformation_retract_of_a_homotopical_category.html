left deformation retract of a homotopical category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a homotopical category. Then a left deformation retract is given by a subcategory $\mathcal{C}_0 \subseteq \mathcal{C}$, a homotopical functor $r: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_0$ and a natural weak equivalence

\begin{align*} s: \iota \circ r \rightarrow \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{C}} \end{align*}

where $\iota: \mathcal{C}_0 \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is the inclusion
