homology of a moore space

Proposition

Let $A$ be an abelian group, then the singular homology of the Moore space $M_n(A)$ with $n \geq 1$ is given by

\begin{align*} H_k(M_n(A)) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } k = 0 \\ 0 & \mbox{if } 0 < k < n \\ A & \mbox{if } k = n \\ 0 & \mbox{if } k > n \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

$k = 0$

  • connected ness

$0 < k < n$

  • n-th homology of a CW complex without n-cell

$k = n$

  • follows from the definition as the reduced singular homology and wedge product and long exact sequence of homology for a mapping cone
