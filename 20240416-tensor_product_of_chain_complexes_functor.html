tensor product of chain complexes functor

Definition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}, \mathrm{ModR}$ the category RMod (of left and right modules). Suppose $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod}), \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{ModR})$ the categories of chain complexes.

Then the tensor functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} (- \otimes_R -): \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod}) \times \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{ModR}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{Ab}) \\ (C_*, D_*) \mapsto& \bigoplus_{p + q = n} C_p \otimes_R D_q ((f,g): (C_*,D_*) \rightarrow (C'_*,D'_*)) \mapsto& \bigoplus (\prod d^C \otimes (-1)^{?} d^D) \end{align*}
