compact operator maps weakly convergent sequences to strongly convergent sequences

Proposition

Let $T \in \mathrm{BL}(X,Y)$ be a compact operator, $x_n \xrightarrow{w} x$ a weakly convergent sequence. Then $T(x_n) \rightarrow T(x)$ converges strongly.

Proof

Now suppose that the sequence $T(x_n)$ does not converge to $T(x)$. Thne there exists some $\varepsilon > 0$ and a subsequence $y_m$ of $T(x_n)$ such that $\| y_m - T(x) \| > \varepsilon$ for all $m$. But $T(x_n)$ is bounded by the norm (cf. supremum of norms of a weakly convergent sequence).
