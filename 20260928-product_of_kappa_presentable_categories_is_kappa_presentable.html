(naive) product category of kappa presentable categories is kappa presentable

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal and $\mathcal{C}_i$ kappa presentable categories. Then the product category $\prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{C}_i$ is also $\kappa$ presentable.

Proof

follows from:

  • coproduct in PrL is product
  • forgetful functor from PrL kappa to PrL preserves colimits

This shows

Warning

This category is in general not the categorical product.
