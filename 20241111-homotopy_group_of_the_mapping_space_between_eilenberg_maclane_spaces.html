homotopy group of the mapping space between Eilenberg-MacLane spaces

Proposition

Let $K(A,n)$ be a locally finite cw-complex and Eilenberg-MacLane space for $n \geq 1$. Then the homotopy group of the mapping space

\begin{align*} \pi_k(\mathrm{Map}(K(A,n), K(B,n)), \mathrm{const}) = \begin{cases} \mathrm{Hom}(A,B) & \mbox{if } k = 0 \\ B & \mbox{if } k = n \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

under the mapping space product adjunction for topological spaces and Eckman-Hilton duality for suspension / loop space we have

\begin{align*} \pi_k(\mathrm{Map}(K(A,n), K(B,n))) =& \pi_0(\mathrm{Map}(S^k, \mathrm{Map}(K(A,n), K(B,n))) \\ =& \pi_0(\mathrm{Map}(K(A,n), \mathrm{Map}(S^k, K(B,n)))) \\ =& \pi_0(\mathrm{Map}(K(A,n), \mathrm{Map}(S^0, \Omega^k K(B,n))) \\ =& \pi_0(\mathrm{Map}(K(A,n), \mathrm{Map}(S^0, K(B,n-k))) \\ =& \pi_0(\mathrm{Map}(S^0, \mathrm{Map}(K(A,n), K(B,n-k)) \\ =& \pi_0(\mathrm{Map}(K(A,n), K(B,n-k))) \end{align*}

where

  • Loop space of an Eilenberg-MacLane Space as Eilenberg-MacLane Space

here the path components of a mapping space are maps up to homotopy, which are characterized in maps up to homotopy between Eilenberg-MacLane Spaces
