joyal equivalence and homotopy equivalence for kan complexes

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be Kan complexes and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an infinity functor

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is a simplicial homotopy equivalence
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ is a joyal equivalence

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
