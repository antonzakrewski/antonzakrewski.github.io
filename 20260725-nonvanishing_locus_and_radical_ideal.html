nonvanishing locus and radical ideal

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor and $F \subseteq \mathcal{O}(X)$ a subset of the commutative ring $\mathcal{O}(X)$. Then the nonvanishing locus only depends on the generated ideal $(F)$

Proof

follows from the definition

\begin{align*} D(F)(R) = \{x \in X(R) \vert (f_i(x)) \textrm{ generates the unit ideal }\} \end{align*}

\begin{align*} 1 = \sum \lambda_i f_i(x) \end{align*}
