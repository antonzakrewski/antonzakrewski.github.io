-1 not a square root in 5-adic integers

Proposition

$(-1)$ is not a square root in the 5-adic integers.

Proof

Suppose there exists an $(a_i) \in \mathbb{Z}_5$, then the projections are ring-homomorphisms. In particular, $(a_i)^{2} = -1 \in \mathbb{Z}/5^i \mathbb{Z}$ and trying out elements shows that $a_1 \in \{2,3\}$. Now we may write $(a_i) = (n_i \cdot 5^{i-1} + a_{i-1})$ for $n_i \in \{0,...,4\}$. Let us try $a_2$: here we have $(n_2 \cdot 5 + a_i)^2 = a_1^2 + 2 a_1 n \cdot 5 + n^2 5^2 \equiv 24 \mathrm{mod} 25$ and therefore

\begin{align*} 2 a_1 \cdot n_2 \cdot 5 \equiv \begin{cases} 28 \equiv 3 & \mbox{if } a_1 = 2 \\ 33 \equiv 8 & \mbox{if } a_1 = 3 \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Here $(2 a_1 \cdot n_2) \cdot 5$ is nilpotent, since $5^2 \equiv 0$ but $3,8,25$ are coprime, hence $3$ and $8$ are units in $\mathbb{Z}/25 \mathbb{Z}$.
