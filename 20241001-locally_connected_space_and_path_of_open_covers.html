locally connected space and path of open covers

Proposition

Let $X$ be a locally connected topological space, $(U_i)$ any open cover. Then for $x,y$ in the same connected component, there exists a finite set of $U_1,...,U_n$ such that

\begin{align*} U_n \cap U_{n+1} \neq \emptyset \end{align*}

and $x \in U_1, y \in U_n$
