delooping of a central extension on classifying spaces is a simple fibration

Proposition

Let $G_1 \rightarrow G_2 \rightarrow G_3$ be a central extension of ordinary groups. Then the delooping

\begin{align*} BG_1 \rightarrow BG_2 \rightarrow BG_3 \end{align*}

is a homology-simple fiber sequence

Proof
