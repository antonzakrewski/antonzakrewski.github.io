tor of kappa small modules is kappa small

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a $\kappa$ small ring for a regular cardinal $\kappa$. Let $A,B$ kappa small \(R\)-modules, then so is the Tor of $A,B$.

Proof

follows from:

  • projective resolution of a kappa small module is countable
  • tensor product of kappa small modules is kappa small
  • kappa small abelian groups is a serre class
