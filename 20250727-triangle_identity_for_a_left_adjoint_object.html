triangle identity for a left adjoint object

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a functor and $c \in \mathcal{C}$ an object. TFAE:

  1. there exists a left adjoint object $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(c, \mathcal{R}(-)) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(c),-)$
  2. there exists an object $\mathcal{L}(c)$ together with a morphism (called unit) $\eta(c): c \rightarrow \mathcal{R}(\mathcal{L}(c))$ such that for every object $d \in \mathcal{D}$ and every morphism $h: c \rightarrow \mathcal{R}(d)$ in $\mathcal{C}$ there exists a unique morphism $f: \mathcal{L}(c) \rightarrow d$ making the left diagram commute

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

By assumption

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(c, \mathcal{R}(-)) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(c),-) \end{align*}

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(c, \mathcal{R}(\mathcal{L}(c))) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(c),\mathcal{L}(c)) \end{align*}

In particular let $\eta(c): c \rightarrow \mathcal{R}(\mathcal{L}(c))$ be the morphism corresponding to $\mathrm{id}_c$ under this isomorphism.
