precomposition with a monomorphism as inner fibration for a functor category into an infinity category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category and $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ a monic morphism of simplicial sets. Then the induced map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(B, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(A, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is an inner fibration

Proof

corollary of:

  • contravariant inner hom as pullback hom
  • infinity category and terminal morphism as inner fibration
  • inner hom of a monomorphism and inner fibration
