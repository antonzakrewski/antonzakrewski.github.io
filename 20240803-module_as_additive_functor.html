module as additive functor

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $M$ a left (right) \(R\)-module Then $M$ is equivalently described as additive functor from the categorified ring

  1. left module
\begin{align*} M: BR \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab} \end{align*}
  1. right module:
\begin{align*} M: BR^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab} \end{align*}
