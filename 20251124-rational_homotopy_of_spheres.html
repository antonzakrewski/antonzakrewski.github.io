rational homotopy groups of spheres

Proposition

Let $n,k \in \mathbb{N}$. Then

\begin{align*} \pi_n(S^k)_{\mathbb{Q}} = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Q} & \mbox{if } n = k \\ \mathbb{Q} & \mbox{if } n = 2k -1 \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from the rational homotopy equivalence between the sphere and the eilenberg-maclane space where we use that R-localization of an eilenberg maclane space, i.e. $K(\mathbb{Z}, n)_{\mathbb{Q}} \simeq K(\mathbb{Q},n)$ (+ the fact that we understand homotopy groups of eilenberg maclane spaces very well :) )
