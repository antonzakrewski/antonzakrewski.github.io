freyd adjoint functor theorem

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a complete, locally small category and $\mathcal{D}$ another category. Let $\mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{G}$ is a right adjoint
  2. $\mathcal{G}$ preserves small limits and satisfies the adjoint solution set condition

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $\mathcal{F}$ be the left adjoint.

a)

  • consider the unit map
\begin{align*} \eta_x: x \rightarrow \mathcal{G}\mathcal{F}(x) \end{align*}

Then this follows from right adjoint and universal morphism

b)

  • left adjoint preserves colimits

2) $\implies$ 1)
