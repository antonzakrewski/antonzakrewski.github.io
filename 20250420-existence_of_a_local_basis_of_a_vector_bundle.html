existence of a local basis of a vector bundle

Proposition

for each topological vector bundle $p: E \rightarrow B$ and each point $x \in B$ there exists a neighbourhood $U_i$ such that

\begin{align*} p_{\vert U_i}: p^{-1}[U_i] \rightarrow U_i \end{align*}

admits a basis of a vector bundle

Proof

follows from the homeomorphism

\begin{align*} U_i \times \mathbb{K}^n \simeq p^{-1}[U_i] \end{align*}

for some covering $U_i$
