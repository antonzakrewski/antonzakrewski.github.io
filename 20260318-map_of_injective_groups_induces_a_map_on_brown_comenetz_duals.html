map of injective groups induces a map on brown-comenetz duals

Proposition

Let $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ be a group-homomorphism between injective abelian groups. Then $\varphi$ induces a map of spectra on brown comenetz duals

\begin{align*} I_{\varphi}: I_{A} \rightarrow I_{B} \end{align*}
