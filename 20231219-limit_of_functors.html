\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_{\mathcal{I}} ( \mathcal{F}_i): \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathcal{D} \\ A \mapsto& \mathrm{lim}(\mathcal{F}_i(A)) \\ (f: A \rightarrow B) \mapsto& \mathrm{lim}(f) \\ \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{lim}$ is the restricted lim functor for the diagrams
