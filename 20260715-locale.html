locale

Definition

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be a partially ordered set. Then $\mathcal{O}$ is said to be a locale, if

  1. $\mathcal{O}$ admits all suprema
  2. for any $u \in \mathcal{O}$ and any family $v_i$, there exists
\begin{align*} u \land (\biglor_{i \in I} v_i) = \biglor_{i \in I} (u \land v_i) \end{align*}
