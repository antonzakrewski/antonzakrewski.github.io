internal hom dualizes colimits

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a closed monoidal category, $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(-,-)$ the internal hom Then $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(-,-)$ functor dualizing colimits to limits in the first component.

Proof

Note that the tensor functor is by assumption a left adjoint. Furthermore note that

  1. contravariant hom-functor dualizes small colimits to limits
  2. covariant hom-functor preserves small limits

Hence by covariant weak yoneda

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(-, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathrm{colim} B_i, -)) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(- \times \mathrm{colim} B_i, -) \\ \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(\mathrm{colim}(- \times B_i), -) \\ \cong& \mathrm{lim} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(- \times B_i, -) \\ \cong& \mathrm{lim} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(-, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(B_i, -)) \\ \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(-, \mathrm{lim} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(B_i,-)) \end{align*}
