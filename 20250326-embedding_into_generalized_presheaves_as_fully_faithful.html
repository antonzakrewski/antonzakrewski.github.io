embedding into generalized presheaves as fully faithful

Proposition

Let $\mathfrak{K}$ be a collection of simplicial sets, $\mathcal{C}$ an infinity category and $\mathcal{R} = \{\overline{p}_{\alpha}: K^{\triangle}_{\alpha} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}\}$ a collection of infinity diagrams in $\mathcal{C}$. Assume that $K_{\alpha} \in \mathfrak{K}$.

Suppose each diagram in $\mathcal{R}$ already is a colimit diagram.

Then the functor into generalized presheaves is already fully faithful

Proof
