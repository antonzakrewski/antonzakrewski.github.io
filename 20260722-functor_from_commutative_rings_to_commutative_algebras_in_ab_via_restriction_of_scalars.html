functor mapping commutative rings to commutative Algebras in Ab via restriction of scalars

Proposition

There exists a $(2,1)$-categorical functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{CRing}_*: \mathrm{CRing}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat} \\ R \mapsto& \mathrm{Mod}_R \\ (f: R \rightarrow S) \mapsto& \mathrm{Res}_f: \mathrm{CAlg}_{S} \rightarrow \mathrm{CAlg}_{R} \end{align*}
