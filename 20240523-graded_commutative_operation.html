graded-commutative ring

Definition

Let \(R = \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0} R_n\) be a N-graded ring Then $R$ is said to be graded commutative, if for homogeneous elements $x,y \in R$ it follows that

\begin{align*} x \cdot y = (-1)^{\vert x \vert \cdot \vert y \vert} y \cdot x \end{align*}

where $\vert - \vert$ is the degree.
