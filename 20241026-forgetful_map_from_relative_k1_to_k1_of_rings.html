forgetful natural transformation from relative K1 to K1 of rings

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq R$ an ideal Then there exists a morphism

\begin{align*} K_1(R, \mathfrak{a}) \rightarrow K_1(R) \end{align*}

natural with respect to category of ideal pairs of rings induced by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{GL}(R, \mathfrak{a}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{GL}(R) \\ (1,B) \mapsto& B \end{align*}
