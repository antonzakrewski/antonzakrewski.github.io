group epimorphism from the tensor product of the abelianization to the lower central series quotient

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group, then the map

\begin{align*} G^n \rightarrow& G \\ (g_1,...,g_n) \mapsto& [[[g_1,g_2],g_3,...]] \end{align*}

induces a group-epimorphism

\begin{align*} \bigotimes_{i=1}^n G^{\mathrm{ab}} \twoheadrightarrow \Gamma^n(G)/\Gamma^{n+1}(G) \end{align*}

Proof

see: warfield 1976, theorem 3.1 nilpotent groups

epimorphism

Proof by induction

induction basis

follows from choosing the right representatives for

\begin{align*} G^{\mathrm{ab}} \otimes G^{\mathrm{ab}} \rightarrow [G,G]/\Gamma^1(G) \end{align*}

where an element in $[G,G]$ is given by $g \cdot g' \cdot g^{-1} \cdot g'^{-1}$

induction step

follows as a representative of an element in $\Gamma^n(G)/\Gamma^{n+1}(G)$ is an element in $[\Gamma^{n-1}(G),G]$ or $g \cdot g' \cdot g^{-1} \cdot g'^{-1}$ for $g \in \Gamma^{n-1}(G), g' \in G$ As

\begin{align*} \prod_{i=1}^n G^{\mathrm{ab} \rightarrow \Gamma^{n-1}(G)/\Gamma_{n+1}(G) \end{align*}

is by induction a surjective multilinear morphism we may choose a an element $(g_1,...,g_{n-1})$ in the fiber and then the equivalence class of $g'$
