multiplication of a strong monoidal functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be monoidal category, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a monoidal functor.

Then the multiplication is a designated natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mu: \mathcal{F}(-) \otimes \mathcal{F}(y) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(- \otimes -) \end{align*}
