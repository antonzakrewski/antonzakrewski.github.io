tensor product of A-algebras over a commutative ring

Proposition

Let \(A\) be a commutative ring, $B,C$ A-Algebras. Then the tensor product as \(A\)-Algebra is defined by

  1. the set of the tensor product of modules over a commutative Ring
  2. the multiplication generated by
\begin{align*} (\beta_1 \otimes_A \gamma_1) \cdot (\beta_2 \otimes_A \gamma_2) \coloneqq (\beta_1 \cdot \beta_2 \otimes_A \gamma_1 \cdot \gamma_2) \end{align*}

Proof
