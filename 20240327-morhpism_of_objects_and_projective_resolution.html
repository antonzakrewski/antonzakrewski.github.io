morphism of objects and morphism unique up to chain homotopy between projective resolutions

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category admitting enough projectives. Suppose $f: A \rightarrow B$ is a morphism, then there exist projective resolutions and a chain map

which is unique up to chain homotopy

Proof

corollary of
