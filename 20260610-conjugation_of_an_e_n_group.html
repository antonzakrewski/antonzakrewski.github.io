conjugation of an E_n group

Definition

Let $G \in \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_n}(\mathrm{An})$, then conjugation is defined as \(\mathbb{E}_n\)-group homomorphism

\begin{align*} G \rightarrow \Omega^{n-1}\mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_n}}(G) \end{align*}

In particular, for $n = 1$ this defines an \(\mathbb{E}_1\) group homomorphism

\begin{align*} G \rightarrow \mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_1}}(G) \end{align*}

We define it by the following steps:

  1. recall the recognition principle for anima valued E_n groups $\mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_n}(\mathrm{An}) \simeq \mathrm{An}_{*}^{\geq n}$
  2. recall the universal pointed fibration $\mathrm{An}_* \rightarrow \mathrm{An}_*$
  3. observe that $\mathrm{B}^nG \rightarrow \mathrm{B}^nG \rightarrow *$ is a fiber sequence, hence it is classified by a (pullback) diagram

or a functor $\mathrm{B}^nG \rightarrow \mathrm{An}_*$ which takes values in $\mathrm{B}^nG$, so $\mathrm{An}_{*,\geq n}$ (alternatively: use that $\mathrm{An}_*^{\geq n} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}^{\geq n}$ also classifies fibrations taking values in $\mathrm{An}_{\geq n}$.

  1. This gives a functor
\begin{align*} \mathrm{B}^n G \rightarrow& \mathrm{B} \mathrm{End}_{\mathrm{An}_{*,\geq n}}(\mathrm{B}^nG) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{B}^n G$ is a groupoid, so this functor restricts by adjunction to $\mathrm{B}G \rightarrow \mathrm{B}\mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{An}_{*,\geq n}}(\mathrm{B}^nG)$

  1. we use the recognition principle $\mathrm{B}\mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{An}_{*,\geq n}}(\mathrm{B}^nG) \simeq \mathrm{B} \mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_n}}(G)$ to get a functor $\mathrm{B}G \rightarrow \mathrm{B} \mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_n}}(G)$
  2. we loop the functor $n$ times (again applying the recognition principle) to get the map
\begin{align*} G \rightarrow \Omega^{n-1}\mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_n}}(G) \end{align*}

Let $* \xrightarrow{g} G$ be arbitrary, then under the equivalence $\Omega BG \simeq G$, the \(\mathbb{E}_1\) conjugation action corresponds to the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{conj}_{\mathbb{E}_1}(g): \Omega B G \rightarrow& \Omega B G \\ \gamma \mapsto \gamma_g \cdot \gamma \cdot \gamma_g^{-1} \end{align*}

where $\gamma_g$ is the composition $* \xrightarrow{g} G \rightarrow \Omega B G$. This can be seen by geometric handwaving.
