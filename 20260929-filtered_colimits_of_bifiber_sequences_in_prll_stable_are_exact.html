filtered colimits preserve bifiber sequences in PrLL stable

Proposition

Taking filtered colimits in category PrLL stable preserves short exact sequences (i.e., bifiber sequnces in $\mathrm{Pr}^L$).

Proof

Let $A_i \rightarrow B_i \rightarrow C_i$ be a filtered diagram of bifiber sequences. Then $\mathrm{colim} A_i \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} B_i \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} C_i$ is a quotient sequence, since colimits commute with colimits. Furthermore, $\mathrm{colim} A_i \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} B_i$ is fully faithful (cf. PrLL stable satisfies weak AB5 axiom), hence identifies with the verdier kernel (cf. colimit of bifiber sequences in PrL stable is a bifiber sequence up to fully faithfullness)
