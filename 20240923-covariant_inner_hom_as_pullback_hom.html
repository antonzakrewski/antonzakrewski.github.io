covariant inner hom as pullback hom

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a closed monoidal category with an initial object, then for $B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ the morphism

\begin{align*} f_{*}: \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(B,X) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(B,Y) \end{align*}

is given by a pullback hom of two morphisms, namely

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(B,X) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\emptyset,X) \times_{\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\emptyset,Y)} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(B,Y) \end{align*}

where $\emptyset$ is the terminal object and

\begin{align*} \emptyset: \emptyset \rightarrow B \end{align*}

is the initial morphism

Proof

  • internal hom out of the initial object as terminal object
  • product with the terminal object canonically iso
  • pullback and terminal objects induce finite products ~ ish
