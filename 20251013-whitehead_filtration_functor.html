whitehead filtration functor

Definition

The whitehead filtration functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \tau_{* \geq } \colon \mathrm{Sp} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fil}(\mathrm{Sp}) \\ X \rightarrow (\tau_{\geq n} X)_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \end{align*}
