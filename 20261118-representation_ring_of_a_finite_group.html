representation ring of a group

Definition

Let $G$ be a group and $k$ a field Then the representation ring is defined as

\begin{align*} \pi_0((\mathrm{Mod}_{k[G]}^{\mathrm{f.g.} K[G]}, \oplus)^{\mathrm{grp}}) \end{align*}

with the tensor product over $k$ as ring structure

Remark

  • common notation is $R(G) = R_{\mathbb{C}}(G)$.
