Linearität des Kurvenintegrals

Proposition

Let $\Sigma \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be open, and $f,g: \Sigma \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ be continuous maps and $\alpha,\beta \in \mathbb{C}$ . Then for $\gamma: [a,b] \rightarrow \Omega$

\begin{align*} \int_{\gamma} \alpha f(z) + \beta g(z) \mathrm{d} z =& \alpha \int_{\gamma} f(z) + \beta \int_{\gamma} g(z) \mathrm{d} z \end{align*}

Proof

Assume that $\gamma$ consist of $\gamma_1,...,\gamma_n$, each picewise $C^1$. Then

\begin{align*} \int_{\gamma} \alpha f(z) + \beta g(z) \mathrm{d} z =& =& \sum_{i=1}^n \int_{a_i}^{a_{i+1}} (\alpha \cdot f(\gamma'(t)) + \beta g(\gamma'(t))) \gamma_i'(z) \mathrm{d}t \\ =& \sum_{i=1}^n \int_{a_i}^{a_{i+1}} (\alpha \cdot f(\gamma'(t)) \gamma'(t) \mathrm{d}t + \sum_{i=1}^n \beta g(\gamma'(t))) \gamma_i'(z) \mathrm{d}t \\ =& \alpha \cdot \left(\sum_{i=1}^n \int_{a_i}^{a_{i+1}} (\cdot f(\gamma'(t)) \gamma'(t) \mathrm{d}t \right) + \beta \cdot \left(\sum_{i=1}^n g(\gamma'(t))) \gamma_i'(z) \mathrm{d}t \right) \\ =& \alpha \cdot \int_{\gamma} \alpha f(z) \mathrm{d}z + \beta \cdot \int_{\gamma} \alpha f(z) \mathrm{d}z \end{align*}
