extension of an ideal for a localization

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $S \subseteq A$ a multiplicatively closed subset and

\begin{align*} \iota: A \rightarrow S^{-1}A \end{align*}

the localization

Then for an ideal $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ the extension is given by

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{e}^{e} = \{ \frac{a}{s} \vert a \in \mathfrak{a}, s \in S\} \end{align*}

Proof
