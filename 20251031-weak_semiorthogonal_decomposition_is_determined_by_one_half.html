weak semiorthogonal decomposition is determined by one half

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category and $\mathcal{A} \perp \mathcal{B}$ a weak semiorthogonal decomposition. Then up to equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathcal{B} \simeq \{x \in \mathcal{C} \vert \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(a,x) = 0 \forall a \in \mathcal{A}\} \\ \mathcal{A} \simeq \{x \in \mathcal{C} \vert \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,b) = 0 \forall b \in \mathcal{B}\} \end{align*}
