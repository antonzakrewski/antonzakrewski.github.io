mapping spectrum functor is exact in both variables

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category, then the mapping spectrum functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,-): \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp} \end{align*}

is exact in both variables

Proof

follows from:

  • left / right and exact functor between stable infinity categories
  • mapping spectrum functor preserves limits
