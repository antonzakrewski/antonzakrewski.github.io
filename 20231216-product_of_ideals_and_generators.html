product of ideals and generators

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a} = (a_i)$ and $\mathfrak{b} = (b_i)$ be ideals, generated by $a_i$ resp. $b_i$ Then it holds for the product

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a} \cdot \mathfrak{b} = (a_i \cdot b_j) \end{align*}

Proof

$\supseteq$

$\subseteq$

Let $\alpha \cdot \beta \in \mathfrak{a} \cdot \mathfrak{b}$. Then there exist a sum

\begin{align*} \alpha =& \sum a_i \\ \beta =& \sum b_j \end{align*}

hence by distributivity, we conclude, that

\begin{align*} \alpha \cdot \beta =& \left(\sum a_i \right) \cdot \left(\sum b_j \right) \\ =& \sum \sum a_i b_j \end{align*}
