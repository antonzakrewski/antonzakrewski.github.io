essential image of a torsion pair

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{F}) \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ be a torsion pair Suppose $X\in \mathcal{A}$. TFAE:

  1. $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(X,F) = 0$ for all $F \in \mathcal{F}$
  2. $X$ lies in the essential image of the inclusion $\mathcal{T} \subseteq \mathcal{A}$

Analogously TFAE:

  1. $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(T,X) = 0$ for all $T \in \mathcal{T}$
  2. $X$ lies in the essential image of the inclusion $\mathcal{F} \subseteq \mathcal{A}$
