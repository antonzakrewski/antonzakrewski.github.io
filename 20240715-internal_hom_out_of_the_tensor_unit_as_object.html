internal hom out of the tensor unit as object

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a closed monoidal category. Then

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathds{1}, -) \simeq \mathrm{id}(-) \end{align*}

Proof

using contravariant weak yoneda for $X$ we get natural isomorphisms

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(-, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathds{1},X)) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(- \otimes \mathds{1}, X) \\ \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(-, X) \end{align*}

where the first is given by the adjunction and the second by the monoidal structure
