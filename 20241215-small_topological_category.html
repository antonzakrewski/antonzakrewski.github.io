small topological category

Definition

A small topological category consist of topological spaces

  1. $\mathrm{ob}(\mathcal{C})$
  2. $\mathrm{mor}(\mathcal{C})$

and continuous maps

  1. $\mathrm{id}: \mathrm{ob}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{mor}(\mathcal{C})$
  2. $\mathrm{domain}: \mathrm{mor}(\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{ob}(\mathcal{C})$
  3. $\mathrm{codomain}: \mathrm{mor}(\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{ob}(\mathcal{C})$
  4. $\circ: \mathrm{mor}(\mathcal{C}) \times_{\mathrm{ob}(\mathcal{C})} \mathrm{mor}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{mor}(\mathcal{C})$
