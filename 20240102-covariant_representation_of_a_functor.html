covariant representation of a functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a locally small category and $\mathrm{Set}$ the category set and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ be a covariant functor

Then a representation of $\mathcal{F}$ is an object $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$, an element $\mathcal{F}(A)$ and a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \eta_u: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,-) \rightarrow \mathcal{F} \end{align*}

here $\eta_u$ is the natural transformation obtained by Yoneda
