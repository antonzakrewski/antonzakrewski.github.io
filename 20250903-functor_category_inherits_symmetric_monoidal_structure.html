functor category inherits pointwise symmetric monoidal structure

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a symmetric monoidal category Then the functor category also inherits an \(\mathbb{E}_\infty\) monoidal structure

Proof

  • functor category functor preserves limits
  • symmetric monoidal infinity category as commutative monoid in Cat infinity
  • product preserving functor induces functor on cartesian commutative monoids
