stupid homotopy pullback

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a continuous map, then the stupid homotopy pullback is defined as 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\empytset \rar \dar & X \dar{f}\\
\empytset  & X \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Proof

follows as vacuous truth, since there does not exist a point $c \in \emptyset$
