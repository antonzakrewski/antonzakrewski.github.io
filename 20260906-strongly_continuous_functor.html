strongly continuous functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor between compactly assembled categories. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be strongly continuous, if it preserves filtered colimits and commutes with $\widehat{よ}$, i.e.t. the natural transformation in the square 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{C} \dar{f} \rar{\widehat{よ}} & \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}) \dar{\mathrm{Ind}(f)} \\
\mathcal{D} \rar{\widehat{よ}}& \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{D})
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

is an equivalence

Remark

  • strongly continuous functor and right adjoint preserving colimits
