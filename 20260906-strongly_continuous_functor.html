strongly continuous functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor between presentable categories. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be strongly dualizable, if it is a left adjoint and its right adjoint $\mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{F}$ also admits a left adjoint.
