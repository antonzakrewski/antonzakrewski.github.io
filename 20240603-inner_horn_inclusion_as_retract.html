inner horn inclusion as retract

Proposition

Let $\Lambda_j^n$ be an inner horn, $\iota: \Lambda_j^n \rightarrow \Delta^n$ an inner horn inclusion. Then $\iota$ is a <retract> of

\begin{align*} \Lambda_j^n \times \Delta^n \coprod_{\Lambda_j^n \times \Lambda_1^2} \Delta^n \times \Lambda_1^2 \rightarrow \Delta^n \times \Delta^2 \end{align*}

Proof

We have to show that there exists a commutative diagram

where

\begin{align*} s(i) =& \begin{cases} (i,0) & \mbox{if } i < j \\ (i,1) & \mbox{if } i = j \\ (i,2) & \mbox{if } i > j \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} r(i,k) =& \begin{cases} i & \mbox{if } i < j, k = 0 \\ i & \mbox{if } i > j, k = 2 \\ j & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

a) identity

\begin{align*} rs = \mathrm{id} \end{align*}

b) welldefined image

i)

\begin{align*} s(\Lambda_j^n) \subseteq \Lambda_j^n \times \Delta^2 \cup \Delta^n \times \Lambda_1^2 \end{align*}

ii)

\begin{align*} r(\Lambda_j^n \times \Delta^2 \cup \Delta^n \times \Lambda_1^2) \subseteq \Lambda_j^n \end{align*}
