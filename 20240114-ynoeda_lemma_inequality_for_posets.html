yoneda lemma inequality for posets

Proposition

Let $P$ be a partially ordered set. TFAE:

  1. $a \leq b$
  2. for all $c \in P$ it holds, that $c \leq a \Rightarrow c \leq b$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from transitivity, as $c \leq a, a \leq b$ implies $c \leq b$

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from $c = a$, as $a \leq a$ is a true and hence also $a \leq b$
