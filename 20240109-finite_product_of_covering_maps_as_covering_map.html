finite coproduct of covering maps as covering map

Proposition

Let $B$ be a topological space and $p_1: E_1 \rightarrow B, p_2: E_2 \rightarrow B$ be covering maps. Then the categorical coproduct

\begin{align*} p_1 \coprod p_2: E_1 \coprod E_2 \rightarrow B \end{align*}
