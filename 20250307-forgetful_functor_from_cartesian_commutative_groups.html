forgetful functor from cartesian commutative groups

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category Then the forgetful functor from the category of cartesian commutative groups

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Grp}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

is defined as infinity evaluation functor at $1 \in \Delta$

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Grp}(\mathcal{C}) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(N(\Delta), \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}
