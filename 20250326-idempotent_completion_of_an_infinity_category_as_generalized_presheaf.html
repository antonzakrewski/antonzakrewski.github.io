idempotent completion of an infinity category as generalized presheaf

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathrm{Idem}$ the simplicial set Idem. Then the simplified generalized presheaf construction

\begin{align*} \mathcal{P}_{\emptyset}^{\mathrm{Idem}}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is an idempotent completion

Proof
