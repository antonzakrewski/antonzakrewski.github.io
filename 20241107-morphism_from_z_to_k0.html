morphism from Z to k0

Definition

Let $R$ be a ring. Then the morphism

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow K_0(R) \end{align*}

is defined as image of the grothendieck group functor for the monoid-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathbb{N} \rightarrow \mathrm{Proj}(R) n \mapsto [R^n] \end{align*}
