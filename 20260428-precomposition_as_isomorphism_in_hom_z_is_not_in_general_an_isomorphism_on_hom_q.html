precomposition as isomorphism in Hom(-,Z) is not in general an isomorphism on Hom(-,Q)

Proposition

Proof

consider $\mathbb{Q} \rightarrow \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$. Then $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\mathbb{Q},\mathbb{Z}), \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Z})$ are both trivial, hence precomposition with $\mathbb{Q} \rightarrow \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$ is the zero map.

But $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{Q}) \simeq \mathbb{Q}$ is nontrivial and $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Q}) \simeq $ is trival. Hence precomposition with it can not be trivial
