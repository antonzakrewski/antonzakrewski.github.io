A-central pi-series

Definition

Let $G$ be a pi-group and $\mathcal{A} \subseteq \mathrm{Grp}$ a collection of groups, then a finite normal series $\{1\} \subseteq \{G_q\} \subseteq ... \subseteq G_0 = G$ of subgroups is said to be an \(\mathcal{A}\)-central \(\pi\)-series if

  1. $G_{j-1}/G_j$ is in $\mathcal{A}$ and a central subgroup
  2. the action of $\pi$ restricts to $G_j$ and $\pi$ acts trivially on $G_{j-1}/G_j$
