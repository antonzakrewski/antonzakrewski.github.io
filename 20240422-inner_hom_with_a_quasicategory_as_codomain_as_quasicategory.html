inner hom with a quasicategory as codomain as quasicategory

Proposition

Let $X$ be a quasicategory, $Y \in \mathrm{sSet}$ a simplicial set. Then the inner hom of simplicial Sets

\begin{align*} \underline{Hom}(Y,X) \end{align*}

is also a quasicategory

Proof

Let

\begin{align*} \iota: \emptyset \rightarrow& Y \\ f: X \rightarrow& \Delta^1 \end{align*}

Then $f$ is an inner fibration (cf. infinity category and terminal morphism as inner fibration) and $\iota$ a monomorphism

Let

\begin{align*} <f,\iota>: \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(Y,X) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\emptyset,K) \times_{\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\emptyset,X)} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(Y,\Delta^1) \cong 1 \times_{1} 1 \cong 1 \end{align*}

where the latter isomorphism follows from

  1. internal hom into a terminal object as terminal object
  2. internal hom out of the initial object as terminal object

by inner hom of a monomorphism and inner fibration it is a kan fibration, hence $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(Y,K)$ is an infinity category
