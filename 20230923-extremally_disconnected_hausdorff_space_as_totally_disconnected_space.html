extremally disconnected Hausdorff-Space is totally disconnected space

Proposition

Let $X$ be a extremally disconnected, hausdorff space. Then $X$ is totally disconnected space

Proof

Let $x,y\in X$ be different points. Thne we have to show that $x \not\in C(y)$. So let $U_x,U_y$ be disjoint open neighbourhoods (exist hausdorffness). Then consider the closure $\overline{U}_x$, which is closed (by definition) and open (since $X$ is extremally disconnected).

We claim that $y \not\in \overline{U}_x$: Here $\overline{U}_x \subseteq X \setminus U_y$ with $y \not\in X \setminus U_y$. So $C(x) \cap \overline{U}_x$ is clopen in $C(x)$ and nonempty, so in particular $C(x) = C(x) \cap \overline{U}_x$ since $C(x)$ is connected
