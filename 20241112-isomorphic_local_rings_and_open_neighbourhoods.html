isomorphic local rings and open neighbourhoods

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X,Y \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be affine varieties. Suppose $x \in X,y \in Y$ are points, such that the local ring of regular functions are isomorphic as K-Algebras

Then there exist open neighbourhoods $U \subsetex X,V \subseteq Y$ and an isomorphism of quasi affine varieties $p: U \rightarrow V$ such that $p(x) = y$
