functor mapping commutative rings to affine schemes via base change

Definition

The functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Aff}_*: \mathrm{CAlg} \rightarrow \widehat{\mathrm{Cat}} \end{align*}

is defined as composition of functor mapping commutative rings to commutative Algebras in Ab via extension of scalars and the opposite category functor.
