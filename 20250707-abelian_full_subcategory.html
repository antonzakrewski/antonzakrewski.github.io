abelian full subcategory

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathcal{B} \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ a full abelian subcategory. Then $\mathcal{B}$ is said to be full abelian, if

  1. $\mathcal{B}$ is closed under Subobject and quotients in $\mathcal{A}$
  2. each object admits a finite filtration
\begin{align*} 0 = a_0 \hookrightarrow a_1 ... \hookrightarrow a_n = 0 \end{align*}

where the morphisms are monomorphisms and the quotients lie in $\mathcal{B}$, i.e. $a_n/a_{n-1} \in \mathcal{B}$
