stable homotopy groups preserves filtered colimits

Proposition

the stable homotopy group functor preserves filtered colimits.

Proof

note that

\begin{align*} \pi_n^{\mathrm{st}}(\mathrm{colim} X) \coloneqq \mathrm{colim}_* \pi_{n+*}(\Sigma^n (\mathrm{colim} X)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_* \pi_{n+*}(\mathrm{colim} \Sigma^n X) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_* \mathrm{colim} \pi_{n+*}(\Sigma^n X) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{colim}_* \pi_{n+*}(\Sigma^n X) \\ = \mathrm{colim} \pi_n^{\mathrm{st}}( X) \end{align*}
