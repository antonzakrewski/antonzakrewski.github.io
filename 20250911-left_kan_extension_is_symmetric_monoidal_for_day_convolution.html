left kan extension along a symmetric monoidal functor is symmetric monoidal with respect to day convolution

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{C}', \mathcal{D}$ be symmetric monoidal categories (satisfying the conditions in day convolution) and $p: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}'$ a symmetric monoidal functor. Then the left kan extension

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Lan}_{p}: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}', \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

admits a symmetric monoidal structure.

Proof
