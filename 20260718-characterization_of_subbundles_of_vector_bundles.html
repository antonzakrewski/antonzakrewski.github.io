characterization of subbundles of vector bundles over a ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $f: U \rightarrow V$ a map between vector bundles. TFAE:

  1. $U$ is a subbundle
  2. $f$ is universally injective map
  3. for every R-field $k$, the map $f \otimes_{R} k$ is injective
  4. $f$ is injective and the cokernel of $f$ is a vector bundle
  5. $f$ is a split mono
  6. the dual map $f^{\vee}: V^{\vee} \rightarrow U^{\vee}$ is surjective
