fully faithful functor between infinity categories

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an infinity functor Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be fully faithful, if the induced map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(f(x), f(y)) \end{align*}

is a joyal equivalence for all $x,y \in \mathcal{C}_0$
