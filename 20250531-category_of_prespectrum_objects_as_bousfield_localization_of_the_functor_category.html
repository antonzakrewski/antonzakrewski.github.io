category of prespectrum objects as bousfield localization of the functor category

Proposition

The category of prespectrum objects is a bousfield localization

\begin{align*} \mathrm{PSp}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathbb{N}\times \mathbb{N}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
