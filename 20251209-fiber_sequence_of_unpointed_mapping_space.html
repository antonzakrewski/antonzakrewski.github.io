fiber sequence of the free loop space

Proposition

Let $(X,x_0)$ be pointed anima. Then there exists a right split fiber sequence

\begin{align*} \Omega_{x_0}^n X \rightarrow \mathrm{map}(S^n,X) \xrightarrow{\mathrm{ev}_{x_0}} X \end{align*}

where we implicitly use $X \simeq \mathrm{map}(*,X)$ and precompose with the inclusion $\{x_0\} \rightarrow X$

Proof

follows from:

  • evaluation out of the mapping space for a sphere as serre fibration
  • where we identify the actual fibre as based loop space
