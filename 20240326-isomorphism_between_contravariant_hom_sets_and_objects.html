isomorphism between contravariant hom-sets and objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $X,Y$ a objects.

TFAE:

  1. $X \cong Y$ are isomorphic
  2. there exists a morphism $f: X \rightarrow Y$ such that
\begin{align*} f^{*}: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y,X) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,X) \end{align*}

is a surjection and $f^*: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y,Y) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y)$ is a monomorphism
