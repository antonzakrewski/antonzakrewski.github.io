Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $f: x \rightarrow y$ a monomorphism in the homotopy category Then $f$ is in general not a monomorphism in $\mathcal{C}$.

Proof

Let $\mathcal{C} = \mathrm{An}$ be the category of Anima and consider $f: * \rightarrow S^1$. Then $f$ is not a monomorphism in the category of anima (cf. monomorphisms in Anima) but it is one in the homotopy category, since $*$ is the terminal object in both $\mathrm{An}$ and $\mathrm{hAn}$ (cf. projection to the homotopy category preserves products).
