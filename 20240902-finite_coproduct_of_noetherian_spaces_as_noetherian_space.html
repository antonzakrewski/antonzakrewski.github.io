finite coproduct of noetherian spaces as noetherian space

Proposition

Let $X_1,...,X_n$ be noetherian topological spaces. Then so is the topological sum $\coprod X_i$

Proof

Let $Z_i \subseteq \coprod X_i$ be a descending chain of closed subsets. Then it follows that $Z_i \cap X_i$ is a descending chain for each $i$. This stabilizes for each $i$, as $X$ is noetherian.

Therefore $Z_i$ also stabilizes, as $n$ is finite
