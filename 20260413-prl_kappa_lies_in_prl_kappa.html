PrL kappa lies in PrL kappa

Proposition

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Pr}^L_{\kappa} \in \mathrm{Pr}_{\kappa}^L \end{align*}

up to equivalence

Proof

recall that $\mathrm{Pr}_{\kappa}^L$ is equivalent to $\mathrm{Rex}_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{idem}}$ (cf. gabriel-ulmer duality).

So consider the

\begin{align*} \mathrm{forget}: \mathrm{Rex}_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{idem}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat} \end{align*}

which admits a left adjoint, namely the free cocompletion.

Then this adjunction is monadic adjunction:

using Barr-Beck-Lurie we need to show that $\mathrm{Rex}_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{idem}}$ admits <enough colimits> the forgetful functor is

  1. conservative
  2. preserves <split simplicial colimits>.
