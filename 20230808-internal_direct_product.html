internal direct product of groups

Definition

Let $G$ be a group and $U,N$ subgroups. Then $U,N$ are said to be an internal direct product, if

  1. $U,N \trianglelefteq G$ are normal subgroups
  2. $UN = G$ see product of group subset
  3. $U \cap N = \emptyset$
