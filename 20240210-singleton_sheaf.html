singleton sheaf

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category admitting products. Then for $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$, the singleton sheaf is defined as sheaf

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Op}( \{x\}) \rightarrow \emptyset \mapsto& 1 \\ \{x\} \mapsto& A \\ (\mathrm{res}: \{x\} \mapsto \emptyset) \mapsto& (1: A \rightarrow 1) \end{align*}

where $1$ is the terminal object
