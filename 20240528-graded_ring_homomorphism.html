graded ring homomorphism

Definition

Let $M$ be a monoid and $R \coloneqq \bigoplus_{m \in m} R_m, S \coloneqq \bigoplus_{m \in M} S_m$ graded rings Then a ring-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: R \rightarrow S \end{align*}

is said to be a a graded ring homomorphism, if for $m \in M$

\begin{align*} \varphi[R_m] \subseteq \varphi[S_m] \end{align*}
