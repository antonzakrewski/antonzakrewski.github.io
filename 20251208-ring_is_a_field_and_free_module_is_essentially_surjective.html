ring is a skew field and free module is essentially surjective

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring. TFAE:

  1. $R$ is a skew field
  2. the free module functor is essentially surjective

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

  • Existenz einer Basis in einem Vektorraum

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $r \in R \setminus \{0\}$. Then consider the generated, left ideal $(r) \subseteq R$. Then $R/(r)$ is free, so in particular torsion-free. Consider the element $[1] \in R/(r)$, then $r \cdot [1] = [r] = 0$. But as $R/(r)$ is torsion free, we may conclude $[1] = 0$ or $1 \in (r)$ resp. $r$ is a left unit. Now $r$ has a left inverse $r^{-1}$, which has another left inverse $r'$ and it follows that $r^{-1} = r'$ (cf. something like epic split mono as isomorphism)
