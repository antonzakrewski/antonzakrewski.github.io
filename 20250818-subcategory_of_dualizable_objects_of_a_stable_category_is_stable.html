subcategory of dualizable objects of a stable category is stable

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \otimes$ be a stably monoidal infinity category. Then the full subcategory of dualizable object is a stable subcategory.

Proof

corollary of:

  • dualizable object closed under finite limit in a stably symmetric monoidal category_
  • dualizable object closed under finite colimit in a stably symmetric monoidal category
