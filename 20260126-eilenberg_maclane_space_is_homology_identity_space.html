eilenberg-maclane space is homology-identity space

Proposition

Let $A$ be an abelian group. Then an Eilenberg-MacLane space $K(A,n)$ is a homology identity is identity space.

Proof

follows from representability of singular cohomology: If any map $f: K(A,n) \rightarrow K(A,n)$ is the identity on homology, then in particular it is so on cohomology (cf. using universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology).
