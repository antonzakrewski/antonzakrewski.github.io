excision of homology

Definition

Given a pair $(X,A)$ and $U \subseteq A$ such that

\begin{align*} \overline{U} \subseteq \mathrm{int}(U) \end{align*}

then there exist an evident inclusion

which induces an isomorphism on homology

\begin{align*} H_n(X \setminus U, A \setminus U) \cong H_n(X,A) \end{align*}
