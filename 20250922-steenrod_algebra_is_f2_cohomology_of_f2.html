Proposition

The canonical maps

\begin{align*} \mathbb{F}_2^*(\mathbb{F}_2) \rightarrow \pi_*(\underline{\mathrm{map}}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{F}_2, \mathbb{F}_2)) \rightarrow \mathrm{lim} \tilde{H}^*(K(\mathbb{F}_2,n); \mathbb{F}_2)[-n] \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{A}^* \rightarrow \mathrm{lim}_{n} \tilde{H}^*(K(\mathbb{F}_2,n),\mathbb{F}_2)[-n] \end{align*}

for the F2-Steenrod Algebra are isomorphisms

In particular $\mathcal{A}^* \cong \mathbb{F}_2^*(\mathbb{F}_2)$.

Proof

Recall that by abelian group is colimit of shifts of infinite suspensions of Eilenberg MacLane spaces the canonical map $\mathrm{colim}_n \Omega^n \tilde{\mathbb{S}}[K(\mathbb{F}_2,n)] \rightarrow \mathbb{F}_2$ is an equivalence of spectra. Thus $\underline{\mathrm{map}}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{F}_2,\mathbb{F}_2) \simeq \mathrm{lim}_n \underline{\mathrm{map}}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(K(\mathbb{F}_2,n), \Sigma^n \mathbb{F}_2)$.
