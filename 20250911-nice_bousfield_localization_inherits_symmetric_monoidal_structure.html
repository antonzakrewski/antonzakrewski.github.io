nice bousfield localization inherits symmetric monoidal structure

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_0 \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a left bousfield localization, $\mathcal{C}^{\otimes}$ a symmetric monoidal structure. Suppose the left adjoint $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_0$ satisfies that the unit $u_{x}: x \rightarrow \mathcal{L}(x)$ induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}(x \otimes y) \rightarrow \mathcal{L}(\mathcal{L}(x) \otimes y) \end{align*}

Then $\mathcal{C}_0$ inherits a symmetric monoidal structure.

Then

  1. $\mathcal{C}_{0}^{\otimes} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fin}_*$ is a cocartesian fibration (where $\mathcal{C}_0^{\otimes}$ denotes the full subcategory of $\mathcal{C}^{\otimes}$ containing $\mathcal{C}_0$ and "lists of it")
  2. the inclusion $\mathcal{C}_0^{\otimes} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\otimes}$ is lax
  3. there exsist a symmetric monoidal structure on the left adjoint $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C}^{\otimes} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_0^{\otimes}$

Proof
