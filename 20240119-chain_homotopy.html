chain homotopy

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes Suppose $(M_n),(M_n)' \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}))$ are chain complexes and $f,g: (M_n) \rightarrow (M_n')$ chain maps

A chain homotopy between $f$ and $g$ consists of morphisms

\begin{align*} H_{n}: M_n \rightarrow M_{n+1}' \end{align*}

or (in general not commuting!)

such that

\begin{align*} d_{n+1}' H_{n} + H_{n-1} d_n = f_n - g_n \end{align*}

or equivalently
