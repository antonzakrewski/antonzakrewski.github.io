boundary in the cofinite topology of R

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{R}$ be the real numbers with the cofinite topology and $A$ a subspace Then the boundary is given by

\begin{align*} \partial A = \begin{cases} A & \mbox{if } \vert A \vert < \infty \\ \mathbb{R} & \mbox{if } \vert A \vert = \infty, \vert X \setminus A \vert < \infty \\ \mathbb{R} \setminus A & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • Boundary as Closure without interior
  • closure in the cofinite topology of R
  • interior in the cofinite topology of R
