yoneda embedding of an additive category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a small additive category Then there exists a fully faithful functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{A} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Ab}) \\ A \mapsto& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(-,A) \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of:

  • yoneda embedding of a semiadditive category

where each functor restricts to

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(-,A): \mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab} \end{align*}
