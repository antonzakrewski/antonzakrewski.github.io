integral affine scheme and integral domain

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Spec}(R)$ be an affine scheme TFAE:

  1. $\mathrm{Spec}(R)$ is an integral scheme
  2. $R$ is an Integral domain

Proof

By the irreducible affine scheme and nilradical, we have that $\mathrm{Spec}(R)$ is irreducible if and only if the nilradical is a prime ideal Furthermore, $\mathrm{Spec}(R)$ is reduced scheme if and only if the nilradical vanishes. But an integral domain is precisely a ring where the zero ideal is a prime ideal.
