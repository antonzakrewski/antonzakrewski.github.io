characterization of extremally disconnected, compact hausdorff spaces via sections

Proposition

Let $X$ be a compact hausdorff space. TFAE:

  1. $X$ is extremally disconnected space
  2. each surjection $f: Y \rightarrow X$ from a compact hausdorff space admits a section

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $Y \subseteq X$ be open. We need to show that the closure $\overline{U}$ is again open.

Let

\begin{align*} Y \coloneqq (X \setminus U) \times \{0\} \sqcup \overline{U} \times \{1\} \subseteq X \times \{0,1\} \end{align*}

be the compact hausdorff space (cf. finite union of compact sets, subspace of a hausdorff space is hausdorff)

Then the map

is surjective, hence admits a section $s: X \rightarrow Y$.

In particular $s(U) \subseteq \overline{U} \times \{1\}$ (as $f_{\vert U \times \{1\}}$ is injective) so by closure and continuous map we get

\begin{align*} s(\overline{U}) \subseteq \overline{\overline{U}\times \{1\}} = \overline{U} \times \{1\} \end{align*}

and furthermore

\begin{align*} s^{-1}[ \overline{U}\times \{1\}] = \overline{U} \end{align*}

where $\overline{U} \times \{1\}$ is open. Therefore $\overline{U}$ is also open.

Note that any $x \in X \setminus \overline{U}$ must map to $X \setminus U\times \{0\}$ (by construction of the surjection), hence we get equality.
