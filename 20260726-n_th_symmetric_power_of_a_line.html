n-th symmetric power of a line

Proposition

Let $L$ be a line over a commutative ring $R$ and let $\mathrm{Sym}_R^d(L)$ be the \(d\)-th component of thesymmetric algebra. Then the canonical quotient map induces an isomorphism

\begin{align*} L^{\otimes d} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Sym}_{R}^d(L) \end{align*}
