rationally trivial spectrum and mod p trivial spectrum is trivial

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum TFAE:

  1. $X$ is the zero object
  2. $X_{\mathbb{Q}} \simeq 0$ and $X/p \simeq 0$ for all primes $p$.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

clear

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from R-local spectrum and mod p and the <fact that \(R\)-localization is a left bousfield localization>, so $X \simeq X_{\mathbb{Q}}$
