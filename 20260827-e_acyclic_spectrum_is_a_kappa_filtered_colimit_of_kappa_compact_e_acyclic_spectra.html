E-acyclic spectrum is a kappa filtered colimit of kappa compact E-acyclic spectra

Proposition

Let $E$ be a spectrum and $\kappa$ an uncountable regular cardinal such that $E$ is a kappa compact object (cf. object in an accessible category is a compact object for large enough cardinal, category of spectra is presentable category). Then every E-acyclic spectrum $X$ is the kappa filtered colimit of kappa compact, \(E\)-acyclic spectra.

Proofsketch

Let $X \simeq \mathrm{colim}_{i \in I} X_i$ be the $\kappa$ filtered colimit of $\kappa$ compact spectra (cf. spectrum is kappa filtered colimit of kappa compact spectrum). Using cofinal approximation of a kappa filtered category by a directed set we may assume that $I$ is a directed poset.

Then $E \otimes_{i \in I} X_i$ is a $\kappa$ compact object (cf. infinite extension of scalars preserves kappa compact objects) and hence $\pi_*(E \otimes_{i \in I} X_i)$ is $\kappa$ compactly presented (cf. kappa compact in modules over a ring spectrum and kappa small presentation). So we may choose less than $\kappa$ many generators $g_j \in \pi_{*}(E \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} X_i)$ and for each $g_i$ find a map $f_j: X_i \rightarrow X_j$ such that $g_j$ is killed by $f_j$.

The collection $\{f_j\}$ is a $\kappa$ small set, so we may find a map $\varphi_i: X_i \rightarrow X_{i'}$ which is nullhomotopic after applying $- \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} E$.

Then consider the (in general non-full), wide subcategory $J$ containing maps which are nullhomotopic after applying $- \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} E$. It is also a directed poset: for a $\kappa$ small amount of $X_l, l \in L$, we may first find an upper bound $Y$ and then a map $Y \rightarrow Y'$ which kills everything on homotopy groups.

By the previous argument (+ handwaving), $J \rightarrow I$ is a coinitial approximation (cf. cofinal morphism of posets), so we may assume that every map is nullhomotopic after applying $- \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} E$

Now we may w.l.o.g. assume that for each $i \in I$ we may assume that there exist infinitely many $j \in J$ with $i < j$.

Otherwise, the diagram has a supremum, so the colimit is given by evaluating at the supremum and therefore also $\kappa$ compact. The colimit is also by assumption acyclic.
