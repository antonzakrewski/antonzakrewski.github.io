tor independent quasi-coherent modules

Definition

Let $M,N$ be quasi-coherent modules over a scheme $X$. Then $M$ and $N$ are said to be tor independent, if $\mathrm{Tor}^{X}_i(M,N) = 0$ for all $i \geq 2$ (cf. tor of quasi-coherent modules).

Remark

We say that schemes $Y,Z$ are tor independent over $X$ if $\mathcal{O}_{Y}, \mathcal{O}_{Z}$ are tor independent over $X$.
