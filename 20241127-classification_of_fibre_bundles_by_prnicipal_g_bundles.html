classification of fibre bundles by principal g-bundles

Proposition

Let $B$ be a CW-Complex, $F$ a locally regular compact topological space, then Fibre bundles $p: E \rightarrow B$ with typical fibres $F$ are given by pullback of

\begin{align*} E \mathrm{Homeo}(F) \times_{\mathrm{Homeo}(F)} F \rightarrow B \mathrm{Homeo}(F) \end{align*}

which is the associated bundle
