right exact functor as additive functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be additive categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ be a right exact functor Then $\mathcal{F}$ is an additive functor
