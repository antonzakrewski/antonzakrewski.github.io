Quillens Theorem A for cofinal functors

Proposition

Let $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor where $\mathcal{D}$ is an infinity category TFAE:

  1. $f$ is cofinal
  2. for all $d \in \mathcal{D}$ the infinity coslice category $\mathcal{C}_{d/} = \mathcal{C} \times_{\mathcal{D}} \mathcal{D}_{d/}$ is weakly contractible

Proof

model independent
