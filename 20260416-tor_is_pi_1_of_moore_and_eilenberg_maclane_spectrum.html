Tor is pi_1 of moore and Eilenberg-MacLane spectrum

Proposition

Let $A,B$ be abelian groups. Then

\begin{align*} \pi_1(\mathbb{S}_{A} \otimes HB) \simeq \mathrm{Tor}_{1}^{\mathbb{Z}}(A,B) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • existence of a moore space & suspension spectrum of a moore space is a moore spectrum
  • universal coefficient theorem for singular homology
