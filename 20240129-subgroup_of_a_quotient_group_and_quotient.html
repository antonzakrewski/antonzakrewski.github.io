subgroup of a quotient group and quotient

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group and $N \trianglelefteq G$ be a normal subgroup Suppose $H \subseteq G$ is a subgroup and $\overline{H} = \pi_N[H]$ the subgroup of the quotient group $G/N$. Then for $N_{H} = N \cap H$

\begin{align*} \overline{H} \cong H/N_H \end{align*}

Proof
