category of prespectrum objects closed under limits in the functor category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category. Then the subcategory of prespectrum objects

\begin{align*} \mathrm{PSp}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathbb{N}\times \mathbb{N}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is closed under limits, i.e. the inclusion preserves and reflects those limits.

Proof

corollary of:

  • pointwise calculation of limits
  • infinity limit of terminal objects as terminal object

where infinity fully faithful functor reflects limits
