postcomposition of geometric realization and unstraightening up to geometric realization

Proposition

Let $p: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a cocartesian fibration. Let $\mathrm{Str}(p): \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}$ be the cocartesian unstraightening and $\vert - \vert \circ \mathrm{Str}(p): \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ the composition with the geometric realization.

Then left unstraightening is up to geometric realization

\begin{align*} \vert \mathrm{Un}(\vert - \vert \circ \mathrm{Str}(p) \vert \simeq \vert \mathcal{C} \vert \end{align*}

Proof

consider Lurie theorem about colimits in Anima & colimits in a bousfield localization which shows that

\begin{align*} \vert \mathrm{Un}(\vert - \vert \circ \mathrm{Str}(p)) \vert \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{\mathcal{D}} (\vert - \vert \circ \mathrm{Str}(p)) \\ \simeq& \vert \mathrm{colim}_{\mathcal{D}} \mathrm{Str}(p) \vert \\ \simeq& \vert \mathrm{Un}(\mathrm{Str}(p))[?^{-1}] \vert \\ \simeq& \vert \mathcal{C}[?^{-1}] \vert \\ \simeq& \vert \mathcal{C} \vert \end{align*}

where we don't care about inverting some morphisms, since at the end we invert all morphisms.
