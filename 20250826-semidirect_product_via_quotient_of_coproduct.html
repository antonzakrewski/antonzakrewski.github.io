semidirect product via quotient of coproduct

Proposition

Let $\varphi: G \rightarrow \mathrm{Aut}(H)$ be a group action, then the semidirect product is given by

\begin{align*} G \rtimes_{\varphi} H \coloneqq (G \coprod H)/(h \cdot g h^{-1} = \varphi_g(h) \vert g \in G,h \in H ) \end{align*}

Proof
