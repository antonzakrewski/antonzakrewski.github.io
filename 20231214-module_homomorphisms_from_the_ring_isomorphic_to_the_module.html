module homomorphisms from the ring isomorphic to the module

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $M$ an module. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{RMod}}(R,M) \cong M \end{align*}

are (canonically) isomorphism for

\begin{align*} \varphi: M \rightarrow& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{RMod}}(R,M) \\ m \mapsto& (f: 1 \mapsto m) \\ \varphi^{-1}: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{RMod}}(R,M) \rightarrow& M \\ m \mapsto& (f: 1 \mapsto m) \end{align*}

Proof

Note that an module-homomorphism from $R$ is determined by $f(1)$ since

\begin{align*} f(r) =& f(r \cdot 1) \\ =& r \cdot f(1) \end{align*}
