mapping anima from upper to lower truncation

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category equipped with a t-structure Suppose $X \in \mathcal{C}_{\geq 0}, Y \in \mathcal{C}_{\leq 0}$, then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X[1], Y) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, Y[-1]) \\ \simeq& \Delta^0 \end{align*}

Proof

one equivalence follows from the loop suspension adjunction for a pointed infinity category.

The other follows from

  • whitehead theorem for anima

after applying mapping space and suspension/loop space for a pointed category
