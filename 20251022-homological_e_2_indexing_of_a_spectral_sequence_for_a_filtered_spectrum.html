homological E_2 indexing of a spectral sequence for a filtered spectrum

Proposition

Let $X: \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}$ be a filtered spectrum. Then after reindexing there exists a spectral sequence with

  1. $E_{p,q}^2 = \pi_{p+q}(\mathrm{gr}^{q}(X))$

see:

Proof

Let

\begin{align*} E_{s,t}^1 = \pi_{s + t} \mathrm{gr}^{-s}X \end{align*}

Then set $p = 2s + t, q = -s$ and hence we get

\begin{align*} E_{p,q}^2 = \pi_{p+q}(\mathrm{gr}^q(X)) \end{align*}

We want to show that it is in fact the second page, i.e. it remains to understand the differential. this is given by

\begin{align*} \pi_{p+q} \mathrm{gr}^q(X) = \pi_{s + t} \mathrm{gr}^{-s} X \rightarrow \pi_{s-1,t} \mathrm{gr}^{-s-1} = \pi_{p-2+q} \mathrm{gr}^{?}(X)) \end{align*}
