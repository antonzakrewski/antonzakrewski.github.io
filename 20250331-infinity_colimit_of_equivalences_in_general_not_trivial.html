infinity colimit of equivalences in general not trivial

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category and

\begin{align*} K \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

be a diagram for a simplicial set $K$, where $K$ is an infinity-groupoid (or each vertex is an equivalence)

Then the colimit is in general not trivial

Proof

consider:

  • colimit of a point in Anima

for a nontrivial infinity-groupoid $K$
