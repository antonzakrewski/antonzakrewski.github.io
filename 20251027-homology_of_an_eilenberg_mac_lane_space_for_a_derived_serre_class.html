homology of an eilenberg-mac lane space for a nice derived serre class

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a nice derived serre ring. Then for all $A \in \mathcal{C}$ it follows that for all $k \geq , n \> 0$

\begin{align*} H_k(K(A,n); \mathbb{Z}) \in \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

\begin{align*} H_1(K(A,1), \mathbb{Z}) \simeq& \pi_1(A)^{\mathrm{ab}} \\ \simeq& A \end{align*}
