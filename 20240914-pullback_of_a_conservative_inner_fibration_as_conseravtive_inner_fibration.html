pullback of a conservative inner fibration between quasicategories as conservative inner fibration

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a conservative inner fibration Then

the map $\mathcal{G}: \mathrm{Pull} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}'$ is also a conservative inner fibration
