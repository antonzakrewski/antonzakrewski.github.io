Image of a functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $F: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor. The Image of the functor consists of the classes of all objects and morphisms in $\mathcal{D}$, namely $\{FA \vert A \in \mathcal{C}\}$ and $\{F f \vert f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,B)\}$
