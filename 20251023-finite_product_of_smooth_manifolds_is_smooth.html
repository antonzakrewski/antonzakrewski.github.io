finite product of smooth manifolds is a smooth manifold

Proposition

Let $M_1,M_2$ be smooth manifolds, then the product $M_1 \times M_2$ is canonically equipped with a smooth structure given by charts

\begin{align*} \mathbb{R}^n \times \mathbb{R}^m \simeq U_i \times V_j \xrightarrow{\varphi_i \times \psi_j} M_1 \times M_2 \end{align*}

for charts $U_1 \times V_j$

Furthermore the projections

\begin{align*} \pi_i: M_1 \times M_2 \rightarrow M_i \end{align*}

are smooth

Proof

a)

  • finite Product of topological manifolds is a topological manifold

b)

smooth structure:

  • follows as the transition maps are given by products of transition maps, hence in particular also smooth

smooth projections

follows as we are locally given by the projection, which is a smooth map

\begin{align*} \mathbb{R}^n \times \mathbb{R}^m \simeq U_i \times V_j \rightarrow U_i \simeq \mathbb{R}^n \end{align*}
