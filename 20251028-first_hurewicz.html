first hurewicz

Proposition

Let $X$ be a connected anima. Then the hurewicz homomorphism $\pi_1(X) \rightarrow H_1(X,\mathbb{Z})$ exhibits $H_1(X,\mathbb{Z})$ as Abelianization, i.e.,

\begin{align*} \pi_1(X)^{\mathrm{ab}} \simeq H_1(X) \end{align*}

Proof

sphere

recall that

  1. homology of the n-sphere
  2. pi_1 of a circle

Now by construction, the natural transformation maps a generator

\begin{align*} \pi_1(X) \rightarrow& H_1(X, \mathbb{Z}) \\ \mathrm{id} \mapsto& 1 \end{align*}

to a generator in $H_1(X,\mathbb{Z})$.

In particular, both groups are abstractly isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}$, so this map must be an isomorphism

general case

Let $X = \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{Sk}_n(X)$ be connected.
