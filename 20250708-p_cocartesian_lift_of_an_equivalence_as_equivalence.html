p-cocartesian lift of an equivalence as equivalence

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an infinity functor Let $f \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{D})$ be an equivalence and $g \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$ a lift of $f$ under $p$.

TFAE:

  1. $g$ is an equivalence
  2. $g$ is a p-cartesian lift

Proof

Assume $g: x \rightarrow y$.

note that by definition we get a digaram

which is an pullback iff $g$ is a p-cartesian lift.

here the bottom morphism is an equivalence since $f$ is one.

Hence apply square with parallel equivalences as infinity pullback to conclude that the diagram is a pullback square iff the top morphism is an equivalence. But that holds iff $g_*$ is one (weak infinity yoneda lemma)
