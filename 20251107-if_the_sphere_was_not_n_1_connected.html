If the sphere was not n-1 connected

one of the most underrated statements

  • no stable homotopy groups:
    • freudenthal suspension theorem seems difficult
    • homotopy excision
  • unsure about higher algebra:
    • what would be the monoidal unit ?
  • brown representability ?
  • no hurewicz isomorphism / theorem
  • unsure about spherical fibrations:
    • characteristic classes :/
  • poincare duality complexes would fail for spheres
    • unsure how to talk about spectra
  • no cellular approximation
  • presumably a lot more spaces would not be simple / simply connected
  • truncation of spheres would probably not work
    • obstruction theory
