cofiber functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an (sufficiently) finitely cocomplete infinity category with terminal object Then the cofiber functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ar}(\mathcal{C}) \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Ran}_{\iota}} \mathrm{Fun}(\Lambda_2^2, \mathcal{C}) \xrightarrow{\mathrm{colim}} \end{align*}

where we first left kan extend and then right kan extend. Then we may identify this with the cofiber functor by the right kan extension formula & left kan extension formula
