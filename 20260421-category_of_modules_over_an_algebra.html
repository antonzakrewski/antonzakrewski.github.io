category of left modules over an algebra

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a symmetric monoidal category and $A$ an E_n Algebra. Then the category of (left) modules over $A$ is defined as fiber of $\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathrm{Mod}}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbb{E}_n\)}(\mathcal{C})$ at $A$.
