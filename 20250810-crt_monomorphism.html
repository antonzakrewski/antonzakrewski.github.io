CRT monomorphism

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an abelian category. Let $X$ be an object and $Y_1,Y_2 \subseteq X$ be subobjects.

Then

\begin{align*} X/(Y_1 \cap Y_2) \rightarrow X/Y_1 \oplus X/Y_2 \end{align*}

is a monomorphism

where

proof

  • first isomorphism theorem for categories
  • intersection of two subobjects

alternatively

  • Mitchell's embedding theorem
  • proof it in R-Modules

elementary

An elementary calculation in an abelian category yields

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ker}(X \rightarrow X/Y_1 \oplus X/Y_2) \cong& \mathrm{ker}(X \rightarrow X/Y_1) \cap \mathrm{ker}(X \rightarrow X/Y_2) \\ \cong& Y_1 \cap Y_2 \\ \end{align*}

which shows that $X/(Y_1 \cap Y_2) \rightarrow X/Y_1 \oplus X/Y_2$ is a monomorphism.
