Field as noetherian ring

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field Then $K$ is a noetherian ring

Proof

Let $\mathfrak{a}$ be an ideal, then we conclude that

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a} = \begin{cases} \{0\} & K & \end{cases} \end{align*}

Therefore, $\{0\}$ is generated by $0$ and $K$ by $1$ (see: von einer Einheit erzeugtes Ideal und triviales Ideal)
