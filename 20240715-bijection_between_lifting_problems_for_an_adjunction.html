bijection of lifting problem solutions for an adjunction

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{L} \dashv \mathcal{R}$ an adjunction

Suppose there are given two lifting problems

where

  1. the squares commute
  2. $\mathcal{L}(d_i) \rightarrow c_i$ and $d_i \rightarrow \mathcal{R}(c_i)$ are the morphism corresponding via the adjunction

then the adjunction induces a bijection between solutions of the first and second lifting problem

Proof

corollary of:

  • bijection of coextension problem solutions via adjoint
  • bijection of extension problem solutions via adjoint

where a morphism is a solution iff it is a solution to the seperate extension / coextension problems
