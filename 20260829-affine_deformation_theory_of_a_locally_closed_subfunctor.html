affine deformation theory of a locally closed subfunctor

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $\mathrm{Spec}(R/I) \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(R)$ a closed subfunctor and $D(\overline{F}) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(R/I)$ an open subfunctor, or in other words a locally closed subfunctor $D(\overline{F}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(R)$. Then there exists a pullback diagram

for $F = \pi^{-1}[\overline{F}]$ for the projection $\pi: R \twoheadrightarrow R/I$.

Proof
