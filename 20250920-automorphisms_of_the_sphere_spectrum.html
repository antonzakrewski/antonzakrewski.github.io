Proposition

The anima valued automorphism group of the sphere spectrum is given by stable BG

Proof

Let $n \geq 1$. Then

\begin{align*} \Sigma: \mathrm{End}_*(S^n) \rightarrow \mathrm{End}_*(S^{n+1}) \end{align*}

induces an isomorphism on connected components, in particular it restricts to

\begin{align*} \Sigma: \mathrm{Aut}_*(S^n) \rightarrow \mathrm{Aut}_{*}(S^{n+1}) \end{align*}

since the endomorphisms of $S^n$ (for $n \geq 1$) are classified by $\mathbb{Z}$ and only $\pm 1$ are equivalences

(implicitly we are cofinally replacing $\mathbb{N}_0$ with $\mathbb{N}$)

now we use the mapping anima of the groupoid core for suspension spectra to get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}) \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{n} \mathrm{Aut}_*(S^n) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{n} G(n) \\ \simeq& G \end{align*}
