forgetful functor from E_n to E_m anima valued groups does not preserve compact objects

Proposition

Let $n > m$, then the forgetful functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_n}(\mathrm{An}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_m}(\mathrm{An}) \end{align*}

does not preserve compact object

Proof

recall compact object in anima valued E_n groups and compact n-fold delooping, so it suffices to give a compact anima $X$ such that

  1. $\Omega^{n-m} X$ is not compact
  2. $\Omega^{n} X$ is nontrivial.

$n - m = 1$

Consider $X = S^2$. Then loop space of higher spheres never vanishes so consider the \(\mathbb{E}_n\)-group $\Omega^n S^2$. It follows that it is a compact \(\mathbb{E}_n\) group, since $B^n \Omega^n S^2 \simeq S^2$ is compact

But $B^{n-m} \Omega^{n} S^2 \simeq \Omega S^2$ is not compact (cf. compact anima has finitely generated homology, homology of the loop space of the sphere).

$n - m > 1$

Consider $\Omega^{n} S^{n-m}$ which again does not vanish. Then $B^n \Omega^{n} S^{n-m} \simeq S^{n-m}$ is compact, but $B^{m} \Omega^{n} S^{n-m} \simeq \Omega^{n-m} S^{n-m}$ is not compact, since $\pi_0(\Omega^{n-m} S^{n-m}) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ (cf. compact anima contains finitely many connected components).
