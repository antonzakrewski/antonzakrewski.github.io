finitely presented commutative A-Algebra

Definition

Let \(A\) be a commutative ring and $B$ a commutative A-Algebra Then $B$ is said to be finitely presented, if it is

  1. of finite type
  2. the kernel
\begin{align*} \varphi: A[X_1,...,X_n] \rightarrow B \end{align*}

is a finitely generated ideal
