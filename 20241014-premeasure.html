premeasure

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)$ be a ring of sets and

\begin{align*} \mu: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow [0,\infty] \end{align*}

a content.

Then $\mu$ is said to be a pre measure, if it is in addition sigma additive function

i.e.

  • $\emptyset$
  • $\sigma$ additive
