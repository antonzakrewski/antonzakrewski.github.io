product in PrL kappa

Proposition

Let $I$ be a set and $\mathcal{C}_i$ kappa presentable categories. Then the product in $\mathrm{Pr^L}_{\kappa}$ is given by

\begin{align*} \prod_{i}^{\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\kappa}} \mathcal{C}_i \simeq \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\prod_{i} \mathcal{C}_i^{\kappa}) \end{align*}
