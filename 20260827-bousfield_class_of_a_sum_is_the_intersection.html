bousfield class of a sum is the intersection

Proposition

Let $E_i$ be spectra Then

\begin{align*} \langle \bigoplus_{i} E_i \rangle = \bigcap_{i} \langle E_i\rangle \end{align*}

Proof

Let $X$ be a spectrum, then $X \otimes \bigoplus_{i} E_i = \bigoplus_{i} (X \otimes E_i)$ which shows that the LHS vanishes if and only if $X \otimes E_i$ vanishes for all $i \in I$
