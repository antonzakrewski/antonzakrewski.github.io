natural transformation from identity to the constant terminal object

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $1$ the terminal object. Then there exists a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \mathrm{id} \rightarrow \underline{1} \end{align*}

from the identity functor to the constant functor to the terminal object.

Proof

follows from uniqueness of the terminal morphism
