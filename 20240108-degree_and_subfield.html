degree and subfield

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $k \subseteq K$ a subfield and $L/K$ a field extension. Then for the degree it holds, that

\begin{align*} [L : K] \leq [L : k] \end{align*}

Proof

follows from Gradsatz, as

\begin{align*} [L : k] = [L : K] \cdot [K : k] \end{align*}

where $[K : k] \geq 1$
