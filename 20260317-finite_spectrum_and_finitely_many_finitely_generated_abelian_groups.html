finite spectrum and bounded below, finitely many, finitely generated homology

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum TFAE:

  1. $X$ is a finite spectrum.
  2. $X$ is bounded below spectrum and has finitely many, finitely generated Z-homology groups.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from some long exact sequence argument

2) $\implies$ 1)

assume that $X$ has finitely many, finitely generated homology groups. Observe that $\pi_*(X)$ is finitely generated group (cf. fundamental theorem of serre class theory for spectra).

Then we may use a cw structure of a bounded below spectrum $X_i$ and it suffices to show that $X \simeq X_n$ for some finite $n$ (observe that $\pi_*()$ is always degreewise finite by finite type spectrum and finitely generated homotopy and finitely generated homology). Here $X_i \rightarrow X$ becomes increasingly connective and $- \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z}$ preserves connective maps.

Hence the map becomes an equivalence on homology after finitely many steps and therefore also a equivalence of spectra.
