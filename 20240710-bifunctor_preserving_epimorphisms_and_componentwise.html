Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_1,\mathcal{C}_2, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}_1 \times \mathcal{C}_1 \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

be a bifunctor

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ preserves epimorphisms
  2. $\mathcal{F}(A,-)$ and $\mathcal{F}(-,B)$ preserve epimorphisms for arbitrary objects $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C}_1), B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C}_2)$
