noetherian ring and finite generation of prime ideals

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring. TFAE:

  1. $A$ is a noetherian ring
  2. each prime ideal $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ is finitely generated

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Special case

2) $\implies$ 1)

Suppose there exists a non finitely generated ideal. Then by union of a chain of non-finitely generated ideals non-finitely generated and maximal non finitely generated ideal as prime ideal it follows that there also exists a non finitely generated prime ideal.

Hence contradiction to the assumption.
