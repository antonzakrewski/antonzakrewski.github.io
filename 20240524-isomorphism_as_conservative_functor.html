isomorphism as conservative functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an isomorphism as functor Then $\mathcal{F}$ is conservative

Proof

corollary of

  • isomorphism in category cat as object-bijective, fully faithful functor
