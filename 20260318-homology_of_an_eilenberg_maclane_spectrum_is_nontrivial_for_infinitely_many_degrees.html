homology of an Eilenberg-MacLane spectrum for a finitely generated group is nontrivial for infinitely many degrees

Proposition

Let $A$ be an Eilenberg-MacLane spectrum for a finitely generated abelian group. Then $\pi_*(A \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z}$) is nontrivial for infinitely many degrees.

Proof

follows as $A$ is

  1. bounded below
  2. fundamental theorem of serre class theory for spectra, which shows that
  3. finite spectrum and finitely many, finitely generated homology
  4. eilenberg maclane spectrum is not finite
