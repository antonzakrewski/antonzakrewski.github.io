retract of a colimit diagram is a colimit diagram

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $\mathcal{F}: I^{\triangleleft} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a colimit cocone. Let $\mathcal{F}': I^{\triangleleft} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a retract of $\mathcal{F}$ in $\mathrm{Fun}(I^{\triangleleft}, \mathcal{C})$, then $\mathcal{F}'$ is also a colimit

Proof

follows from:

  • colimit diagram and counit of left kan extension is an equivalence
  • equivalences closed under retracts in the arrow category
