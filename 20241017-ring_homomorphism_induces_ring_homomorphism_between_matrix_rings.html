ring homomorphism induces ring homomorphism between matrix rings

Proposition

Let \(A,B\) be rings, $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ a ring-homomorphism. Then $\varphi$ induces a ring-homomorphism between matrix rings

\begin{align*} \varphi^*: \mathrm{M}_n(R) \rightarrow& \mathrm{M}_n(S) \\ (\alpha_{i,j}) \mapsto& \varphi(\alpha_{i,j}) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from additive functor as ring homomorphism on endomorphism rings and the extension of scalars
