equivalent pushout/pullback squares in a stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category Suppose one of these squares 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
A \rar{f} \dar{g} & B \dar{h} \dar{h} & & A \oplus A \dar{f \oplus g} \rar{\nabla} & A \dar & & A \oplus B \rar{(f,g)} & C & & A \rar{(f,g)}& B \oplus C \dar{h-k}\\
C \rer{k} & D & & B \oplus C \rar{h + k} & D & & D \oplus D \rar{\nabla} & D & & 0 & D \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

commutes and is a pushout/pullback.

Then there exists a canonical homotopy witnessing the commutativity of the other three and they are pushouts/pullbacks as well.
