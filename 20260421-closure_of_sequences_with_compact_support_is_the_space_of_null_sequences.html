closure of sequences with compact support in lp is the space of null sequences

Proposition

Let $c_0$ be the sequences with compact support. Then the closure in $l^{\infty}$ (cf. ??) is the space of null sequences.

Proof

$\subseteq$

Let $(x_n)$ be a null sequence. Then it follows that the sequence $(y_n^m) \coloneqq ((x_1,...,x_m,0,0,...))_{m \in \mathbb{N}}$ converges to $x_n$ as

\begin{align*} \| x_n - y_n^m \| = \mathrm{sup}(x_n \vert n > m) \xrightarrow{m \rightarrow \infty} 0 \end{align*}

$\supseteq$

Let $(x_n^m)$ be convergent sequence of sequences with compact support for $m \in \mathbb{N}$. Suppose it converges to $y_n$, then we have to show that $y_n$ is a null sequence. Observe that for every $\varepsilon > 0$, there exists an $m >> 0$ such that $\| y_n - x_n^m \| = \mathrm{max}(y_n \vert n > m) < \varepsilon$. But this implies, that $y_n$ converges to $0$.
