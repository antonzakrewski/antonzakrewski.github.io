homotopy category of a simplicial category functor

Definition

Given $\mathrm{Cat}_{\Delta}$ as category of simplicially enriched categories, the homotopy category functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \pi_0: \mathrm{Cat}_{\Delta} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat} \end{align*}

given by lax monoidal functor induces a 2-functor on category of of enriched functors for

\begin{align*} \pi_0: \mathrm{sSet} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} \end{align*}

pi_0 as pi_0 preserves finite products

I.e. a simplicial category $\mathcal{C}$ gets send to the category containing

  1. $\mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ as objects
  2. $\pi_1 \mathcal{C}(x,y)$ as hom-set
