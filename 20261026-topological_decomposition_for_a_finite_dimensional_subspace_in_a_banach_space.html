topological decomposition for a finite dimensional subspace in a banach space

Proposition

Let $X$ be a banach space and $T \subseteq X$ a finite dimensional subspace Then there exists a closed linear subspace $X_0 \subseteq X$ such that $X_0 \oplus T = X$

Proof

choose a basis $e_1,...,e_n$ of $T$ and then you get a dual basis $\partial_{i}$ of $T^*$. using hahn-bach theorem we can extend these functionals to functions $f$. Now the intersection of $\mathrm{ker}(f_n)$ suffices as $X_0$.
