equivalence is detected by the fiber of the inclusion into the arrow category

Proposition

Let $f: * \rightarrow \mathrm{Ar}(\mathcal{C})$ be a morphism in a category. TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an equivalence
  2. the fiber of $f$ for the inclusion $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ar}(\mathcal{C})$ is the point, i.e.

is a pullback.

  1. the fiber of $f$, as in 2), is not the empty category

Proof

follows from:

  • inclusion of the category into the arrow category is fully faithful with equivalences as essential image
