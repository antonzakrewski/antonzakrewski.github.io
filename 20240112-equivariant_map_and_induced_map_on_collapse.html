Proposition

Let $G$ be a group, $X,Y$ be G-Sets and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ an G-equivariant map. Then for the set of orbits $X/G,Y/G$, there exists a canonical morphism

Proof

Let

\begin{align*} \overline{f}: X/G \rightarrow& Y/G [x] \mapsto& [f(x)] \end{align*}

welldefined

Let $x,x' \in X$ such that $[x] = [x']$. Then there exists an $g \in G$ with $g \cdot x = x'$. Thus

\begin{align*} f(x') = f(g \cdot x) = g \cdot f(x) \end{align*}

and hence

\begin{align*} [f(x')] = [f(x)] \end{align*}

G-equivariant

by construction of the orbital collapse as g-set, both group actions are the trivial group action hence

\begin{align*} f(g \cdot x) =& f(x) \\ =& g \cdot f(x) \end{align*}
