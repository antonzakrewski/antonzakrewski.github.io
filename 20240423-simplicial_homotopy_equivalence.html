simplicial homotopy equivalence

Definition

Let $A,B$ be simplicial sets and $f: A \rightarrow B$ a morphism of simplicial objects. Then $f$ is said to be simplicial homotopy equivalence, if there exists a morphism $g: B \rightarrow A$ and homotopies

\begin{align*} p: \Delta^1 \times A \rightarrow& A \\ q: \Delta^1 \times B \rightarrow& B \end{align*}

between

  1. $g \circ f$ and $\mathrm{id}_{A}$ relative $\emptyset$
  2. $f \circ g$ and $\mathrm{id}_{B}$ relative $\emptyset$
