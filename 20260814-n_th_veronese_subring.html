r-th veronese subring

Definition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $r \in R$ and $A$ an $R$ graded ring. Then the \(r\)-th veronese subring is defined as

\begin{align*} R^{(r)} = \bigoplus_{s \in R} R_{sr} \end{align*}
