rigid stable presentable category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C} \in \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}^{L, \mathrm{st}})$ be a Then $\mathcal{C}$ is said to be rigid, if for every finite set, the map

\begin{align*} \mu: \mathcal{C}^{\otimes I} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

admits a right adjoint in $\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathcal{C}^{\otimes I}(\mathrm{Pr}^{L, \mathrm{st}})$.
