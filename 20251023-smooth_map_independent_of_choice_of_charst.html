smooth map independent of choice of charts

Proposition

Let $f: M \rightarrow N$ be a continuous map between smooth manifold TFAE:

  1. $f$ is smooth at each point for some chart $(U, \varphi)$
  2. $f$ is smooth at each point for each (appropriate) chart $(U, \varphi)$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Smoothness at a point is a local property. So let $x \in M$ and $(U_1, \varphi_1),(U_2, \varphi_2)$ be charts around $x$. w.l.o.g. assume that $N$ is $\mathbb{R}^n$ after picking a chart and restricting the map $M \rightarrow N$ to the preimage of the chart (which is open by continuouity).
