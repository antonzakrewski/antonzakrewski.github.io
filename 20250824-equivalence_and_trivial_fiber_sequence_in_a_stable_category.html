equivalence and trivial fiber/cofiber sequence in a stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a morphism.

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an equivalence
  2. there exists a fiber sequence $0 \rightarrow X \xrightarrow{f} Y$
  3. there exists a fiber sequence $X \xrightarrow{f} Y \rightarrow 0$

Proof

use:

  • mapping spectrum functor is exact in both variables
  • weak infinity yoneda lemma
  • whitehead theorem for spectra
