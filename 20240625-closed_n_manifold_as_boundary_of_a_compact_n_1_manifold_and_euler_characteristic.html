closed n manifold as boundary of a compact n+1 manifold and euler characteristic

Proposition

Let \(M\) be a closed manifold and $W$ a compact topological manifold with boundary such that the boundary is

\begin{align*} \partial W = M \end{align*}

Then the euler characteristic of $M$ is even

Proof

$n$ odd

euler characteristic of a closed odd dimensional manifold

$n$ even, $n = 2k$

Suppose $W$ is such a manifold. Then the product

\begin{align*} W \times I \end{align*}

is a compact \(n+2\)-manifold (cf. finite Product of topological manifolds as topological manifold, Tychonoff's Theorem) with boundary

\begin{align*} \partial (W \times I) = W \times \{0\} \cup \partial W \times I \cup W \times \{W\} \end{align*}

(cf. product rule for the boundary of a manifold)

Then

\begin{align*} \{\partial(W \times I) \setminus W \times \{i\}_{i = 0,1}\} \end{align*}

is an open cover of $\partial (W \times I)$ such that both members are homotopy equivalent to $W$ (cf. inclusion of the interior of a manifold as homotopy equivalence)

But as $\partial (W \times I)$ is an odd dimensional compact manifold we get

\begin{align*} 0 =& \chi(\partial (W \times I)) = \chi(W) + \chi(W) - \chi(X) \end{align*}

showing

\begin{align*} \chi(X) = 2\chi(W) \end{align*}

(cf. euler characteristic of an open cover)
