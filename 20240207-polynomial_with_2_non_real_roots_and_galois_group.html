polynomial with 2 non real roots and galois group

Proposition

Let $p \in \mathbb{P}$ be a prime number and $f \in \mathbb{Q}[X]$ irreducible of degree $p$ with $2$ non real root Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Gal}(f) \cong S_p \end{align*}

Proof

Let $L$ be a splitting field of $f$ and $\alpha \in L$ a root of $f$ Then

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Q} \subseteq \mathbb{Q}(\alpha) \subseteq L \end{align*}

and by

\begin{align*} p = \vert \mathbb{Q}(\alpha) : \mathbb{Q} \vert \mid \vert L : \mathbb{Q} \vert = \vert \mathrm{Gal}(f) \vert \end{align*}

(cf. degree of a simple field extension and degree of the minimal polynomial)

By Satz von Cauchy über die Gruppenordnung there exists an element of group order $p$.

Let $\alpha_1,...,\alpha_p$ be the roots of $p$ and $\alpha_i,\alpha_j$ not $\mathbb{R}$.

w.l.o.g. after renumbering, we may assume that $p = (1,2,...,p)$

Since $f \in \mathbb{Q}[X]$, there exists the complex conjugation as field-automorphism
