forgetful functor from E_n to E_1 does not reflect compact objects

Proposition

The forgetful functor $\mathrm{Grp}_{\mathrm{E}_n}(\mathrm{An}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathrm{E}_1}(\mathrm{An})$ does not reflect compact objects

Proof

consider $\mathbb{Z}$: it is compact as \(\mathbb{E}_\infty\) group. It is a compact \(\mathbb{E}_1\) group, but not compact as \(\mathbb{E}_n\) group (cf. classification of compact Eilenberg-MacLane spaces).
