map form steinberg group to infinite elementary linear group

Definition

Let $R$ be a ring Then the map from the steinberg group to infinite elementary linear group is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{St}(R) \rightarrow& \mathrm{E}(R) \\ e_{i,j}(r) \mapsto& e_{i,j}(r) \end{align*}

defined via

  • free group functor as left adjoint
  • fundamental theorem of group homomorphism

i.e. $E(R)$ satisfies the relations imposed on $\mathrm{St}(R)$
