C_0 of a locally compact hausdorff space is a C-star algebra

Proposition

Let $X$ be a locally compact, hausdorff space. Then $C_0(X)$ is a C-star Algebra with the star structure given by pointwise conjugation (postcomposition with the conjugation)

Proof

Observe that $\mathbb{C}$ with conjugation is a star-algebra. We proceed to show that $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Set}}(X, \mathbb{C})$ admits a pointwise star algebra structure. Furthermore, $C_0(X) \subseteq C(x) \subseteq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Set}}(X, \mathbb{C})$ is closed under the operations, since multiplication / addition / conjugation is continuous on $\mathbb{C}$ and the product of functions in $C_0$ lies in $C_0$: Here for $f_1,f_2 \in C_0(X)$ and an $\varepsilon > 0$ choose compacts $K_1,K_2$ such that $\vert f_1(X \setminus K_1) \vert \subseteq [0,1), \vert f_2(X \setminus K_2) \vert \subseteq [0,\varepsilon)$.

let $l^2(X)$ be the l^2 of the underlying set. Now any element $f \in C_0(X)$ is bounded: choose a compact $K$ such that $\vert f(X \setminus K) \vert < 1$ and then choose the maximum of $\vert f \vert$ restricted to $1$ and $K$.

So in particular, for any $h \in l^2(X)$ we have $\| f \cdot h \|_2 \leq \vert f \vert_{\infty} \cdot \| h \|_2$ which also implies that $\| f \| \leq \| f \|_{\infty}$. Now we show $\| f \| = \| f \|_{\infty}$: For this choose a sequence $x_n \in X$ such that $\vert f(x_n) \vert \rightarrow \| f \|_{\infty}$. Then $f ( (1 \cdot x))$ shows $\|f \| > \| f \|_{\infty}$.

Now let $f_n$ be a cauchy sequence in $C_0$ with respect to $\| - \|_{\infty}$, then the pointwise limit of this lies in $C(X)$ and it remains to show that it lies in $C_0(X)$: Let $\varepsilon > 0$, then choose an $n >> 0$ such that $\vert f - f_n \|_{\infty} \vert < \frac{\varepsilon}{2}$ and a $K$ such that $\vert f_{n}(X \setminus K) \vert < \frac{\varepsilon}{2}$ In particular, the triangle inequality then implies that $\vert f[X \setminus K] \vert < \varepsilon$.
