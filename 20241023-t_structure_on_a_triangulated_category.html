t-structure on a triangulated category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a triangulated category. Then a \(t\)-structure is given by two replete full subcategories $\mathcal{D}_{\geq 0}, \mathcal{D}_{\leq 0}$ such that

  1. for $X \in \mathcal{D}_{\geq 0}, Y \in \mathcal{D}_{\leq 0}$ it holds that
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(X,Y[-1]) = 0 \end{align*}
