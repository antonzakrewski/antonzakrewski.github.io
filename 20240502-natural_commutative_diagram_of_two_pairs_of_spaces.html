natural diagram of singular chains for two pairs of spaces

Proposition

Let $(X,A), (Y,B)$ be pairs of spaces. Then for the singular chain complex there exists a natural diagram 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
0 \arrow[]{r}{} & C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X) \otimes C^{\mathrm{sing}}(B) + C^{\mathrm{sing}}(A) \otimes C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{\mathrm{EZ}_{X,B} + \mathrm{EZ}_{A,Y}} & C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X) \otimes C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{\mathrm{EZ}_{X,Y}} & C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X,A) \otimes C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y,B) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{}& 0 \\
0 \arrow[]{r}{} & C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times B) + C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y \times A) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times Y) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times Y)/(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times B) \oplus C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y \times A)) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & 0 \\
0 \arrow[]{r}{} & C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times B \cup A \times Y) \arrow[]{r}{} & C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times Y)  \arrow[]{r}{} & C^{\mathrm{sing}((X,A) \times_p (Y,B)) \arrow[]{r}{}& 0
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

which commutes up to chain homotopy

Here

\begin{align*} C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X) \otimes C^{\mathrm{sing}}(B) + C^{\mathrm{sing}}(A) \otimes C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y) \coloneqq& \mathrm{im}(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X) \otimes C^{\mathrm{sing}}(B) \oplus C^{\mathrm{sing}}(A) \otimes C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y) \rightarrow C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X) \otimes C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y)) \\ C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times B) + C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y \times A) \coloneqq& \mathrm{im}( C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times B) \oplus C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y \times A) \rightarrow C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times Y)) \end{align*}
  1. $(X,A) \times_p (Y,B)$ is defined as pseudo product of pairs of spaces
  2. $\mathrm{EZ}_{-,-}$ is the eilenberg-zilber transformation

Proof
