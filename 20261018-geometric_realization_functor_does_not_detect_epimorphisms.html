geometric realization functor does not detect epimorphisms

Proposition

the geometric realization from Cat infinity does not detect epimorphisms

Proof

consider $\Delta^0 \rightarrow \Delta^1$ (either inclusion) which becomes an equivalence after applying the geometric realization functor, because both categories contain a termina/inital object. But the pushout $\Delta^1 \leftarrow \Delta^0 \rightarrow \Delta^1$ is the category classifying spans/cospans, i.e. in particular not equivalent to $\Delta^0$.
