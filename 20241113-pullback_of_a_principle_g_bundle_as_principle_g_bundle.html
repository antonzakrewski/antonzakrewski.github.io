pullback of a principal g-bundle as principal g-bundle

Proposition

Let $G$ be a topological group, $p: E \rightarrow B$ a principal g-bundle and $f: B' \rightarrow B$ a continuous map Then the pullback is also a principal g-bundle

Proof

Define the action

\begin{align*} (e,b') \cdot g \mapsto (e \cdot g,b') \end{align*}

which is continuous as it is continuous in both components (in the first, its the g-action on $E$, on the second, its the projection and this restricts to the subspace $E \times_B B' \subseteq E \times B'$).
