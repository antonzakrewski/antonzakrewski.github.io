sequences with compact support are dense in lp

Proposition

Let $c_0$ be the sequences with compact support in <lp>. Then $c_0$ is dense in $l^p$

Proof

Let $x_n$ be a sequence in $l^p$. Then consider $(y_n)^m = (x_0,x_1,...,x_m,0,0,...)$, which has compact support. We claim that $(y_n)^m \rightarrow (x_n)$. This follows as $\| y_n^m - x_n \| = \sqrt[p}{\sum_{i=m+1}^\infty \vert x_n \vert^p}$ which converges to $0$: Here it suffices to observe that $\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} \vert x_n \vert^p$ converges to $\|(x_n)\|^p$.
