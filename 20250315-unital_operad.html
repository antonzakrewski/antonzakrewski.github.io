unital infinity operad

Definition

Let $\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}$ be an infinity operad Then $\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}$ is said to be unital, if

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mul}_{\mathcal{O}}(\emptyset, X) \end{align*}

is contractible

see:

  • Mul of an infinity operad

Note that this is the condition, that $\mathcal{O}$ has a unique nullary operation, not a unary operation $\mathrm{id}$ (which exists by definition)
