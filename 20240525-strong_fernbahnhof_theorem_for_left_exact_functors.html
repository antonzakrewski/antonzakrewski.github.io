strong Fernbahnhof theorem for left exact functors

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}), \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{B})$ the category of chain complexes and $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{B})$ a left exact functor, which preserves trivial boundary chain complexes Then for the homology chain complex functor, there exists a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}H_*(-) \rightarrow H_*(\mathcal{F}(-)) \end{align*}

of functors

Note that $\mathcal{F}$ may be induced by a left exact functor $\mathcal{F}': \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ (cf. left exact functor induces functor on chain complexes)
