section functor from Bundles as fully faithful

Proposition

Let $X$ be a normal space, then the section functor from Bundles to R-Mod is fully faithful.

Proof

faithful

Let $E_1,E_2$ be bundles and $f,g: E_1 \rightarrow E_2$ be morphism of topological vector bundles. Suppose $\Gamma(f) = \Gamma(g)$

Let $e \in E_1$ be arbitrary and $x \coloneqq p(e_1) \in X$. CHoose a local basis $s_i$ (cf. existence of a local basis of a vector bundle) such that

\begin{align*} e = \sum_{i=1}^n a_i s_i(x) \end{align*}

Define

\begin{align*} s: U \rightarrow E \end{align*}

as

\begin{align*} s(y) = \sum_{i=1^n a_i s_i(-) \end{align*}

and use local extension of a section for a normal space to get a section $s'$ with $s'(x) = e$

Then

\begin{align*} f(e) =& f(s'(x)) \\ =& \Gamma(f)(s')(x) \\ =& \Gamma(g)(s')(x) \\ =& g(s'(x)) \\ =& g(e) \end{align*}

full

Let $I_X \subseteq C(X)$ be the ideal with fucntions vanishing on $x$.

Then we get an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \Gamma(E_1)/I_x \simeq& p^{-1}[x] [s] \mapsto& s(x) \end{align*}

Let $F: \Gamma(E_1) \rightarrow \Gamma(E_2)$ be a \(C(X)\)- module-homomorphism We have to construct a bundle map $f$ with $\Gamma(f) = F$

This induces a map 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\Gamma(E_1)/I_x \rar \dar & \Gamma(E_2)/I_y \dar \\
p_1^{-1}_{1}( \{x\}) \rar & p_2^{-1}_{1}( \{x\}) \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Now define

\begin{align*} f: E_1 \rightarrow E_2 \\ f_{\vert p_1^{-1}[ \{x\}]} = f_x \end{align*}

it is by definition linear.

it remains to show that this map is continuous
