derived adjunction of a quillen adjunction

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$ be a quillen adjunction. Then the adjunction descends on the homotopy category, i.e.

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ho}(\mathcal{F}) \dashv \mathrm{Ho}(\mathcal{G}) \end{align*}

for

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ho}(\mathcal{F}): \mathrm{Ho}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ho}(\mathcal{D}) \\ \mathrm{Ho}(\mathcal{G}): \mathrm{Ho}(\mathcal{D}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ho}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

Proof
