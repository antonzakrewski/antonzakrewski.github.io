equivalent characterization of a phantom map in spectra

Proposition

Let $\varphi: E \rightarrow E'$ be a map of spectra. TFAE:

  1. for every anima $X$, the map $E_*[\tilde{\mathbb{S}}[X]] \rightarrow E'_*[\tilde{\mathbb{S}}[X]]$ is the zero map
  2. for every spectrum $X$, the map $E_*(X) \rightarrow E'_*(X)$ is the zero map
  3. for every finite spectrum $X$, the map $E_*(X) \rightarrow E'_*(X)$ is the zero map
  4. for every finite spectrum $X$, the map $E^*(X) \rightarrow E'^*(X)$ is the zero map
  5. $\varphi$ is a phantom map for every finite spectrum $X$ and every map $g: X \rightarrow E$, the composition

is nullhomotopic.
