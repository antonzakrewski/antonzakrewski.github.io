steenrod Algebra as N-graded Algebra

Proposition

The Steenrod Algebra is an \(\mathbb{N}\)-graded $\mathbb{F}_2$ Algebra with \begin{align*} \vert \mathrm{Sq}^n \vert = n \end{align*}

Proof
