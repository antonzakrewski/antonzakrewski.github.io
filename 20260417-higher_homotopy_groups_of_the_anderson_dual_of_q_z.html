homotopy groups of the brown-comenetz dual of Q/Z

Proposition

Let $I_{\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}}$ be the brown comenetz dual of Q/Z. Then

\begin{align*} \pi_n(I_{\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}}) \simeq& \begin{cases} \pi_{-n} \mathbb{S} & \mbox{if } n < 0 \\ \mathbb{Q}/ \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } n = 0 \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

Recall that

\begin{align*} \pi_n(I_{\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}}) \simeq& \pi_n(\mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}, I_{\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}}) \\ \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\pi_{-n}(\mathbb{S}), \mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

which evaluates as desired.
