homology of the third wu manifold

Proposition

The integral homology of the Wu Manifold $\mathrm{Wu}$ is given by

\begin{align*} H_*(\mathrm{Wu}(3),\mathbb{Z}) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } * = 0 \\ 0 & \mbox{if } \\ * = 1 \\ \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } * = 2 \\ \\ 0 & \mbox{if } \\ * = 3 \\ 0 & \mbox{if } \\ * = 4 \\ \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } \\ * = 4 \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

low homotopy groups of the Wu 3 Manifold shows that $\mathrm{Wu}(3)$ is simply connected, in particular it is orientable (cf. manifold with pi_1 without index 2 subgroup is orientable) and $\pi_1(\mathrm{Wu}(3)) \cong \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}$. Then Hurewicz theorem shows $H_2(\mathrm{Wu}(3)) = \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}$.

Now the universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology shows $H^1(\mathrm{Wu}(3)),H^2(\mathrm{Wu}(3)) = 0$ and poincare duality gives the rest.
