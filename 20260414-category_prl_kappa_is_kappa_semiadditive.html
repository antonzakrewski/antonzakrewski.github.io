category PrL kappa is kappa preadditive

Proposition

The category PrL kappa is \(\kappa\)-preadditive

Proof

follows from:

  • terminal category is presentable
  • terminal category is initial object in PrL (and moreover PrL kappa)
  • product in PrL kappa / coproduct in PrL kappa
  • sufficiently small product in PrL kappa is computed in PrL
