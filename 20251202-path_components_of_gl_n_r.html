path components of GL_n(R)

Proposition

$\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{R})$ has two connected components, given by those matrices with $\mathrm{det}(A) < 0$ resp. $\mathrm{det} > 0$

Proof

at least 2

follows as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{det}: \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{R}) \twoheadrightarrow \mathbb{R} \setminus \{0\}\\ \end{align*}

is a continuous, surjective map and the LHS has 2 connected components.

at most 2
