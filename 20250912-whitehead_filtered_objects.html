whitehead filtered objects

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category equipped with a t-structure. Then a filtered object $X_n$ is said to be a whitehead filtered object if there exists some $X \in \mathcal{C}$ and a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} X_n \simeq \tau_{\geq n} X \end{align*}
