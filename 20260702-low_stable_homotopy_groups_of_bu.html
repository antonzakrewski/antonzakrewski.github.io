Proposition

Let $\mathrm{BU}$ (cf. BU) be the classifying space. Then

\begin{align*} \pi_0(\widehat{\mathbb{S}}[\mathrm{BU}]) =& 0 \\ \pi_1(\widehat{\mathbb{S}}[\mathrm{BU}]) =& 0 \\ \pi_2(\widehat{\mathbb{S}}[\mathrm{BU}]) =& 0 \\ \pi_3(\widehat{\mathbb{S}}[\mathrm{BU}]) =& 0 \\ \end{align*}
