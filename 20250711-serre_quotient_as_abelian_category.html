explicit description of a serre quotient with homset

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, $\mathcal{B}$ a serre subcategory. Then the serre quotient exists and is given as abelian category with

  1. objects the same as $\mathcal{A}$
  2. the homset given by
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}/\mathcal{B}}(a,b) = \mathrm{colim}_{a'\subseteq a, b' \subseteq b, a/a', b' \in \mathcal{B}} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(a',b') \end{align*}
