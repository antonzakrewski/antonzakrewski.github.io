category of chain complexes of an AB3 opp category as AB3 opp category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an Ab3 opp category Then so is $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$

Proof

  1. category of chain complexes as functor category of enriched functors
  2. colimit of additive functors as additive functor
  3. colimit of functors for a cocomplete codomain
