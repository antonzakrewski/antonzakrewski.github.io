locally convex topology is hausdorff for a separating family

Proposition

Let $X$ be a vector space and $\{p_i\}$ be a separating family of seminorms. Then the locally convex topology is hausdorff.

Proof

Let $x,y$ be different points.

We claim that these are disjoint: w.l.o.g. we may assume that $x = 0$ after using the homeomorphism $- x: X \rightarrow X$ (cf. locally convex topology induces a topological vector space).

so choose a seminorm $p_i$ with $p_i(y) > 0$ and let $d \coloneqq p_i(y)$. Then consider $p_i^{-1}[[0,\frac{d}{2})], p_i^{-1}[(\frac{d}{2},0]]$ which are open disjoint neighborhoods, since $[0,\frac{d}{2}), (\frac{d}{2},0]$ are disjoint and open subsets of $[0,\infty)$
