multiplication by 2 in general not nullhomotopic on X/2

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum Then the map

\begin{align*} \cdot 2: X/2 \rightarrow X/2 \end{align*}

is in general not nullhomotopic

Proof

Consider $X = \mathbb{S}$ and suppose the map is nullhomotopic. Then the cofiber is given by

since $\mathbb{S}/2 \otimes -$ is an exact functor.

But $\mathbb{S}/2\otimes \mathbb{S}/2$ does not split as $\mathbb{S}/2 \oplus \mathbb{S}/2[1]$ as can be seen by $\mathrm{Sq}^2$ (cf. product of M(F2,n) is not wedge)
