lifting inverse and isomorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and suppose that 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
& B \arrow[dashed]{rd}{g}& \\
A \arrow[]{ru}{f} \arrow[]{rr}{\mathrm{id}} & A 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

and

are suitable solutions.

Then $f$ is an isomorphism with inverse $g$

Proof

follows from:

  • monic split epi as isomorphism / epic split mono as isomorphism
  • split mono as monomorphism / split epi as epimorphism
  • left inverse of an isomorphism as right inverse
