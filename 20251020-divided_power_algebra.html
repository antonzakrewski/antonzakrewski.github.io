divided power algebra

Definition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}^+$, then the divided power algebra is defined as graded ring which is additively given by

\begin{align*} \Gamma_{\mathbb{Z}} = \bigoplus_{n \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

with a generator $x \in (\Gamma_{\mathbb{Z}})_n$ with degree $\vert x \vert = n$ and multiplication determined by

\begin{align*} x^k = k! \cdot x^{\lfloor x \rfloor} \end{align*}

where $x^{\lfloor x \rfloor} \in (\Gamma_{\mathbb{Z}})_{k \cdot n}$ is a generator.
