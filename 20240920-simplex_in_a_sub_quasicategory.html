simplex in a sub quasicategory

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathcal{C}' \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a sub quasicategory and $\sigma: \Delta^n \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a simplex

TFAE:

  1. $\sigma \in \mathcal{C}'$
  2. each 1-simplex of the spine $I^n \rightarrow \Delta^n \rightarrow \mathcal{C}'$ lies in $\mathcal{C}_1$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
