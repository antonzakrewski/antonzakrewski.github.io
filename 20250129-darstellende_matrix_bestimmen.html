Definition

Let $\varphi: V \rightarrow W$ be a vectorspace-endomorphism between finite dimensional vector spaces $V,W$. Suppose $v_1,...,v_n$ is an ordered basis of $V$, $w_1,...,w_m$ an ordered basis of $W$.

Let

\begin{align*} \varphi(v_i) = \sum_{j=i}^m \lambda_{i,j} \varphi(v_i) \end{align*}

with $\lambda_{i,j} \in K$ where such an equation exists and is unique, since $w_i$ is a basis.
