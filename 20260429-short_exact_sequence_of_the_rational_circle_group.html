short exact sequence of the rational circle group

Proposition

The sequence

\begin{align*} 0 \rightarrow \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{Q} \rightarrow \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z} \rightarrow 0 \end{align*}

is a short exact sequence

Proof

clear

Remark

  • both an injective resolution of $\mathbb{Z}$ and flat resolution of $\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$
