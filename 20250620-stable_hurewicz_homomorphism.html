stable hurewicz homomorphism

Definition

Let $R$ be a ring spectrum. Then there exists a canonical map

\begin{align*} \pi_*^{\mathrm{st}}(-) \rightarrow H(-,R) \end{align*}

induced via the map of spectra

\begin{align*} \mathbb{S}[-] \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{S} \rightarrow \mathbb{S}[-] \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} R \end{align*}
