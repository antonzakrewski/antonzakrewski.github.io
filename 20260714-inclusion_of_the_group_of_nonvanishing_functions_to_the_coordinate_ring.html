inclusion of the group of nonvanishing functions to the coordinate ring

Proposition

There exists an pointwise monomorphism $\eta: \mathcal{O}^{\times}(X) \rightarrow \mathcal{O}(X)$.

Proof

Let $\mathbb{G}_m \hookrightarrow \mathbb{A}^1$ be the inclusion. This is given by a map $k[x] \rightarrow k[x,x^{-1}]$ which is an epimorphism of commutative rings: Any map $k[x,x^{-1}] \rightarrow R$ is already determined by $x \mapsto R$. now precomposition with an epimorphism is a monomorphism
