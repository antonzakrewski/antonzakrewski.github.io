equivalent characterizations of a valuation ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be an Integral domain with fraction field $K$. TFAE:

  1. $R$ is a local prüfer domain
  2. the divisiblitiy relation on $R$ is a total relation
  3. the partially ordered set of ideals is totally ordered
  4. the poset of divisors $\mathrm{Div}(\mathrm{Spec}(R))$ is totally ordered
  5. for every $x \in K$, $x$ or $x^{-1}$ belongs to $R$
  6. there exists a valuation $\eta:K \rightarrow \Gamma \cup \{\infty\}$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 3)

3) $\implies$ 4)

4) $\implies$ 5)

5) $\implies$ 6)

6) $\implies$ 1)
