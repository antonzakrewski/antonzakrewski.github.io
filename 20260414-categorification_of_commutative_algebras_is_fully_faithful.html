categorification of commutative Algebras is fully faithful

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C} \in \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\kappa})$ be kappa presentable, Then the categorification functor is

\begin{align*} \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Mod}_{C}(\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\kappa})) \\ \end{align*}

is fully faithful
