dense subcategory of a grothendieck topology

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \tau)$ be a site. Thne a dense subcategory of $(\mathcal{C}, \tau)$ is a full subcategory $\mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ such that every $X \in \mathcal{C}$ admits a $\tau$ cover $(U_i \rightarrow X)$ with $U_i \in \mathcal{D}$.
