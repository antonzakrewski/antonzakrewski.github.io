a-irreduzibler Unterraum und indecomposable module

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $M$ an \(R\)-module, $U \subseteq M$ a submodule and $A \in \mathrm{End}_{R}(M)$ a module-endomorphism. TFAE:

  1. $U$ is A-irreducible
  2. $U$ as $R[X]$ module is indecomposable module

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
