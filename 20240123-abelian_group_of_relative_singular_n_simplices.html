abelian group of relative singular n-simplices

Definition

Let $(X,A)$ be a pair of spaces. Then the abelian group of relative singular n-simplices is defined as quotient group of the abelian group of relative singular n-simplices

\begin{align*} C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X,A) = C^{\mathrm{sing}}_n(X)/C^{\mathrm{sing}}(A) \end{align*}
