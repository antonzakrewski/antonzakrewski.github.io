grothendieck group and cofinal submonoid

Proposition

Let $M$ be a commutative monoid, $N$ a cofinal submonoid. Then each element in the grothendieck group $M^#$ is an equivalence class of the form

\begin{align*} [(m,n)] \end{align*}

where $n \in N$

Proof

Let $[m_1,m_2] \in M^#$ be an arbitrary element. Then by assumption there exists an $m' \in M$ such that $m_2 + m' \eqqcolon n \in N$.

Then it follows that

\begin{align*} [m_1,m_2] =& [m_1 + m', m_2 + m'] \\ =& [m_1 + m', n] \end{align*}

where $m_1 + m' \in M$
