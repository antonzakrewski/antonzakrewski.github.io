presentation of the cotangent module

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $S$ an R-Algebra with a presentation $S = R[x_i \vert i \in I](f_j \vert j \in J)$. Then the \(S\)-module $\Omega_{S/R}$ is generated by $\mathrm{d}x_i$ for all $i \in I$ iwth the relations given by $\sum_{i \}n I} \frac{\partial f_i}{\partial x_i} \mathrm{d}x_i$

elementary proof

Let $M$ be an module. Then we have to show that a section $S \hookrightarrow S \oplus M$ is represented by a \(R\)-linear map $\Omega_{S/R} \rightarrow M$. Now any derivation satisfies for $r \in R$ and generators $x_1,x_2$:

\begin{align*} \partial(r \cdot x_1 \cdot x_2) = r x_1 d(x_2) + r x_2 d(x_1) \end{align*}

which inductively (by handwaving) shwos $d(f) = \sum \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i} \mathrm{d} x_i$.

In particular, if $f = 0$ in $S$, then this explains the relation.

On the other hand, a derivation is -- if it exists -- uniquely determined -- by the leibniz rule - by mapping generators.
