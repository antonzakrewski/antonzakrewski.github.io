devissage for closed immersions

Proposition

Let $C$ be a collection of closed immersions in $\mathrm{Sm}_{S}$. Suppose that

  1. $C$ contains the zero sections of affine schemes $X \hookrightarrow \mathbb{A}_X^n$
  2. $C$ is invariant under etale neighbourhoods, that is if $V \rightarrow X$ is an etale neighbourhood of $Z \hookrightarrow X$, then the following are equivalent: $Z \hookrightarrow X$ and $Z \hookrightarrow X$ in $C$
  3. if $U_i \hookrightarrow X$ is an open covering. If for all $n \geq 1$, $U_{i_1} \cap U_{i_2} ... \cap U_{i_n} \cap Z \hookrightarrow U_{i_1} \cap ... \cap U_{i_n}$ is in $C$, then $Z \hookrightarrow C$ lies in $C$

Then $C$ contains all closed immersions.
