Ind for category with small colimits is closed under limits in the presheaf category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category with finite colimits and $\kappa$ a regular cardinal Then $\mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ preserves limits

Proof

use that $\mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C})$ can be identified with the $\kappa$ small limit preserving functors. then limits commute with limits
