connectivity of BO(n) to BO

Proposition

The map $\mathrm{BO}(n) \rightarrow \mathrm{BO}$ is $n-1$ connected map.

Proof

follows from the fiber sequence fiber sequence of O(n)

\begin{align*} S^n \rightarrow \mathrm{BO}(n) \rightarrow \mathrm{BO}(n+1) \end{align*}

so $\mathrm{BO}(n) \rightarrow \mathrm{BO}(n+1)$ is \(n-1\)-connected.

In particular each morphism in $\mathrm{BO}(n) \rightarrow \mathrm{BO}(n+1) \rightarrow \mathrm{BO}(n+2) \rightarrow ...$ is at least $n-1$ connected, so apply filtered colimit of n-connected morphisms.
