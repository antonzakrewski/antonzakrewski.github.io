intersection of positive and negative elements

Proposition

The set of positive elements $A^{+}$ is a closed convex cone and $A^+ \cap (- A^+) = \{0\}$.

Proof

Let $a \geq 0$ and $a \leq 0$, then $\sigma_{A}(a) \in [0,\infty)$ and $\sigma_{A}(a) = - \sigma_{-a) \subseteq (- \infty, 0)$. Hence $\sigma_{A}(a) = 0$ which implies $\| a \| = 0$ or $a = 0$.
