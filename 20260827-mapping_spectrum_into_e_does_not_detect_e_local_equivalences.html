mapping spectrum into E does not detect E-local equivalences

Proposition

Let $E$ be a spectrum. Then there may exist a map $f: X \rightarrow Y$ which is not an E-equivalence but such that $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(f,E)$ is an equivalence

Proof

stable miller's theorem implies $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{F}_p, \mathbb{S}) \simeq 0$, so $\mathbb{F}_p \rightarrow 0$ induces an equivalence after applying $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(-,\mathbb{S})$. But this is of course not an equivalence
