spectrum with 2-torsion homotopy groups but multiplication by 2 is not nullhomotopic

Proposition

There exists a spectrum $X$ such that $\pi_*(X)$ is degreewise 2-torsion but the multiplication by 2 map is not nullhomotopic.

Proof

Let $X = \tau_{\leq 1}(\mathbb{S}/2)$. using some low homotopy groups of S/n shows that $\pi_*(X)$ is degreewise 2-torsion.

So it suffices to show that multiplication with $2$ is not nullhomotopic for $X$.
