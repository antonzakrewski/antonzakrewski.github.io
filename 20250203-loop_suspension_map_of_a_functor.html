loop suspension map of a functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be pointed infinity categories where $\mathcal{C}$ is finitely cocomplete, $\mathcal{D}$ is finitely complete. Then for a functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ the loop suspension map is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(-) \rightarrow \Omega_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(\Sigma -)) \end{align*}
