T1 topological group as hausdorff group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a topological group, which is T1. Then $G$ is hausdorff

Proof

consider

\begin{align*} p: G \times G \rightarrow G (x,y) \rightarrow (x \cdot y^{-1}) \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} p^{-1}[ \{1\}] = \mathrm{im}(\Delta) \end{align*}

is closed as $p$ is continuous Then Abgeschlossenheit der Diagonale in Hausdorff-Räumen shows the statement.
