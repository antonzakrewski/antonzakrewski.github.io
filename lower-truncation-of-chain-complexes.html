lower truncation of chain complexes

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch}_{\geq 0}(\mathcal{A})$ the category of chain complexes. Then the truncation is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \tau_{\geq 0}: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ch_*}_{\geq 0}(\mathcal{A}) \\ (... \rightarrow M_1 \rightarrow M_0 \rightarrow M_{-1} \rightarrow ...) \mapsto& (... \rightarrow M_1 \rightarrow \mathrm{ker}(M_{0} \rightarrow M_{-1}) \rightarrow 0 \rightarrow ...) \\ f_* \mapsto& (...,f_1,g,0,...) \end{align*}
