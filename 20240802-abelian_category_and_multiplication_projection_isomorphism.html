additive category and multiplication projection isomorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a semiadditive category

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{A}$ is additive category
  2. for each object $A \in \mathcal{A}$ the morphism

is an isomorphism

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $-\mathrm{id}$ be the inverse of $\mathrm{id}$. Then the map

suffices inverse

2) $\implies$ 1)

analogously: use the universal property of maps

\begin{align*} A \oplus A \rightarrow A \oplus A \end{align*}

which are given by $2 \times 2$ matrices with matrix multiplication

Then the bottom left morphism suffices as inverse
