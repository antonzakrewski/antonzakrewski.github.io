category of spectra not generated by limits and the sphere spectrum

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C} \subseteq \mathrm{Sp}$ be the full stable subcategory, which is closed under limits and contains the sphere spectrum. Then $\mathcal{C} \subsetneq \mathrm{Sp}$ is not equivalent.

Proof

stable miller's theorem shows that $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{F}_p, \mathbb{S}) \simeq 0$ is the zero object. Now the collection of spectra $X$, such that $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{F}_p,X) \simeq 0$ is the zero object, is closed under limits since $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{F}_p,-)$ preserves limits and limits of the terminal objects is the terminal object. Hence any spectrum $X \in \mathcal{C}$ satisfies $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{F}_p,x) \simeq 0$, so it suffices to see that $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{F}_p,\mathbb{F}_p) \simeq \mathbb{F}_p \neq 0$, i.e. e.g. $\mathbb{F}_p$ does not lie in $\mathcal{C}$.
