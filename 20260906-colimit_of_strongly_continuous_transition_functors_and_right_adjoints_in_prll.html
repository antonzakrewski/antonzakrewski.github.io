colimit of strongly continuous transition functors and right adjoints in PrLL

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_i$ be a filtered diagram of presentable categories and $\mathcal{C}$ be the colimit in $\mathrm{Pr^L}$. Let $\mathcal{F}_{i,j}: \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_j$ be strongly continuous transition functors and $\mathcal{F}_i: \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ the functor to the colimit. Let $\mathcal{G}_{i,j}, \mathcal{G}_i$ be the corresponding right adjoints.

Then there exists an natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{k \geq i,j} \mathcal{G}_{j,k} \mathcal{F}_{i,k} \simeq \mathcal{G}_{j} \mathcal{F}_i \end{align*}

where the LHS is an endomorphism in the functor category.

Proof

Observe that $\mathcal{C}$ is also the colimit in category PrLL, so in particular $\mathcal{F}_i: \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is strongly continuous or $\mathcal{G}_{i}$ is a left adjoint.

Using that $\mathcal{G}_{i,j}$ preserves colimits, we have that a well-defined functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_i': \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \lim_j \mathcal{C}_j \simeq \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

given by $\mathcal{F}_i(-) \simeq ( \mathrm{colim}_{k \geq i,j} \mathcal{G}_{j,k} \mathcal{F}_{i,k}(-))_j$, which is a natural transformation into the diagram,as

\begin{align*} \mathcal{G}_{j',j}(\mathrm{colim}_{k \geq i,j} \mathcal{G}_{j,k} \mathcal{F}_{i,k}(-)) \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{k \geq i,j} \mathcal{G}_{j',j} \mathcal{G}_{j,k} \mathcal{F}_{i,k}(-) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{k \geq i,j'} \mathcal{G}_{j',j} \mathcal{G}_{j,k} \mathcal{F}_{i,k}(-) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{k \geq i,j'} mathcal{G}_{j',k} \mathcal{F}_{i,k}(-) \\ \end{align*}

where we have used that $\mathcal{G}_{j',j}$ preserves colimits, $\mathrm{colim}_{k \geq i,j'}$ is a coinitial approximation of $\mathrm{colim}_{k \geq i,j}$ and $\mathcal{G}_{j',j} \mathcal{G}_{j,k} \simeq \mathcal{G}_{j',k}$ are homotopic

Then we want to show that $\mathcal{F}_i \simeq \mathcal{F}_i'$ since then we would have

\begin{align*} \mathcal{G}_j \mathcal{F}_i \simeq& \mathcal{G}_j \mathcal{F}_i' \\ \simeq& \mathcal{G}_j ( \mathrm{colim}_{k \geq i,j} \mathcal{G}_{j,k} \mathcal{F}_{i,k}(-))_j \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{k \geq i,j} \mathcal{G}_{j,k} \mathcal{F}_{i,k}(-) \end{align*}

where $\mathcal{G}_j$ is just the projection to the $j$-th component under the equivalence.

so by uniqueness of adjoints, it suffices to show $\mathcal{F}_i' \dash \mathcal{G}_i$. So let $x \in \mathcal{C}_i, y = (y_i) \in \lim_I \mathcal{C}_i \simeq \mathcal{C}$. Then we have equivalences

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{F}_i(x)', y) \simeq& \lim_{j} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_j}(\mathrm{colim}_{k \geq i,j} \mathcal{G}_{j,k} \mathcal{F}_{i,k}(x), y_i) \\ \simeq& \lim_{j} \lim_{k \geq i,j} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_i}(\mathcal{G}_{j,k}^R \mathcal{F}_{i,k}(x), y_j) \\ \simeq& \lim_{j = k \geq i} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_j}(\mathcal{F}_{i,j}(x), y_j) \\ \simeq& \lim_{j \geq i} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_i}(x, \mathcal{G}_{i,j}^R(y_j)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_i}(x,y_i) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_i}(x, \mathcal{G}_{i}(y)) \end{align*}

where the first step computes the Hom anima in the limit category and writes $\mathcal{F}_i'(x)$ out, the second step pulls the colimit out of the hom, the third step initially approximates the double limit (???), the fourth step sets $j = k$ and the fifth step initially approximates by setting $j = i$. The last step finally uses, that the projections are precisely the right adjoints.
