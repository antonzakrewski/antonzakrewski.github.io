S-horn inclusion as inner anodyne

Proposition

Let $S \subseteq [n]$ be a a non empty subset, such that $[n] \setminus S$ is not an interval, i.e. there exists $a < b < c$ with $a,c \not\in S$ and $b \in S$

Then the S-horn inclusion

\begin{align*} \Lambda_S^n \hookrightarrow \Delta^n \end{align*}

is inner anodyne map

Proof
