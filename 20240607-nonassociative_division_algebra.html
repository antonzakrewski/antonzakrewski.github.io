nonassociative division algebra

Definition

Let $K$ be a field and $V$ a vector space. Then $K$ is said to be a nonassociative division algebra, if it is a non associative algebra over $K$ and

\begin{align*} \mu: V \times V \rightarrow V \end{align*}

is a bilinear map such that for each $v,w \in V, v \neq 0$ there exists an element $v^{-1}_{L,w}, v^{-1}_{R,w}$ such that

\begin{align*} \mu(v,v^{-1}_{R,w}) =& w \\ \mu(v^{-1}_{L,w},v) =& w \end{align*}
