tangent space of a point of an affine algebraic set

Definition

Let $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_k^m$ be an affine algebraic with vanishing ideal $I(X)$ and $P \in X$ a point. Then the tangent space at $P$ is defined as algebraic set

\begin{align*} T_p = Z( (d F_1)_P,...,(dF_r)_P) \subseteq \mathbb{A}_k^m \end{align*}

where $d F_i$ is given by the formal derivation of a multivariate polynomial at a point
