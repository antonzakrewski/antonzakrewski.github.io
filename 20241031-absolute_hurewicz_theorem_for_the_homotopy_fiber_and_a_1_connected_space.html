relative hurewicz theorem for the homotopy fiber and a 1 connected space

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be an \(n-1\)-connected map, $n \geq 2$ and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{hofib}(X \rightarrow Y) C(f) = \mathrm{Cone}(f: X \rightarrow Y) \end{align*}

the homotopy fibre resp. homotopy cofiber

If $X$ is \(1\)-connected space, then this induces an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \pi_{n-1}(F) \rightarrow H_n(C(f)) \end{align*}

and surjection

\begin{align*} \pi_n(F) \twoheadrightarrow H_{n+1}(C(f)) \end{align*}

Proof
