S-complete module spectrum

Definition

Let $R$ be an (\mathbb{E}_1)-ring spectrum and $S \subseteq \pi_*(R)$ be a homogeneous subset satisfying the left ore condition. Then a left module spectrum $M$ is said to be \(S\)-complete, if $\mathrm{Ext}_R^i(N,M) = 0$ for every S-local module spectrum $N$ and $i \in \mathbb{N}_0$.
