colimit of a product diagram

Proposition

Let $I,I'$ be simplicial sets and $I \times I' \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a diagram for an infinity category $\mathcal{C}$. Then the colimit of $I \times I' \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ - if it exists - is given under currying by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{I} \mathrm{colim}_{I'} \end{align*}

or equivalently

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{I'} \mathrm{colim}_{I} \end{align*}

Proof

$\mathcal{C}$ (sufficiently) cocomplete

follows as the colimit is defined as infinity left adjoint to the constant diagram functor But under currying

But adjoints compose and adjoints are unique up to natural equivalence, hence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_I \mathrm{colim}_{I'} \simeq \mathrm{colim}_{I \times I'} \end{align*}
