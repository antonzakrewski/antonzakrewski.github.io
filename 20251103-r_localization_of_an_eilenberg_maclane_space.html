R-localization of an Eilenberg-MacLane space

Proposition

Let $R \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ be a subgroup and $A$ an abelian group.

Then the unit of the R-localization is given by

\begin{align*} \varphi: K(A,n) \rightarrow K(A \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} R,n) \end{align*}

note that the eilenberg maclane space for an R-module is R-local

So it suffices to show that the map is an R-local equivalence
