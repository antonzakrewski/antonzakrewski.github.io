generalized cuspodial curve

Definition / Proposition

Let $F$ be an algebraically closed field. Then for coprime $n,m$ the generalized cuspodial curve is defined as algebraic set

\begin{align*} C \coloneqq \{(t^m, t^n \vert t \in k\} \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^2 \end{align*}

Proof

Consider

\begin{align*} (X^n - Y^m) \subseteq K[X,Y] \end{align*}

Then it is clear, that $C \subseteq Z(X^n - Y^m)$

Assume that $(a,b) \in Z(X^n - Y^m)$. Then it follows that $a^n = b^m$.

Choose an \(m\)-th root of $a$ $\sqrt[m]{a}$

Then $\sqrt[m]{a}^n$ and $b$ differ only up to a root of unity $\zeta$ Then as $n,m$ are coprime, we may choose an \(n\)-th root of $\sqrt[n]{\zeta}$

Let $t \coloneqq \zeta \cdot \sqrt[m]{a}$
