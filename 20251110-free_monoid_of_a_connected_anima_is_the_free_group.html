free monoid of a connected anima is the free group

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{An}_{*, \geq 1}$ be a connected anima. Then the free cartesian monoid functor restricts to the full subcategory of category of groups.

In particular

\begin{align*} \mathrm{FreeMonoid}(X) \simeq \mathrm{FreeGrp}(X) \end{align*}

Proof

We claim that this diagram

commutes, where the right vertical map is given since $\pi_0$ preserves products (cf. postcomposition with product preserving left adjoint induces adjunction on category of cartesian monoids)

This diagram commutes by passing to right adjoints (cf. commuting diagram of adjoints and dualizing adjoint)

Now we may recognize groups by their $\pi_0$ (cf. anima valued group and pi_0 is a group). But the free group of $(*,*)$ as pointed set is given by the free group of the empty set, so

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Free}_*(*) =& \mathrm{Free}(\emptyset) \\ =& 0 \end{align*}

Here the monoid generated by the empty set happens to be a group, so we are done :) (cf. free group of a pointed set)
