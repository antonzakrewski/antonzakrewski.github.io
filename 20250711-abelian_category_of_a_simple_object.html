abelian category of a simple object as module category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, $S \in \mathcal{A}$ a simple object. Let $\mathcal{A}_{S}$ be the full subcategory of $\mathcal{A}$ containing sums of $S$.

Assume that $\mathcal{A}_S$ is Ab4.

Then there exists an equivalence of categories

\begin{align*} \mathcal{A}_{S} \simeq \mathrm{Mod}(\mathrm{End}_{\mathcal{A}}(S)) \end{align*}
