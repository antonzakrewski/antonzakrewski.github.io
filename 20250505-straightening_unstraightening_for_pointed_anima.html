straightening unstraightening for pointed Anima out of an Anima

Proposition

Let $B \in B$ an anima. Then Anima coslice as Functors into Anima gives an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(B,\mathrm{An}_*) \simeq& (\mathrm{An}_{/B})_{B/} \\ \end{align*}

where the latter is the infinity category of pointed objects of $\mathrm{An}_{/B}$

Proof

consider ordinary straightening-unstraightening. Then in particular $\mathrm{An}_{*} = * \times_{\mathrm{An}} \mathrm{Ar}(\mathrm{An})$.

Then under straightening unstraightening and currying in cat infinity this gives

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(B, * \times_{\mathrm{An}} \mathrm{Ar}(\mathrm{An})) \simeq& * \times_{\mathrm{Fun}(B, \mathrm{An})} \mathrm{Fun}(B \times \Delta^1, \mathrm{An}) \\ \simeq& * \times_{\mathrm{Fun}(B, \mathrm{An})} \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathrm{Fun}(B,\mathrm{An})) \\ \simeq& * \times_{\mathrm{An}_{/B}} \mathrm{Ar}(\mathrm{An}_{/B}) \simeq& (\mathrm{An}_{/B})_{B/} \end{align*}
