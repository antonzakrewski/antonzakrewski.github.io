n-th cohomology of the Eilenberg-MacLane space with coefficients

Proposition

Let $A$ be an abelian group, $n \geq 1$. Then the map

\begin{align*} H^n(K(A,n),A) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}(A,A) \end{align*}

induced by the universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology is a group-isomorphism

Proof

Note that by universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology

\begin{align*} H^n(K(A,n);A) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(H_n(K(A,n), \mathbb{Z}) \otimes \mathrm{Ext}(H_{n-1}(K(A,n); \mathbb{Z}), A) \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} H_{n-1}(K(A,n), \mathbb{Z}) = 0 \end{align*}

since $K(A,n)$ is \(n-1\)-connected space (c.f homology of an n-connected space)
