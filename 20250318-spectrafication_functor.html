free spectrum functor from groups

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category. Then the spectrafication functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Grp}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is defined as left adjoint to the forgetful functor from spectra to cartesian commutative groups
