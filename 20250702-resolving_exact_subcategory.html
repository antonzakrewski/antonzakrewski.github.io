resolving exact subcategory

Definition

Let $\iota: \mathcal{E} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{A}$ be an exact infinity subcategory. Then $\iota$ is said to be resolving if

  1. for all exact squares $x \hookrightarrow y \twoheadrightarrow z$ with $y \in \mathcal{A}$ we get that $x \in \mathcal{A}$
  2. for all $z \in \mathcal{A}$ there exists a projection $y \twoheadrightarrow y$ with $y \in \mathcal{A}$
