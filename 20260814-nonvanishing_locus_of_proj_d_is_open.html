nonvanishing locus of Proj_d is open

Proposition

Let $A$ be a N-graded ring, $f \in A_d$. Then $D(f) \subseteq \mathrm{Proj}_d(A)$ is an open subfunctor

Proof

Let $\mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{Proj}_d(A)$ be an R-point. Then the pullback consists precisely of the epimorphism locus $A_1 \otimes_{A_0} R \rightarrow L$, which is an open subfunctor, as $L$ is an vector bundle (even a line, epimorphism locus is an open subfunctor).
