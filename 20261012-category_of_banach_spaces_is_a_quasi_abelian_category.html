category of banach spaces is a quasi-abelian category

Proposition

The category of banach spaces is a quasi-abelian category where all epimorphisms are strict and a monomorphism is strict if and only if it is a closed subspace inclusion.

Proof

a)

it suffices to observe that $\mathrm{Ban} \subseteq \mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb{C}}$ is closed under pushouts / pullbacks along strict monomorphisms / strict epimorphisms.

  • quotient of a banach space by a closed subspace
  • category of banach spaces is closed under limits in the category of (semi-)normed spaces
