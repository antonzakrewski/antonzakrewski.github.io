dual of a module is locally of finite presentation for a finitely presented module

Proposition

Let $k$ be a commutative ring and $M$ a \(k\)-module. TFAE:

  1. $M$ is a finitely presented module
  2. $\mathbb{A}(M)$ is locally of finite presentation

Proof

recall that $\mathbb{A}(M)$ is equivalent to $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathrm{Sym}(M))$ for the free E_2 Algebra. So we have to consider the case of affine schemes: idempotent classifier scheme is locally of finite presentation So it suffices to observe that the free algebra functor from Mod_k to CAlg_k preserves compact objects. Hence $\mathrm{Sym}(M)$ is finitely presented (as k-algebra) if $M$ is (as k-module).
