stabilization of abelian categories induces t-exact functors

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ be an exact functor.

Then the induced functor on stabilization

\begin{align*} \mathrm{St}(\mathcal{F}): \mathrm{St}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow \mathrm{St}(\mathcal{B}) \end{align*}

is a t-exact functor

Proof

Note that $\mathrm{St}(\mathcal{A})$ is by construction (cf. stabilization functor from Exact) the smallest stable subcategory generated by $\mathcal{A}$.
