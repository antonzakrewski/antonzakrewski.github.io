single-epi grothendieck topology

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a small abelian category (or regular category ?). Then the single epi grothendieck topology is defined such that a sieve on $X$ is a covering sieve if and only if it contains an epimorphism $Y \twoheadrightarrow X$

welldefined

identity

follows as $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(-,A)$ contains the $\mathrm{id}$ in $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(A,A)$.

base change

Let $\mathcal{S}$ be a covering sieve on $X$ and $f: Y \rightarrow X$ a morphism. Then there exists an epimorphism $Z \twoheadrightarrow X$ and it follows that epimorphisms are closed under pullbacks (cf. epimorphism closed under pullback in an abelian category)

hence the map $Z \times_{X} Y \rightarrow Y$ is epic

local character

Suppose $S$ is a covering sieve on $X$ and $T$ is any sieve on $X$. Suppose that for each morphism $f: Y \rightarrow X$ in $S$ that $f^*T$ is a covering sieve. Then we have to show that $T$ is also a covering sieve. For that we choose an epimorphism $f: Y \twoheadrightarrow X$ in $S$ and then there exists an epi $Z \twoheadrightarrow Y \in f^*(T)$, i.e. a pullback

where the dashed map lies in $T$. Hence $Z' \rightarrow X$ must be an epimorphism (epimorphism closed under pullback in an abelian category).
