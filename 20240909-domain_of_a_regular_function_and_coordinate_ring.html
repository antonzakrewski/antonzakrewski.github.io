domain of a regular function between affine varieties and coordinate ring

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be an affine variety. Assume that $f \in K(x)$ is a regular function

TFAE:

  1. $f \in \mathcal{A}(X)$
  2. $\mathrm{def}(f) = X$ (cf. domain of a regular function between affine varieties)

Proof

Note that $\mathrm{def}(f) = X \setminus Z(D_f)$. By weakest nullstellensatz we may conclude that the following are equivalent

  1. $Z(D_f) = \emptyset$
  2. $\sqrt{D_f) = I(Z(D_f)) = I(\emptyset) = A(X)$
  3. $1 \in D_f$
  4. $f \in A(X)$

where $D_f = \{h \in A(X) \vert h \cdot f \in A(X)\}$ is the ideal quotient
