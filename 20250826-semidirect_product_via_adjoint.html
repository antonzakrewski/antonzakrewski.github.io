semidirect product via adjoint

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Ar}(\mathrm{Grp})$ be the arrow category and $\mathrm{Equiv}(\mathrm{Grp})$ be the category of equivariant groups. Then the forgetful functor category from Arrows of group to the category of equivariant groups admits a left adjoint, which is the semidirect product functor.

Proof

follows from

  • pointwise construction of left adjoints

So let $(H,N), N \rightarrow \mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{Grp}}(N)$ be an element in $\mathrm{EqGrp}$ and $G_1 \rightarrow G_2$ a group homomorphism in $\mathrm{Ar}(\mathrm{Grp})$. Then group homomorphisms in $\mathrm{EqGrp}$ from $(H,N)$ to $(G_1,G_2)$ are precisely given by group homomorphisms

\begin{align*} \psi_{\vert H}: H \rightarrow& G_1 \\ \psi_{\vert N}: N \rightarrow& G_2 \\ \end{align*}

such that $\psi_N$ is equivariant, i.e.

\begin{align*} \psi_{\vert N}(\varphi_h(n)) = \psi_{\vert H}(h) \cdot \psi_{\vert N}(n) \cdot \psi_{\vert H}(h^{-1}) \end{align*}

which is precisely the condition in the universal property of the semidirect product
