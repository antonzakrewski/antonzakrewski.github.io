regularity over a non-perfect field does not imply smoothness

Proposition

Let $X$ be a regular scheme over a non-perfect field $k$. Then $X$ is in general not a smooth scheme over $k$.

Proof

Let $k = \mathbb{F}_p[u]$ and $X = \mathrm{Spec}(k[x]/(x^p - u))$. Then $k[x]/(x^p - u)$ is a field and therefore a regular ring.
