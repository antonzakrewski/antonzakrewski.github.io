explicit description of the mapping spectrum of rational spectra

Proposition

Let $X \simeq \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \pi_n(X), Y \simeq \bigoplus_{m \in \mathbb{Z}} \pi_m(Y)$ be rational spectra (cf. rational spectrum splits). Then

\begin{align*} \pi_l(\mathrm{map}(X,Y)) \simeq \bigoplus_{l = n - m} \mathrm{map}(\pi_n(X), \pi_m(Y)) \end{align*}
  • warning: $n - m$ might be $m - n$ ?

Proof

a) $X,Y$ shifted eilenberg maclane spaces

Assume that $X \simeq \pi_n(X)[n] \simeq \bigoplus_i \mathbb{S}_{\mathbb{Q}}[n], Y \simeq \pi_m(Y)[m]$. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}(X,Y) \simeq& \mathrm{map}(\bigoplus_{i} \mathbb{S}_{\mathbb{Q}}[n], Y) \\ \simeq& \prod_i \mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}_{\mathbb{Q}}[n],Y) \\ \simeq& \prod_i \mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S},\pi_m(Y)[m-n]) \\ \simeq& \begin{cases} \prod_{i} \pi_n(Y) \cong \mathrm{map}(\pi_m(X), \pi_n(Y)) & \mbox{if } n = m \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
