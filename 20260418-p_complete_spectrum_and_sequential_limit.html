p-complete spectrum and vanishing sequential limit of multiplication

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum. TFAE:

  1. $X$ is p-complete spectrum
  2. $\lim (... \rightarrow X \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X) \simeq 0$
