image of the fiber for the homological serre spectral sequence

Proposition

Let $F \xrightarrow{\iota} E \xrightarrow{\pi} B$ be a fiber sequence with $F$ connected and $M$ any \(R\)-module Then under the homological serre spectral sequence the image of the map

\begin{align*} H_p(F,M) \rightarrow H_p(E,M) \end{align*}

identifies with $E_{0,p}^{\infty}(\iota)$

Proof

follows from the map of fiber sequences

and naturality of the homological serre spectral sequence functor
