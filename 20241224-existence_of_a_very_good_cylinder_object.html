existence of a very good cylinder object

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}, \mathrm{Cof}, \mathrm{Fib})$ be a model category Then for each $A \in \mathcal{C}$, there exists a very good cylinder object

Proof

follows from the factorization of the codiagonal morphism

\begin{align*} \nabla: A \coprod A \rightarrow A \end{align*}

as cofibration followed by a trivial fibration
