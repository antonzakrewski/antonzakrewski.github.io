stable miller's theorem (sullivans conjecture)

Proposition

Let $G$ be a finite group and $X$ a finite spectrum. Then $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(G, X)$ is the zero object

Proof

reduction to $\mathbb{F}_p$

observe that any finite abelian group is given by $G = \bigoplus_{i=1}^n \mathbb{Z}/p_{i}^{n_i} \mathbb{Z}$ Then $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(-,X)$ is exact and $G$ is given by a filtration with associated graded $\mathbb{Z}/ p_i \mathbb{Z}$.

proof strategies

a) morava k-theory

  • $\mathbb{F}_p$ is $\bigoplus K(n)$ acyclic, while $X$ is $\bigoplus K(n)$-local.

b) steenrod algebra

  • use adams spectral sequence
  • steenrod algebra is injective over itself
  • some trivial spectral sequence
