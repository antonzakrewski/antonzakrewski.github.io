supremum and subset

Proposition

Let $X$ be a partially ordered set, $A \subseteq B \subseteq X$ subsets such that the suprema $\mathrm{sup}(A), \mathrm{sup}(B)$ exist. Then $\mathrm{sup}(A) \leq \mathrm{sup}(B)$

Proof

Note that for each $b \in B$ it follows that $b \leq \mathrm{sup}(B)$. Hence for each $a \in A$ it follows that $a \leq \mathrm{sup}(B)$ as $A \subseteq B$.

But then by minimality of the supremum of $A$ it follows that $\mathrm{sup}(A) \leq \mathrm{sup}(B)$
