homology of the truncation of a sphere

Proposition

Let $n \geq m \geq 3$ (or $2$) and $X$ an \(m-1\)-connected anima, then there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} H_k(\tau_{\leq n-1}(X); \mathbb{Z}) \cong \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } k = 0,m \\ 0 & \mbox{if } 1 \leq k < m \textrm{ and} m < k \leq n \\ \pi_n(X) & \mbox{if } k = n+1 \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

the map $X \rightarrow \tau_{\leq n-1} X$ is n+1-connected, hence induces induces an isomorphism in degrees $\leq n$ (cf. n-connected map and homology)

So it remains to show that $H_k(\tau_{\leq n-1} X) \cong \pi_n(X)$. For that consider the fiber sequence

\begin{align*} K(\pi_n(X),n) \rightarrow \tau_{\leq n}X \rightarrow \tau_{\leq n-1}(X) \end{align*}

and the associated homological serre spectral sequence

But there the differential

\begin{align*} H_{n+1}(\tau_{\leq n -1} X,\mathbb{Z}) \rightarrow H_n(K(\pi_n(X),n),\mathbb{Z}) \cong \pi_n(X) \end{align*}

has to be an isomorphism, since both entries can't contribute to their respictive homology $H_k(\tau_{\leq n-1}(X),\mathbb{Z})$ since they are $0$ by the previous argument
