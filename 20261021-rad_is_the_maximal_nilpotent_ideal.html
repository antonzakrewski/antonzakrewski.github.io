Rad is the maximal nilpotent ideal

Proposition

Proof

Let $A$ act on $A$ be the left regular representation. Then choose a filtration, $V_0 \subseteq ... \subseteq V_n = A$. Now pick $a \in \mathrm{Rad}(a)$. then $a$ acts trivially on $V_n/V_{n-1}$, so $a V_n \subseteq V_{n-1}$. but then inductively $a^i A \subseteq V_{n-i}$. So $a^n A \subseteq V_0 = 0$. So $a^n 1 = 0$, which implies $a^n = 0$.
