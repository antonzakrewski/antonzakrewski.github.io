vanishing locus and ideal

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor and $F \subseteq \mathcal{O}(X)$ a subset of the commutative ring $\mathcal{O}(X)$. Then the vanishing locus only depends on the generated ideal $(F)$

Proof

follows from the definition

\begin{align*} V(F)(R) = \{x \in X(R) \vert f(x) = 0 \textrm{ for all } f \in F\} \end{align*}

where $(f+f_1 \cdot f_2)(x) = f(x) + f_1(x) \cdot f_2(x) = 0 + f_1(x) \cdot 0 = 0$.
