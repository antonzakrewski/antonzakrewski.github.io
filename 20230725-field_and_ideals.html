field and ideals

Proposition

Let $R \neq \{0\}$ be a commutative ring. TFAE:

  1. $R$ is a field
  2. the only ideals are $(0)$ and $R$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $I \subseteq R$ be an ideal with $I \neq \{0\}$. Then there exists an $r \in I \cap R \setminus \{0\}$ and $r \in R^{\times}$. Therefore $R = (r) \subseteq I \Rightarrow R = I$ (see: von einer Einheit erzeugtes Ideal und triviales Ideal)

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $r \in R \setminus \{0\}$, then $(r) \neq (0)$ and therefore $(r) = R$ with $1 \in (r)$ Therefore, there exists an $r^{-1} \in R$ such that

\begin{align*} r \cdot r^{-1} = 1 \end{align*}
