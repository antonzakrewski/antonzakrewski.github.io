high dimensional vector bundle over a cw complex admits globally nontrivial section

Proposition

Let $X$ be a CW-Complex with $\mathrm{dim}(X) = n$. Suppose $E \rightarrow X$ is a topological vector bundle with $\mathrm{dim}(E) > n$.

Then there exists a section $s: X \rightarrow E$ with $s(x) \neq 0$ for all $x \in X$

Proof
