mapping spectrum formula in the continuous calkin category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a dualizable category and let $x,y \in \mathcal{C}$ be objects. Then under the description of $\mathrm{Calk}^{\mathrm{cont}}(\mathcal{C})$ as the idempotent completion of the essential image of the functor $\mathrm{Cone}(\widehat{よ} \Rightarrow よ): \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C})$ (cf. explicit description of the continuous calkin category of dualizable category), the mapping spectrum for $x,y \in \mathcal{C}$ is identified

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Calk}^{\mathrm{cont}}}(x,y) \simeq& \mathrm{cofib}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C})}(よ(x), \widehat{よ}(y)) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{cofib}(\mathrm{ev}_{\mathcal{C}}(y \boxtimes x^{\vee}) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y)) \end{align*}

Proof

Let $\mathcal{L}: \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\omega_1}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\omega_1})/ \widehat{よ}(\mathcal{C}) \simeq \mathrm{Ind}(\mathrm{Calk}(\mathcal{C})^{\mathrm{cont}})$ be the projection to the cofiber in category PrL stable and $\mathcal{R}$ its right adjoint. Then there exists an equivalence $\mathcal{RL} \simeq \mathrm{cofib}( \widehat{よ}(\mathrm{colim}(-)) \Rightarrow (-))$ where the natural transformation is the counit of the adjunction $\widehat{よ} \dashv \mathrm{colim}$ (cf. find correct phil pützstück exercise). Now for representables, we have $\mathrm{colim}( よ(x)) \simeq x$.

So let $x,y \in \mathcal{C}$ be objects, then we are interseted in $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\omega_1})/ \widehat{よ}(\mathcal{C})}(\mathcal{L}(よ(x)), \mathcal{L}(よ(y)))$ and using the adjunction plus the description of $\mathcal{R}$ we have a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\omega_1})/ \widehat{よ}(\mathcal{C})}(\mathcal{L}(よ(x)), \mathcal{L}(よ(y))) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\omega_1})/ \widehat{よ}(\mathcal{C})}(\mathcal{L}(よ(x)), \mathcal{L}(よ(y))) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\omega_1})}(\mathcal{L}(よ(x)), \mathcal{RL}(よ(y))) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\omega_1})}(\mathcal{L}(よ(x)), \mathrm{cofib}(\widehat{よ}(y) \rightarrow よ(x))) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{cofib}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\omega_1})}(よ(x), \widehat{よ}(y)) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\omega_1})}(よ(x)), よ(y)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{cofib}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C})}(よ(x), \widehat{よ}(y)) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x, y) \\ \end{align*}

here the last step used fully faithfulness of $\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\omega_1}) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C})$.

Now for the second isomorphism, we first note that the mapping spectrum functor $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C})}(よ(x), \widehat{yo}(-)) \colon \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}$ preserves colimits: For this it suffices to observe that it preserves filtered colimits since $\widehat{よ}(-)$ is a left adjoint and $よ(x)$ is compact and it is an (left) exact functor since $\widehat{よ}$ and the mapping spectrum functor is exact. therefore it preserves all colimits, i.e. it lies in $\mathrm{Fun}^{L}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Sp}) \simeq \mathcal{C}^{\vee}$.

Under this identification, we get $\mathrm{ev}_{\mathcal{C}}(y \boxtimes x^{\vee}) \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\omega_1})}(よ(x), \widehat{よ}(y))$.
