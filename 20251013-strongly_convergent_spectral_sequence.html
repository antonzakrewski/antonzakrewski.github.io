strongly convergent spectral sequence

Definition

A strongly convergent spectral sequence consists of the following data

  1. exhaustive filtration & hausdorff filtration $F_*(M)$ of a graded abelian group $M$ where $M_{1} \subseteq M_0 \subseteq M_{-1} ...$
  2. for all $r \geq 1$ a bigraded abelian group $E_{p,q}^r$ (called the \(r\)-th page) for $p,q \in nit$ equipped with a differential $d_{p,q}^r: E_{p,q}^r \rightarrow E_{p-r,q+r-1}^r$ such that $(d^r)^2 = 0$ (abusing indices)
  3. a specified isomorphism $E_{*,*^{r+1} \simeq H_*(E_{*,*}^r)$
  4. for every pair $p,q \in \mathbb{Z}$ there exists a number $N(p,q)$ suhc that $d_{p,q}^r = 0$ and $d_{}$ so in particular $E^r_{p,q} = E^{N(p,q)}_{p,q} \eqqcolon E^{\infty}_{p,q}$
  5. an isomorphism $g^*(F_*(M))\simeq E_{*,*}^{\infty}$ where the bigraded $\mathrm{gr}^*(F_*(M))$ is given by $\mathrm{gr}^n(F_*(M))$, a graded abelian group with graded $k$ piece $\mathrm{gr}^n(F_*(M_k)) = F_n(M_k)/F_{n+1}(M_k)$
