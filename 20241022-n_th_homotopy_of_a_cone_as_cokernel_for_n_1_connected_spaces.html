n-th homotopy of a mapping cylinder as cokernel for n-1 connected spaces

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be (n-1)-connected spaces and $f: (X,x) \rightarrow (Y,y)$ a based map, where $X$ is \(n-1\)-conencetd Then the \(n\)-th homotopy group of the mapping cylinder is given by the cokernel

\begin{align*} \pi_n(X) \cong \mathrm{coker}(\pi_n(f)) \end{align*}
