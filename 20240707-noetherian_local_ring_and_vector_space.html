noetherian local ring and vector space

Proposition

Let $A$ be a noetherian local commutative ring, $\mathfrak{m}$ the maximal ideal. Then the ring dimension is smaller than the vector space dimension

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(A) \leq \mathrm{dim}_{A/\mathfrak{m}}(\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2) \end{align*}

Proof

Note that $\mathfrak{m}$ is a finitely generated module, as $A$ is a noetherian ring. By generators of a module and ideal quotient we conclude that $\mathrm{dim}_{A/\mathfrak{m}}(\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2) < \infty$

Let $\overline{d_1},...,\overline{d_n}$ be a basis of $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$. Then by finitely generated Module over a local ring and fibres of a vector space basis as generators it follows that fibres $d_1,...,d_n$ generate $\mathfrak{m}$. By the height theorem this implies

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(A) \leq n = \mathrm{dim}_{A/\mathfrak{m}}(\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2) < \infty \end{align*}
