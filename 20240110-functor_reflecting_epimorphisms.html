functor reflecting epimorphisms

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to reflect epimorphisms, if for an epimorphism $\mathcal{F}(f) \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{D})$ it follows, that

\begin{align*} f \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is also an epimorphism
