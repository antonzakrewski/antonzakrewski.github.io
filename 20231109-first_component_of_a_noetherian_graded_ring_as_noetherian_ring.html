zeroth component of a noetherian graded ring as noetherian ring

Proposition

Let $M$ be a monoid and $R = \bigoplus_{m \in M} R_m$ a noetherian, graded ring Then $R_0$ is a noetherian ring

Proof

corollary of:

  • quotient ring of a noetherian ring as noetherian ring

and the ring-isomorphism

\begin{align*} (\bigoplus_{m \in M} R_m) /(0 \oplus (\bigoplus_{m \neq 0} R_m)) \cong R_0 \end{align*}
