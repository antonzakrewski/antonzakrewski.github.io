smash product and one point compactification

Proposition

Let $(X,\mathcal{T}_X)$ and $(Y,\mathcal{T}_Y)$ be topological spaces. Then for the smash product and the one point compactification, it holds that

\begin{align*} (X \times Y)^+ \cong X^+ \wedge Y^+ \end{align*}

Note, here it is assumed, that $X^+ = X \sqcup \{\infty\}$. Therefore, for a compact space, this is not a true compactification, as the image is not dense, because $\infty$ is an isolated point

This map is welldefined, as the cardinality $\vert [(x,y)] \vert = 1$ for $x,y \neq \infty$.
