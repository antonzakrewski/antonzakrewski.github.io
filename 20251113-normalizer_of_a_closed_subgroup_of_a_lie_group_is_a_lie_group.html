normalizer of a closed subgroup of a lie group is a closed lie group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a lie group and $H$ a closed subgroup Then the Normalizer is also a closed lie group

Proof

closed subgroup

consider the map

\begin{align*} G \rightarrow& \prod_{h \in h} G \\ g \mapsto& (h \cdot g \cdot h^{-1})_{h \in G} \end{align*}

which is continuous as it is given by products & multiplicaitons & inverses.

Then the normalizer is given by the preimage of $\prod H$, which is closed

lie group

follows from closed subgroup of a lie group is a lie group
