homology ring of infinite complex projective space

Proposition

the singular homolgoy ring of $K(\mathbb{Z},2)$ is given by the divided power algebra

Proof

consider the multiplicative homological serre spectral sequence.

then a computation similar to the cohomology ring of the loop space of the sphere shows that it is given by the divided power algebra.

Here we use that $S^1 \simeq K(\mathbb{Z},1)$ is an \(\mathbb{E}_\infty\)-monoid in Anima, so in particular it is graded-commutative resp. commutative in every nontrivial degree.
