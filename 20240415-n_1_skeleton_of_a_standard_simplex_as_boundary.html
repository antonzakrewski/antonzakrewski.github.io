n-1 skeleton of a n standard simplex as boundary

Proposition

Let $\Delta^{n-1}$ be the represented simplicial set, $\mathrm{sk}_n$ the n skeleton functor. Then it holds

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sk}_n(\Delta^{n-1}) \cong \partial \Delta^n \end{align*}

where $\partial \Delta^n$ is the boundary of a standard simplex

Proof
