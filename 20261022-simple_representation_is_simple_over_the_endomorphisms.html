simple representation is simple over the endomorphism ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be an R-Algebra and $V$ a simple representation. Then $V$ is also a simple representation over $D \coloneqq \mathrm{End}_{A}(V)$.

Proof

Recall that the module structure is given by the canonical lift

Suppose $V$ is not simple over $D$, i.e. there exists a nontrivial D-submodule of $V$ . then restriction of scalars would induce a nontrivial $A$ submodule, contradiction to our assumption.
