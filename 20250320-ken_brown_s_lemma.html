Ken Brown's lemma on fibrant objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category of fibrant objects, $\mathcal{D}$ a category with weak equivalences and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor which sends trivial fibrations to weak equivalences Then $\mathcal{F}$ preserves weak equivalences.
