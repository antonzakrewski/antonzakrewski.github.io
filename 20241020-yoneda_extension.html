yoneda extension

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small infinity category and $\mathcal{D}$ an cocomplete category and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an infinity functor Then the yoneda extension is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Yo}(\mathcal{F}): \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

given by the infinity left kan extension

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

along the infinity yoneda embedding
