standard open of an affine variety as affine variety up to isomorphism

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be an affine variety and $f \in \mathcal{A}(X)$ an elemnet in the coordinate ring Then the standard open

\begin{align*} D(f) \cong X' \end{align*}

is isomorphic to an $Y$ with respect to regular functions

Proof

Let $I(X) \subseteq K[X_1,...,X_n]$ be the vanishing ideal and $F \in K[X_1,...,X_n]$ a representative of $f$. Then $F(P) \neq 0$ for all $P \in D(f)$ and hence

\begin{align*} p: D(f) \rightarrow \mathbb{A}_k^{m+1} (x_1,...,x_m) \mapsto (x_1,...,x_{m}, \frac{1}{F(x_1,...,x_m)} \end{align*}

is a regular function on $D(f)$

Then the projection

\begin{align*} q: \mathbb{A}_k^{m+1} \rightarrow& \mathbb{A}_k^m \\ (x_1,...,x_{m+1}) \mapsto& (x_1,...,x_m) \end{align*}

is regular and an inverse on $(x_1,...,x_{m+1})$ mit $x_{m+1} = \frac{1}{f(x_1,...,x_m)}$, i.e.

\begin{align*} x_{m+1} F(x_1,...,x_m) - 1 = 0 \end{align*}

Let $J = (I(X), X_{m+1}F - 1) \subseteq K[X_1,...,X_n]$ and $Y = Z(J) \subseteq \mathbb{A}_k^{m+1}$.

Then $p$ and $q$ are regular maps between $D(f)$ and $Y$, which are inverse to each other.
