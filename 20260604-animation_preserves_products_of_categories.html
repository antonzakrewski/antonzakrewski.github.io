animation preserves products of categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be animatable categories. Then the animation functor preserves products, i.e.

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An}(\mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{D}) \simeq& \mathrm{An}(\mathcal{C}) \times \mathrm{An}(\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

Proof

use:

  • Cat^prod is preadditive
  • 1-sfp objects closed under finite coproducts
