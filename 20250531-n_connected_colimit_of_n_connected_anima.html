n-connected colimit of n-connected anima as n-connected anima

Proposition

Let $I$ be an n-connected anima and $I \rightarrow \mathrm{An}_{\geq n+1}$ be a diagram consisting of n-connected anima. Then the colimit of $I \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ is also $n$ connected.

Proof

Note that connectedness can still be detected after applying the n-truncation functor of anima (right index ?). This functor preserves colimits (cf. n-truncation functor as left adjoint, infinity left adjoint preserves colimits). Thus we get a diagram

which consists objectwise of terminal objects (by assumption of connectivity).
