supremum of norms of a weakly convergent sequence

Proposition

Let $x_n \xrightarrow{w} x$ be a weakly convergent sequence. Then $\mathrm{sup}( \| x_n \|) < \infty$

Proof

Let $x_n \xrightarrow{n \rightarrow \infty} x$, so $f(x_n) \rightarrow f(x)$ for every $f \in X^*$. This shows that for all $f$,

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sup}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \vert (J x_n)(f) \vert = \mathrm{sup}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \vert f(x_n) \vert < \infty \end{align*}

where we have used the embedding into the bidual normed space $J: X \rightarrow X^**$. Now $X^*$ is Banach (cf. dual space of a normed space is a banach space) hence the uniform boundedness principle shows

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sup}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \| J x_n \|_{**} =& \mathrm{sup}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \| x_n \| \\ <& \infty \end{align*}
