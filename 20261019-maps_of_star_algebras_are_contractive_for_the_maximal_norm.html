maps of star algebras are contractive for the maximal seminorm

Proposition

Let $f: A \rightarrow B$ be a morphism of nonunital star algebras. Then $f$ is contractive with respect to the maximal seminorm, i.e.,

\begin{align*} \| f(a) \|_{B, \mathrm{max}} \leq \| a \|_{A, \mathrm{max}} \end{align*}

Proof

Use that for any representation $\rho: B \rightarrow B(H)$, precomposition with $f$ induces a representation $\rho \circ f: A \rightarrow B(H)$.
