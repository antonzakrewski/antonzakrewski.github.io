fiber of functor into a pointed category is full subcategory

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories. Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor and $* \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a fully faithful functor.

Then the fiber in category Cat is a full subcategory of $\mathcal{C}$.
