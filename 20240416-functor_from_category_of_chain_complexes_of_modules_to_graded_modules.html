functor from category of chain complexes of modules to graded modules

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod})$ the category of chain complexes. Then there exists a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Forget}: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{GradedMod}_{\mathrm{triv}} \\ \end{align*}

for the trivial gradation of $R$ and the category of graded modules

Proof
