sheaf of modules over a ringed space

Definition

Let $(X,\mathcal{O}_X)$ be a ringed spaced. A sheaf of a module over $\mathcal{O}_X$ is a sheaf $\mathcal{F}$, where $\mathcal{F}(U)$ is a module over the ring $\mathcal{O}_X(U)$ such that the modulestructure is compatible with $\mathcal{O}_X$, i.e.: For the restriction $\mathrm{res}_{U,O}: \mathcal{F}(U) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(O)$ for $O \subseteq U \subseteq X$ open and $r \in \mathcal{O}_X(U), m \in \mathcal{F}(U)$ it holds:

\begin{align*} \mathrm{res}_{U,O}(r \cdot m) =& \mathrm{res}_{\mathcal{O}}(r) \cdot \mathrm{res}_{U,O}(m) \end{align*}

where $\rho_{\mathcal{O}}: \mathcal{O}_X(U) \rightarrow \mathcal{O}_X(O)$ is the restriction of the ringed space $(X, \mathcal{O}_X)$
