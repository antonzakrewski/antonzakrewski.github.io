exponential as covering map of the circle

Proposition

The exponential

\begin{align*} \mathrm{exp}: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow& S^1 \\ r \mapsto& \mathrm{e}^{r \cdot \mathrm{i} \pi} \end{align*}

is a covering map

Proof

follows from:

  • orbital projection of a free action as covering map and covering-like
  • translation of the reals as covering-like group action
