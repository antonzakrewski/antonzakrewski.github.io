semi-locally simply connected space

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space.

Then $X$ is called semi-locally simply connected, if for each $x \in X$ and open neighbourhood $V \subseteq X$, there exists a neihgbourhood $U \subseteq V$, such that

\begin{align*} \iota: \pi_1(U,x) \rightarrow \pi_1(X,x) \end{align*}

is the zero morphism

i.e. every Loop inside $U$ is nullhomotopic if considered as loop inside $X$
