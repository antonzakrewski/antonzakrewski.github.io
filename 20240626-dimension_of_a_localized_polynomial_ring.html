dimension of a localized polynomial ring over a field

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $K[X,X^{-1}]$ the localized polynomial ring Then the dimension is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(K[X,X^{-1}]) = 1 \end{align*}

Proof

$\leq 1$

Note that

\begin{align*} K[X,X^{-1}] \cong K[X,Y]/(XY - 1) \end{align*}

(cf. localization and polynomial ring)

using dimension of a polynomial ring over a noetherian ring / dimension of a finitely generated k-algebra as transcendence degree we get that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(K[X,Y]) = 2 \end{align*}

Furthermore the height of $XY - 1$ is given by at least $1$ as $K[X,Y]$ is an integral domain, showing $(0) \subsetneq (XY - 1)$

Then by height of a prime ideal and quotient we get the inequality

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ht}((XY - 1)) + \mathrm{dim}(K[X,X^{-1}]) \end{align*}

$\geq 1$

Note that $K[X,X^{-1}] \hookrightarrow Q(K[X])$ is a subring of an integral domain, hence also an integral domain. But $K[X,X^{-1}]$ is not a field, hence has dimension at least $1$ finite dimensional integral domain as field

therefore

\begin{align*} (0) \subseteq (X - 1) \end{align*}
