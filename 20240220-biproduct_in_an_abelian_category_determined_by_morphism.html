biproduct in an ab-enriched category determined by morphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an Ab-enriched category, $A_1,A_2,B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{A})$ objects with morphisms

such that

\begin{align*} p_k \circ \iota_l = \begin{cases} 0 & \mbox{if } k \neq l \\ \mathrm{id} & \mbox{if } k = l \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} p_1 \circ \iota_1 + \iota_2 \circ p_2 = \mathrm{id}_{C} \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} C \cong A_1 \oplus A_2 \end{align*}

is the biproduct

Proof

product

existence

Suppose there exist morphisms

Then let

\begin{align*} h: X \rightarrow& C \\ x \mapsto& \iota_1(f(x) + \iota_2(g(x)) \end{align*}

then this commutes, as for $i = 1$ (analogous for $i = 2$)

\begin{align*} p_1(h(x)) =& p_1(\iota_1(f(x)) + p_1(\iota_2(g(x)) ) \\ =& \mathrm{id}(f(x)) + 0(g(x)) \\ =& f(x) \end{align*}

uniqueness

Suppose $h,h'$ are morphisms satisfieng this.

Then it follows, that

\begin{align*} h =& h \circ \mathrm{id}_{X} \\ =& h \circ (p_1 \iota_1 + p_2 \iota_2) \\ =& h(p_1 \iota_1) + h(p_2 \iota_2) \\ =& h'(p_1 \iota_1) + h'(p_2 \iota_2) \\ =& h'(p_1 \iota_1 + p_2 \iota_2) \\ =& h'(\mathrm{id}_{C}) \\ =& h' \end{align*}
