n-connected pair of spaces and relative homotopy

Proposition

Let $(X,A)$ be an \(n\)-connected topological space, where $A$ is \(p\)-connected. Then the map

\begin{align*} \pi_l(X,A) \rightarrow \pi_l(X/A, [A]) \end{align*}

for the

  • relative homotopy
  • topological collapse

is

  1. an isomorphism for $l \leq n + p -1$
