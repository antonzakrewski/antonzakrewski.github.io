internal hom functor of symmetric monoidal categories

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathbb{1}, \otimes)$ be a symmetric monoidal, closed category. Then the internal hom functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(-,-): \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ \end{align*}

such that for each $C \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(C,-) \end{align*}

is a right adjoint to $- \otimes C$
