natural isomorphism as adjoint

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be locally small categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ and $\mathcal{F}^{-1}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be natural isomorphic. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is a left adjoint and right adjoint to $\mathcal{F}^{-1}$

Proof

w.l.o.g. for $\mathcal{F}$ as left adjoint, the other side follows from symmetry of $\mathcal{F} = (\mathcal{F}^{-1})^{-1}$ We let

\begin{align*} \mathcal{G}: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,\mathcal{F}^{-1}(B)) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(A), B) \\ (f: A \rightarrow \mathcal{F}^{-1}(B)) \mapsto& (\mathcal{F}(f): \mathcal{F}(A) \rightarrow (\mathcal{F}(f): \mathcal{F}(A) \rightarrow B) = (\mathcal{F}(f: A \rightarrow \mathcal{F}^{-1}(B))) \end{align*}
