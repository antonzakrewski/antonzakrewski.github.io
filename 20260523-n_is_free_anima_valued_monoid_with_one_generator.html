Proposition

The natural numbers $\mathbb{N}$ are the free cartesian monoid generated by the singleton (in $\mathrm{An}$).

Proof

recall that the inclusion $\mathrm{Set} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ is strong symmetric monoidal functor (cf. n-truncated anima closed under limits) and preserves coproducts. So the explicit description of the free E1 Algebra shows that the free \(\mathbb{E}_1\) monoid in $\mathrm{Set}$ and $\mathrm{An}$ coincide. Fortunately, we know that the free \(\mathbb{E}_1\) monoid in $\mathrm{Set}$ is $\mathbb{N}$.
