product of stable infinity categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_{\alpha}$ be stable infinity categories, then the product

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \coloneqq \prod_{\alpha \in A} \mathcal{C}_{\alpha} \end{align*}

is also a stable infinity category

Proof

follows from

  • limit in an infinity functor category
  • colimit in an infinity functor category
  • terminal object in an infinity category as empty limit
  • initial object in an infinity category as empty colimit

where computing them pointwise offers a zero object.
