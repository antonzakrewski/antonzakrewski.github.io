degree of maps from S^n to CP^n

Proposition

Let $n \geq 2$, then the degree $D(S^n, \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n)$ is zero.

Proof

use some

  • degree of a map between closed oriented manifolds is determined by cohomology
  • any map $S^n \rightarrow \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n$ must be the zero map on top homology, since the generator $x \in H_2(\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n)$ must be mapped to zero.
