1 dimensional representations of S_n

Proposition

Let $n \geq 2$. Then there exists only two one dimensional representations of $S_n$, namely given by

  1. the sign action
  2. the constant action

Proof

a)

the abelianization of $S_n$ is given by $\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathrm{GL}_1(k)$ is an abelian group.

b)

Observe that $(1,2)$ squares to $1$, hence must act by $\pm 1$. Now $(1,n) (1,2) (1,n) = (2,n)$ and similarly we can generate $(n,m)$ Now transpositions generate any permutation (cf. Darstellung einer Permutation als Produkt von Transpositionen).
