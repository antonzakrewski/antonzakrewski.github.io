zariski descent for Modules over a ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $f_i$ a family of elements generating the unit ideal. Then there exists a limit diagram 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{Mod}_{R} \rar & \prod_{i \in I} \mathrm{Mod}_{R_{f_i}} \arrow[shift right=1, swap]{r}{} \arrow[shift left=1]{r}{} & \prod_{i,j \in I} \mathrm{Mod}_{R_{f_i f_j}} \arrow[shift right=2, swap]{r}{} \arrow[shift left=2]{r}{} \rar & \prod_{i,j,k \in I} \mathrm{Mod}_{R_{f_i f_j f_k}
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Proof
