left bousfield localization of Anima preserves finite products

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ be a left bousfield localization of anima Then both functors preserves finite products (in particular also the right adjoint)

Proof

Let this be a localization for some $S \subseteq \mathrm{Mor}(\mathrm{An})$. Let $\mathcal{C} \subseteq \mathrm{An}$ be the full subcategory of \(S\)-local objects. Then we need to show that $\mathcal{F}((X \times Y)) \simeq \mathcal{F}(x) \times \mathcal{F}(y)$. For that we use that mapping anima into local object as local object, i.e. for hypoabelian $A$ and arbitrary $T$ we get that $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(T,A)$ is hypoabelian. Thus we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{F}(X \times Y), A) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X \times Y, A) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X, \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(Y,A)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X, \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{F}(Y),A)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{F}(X), \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(\mathcal{F}(Y),A)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(\mathcal{F}(X) \times \mathcal{F}(Y), A) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{F}(X) \times \mathcal{F}(Y), A) \end{align*}

Here we use that $\mathcal{F}(X) \times \mathcal{F}(Y)$ lies in the full subcategory, since local object are closed under limits
