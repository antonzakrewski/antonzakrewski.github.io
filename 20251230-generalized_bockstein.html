generalized bockstein sequence

Definition

Let $A \rightarrow B \rightarrow C$ be a fiber sequence in category of Spectra and $X$ any spectrum Then there exists a long exact sequence

\begin{align*} \pi_{n+1}(X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} A) \rightarrow \pi_n(X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} A) \rightarrow \pi_n(X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} B) \rightarrow \pi_n(X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} C) \rightarrow \pi_{n-1}(X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} C) \end{align*}

proof

  • long exact sequence homotopy groups for a spectrum
  • $- \otimes A$ is exact infinity functor
