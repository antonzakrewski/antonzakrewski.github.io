functor creating classes of morphisms

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor and $\Sigma \subseteq \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C}), \Sigma' \subseteq \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{D})$ be classes of morphisms Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to create $\Sigma, \Sigma'$ if for $f \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$ TFAE:

  1. $f \in \Sigma$
  2. $\mathcal{F}(f) \in \Sigma'$
