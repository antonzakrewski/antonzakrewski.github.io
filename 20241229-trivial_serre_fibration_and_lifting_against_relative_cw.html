trivial serre fibration and lifting against relative cw

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a continuous map TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a trivial serre fibration
  2. $f$ has the rlp against $S^{n-1} \rightarrow D^n$ for $n \geq 0$
  3. $f$ has the right lifting property against relative cw inclusions

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

serre fibration and injective on homotopy group and strict lift serre fibration and surjective on homotopy group and lift

2) $\implies$ 3)

follows from:

  • set with left lifting property as saturated set

3) $\implies$ 1)
