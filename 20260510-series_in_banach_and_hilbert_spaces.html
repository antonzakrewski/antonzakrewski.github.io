series in a banach space

Proposition

Let $X$ be a banach space and $x_n \in X$ a sequence. Suppose $\sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \| x_n \| < \infty$. Then $\sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \coloneqq \mathrm{lim}_{N \rightarrow \infty} \sum_{n = 1}^N x_n$ exists in $X$.

Proof

Observe that $(y_m) \coloneqq \sum_{n \leq m} \| x_n \|$ is a Cauchy sequence, as it converges. Hence $\sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} x_n$ is a Cauchy seuqence in $X$. But $X$ is complete, so $\sum_{n\in \mathbb{N}} x_n$ exists.
