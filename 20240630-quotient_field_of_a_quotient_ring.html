quotient field of a quotient ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{p} \subseteq A$ a prime ideal, $A /\mathfrak{p}$ the quotient ring as A-Algebra Then the quotient field is canonically isomorphic

\begin{align*} Q(A/\mathfrak{p}) \simeq A_{\mathfrak{p}}/\mathfrak{p} A_{\mathfrak{p}} \end{align*}

Proof

a)

  • Quotient Field as localization
  • localization as exact functor

where an element $[a] \in A/\mathfrak{p}$ is nonzero if and only if the representative $a \not\in \mathfrak{p}$
