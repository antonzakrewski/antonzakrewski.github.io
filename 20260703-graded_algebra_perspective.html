perspective on graded algebras

Idea

Let $\mathbb{Z}^{\mathrm{disc}}$ be the symmetric monoidal category with the monoidal strutcure given by addition and $\mathcal{C}$ be any (symmetric) monoidal categories (might miss some assumptions). Then there are two lax (symmetric) monoidal functors

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{day}}(\mathbb{N}^{\mathrm{disc}}, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{day}}(*,\mathcal{C}) \simeq \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

which then induce functors on category of O-algebras

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Alg}(\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{day}}(\mathbb{Z}^{\mathrm{disc}}, \mathcal{C})) \rightarrow \mathrm{Alg}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

namely left kan extension and right kan extension along $\mathbb{Z} \rightarrow *$ or equivalently taking colimits and limits
