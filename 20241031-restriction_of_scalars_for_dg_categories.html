restriction of scalars for dg categories

Proposition

Let $\varphi: R \rightarrow R'$ be ring-homomorphism between commutative rings Then there exists a \(2\)-functor on the category of enriched categories

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{dg}}^{R} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{dg}}^{R'} \end{align*}

Proof

  • restriction of scalars for hom chain complexes as lax monoidal functor
  • lax monoidal functor induces a 2-functor on category of of enriched functors
