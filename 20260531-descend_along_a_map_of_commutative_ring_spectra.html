descend of module spectra along a map of commutative ring spectra

Definition

Let $S \rightarrow R$ be a map of $\mathbb{E}_{\infty}$-ring spectra and $M$ an \(S\)-module spectrum. Then $M$ descends along $S \rightarrow R$ if

\begin{align*} M \rightarrow \lim_{\Delta} (R^{\otimes_S \bullet + 1} \otimes_S} M) \end{align*}

is an equivalence
