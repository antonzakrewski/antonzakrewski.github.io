brown comenetz dual for a product of injective groups

Proposition

Let $D_i$ be a set of injective abelian groups. Then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} I_{\prod_{i \in I} D_i} \rightarrow \prod_{i \in I} I_{ D_i} \end{align*}

for brown comenetz duals.

Proof

Observe that $\prod D_i$ is also an injective group (cf. product of injective objects as injective object). The projections $\prod_{i \in I} D_i \rightarrow D_i$ induces a map $I_{\prod_{i \in I} D_i} \rightarrow I_{D_i}$ (cf. map of injective groups induces a map on brown-comenetz duals) for each $i \in I$ and hence in particular a map

\begin{align*} I_{\prod_{i \in I} D_i} \rightarrow \prod_{i \in I} I_{D_i} \end{align*}
