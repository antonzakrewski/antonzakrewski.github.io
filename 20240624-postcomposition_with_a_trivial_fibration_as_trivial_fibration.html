postcomposition with a trivial fibration is a trivial fibration

Proposition

Let $p: X \rightarrow Y$ a trivial fibration. Then for any simplicial set $A$, postcomposition on the inner hom

\begin{align*} p_*: \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(A,X) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(A,Y) \end{align*}

is also a trivial fibration
