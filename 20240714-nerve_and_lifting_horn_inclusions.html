nerve and lifting horn inclusions

Proposition

Let $X$ be a simplicial set.

TFAE:

  1. there exists a category $\mathcal{C}$ such that the nerve $\mathcal{N}(\mathcal{C}) \cong X$
  2. $X$ has a unique extension for inner horn inclusions, i.e.
  1. $X$ has unique extension for spine inclusions for $n \geq 2$
