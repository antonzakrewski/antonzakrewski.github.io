filtered colimit of strongly continuous functors and image generating category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable presentable category and $\mathcal{C}_i \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ a filtered system of stable full presentable categories, where the inclusion $\mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is strongly continuous. Suppose $\mathcal{C}$ is generated by $\mathcal{C}_i$ as localizing category, then the induced functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim} \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

is an equivalence, where the colimit is taken in PrL.

Proof

Using PrLL stable satisfies weak AB5 axiom and monomorphisms in PrLL stable are fully faithful functors this implies that the functor $\mathrm{colim} \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is fully faithful. Now by assumption $\mathcal{C}_i$ generate $\mathcal{C}$ under colimits, which implies that $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{lim} \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \prod \mathcal{C}_i$ is conservative. Hence so is $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{lim} \mathcal{C}_i$ and therefore both adjoints are equivalences (cf. fully faithful left adjoint and conservative right adjoint as equivalence).
