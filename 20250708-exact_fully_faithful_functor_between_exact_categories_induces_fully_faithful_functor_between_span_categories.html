exact fully faithful functor between exact categories induces fully faithful functor between span categories

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{in}(\mathcal{C}), \mathrm{proj}(\mathcal{C})), (\mathcal{D}, \mathrm{in}(\mathcal{D}), \mathrm{proj}(\mathcal{D}))$ be exact categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a fully faithful exact infinity functor which restricts fully faithfully to $\mathrm{inf}(\mathcal{C})$ and $\mathrm{proj}(\mathcal{C})$. Then $\mathcal{F}$ induces a fully faithful functor upon applying span category functor
