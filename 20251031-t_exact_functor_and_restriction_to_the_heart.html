t-exact functor and restriction to the heart via left completeness

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be stable categories equipped with a t-structure. Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an exact functor.

Assume that $\mathcal{C}$ is a left complete t structure and bounded below t-structure / bounded above t-structure and that $\mathcal{F}$ restricts to the hearts Then $\mathcal{F}$ is also t-exact

Proof
