category of Algebra objects for 2 operads

Definition

Let $\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}, \mathcal{O}'^{\otimes}$ be infinity operads Then the category of Algebra objects $\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}}(\mathcal{O}'^{\otimes})$ is defined as full sub infinity category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}, \mathcal{O}'^{\otimes}) \end{align*}

containing maps of infinity operads
