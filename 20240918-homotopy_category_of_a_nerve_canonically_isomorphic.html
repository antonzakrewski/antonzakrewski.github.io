homotopy category of a nerve canonically isomorphic

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $h$ the homotopy category functor and $N$ the nerve functor Then there exists a canonical natural isomorphism given by the counit

\begin{align*} h(N(\mathcal{C})) \cong \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

or more explicit

\begin{align*} h(N(\mathcal{C})) \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ x \mapsto& x \\ [f] \mapsto& f \\ \end{align*}

Proof

a)

corollary of:

  • fully faithful right adjoint and counit as isomorphism
  • nerve functor as fully faithful functor

b)

it is clearly an isomorphism if welldefined

welldefined

Suppose $[f] = [f']$, then there exist $g,h$ with $f' = h \circ g$ and a 2-simplex

witnessing that $h \circ g = f$ or $f = f'$

Hence the map respects the relationnerve functor as right adjoint.
