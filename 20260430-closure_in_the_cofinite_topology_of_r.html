closure in the cofinite topology of R

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{R}$ be the real numbers with the cofinite topology and $A$ a subspace Then the closure is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{int}(A) =& \begin{cases} A & \mbox{if } \vert X \setminus A \vert < \infty \\ \emptyset & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof
