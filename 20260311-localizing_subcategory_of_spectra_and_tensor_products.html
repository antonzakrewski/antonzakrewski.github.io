localizing subcategory of spectra and tensor products

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a localizing subcategory of category of Spectra. Then for $X$ in $\mathcal{C}$ and any spectrum $Y$, it follows that

\begin{align*} X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} Y \in \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

Proof

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be the pullback

i.e. the collection of spectra $Y$, such that $X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} Y$ lies in $\mathcal{C}$ Then we want to use structural induction in Spectra

  1. $X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} -$ preserves colimits
  2. $X\otimes_{\mathbb{S}} Y[-1] \simeq (X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} Y)[-1]$ and $\mathcal{C}$ is closed under shifts
  3. $\mathbb{S}$ is the monoidal unit, hence in $\mathcal{D}$
