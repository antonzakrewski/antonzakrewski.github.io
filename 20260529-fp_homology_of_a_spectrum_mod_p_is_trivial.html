Fp homology of a spectrum mod q is trivial

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum, $X/q$ the mod q spectrum for $p,q$ coprime. Then $H_*(X/q, \mathbb{F}_p)$ is trivial.

Proof

observe that there exists a fiber sequence $X \xrightarrow{\cdot q} X \rightarrow X/q$. Then we may tensor with $\mathbb{F}_p$ and observe that it is an equivalence
