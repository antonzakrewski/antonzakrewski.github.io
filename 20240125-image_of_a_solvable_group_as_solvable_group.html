image of a solvable group as solvable group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a solvable group and $\varphi: G \rightarrow H$ a group-homomorphism for another group $H$. Then the image $\mathrm{im}(\varphi)$ is also solvable

Proof

By assumption, there exists normal subgroups

\begin{align*} 1 = G_0 \trianglelefteq ... \trianglelefteq G_n = G \end{align*}

Then $\varphi[G_i] \subseteq \varphi[G_{i+1}]$ is a normal subgroup (cf. Bild eines Normalteilers für einen Gruppenepimorphismus als Normalteiler)

Thus consider

\begin{align*} \varphi[G_0] = \varphi[1] = 1 \trianglelefteq ... \trianglelefteq \varphi[G_n] = \varphi[G] \end{align*}

Note that, there exists a surjection $G_i/G_{i+1} \rightarrow \varphi[G_{i}]/ \varphi[G_{i+1}]$ (cf. group homomorphism of quotient groups induces group epimorphism on quotients of images). By image of an abelian group as abelian group it holds, that $\varphi[G_{i}]/ \varphi[G_{i+1}]$ is abelian
