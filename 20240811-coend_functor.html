coend functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{coend}}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ the full subcategory containing bifunctors which admit a Coend Then the end functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \int_{c \in \mathcal{C}}: \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{end}}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow& \mathcal{D} \\ \mathcal{F} \mapsto& \int_{c: \mathcal{C}} \mathcal{F}(c,c) \\ \eta: \mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \mathcal{G} \mapsto& \eta': \int_{c: \mathcal{C}} \mathcal{F}(c,c) \rightarrow \int_{c: \mathcal{C}} \mathcal{G}(c,c) \end{align*}

where $\eta'$ is the induced morphism.
