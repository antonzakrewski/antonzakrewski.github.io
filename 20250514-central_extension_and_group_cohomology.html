correspondence between central extension and second group cohomology

Proposition

Let $G,A$ be groups, where $A$ is abelian. Then there exists a natural correspondence between

  1. elements in $H^2(\mathrm{B}G,A)$
  2. central extension of groups

Proof

a)

  • central extension induces element on group cohomology

b)

  • element in second group cohomology induces central extension
