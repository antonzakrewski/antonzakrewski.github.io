bijection of reduced closed subschemes and closed subsets

Proposition

Let $X$ be a scheme Then the composite

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Closed}^{\mathrm{red}}(X) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Closed}(X) \xrightarrow{\vert - \vert} \mathrm{Closed}(\vert X \vert) \end{align*}

is a bijection
