singular homology of a 0-sphere

Proposition

The singular homology of a 0-sphere is given by

\begin{align*} H_k(S^0) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} \oplus \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } k = 0 \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

Follows from

\begin{align*} S^0 \cong \{a,b\} = \{a\} \coprod \{b\} \end{align*}

with the discrete topology and singular homology commutes with coproducts, where $\{a\}, \{b\}$ are contractible space (cf. singular homology of the point) showing that

\begin{align*} H_k(S^0) \cong H_k(*) \coprod H_k(*) \end{align*}
