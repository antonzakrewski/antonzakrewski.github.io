trivial fibration as kan fibration

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a trivial fibration. Then $f$ is a kan fibration

Proof

follows from 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\Lambad_i^n \arrow[]{d}{\iota}& \\
\partial \Delta^n \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & X \arrow[]{d}{}\\
\Delta^n \arrow[]{r}{} & Y
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
