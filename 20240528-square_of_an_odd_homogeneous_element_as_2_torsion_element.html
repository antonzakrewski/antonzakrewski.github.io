square of an odd homogeneous element as 2-torsion element in a graded-commutative ring

Proposition

Let \(R = \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0} R_n\) be a N-graded ring and $\alpha \in R$ a homogeneous element of odd degree $2n + 1$. Then

\begin{align*} \alpha^2 \in R_{4n + 2} \end{align*}

is 2-torsion, i.e.

\begin{align*} 2 \cdot \alpha^2 = 0 \end{align*}

Proof

follows from graded commutativity as

\begin{align*} \alpha \cdot \alpha =& \alpha \cdot \alpha \\ =& (-1)^{(2n + 1)^2} \alpha \cdot \alpha \\ =& (-1) \alpha \cdot \alpha \end{align*}

showing

\begin{align*} \alpha^2 =& - \alpha^2 \\ 0 =& 2 \alpha^2 \end{align*}
