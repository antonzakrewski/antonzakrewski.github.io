change of target of a left kan extension

Proposition

Let

be a (non-)commuting diagram where $\mathcal{D}_0 \hookrightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is fully faithful

Suppose that the composition $\mathcal{C}' \rightarrow \mathcal{D}_0 \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is the left kan extension of $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ along $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}'$. Then $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}_0$ already was the left kan extension of $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}_0$ along $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}'.

Proof

follows from:

  • adjoint object and restriction
  • postcomposition with a fully faithful is functor fully faithful on functor categories
