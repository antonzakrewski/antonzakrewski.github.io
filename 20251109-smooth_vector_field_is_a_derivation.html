smooth vector field induces pointwise derivations

Proposition

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold, $X: M \rightarrow \mathrm{T}M$ a smooth vector field and $p$ a point. Then $X$ induces a derivation at $p$ via

\begin{align*} X(-): C^{\infty}(M) \rightarrow& \mathbb{R} \\ f \mapsto& \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}(f \circ X(p))(1) = \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}(f \circ \gamma(t))(1) = (D_p(f) \circ D_{0}(\gamma))(1) \end{align*}
