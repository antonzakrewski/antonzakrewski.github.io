singleton sheaf functor

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category admitting products. Then the singleton sheaf functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{singeltonsheaf}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Sh}( \{x\}) \\ A \mapsto& \mathcal{F}_A \\ (f: A \rightarrow B) \mapsto& (\eta( \{x\})): f) \end{align*}
