equivalences are precisely the S-local equivalences

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a map of spectra TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an $\mathbb{S}$ -local equivalence
  2. $f$ is an equivalence

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

by assumption, $\mathrm{cofib}(f)$ is an \(\mathbb{S}\)-acyclic spectrum. That is $E \otimes \mathbb{S} \simeq 0$, which implies $E \simeq 0$.

2) $\implies$ 1)

  • equivalence is an E-local equivalence
