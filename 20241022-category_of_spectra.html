category of spectrum objects

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a finitely complete infinity category Then the category of Spectra is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sp}(\mathcal{C}) =& \mathrm{Exc}_*(\mathrm{Spc}_*^{\mathrm{fin}}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

see:

  • category of reduced excisive functors
  • pointed An
  • $\mathrm{Spc}_*^{\mathrm{fin}} = (*)^{\mathrm{fin}} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(*)$
  • category of finite spaces

idea

For $X \in \mathrm{Sp}(\mathcal{C})$ think of $X(n)$ as $n-l$ fold delooping of $X(l)$
