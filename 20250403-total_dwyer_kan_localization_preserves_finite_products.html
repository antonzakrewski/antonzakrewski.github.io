total dwyer-kan localization from Cat preserves finite products

Proposition

The total dwyer-kan localization

\begin{align*} \vert \cdot \vert: \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty} \rightarrow \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

preserves finite product

Proof

follows from:

  • quillen formula for infinity limits in cat infinity & quillen formula for infinity limits in Anima
  • homotopy product as categorical product up to fibrant replacement

where

  1. infinity categories are fibrant objects in the joyal model structure (cf. infinity category as fibrant object with respect to the joyal model structure)
  2. Kan complexes are fibrant objects in the Kan-quillen Model structure (cf. kan complexes as fibrant objects wrt the kan-quillen )

here we may choose Ex infinity as model for the localization, which preserves finite limits
