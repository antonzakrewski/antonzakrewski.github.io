filtered colimit consisting of fully faithful functors as union

Proposition

Let $I \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}$ be a diagram where each morphism is fully faithful. Then the colimit in $\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}$ is given by

\begin{align*} \bigcup I(i) \end{align*}

i.e.

  1. $\pi_0(\mathrm{ob}(\mathrm{colim}_I \mathcal{C}_i)) \cong \mathrm{colim}_I \pi_0(\mathcal{C}_i)$ (so up to essentially surjectivity)
  2. $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{colim}_I}([x],[y]) = \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}_i}(x,y)$

Proof

model independent proof

follows from:

  • mapping space of a filtered colimit
  • isomorphism classes of objects of a filtered colimit
  • composition of a filtered colimit of infinity categories
