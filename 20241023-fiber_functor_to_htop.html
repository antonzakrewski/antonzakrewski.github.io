fiber functor to hTop

Definition / Proposition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a hurewicz fibration. Then the fiber functor to Category hTop is defined as

\begin{align*} \Phi_{p}: \tau_{\leq 1}(B) \rightarrow& \mathrm{hTop} b \mapsto& E_b \\ (\gamma: b \leadsto b') \mapsto& (\Phi_{p}(\gamma): E_b \rightarrow E_{b'}) \end{align*}

where $\tau_{\leq 1}(B)$ is the fundamental groupoid and $\Phi_{p}(\gamma)$ is the homotopoy class of

\begin{align*} h(-,1): E_b \rightarrow E_{b'} \end{align*}
