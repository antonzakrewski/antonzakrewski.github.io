epimorphism in the product category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_i$ be categories, $\prod \mathcal{C}_i$ the product category and $(f_i) \in \mathrm{Mor}(\prod \mathcal{C}_i)$ a morphism

TFAE:

  1. $(f_i)$ is an epi in $\prod \mathcal{C}_i$
  2. $f_i$ is an epimorphism in $\mathcal{C}_i$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
