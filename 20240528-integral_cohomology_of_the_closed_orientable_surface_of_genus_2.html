integral cohomology of the closed orientable surface of genus g

Proposition

Let $\sigma_g$ be the closed orientable surface of genus $g$ Then the singular cohomology is given by 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
H^n(\sigma_{g}) = 
    \begin{cases}
 \mathbb{Z}     & \mbox{if } n = 0 \\
\mathbb{Z}^{4g}  & \mbox{if } n = 1 \\ 
     0 & \mbox{else } \\ 
    \end{cases}
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Proof

using

  • universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology
  • integral homology of the closed orientable surface of genus g
