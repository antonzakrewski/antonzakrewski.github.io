intersection of a strict chain of ideals in a PID

Proposition

Let $A$ be a principal ideal domain and

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a}_1 \supsetneq \mathfrak{a}_2 ... \end{align*}

be a chain of ideals.

Then the intersection

\begin{align*} \bigcap_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0} \mathfrak{a}_i = (0) \end{align*}

Proof

Since $A$ is a PID, it is also a unique factorization domain (cf. PID as UFD)

Let $a \in \bigcap_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0 } \mathfrak{a}_i$, then there exists a prime decomposition $a = \varepsilon \cdot p_1 \cdot ... p_n$
