relative cup product

Definition

Let $R$ be a principal ideal domain, $X$ a topological space, $A_1,A_2 \subseteq X$ subspaces such that $(X \times X, X \times A_2, A_1 \times X)$ is an excisive triad. Then the relative cup product is defined as 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
H^*(X,A_1;R) \otimes_R H^*(X,A_2;R) \arrow[]{r}{\Delta^*'} & H^*((X,A_1) \times (X,A_2); R) \arrow[]{r}{} & H^*((X,A_1 \cup A_2);R)
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

where $\Delta^*'$ is the pseudo diagonal of pairs of spaces pseudo product of pairs of spaces
