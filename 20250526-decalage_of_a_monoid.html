decalage of a simplicial object

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathrm{s}\mathcal{C} = \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{C})$ the infinity category of simplicial objects The decalage functor is defined as precomposition with the addition endofunctor of the simplex category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dec}: \mathrm{s}\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{s}\mathcal{C} \end{align*}
