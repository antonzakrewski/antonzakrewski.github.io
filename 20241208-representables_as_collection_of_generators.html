representables as collection of generators

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a locally small infinity category, $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ be the infinity presheaf category. Then the colleciton of representable functors is a collection of generators in $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$

Proof

follows from the contravariant infinity yoneda lemma, where

  1. $\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,c), \mathcal{F}) \cong \mathcal{F}(C)$
