generator and coproduct implies epimorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $P$ a generator, $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ an object such that

\begin{align*} \coprod_{f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(P,A)} P \end{align*}

exists.

Then there exists a canonical epimorphism

\begin{align*} \coprod_{f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(P,A)} P \rightarrow A \end{align*}

Proof

Consider

Let $f,g: A \rightarrow B$ be morphisms with $f \neq g$. Then by assumption there exists a morphism

\begin{align*} h: P \rightarrow A \end{align*}

such that $f \circ h \neq g \circ h$

Thus 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
P_f \arrow[shift right=1, swap]{rrrd}{f \circ h} \arrow[shift left=1]{rrrrd}{g \circ h} \arrow[]{rrd}{f}& &  \\
 & \coprod P \arrow[]{r}{}& A \arrow[shift right=1, swap]{r}{f} \arrow[shift left=1]{r}{g}& B
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

does not commute

Hence the induced mophsim

\begin{align*} \coprod_{f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(P,A)} P \rightarrow A \end{align*}

is an epi
