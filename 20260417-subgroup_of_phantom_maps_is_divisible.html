subgroup of phantom maps is divisible for a spectrum with finitely generated homotopy groups

Proposition

Let $Z$ be a spectrum with finitely generated abelian groups. Let $X$ be another spectrum, then the subgroup

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Phantom}(X,Z) \subseteq \pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X,Y)) \end{align*}

is a divisible group.

Proof
