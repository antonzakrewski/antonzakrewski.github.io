monomorphism in the coslice category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $x$ an object in $\mathcal{C}$ and $\mathcal{C}_{x/}$ the coslice category Then a map in $\mathcal{C}_{/x}$ is a monomorphism if and only if it is an monomorphism in $\mathcal{C}$.
