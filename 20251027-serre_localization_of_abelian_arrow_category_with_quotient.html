serre localization of abelian arrow category with quotient

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A} \subseteq \mathcal{B}$ be an abelian subcategory which is closed under quotient objects, subobject and where each object in $B$ is the extension of objects in $\mathcal{A}$. Let $\mathrm{Fun}_{\mathcal{A}}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{B})$ be the abelian arrow category.

Let $\mathcal{A} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Fun}_{\mathcal{A}}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{B})$ be the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathcal{A} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Fun}_{\mathcal{A}}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{B}) \\ A \mapsto (A,0) \end{align*}

Then the sequence

is a serre quotient (even bifiber sequence).

Proof

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be any other abelian category and $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Fun}_{\mathcal{A}}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{B}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ an exact functor

Then define

\begin{align*} \tilde{\mathcal{F}}: \mathcal{B} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} b \mapsto \mathcal{F}((0,b)) \end{align*}

It follows that the diagarm above commutes (up to natural equivalence): Given $a \rightarrow b$ we have an exact sequence

where by assumption the top part lies in the essential image of the kernel.
