yoneda embedding reflects exactness

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category. Then the yoneda embedding reflects exactness, i.e. a sequence

is exact at $B$ is exact if for all $M$

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(M,A) \arrow[]{r}{}& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(M,B) \arrow[]{r}{}& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(M,C) \end{align*}
