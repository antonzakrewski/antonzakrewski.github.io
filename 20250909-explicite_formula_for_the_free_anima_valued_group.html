explicit description for the free anima valued group

Proposition

Let

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ev}_1: \mathrm{Grp}(\mathrm{An}) \rightarrow \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

be the evaluation functor.

Then the left adjoint to $\mathrm{ev}_1$ exists (cf. infinite free group functor) and is given by

\begin{align*} \Omega (\Sigma (-)_+) \end{align*}

Proof

use

  1. recognition principle for connective spectra and commutative groups
  2. pointification functor for Anima
  3. loop suspension adjunction for a pointed infinity category
