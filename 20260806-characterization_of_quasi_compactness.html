characterization of quasi-compactness

Proposition

Lte $X$ be an algebraic functor TFAE:

  1. $X$ is quasi-compact,
  2. the terminal morphism $X \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$ is a quasi-compact morphism,
  3. there exists a zariski-local epimorphism $\coprod_{i=1}^n \mathrm{Spec}(R_i) \rightarrow X$ from a finite coproduct of affine schemes $\mathrm{Spec}(R_i)$.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

observe that the product is a pullback into the terminal object, i.e. we need to show that the pullback $Z \times X$ is quasi-compact for any quasi-compact $Z$ This was done in finite product of quasi-compact algebraic functors

2) $\implies$ 3)

3) $\implies$ 1)
