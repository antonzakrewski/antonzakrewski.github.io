localizing subcategory

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, $\mathcal{S}$ a serre subcategory and $\mathcal{A}/\mathcal{S}$ the serre quotient Then $\mathcal{S}$ is said to be localizing, if the quotient

\begin{align*} \pi: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{A}/\mathcal{S} \end{align*}
