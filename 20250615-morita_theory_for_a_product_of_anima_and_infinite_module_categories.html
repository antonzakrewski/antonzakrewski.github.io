morita theory for a product of anima and infinite module categories

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be a anima, $R$ a commutative ring spectrum. Then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(X \times Y, \mathrm{Mod}(R)) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}^{L}(\mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{Mod}(R)), \mathrm{Fun}(Y, \mathrm{Mod}(R)))\\ \mathcal{F} \rightarrow& (\mathcal{G} \mapsto (y \mapsto \mathrm{colim}( \mathcal{G}(-) \otimes \mathcal{F}(-,y))) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from morita theory of infinite module categories and cat infinity as cartesian closed category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(X \times Y, \mathrm{Mod}(R)) \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(Y \times X, \mathrm{Mod}(R)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(Y, \mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{Mod}(R)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(Y, \mathrm{Fun}^L(\mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{Mod}(R)), \mathrm{Mod}(R))) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{/, L}(Y \times \mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{Mod}(R)), \mathrm{Mod}(R)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{L}(\mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{Mod}(R)), \mathrm{Fun}(Y, \mathrm{Mod}(R))) \end{align*}

where we have used currying of functors preserving limits / colimits to get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(Y, \mathrm{Fun}^L(X,-)) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{/,L}}(Y \times X, -) \end{align*}

Here the RHS denotes functors which preserve colimits in the second variable.

here tracing the maps for $\mathcal{F}(-,-) \in \mathrm{Fun}(X \times Y, \mathrm{Mod}(R))

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(-,-) \mapsto& (y \mapsto \mathcal{F}(-,y)) \\ \mapsto& (y \mapsto (\mathcal{G} \mapsto \mathrm{colim}_X(\mathcal{G}(-) \otimes \mathcal{F}(-,y))) \\ \mapsto& (y,\mathcal{G} \mapsto \mathrm{colim}_X(\mathcal{G}(-) \otimes \mathcal{F}(-,y))) \\ \mapsto& (\mathcal{G} \mapsto (y \mapsto \mathrm{colim}_X (\mathcal{G}(-) \otimes \mathcal{F}(-,y))) \end{align*}
