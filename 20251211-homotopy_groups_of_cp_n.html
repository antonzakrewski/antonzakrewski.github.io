homotopy groups of CP^n

Proposition

Let $n\geq 1$, then the homotopy groups of $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n$ are given by

\begin{align*} \pi_*(\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n) = \begin{cases} 0 & \mbox{if } * \leq 1 \\ \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } * = 2 \\ \pi_{*}(S^{2n+1}) & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from the long exact sequence of homotopy groups of

\begin{align*} S^{1} \rightarrow S^{2n+1} \rightarrow \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n \end{align*}
