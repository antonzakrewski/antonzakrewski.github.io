existence of non equivalent, simply connected anima with equivalent truncations

Proposition

There exist non equivalent simply connected anima $X,X'$ such that for each $n$, there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \tau_{\leq n} X \simeq \tau_{\leq n} X' \end{align*}

Proof

Recall that the 2-sphere $S^2$ has infinitely many, nontrivial homotopy groups (cf. some p-local homotopy groups of a sphere, possibly using the fiber sequence $S^1 \rightarrow S^3 \rightarrow S^2$). We claim that $X = \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \tau_{\leq n} S^2$ and $X' = X \times S^2$ suffice:

equivalent truncations

Recall that the truncation functor $\tau_{\leq n}$ preserve colimits and products, which shows

\begin{align*} \tau_{\leq n} X \simeq& \bigoplus_{m \leq n} \tau_{\leq m} S^2 \oplus \bigoplus_{m > n} \tau_{\leq n} S^2 \\ \tau_{\leq n} (X \times S^2) \simeq& \bigoplus_{m \leq n} \tau_{\leq m} \mathbb{S} \oplus \bigoplus_{m > n} \tau_{\leq n} S^2 \oplus \tau_{\leq n} S^2 \end{align*}

where some indexing shift of $\bigoplus_{m > n} \tau_{\leq n} S^2$ shows that $\bigoplus_{m > n} \tau_{\leq n} S^2$ and $\bigoplus_{m > n} \tau_{\leq n} S^2 \oplus \tau_{\leq n} S^2$ are equivalent. Therefore, $\tau_{\leq n} X \simeq \tau_{\leq n} X'$ are also equivalent.

nonequivalent spectra

observe that $S^2$ is a retract of $X \times S^2$. But we claim that $S^2$ is not the retract of $\bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \tau_{\leq n} S^2$:

Here we can write $\bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \tau_{\leq n} S^2 \simeq \mathrm{colim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \bigoplus_{m \leq n} \tau_{\leq m} S^2$ so any map $S^2 \rightarrow X$ factors through some finite sum $\bigoplus_{m \leq n} \tau_{\leq m} S^2$ as $S^2$ is a compact anima (cf. compact anima as retract of finite anima). Suppose there exists a split mono $f: S^2 \rightarrow X$, which factors through some finite sum $\bigoplus_{m \leq n} \tau_{\leq m} S^2$. Moreover, the map $f: S^2 \rightarrow \bigoplus_{m \leq n} \tau_{\leq m} S^2$ is still a split mono, since the composition $S^2 \rightarrow \bigoplus_{m \leq n} \tau_{\leq m} S^2 \rightarrow X \rightarrow S^2$ would be the identity.

This is a contradiction to the statement, that finite, nontrivial spectrum has infinitely many nontrivial homotopy groups (or homotopy group of the sphere spectrum nonzero in infinitely many degrees)
