module over the zero ring is zero module

Proposition

Let $0$ be the zero ring and $M_1,M_2$ modules over $0$. Then

\begin{align*} M_1 \cong M_2 \end{align*}

Proof

By Assumption, it holds that for $m \in M_i$

\begin{align*} m =& 1 \cdot m \\ =& 0 \cdot m \\ =& 0 \end{align*}

Hence $M_i = \{0\}$
