free R-module spectrum functor induces left adjoint on E_n Algebras

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring spectrum. Then the free R-module spectrum functor induces a left adjoint

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_n}(\mathrm{An}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbb{E}_n}(\mathrm{Mod}_{R}) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • free R-module spectrum functor
  • free R-module spectrum functor is symmetric monoidal to module spectra
  • free R-module spectrum functor
