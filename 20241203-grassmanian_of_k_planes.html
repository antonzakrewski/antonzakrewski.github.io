grassmanian of k-planes

Definition

Let $\mathbb{K} \in \{\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}, \mathbb{H}\}$ and $k,n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $k \leq n$. Then the grassmanian of $k$ planes in $n$ is defined as topological space

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Gr}_k(\mathbb{K}^n) = \{k-\mathrm{dimensional subspaces}\} \end{align*}

with the quotient topology of

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fr}_k(\mathbb{K}^n) \rightarrow \mathrm{Gr}_k(\mathbb{K}^nr \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fr}_k(\mathbb{K}^n) \subseteq \mathbb{K}^{n \cdot k} \end{align*}

consists of the subspace of linearly independent subspaces.
