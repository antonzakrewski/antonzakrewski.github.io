enriched natural transformation

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a monoidal category, $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be \(\mathcal{V}\)-enriched categories and $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}$ enriched functors Then an enriched natural transformatinn $\eta$ is defined as a family of morphisms

\begin{align*} \eta_C: \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}(\mathcal{F}(c), \mathcal{G}(c)) \end{align*}

for $c \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ such that for any pair $c,c' \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ the following diagram commutes 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
& \mathcal{C}(c,c') \otimes \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V} & \mathcal{D}(\mathcal{G}(c), \mathcal{G}(c')) \otimes \mathcal{D}(\mathcal{F}(c), \mathcal{G}(c)) & \\ 
\mathcal{C}(c,c') & & & \mathcal{D}(\mathcal{F}(c), \mathcal{G}(c')) \\
& \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V} \otimes  \mathcal{C}(c,c') & \mathcal{D}(\mathcal{F}(c'), \mathcal{G}(c')) \otimes \mathcal{D}(\mathcal{F}(c), \mathcal{F}(c')) & \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
