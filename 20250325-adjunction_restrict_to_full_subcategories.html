infinity adjunctions restrict to full subcategories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{R}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be infinity functors forming an infinity adjunction $\mathcal{L} \dashv \mathcal{R}$. Suppose $\mathcal{C}_0 \subseteq \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}_0 \subseteq \mathcal{D}$ are full sub infinity categories such that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}_{\vert \mathcal{C}_0}: \mathcal{C}_0 \rightarrow& \mathcal{D}_0 \\ \mathcal{R}_{\vert \mathcal{D}_0}: \mathcal{D}_0 \rightarrow& \mathcal{C}_0 \end{align*}

restrict appropriately.

Then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}_{\vert \mathcal{C}_0} \dashv \mathcal{R}_{\vert \mathcal{D}_0} \end{align*}

form an adjunction

Proof

follows from

  • infinity left adjoint and objectwise representable
  • infinity right adjoint and objectwise representable
