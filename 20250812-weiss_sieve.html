weiss sieve

Definition

Let $T$ be a topological space, then a sieve $R \subseteq よ(O)$ for on open subset $O \in \mathrm{Open}(T)$ is said to be a weiss sieve, if any subset $A \subseteq O$ - which is in general not open - with $\vert A \vert < \kappa$ is contained in some $U$ with $R(U) \neq \emptyset$.
