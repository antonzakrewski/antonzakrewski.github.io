exercise for approximate unit in l2

Proposition

Consider $l^2$ with the orthonormal basis $e_k$ and $p_n$ the projection onto $\mathrm{span}(e_1,...,e_n)$. Then

  1. $(p_n)$ is an approximate unit for $\mathbb{K}(H)$
  2. $(p_n)$ is not an approximate unit for $\mathbb{B}(H)$
  3. $p_n \rightarrow 1$ strongly convergent
  4. recall $\mathbb{B}(H) \simeq M(\mathbb{K}(H))$ (cf. multiplier algebra of the compact operators). Then show that in the strict topology, for every $T \in \mathbb{B}(H)$, we have that $T p_n \rightarrow T$ and $p_n \rightarrow T$.

Proof

1)

finite rank operator

for a finite rank operator $T: l^2 \rightarrow l^2$, there exists an $k >> 0$ such that $\mathrm{im}(l^2) \subseteq \mathrm{span}(e_0,...,e_k)$. Hence $p_{k'} T = T$ for $k' \geq k$.

general case

an $\frac{\varepsilon}{n}$ argument works

2)

Consider $\mathrm{id}$. Then $\| \mathrm{id} - p_k \| = 1$ since $( \mathrm{id} - p_k)(e_{k+1}) = e_{k+1}$ which shows that $p_k \mathrm{id} \not\rightarrow \mathrm{id}$.

3)

Let $(x_n) \in l^2$, in particular $\sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \vert x_n \vert^2 < \infty$ or $\lim_{k \rightarrow \infty} \sum_{n \geq k} \vert x_n \vert^2 = 0$. Then

\begin{align*} \| \mathrm{id}((x_n)) - p_k((x_n)) \| = \sum_{n \geq k} \vert x_n \vert^2 \xrightarrow{k \rightarrow \infty} 0 \end{align*}

4)

recall that the compact operators form a two sided ideal: Let $k \in \mathbb{K}(H)$ be a compact operator.

a) $T p_n \rightarrow T$

Then $k T p_n \rightarrow k T \cdot P$ since $p_n$ is an approximate unit for $\mathbb{K}(H)$ and $(k T)$ lies in $\mathbb{K}(H)$. Similarly, $T p_n k \rightarrow T k$ since $p_n k \rightarrow k$ and multiplication with $T$ preserves convergent sequences.

b) $p_n T \rightarrow T$

Then $k p_n T \rightarrow k T$ since $k p_n \rightarrow k$ and multiplication with $T$ preserves convergent sequences. Similarly, $p_n T k \rightarrow Tk$ since $(Tk)$ is a compact operator.
