smash product functor

Definition

Given category Top*, the smash product functor is defined as covariant functor

\begin{align*} (- \wedge -): \mathrm{Top}_* \times \mathrm{Top}_* \rightarrow \mathrm{Top}_* \\ (X,x_0), (Y,y_0) \mapsto& (X \wedge Y) \\ ((f: X_0 \rightarrow X_1), (g: Y_0 \rightarrow Y_1)) \mapsto& (f \wedge g: X_0 \wedge Y_0 \rightarrow X_1 \wedge Y_1) \\ \end{align*}
