external product on topological k0

Definition

Let $X,Y$ be compact hausdorff. Then the external product is defined as

\begin{align*} K_{\mathbb{K}}^{0}(X) \otimes K_{\mathbb{K}^0(Y) \rightarrow& K_{\mathbb{K}}^0(X \times Y) [E_1] \otimes [E_2] \mapsto& [p_1^*(E_1) \otimes p_2^*(E_2)] \end{align*}
