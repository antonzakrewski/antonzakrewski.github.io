functor induces natural transformation for hom functors

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor Then there exists a natural transformation for the hom-functor and the postcomposition of a functor for the hom functor

\begin{align*} \eta: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,-) \rightarrow& \mathcal{F}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(-,-)) \\ (A,B) \mapsto& \begin{cases} \mathcal{R}(-): \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,B) &\rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(A), \mathcal{F}(B)) \\ f &\mapsto \mathcal{R}(f) \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

by element chasing we show, that

commutes

Let $h \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,B)$, then it follows, that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{R}(g)^{ \rightarrow} \mathcal{R}(f)^{ \leftarrow} \circ \mathcal{}\mathcal{R}(h) =& \mathcal{R}(f) \circ \mathcal{R}(h) \circ \mathcal{R}(g) \\ =& \mathcal{R}(f \circ h \circ g) \\ \end{align*}
