tensor product of bounded above chain complexes as bounded chain complex

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod})$ be the category of chain complexes. Suppose $M_*,N_*$ are bounded above chain complexes, then so is the tensor product of chain complexes

\begin{align*} M_* \otimes_R N_* \end{align*}

Proof

follows from the definition of a tensor product, as for sufficiently large $n$ it holds that

\begin{align*} (M_* \otimes_R N_*)_n =& \bigoplus_{p + q = n} M_p \otimes_R N_q \\ =& \bigoplus 0 =& 0 \end{align*}
