precomposition with an infinity adjoint as adjoint on functor categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $\mathcal{L} \dashv \mathcal{R}$ an infinity adjunction Then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}^{*} \vdash \mathcal{R}^* \end{align*}

is an adjunction on infinity functor categories $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{E}), \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E})$
