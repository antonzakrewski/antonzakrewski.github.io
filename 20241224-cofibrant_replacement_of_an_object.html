cofibrant replacement of an object

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}, \mathrm{Cof}, \mathrm{Fib})$ be a model category Then for $X \in \mathcal{C}$ a cofibrant replacement is defined as cofibrant object $QX$ with a weak equivalence

\begin{align*} QX \rightarrow X \end{align*}

Existence

Note that there always exists one, since we may factorize 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\emptyset \arrow[]{rr}{} \arrow[rightarrowtail, ]{rd}{} & & X \\
& QX \arrow[dashed]{rd}{\simeq}& 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
