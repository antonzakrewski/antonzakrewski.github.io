zariski tangent space of a union of closed subschemes is in general not the span

Proposition

Let $Z_1,Z_2 \subseteq X$ be closed subschemes. Then the zariski tangent space of the union (in the category of schemes) $Z_1 \cup Z_2$ is in general not the span of the zariski tangent spaces of $Z_1,Z_2 \subseteq X$.

Proof

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field and consider $\mathrm{Spec}(k[x,y]/(y - x^2)), \mathrm{Spec}(k[x,y]/(y)) \subseteq \mathrm{Spec}(k[x,y])$. Then we have the intersection

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Spec}(k[x,y]/(y - x^2)) \cap \mathrm{Spec}(k[x,y]/(y)) \cong& \mathrm{Spec}(k[x,y]/(y - x^2, y)) \\ \cong& \mathrm{Spec}(k[x,y]/(y,x^2) \end{align*}

and the union is classified by the ideal $(y) \cap (y - x^2) = (y^2 - yx^2)$.

Then the zariski tangent space of $\mathrm{Spec}(k[x,y]/(y - x^2)), \mathrm{Spec}(k[x,y]/(y))$ at the point $(0,0)$ is given by the $k$ vector spaces $(y,x)/(y^2,x^2,yx) \subseteq k[x,y]/(y - x^2), (y,x)/(y^2,x^2,yx) \subseteq k[x,y]/(y - x^2)$ and hence both are isomorphic to $(y)$. but the zariski tangent space of $\mathrm{Spec}(k[x,y]/(y^2 - yx^2)$ is isomorphic to $(x,y)/(x^2,y^2) \subseteq k[x,y]/(y^2,yx^2)$ and hence isomorphic to $(x,y)$
