suspension isomorphism of singular cohomology

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Top}_*$ be the category Top*, $\Sigma$ the suspension functor Then there exists a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} H^n(\Sigma X, N;M) \cong H^{n+1}(X, x; M) \end{align*}

where $N$ is the north pole of the suspension $\Sigma X$

Proof
