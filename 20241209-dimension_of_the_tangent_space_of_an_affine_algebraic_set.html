dimension of the tangent space of an affine algebraic set

Definition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be an algebraic set Then for $P \in X$, the dimension of the tangent space is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}_{k}(T_P(X)) \leq n \end{align*}
