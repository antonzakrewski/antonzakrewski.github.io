characterization of quasi-separatedness

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor TFAE:

  1. $X$ is quasi-separated
  2. the terminal morphism $X \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$ is a quasi-separated morphisms
  3. for all maps $Y \rightarrow X, Z \rightarrow X$ where $Y$ and $Z$ are affine schemes, $Y \times_{X} Z$ is quasi-compact

Proof
