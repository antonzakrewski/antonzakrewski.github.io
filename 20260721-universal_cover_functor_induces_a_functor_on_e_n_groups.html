universal cover functor induces a functor on E_n groups

Proposition

The universal cover functor induces a functor

\begin{align*} \tilde{(-)}: \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_n}(\mathrm{An}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_n}(\mathrm{An}) \end{align*}

Proof

  • universal cover functor is pointed
  • universal cover functor is product preserving
  • product preserving functor induces functor on cartesian groups
