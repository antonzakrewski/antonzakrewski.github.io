postcomposition with right adjoint on cartesian commutative groups inherits lax symmetric monoidal structure

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}$ be closed symmetric monoidal infinity categories and $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a symmetric monoidal left adjoint with right adjoint $\mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$. Then postcomposition

\begin{align*} \mathcal{R}_*: \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathrm{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathrm{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

inherits a lax symmetric monoidal structure
