cup product pairing non degenerate

Proposition

Let $R$ be an Integral domain, $X$ a closed, connected Manifold with an R-Orientation \sigma$. Then the cup product pairing

is non degenerate

Proof

consider

H^p(X;R) \otimes H_p(X;R) \arrow[]{r}{\mathrm{ev}}& H_0(X;R) \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
