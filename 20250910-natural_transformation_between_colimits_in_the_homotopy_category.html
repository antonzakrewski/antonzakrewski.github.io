natural transformation between limits/colimits in the homotopy category

Proposition

Suppose $I_1,I_2: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ are diagrams in a category and $\eta: I_1 \Rightarrow I_2$ a natural transformation. Let $\mathrm{colim} I_1, \mathrm{colim} I_2$ be the colimits. Let $h(\eta)$ be the natural transformation in the homotopy category.

Then the there exist in general multiple morphisms $\mathrm{colim} I_1 \Rightarrow \mathrm{colim} I_2$ which form a commutative diagram
