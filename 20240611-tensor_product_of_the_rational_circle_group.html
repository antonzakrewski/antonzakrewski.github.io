tensor product of the rational circle group

Proposition

The tensor product of the rational circle group is given by the zero group

Proof

Given

\begin{align*} (\frac{p_1}{q_1} \otimes \frac{p_2}{q_2}) =& ( \frac{p_1 q_2}{q_1 q_2} \otimes \frac{p_2}{q_2}) \\ =& q_2 ( \frac{p_1}{q_1 q_2} \otimes \frac{p_2}{q_2} ) \\ =& ( \frac{p_1}{q_1 q_2} \otimes p_2) \\ =& ( \frac{p_1}{q_1 q_2} \otimes 0) \\ =& 0 ( \frac{p_1}{q_1 q_2} \otimes 0) \\ =& 0 \end{align*}
