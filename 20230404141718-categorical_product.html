categorical product

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $(X_i)_{i \in I}$ a set of objects, then the categorical product is - if it exists - an object

\begin{align*} \prod_{i \in I} X_i \in \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

with morphisms

\begin{align*} \pi_{i \in I}: \left(\prod_{i \in I} X_i \right) \rightarrow X_i \end{align*}

such that for every diagram

there exists a unique morphism $f_i$, making the diagram commute
