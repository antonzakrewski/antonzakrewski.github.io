finite type k-algebra as field and finite field extension

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $A$ a K-Algebra. TFAE:

  1. $A$ is a finite field extension
  2. $A$ is of finite type and a field

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

corollary of algebra finitely generated as module and finite type algebra
