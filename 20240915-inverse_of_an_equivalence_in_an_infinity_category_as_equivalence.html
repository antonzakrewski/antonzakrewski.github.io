inverse of an equivalence in an infinity category as equivalence

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\psi \in \mathcal{C}_1$ a morphism. Suppose $\psi$ is an equivalence with right inverse $\varphi$.

Then $\varphi$ is also an equivalence

Proof

follows: from:

  • left inverse of an isomorphism as right inverse
  • after applying the homotopy category functor
