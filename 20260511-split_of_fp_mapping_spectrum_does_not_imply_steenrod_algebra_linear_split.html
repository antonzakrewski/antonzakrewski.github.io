split of Fp-mapping spectrum does not imply Steenrod-Algebra-linear split

Proposition

Let $X \rightarrow Y$ be a map of spectra, such that $\mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(Y,\mathbb{F}_2) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(X, \mathbb{F}_2)$ splits. Then this splitting is in general not linear over the Fp steenrod algebra, i.e. any split

\begin{align*} \pi_*(\mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(X,\mathbb{F}_2)) \rightarrow \pi_*(\mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(Y, \mathbb{F}_2)) \end{align*}

is in general not linear over the Fp steenrod algebra.

Proof

consider the sequence

\begin{align*} S^1 \xrightarrow{\cdot 2} S^1 \rightarrow \mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^2 \end{align*}

resp.

\begin{align*} \mathbb{S} \xrightarrow{\cdot 2} \mathbb{S} \rightarrow \mathbb{S}/2 \end{align*}

But this sequence induces a spilt sequence:

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}/2, \mathbb{F}_2) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}, \mathbb{F}_2) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}, \mathbb{F}_2) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(-,-)$ is additive in both variables, so precomposition with multiplication by $2$ coincides with postcomposition with multiplication by $2$. But the map $\mathbb{F}_2 \xrightarrow{\cdot 2} \mathbb{F}_2$ coincides with the zero map. But this shows that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}, \mathbb{F}_2) \xrightarrow{0} \mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}, \mathbb{F}_2) \end{align*}

is the zero map or equivalenty that the fiber is given by $\mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}, \mathbb{F}_2) \oplus \mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}, \mathbb{F}_2)[-1]$, i.e., $\mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}/2, \mathbb{F}_2) \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}, \mathbb{F}_2) \oplus \mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}, \mathbb{F}_2)[-1]$. This shows, that the map $\mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}/2, \mathbb{F}_2) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}, \mathbb{F}_2)$ is a split epi with splitting given by the inclusion (under the equivalence above)

On the other hand, the steenrod operations on $\mathbb{S}$ is trivial, but not on $\mathbb{S}/2$ (cf. Sq^1 steenrod square of M(F2,n)).
