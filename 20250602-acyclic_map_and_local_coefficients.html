acyclic map and local coefficients

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a map of anima. TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an acyclic map
  2. for every local system $\mathcal{L}$ on $Y$ the map on homology with local coefficients is an isomorphism
\begin{align*} f_*: H_*(X, f^*(\mathcal{L})) \rightarrow H_*(Y, \mathcal{L}) \end{align*}

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
