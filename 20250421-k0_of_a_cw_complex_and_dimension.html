K0 of a cw complex and dimension

Proposition

Let $X$ be a CW-Complex with $\mathrm{dim}(X) = n$, $\mathbb{K} \in {\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}, \mathbb{H}}$ Then the topological k0 $K^{0}_{\mathbb{K}}(X)$ is generated by topological vector bundles of dimension

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim} < n / \mathrm{dim}(\mathbb{K}) \end{align*}

and possibly the trivial bundle of dimension $1$

Proof

follows from:
