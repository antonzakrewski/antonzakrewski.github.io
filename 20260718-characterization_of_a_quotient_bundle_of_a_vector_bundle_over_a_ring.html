characterization of a quotient bundle of a vector bundle over a ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $V,W$ be vector bundles over $R$ and $f: V \rightarrow W$ a map . TFAE:

  1. $W$ is a quotient bundle
  2. $f$ is surjective
  3. for every R-field $k$, the map $f \otimes_R k$ is surjective
  4. $f$ is surjective and the kernel is a is a vector bundle
  5. $f$ is a split epi
  6. the dual map $f^{\vee}$ is a universally injective map

Proof

1) $\equiv$ 2)

equivalent

2) $\implies$ 3)

special case

3) $\implies$ 4)

Observe that $\mathrm{cofib}(f)$ is finitely generated module and $\mathrm{cofib}(f \otimes k)$ vanishes for each field, hence by Nakayama Lemma for Jacobson Radical $\mathfrak{m} \mathrm{cofib}(f) = \mathrm{cofib}(f)$. Thus $\mathrm{cofib}(f) = 0$.

4) $\implies$ 5)

Splitting Lemma

5) $\implies$ 6)

functor preserves split epi

6) $\implies$ 1)

follows from some internal hom out of a dualizable object & precomposition with a surjection as injection
