Proposition

Let $A$ be a nonunital star algebra $A^+ = \{a^* a \vert a \in A\}$.

Proof

a)

we have $a = (\sqrt{a})^2$ where $\sqrt{a}$ is self adjoint.

b)

Let $b = a^* a$ and $b = b_+ - b_-$ for $b_{\pm} = \frac{\vert b \vert \pm b}{2}$. Let $c = a b_-$ Then

\begin{align*} c^* c =& b_{-} a^* a b_{-} \\ =& b_{-} (b_{+} b_{-}) b_{-} \\ =& - (b_{-})^3 \leq 0 \end{align*}
