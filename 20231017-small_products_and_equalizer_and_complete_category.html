small products and equalizer and complete category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ is complete
  2. there exist small products and equalizers

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

special case, see

  • discrete limit as product
  • definition of an equalizer

2) $\implies$ 1)

product and equalizer imply existence of a limit
