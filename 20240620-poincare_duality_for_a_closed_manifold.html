poincare duality for a closed orientable manifold

Proposition

Let \(M\) be a closed \(n\)-dimensional manifold and $\sigma_{X} \in H_{n}^{\mathrm{lf}}(X;R)$ be an R-Orientation Then

\begin{align*} H^p(X;M) \rightarrow H_{n-p}(X;M) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism

Proof

  • poincare duality
  • cofinal morphism of posets and colimit
