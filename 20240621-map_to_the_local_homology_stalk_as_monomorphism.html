map to the orientation stalk as monomorphism for a connected manifold

Proposition

Let \(M\) be an \(n\)-dimensional connected manifold, $m \in M$ and $R$ a ring Then the map

\begin{align*} H_n(M,R) \rightarrow H_n(M, M \setminus \{m\};R) \end{align*}

for the local singular homology of the orientation sheaf is a monomorphism

Proof

follows from the long exact sequence of homology of a pair of spaces

where $H_n(M \setminus \{m\};R) \cong 0$ follows from

  • punctured manifold not compact
  • homology of a manifold without compact component
