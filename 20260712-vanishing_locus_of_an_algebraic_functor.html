vanishing locus of an algebraic functor

Definition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor and $F \subseteq \mathcal{O}(X)$ a set of functions on $X$. Then the vanishing locus of $F$ is the subfunctor $V(F) \subseteq X$ given by

\begin{align*} V(F)(R) = \{x \in X(R) \vert f(x) = 0 \forall f \in F\} \end{align*}
