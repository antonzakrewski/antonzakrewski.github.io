finitely generated k-algebra contains only finitely many maximal ideals

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $B$ an finitely generated K-Algebra Then

Proof

Let $\mathfrak{m}_1,...,\mathfrak{m}_r$ be maximal ideals. Then

\begin{align*} \pi: B \rightarrow \prod_{i=1}^n B/\mathfrak{m}_1 \end{align*}

is a surjection by the general chinese remainder theorem

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}_k(B) \geq& \sum_{i=1}^r \mathrm{dim}_{k}(B/\mathfrak{M}_i) \geq n \end{align*}

Hence $B$ contains at most $\mathrm{dim}_k(B)$ many maximal ideals.
