Going down theorem

Proposition

Let $A \subseteq B$ be rings such that $B$ is integral over $A$ and $\mathfrak{p}_1 \subseteq ... \subseteq \mathfrak{p}_m ... \subseteq \mathfrak{p}_n \subseteq A$ be an ascending chain of prime ideals in $A$.

If there are prime ideals $\mathfrak{q}_{m+1} \subseteq ... \subseteq \mathfrak{q}_{n} \subseteq B$ such that $\mathfrak{p}_i = \mathfrak{q}_i \cap A$, then there are prime ideals

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{q}_{1} \subseteq ... \subseteq \mathfrak{q}_m \subseteq B \end{align*}

with $\mathfrak{p}_i = \mathfrak{q}_i \cap A$

Proof
