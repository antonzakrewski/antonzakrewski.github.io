Tensor functor from Ab to RMod

Definition

Let \(R\) be a ring, then the tensor functor is defined as functor from Category Ab to category RMod

\begin{align*} R \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} -: \mathrm{Ab} \rightarrow& \mathrm{RMod} \\ A \mapsto& R \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} A \end{align*}
