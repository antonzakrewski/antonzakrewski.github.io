profinite completion

Definition / Proposition

Let $G$ be a group. Then the profinite completion is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_{\Gamma \hookrightarrow G} G/\Gamma \in \mathrm{Grp}(\mathrm{Top}) \end{align*}

in the category of topological groups where

  1. $G$ is regarded as discrete topological group
  2. $\Gamma \hookrightarrow G$ denotes the partially ordered set containing normal, finite index subgroups

Proof

note that the poset is cofiltered by intersection of normal subgroups with finite index as normal subgroup with finite index
