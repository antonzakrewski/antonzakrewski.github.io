generalized cw complex

Definition

Let $X$ be a topological space. Then $X$ admits a generalized cw structure, if there exists a filtration $(X, Y, \mathrm{SK}_n(X))$ such that

  1. $\mathrm{Sk}_{-1}(X) = Y$ (?)
  2. $\mathrm{Sk}_{n}(X) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Sk}_{n+1}(X)$ is a relative cw complex
  3. $X \cong \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{Sk}_n(X)$
