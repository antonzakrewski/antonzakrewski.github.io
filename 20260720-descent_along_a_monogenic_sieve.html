descent along a monogenic sieve

Proposition

Let $R \subseteq X$ be a monogenic sieve for $Y \rightarrow X$ and $\mathcal{F}$ a 1-categorical presheaf Suppose that the pullback $Y \times_{X} Y$ exists in $\mathcal{C}$.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ satisfies descent along $R$
  2. the following diagram is an equalizer
\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(X) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(Y) \rightrightarrows \mathcal{F}(Y \times_{X} Y) \end{align*}

Proof

Use that 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
よ(Y \times_{X} Y) \rar \dar & よ(Y) \dar \\
よ(Y) \rar & R
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

exhibits $R$ as the colimit. Then use that $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(-,\mathcal{F})$ preserves limits
