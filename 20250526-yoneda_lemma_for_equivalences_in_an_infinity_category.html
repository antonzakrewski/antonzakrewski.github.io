weak yoneda lemma for the homotopy category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category and $f: c \rightarrow d$ a morphism.

TFAE:

  1. postcomposition $f^*: \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,c) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,d)$ is an equivalence
  2. precomposition $f_*: \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(d,-) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(c,-)$ is an isomorphism
  3. postcomposition $\pi_0(f^*): \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,c)) \rightarrow \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,d))$ is an isomorphism
  4. precomposition $\pi_0(f_*): (\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(d,-) \rightarrow \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(c,-)$ is an isomorphism
  5. $f$ is an equivalence

proof

1) $\implies$ 3)

each step is fully functorial

2) $\implies$ 4)

  • weak infinity yoneda lemma

5) $\implies$ 1)
