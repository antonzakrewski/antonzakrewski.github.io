kappa filtered category and order of cardinals

Proposition

Let $\kappa < \kappa'$ be (regular) cardinals, then each $\kappa'$ filtered category is a $\kappa$ filtered category.

Proof

Given a collection of less than $\kappa$ many objects, it suffices to show that there exists a cone. But by assumption each collection of less than $\kappa'$ many objects admit a cone, hence by transitivity also each collection of less than $\kappa$ many objects.
