category of triples of spaces

Definition

The category of triples of spaces is defined as category containing

  1. triples of topological spaces $(X,A,B)$ where $A \subseteq X,B \subseteq X$
  2. $\mathrm{Hom}((X_1,A_1,B_1), (X_2,A_2,B_2))$ contains continuous maps $f: X_1 \rightarrow X_2$ such that
\begin{align*} f[A_1] \subseteq& A_2 \\ f[B_1] \subseteq& B_2 \\ \end{align*}
