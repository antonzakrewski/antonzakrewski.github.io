Mengenalgebra als Mengenring mit der ganzen Menge

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{R} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)$ be a family of sets TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{R}$ is a ring of sets and $X \in \mathcal{R}$
  2. $\mathcal{R}$ is an algebra of sets

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

note that by definition $X \in \mathcal{R}$. Then it is also closed under absolute complements, as it is closed under relative complements and $X \in \mathcal{R}$

2) $\implies$ 1)

algebra of sets as ring of sets and $X \in \mathcal{R}$ is there by assumption.
