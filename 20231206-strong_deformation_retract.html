strong deformation retract

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space and $A \subseteq X$ a subspace.

Then $A$ is said to be a strong deformaiton retract, if there exists a homotopy relative to $\mathrm{pr}_1: A \times [0,1] \rightarrow A$ from $\mathrm{id}_{X}$ to a retraction $f: X \rightarrow A$
