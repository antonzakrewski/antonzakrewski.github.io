projective scheme

Definition

An algebraic functor $X: \mathrm{CAlg}_k \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ is a projective k-scheme for an commutative ring $k$ if it is isomorphic to $\mathrm{hSol}_{\Sigma}$ for some system of homogeneous polynomial equations $\Sigma$ in finitely many variables over $k$
