forgetful functor from the slice category

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $X \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ an object and $\mathcal{C} \downarrow X$ the slice category. Then there exists the canonical forgetful functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \uparrow X \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ (p: A \rightarrow X) \mapsto& A \\ (f: (A \rightarrow X) \mapsto& (B \rightarrow X)) \mapsto& (f: A \rightarrow B) \end{align*}
