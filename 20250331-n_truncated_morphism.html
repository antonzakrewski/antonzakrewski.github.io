Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a morphism. Then $f$ is said to be \(n\)-truncated, if

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(Z,X) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(Z,Y) \end{align*}

is n-truncated for any $Z$
