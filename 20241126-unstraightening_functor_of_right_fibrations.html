unstraightening functor of right fibrations

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category Then unstraightening functor of right fibrations is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{unstraight}_{\mathrm{RFib}}: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An}) \rightarrow \mathrm{RFib}(B) \end{align*}

by restricting to $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An}) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty})$
