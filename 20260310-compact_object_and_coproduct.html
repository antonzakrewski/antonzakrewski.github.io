compact object and coproduct

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal and $x_i$ objects for $i \in I$. Suppose $\coprod_{j \in J} x_j$ exists for each subset $J \subseteq I$ and $y \rightarrow \coprod_i x_i$ is a morphism from a kappa compact object $y$. Then there exists a $\kappa$ small set $J$ such that

\begin{align*} y \rightarrow \coprod_{j \in J} x_j \hookrightarrow \coprod_{I \in I} x_i \end{align*}
