jacobson radical and simple module

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $x \in R$. TFAE:

  1. $x \in \mathrm{Jac}(R)$
  2. $Mx = 0$ for all simple modules (right modules)

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $\mathfrak{m} \subseteq R$ be a maximal ideal. Then the quotient $R/\mathfrak{m}$ is a simple module (cf. simple module and quotient module). By assumption

\begin{align*} (R/\mathfrak{m}) x = 0 \end{align*}

which shows that $1 \cdot x = x \in \mathfrak{m}$
