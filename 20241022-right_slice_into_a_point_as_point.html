right slice into a point as point

Proposition

Let $X$ be a simplicial set. Then the right simplicial set of a morphism

\begin{align*} (\Delta^0)_{\varphi/} \end{align*}

is given by $\Delta^0$

Proof

follows form

\begin{align*} ((*)_{\varphi/})_n =& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(X \star \Delta^n, * ) \\ =& * \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Hom}_{\Delta}([n],[0]) = (\Delta^0)_n$ is also the point.
