equivalent characterization of a perfect module over a commutative ring spectrum

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring spectrum and $M \in \mathrm{Mod}(R)$ a module in the infinity module category of a ring spectrum.

TFAE:

  1. $M$ is an compact object
  2. $M$ is a dualizable object

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
