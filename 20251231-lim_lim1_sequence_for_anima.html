lim-lim1 sequence for homotopy groups in anima

Definition / Proposition

Let $X_n$ be a filtered object in the category of Anima. Then there exists a short exact sequence

\begin{align*} 0 \rightarrow \mathrm{lim} \mathrm{lim}^1_{n \geq 0} \pi_{k+1}(X_n) \rightarrow \pi_k(\mathrm{lim}_{n \geq 0} X_n) \rightarrow \mathrm{lim}_{n \geq 0} \pi_k(X_n) \rightarrow 0 \end{align*}

Proof

w.l.o.g. we may assume that the sequence is bounded (after cofinal/coinitial replacement) consider the fiber sequence given by the lim-lim1 fiber sequence for anima. Then the long exact sequence of homotopy groups yields a long exact sequence

now cutting a short exact sequence out of a long exact sequence gives

\begin{align*} 0 \rightarrow \mathrm{coker}(\prod_{n \geq 0} \pi_{k+1}(X_n) \xrightarrow{(\mathrm{id}_n - f_{n+1})} \prod_{n \geq 0} \pi_{k+1}(X_n)) \rightarrow \pi_k(\mathrm{lim}_{n \geq 0} X_n) \rightarrow \mathrm{ker}(\prod_{n \geq 0} \pi_k(X_n) \xrightarrow{(\mathrm{id}_n - f_{n+1})} \prod_{n \geq 0} \pi_k(X_n)) \rightarrow 0 \end{align*}

where we may define the left term as lim-lim1 for abelian groups,

Furthermore the right side by product and equalizer imply existence of a limit is given by the $\mathrm{lim}_{n \geq 0} \pi_k(X_n)$.

So this sequence simplifies to the desired sequnce

\begin{align*} 0 \rightarrow \mathrm{lim} \mathrm{lim}^1_{n \geq 0} \pi_{k+1}(X_n) \rightarrow \pi_k(\mathrm{lim}_{n \geq 0} X_n) \rightarrow \mathrm{lim}_{n \geq 0} \pi_k(X_n) \rightarrow 0 \end{align*}
