integral homology of the classifying space of a cyclic group

Proposition

The integral homology of $B \mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z}$ is given by

\begin{align*} H_*(B \mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Z}) \simeq \begin{cases} 0 & \mbox{if } k \text{ even} \\ \mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
