Proposition

\begin{align*} H_*(X, \mathbb{F}_p) \otimes_{\mathbb{F}_p} H_*(Y, \mathbb{F}_p) \rightarrow H_*(X \otimes Y, \mathbb{F}_p) \end{align*}

is an equivalence
