Eilenberg-MacLane space

Definition

Let $A \in \mathrm{Ab}$ be an abelian group Then an Elenberg-Maclane Space of type $(A,n)$ for $n \geq 0$ is a CW-Complex $(X, \mathrm{Sk}_n(X), \emptyset)$ with an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \theta: \pi_n(X) \rightarrow A \end{align*}

such that $\pi_k(X)$ is trivial if $k \neq n$

here $A$ is regarded as set for $n = 0$
