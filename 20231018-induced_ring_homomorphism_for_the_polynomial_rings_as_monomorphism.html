induced ring homomorphism for the polynomial rings as monomorphism

Proposition

Let \(A,B\) be rings and $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ an algebraic ring-monomorphism. Then the induced ring homomorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi^X: A[X] \rightarrow B[X] \end{align*}

is an algebraic ring-monomorphism

Proof

minimal kernel and monomorphism

Let $f \in \mathrm{ker}(\varphi^X)$. Then we conclude, that $f = 0$, since otherwise for $f = \sum_{i=0}^{n} \alpha_n X^n$ we get

\begin{align*} \varphi^X(\alpha_n X^n) =& \varphi(\alpha) X^n \\ \neq 0 \end{align*}
