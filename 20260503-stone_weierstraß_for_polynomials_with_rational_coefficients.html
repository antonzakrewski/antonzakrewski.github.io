stone-weierstraß for polynomials with rational coefficients

Proposition

Let $G \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$ be a compact subset. Then the set of all polynomials over $G$ with coefficients from

\begin{align*} \mathbb{K}_0 =& \begin{cases} \mathbb{Q} & \mbox{if } \mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R} \\ \mathbb{Q} + \mathrm{i} \mathbb{Q} & \mbox{if } \mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R} \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

is dense in $C(G)$

Proof

observe that functions which are represented by polynomials are closed under addition & multiplication. Moreover, the multiplicative unit is also represented by a polynomial (namely $1$).

If $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{C}$, then these polynomials are closed under complex conjugation. At last, for $x = (x_1,...,x_n), y = (y_1,...,y_n)$ with w.l.o.g. $x_1 \neq y_1$ we may choose the polynomial $X^1$, which separates $x$ and $y$
