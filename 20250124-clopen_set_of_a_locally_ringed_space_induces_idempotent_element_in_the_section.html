clopen subset of a ringed space induces idempotent element in the section

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{O}_X$ be a ringed space, $A \subseteq X$ a clopen subset. Then this induces an idempotent section $s \in \mathcal{O}_X(X)$ with

  1. $s(A) = 1$
  2. $s(A) = 0$

Proof

follows from glueing of a sheaf: consider

  • $1 \in \mathcal{O}_X(A)$
  • $0 \in \mathcal{O}_X(X \setminus A)$.

Then both elements coincide on their intersection (it is empty) and $X \setminus A, A$ is an open cover. Thus this induces an element.

It is idempotent, since $s^2$ has the same germs
