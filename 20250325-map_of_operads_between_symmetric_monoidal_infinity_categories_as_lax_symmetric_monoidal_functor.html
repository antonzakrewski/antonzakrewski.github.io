map of operads between symmetric monoidal infinity categories as lax symmetric monoidal functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be symmetric monoidal infinity categories, regarded as infinity operad (cf. symmetric monoidal infinity category as operad) Then a map of infinity operads $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is equivalently given by a lax symmetric monoidal infinity functor.
