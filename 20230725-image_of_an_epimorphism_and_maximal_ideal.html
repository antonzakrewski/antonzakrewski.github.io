Image of an epimorphism and maximal ideal

Proposition

Let $A,B$ be commutative rings, $f: A \twoheadrightarrow B$ an epimorphism and $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ an ideal $\mathrm{ker}(f) \subseteq \mathfrak{a}$. TFAE:

  1. $\mathfrak{a}$ is maximal
  2. $f[\mathfrak{a}]$ is maximal

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Suppose there exists an ideal $I \subseteq B$ such that $f[\mathfrak{a}] \subsetneq I$. Then $\mathfrak{a} \subsetneq f^{-1}[I]$ since $f$ is an epimorphism and hence $f^{-1}[I] = A$. Since $\mathrm{ker}(f) \subseteq f^{-1}[I]$ we get $I = f[f^{-1}[I] = f[A] = B$ (see: preimage of an image and kernel)

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $I \subseteq A$ an ideal with $\mathfrak{a} \subsetneq I$. Then $f[\mathfrak{a}] \subsetneq f[I] = B$ image of a homomorphism preserving order and kernel Hence $I = f^{-1}[f[I]] = f^{-1}[B] = A$
