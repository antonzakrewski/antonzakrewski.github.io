image of a functor in general not a category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor. Then the Image of $\mathcal{F}$ is in general not a category

Proof

consider the functor

Then $0 \rightarrow 1, 1 \rightarrow 2$ are in the image, but not $0 \rightarrow 1 \rightarrow 2$
