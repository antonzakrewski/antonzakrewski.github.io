covariant ext preserves products

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an Ab4 opp abelian category and $\mathrm{Ext}$ the Ext functor. Then Ext preserves products

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{A}}^{n}(-, \prod -) \cong \prod \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{A}}^{n}(-,-) \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of right derived functor from an ab4 category commuting with coproducts commutes with coproducts
