adjoint exterior derivative

Definition

Let $(M,g)$ be a riemannian manifold. Then the adjoint derivative is defined as map on the de rham chain complex

\begin{align*} d: \Omega^{m-1}(-) \rightarrow& \Omega^m \\ \alpha \mapsto& \frac{\mathrm{det}(g)}{\vert \mathrm{det}(g) \vert } (-1)^{n(k-1) + 1} * \mathrm{d} * \alpha \end{align*}
