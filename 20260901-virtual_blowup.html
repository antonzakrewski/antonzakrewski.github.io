virtual blowup

Definition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor and $z: M \rightarrow \mathcal{O}_{X}$ a map of quasi-coherent modules over $X$. Then the virtual blowup $\mathrm{vBL}_z(X)$ of $X$ at $z$ is the algebraic functor over $X$ classifying factorizations of $z$ through a quotient line of $M$.

In other words, $\mathrm{Bl}_z(X) \subseteq \mathbb{P}(M)$ is the Subfunctor of quotient lines $q: M(x) \twoheadrightarrow L$ such that $z(x)$ factors through $q$
