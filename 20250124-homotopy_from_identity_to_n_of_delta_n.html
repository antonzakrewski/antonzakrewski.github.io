homotopy from identity to constant n of Delta^n

Proposition

There exists a unique homotopy between morphisms of simplicial sets

\begin{align*} \Delta^n \times \Delta^1 \rightarrow& \Delta^n \\ \end{align*}

from $\mathrm{id}_{\Delta^n} \rightarrow \mathrm{const}_n$

Proof

follows from the

  • nerve functor as fully faithful functor
  • nerve functor preserves limits

where this simplicial map corresponds to a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \mathrm{id}_{[n]} \Rightarrow (\mathrm{const}_n)_{[n]} \end{align*}

which exists as $n \in [n]$ is a terminal object (cf. natural transformation from identity to the constant terminal object)
