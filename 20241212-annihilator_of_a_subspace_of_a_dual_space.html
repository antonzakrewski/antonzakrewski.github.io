annihilator of a subspace for a dual space

Definition

Let $V$ be a vector space over a field $K$, $V^*$ the dual space. Let $U \subseteq V$ be a subspace.

Then the annihilator is defined as subspace $U^{\circ} \subseteq V^*$

\begin{align*} U^{\circ} =& \{f \in V^* \vert U \subseteq \mathrm{ker}(f)\} \\ =& \{f \in V^* \vert f(u) = 0 \forall u \in U\} \\ \end{align*}
