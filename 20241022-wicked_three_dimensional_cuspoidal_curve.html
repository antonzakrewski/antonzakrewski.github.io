wicked three dimensional cuspoidal curve

Definition / Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space. Then the wicked three dimensional cuspoidal curve is the affine variety

\begin{align*} C = \{(t^3,t^4,t^5)\} \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^3 \end{align*}

with vanishing ideal

\begin{align*} I(C) = (Y^2 - XZ, X^3 - YZ, Z^2 - X^2Y) \end{align*}
