associated ideal and monomorphism for a commutative ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $\mathfrak{p} \subseteq A$ a prime ideal and $M$ an \(A\)-module

TFAE:

  1. $\mathfrak{p}$ is an associated prime ideal for $M$
  2. $\mathfrak{p} = \mathrm{Ann}(m)$ for some $m \in M$ (cf. annihilator)
  3. there exists a module-monomorphism $A/\mathfrak{a} \rightarrow A$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 3)

3) $\implies$ 1)
