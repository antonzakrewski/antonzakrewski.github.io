core of a segal anima

Definition

Let $X$ be a segal anima, then the core $X^{\times}$ is defined as segal anima with

  1. $(X_0)^{\times} = X_0$
  2. $X_1^{\times}$ as pullback
  1. $X_n^{\times}$ as maximal subobject which restricts to $X_1$, i.e. inductively as pullback
