presheaf image

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, $\mathcal{F}_1,\mathcal{F}_2$ $\mathcal{A}$-valued presheaves and $\eta: \mathcal{F}_1 \rightarrow \mathcal{F}_2$ a natural transformation. Then the presheaf image is defined as image

\begin{align*} \mathrm{im}(\eta): \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathcal{A} \\ A \mapsto& \mathrm{im}(\varphi(A)) \\ (f: A \rightarrow B) \mapsto& (f^*: \mathrm{im}(\varphi(A)) \rightarrow \mathrm{im}(\varphi(B))) \end{align*}
