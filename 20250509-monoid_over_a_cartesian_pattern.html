monoid over a cartesian pattern

Definition

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be a cartesian pattern, $\mathcal{C}$ a infinity category with finite products . Then an \(\mathcal{O}\)-monoid is an infinity functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{O} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

such that the induced map

\begin{align*} \prod F(\rho_{i}^O}: \mathcal{F}(O) \rightarrow \prod \mathcal{F}(O_i) \end{align*}

is an equivalence

The latter is a generalized segal condition
