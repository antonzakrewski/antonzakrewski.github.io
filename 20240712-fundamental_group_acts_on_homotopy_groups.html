pi_1 action on homotopy groups

Definition

Let $(X,x)$ be a based space $\pi_n(X,x)$ the homotopy group Then the fundamental group acts on $\pi_n(X,x)$ via the fibre functor from the fundamental groupoid for the serre fibration

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}(S^n,X) \rightarrow X \end{align*}

i.e.

\begin{align*} \Phi: \pi_{\leq 1}(X) \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from the fiber functor to pointed hTop for the terminal morphism as hurewicz fibration (cf. hurewicz fibration as serre fibration) which determines a group action

\begin{align*} \pi_{\leq 1}(X,x) \rightarrow [E_*,E_*]_* = [X,X]_* \end{align*}

where applying the functor $\pi_n(-)$ (homotopy group) gives a group action on $\pi_n(X,x)$ (cf. group action and composition with a functor)
