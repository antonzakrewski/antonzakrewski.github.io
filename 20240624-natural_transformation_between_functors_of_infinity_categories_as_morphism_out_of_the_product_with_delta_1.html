natural transformation between functors of infinity categories as morphism out of the product with delta^1

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}s \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an infinity functor Then a natural transformation is equivalently described by

  1. \(1\)-simplices in $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ which restricts to $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}$
  2. a morphism of simplicial sets
\begin{align*} \eta: \mathcal{C} \times \Delta \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

which restricts appropriately to $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}$

Proof

follows from the adjunction

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\mathcal{C} \times \Delta^1, \mathcal{D}) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^1, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})) \end{align*}

of a cartesian monoidal structure as symmetric monoidal structure
