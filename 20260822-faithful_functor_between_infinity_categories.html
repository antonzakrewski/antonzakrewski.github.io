faithful functor between infinity categories

Definition

A functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is said to be faithful, if for every $x,y \in \mathcal{C}$, the induced map $\mathcal{F}_{x,y}: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(x), \mathcal{F}(y))$ is (-1)-truncated or an inclusion of path components.
