CP infinity as Eilenberg-MacLane Space

Proposition

The infinite real projective space is an Eilenberg-MacLane space for $K(\mathbb{Z},2)$

Proof

follows from the fiber sequence in top

\begin{align*} S^{1} \rightarrow S^{\infty} \rightarrow \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty} \end{align*}

where $S^{\infty}$ is infinite sphere as contractible space
