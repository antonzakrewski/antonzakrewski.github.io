Endomorphism spectrum is a ring spectrum for a stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category, $c$ in $\mathcal{C}$ an object and $\mathrm{End}(c) = \mathrm{map}(c,c)$ the endomorphism spectrum. Then $\mathrm{End}(c)$ is canonically a ring spectrum with composition as multiplication
