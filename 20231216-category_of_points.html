category of points

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. Then the category of points, $\mathrm{Pt}(C)$ is defined as category containing

  1. objects $\mathrm{Ob}(\mathrm{Pt}(\mathcal{C})) = \coprod_{I \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})} \mathrm{Ob}(\mathrm{Pt}_I(C))$ from category of points over an object, i.e. chosen retraction
  2. morphisms $(u,v): (q: Y \leftrightarrow J) \rightarrow (p: X \leftrightarrow I)$ such that $u: Y \rightarrow X, v: J \rightarrow I$
