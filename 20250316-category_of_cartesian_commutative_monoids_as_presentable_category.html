category of cartesian commutative monoids as presentable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an presentable infinity category Then so is the category of cartesian commutative monoids

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

Proof

Note that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(N(\mathrm{Fin}_*), \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is a full sub infinity category of a presentable category (cf. infinity functor category into a presentable category as presentable category)

Hence it suffices to show, that Monoids are S-local objects for some small collection $S$ of morphisms in $\mathrm{Fun}(N(\mathrm{Fin}_*), \mathcal{C})$ (cf. S-local objects of a presentable category as presentable category)

By evaluation for a presentable categories admits left adjoint / evaluation for a presentable category admits right adjoint the infinity evaluation functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ev}_( \langle n\rangle): \mathrm{Fun}(N(\mathrm{Fin}_*),\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

admits an infinity left adjoint

\begin{align*} F_{ \langle n\rangle}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(N(\mathrm{Fin}_*),\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

Then an element $M \in \mathrm{Fun}(N(\mathrm{Fin}_*),\mathcal{C})$ lies in $\mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{C})$ if

\begin{align*} M( \langle n\rangle) \rightarrow \prod M( \langle 1\rangle) \end{align*}

is an equivalence.

By contravariant infinity yoneda functor as fully faithful functor & fully faithful functor between infinity categories as conservative functor it suffices to show that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,M( \langle n\rangle)) \rightarrow \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,\prod M( \langle 1\rangle)) \simeq \prod \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,M( \langle 1\rangle)) \end{align*}

is an equivalence.

Using natural equivalence between infinity functors and objectwise equivalence it suffices to show that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(C,M( \langle n\rangle)) \rightarrow \prod \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(C,M( \langle 1\rangle)) \end{align*}

is an equivalence for each $C \in \mathcal{C}$

by accessible category and set of compact generators it suffices to show that for $C \in \mathcal{C}^{\kappa}$ for the essentially small subcategory of kappa compact objects

Now using the adjunctions we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(C,M( \langle n\rangle)) \simeq& \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(N(\mathrm{Fin}_*),\mathcal{C})}(F_{<n>}(C),M) \\ \prod \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(C, M( \langle 1\rangle)) \simeq& \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(N(\mathrm{Fin}_*),\mathcal{C})}(\coprod F_{ \langle 1\rangle}(C), M) \end{align*}

Hence we want to check if the bottom morphism

is an equivalence.

By contravariant infinity yoneda lemma the bottom map is induced by a morphism

\begin{align*} \varphi_{n,C} \coprod \mathcal{F}_{ \langle 1\rangle}(C) \rightarrow F_{ \langle n\rangle}(C) \end{align*}

Hence $\mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{C})$ contains precisely the $S$ local objects for

\begin{align*} S = \{\varphi_{n,C} \vert n \in \mathbb{N}, C \in \mathcal{C}^{\kappa}\} \end{align*}
