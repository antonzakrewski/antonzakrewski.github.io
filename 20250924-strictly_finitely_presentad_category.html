strictly finitely presented 1-category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a 1-category Then it is said to be strictly finitely presented, if it lies in the smallest \(1,1\)-subcategory of $\mathrm{Cat}_{1,1}$ containing $\emptyset, *, \Delta^1$ and which is closed under pushout

Note that this category is not a full subcategory of the \(2,1\)-category of 1-categories
