currying of limit preserving functors into cartesian commutative groups

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories admitting finite products. Then there exists a canonical equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Fun}^{R}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{R}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{D})) \end{align*}
