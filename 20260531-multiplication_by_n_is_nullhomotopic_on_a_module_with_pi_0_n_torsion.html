multiplication by n is nullhomotopic on a module with pi_0 n-torsion

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring spectrum such that $1 \in \pi_0(R)$ is n-torsion Let $M$ be a module spectrum over $R$, then the map $M \xrightarrow{\cdot n} M$ is nullhomotopic

Proof

using that

identifies, it suffices to show that $R \xrightarrow{\cdot n} R$ is nullhomotopic or in other words that $[\mathrm{id}] \in \pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{LMod}_R}(R,R))$ is n-torsion. But <some adjunction> shows

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{LMod}_{R}}(R,R) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{LMod}_{R}}(R \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{S},R) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S},R) \simeq R \end{align*}

where tracing elements in $\pi_0(-)$ shows that $\mathrm{id}$ -- which corresponds to $1 \in \pi_0(R)$ -- is n-torsion or equivalently $n \cdot \mathrm{id} = 0$.
