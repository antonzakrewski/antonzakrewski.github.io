etale map in general not a local isomorphism

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be an etale map. Then $f$ is not a local isomorphism (i.e. birational equivalence)

Proof

Consider

\begin{align*} Y \coloneqq X \coloneqq \mathbb{A}_k^1 \setminus \{0\} \end{align*}

with $\mathrm{char}(K) \neq 2$

Then consider the map

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow& X \\ P \mapsto& P^2 \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{d}tf) = (2P) \end{align*}

and since $2, P \neq 0$ for $P \in \mathbb{A}_k^1 \setminus \{0\}$. It follows that $(2P) \neq 0$ hence the map is etale map

Assume $f$ is a <local iso> Then choose for $P \in X$ neighbourhoods

\begin{align*} P \in U \subseteq X \\ f(P) \in V \subseteq X \end{align*}

with $f_{\vert U}: U \cong V$

Then we get an isomorphism

\begin{align*} f^*: k(Y) \cong k(V) \cong k(U) \cong k(X) \end{align*}

But this is given by

\begin{align*} \frac{G(Y)}{H(Y)} \mapsto \frac{G(X^2)}{H(X^2)} \end{align*}

which is not surjective (todo)
