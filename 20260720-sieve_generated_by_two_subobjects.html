sieve generated by two subobjects

Proposition

Let $X \in \mathcal{C}$ be an object and let $U,V \subseteq X$ be a pair of subobjects of $X$ and let $R$ be the sieve on $X$ generated by the inclusion of $U$ and $V$. Suppose the pullback $U \cap V = U \times_{X} V$ exists, then $R$ is the pushout $U \coprod_{U \cap V} V$ in $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$.

Proof

Follows from:

  • image in category set is isomorphic to the pushout
  • pointwise calculation of colimits

Remark

instead of the intersection in $\mathcal{C}$, we could have taken the pullback in $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$. The assumption, that the object $U \cap V$ exists in $\mathcal{C}$ implies that the pullback in $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ coincides the with the yoneda image. Existence of $U \cap V$ will give a more useful criterion for descent (cf. descent along a sieve generated by two subobjects).
