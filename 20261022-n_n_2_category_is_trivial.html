(n,n+2)-category is trivial

Proposition

Let $n \in [-2,\infty)$, then an $(n,n+2)$ category is just contractible.

Proof

Proof by induction

induction basis

for $n = -2$, this is just a $-2$ truncated anima, hence a contractible aniam. Now observe that the category of contractible anima is itself contractible.

induction step

So suppose we have a category $\mathcal{C}$ enriched in $\{*\}$. Then for each two objects $x,y$ we get $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \cong *$ which implies that every two objects are equivalence (e.g. after passing to the homotopy category). Now furthermore it happens to be a groupoid (e.g. check on the homotopy category) and $\mathrm{Aut}(x) \cong *$ implies that it is contractible.
