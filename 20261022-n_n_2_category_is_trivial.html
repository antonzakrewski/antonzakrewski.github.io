(n,n+2)-category is

Proposition

Let $n \in [-2,\infty)$, then an $(n,n+2)$ category is just an $(n,1)$ category for $n \geq 0$.

Proof

Proof by induction

induction basis

for $n = -2$, this is just a $-2$ truncated anima, hence a contractible aniam. Now observe that the category of contractible anima is itself contractible.

induction step

So suppose we have a category $\mathcal{C}$ enriched in $\{*\}$. Then it may be the empty category or it a point.

Then it is the same inductive argument as for a general category.
