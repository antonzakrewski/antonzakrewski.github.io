category of S-local module spectra

Definition

Let $R$ be an (\mathbb{E}_1)-ring spectrum and $S \subseteq \pi_*(R)$ be a homogeneous subset satisfying the left ore condition. Then the category of S-local module spectra is defined as full subcategory of the category of module spectra containing S-local module spectra
