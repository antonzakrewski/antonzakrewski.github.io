opposite morphism of a cofibration as cofibration

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be simplicial sets, $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a morphism of simplicial sets

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a cofibration
  2. the opposite morphism $f^{\mathrm{op}}$ is a cofibration
