pointwise calculation of limits in functor categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and let

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(C,\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

be the functor category. Let $\mathcal{F}: I \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ be a diagram.

Suppose $\mathcal{F}$ admits pointwise a limit, i.e. for each $i \in I$ the functor $\mathcal{F}(i) \in \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ admits a limit. Then $\mathcal{F}$ admits a limit in the functor category

Furthermore the evalutaion preserves \(I\)-shaped colimits

Proof

by assumption we have the adjunction $\begin{tikzcd} \mathrm{Fun}(I, \mathcal{D}) \arrow[r, shift left=1ex, "\mathrm{colim}"{name=F}] & \mathcal{D}\arrow[l, shift left=.5ex, "\mathrm{const}"{name=G}] \arrow[phantom, from=G, to=F, , "\scriptscriptstyle\boldsymbol{\bot}"] \end{tikzcd}$

so in particular postcomposition with adjoints is an adjoint

where under currying in cat infinity we may identify the composition from $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D})$ to $\mathrm{Fun}(I, \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}))$

Hence $\mathrm{const}: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(I, \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}))$ has a left adjoint.
