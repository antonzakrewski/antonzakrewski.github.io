different functors and different assignment

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be functors TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F} \neq \mathcal{G}$ are different functors
  2. there exists a morphism $f \in \mathcal{C}$ such that
\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(f) \neq \mathcal{G}(f) \end{align*}

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

suppose there exists an object $x \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ with $\mathcal{F}(x) \neq \mathcal{G}(x)$. Then $f = \mathrm{id}_x$ suffices, as

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(\mathrm{id}_x) = \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{F}(x)} \neq \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{G}(x)} = \mathcal{G}(\mathrm{id}_x) \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)
