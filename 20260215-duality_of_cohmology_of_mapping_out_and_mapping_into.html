duality of cohmology of mapping out and mapping into

Proposition

"cohomology" for topological (!) spaces contains the following duality

  1. it is given by taking $\mathrm{Hom}(C_*(X),R)$ - so it heavily depends on mapping into $X$ (out of topological simplices)
  2. if one takes $\pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,K(R,n))$ then it heavily depends on mapping out of $X$

Proof
