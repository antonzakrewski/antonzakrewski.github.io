Anima slice as Functors into Anima

Proposition

Let $X$ be an infinity-groupoid, then straightening-unstraightening of left fibrations induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An}_{/X} \simeq \mathrm{Fun}(X,\mathrm{An}) \end{align*}

Proof

We need to show, that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{LFib}(X) \simeq \mathrm{Spc}_{/X} \end{align*}

Let $\mathcal{E} \rightarrow X$ be any left fibration, then it is a kan fibration (cf. left fibration into an infinity groupoid as kan fibration). In particular, $\mathcal{E}$ is a Kan complex since $X \rightarrow *$ is a kan fibration (cf. kan complex and terminal morphism as left/right/kan fibration)

Hence we get 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{LFib}(X) \rar \arrow[]{rd}{} & \mathrm{Spc}_{/X} \dar \\
& \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}_{/X} 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
