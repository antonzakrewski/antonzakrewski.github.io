fundamental theorem of serre class theory wrong for non bounded, Z-local spectra

Proposition

Let $X$ be a (non-bounded below) Z-local spectrum such that $H_*(X, \mathbb{Z})$ is degreewise finitely generated. Then $\pi_*(X)$ is in general not degreewise finitely generated.

Proof

finitely generated homotopy groups, non finitely generated homology

Consider $\prod_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z}[n] \simeq \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z}[n]$ (cf. product and coproduct of shifted uniformly bounded objects in a nice stable category). It is $\mathbb{Z}$ local (cf. category of E-local spectra is closed under limits) and has $\pi_m(\bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z}[n]) \simeq \mathbb{Z}$ for all $m$ - so finitely generated.

Now we claim that it does not have finitely generated homology: This follows from Z homology of Z is unbounded, hence

\begin{align*} \pi_*(\mathbb{Z} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z}[n]) \simeq \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}}\pi_*(\mathbb{Z} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z}[n]) \end{align*}

consists of infinitely many, nontrivial summands.

finitely generated homology, non finitely generated homotopy groups

Consider $\mathcal{L}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\bigoplus_{z \in \mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{S}[z])$. The homotopy groups of $\bigoplus_{z \in \mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{S}[z])$ are not degreewise finitely generated, since $\pi_m(\bigoplus_{z \in \mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{S}) \simeq \bigoplus_{z \in \mathbb{Z}}\pi_m(\mathbb{S})$ where $\pi_*(\mathbb{S})$ is unbounded (cf. finite, nontrivial spectrum has infinitely many nontrivial homotopy groups). Furthermore, the unit $\bigoplus_{z \in \mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{S}[z]) \rightarrow \mathcal{L}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\bigoplus_{z \in \mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{S}[z])$ induces an injection on homotopy groups, so the homotopy groups of $\mathcal{L}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\bigoplus_{z \in \mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{S}[z])$ also can't be finitely generated (cf. submodule of a noetherian module as noetherian module).

But the unit is a Z-local equivalence, hence induces an isomorphism on homology. In other words, $H_*(\mathcal{L}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\bigoplus_{z \in \mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{S}[z])) \simeq \bigoplus_{z \in \mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z}$ is degreewise finitely generated.
