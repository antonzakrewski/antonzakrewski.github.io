cofiber functor preserves limits for a stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category, then the cofiber functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{cofib}: \mathrm{Ar}(\mathcal{C}) \xrightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

preserves limits

Proof

Obesrve that the cofiber is also given by the composition $\Sigma \circ \mathrm{fib}(-)$. So it suffices to show that both functors preserve limits:

  1. the fiber functor always preserves limits,
  2. the suspension functor is an equivalence, hence also preserves limits.
