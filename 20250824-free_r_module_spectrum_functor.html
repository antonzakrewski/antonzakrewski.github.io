free R-module spectrum functor

Definition

Let $R$ be a ring spectrum, then the free \(R\)-module spectrum functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathrm{LMod}_{R}(\mathrm{Sp}) \end{align*}

corresponding to $R$ under anima arises by freely adding colimits to a point. see:

  • left adjoints out of a presheaf category
  • $\mathrm{An} \simeq \mathcal{P}(*)$
