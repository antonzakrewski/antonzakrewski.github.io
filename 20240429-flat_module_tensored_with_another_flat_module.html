flat module tensored with another flat module

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $M,N$ flat modules and

\begin{align*} M \otimes_A N \end{align*}

the tensor product

Then $M \otimes_A N$ is also flat

Proof

follows from associativity of a tensor product,

\begin{align*} ((M \otimes_A N) \otimes_A -) \cong (M \otimes_A (N \otimes_A -)) \end{align*}

where both $(M \otimes_A -), $(N \otimes_A -)$ are exact functors, therefore also the functor composition (cf. composition of exact functors as exact functor)
