localization functor idempotent

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a commutative ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod Then for a multiplicatively closed subset $S \subseteq R$ the localization is idempotent

Proof

Follows from tensor product with the localized ring and localization of a module and associativity of a tensor product

\begin{align*} S^{-1}(S^{-1} -) \cong& S^{-1}(S^{-1} R \otimes_R -) \\ \cong& (S^{-1}R \otimes_R S^{-1}R) \otimes_R - \\ \cong& S^{-1}(S^{-1}R) \otimes_R - \\ \cong& S^{-1}R \otimes_R - \end{align*}

where the last isomorphism follows from localization of a ring idempotent
