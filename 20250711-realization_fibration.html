realization fibration

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $p: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an infinity functor Then $f$ is said to be a realization fibration, if for any map $f: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ the infinity diagram

is a pullback in Anima.
