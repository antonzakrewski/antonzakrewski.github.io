homotopy group in a topos

Definition

Let $\mathcal{X}$ be a topos and $X \in \mathcal{X}$. Then consider the object $X \times X \xrightarrow{\pi_1} X$ in $\mathcal{X}_{/X}$ and define the homotopy group as

\begin{align*} \tau_{\leq 0} \Omega(X \times X \xrightarrow{\pi_1} X, \Delta: X \rightarrow X \times X) \end{align*}

for the map from the terminal object $X \xrightarrow{\mathrm{id}} X$ given by the diagonal morphism
