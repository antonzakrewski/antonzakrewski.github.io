fiber of the relative codiagonal in Anima

Proposition

Let $f: Y \rightarrow X$ be a map of Anima. Then the fiber of the relative codiagonal $X \coprod_{Y} X \rightarrow X$ is given by $\Sigma F_x$ where $F_x$ is the fiber of $f: Y \rightarrow X$ at $x \in X$.

Proof

Recall that colimits are universal in Anima, so consider the pullback functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An}_{/X} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}_{/*} \simeq \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

Now colimits in $\mathrm{An}_{/X}$ or $\mathrm{An}_{/*}$ are computed underlying, so we have diagrams

where $\Sigma F_x$ is both the pushout and the pullback of 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
|[label={[label distance=-2mm]-45:\lrcorner}]|
\Sigma F_x \rar \dar & X \copord_{Y} X \dar \\
\mbox{} *  \rar & X 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
