animation of an additive category is prestable

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an additive category.

Proof

follows as:

  • product preserving functor out of an additive category lifts to commutative groups
  • functor category into a prestable category is prestable
  • product preserving functors into a preadditive category is closed under limits and colimits
  • 1-sfp objects closed under finite coproducts
  • 1-sfp object of an additive category is an additive category
