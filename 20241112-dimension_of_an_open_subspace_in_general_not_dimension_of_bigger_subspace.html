dimension of a dense open subspace in general not dimension of bigger subspace

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $O \subseteq X$ a dense open subset. Then in general $\mathrm{dim}(O) \neq \mathrm{dim}(X)$.

Proof

consider the Sierpinski-Space $\{0,1\}$ with the open set $O \coloneqq \{1\}$. Then each nontrivial open set is dense, in particular also $O$. Hence by irreducible space and dense open set we conclude that the sierpinski-space is dense.

But $\{0\} \subsetneq \{0,1\}$ is a proper chain of closed irreducible subspaces showing

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(O) < \mathrm{dim}(X) \end{align*}
