mapping space in Anima

Proposition

Let $X,Y \in \mathrm{An}$ be anima. Then the mapping space is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,Y) \cong \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Y) \end{align*}

where $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Y)$ is the inner hom of simplicial Sets
