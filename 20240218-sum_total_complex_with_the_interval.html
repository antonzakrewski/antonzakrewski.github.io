sum total complex with the interval

Definition / Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod})$ be the category of chain complexes. Let $C_*$ be a chain complex then taking the sum total complex of the tensored double complex $I \otimes_{R} R$ is given as

\begin{align*} (\bigoplus C \otimes_R I)_n =& C_n \otimes C_n \otimes C_{n-1} \end{align*}

and differential

\begin{align*} d: C_n \otimes C_n \otimes C_{n-1} \rightarrow& C_{n-1} \otimes C_{n-1} \otimes C_{n-2} \\ (x,y,z) \mapsto& (d(x) + z, d(y) - z, d(z)) \end{align*}

Proof

n-th component

by definition, we have

\begin{align*} (\bigoplus C \otimes_I R)_n =& \bigoplus_{k + l = n} I_k \otimes_R C_l \end{align*}

where for $k \neq 0,1$ it holds, that $I_k = 0$ and thus by tensor of the zero module

\begin{align*} I_k \otimes C_l = 0 \end{align*}

and by coproduct with the initial object canonically iso it is canonically isomorphic to

\begin{align*} (\bigoplus C \otimes_I R)_n =& I_0 \otimes_R C_n \oplus I_1 \otimes_R C_{n-1} \\ =& (R \otimes_R C_n) \oplus ((R \oplus R) \otimes_R C_{n-1}) \end{align*}

using distributivity of the tensor product and tensor product with the Ring isomorphic we get canonically

\begin{align*} (\bigoplus C \otimes_I R)_n =& (R \otimes_R C_n) \oplus ((R \oplus R) \otimes_R C_{n-1}) \\ =& (R \otimes_R C_n ) \oplus (R \otimes_R C_n) \oplus (R \otimes_R C_{n-1}) \\ =& C_n \oplus C_n \oplus C_{n-1} \end{align*}

differential

Here

where restricting to

\begin{align*} d: (R \oplus R) \otimes_R C_n) \oplus (R \otimes_R C_{n-1}) \rightarrow& (R \oplus R) \otimes_R C_{n-1}) \oplus (R \otimes_R C_{n-2}) \\ ((r_1,r_2) \otimes x, r_3 \otimes y) \mapsto& ( (r_1,r_2) \otimes d(x)) + (r_3, - r_3) y ,(-1)^{1} \cdot (r_3 \otimes_R d(y))) \end{align*}

and applying the isomorphism with $x' = r_1 \cdot x, y' = r_2 \cdot x, z' = r_3 \cdot y$ (with linearity) gives

\begin{align*} d: C_n \oplus C_n \oplus C_{n-1} \rightarrow& C_{n-1} \oplus C_{n-1} \oplus C_{n-2} \\ (x',y',z') \mapsto& (d(x') + z', d(y') - z', d(z')) \end{align*}
