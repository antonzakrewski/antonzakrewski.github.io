tor independent square of rings

Definition

Let

be a pushout square of commutative rings Then it is called tor independent, if $B$ and $C$ are tor independent \(A\)-modules.

Remark

This is equivalent to the following statement: The coproduct of $B$ and $C$ in $\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{D}(A))$ is a discrete ring or the pushout in $\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{D}(\mathbb{Z}))$
