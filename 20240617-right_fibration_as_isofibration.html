right fibration as isofibration

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a right fibration Then $\mathcal{F}$ is an isofibration between infinity categories

Proof

corollary of:

  • isofibration between infinity categories and opposite functor
  • left fibration as isofibration
  • left fibration and opposite map as right fibration
