Proposition

Let $U \xhookrightarrow{f} A \hookrightarrow{g} X$ be maps between algebraic functors, where $U \hookrightarrow A$ is an open immersion and $A \hookrightarrow X$ is an closed immersion Then the composition $U \hookrightarrow A$ is an open immersion.

Proof

first observe that composition of monomorphisms is a monomorphism.

Let $\mathrm{Spec}(r) \rightarrow X$ be a map. Then we can glue pullbacks

here the pullback of the bottom square is of the form $\mathrm{Spec}(R/I)$ since it is a classification of closed subfunctor of $\mathrm{Spec}(R)$ and hence the pullback along the open immersion $U \hookrightarrow A$ is given by $D(\overline{F})$ for some $\overline{F} \subseteq R/I$. This shows $U \hookrightarrow X$ is an open immersion.
