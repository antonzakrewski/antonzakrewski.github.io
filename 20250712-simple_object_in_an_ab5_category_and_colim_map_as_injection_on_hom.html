simple object in an Ab5 category and filtered colim map as injection on hom

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an Ab5 category and $S$ an object. Let $I \rightarrow \mathcal{A}$ be a filtered diagram Then the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_I \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(S,-) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(S, \mathrm{colim}_I -) \end{align*}

is an injection

Proof

using minimal kernel and monomorphism Let $f \in \mathrm{colim}_I \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(S,-)$ be an morphism which becomes the zero morphism Then $f$ lives in some $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(S,S_i)$

using coslice category of a filtered category as filtered category consider $I_{i/}$. This is coslice category of a filtered category as cofinal replacement.

Then consider 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
0 \rar & \mathrm{ker}(f) \rar & \mathrm{colim}_I S & S_i \rar{f_i} \\
0 \rar & \mathrm{ker}(f) \rar & \mathrm{colim}_I S &  S_j \rar{f_j} \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
