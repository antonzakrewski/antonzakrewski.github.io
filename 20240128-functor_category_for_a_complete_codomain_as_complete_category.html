functor category for a complete codomain as complete category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a complete category, $\mathcal{J}$ an indexing category and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{J}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

the functor category

Then $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{J}, \mathcal{C})$ is also complete

Proof

corollary of limit of functors for a complete domain
