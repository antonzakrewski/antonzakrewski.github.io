Borel Theorem of spectral sequences

Proposition

Let $X$ be a simply connected anima. Assume that $H^*(\Omega B, \mathbb{F}_2)$ has a simple system of generators $\{x_i\}$of positive degree with only fintiely many in the same degre.

Assume further that for all $i \geq 1$ there exsits an $y_i \in H^*(B, \mathbb{F}_2)$ such that $(x_i,y_i)$ is a transgressive pair.

Then the map

\begin{align*} \mathbb{F}_2[y_1,y_2,...] \xrightarrow{\cong} H^*(B) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism

  2. $(x_i,y_i)$ are transgressive pairs in $F_*^{*,*}$
  3. the abutment is $\mathbb{F}_2$ in degree $0$
