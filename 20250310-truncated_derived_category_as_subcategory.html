truncated derived category as subcategory

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring Then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{D}(R)_{\geq n} \subseteq \mathcal{D}(R) \end{align*}

is a full sub infinity category.

Proof

note that the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathcal{D}_{R}(\geq n} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{D}(R) \end{align*}

admits a left adjoint (cf. truncation adjunction of a derived category)

Then by fully faithful infinity left adjoint and unit as equivalence
