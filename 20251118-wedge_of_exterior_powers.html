wedge of exterior powers

Definition

Let $\mathrm{Alt}^{k_1}(V), \mathrm{Alt}^{k_2}(V)$ be exterior powers. Then the wedge map is defined as bilinear map

\begin{align*} \wedge: \mathrm{Alt}^{k_1}(V) \times \mathrm{Alt}^{k_1}(V) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Alt}^{k_1+k_2)}(V) \\ (w_1,w_2) \mapsto& \frac{1}{k_1!} \frac{1}{k_2!} \sum_{\sigma \in S_{k_1 + k_2}} \mathrm{sgn}(\sigma) w_1(v_{\sigma(1)},...,v_{\sigma(k_1)}) \cdot w_2(v_{\sigma(k_{1}+1}),...,v_{\sigma({k_1}+ k_2)}) \end{align*}
