infinity groupoid core functor

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}, \mathrm{An}$ be the category of infinity categories resp. Anima. Then the inclusion

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathrm{An} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty} \end{align*}

admits an right adjoint, which is (at least objectwise) given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}(*, -): \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty} \rightarrow \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

where $*$ is the terminal object in Cat infinity

Proof

using the fact that $\mathrm{An} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_\infty$ is fully faithful & cat infinity is cartesian closed:

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,\mathrm{map}(*, \mathcal{C})) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(X, \mathrm{map}(*, \mathcal{C})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(X \times *, \mathcal{C}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_\infty}(X, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

where the last step uses product with infinity terminal object canonically equivalent
