units in an I-adic completion

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $I$ an ideal. Suppose $x \in \widehat{R}_I$ TFAE:

  1. $x$ is a unit in $\widehat{R}_I$
  2. $x + I \widehat{R}_I$ is invertible in $\widehat{R}_{I}/I \widehat{R} \simeeq R/I$ (cf. I-adic completion and quotient).

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from the projection $\widehat{R}_I \rightarrow R/I$ where units are preserved under ring-homomorphisms.

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $x = (r_i + I^i)$ be an element. Then we may inductively define $s_1 = r_1^{-1}$ in $R/I$ and further terms by

\begin{align*} ((r_i) \cdot (s_i))_n = \sum_{i + j = n} r_i s_j \end{align*}

and setting $s_{n-1} = r_1^{-1} (1 - \sum_{i + j = n, i \neq 1 } r_i s_j)$ mod $I$ (todo).
