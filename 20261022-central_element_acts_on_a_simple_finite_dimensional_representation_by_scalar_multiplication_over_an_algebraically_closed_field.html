central element acts on a simple, finite dimensional representation by scalar multiplication over an algebraically closed field

Proposition

Let $k$ be algebraically closed field and $A$ a K-Algebra Let $V$ be a finite dimensional, simple representation of $A$. Let $x \in A$ be a central element, then $x$ acts by a scalar.

Proof

Recall that the map $A \rightarrow \mathrm{End}_{D}(V)$ is surjective. Here $\mathrm{End}_{D}(V)$ is a finite dimensional k-algebra over $k$, which is also a skew field by schur lemma. Now simple representation is simple over the endomorphism ring & schur lemma shows that $\mathrm{End}_{D}(V)$ is a skew field. The map $A \rightarrow \mathrm{End}_{D}(V)$ is a surjection by the density theorem for representations. In particular, the centers restrict to each other (cf. center under ring epimorphisms). But $Z(\mathrm{End}_{D}(V))$ is a finite dimensional field extension of the algebraically closed field $k$ (cf. center of a division algebra is a field), so itself $k$.
