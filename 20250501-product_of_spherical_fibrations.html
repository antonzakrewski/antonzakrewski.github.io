smash product of pointed spherical fibrations

Definition

Let $\pi_i: B_i \rightarrow BG(d_i)$ be pointed spherical fibrations for $i \in \{1,2\}$. Then the smash product is defined as composition

\begin{align*} \pi_i \wedge \pi_2: B_1 \times B_2 \rightarrow BG(d_1) \times BG(d_2) \rightarrow BG(d_1 + d_2) \end{align*}

where the latter map is given by

  1. <B functor preserves products>
  2. smash homomorphism of G(d)
