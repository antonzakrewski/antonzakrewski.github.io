quotient ring of a von neumann regular ring as von neumann regular ring

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a von Neumann regular Ring and $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq R$ an ideal. Then the quotient ring $R/\mathfrak{a}$ is also a von Neumann regular ring.

Proof

corollary of

  • image of a von neumann regular ring as von neumann regular ring

and the canonical epimorphism

\begin{align*} \pi_{\mathfrak{a}} R \rightarrow R/\mathfrak{a} \end{align*}
