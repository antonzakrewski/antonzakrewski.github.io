segal condition as coproduct to product

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathcal{F}: N(\mathrm{Fin}_*) \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a gamma object candidate Then the segal condition is precisely given by the requirement, that $\mathcal{F}$ sends

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}( \coprod_{i =1}^n <1> ) \simeq \prod_{i = 1}^n \mathcal{F}(<1>) \end{align*}

where the maps

\begin{align*} \rho^i: \mathcal{F}(<n>) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(<1>) \end{align*}

are given by the projection morphism in Fin*

\begin{align*} \rho^i: \langle n\rangle \rightarrow <1> \\ j \mapsto& \begin{cases} 1 & \mbox{if } j = i \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

(and sadly not some coprojections)

Note that this is sadly not some dualizing etc.

Proof

note that a pointed set

\begin{align*} \langle n\rangle = \{1,...,n, *\} \end{align*}

is precisely the coproduct of

\begin{align*} \langle 1\rangle \coprod ... \coprod \langle n\rangle \end{align*}

in $\mathrm{Fin}_*$ category Fin
