direct product of solvable groups is solvable

Proposition

Let $G,H$ be solvable groups. Then the direct product of groups is also solvable

Proof

By assumption there exists

\begin{align*} 1 = G_0 \trianglelefteq G_1 ... G_n \trianglelefteq G \\ 1 = H_0 \trianglelefteq H_1 ... H_m \trianglelefteq H \\ \end{align*}

Then we get

\begin{align*} 1 \times 1 = G_0 \times 1 \trianglelefteq G_1 \times 1 ... \trianglelefteq G_n \times 1 = G_n \times H_0 \trianglelefteq G_n \times H_1 ... \trianglelefteq G_n \times H_n = G \times H \end{align*}
