universal property of the homotopy fibre

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a continuous map.

\begin{align*} \iota: A \rightarrow& X \end{align*}

with a nullhomotopy $A$ from the constant map with value $y$ to $f \circ \iota$
