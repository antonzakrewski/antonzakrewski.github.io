colimit of colimit of indexing categories

Proposition

Let $I: J \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}$ be a diagram and let $\mathcal{I} = \mathrm{colim}_I$ be the colimit of $I$. Let $\mathcal{C}$ be another category and let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a functor.

Suppose

  1. each restriction $I_j \rightarrow \mathcal{I} \xrightarrow{\mathcal{F}} \mathcal{C}$ admits a colimit
  2. the resulting diagram
\begin{align*} J \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ j \mapsto& \mathrm{colim}_{I_j} \end{align*}

admits a colimit

Then $\mathcal{F}$ also admits a colimit which is given by $\mathrm{colim}_I \mathrm{colim}_{I_j}$

Proof

follows as

  1. functor category functor preserves limits
  2. mapping anima in the limit of categories

which shows

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{[\mathcal{I}, \mathcal{C}]}(\mathcal{F}, \mathrm{const}(-)) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{[\mathrm{colim}_I, \mathcal{C}]}(\mathcal{F}, \mathrm{const}(-)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{lim}_I \mathrm{map}_{[I_j, \mathcal{C}]}(\mathcal{F}_{\vert I_j}, \mathrm{const}(-)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{lim}_I \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathrm{colim}_{I_j}, -) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathrm{colim}_I \mathrm{colim}_{I_j}, -) \end{align*}

which shows that $\mathrm{colim}_I \mathrm{colim}_{I_j}$ the left adjoint object
