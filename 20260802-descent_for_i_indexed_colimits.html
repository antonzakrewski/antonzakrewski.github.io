descent for I-indexed colimits

Definition

Let $I$ be a small category and $T$ be a category with pullbacks and $I$ indexed colimits Then $T$ satisfies descent for $I$-indexed colimits if the functor

\begin{align*} T_{-}: T^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat} \\ X \mapsto& T_{/X} \end{align*}

preserves \(I\)-indexed limits
