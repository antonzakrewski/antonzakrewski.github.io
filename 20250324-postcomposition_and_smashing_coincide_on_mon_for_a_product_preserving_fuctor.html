postcomposition and smashing coincide on Mon E_infinity for a product preserving fuctor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a product preserving infinity left adjoint between presentable infinity categories. Then the induced functors by monoid category functor as smashing localization and postcomposition coincide

Proof

consider

Then it suffices to show that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{L}), \mathcal{L}_* \end{align*}

are left adjoints to the same right adjoint, as mon is the free preadditive monoidal infinity category, i.e. since precomposition with $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{C})$ induces an equivalence:

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}, \otimes}(\mathrm{Mon}_{\mathrm{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{C}),\mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{D})) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}, \otimes}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{D})) \end{align*}

$\mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{L})$ as left adjoint

follows as $\mathrm{Mon}(-)$ is an infinity endofunctor in $\mathrm{Pr}^L$, where morphism in $\mathrm{Pr}^L$ are left adjoints

$\mathcal{L}_*$ as left adjoint

follows from:

  • postcomposition with infinity adjoints as infinity adjoint
  • adjunction restricts to full subcategories

where $\mathcal{L}, \mathcal{R}$ both restrict appropriately, as they preserve products (by assumption resp. by right adjoint preserves limits)

same right adjoint

note that the right adjoint to

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Free}_{\mathcal{D}} \circ \mathcal{L} \end{align*}

is given by

\begin{align*} \mathcal{R} \circ \mathrm{ev}_1(-) \end{align*}

(cf. composition of infinity adjoints)

Furthermore the right adjoint of

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}_* \circ \mathrm{Free}_{\mathcal{C}} \end{align*}

is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ev}_1 \circ \mathcal{R}_*(-) \end{align*}

But pre and postcomposition commute (cf. infinity pre and infinity postcomposition commute).
