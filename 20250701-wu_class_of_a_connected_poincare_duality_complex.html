wu class of a connected poincare duality complex

Definition

Let $X$ be a connected poincare duality complex of dimension $n$. Then the Wu classes are defined as the unique class $v_i(X) \in H^i(X, \mathbb{F}_2)$ such that for all $x \in H^{n-i}(X; \mathbb{F}_2)$ we have

\begin{align*} \langle v_i(X) \cdot x, [X]\rangle = \langle \mathrm{Sq}^i(x), [X]\rangle \end{align*}
