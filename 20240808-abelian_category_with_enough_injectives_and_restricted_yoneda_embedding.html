abelian category with enough injectives and restricted yoneda embedding

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category with enough injectives. Let $\mathcal{I}$ be the full subcategory of injective objects

Then the restricted yoneda embedding

\begin{align*} \gamma: \mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{I}, \mathrm{Ab}) \\ A \mapsto& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(A,-) \\ (f: A \rightarrow B) \mapsto& f^* \end{align*}

is fully faithful

see:

  • yoneda embedding of an additive category

Proof

faithful

  • restriction of a contravariant yoneda embedding to enough monos

fully

  • extension of a natural transformation from a covariant hom functor for a category with enough injectives and regular monos
