bijection between radical ideals and closed subsets of spec

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ the zariski spectrum with the Zariski topology. Then there exists an order reversing Bijection

\begin{align*} \{\mathrm{radical ideals}\} \cong& \{\mathrm{closed subsets}\} \\ \mathfrak{a} \mapsto& \mathcal{V}(\mathfrak{a}) \\ \mathcal{I}(V) \mapsfrom& V \end{align*}

where $\mathcal{V}$ is the Vanishing set and $\mathcal{I}(-)$ the ideal associated to a vanishing set

Proof

follows from radical of an ideal as intersection of prime ideals where

a)

Let $\mathfrak{a}$ be a radical ideal, then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{I(V(}\mathfrak{a}) =& \bigcap_{\mathfrak{p} \in \mathcal{V}(\mathfrak{a})} \mathfrak{p} \\ =& \bigcap_{\mathfrak{p} \supseteq \mathfrak{a}} \mathfrak{p} \\ =& \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}} \\ =& \mathfrak{a} \end{align*}

b)

Let $V$ be a closed set, then $V = \mathcal{V}(\mathfrak{a})$ for some ideal $\mathfrak{a}$. Thus

\begin{align*} \mathcal{I}(V) =& \bigcap_{\mathfrak{p} \in V} \mathfrak{p} \\ =& \bigcap_{\mathfrak{p} \in \mathcal{V}(\mathfrak{a})} \mathfrak{p} \\ =& \bigcap_{\mathfrak{p} \subseteq \mathfrak{a}} \mathfrak{p} \\ =& \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}} \end{align*}
