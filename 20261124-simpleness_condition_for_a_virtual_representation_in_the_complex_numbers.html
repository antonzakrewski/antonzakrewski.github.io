simpleness condition for a virtual representation in the complex numbers

Proposition

Let $V$ be a virtual representation. Suppose that $\langle \chi_{V}, \chi_{V}\rangle = 1$ and $\chi_{V}(e) > 0$. Then $V$ is a simple represntation.

Proof

Write $\chi_{V} = \sum n_i \chi_{S_i}$ where $n_i \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $S_i$ are simple. Then

\begin{align*} \langle \chi_{V}, \chi_{V}\rangle = \sum_{i} n_i^2 \end{align*}

by orthogonality of simple characters.

so all $n_i$ are zero except for one $n_i$. but $\chi_{S_i}(e) = \mathrm{dim}(S_i) > 0$ implies that $n_i \geq 1$
