eilenberg mac lane space as infinite dimensional space

Proposition

Let $G$ be an abelian group and $n \geq 2$. Then a model of Eilenberg-MacLane space with CW-Structure is not finite dimensional CW-complex.

Proof

special cases: $G = \mathbb{F}_2$ or $n$ even and $G = \mathbb{Z}$

We claim that

\begin{align*} H^k(K(G,n),G) \end{align*}

is nonzero for arbitrary large $k$, hence finite dimensional cw complex and finite cohomological dimension applies.

This follows as $H^k(K(G,n),G)$ is the set of natural transformations from $H^n(-,G) \rightarrow H^k(-,G)$

Here the iterated cup product of singular cohomology suffices, as it is nontrivial for

  1. singular cohomology of the quaternionic space
  2. singular cohomology ring of the real projective space with F2 coefficients (or steenrod square)
  • finite dimensional ~> finite homology ~> finite cohomology

general case
