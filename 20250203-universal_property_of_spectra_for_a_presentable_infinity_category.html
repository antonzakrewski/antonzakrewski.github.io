universal property of Spectra for a presentable infinity category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be presentable infinity categories. Suppose $\mathcal{D}$ is also stable.

Then <precomposition> with $\Sigma_{+}^{\infty}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}(\mathcal{C})$ induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{LFun}(\mathrm{Sp}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{LFun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ \end{align*}

Proof
