fundamental theorem of verdier injections

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{C}'$ be stable infinity categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}'$ be an exact infinity functor

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is a verdier injection
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ is fully faithful functor and its essential image is closed under infinity retracts

Proof
