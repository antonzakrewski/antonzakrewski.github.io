natural transformation between covariant hom functors as precomposition

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a locally small category, $A,B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ objects and $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,-), \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(B,-)$ covariant functors Then a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \eta: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,-) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(B,-) \end{align*}

is precisely given by a morphism $f: B \rightarrow A$, hence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{NatTrans}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,-),\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(B,-)) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(B,A) \end{align*}
