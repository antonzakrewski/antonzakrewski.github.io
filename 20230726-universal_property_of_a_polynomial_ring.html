universal property of a polynomial ring

Proposition

Let $A,B,R$ be commutative rings and $A[X_m]$ a polynomial ring. Then there exists a Bijection

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{R-\mathrm{CAlg}}(A[X_m], B) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{R-\mathrm{CAlg}}(A,B) \times \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Set}}(M,B) \end{align*}
