freudenthal suspension theorem

Proposition

Let $(X,x_0)$ be a well pointed space Suppose $X$ is a p-connected space, then the unit

\begin{align*} u_x: X \rightarrow \Omega \Sigma X \end{align*}

is \(2p+1\)-connected

In other words:

  1. if up to $\pi_{n}(X)$ is trivial (e.g. $S^n$ is $n-1$ connected).
  2. then up to $\pi_{2n}(X) \rightarrow \pi_{2n+1}(\Omega X)$ is an isomorphism and $\pi_{2n+1}(X) \rightarrow \pi_{2n+1}(\Omega X)$ is a surjection.
