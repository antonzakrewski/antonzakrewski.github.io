constructible factorization algebra

Definition

Let $X$ be a stratified smooth manifold and $\mathcal{A} \in \mathrm{Fact}_X(\mathcal{C}^{\otimes})$ a factorization algebra. Then $\mathcal{A}$ i ssaid to be constructible, if the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathcal{A}(U) \rightarrow \mathcal{A}(V) \end{align*}

whenever $U \subseteq V$ are conical disks of the same type
