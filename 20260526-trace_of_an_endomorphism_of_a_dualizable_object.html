trace of an endomorphism of a dualizable object

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a closed (do I need that ?) symmetric monoidal category and $X$ a dualizable object. Then the trace is defined as map

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,X) \simeq X \otimes X^{\vee} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{coev}} \mathbbm{1} \end{align*}

or passing to $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbbm{1},-)$ to get a map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbbm{1},\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,X)) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbbm{1},X \otimes X^{\vee}) \xrightarrow{\mathrm{coev}_*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbbm{1},\mathbbm{1}) \end{align*}
