scheme of a field

Proposition

The affine scheme of a field is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Spec}(k) = \mathrm{Op}(0) \rightarrow& \mathrm{CRing} \\ \emptyset \mapsto& 0 0 \mapsto& k \end{align*}

Proof

  1. follows from global section functor for affine schemes equivalence between the category of affine schemes and commutative rings
  2. follows from sheaf at the empty set as terminal object
