sieve containing a covering sieve is also a covering sieve

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \tau)$ be a site and $S \subseteq よ(X)$ a covering sieve in $\tau$. Then any sieve $R \subseteq よ(X)$ with $S \subseteq R$ is also a covering sieve.

Proof

We use axiom 2: Let $R \subseteq S$, where $R$ is a \(\tau\)-covering sieve. Then for $f: Y \rightarrow X$, the pullback informally consists of $\{Z \rightarrow Y \vert Z \rightarrow Y \rightarrow X \in R\}$. But a sieve is a subpresheaf, so closed under precomposition with any morphism.

So for $f \in R$, we get $f^*(S) = よ(Y)$, which is a covering sieve. Hence by axiom 2, $S$ is a covering sieve.
