conservative functor and RLP

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an functor TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a conservative functor
  2. $f$ admits the right lifting property against $[1] \rightarrow *$ (in the (infinity,1) category $\mathrm{Cat}$)
