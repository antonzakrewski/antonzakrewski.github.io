kappa filtered colimits and kappa small colimits commute in a presentable category for some kappa

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category. Then there exists a regular cardinal $\kappa$ such that kappa filtered and kappa-small limits commute.

Proof

using presentable category as accessible localization of a presheaf category the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is fully faithful, preserves limits and preserves kappa filtered colimits for some regular cardinal $\kappa$

Let $I$ be kappa filtered and $J$ be a kappa-small. Then whether $I$ shaped colimits commute with $J$ shaped limits can be checked in $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$, since the inclusions preserves those (co-)limits and detects them (cf. infinity fully faithful functor reflects limits, infinity fully faithful functor reflects colimits).

But we calculate (co-)limits in $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) = \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{C})$ pointwise (cf. pointwise calculation of limits / pointwise calculation of colimits), so it suffices to show this for Anima. But there we have kappa filtered colimits and kappa small limits commute in Anima
