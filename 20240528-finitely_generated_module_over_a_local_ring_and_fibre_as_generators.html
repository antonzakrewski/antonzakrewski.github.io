finitely generated Module over a local ring and fibres of a vector space basis as generators

Proposition

Let $A$ be a local ring with Residue field $k = A/\mathfrak{m}$

Suppose $M$ is a finitely generated \(A\)-modules, $M/\mathfrak{m}M$ the vector space over $k$ (cf. functor from modules to vector spaces for the residue field). Assume further that $\overline{m_1},...,\overline{m_n} \in M/\mathfrak{m}M$ generate $M/\mathfrak{m}M$ as \(k\) module, and $m_1,...,m_n$ are fibres of $\overline{m_1},...,\overline{m_n}$.

Then $m_1,...,m_n$ form a generating set

Proof

w.l.o.g. we may assume that $\overline{m}_1,...,\overline{m}_n$ form a \(k\)-basis

Let $N \coloneqq \langle m_1,...,m_n\rangle$ be the generated submodule . Then it remains to show that $M = N$

and hence an isomorphism

\begin{align*} N/\mathfrak{m}N = M/\mathfrak{m}M \end{align*}

This shows

\begin{align*} N + \mathfrak{m}M = M + \mathfrak{m}M = M \end{align*}
