brown comenetz dual functor to hSp

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Ab}^{\mathrm{inj}}$ be the full subcategory of the Category Ab containing injective abelian groups. Then taking the brown comenetz dual is a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ab}^{\mathrm{inj}} \rightarrow& h\mathrm{Sp} \end{align*}
