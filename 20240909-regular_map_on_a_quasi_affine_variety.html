regular map on a quasi affine variety

Definition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n, Y \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^m$ quasi-affine varieties. Then a map

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

is said to be regular, if it is regular map on quasi-projective algebraic sets
