eilenberg's swindle for projective modules

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $(\mathrm{Proj}(R), \oplus)$ the category of projective modules. Then the grothendieck group is the zero group.

Proof

Let $P$ be projective with $P \oplus Q = R^{\kappa}$. Then consider

\begin{align*} R^{\mathbb{N} \cdot \kappa} =& (P \oplus Q)^{\mathbb{N}} \\ =& (P \oplus Q) \oplus (P \oplus Q) \oplus ... \\ =& P \oplus (Q \oplus P) \oplus (Q \oplus P) ... \\ =& P \oplus (P \oplus Q)^{\mathbb{N}} \\ =& P \oplus R^{\mathbb{N} \cdot \kappa} \end{align*}

showing that

\begin{align*} [P] + [\mathbb{N}] =& [\mathbb{N}] \end{align*}

Thus after inverting $[\mathbb{N}]$ we get

\begin{align*} [P] = 0 \end{align*}

in the grothendieck group completion
