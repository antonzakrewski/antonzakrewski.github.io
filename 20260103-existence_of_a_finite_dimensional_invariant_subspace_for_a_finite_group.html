existence of a finite dimensional, invariant subspace for a finite group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a finite group and $V \neq 0$ be a representation. Then there exists a finite dimensional, G-invariant subspace $U$.

Proof

Choose any vector $v \in V$. Then consider $\langle \rho(g)(v) \vert g \in G \rangle$ which is finite dimensional since it is finitely generated and one can show that it is \(G\)-invariant
