monomorphism locus of a map of A-modules

Definition

Let $f: M \rightarrow N$ be a map of \(A\)-modules for a commutative ring $A$ Then the monomorphism locus of $f$ is defined as locus for the property of being a universally injective map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mono}(f)(R) = \{\varphi: A \rightarrow R \vert M \otimes_{A} R \xrightarrow{f \otimes_{A} R} N \otimes_{A} R \textrm{ is universally injective}\} \end{align*}
