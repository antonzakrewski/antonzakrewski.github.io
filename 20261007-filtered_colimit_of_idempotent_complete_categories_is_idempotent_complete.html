filtered colimit of idempotent complete categories is idempotent complete

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be the filtered colimit of idempotent complete categories $\mathcal{C}_i$ Then $\mathcal{C}$ is also idempotent complete.

Proof

recall that $\Delta^1$ is a compact object in category Cat. So $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}}(\Delta^1, \mathrm{colim} \mathcal{C}_i) \simeq \mathrm{colim}\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{C}_i)$, hence any idempotent morphism in the colimit lifts to some $\mathcal{C}_i$. There it splits and any functor preserves the spilt, hence the image will be the required splitting.
