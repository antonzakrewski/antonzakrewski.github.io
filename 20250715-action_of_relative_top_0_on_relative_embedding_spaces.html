Proposition

Let $M \hookrightarrow W$ be a embedding of Manifolds with dimension $m$ resp. $w$ (and $m \leq w$). Then Top_0(d,m) acts on the relative topological embedding space

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Emb}^t(\mathbb{R}^w;\mathbb{R}^m, W; M) \end{align*}
