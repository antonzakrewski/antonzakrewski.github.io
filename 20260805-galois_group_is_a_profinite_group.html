galois group is a profinite group

Proposition

Let $L/K$ be a galois field extension. Then the galois group is a profinite group, i.e.,

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Gal}(L/K) \simeq \lim_{E} \mathrm{Gal}(E/K) \end{align*}

Proof

observe that the set of finite field extensions with respect to $\supseteq$ is a cofiltered system. Moreover, let $(\sigma_E) \in \lim_{E} \mathrm{Gal}(E/K)$ be an element, then this defines an automorphism $\sigma = \bigcup \sigma_{E}$ of $\mathrm{Gal}(L/K)$: It is injective (which can be checked on finite field extensions and it is surjective, which can also be checked on finite field extensions.

Moreover, any nontrivial automorphism over $K$ maps an element $\alpha$ to $\alpha'$ with $\alpha \neq \alpha'$ and hence is notrivial on the galois field extensions generated by $\alpha, \alpha'$.
