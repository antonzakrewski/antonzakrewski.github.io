grothendieck group as quotient of the free group

Proposition

Let $M$ be a commutative monoid, then the grothendieck group is isomorphic to the quotient group

\begin{align*} F(M)/R(M) \end{align*}

where

  1. $F(M)$ is the free abelian group generated by $M$
  2. $R(M)$ is the subgroup generated by Elements
\begin{align*} [m b n] - [m] - [n] \in R(M) \end{align*}

Proof(sketch)

follows from the universal property of a quotient group and the universal property of $M$, where a morphism out of $F(M)/R(M)$ is precisely given by a set theoretic map out of $M$ satisfying the relations of $R(m)$, which enforce, that the set theoretic map is a monoid homomorphism
