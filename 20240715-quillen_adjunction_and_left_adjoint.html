quillen adjunction and left adjoint

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be model categories, $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$ an adjunction

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$ is a quillen adjunction
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ is a left-quillen functor
  3. $\mathcal{G}$ is a right-quillen functor

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
