left unit and right unit for a maximal ideal

Proposition

Let $R$ be a local ring and $x \in R$.

TFAE:

  1. $x$ is a unit
  2. $x$ is a left unit
  3. $x$ is a right unit

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

special case

2) $\implies$ 3)

By assumption there exists a left maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$. Furthermore there exists right inverse $y$ i.e. $xy = 1$ with $x \not\in \mathfrak{m}$ Then we get $xyx = x$ or $x(yx - 1) = 0$. Consider the annihilator of $(yx - 1)$. It is a left ideal with $x \in \mathrm{Ann}(yx - 1)$. Hence $\mathrm{Ann}(yx - 1) = R$ since $\mathfrak{m} \subsetneq \mathrm{Ann}(yx -1)$. Thus $1 \in \mathrm{Ann}(yx - 1)$ or $1(yx - 1) = yx - 1 = 0$ or $yx = 1$.

3) $\implies$ 1)
