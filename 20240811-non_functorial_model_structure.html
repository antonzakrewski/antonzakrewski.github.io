non functorial model structure

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a bicomplete category A model structure on $\mathcal{C}$ is given by three distinguished wide subcategories

  1. weak equivalence $\mathcal{W} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$
  2. cofibration $\mathrm{Cof} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$
  3. fibration $\mathrm{Fib} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$

such that a) category with weak equivalences b) the following are weak factorization systems i) $(\mathrm{Cof}, \mathrm{Fib} \cap \mathcal{W})$ ii) $(\mathrm{Cof} \cap \mathcal{W}, \mathrm{Fib})$
