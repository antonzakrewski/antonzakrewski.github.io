connectivity of a tree

Proposition

Let $T = (V,E)$ be a tree with Then it has knot connectivity, vertex connectivity and minimal degree $1$

Proof

by connectivity it is at least $1$ knot/vertex connected.

  • tree contains at least two leafs

knot

choose a leaf $v$. Then remove the vertex connected to $v$ respectively the edge connected to $v$. it follows that $T$ splits into two connected components

degree
