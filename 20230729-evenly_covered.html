evenly covered

Definition

Let $(X,\mathcal{T})$ and $(Y,\mathcal{T}_Y)$ be topological spaces, $A \subseteq Y$ and $p: X \rightarrow Y$ a continuous map Then $A$ is evenly covered if the restriction

\begin{align*} p_{\vert p^{-1}[A]}: p^{-1}[A] \rightarrow A \end{align*}

is a trivial covering map
