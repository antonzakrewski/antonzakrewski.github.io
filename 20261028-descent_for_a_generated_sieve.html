descent for a generated sieve

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F} \in \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ be a presheaf. Let $U_i \rightarrow X$ be a set of maps in $\mathcal{C}$ and that all limit of the form $U_{i_0} \times_{X} U_{i_1} \times_{X} ... \times_{X} U_{i_n}$ exists.

Then $\mathcal{F}$ satisfies descent with respect to $\langle U\rangle \hookrightarrow よ(x)$ if and only if

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(X) \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}( \langle \mathcal{U}\rangle, \mathcal{F}) \\ \simeq& \lim_{[n] \in \Delta} \prod \mathrm{Hom}(よ(U_{i_0} \times_{X} ... \times_{X} U_{i_n}), \mathcal{F}) \end{align*}
