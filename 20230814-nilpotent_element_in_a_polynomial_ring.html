nilpotent element in a polynomial ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $A[X]$ the polynomial ring and $f = \sum_{i=0}^{n} a_i X^i \in A[X]$ TFAE:

  1. $f \in \mathcal{N}_{A[X]}$ is nilpotent
  2. all coefficients $a_i$ are nilpotent

Proof

graded ?

1) $\implies$ 2)

by assumption, there exists an $m \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $f^m = 0$. Then, we conclude, that the $n \cdot m$ component is zero, hence

\begin{align*} (\alpha_n x^{n})^m =& \alpha_n^{m \cdot n} \cdot x^{nm} \\ =& 0 \end{align*}

Since $x^{nm}$ is by construction not a zero divisor, it follows, that $\alpha_n^{m \cdot n} = 0$.

Thus by sum of nilpotent elements we can reduce the case to $n-1$ while having shown, that $\alpha_n$ is nilpotent

Hence by iteration, we conclude, that $a_n,...,a_{0}$ are nilpotent

2) $\implies$ 1)
