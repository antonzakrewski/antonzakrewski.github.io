natural transformations between constant Z-diagram

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a nonempty, connected category, $\mathrm{Ab}$ the Category Ab and

\begin{align*} \Delta \mathbb{Z}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab} \end{align*}

the constant functor with value $\mathbb{Z}$ as abelian group

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Nat}(\Delta \mathbb{Z}, \Delta \mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • endomorphism ring of the integers canonically isomorphic to the integers
  • natural transformations between constant diagrams
