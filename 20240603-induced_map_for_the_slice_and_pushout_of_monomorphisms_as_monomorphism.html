induced map for the slice and pushout of monomorphisms as monomorphism

Proposition

Let $A,B,S,T$ be simplicial sets, $\iota: A \rightarrow B, g: S \rightarrow T$ be monomorphisms of simplicial sets. Then the canonical map

\begin{align*} \iota \widehat{\star} g: A \star T \coprod_{A \star S} B \star S \rightarrow B \star T \end{align*}

is also a monomorphism

Proof

using colimit of functors for a cocomplete codomain we may calculate

\begin{align*} (A \star T \coprod_{A \star S} B \star S) \end{align*}

at each $n$.

Then this is given by

\begin{align*} ((\coprod_{J_1,J_2 \in \mathrm{Cut}([n])} A[J_1] \times T[J_2]) \coprod (\coprod_{J_1,J_2 \in \mathrm{Cut}([n])} B[J_1] \times S[J_2]))/\sim \end{align*}

using natural transformation and morphisms as monomorphism it remains to show the map is injective

\begin{align*} ((\coprod_{J_1,J_2 \in \mathrm{Cut}([n])} A[J_1] \times T[J_2]) \coprod (\coprod_{J_1,J_2 \in \mathrm{Cut}([n])} B[J_1] \times S[J_2]))/\sim \rightarrow B[J_1] \times S[J_2] \end{align*}

Note that the maps

\begin{align*} A[J_1] \times T[J_2] \rightarrow& B[J_1] \times T[J_2] \\ B[J_1] \times S[J_2] \rightarrow& B[J_1] \times T[J_2] \end{align*}

are injective.

Let $(a_{J_1}, t_{J_2}) \in A[J_1] \times T[J_2], (b_{J_1}, s_{J_2}) \in B[J_1] \times S[J_2]$ be elements. Then it follows that

\begin{align*} \iota(a_{J_1}), t_{J_2} =& (b_{J_1}, \iota(s_{J_2}) \end{align*}

if and only if

hence showing $(a_{J_1}, t_{J_2}) = (b_{J_1}, s_{J_2})$ in the pushout
