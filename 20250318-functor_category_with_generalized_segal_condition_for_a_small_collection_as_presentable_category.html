functor category with generalized segal product condition for a small collection as presentable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category, $\mathcal{D}$ any small category Suppose $\rho_{i,j}: c_{i} \rightarrow c_j$ is a small collection of morphisms in $\mathcal{C}$ indexed by some subset of $I \times J$

Let $\mathrm{Fun}^{i,j}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ denote the full sub infinity category containing functors $M$ such that

\begin{align*} \prod M(\rho_{i,j}): M(c_i) \rightarrow \prod M(c_j) \end{align*}

is an equivalence

Then $\mathrm{Fun}^{i,j}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ is presentable
