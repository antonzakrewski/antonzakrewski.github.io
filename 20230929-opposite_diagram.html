opposite diagram

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a diagram Then the opposite diagram $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{op}}$ is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{op}}: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ A \mapsto& \mathcal{F}(A) \\ (f: A \rightarrow B) \mapsto& \mathcal{F}(f^{\mathrm{op}}: B \rightarrow A) \end{align*}
