second countable, locally compact space admits compact exhaustion

Proposition

Let $X$ be a secondcountable, locally regular compact Space. Then $X$ admits a compact exhaustion.

Proof

Let $\mathcal{B}$ be a countable basis. For each point $x \in X$ choose a neighbourhood $V_x$ with compact closure. Then for each $V_x$ choose an open neighbourhood $U_x$ which lies in $\mathcal{B}$.

Then we claim that $\overline{U_x}$ is a countable (in general non open) cover containing compact subsets. It is countable, since $\{U_x\}$ is a subset of $\mathcal{B}$, so w.l.o.g. assume that it is indexed by $\mathbb{N}$.

It is compact since $\overline{U_x}\subseteq \overline{V_x}$ and it covers $X$ since each point $x$ lies in $V_X$. Then using finite union of compact subspaces we set

\begin{align*} X_n = \bigcup_{i=1}^n \overline{U_n} \end{align*}

and get $X = \bigcup X_n$ as compact exhaustion.
