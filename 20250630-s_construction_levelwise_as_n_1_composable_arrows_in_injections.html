S construction levelwise as n-1 composable arrows in injections

Proposition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\mathcal{E}$ an exact infinity category. Then $S_n(\mathcal{E})$ is given by the full subcategory

\begin{align*} S_n(\mathcal{E}) \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^{n-1}, \mathcal{E}) \end{align*}

where the objects are given by functors which restrict to $\mathrm{in}(\mathcal{E})$ (but the natural transformations are not restricted to $\mathrm{inj}(\mathcal{E})$)

Proof
