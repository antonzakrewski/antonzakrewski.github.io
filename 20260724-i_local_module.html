I-local module

Definition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $I \subseteq R$ an ideal and $M$ an \(R\)-module. Then an \(R\)-module $M$ is said to be \(I\)-local, if for every \(R\)-linear map $h: P \rightarrow Q$ with I-nilpotent kernel & cokernel, precomposition

\begin{align*} h^*: \mathrm{Hom}_{R}(Q,M) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{R}(P,M) \end{align*}
