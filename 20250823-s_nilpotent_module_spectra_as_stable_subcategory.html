S-nilpotent module spectra as stable subcategory

Proposition

The full subcategory of S-nilpotent module spectra is a stable subcategory.

Proof

follows as

  1. $0$ is trivially \(S\)-nilpotent.
  2. it is closed under bifiber sequences (cf. bifiber two out of three for S-nilpotent module spectra)
