finite moore spectrum and finitely generated abelian group

Proposition

Let $A$ be an abelian group and $M(A,n)$ a moore spectrum TFAE:

  1. $M(A,n)$ is a finite spectrum
  2. $A$ is a finitely generated abelian group

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

  • finite spectrum has finitely generated homotopy groups

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows as any finite generation implies a finite presentation

\begin{align*} \bigoplus_{i=1}^n \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \bigoplus_{i=1}^m \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow A \end{align*}

which translates to a cofiber sequence

\begin{align*} \bigoplus_{i=1}^n \mathbb{S}[n] \rightarrow \bigoplus_{i=1}^m \mathbb{S}[n] \rightarrow M(A,n) \end{align*}
