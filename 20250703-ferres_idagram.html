ferres diagram

Definition

Let $n,k \in \mathbb{N}$ and $(x_1,..,x_k) \in \mathbb{N}^k$ a ordered partition of $n$. Assume $x_i \geq x_{i+1}$ Then define the ferres diagram is defined as matrix $\mathbb{F}_2^{k,x_1}$ with

\begin{align*} (\alpha_{i,j}) = \begin{cases} 1 & \mbox{if } x_i \leq j \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
