lie algebra of a lie group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a lie group Then the Lie-Algebra associated to $G$ is given by $T_{e}G$ together with the commutator pairing.

\begin{align*} [X,Y](-) = XY(-) - YX(-) \end{align*}

where we identify correspondence between left invariant vector fields on a lie group and the tangent space
