representation of a group

Definition

Let $G$ be a group, $R$ a commutative ring and $V$ an \(R\)-module A \(R\)-representation of $G$ is a group-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \rho: G \rightarrow \mathrm{GL}(V) \end{align*}

or equivalently an F-Algebrahomomorphismus

\begin{align*} R[G] \rightarrow \mathrm{End}_{R} \end{align*}
