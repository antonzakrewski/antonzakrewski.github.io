subgroup of a perfect group is in general not perfect

Proposition

Let $G$ be a perfect group and $H \subseteq G$ a subgroup. Then $H$ is in general not perfect.

Proof

Let $G$ be any nontrivial perfect group, e.g. elementary linear group is perfect group for n greater 2 Then take any nontrivial element $g \in G$ and let $H = \langle g\rangle$ be the generated group. It is a cyclic group, hence of the form $\mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z}$ and in particular also abelian and nontrivial.
