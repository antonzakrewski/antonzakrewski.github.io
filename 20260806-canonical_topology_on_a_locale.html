canonical grothendieck topology on a locale

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be a locale. Then the canonical topology on $\mathcal{O}$ is defined as following: A sieve $R \subseteq よ(u)$ is a covering sieve if and only if the supremum of $\{v \vert u(v) \neq \emptyset\}$ is $u$.

Proof

a)

Let $\{B_i \subseteq C\}$ be a cover. Then $\{B_i \cap A \subseteq A \cap C\}$ is a cover of $A$ as

\begin{align*} A \cap C = A \cap (\bigvee B_i) = \bigvee (B_i \cap C) \end{align*}

b)

Let $\{B_i \subseteq A\}$ be covering and $\{C_i \subseteq A\}$ a subset, such that $\{C_j \cap B_i \subseteq B_i \vert j \in J\}$ is a covering of $B_i$ for all $i \in I$. Then we may compute

\begin{align*} \bigvee C_j =& \bigvee C_j \cap A \\ =& \bigvee (C_j \cap A) \\ =& \bigvee (C_j \cap \bigvee B_i) \\ =& \bigvee \bigvee (C_j \cap B_i) \\ =& \bivee B_i \\ =& \bigvee A \end{align*}

c)

Let $よ(u) \subseteq よ(u)$ be a representable. Then the supremum of $\{v \vert v \leq u\}$ is evidently $u$: It is at least $u$, since $u \in よ(u)$ and at most $u$ since $u$ is an upper bound.
