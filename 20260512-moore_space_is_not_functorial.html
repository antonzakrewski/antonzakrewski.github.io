taking moore spaces is not functorial

Proposition

There does not exist a functor

\begin{align*} M(-,n): \mathrm{Ab} \rightarrow \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

which is objectwise given by taking the Moore spaces and which composed with $H_n(-,\mathbb{Z})$ is the identity.

Proof

follows from:

  • taking moore spectra is not functorial
  • suspension spectrum of a moore space is a moore spectrum
