non minimal primary decomposition in general does not contain redundant ideals

Proposition

Proof

Consider $A = K[X,Y]$ and the ideals

\begin{align*} (X^2, XY, Y^2) \\ (X^2, Y) \\ (X,Y^2) \end{align*}

Then all three are primary ideal, as

\begin{align*} \sqrt{((X^2, XY, Y^2)} = (X,Y) \end{align*}

as maximal ideal (cf. radical as maximal ideal and primary ideal)

Then

\begin{align*} (X^2, XY, Y^2) \end{align*}

has the trivial decomposition into $(X^2, XY, Y^2)$ but also

\begin{align*} (X^2, XY, Y^2) =& (X^2,Y) \cap (X,Y^2) \end{align*}
