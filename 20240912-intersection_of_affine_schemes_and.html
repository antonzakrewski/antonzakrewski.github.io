intersection of affine schemes and open subschemes of the quotient ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a ring and $(\mathrm{Spec}(A), \mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{Spec}(A)})$ an affine scheme. Let $\mathrm{Spec}(A/\mathfrak{a}), \mathrm{Spec}(A/\mathfrak{b})$ be the open subscheme for $A \rightarrow A/\mathfrak{a}, A \rightarrow A/\mathfrak{b}$.

Then the intersection is given by the affine scheme

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Spec}(A/(\mathfrak{a} + \mathfrak{b})) \end{align*}

Proof
