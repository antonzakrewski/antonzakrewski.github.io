ringmorphism and IBP

Proposition

Let \(R,S\) be rings, $f: R \rightarrow s$ a ring-homomorphism Suppose $S$ satisfies the invariant basis property, then so does $R$ Given $R^n \cong R^m$ use extension of scalars to get an isomorphism

\begin{align*} S^n \cong S \otimes_R R^n \cong S \otimes_R R^m \cong S^m \end{align*}

this shows $n = m$
