stable closure of a subcategory of a stable category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category and $\mathcal{C}_0$ a subcategory. Then the stable retract closure of $\mathcal{C}_0$ is defined as smallest full subcategory

\begin{align*} \overline{\mathcal{C}_0}^{\mathrm{st}} \subseteq \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

which is a stable subcategory
