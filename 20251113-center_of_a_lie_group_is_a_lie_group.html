center of a lie group is a closed lie group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a lie group Then the center of $G$ is also a closed lie group

Proof

consider the maps

\begin{align*} \varphi: G \rightarrow& \prod_{h \in H} G \\ g \mapsto& (ghg^{-1}h^{-1})_{h \in G} \end{align*}

which is given as composition of diagonals, inverses and multiplications.

Then the center is given by the preimage of $\{1\}_{h \in G}$ which is the product of closed sets, hence closed (cf. product of closed subsets is closed)

now use closed subgroup of a lie group is a lie group
