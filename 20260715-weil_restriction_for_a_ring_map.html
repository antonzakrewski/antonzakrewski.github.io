weil restriction for a ring map

Definition

Let $\varphi: k \rightarrow k'$ be a ring-homomorphism. Then weil restriction is defined as precomposition with the extension of scalars functor

\begin{align*} \varphi^* = (- \otimes_{k} k'): \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k',\mathrm{Set}) \rightarrow : \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k,\mathrm{Set}) \end{align*}
