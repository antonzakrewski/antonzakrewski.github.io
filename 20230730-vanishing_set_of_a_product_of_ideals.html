vanishing set of a product of ideals

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a}_1, \mathfrak{a}_2 \subseteq A$ be Ideals and $\mathfrak{a}_1 \cdot \mathfrak{a}_2$ the product of ideals , then for the vanishing set it holds, that

\begin{align*} V(\mathfrak{a}_1 \cdot \mathfrak{a}_2) = V(\mathfrak{a}_1) \cup V(\mathfrak{a}_2) \end{align*}
