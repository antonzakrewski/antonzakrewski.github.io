category Fin*

Definition

The category $\mathrm{Fin}*$ is defined as category containing

  1. sets $<n> = \{*,1,...,n\}$
  2. maps $\alpha: <n> \rightarrow <m>$ such that $\alpha(*) = *$

i.e. it is a convenient skeleton of the subcategory of the full subcategory of category set* containing finite sets

Lurie uses the notation

\begin{align*} \langle n\rangle^{\circ} \coloneqq \{1,...,n\} = \langle n\rangle \setminus \{*\} \end{align*}
