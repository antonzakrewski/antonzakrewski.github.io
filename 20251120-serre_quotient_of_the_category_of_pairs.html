serre quotient of the category of pairs

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{}$ Then there exists a serre quotient 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{B} \arrow[hookrightarrow, ]{r}{} & \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}} \rar{\mathrm{pr}_{\mathcal{A}} & \mathcal{A}
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Proof

we want to show that $\pr: \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}}/\mathcal{B} \rightarrow \mathcal{A}$ is an equivalence

fully faithful

Let $B \rightarrow A, B' \rightarrow A'$ be any objects. We want to show that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}}/\mathcal{B}}((B \rightarrow A), (B' \rightarrow A')) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(A,A') \end{align*}

is fully faithful

Since each object is a quotient of some object in $\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}}$ we may w.l.o.g. assume that both morphisms are monomorphism. Now any subobject $(\tilde{B} \rightarrow \tilde{A}) \subseteq (B \rightarrow A)$ with quotient in $\mathcal{B}$ has $\tilde{A} = A$.

essentially surjective

By assumptions we can find for each $A\in \mathcal{A}$ a short exact sequence

with $B,B' \in \mathcal{B}$. So in particular the kernel of $(B \hookrightarrow A) \rightarrow (B \rightarrow 0)$ is given by $0 \rightarrow A$.
