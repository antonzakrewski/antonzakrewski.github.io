profinite abelianization

Proposition

let $\mathrm{Pro}(\mathrm{Grp}), \mathrm{Pro}(\mathrm{Ab})$ be the category of profinite groups and category of profinite abelian groups. Then the forgetful functor admits a left adjoint, which is pointwise given by

\begin{align*} G \mapsto G/\overline{[G,G]} \end{align*}
