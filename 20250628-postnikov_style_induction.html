postnikov style induction

Approach

When trying to understand an anima $X$ one can try find a diagram

where the maps $X_m \rightarrow X_{m-1}$ become increasingly more connected and where

\begin{align*} X \simeq \mathrm{lim}_{\mathbb{N}} X_n \end{align*}

by checking the induced map on homotopy groups (here argue by coinitiality that $\pi_*$ commutes with the sequential limit)

Then one can try to

  1. understand $X_1$ as base case
  2. understand the fiber $F_n \rightarrow X_n \rightarrow X_{n-1}$
