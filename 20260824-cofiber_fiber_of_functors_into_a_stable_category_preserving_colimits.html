cofiber / fiber of functors into a stable category preserving colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{K}$ be a collection of categories, $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{D}$ a stable category which admits $\mathcal{K}$ shaped colimits and $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ functors, which preserve $\mathcal{K}$ shaped colimits. Suppose $\eta: \mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ is a natural transformation and $\mathcal{H} \coloneqq \mathrm{fib}(\eta)$ is the fiber (cofiber) in the stable functor category $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$.

Then $\mathcal{H}$ preserves $\mathcal{K}$ shaped colimits.
