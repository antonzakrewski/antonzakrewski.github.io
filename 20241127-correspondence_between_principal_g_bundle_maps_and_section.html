correspondence between principal g-bundle maps and section

Proposition

Let $G$ be a topological group, then there exists a correspondence between

  1. map of principal g-bundles
  2. sections $E \times_G E' \rightarrow B$

Proof

construction 1) $\Rightarrow$ 2)

Let $f: (p: E \rightarrow B) \rightarrow (p': E' \rightarrow B')$ be a map of principal g-bundles. Then consider

\begin{align*} E \rightarrow E \times E' \rightarrow E \times_{G} E' e \mapsto [e,f(e)] \end{align*}

note that

\begin{align*} g^{-1} \cdot e \coloneqq e \cdot g \end{align*}

Then it follows that $f: E \rightarrow E \times_{G} E'$ is a \(G\)-equivariant map.

As $B$ is homeomorphic to $E/G$ (cf. ) we get a factorization

Then $s$ is a section by

construction 2) $\Rightarrow$ 1)

let $s: B \rightarrow E \times_G E'$ be a section. Then pick a trivializing open cover $U_i$ of $E \rightarrow B$

Then the bundle $E \times_G E' \rightarrow U_i$ is trivial.

Then over $U_i$, the map $s_i = s_{\vert U_i}$ is just a map

\begin{align*} U_i \rightarrow E \end{align*}

Define

\begin{align*} f_i: U_i \times G \rightarrow& E \\ (u,g) \mapsto& (s_i(u) \cdot g) \end{align*}

Then $f_i$ is continuous and \(G\)-equivariant by construction.

Here $U_i \times G \subseteq E$ is open.

Then by glueing lemma for an open covering this amounts to a map

\begin{align*} f_i: E \rightarrow E' \end{align*}
