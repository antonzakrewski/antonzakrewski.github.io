Ramzi: "An example of this is the equality 1+1 = 1+1. It looks silly, I know. But there are two reasons why it is true! One is the silly one: I wrote the same thing on both sides. Another, less silly one, is that for every two numbers a and b, a+b = b+a. Now, I can decide to use this property for a = b = 1, and this is a (subtly) different reason why 1+1 = 1+1."
