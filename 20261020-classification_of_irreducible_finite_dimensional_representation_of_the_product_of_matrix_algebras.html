classification of irreducible, finite dimensional representation of the product of matrix algebras

Proposition

Let $A = \bigoplus_{i=0}^n \mathrm{Mat}_{d_i}(D_i)$ where $D_i$ is a finite dimensional division algebra over a field $k$. The irreducible $A$ representations are given by $D_i^{d_i} = V_i$ acted on $A$ via projecting to the \(i\)-th coordinate and acting. Moreover, any finite dimensional representation is a direct sum of the $V_i$

Proof

First we note the $V_i$ are clearly simple: Given any $v \in V_i$, then $A v \subseteq V$ is everything. I.e. any every element is a cyclic element.

Now we note that $A$ acting on $A$ is isomrophic to $\bigoplus D_i^{d_i^2}$ by the projection onto columns.

Now let $V$ be any finite dimensional representation and pick a basis $\{v_i\}$. Then the map

\begin{align*} \varphi: A^n \rightarrow& V \\ (a_1,...,a_n) \mapsto& a_i v_1 + ... + a_n v_n \end{align*}

is surjective and its kernel is a subrepresentation of $A^n$.

So its a direct sum of $V_i$ and therefore so is $V$ (as the kernel of a split exact sequence). This implies that all fintie dimensional $V$ are of the form $\bigoplus n_i V_i$
