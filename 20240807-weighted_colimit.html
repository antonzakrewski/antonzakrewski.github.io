simple weighted colimit

Definition

Let $\mathcal{V}$ be a complete, cocomplete symmetric closed monoidal category Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a \(\mathcal{V}\)-enriched category and let $J$ be small \(\mathcal{V}\)-enriched category

Suppose $W: \mathcal{J}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{V}$ (the weight) and $\mathcal{F}: J \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ are enriched functors

Then the weighted colimit of $\mathcal{F}$ with respect to $W$ is an object $W \cdot F$ with an enriched natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}(W \cdot F, c) \cong \mathrm{Fun}(J^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{V})(W, \mathcal{C}(\mathcal{F}(-), c)) \end{align*}
