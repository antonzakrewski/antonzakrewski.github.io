coordinate ring of an irreducible as reduced K-Algebra

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space. Suppose $V \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^n$ is an affine subvariety, then the coordinate ring

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O}(V) \end{align*}

is an reduced ring

Proof

  • radical ideal and reduced quotient ring
