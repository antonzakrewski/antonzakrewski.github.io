intersection of ideals and linearization with another polynomial

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $A[X_1,...,X_n]$ the polynomial ring and $I,J \subseteq A[X_1,...,X_n]$ ideals. Then for the Intersection it holds that

\begin{align*} I \cap J = (t \cdot I + (1 - t) J) \cap A[X_1,...,X_n] \end{align*}

for another variable $t$

Proof

$I \cap J \subseteq (t \cdot I + (1 - t) J) \cap A[X_1,...,X_n]$

Suppose $f \in I \cap J$, then we conclude that

\begin{align*} t \cdot f + (1 - t) \cdot f = f \in A[X_1,...,X_n] \end{align*}

$I \cap J \supseteq (t \cdot I + (1 - t) J) \cap A[X_1,...,X_n]$

Suppose

\begin{align*} t \cdot g + (1 - t) \cdot f \in A[X_1,...,X_n] \end{align*}

then we conclude, that

\begin{align*} t \cdot (g - f) = 0 \end{align*}

and since by construction $t$ is not a zero divisor, it follows that $g = f$ Hence

\begin{align*} t \cdot g + (1 - t) \cdot f =& f \\ =& g \end{align*}

and hence $g = f \in I \cap J$
