quasi-projective scheme

Definition

Let $k$ be a commutative ring. An algebraic functor $X$ is a quasi-projective scheme if there exists an $\mathbb{N}$ graded k-algebra $A$, generated by a finite subset of $A_1$ and a finite homogeneous subset $F \subseteq A$ such that $X \cong D(F) \subseteq \mathrm{Proj}(A)$.
