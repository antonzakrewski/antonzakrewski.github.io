anima has never free cohomology over the steenrod algebra

Proposition

Let $X$ be an anima, then $H^*(X, \mathbb{F}_p)$ is never free (and notrivial) over the Fp steenrod algebra.

Proof

$p = 2$, mutatis mutandis should be also true for odd $p$

Let $x \in H^n(X, \mathbb{F}_p)$ then $\mathrm{Sq}^{n+1}(x) = 0$ by triviality of steenrod operation. But if $H^n(X, \mathbb{F}_p)$ was free (and nontrivial), then $\mathrm{Sq}^{n+1}(x)$ would be nontrivial.
