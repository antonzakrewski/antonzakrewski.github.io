tensor ideal in a symmetric monoidal category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \otimes, \mathbbm{1}$ be a symmetric monoidal category Then a tensor ideal is a full subcategory $\mathcal{C}_0$ such that the functor restricts as 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{C}_0 \dar \\
\mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C}_0 \rar{\mathrm{id} \times \iota} \arrow[dashed]{rru}{}& \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C} \rar{- \otimes -} & \mathcal{C}
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
