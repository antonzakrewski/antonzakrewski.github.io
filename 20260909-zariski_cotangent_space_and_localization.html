zariski cotangent space and localization

Proposition

Let $(A, \mathfrak{m})$ be a local ring Then there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2 \cong (\mathfrak{m} A_{\mathfrak{m}})/(\mathfrak{m}^2 A_{\mathfrak{m}}) \end{align*}

Proof

there exists an isomorphism $A/\mathfrak{m} \otimes_{A} A_{\mathfrak{m}}$ since any element not in $\mathfrak{m}$ is nonzero and acts invertibly, as $A/\mathfrak{m}$ is a field Hence applying $\mathfrak{m} \otimes_{A} -$ preserves this isomorphism
