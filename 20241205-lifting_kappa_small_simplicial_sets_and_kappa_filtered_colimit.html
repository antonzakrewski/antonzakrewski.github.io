lifting kappa small simplicial sets and kappa filtered colimit

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal, $\mathcal{C}_i$ be simplicial sets indexed by a kappa filtered diagram $I \rightarrow \mathrm{sSet}$, $S,T$ simplicial sets, where $S$ is kappa-small. Suppose $\mathrm{colim}_i \rightarrow X$ is a morphism, such that the composition

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathrm{colim}_I \rightarrow X \end{align*}

has the right lifting property against $S \rightarrow T$

Then the induced map $\mathrm{colim} \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow X$ has also the Right Lifting property against $S \rightarrow T$
