flat ring maps are stable under base change

Proposition

Let $\varphi: R \rightarrow S$ be a flat ring morphism and $R'$ another commutative ring . Then the map $\varphi \otimes_R R': R' \rightarrow R' \otimes_{R} S$ is also a flat map

see:

  • tensor product of rings as categorical coproduct in CRing

Proof

recall that $\varphi: R \rightarrow S$ ($\varphi: R' \rightarrow R' \otimes_{R} S$) is a flat map if and only if $S$ ($S')$ is flat as an \(R\)-module (\(R'\)-module). But extension of scalars preserves flat modules, so in particular $\varphi: R' \rightarrow R' \otimes_{R} S$ is a flat map.
