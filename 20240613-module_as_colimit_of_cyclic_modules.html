module as quotient of the sum of cyclic submodules

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $M$ an \(R\)-module Then $M$ is a quotient module of the coproduct of cyclic submodule

Proof

Let

\begin{align*} \overline{M} \coloneqq \bigoplus_{m \in M} \langle m\rangle \end{align*}

Then consider the module-epimorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: \overline{M} \rightarrow M m \mapsto& m \end{align*}

induced by the submodule inclusions $\langle m\rangle \rightarrow M$

using epimorphism in abelian category and quotient object we get the isomorphism
