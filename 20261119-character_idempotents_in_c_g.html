character idempotents in C[G]

Proposition

Let $G$ be a finite group and $V_i$ an enumeration of the isomorphism classes of simple C-representations of $G$. Let

\begin{align*} \psi_{i} = \frac{\mathrm{dim}(V_i)}{\vert G \vert} \sum_{g \in G} \chi_{V_i}(g) \cdot g^{-1} \in \mathbb{C}[G] \end{align*}

then

  1. $\psi_i^2 = \psi_i$
  2. $\psi_i \psi_j = 0$

Proof

$\psi_i$ central

We can calculate (ignoring scalars)

\begin{align*} h \cdot (\sum_{g \in G} \chi_{V_i}(g) \cdot g^{-1}) (h)^{-1} =& \sum_{g \in G} \chi_{V_i}(g) \cdot h g^{-1} (h)^{-1} \\ =& \sum_{g' = h g h^{-1} \in G} \chi_{V_i}(g') (g')^{-1} \end{align*}

where we use that $\chi_{V}$ is a class function.

action

now by schurs lemma it acts by a scalar $\alpha$. We can calculate on $V_j$ using $\chi_{V_j}(g^{-1}) = \overline{\chi_{V_j}(g)}$:

\begin{align*} \mathrm{tr}(\rho_{V_j}(\psi_i)) =& \frac{\mathrm{dim}(V_i)}{\vert G \vert} \sum_{g \in G} \chi_{V_i}(g) \cdot \chi_{V_j}(g^{-1}) \\ =& \mathrm{dim}(V_i) \cdot \langle \chi_{V_i}, \chi_{V_j}\rangle \end{align*}

which shows that $\psi_i$ acst on $V_j$ by $1$ if $V_i = V_j$ and by zero else.

multiplication

Consider the isomorphism from maschkes theorem

\begin{align*} f: \mathbb{C}[G] \rightarrow& \bigoplus_{i} \bigoplus_{j} V_i \\ 1 \mapsto& (e_{i,j}) \end{align*}

as $\mathbb{C}[G]$ modules.

It is $\mathbb{C}[G]$ linear so we may compute

\begin{align*} f(\phi_k) =& \phi_k (e_{i,j}) \\ =& (\partial_{i,k} e_{i,j}) \\ =& f(\phi_k^2) \end{align*}

which shows that - as $f$ is an injection - that $\phi_k^2 = \phi_k$ and similarly

\begin{align*} \phi_l f(\phi_k) =& (\partial_{l,i} \partial_{k,i} e_{i,j}) \\ \end{align*}

which implies that $\phi_l \phi_k = 0$ for $l \neq k$.
