some p-local homology of the double loop space of the odd sphere

Proposition

Let $n \geq 3$, then

\begin{align*} H_k(\Omega^{2} S^{n+2}; \mathbb{Z}_{(p)}) \cong \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z}_{(p)} & \mbox{if } k = 0,n \\ \mathbb{F}_p & \mbox{if } k = p(n+1) - 2 \\ 0 & \mbox{if } k < p(n+1) - 2 \textrm{ and} k \neq 0,n \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from

  • universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology

and some p-local cohomology of the double loop space of the odd sphere
