dimension of a product of algebraic sets

Proposition

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field, $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^m, Y \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^n$ be nonempty algebraic set Then the dimension of the product of algebraic sets is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(X \times Y) = \mathrm{dim}(X) + \mathrm{dim}(Y) \end{align*}

Proof

Note that the coordinate rings of $X,Y$ are finitely generated K-Algebra. Therefore also the coordinate ring of the product, which is the tensor product (cf. coordinate ring functor dualizes products to coproducts, tensor product of finite type algebras as finite type algebra)

Hence by dimension of a finitely generated k-algebra as transcendence degree and tensor product and transcendence degree
