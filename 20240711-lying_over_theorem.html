Proposition

Let $A \hookrightarrow B$ be an integral extension Then for each prime ideal $\mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ there exists a prime idela $\mathfrak{q} \in \mathrm{Spec}(B)$ such that

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{p} = \mathfrak{p} \cap A \end{align*}
