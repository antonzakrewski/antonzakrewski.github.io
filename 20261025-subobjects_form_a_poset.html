subobjects form a poset

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $X$ an object Then the full subcategory of $\mathcal{C}_{/X}$ containing monomorphisms $Y \rightarrow X$ is a partially ordered set

Proof

Let $(Y' \hookrightarrow X)$ and $(Y \hookrightarrow X)$ be subojbects. follows from the fiber sequence of Hom animae in the slice category and pullback of a monomorphism is a monomorphism

which implies that $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_{/X}}((Y' \rightarrow X), (Y \rightarrow X)) \hookrightarrow \{*\}$ is a subobject in anima / category set and hence either $\emptyset$ or $\{*\}$.
