unit of a separated presheaf is injective

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F} \in \mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal{C})$ be a separated presheaf for a system of local isomorphisms. Then the sheafification

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{F}^{#} \end{align*}

is a monomorphism
