dimension of a local ring and vector space

Proposition

Let $A$ be a local noetherian commutative. Then the vector space dimension of $\frac{\mathfrak{m}}{\mathfrak{m}^2}$ as \(\frac{A}{\mathfrak{m}}\)-vector space is smaller than the dimension

Proof

follows from the height theorem and finitely generated Module over a local ring and fibres of a vector space basis as generators, where a generating set of

\begin{align*} \frac{\mathfrak{m}}{\mathfrak{m}^2} = \frac{M}{\mathfrak{m}M} \end{align*}

over $\frac{A}{\mathfrak{m}}$ is also a generating set of $\mathfrak{m}$ as \(A\)-module.

Then this follows from the height theorem
