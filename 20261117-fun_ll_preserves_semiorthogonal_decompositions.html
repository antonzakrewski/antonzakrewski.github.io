Fun^LL preserves semiorthogonal decompositions

Proposition

The functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{LL}(-,-): \mathrm{Pr}^{LL}_{\mathrm{st}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{perf}} \end{align*}

preserves semiorthogonal decompositions in both variables separately.

Proof

idea

follows from semiorthogonal decomposition and adjoint as well as $\mathrm{Fun}^{LL}(-,-)$ ought to be a 2-functor.
