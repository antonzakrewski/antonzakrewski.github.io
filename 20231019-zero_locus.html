zero locus of an affine space

Definition

Let $K$ be a field and $f_i \in K[X_1,...,X_n]$ polynomials. Then the zero locus $Z(f_i)$ is defined as

\begin{align*} Z(f_i) \coloneqq \{(a_1,...,a_n) \in \mathbb{A}_K^n \vert f_i(a_1,...,a_n) = 0 \forall i \in I\} \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^n \end{align*}

where $\mathbb{A}_K^n$ is the affine space
