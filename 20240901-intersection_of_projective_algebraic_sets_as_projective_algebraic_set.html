intersection of projective algebraic sets as projective algebraic set

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X_i$ projective algebraic sets Then the intersection

\begin{align*} \bigcap X_i \end{align*}

is also a projective algebraic set

Proof

follows from:

  • sum of homogeneous ideals as homogeneous ideal
