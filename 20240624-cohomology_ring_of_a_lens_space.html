Proposition

Let $p,q$ be coprime prime numbers. Then the $\mathbb{Z}$ singular cohomology is given by

Proof

using the universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology this results in

\begin{align*} H^n(L(p,q); \mathbb{Z}) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } n = 1 \\ 0 & \mbox{if } n = 1 \\ \mathbb{Z}/p \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } n = 2 \\ \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
