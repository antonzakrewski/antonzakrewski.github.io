intersection form of a oriented 2n manifold

Definition

Let \(M\) be a \(2n\)-dimensional manifold with an orientation $\sigma$ Then the intersection form is defined as bilinear map

\begin{align*} I: H_n(X) \otimes H_n(X) \rightarrow& H_0(X) \\ (x \otimes y) \mapsto& \mathrm{PD}^{-1}(x) \cap y \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{PD}^{-1}$ is the inverse of the poincare duality isomorphism of a 2n manifold
