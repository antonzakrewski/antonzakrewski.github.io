rational cohomology of the rational eilenberg maclane space

Proposition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, then the rational cohomology of

\begin{align*} H^*(K(\mathbb{Q}, n), \mathbb{Q}) \cong \begin{cases} \mathbb{Q}[x_n] & \mbox{if } n \textrm{ even} \\ \Lambda_{\mathbb{Q}}[e_n] & \mbox{if } n \textrm{ odd} \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • R-localization of Anima / R-local equivalence
