dimension estimate of Rad

Proposition

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim(A)} - \mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{Rad}(A)) = \sum n_i^{2} \mathrm{dim}_k D_i \end{align*}

So in particular $\sum n_i^2 \mathrm{dim}_i D_i \leq \mathrm{dim}(A)$.

Proof

follows rfom:

  • k-algebra modulo Rad is a product of matrix algebras
