weakly S-effacable presheaf into spectra

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $S \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ a collection of morphisms. Then a functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}$ is said to be weakly \(S\)-effable, if for each $x \in \mathcal{C}$ and each $\alpha \in X(x)$ there exists some $s: x' \rightarrow x$ in $S$ such that $s^*(\alpha) = 0 \in X(x') \in \mathrm{Sp}$
