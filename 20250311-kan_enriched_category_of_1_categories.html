kan enriched category of 1-categories

Definition

The kan enriched category of 1-categories is defined as kan enriched category with

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) = N(\mathrm{core}(\mathrm{Fun}_{\mathrm{1-Cat}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}))) \cong \mathrm{core}(\mathrm{Fun}(N(\mathcal{C}), N(\mathcal{D}))) \end{align*}

which is obtained by

  1. considering $\mathrm{1-Cat}$ as groupoid-enriched category via the groupoid core
  2. using the nerve (cf. nerve functor preserves limits) as lax monoidal functor induces a 2-functor on category of of categories of enriched categories
  3. which restricts appropriately nerve of a category as kan complex and groupoid
