product of kappa filtered categories is kappa filtered

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal and $\mathcal{C}_i$ be kappa filtered infinity category Then so is the product

Proof

follows from the universal property 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
& J \star \Delta^0 \arrow[dashed]{rr}{} & & \mathcal{C}_i \\
J \arrow[]{rd}{} \arrow[]{ru}{\iota} & \prod \mathcal{C}_i \arrow[]{ru}{} & \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
