underlying category of a cocomplete slice category in general not cocomplete

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ an object and $\mathcal{C} / A$ the slice category. Suppose $\mathcal{C} / A$ is cocomplete, then $\mathcal{C}$ is in general not.

Proof

Consider $\mathbb{N}_0$ as partially ordered set and hence as category. Then a colimit is precisely a supremum.

Consider the initial object $0$ as $A$. Then it follows that $\mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C} / A) = \mathrm{id}: 0 \rightarrow 0$ with $\mathrm{Hom}_{ \mathbb{N}_0 / 0}(0,0) = \mathrm{id}$.

Therefore for each diagram $I \rightarrow \mathbb{N}_0 / 0$ there exists the trivial colimit of $0$.

But $\mathbb{N}_0$ is not cocomplete because for example there does not exist a natural number, which is a supremum of $\mathbb{N}$.
