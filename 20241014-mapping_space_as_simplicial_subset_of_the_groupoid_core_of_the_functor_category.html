mapping space is a simplicial subset of the groupoid core of the functor category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $x,y \in \mathcal{C}_0$ objects. Then the mapping space is a simplicial subset

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

of the infinity groupoid core.

Proof

follows as the mapping space is defined as pullback

and

  • infinity groupoid core functor as right adjoint
  • infinity right adjoint preserves limits
