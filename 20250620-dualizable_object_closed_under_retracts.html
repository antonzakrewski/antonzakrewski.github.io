dualizable object in a closed monoidal category closed under retracts

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a closed symmetric monoidal category. Then an retract of a left dualizable (reps. right dualizable) is again left (resp. right) dualizable.

Proof

right dualizable

using dualizable object in a closed symmetric monoidal category as inner hom Let $Y \xrightarrow{\iota} X \xrightarrow{p} Y$ be a retract where $X$ is dualizable. Let

\begin{align*} Y^{\vee} = \underline{\mathrm{map}}(Y,1) \end{align*}

and define

\begin{align*} \mathrm{coev}_Y: 1 \xrightarrow{\mathrm{coev}_X} X \otimes X^{\vee} \xrightarrow{p \otimes \iota^*} Y \otimes Y^{\vee} \\ \mathrm{ev}_Y: Y^{\vee} \otimes Y \xrightarrow{\iota \otimes p^*} \xrightarrow{} X^{\vee} \otimes X \xrightarrow{} \mathbb{S} \end{align*}

Then by construction

commutes

so in particular the glueing commutes (since the middle vertical arrows compose to the identity)

where the composite is given by $p \circ \mathrm{id}_X \circ \iota = \mathrm{id}_Y$
