formula for left kan extensions

Let $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an infinity functor between infinity categories $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$. Then for a cocomplete category $\mathcal{X}$ and a functor $\mathcal{G}$ the left kan extension
