continuous map into a discrete space as locally constant map

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be topological spaces, where $Y$ is a discrete space. Then a continuousd map

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

is equivalently given by a locally constant map

Proof

a)

locally constant map as continuous map

b)

Let $x \in X$, then $f(x)$ is an open point. Then by continuity $f^{-1}[f(x)]$ is open and hence a neighbourhood of $f(x)$
