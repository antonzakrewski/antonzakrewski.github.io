gabriel-ulmer duality for stable categories

Proposition

The gabriel-ulmer duality restricts on a equivalence of categories

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Pr}^L_{\kappa}_{\mathrm{st}} \simeq \mathrm{Rex}_{\kappa, \mathrm{st}} \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}^{\mathrm{perf}} \end{align*}

where

  1. the LHS is the category PrL kappa stable
  2. the right hand side is category Rex kappa, stable

Proof
