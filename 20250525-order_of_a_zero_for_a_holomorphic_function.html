order of a zero for a holomorphic function

Definition / Proposition

Let $\Omega \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be open and $f: \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ be nonconstant and holomorphic function with $f(z_0) = 0$ Then there is a neighbourhood $U$ of $z_0$ and a holomorphic function $g: U \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ such that

  1. $g(z_0) \neq 0$
  2. there exists a unique $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with
\begin{align*} f(z) = (z - z_0)^n g(z) && \forall u \in U \end{align*}
