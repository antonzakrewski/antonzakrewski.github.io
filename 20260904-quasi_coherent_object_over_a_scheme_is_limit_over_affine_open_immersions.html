quasi-coherent object over a scheme is limit over affine open immersions

Proposition

Let $X$ be a scheme and $\mathrm{QC}$ the functor of quasi-coherent objects for <todo>. Then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{QC}(X) \simeq \lim_{\mathrm{Spec}(R) \hookrightarrow X, \textrm{ open immersion }} \mathrm{CAlg}_{R} \end{align*}
