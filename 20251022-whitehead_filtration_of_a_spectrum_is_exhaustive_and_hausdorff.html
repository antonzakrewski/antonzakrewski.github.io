whitehead filtration of a spectrum is exhaustive and hausdorff

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum and $\tau_{\geq *} X$ its whitehead filtration. Then it is both exhaustive and hausdorff filtration

Proof

a) exhaustive

  • homotopy group functor of spectra preserves filtered colimits where the homotopy group of $\pi_{n} \tau_{\geq l} X = \pi_n X$ for $l \geq n$

b) hausdorff

  • limit of anima with eventually constant homotopy groups
  • limit of spectra with eventually constant homotopy groups
  • lim-lim1 sequence for a stable category
