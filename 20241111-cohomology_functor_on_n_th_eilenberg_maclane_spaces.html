singular cohomology functor on n-th Eilenberg-MacLane spaces

Proposition

Let $A,B$ be abelian groups, then the canonical map

\begin{align*} [K(A,n), K(B,n)] \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(A,B) \end{align*}

obtained by applying the singular cohomology functor

see: n-th cohomology of the Eilenberg-MacLane space with coefficients

Proof

follows from

  • brown representability theorem for singular cohomology
  • absolute Hurewicz theorem
