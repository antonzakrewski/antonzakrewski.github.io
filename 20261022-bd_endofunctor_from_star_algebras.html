Bd endofunctor from star algebras_

Definition / Proposition

There exists a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Bd}(-): \mathrm{*Alg}^{\mathrm{nu}} \rightarrow \mathrm{*Alg}^{\mathrm{nu}} \end{align*}

which is on objects given by $\mathrm{Bd}(A) \coloneqq \{ a \in A \vert \| a \|_{A, \mathrm{max}} < \infty\}$ and on morphisms it is the restriction.

Proof

follows from:

  • maps of star algebras are contractive for the maximal seminorm
