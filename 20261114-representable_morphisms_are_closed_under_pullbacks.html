representable morphisms are closed under pullbacks

Proposition

The class of representable morphism is closed under pullbacks in $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$

Proof

follows from the pasting lemma: For any representable morphism $\mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{G}$, any $\mathcal{H} \rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ and any $よ(c) \rightarrow \mathcal{H}$ there exists a diagram 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
よ(c) \times_{\mathcal{G}} \mathcal{F} \rar \dar & \mathcal{H} \times_{\mathcal{G}} \mathcal{F} \rar \dar & \mathcal{F} \dar \\
よ(c) \rar & \mathcal{H} \dar & \mathcal{G}
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
