trivial fibration between kan complexes and homotopy equivalent kan fibration

Proposition

Let $K,L$ be Kan complexes and $f: K \rightarrow L$ a morphism of simplicial sets

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a kan fibration and simplicial homotopy equivalence
  2. $f$ is a trivial fibration

Proof
