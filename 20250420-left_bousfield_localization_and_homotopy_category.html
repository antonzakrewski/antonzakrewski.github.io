left bousfield localization and homotopy category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ infinity functors and $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$ an infinity adjunction. TFAE:

  1. it is a left bousfield localization
  2. the induced adjunction on the homotopy category is a left bousfield localization

Proof

use:

  • fully faithful infinity right adjoint and counit as equivalence
  • where one can detect equivalences on the level of homotopy categories
