regular function at a point (affine variety)

Definition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $Y \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ a quasi-affine variety.

A set theoretic map $f: Y \rightarrow K$ is said to be regular at $p \in O$, if there exists an open set $O \subseteq Y$ and polynomials $f,h \in K[X_1,...,X_n]$ such that $0 \not\in h[O]$ and

\begin{align*} f = \frac{g}{h} && \text{ on O} \end{align*}
