internal hom of a monoidal category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a monoidal category and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

a bifunctor

Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be an inner hom, if there exists a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbb{1}, \mathcal{F}(-,-)) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,-) \end{align*}
