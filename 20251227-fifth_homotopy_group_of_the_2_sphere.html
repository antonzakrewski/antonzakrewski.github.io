fifth homotopy group of the 2-sphere

Proposition

There exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \pi_5(S^3) \cong \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

Proof

using homology of the truncation of a sphere we get

\begin{align*} \pi_5(S^3) \cong& H_6(\tau_{\leq 4} S^3, \mathbb{Z}) \\ \cong& H^7(\tau_{\leq 4} S^3, \mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

where the last step follows from universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology
