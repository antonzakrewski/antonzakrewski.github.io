tensor product of chain complexes functor over a commutative ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod})$ the category of chain complexes over the category RMod. Then the tensor product of chain complexes functor naturally restricts to

\begin{align*} - \otimes_R -: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{R-Mod}) \times \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{R-Mod}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{R-Mod}) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • module over a commutative ring as canonical bimodule
  • tensor product of chain complexes of bimodules functor
