slice category of a category admitting I shaped colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $X \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ an object and $\mathcal{C} / X$ the slice category Suppose $I$ is an indexing category and $\mathcal{C}$ admits colimits of the shape $I$

Then so does $\mathcal{C} / X$

Proof

corollary of colimit in a slice category as one can use the construction to get a limit
