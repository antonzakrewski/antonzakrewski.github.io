infinity category with weak equivalences and fibrations

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category. Then an infinity category with weak equivalences and fibrations is given by two subcollections $\mathrm{W},\mathrm{F}$ such that

  1. $F$ is closed under composition, contains all identities and is closed under pullbacks
  2. $W$ satisfies two out of three and contains all identities
  3. $F \cap W$ is closed under pullbacks
  4. factorization
