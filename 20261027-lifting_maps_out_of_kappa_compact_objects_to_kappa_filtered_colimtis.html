lifting maps out of kappa compact objects to kappa filtered colimits

Proposition

Let $A$ be a kappa compact object in a category (with enough $\kappa$ filtered colimit). Let $I$ be a $\kappa$ filtered category and $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}: I \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be functors and $\eta: \mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ is a natural transformation. Let $f: A \rightarrow \mathcal{G}(i)$ be a map

Let $\mathrm{Lift}_{f,\eta(i): \mathcal{F}(i) \rightarrow \mathcal{G}(i)}$ be the anima of lifts, i.e., the pullback

Then for any map $g: A \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} \mathcal{G}$, choose a lift $g_{i_0}: A \rightarrow \mathcal{G}(i_0)$ (by compactness). Then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{i \in I_{i_0/}} \mathrm{Lift}_{g_{i}, \eta(i)} \simeq \mathrm{Lift}_{g, \mathrm{colim}_{i \in I_{/i_0}} \eta} \end{align*}

of anima.

Here $g_i$ is the map given by evaluating the map $\mathrm{const}_{I_{i_0/}}(*) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A, \mathcal{G}(-))$ which is adjoint to the map $* \rightarrow \lim_{I_{i_0/}} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A, \mathcal{G}(-)) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A, \mathcal{G}(i_0))$ since $i_0$ is the initial object in $I_{i_0/}$ (cf. inclusion of the initial object is initial).

In particular, any lift

can be lifted to a commutative diagram
