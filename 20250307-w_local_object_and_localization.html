S-local object and localization

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $x \in \mathcal{C}$ an object and $W \subseteq \mathcal{C}_1$ a collection of morphisms Suppose $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is a right bousfield localization for $W$.

TFAE:

  1. $x$ is W-local
  1. $x \in \mathrm{ess im}(\mathcal{R}(\mathcal{C}))$ lies in the essential image of a functor

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

where $\mathcal{L}(f)$ is an equivalence.

2) $\implies$ 1)

Note that the infinity counit lies in $W$

\begin{align*} x \rightarrow \mathcal{RL}(x) \in W \end{align*}

since the infinity unit

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}(x) \rightarrow \mathcal{RL}(x) \end{align*}

is an equivalence

Then we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{RL}(x),x) \rightarrow \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,x) \end{align*}

as equivalence, hence also $x \simeq \mathcal{LR}(x)$
