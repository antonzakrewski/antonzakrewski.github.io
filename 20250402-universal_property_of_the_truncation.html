universal property of the n-truncation

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be presentable infinity categories, where $\mathcal{D}$ is in addition an n-category. Then composition with the <>

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}}(\tau_{\leq n}\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}
