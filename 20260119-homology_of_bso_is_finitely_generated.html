homology of BSO is finitely generated

Proposition

The homology of $\mathrm{BSO}$ is degreewise finitely generated group.

Proof

a)

using the fibre sequence of SO(n) we get that the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{SO}(n) \rightarrow \mathrm{SO}(n+1) \end{align*}

is (roughly ?) $n+1$ n-connected map.

So

b)

  • homology of SO(n) is finitely generated
  • finitely generated homology of a group implies finitely generated homology of its classifying space
