pullback functor of a 3-functor formalism

Definition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Corr}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{E}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}$ be a 3-functor formalism and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ any morphism. Then the pullback functor is defined as induced map of

\begin{align*} Y \leadsto X \end{align*}
\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
& X \arrow[equal, ]{rd}{} \arrow[]{ld}{f}& \\
Y & X
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
