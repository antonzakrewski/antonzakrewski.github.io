abelian category C_A,B

Definition

Let $\mathcal{B} \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ be an abelian subcategory closed under quotients and subobjects. Then $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}}$ is defined as subcategory of $\prod_{i=1}^2 \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathrm{Pro}(\mathcal{A}))$ containing

  1. objects given by $(B,A,u,v)$ where $u: B \rightarrow A$ and $v: \tilde{j}(A) \rightarrow B$ such that

a) $\tilde{j}(B) \xrightarrow{\tilde{j}(v)} \tilde{j}(A) \xrightarrow{v} B$ is the zero morphism b) the square

commutes where the left and bottom maps are induced by their respective counit

  1. morphisms are given by pairs of morphisms
\begin{align*} A \rightarrow A' B \rightarrow B' \end{align*}

making everything commute
