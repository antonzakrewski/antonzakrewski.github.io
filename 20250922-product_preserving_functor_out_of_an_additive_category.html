product preserving functor out of an additive category lifts to commutative groups

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{E}$ be an additive category. Then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{E}, \mathrm{CGrp}(\mathcal{C})) \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{E}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

Proof

use:

  • $\mathrm{CGrp}(\mathcal{E}) \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ is an equivalence.
  • $\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{red,segal, group}}(\mathrm{Fin}_*, -)$ is a functor out of $\mathrm{Cat}^{\prod}$ (wide subcategory containing product preserving functors)
  • composition should give identity

universality: 2.5
