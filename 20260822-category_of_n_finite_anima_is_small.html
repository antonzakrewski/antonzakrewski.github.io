category of n-pi-finite anima is small

Proposition

The full subcategory of n-pi finite anima is a small category.

Proof

Let $X$ be a n-pi finite anima, w.l.o.g. connected. Then it is a colimit of a functor $B \pi_1 \rightarrow \{\tilde{X}\}$ over the universal cover.

So it suffices to show that the category of simply connected, \(n\)-$\pi$ finite anima is a small category, as

\begin{align*} \coprod_{G \textrm{finite}} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{BG}, \mathrm{An}_{\geq 1, n-\pi-\textrm{finite}}) \end{align*}

is a small category.

But for any simply connected $\pi$ finite anima we may use fundamental theorem of serre class theory for finitely generated groups and hence have countably many, countable choices.
