hom chain complex functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes. Then the hom chain functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}(-,-)}: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}}) \times \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ab} \\ (A,B) \mapsto& \underline{\mathrm{Hom}(A,B)} \\ (f^{\mathrm{op}}, g) \mapsto& <> \end{align*}
