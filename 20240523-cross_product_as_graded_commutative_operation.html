cross product as graded commutative operation

Proposition

Let $X,X',Y,Y',Z,Z'$ be topological spaces and $f: X' \rightarrow X, g: Y' \rightarrow Y, h: Z' \rightarrow Z$ be continuous maps Suppose furthermore $L,M,N$ are \(R\)-modules over a commutative ring $R$ and $\alpha \in H^p(X;L), \beta \in H^q(Y;M), \gamma \in H^r(Z;N)$

\begin{align*} t^*(\alpha \times \beta) = (-1)^{\vert \alpha \vert \vert \beta \vert} (\beta \times \alpha) \end{align*}

where $t$ is given by

\begin{align*} t: X \times Y \rightarrow& Y \times X \\ (x,y) \mapsto& (y,x) \end{align*}

and $\vert - \vert$ is the degree of an element

Proof
