initial scheme

Proposition

The affine scheme $\mathrm{Spec}(0)$ is the initial object in the category of schemes

Proof

Observe that it is a scheme. Then let $X$ be any scheme. The empty sieve on the zero ring is Zariski-covering, since it generates the unit ideal. Hence descent along the empty sieve implies that $X(0) \cong *$.

Therefore $\mathrm{Spec}(0)$ is the initial object.
