anderson dual spectrum

Definition

The anderson dual spectrum $I_{\mathbb{Z}}$ is defined as fiber

\begin{align*} I_{\mathbb{Z}} \coloneqq \mathrm{fib}(I_{\mathbb{Q}} \rightarrow I_{\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}}) \end{align*}

of the

  1. brown comenetz dual for an injective abelian group for $\mathbb{Q}$
  2. brown comenetz dual of Q/Z

where the map is induced by the canonical group-homomorphism $\mathbb{Q} \rightarrow \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$.
