upper bound of minimal degree for a graph without perfect matching

Proposition

Let $G = ([n],E)$ be a Graph without perfect matching. Then

\begin{align*} \delta(G) \leq& \begin{cases} \frac{n}{2} - 1 & \mbox{if } n \mathrm{ even} \\ (n-1) & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Furthermore this bound is optimal
