joyal equivalence and inner hom

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{sSet}$ be the category sSet and $p: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor between infinity categories TFAE:

  1. $p$ is a joyal equivalence
  2. postcomposition on the internal hom is a joyal equivalence
  3. precomposition on the internal hom is a joyal equivalence

Proof
