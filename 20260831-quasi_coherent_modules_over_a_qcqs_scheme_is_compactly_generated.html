quasi-coherent modules over a qcqs scheme is compactly generated

Proposition

The category of quasi-coherent modules over a qcqs scheme is compactly generated.

Proof

follows from:

  • inclusion of PrL kappa into PrL preserves kappa small limits
  • qcqs scheme is finite colimit of affine schemes
  • where the functor is limit preserving $\mathcal{P}(\mathrm{Aff})^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{PrL}_{\omega_0}$
