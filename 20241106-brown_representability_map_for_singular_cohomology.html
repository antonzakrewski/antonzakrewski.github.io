brown representability map for singular cohomology

Definition / Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $A$ an abelian group Then the brown representability map is defined as set theoretic map

\begin{align*} \chi_n^A: [X, K(A,n)] \rightarrow& H^n(X;A) \\ [f] \mapsto& f^*(\iota_n^A) \end{align*}

which is natural with respect to $X$

here $\iota_n^A$ is defined as unique preimage of the $\mathrm{id}: A \rightarrow A$ under n-th cohomology of the Eilenberg-MacLane space with coefficients

Proof

follows as singular cohomology functor is homotopy invariant
