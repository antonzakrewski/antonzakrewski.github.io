torsion free module in general not flat

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod and $N$ a torsion-free module Then $N$ is in general not flat

Proof

Consider $R = K[x,y]$ for $K$ a field and $N = (x,y)$. Then it is torsion free (cf. submodule of a torsion free module as torsion free module) but not flat: consider

\begin{align*} \cdot y: k[x,y]/(x) \rightarrow k[x,y]/(x) \end{align*}

which is injective

it follows that

\begin{align*} (1 \otimes x) \neq 0 \end{align*}

in $K[x,y]/(x)$ by (??)

But

\begin{align*} (x \otimes y) = 0 \end{align*}

since

\begin{align*} (y \otimes x) =& (1 \otimes y \cdot x) \\ =& (1 \otimes x \cdot y) \\ =& (x \otimes y) \\ =& (0 \otimes y) \\ =& 0 \end{align*}
