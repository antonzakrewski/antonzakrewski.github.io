limit formula for the coordinate ring

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor, then there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O}(X) \cong \lim_{x: \mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow X} R \end{align*}

where the limit is indexed by the category of elements $\mathrm{El}(X)^{\mathrm{op}}$.

Proof

use:

  • infinity presheaf as colimit of representables

which shows that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O}(X) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k^{\mathrm{op}})}(\mathrm{colim}_{?} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{CAlg}}(R,-) ,\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{CAlg}}(K[x],-)) \\ \lim_{x: \mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow X} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k^{\mathrm{op}})}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{CAlg}}(R,-) ,\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{CAlg}}(K[x],-)) \\ \lim_{x: \mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow X} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{CAlg}}(K[x],R) \\ \lim_{x: \mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow X} R \end{align*}

where we have used yoneda.
