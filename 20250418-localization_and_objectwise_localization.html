localization and objectwise left bousfield localization

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathcal{C}_W \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ a full sub infinity category.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}_W \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is a left bousfield localization
  2. for each $X \in \mathcal{C}$ there exists an $X_W$ and a morphism $f: X \rightarrow X_W$ such that the induced morphism on mapping anima
\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X_W,Y) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y) \end{align*}

is an equivalence for $Y \in \mathcal{C}$
