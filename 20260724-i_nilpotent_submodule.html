maximal I-nilpotent submodule

Definition / Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $I \subseteq R$ an ideal. Then the collection of I-nilpotent elements is a submodule

Proof

Let $m_1, m_2 \in M$ and $r \in M$. Let $f \in R$ be an arbitrary element, then there exist $n_1,n_2 \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $f^{n_i} m_i = 0$. In particular for $n = \mathrm{max}(n_1,n_2)$ we get

\begin{align*} f^n(m_1 + r m_2) =& f^n m_1 + r \cdot f^n m_2 \\ =& 0 + 0 \\ =& 0 \end{align*}
