cutting a short exact sequence out of a long exact sequence

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and

a sequence

  1. the sequence is long exact sequence
  2. the diagram

admits short exact sequences

or

or

  1. rewrite $\mathrm{coker}(?) = \mathrm{im}(?) \simeq \mathrm{ker}(?)$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

exact at $\mathrm{ker}(d_n)$

see:

  • exact sequence and monomorphism
  • map out of kernel as monomorphism

exact at $\mathrm{coker}(d_{n+1})$

  • exact sequence and epimorphisms
  • map into cokernel as epimorphism

exact at $A_n$

sloppy

follows from the natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ker}(d_n) \cong \mathrm{im}(d_{n+1}) \end{align*}

and quotient modulo image as cokernel, as

\begin{align*} A_n/\mathrm{ker}(d_n) \cong A_n/\mathrm{im}(d_{n+1}) \end{align*}

showing that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ker}(A_n \rightarrow A_n/\mathrm{im}(d_{n+1})) \cong& \mathrm{ker}(A_n \rightarrow A_{n}/\mathrm{ker}(d_n)) \\ \cong& \mathrm{ker}(d_n) \\ \cong& \mathrm{im}(d_{n+1}) \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)

it follows that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{im}(\mathrm{ker}(d_n) \hookrightarrow A_n) \cong \mathrm{ker}(d_n) \end{align*}

(cf. image of a monomorphism isomorphic to the domain)

and furthermore

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ker}(\mathrm{coker}(d_{n+1}) = \mathrm{im}(d_{n+1}) \end{align*}

cf. image in an abelian category
