rational spectrum and homotopy groups

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum. TFAE:

  1. $\pi_*(X)$ is a $\mathbb{Q}$ (graded) vector space
  2. $X$ lifts to $\mathrm{LMod}_{\mathbb{Q}}(\mathrm{Sp})$
  3. $X$ is a rational spectrum

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

we claim that $X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Q}$ and $X$ are equivalent, hence $X$ lifts as $X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Q}$. For that observe that $\mathbb{S}_{\mathbb{Q}} \simeq \mathbb{Q}$ (cf. stable rational homotopy groups of spheres) and the R-localization is objectwise a sequential colimit given by multiplication by $p_1,...,p_n$. But $- \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} -$ preserves colimits in both variables and multiplication by $p_1 \cdot ... \cdot p_n$ is an equivalence on $X$: This can be seen as taking homotopy groups is an additive functor and multiplication by $p$ is an equivalence on homotopy groups, so whitehead shows this claim.

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from extension of scalars, i.e.
