Proposition

Let $Z \hookrightarrow X$ be a closed immersion of affine schemes and $\mathcal{E}$ a finite locally free sheaf on $Z$. Then there exists an affine etale neighbourhood $V \rightarrow X$ of $Z$ and a finite locally free sheaf $\widehat{\mathcal{E}}$ such that $\widehat{\mathcal{E}}_{Z} = \mathcal{E}$.
