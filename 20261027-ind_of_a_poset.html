explicit description of Ind of a poset

Proposition

LEt $P$ be a (nice ?) partially ordered set. Then $\mathrm{Ind}(P)$ is given by the directed subsets of $P$ (+ some quotient) with

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ind}(P)}(S,T) = \begin{cases} * & \mbox{if } \forall s \in S \exists t \in T : s \leq t \\ \emptyset & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

use that one can pull out limits colimits (compactness).

Proof
