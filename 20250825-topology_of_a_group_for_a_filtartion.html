topology of a group for a filtration

Definition

Let $R = R_0$ be a ring and $\mathrm{GrRMod}$ the category of graded modules and $M$ a graded module. Let $F_s$ be a filtration, then the topology of $M$ is given by the basis consisting of (preimages of) cosets, i.e.

\begin{align*} x + F_s \subseteq G \end{align*}

for $x \in G$
