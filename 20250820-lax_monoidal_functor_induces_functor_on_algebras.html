lax monoidal functor induces functor on Algebras

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}$ be an infinity operad. A lax monoidal functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\otimes} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}^{\otimes}$ induces a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}}(\mathcal{C}^{\otimes}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}}(\mathcal{D}^{\otimes}) \end{align*}

Proof

follows as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}}(\mathcal{D}^{\otimes}) \coloneqq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{operad}}(\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}, \mathcal{C}^{\otimes}) \\ \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}}(\mathcal{D}^{\otimes}) \coloneqq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{operad}}(\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}, \mathcal{D}^{\otimes}) \end{align*}
