finite product of locally homeomorphic spaces locally homeomorphic

Proposition

Let $(X,\mathcal{T}_X)$ and $(Y,\mathcal{T}_Y)$ and $(X', \mathcal{T}_X'), (Y, \mathcal{T}_Y')$ be topological spaces, such that $X$ is locally homeomorphic to $X'$ and $Y$ locally homeomorphic to $Y'$. Then the product topology $X \times Y$ is locally homeomorphic to $X' \times Y'$

Proof

Given a point $(x,y) \in X \times Y$, there exists local homeomorphisms $\varphi: U_x \rightarrow V_x, \psi: U_y \rightarrow V_y$. Thus

\begin{align*} \varphi \times \psi: U_x \times V_x \rightarrow& V_y \times V_y \end{align*}

is a homeomorphism (see: Produkt von Homeomorphismen als Homeomorphismus)
