left derived functor for an Ab5 category commutes with filtered colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, where $\mathcal{A}$ admits enough projectives and $\mathcal{B}$ is Ab5. Suppose $\mathcal{F}_i: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ are right exact functors, then the left derived functor commutes with filtered colimits

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_I(\mathcal{LF}_i) \cong \mathcal{L \mathrm{colim}(F_i)} \end{align*}
