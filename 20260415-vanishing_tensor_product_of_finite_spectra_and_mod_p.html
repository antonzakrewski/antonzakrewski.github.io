vanishing tensor product of bounded below spectra and mod p

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be bounded below spectra such that $X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} Y = 0$. Then there exists a prime $p$ such that $X/p = 0$ or $Y/p = 0$.

Proof

We need to show that multiplication with $p$ is an equivalence on $X$. using Fp kunneth for spectra we get

\begin{align*} H_*(X; \mathbb{F}_p) \otimes_{\mathbb{F}_p} H_*(X; \mathbb{F}_p) \simeq H_*(X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} Y, \mathbb{F}_p) \simeq 0 \end{align*}

as $\mathbb{F}_p$ is a field, this is just the tensor product of graded vector spaces. Hence we may assume that $H_*(X; \mathbb{F}_p)$ vanishes or equivalenty $X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{F}_p$ is trivial.

Now we may consider the short exact sequence $\mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{F}_p$ and tensoring with it gives the exact sequence

Now the right term vanishes, hence the map $X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z}$ is an equivalence. Therefore homology whitehead for bounded below spectra shows that multiplication with $\cdot p$ is an equivalence on $X$.

(we have used that $- \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} -$ is additive in both variables.)
