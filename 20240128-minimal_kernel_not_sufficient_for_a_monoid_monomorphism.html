minimal kernel not sufficient for a monoid monomorphism

Proposition

Let $M,M'$ be monoids and $\varphi: M \rightarrow M'$ a monoid-homomorphism, such that

\begin{align*} \varphi^{-1}[ \{1\}] = \{1\} \end{align*}

then $\varphi$ is in general not injective

Proof

Consider $\mathbb{N}_0$ as multiplicative monoid and the map

\begin{align*} \varphi: \mathbb{N}_0 \rightarrow& \mathbb{N}_0 \\ n \mapsto \begin{cases} 1 & \mbox{if } 1 \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
