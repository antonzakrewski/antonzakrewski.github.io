general linear group functor

Definition

Given category CRing, the general linear group functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{GL}_n: \mathrm{CRing} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Grp} \\ R \mapsto& \mathrm{GL}_n(R) \\ f: R \rightarrow S \mapsto& (A \mapsto f[A]) \\ \end{align*}

given as composite of the:

  • unit group functor
  • matrix ring functor
