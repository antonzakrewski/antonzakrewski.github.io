norm of the power of a self-adjoint operator

Proposition

Let $A \in \mathcal{B}(H)$ be a self-adjoint operator. Then $\| A^n \| = \| A \|^n$.

Proof

Observe that $A^n$ is self-adjoint, so $\| A^{2^n} \| = \| A \|^{2^n}$ by the c star identity and induction. Then by submultiplicativity we have

\begin{align*} \| A^n \| \leq \| A \|^n \end{align*}

To get the other equality, choose an $m$ such that $2^m \geq n$. Then

\begin{align*} \| A \|^{2^m} \leq \| A^{2^m} \| \leq \| A^n \| \cdot \| A^{2^m - n} \| \geq \| A \|^n \cdot \| A \|^{2^m - n} \end{align*}

which shows the other equality.
