spectral sequence functor from filtered spectra

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Fil}(\mathrm{Sp})$ be the category of filtered spectra and $\mathrm{SpecSeq}(\mathrm{Ab})$ the category of spectral sequences. Then the assignment of of a filtered spectrum to its spectral sequence refines to a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fil}(\mathrm{Sp}) \rightarrow \mathrm{SpecSeq}(\mathrm{Ab}) \end{align*}

Proof
