radical of a modul quotient for a primary ideal

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ a prime ideal and $\mathfrak{q}$ a p-primary ideal Then the radical of the module quotient

\begin{align*} \sqrt{(\mathfrak{q} : a)} = \begin{cases} A & \mbox{if } a \in \mathfrak{q} \\ \mathfrak{p} & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

a)

Suppose $a \in \mathfrak{q}$, then $1 \cdot a = a \in \mathfrak{q}$ showing that $1 \in (\mathfrak{a}: a)$ and hence $(\mathfrak{q} : a) = A$. Therefore

\begin{align*} \sqrt{(\mathfrak{q} : a)}\sqrt{A} = A \end{align*}

b)

Note that

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{q} \subseteq (\mathfrak{q} : a) \end{align*}

hence $\sqrt{(\mathfrak{q} : a)} \supseteq \sqrt{\mathfrak{q}} = \mathfrak{p}$

Let $b \in (\mathfrak{q} : a)$. Then $b \cdot a \in \mathfrak{q}$ and as $a \not\in \mathfrak{q}$ there exists an $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that

\begin{align*} b^n \in \mathfrak{q} \end{align*}

as $\mathfrak{q}$ is primary.

Therefore $b \in \sqrt{\mathfrak{q}} = \mathfrak{p}$

Hence $\mathfrak{q} \subseteq (\mathfrak{q} : a) \subseteq \mathfrak{p}$ and as prime ideal as radical ideal
