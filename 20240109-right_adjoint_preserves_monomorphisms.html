right adjoint preserves monomorphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{R}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a right adjoint. Then $\mathcal{R}$ preserves monomorphisms

Proof

corollary of

  1. right adjoint preserves limits
  2. pullback as limit
  3. functor preserving pullbacks preserves monomorphisms
