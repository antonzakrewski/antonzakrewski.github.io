cartesian product and intersection

Proposition

Let $S_i, T_i$ be sets, then it holds for the Cartesian product

\begin{align*} \left(\prod_{i \in I} S_i\right) \cap \left(\prod_{i \in I} T_i\right) = \prod_{i \in I} \left(S_i \cap T_i\right) \end{align*}

Proof

Let $(x_i) \in \prod_{i \in I} \left(S_i \cap T_i\right)$, then it is equivalent, that for any $i \in I: x_i \in (S_i \cap T_i)$. Thus it is equivalent to $x_i \in S_i$ and $x_i \in T_i$, hence $(x_i) \in \prod_{i \in I} S_i, \prod_{i \in I} T_i$
