category of pro objects as subcategory

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category, then the category $\mathrm{Pro}(\mathcal{C})$ is equivalently given by the full subcategory

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Pro}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{An})^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}

which contains the covariant representable functor $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,-)$ and is closed under filtered colimits in $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{An})$ or cofiltered limits in $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{An})^{\mathrm{op}}$.
