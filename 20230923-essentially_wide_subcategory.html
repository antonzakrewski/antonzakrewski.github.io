essentially wide subcategory

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. An essentially wide subcategory is a category, such that for each isomorphism class $[A]$ for $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ there exists an $B \in \mathcal{D}$ such that

\begin{align*} [A] = [B] \end{align*}

in $\mathcal{C}$
