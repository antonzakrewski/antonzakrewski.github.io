Fp-cohomology of the Eilenberg-MacLane spectrum of a finitely generated abelian group

Proposition

Let $M \oplus \mathbb{Z}^n \oplus \bigoplus_{i=1}^n \mathbb{Z}/p_{i}^{n_i}$ be a finitely generated group, regarded as Eilenberg-MacLane spectrum. Then the $\mathbb{F}_p$ cohomology of $M$ is given by

\begin{align*} H^*(M, \mathbb{F}_p) \cong H^*(\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{F}_p)^n \oplus \bigoplus_{p_i = p, n_i \geq 2} (H^*(\mathbb{F}_p,\mathbb{F}_p) \oplus H^{*\pm 1}(\mathbb{F}_p, \mathbb{F}_p)) \oplus \bigoplus_{p_i = p, n_i = 1} H^*(\mathbb{F}_p, \mathbb{F}_p) \end{align*}

but the splitting on the right hand site is/might not be compatible with the Fp steenrod algebra

Proof

$p$ and $q$ coprime

We claim that $H^*(\mathbb{Z}/q^n \mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{F}_p) \cong 0$. For this consider the cofiber sequence $\mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\cdot p} \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{F}_p$ which induces a cofiber sequence $\mathbb{Z} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z}/q^n \xrightarrow{\cdot p \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathrm{id}} \mathbb{Z} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z}/q^n \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{F}_p \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z}/q^n \mathbb{Z}$. But $- \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} -$ is additive in both variables, so $(\cdot p) \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathrm{id}_{\mathbb{Z}/q^n \mathbb{Z}}$ is the same map as $\mathrm{id}_{\mathbb{Z}} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} (\cdot p)$. But multiplication with $p$ is an isomorphis on $\mathbb{Z}/q^n \mathbb{Z}$, so the cofiber of $(\cdot p) \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathrm{id}_{\mathbb{Z}/q^n \mathbb{Z}}$ or equivalently of $\mathrm{id}_{\mathbb{Z}} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} (\cdot p)$ is given by $0$.

$p^n$

Observe that there exists a cofiber sequence $\mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\cdot p^n} \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}/p^n \mathbb{Z}$. Then tensoring with $- \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{F}_p$ gives the cofiber sequence

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Z} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{F}_p \xrightarrow{\mathrm{id} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \cdot p} \mathbb{Z} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{F}_p \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}/p^n \mathbb{Z} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{F}_p \end{align*}

where the first map is equivalent to the zero map (cf. bi-additive functor and multiplication with p, multiplication by $p$ is the zero map on $\mathbb{F}_p$).
