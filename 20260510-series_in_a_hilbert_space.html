series in a hilbert space

Proposition

Let $X$ be a Hilbert space and $x_n$ a sequence of pairwise orthogonal vectors. TFAE:

  1. $\sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \| x_n \|^2 < \infty$
  2. $\sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} x_n$ exists in $X$

Proof

Let $S_n \coloneqq \sum_{j=1}^n x_j$ or $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Let $m \geq n$, then

\begin{align*} \| S_m - S_n \|^2 =& \| \sum_{j=n+1}^m x_j \|^2 \\ =& \sum_{j,k = n+1}^m \langle x_j,x_k\rangle \\ =& \sum_{j=n+1}ym \| x_j\|^2 = \sum_{j=1}^n \| x_j \|^2 - \sum_{j=1}^n \| x_j \|^2 \end{align*}
