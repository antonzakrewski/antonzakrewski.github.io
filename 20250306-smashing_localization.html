smashing localization

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a symmetric monoidal infinity category. Then a right bousfield localization $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is said to be a smashing localization, if there exists an object $I$ such that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L} \simeq (-) \otimes I \end{align*}
