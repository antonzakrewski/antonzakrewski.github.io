coskeleton of a simplicial set

Proposition

Let $\Delta$ be the simplex category, $\Delta_{\leq n}$ the n-reduced simplex category and $X$ a simplicial set Then the \(n\)-th coskeleton is given by the simplicial set

\begin{align*} \mathrm{cosk}_n(X)_k = \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\mathrm{sk}_n(\Delta^k), X) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from <yoneda> and the adjunction

\begin{align*} \mathrm{cosk}_n(X)_k \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^k, \mathrm{cosk}_n(X)) \\ \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\mathrm{sk}_n(\Delta^k), X) \end{align*}
