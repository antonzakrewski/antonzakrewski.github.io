Wu's Formula 1

Proposition

Let $\pi: E \rightarrow B$ be a stable spherical fibration.

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}(v(\pi)) = w(\pi^{-1}) \end{align*}

where $\pi^{-1}$ is the stable spherical fibration

see:

  • total steenrod square
  • total wu class
  • total stiefel whitney class

Proof

Lets compute

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}(v(\pi) \cdot u(\pi)) \in \widehat{H^*}(\mathrm{Th}(\pi); \mathbb{F}_2) \end{align*}

see:

  • completed singular cohomology ring

By definition of $v(\pi)$ we find that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}(v(\pi)\cdot u(\pi)) = \mathrm{Sq}[\chi(\mathrm{Sq})(u(\pi))] = u(\pi) \end{align*}

where the second equality is given by <topo3, 6.41.

On the other hand, using the compatibility of the steenrod squares iwth the module multiplication $v(\pi) \cdot u(\pi)$ (?? Topo3 6.24) we also have

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}(v(\pi) \cdot u(\pi)) =& \mathrm{Sq}(v(\pi)) \cdot \mathrm{Sq}(u(\pi)) \\ =& [\mathrm{Sq}(v(\pi)) \cdot w(\pi)] \cdot u(\pi) \end{align*}

The thom isomorphism for $\pi$ then implies that

\begin{align*} 1 = \mathrm{Sq}(v(\pi)) \cdot w(\pi) \end{align*}

so the fact that $w(\pi^{-1}) = w(\pi)^{-1}$ implies the theorem
