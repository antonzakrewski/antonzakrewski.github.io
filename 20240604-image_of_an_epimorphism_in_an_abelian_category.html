image of an epimorphism in an abelian category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, $f: A \rightarrow B$ a morphism. TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an epimorphism
  2. there exists a natural isomorphism $\mathrm{im}(f) \cong B$
  3. there exists a natural epimorphism $\mathrm{im}(f) \twoheadrightarrow B$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from

\begin{align*} \mathrm{im} \cong \mathrm{ker}(\mathrm{coker}) \end{align*}

and

  • minimal cokernel and epimorphism
  • isomorphic kernel and zero morphism

2) $\implies$ 3)

follows from map into image in an abelian category as epimorphism and composition of epimorphisms as epimorpgism Closure of Monomorphisms under Composition

3) $\implies$ 1)
