tensor algebra of a module

Definition / Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $M$ an \(R\)-module.

Then the tensor algebra of $M$ is defined as direct sum of modules

\begin{align*} T(M) \coloneqq& \bigoplus_{r = 0}^{\infty} V^{\otimes r} \\ \coloneqq& R \oplus V \oplus (V \otimes V) \oplus ... \end{align*}

with the multiplication induced by

\begin{align*} (v_1 \otimes v_2 \otimes ... \otimes v_n) \cdot (w_1 \otimes ... \otimes w_m) \coloneqq& (v_1 \otimes v_2 \otimes ... \otimes v_n \otimes w_1 \otimes ... \otimes w_m) \end{align*}
