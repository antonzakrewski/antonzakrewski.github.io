sheaf on a grothendieck topology

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category equipped with a grothendieck topology. Then a $\tau$ sheaf is a presheaf $\mathcal{F} \in \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ such that for all $\tau$ covering sieves $R \hookrightarrow よ(X)$, precomposition induces an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(R,\mathcal{F}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(よ(X), \mathcal{F}) \simeq \mathcal{F}(X) \end{align*}
