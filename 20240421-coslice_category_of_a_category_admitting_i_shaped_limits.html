coslice category of a category admitting I shaped limits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $X \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ an object and $X / \mathcal{C}$ the coslice category. Suppose $I$ is an indexing category and $\mathcal{C}$ admits limits of the shape $I$

Then so does $X / \mathcal{C}$

Proof

corollary of limit in a coslice category as one can use the construction to get a limit
