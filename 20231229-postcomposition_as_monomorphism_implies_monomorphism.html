precomposition as monomorphism implies monomorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $g \circ f$ a monomorphism. Then $f$ is also a monomorphism

Proof

Suppose there exists morphisms $h_1,h_2$ such that

\begin{align*} f \circ h_1 = f \circ h_2 \end{align*}

then we also get

\begin{align*} g \circ f \circ h_1 =& g \circ f \circ h_2 \\ (g \circ f) \circ h_1 = (g \circ f) \circ h_2 \end{align*}

and thus by assumption of $g \circ f$ being a monomorphism

\begin{align*} h_1 = h_2 \end{align*}

hence $f$ is a monomorphism
