inverse image sheaf functor preserves stalks

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a continuous map and $\mathcal{F}$ a sheaf on $Y$. Then for every point $x \in X$, there exists an equivalence of stalks

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_{f(x)} \simeq f^{-1}(\mathcal{F})_{x} \end{align*}
