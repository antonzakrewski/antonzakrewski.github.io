homotopy groups of the weak product

Proposition

Let $X_i$ be pointed anima and $\bigoplus X_i$ their weak product. Then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \pi_n(\bigoplus_{i \in I} X_i) \cong \bigoplus_{i \in I} \pi_n(X_i) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from

  • Category Rmod as abelian category
  • homotopy group functor preserves filtered colimits & homotopy group functor preserves products
