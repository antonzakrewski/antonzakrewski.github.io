completion of a module

Definition / Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $I \subseteq R$ an ideal and $M$ an \(R\)-module. Then the completion of $M$ with respect to $I$ is defined as \(\widehat{R}\)-module

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim} M/I^n \end{align*}

with the module structure

\begin{align*} (r_n + I^n) \cdot (m_n + I^n \cdot M) \coloneqq (r_n m_n + I^n \cdot M) \end{align*}

resp.

or

Proof

module structure follows from product of modules over different rings as module over the product, if the restriction is welldefined

Let $(r_n), (m_n)$ be elements. Then

\begin{align*} m_{n+1} \equiv& m_n \mod{I^n \cdot M} \\ r_{n+1} \equiv& r_n \mod{I^n} \\ \end{align*}

and therefore

\begin{align*} r_{n+1} m_{n+1} \equiv r_n m_n \mod{I^n M} \end{align*}
