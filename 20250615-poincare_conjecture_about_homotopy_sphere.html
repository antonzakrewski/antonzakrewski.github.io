classical poincare conjecture about homotopy 3-sphere

Proposition

Let $X$ be a

  1. compact
  2. simply connected
  3. topological 3-manifold (without boundary)

Then $X$ is a homotopy 3-sphere

Proof

a) use Poincare duality

since $X$ is simply connected, it is orientable (cf. simply connected manifold as orientable manifold). Then as it is compact we get

\begin{align*} H_3(X, \mathbb{Z}) = \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

By absolute Hurewicz theorem we get

\begin{align*} H_0(X, \mathbb{Z}) =& \mathbb{Z} \\ H_1(X, \mathbb{Z}) =& 0 \\ \end{align*}

and hence by <poincare duality> $H^2(X, \mathbb{Z}) = 0$.

Now use that $\mathrm{Ext}^{0,1}(-,\mathbb{Z})$ detects zeros (missing) and closed orientable manifold and n-1 homology as torsion free to conclude that $H_2(X, \mathbb{Z}) = 0$.

The higher homology groups are zero by manifold argument

Now use homology isomorphism between simply connected spaces as weak equivalence.
