ring homomorphism and induced morphism by relative k1

Proposition

Let $f: R \rightarrow S$ be a ring-homomorphism and $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq R, \mathfrak{b} \subseteq S$ be ideals such that

\begin{align*} f[\mathfrak{a}] \subseteq f[\mathfrak{b}] \end{align*}

Then there exists an induced morphism

\begin{align*} K_1(f): K_1(R, \mathfrak{a}) \rightarrow K_1(S, \mathfrak{b}) \end{align*}

between the relative K1-Group of a ring and ideal

Proof
