Axiom of dependent choice

Axiom

Let $A$ be a set and $R \subseteq A \times A$ a left total relation.

Then for each $x \in A$ there exists a map

\begin{align*} f: \mathbb{N} \rightarrow A \end{align*}

such that

\begin{align*} (f(n), f(n+1)) \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} f(0) = x \end{align*}
