transcendental field functor

Definition

Let $K$ be a field, then the transcendental field functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Trans}: \mathrm{Set}_{\mathrm{inj}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fld} \\ X \mapsto& K(X) (f: X \rightarrow Y) \mapsto& (f': K(X) \rightarrow K(Y)) \end{align*}

where

  1. $K(X)$ is the purely transcendental field extension
  2. $f'$ is the field-homomorphism
\begin{align*} f': K(X) \rightarrow K(Y) \\ k \mapsto& k \\ x \mapsto f(x) \\ \end{align*}
