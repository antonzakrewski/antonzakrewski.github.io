group ring unit group adjunction

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $\mathrm{R-Alg}$ the category RAlg, $\mathrm{Grp}$ the category Group Then the group ring functor $R[-]$ is a left adjoint to the unit group functor $(-)^{\times}$

Proof

right adjoint and universal morphism

Let $A$ be a group, then define

\begin{align*} \iota: A \rightarrow& R[A]^{\times} \\ a \mapsto& a \end{align*}

This is welldefined as $a^{-1}$ is an inverse and a group homomorphism as

\begin{align*} \iota(a \cdot b) =& a \cdot b \\ =& \iota(a) \cdot \iota(b) \end{align*}

Then define

\begin{align*} f: R[A] \rightarrow B \\ r \cdot a \mapsto& r \cdot \varphi(a) 0 \mapsto& 0 \\ (a + b) \mapsto& \varphi(a) + \varphi(b) \end{align*}

Then restriction to the units commutes by construction. It respects the additive group by definition, sends $1 = e \mapsto \varphi(e) = 1$ and by assumption of $\varphi$ as group homomorphism it also preserves the multiplicative structure.
