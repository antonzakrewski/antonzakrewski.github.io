multiplication by coprime q is an equivalence on X/p^n

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category, $X$ an object in $\mathcal{C}$, $p,q$ coprime primes, $n,m \geq 1$. Then multiplication by $q^m$ is an equivalence :

\begin{align*} \cdot q^m \colon X/p^n \rightarrow X/p^n \end{align*}

\begin{align*} l_p \cdot (p^{n+1}) + 1 \sim 0 + 1 \sim \mathrm{id} \end{align*}
