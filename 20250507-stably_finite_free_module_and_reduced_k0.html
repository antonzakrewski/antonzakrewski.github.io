stably finite free module and reduced K0

Proposition

Let $P$ be a finite projective module over $R$.

TFAE:

  1. $P$ is stably finite free module
  2. $[P] = 0 \in \tilde{K}_0(R)$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)

Note that

\begin{align*} \tilde{K}_0(R) \coloneqq \mathrm{coker}(\mathbb{Z} \rightarrow K_0(R)) \end{align*}

Then $[p] = \pm [R^n]$ in $K_0(R)$.

$+$

Then there exists some $m$ with

\begin{align*} P \otimes R^m \ccong R^{n+m} \end{align*}

$-$

Then there exists some $m$ with

\begin{align*} P \oplus R^{n+m} \cong R^m \end{align*}
