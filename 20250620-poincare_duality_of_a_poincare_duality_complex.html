poincare duality of a poincare duality complex

Proposition

Let $X$ be a connected poincare duality complex of dimension $d$. Then the unit

\begin{align*} \mathbb{S} \rightarrow r_*r^*(\mathbb{S}) \simeq r_{!} D_X \end{align*}

determines via the stable hurewicz homomorphism a class $[X] \in H_d(X; \omega_X)$ where the generalized cap product induces an isomorphism

\begin{align*} H^*(X;\mathfrak{M}) \rightarrow H_{d-*}(X; \mathfrak{M}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \omega_X) \end{align*}
