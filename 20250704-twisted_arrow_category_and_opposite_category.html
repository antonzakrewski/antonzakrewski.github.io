twisted arrow category and opposite category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category. Then there exists a natural equvialence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tw}(\mathcal{C}) \simeq \mathrm{Tw}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}) \end{align*}

Proof

note that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tw}(\mathcal{C})_n \coloneqq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^n \star (\Delta^n)^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{C} ) \end{align*}

Thus we may consider the natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^n \star ((\Delta^n)^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{C}) \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}((\Delta^n \star ((\Delta^n)^{\mathrm{op}})^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(((\Delta^n^{\mathrm{op}})^{\mathrm{op}} \star (\Delta^n)^{\mathrm{op}}), \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}((\Delta^n \star (\Delta^n)^{\mathrm{op}}), \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}) \\ \end{align*}

where we use that

  1. the opposite simplicial set functor induces equivalence on inner hom
  2. the opposite of the join is given by switching factors (citation needed)
  3. $((-)^{\mathrm{op}})^{\mathrm{op}}$ is given by the identity

Hence we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tw}(\mathcal{C})_n \cong \mathrm{Tw}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}_n \end{align*}

in a natural way, i.e.

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tw}(\mathcal{C}) \cong \mathrm{Tw}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}) \end{align*}
