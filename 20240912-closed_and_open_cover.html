closed and open cover

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space, $U_i$ an open cover and $A \subseteq X$ a subset TFAE:

  1. $A$ is closed
  2. $A \cap U_i \subseteq U_i$ is closed in the subspace

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

then $U_i \setminus A$ is open in the subspace by Übertragung der Offenheit von der Unterraumtopologie it follows that $X \setminus A$ is a union of opens. Hence $A$ is closed

2) $\implies$ 1)
