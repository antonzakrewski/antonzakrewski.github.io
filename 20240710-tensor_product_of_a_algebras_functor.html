tensor product of A-Algebras functor

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, category Alg_A the category of A-Algebras. Then the tensor product fuctor is defined

\begin{align*} \otimes: \mathrm{Alg}_A \times \mathrm{Alg}_A \rightarrow& \mathrm{Alg}_A \\ (B,C) \mapsto& B \otimes_A C \\ (f,g) \mapsto& f \otimes_A g \end{align*}

where the underlying set is given by the commutative tensor functor of modules and the algebra structure via:

  1. $(\beta_1 \otimes_A \gamma_1) \cdot (\beta_2 \otimes_A \gamma_2) = (\beta_1 \cdot \beta_2 \otimes \gamma_1 \cdot \gamma_2)$
  2. $\alpha \mapsto (\alpha \otimes 1) = \alpha \cdot (1 \otimes 1) = (1 \otimes \alpha)$

Proof
