slice category over a monoid inherits a monoidal structure

Definition / Proposition

Let $M$ be an anima valued monoid, then under the Anima slice as Functors into Anima and day convolution of $\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{day}}(M, \mathrm{An}^{\times})$ (where we view $\mathrm{An}$ with the cartesian monoidal structure) the slice category $\mathrm{An}_{/M}$ inherits a monoidal structure, which is pointwise given by

\begin{align*} (N \rightarrow M) \boxtimes (N' \rightarrow M) = N \times N' \rightarrow M \times M \rightarrow M \end{align*}

where the latter map is the multiplication map
