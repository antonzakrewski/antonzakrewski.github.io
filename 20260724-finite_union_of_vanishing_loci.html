finite union of vanishing loci

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor and $F_i$ a finite family of subsets of $\mathcal{O}(X)$. Then

\begin{align*} V(F_1) \cap ... \cap V(F_n) \subseteq V(F_1 \cdot ... \cdot F_n) \end{align*}

where the intersection is either the categorical colimit or the pointwise union.
