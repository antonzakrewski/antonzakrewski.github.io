monomorphism in Grp in general not normal

Proposition

Let $\iota: G \rightarrow H$ be a monomorphism in category Group. Then $\iota$ is in general not a normal monomorphism

Proof

Let $\iota: g \rightarrow H$ be a contranormal subgroup inclusion (cf. existence of a contranormal subgroup) Then by kernel as normal subgroup this can not be a kernel.
