relative infinite general linear group and projection

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq R$ an ideal and $\mathrm{GL}(R,\mathfrak{a})$ the relative infinite general linear group. Then

\begin{align*} \varphi: \mathrm{GL}(R,\mathfrak{a}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{ker}(\pi_{\mathfrak{a}}: \mathrm{GL}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{GL}(R/\mathfrak{a})) \\ (A,B) \mapsto& B \\ \end{align*}

is a group-isomorphism
