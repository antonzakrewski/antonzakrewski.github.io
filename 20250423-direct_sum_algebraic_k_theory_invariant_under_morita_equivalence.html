direct sum algebraic k-theory invariant under morita equivalence

Proposition

Let $R,S$ be rings and $\varphi: \mathrm{RMod} \simeq \mathrm{SMod}$ be a Morita equivalence. Then $\varphi$ induces an isomorphism on K-theory

Proof

follows from:

  • morita equivalence induces equivalence on finitely generated projective modules
  • + functorality
