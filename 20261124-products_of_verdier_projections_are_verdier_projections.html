products of verdier projections are verdier projections

Proposition

the class of (karoubi-)verdier projections is stable under products in category Cat Perf

Proof

a)idempotent completion functor

idempotent completion preserves idempotent completion preserves limits which exist

b) <calculus of fractions>

We need to show

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\pi_i \mathcal{C}_i}((p_i x_i)_i, (p_i y_i))_i \simeq& \prod_{i} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_i}(p_i x_i, p_i y_i) \\ \simeq& \prod_{i} \mathrm{colim}_{…} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_i} x_i, y_i') \\ \end{align*}

now using Anima satisfies Ab6 to get

\begin{align*} \prod_{i} \mathrm{colim}_{…} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_i} x_i, y_i') \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{… + \text{distribute}}\prod_{i} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_i} x_i, y_i') \\ \end{align*}

where the RHS turns out to be the calculus of fractions of the product category.
