being a zero map is a zariski local property

Proposition

Being a zero morphism is a zariski-local property of modules.

Proof

recall that localization commutes with almost everything. Then a module-homomorphism $f: M \rightarrow N$ is the zero morphism if and only if $N \rightarrow \mathrm{coker}(f)$ is an injection (cf. injectivity of a map is a zariski local property).
