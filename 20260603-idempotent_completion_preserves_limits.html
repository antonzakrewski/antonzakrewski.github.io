idempotent completion preserves limits which exist

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, then the inclusion $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\natural}$ into the idempotent completion preserves limits

Proof

follows from the concrete:

  • idempotent completion is retract closure of yoneda embedding
  • yoneda embedding preserves limits
