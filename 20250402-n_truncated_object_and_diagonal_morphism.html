n-truncated object and diagonal morphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $X \in \mathcal{C}$ be an object.

TFAE:

  1. $X$ is n-truncated object
  2. the diagonal morphism
\begin{align*} X \rightarrow X \times X \end{align*}

is (n-1)-truncated morphism

Proof

corollary of:

  • trivial homotopy group and homotopy group of the fiber of the diagonal
