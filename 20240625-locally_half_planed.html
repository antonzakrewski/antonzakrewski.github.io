locally half planed

Definition

Let $(M, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space. Then $M$ is said to be \(n+1\)-dimensional locally half planed, if for each point $m \in M$ there exists an open neighbourhood $U$ such that

\begin{align*} U \cong \begin{cases} \mathbb{R}^n \times \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0} \\ \mathbb{R}^{n+1} \end{cases} \end{align*}
