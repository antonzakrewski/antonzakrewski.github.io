cat dual stable satisfies ab6

Proposition

The category of stable dualizable categories satisfies Ab6 axiom

Proof

We want to show that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\prod I_i} \prod^{\mathrm{dual}} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \prod_{i}^{\mathrm{dual}} \mathrm{colim}_{I_i} \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

is an equivalence

here both rows are exact as $\prod$ and filtered colimits preserve exact sequence (cf. products of exact sequences in Cat dual stable & filtered colimit of dualizable stable categories).

Now $\mathrm{cat}^{\mathrm{perf}}$ satisfies Ab6, since cat perf is compactly generated & compactly generated category satisfies AB6.
