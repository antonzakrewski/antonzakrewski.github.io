associated graded functor of a finite exhaustive filtered object is conservative

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Fil}^{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal{A})$ the category of finitely filtrated objects. Then the underlying graded object functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{gr}_*: \mathrm{Fil}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Gr}(\mathcal{A}) \end{align*}

is a conservative functor

Proof

Proof by induction: we induct on the length of the filtration:

induction basis

assume that the filtration has length one. Then $\mathrm{gr}_*(-)$ recovers the original object

induction step

By assumption we have an isomorphism between $X(i),...,X(i-n+1)$. So assume that the graded is isomorphic.

Then we have short exact sequences

and the Five-Lemma shows that $X(i-n+1) \simeq Y(i-n+1)$
