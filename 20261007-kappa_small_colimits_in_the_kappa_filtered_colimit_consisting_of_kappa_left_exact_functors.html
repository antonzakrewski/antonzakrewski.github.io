kappa small colimits in the kappa filtered colimit consisting of kappa left exact functors

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal and $\mathcal{C}_i$ be a $\kappa$ filtered diagram of kappa left exact functor and categories with $\kappa$ small colimits Then the colimit $\mathrm{colim} \mathcal{C}_i$ also admits $\kappa$ small colimits and each projection $\mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} \mathcal{C}_i$ is $\kappa$ left exact.
