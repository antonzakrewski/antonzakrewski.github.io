unitalization functor to augmented Ring

Definition

Let $\mathrm{Rng}$ be the category Rng. Then the unitalization functor to category of augmented Rings is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{unit}: \mathrm{Rng} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ring}_* A \mapsto& (\mathbb{Z} \times A \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}) \\ f: A \rightarrow B \mapsto& f \end{align*}

where the multiplication in $\mathbb{Z} \times A$ is defined as

\begin{align*} (m, a) \cdot (n,b) =& (mn, n \cdot a + m \cdot b + ab) \end{align*}

the augmentation map

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Z} \times A \rightarrow& \mathbb{Z} \\ (n,a) \mapsto& n \end{align*}

and the ring-homomorphism

\begin{align*} f: \mathbb{Z} \times A \rightarrow& \mathbb{Z} \times B \\ (m,a) \mapsto (m, f(a)) \end{align*}
