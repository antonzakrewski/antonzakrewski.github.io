tensor square of abelian groups may be trivial

Proposition

Let $M$ be an abelian group such that $M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} M$ is the zero group Then $M$ is in general not trivial.

Proof

consider $M = \mathbb{Q}/ \mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{Q} / \mathbb{Z} \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$. Let $\frac{a}{a'} \otimes \frac{b}{b'}$ be an elementary tensor, then

\begin{align*} \frac{a}{a'} \otimes \frac{b}{b'} =& b' \cdot (\frac{a}{a'b'} \otimes \frac{b}{b'}) \\ =& \frac{a}{a'b'} \otimes b \\ =& \frac{a}{a'b'} \otimes 0 \\ =& 0 \end{align*}
