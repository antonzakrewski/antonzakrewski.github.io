saturated set and opposite morphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathrm{s}\mathcal{C}$ the category of simplicial objects and $S \subseteq \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$ a subcollection of morphisms

TFAE:

  1. $S$ is a saturated set
  2. $S^{\mathrm{op}}$ is saturated, where $()^\mathrm{op}$ is the opposite simplicial object functor

Proof

isomorphism preserves saturated sets
