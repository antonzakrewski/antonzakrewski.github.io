left bousfield localization as fully faihtful or localization

Proposition

Let $\gamma: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an infinity left adjoint and let $W := \gamma^{-1}[\mathcal{D}^{\simeq}]$ denote the morphisms, which become equivalences after applying $\gamma$ Let $\iota$ be the infinity right adjoint

TFAE:

  1. $\iota$ is fully faithful
  2. $\gamma$ is a dwyer-kan localization at $W$

In both cases $\gamma$ is called a left bousfield localization
