formal derivative of a power series ring

Definition

Let \(R\) be a ring, $R[[ X] ]$ the power series ring. Then the formal derivative is defined as group-homomorphism

\begin{align*} D: R[ [ X] ] \rightarrow R [ [X]] \end{align*}

which maps

\begin{align*} D(f)_n = n+1 \alpha_{n+1} X^{n} \end{align*}
