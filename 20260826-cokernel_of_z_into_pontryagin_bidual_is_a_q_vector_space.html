cokernel of Z into pontryagin bidual is a Q vector space

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \widehat{\mathbb{Z}}$ be the profinite completion of the integers. Then the cokernel is a $\mathbb{Q}$ vector space.

Proof

We can identify the map $\mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}), \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})$ with the profinite completion Then the stable arithmetic square for p-completion shows that there exists a pushout

Here the cofiber of the horizontal morphism is the cokernel of $\mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \widehat{\mathbb{Z}}$ and this agrees with the cofiber of $\mathbb{Q} \rightarrow \mathbb{Q} \otimes \widehat{\mathbb{Z}}$. But rational spectra are closed under colimits, so the cofiber of the map $\mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \widehat{\mathbb{Z}}$ is a rational eilenberg maclane spectrum (cf. short exact sequence in the heart is a bifiber sequence in a stable category).
