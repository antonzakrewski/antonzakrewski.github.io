preserved limit

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $J: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a diagram and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor. Suppose $L \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ together with morphisms $\pi_j$ is a limit, then $L$ is preserved under the functor, if the induced morphism between limits of a functor is an isomorphism 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{F}(C_i) \arrow[]{rr}{}& & \mathcal{F}(C_j) \\
& \mathrm{lim}_{\mathcal{F} \circ I} \arrow[]{ru}{} \arrow[]{lu}{} & \\
& \arrow[bend left]{uul}{} \mathcal{F}(\mathrm{lim}_I) \arrow[dashed]{u}{\rotatebox{90}{\sim}} \arrow[bend right]{uur}{}& \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Note that $\mathcal{F}(\mathrm{lim}_I)$ with $\mathcal{F}(\pi_j)$ is a limit, as existence is given by the composition 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{F}(C_i) \arrow[]{rr}{}& & \mathcal{F}(C_j) \\
& \mathcal{F}(\mathrm{lim}_{I}) \arrow[]{ru}{} \arrow[]{lu}{} & \\
& \arrow[bend left]{uul}{} \mathrm{lim}_{\mathcal{F} \circ I} \arrow[dashed]{u}{\rotatebox{90}{\sim}} \arrow[bend right]{uur}{}& \\
& \arrow[bend left=50]{uuul}{} C \arrow[]{u}{\exists!} \arrow[bend right=50]{uuur}{} & 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

and uniquness is given by commutativity of the composition, hence 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{F}(C_i) \arrow[]{rr}{}& & \mathcal{F}(C_j) \\
& \mathrm{lim}_{\mathcal{F} I} \arrow[]{ru}{} \arrow[]{lu}{} & \\
& \arrow[bend left]{uul}{} \mathcal{F}(\mathrm{lim}_{I}) \arrow[dashed]{u}{\rotatebox{90}{\sim}} \arrow[bend right]{uur}{}& \\
& \arrow[bend left=50]{uuul}{} C \arrow[shift right=1, swap]{u}{} \arrow[shift left=1]{u}{} \arrow[bend right=50]{uuur}{} & 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
