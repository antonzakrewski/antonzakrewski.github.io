unitary equivalence preserves scalar products

Proposition

Let $X,X'$ be prehilbert spaces. Then any unitary equivalence preserves the scalar product, i.e.,

\begin{align*} \langle x,y\rangle = \langle Ux, Uy\rangle \end{align*}

for all $x,y \in X$

Proof

follows from the jordan-von neumann theorem: A scalar product is -- if well-defined -- uniquely determined by $\| - \|$
