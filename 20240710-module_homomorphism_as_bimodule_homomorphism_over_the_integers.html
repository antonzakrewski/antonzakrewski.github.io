module homomorphism as bimodule homomorphism over the integers

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $M,N$ left \(R\)-modules and $\varphi: M \rightarrow N$ a module-homomorphism Then

\begin{align*} \varphi: M \rightarrow N \end{align*}

is a bimodule homomorphism for $M,N$ as \(R, \mathbb{Z}\)-bimodule
