cokernel projective cofibration

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes Then a chain map $f: C_* \rightarrow B_*$ between nonnegatively graded chain complex is said to be a cokernel projective cofibration, if the cokernel is degreewise projective
