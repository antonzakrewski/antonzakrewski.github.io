classification of compact, complex submanifolds of C^n

Proposition

the compact complex <submanifolds> of $\mathbb{C}^n$ are given by discrete points.

Proof

Let $X$ be a complex submanifold. By assumption we have a holomorphic map $\iota: X \rightarrow \mathbb{C}^n$.

Then using maximum modulus principle for complex manifolds we get that $X$ is locally constant map. Furthermore it $\iota$ is injective, so it must be given by discrete points.
