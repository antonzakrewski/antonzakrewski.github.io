K_0 functorial on bimodule morphisms

Proposition

Let $M$ be an \(R,S\)-bimodule, which is finite projective module regarded as $S$ module. Then the tensor product

\begin{align*} - \otimes_{R} M: \mathrm{RMod} \rightarrow \mathrm{SMod} \end{align*}

induces a group-homomorphism

\begin{align*} K_0(- \otimes_{R} M): K_0(R) \rightarrow K_0(S) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • tensor product of bimodules as right exact functor
  • functor preserving finite free projectives and finite free modules
  • additive functor induces functor on K of an abelian category
