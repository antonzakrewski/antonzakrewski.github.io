limit of abelian categories for exact functors is again abelian

Proposition

Let $I: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}$ be a diagram consisting of abelian categories and exact functors. Then the limit category is also abelian

Proof

follows from:

  • limit of additive categories and additive functors is additive
  • limits in a limit category & colimits in a limit category

where being an abelian category is a property characterized by existence of finite limits / colimits and
