inclusion of an open subset induces map on compactly supported cohomology

Proposition

Let $X$ be a hausdorff topological space and $U \hookrightarrow X$ an open set. Then there exists a canonical map

\begin{align*} H_c^n(U;M) \rightarrow H_c^n(X;M) \end{align*}

Proof

using relative excision gives a map

\begin{align*} H^n(U,U \setminus K;M) \rightarrow H^n(X, X \setminus K;M) \rightarrow H_c^n(X;M) \end{align*}
