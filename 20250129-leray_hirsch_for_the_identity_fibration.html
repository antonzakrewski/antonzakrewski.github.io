Leray-Hirsch for the identity fibration

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring,

\begin{align*} F \rightarrow E \rightarrow B \end{align*}

be a fiber sequence in top.

Assume that there exists homogeneous classes $x_i \in H^*(B,E;R)$ - at most finitely many for each degree - such that the image of $x_i$ under

\begin{align*} H^*(B,E;r) \rightarrow H^*(\Sigma F;R) \end{align*}

form a basis of the graded R-module $H^*(\Sigma F,R)$

Then this induces

an module-isomorphism

\begin{align*} \Phi_{\pi}: H^*(\Sigma F,R) \otimes_{R} H^*(B;R) \rightarrow H^*(B,E;R) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from

  • Leray-Hirsch Theorem
  • suspension as mapping cone
