evaluation morphism for a closed monoidal category

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a closed monoidal category. Then the evaluation morphism is defined as morphism

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Y) \otimes X \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

corresponding to

\begin{align*} \mathrm{id}: \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Y) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Y) \end{align*}

via the adjunction

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Y) \otimes Y, Y) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Y), \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Y)) \end{align*}
