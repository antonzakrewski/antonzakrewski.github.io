3.4.2 orthogonality of characters

Proposition

Let $g,h \in G$ and $z_g$ denote the centralizer of $g$ in $G$. Then

\begin{align*} \sum_{V_i \textrm{ simples}} \chi_{V_i}(g) \cdot \overline{\chi_{V_i}(h)} =& \begin{cases} \vert Z_g \vert & \mbox{if } g \textrm{ is conjugate to } h \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

Let

\begin{align*} \sum_{V \textrm{ simple}} \chi_{v}(g) \cdot \overline{\chi_{V}(h)} =& \sum_{V \textrm{ simple}} \mathrm{Tr}_{V \otimes V^*}(g \otimes (h^{-1})) \\ =& \sum \mathrm{Tr}_{\bigoplus \mathrm{End}(V)}(x \mapsto g x h^{-1}) \\ =& \mathrm{Tr}_{\mathbb{C}[g]}(x \mapsto g x h^{-1}) \end{align*}
