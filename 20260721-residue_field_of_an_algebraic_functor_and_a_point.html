residue field of an algebraic functor and a point

Definition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor and $x \in \vert X \vert$ a point. Let $\mathrm{Field}_{(X,x)}$ be the full subcategory of $\mathrm{Field}_{X}$ (cf. category of Fields of an algebraic functor) spanned by the field-valued points hitting $x$. Then the residue field of $X$ at $x$ - if it exists - is defined as initial object
