homology of SO(n) is finitely generated

Proposition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, then the homology of $\mathrm{SO}(n)$ is finitely generated group

Proof

Proof by induction

induction basis

e.g. SO(2) as U(1), $U(1) \simeq S^1$

induction step

follows from the fiber sequence of SO(n)

\begin{align*} \mathrm{SO}(n) \rightarrow \mathrm{SO}(n+1) \rightarrow S^n \end{align*}
  • 2 out of three for homology of a simple fiber sequence and derived serre ring
