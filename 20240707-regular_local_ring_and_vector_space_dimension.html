regular local ring and vector space dimension

Proposition

Let $A$ be a noetherian local commutative ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$. TFAE:

  1. $A$ is a regular local ring
  2. $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$ as \(A/\mathfrak{m}\)-vector space has dimension $\mathrm{dim}(A)$ (cf. ring dimension)

Proof

Let $m \coloneqq \mathrm{dim}(A) < \infty$ (cf. noetherian local ring as finite dimensional ring)

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $(d_1,...,d_m)$ be the generators of $\mathfrak{m}$ Then consider $\overline{d_1},...,\overline{d_n} \in \mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$. It follows that $\overline{d_1},...,\overline{d_m}$ generate $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$, e.g. by looking at representatives. Take a maximal linearly independent subset, w.l.o.g. $\overline{d_1},..,\overline{d_n}$, which still generates $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$. Then it follows that the fibres $d_1,...,d_n$ generate $\mathfrak{m}$ (cf. finitely generated Module over a local ring and fibres of a vector space basis as generators) By the height theorem we conclude that $m = \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{m}) \leq n$ showing $m = n$. Hence $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$ has dimension $m$

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $\overline{d}_1,...,\overline{d}_m$ be generators of $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$. Then $(d_1,...,d_m)$ generate $\mathfrak{m}$ by finitely generated Module over a local ring and fibres of a vector space basis as generators
