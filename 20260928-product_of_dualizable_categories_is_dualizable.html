coproduct of dualizable categories is dualizable

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_i$ be dualizable categories for $i \in I$. Then so is the product $\prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{C}_i$.

Proof

follows as

  • dualizable category is a retract of a compactly generated category.
  • product of stable compactly generated categories is compactly generated
