division algebra

Definition

Let $\mathbb{K} \in {\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}, \mathbb{H}}$. Then a division algebra $D$ is defined as unital magma object in category RMod with the property, that the multiplication

\begin{align*} a \cdot -: D \rightarrow& D\\ - \cdot a: D \rightarrow& D\\ \end{align*}

is a bijection for non zero divisors $a \in D$

Here the multiplication is given under the closed monoidal adjunction

\begin{align*} \cdot: D \times D \rightarrow D \end{align*}
