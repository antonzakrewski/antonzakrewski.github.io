smash homomorphism of F(d)

Definition

Let $d_1,d_2 \in \mathbb{N}$. Then the smash homomorphism is defined as applying the infinity smash product functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(S^{d_1},S^{d_1}) \times \mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(S^{d_2},S^{d_2}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(S^{d_1 + d_2},S^{d_1 + d_2}) \end{align*}

use:

  • mapping anima in the product category
  • functor induces E_1 group homomorphism between E_1 Auts
