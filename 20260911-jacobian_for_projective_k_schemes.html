jacobian for projective k-schemes

Proposition

Let $V(f_1,...,f_r) \subseteq \mathbb{P}_k^n$ be a closed subfunctor of the projective space. Then the jacobian corank function of $X$ is given by

\begin{align*} JC(\mathfrak{p}) = \mathrm{corank} \left(\frac{\partial f_i}{\partial x_j}(\mathfrak{p})\right)_{j \in \{0,...,n\}} - 1 \end{align*}

Proof

first we use that the jacobian is defined on affine open subschemes Then for the zeroth standard affine chart (or equivalently any standard affine chart after renumbering) $\mathbb{A}_k^n \subseteq \mathbb{P}_k^n$ we have a pullback diagram

So we have to compute the corank of $(f_1,...,f_r)$.

For this let $d_i = \mathrm{deg}(f_i)$, then there exists a relation

\begin{align*} d_i f_i =& \sum_{j=0}^n x_j \frac{\partial f_i}{\partial x_j} \\ =& \mathrm{deg}_{f_i}(x_j) f_i \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{deg}_{f_i}(x_j)$ is the monomial degree of $x_j$ in $f_i$.

Moreover, for any point $\mathfrak{p} \in V(f_1,...,f_r)$ we have $f_i(p) = 0$, so inserting the point $p$ gives

\begin{align*} 0 =& d_i f_i(p) \\ =& \sum_{j=0}^n x_j \frac{\partial f_i}{\partial x_j}(p) \end{align*}

which shows that the vector $(\frac{\partial f_i}{\partial x_0}(p), ..., \frac{\partial f_i}{\partial x_0}(p))$ lies in the span of $\frac{\partial f_i}{\partial x_1}(p))_{i},...,\frac{\partial f_i}{\partial x_n}(p))_{i}$.

This implies the corank equality
