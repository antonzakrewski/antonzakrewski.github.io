filtered colimit of a natural transformation of n-connected morphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{I}_1, \mathcal{I}_2: I \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ be filtered diagrams and $\eta: \mathcal{I}_1 \rightarrow \mathcal{I}_2$ be a natural transformation consisting of n-connected maps. Then $\mathrm{colim}(\eta): \mathrm{colim} \mathcal{I}_1 \rightarrow \mathcal{I}_2$ is also n-connected.

Proof
