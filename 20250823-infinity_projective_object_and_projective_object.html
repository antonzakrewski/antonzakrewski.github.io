infinity projective object and projective object for a (1,1)-category with coproducts

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an abelian category and $P \in \mathcal{C}$ TFAE:

  1. $P$ is projective in the sense that $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(P,-)$ preserves geometric realizations
  2. $P$ is projective in the sense of lifting
  1. $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(P,-)$ is (right) exact

Proof

1) $\implies$ 3)

follows from:

  • coequalizer and reflexive coequalizer in a category with coproducts
  • coequalizer as cokernel in an ab-enriched category

where every right exact sequence can be rewritten as

1) $\implies$ 3)

3) $\implies$ 2), 2) $\implies$ 3)

cf. projective module and hom covariant functor as right exact functor
