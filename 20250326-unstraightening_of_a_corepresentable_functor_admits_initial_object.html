unstraightening of a corepresentable functor admits initial object

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F} \in \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{An})$ be a corepresentable infinity functor. Then the domain of the unstraightened

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Un}(F) \end{align*}

admits an initial object

Proof

Let $F \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(c,-)$. Then consider $\mathrm{id}_c$ over $c$.
