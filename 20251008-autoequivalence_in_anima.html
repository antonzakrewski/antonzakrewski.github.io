autoequivalence in Anima

Proposition

The autoequivalences in Anima are given by the identity, i.e. after assuming large cardinals

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^\mathrm{Aut}(\mathrm{Cat}, \mathrm{Cat}) \simeq \mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{Cat}}(\mathrm{Cat}) \simeq& * \end{align*}

Proof

use:

  • presheaf category arises by freely adding colimits so we get
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}}(\mathcal{P}(*), \mathrm{An}) \xrightarrow{\mathrm{ev}_*} \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

where an equivalence preserves colimits and furthermore the terminal object $*$ must be preserved

Therefore the full subcategory of equivalences consists of objects equivalent to the terminal object, i.e.

\begin{align*} \mathrm{BEnd}_{\mathrm{An}}(*) \simeq * \end{align*}

Note that it also already is a groupoid
