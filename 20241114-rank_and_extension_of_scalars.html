rank and extension of scalars

Proposition

Let $f: R \rightarrow S$ be a ring-homomorphism of commutative rings and $f^*: \mathrm{Spec}(S) \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(R)$ the contravariant Spec functor from Ring to Top. Then it follows for a finitely generated projective modulue

\begin{align*} \mathrm{rk}_{(-)}(S \otimes_{R} P) = \mathrm{rk}_{(-)}(P) \circ f^*: \mathrm{Spec}(S) \rightarrow \mathbb{N} \end{align*}
