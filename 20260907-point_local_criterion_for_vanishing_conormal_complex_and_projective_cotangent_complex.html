point local criterion for vanishing conormal complex and projective cotangent complex

Proposition

Let $R$ be commutative ring and let $S = R[x_1,...,x_n]/I$ with $I = (f_1,...,f_m)$ and $J = (\partial f_i/\partial x_j)_{i,j} \in \mathrm{Mat}(R[x_1,...,x_n])$. Suppose that the image of $J$ in $\mathrm{Mat}(\kappa(\mathfrak{m}))$ has rank $m$ for every maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m} \subseteq S$. Then

  1. $\Omega_{S/R}$ is a vector bundle of rank $n - m$
  2. $\mathcal{N}_{S/R}$ vanishes

Proof

use:

  • finitely presented module as projective module and free localization
  • invertible submatrix and fundamental exact sequence
