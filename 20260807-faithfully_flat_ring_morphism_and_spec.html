faithfully flat ring morphism and spec

Proposition

Let $\varphi: R \rightarrow S$ be a ring-homomorphism TFAE:

  1. $\varphi$ is faithfully flat morphism
  2. $\mathrm{Spec}(\varphi)$ is a flat morphism between schemes and $\mathrm{Spec}(\varphi)$ is surjective

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
