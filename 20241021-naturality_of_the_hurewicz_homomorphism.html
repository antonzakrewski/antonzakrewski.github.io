Naturality of the Hurewicz Homomorphism

Proposition

The hurewicz homomorphism is natural with respect to based maps, i.e.

commutes

Proof

This follows as $H_n(-)$ is a functor, which shows that for each element $[f] \in \pi_n(X,x)$

\begin{align*} \varphi_*( f_*( [S^n])) = (\varphi \circ f)_*([S^n]) \end{align*}
