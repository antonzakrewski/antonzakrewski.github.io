category of additive categories as right bousfield localization of Cat Ex

Proposition

The infinity category of additive categories is a right bousfield localization of Cat Ex via the minimal exact structure functor from additive to stable

\begin{align*} \mathrm{min}: \mathrm{Cat}^{\prod}_{\infty} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{st}}_{\infty} \end{align*}

and the forgetful functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{st}}_{\infty} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{prod}}_{\infty} \end{align*}

(which is not conservative or fully faithful)
