map into brown comenetz dual of Q/Z is conservative

Proposition

the functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(-,I_{\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}}): \mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp} \end{align*}

is a conservative functor

Proof

exact functor reflecting zero object and conservative Let $X$ be a spectrum such that $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X,I_{\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}})$ is trivial, then it remains to show that $X$ is also contractible. Recall that $0 = \pi_n(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X,\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\pi_{-n}(X), \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})$. This shows that $\pi_{-n}(X)$ is the zero group (cf. trivial Hom into Q/Z in abelian groups) and hence so is $X$.
