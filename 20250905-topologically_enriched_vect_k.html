topologically enriched Vect_K^fin

Definition

Let $\mathbb{K} \in \{\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}, \mathbb{H}\}$, then the topologically enriched category $\mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb{K}}^{\mathrm{fin}}$ is defined as topologically enriched category containing finite dimensional vector spaces as objects and $\mathrm{GL}(\mathbb{K})$.
