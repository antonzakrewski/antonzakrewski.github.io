small coproducts and coequalizer imply general colimit

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category which admits

  1. coproducts
  2. coequalizer

Then $\mathcal{C}$ is cocomplete

Proof

corollary of:

  • kappa small coproducts and coequalizer imply kappa small colimits
  • for large enough $\kappa$
  • existence of arbitrary large regular cardinals
