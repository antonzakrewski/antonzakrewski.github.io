epimorphism locus is an open subfunctor

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $f: M \rightarrow N$ a map of \(A\)-modules. Suppose that $N$ is a vector bundle, then the epimorphism locus $V(f) \subseteq \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ is an open subfunctor

Proof

Let $N'$ be such that $N' \oplus N = R^n$. Then observe that $\mathrm{Epi}(f) = \mathrm{Epi}(f \otimes \mathrm{id}: M \oplus N' \rightarrow N \oplus N' \cong R^n)$ and hence we may assume that $N = R^n$. Then $\mathrm{Epi}(f) = \mathrm{Epi}(\wedge^n f)$ <todo>

Hence we may assue that $N = R$.

Then $\mathrm{Epi}(f: M \rightarrow R)$ is precisely given by $D(\mathrm{Im}(f))$
