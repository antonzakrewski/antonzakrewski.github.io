surjectivity as point local property

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod and $f: M \rightarrow N$ a module-homomorphism of \(R\)-modules \(M,N\).

Then surjectivity of $f$ is a point local property

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

corollary of:

  • localization as exact functor
  • exact functor preserves epimorphisms

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $\mathfrak{m}$ be an arbitrary maximal ideal

be a sequence such that 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
M_{\mathfrak{m}} \arrow[]{r}{f_{\mathfrak{m}}} & N_{\mathfrak{m}} \arrow[]{r}{} 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

is surjective. Then $\mathrm{coker}(f_{\mathfrak{m}}) = 0$

Then as $f$ commutes with cokernels we conclude that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{coker}(f_{\mathfrak{m}}) = \mathrm{coker}(f)_{\mathfrak{m}} \end{align*}

Thus we conclude that $\mathrm{coker}(f) = 0$ (cf. zero module and localization at a maximal ideal)
