freudenthal suspension map for p-connected, well pointed spaces

Proposition

Let $(X,x)$ be a well pointed, p-connected based space Then the freudenthal suspension map for homotopy groups

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Freud}: \pi_n(X,x) \rightarrow \pi_{n+1}(\Sigma X, x) \end{align*}

is

  1. an isomorphism for $n \leq 2p$
  2. an epimorphism for $n = 2p + 1$

Proof
