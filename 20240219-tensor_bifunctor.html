tensor multifunctor

Definition / Proposition

Let \(R\) be a commutative ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod. Then for a small discrete diagram $I$ and the product category $\prod_{i \in I} \mathrm{RMod}$, the tensor multifunctor is defined as

\begin{align*} \bigotimes_{i \in I}: \prod_{i \in I} \mathrm{Rmod} \rightarrow& \mathrm{RMod} \\ \prod_{i \in I} M_i \mapsto& \bigotimes_{i \in I} M_i \\ ... \end{align*}
