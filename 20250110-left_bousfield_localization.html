left bousfield localization

Definition

A left bousfield localization is a dwyer-kan localization $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ which is a left adjoint to some $\mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$

Note that we may often identify this adjunction just as full subcategory

\begin{align*} \mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

where the inclusion determines the left adjoint
