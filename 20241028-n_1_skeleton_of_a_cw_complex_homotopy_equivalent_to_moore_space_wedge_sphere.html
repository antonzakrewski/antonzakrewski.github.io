n+1 skeleton of a n-1 connected cw complex homotopy equivalent to moore space wedge sphere

Proposition

Let $X$ be a \(n-1\)-connected space CW-Complex for $n > 1$ Then up to homotopy equivalence we may w.l.o.g. assume that the \(n+1\)-skeleton is given by

\begin{align*} M(A,n) \vee \bigvee_{\beta} S^{n+1} \end{align*}

see:

  • Moore space
  • wedge product

Proof

we may argue by cellular approximation and Whitehead theorem:

We may choose generators of $\pi_n(X,x)$ and then get a map

\begin{align*} M(A,n) \rightarrow X \end{align*}

furthermore we may proceed by adding $\bigvee S^{n+1}$ via mapping to $\vee S^{n+1}$ to generators of $\pi_{n+1}(X,x)$

by <cellular approximation> we may repeat this indefinitely and get a cw complex and weak homotopy equivalence. Thus also a homotopy equivalence
