classification of closed subfunctors of an algebraic functor

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor, then there exists an isomorphism of partially ordered sets

\begin{align*} V: \{\texrm{ quasi coherent ideals over } X\}^{\mathrm{op}} \simeq \{\textrm{ closed subfunctors of } X\} \end{align*}

Proof
