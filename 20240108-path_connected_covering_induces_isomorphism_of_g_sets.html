path connected covering induces isomorphism of G-Sets

Proposition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a path connected covering, $b \in B$ and $e \in p^{-1}(b)$. Then the map

\begin{align*} p_*: \pi_1(E,e) \rightarrow \pi_1(B,b) \end{align*}

on the fundamental groups (cf. homotopy group functor) induces an isomorphism on G-Sets

\begin{align*} p^{-1}(b) \cong \pi_1(B,b)/\pi_1(E,e) \end{align*}

where $G = \pi_1(B,b)$

Proof
