right derived functor of an injective object

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories where $\mathcal{A}$ admits enough injectives. Suppose $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ is a left exact functor and $I$ an injective object Then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{LF}_n(I) = 0 \end{align*}
