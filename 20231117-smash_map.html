smash map

Definition

Let $(X_0,x_0), (X_1,x_1), (Y_0,y_0), (Y_1,y_1)$ be based spaces and $f: X_0 \rightarrow X_1$ and $g: Y_0 \rightarrow Y_1$ be based maps.

Then the smash map is the based map defined as

\begin{align*} f \wedge g: X_0 \wedge Y_0 \rightarrow& X_1 \wedge X_2 \\ (x_0,y) \mapsto& (x_1,y_1) \\ (x,y_0) \mapsto& (x_1,y_1) \\ (x,y) \mapsto& (f(x),g(y)) & \mbox{if } x \neq x_0, y \neq y_0 \\ \end{align*}
