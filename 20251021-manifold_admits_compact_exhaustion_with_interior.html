locally compact space with compact exhaustion admits compact exhaustion with interior

Proposition

Let $M$ be a locally regular compact which admits a compact exhaustion $K_n$. Then $M$ admits a compact exhaustion where $\mathrm{int}(K_n) \supseteq K_{n-1}$.

Proof

Choose an open cover $U_i$ where each $U_i$ has compact closure.

Then choose a finite subcover $U_1,...,U_n$ of $K_1$ and define $C_1 \coloneqq \bigcup \overline{U_i}$ which is compact since it is the union of finitely many compact subspaces. Now we inductively define $C_n$ by choosing a finite subcover $U_1',...,U_m'$ of $K_n \cup C_{n-1}$ (which is again compact, so we may find a finite subcover) and setting $C_n \coloneqq \bigcup \overline{U_i}'$.

We claim that $C_n$ is such a compact exhaustion:

  1. each $C_n$ is compact since it is the finite union of compact subsets
  2. by construction $K_n \subseteq C_n$ so in particular $\bigcup C_n \supseteq \bigcup K_n = M$
  3. for each $C_n$ we get $\mathrm{int}(C_n) = \mathrm{int}(\bigcup \overline{U_1}) \supseteq \bigcup \mathrm{int}(\overline{U}_i) \supseteq \bigcup U_i \supseteq K_n$ since $U_i$ is a cover of $K_n$ (cf. Innere einer Vereinigung).
