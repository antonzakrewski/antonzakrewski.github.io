functor from cat to grouoid

Definition

Given the category of groupoids $\mathrm{Grpd}$ and Category Cat $\mathrm{Cat}$, the isomorphism functor si defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{Iso}: \mathrm{Cat} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Grpd} \\ \mathcal{C} \mapsto& \mathcal{C}' \\ (\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}) \mapsto& (\mathcal{F}': \mathcal{C}' \rightarrow \mathcal{D}') \end{align*}

where $\mathcal{C}'$ is the wide subcategory containing only isomorphisms and $\mathcal{F}'$ is the restriction of $\mathcal{F}$)

welldefined

this follows from:

  1. composition of isomorphisms as isomorphism, hence $\mathcal{C}'$ is a welldefined category
  2. isomorphisms under functors, hence $\mathcal{F}'$ is welldefined
