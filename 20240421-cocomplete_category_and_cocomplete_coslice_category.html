cocomplete category and cocomplete slice category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $\mathcal{C} / X$ the coslice category for $X \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C} \uparrow X$ is cocomplete
  2. $\mathcal{C}$ is cocompelete

Proof

follows from colimit in a slice category
