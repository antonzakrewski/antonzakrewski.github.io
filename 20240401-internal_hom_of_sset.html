internal hom of sSet

Definition / Proposition

The internal hom object of category sSet is defined as

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}(X,Y)}: \Delta \rightarrow& \mathrm{Set} \\ [n] \mapsto& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^n \times X, Y)\\ d \mapsto& (d \times \mathrm{id})^* \end{align*}

Proof

Using left adjoint by dense subcategory and the contravariant co-Yoneda Lemma it suffices to show that

  1. $X \times -$ preserves colimits
  2. the adjunction holds for represantables

1)

  • finite product commute with colimits in sSet

2)

follows as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^n \times X, Z) =& \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Z)_n \\ \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^n, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Z)) \end{align*}

where a) the first isomorphism follows from definition b) the second isomrophism follows from the contravariant Yoneda Lemma
