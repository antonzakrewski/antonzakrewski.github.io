birational equivalence and regular isomorphism on open subsets

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n, Y \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^m$ be affine varieties. TFAE:

  1. $f: X \rightarrow Y$ is a birational equivalence
  2. there exist nonempty open subsets $U \subseteq X, V \subseteq V$ such that $f$ restricts to an isomorphism of quasi-affine varieties (in the category of classical varieties)
\begin{align*} f_{\vert U}: U \cong V \end{align*}

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

By assumption there exists an $g: Y \rightarrow X$ such that $f \circ g = \mathrm{id}, g \circ f = \mathrm{id}$ where defined.

Let $U = \mathrm{def}(f \circ g), V = \mathrm{def}(g \circ f)$ Then it follows for each $P \in \mathrm{def}(f \circ g)$ that $(f \circ g)(P) = \frac{h_1}{h_2}(P) = P$ for some $h_1,h_2$.

2) $\implies$ 1)
