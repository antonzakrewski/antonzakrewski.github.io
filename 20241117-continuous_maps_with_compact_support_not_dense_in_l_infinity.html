continuous maps with compact support not dense in L infinity

Proposition

The set of continuous maps with compact support is not dense in L infinity

Proof

Consider

\begin{align*} \mathrm{const}_1: \mathbb{R}^d \rightarrow \mathbb{C} \end{align*}

Let $f \in C_c(\mathbb{R}^d)$. Then the support is compact, hence $\| \mathrm{const}_1 - f \|_{\infty} \geq 1$
