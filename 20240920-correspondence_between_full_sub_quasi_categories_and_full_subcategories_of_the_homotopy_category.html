correspondence between full sub quasi categories and full subcategories of the homotopy category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category Then there exists a bijective correspondence between

  1. full sub quasicategories
  2. full subcategories of the homotopy category

Proof

follows from

  • correspondence between sub quasi categories and subcategories of the homotopy category

where fullness follows from the explicit descripiton:

  • subcategory of the homotopy category determines sub quasi category
  • sub quasi category induces subcategory of the homotopy category
