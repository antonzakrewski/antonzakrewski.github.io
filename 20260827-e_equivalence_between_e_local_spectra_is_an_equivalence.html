E-equivalence between E-local spectra is an equivalence

Proposition

Let $X,X'$ be E-local spectra and $f: X \rightarrow X'$ an equivalence

Proof

follows from weak infinity yoneda lemma where both $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(f,X), \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(f',X)$ are equivalences as $X,X'$ are \(E\)-local.
