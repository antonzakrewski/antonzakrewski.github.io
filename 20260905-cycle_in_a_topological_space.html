cycle in a topological space

Definition

Let $T$ be a topological space and $A$ a commutative monoid Then a cycle in $T$ with coefficients in $A$ is a formal sum $\sum_{V} a_V \cdot V$ where $V$ runs over all irreducible closed subsets of $T$, which is locally finite: Every point has a neighborhood, which intersects only finitely many $V$'s wtih $a_{V} \neq 0$.
