algebra structure of A1

Proposition

Let $k$ be a commutative ring, then the affine line admits a \(k\)-algebra structurei.e. lies in

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k^{1-\mathrm{sfp}, \mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}, \mathrm{Set})) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from currying of functors preserving limits / colimits: Recall that $\mathrm{CAlg}_k \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k, \mathrm{Set})$ and hence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k, \mathrm{CAlg}_k) \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k, \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{prod}}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k^{1-\mathrm{sfp}, \mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Set})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{prod}}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k^{1-\mathrm{sfp}, \mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k,\mathrm{Set})) \end{align*}

where we may trace the identity functor, which lifts the representable presheaf $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{CAlg}_k}(k[x],-) \cong \mathrm{Spec}(k[x]) \cong \mathbb{A}^1$.
