category of G-Anima

Definition

Let $G$ be a compact lie group. Then the category of G-spaces is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An}_G = \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Orb}_{G}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An}) \end{align*}

see:

  • orbit category of a discrete group

where we think of evaluation at $G/H$ as H-fixed points of the group action.
