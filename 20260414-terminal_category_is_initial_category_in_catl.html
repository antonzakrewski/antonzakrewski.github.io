terminal category is initial object in PrL

Proposition

The terminal category is also the initial object in the category PrL (or even the full subcategory of $\mathrm{Cat}^L$ containing categories with initial object).

Proof

follows as any left adjoint from $*$ to a cocomplete category $\mathcal{C}$ must map $*$ to the initial object. moreover, the inclusion $* \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is a welldefined object.

At last, the functor $\emptyset: * \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ has no nontrivial Endomorphism / the $\mathrm{End}$ is contractible
