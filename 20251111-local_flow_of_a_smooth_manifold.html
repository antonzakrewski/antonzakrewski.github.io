local flow of a smooth manifold

Definition

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold Then a local flow of $X$ is an open cover $U_i$ of $M$ with a family of smooth maps

\begin{align*} \varphi_{i}: (-\varepsilon, \varepsilon) \times U_i \rightarrow& M \\ (t,p) \mapsto& \varphi_t^i(p) \end{align*}

such that for $p \in U_i$

\begin{align*} \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}_{\vert t = 0} \varphi^i_t(p) = X_p \end{align*}
