positivity criterion for a nonunital star algebra

Proposition

Let $h \in A^{\mathrm{sa}}$ be a self-adjoint element in a nonunital star algebra TFAE:

  1. $h \geq 0$ is a positive element
  2. $\| h - t \| \leq t$ for some $t \geq 0$

Moreover, if $h \geq 0$ and $t \geq \| h \|$ , then $\| h - t \| \leq t$.

Proof

Here we have

\begin{align*} \| h - t \| \leq \mathrm{max}_{s \in \sigma_{A^u}(h)} \vert s - t \vert \end{align*}

1) $\implies$ 2)

Assume that $h \geq 0$ and $t \geq \| h \|$. Then $\mathrm{max}_{s \in \sigma_{A^u}(h)} ( s - t) \leq t$

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $K \subesteq \mathbb{R}$ be compact and $\mathrm{max}_{s \in K} \vert s - t \vert \leq t$ then we have $K \in [0,\infty).
