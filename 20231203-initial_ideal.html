initial ideal

Definition

Let $K$ be a field, $K[X_1,...,X_n]$ the polynomial ring and $I \subseteq K[X_1,...,X_n]$ an ideal. Then the initial ideal $\mathrm{in}(I)$ is defined as generated Ideal by the initial terms

\begin{align*} \mathrm{in}(I) \coloneqq \langle \mathrm{in}(f) \vert f \in I\rangle \end{align*}
