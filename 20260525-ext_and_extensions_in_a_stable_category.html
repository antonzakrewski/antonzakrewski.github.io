Ext1 and extensions in the heart of a stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category with t-structure and $X,Z$ objects in the heart $\mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}$. Then there exists a canonical bijection between $\mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{C}}^{1}(Z,X)$ and short exact sequences $X \hookrightarrow Y \twoheadrightarrow Z$ in the $\mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}$.

Proof

Let $f \in \pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(Z,X[1])) = \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{C}}^{1}(Z,X)$ be a morphism. Then define $Y$ to be the fiber and rotating the fiber sequence results in

\begin{align*} X \rightarrow Y \rightarrow Z \rightarrow X[1] \end{align*}

Now this gives a long exact sequence in the heart where the heart of a t-structure is closed under extensions.

so now assume that we have a short exact seuqence $X \rightarrow Y \rightarrow Z$. Then we may extend this to get a morphism $Z \rightarrow X[1]$ (cf. cofiber puppe sequence of an infinity category).

We claim that these operations are inverse to each other: for that it suffices to note that the category of fiber sequences is equivalent to arrow category / category of cofiber sequences is equivalent to arrow category
