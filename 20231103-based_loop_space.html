based loop space

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space and $x \in X$. Then the based loop space with basepoint $x \in X$ is defined as mapping space

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{*}(S^1, X) = \{f \in \mathcal{L}(X) \vert f(1) = x\} \end{align*}

equipped with the compact open topology
