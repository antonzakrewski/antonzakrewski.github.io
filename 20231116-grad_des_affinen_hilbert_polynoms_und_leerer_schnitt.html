grad des affinen Hilbert-Polynoms und leerer schnitt

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $K[X_1,...,X_n]$ the polynomial ring, $I \subseteq K[X_1,...,X_n]$ an ideal.

Then the degree of the affine hilbert polynomial $d \coloneqq \mathrm{deg}(\mathrm{HP}_{I})$ is the maximal number, such that for a permutation $\sigma \in S_n$

\begin{align*} \langle X_{\sigma(1)}, X_{\sigma(2)},...,X_{\sigma(d)}\rangle \cap I = 0 \end{align*}

Proof
