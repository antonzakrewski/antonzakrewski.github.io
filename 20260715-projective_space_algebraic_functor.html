projective space algebraic functor

Definition

Let $k$ be a commutative ring and $I$ a set. Then the projective \(I\)-space functor over $k$ is defined as the algebraic functor

\begin{align*} \mathbb{P}_k^I: \mathrm{CAlg}_{k} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Set} \\ R \mapsto& \{\textrm{quotient lines of } R^{(I)}\} \\ (f: R \rightarrow S) \mapsto& (f \otimes_{R}) -) \end{align*}

i.e. quotient modules which are algebraic line bundles.

moreover, we write

\begin{align*} \mathbb{P}_k^n \coloneqq \mathbb{P}_k^{ \{0,...,n\}} \end{align*}

for the set with $n+1$ elements.
