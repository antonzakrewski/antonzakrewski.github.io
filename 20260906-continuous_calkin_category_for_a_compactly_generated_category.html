continuous calkin category for a compactly generated category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a compactly generated category. Then there exists a natural equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Calk}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C}^{\omega}) \simeq \mathrm{Calk}_{\kappa}^{\textrm{cont}}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

Proof

In this case, the functor $\widehat{よ}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\kappa)$ is identified with $\mathrm{Ind}(よ: \mathcal{C}^{\omega} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\kappa})$ (cf. widehat yoneda of a compactly generated category). Hence we can pass the cofiber sequence

to get a cofiber sequence in $\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{rex}, \omega_0, \mathrm{st}}$ (cf. gabriel-ulmer duality for stable categories).

which is the definition of the calkin category.

(note that we have to take idempotent completion here for the verdier quotient )
