compatible family of orientations

Definition

Let \(M\) be an \(n\)-dimensional manifold and $U_{\alpha}$ open subsets and $R$ a ring . Then a family $(s_{\alpha})$ for $s_{\alpha} \in H_n(X, X \setminus U_{\alpha};R)$ is said to be a compatible family of \(R\)-orientations, if

  1. $s_{\alpha} \in$ is an R-Orientation at $U_{\alpha}$
  2. for every $U_{\alpha}, U_{\beta}$ and the orientation inclusion map
\begin{align*} \rho_{U_{\alpha}, U_{\beta}}(s_{\alpha}) = \rho_{U_{\beta}, U_{\alpha}}(s_{\beta}) \end{align*}
