localization of an UFD as UFD

Proposition

Let $R$ be a unique factorization domain, $S \subseteq R$ a multiplicatively closed subset with $0 \not\in S$ Then $S^{-1}R$ is also a UFD

Proof

Let $\frac{r}{s} \in S^{-1}R$. Then take a factorization

\begin{align*} r = \prod r_i^\nu_i \end{align*}

into primes $r \in R$.

Then $\frac{1}{s}$ is a unit By prime element and localization it follows that $\frac{r_i}{1}$ is a prime

Hence

\begin{align*} \frac{r}{s} = \frac{1}{s} \cdot \prod \frac{r_i^\nu_i}{1} \end{align*}
