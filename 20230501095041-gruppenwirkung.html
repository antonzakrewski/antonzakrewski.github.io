Group Action

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \times_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a cartesian category and \(G\) a group object. Then a group action on an object $X$ is given by monoid action

\begin{align*} \mu: G \times X \rightarrow X \end{align*}
