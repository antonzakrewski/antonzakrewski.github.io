integral cohomology of BZ/nZ

Proposition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, then

\begin{align*} H^*(B \mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Z}) \simeq \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } * = 0 \\ \mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } * = \textrm{ even} \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

and with the ring structure $\mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z}[x]$
