union of a chain of field extensions as field extension

Proposition

Let $F$ be a field and $L_1 \subseteq L_2 ...$ be a chain of field extensions $L_n/K$. Then $L \coloneqq \bigcup_{n \in \mathbb{N}} L_n$ is also a field extension
