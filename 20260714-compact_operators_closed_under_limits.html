compact operators closed under uniform convergence

Proposition

Let $T_n \in \mathrm{BL}(X,Y)$ be a sequence of compact operators such that $\| T_n - T \| \xrightarrow{n \rightarrow \infty} 0$ for some operator $T$. Then $T$ is compact.

Proof

Let $(x_k)$ be a bounded sequence, w.l.o.g. after possibly scaling with $\frac{1}{\mathrm{sup} \| x_k \|}$ we may assume that $\| x_k \| \leq 1$. Then for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $T_n$ is a compact operator, hence there exists a Cauchy subsequence $T_n(x_{m_k}^(n))$ of $T_{n}(x_k)$.

Let $\varepsilon > 0$, then there exists an index $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $\| T - T_n \| < \frac{\varepsilon}{3}$. Now fix such an $n$, then there exists a $K \in \mathbb{N}$ scuh that $\| T_n(x_{m_k}^n) - T_{n}(x_{m_{k'}^{(n)}) \| < \frac{\varepsilon}{3}$.

Now use the triangle inequality

\begin{align*} \| T(x_{m_k}^{(n)}) - T(x_{m_{k'}}^{(n)})\| \leq& \| T(x_{m_k}^{(n)}) - T_n(x_{m_{k}}^{(n)})\| + \| T_n(x_{m_k}^{(n)}) - T_n(x_{m_{k'}}^{(n)})\| + \| T_n(x_{m_k}^{(n)}) - T_n(x_{m_{k'}}^{(n)})\| \\ \leq& \| T - T_n \| \cdot \| x_{m_{k}^{(n)} \| + \frac{\varepsilon}{3} + \| T - T_n \| \| x_{m_{k'}^{(n)} \| \\ <& \frac{\varepsilon}{3} + \frac{\varepsilon}{3} + \frac{\varepsilon}{3} \\ <& \varepsilon \end{align*}

where we have used $\| x_{m_{k}^{(n)} \|, \| x_{m_{k'}^{(n)} \| \leq 1$
