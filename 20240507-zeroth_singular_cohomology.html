zeroth singular cohomology

Proposition

Let $X$ be a weakly locally path connected space, then the zeroth singular cohomology is given by

\begin{align*} H^0(X;M) \cong M^{\pi_0(X)} \end{align*}

Proof

using weakly locally path connected space and sum of path components and singular cohomology and coproduct we get

\begin{align*} H^0(X;M) \cong& H^0(\coprod \pi_0[x];M) \\ \cong& \coprod \end{align*}
