union of a chain of algebraic field extensions as algebraic field extension

Proposition

Let $F$ be a field and $L_1 \subseteq L_2 ...$ be a chain of algebraic field extension $L_n/K$. Then $L \coloneqq \bigcup_{n \in \mathbb{N}} L_n$ is also an algebraic field extension

Proof

field extension

cf. union of a chain of field extensions as field extension

algebraic

Let $\alpha \in L$, then there exists an $L_m$ such that $\alpha \in L_m$. By assumption, $\alpha \in L_m$ is algebraic, i.e. there exists an polynomial $f \in K[X]$ with $f(\alpha)$ Hence $\alpha$ is an algebraic element in $L/K$
