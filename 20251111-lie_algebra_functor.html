lie algebra functor from lie groups

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{LieGroup}$ be the category of lie groups and $\mathrm{LieAlg}_{\mathbb{R}}$ the category of lie algebras. Then there exists a functor

\begin{align*} T_{e}: \mathrm{LieGroup} \rightarrow& \mathrm{LieAlg}_{\mathbb{R}}\\ G \mapsto& T_{e}(G) \\ f \mapsto& Df_{e} \end{align*}

Proof
