homology of an exact sequence

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an additive category, $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ the category of chain complexes and 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
... \arrow[]{r}{} & A_{n-1} \arrow[]{r}{}& A_{n} \arrow[]{r}{}& A_{n+1}  \arrow[]{r}{} &
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

an sequence

  2. it holds for the homology functor
\begin{align*} H_n(A_*) = 0 \end{align*}
