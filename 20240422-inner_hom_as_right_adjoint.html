inner Hom of a simplicial set as right adjoint

Proposition

Let $X$ be a simplicial set and $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,-)$ the inner hom functor. Then $\underline{Hom}(X,-)$ is a right adjoint to the product functor $X \times -$

Proof

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^n, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Z)) \overset{!}{\cong} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^n \times X, Z) \end{align*}

Note that a natural transformation $\Delta^n \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Z))$ corresponds by Yoneda to an element

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Z)[n] =& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^n \times X, Z) \end{align*}

furthermore right adjoint by codense subcategory by left adjoint by dense subcategory
