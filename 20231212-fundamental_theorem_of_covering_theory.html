fundamental theorem of covering theory

Proposition

Let $B$ be a locally path connected, semi-locally simply connected topological space.

Then the functor functor from the category of coverings

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cov}(B) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\tau_{\leq 1}(B), \mathrm{Set}) \end{align*}

is an adjoint equivalence

Proof

  1. path connected fundamental theory of covering theory about fully faithfullness / functor from category of coverings to fundamentalgroup-sets as fully faithful
  2. path connected fundamental theorem of covering theory by G-Sets as essentially surjective functor
