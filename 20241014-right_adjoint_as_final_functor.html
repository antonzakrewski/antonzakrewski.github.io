right adjoint as final functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{R}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a right adjoint $\mathcal{L} \dashv \mathcal{R}$ Then $\mathcal{R}$ is a final functor
