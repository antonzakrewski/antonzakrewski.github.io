classification of indecomposable steenrod operations

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Sq}^k$ be a steenrod operation_

TFAE:

  1. $k = 2^n$
  2. $\mathrm{Sq}^k$ is indecomposable steenrod operation

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $t \in H^1(\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}; \mathbb{F}_2) \setminus \{0\}$ be the nontrivial element. Thne

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}^{2^n}(t^{2^n}) =& t^{2^n} \\ \neq 0 \end{align*}

If $0 < i < 2^n$ then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}^i(t^{2^n}) =& \binom{2^n}{i} t^{2^n + i} \\ =& 0 \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)

Write

\begin{align*} k = \sum_{i=0}^n a_{i} 2^i \end{align*}

as dyadic expansion for $a_n \neq 0, 0 \leq a_i < 2$.

Let $b = 2^n$, $a = k - b$. Then $a > 0$ is equivalent to $k$ being a power of $2$

Note that by construction $a < 2b$, hence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}^0 \mathrm{Sq}^b = \sum_{j=0}^{\mathrm{floor}(\frac{a}{2})} \binom{b-j-1}{a-2j} \mathrm{Sq}^{a+b-j} \mathrm{Sq}^j \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} \binom{b-1}{a} \not\equiv 0 \mod{2} \end{align*}

by the Lucas Theorem for F2

Hence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}^k =& \mathrm{Sq}^{a + b} \\ =& \sum_{j=1}^{\mathrm{floor}(\frac{a}{2})}(...) \mathrm{Sq}^{a + b - j} \mathrm{Sq}^{j} + \mathrm{Sq}^{a} \mathrm{Sq}^b \end{align*}
