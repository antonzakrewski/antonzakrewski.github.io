E-localization of the sum of E-local spectra is injective on homotopy groups

Proposition

Let $X_i$ be a collection of E-local spectra, then the unit of the E-localization of spectra

\begin{align*} \eta: \bigoplus_{i \in I} X_i \rightarrow \mathcal{L}_{E}(\bigoplus_{i \in I} X_i) \end{align*}

is an injection on homotopy groups.

Proof

E-localization is an exact functor Let $\mathbb{S}[n] \rightarrow \bigoplus_{i \in I} X_i$ represent an element in $\pi_n(\bigoplus X_i)$, then it lifts to $\bigoplus_{i=1}^n X_i$ (after reindexing, cf. Erwartungswert (Q12)compact spectrum is a finite spectrum)

By assumption $\bigoplus_{i=1}^n X_i \rightarrow \mathcal{L}_E(\bigoplus_{i=1}^n X_i)$ is an equivalence. Furthermore $\bigoplus_{i=1}^n X_i \hookrightarrow \bigoplus_{i \in I} X_i$ is a split mono, hence so is $\mathcal{L}_{E}(\bigoplus_{i=1}^n X_i) \hookrightarrow \mathcal{L}_{E}(\bigoplus_{i \in I} X_i)$ is also a split mono.

Therefore the composite $\mathbb{S}[n] \rightarrow \mathcal{L}_{E}(\bigoplus_{i \in I} X_i)$ is nullhomotopic if and only if $\mathbb{S}[n] \rightarrow \mathcal{L}_E(\bigoplus_{i=1}^n X_i)$ is nullhomotopic and hence if and only if $\mathbb{S}[n] \rightarrow \bigoplus_{i=1}^n X_i$.
