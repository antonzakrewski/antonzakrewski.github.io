cocartesian fibration and composition of locally cocartesian lifts

Proposition

Let $p: \mathcal{U} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a functor TFAE:

  1. $p$ is a cocartesian fibration
  2. $p$ is a locally cocartesian fibration & locally p-cocartesian morphism are closed under composition

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

composition of p-cartesian morphisms is p-cartesian

2) $\implies$ 1)
