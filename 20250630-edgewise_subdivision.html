edgewise subdivision on the simplex category

Definition

The edgewise subdivision is defined as functor on the simplex category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sd}: \Delta \rightarrow& \Delta \\ [n] \mapsto& [n] \star [n]^{\mathrm{op}} = \{0\}\simeq [2n + 1] \end{align*}

or even further as 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\Delta \rar{\Delta} & \Delta \times \Delta \rar{\mathrm{id} \times (-)^{\mathrm{op}}} & \Delta \times \Delta \rar{- \star - }& \Delta \\
[n] \arrow[mapsto, ]{r}{} & [n] \times [n] \arrow[mapsto, ]{r}{}& [n] \times [n}^{\mathrm{op}} \arrow[mapsto, ]{r}{}& [n] \star [n]^{\mathrm{op}}
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
