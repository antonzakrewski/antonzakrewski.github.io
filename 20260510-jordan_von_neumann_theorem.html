jordan-von neumann theorem

Proposition

Let $(X, \| \cdot \|)$ be a normed vector space. TFAE:

  1. $X$ is an inner product space with $\| \cdot \|^{\frac{1}{2}}$
  2. $\| \cdot \|$ satisfies the parallelogram identity:
\begin{align*} \| x + y\|^2 + \| x - y \|^2 = 2 \cdot (\| x \|^2 + \| y \|^2) \end{align*}

for all $x,y$ in $X$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from

\begin{align*} \| x + y\|^2 + \| x - y \|^2 = 2 \cdot (\| x \|^2 + \| y \|^2) =& \langle x,x\rangle + \langle y,y\rangle + \langle x,y\rangle + \langle y,x\rangle + \langle x,x\rangle + \langle -y,-y\rangle + \langle x,-y\rangle + \langle -y,x\rangle \\ =& 2 \cdot \langle x,x\rangle + 2 \cdot \langle y,y\rangle \\ =& 2 \cdot ( \|x \|^2 + \| y \|^2) \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)

Define the inner product

\begin{align*} \langle x,y\rangle = \begin{cases} \frac{1}{4} (\| x + y\|^2 - \| x - y\|^2) & \mbox{if } \mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R} \\ \frac{1}{4} (\| x + y\|^2 - \| x - y \|^2) - \frac{\mathrm{i}}{4} ( \| x + i \|^2 - \| x - i y\|^2) & \mbox{if } \mathbb{K} = \mathbb{C} \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

We claim that this defines an inner product

symmetry, definiteness
