unordered partition of a number

Definition

Let $n,k$ An unordered partition of $n$ with $k$ many summandes is an equivalence class of ordered partitions of $n$ with $k$ many summands up to permutation, i.e. $\Sigma_k$

Note that a convenient model of these numbers is given by ordered partitions $(x_1,...,x_k)$ where $x_i \geq x_{i+1}$
