collection of functions induces a function to the affine space

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic k-functor and $F \subseteq \mathcal{O}(X)$ a collection of functions. Then this is equivalently given by a morphism

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow \mathbb{A}^F \end{align*}

Proof

use:

  • products of affine spaces is again an affine space
