endofunctor as localization and equivalence

PROPERTIES: :ID: 526ee656-76be-48b2-a118-c7566481091b :END:

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathcal{M}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ an infinity endofunctor.

TFAE:

  1. There exists a natural transformation $\mu: \mathrm{id} \rightarrow \mathcal{M}$ such that for each object $X \in \mathcal{C}$ the morphisms
\begin{align*} M(\mu(X)), \mu(M(X)): M(X) \rightarrow M(M(X)) \end{align*}

are equivalences

  1. there exists an infinity category $\mathcal{D}$ and functors $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ such that
\begin{align*} M \simeq \mathcal{R} \circ \mathcal{L} \end{align*}

where $\mathcal{L}$ is a left bousfield localization / right bousfield localization

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
