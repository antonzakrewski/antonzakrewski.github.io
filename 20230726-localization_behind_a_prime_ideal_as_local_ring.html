localization at a prime ideal as local ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{p} \subseteq R$ a prime ideal and $S \setminus \mathfrak{p}$ Then the localization

\begin{align*} S^{-1}R = R_{\mathfrak{p}} \end{align*}

is a local ring with maximal ideal

\begin{align*} S^{-1}\mathfrak{p} \end{align*}

Proof

see: local ring and non-units as maximal ideal

strict ideal

Suppose $\frac{r}{s} = 1$, then $r \in S$, hence $\frac{r}{s} \not\in S^{-1}\mathfrak{p}$

units

Let $\frac{r}{s} \not\in S^{-1}\mathfrak{p}$, then we conclude, that $r \not\in \mathfrak{p}$, hence $r \in S$ and therefore

\begin{align*} \frac{r}{s} \cdot \frac{s}{r} = 1 \end{align*}
