Proposition

Let $A,k$ be fields, where $A$ is also a k-Algebra. Suppose there exists an Integral finite type \(k\)-Algebra $A'$ such $A \hookrightarrow A'$ is a A-subalgebra

Then $A$ is also integral over $k$

Proof

Assume that $A$ is not integral over $k$, then there exists an $\alpha \in A$ such that $f(\alpha) \neq 0$ for all $f \in K[X] \setminus \{0\}$

Furthermore by finite generation of $A'$ there exists a k-algebra epimorphism

\begin{align*} K[X_1,...,X_n,Y] \twoheadrightarrow A' \\ X_i \mapsto& \alpha_i \\ Y \mapsto& \alpha \eqqcolon \alpha_{n+1} \end{align*}

(see: free A-Algebra)

\begin{align*} k[\alpha_1,...,\alpha_r] \cong K[X_1,...,X_r] \end{align*}
