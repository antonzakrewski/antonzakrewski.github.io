cofinal approximation of a kappa filtered category by a directed set

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal, $\mathcal{C}$ be a kappa filtered infinity category. Then there exists a cofinal infinity functor

\begin{align*} N(A) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

where $A$ is a kappa filtered partially ordered set

Proof
