universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology

Proposition

Let $R$ be a hereditary ring, $X$ a topological space and $M$ an \(R\)-module. Then there exists a natural short exact sequence

of singular cohomology and singular homology

which also splits, albeit not naturally

see: Ext Functor

Proof

corollary of algebraic universal coefficient theorem

for $D = M[0]$ and $C = C^{\mathrm{sing}}(-,-)$ where $H(M[0]) = M[0]$ (cf. homology of a trivial boundary complex)

as chain complex of projectives and $C = C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X;R)$
