clutching construction for vector bundles

Definition

Let $f: X \rightarrow \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{C})$ be a continuous map Then the vector bundle $E_f \in \mathrm{Vect}(\Sigma X)$ is defined as

Let $X$ be a compact hausdorff space, $A,B \subseteq X$ be closed and $p_1: E_1 \rightarrow A, p_2: E_2 \rightarrow B$ be vector bundles and

\begin{align*} g: E_1_{\vert A \cap B} \rightarrow E_2_{\vert A \cap B} \end{align*}

be an isomorphism

Then the clutching construction is defined as

\begin{align*} (E_1,E_2,g) \coloneqq (E_1 \coprod E_2)/\sim \end{align*}

where $\sim$ is the equivalence relation generated by

\begin{align*} e \sim g(e) \end{align*}
