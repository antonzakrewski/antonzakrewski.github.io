cone as pointed contractible space

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $C(X)$ the Cone. Then $C(X)$ is pointed contractible

Proof

Consider

\begin{align*} H: [0,1] \times C(X) \rightarrow C(X) \\ (t, (h,x)) \mapsto& (t \cdot h,x) \end{align*}

where for $t = 0$ it holds, that

\begin{align*} H(0,(h,x)) = (0,x) = * \end{align*}
