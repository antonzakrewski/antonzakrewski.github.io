shift of a chain complex

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes. Let $C \in \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be a chain complex, then the shift $C[k]$ is defined as chain complex with

\begin{align*} (C[K])_n = C_{n-k} \end{align*}

with differential

\begin{align*} (-1)^{k} d^C \end{align*}
