some low homotopy groups of MSO

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{MSO}$ be the MSO spectrum. Then

  1. $\pi_0(\mathrm{MSO}) = \mathbb{Z}$
  2. $\pi_1(\mathrm{MSO}) = 0$
  3. $\pi_2(\mathrm{MSO}) = 0$

Proof

1)

here an oriented manifold is characterized by

  1. the amount of negatively oriented points
  2. the amount of positively oriented points

it is bordant to the difference. Furthermore, taking the difference is compatible with the sum. Therefore $\pi_0(\mathrm{MSO}) \simeq \mathbb{Z}$

2)

using classification of one dimensional manifolds the only 1 dimensional manifold is nullbordant.
