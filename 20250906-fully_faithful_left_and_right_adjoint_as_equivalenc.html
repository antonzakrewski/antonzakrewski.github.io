fully faithful left and right adjoint as equivalence

Proposition

Let $\begin{tikzcd} \mathcal{C} \arrow[r, shift left=1ex, "\mathcal{F}"{name=F}] & \mathcal{D}\arrow[l, shift left=.5ex, "\mathcal{G}"{name=G}] \arrow[phantom, from=G, to=F, , "\scriptscriptstyle\boldsymbol{\bot}"] \end{tikzcd}$ be an adjunction. Suppose both $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}$ are fully faithful.

Then $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}$ are both equivalences of categories.

Proof
