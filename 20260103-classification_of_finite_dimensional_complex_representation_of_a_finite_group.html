finite dimensional complex representation of a finite group splits into irreducible components

Proposition

Let $G$ be a finite group and $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{BG} \rightarrow \mathrm{FinVect}_{\mathbb{C}}$ be a finite dimensional vector space. Then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F} \cong \bigoplus_{i=1}^n \mathcal{F}_i^{\nu_i} \end{align*}

splits uniquely (up to isomorphism) into irreducible representations
