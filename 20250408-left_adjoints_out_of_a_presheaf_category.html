left adjoints out of a presheaf category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category and $\mathcal{D}$ a small category. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{D}), \mathcal{C}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

Proof

see:

  • infinity presheaf category as presentable category
  • infinity presheaf category arises by freely adding colimits
  • infinity right adjoint functor theorem for presentable categories

where

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{D}), \mathcal{C}) \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{D}), \mathcal{C}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C}) \\ \end{align*}
