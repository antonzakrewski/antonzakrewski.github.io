cardinality of unordered partitions with k summands and maximal number k

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{P}_{n,k}$ be the set of unordered partition with $k$ summands and $\mathcal{P}_{n}^k$ the unordered partitions with arbitrary many summands but $k$ as maximum number. Then the transposition of ferres diagrams induces a Bijection

\begin{align*} \mathcal{P}_{n,k} \rightarrow \mathcal{P}_{n}^k \end{align*}

Proof
