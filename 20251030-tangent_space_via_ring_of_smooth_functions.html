tangent space via ring of smooth functions

Proposition

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold and $p\in M$ a point. Let $C^{\infty}(M)$ be the ring of smooth, real valued functions.

Then the tangent space is given by

\begin{align*} T_p^{\mathrm{alg}}(M) = \{\text{ derivations at } p\} \end{align*}
