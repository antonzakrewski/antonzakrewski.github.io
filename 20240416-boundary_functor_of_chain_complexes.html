boundary functor of chain complexes

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes. Then the boundary functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Bound}: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \\ A_{*} \mapsto& (\mathrm{im}(\partial^n)) \\ (f: A_{*} \rightarrow B_{*}) \mapsto& (\mathrm{bound}(f): \mathrm{Bound}(A_*) \rightarrow \mathrm{Bound}(A_*) \end{align*}
