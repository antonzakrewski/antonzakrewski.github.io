Proposition

Every finite dimensional representation admits a filtration.

Proof

Proof by induction, we argue by induction on $\mathrm{dim}(V)$. Choose a simple subrepresentation $V_i \subseteq V$ (cf. finite dimensional representation admits an irreducible subrepresentation). Then consider $V/V_i$, which has $\mathrm{dim}(V/V_i < \mathrm{dim}(V)$. Then our inductive hypothesis gives a filtration $V_0 \subseteq V_1 \subseteq ... \subseteq V_n$ of $V/V_i$ Now we define $V_i = \pi^{-1}(V_{i-1})$ for $\pi: V \twoheadrightarrow V/V_i$ for $i \geq 1$. Thne $W_i$ give a filtration by $V_{i+1}/V_i \cong (V_{i+1}/V_1)/(V_{i}/V_1) \cong U_i/V_{i-1}$ is simple.
