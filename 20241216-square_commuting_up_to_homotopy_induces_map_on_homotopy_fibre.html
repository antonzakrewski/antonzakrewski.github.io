square commuting up to homotopy induces map on homotopy fibre

Proposition

Let

be a diagram commuting up to a specified homotopy $H: A \times [0,1] \rightarrow D$

Let $c \in C, d \in D$ such that $g(c) = d$

Then this induces a map on homotopy fibres

where the upper square commutes strictly

Proof

by precomposition of homotopic maps we get a specified homotopy from

  1. $\mathrm{hofib}_c(f_1) \rightarrow A \rightarrow B \rightarrow D$

to

  1. $\mathrm{hofib}_c(f_1) \rightarrow A \rightarrow C \rightarrow D$

Then as the square

commutes we get a homotopy between

  1. $\mathrm{hofib}_c(f_1) \rightarrow A \rightarrow C \rightarrow D$
  2. $\mathrm{hofib}_c(f_1) \rightarrow P_c(C) \rightarrow C \rightarrow D$

here we have a specified nullhomotopy between

  1. $\mathrm{hofib}_c(f_1) \rightarrow P_c(C) \rightarrow C$
  2. $\mathrm{const}_c$

this induces a specified nullhomotopy between

  1. $\mathrm{hofib}_c(f_1) \rightarrow P_c(C) \rightarrow C \rightarrow D$
  2. $\mathrm{const}_d$

given by postcomposition with $C \rightarrow D$

Then glueing (and reversing) homotopies we get a homotopy between

  1. $\mathrm{const}_d$
  2. $\mathrm{hofib}_c(f_1) \rightarrow A \rightarrow B \rightarrow D$

which we take as defining map for the universal property of the homotopy fibre
