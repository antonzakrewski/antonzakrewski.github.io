tensor product with a connective spectrum preserves connective maps

Proposition

Let $X$ be an $n$ connective spectrum, that is $X \in \mathrm{Sp}_{\geq n}$. Then the functor $X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} -$ restricts to a functor

\begin{align*} X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} -: \mathrm{Sp}_{\geq m} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}_{\geq n + m} \end{align*}
