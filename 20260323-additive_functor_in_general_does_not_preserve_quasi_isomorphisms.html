induced functor on chain complexes preserves quasi-isomorphisms and exact

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ be an additive functor and $\mathrm{Ch}(\mathcal{F}): \mathrm{Ch}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ch}(\mathcal{B})$ the induced functor. TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is exact
  2. $\mathrm{Ch}(\mathcal{F})$ preserves quasi-isomorphisms

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Proof by contraposition Assume that $\mathrm{Ch}(\mathcal{F})$ preserves quasi-isomorphisms.

2) $\implies$ 1)
