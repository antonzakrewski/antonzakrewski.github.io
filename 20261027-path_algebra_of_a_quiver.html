path algebra of a quiver

Definition

Let $A$ be a ring and $Q$ a quiver Then the path algebra is defined as $KQ = \bigoplus_{\mathrm{path}} K[p]$ and multiplication defined as

\begin{align*} p \cdot q = \begin{cases} p \circ q & \mbox{if } t(q_n) = s(p_0) \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Remark

here the unit is the sum of $1$ times the empty paths
