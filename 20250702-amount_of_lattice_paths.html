amount of lattice paths

Proposition

Let $m,n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then there exist

\begin{align*} \binom{m+n}{m} \end{align*}

many lattice paths

Proof

follows from the combinatorial interpretation of the binomial coefficient: from $m + n$ many steps (step number 1,...,n+m) we choose $m$ many to go in one specific direction
