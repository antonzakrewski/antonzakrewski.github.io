F2 hopf invariant

Definition

Let $f: S^{2n-1} \rightarrow S^n$ be a continuous map for $n \geq 2$. Then the hopf invariant is defined as

\begin{align*} h(f) \coloneqq \alpha \cup \alpha \in H^{2n}(X(f);\mathbb{F}_2) \end{align*}

where $\alpha$ is the unique nonzero element in $H^n(X(f), \mathbb{F}_2) = \mathbb{F}_2$
