derived tensor product and product of degreewise projective homology in a hereditary ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be a hereditary (?) ring, $M,N$ objects in the derived category such that $H_n(N)$ is a projective module for $n \in \mathbb{Z}$. Then

\begin{align*} H_n(M \otimes_{R} N) \simeq \bigoplus_{m + l = n} H_m(M) \otimes_R H_n(N) \end{align*}

where the RHS is the ordinary tensor product.
