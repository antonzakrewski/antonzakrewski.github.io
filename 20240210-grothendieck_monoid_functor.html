grothendieck group functor

Definition / Proposition

Gvien Category CMon and Category Ab, the grothendieck monoid functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Grothen}: \mathrm{CMon} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ab} \\ M \mapsto& \mathrm{Groth}(M) \\ (f: M \rightarrow N) \mapsto& (\mathrm{Groth}(f): \mathrm{Groth}(M) \rightarrow \mathrm{Groth}(N)) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Groth}(M)$ is the grothendieck group and $\mathrm{Groth}(f)$ is the induced morphism
