relative infinite general linear group

Definition

Let \(A\) be a ring, $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq R$ an ideal. Let $R \times_{R/\mathfrak{a}} R$ denote the pullback in category Ring

resp.

Then the relative infinite general linear group is defined as kernel

\begin{align*} \mathrm{GL}(R,\mathfrak{a}) := \mathrm{ker}(\pi_*: \mathrm{GL}(R \times_{R/\mathfrak{a}} R) \rightarrow \mathrm{GL}(R)) \end{align*}

in category Group
