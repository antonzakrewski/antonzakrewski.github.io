homotopy groups of highly connected wedges

Proposition

Let $X_i$ be a collection of n-connected anima. Then for $k \leq 2n + 2???$, we have

\begin{align*} \pi_k(\bigvee_{i \in I} X_i) \cong \bigoplus_{i} \pi_k(X_i) \end{align*}

Proof

If I haven't messed the indices up, then we are in the stable range, i.e. freudenthals suspension theorem implies that

\begin{align*} \pi_k(\bigvee_{i \in I} X_i) \cong& \pi_k(\widehat{\mathbb{S}}[\bigvee_{i \in I} X_i]) \\ \cong& \pi_k(\bigoplus_{i \in I} \widehat{\mathbb{S}}[X_i]) \\ \cong& \bigoplus_{i \in I} \pi_k( \widehat{\mathbb{S}}[X_i]) \\ \cong& \bigoplus_{i \in I} \pi_k(X_i) \end{align*}

where we have used that the homotopy group functor is an additive functor from spectra.
