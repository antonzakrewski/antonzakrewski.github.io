homotopy orbit functor preserves weak equivalences

Proposition

The homotopy orbit functor sends weak homotopy equivalences to weak homotopy equivalences

Proof

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a G-equivariant weak homotopy equivalence

Then we get the <fibration sequences> 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}Y & Y_{hG} & BG 

X \arrow[]{d}{\cong} \rar  & X_{hG} \rar \dar{f_{hG} & BG \dar \arrow[]{d}{\cong}\\
Y \rar & Y_{hG} \rar & BG 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

or the long exact sequences of a serre fibration

Then by the Five-Lemma this is an isomorphism for $\pi_n(-)$ and $n \geq 1$

$n = 0$
