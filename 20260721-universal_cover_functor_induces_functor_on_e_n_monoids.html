universal cover functor induces functor on E_n monoids

Proposition

The universal cover functor induces a functor

\begin{align*} \tilde{(-)}: \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_n}(\mathrm{An}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_n}(\mathrm{An}) \end{align*}

Proof

  • universal cover functor is pointed
  • universal cover functor is product preserving
  • product preserving functor induces functor on cartesian monoids
