section of a vector bundle as morphism from 1-dimensional trivial bundle

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{K} \in {\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}, \mathbb{H}}$, $X$ a topological space and $p: E \rightarrow X$ a topological vector bundle Then a section is equivalently given by a morphism of topological vector bundles

\begin{align*} X \times \mathbb{K} \rightarrow E \end{align*}

Proof
