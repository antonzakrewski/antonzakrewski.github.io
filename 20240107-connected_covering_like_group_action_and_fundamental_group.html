simply connected covering like group action and pi_1

Proposition

Let $B$ be a topological space, $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a simply connected covering given by a covering-like group action

\begin{align*} E \times G \rightarrow E/G = B \end{align*}

then for the fundamental group, it holds, that

\begin{align*} \pi_1(B,b) = G \end{align*}
