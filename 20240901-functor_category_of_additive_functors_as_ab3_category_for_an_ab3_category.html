functor category of additive functors as ab3 category for an ab3 category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an Ab-enriched category, $\mathcal{B}$ an Ab3 category. Then the functor category of additive functors

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{add}}{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}} \end{align*}

is also an AB3 category

Proof
