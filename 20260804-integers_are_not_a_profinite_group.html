integers are not a profinite group

Proposition

The integers are not a profinite group.

Proof

Consider the map $\iota: \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \widehat{\mathbb{Z}} \simeq \lim_{n} \mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z}$.

injective

We claim that $\iota$ is injective: Let $z \in \mathbb{Z} \setminus \{0\}$, then $z \neq 0$ in $\mathbb{Z}/(\vert z \vert + 1) \mathbb{Z}$ and hence $\iota(z) \neq 0$.

dense image

Let $U$ be an open subset, then it is contained in $\prod_{n' \geq n} \mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z}$ for some $n \in \mathbb{N}$. In particular, $\iota(n+1)$ lies in $\prod_{n' \geq n} \mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z}$ and hence in $U$.

not surjective
