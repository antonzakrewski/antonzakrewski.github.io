degree under the induced ring monomorphism for the polynomial rings

Proposition

Let \(A,B\) be rings, $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ a algebraic ring-monomorphism and $\varphi^X: A[X] \rightarrow B[X]$ the induced ring homomorphism for the polynomial rings Then for $f \in A[X]$ and $\varphi^X(f)$ it follows that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{deg}(\varphi^X(f)) =& \mathrm{deg}(f) \end{align*}

Proof

induced ring homomorphism for the polynomial rings as monomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{deg}(\varphi^K(\alpha X^n)) = n \end{align*}

Therefore we conclude $\mathrm{deg}(\varphi^X(f)) \geq \mathrm{deg}(f)$
