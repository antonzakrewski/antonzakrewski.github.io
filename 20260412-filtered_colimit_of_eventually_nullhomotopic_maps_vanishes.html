filtered colimit of eventually nullhomotopic maps vanishes

Proposition

Let $(X_i)_{I}$ be a filtered diagram such that for each $i$ there exists some $j$ in $I$ and a nullhomotopic map $X_i \rightarrow X_j$. Then $\mathrm{colim}_I X_i$ is equivalent to $0$

Proof

follows from

  • homotopy group functor of spectra preserves filtered colimits
  • explicit description of colimits in $\mathrm{Ab}$
