symmetrische differenz als leere menge

Proposition

Let $X$ be a set, $A,B \subseteq X$ subsets and $A \Delta B$ the symmetric difference TFAE:

  1. $A = B$
  2. $A \Delta B = \empytset$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Note that

\begin{align*} A \Delta A =& \{x \in A \cup A \vert x \not\in A \cap A\} \\ =& \{x \in A \vert x \not\in A\} \\ =& \emptyset \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)
