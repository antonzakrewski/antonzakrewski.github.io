infinity subcategory

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category. Then a sub infinity category is defined as sub simplicial set $\mathcal{C}^'$ such that

  1. $\mathcal{C}'$ contains identities in an quasicategory
  2. $\mathcal{C}'$ is closed under a) composition in a quasicategory

b) equivalence in a quasicategory
