mapping space and product as continuous bijection

Proposition

Let $X_i$ be a familiy of topological spaces and $Y$ another space. Suppose $\mathrm{Map}()$ is the mapping space with the compact open topology. Then the for the categorical product, the canonical map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}(Y, \prod_{i \in I} X_i) \rightarrow \prod_{i \in I} \mathrm{Map}(Y, X_i) \end{align*}

is a bijective continuous map
