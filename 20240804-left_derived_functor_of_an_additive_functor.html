left derived functor of an additive functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, where $\mathcal{A}$ admits enough projectives Suppose $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ is an additive functor

Then the left derived functor is defined as delta functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{LF}_n \end{align*}

as constructed in existence of a left derived functor for a category with enough projectives
