mapping anima into local object as local object

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a and $S$ a collection of morphisms and $C$ an S-local object Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(B,C) \end{align*}

is also s-local for $B \in \mathrm{An}$

Proof

follows from

  • local object closed under limits

and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(B, C) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(\mathrm{colim}_B *, C) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{lim}_{B} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(*,C) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{lim}_{B} C \\ \end{align*}
