site of G-Actions

Definition / Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be some inaccessible cardinal, $G$ a group with $\vert G \vert \leq \kappa$ and $\mathrm{G-Set}_{\alpha}$ the Category of G-Sets. Then the site of \(G\)-actions is defined by the covering families

\begin{align*} \{\{f_i: U_i \rightarrow U \} \vert \bigcup f[U_i] = U\} \end{align*}

Proof

1) iso

follows as isos in the category of \(G\)-Sets are surjective

2) refinement

follows analogously

  • site of union in Set kappa
  • faithful functor and transitivity of a site

3) pullback

corollary of:

  • site of union in Set kappa
  • faithful functor preserving pullbacks and site
