inner product on class functions for a finite group

Definition / Proposition

Let $G$ be a finite group and $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Set}}(C_{G},\mathbb{C}) \cong \prod_{C_G} \mathbb{C}$ be the vector space, where $C_G$ is the set of conjugation classes. Then

\begin{align*} \langle -, -\rangle: \mathrm{Hom}(C_G, \mathbb{C}) \times \mathrm{Hom}(C_G, \mathbb{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}(C_G, \mathbb{C}) \\ (\varphi, \psi) \mapsto \frac{1}{\vert G \vert} \sum_{g \in G} \overline{\varphi([g]} \psi[g]) \end{align*}

is an inner product.
