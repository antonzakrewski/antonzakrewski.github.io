finitely generated submodule of a free module as submodule of a finitely generated free module

Proposition

Let \(A\) be a ring, $A[B]$ the free module for some set $B$ and $M$ a finitely generated submodule Then there exists a finite subset $B' \subseteq B$ such that

\begin{align*} M \subseteq R[B'] \subseteq R[B] \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of finitely generated submodule of a module and finite subset of a generating set
