functorality of straightening-unstraightening for cocartesian fibrations

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an infinity functor Then precomposition on the functor categories

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}^*: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D},\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C},\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}) \rightarrow \end{align*}

induces the pullback functor of cocartesian fibrations under the straightening-unstraightening of cocartesian fibrations

Proof
