infima and subset

Proposition

Let $X$ be a partially ordered set, $A \subseteq B \subseteq X$ subsets such that the Infima $\mathrm{inf}(A), \mathrm{inf}(B)$ exist. Then $\mathrm{inf}(A) \leq \mathrm{inf}(B)$

Proof

Note that for each $b \in B$ it follows that $b \geq \mathrm{inf}(B)$. Hence for each $a \in A$ it follows that $a \geq \mathrm{inf}(B)$ as $A \subseteq B$.

But then by maximality of the infimum of $A$ it follows that $\mathrm{inf}(A) \geq \mathrm{inf}(B)$
