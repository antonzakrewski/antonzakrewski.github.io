equivalence and p-cartesian

Proposition

Let $p: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be an inner fibration between infinity categories and $f: \Delta^1 \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a \(1\)-simplex TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an equivalence
  2. $f$ is p-cartesian and $p(f)$ is an equivalence

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $1: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \Delta^0$ and let $f$ be \(p\)-cartesian and $p(f)$ an equivalence.

Then $p(f)$ is \(1\)-cartesian (cf. terminal morphism cartesian morphism and equivalence)

By preimage of a q-cartesian morphism and composition as qp-cartesian $f$ being p-cartesian is equivalent to $f$ being \(1\circ p\)-cartesian or \(1\)-cartesian. Here $f$ is \(p\)-cartesian, thus $f$ is \(1\)-cartesian and it follows that $f$ is an equivalence (cf. terminal morphism cartesian morphism and equivalence )
