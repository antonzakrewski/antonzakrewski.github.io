valuation of a Field

Definition

Let $F$ be a field and $(\Gamma, +, \geq)$ an abelian totally ordered group. Then a valuation of $F$ is a map

\begin{align*} \nu: F \rightarrow \gamma \sqcup \{\infty\} \end{align*}

such that:

  1. $\nu(a) = \infty$ if and only if $a = 0$
  2. $\nu(a \cdot b) = \nu(a) + \nu(b)$
  3. $\nu(a + b) \geq \mathrm{min}(\nu(a), \nu(b))$
