Homology of a lens space with coefficients in F_p

Proposition

Let $p \neq q$ be a prime number, $L = L(q;q_1,...,q_d)$ the \(2d-1\)-dimensional lens space and $\mathbb{F}_p$ a finite field Then the homology of the lens space is given by

\begin{align*} H_*(L;F_{p}) = \end{align*}

Proof

\begin{align*} H_*(L;\mathbb{F}_p) =& H_*(S^{2d-1}/ \mathbb{F}_q; \mathbb{F}_p) \\ =& H_*(S^{2d-1}; \mathbb{F}_p) \otimes_{\mathbb{F}_p \mathbb{F}_q} \mathbb{F}_p \end{align*}
