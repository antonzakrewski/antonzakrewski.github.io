cosimplicial 2-category of simplices

Definition

The cosimplicial \(2\)-category of simplices is defined as cosimplicial 2-category $\mathfrak{c}(\Delta^n)$ with

  1. $\mathrm{Ob}(\mathfrak{c}(\Delta^n)) = \{0,...,n\}$

2)

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathfrak{c}(\Delta^n)}(i,j) =& \begin{cases} \emptyset & \mbox{if } i > j \\ P_{i,j} & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

where a) $P_{i,j}$ is the cube poset b) the unit is defined as $* \mapsto \{i\} \subseteq P_{i,i}$ c) composition is defined by the cube poset union / cube poset union functor

Proof

unit

follows as union with $\{i\}$ is the identity, since each element in $P_{i,j}$ resp. $P_{j,i}$ already contains $i$

composition

note that image and union commute.
