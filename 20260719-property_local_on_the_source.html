property of morphisms local on the source

Definition

Let $P$ be a property of morphisms of schemes. Then $P$ is local on the source if for any morphism $f: Y \rightarrow X$:

  1. If $f$ has property $P$ and $V \xhookrightarrow{\iota} Y$ is an open subscheme, then $f \circ \iota: V \rightarrow X$ also has the property $P$
  2. If $V_i \xhookrightarrow{\iota_i} X$ is an <open covering> and each $f \circ \iota_i$ has the property $P$, then $f$ has the property P.
