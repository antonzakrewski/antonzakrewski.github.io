inverse image sheaf

Definition

Let $(X,\mathcal{T}_X)$ and $(Y,\mathcal{T}_Y)$ be topological spaces and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a continuous map. Given a (sufficiently) bicomplete category $\mathcal{C}$ the \(\mathcal{C}\)-valued category of sheaves $\mathrm{Sh}(X), \mathrm{Sh}(Y)$, the inverse image sheaf of a sheaf $\mathcal{F} \in \mathrm{Sh}(Y)$ is given as sheafification of

\begin{align*} f^{-1} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Op}(X) \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ U \mapsto& \mathrm{colim}_{V \supseteq f[U]} \mathcal{F}(V) \\ (\iota^{\mathrm{op}}: U \rightarrow U') \mapsto& (\iota': \mathrm{colim}_{V \supseteq f[U]} \mathcal{F}(V) \rightarrow \mathrm{colim}_{V \supseteq f[U']} \mathcal{F}(V)) \end{align*}

where the morphism is given by applying the universal property, since for $U' \subseteq U$ it follows, that

\begin{align*} f[U'] \subseteq f[U] \end{align*}

and hence for $V \supseteq f[U']$ also $f[U'] \subseteq V$

functorality follows analogously to the colim functor
