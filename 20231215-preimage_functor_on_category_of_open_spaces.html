preimage functor on category of open spaces

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be topological spaces, $\mathrm{Op}_X,\mathrm{Op}_Y$ the Category of Open Subsets and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a continuous map Then the map

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_f: \mathrm{Op}_Y \rightarrow& \mathrm{Op}_X \\ O \mapsto& f^{-1}[O] \\ (\iota: O \rightarrow O') \mapsto& (\iota: f^{-1}[O] \rightarrow f^{-1}[O']) \end{align*}

Proof

welldefined

  • on objects follows from continuity
  • on morphisms follows from preimage preserves order

functorial

follows from

  • partially ordered set as thin category
  • commuting in a posetal category

as $\iota: f^{-1}[O] \rightarrow f^{-1}[O]$ has to be the identity
