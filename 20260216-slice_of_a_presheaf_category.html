slice of a presheaf category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F} \in \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ be a presheaf Then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})_{\mathcal{F}} \simeq \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C} \times_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})} \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})_{/\mathcal{F}}) \end{align*}

Proof

consider the pullback

Then $\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ is fully faithful (cf. yoneda embedding) so $\mathcal{C}/ \mathcal{F} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})_{/\mathcal{F}}$ is also fully faithful.
