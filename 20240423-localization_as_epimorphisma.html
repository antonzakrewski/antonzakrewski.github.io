localization of commutative rings is an epimorphism

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $S \subseteq A$ a multiplicatively closed subset and $S^{-1}A$ the localization Then the inclusion

\begin{align*} \iota: A \rightarrow S^{-1}A \end{align*}

is an epimorphism in category Ring

Proof

follows from the uniqueness of the universal property of a ring localization todo
