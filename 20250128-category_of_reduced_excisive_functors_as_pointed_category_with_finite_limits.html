category of reduced excisive functors as pointed category with finite limits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, where $\mathcal{C}$ is pointed infinity category and $\mathcal{D}$ is finitely complete.

Then $\mathrm{Exc}_*(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ is a pointed category which is finitely cocomplete

Proof

pointed

Let $x \in \mathcal{C}$ and $\tilde{x} \in \mathcal{D}$ be the terminal object in $\mathcal{D}$ Then $\mathrm{const}_{\tilde{x}} \in \mathrm{Exc}_*(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$

Now consider $Y \in \mathrm{Exc}_{*}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})}(\mathrm{const}_{\tilde{x}},Y) \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\tilde{x}, \mathrm{lim}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y)) \end{align*}

note that $* \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is both left / right anodyne map By right anodyne and cofinal / left anodyne map as coinitial map the map is both coinitial / cofinal

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})}(\mathrm{const}_{\tilde{x}},Y) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\tilde{x}, \mathrm{lim}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\tilde{x}, Y(x)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\tilde{x}, \tilde{x}) \\ \simeq& * \end{align*}
