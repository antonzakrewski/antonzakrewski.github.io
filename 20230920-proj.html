Proj

Definition

Let \(R = \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0} R_n\) be a N-graded ring Then $\mathrm{Proj}$ on $S$ is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Proj} = \{\mathfrak{p} \subseteq R, \text{ homogeneous prime ideal }, R_{+} \not\subseteq \mathfrak{p}\} \end{align*}

where $r_+$ is the irrelevant ideal
