finite intersection of covering sieves is a covering sieve

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \tau)$ be a site and $R_1,...,R_n \subseteq よ(X)$ be covering sieves. Then so is $\bigcap R_i$.

Proof

Let $f \in R_1$. Then it suffices to show that $f^*(R_1 \cap R_2)$ is a covering sieve for all $f \in R_1$. Then base change functor preserves pullbacks, so we have a diagram

Here the back square is a pullback and the top left, back entry is given by

\begin{align*} f^*(R_1 \cap R_2) \simeq f^*(R_2) \end{align*}

Here $f^*(R_2)$ is a covering sieve since $R_2$ is a covering sieve.
