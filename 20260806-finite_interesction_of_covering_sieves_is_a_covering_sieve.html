finite intersection of covering sieves is a covering sieve

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \tau)$ be a site and $R_1,...,R_n \subseteq よ(X)$ be covering sieves. Then so is $\bigcap R_i$.

Proof

observe that the empty intersection is the maximal sieve. So let $R_1,R_2$ be covering sieves and $f: Y \rightarrow X \in R_1$. Then $f^*(R_1) = よ(Y)$ (cf. pullback of a sieve along an element of the sieve as maximal sieve) and hence we get

\begin{align*} \{Z \rightarrow Y \vert Z \rightarrow Y \rightarrow X \in R \cap S\} =& \{Z \rightarrow Y \vert Z \rightarrow Y \rightarrow X \in S\} \\ =& f^*(S) \end{align*}

which is by assumption a covering sieve.
