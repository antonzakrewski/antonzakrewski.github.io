\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sch}_{S}^{\mathrm{qcqs}} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Pro}(\mathrm{Sch}^{\mathrm{fp}}_{S}) \end{align*}

is fully faithful with essential image given by the digarams with affine transition morphisms.
