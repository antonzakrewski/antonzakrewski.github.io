localization preserves projective modules

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $S \subseteq A$ a multiplicatively closed subset and

\begin{align*} S^{-1}(-) \end{align*}

the localization functor

Then $S^{-1}$ preserves projective objects

Proof

a)

Let $A^\kappa = M \oplus N$, then by additivity of

\begin{align*} (S^{-1}A)^{\kappa} \cong S^{-1}(A^{\kappa}) \cong S^{-1}(M \oplus N) \cong S^{-1}M \oplus S^{-1}N \end{align*}

b)

restriction of scalars as exact functor localization functor as left adjoint left adjoint of an exact functor preserves projective objects
