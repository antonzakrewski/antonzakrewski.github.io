f2 Homology of the universal cover of a f2 moore space

Proposition

Let $M(\mathbb{F}_2,1)$ be a Moore space and $Y$ its universal cover Then the $\mathbb{F}_2$ homology is given by

\begin{align*} H_*(M(\mathbb{F}_2,n),\mathbb{F}_2) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{F}_2 & \mbox{if } * \in \{0,2\} \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from short exact sequence of f2-chains for the universal cover and absolute Hurewicz theorem
