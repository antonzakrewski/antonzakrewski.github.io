homogeneous localization at a prime ideal

Definition / Proposition

Let \(R = \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} R_n\) be a Z-graded ring, $\mathfrak{p} \subseteq R$ a homogeneous prime ideal. Then the set $T = \{F \in S \setminus P \vert f \mathrm{homogeneous}\}$ is a multiplicatively closed subset and the homogeneous localization is defined as localization with the induced grading

\begin{align*} \mathrm{deg}(\frac{G}{H}) = \mathrm{deg}(G) - \mathrm{deg}(H) \end{align*}
