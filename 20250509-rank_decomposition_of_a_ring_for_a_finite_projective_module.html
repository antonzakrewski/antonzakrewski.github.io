rank decomposition of a ring for a finite projective module

Definition / Proposition

Let \(R\) be a commutative ring and $P$ a finite projective module. Then $P$ induces a decomposition

\begin{align*} R \simeq \prod_{i=1}^n R_i \end{align*}

into some $R_i$

Proof

use:

  • rank decomposition of spec for a finite projective module
  • equivalence between the category of affine schemes and commutative rings
