wu class of a poincare duality complex vanishing above half of the dimension

Proposition

let $X$ be a $d$ dimensional poincare duality complex and $2i > d$. Then $v_i(D_X) = 0$

Proof

Note that $v_i(D_X) = v_i(X)$ is the unique class such that

\begin{align*} v_i(X) \cdot - = \mathrm{Sq}^i(-): H^{d-i}(X) \rightarrow H^d(X) \end{align*}

Now if $2i > d$ then $i > d - 1$ and hence by triviality of steenrod operation the map $\mathrm{Sq}^i(-)$ is zero
