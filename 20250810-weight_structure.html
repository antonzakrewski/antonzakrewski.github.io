weight structure

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category Then a weight structure on $\mathcal{C}$ is given by subcategories $\mathcal{C}_{w \leq 0}, \mathcal{C}_{w \geq 0}$

  1. $\Sigma \mathcal{C}_{w \geq 0} \subseteq \mathcal{C}_{w \geq 0}$ and $\Omega \mathcal{C}_{w \leq 0} \subseteq \mathcal{C}_{w \leq 0}$. Write $\mathcal{C}_{w \leq n} \coloneqq \Sigma^n \mathcal{C}_{w \leq 0}, \mathcal{C}_{w \geq n} \coloneqq \Sigma^n \mathcal{C}_{w \geq 0}$
  2. for $X \in \mathcal{C}_{w \leq 0}$ and $Y \in \mathcal{C}_{w \geq 1}$ we have $\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y) = 0$ (this is reversed compared to a t-structure)
  3. for any $X \in \mathcal{C}$ there exists a bifiber sequence

where $\tau_{\leq 0}(X) \in \mathcal{C}_{w \leq 0}, \tau_{\geq 0}(X) \in \mathcal{C}_{w \geq 0}$ (this is also reversed compared to a t-structure)

Remark

If we take the opposite category we still are not a t-structure, since then 1) is reversed
