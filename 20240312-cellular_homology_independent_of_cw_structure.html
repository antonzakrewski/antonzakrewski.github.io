cellular homology independent of CW Structure

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space and $(X, \emptyset, \mathrm{SK}_n(X)), (X, \emptyset, \mathrm{SK}_n(X)')$ be CW-structures on $X$. Then it holds for the cellular homology

\begin{align*} H_n^{\mathrm{cell}}(X, \emptyset, \mathrm{SK}_n(X)) \cong H_n^{\mathrm{cell}}( X, \emptyset, \mathrm{SK}_n(X)') \end{align*}

Proof

follows from cellular homology isomorphic to singular homology
