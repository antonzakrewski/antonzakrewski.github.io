E-local and E-acyclic spectrum is trivial

Proposition

Let $E$ be a spectrum and $X$ an E-local and E-acyclic spectrum. Then $X \simeq 0$.

Proof

Recall that for an \(E\)-local spectrum, we have

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(A,X) = 0 \end{align*}

for an \(E\)-acyclic spectrum $A$. Now this implies $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X,X) = 0$ so the identity morphism is the zero morphism, hence $X$ is the zero object.
