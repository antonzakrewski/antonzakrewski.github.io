descent of a presheaf with respect to a sieve

Definition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ be a presheaf and $S$ a sieve on $X$, i.e. $S \rightarrow X$ Then $\mathcal{F}$ satisfies descent with respect to $S$ if the canonical map

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(X) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(S) = \mathrm{Hom}(S,\mathcal{F}) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism
