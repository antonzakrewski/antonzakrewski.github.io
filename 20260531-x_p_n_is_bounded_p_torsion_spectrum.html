X/p^n is bounded p-torsion spectrum

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then $X/p^n$ is a bounded p-torsion spectrum (in fact $\pi_*(X/p^n)$ is a $\mathbb{Z}/p^{2n} \mathbb{Z}$ module).

Proof

follows from the long exact sequence in the heart for a t-structure for

\begin{align*} X \xrightarrow{\cdot p^n} X \rightarrow X/p^n \end{align*}

in particular we may cutting a short exact sequence out of a long exact sequence and we get a short exact sequence todo
