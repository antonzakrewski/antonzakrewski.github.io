cosheaf on a topological space

Definition

Let $X$ be a topological space and $\mathcal{C}$ an infinity category. Then a cosheaf is an infinity functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Op}(X) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

such that for a collection of open subsets $\mathcal{U} \subseteq \mathrm{Op}(X)$ which is closed under finite intersection

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{U\in\mathcal{U}} \mathcal{F}(U) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(\bigcup_{U \in \mathcal{U}} U) \end{align*}

is an equivalence
