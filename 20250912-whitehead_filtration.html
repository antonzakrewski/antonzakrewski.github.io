whitehead filtration and tensor product in spectra

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Fil}_{\geq *}(\mathrm{Sp})$ be the full subcategory containing filtered spectra $\mathrm{fil} X^*$ such that $\mathrm{fil} X^n \in \mathrm{Sp}_{\geq n}$ for all $n \in \mathbb{Z}$. Then the tensor product of spectra restricts to $\mathrm{Fil}_{\geq *} \mathrm{Sp}_{\geq n}$
