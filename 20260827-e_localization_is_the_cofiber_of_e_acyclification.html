E-localization is the cofiber of E-acyclification

Definition / Proposition

Let $E,X$ be spectra. Let $A_{E}(X)$ be the E-acyclification and $A_{E}(X) \rightarrow X$ the counit

Then the cofiber is an E-local equivalence $X \rightarrow \mathrm{cofib}(\epsilon(X))$ into an E-local spectrum

Proof

E-local

equivalent characterization of E-local spectrum Let $Y$ be an E-acyclic spectrum, then the map $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(Y, A_{E}(X)) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(Y, X)$ by some right bousfield localization argument. This implies that $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(Y, \mathrm{cofib}(\epsilon(X))) \simeq 0$.

E-equivalence

follows as the fiber is by construction $E$ acyclic.
