lie algebra functor is faithful on connected lie groups

Proposition

The lie algebra functor is a faithful on the full subcategory of connected lie groups

Proof

Consider the <exponential map>

\begin{align*} \mathrm{exp}^G: \mathfrak{g} \rightarrow& G \\ X \mapsto& \gamma_{e}^X(1) \end{align*}

Then

If $\mathrm{exp}(\mathfrak{g}) = G$, then this would determine $f(g)$ for all $g \in G$

But $\mathrm{exp}^G$ is in general not surjective (cf. exponential map for a lie group) but

\begin{align*} \mathrm{D}\mathrm{exp}_{e}^G: T_{e} \mathfrak{g} = \mathfrak{g} \rightarrow T_{e} G = \mathfrak{g} \end{align*}
