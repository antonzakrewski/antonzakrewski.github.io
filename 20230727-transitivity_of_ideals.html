transitivity of ideals

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a}$ an ideal and

\begin{align*} \pi_{\mathfrak{a}}: A \rightarrow A/\mathfrak{a} \end{align*}

the canonical Projektion TFAE:

  1. $\mathfrak{b} \subseteq A/\mathfrak{a}$ is an ideal
  2. $\pi_{\mathfrak{a}}^{-1}(\mathfrak{b}) \subseteq A$ is an ideal

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

surjektives Bild eines Ideals als Ideal

2) $\implies$ 1)

preimage of an ideal as ideal
