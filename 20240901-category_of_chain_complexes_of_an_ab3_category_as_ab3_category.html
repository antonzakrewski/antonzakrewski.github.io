category of chain complexes of an AB3 category as AB3 category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an Ab3 category Then $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ is also an AB3 category

Proof

  1. category of chain complexes as functor category of enriched functors
  2. limit of additive functors as additive functor
  3. limit of functors for a complete domain
