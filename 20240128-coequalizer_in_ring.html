coequalizer in Ring

Proposition

Given category Ring and rings $R,S$ and ring-homomorphism

the coequalizer exists and is given by the quotient ring

\begin{align*} S/( \varphi(r) - \psi(r)) \end{align*}

for the generated Ideal $(\varphi(r) - \psi(r) \vert r \in R)$

Proof

cofork

Note that

\begin{align*} \pi(\psi(r)) (\varphi(r) - \psi(r)) =& \pi(\psi(r) + \pi(\varphi(r)) - \pi(\psi(r)) (\varphi(r) - \psi(r)) \\ =& \pi(\varphi(r)) (\varphi(r) - \psi(r)) \end{align*}

\begin{align*} f(\varphi(r) - \psi(r)) = 0 \end{align*}

Thus by Kern eines Ringhomomorphismus als Ideal it follows, that

\begin{align*} (\varphi(r) - \psi(r)) \subseteq \mathrm{ker}(f) \end{align*}

and by fundamental theorem of ring homomorphisms, there exists a unique morphism
