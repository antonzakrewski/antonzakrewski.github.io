cofree category

Definition / Proposition

The cofree category functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{CoFreeCat}: \mathrm{Set} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat} \\ X \mapsto& \mathrm{Cof}(X) \\ (f: X \rightarrow Y) \mapsto& \mathrm{Cof}(f): \mathrm{Cof}(X) \rightarrow \mathrm{Cof}(Y) \end{align*}

where $X$ is regarded as category with $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cof}(X)}(x,y) = \{*\}$ and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cof}(f): \mathrm{Cof}(X) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cof}(Y) \\ x \mapsto& f(x) \\ (x \rightarrow y) \mapsto (f(x) \rightarrow f(y)) \end{align*}

Proof

note that a functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cof}(X)$ is uniquely determined by the mapping of the objects:

  • uniqueness follows from $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cof}(X)}(x,y) = \{*\}$
  • existence follows as each diagram commutes
