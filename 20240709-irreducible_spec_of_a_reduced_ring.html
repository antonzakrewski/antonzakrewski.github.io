irreducible Spec of a reduced ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be a reduced ring.

TFAE:

  1. $R$ is an Integral domain
  2. $\mathrm{Spec}(R)$ is an irreducible topological space

Proof

note that by the bijection between irreducible components and minimal prime ideals the following are equivalent:

  1. $\mathrm{Spec}(R)$ is irreducible.
  2. there exists a unique minimal prime ideal

Then by the nilradical as intersection of minimal prime ideals this is equivalent to

\begin{align*} \mathcal{N}_A \end{align*}

being a prime ideal

As $\mathcal{N}_A = (0)$ ($R$ is reduced) we conclude by zero ideal as prime ideal and integral domain that $R$ is an integral domain
