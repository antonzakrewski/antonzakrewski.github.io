equivalence between quasicategories and pointwise equivalence

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: X \rightarrow Y$ be a functor between quasicategories.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is an natural equivalence between infinity functors
  2. $h \circ \mathcal{F}: X \rightarrow hY$ is essentially surjective and for each $x_1,x_2 \in X$ the induced map between the quasi hom
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{X}(x_1,x_2) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{Y}(\mathcal{F}(x_1), \mathcal{F}(x_2)) \end{align*}

is a simplicial homotopy equivalence of Kan complexes

Proof
