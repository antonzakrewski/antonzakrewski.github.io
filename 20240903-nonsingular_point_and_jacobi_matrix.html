nonsingular point and jacobi matrix

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be an affine variety. Let $P \in X$ be a point.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathrm{dim}(X) = m - \mathrm{Rank}(J(P))$
  2. $P$ is a nonsingular point
