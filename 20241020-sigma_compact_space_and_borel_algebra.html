sigma compact space and borel algebra

Proposition

Let $X$ be a sigma compact hausdorff topological space Then the borel-sigma algebra is given by

\begin{align*} \sigma( \{\mathrm{compact subsets}\}) \end{align*}

Proof

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be the closed subsets of $X$

Then by closedness of compact sets in Hausdorff spaces we have

\begin{align*} \{\mathrm{compact subsets}\} \subseteq \mathcal{A} \end{align*}

hence $\sigma(\mathrm{compact}) \subseteq \sigma(\mathcal{A})$

Let $A_n \subseteq X$ be compact with $\bigcup A_n = X$.

Then each closed subset is given by

\begin{align*} A =& A \cap X \\ =& A \cap (\bigcup A_n) \\ =& \bigcup (A \cap A_n) \end{align*}

Then each $A \cap A_n$ is a closed subspace of a compact space, hence itself compact (cf. closed subset of a compact space as compact set). Thus $A \cap A_n \in \{\mathrm{compact subsets}\}$ and $A$ is therefore a countable union of sets in $\{\mathrm{compact subset}\}$ resp.

\begin{align*} A \in \sigma(\mathrm{compact}) \end{align*}
