hopf invariant of composition of attaching map and comultiplication

Proposition

Let

be the composition of the attaching map of $S^{2n} \times S^{2n}$ and the comultiplicatino of the sphere.

Then its hopf invariant is given by $\pm 2$

Proof

consider the pushout pasting

which gives a diagram of cofiber sequences

and hence via the long exact sequence of singular cohomology

Now take a generator $x$ of $\tilde{H}^{2n}(H)$, then it gets mapped (under the inverses of the isomorphisms) to $x\otimes 1 + 1 \otimes x$ with $x$ as generator of $\tilde{H}^{2n}(S^{2n})$ (using kunneth). By naturality of the square we may compute

\begin{align*} ((x \otimes 1) + (1 \otimes x))^2 =& (x \otimes 1) \cdot (1 \otimes x) + (1 \otimes x) \cdot (x \otimes 1) + (x \otimes 1)^2 + (1 \otimes x)^2 \\ =& 2 \cdot (x \otimes x) + 0 \end{align*}

where $(x \otimes x)$ is a generator of $H^{4n}(S^{2n} \times S^{2n})$
