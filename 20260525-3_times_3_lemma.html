3 times 3 lemma for cofibers

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a finitely cocomplete pointed category and

be a diagram, where

  1. all three rows are cofiber sequences
  2. $A_1 \rightarrow B_1 \rightarrow C_1, A_2 \rightarrow B_2 \rightarrow C_2$ are cofiber sequences.

Then so is $A_3 \rightarrow B_3 \rightarrow C_3$.
