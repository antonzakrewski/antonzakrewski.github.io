singular chain complexes for pushout of cells quasi isomorphic

Proposition

Let

be a pushout in category Top

Then

\begin{align*} C_{\mathrm{sing}}(X,A) \cong \bigoplus_{i \in I} \mathrm{C}_*^{\mathrm{sing}}(S^n, *) \end{align*}

are quasi-isomorphic

Proof

since $\iota: A \rightarrow X$ is a cofibration, it follows, that

\begin{align*} C(i) \cong X/A \cong \bigvee_{i \in I} S^n \end{align*}

up to Homotopy equivalence (cf. map from the mapping cone of a cofibration to the collapse as homotopy equivalence)

Thus by relative singular chains of a mapping cylinder and mapping cone quasi isomorphic we get a quasi-isomorphism

\begin{align*} C_{*}^{\mathrm{sing}}(C(\iota), *) \cong C_{*}^{\mathrm{sing}}(\mathrm{Cyl}(\iota), A) \end{align*}

Furthermore, by mapping cylinder of a subspace inclusion homotopy equivalent and relative singular homology as homotopy invariant functor, this gives the quasi-isomorphism

\begin{align*} C_{*}^{\mathrm{sing}}(\mathrm{Cyl}(\iota),A) \cong C_{*}^{\mathrm{sing}}(X,A) \end{align*}

Thus showing the quasi isomorphism

\begin{align*} C_{*}^{\mathrm{sing}}(X,A) \cong C_*^{\mathrm{sing}}(\bigvee_{i \in I} S^n) \end{align*}
