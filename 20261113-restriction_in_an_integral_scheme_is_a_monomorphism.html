Proposition

Let $X$ be an integral scheme with function field $K(X)$. Then for open subschemes $O_1 \subseteq O_2 \subseteq X$, there exist monomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O}(O_2) \rightarrow \mathcal{O}(O_1) \rightarrow K(X) \end{align*}

Proof

it suffices to show that the composite is a monomorphism let $s,t \in \mathcal{O}(O_2)$ be two functions such that $s - t$ agree in $\mathcal{K}(X) = \mathcal{O}_{X,\eta}(X)$. Then $V(s - t)$ is a closed subscheme containing the generic point, hence $V(s - t) = O_2$ or equivalently $s = t$.
