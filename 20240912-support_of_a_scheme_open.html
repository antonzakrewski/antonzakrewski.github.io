support of a scheme open

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{O}_X, O_i)$ be a scheme, $U \subseteq X$ an open. Then the support of a scheme $U_f$ is an open subset of $U$

Proof

it suffices to show that $U \setminus U_{f}$ is open: by closed and open cover it remains to show that $U_f \cap O_i \subseteq O_i$ is closed, i.e. we may consider only the case of $U_f \subseteq \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ an affine scheme

Then for a point $\mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ it follows that

\begin{align*} f_{\mathfrak{p}} = 0 \end{align*}
