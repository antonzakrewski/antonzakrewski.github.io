power of ideals as subset

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq R$ an ideal. Then for $n \in \mathbb{N}$

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a}^n \subseteq \mathfrak{a} \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of product of ideals as subset of the intersection and proof by induction

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a}^2 =& \mathfrak{a} \cdot \mathfrak{a} \\ \subseteq& \mathfrak{a} \cap \mathfrak{a}' \\ \mathfrak{a}^{n+1} =& \mathfrak{a}^n \cdot \mathfrak{a} \\ \subseteq& \mathfrak{a}^n \cap \mathfrak{a} \end{align*}
