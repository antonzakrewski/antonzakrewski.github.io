homotopical left quillen adjoint preserves cofibrant replacement

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}_{\mathcal{C}}, \mathrm{Cof}_{\mathcal{C}}, \mathrm{Fib}_{\mathcal{C}}), (\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{W}_{\mathcal{D}}, \mathrm{Cof}_{\mathcal{D}}, \mathrm{Fib}_{\mathcal{D}})$ be model categories. Suppose $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is a homotopical left quillen adjoint

Then a cofibrant replacement

\begin{align*} \tilde{X} \rightarrow X \end{align*}

gets mapped to a fibrant replacement

\begin{align*} \mathcal{R}(\tilde{X}) \rightarrow \mathcal{R}(X) \end{align*}

Proof

dual to homotopical right quillen adjoint preserves fibrant replacement
