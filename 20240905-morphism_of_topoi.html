morphism of topoi

Definition

Let $\mathrm{Sh}(\mathcal{C}), \mathrm{Sh}(\mathcal{D})$ be a topos. Then a morphism of topoi

\begin{align*} f: \mathrm{Sh}(\mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Sh}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is a pair of functors

\begin{align*} f_*: \mathrm{Sh}(\mathcal{D}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Sh}(\mathcal{C}) \\ f^{-1}: \mathrm{Sh}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Sh}(\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

such that

  1. $f^{-1}$ is the left adjoint to $f_*$, i.e.
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sh}(\mathcal{D})}(f^{-1}(-), -) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sh}(\mathcal{C})}(-, f_*(-)) \end{align*}
