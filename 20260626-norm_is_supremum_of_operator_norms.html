norm is supremum over linear functionals

Proposition

Let $X$ be a normed vector space and $x \in X$. Then

\begin{align*} \| x \| = \mathrm{sup}_{f \in X^* \setminus \{0\}} \frac{\vert f(x) \vert }{\| f \|} \end{align*}

Proof

observe that by construction

\begin{align*} \vert f(x) \vert \leq \| f \| \cdot \vert x \vert \end{align*}

so passing to $\mathrm{sup}$ shows $\| x \| \leq \mathrm{sup}_{f \in X^* \setminus \{0\}} \frac{\vert f(x) \vert }{\| f \|}$.

Now consider the linear functional

\begin{align*} l: \mathrm{span}(x) \rightarrow& \mathbb{K} \\ \lambda x \mapsto& \lambda \| x \| \end{align*}

which satisfies $\| l \| = 1$ and $\| x \| = \frac{\vert l(x) \vert }{\| l \|}$. and use bounded linear extension theorem
