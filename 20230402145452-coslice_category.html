coslice category

Definition

A coslice category is a slice category in the opposite category

Explicite

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $X \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$. Then the coslice category $\mathcal{C} \downarrow X$ is defined with:

  1. objects: morphisms $f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,A)$
