precomposition with a continuous functor and sheaf

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be sites and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a continuous functor. Then for a sheaf $\mathcal{X} \in \mathrm{Sh}_{\mathcal{E}}(\mathcal{D}) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E})$ the precomposition

\begin{align*} \mathcal{X} \circ \mathcal{F} \in \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{E}) \end{align*}

is also a sheaf

Proof
