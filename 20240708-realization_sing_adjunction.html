realization / sing adjunction

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be categories, where $\mathcal{A}$ is a small category and $\mathcal{B}$ is cocomplete. Then for a functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ the realization functor is a left adjoint to the sing functor Then the realization functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \vert \cdot \vert_{\mathcal{F}}: \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow \mathcal{B} X \mapsto& \mathrm{colim}_{a \in A/X} \mathcal{F}(a) \end{align*}

Then the sing functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sing}_{\mathcal{F}}(-): \mathcal{B} \rightarrow& \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{A}) \\ b \mapsto& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{B}}(\mathcal{F}(-), b) \end{align*}

Proof

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{B}}(\vert x \vert_{\mathcal{F}}, y) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{A})}(x, \mathrm{Sing}_{\mathcal{F}}(y)) \\ \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{B}}(\mathrm{colim}_{a \in A/x} \mathcal{F}(a), y) \\ \cong& \mathrm{lim}_{a \in A/x} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{B}}(\mathcal{F}(a), y) \\ =& \mathrm{lim}_{a \in A/x} \mathrm{Sing}_{\mathcal{F}}(y)(a) \end{align*}

where the first isomorphism is given by the contravariant co-Yoneda Lemma and the second isomorphism is given by contravariant hom-functor dualizes small colimits to limits

The second part

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{A})}(x,\mathrm{Sing}_{\mathcal{F}}(y)) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{A})}(\mathrm{colim}_{a \in A/x} a, \mathrm{Sing}_{\mathcal{F}}) \\ \cong& \mathrm{lim}_{a \in A/x} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(a, \mathrm{Sing}_{\mathcal{F}}(y)) \\ \cong& \mathrm{lim}_{a \in A/x} \mathrm{Sing}_{\mathcal{F}}(y)(a) \end{align*}

where the first isomorphism is given by the contravariant yoneda lemma, the second isomorphism by the contravariant yoneda lemma
