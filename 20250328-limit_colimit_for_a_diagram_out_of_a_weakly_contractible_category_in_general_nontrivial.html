limit / colimit for a diagram out of a weakly contractible category in general nontrivial

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category and

\begin{align*} K \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

be a diagram where $K$ is weakly contractible

Then the colimit / pushout is in general nontrivial

Proof

take a nontrivial pushout / pullback, in particular $\mathcal{C}$ as \(1\)-category.

But

is weakly contractible, e.g. after taking the geometric realization.
