Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $I$ a collection of small indexing categories. Suppose $\mathcal{D}$ admits all colimits of shapes from $I$ and denote $\mathcal{C}^{I}$ the full sub infinity category of $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ which

  1. contains the image of the infinity yoneda embedding
  2. is closed under colimits of shapes from $I$

Then the induced map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{I}(\mathcal{C}^I, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}
