open subscheme of noetherian scheme is noetherian

Proposition

Let $U \subseteq X$ be an open subscheme of a noetherian scheme. Then it is noetherian.

Proof

affine case

Suppose we have an open subscheme $D(F)$ of $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ for $F \subseteq A$. Then $F = (f_1,...,f_n)$ is finitely generated as ideal (so in particular also finitely generated as radical ideal). Hence there exists a Zariski-local epimorphism $\cpord_{i=1}^n \mathrm{Spec}(A_{f_i}) \rightarrow D(F)$
