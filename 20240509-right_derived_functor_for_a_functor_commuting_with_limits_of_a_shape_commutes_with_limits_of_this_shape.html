right derived functor for a functor commuting with limits of a shape commutes with limits of this shape

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ a left exact functor preserving limits of a shape $I$. Then there exists isomorphism of delta functors

\begin{align*} \mathcal{RF}(\mathrm{lim}_i(-)) \cong \mathrm{lim}_i(\mathcal{RF}(-)) \end{align*}

Proof
