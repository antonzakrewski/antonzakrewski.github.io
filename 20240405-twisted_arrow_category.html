twisted arrow category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. Then the twisted arrow category is defined as category containing

  1. morphisms $\alpha: X \rightarrow X'$ as objects
  2. a morphism $\beta \rightarrow \alpha$ is given by a pair of morphisms $(f,f')$ such that

or that

\begin{align*} \alpha = f' \circ \beta \circ f \end{align*}

Here composition is defined by glueing

where

\begin{align*} \alpha =& f' \circ \beta \circ f \\ =& f' \circ g' \circ \beta \circ g \circ f \\ \end{align*}
