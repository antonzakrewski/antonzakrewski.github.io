infinite complex projective space

Definition

Let $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n$ be the complex projective space Then $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}$ is defined as filtered colimit

\begin{align*} \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty} \coloneqq \mathrm{colim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n \end{align*}
