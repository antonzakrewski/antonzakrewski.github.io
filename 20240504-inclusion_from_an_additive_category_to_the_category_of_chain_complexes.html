inclusion from an additive category to the category of chain complexes

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes. Then for $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ the inclusion functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \\ A \mapsto& (... \rightarrow 0 \rightarrow A \rightarrow 0 \rightarrow ... ) \\ (f: A \rightarrow B) \mapsto& f \end{align*}

where $A$ is the \(n\)-th component of $(... \rightarrow 0 \rightarrow A \rightarrow 0 \rightarrow ... )$
