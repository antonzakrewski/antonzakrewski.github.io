right exact functor determined by projective objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, where $\mathcal{A}$ admits enough projectives. Then an right exact functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ is uniquely determined up to natural isomorphism by the full subcategory of projective objects
