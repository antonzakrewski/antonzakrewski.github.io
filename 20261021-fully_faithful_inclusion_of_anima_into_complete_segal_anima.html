fully faithful inclusion of anima into complete segal anima

Proposition

The inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat} \simeq \mathrm{csAn} \end{align*}

is fully faithful.

Proof

a)

Observe that the inclusion $\mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathcal{P}(\Delta^{\mathrm{op}})$ factors through the full subcategory of complete segal anima. Hence it suffices to show that $\mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathcal{P}(\Delta^{\mathrm{op}})$ is fully faithful. But this functor admits a right adjoint, namely taking limits so it suffices to show that the unit $\mathrm{id} \Rightarrow \lim \mathrm{const}$ is an equivalence. But $\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}$ admits an initial object, so the inclusion of the initial object is an initial functor. Hence the unit is an equivalence (since evaluation at the initial object together with the projections to the diagram is a limit diagram).
