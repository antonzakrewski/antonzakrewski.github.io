base change of open coverings of algebraic functors

Proposition

Let $(U_i \subseteq X)$ be an open covering and $f: Y \rightarrow X$ a map of algebraic functors Then $f^{-1}[U_i] \subseteq Y)$ is an open covering.

Proof

observe that $f^{-1}[U_i] \hookrightarrow Y$ is an open immersion (cf. open immersion closed under pullbacks). Moreover, underlying space of pullbacks shows that $\vert Y \times_{X} Z \vert \twoheadrightarrow \vert Y \vert \times_{\vert X \vert} \vert Z \vert$ is surjective and hence a cover (cf. pullback of an open cover of topological spaces).
