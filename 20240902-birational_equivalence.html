birational equivalence

Definition

Let $X,Y$ be affine varieties. Then a birational equivalence is given by dominant rational maps $f: X \rightarrow Y, g: Y \rightarrow X$ such that

\begin{align*} f \circ g =& \mathrm{id}_{\mathrm{def}(f \circ g)} \\ g \circ f =& \mathrm{id}_{\mathrm{def}(g \circ f)} \end{align*}
