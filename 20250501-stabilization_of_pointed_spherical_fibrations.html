stabilization of pointed spherical fibrations

Definition

The stabilization of pointed spherical fibrations is via postcomposition with

\begin{align*} \sigma^i: BG(d) \simeq BG(d) \times \Delta^0 \rightarrow BG(d) \times BG(i) \rightarrow BG(d + i) \end{align*}

where $\Delta^0 \rightarrow BG(i)$ is the terminal spherical fibration $S^{i} \rightarrow *$ and smash homomorphism of G(d)
