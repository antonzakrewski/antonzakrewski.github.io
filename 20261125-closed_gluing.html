closed gluing

Definition

A presheaf $\mathcal{F} \in \mathcal{P}(\mathrm{Sch}_{/S})$ satisfies closed glueing, if for every span of closed immersion

The map

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}( X \coprod_{Z} Y ) \xrightarrow{\simeq} \mathcal{F}(X) \times_{\mathcal{F}(Z)} \mathcal{F}(Y) \end{align*}

is an equivalence and $\mathcal{F}(\emptyset) = *$.
