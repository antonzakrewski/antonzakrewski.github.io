Proposition

Suppose that $\mathrm{Fun}([n], \mathcal{C})^{\simeq} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}([n], \mathcal{D})^{\simeq}$ is an epimorphism of anima for all $n \geq 1$, then $\mathcal{F}$ is an epimorphism of categories.
