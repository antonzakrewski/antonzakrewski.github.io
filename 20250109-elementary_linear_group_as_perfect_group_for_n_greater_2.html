elementary linear group as perfect group for n greater 2

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $n \geq 3$. Then the elementary linear group

\begin{align*} E_n(R) \end{align*}

is a perfect group

Proof

Let $e_{i,l}(r) \in E_n(R)$. We want to show that $e_{i,l} \in [E_n(R), E_n(R)]$.

Here we may choose $j \neq i,l$ and get by commutator of elementary matrices

\begin{align*} e_{i,l}(r) = [e_{i,j}(r), e_{j,l}(1)] \end{align*}

hence $e_{i,l}(r) \in [E_n(R), E_n(R)]
