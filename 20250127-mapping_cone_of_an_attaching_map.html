mapping cone of an attaching map

Proposition

Let

\begin{align*} f: S^n \rightarrow \mathrm{Sk}_n(Y) \end{align*}

be a continuous map

Then the mapping cone

\begin{align*} C(f) \end{align*}

and the pushout

coincide

Proof

  • map out of a cw complex and nullhomotopy
  • universal property of the mapping cone
