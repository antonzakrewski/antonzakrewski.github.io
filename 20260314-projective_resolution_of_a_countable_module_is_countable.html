projective resolution of a kappa small module is kappa small

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal and $M$ a $\kappa$ small \(R\)-modules for a $\kappa$ small ring $R$. Then there exists a projective resolution

\begin{align*} ... P_n \rightarrow P_{n-1} \rightarrow ... \rightarrow M \end{align*}

where each $P_i$ is also \(\kappa\)-small

Proof

choose generators, namely a $\kappa$ small amount. Then a projection $R^{\kappa} \rightarrow M$ suffices, i.e. it suffices to show that $R^{\kappa}$ is \(\kappa\)-small. Here $R^{\kappa}$ is given by

\begin{align*} \coprod_{i < \kappa} R^i \end{align*}

where $\vert R^i \vert < \kappa$ (cf. finite product of kappa small sets).
