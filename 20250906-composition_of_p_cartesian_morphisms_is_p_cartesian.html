composition of p-cartesian morphisms is p-cartesian

Proposition

Let $p: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor and $f: x \rightarrow y, g: y \rightarrow z \in \mathcal{C}$ be p-cartesian morphisms. Then the composition $g \circ f$ is also p-cartesian

Proof

follows from glueing lemma for pullbacks where we glue
