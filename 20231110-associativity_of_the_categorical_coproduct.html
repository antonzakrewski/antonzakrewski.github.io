associativity of the categorical coproduct

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $A_i, A_j \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ be objects for disjoint $I,J$ (diagrams :( ) and $\coprod$ the categorical coproduct Then

\begin{align*} \coprod_{i \in I} A_i \sqcup \coprod_{j \in J} A_j \cong \coprod_{a \in I \sqcup J} A_a \end{align*}

are canonically isomorphic
