filtered colimit of zero morphisms is the zero object in spectra

Proposition

Let $I: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}$ be a filtered diagram where each morphism is the zero morphism Then the colimit is the zero object

Proof

follows as homotopy group functor of spectra preserves filtered colimits and the filtered colimit of zero morphism in Category Ab is the zero group hence

\begin{align*} \pi_*(\mathrm{colim} X_i) \simeq 0 \end{align*}

which shows that $\mathrm{colim} X_i$ is the zero object.
