tree minus edge as forest with two components

Proposition

Let $T = (V,E)$ be a tree and $e \in E$ an edge. Then $T \setminus \{e\}$ is a forest with precisecy 2 connected components

Proof

note that $T$ is by definition acyclic, therefore in particular $T \setminus \{e\}$ is also acyclic. It remains to show that $T$ contains precisely two connected components (via contraposition)

$\leq 2$

Assume that $T \setminus \{e\}$ contains $3$ connected components. Then we may add $e$ back and connect two connected components and hence $T$ contains $2$ connected components. Contradiction to the assumption, that $T$ is connected (since it is a tree)

$\geq 2$

Assume that $T \setminus \{e\}$ is connected. Then in particular $T$ is not minimally connected, contradiction to the assumption that $T$ is a tree
