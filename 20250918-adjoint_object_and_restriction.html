adjoint object and restriction

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor. Suppose $\mathcal{C}_0 \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ is a full subcategory and $\mathcal{G}(d) \in \mathcal{C}_0 \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ be a left adjoint object resp. right adjoint object under $\mathcal{F}$.

Then $G(d)$ is also a left adjoint object under $\mathcal{F}_{\vert \mathcal{C}_0}$.

Proof

follows as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{G}(d),-) \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(d,\mathcal{F}(-)) \end{align*}

restricts to an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}_0}(\mathcal{G}(d),-) \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(d,\mathcal{F}_{\vert \mathcal{C}_0}(-)) \end{align*}
