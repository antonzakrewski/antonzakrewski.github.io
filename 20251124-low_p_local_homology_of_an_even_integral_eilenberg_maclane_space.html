low p-local homology of an even integral eilenberg-maclane space

Proposition

Let $2n \geq 3$ and $p \in \mathbb{P}$. Then the canonical map

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Z}_{(p)}[\iota] \rightarrow H^*(K(\mathbb{Z},n); \mathbb{Z}_{(p)}) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism in degrees $* < 2p-1 + n$ and

\begin{align*} H^{2p-1+n}(K(\mathbb{Z},n), \mathbb{Z}_{(p)}) \cong \mathbb{F}_p \end{align*}

and $2p-1+n$ is the lowest degree in which non-trivial p-torsion appears.

Proof
