restricted infinity yoneda embedding of an accessible category

Definition / Proposition

The $\mathcal{C}$ be a \(kappa\)-accessible infinity category for a regular cardinal $\kappa$ Then the restricted yoneda embedding is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}^{\kappa}) \end{align*}

given by 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{C} \rar{\simeq} & \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\kappa}) \rar{\iota} & \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})
\end{tikzcd*}
\end{displaymath}

Proof
