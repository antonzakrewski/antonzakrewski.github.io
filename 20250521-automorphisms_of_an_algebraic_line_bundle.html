automorphisms of an algebraic line bundle

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a commutative ring and $L$ be a algebraic line bundle , then the automorphisms

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Aut}_{R}(L) \cong R^{\times} \end{align*}

Proof

a) $L = R$

then this follows from

\begin{align*} \mathrm{End}_{R}(R) \cong& C(R) \\ \cong& R \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Aut}(R) = R^{\times} \end{align*}

see also: center of a module category as center of the ring

general case

follows as

\begin{align*} - \otimes_{R} L \end{align*}

is an equivalence of categories and thus induces

\begin{align*} \mathrm{End}(R) \cong& \mathrm{End}(L) \\ \mathrm{Aut}(R) \cong& \mathrm{Aut}(L) \\ \end{align*}
