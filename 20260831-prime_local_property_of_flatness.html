prime local property of flatness

Proposition

Let $\varphi: R \rightarrow S$ be a ring-homomorphism TFAE:

  1. $\varphi$ is a flat ring morphism
  2. $\varphi: R_{\varphi^{-1}[\mathfrak{q}] \rightarrow S_{\mathfrak{q}}$ is flat for every prime ideal $\mathfrak{q} \subseteq S$

Proof

follows as the functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mod}_{R} \rightarrow \prod_{\mathfrak{p}} \mathrm{Mod}_{R_{\mathfrak{p}}} \end{align*}

is conservative and exact.
