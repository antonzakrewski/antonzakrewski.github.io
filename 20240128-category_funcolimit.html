Category FunColimit

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, then the category $\mathrm{FunColimit}$ is the full subcategory of the functor category $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ containing diagrams $\mathcal{J}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$, which admit a colimit

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}(\mathcal{J}) \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
