classification of cw complexes with four cells and one in dimension 1 and stability assumption

Proposition

Let $X$ be a CW Complex with cells in dimension $0,1,n,m$ with $n \leq m$. Then

  1. if $n = 1$, this implies $X \simeq S^1 \vee S^1 \vee S^m$
  2. if $n > 1$ and $n < m \leq 2n$ (todo: find right index for stable range), then CW complexes are classified finite tuples $(g_1,...,g_n)$ for $n \in \mathbb{N}_0$, $g_i \in \pi_{m-1}(S^n)$ modulo the actions given by a) flipping the order, i.e. $(g_1,...,g_n) \sim (g_n,...,g_1)$ b) acting by the sign on $\pi_{m-1}(S^n)$, i.e. $(g_1,...,g_n) \sim (-g_1,...,-g_n)$
