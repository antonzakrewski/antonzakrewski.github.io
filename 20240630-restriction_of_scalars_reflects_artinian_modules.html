restriction of scalars reflects artinian modules

Proposition

Let \(A,B\) be rings, $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ a ring-homomorphism and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Res}_{\varphi}: \mathrm{B-Mod} \rightarrow \mathrm{A-Mod} \end{align*}

the restriction of scalars functor

Suppose $M$ is a \(B\)-module such that $M$ as \(A\)-module is Artinian. Then $M$ as a \(B\)-module is also artinian.

Proof

follows from functorality, as a descending chain of submodules

is an descending chain of submodules of $M$ as \(B\)-module. Hence it stabilizes and as restriction of scalars does not the underlying set, this chain also stabilizes
