1-sifted colimit preserving functor induces functor on animation

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be animatable categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor which preserves 1-sifted colimits Then $\mathcal{F}$ induces a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An}(\mathcal{F}): \mathrm{An}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{An}(\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

Proof
