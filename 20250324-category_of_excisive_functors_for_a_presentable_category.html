category of excisive functors for a presentable category_

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a presentable infinity category and $\mathcal{C}$ a small pointed category admitting finite infinity colimits. Then the category of excisive functors

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Exc}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

is also presentable

Proof
