stabilization of a quasi-abelian category and the right abelian envelope

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an quasi-abelian category. Then the canonical morphism

\begin{align*} \mathcal{A} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{LH}(\mathcal{A}) \end{align*}

induces an equivalence on stabilization

\begin{align*} \mathcal{D}^b(\mathcal{A}) = \mathcal{D}^b(\mathrm{LH}(\mathcal{A})) \end{align*}

Proof
