Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum and $n \geq 0$. Then

\begin{align*} \pi_n(\Omega^{\infty} X) \cong \pi_n(X) \end{align*}

are canonically equivalent.

Proof

Recall that $\Omega^{\infty}: \mathrm{Sp} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}_*$ is the adjoint to the reduced suspension spectrum functor $\tilde{\mathbb{S}}$. Moreover, $\tilde{\mathbb{S}}[S^n] \simeq \Sigma^n \mathbb{S}$ for $n \geq 0$.. Therefore, we get an equivalence of anima

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}^n, X) \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(S^n, \Omega^{\infty} X) \end{align*}

and passing to $\pi_0$ shows

\begin{align*} \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}^n, X)) \simeq \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(S^n, \Omega^{\infty} X)) \end{align*}
