boardmans criterion for an isomorphism of strictly filtered groups

Proposition

Let $X,Y: \mathbb{Z}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab}$ be strictly filtered abelian groups and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a map of filtered objects. Suppose that

  1. $\mathrm{colim}_{\mathbb{Z}^{\mathrm{op}}} f$ is an isomorphism
  2. the lim-lim1 of $X$ vanishes
  3. $f$ induces an isomorphism on graded objects
