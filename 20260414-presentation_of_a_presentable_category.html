presentation of a presentable category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a $\kappa$-presentable category.

Then a $\kappa$ presentation is given by

  1. a small category $S$
  2. a set of morphisms $R$ in $S$

such that $\overline{R}$ - the generated collection of morphism by $\kappa$ small colimits - witnesses

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \simeq \mathrm{Fun}(S^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An})[\overline{R}^{-1}] \end{align*}
