principal g-bundle

Definition

Let $B,E$ be topological spaces, $p: E \rightarrow B$ a continuous map and $G$ a topological group. Then $p$ is said to be a principal G-Bundle, if it is equipped with a principal G-action on $E$ such that

  1. $p: E \rightarrow B$ is \(G\)-equivariant with respect to the trivial group action on $B$
  2. $p$ induces a homeomorphism $E/G \rightarrow B$
