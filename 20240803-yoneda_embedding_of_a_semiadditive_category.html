yoneda embedding of a semiadditive category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a small additive category Then there exists a fully faithful functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{A} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{CMon}) \\ A \mapsto& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(-,A) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from Yoneda Lemma by yoneda embedding where each functor restricts naturally to $\mathrm{CMon}$
