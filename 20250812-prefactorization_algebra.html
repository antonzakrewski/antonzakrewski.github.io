prefactorization algebra

Definition

Let $X$ be a (Hausdorff) topological space and $\mathcal{U} \subseteq \mathrm{Op}(X)$ a partially ordered subset. Let $\mathcal{U}^{\otimes}$ be the operad of a subset of subsets

A prefactorization algebra on $\mathcal{U}$ is an o-algebra over $\mathcal{U}^{\otimes}$ in $\mathcal{C}, \otimes$

\begin{align*} \mathcal{A}: \mathcal{U}^{\otimes} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\otimes} \end{align*}
