based compact open functor as right adjoint for a locally compact space

Proposition

Let $(X,x)$ be a based space, then the covariant based compact open functor for category Top* is a right adjoint

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_*((X,x),-): \mathrm{Top}_* \rightarrow \mathrm{Top}_* \end{align*}
