projection to the homotopy category preserves products

Proposition

The infinity homotopy category functor preserves product

Proof

general infinity category $\mathcal{C}$

Note that

\begin{align*} \prod X_i \end{align*}

is characterized via

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,\prod X_i) \simeq \prod \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,X_i) \end{align*}

Furthermore the hom of the homotopy category as path component of the mapping space commutes with the homotopy categor, i.e

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{h \mathcal{C}}(-,-) \cong h \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,-) \end{align*}

Thus it suffices to show that

\begin{align*} h(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,\prod X_i)) \simeq \prod h(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,X_i)) \end{align*}

which can be checked pointwise (cf. natural equivalence between infinity functors and objectwise equivalence)
