localization of a stable category for morphisms closed under suspension and loop

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category and $S$ a strongly saturated collection of morphisms. Let $\mathcal{C}' \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ be the left bousfield localization for $W$.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}'$ is a stable subcategory
  2. $S$ is closed under suspensions and loops

Remark:

  • closure under only suspension or only loops does not suffice

Proof

see:

  • S-local object and localization
  • stable subcategory and fiber/suspension or cofiber/loop criterion

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $f \in S$, then we need to show that $\Sigma f, \Omega(f) \in \mathcal{W}$ Note that the localization functor $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_0$ is a left adjoint (or right adjoint) so in particular exact functor (cf. left / right and exact functor between stable infinity categories) First note that $f \in S$ if and only if $\mathcal{L}(f)$ is an equivalence. But $\mathcal{L}(\Sigma f) \simeq \Sigma \mathcal{L}(f)$ so it follows from equivalences under functors that $\Sigma (f) \in S$.

similarly for $\Omega f$

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $X$ be an S-local object, then we need to show that $\Sigma X$ is also an S-local object (cf. stable subcategory and fiber/suspension or cofiber/loop criterion, limits in a bousfield localization). So let $f \in S$, then we may identify $\mathrm{map}(f^*,\Sigma X)$ with $\Sigma(\mathrm{map}(f,X))$ which is an equivalence, so $\Sigma X$ is S-local. Analogously for $\Omega f$
