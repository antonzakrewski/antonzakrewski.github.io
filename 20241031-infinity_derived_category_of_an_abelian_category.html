infinity dash-derived category of an abelian category with enough projectives

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category enough projectives. Then the infinity category $D^{-}(\mathcal{A})$ is defined as dg nerve

\begin{align*} N_{\mathrm{dg}}(\mathrm{Ch_*}_{\geq 0}}(\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{proj}}) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Ch_*}_{\geq 0}(\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{proj}})$ is the category containing

  1. nonnegatively graded chain complex

spanned by

  1. degreewise projective chain complexes
