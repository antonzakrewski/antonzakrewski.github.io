characterization for sheaves for a collection of sieves closed under pullbacks

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $\sigma$ a collection of sieves on $\mathcal{C}$ which are closed under pullbacks Let $\overline{\sigma}$ be the grothendieck topology on $\mathcal{C}$ generated by $\sigma$.

Then a presheaf is a $\overline{\sigma}$ sheaf if and only if it satisfies descent along all sieves in $\sigma$.

Proof

Let $\mathcal{E} = \{\mathcal{F} \in \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) \vert \textrm{ satisfies descent}\}$. Then there exists a finest topology $\tau$ on $\mathcal{E}$ such that $\mathcal{E} \subseteq \mathrm{Sh}_{\tau}(\mathcal{C})$.

Since $\sigma$ is stable under pullbacks, we get $\sigma \subseteq \tau$ by the explicit description of $\tau$ given in functor from grothendieck topologies to subcategories admits a left adjoint. hence $\overline{\sigma} \leq \tau$ as $\overline{\sigma}$ is the generated topolog. This implies

\begin{align*} \mathcal{E} \subseteq \mathrm{Sh}_{\tau}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathrm{Sh}_{\overline{\sigma}}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathcal{E} \end{align*}
