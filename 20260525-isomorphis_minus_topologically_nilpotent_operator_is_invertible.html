isomorphism minus sufficiently bounded operator is invertible

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be a normed vector spaces where $Y$ is a banach space. Let $T, \varphi: X \rightarrow Y$ be bounded linear operators where $\varphi$ is an isomorphism (in particular $\varphi^{-1}$ is also bounded) $\| T \| < \| \varphi \|$. Then $\varphi \pm T$ is also invertible.

Proof

w.l.o.g. we consider the case $\varphi - T$, then it suffices to prove the case $1 - \varphi^{-1} T$ since $\varphi \circ 1 - \varphi^{-1} T = \varphi - T$.

We define $\sum_{n=0}^\infty T^n$. Then $(1 - T) \cdot \sum_{n=0}^\infty T^n = \mathrm{lim}_{n \rightarrow \infty} 1 + T^n$ where $\mathrm{lim}_{n \rightarrow \infty} T^n = 0$. This shows that $(1 - T)$ is an isomorphism.
