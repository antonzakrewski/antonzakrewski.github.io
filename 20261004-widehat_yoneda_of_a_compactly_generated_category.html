widehat yoneda of a compactly generated category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a compactly generated category and $\kappa$ an uncountable regular cardinal, then the widehat yoneda $\widehat{よ}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\kappa})$ is identified with applying $\mathrm{Ind}$ to the inclusion $\mathcal{C}^{\omega} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\kappa}$.

Proof

Recall that $\mathrm{colim}: \mathrm{INd}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is the unique filtered colimit preserving retract of the inclusion $\mathcal{C} \subseteq \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C})$. But so is precomposition with the fully faithful functor $\mathcal{C}^{\omega} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ (cf. precomposition induces functor on Ind). Now $\iota^*$ also has a left adjoint, namely left kan extension $\iota_!: \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\omega)) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C})$, which restricts to $\mathrm{Ind}$ (cf. Ind functor).
