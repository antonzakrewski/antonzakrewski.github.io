fully faithful functor is a monomorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a fully faithful functor. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is a monomorphism

Proof

  • fully faithful essentially surjective functor is an equivalence

Observe that $\mathcal{C} \times_{\mathcal{D}} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is fully faithful (cf. fully faithful functors under pullbacks) and essentially surjective
