existence of a riemannian metric on a smooth manifold

Proposition

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold. Then $M$ admits a riemannian metric.

Proof

Choose a manifold admits open cover with partition of unity $U_i\subesteq \mathbb{R}^n$, $\varphi: U_i \rightarrow M$ and $\lambda_i(-): M \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$. Then choose a riemannian metric $g_i$ for $U_i$, e.g. the standard euclidean metric $\langle -, -\rangle: \mathbb{R}^n \times \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ and define

\begin{align*} g = \sum_{i} \lambda_i g_i \end{align*}
