property of an image of an epimorphism and quotient

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group. TFAE:

  1. for each normal subgroup $S \subseteq G$ (or ideal) the quotient group $G/S$ satisfies some property
  2. for any homomorphism $\varphi$ the image $\varphi[G]$ satisfies the property

Proof

corollary of the <Homomorphiesatz für Gruppen>
