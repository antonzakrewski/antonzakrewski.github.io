set theoretic quotient as colimit

Proposition

Let $X$ be a set and $\sim$ an equivalence relation on $X$. Then the set

\begin{align*} X/\sim \end{align*}

is a colimit for 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mbox{[x]} \arrow[]{rd}{} &  & [y] \arrow[]{ld}{} \\
& * & 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Proof
