bundle map of locally constant rank

Definition

Let $\varphi: E_1 \rightarrow E_2$ be a morphism of topological vector bundles Then $\varphi$ is said to be of locally constant rank, if

  1. $\mathrm{rank}(\mathrm{im}(\varphi)$ is locally constant

or equivalently

  1. $\mathrm{rank}(\mathrm{ker}(\varphi))$ is locally constant

see: rank theorem
