free module functor for a commutative Ring is symmetric monoidal functor

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. Then the free module functor from $\mathrm{Set}$ to $\mathrm{RMod}$ is a symmetric monoidal functor

Proof

$R = \mathbb{Z}$

  • unique extension of a closed symmetric monoidal structure on commutative groups

general case

follows from

\begin{align*} R[X_i] \otimes R[Y_i] \cong R[X_i \cup Y_i] \end{align*}
