right shear map of a bialgebra

Definition

Let $X$ be a bialgebra in $\mathrm{BiAlg}(\mathcal{C}^{\otimes})$, then the right shear map is defined as composition

\begin{align*} \textrm{rshear}: R \otimes R \xrightarrow{\delta \otimes \mathrm{id}} R \otimes R \otimes \xrightarrow{\mathrm{id} \otimes \eta} R \otimes R \end{align*}

where

  1. $\delta$ is the comultiplication,
  2. $\eta$ is the multiplication.
