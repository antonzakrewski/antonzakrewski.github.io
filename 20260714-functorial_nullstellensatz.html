functorial nullstellensatz

Proposition

Sending a subset $F \subseteq k[x_i \vert i \in I]$ to its vanishing locus $V(F) \subseteq \mathbb{A}_k^I$ induces an order-reversing bijection.

\begin{align*} V: \{\textrm{ideals in } k[x_i \vert i \in I]\} \xrightarrow{\simeq} \{\texrm{vanishing loci in} \mathbb{A}_k^I\} \end{align*}

Proof
