existence of Eilenberg-MacLane spaces via moore spaces

Proposition

Let $A$ be an abelian group, $n \geq 0$ then there exists an Eilenberg-Maclane space

Proof

$n = 0$

Take the set of $A$, equipped with the discrete topology

$n = 1$

  • presentation complex induces group isomorphism with fundamental group

$n \geq 2$

  • Moore space

use

  • hurewicz homomorphism
\begin{align*} \pi_n(M(A,n)) \rightarrow H_n(M(A,n)) \cong A \end{align*}
\begin{align*} \pi_k(M(A,n)) = 0 \end{align*}

for $k < n$

  • homotopy groups of a moore space
  • Seifert-Van Kampen Theorem
