prime ideal of a von Neumann regular ring as maximal ideal

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a commutative von Neumann regular ring and $\mathfrak{p} \subseteq R$ an ideal. Then $\mathfrak{p}$ is an maximal ideal

Proof

Since $R/\mathfrak{p}$ is an Integral domain (see: prime ideal and quotient ring as integral domain)
