explicit description of the universal property of K-theory on Cat perf

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{perf}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}$ a localizing invariant. Then there exists a natural transformation $\Sigma_{+}^{\infty}(-)^{\simeq} \rightarrow K(-)$ which induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Fun}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{perf}}, \mathrm{Sp})}(\Sigma_{+}^{\infty}(-)^{\simeq}, \mathcal{F}(-)) \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Fun}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{st}}, \mathrm{Sp})}(K(-), \mathcal{F}(-)) \end{align*}

upon precomposition.

Here there exists an equivalence $(-)^{\simeq} \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{perf}}}(\mathrm{Sp}^{\omega}, -)$ and hence an adjunction

\begin{align*} \Omega^{\infty} \mathcal{F}(\mathrm{Sp}^{\omega})) \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{perf}}, \mathrm{An})}(\mathrm{Hom}(\mathrm{Sp}^{\omega},-), \Omega^{\infty} \mathcal{F}(-)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{perf}}, \mathrm{An})}((-)^{\simeq}, \Omega^{\infty} \mathcal{F}(-)) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{perf}}, \mathrm{Sp})}(\Sigma_{+}^{\infty} (-)^{\simeq}, \mathcal{F}(-)) \end{align*}

where we use that postcomposition with adjoints as adjoint. and hence there exists a natural transformation $\Sigma_{+}^{\infty} (-)^{\simeq} \Rightarrow K(-)$ classified by $[\mathbb{S}] \in \pi_0(\Omega^{\infty} K(\mathrm{Sp}^{\omega}))) \cong K_0(\mathbb{S})$.

Proof
