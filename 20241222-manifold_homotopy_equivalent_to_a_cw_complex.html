manifold homotopy equivalent to a cw complex

Proposition

Let $M$ be a topological manifold. Then $M$ is homotopy equivalent to a CW-Complex

Proof

$\mathrm{dim}(M) \neq 4$

  • manifold admits cw structure aside from dimension 4

$\mathrm{dim}(M) = 4$

consider

\begin{align*} M \times (0,1) \end{align*}

it is a manifold of dimension $5$ (cf. finite Product of topological manifolds as topological manifold)

hence this admits a CW structure.

using product with the interval homotopy canonically equivalent provides a homotopy equivalence
