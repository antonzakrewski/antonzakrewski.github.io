presheaves as generators

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a locally small infinity category, $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ the infinity category of presheaves. Then the collection of representables form a collection of generators

Proof

follows from the contravariant infinity yoneda lemma where

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,c),\mathcal{G}(-)) \cong \mathcal{G}(c) \end{align*}

and naturality of the yoneda lemma checks the morphisms.
