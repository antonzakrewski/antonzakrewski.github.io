category of spectra is sequential colimit in PrL

Proposition

The category of spectra is equivalent to the sequential colimit

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{\mathbb{Z}} ( ... \xrightarrow{\Sigma } \mathrm{An}_* \xrightarrow{\Sigma} \mathrm{An}_* \xrightarrow{\Sigma} ...) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • colimits in PrL via PrR
  • limits in PrL are computed in Cat / limits in PrR are computed in Cat
  • category of spectra as sequential limit
