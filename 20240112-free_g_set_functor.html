free G-Set functor

Definition

Let $G$ be a group. Then the free G-Set functor is defined as functor from category set to the Category of G-Sets

\begin{align*} \mathrm{FreeG-Set}: \mathrm{Set} \rightarrow& \mathrm{G-Set} \\ X \mapsto& X \times G \\ (f: X \rightarrow Y) \mapsto& (f \times \mathrm{id}_{G}: X \times G \rightarrow Y \times G) \end{align*}
