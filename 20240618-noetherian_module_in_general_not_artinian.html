noetherian module in general not artinian

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $M$ a noetherian module over $R$ Then $M$ is in general not an Artinian module

Proof

Consider $\mathbb{Z}$ as \(\mathbb{Z}\)-module. Then $\mathbb{Z}$ is noetherian (cf. PID as noetherian ring, noetherian ring as noetherian module)

But $\mathbb{Z}$ is not a artinian, as for example

\begin{align*} (2) \subseteq (2^2) \subseteq (2^3) ... \end{align*}

is a descending chain which does not stabilize
