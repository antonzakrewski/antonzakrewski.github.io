simplified generalized presheaf construction

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $K \subseteq K'$ be collections of simplicial sets Then the simplified presheaf construction is given by the generalized presheaf construction for

\begin{align*} \mathcal{P}_K^{K'}(\mathcal{C}) \coloneqq \mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{R}}^{K'}(\mathcal{C}) \coloneqq \end{align*}

for $\mathcal{R}$ is the collection of colimit diagrams $\overline{p}: K^{\triangleleft} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ with $K \in K'$
