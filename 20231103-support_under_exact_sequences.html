support under exact sequences

Proposition

Let $A$ be a ring and $N,M,P$ be \(A\)-modules and

a short exact sequence Then for the support, it holds that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Supp}(M) = \mathrm{Supp}(N) \cup \mathrm{Supp}(P) \end{align*}

Proof

Let $\mathfrak{p} \subseteq A$ be a prime ideal. Since localization is an exact functor, $M_\mathfrak{p} = 0$ holds only if and only if $N_{\mathfrak{p}}, P_{\mathfrak{p}} = 0$. (see: exact sequence and zero object in the middle)
