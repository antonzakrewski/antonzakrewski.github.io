uniformly equicontinuous collection of maps

Definition

Let $M, N$ be metric spaces and $\mathcal{F} \subseteq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{met}}(M,N)$ be a collection of continuous maps Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be uniformly equicontinuous, if for all $\varepsilon > 0$, there exists a (global) $\delta > 0$ such that for all $m \in M$ and $f \in \mathcal{F}$: $f[B_{\delta}(m)] \subseteq B_{\varepsilon}(f(m))$
