pi_1 of a wedge for deformation retractible points

Proposition

Let $(X,x)$ and $(Y,y)$ be based spaces, $U \subseteq X, V \subseteq Y$ open subspaces with $x \in U, y \in V$, such that $U,V$ deformation retract

Then the canonical map

\begin{align*} \pi_1(X,x) * \pi(Y,y) \rightarrow \pi(X \wedge Y, [x]) \end{align*}

is a group-isomorphism

Proof

Consider the open cover 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
U \wedge V  \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & \arrow[]{d}{}U \wedge Y \\
X \wedge V \arrow[]{r}{} &  \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

by the Seifert-Van Kampen Theorem for groups we get a pushout 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
|[label={[label distance=-2mm]-45:\ulcorner}]|
\pi_1(U \wedge V,*)  \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & \arrow[]{d}{} \pi_1(U \wedge Y,*) \\
\pi_1(X \wedge V,*) \arrow[]{r}{} &  \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

by wedge product with deformation retractible space homotopy equivalent this reduces to 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
|[label={[label distance=-2mm]-45:\ulcorner}]|
\pi_1(*,*)  \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & \arrow[]{d}{} \pi_1(U,*) \\
\pi_1(X,*) \arrow[]{r}{} &  \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

where $\pi_1(*,*) = 0$ and therefore it follows,

\begin{align*} \pi_1(X \wedge Y, *) \cong \pi_1(X,*) \times \pi_1(Y,*) \end{align*}
