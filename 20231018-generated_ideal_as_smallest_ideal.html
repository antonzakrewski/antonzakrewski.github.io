generated ideal as smallest ideal

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a (commutative) ring and $S \subseteq R$. Then $(S) = R$ is the minimal ideal containing $S$

Proof

Let $I \subseteq R$ be an ideal, For $s \in (S)$, by assumption $s = \sum \alpha_i s_i \beta_i$ Furthermore, $I$ contains $s_i \in (S)$ and by multiplicative closure, we conclude that $\alpha_i \cdot s_i \cdot \beta_i \in I$. By additive closure, we conclude, that $\sum \alpha_i \cdot s_i \cdot \beta_i \in (S)$
