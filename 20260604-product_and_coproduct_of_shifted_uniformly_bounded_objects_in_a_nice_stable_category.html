product and coproduct of shifted uniformly bounded objects in a nice stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category equipped with a complete t-structure compatible with products and coproducts. Let $X_n$ be a collection of objects such that there exists an $n_{\mathrm{min}}$ and an $n_{\mathrm{max}}$ such that

  1. $\tau_{\leq n_{\mathrm{min}}}(X_n) \simeq 0$
  2. $\tau_{\geq n_{\mathrm{max}}}(X_n) \simeq 0$

Then the canonical map $\coprod_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \Sigma^n X_n \rightarrow \prod_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \Sigma^n X_n$ is an equivalence
