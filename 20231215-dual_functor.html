dualized functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a covariant (resp. contravariant) Then the dual functor is defined as contravariant (resp. covariant) functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}
