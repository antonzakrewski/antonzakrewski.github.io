projective resolution and enough projectives

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category. TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{A}$ admits enough projectives
  2. each object $A \in \mathcal{A}$ admits a projective resolution

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows by inductive definition: Chooose a projective object with an epimorphism $\pi_0: P_0 \twoheadrightarrow A$. Then consider the kernel $\mathrm{ker}(\pi_0)$. By assumption there exists a projective object $P_1$ and an epimorphism $\pi_1: P_1 \twoheadrightarrow \mathrm{ker}(\pi_0)$. Iterating this process results in a projective resolution

Using image of an epimorphism this is also exact

2) $\implies$ 1)
