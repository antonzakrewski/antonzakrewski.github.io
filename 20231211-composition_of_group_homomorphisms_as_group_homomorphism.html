composition of group homomorphisms as group homomorphism

Proposition

Let $G_1,G_2,G_3$ be groups and $\varphi_1: G_1 \rightarrow G_2$ and $\varphi_2: G_2 \rightarrow G_3$ be group-homomorphism. Then the composition

\begin{align*} \varphi_2 \circ \varphi_1: G_1 \rightarrow G_3 \end{align*}

is also a group homomorphism

Proof
