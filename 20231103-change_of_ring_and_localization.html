change of ring of an localization

Definition / Proposition

Let \(A,B\) be rings, $S \subseteq A, S' \subseteq B$ be multiplicatively closed subsets and $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ a ring-homomorphism such that

\begin{align*} \varphi[S] \subseteq \varphi[S'] \end{align*}

Then the change of ring is defined as ring homomorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi^S: S^{-1}A \rightarrow& S'^{-1}B \\ \varphi^S(\frac{a}{s}) \mapsto& \frac{\varphi(a)}{\varphi(s)} \end{align*}

Proof
