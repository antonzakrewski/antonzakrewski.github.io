slice functor from twisted arrows preserves monomorphisms

Proposition

Let $A,B,S,T$ be simplicial sets, $\iota: A \rightarrow B, g: S \rightarrow T$ be monomorphisms and

\begin{align*} \iota \widehat{\star} g: A \star T \coprod_{A \star S} B \star S \rightarrow B \star T \end{align*}

be the induced map for the slice and pushout

Then $\iota \widehat{\star}g$ is a monomorphism

Proof
