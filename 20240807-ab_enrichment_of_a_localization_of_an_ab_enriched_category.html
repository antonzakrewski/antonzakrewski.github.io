ab-enrichment of a category of fractions of an ab-enriched category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an Ab-enriched category, $S$ a left multiplicative system or right multiplicative system and $S^{-1} \mathcal{A}$ the category of fractions Then there exists a unique ab-enrichment such that the induced functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{A} \rightarrow S^{-1} \mathcal{A} \end{align*}

is additive

Proof
