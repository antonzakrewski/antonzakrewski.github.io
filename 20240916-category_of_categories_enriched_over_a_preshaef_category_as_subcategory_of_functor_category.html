category of categories enriched over a presheaf category as subcategory of functor category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category, $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ the presheaf category. Consider the cartesian monoidal structure on $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ and the category of enriched categories $\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}$.

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cat}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Cat}) \end{align*}

is a full subcategory of the functor category with full image given by functors $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}$ where

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ob} \circ \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} \end{align*}

is constant

Proof

welldefined

  • lax monoidal functor induces a 2-functor on category of of enriched functors

follows from

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ev}_c: \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{ev}_c$ preserves limits (cf. limit of functors for a complete domain)

faithful

Suppose $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ are different functors. Then

\begin{align*} \eta_{A,B, \mathcal{F}}: \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(A,B) \rightarrow& \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathcal{F}(A), \mathcal{F}(B)) \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}))\\ \eta_{A,B, \mathcal{G}}: \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(A,B) \rightarrow& \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathcal{G}(A), \mathcal{G}(B)) \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}))\\ \end{align*}

are different.

Hence they differ for some $c \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$. Thus

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(A,B)(c) = \mathrm{Hom}(A,B) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathcal{F}(A), \mathcal{F}(B))(c) = \mathrm{Hom}(\mathcal{F}(A),\mathcal{F}(B))\\ \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(A,B)(c) = \mathrm{Hom}(A,B) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathcal{G}(A), \mathcal{G}(B))(c) = \mathrm{Hom}(\mathcal{G}(A),\mathcal{G}(B)) \end{align*}

differ in $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Cat})$

image

the constant on objects part is clear from the construction Suppose

\begin{align*} f: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat} \end{align*}

is a functor with $\mathrm{Ob} \circ \mathcal{F}$ as constant presheaf.

Then define the enriched category $\mathcal{F}$ with

  1. $\mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{F}) = \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{F}(c))$ for any $c \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$
  2. $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(a,b) \coloneqq \mathrm{Hom}_{f(c)}(a,b)$
