characterization of semi-simple algebras

Proposition

Let $A$ be a finite dimension K-Algebra over an algebraically closed field $k$ TFAE:

  1. $A$ is semi-simple algebra
  2. $\mathrm{dim}(A) = \sum_{V_i} n_i^2 \mathrm{dim}(D_i)$
  3. $A \cong \bigoplus \mathrm{Mat}_{\tilde{n_i}}(\tilde{D_i})$ for skew fields $\tilde{D_i}$ and numbers $\tilde{n}_i$ (which happen to be $D_i^{\mathrm{op}}$ and the dimensions).
  4. every finite dimensional representation of $A$ is semi-simple representation
  5. the regular representation of $A$ is semi-simple

1) $\implies$ 2), 2) $\implies$ 1)

1) $\implies$ 3), 3) $\implies$ 1)

Artin-Wedderburn

3) $\implies$ 4)

4) $\implies$ 5)
