fiber of the map into brown comenetz bidual is a phantom map

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum and $X \rightarrow I_{\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}}^2X$ be the map into brown comenetz bidual. Then the fiber

\begin{align*} \mathrm{fib}(X \rightarrow I_{\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}}^2 X) \rightarrow X \end{align*}

is a phantom map.

Proof
