morphism to the p-cartesian lift

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor, $f: c_1 \rightarrow c_2$ a morphism. Let $p: c' \rightarrow c_2$ be a p-cartesian lift of $p(f)$.

Then there exists a unique morphism $c_1 \rightarrow c'$ making the diagram

commute (in a higher categorical way)

Proof

By assumption we have a pullback

so we may specify (pointwise calculation of limits)

and get a lift to $\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(c_1,c')$ which makes the the diagram commute.
