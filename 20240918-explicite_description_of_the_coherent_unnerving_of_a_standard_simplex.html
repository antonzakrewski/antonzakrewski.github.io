explicit description of the coherent unnerving of a standard simplex

Proposition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}_0$. Then the coherent unnerving of a standard simplex is given by the simplicial category containing

  1. $\{0,...,n\}$ as objects
  2. hom objects given by
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathfrak{C}[\Delta^n]}(i,j) =& \begin{cases} \emptyset & \mbox{if } i > j \\ \Delta^0 & \mbox{if } i = j \\ \Delta^0 & \mbox{if } i = j + 1 \\ \square^{i - j - 1} & \mbox{else } \\ \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

where $\square^{i - j - 1}$ is the simplicial n-cube

Proof
