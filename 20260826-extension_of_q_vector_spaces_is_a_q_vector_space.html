extension of Q-vector spaces is a Q-vector space

Proposition

Let $V_1,V_3$ be a $\mathbb{Q}$ vector spaces and

\begin{align*} 0 \rightarrow V_1 \rightarrow V_2 \rightarrow V_3 \rightarrow 0 \end{align*}

a short exact sequence Then $V_2$ is also a $\mathbb{Q}$ vector space.
