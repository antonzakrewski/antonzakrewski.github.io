Q-Vector space is uniquely divisible abelian group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a uniquely divisible abelian group.

Then $G$ admits a unique \(\mathbb{Q}\)-vectorspace structure.

Proof

Let $g \in G$ and $\frac{a}{b} \in \mathbb{Q}$. Then there exists an $g_b \in G$ such that $\sum_{i=1}^b g_b = g$. Then define

\begin{align*} \frac{a}{b} \cdot g \coloneqq \sum_{i=1}^a g_b \end{align*}

One can then show that this then is a welldefined \(\mathbb{Q}\)-vector space.

Here one would use the uniqueness to show (pseudo-)associativity, distributivity etc.
