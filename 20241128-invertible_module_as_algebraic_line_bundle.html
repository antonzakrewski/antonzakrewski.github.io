invertible module as algebraic line bundle

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $M$ a module

TFAE:

  1. $M$ is an algebraic line bundle over a commutative ring
  2. $M$ is an invertible module

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $L$ be a line bundle and consider the dual $L^* = $\mathrm{Hom}_{R}(L,R)$. Then we want to show that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ev}: L \otimes_{R} L^* \rightarrow R \end{align*}

is an isomorphism.

surjective

using surjectivity as point local property we may do this after localizing. Then localization and hom-functor localization and hom-functor commute for finitely generated Modules over a Noetherian Ring

injective

Then

2) $\implies$ 1)

Assume that $P$ is invertible, as is $P^{-1}$. Then by rank map and tensor product

\begin{align*} \mathrm{rk}(P) \cdot \mathrm{rk}(P^{-1}) = \mathrm{rk}(P \otimes_{R} P^{-1}) = \mathrm{rk}(R) = 1 \in \mathbb{N} \end{align*}

and since we map to $\mathbb{N},\cdot$ we conclude that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{rk}(P) = 1 \end{align*}
