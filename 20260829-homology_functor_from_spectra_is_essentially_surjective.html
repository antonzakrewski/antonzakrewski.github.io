homology functor from spectra is essentially surjective

Proposition

The functor

\begin{align*} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z}: \mathrm{Sp} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}(\mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

is essentially surjective

Proof

follows from:

  • object in the derived category of a hereditary ring splits
  • existence and uniqueness of a moore spectrum
