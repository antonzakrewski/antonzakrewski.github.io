n-categorical eckmann-hilton argument

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \otimes)$ be an (n,1)-category with symmetric monoidal structure, then for $m > n$

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_{m}}(\mathcal{C}) = \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{C}) \end{Align*} resp. \begin{align*} \mathrm{\mathrm{Alg}}_{\mathbb{E}_{m}}(\mathcal{C}) = \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

Proof
