zariski tangent space of an open subscheme coincides

Proposition

Let $O \subseteq X$ be an open subscheme. Then for any point $o \in O$, the zariski tangent spaces are canonically isomorphic.

Proof

affine

follows from:

  • zariski cotangent space and localization
  • passing to dual objects is a functor

reduction to affine

  • follows as the tangent space only depends on the stalk of a point
