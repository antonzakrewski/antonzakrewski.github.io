X/p has p^2 torsion homotopy groups

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum, then $\pi_k(X/p)$ is a $\mathbb{Z}/p^2 \mathbb{Z}$ module for all $k \in \mathbb{Z}$.

Proof

follows from the long exact sequence of homotopy groups of spectra for the sequence

\begin{align*} X \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X \rightarrow X/p \end{align*}

which shows that $\pi_*(X/p)$ is an extension of $\pi_{*}(X)/p$ and the \(p\)-torsion subgroup of $\pi_{*-1}(X)$. In particular, this shows that any element of $\pi_*(X)$ is $p^2$-torsion (cf. todo)
