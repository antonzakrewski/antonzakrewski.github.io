forgetful functor from the infinity slice category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $x \in \mathcal{C}_0$ an object, and $\mathcal{C}_{/x}$ the infinity slice category. Then the forgetful functor is defined as morphism of simplicial sets

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}_{x/} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \mathrm{Hom}_{x / \mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^n \star \Delta^0, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\emptyset / \mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^n, \mathcal{C}) = \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\Delta^n, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
