special orthogonal group is a lie group

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{K} \in \{\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}, \mathbb{H}\}$, then the Special orthogonal group $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{K})$ is a lie group

Proof

follows from the regular value theorem for

\begin{align*} \mathrm{det}: \mathrm{O}(n) \rightarrow \{\pm 1\} \end{align*}
