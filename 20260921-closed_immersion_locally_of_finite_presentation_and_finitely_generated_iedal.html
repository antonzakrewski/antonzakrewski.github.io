closed immersion locally of finite presentation and finitely generated ideal

Proposition

Let $\iota: Y \hookrightarrow X$ be a closed immersion of algebraic functors TFAE:

  1. $\iota$ is of locally of finite presentation
  2. the quasi-coherent ideal classified by $\iota$ is a finitely generated, quasi-coherent ideal

Proof

affine case

Let $Y = V(I) \subseteq \mathrm{Spec}(R) = X$. Then $R/I$ is an finitely presented algebra if and only if $I$ is a finitely generated ideal.
