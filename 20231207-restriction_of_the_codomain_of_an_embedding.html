Let $X,Y$ be topological spaces such that $\iota: X \rightarrow Y$ is a topological embedding.

Suppose $Z \subseteq Y$ is a subspace with $\mathrm{im}(\iota) \subseteq Z$. Then the restriction

\begin{align*} \iota: X \rightarrow& Z \\ x \mapsto& \iota(x) \\ \end{align*}
