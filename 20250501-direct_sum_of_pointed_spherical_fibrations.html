direct sum of pointed spherical fibrations

Definition

Let $\pi_1,\pi_2: B \rightarrow BF(d_i)$ be pointed spherical fibration for $i \in \{1,2\}$d Then the direct sum is defined as composition

\begin{align*} \pi_1 \oplus \pi_2: B \rightarrow B \times B \rightarrow BG(d_1 + d_2) \end{align*}

of

  1. the infinity diagonal morphism
  2. smash product of pointed spherical fibrations
