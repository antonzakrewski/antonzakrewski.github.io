nilpotent endomorphism and linear independent elements

Proposition

Let $V$ be a vector space, $\varphi: V \rightarrow V$ a vectorspace-endomorphism. Suppose $v \in V$ such that $\varphi^{n-1}(v) \neq 0$, $\varphi^n(v) = 0$. Then

\begin{align*} v,\varphi^1(v),...,\varphi^{n-1}(v) \end{align*}

is linearly independent

Proof

Let

\begin{align*} \sum_{i=0}^n \lambda_i \varphi^i(v) = 0 \end{align*}

We want to show that $\lambda_i = 0$.

Proof by induction

induction basis

We show that $\lambda_0 = 0$. Application of $\varphi^{n-1}$ shows

\begin{align*} 0 =& \varphi^{n-1}(\sum_{i=0}^n \lambda_i \varphi^i(v)) \\ =& \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} \varphi^{n+i}(\lambda_{i+1} v) + \lambda_0 \varphi^{n-1}(v) \\ =& 0 + \lambda_0 \varphi^{n-1}(v) \end{align*}

where $\varphi^{n-1}(v) \neq 0$, hence $\lambda_0 = 0$

induction step

Assume that $\lambda_0,...,\lambda_j = 0$. Then this follows similarly to the induction basis.

Alternatively define

\begin{align*} w \coloneqq \varphi^{j+1}(v) \end{align*}

and consider

\begin{align*} 0 =& \sum_{i=0}^{n-j} \lambda_{j+1} \varphi^i(w) \end{align*}

Then applying the induction basis shows that $\lambda_{j+1} = 0$ (hopefully the indices are right)
