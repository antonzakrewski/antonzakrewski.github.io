1-projectively generated category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a \(1\) category Then $\mathcal{C}$ is said to be \(1\)-projectively generated, if

  1. it is cocomplete
  2. the inclusion of the 1-sfp objects
\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sInd}_1(\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}}) \simeq \mathcal{C} \end{align*}
