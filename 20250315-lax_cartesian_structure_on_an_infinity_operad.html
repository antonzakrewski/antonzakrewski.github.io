lax cartesian structure on an infinity operad

Definition

Let $p: \mathcal{O}^{\otimes} \rightarrow N(\mathrm{Fin}_*)$ be an infinity operad Then a lax cartesian structure on $\mathcal{C}^{\otimes}$ is a functor

\begin{align*} \pi: \mathcal{C}^{\otimes} \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

where the segal condition for Triv holds for the composition

\begin{align*} N(\mathrm{Triv}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\otimes}_{\mathrm{cocart}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}
