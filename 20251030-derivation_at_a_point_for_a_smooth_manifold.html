derivation at a point for a smooth manifold

Definition

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold and $p\in M$ a point. Then a derivation at $p$ is an \(\mathbb{R}\)-linear map

\begin{align*} \nu: C^{\infty}(M) \rightarrow \mathbb{R} \end{align*}

so that

\begin{align*} \nu(f \cdot g) = \nu(f) \cdot g(p) + f(p) \cdot \nu(g) \end{align*}
