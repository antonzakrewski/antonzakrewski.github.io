G theory of a coherent ring modulo two sided nilpotent ideal

Proposition

Let $R$ be a left coherent ring and $I$ an ideal which is nilpotent ideal. Then the canonical map

\begin{align*} G(R) \rightarrow G(R/I) \end{align*}

induced by

\begin{align*} - \otimes_{R} R/I \end{align*}

is an equivalence

Proof
