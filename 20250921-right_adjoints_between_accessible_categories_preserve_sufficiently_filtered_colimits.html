right adjoints between accessible categories preserve sufficiently filtered colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be a accessible categories and $\mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a right adjoint with left adjoint $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$. Then $\mathcal{G}$ preserves \(\tau\)-filtered colimits for sufficiently large $\tau$

Proof

Write $\mathcal{C} \simeq \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C}^{\kappa}), \mathcal{D} \simeq \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa'}(\mathcal{D}^{\kappa'})$ (cf. accessible category and Ind). Then then find a common $\tau$ such that each object $\mathcal{F}(x)\in \mathcal{D}$ for $x \in \mathcal{C}^{\kappa}$ is \(\tau\)-compact.
