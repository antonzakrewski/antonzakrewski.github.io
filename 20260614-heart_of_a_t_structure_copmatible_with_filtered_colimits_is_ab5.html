Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category equipped with a t-structure compatible with filtered colimits. Then the heart is Ab5

Proof

We want to show that $\mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit} \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ is closed under flitered colimits, because then we may pass to the long exact sequence in the heart to see that filtered colimits are exact in $\mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}$. Recall that $\mathcal{C}_{\geq 0}$ a right Bousfield localization and $\mathcal{C}_{\leq 0}$ is a left Bousfield localization: Then $\mathcal{C}_{\geq 0} \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ is closed under colimits (cf. colimit in a right bousfield localization) and $\mathcal{C}_{\leq 0}$ is closed by assumption. colimits in a bousfield localization
