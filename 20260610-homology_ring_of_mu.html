homology ring of MU

Proposition

The MU spectrum has $\mathbb{Z}$ homology given by

\begin{align*} H_*(\mathrm{MU}) = \mathbb{Z}[b_1,...,] \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • thom isomorphism in spectra, i.e. $H_*(\mathrm{MU}, \mathbb{Z}) \cong H_*(\mathrm{th}(\mathrm{BU},F); \mathbb{Z}) \cong H_*(\mathrm{BU},\mathbb{Z})$
  • homology ring of BU
