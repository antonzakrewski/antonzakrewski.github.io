R-local spectrum and mod p

Proposition

Let $R = \mathbb{Z}[p^{-1} \vert p \in P] \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ be a subring and $X$ a spectrum TFAE:

  1. $X$ is an R-local spectrum
  2. $X/p \simeq 0$ is trivial for $p \in P$.
  3. $\mathrm{map}(-/p,X)$ is trivial.

Proof

1) $\iff$ 2)

follows from R-local spectrum and multiplication by p

1) $\implies$ 3)

follows from mapping spectrum and mod n and 1) $\iff$ 2)

3) $\implies$ 2)

follows from $\mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}/p, X) \cong (X/p)[-1]$.
