colimit over an E_n anima valued monoid is E_n monoidal with respect to the E_n day convolution

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a monoidal category where the colimit exists and commutes with the tensor product in each variable, \(B \in \mathbb{E}_n-\mathrm{Mon}(\mathrm{An})\) Then the colimit functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(B, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is an E_n monoidal functor.

Proof

follows as the colimit functor is given by the left kan extension along $B \rightarrow *$. But this map lifts canonically to $\mathrm{Mon}(\mathrm{An})$ since $*$ is the terminal object.

Therefore we may apply left kan extension is symmetric monoidal for day convolution
