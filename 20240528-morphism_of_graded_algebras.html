morphism of graded Algebras

Definition

Let $R$ be a ring, $H,H'$ be graded R-algebras. Then a morphism of graded Algebras is defined as graded module homomorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: H \rightarrow H' \end{align*}

such that

\begin{align*} \varphi(\alpha \cdot \beta) = \varphi(\alpha) \cdot \varphi(\beta) \end{align*}
