stalk of a direct image sheaf functor for a closed subset

Proposition

Let $Z \subseteq X$ be a closed subset, $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Op}(Z)^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab}$ a sheaf Then for $x \in X$ the stalk of the direct image sheaf is given by

\begin{align*} (\iota_*\mathcal{F})_x = \begin{cases} 0 & \mbox{if } x \not\in Z \\ \mathcal{F}_x & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

$x \not\in Z$

Then

\begin{align*} \iota_*\mathcal{F}(X \setminus Z) =& \mathcal{F}(\emptyset) \\ =& 0 \end{align*}

hence the colimit is eventually constant and therefore equal to $0$

$x \in Z$
