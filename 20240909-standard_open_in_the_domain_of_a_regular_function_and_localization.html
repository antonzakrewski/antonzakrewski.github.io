standard open in the domain of a regular function and localization

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be an affine variety and $f \in k(X)$ an element of the function field Let $h \in A(X)$ be an element in the coordinate ring and

\begin{align*} D(h) =& X \setminus Z(h) \\ =& \{P \in X \vert h(P) \neq 0\} \end{align*}

the standard open of an affine variety

TFAE:

  1. $D(h) \subseteq \mathrm{def}(f)$
  2. $f \in \mathcal{A}(X)[h^{-1}]$

Proof

each element of $\mathcal{A}(X)[h^{-1}]$ is of the form $\frac{g}{h^n}$ for $g \in \mathcal{A}(X)$ and $n \geq 0$. Then the following are each equivalent

  1. $f = \frac{g}{h^n}$
  2. $h^n \in D_f$ (cf. ideal quotient)
  3. $h \in \sqrt{D_f} = I(Z(D_f))$
  4. $h(P) = 0$ for all $P \in Z(D_f)$

where $2 \Rightarrow 3$ uses the strong nullstellensatz

Furthermore it follows by

\begin{align*} Z(D_f) = X \setminus \mathrm{def}(f) \end{align*}

also, that the following are equivalent

  1. $D(h) \subseteq \mathrm{def}(f)
  2. $D(h) \cap Z(D_f) = \emptyset$
  3. h(P) = 0$ for all $P \in Z(D_f)$
