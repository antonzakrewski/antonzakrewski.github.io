derivation of a constant map

Proposition

Let $\nu: C^{\infty}(M) \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a derivation. let $f = \mathrm{const}_p$, then $\nu(f) = 0$

Proof

\begin{align*} \nu(f) = \nu(f(p)\cdot 1) = f(p) \nu(1) \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} \nu(1) =& \nu(1 \cdot 1) \\ =& 1 \cdot \nu(1) + 1 \cdot \nu(1) \\ \end{align*}

which implies $\nu(1) = 0$
