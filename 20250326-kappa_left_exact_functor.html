kappa left exact functor

Definition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal. Then an infinity functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is said to be \(\kappa\)-left exact, if for any left fibration

\begin{align*} \mathcal{B}' \rightarrow \mathcal{B} \end{align*}

where $\mathcal{B}'$ is kappa cofiltered, so is the \(1\)-categorical pullback

\begin{align*} \mathcal{A}' = \mathcal{A} \times_{\mathcal{B}} \mathcal{B}' \end{align*}
