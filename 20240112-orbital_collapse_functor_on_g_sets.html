orbital collapse functor on g-sets

Definition

Let $G$ be a group and $\mathrm{G-Set}$ be the Category of G-Sets. Then the orbital collapse functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Orbit}: \mathrm{G-Set} \rightarrow& \mathrm{G-Set} \\ X \mapsto& X/G \\ (f: X \rightarrow Y) \mapsto& (\overline{f}: X/G \rightarrow Y/G) \end{align*}

where $\overline{g}$ is the induced map on the orbit set
