isomorphism invariant under opposite category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}$ the opposite category and $f \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$ a morphism. TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an isomorphism
  2. $f^{\mathrm{op}} \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}})$ is an iso
