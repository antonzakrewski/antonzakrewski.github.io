preserved limit by an infinity functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an infinity functor Assume that $\mathcal{F}: K \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is a diagram, which admits a limit

Then the limit of $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be preserved under $f$ if for some limit cocone $\overline{\mathcal{F}}$ the resulting diagram
