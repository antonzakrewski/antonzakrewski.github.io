connected spectrum and constant rank

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. TFAE:

  1. the Zariski topology on the zariski spectrum $\mathrm{Spec}(R)$ is a connected space.
  2. each finitely generated projective module is of constant rank

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

  • locally constant map
  • locally constant map from a connected space as constant map

2) $\implies$ 1)
