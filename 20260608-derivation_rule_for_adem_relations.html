derivation rule for adem relations

Proposition

The Adem Relations follows from

  1. $\mathrm{Sq}^{2n-1} \mathrm{Sq}^n = 0$
  2. the map $\mathrm{Sq}^n \rightarrow \mathrm{Sq}^{n-1}$ is a derivation, that is $d(\mathrm{Sq}^n \mathrm{Sq}^m) = \mathrm{Sq}^{n-1} \mathrm{Sq}^m + \mathrm{Sq}^n \mathrm{Sq}^{m-1}$ is well-defined
