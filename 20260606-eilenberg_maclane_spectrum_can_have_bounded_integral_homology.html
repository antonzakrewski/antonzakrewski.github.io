Eilenberg-MacLane spectrum can have bounded integral homology

Proposition

Proof

Consider $\mathbb{Q}$. Then $\mathbb{Q} \simeq \mathbb{S}_{\mathbb{Q}}$ (cf. stable rational homotopy groups of spheres).

This also shows

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Q} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z} \simeq& \mathbb{S}_{\mathbb{Q}} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z} \\ \simeq& (\mathbb{S} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z})_{\mathbb{Q}} \\ \simeq& \mathbb{Z}_{\mathbb{Q}} \\ \simeq& \mathbb{Q} \end{align*}
