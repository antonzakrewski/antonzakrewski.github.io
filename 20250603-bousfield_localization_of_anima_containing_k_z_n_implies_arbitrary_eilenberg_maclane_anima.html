bousfield localization of anima containing K(Z,n) implies arbitrary eilenberg-maclane anima

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{An}$ be a bousfield localization of Anima. Suppose $K(\mathbb{Z}, n)\in \mathcal{C}$ is contained for $ n\in \mathbb{N}$.

Then $\mathcal{C}$ already contains all eilenberg-maclane anima as hypoabelian anima

Proof

corollary of:

  • unit of a localization of anima containing eilenberg-macLane anima as isomorphism on cohomology
  • integral cohomology isomorphism induces homology isomorphism
  • characterization of objects as S-local for the unit maps
