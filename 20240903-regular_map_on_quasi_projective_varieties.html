regular map on quasi-projective algebraic sets

Definition

Let $X,Y$ be quasi-projective algebraic set. Then a map $f: X \rightarrow Y$ is a regular map if it is

  1. continuous map
  2. for each open $V \subseteq Y$ and $g \in \mathcal{O}(V)$ it follows that
\begin{align*} f\circ g \in \mathcal{O}(f^{-1}[V]) \end{align*}
