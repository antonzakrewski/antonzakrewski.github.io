rational homotopy equivalence between the sphere and the eilenberg-maclane space

Proposition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, then the maps

\begin{align*} S^{2n+1} \rightarrow& K(\mathbb{Z},2n+1) S^{2n} \rightarrow& \mathrm{fib}(K(\mathbb{Z}, 2n) \xrightarrow{(-)^2} K(\mathbb{Z}, 4n)) \end{align*}

are rational equivalences

Proof

a) odd case, i.e. $2n +1$
