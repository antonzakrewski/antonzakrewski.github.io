cohomology ring the infinite complex projective space via serre spectral sequence

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, then the cohomology of $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}$ is given by

\begin{align*} H^*(\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n, R) \simeq R[x] \end{align*}

for $\mathrm{deg}(x) = 2$

Proof

consider the fiber sequence

\begin{align*} S^1 \rightarrow * \rightarrow \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty} \end{align*}

and the multiplicative cohomological serre spectral sequence. Now use that $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}$ is connected and hence $H^{0}(\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}, R) = R$ We also know that

\begin{align*} H^*(S^1,R) = \begin{cases} R & \mbox{if } * \in \{0,1\} \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

so in particular we get $E^2_{*,n} = 0$ for $n > 2$ and $E^{2}_{*,1} = E^{2}_{*,0}$

Then we get

Now $H^*(*,R) = R$ and the $E^3$ page collapses, so we have to kill everything that would nontrivially contribute to $H^{n}(*,R)$ for $n < 0$ But we can't kill for example the Term in $E_{1,0}^2$ so it follows by induction that every odd term must be zero.

Thus

Now let $x \in H^2(\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}, R)$ be a generator. Let $y \in H^0(\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}, H^1(S,R))$ be the element corresponding to $x$ under the differential.

Then the differential satisfies the leibniz rule, so for $x\cdot y \in H^2(\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}, H^1(S,R))$

\begin{align*} d^r(x \cdot y) = d^r(x) \cdot y + (-1)^{\mathrm{deg}(x)} x \cdot d^r(y) \\ =& 0 \cdot y + x \cdot x \\ =& x^2 \end{align*}
