kappa right exact functor for a sufficiently cocomplete domain

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories where $\mathcal{C}$ admits kappa-small colimits. Suppose $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is an infinity functor.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ preserves kappa small colimits.
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ is kappa right exact
