map from K(G,n) to K(Q,m) for G torsion nullhomotopic

Proposition

Let $G$ be an abelian group containing only torsion elements. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(K(G,n), K(\mathbb{Q},m)) \cong \Delta^0 \end{align*}

is it really a point or not some connected type ?

Proof

note that

\begin{align*} H^*(-,G) \end{align*}

by definition of cohomology only contains torsion elements, as it is a quotient of a subgroup torsion elements (namely $\mathrm{Hom}(-, G)$).

Then using <map induces a natural transformation> we get a natural transformation

\begin{align*} H^n(-,G) \rightarrow H^*m(-,\mathbb{Q}) \end{align*}

where the left contains abelian groups of torsion and the right contains $\mathbb{Q}$ modules, hence the map must be trivial
