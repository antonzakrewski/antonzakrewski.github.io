idempotent element induces projective module

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring, $e \in R$ an idempotent element. Then

\begin{align*} eR \end{align*}

is a projective module

Proof

consider

\begin{align*} R \rightarrow& eR \\ 1 \mapsto& e \\ eR \rightarrow& R \\ e \cdot r \mapsto& e r \end{align*}

where retract of a projective object as projective object
