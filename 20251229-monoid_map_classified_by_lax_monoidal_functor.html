monoid map classified by lax monoidal functor

Proposition

Let $M \rightarrow N$ be a map of anima valued monoid. Then under straightening-unstraightening this refines to a lax monoidal functor

Proof

Note that slice category of anima valued monoids is algebras in slice so we find an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}(\mathrm{An})_{/M} \simeq& \mathrm{Alg}(\mathrm{An}_{/M})\\ \simeq& \mathrm{Alg}(\mathrm{Fun}(M, \mathrm{An})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{lax}}(M, \mathrm{An}) \end{align*}

where we use

  1. slice category of anima valued monoids is algebras in slice
  2. slice category over a monoid inherits a monoidal structure, i.e. in particular a monoidal equivalence
  3. E_n monoids with respect to day convolution are E_n lax monoidal functors
