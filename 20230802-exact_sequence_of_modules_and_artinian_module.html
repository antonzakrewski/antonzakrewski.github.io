exact sequence of Modules and artinian Module

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $M_1,M_2,M_3$ \(R\)-module such that

is an exact sequence TFAE:

  1. $M_2$ is an artinian module
  2. $M_1,M_3$ are artinian modules

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

$M_1$

Let $S_1 \supseteq S_2 ...$ be a descending sequence of submodules of $M_1$. Then $f[S_1] \subseteq S[S_2] \subseteq ... $ is a descending chain in $M_2$, hence it stabilizes. Furthermore $S_1 = f^{-1}f[S_1]$, since $f$ is injective (see: exact sequence and monomorphism)

$M_3$

Let $S_1 \supseteq S_2 ...$ be a descending sequence of submodules of $M_3$. Then $g^{-1}[S_1] \supseteq g^{-1}[S_2] \supseetq ...$ is a descending chain in $M_2$, hence it stabilizes. Furthermore, since $g$ is surjective, $g[g^{-1}[S_1] = S_1$ (see: exact sequence and epimorphism)

2) $\implies$ 1)
