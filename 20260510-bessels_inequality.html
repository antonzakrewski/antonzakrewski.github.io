bessels inequality

Proposition

Let $X$ be a prehilbert space, $x \in X$ and $\{e_{\alpha}\}_{\alpha \in J}$ an orthonormal set. Then

\begin{align*} \| x \|^2 \geq \sum_{\alpha \in J} \| \langle e_{\alpha}, x\rangle \|^2 \end{align*}

Proof

Let $J_0 \subseteq J$ be a finite subset Then we can write

\begin{align*} x =& \sum_{\alpha \in J_{0}} \langle e_{\alpha},x\rangle e_{\alpha} + (x - \sum_{\alpha \in J_{0}} \langle e_{\alpha}, x\rangle e_{\alpha}) \end{align*}

and define $u \coloneqq \sum_{\alpha \in J_{0}} \langle e_{\alpha},x\rangle e_{\alpha}, v \coloneqq (x - \sum_{\alpha \in J_{0}} \langle e_{\alpha}, x\rangle e_{\alpha})$.

then $\{e_{\alpha}\}$ is an orthonormal set, so we get

\begin{align*} \langle u,u\rangle =& \langle \sum_{\alpha \in J_{0}} \langle e_{\alpha}, \sum_{\beta \in J_{0}} \langle e_{\beta} \rangle \\ =& \sum_{\alpha, \beta \in J_0} \langle \overline{e_{\alpha}, x\rangle} \langle e_{\beta}, x\rangle \langle e_{\alpha}, e_{\beta}\rangle \\ =& \sum_{\alpha \in J_0} \langle \overline{e_{\alpha}, x\rangle} \langle e_{\alpha}, x\rangle \\ =& \sum_{\alpha \in J_0} \vert \langle e_{\alpha}, x\rangle \vert^2 \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} \langle u,v\rangle =& \langle u, x - u\rangle \\ =& \langle u,x\rangle - \langle u,u\rangle \\ =& \sum_{\alpha \in J_0} \overline{ \langle e_{\alpha}, x \rangle} \langle e_{\alpha}, x\rangle - \langle u,u\rangle \\ =& 0 \end{align*}

Thererfore we can estimate $\| x \|^2$:

\begin{align*} \| x \|^2 = \langle u + v, u + v\rangle =& \langle u,u\rangle + \langle v,v\rangle \\ \geq& \sum_{\alpha \in J_0} \vert \langle e_{\alpha}, x\rangle \vert^2 \end{align*}

for all finite subsets.

Now taking the supremum preserves this $\geq$
