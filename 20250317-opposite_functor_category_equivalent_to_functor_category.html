opposite functor category equivalent to functor category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories. Then there exists a natural equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}},\mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{op}})^{\mathrm{op}} \simeq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

Proof

consider the equivalence which is given on objects by

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F} \mapsto \mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}

This can be made precise by currying in cat infinity

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{op}})^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})) \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{op}})^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Fun}((\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{op}}) \times \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}})^{\mathrm{op}},\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}
