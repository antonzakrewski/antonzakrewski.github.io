projective vanishing locus

Definition

let $A$ be a N-graded ring and $F \subseteq a$ a homogeneous subset of a graded ring. Then the vanishing locus of $F$ is the subfunctor $V(F) \subseteq \mathrm{Proj}(A)$ given by

\begin{align*} V(F)(R) = \{a: \bigoplus_{I} R \twoheadrightarrow L \vert f(a) = 0 \in L^{\otimes \vert f \vert} \forall f \in F\} \end{align*}
