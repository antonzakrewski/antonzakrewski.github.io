sheaves on a generated grothendieck topology

Proposition

Let $\tau_i$ be a family of grothendieck topologies on $\mathcal{C}$. Let $\tau = \bigvee_{i \in I} \tau_i$ the supremum - i.e., the generated grothendieck topology. Then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sh}_{\tau}(\mathcal{C}) \cong \bigcap_{i \in I} \mathrm{Sh}_{\tau_i}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
