cauchy sequence for a filtration

Definition

Let $R = R_0$ be a ring and $\mathrm{GrRMod}$ the category of graded modules and $M$ a graded module. Let $F_s$ be a filtration.

A sequence $n \mapsto x_n$ is said to be cauchy, if

\begin{align*} x_m - x_n \rightarrow 0 \end{align*}

converges to $0$ as $m,n \rightarrow 0$
