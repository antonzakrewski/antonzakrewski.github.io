singular points of a quasi-projective variety as closed set

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ projective varietyK

Proof

w.l.o.g. we may assume that $X$ is affine (cf. closed in a finite subcover and closed in the whole space)

Then $P \in X$ is a singular point if and only if $d \leq \mathrm{dim}_k(T_p(X)) = m - \mathrm{rang}(J(p))$, i.e.

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Rang}(P) < m - d \end{align*}

Hence $X^{\mathrm{sing}}$ is the zero locus of the ideal, which is generated by

  1. $I(X)$

and

  1. determinants of submatrices of
\begin{align*} J(P) =& J(F_1,...,F_r) \\ =& ( \frac{\partial F_j}{\partial X_i}_{m \times r}) \end{align*}

i.e. $X^{\mathrm{sing}} \subseteq X$ is closed
