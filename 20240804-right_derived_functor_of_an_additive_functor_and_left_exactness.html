Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, where $\mathcal{A}$ admits enough projectives Suppose $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ is an additive functor and $\mathcal{RF}_n$ the right derived functor

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is left exact
  2. $\mathcal{RF}_0 \cong \mathcal{F}$ are naturally isomorphic
