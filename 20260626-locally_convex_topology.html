locally convex topology on a vector space

Definition

Let $V$ be a $\mathbb{K}$ vector space and $\{p_i\}$ a family of seminorms. Then the locally convex topology on $V$ is defined as the topology generated by the subbasis containing $\{y + U_{i,r}\}$ for $i \in I, y \in X r > 0$ where $U_{i,r}$ is the set $\{x \in X \vert p_i(x) < r\}$.
