right quillen adjoint preserves weak equivalences between fibrant objects

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}_{\mathcal{C}}, \mathrm{Cof}_{\mathcal{C}}, \mathrm{Fib}_{\mathcal{C}}), (\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{W}_{\mathcal{D}}, \mathrm{Cof}_{\mathcal{D}}, \mathrm{Fib}_{\mathcal{D}})$ be model categories Suppose $\mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is a right quillen adjoint, then $\mathcal{F}$ preserves weak equivalences between fibrant objects
