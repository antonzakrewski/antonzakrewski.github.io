homotopy left derived functor of a left quillen functor

Proposition

let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a left-quillen functor Then the (up to natural isomorphism) functor is given as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{LF} \coloneqq \mathcal{F} \circ Q \end{align*}

where $Q$ is the cofibrant replacement functor
