inclusion of seminormed vector spaces admits left adjoint

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Norm} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{semNorm}$ be the full subcategory inclusion. then this admits a left adjoint, given by

\begin{align*} X \mapsto X/N \end{align*}

where $N = \{x \vert \| x \| = 0\}$.

see:

  • category of normed vector spaces
  • category of seminormed vector spaces

Proof

follows from:

  • quotient of a normed vector space for a closed subspace is a normed vector space

where any bounded map $T: X \rightarrow Y$ into a normed vector space requires that $N \subseteq \mathrm{ker}(T)$.
