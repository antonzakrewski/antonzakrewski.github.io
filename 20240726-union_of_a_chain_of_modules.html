union of a chain of submodules as module

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $M$ an \(R\)-module. Suppose $N_i \subseteq N_{i+1}$ is a chain of submodules

Then

\begin{align*} \bigcup N_i \subseteq M \end{align*}

is also submodule

Proof

  • union of a chain of abelian groups as abelian group

action of $R$

note that $\bigcup N_i$ is closed under multiplication with $R$
