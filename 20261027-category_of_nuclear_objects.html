category of nuclear objects for a compactly generated, presentably symmetric monoidal stable category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C} \in \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}L_{\mathrm{st}})$ be a compactly generated category with compact monoidal unit. Define the full subcategory $\mathrm{Nuc}(\mathcal{C}) = \{X \in \mathcal{C} \vert \text{ every map from a compact object of } C \text{ to } X \text{ is trace class}\}
