category with kappa small colimits is kappa filtered

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category with $\kappa$ small colimits Then $\mathcal{C}$ is $\kappa$ filtered

Proof

a) - extending cones

  • take the colimit

b) - cofinal diagonal

we want to show that $\mathcal{C} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{const}(-)} \mathrm{Fun}(K, \mathcal{C})$ is coinitial for any $\kappa$ small category $K$. We use quillens theorem A for coinitial functors, i.e., for any $\mathcal{F}$ in $\mathrm{Fun}(K, \mathcal{C})$, the category $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{F}/}$ is weakly contractible category.

first observe that $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{F}/}$ is nonempty, since $\mathrm{colim} \mathcal{F}$ is canonically an object.

Moreover, $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{F}/}$ admits finite colimits (cf. colimits in a coslice category), so in particular is weakly contractible (cf. infinity category with finite coproducts as weakly contractible).
