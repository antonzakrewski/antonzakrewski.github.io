spanier whitehead category is not cocomplete

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{SW}$ be the spanier whitehead category. Then it is not cocomplete.

Proof

Recall that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{SW} = \mathrm{colim}_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathrm{An}_* \xrightarrow{\Sigma} \mathrm{An}_* ... \end{align*}

Recall that isomorphism classes of objects of a filtered colimit, so let $(S^1,n)$ denote the object coming from $S^1$ in the \(n\)-th component. Then $(S^1,n)$ corresponds to $\mathbb{S}^{1-n}$ Then we claim that

\begin{align*} \coprod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{S}^{1-n} \end{align*}

does not exist:

For this observe that an object in spanier whitehead category has bounded homotopy groups and that $\mathbb{S}^{1-n}$ is a retract of $\coprod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{S}^{1-n}$ (cf. object in a pointed category is a retract of a coproduct). The latter shows that $\pi_{1-n}(\mathbb{S}^{1-n}) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ is a retract of $\pi_{1-n}(\coprod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{S}^{1-n})$, hence $\pi_{1-n}(\coprod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{S}^{1-n})$ is nontrivial.
