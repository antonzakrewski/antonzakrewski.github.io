product of modules over different rings as module over the product

Proposition

Let $R_i$ be rings, $M_i$ be \(R_i\)-modules. Then the cartesian product

\begin{align*} \prod M_i \end{align*}

is a module over the product $\prod R_i$ with

\begin{align*} (r_i) \cdot (m_i) = (r_i \cdot m_i) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from definitions
