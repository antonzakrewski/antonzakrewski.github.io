functorality of straightening-unstraightening for right fibrations

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an infinity functor and $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{op}}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{op}}$ the corresponding opposite functor Then precomposition on the functor categories

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}^*: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{op}},\mathrm{An}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}},\mathrm{An}) \end{align*}

induces the pullback functor of cartesian fibrations under the straightening-unstraightening of right fibrations

Proof
