glueing of sheaves

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a (sufficently) complete category, $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space, $U_i \subseteq X$ be an open cover and $\mathcal{F}_i: \mathrm{Op}(U_i)^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a family of sheaves Then there exists a sheaf

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Op}(X)^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

with

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(U_i) \cong \mathcal{F}_i(U_i) \end{align*}

given by

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(O) \coloneqq \mathrm{lim}(\prod_{i} \mathcal{F}_i(U_i \cap O) \rightarrow \prod_{i,j} \mathcal{F}_i(U_i \cap U_j \cap X)) \end{align*}

and morphism induced by lim functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(U_i \cap O) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(U_i \cap O') \end{align*}

for $O' \subseteq O$

Proof

equalizer condition

follows mostly from limits commute with limits, as for another open cover $O_i$
