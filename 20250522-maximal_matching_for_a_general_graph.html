maximal matching for a general graph

Proposition

Let $G = (V,E)$ be a graph and $d \in \mathbb{N}_0$

Then

\begin{align*} 2 m(G) =& v(G) - \mathrm{max}_{S \subseteq V} \{q(G \setminus S) - \vert S \vert \} - \vert S \vert =& v(G) - \Delta \end{align*}

Proof

$\Delta \geq 0$

choose $S = \emptyset$, then

\begin{align*} q(G \setminus S) - \vert S \vert = 0 \end{align*}

$v(G) + \Delta$ even

Let $S^* \subseteq V$ with $\Delta = q(G \setminus S^*) - \vert S^* \vert$ and $c_1,...,c_l$ the components of $G \setminus S^*$. Then

\begin{align*} v(G) + \Delta =& \sum_{V(C_i) \mathrm{ even}} v(C_i) + \sum_{v(C_i) \mathrm{ odd}} v(C_i) + \vert S^* \vert + \Delta =& \sum_{V(C_i) \mathrm{ even}} v(C_i) + \sum_{v(C_i) \mathrm{ odd}} v(C_i) + q(G \setminus S^*) =& \sum_{V(C_i) \mathrm{ even}} v(C_i) + \sum_{v(C_i) \mathrm{ odd}} (v(C_i) + 1) \end{align*}

where $q(-)$ denotes (?).

The last term is clearly even.

Now consider

\begin{align*} G' \coloneqq K_{\Delta} \cup G \end{align*}

where $K_{\Delta}$ is the complete graph and all edges from $K_{\Delta}$ to $G$.

Then

\begin{align*} E(G') = \binom{\Delta}{2} \cup \{vd : v \}n V, d \in K_{\Delta}\} \end{align*}

a) $S = \emptyset$

then

\begin{align*} q(G' \setminus S) = 0 \leq \vert S \vert \end{align*}

b) $S \neq \empytset$, $S \cap D \neq D$

Then

\begin{align*} q(G' \setminus S) \leq 1 \leq \vert S \vert \end{align*}

since $G' \setminus S$ is still connected.

c) $S \cap D = D$

Then

\begin{align*} q(G' \setminus S) =& q(G \setminus (S \setminus D)) \\ \leq& \vert S \vert - \Delta + \Delta =& \vert S \vert \end{align*}

Now by tutte theorem $G'$ contains a perfect matching which shows

\begin{align*} m(G) \geq& m(G') - \Delta \\ =& \frac{v(G) + \Delta}{2} - \Delta \end{align*}
