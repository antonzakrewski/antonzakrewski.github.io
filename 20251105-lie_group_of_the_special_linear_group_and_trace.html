lie algebra of the special linear group and traceless matrices

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{SL}_n$ be the Special linear Group. Then the special linear lie algebra is given by

\begin{align*} T_{e}(\mathrm{SL}_n) = \{A \in M_n(\mathbb{R}) \vert \mathrm{tr}(A) = 0\} \end{align*}

use exponential of a matrix

Proof

consider the exponential map. Here we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{det}(\mathrm{e}^{A}) = \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{tr}(A)} \end{align*}

which is one if and only if $\mathrm{tr}(A) = 0$
