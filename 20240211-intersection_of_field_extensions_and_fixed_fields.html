intersection of field extensions and fixed fields

Proposition

Let $L/K$ be a finite galois field extension and $K \subseteq E,E' \subseteq L$ intermediate field extension.

Then for the intersection of fields

\begin{align*} E \cap E' =& L^{ \mathrm{Gal}(L/E') \cup \mathrm{Gal}(L/E)} \\ =& L^{ \langle \mathrm{Gal}(L/E') \cup \mathrm{Gal}(L/E) \rangle} \end{align*}
