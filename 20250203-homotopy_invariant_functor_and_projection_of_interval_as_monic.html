homotopy invariant functor and projection of interval as monic

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Top} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ resp. $\mathcal{G}: \mathrm{Top} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a functor

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ resp. $\mathcal{G}$ are homotopy invariant functor
  2. for each topological space $B$ the induced morphism
\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{F}(B \times [0,1]) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(B) \end{align*}

resp.

\begin{align*} \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{G}(B) \rightarrow \mathcal{G}(B \times [0,1]) \end{align*}

is a monomorphism resp. epimorphism

Proof

corollary of:

  • homotopy invariant functor and interval
  • monic split epi as isomorphism / epic split mono as isomorphism

where $B \times [0,1] \rightarrow B$ is a split epi resp. split mono in the opposite category
