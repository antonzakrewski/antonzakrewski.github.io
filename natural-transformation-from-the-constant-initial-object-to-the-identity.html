natural transformation from the constant initial object to the identity

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\emptyset$ the initial object. Then there exists a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \underline{\emptyset} \rightarrow \mathrm{id} \end{align*}

from the constant functor to the initial object to the identity functor

Proof
