n skeleton inclusion as n-1 connected map

Proposition

Let $(X, \emptyset, \mathrm{SK}_n(X))$ be a CW-Complex, $\mathrm{Sk}_n(X)$ the n-skeleton. Then the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sk}_n \hookrightarrow X \end{align*}

is an n-connected map

Proof

$\mathrm{Sk}_n \rightarrow \mathrm{Sk}_{n+1}$

special case of attaching n-cells and n-1 connected inclusion

general case

follows from:

  • isomorphism of homotopy groups between skeleton and CW complex

alternatively

  • blakers massey and n-connected maps under pushout
  • use inductive argument
  • sequential colimit of n-connected map
