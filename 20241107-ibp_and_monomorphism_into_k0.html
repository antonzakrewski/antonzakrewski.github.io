IBP and monomorphism into K0

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring

TFAE:

  1. $R$ satisfies the invariant basis property
  2. the morphism
\begin{align*} \iota: \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathrm{Proj}(R) \end{align*}

is a monomorphism

Proof

follows from the definitions, where

\begin{align*} \iota[n] = \iota[m] \end{align*}

implies that

\begin{align*} R^n \cong R^m \end{align*}
