pointwise formula for the dualizing spectrum

Proposition

Let $X$ be a compact anima and $x \in X$. Then the

\begin{align*} (D_X)_x \simeq \mathrm{lim}_{X} \mathbb{S}[\Omega X] \end{align*}

Proof

we need to compute $\iota^*(D_X)$ for $\iota: \{x\} \rightarrow X$ the inclusion. For that we may use $\iota_!(\mathbb{S}) \otimes D_X = \iota_!(\mathbb{S} \otimes \iota^*(D_X))$ and hence get

\begin{align*} r_*(\iota_!(\mathbb{S})) \simeq& r_!(\iota_!(\mathbb{S}) \otimes D_X) \\ \simeq& r_!\iota_!\iota^*(D_X) \\ \simeq& \iota^*(D_X) \end{align*}
