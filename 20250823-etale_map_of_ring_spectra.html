etale map of ring spectra

Definition

Let $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ be a map of (\mathbb{E}_1)-ring spectra then $\varphi$ is said to be etale, if

  1. $A,B$ are quasi commutative
  2. the map induces an etale map
  3. for every $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ the map $\mathrm{Tor}_0^{\pi_0}(\pi_n(A), \pi_0(B)) \rightarrow \pi_n(B)$ is an isomorphism
