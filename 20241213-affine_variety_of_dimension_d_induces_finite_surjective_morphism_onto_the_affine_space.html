affine variety of dimension d induces finite surjective morphism onto the affine space

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be an affine variety of dimension $d$. Then there exists a finite surjective morphism

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow \mathbb{A}_K^d \end{align*}

Proof

Let $A(X)$ be the affine coordinate ring Then there exists an injective, integral (?) ring-homomorphism

\begin{align*} K[X_1,...,X_n] \hookrightarrow A \end{align*}

since $A$ is a finite type \(K\)-Algebra (cf. noether Normalization).

\begin{align*} X \rightarrow \mathbb{A}_k^d \end{align*}
