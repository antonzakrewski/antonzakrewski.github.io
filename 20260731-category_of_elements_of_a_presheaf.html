category of elements as pullback along yoneda

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ be a presheaf, then the category of elements of a presheaf is equivalently described as pullback 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
|[label={[label distance=-2mm]-45:\lrcorner}]|
\mathrm{El}(\mathcal{F}) \dar \rar & \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})_{/\mathcal{F}} \dar \\
\mathcal{C} \rar{よ} & \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Proof
