cross product of singular cohomology with F2 coefficients given as map out of the smash product

Proposition

Given $\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}$ and singular cohomology, the cross product of singular cohomology for $n \geq 1$ is equivalently given by a map

\begin{align*} \iota_n \times \iota_n: K(\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z},n) \wedge K(\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z},n) \rightarrow H(\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}, 2n) \end{align*}

where $\iota_n$ is the tautological element

see:

  • smash product of topological spaces
  • here $K(\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z},n)$ is equipped with an (arbitrary) basepoint.

Proof

We want to show that the restriction to $\iota_n \wedge \iota_n$ is nullhomotopic Therefore it suffices to show, that the element corresponding to $\iota_n \wedge \iota_n$ in

\begin{align*} H^{2n}(K(\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}, n) \wedge K(\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}, n)) \end{align*}

is zero.

But by reduced singular cohomology and wedge product we get

\begin{align*} H^{2n}(K(\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}, n) \wedge K(\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}, n)) \cong& H^{2n}(K(\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}, n)) \oplus H^{2n}(K(\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}, n)) \cong& 0 \oplus 0 \\ \cong& 0 \end{align*}

by cohomology of an Eilenberg-MacLane space this evaluates as zero.
