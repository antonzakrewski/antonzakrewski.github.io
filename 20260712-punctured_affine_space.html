punctured affine space

Definition

Let $I$ be a set. The punctured affine \(I\)-space $\mathbb{A}^I \setminus \{0\}$ is the nonvanishing locus of the coordinate functions $\{x_i \vert i \in I\}$ on $\mathbb{A}^I$

Remark

Explicitely:

\begin{align*} (\mathbb{A}^I - 0)(R) = \{a \in R^I \vert (a) = R\} \end{align*}
