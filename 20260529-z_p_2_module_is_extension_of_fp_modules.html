Z/p^2 module is extension of Fp modules

Proposition

Let $M$ be a $\mathbb{Z}/p^2 \mathbb{Z}$ module Then there exists $\mathbb{F}_p$ modules $N_1,N_2$ such that $M$ is the extension

\begin{align*} N_1 \rightarrow M \rightarrow N_2 \end{align*}

Proof

let $pM = \{p \cdot m \vert m \in M\}$ and $M/pM$ be the quotient. In particular, each element in $pM$ is \(p\)-torsion and hence an $\mathbb{F}_p$ module (cf. F_p vector space is p-torsion abelian group). Moreover, there exists an evident short exact sequence

\begin{align*} pM \rightarrow M \rightarrow M/pM \end{align*}
