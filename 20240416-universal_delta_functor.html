universal delta functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, $(\mathcal{F}_n)$ a delta functor. Then $\mathcal{F}_n$ is said to be universal, if for each $\delta$ functor $(\mathcal{G}_n)$ and natural transformation

\begin{align*} \tau_0: \mathcal{G}_0 \rightarrow \mathcal{F}_0 \end{align*}

there exists a unique morphism of delta functors $(\tau_n)$
