integral curve of a vector field

Proposition

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold and $\gamma: (-\varepsilon, \varepsilon) \rightarrow M$ a smooth map Then $\gamma$ is said to be an integral curve if for all $t \in (-\varepsilon, \varepsilon)$

\begin{align*} \dot{\gamma(t)} = X_{\gamma(t)} \end{align*}

Proof
