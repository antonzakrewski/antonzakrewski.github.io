finite field extension of finite fields as normal field extension

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{F}_q,\mathbb{F}_q'$ be finite fields with $\mathbb{F}_q \hookrightarrow \mathbb{F}_q'$ Then $\mathbb{F}_q'$ is a normal field extension

Proof
