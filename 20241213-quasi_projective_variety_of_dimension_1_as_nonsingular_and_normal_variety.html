quasi-projective variety of dimension 1 as nonsingular and normal variety

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ a quasi-projective variety with dimension $1$.

TFAE:

  1. $X$ is a nonsingular quasi-projective variety
  2. $X$ is a normal quasi-projective variety

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

  • non-singular quasi-projective variety as normal variety

2) $\implies$ 1)

  • dimension of singularities of a normal quasi-projective variety
