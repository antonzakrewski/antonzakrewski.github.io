absolute galois group of $\mathbb{Q}$

Definition

The absolute galois group of $\mathbb{Q}$ is defined as galois group

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q}) \end{align*}

of the algebraic closure of $\mathbb{Q}$

Remark (some statements)

  1. uncountable
  2. two concrete elements: $\mathrm{id}$ and conjugation.
  3. aany finite abelian group can be obtained as a quotient group
