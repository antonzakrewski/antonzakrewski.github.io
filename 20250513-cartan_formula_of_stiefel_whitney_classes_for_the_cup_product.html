cartan formula of stiefel-whitney classes for the cup product

Proposition

Let $\pi: E \rightarrow B, \pi': E' \rightarrow B$ be spherical fibrations

Then

\begin{align*} w(\pi \oplus \pi') = w(\pi) \cup w(\pi') \in H^*(B; \mathbb{F}_2)^{^} \end{align*}
