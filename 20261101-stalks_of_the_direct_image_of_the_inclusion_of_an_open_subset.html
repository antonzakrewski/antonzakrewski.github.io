stalks of the direct image of the inclusion of an open subset

Proposition

Let $j: U \hookrightarrow X$ be the inclusion of an open subspace and $\mathcal{F} \in \mathrm{PSh}(U)$ a presheaf. then for $x \in U$, we have $j_*(\mathcal{F})_x \cong \mathcal{F}_x$

Proof

good proof

use that $\{O \subseteq U \vert x \in O\}^{\mathrm{op}} \hookrightarrow \{O \subseteq X \vert x \in O \}^{\mathrm{op}}$ is a coinitial map of directed posets: see also:
