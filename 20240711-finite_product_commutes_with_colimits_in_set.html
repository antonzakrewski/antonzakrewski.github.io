finite product commutes with colimits in set

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Set}$ be the category set, $X_1,...,X_n,Y_i \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathrm{Set})$ be sets. Then finite products commute with colimits.

\begin{align*} (\prod X_j) \times \mathrm{colim} Y_i \simeq \mathrm{colim} \prod X_j \times Y_i \end{align*}

Proof

follows from

  • category set as cartesian closed category
  • left adjoint preserves colimits

and associativity of the categorical product where

\begin{align*} (X_1 \times ... X_n) \times \mathrm{colim} Y_i \cong& (X_1 \times ... X_{n-1}) \times (X_n \times \mathrm{colim} Y_i) \end{align*}
