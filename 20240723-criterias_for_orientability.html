criterias for orientability

List

sufficient

  • manifold with fundamental group without index 2 subgroup as orientable manifold
  • unique orientation for a normal covering map with orientation preserving deck transformations
  • orientable manifold, integers and Fp orientable
  • orientations of a closed and connected manifold

necessary

  • poincare dualities
  • closed orientable manifold and n-1 homology as torsion free

other

  • n-1 homology of a closed non orientable manifold
