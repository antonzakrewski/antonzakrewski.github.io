map out of the chain cone

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes, $A_*, B_*$ be chain complexes and

\begin{align*} f: A_* \rightarrow B_* \end{align*}

a chain map

Then for the cone $C(f)$ the map

\begin{align*} \varphi_*: C(f) \rightarrow& B_*[1] \end{align*}

defined on each component by

\begin{align*} (d^B + \varphi): B_n \oplus A_{n-1} \rightarrow B_{n-1} \\ \end{align*}

is a chain map

Proof
