colimit preserving functor out of a presentable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor out of a presentable category $\mathcal{C}$ into a locally small category $\mathcal{D}$. TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ admits a right adjoint
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ preserves colimits
