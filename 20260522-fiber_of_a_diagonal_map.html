fiber of a diagonal morphism

Proposition

Proof

observe that the diagonal morphism is split mono with $\mathrm{pr}_1$ as one sided inverse. using fiber of a split mono this shows that $\mathrm{fib}(\Delta)$ is canonically equivalent to $\Omega \mathrm{fib}(\mathrm{pr}_1)$. But fiber of a projection shows that $\mathrm{fib}(\mathrm{pr}_1) \simeq X$ or in other words $\mathrm{fib}(\Delta) \simeq\Omega \mathrm{fib}(\mathrm{pr}_1)$

Remark

also works for $\prod_{i \in I} X$
