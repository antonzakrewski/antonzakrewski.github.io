eilenberg-zilber transformation

Definition

\begin{align*} \mathrm{EZ}: C^{\mathrm{sing}}(\pi_1(-)) \times C^{\mathrm{sing}}(\pi_2(-)) \Rightarrow C^{\mathrm{sing}}(-) \end{align*}

as a natural transformation corresponding to $1 \in \mathbb{Z}$ via equivalence class of chain maps from tensored augmented singular chain complexes to the product

Note that this is only defined up to chain homotopy
