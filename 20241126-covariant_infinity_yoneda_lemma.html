covariant infinity yoneda lemma

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Spc}$ be an infinity functor to Anima / Spaces Assume furthermore that $x \in \mathcal{C}_0$ is an object.

Then the canonical map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Spc})}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,-),\mathcal{F}) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(x) \end{align*}

is an joyal equivalence

Proof

using <straightening - unstraightening>

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}})}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,-), \mathcal{F}) \cong& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty/\mathcal{C}}}(\mathcal{C}_x \rightarrow \mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Un}(f)) \\ \cong& \mathrm{Fun}_{q}(\mathcal{C}_{x/}, \mathrm{Un}(f)) \\ \cong& \mathrm{Un}(f^{-1})(x) \\ \cong& f(x) \end{align*}
