functor category of a balanced, finitely bicomplete category as balanced category

proposition

let $\mathcal{c}$ be a balanced category admitting pullbacks and pushouts and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(I,\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

the functor category

then $\mathrm{fun}(i,\mathcal{c})$ is also balanced

proof

see:

  • natural isomorphism and isomorphism on the objects
  • natural transformation and morphisms as monomorphism
  • natural transformation and morphisms as epimorphism

showing that for a bimorphism $\eta \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathrm{Fun}(I,\mathcal{C}))$ each component $\eta(X)$ is a bimorphism
