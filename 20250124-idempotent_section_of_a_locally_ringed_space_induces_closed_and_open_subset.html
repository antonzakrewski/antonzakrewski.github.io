idempotent section of a locally ringed space induces closed and open subset

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{O}_X$ be a locally ringed space, $e \in \mathcal{O}_{X}(X)$ an idempotent section. Then the

\begin{align*} X_e \coprod X_{1 - e} = X \end{align*}

is a disjoint union of open subsets.

Proof

disjoint

Let $x \in X_{1 - e}$. Then $(1 - e)(x) \neq 0$. But $(1 - e)(x)$ is still idempotent in a field, hence $(1 - e)(x) = 1$ or $e(x) = 0$ in the residue field Hence $x \not\in X_{e}$

cover

Let $x \not\in X_{1 - e}$, hence $(1 - e)(x) = 0$ in the residue field. Then $1 = e(x)$ showing that $x \in X_e$

open

follows from nonzeros of a section of a locally ringed space as open subset
