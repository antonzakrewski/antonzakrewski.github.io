replete infinity subcategory

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category Then an infinity subcategory $\mathcal{C}'$ is said to be replete, if the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}' \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

is an isofibration

Alternatively

for $c \in \mathcal{C}, c' \in \mathcal{C}'$ and an equivalence $f: c \rightarrow c'$ it follows that $c' \in \mathcal{C}$
