prespectrafication

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category. Then the prespectrafication is defined as infinity left adjoint to the evaluation functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ev}_{(0,0)}: \mathrm{PSp}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

Proof

using infinity left adjoint functor theorem for presentable categories we need to show that the evaluation preserves limits and is accessible This follows from:

  • category of prespectrum objects closed under sufficiently filtered colimits
  • category of prespectrum objects closed under limits in functor category
