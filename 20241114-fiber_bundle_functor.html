fiber bundle functor

Definition

The fibre bundle functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Top}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat} \\ B \mapsto& \mathrm{Fib}(B) \\ (f: B \rightarrow B') \mapsto& f^*(-): \mathrm{Fib}(B') \rightarrow \mathrm{Fib}(B) \end{align*}

where $f^*(-)$ is the base change functor
