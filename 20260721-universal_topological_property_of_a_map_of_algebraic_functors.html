universal topological property of a map of algebraic functors

Definition

Let $f: Y \rightarrow X$ be a map of algebraic functors and $P$ a property in $\mathrm{Ar}(\mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}, \mathrm{Set}))$. Then $f$ is said to be universally $P$, if for every $X' \rightarrow X$, the base change $Y \times_{X} X' \rightarrow X'$ has property $P$.
