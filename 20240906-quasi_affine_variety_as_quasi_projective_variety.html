quasi-affine variety as quasi-projective variety

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ a quasi-affine variety. Then $X$ is a quasi-projective variety.

Proof

By assumption there exist an open map

\begin{align*} \iota: X \hookrightarrow \mathbb{A}_K^n \end{align*}

Furthermore by affine covering of a projective space there exists an open map

\begin{align*} \mathbb{A}_K^n \hookrightarrow \mathbb{P}_K^n \end{align*}

Thus

\begin{align*} \iota: X \hookrightarrow \mathbb{P}_K^n \end{align*}

is also an open map
