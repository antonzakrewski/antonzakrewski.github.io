right adjoint and absolute left kan extension

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ admits a right adjoint
  2. the identity functor $\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{C}}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ admits an absolute left kan extension along $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$.
  3. the identity functor $\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{C}}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ admits a left kan extension along $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ and this is preserved under postcomposition with $\mathcal{F}$, i.e.
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Lan}_{\mathcal{F}}(\mathcal{F}) = \mathcal{F}\mathrm{Lan}_{\mathcal{F}}(\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{C}}) \end{align*}

Let $\mathcal{G} \coloneqq \mathrm{Lan}_{\mathcal{F}}(\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{C}})$. Then we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{C})}(\mathrm{id},\mathcal{F}^*(-)) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C})}(\mathrm{Lan}_{\mathcal{F}}(\mathrm{id}), -) \\ \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})}(\mathcal{F},\mathcal{F}^*(-)) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C})}(\mathrm{Lan}_{\mathcal{F}}(\mathcal{F}), -) \end{align*}

which specifies to

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{C})}(\mathrm{id},\mathcal{F}^*(-)) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C})}(\mathcal{G}, -) \\ \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})}(\mathcal{F},\mathcal{F}^*(-)) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C})}(\mathcal{F}\mathcal{G}, -) \end{align*}

and then to

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{C})}(\mathrm{id},\mathcal{GF}) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C})}(\mathcal{G}, \mathcal{G}) \\ \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})}(\mathcal{F},\mathcal{F}) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C})}(\mathcal{F}\mathcal{G}, \mathrm{id}) \end{align*}
