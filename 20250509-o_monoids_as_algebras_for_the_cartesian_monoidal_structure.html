o-monoids as algebras for the cartesian monoidal structure

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category with finite products and $\mathcal{O}$ a cartesian pattern. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{C}) \simeq \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}/\mathrm{Fin}_*}(\mathcal{C}^{\times}) \end{align*}

where $\mathcal{C}^{\times}$ is the cartesian monoidal structure

Proof

define a cocartesian fibration

\begin{align*} \overline{\mathcal{C}^{\times}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fin}_* \end{align*}

given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}_{\mathrm{Fin}_*}(K, \overline{\mathcal{C}^{\times}}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}(K \times_{\mathrm{Fin}_*} \mathrm{Arr}_{\mathrm{int}}(\mathrm{Fin}_*),\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

Define

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}^{\times} \subseteq \overline{\mathcal{C}^{\times}} \end{align*}

as the full subactegory such that objects over $<n>$ are

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Arr}_{\mathrm{int}}(\mathrm{Fin}_*) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(\psi) \rightarrow \prod \mathcal{F}(p_i,\psi) \end{align*}

for all $\varphi: <n> \rightarrow <m>$ inert.

i.e. "full subcategory such that segal condition holds)

then it is a fact

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}^{\times} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fin}_* \end{align*}

is a symmetric monoidal infinity category.

Now let $\mathcal{O}$ be a cartesian pattern, then $\mathcal{O} \times_{\mathrm{Fin}_*} \mathrm{Arr}_{\mathrm{int}}(\mathrm{Fin}_*)$ can be given hte structure of a cartesian pattern and you prove

  1. $\mathrm{Mon}{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{C}) \simeq \mathrm{Mon}{\mathcal{O} \times_{\mathrm{Fin}*} \mathrm{Arr}{\mathrm{int}}(\mathrm{Fin}_*)}(\mathcal{C})
  2. the equivalence of the definition of \overline{\mathcal{C}^{\times}$ restricts to the desired equivalence
