rank of a finitely generated projective module

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{p} \subseteq A$ a prime ideal and $k = A_{\mathfrak{p}}/\mathfrak{p} A_{\mathfrak{p}}$ the residue field at $\mathfrak{p}$. Then the rank of a finitely generated projective module $P$ is defined as dimension

\begin{align*} \mathrm{rk}_{\mathfrak{p}}(P)= \mathrm{dim}_{k}(k \otimes_{A} P) \end{align*}

Proof
