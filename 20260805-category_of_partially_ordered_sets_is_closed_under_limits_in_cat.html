category of partially ordered sets is closed under limits in Cat

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Pos} \subseteq \mathrm{Cat}$ be the category Cat. then $\mathrm{Pos}$ is closed under limits

Proof

observe that the inclusion is fully faithful and a fully faithful functor reflects limits. hence it suffices to show that the limit of partially ordered sets in $\mathrm{Cat}$ - partially ordered set as category - is again a partially ordered set. This follows from Hom anima in the limit of categories, which is given by a product of $*$ and $\emptyset$, hence it is again $*$ or $\emptyset$.
