heart of a t-structure

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category with a t-structure $(\mathcal{C}_{\geq 0}, \mathcal{C}_{\leq 0}$ Then the heart $\mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}$ is defined as full infinity subcategory containing precisely

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit} \coloneqq \mathcal{C}_{\geq 0} \cap \mathcal{C}_{\leq 0} \end{align*}
