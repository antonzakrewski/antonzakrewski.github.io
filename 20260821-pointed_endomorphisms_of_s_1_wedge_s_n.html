pointed Endomorphisms of S^1 wedge S^n

Proposition

Let $n \geq 2$, then there exists a bijection

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(S^1 \vee S^n, S^1 \vee S^n) \cong \mathbb{Z} \times (\bigoplus_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(S^1 \vee S^n, S^1 \vee S^n) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(S^1, S^1 \vee S^n) \times \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(S^n, S^1 \vee S^n) \end{align*}

where we may compute

  1. $\pi_1(S^1 \vee S^n) \cong \mathbb{Z}$
  2. $\pi_n(S^1 \vee S^n) \cong \pi_n(\bigvee S^n) \cong \bigoplus_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z}$ (cf. low homotopy group of S^n wedge S^1).
