contravariant ab-hom functor dualizes colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{A})$ an object and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}^{\mathrm{Ab}}(-,A) \end{align*}

the ab-hom functor

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}^{\mathrm{Ab}}_{\mathcal{A}}( \mathrm{colim}(-),A) \cong \mathrm{lim} \mathrm{Hom}^{\mathrm{Ab}}_{\mathcal{A}}(-,A) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from

  • contravariant hom-functor dualizes small colimits to limits
  • forgetful functor from Ab to Set preserves limits_
  • forgetful functor from Ab to Set as conservative functor
  • conservative functor reflects preserved limits
