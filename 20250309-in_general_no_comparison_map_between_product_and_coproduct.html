in general no comparison map between product and coproduct

Proposition

In general there does not exist a comparison map

\begin{align*} X \coprod Y \rightarrow X \times Y \end{align*}

or

\begin{align*} X \times Y \rightarrow X \coprod Y \end{align*}

Proof

consider category set and (the opposite category for the second case), $X = \emptyset$ and $Y = *$
