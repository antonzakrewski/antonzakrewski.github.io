infinity coslice category of a kan complex

Proposition

Let $K$ be a Kan complex, $x \in K$ an object. Then the infinity coslice category

\begin{align*} K_{x/} \end{align*}

is also kan

Proof

follows from

and

  1. right slice functor from twisted arrows and monomorphisms plus inner fibrations

which gives a left fibration

\begin{align*} X_{x/} \rightarrow X \end{align*}

which composed with $X \rightarrow *$ stays a left fibration. Thus we are done (cf. kan complex and terminal morphism as kan fibration)
