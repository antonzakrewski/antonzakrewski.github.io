Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, $\mathrm{Fun}([1], \mathcal{A})$ the arrow category and $\mathrm{coker}(-)$ the cokernel functor Then $\mathrm{coker}(-)$ is left exact functor
