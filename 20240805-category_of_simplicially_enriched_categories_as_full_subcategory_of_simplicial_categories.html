category of simplicially enriched categories as full subcategory of simplicial categories

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Cat}_{\Delta}$ be the category of simplicially enriched categories. Then there exists a canonical fully faithful functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cat}_{\Delta} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Cat}) \end{align*}

Proof
