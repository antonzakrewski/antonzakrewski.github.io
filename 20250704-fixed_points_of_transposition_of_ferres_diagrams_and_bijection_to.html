fixed points of transposition of ferres diagrams and bijection to another set

Proposition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $\mathcal{P}_n$ be the set of unordered partitions of a number with arbitrary length. Then there exist a bijection

\begin{align*} Q_n \coloneqq& \{p \in \mathcal{P}_n : p^* = p\} \\ \mathcal{D}_n \coloneqq& \{(x_1,...,x_k) \in \mathcal{P}_n : \mathrm{ all } x_i \mathrm{ different and odd}\} \end{align*}

see:

  • transposition of ferres diagram as bijection of unordered partitions

Proof

Let $p$ be a ferres diagram. Then consider the inductively defined map

\begin{align*} p \mapsto& (\mathrm{top row} + \mathrm{left column}, p \setminus \{\mathrm{top row} \cup \mathrm{left column}\} \end{align*}

which adds the top row and left column

It induces a bijection
