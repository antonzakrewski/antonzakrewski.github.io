sum of two bounded subsets

Proposition

Let $X,Y \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ be two bounded subsets, then so is the sum $X + Y$ resp. the difference $X - Y$

Proof

Take $c_X,c_Y$ as upper bounds. Then $c_X + c_Y$ is an upper bound as for

\begin{align*} \vert x + y \vert \leq \vert x \vert + \vert y \vert \leq c_X + c_Y \end{align*}

difference

follows from $Y' \coloneqq - Y$.
