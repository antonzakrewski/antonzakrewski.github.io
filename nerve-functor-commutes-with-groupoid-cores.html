nerve functor commutes with groupoid cores

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $N$ the nerve functor and $()^{\cong}$ the infinity groupoid core functor resp. the groupoid core functor. Then there exists a canonical isomorphism

\begin{align*} N(\mathcal{C}^{\cong}) \cong N(\mathcal{C})^{\cong} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • explicit description of a nerve of a category
  • explicit description of an infinity groupoid core

and

  • equivalence in a nerve and isomorphism
