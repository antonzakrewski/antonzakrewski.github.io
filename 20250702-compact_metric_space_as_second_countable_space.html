Proposition

Any compact metrizable space is secondcountable

Proof

Let $(M,d)$ be a metric space Then for $\frac{1}{n}$ choose via the self indexing trick the open cover

\begin{align*} \cup_{m \in M} B_{m}(\frac{1}{n}) \end{align*}

and choose by compactness finitely many points $m \in M$ such that $b_m(\frac{1}{n})$ covers $M$. Then as $n \rightarrow \infty$ we get countably many points and therefore take the union of countable neighbourhood basis (as $M$ is erste Abzählbarkeitsaxiom in metrischen Räumen)
