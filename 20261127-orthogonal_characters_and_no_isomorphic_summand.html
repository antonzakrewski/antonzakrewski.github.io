orthogonal characters and no isomorphic summand

Proposition

Let $V,W$ be finite dimensional representations of a finite group $G$ over the $\mathbb{C}$ . TFAE:

  1. $\langle V, W\rangle = 0$
  2. $V$ and $W$ dont contain any isomorphism, nonzero summands.

Proof

observe that if two representations $V,W$ contain isomorphic, nonzero summands $V_0$, then $\langle V, W\rangle = \langle … \rangle + \langle V_0,V_0\rangle \geq \langle V_0,V_0\rangle > 0$. If they dont, then use maschke to decompose into simples and use orthogonality of simple characters.
