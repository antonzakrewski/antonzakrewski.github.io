explicit description of a nerve of a category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $N(\mathcal{C})$ the nerve. Then a \(n\)-simplex is precisely a sequence of \(n\)-composable morphisms.

geometrically:

  • the spine is the sequence of morphisms
  • each simplex is the composition
  • witnessing the equality of the composition, e.g. for $n = 2q
