principal Aut Bundle

Definition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a Fibre bundle with local trivialization (fibre bundle) $U_i$ and typical fibre $F$, where $F$ is locally regular compact and $\mathrm{Aut}(F)$ the topological Aut group. Then there exists a principal \(\mathrm{Aut}(F)\)-bundle induced by

\begin{align*} U_{i,j} \rightarrow \mathrm{Aut}(F) \end{align*}

where the map corresponds to

\begin{align*} U_{i,j} \times F \rightarrow F \end{align*}
