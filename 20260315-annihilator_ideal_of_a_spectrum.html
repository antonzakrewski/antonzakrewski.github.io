annihilator ideal of a spectrum

Definition

Let $E$ be a spectrum, $A$ a finite spectrum and $x \in E_*A = \pi_*(E \otimes A)$ an \(E\)-homology class. Then the annihilator ideal is defined

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ann}_{A}(x) \coloneqq \{f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(A,B) \vert B \in \mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{fin}}, x \in \mathrm{ker}(E_*(f))\} \end{align*}
