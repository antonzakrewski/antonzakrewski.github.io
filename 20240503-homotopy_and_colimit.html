homotopy out of a colimit

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Top}$ be category Top, $I: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathrm{Top}$ a diagram, $\mathrm{colim}(I)$ the colimit and $Y$ another topological space. Suppose for $X_i$ there exist continuous maps (aka homotopies)

\begin{align*} H_i: X_i \times [0,1] \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

such that

commutes for each $t \in [0,1]$.

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}(I) \times [0,1] \rightarrow& Y \\ ([x_i],t) \mapsto& H_i(i,t) \end{align*}

is a welldefined continuous map

Proof

follows from:

  • product functor of topological spaces for a locally compact space as left adjoint
  • left adjoint preserves colimits
