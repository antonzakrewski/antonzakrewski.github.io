fully faithful, essentially surjective functor preserves epimorphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a fully faithful functor Then $\mathcal{F}$ preserves epimorphisms
