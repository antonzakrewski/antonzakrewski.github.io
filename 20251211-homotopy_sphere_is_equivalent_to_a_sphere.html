anima with homotopy groups of a sphere is in general not equivalent to a sphere

Proposition

Let $X$ be an Anima such that its homotopy groups are abstractly isomorphic to $S^n$ for $n \geq 1$ Then $X \simeq S^n$ are in general not equivalent

Proof

a) specific counterxample for $n = 2$

Consider

  1. $S^2$
  2. $S^3 \times \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}$

Then both have abstractly the same homotopy groups (cf. hopf fibration from S^3,

But neither spaces are homotopy equivalent: this follows e.g. from the kunneth formula for singular homology & singular homology of a complex projective space.

b) general example

consider the space

\begin{align*} \prod K(\pi_i(S^n),i) \end{align*}

It has by construction the desired homotopy groups (cf. homotopy group functor preserves products).

Furthermore each $K(\pi_1(S^n),i)$ is a retract of $\prod K(\pi_i(S^n),i)$. Since $\pi_3(S^2) = \mathbb{Z}, \pi_4(S^3) = \mathbb{Z}/4 \mathbb{Z}, \pi_5(S^4) = \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} = \pi_2(\mathbb{S})$ we conclude that it has a nonzero eilenberg mac-lane space as retract with homotopy group in $n+1$. But using Hurewicz theorem this shows that $H_{n+1}(\prod K(\pi_i(S^n),i))$ contains $\pi_{n+1}(S^n)$ as retract, contradiction to $H_*(S^n)$ being bounded by $n$
