left exact functor commutes with n-truncation

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be presentable infinity categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a infinity left exact functor. Let $\tau_{\leq n}^{\mathcal{C}}, \tau_{\leq n}^{\mathcal{D}}$ denote the respective n-truncation functors

Then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F} \circ \tau_{\leq n}^{\mathcal{C}} \simeq \tau_{\leq n}^{\mathcal{D}} \circ \mathcal{F} \end{align*}

are equivalent

Proof
