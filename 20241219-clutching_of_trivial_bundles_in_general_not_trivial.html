clutching of trivial bundles in general not trivial

Proposition

Proof

Consider $X = S^2$, $A,B$ as northern and southern hemisphere. Let $E_1 = A \times \mathbb{C}, E_2 = B \times \mathbb{C}$ be the trivial bundles.

Consider

\begin{align*} g: (A \cap B) \times \mathbb{C} \rightarrow& (A \cap B) \times \mathbb{C} \\ (z,w) \mapsto (z, zw) \end{align*}

where $A \cap B$ is identified with $S^1 \subseteq \mathbb{C}$

Then the clutching construction is nontrivial
