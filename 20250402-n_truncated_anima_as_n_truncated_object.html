n-truncated anima as n-truncated object

Proposition

Let $X$ be an anima

TFAE:

  1. $X$ is an n-truncated anima
  2. $X$ is n-truncated object in Anima

Proof

$X = \emptyset$

note that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(Y,\emptyset) = \begin{cases} \emptyset & \mbox{if } Y \neq \emptyset \\ * & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

$X \neq \emptyset$

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $Y$ be another anima.

$Y = \emptyset$

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(\emptyset,X) \simeq * \end{align*}

is as truncated as it gets

$Y \neq \emptyset$

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(Y,X) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(\mathrm{colim}_Y *,X) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{lim}_Y \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(*,X) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{lim}_Y X \\ \end{align*}

where n-truncated anima closed under limits

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(*,X) \simeq X \end{align*}
