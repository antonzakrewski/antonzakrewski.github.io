idempotent decomposition

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $e \in A$

TFAE:

  1. $e$ is idempotent
  2. there exists an ring-isomorphism
\begin{align*} R \cong eR \times (1-e)R 1 \mapsto& (e,0) \end{align*}

where $eR, (1-e)R$ are proper rings

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from: isomorphismus von Ringen für ein Idempotentes Element

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from $(1,0)^2 = (1,0)$ and $(1,0) = e$
