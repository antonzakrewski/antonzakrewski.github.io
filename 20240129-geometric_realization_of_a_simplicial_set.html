explicit description of the geometric realization functor

Definition

The geometric realization $\vert - \vert$ is given as functor

\begin{align*} \vert - \vert: \mathrm{sSet} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Top} \\ X \mapsto& (\coprod_{n \in \Delta} X_n \times \Delta^n_{\mathrm{top}})/((f^*(x),t) \cong (x, f_*(t))) \end{align*}

where $f \in \Delta$ and

  1. $f^*: X_m \rightarrow X_n$
  2. $f_*: \Delta^n_{\mathrm{top}} \rightarrow \Delta^m_{\mathrm{top}}$
