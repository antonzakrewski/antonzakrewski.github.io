pullback and covering map

Proposition

Let $B,E,E'$ be topological spaces $p: E \rightarrow B$ a covering map and $f: E' \rightarrow B$ a continuous map

Then for pullback

$p': E \times_B E' \rightarrow E'$ is also a covering map

Proof

By assumption of $p$ being a covering map, there exists a an open cover $U_i$ of $B$. Then $f^{-1}[U_i]$ is an open covering of $E'$ (cf. open covering under continuous preimage).

Furthermore, by construction of a pullback (cf. limit in Top and underlying Set) we have the subspace topology on the product topology $E \times E'$ satisfying

\begin{align*} E \times_{B} E' = \{(e,e') \in E \times E' \vert f(e') = p(e) \} \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} p'^{-1}[f^{-1}[U_i]] = \{(e,e') \in E \times E' \vert f(e') = p(e) \in U_i\} \end{align*}

Since $U_i \times F_i \cong p^{-1}[U_i]$, we get for each exactly $(x,e')$ $\mathcal{F}_i$ many elements $e$ with $f(x) = p(e)$. Therefore, we get cannonically

\begin{align*} p'^{-1}[f^{-1}[U_i]] \cong (F_i \times f[f^{-1}[U_i]]) \times_B f^{-1}[U_i]) \end{align*}

since for each $u \in f^{-1}[U_i]$, $f(u)$ is unique.

Furthermore, we have for the topology of $p^{-1}[f[f^{-1}[U_i]]] \subseteq p^{-1}[U_i] \subseteq B$

\begin{align*} p^{-1}[f[f^{-1}[U_i]]] \cong (F_i \times f[f^{-1}[U_i]]) \end{align*}

Hence we get

\begin{align*} F_i \times f^{-1}[U_i]) = p'^{-1}[f^{-1}[U_i]] \end{align*}
