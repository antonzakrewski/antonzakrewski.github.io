functor dualizing finite products to coproducts

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a contravariant functor. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be dualizing finite product to finite coproducts if the covariant version

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}': \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

preserves finite coproducts
