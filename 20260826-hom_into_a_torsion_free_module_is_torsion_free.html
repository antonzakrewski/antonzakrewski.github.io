Hom into a torsion free module is torsion free

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $M$ a torsion-free Module and $N$ another \(R\)-module . Then $\mathrm{Hom}_{R}(N,M)$ is also torsion free.

Proof

follows from the embedding

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(G,M) \rightarrow \prod_{g \in G} M \end{align*}

submodule of a torsion free module as torsion free module
