category of topological vector bundles over a space

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space, $\mathbb{K} \in \{\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C} \}$ Then the category of topological vector bundles $\mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb{K}}(B)$ is defined as category containing

  1. topological vector bundles as objects
  2. morphism of topological vector bundles as morphisms
