category of quasi-coherent modules is additive

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor Then the category of quasi-coherent modules is additive

Proof

follows as

  1. PrL additive is modules over anima valued commutative groups
  2. forgetful functor from PrL to Cat preserves limits
  3. the functor $\mathrm{Mod}_*: \mathrm{CRing}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{op}}$ lifts to $\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\mathrm{add}}$
