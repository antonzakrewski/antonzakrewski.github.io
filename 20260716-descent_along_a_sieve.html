descent along a sieve

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{F} \in \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ a presheaf and $R$ a sieve on $X \in \mathcal{C}$. We say that $\mathcal{F}$ satisfies descent along $R$ if the inclusion $R \hookrightarrow よ(X)$ induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(X) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(よ(X), \mathcal{F}) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(R,\mathcal{F}) \end{align*}
