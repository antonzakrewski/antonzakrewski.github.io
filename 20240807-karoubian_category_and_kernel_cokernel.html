karoubian category and kernel / cokernel

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{K}$ be an Ab-enriched category and $f: A \rightarrow A$ an idempotent morphism

TFAE:

  1. $f,1 - f$ admit a kernel
  2. $f,1 - f$ admit a cokernel
