derived p-complete abelian groups form a serre class

Proposition

The class of derived p-complete abelian groups is a serre class.

Proof

Recall that the category of p-complete spectra is a (full!) stable subcategory So if we have a fiber sequence $X \rightarrow Y \rightarrow Z$ where two terms are p-complete spectra, then so is the third. In particular, this holds for fiber sequences in the heart - namely short exact sequences (cf. ) .
