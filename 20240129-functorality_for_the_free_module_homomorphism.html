functorality for the free module homomorphism on the determinant

Proposition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\mathrm{CRing}$ the category CRing. Then there exists a functor on the comma category

\begin{align*} f: \mathrm{CRing} \rightarrow& \mathcal{F} \downarrow \mathcal{G} A \mapsto& (\det: A^{n \times n} \rightarrow A) \\ (f: A \rightarrow B) \mapsto& (f: A^{n \times n} \rightarrow B^{n \times n}) \end{align*}

i.e. this diagram

commutes naturally

Proof

Follows from

\begin{align*} \det(f^{n \times n} A) =& \sum_{\tau \in S_n} \mathrm{sgn}(\tau) \prod_{i = 1}^n f(\alpha_{i, \tau(i)}) \\ =& f(\sum_{\tau \in S_n} \mathrm{sgn}(\tau) \prod_{i = 1}^n \alpha_{i, \tau(i)}) \\ =& f(\det(A)) \end{align*}
