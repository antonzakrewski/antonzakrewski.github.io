p-cartesian morphism by homotopy cartesian diagrams

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{E}, \mathcal{C}$ be infinity categories, $p: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ an inner fibration and $f: \Delta^1 \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ a morphism, such that for each $z \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{E})$ the diagram

is a homotopy-cartesian diagram

Then $f$ is p-cartesian

Proof
