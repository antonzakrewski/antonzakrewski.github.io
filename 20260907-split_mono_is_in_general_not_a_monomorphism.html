split mono is in general not a monomorphism

Proposition

A split mono in an $(\infty,1)$ category is in general not a monomorphism

Proof

e.g. let $\mathcal{C}$ be the category of Anima then monomorphisms in Anima are precisely the inclusions of connected components.

The relative diagonal is split mono, so take for example $S^1 \rightarrow S^1 \times_{*} S^1 \simeq S^1 \times S^1$. It is a split mono, but not the inclusion of connceted components, since $S^1 \not\simeq S^1 \times S^1$.
