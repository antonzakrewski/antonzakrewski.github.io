hom of a serre quotient is a filtered colimit

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{B} \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ be a serre subcategory. Then the hom-set is a filtered colimit

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}/\mathcal{B}}(a,b) = \mathrm{colim}_{a'\subseteq a, b' \subseteq b, a/a', b' \in \mathcal{B}} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(a',b') \end{align*}

in particular the dwyer-kan localization is still a 1-category
