singular cohomology of a sphere

Proposition

The singular cohomology of the n-sphere is given by

\begin{align*} H^m(S^n) =& \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } m = 0 \\ \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } n = m \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

for $n \geq 1$ and

\begin{align*} H^m(S^0) =& \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} \oplus \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } m = 0 \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

e.g. follows from the universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology
