integral domain as intersection of localizations at maximal ideals

Proposition

Let \(R\) be an Integral domain Then

\begin{align*} R \cong \bigcap R_{\mathfrak{m}} \subseteq Q(R) \end{align*}

are canonically isomorphic with the inclusion $R \hookrightarrow Q(R)$

Proof

Note that $R \hookrightarrow R_{\mathfrak{m}}$ for each maximal/prime ideal $\mathfrak{m}$.

Let $\frac{q}{r} \in \bigcap R_{\mathfrak{m}}$. w.l.o.g. suppose $r \not\in R^{\times}$, otherwise $\frac{q}{r} = q \cdot r^{-1} \in R$

Then it follows that there exists a maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$ containing $r$ (cf. existence of a maximal ideal for a strict ideal). But then $\frac{q}{r} \in R_{\mathfrak{m}}$ with $r \in R \setminus \mathfrak{m}$, contradiction
