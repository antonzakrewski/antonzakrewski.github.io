radical field extension

Definition

Let $K$ be a field and $L/K$ an algebraic field extension. Then $L/K$ is said to be a radical extension, if there exists a finite tower of fields

\begin{align*} K = K_1 \subseteq K_2 ... \subseteq K_n = L \end{align*}

such that

\begin{align*} K_{i+1} = K_i( \sqrt[r_i]{a_i}) \end{align*}
