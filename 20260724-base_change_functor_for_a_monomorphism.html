base change functor for a monomorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $f: x \rightarrow x'$ be a monomorphism in $\mathcal{C}$. Then the adjunction

\begin{align*} f_*: \mathcal{C}_{/x} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{/x'} \\ f^*: \mathcal{C}_{/x'} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{/x} \end{align*}

is part of a right Bousfield localization, i.e. $f_* \mathcal{C}_{/x} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{/x'}$ is fully faithful
