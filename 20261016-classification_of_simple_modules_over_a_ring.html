classification of simple modules over a ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a ring and $M$ a simple module. Then there exists an isomorphism $A/\mathfrak{m} \simeq M$ for some maximal left ideal $\mathfrak{m}$.

Proof

Let $m \in M$ be nonzero, then consider the map classified by $m$, i.e. $m: A \rightarrow M$. We claim that is is surjective and the kernel is a maximal left ideal: The image is a nonzero submodule of $M$, hence must be $M$ itself. Furthermoroe, let $\mathfrak{m}$ be a maximal ideal containing the kernel. Then the image of the $\mathfrak{m}$ in the map $A \rightarrow A/\mathrm{ker} \simeq M$ is not surjective as it is a maximal ideal but this implies that the map is the zero map, i.e. $\mathfrak{m} = \mathrm{ker}$.
