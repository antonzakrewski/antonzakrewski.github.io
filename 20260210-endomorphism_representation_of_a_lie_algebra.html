endomorphism representation of a lie algebra

Proposition

Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a Lie-Algebra Then the endomorphism representation is defined as representation

\begin{align*} \rho: \mathfrak{g} \rightarrow \mathrm{End}(\mathfrak{g}) A \mapsto (X \mapsto [A,X]) \end{align*}
