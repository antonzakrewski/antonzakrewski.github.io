upper bound of edges for a bipartite graph

Proposition

Let $G = (V,E)$ be a bipartite graph with $\vert V \vert = n$. Then $\vert E \vert \leq \mathrm{floor}(\frac{n^2}{4})$.

Proof

Note that by definition $G$ is a subgraph of the complete bipartite graph $K_{l,j}$ for $l + j = n$. In particular $\vert E_{G} \vert \leq \vert E_{K_{l,j}} \vert$.

But $E_{K_{l,j}} = l \cdot j$ which is maximal for $l = \mathrm{floor}(\frac{n}{2}), j = \mathrm{ceil}(\frac{n}{2})$. Hence

\begin{align*} \vert E \vert \leq \mathrm{floor}(\frac{n}{2}) \cdot \mathrm{ceil}(\frac{n}{2}) = \mathrm{floor}(\frac{n^2}{4})$ \end{align*}
