singular homological degree of a suspension on the sphere

Proposition

Let $S^n$ be the n-sphere, $f: S^n \rightarrow S^n$ a continuous map and $\Sigma$ the suspension functor. Then it holds for the singular homological degree

\begin{align*} \mathrm{deg}(\Sigma f) =& \mathrm{deg}(f) \end{align*}
