pseudo n-lipschitz maps from closed intervall is a closed subset

Proposition

Let $A_n = \{f \in C([0,1]) \vert \exists x \in [0,1], \forall y \in [0,1], \vert f(x) - f(y) \vert \leq L \vert x - y \vert\}$ Then $A_n$ is a closed subset.

Proof

Let $f_n$ be a convergent sequence with $f_n \in A_n$, then it suffices to show that the limit point $f$ lies in $A_n$. For each $f_n$, choose an $x_n$ such that $f$ is L-lipschitz continuous at $x_n$. Then $x_n$ is a sequence in $[0,1]$ and w.l.o.g. we may assume that $x_n \rightarrow x$ after using Satz von Bolzano-Weierstraß and removing $f_n$'s.

Let $y$ in $[0,1]$ be arbitrary, then we need to show $\vert f(x) - f(y) \vert \leq L \cdot \vert x - y \vert$ Then the triangle inequality shows

\begin{align*} \vert f(x) - f(y) \vert \leq& \vert f(x) - f_n(x) \vert + \vert f_n(x) - f_n(x_n) \vert + \vert f_n(x_n) - f_n(y) \vert + \vert f_n(y) - f(y) \vert \end{align*}

and passing to limits shows

\begin{align*} \vert f(x) - f(y) \vert \leq& \mathrm{lim}_{n} (\vert f(x) - f_n(x) \vert + \vert f_n(x) - f_n(x_n) \vert + \vert f_n(x_n) - f_n(y) \vert + \vert f_n(y) - f(y) \vert ) \\ =& L \vert f(x) - f(y) \vert \end{align*}
