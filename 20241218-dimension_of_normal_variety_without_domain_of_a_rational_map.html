dimension of normal variety without domain of a rational map

Proposition

Let $X$ be a normal quasi-projective variety, $Y$ a projective variety over an algebraically closed field. Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a rational map.

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(X \setminus \mathrm{def}(f)) \leq \mathrm{dim}(X) - 2 \end{align*}
