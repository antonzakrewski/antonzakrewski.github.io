amount of trees containing a forest

Proposition

Let $n,n_1,...,n_k \in \mathbb{N}$ and $F$ a forest with $k$ many components $F_1,...,F_k$, where $F_i$ contains $n_i$ many vertices.

Then there exists

\begin{align*} n^{k-2} \cdot \prod_{1\leq i \leq k} n_i \end{align*}

many trees which contain precisely $F$ as subgraph

Proof
