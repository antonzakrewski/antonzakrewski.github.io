PBW-basis theorem for the q-weyl algebra

Proposition

A basis for the q-Weyl algebra is given by $x^i y^i$ for $i,j \in \mathbb{Z}$.

Proof

The span argument is similar to PBW-basis theorem for the weyl algebra.

so it remains to show linear independence: Here we can consider the representation $E = k[a,a^{-1}]{t,t^{-1}]$ with $xf = tf$ and $yf(t) = a f(qt)$. Then a similar computation shows that this is well-defined and seperates $x^i y^j$.
