homotopy-cartesian diagram

Definition

A diagram of infinity categories and infinity functors

is said to be a homotopy-cartesian diagram, if

  1. $p$ is an isofibration
  2. the induced map
\begin{align*} \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \times_{\mathcal{C}'} \mathcal{E}' \end{align*}

is a joyal equivalence

alternatively:

  • homotopy pullback
  • homotopy pullback and single fibration
  • right proper model category
