covering of a compact space with finite fibres and maximal number of points in a fibre

Proposition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a covering map of a compact topological space, where each fibre $p^{-1}[b]$ is finite. Then there exists an $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with

\begin{align*} \vert p^{-1}[b] \vert \leq n \end{align*}

for all $b \in B$

Proof

Choose a trivializing cover $U_i$ of $B$. By compactness of $B$ we may assume, that $U_i$ is finite. Then it follows, that there exists a maximal $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $\vert p^{-1}[b] \vert = n$, as for $b,b' \in U_i$ it holds, that

\begin{align*} \vert p^{-1}[b] \vert = j \end{align*}

for $p^{-1}[U_i] \cong \coprod_{i = 1}^j U_i^j$
