group action and composition with a functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor Let $G$ be a group, $X \in \mathcal{C}$ an object and $\varphi: BG \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a group action on $X$ Then $\mathcal{F}, \varphi$ induce group actions on

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(\varphi): BG \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • group action as functor with suitable domain
  • functor composition
