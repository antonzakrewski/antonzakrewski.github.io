isomorphism creates colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an isomorphism functor Then $\mathcal{F}$ creates limits

Proof

corollary of:

  • isomorphism as adjoint equivalence of categories
  • equivalence creates colimits
