morphism between affine schemes admits cotangent module

Proposition

Let $f: \mathrm{Spec}(A) \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(B)$ be a morphism of affine schemes Then $f$ admits a cotangent module.

Proof

which shows that the functor is represented.
