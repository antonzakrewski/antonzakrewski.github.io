stable and unstable truncation do not coincide in general

Proposition

Let $X$ be an anima and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then the spectra $\tau_{\leq n} \tilde{\mathbb{S}}[X]$ and $\tilde{\mathbb{S}}[\tau_{\leq n}]$ do not coincide in general.

Proof

consider $X \simeq S^1$. Then $S^1$ is $n$ truncated for all $n \geq 2$. But the suspension $\tilde{\mathbb{S}}[S^1] \cong \mathbb{S}[1]$ is not (cf. homotopy group of the sphere spectrum nonzero in infinitely many degrees).
