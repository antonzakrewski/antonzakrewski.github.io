tensor product of the product of the same algebra

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $M$ a R-Algebra, then

\begin{align*} M \otimes_R M \cong M \end{align*}

Proof

Let

\begin{align*} \cdot: M \times& M \rightarrow M \\ (m_1,m_2) \mapsto m_1 \cdot m_2 \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} \varphi: M \rightarrow& M \otimes_R M m \mapsto& (m \otimes 0) \end{align*}

It is welldefined and a homomorphism. Furthermore, by the uniqueness of $\mathrm{id}_{M \otimes_R M}$ as induced linear map, following diagramm commutes

therefore, $\varphi$ is an isomorphism
