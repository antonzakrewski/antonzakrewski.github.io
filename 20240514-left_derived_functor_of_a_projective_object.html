left derived functor of a projective object

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories where $\mathcal{A}$ admits enough projectives Suppose $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ is a right exact functor and $P$ an projective object Then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{LF}_n(P) = 0 \end{align*}

for $n > 1$

Proof

follows from taking the projective resolution
