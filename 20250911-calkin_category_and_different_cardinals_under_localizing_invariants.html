calkin category and different cardinals under localizing invariants

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{T}$ be a category, $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{idem}}_{\mathrm{st} \rightarrow \mathcal{T}$ a localizing invariant. Let $\mathcal{C}\in \mathrm{PrL}_{\mathrm{st}}$ be a dualizable stable category and let $\kappa \leq \lambda$ be uncountable regular cardinals such that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{Y}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq& \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\kappa}) \\ \mathcal{Y}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq& \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\lambda}) \end{align*}

Then the inclusion of calkin categories

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Calk}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Calk}_{\lambda}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

induces an equivalence after applying the localizing invariant $\mathcal{F}$

Proof
