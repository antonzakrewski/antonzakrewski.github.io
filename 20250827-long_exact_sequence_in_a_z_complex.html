long exact sequence for a Z-complex

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category and $X: \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a \(\mathbb{Z}\)-complex. then for $i \leq j \leq k$ there exists a long exact sequence

\begin{align*} \rightarrow \pi_n(X(i,j)) \rightarrow \pi_n(X(i,k)) \rightarrow \pi_n(X(j,k)) \rightarrow \pi_{n-1}(X(i,j)) \end{align*}
