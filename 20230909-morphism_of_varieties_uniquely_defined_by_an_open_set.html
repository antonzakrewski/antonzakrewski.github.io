morphism of varieties uniquely defined by an open set

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $X,Y$ varieties and $\varphi, \psi: X \rightarrow Y$ a morphism of varieties, such that there exists an open set $O \subseteq X$, such that

\begin{align*} \varphi_{\vert U} = \psi_{\vert U} \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} \varphi = \psi \end{align*}

Proof
