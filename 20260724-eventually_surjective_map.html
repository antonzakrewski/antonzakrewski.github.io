eventually surjective map

Definition

Let $f: A \rightarrow B$ be a morphism of N-graded rings. Then $f$ is said to be eventually surjective, if for every $d \geq 1$ and every $b \in B_d$, there exists an $n \geq 1$ such that

\begin{align*} A_{nd} \otimes_{A_0} B_0 \rightarrow B_{nd} \end{align*}

hits $b^n$.

Other words

for every $b \in B_d$, there exists an $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $A_{nd}$ hits $b^n$ up to scaling with some $B_0$.
