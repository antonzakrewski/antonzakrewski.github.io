localization along an nilpotent element

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $M$ an \(R\)-module and $f \in R$ an element.

TFAE:

  1. the localization along $f$ is the zero module
\begin{align*} M_f = 0 \end{align*}
  1. $f$ is nilpotent

Proof

follows from $f^n = 0$ for an $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and localization as zero ring
