Proposition

Let $X$ be an integral scheme with generic point $\eta$. Then for every nonempty affine open subscheme $U \subseteq X$, the map $\mathcal{O}_{X,x} \rightarrow \kappa(\eta)$ exhibits $\kappa(\eta)$ as the fraction field.
