compact colimit in a stable infinity category commutes with limits

Proposition

Let $X$ be a compact anima and $\mathcal{C}$ a stable infinity category. Assume that $\mathcal{C}$ admits $X$ shaped colimits

Then left kan extension

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}(-) = r_!: \mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

preserves arbitrary (!) limits

Proof

it suffices to show that for an arbitrary limit

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_{Y,i} \mathrm{colim}_{X,j} \mathcal{F}_i(j) \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_X \mathrm{lim}_{Y} \mathcal{F}_i(j) \end{align*}

but under currying in cat infinity this follows from looking at $\mathrm{lim}_(-)$ as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

which is right exact (infinity limits commute with infinity limits), hence also left exact (cf. left / right and exact functor between stable infinity categories) and therefore preserves compact colimits
