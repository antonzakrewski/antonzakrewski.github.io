reduction of a network with multiple sinks and targets to a network

Proposition

Let $N = (G, \{s_i\}, \{t_j\},c)$ be a network with multiple sinks and targets and flow. Then we may construct a network $N^*$ with

  1. $G^*$ by adding two vertices, the (future) source $s$ and (future) target $t$
  2. connecting $s$ to $s_i$ and $t_i$ to $t$
  3. setting the capacity of $s_i$ to $s$ resp. $t_i$ to $t$ to $\infty$ or $\sum_{\mathrm{edge} e} c(e)$

Then a (multi-ish)flow from $\{s_i\}$ to $\{t_i\}$ corresponds precisely to a conventional flow by setting the flow between $s_i,s$ and $t_i,t$ to precisely the input / output
