closed subfunctor of a zariski sheaf is a zariski sheaf

Proposition

Let $X: \mathrm{CAlg} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ be a sheaf for the Zariski topology on CAlg and $O \subseteq X$ a closed subfunctor. Then $O$ is also a zariski sheaf.
