precomposition with a monomorphism as kan fibration for a kan complex

Proposition

Let $\iota: A \rightarrow B$ be a cofibration and $X$ a Kan complex Then precomposition for the internal hom

\begin{align*} \iota^*: \mathrm{Hom}(B,X) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}(A,X) \end{align*}

is a kan fibration

Proof

follows from:

  • contravariant inner hom as pullback hom
  • inner hom of a monomorphism and kan fibration
