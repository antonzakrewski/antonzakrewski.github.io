rational Eilenberg-MacLane spectra closed under tensor products

Proposition

Let $V_1,V_2$ be $\mathbb{Q}$ vector spaces, then $V_1 \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} V_2$ is also a $\mathbb{Q}$ vector space, in particular also concentrated in degree $0$.

Proof

$V_1,V_2 = \mathbb{Q}$

then this follows as the rationalized sphere is precisely $\mathbb{Q}$ (cf. rational equivalence between localized sphere spectrum and Q)
