mackey functor for a discrete group

Definition

Let $G$ be a group, $\mathcal{A}$ an additive category then a mackey functor is a product preserving functor

\begin{align*} M: \mathrm{Span}(\mathrm{FinGSet}) \rightarrow \mathcal{A} \end{align*}

from the span category of the category of finite G-sets to $\mathcal{A}$
