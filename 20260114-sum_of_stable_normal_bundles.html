sum of stable normal bundles

Proposition

Let $M,M'$ be (smooth) Manifolds and $\nu_M, \nu_{M'}$ be the (smooth) stable normal bundles. Then the sum

\begin{align*} \nu_{M} \coprod \nu_M: M \coprod M \rightarrow \mathrm{BO} \end{align*}

resp.

\begin{align*} \nu_{M} \coprod \nu_M: M \coprod M \rightarrow \mathrm{BTop} \end{align*}

identifies with the stable normal bundle of $M \coprod M$
