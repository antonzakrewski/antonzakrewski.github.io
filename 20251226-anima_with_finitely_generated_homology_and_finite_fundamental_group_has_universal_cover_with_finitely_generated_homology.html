anima with finitely generated homology and finite pi_1 has universal cover with finitely generated homology

Proposition

Let $X$ be an anima with finitely generated homology and finite fundamental group. Then the universal cover $\tilde{X}$ has finitely generated homology.

Proof

I think running the strongly convergent spectral sequence and serre class for the fiber sequence

\begin{align*} \pi_1(X) \rightarrow \tilde{X} \rightarrow X \end{align*}
