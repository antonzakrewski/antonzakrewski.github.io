sheafification

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small site, $\mathcal{V}$ a category and $\mathrm{PSh}_{\mathcal{V}}(\mathcal{C}$ the >< and $\mathrm{Sh}_{\mathcal{V}}(\mathcal{C})$ the category of sheaves on a site

Then the sheafification is defined as left adjoint to the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sh}_{\mathcal{V}}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{PSh}_{\mathcal{V}}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
