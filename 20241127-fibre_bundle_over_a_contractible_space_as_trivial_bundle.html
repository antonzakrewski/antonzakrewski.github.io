fibre bundle over a contractible space as trivial bundle

Proposition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a Fibre bundle with typical fibre $F$. Assume that $B$ is contractible space

Then $p$ is trivial fibre bundle

Proof

corollary of:

  • classification of fibre bundles by prnicipal g-bundles
  • homotopy theorem of principal g-bundles

where any fibre bundle $p: E \rightarrow *$ is the trivial bundle
