path homotopic to a smooth loop

Proposition

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold and let $\gamma:[0,1] \rightarrow M$ be a path Then $\gamma$ is homotopic to a smooth map

\begin{align*} \gamma: (- \varepsilon, 1 + \varepsilon) \rightarrow M \end{align*}

Proof

pick an open covering by euclidean subsets $\mathbb{R}^n \subseteq M$. Then by lebesgue's number lemma and compactness of $[0,1]$ we may subdivide $[0,1]$ to paths of the form $[-\delta,+ \delta] \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^n \subseteq M$.

Here may <lineare interpolation> to get a piecewise linear path (for the choosen charts).
