Strategy

  • to show that a functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is an equivalence:
    • find adjunction $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}'$ where $\mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathcal{D}'$ is a full subcategory: this does not have to be an equivalence
    • show that the the essential image is contained in $\mathcal{D}$, adjunctions restrict, i.e. $\mathcal{G}: \tilde{\mathcal{D}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is still an adjoint with $\tilde{\mathcal{D}} \subseteq \mathcal{D}$
