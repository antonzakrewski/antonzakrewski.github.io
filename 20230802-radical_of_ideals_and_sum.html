radical of ideals and sum

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $\mathfrak{a}_1,\mathfrak{a}_2$ ideals. Then

\begin{align*} \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}_1 + \mathfrak{a}_2} =& \sqrt{\sqrt{\mathfrak{a}_1} + \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}_2}} \end{align*}

Proof

$\sqrt{\mathfrak{a}_1 + \mathfrak{a}_2} \subeseteq \sqrt{\sqrt{\mathfrak{a}} + \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}}}$

corollary of radical of an ideal and radical of subsets

$\sqrt{\sqrt{\mathfrak{a}} + \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}}} \subseteq \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}_1 + \mathfrak{a}_2}$

Since $\mathfrak{a}_1, \mathfrak{a}_2 \subseteq \mathfrak{a}_1 + \mathfrak{a}_2$ we conclude

\begin{align*} \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}_1} + \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}_2} \subseteq \sqrt{\mathfrak{a} + \sqrt{\mathfrak{b}} \end{align*}

Therefore, applying a radical results in

\begin{align*} \sqrt{\sqrt{\mathfrak{a}_1} + \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}_2}} \subseteq& \sqrt{\sqrt{\mathfrak{a}_1 + \mathfrak{a}_2}} \\ =& \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}_1 + \mathfrak{a}_2} \end{align*}
