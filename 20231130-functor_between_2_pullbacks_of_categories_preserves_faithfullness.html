functor between 2-pullbacks of categories preserves faithfullness

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_0, \mathcal{C}_1, \mathcal{C}_2$ and $\mathcal{D}_0, \mathcal{D}_1, \mathcal{D}_2$ be categories and

a diagram, such that $\mathcal{L}_1,\mathcal{L}_2$ are faithful

Then for the canonical functor for the 2-categorical pullback of a functor diagram (cf. diagram of 2-categorical pullbacks of functors implies functor)

is also faithful

Proof

Let $(X_1,X_2,\alpha)$ and $(Y_1,Y_2,\beta)$ be objects in $\mathcal{D}_1 \times_{\mathcal{D}_0} \mathcal{D}_2$ and

\begin{align*} (f_1,f_2): (X_1,X_2,\alpha) \rightarrow& (Y_1,Y_2,\beta) \\ (g_1,g_2): (X_1,X_2,\alpha) \rightarrow& (Y_1,Y_2,\beta) \\ \end{align*}

be morphism, such that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}(f_1,f_2) = \mathcal{L}(g_1,g_2) \end{align*}

Then by construction

\begin{align*} (\mathcal{L}_1(f_1), \mathcal{L}_2(f_2)) =& (\mathcal{L}_1(g_1), \mathcal{L}_2(g_2)) \end{align*}

where by faithfullness of $\mathcal{L}_1,\mathcal{L}_2$ it follows, that

\begin{align*} f_1 =& f_2 \\ g_1 =& g_2 \end{align*}
