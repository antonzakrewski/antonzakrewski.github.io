morphism to the double dual

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a closed symmetric monoidal category. Then there exists a canonical natural transformation

\begin{align*} \mathrm{id}(-) \Rightarrow ((-)^*)^* \end{align*}

adjoint to the map $\mathrm{id} \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{End}(\mathcal{C})}((-)^*, (-)^*)$ where $(-)^* = \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,\mathbbm{1})$.
