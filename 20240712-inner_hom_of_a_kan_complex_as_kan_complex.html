inner hom of a kan complex as kan complex

Proposition

Let $Y,K$ be simplicial sets where $K$ is also a Kan complex Then the inner hom

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(Y,K) \end{align*}

is also a Kan complex

Proof

Let

\begin{align*} \iota: \emptyset \rightarrow& Y \\ f: K \rightarrow& \Delta^1 \end{align*}

Then $f$ is a kan fibration (cf. kan complex and terminal morphism as kan fibration) and $\iota$ a monomorphism

Let

\begin{align*} <f,\iota>: \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(Y,K) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\emptyset,K) \times_{\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\emptyset,K)} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(Y,\Delta^1) \cong 1 \times_{1} 1 \cong 1 \end{align*}

where the latter isomorphism follows from

  1. internal hom into a terminal object as terminal object
  2. internal hom out of the initial object as terminal object

by inner hom of a monomorphism and kan fibration it is a kan fibration, hence $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(Y,K)$ is a kan complex
