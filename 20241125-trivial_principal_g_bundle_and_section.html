trivial principal g-bundle and section

Proposition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a principal g-bundle for a topological group $G$.

TFAE:

  1. $p$ is a trivial principal g-bundle
  2. $p$ admits a section

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Choose a section, e.g.

\begin{align*} \varphi: B \rightarrow& B \times G \\ b \mapsto& (b,1) \end{align*}

then this gives a section of $E \rightarrow B$ under the isomophism.

2) $\implies$ 1)

Choose a section $s(-)$.

Then construct a bundle map

\begin{align*} \varphi: B \times G \rightarrow& E \\ (b,g) \mapsto& s(b) \cdot g \end{align*}

it is an isomorphism by morphism of principal G-bundles as isomorphism
