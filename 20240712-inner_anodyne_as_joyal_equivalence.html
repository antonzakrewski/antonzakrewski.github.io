inner anodyne as joyal equivalence

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be simplicial sets and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be inner anodyne Then $\mathcal{F}$ is a joyal equivalence

Proof

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category and consider

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}^{\mathcal{D}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\mathcal{C}} \end{align*}

by infinity category and terminal morphism as inner fibration we conclude that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow * \end{align*}

is an inner fibration

Then by pullback hom of two morphisms and inner hom of a monomorphism and inner anodyne as trivial fibration it follows that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}^{\mathcal{D}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\mathcal{C}} \end{align*}

is a trivial fibration, hence by trivial fibration as joyal equivalence also a joyal equivalence.
