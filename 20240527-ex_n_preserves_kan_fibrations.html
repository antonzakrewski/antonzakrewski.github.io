Ex n preserves Kan fibrations

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{sSet}$ be the category sSet, $X,Y$ simplicial sets and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a kan fibration Then the Ex n functor preserves kan fibrations, i.e.

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ex}(f): \mathrm{Ex}(X) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ex}(Y) \end{align*}

is also a kan fibration

Proof
