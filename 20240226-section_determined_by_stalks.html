section determined by stalks

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}$ be a sheaf on a topological space $X$ Then the morphism

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(U) \rightarrow \prod_{x \in U} \mathcal{F}_x \end{align*}

induced by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}: \mathcal{F}(U) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}_x \end{align*}

is a monomorphism

Proof

idea:

  • compute filtered colimit
  • coproduct of all $\mathcal{F}(U)$
    • modulo same element after restricting to a neighbourhood
    • then for each point same around a neighbourhood
    • gives a compatible family ~> uniqueness of glueing
