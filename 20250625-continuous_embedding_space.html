continuous embedding space

Definition

Let $M,N$ be locally compact topological space. Then the continuous embedding space is defined as subspace of the compact open topology

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Emb}^{\mathrm{Top}}(M,N) \subseteq \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{Top}}(M,N) \end{align*}

containing topological embeddings.
