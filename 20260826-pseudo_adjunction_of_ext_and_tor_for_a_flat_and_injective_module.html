pseudo-adjunction of Ext and Tor for an injective module

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $\mathcal{D}(R)$ the derived category. Let $I$ be an injective module, then there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \pi_{-n}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(A, \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(B, I))) \simeq& \pi_n(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(\pi_n(A \otimes B), I)) \end{align*}

Proof

There exists an adjunction

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(A, \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(B, I)) \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(A \otimes_{R} B, I)) \end{align*}

and hence an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \pi_{-n}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(A, \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(B, I))) \simeq& \pi_{-n}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(A \otimes_{R} B, I))) \end{align*}

By assumption $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(-,I)$ is a t-exact functor and hence

\begin{align*} \pi_{-n}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(A \otimes_{R} B, I))) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(\pi_n(A \otimes_{R} B), I)) \end{align*}

see:

  • homotopy objects of the image of a t-exact functor depend only on the original homotopy objects
