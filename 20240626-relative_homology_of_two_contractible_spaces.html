relative homology of two contractible spaces

Proposition

Let $(X,U)$ be a pair of spaces where both $X,U$ is contractible. Then the relative singular homology

\begin{align*} H_n(X,U) = 0 \end{align*}

for $n > 1$

Proof

corollary of:

  • long exact sequence of homology of a pair of spaces
  • sandwhiched by zero object and isomorphism
