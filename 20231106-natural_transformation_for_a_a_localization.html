natural transformation for a change of denominators for rings

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $S \subseteq T \subseteq A$ multiplicatively closed subsets. Then for the localization functor for modules $S^{-1}(-), T^{-1}(-)$ there exists a canonical natural transformation

\begin{align*} \eta: S^{-1}(-) \rightarrow A \mapsto& \rho^{T,S}: (S^{-1}A \rightarrow T^{-1}A) \\ \end{align*}
