nilpotent element under homomorphisms

Proposition

Let \(A,B\) be rings, $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ a ring-homomorphism and $\alpha \in A$ a nilpotent element. Then $\varphi(\alpha)$ is also nilpotent.

Proof

By assumption there exists an $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $\alpha^n = 0$. Therefore

\begin{align*} 0 =& \varphi(\0) \\ =& \varphi(\alpha^n) \\ =& \varphi(\alpha)^n \end{align*}
