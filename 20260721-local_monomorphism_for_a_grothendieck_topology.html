local monomorphism for a grothendieck topology

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \tau)$ be a site and $f: \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ a natural transformation of set-valued presheaves. Then $f$ is said to be a \(\tau\)-local monomorphism, if the relative diagonal $\mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{F} \times_{\mathcal{G}} \mathcal{F}$ is a $\tau$ local epimorphism
