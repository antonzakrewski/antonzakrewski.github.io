equivalent characterization of a serre subcategory

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, $\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{A}$ a subcategory

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ is a serre subcategory
  2. the following conditions are satisfied a) $0 \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ b) $\mathcal{C}$ is a strictly full subcategory c) $\mathcal{C}$ is closed under quotient objects and subobject d) $\mathcal{C}$ is closed under Extensions in $\mathcal{C}$

Proof
