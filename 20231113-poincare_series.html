Hilbert-Poincare series

Definition

Let $K$ be a field and $S = \bigoplus_{i \geq 0} S_i$ a finitely generated Algebra for $K = S_0$. Then the Hilbert-Poincare Series is defined as formal power series

\begin{align*} HS(t) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \mathrm{dim}(S_i) t^i \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{dim}(S_i)$ is the dimension as vector space
