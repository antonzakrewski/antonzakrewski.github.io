stone cech compactification of a discrete space is extremally disconnected

Proposition

Let $M$ be a discrete space. Then the stone-cech compactification of $M$ is extremally disconnected

Proof

Let $f: Y \twoheadrightarrow \beta M$ be a continuous surjection from a compact hausdorff space $Y$. we need to construct a section (cf. characterization of extremally disconnected, compact hausdorff spaces via sections) Then using stone-cech compactification as embedding and complete regular space we may conclude that the unit is an injection (Note that any discrete space is complete regular). Hence we may choose for $m \in M \subseteq \beta M$ an element $y_m \in Y$ and define

\begin{align*} s: M \rightarrow& Y\\ m \mapsto& y_m \end{align*}

which is then continuous by the universal property of $M$

Now Consider

where $\beta s$ is the induced extension

we claim that

\begin{align*} f \circ \beta s = \mathrm{id}_{\beta M} \end{align*}

This follows as

  1. $M$ is dense in $\beta M$
  2. $\beta M$ is hausdorff
  3. both $\mathrm{id}_{\beta M}$ and $f \circ \beta s$ extend $\mathrm{id}_M$, hence are equal by restriction of a map to dense subspace in a Hausdorff space
