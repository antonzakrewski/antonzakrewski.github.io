relative cross product

Definition

Let $(X,A), (Y,B)$ be pairs of spaces, such that $(X \times Y, A \times Y, X \times B)$ is an excisive triad. Then the relative cross product is defined as

\begin{align*} \times: H^*(X,A,M) \otimes_R H^*(Y,B,N) \rightarrow H^*((X,A) \times (Y,B); M \otimes_R N) \end{align*}

by 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
H(C_{\mathrm{sing}}(X,A;M)) \otimes_R H(C_{\mathrm{sing}}(Y,B;N)) \arrow[]{r}{} & \\
\arrow[r]{\mu}{} & H(\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(C^{\mathrm{sing}(X,A),M[0]) \otimes_R \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(C^{\mathrm{sing}(Y,B), N[0]) \\
\arrow[]{r}{H(\lambda)} & H(\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X,A) \otimes C^{\mathrm{sing}(Y,B), (M \otimes_R N)[0])
\arrow[]{r}{\cong} & H(\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X,A) \times (Y,B), (M \otimes N)[0]) \\
\arrow[]{r}{=}& H^*((X, A) \times (Y,B); M \otimes_R N)
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
