full waldhausen category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a waldhausen category. Then a full subcategory $\mathcal{U}$ is said to be a full waldhausen subcategory if

\begin{align*} \mathcal{U}_{\mathrm{in}} \coloneqq \{j \in \mathcal{U} \cap \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{in}} \vert \mathrm{cofib}(j) \in \mathcal{U}\} \end{align*}
