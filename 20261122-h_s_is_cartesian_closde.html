H(S) is cartesian closed

Proposition

let $S$ be a (qcqs scheme ?). Thne $H(S)$ is a cartesian closed category.

Proof

$S$ qcqs scheme

recall that nisnevich sheaves on smooth schemes over a qcqs scheme is nisnevich sheaves on lfp. Then using localization of a cartesian closed category it suffices to show that the nisnevich covers and the maps $よ(X \times \mathbb{A}^1) \rightarrow よ(X)$ are closed under products. The first follows immediately, the second is also doable.
