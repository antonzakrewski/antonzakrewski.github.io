vanishing locus of an affine space

Definition

Let $F \subseteq k[x_i \vert i \in I]$ be a subset. Then the vanishing locus is the pullback in $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}_{k}, \mathrm{Set})$

Alternatively

It is the Subfunctor $V(F) \subseteq \mathbb{A}_k^I$ given by

\begin{align*} V(F)(R) = \{a \in R^I \vert f(a) = 0 \textrm{ for all } f \in F\} \end{align*}
