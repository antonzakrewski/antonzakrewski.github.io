cofibration diagram of weak equivalances and colimit

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{sSet}$ be the category sSet and

a diagram where the horizontal maps are weak equivalence and the vertical maps are cofibration of simplicial sets

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{i}(X(i)) \rightarrow \mathrm{colim}_{i}(Y(i)) \end{align*}

is also a weak equivalence
