n-th homotopy object functor of a t-structure

Definition

The \(n\)-th homotopy object functor of a t-structure is defined as composition

\begin{align*} \pi_n(-) \coloneqq (\tau_{\leq 0} \circ \tau_{\geq 0})((-)[-n]) \end{align*}
