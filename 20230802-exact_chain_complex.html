exact chain complex

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes and 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\arrow[]{r}{} & A_* \arrow[]{r}{} & B_* \arrow[]{r}{} & C_* \arrow[]{r}{} & 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

a sequence of chain complexes.

TFAE:

  1. the sequence is exact at $B_*$
  2. for each $n$ it holds, that
\begin{align*} \arrow[]{r}{} & A_n \arrow[]{r}{} & B_n \arrow[]{r}{} & C_n \arrow[]{r}{} & \end{align*}

is exact at $B_n$

Proof

see:

  • colimit of functors for a cocomplete codomain
  • limit of functors for a complete domain

where $\mathcal{A}$ admits equalizers and coequalizers, hence also kernel, cokernel and image
