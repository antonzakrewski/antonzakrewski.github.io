forgetful functor from infinity twisted arrow to the product

Definition / Proposition

Let $X$ be a simplicial set. Then the forgetful functor from the infinity twisted arrow simplicial set is given by a morphism of simplicial sets

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tw}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}

which is given by precomposition with

\begin{align*} \Delta^{(-)} \coprod (\Delta^{(-)})^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \Delta^{(-)} \star (\Delta^{(-)})^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}

as the respective inclusion (cf. right coslice join functor, left coslice join functor)

and the natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^n \coprod (\Delta^n)^{\mathrm{op}}) \end{align*}

(cf. contravariant hom-functor dualizes small colimits to limits) and opposite simplicial set functor as hom functor, identity functor of sSet as Hom)
