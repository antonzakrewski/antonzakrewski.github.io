1 categorical stable infinity category as point

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category which is also a \(1\)-category. Then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \simeq \Delta^0 \end{align*}

Proof

  • pointed infinity category with cofibers and suspension functor as equivalence as stable infinity category
  • suspension in a pointed 1-category as trivial functor
