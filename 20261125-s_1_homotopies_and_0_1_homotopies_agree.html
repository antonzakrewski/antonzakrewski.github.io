S^1 homotopic and [0,1] homotopic maps

Proposition

Let $f,g: X \rightarrow Y$ be a maps between topological space . TFAE:

  1. $f$ and $g$ are I-homotopic for $S^1$
  2. $f$ and $g$ are I-homotopic for $[0,1]$

Proof

use the topological maps

\begin{align*} [0,1] \hookrightarrow S^1 \twoheadrightarrow [0,1] \end{align*}
