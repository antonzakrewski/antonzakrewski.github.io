weakly contractible colimit in anima valued groups and delooping

Proposition

Let $I \rightarrow \mathrm{Grp}(\mathrm{An})$ be a diagram in the category of anima valued groups. Then the colimit is given by $\Omega \mathrm{colim}_{i \in I} B(-)$.

Proof

follows as:

  • connected colimit of connected anima as connected anima
  • colimit in a coslice category
  • recognition principle for anima valued groups and connected pointed animas
