isomorphism-closed subcategory

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $\mathcal{D}$ a subcategory. Then $\mathcal{D}$ is said to be isomorphism-closed, if for objects $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{D}), B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and an isomorphism $f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,B)$ we conclude, that $f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(A,B)$ and $B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{D})$
