quotient space of a hausdorff space in general not hausdorff

Proposition

Let $X$ be a Hausdorff space, $\sim$ an equivalence relation. Then the quotient topology $X/\sim$ is in general not hausdorff (even T1)

Proof

Let $\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Q}$ be the real numbers modulo rational numbers topology. Then using <explicit description of the quotient topology> we get that a subset $[Y] \subseteq \mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Q}$ is closed if and only if $ \mathrm{pr}^{-1}[[Y] ] \subseteq X$ is closed. But then the point $[\mathbb{Q}]$ is not closed in $\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Q}$, since $\mathbb{Q}\subsetneq \mathbb{R}$ is dense (cf. rationale Zahlen dicht in den reellen Zahlen)
