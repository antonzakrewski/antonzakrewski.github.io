evaluation of homogeneous polynomials

Definition

Let $k$ be a commutative ring, $I$ a set, $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $R$ a K-Algebra and $L$ a line over $R$. Then there exists an evaluation map

\begin{align*} k[x_i \vert i \in I]_d \times L^I \rightarrow& L^{\otimes d} \\ (f,a) \mapsto& f(a) \end{align*}

which is defined as composition

here we use that the symmetric algebra construction is a functor on modules and that $k \rightarrow R, a: R^I \rightarrow L \in L^I$ are \(R\)-linear maps as well as n-th symmetric power of a line.
