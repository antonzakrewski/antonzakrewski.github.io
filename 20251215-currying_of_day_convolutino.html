currying of day convolution for symmetric monoidal categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}$ be symmetric monoidal categories, such that $\mathcal{C}$ is a small category and $\mathcal{D}$ is a prepresentably symmetric monoidal category. Then for any symmetric monoidal category $\mathcal{X}$ there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\textrm{lax}}(\mathcal{X}, \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\textrm{lax}}(\mathcal{X} \times \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ is endowed with the day convolution

Proof
