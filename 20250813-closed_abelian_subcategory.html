closed abelian subcategory

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category. Then an abelian full subcategory $\mathcal{B}$ is said to be closed if

  1. $\mathcal{B}$ is closed under Subobjects and quotients
  2. each object $A \in \mathcal{A}$ is the extension of some sequence

note

  • e.g. finitely presented abelian groups are not closed, since extension
  • serre subcategory + this notion ~> trivial (full sbucategory)
