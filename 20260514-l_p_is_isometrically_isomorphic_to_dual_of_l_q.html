l^p is isometrically isomorphic to dual of l^q

Proposition

Let $p \in [1,\infty)$ and $q$ the respective hölder conjugate Then $(l^p)^* \cong l^q$ are (isometrically, linear) isomorphic

Proof

estimate

$p \in (1,\infty)$

Let $x = (x_n) \in l^p$ and $f \in (l^p)^*$. Then consider the schauder basis of lp of sequences $e_n$. In particular $x = \sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} x_n e_n$. Now $f$ is continuous and linear, so

\begin{align*} f(x) =& f(\lim_{n \rightarrow \infty} \sum_{n=1}^N x_n e_n) \\ =& \sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} x_n f(e_n) \end{align*}

Now for fixed $N \in \mathbb{N}$ define $\tilde{x} \coloneqq (\tilde{x}_n) \in l^p$ by

\begin{align*} \tilde{x}_n \coloneqq \begin{cases} \frac{ \vert f(e_n) \vert^q }{f(e_n)} & \mbox{if } n \leq N \text{ and} f(e_n) \neq 0 \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} f(\tilde{x}) = \sum_{n=1}^N \vert f(e_n) \vert^q \end{align*}

so that $0 \leq f(\tilde{x}) \leq \| f \|_{(l^p)^*} \| \tilde{x} \|_p$ where

\begin{align*} \| \tilde{x} \|_p = (\sum_{n=p}^N \vert f(e_n) \vert^{(q -1)p})^{\frac{1}{p}}$ \end{align*}

Now $p,q$ are hölder conjugate, i.e., $p = $\frac{q}{q-1}$ and hence we obtain $p(q-1) = q$ and therefore

\begin{align*} \sum_{n=1}^N \vert f(e_n) \vert^q \leq \| f \|_{(l^p)^*} \cdot (\sum_{n=1}^N \vert f(e_n) \vert^q)^{\frac{1}{p}} \end{align*}

which shows

\begin{align*} (\sum_{n=1}^N \vert f(e_n) \vert^q)^{\frac{1}{q}} \leq \| f \|_{(l^p)^*} \end{align*}

$p = 1$

map

Thus for every $p \in [1,\infty)$, the map

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F} \colon (l^p)^* \rightarrow& l^q \\ f \mapsto& (f(e_n))_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \end{align*}

is well defined and also

  1. linear (cf. )
  2. isometric (hence injective): see estimate
  3. surjective: for y = (y_n) \in l^q$, we define
\begin{align*} f_y \colon l^p \rightarrow& \mathbb{K} \\ (x_n) \mapsto& \sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} x_n y_n \end{align*}

Then $f_y$ is linear and it is well-defined by Hölders inequality

\begin{align*} \sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \vert x_n y_n \vert \leq \| x \|_p \cdot \|y \|_q \end{align*}

and hence $\| f_y \|_{(l^p)^*} \leq \| y \|_q$
