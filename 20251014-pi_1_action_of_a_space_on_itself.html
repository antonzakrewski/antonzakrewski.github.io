pi_1 action of a space on itself

Definition

Let $(X,x)$ be a (connected) pointed anima. Then there exists a pi_1 action on the fiber for anima for the initial morphism $(*,*) \rightarrow (X,*)$

more explicit description

After unstraightening we get a functor

\begin{align*} X \rightarrow& \mathrm{An}_* x \mapsto& \Omega(X,x) \end{align*}

so in particular we may postcompose with

  1. $\pi_0$
  2. $\pi_n$

and then get a functor

\begin{align*} X \rightarrow& \mathrm{Set}\\ x \mapsto& \pi_n(X,x) \end{align*}

and then use the homotopy category adjunction to get a functor

\begin{align*} \tau_{\leq 1}(X) \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} \end{align*}

which if $X$ is connected is equivalent to a functor $\pi_1(X,x_0) \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ for some chosen basepoint $x_0$
