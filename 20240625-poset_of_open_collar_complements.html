poset of open collar complements

Definition

Let $W$ be a \(n+1\)-dimensional topological manifold with boundary Thne the poset of open collar complemenst is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}_{W} \coloneqq \{W \setminus C \vert C \textrm{open collar of the boundar}\} \end{align*}
