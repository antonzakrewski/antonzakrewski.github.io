cohomology surjection from a spectrum to a product of Eilenberg-MacLane spectra

Proposition

Let $X,M$ be spectra. Then there exist $i_n$ (possibly infinite) and a map

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow \prod_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \prod_{i_n} M \end{align*}

which induces a surjection on \(M\)-cohomology.
