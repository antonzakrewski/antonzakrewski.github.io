dualizable objects are often compact

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a symmetric monoidal infinity category with kappa compact monoidal unit $\mathbbm{1}$ and $- \otimes -$, which preserves $\kappa$-filtered colimits in both variables. Then any dualizable object is already compact.

Proof

Let $X$ be dualizable, then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,-) \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbbm{1}, \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,-)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbbm{1}, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}}(X,-) \otimes - ) \\ \end{align*}

where the last functor is a composition of $\kappa$-filtered colimit preserving functors, hence also preserves kappa filtered colimits
