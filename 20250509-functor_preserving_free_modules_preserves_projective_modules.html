functor preserving projective modules and free modules

Proposition

Let \(R,S\) be rings, $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{RMod} \rightarrow \mathrm{SMod}$ a functor

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ preserves projective objects
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ sends free modules to projective objects

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

special case

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from

  • retract of a projective object as projective object
  • projective module and sum as free module
  • epimorphism onto a projective splits

where functor preserves split epis, i.e. for any projective module we may find a split epi

\begin{align*} R^{\kappa} \twoheadrightarrow P \end{align*}

which maps to $\mathcal{F}(R^{\kappa}) \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal{F}(P)$
