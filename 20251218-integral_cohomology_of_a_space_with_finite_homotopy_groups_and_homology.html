integral cohomology of a space with finite homotopy groups as shifted homology

Proposition

Let $X$ be an anima with $\pi_*(-)$ always finite Then for $n \geq 1$

\begin{align*} H_{n}(X, \mathbb{Z}) \cong H^{n+1}(X, \mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

are abstractly isomorphic

Proof

use

  • Ext of a finite group and integers
  • fundamental theorem of serre class theory for finite abelian groups is a derived serre ring, hence $H_*(X, \mathbb{Z})$ is always finite
