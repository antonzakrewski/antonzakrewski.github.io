gros zariski site

Definition

Let $\mathrm{fpAlg}^{\mathrm{op}}$ the opposite category of category of finitely presented algebras. Then the gros zariski situs is defined as covering conataiing

  1. for each sum $1 = f_1 + ... + f_n \in B$ a covering family
\begin{align*} \{(f_i)^{-1}B \rightarrow B\} \end{align*}

given by the opposite morphism

\begin{align*} B \rightarrow (f_i)^{-1}B \end{align*}
