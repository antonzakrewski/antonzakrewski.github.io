localization of a noetherian ring is a noetherian ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $S \subseteq A$ a multiplicatively closed subset. Suppose $A$ is noetherian, then so is $S^{-1}A$

Proof

follows from the

  • correspondence theorem for prime ideals and localization
  • noetherian ring and finite generation of prime ideals

where a prime ideal $S^{-1}\mathfrak{p}$ in $S^{-1}A$ corresponds naturally to a prime ideal $\mathfrak{p}$. Then the finite amount of generators of $\mathfrak{p}$ in $A$ suffice as generator
