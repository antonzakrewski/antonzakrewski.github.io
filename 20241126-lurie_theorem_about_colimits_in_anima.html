Lurie theorem about colimits in Anima

Proposition

Let $F: I \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ be an infinity functor Then the colimit is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{I} F \cong \vert \mathrm{Un}(F) \vert \end{align*}

see: geometric realization from Cat infinity

Proof

We want to show that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(\vert \mathcal{U} \vert, -) \overset{\mathrm{!}}{\simeq} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(I, \mathrm{An})}(\mathcal{F}, \mathrm{const}(-)) \end{align*}

We know by straightening-unstraightening that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(I,\mathrm{An})}(-,-) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{LFib}(I)}(-,-) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}/I}(-,-) \end{align*}

where the latst follows from the fact that $\mathrm{LFib}(I) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}/I$ is fully faithful

Now we insert to get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(I, \mathrm{An})}(\mathcal{F}, \mathrm{const} X) \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}/I}((p: \mathcal{U} \rightarrow I), X \times I \rightarrow I) \end{align*}

where we use unstraightening of projection.

Furthermore

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}/I}((p: \mathcal{U} \rightarrow I), X \times I \rightarrow I) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{U}, X \times I) \times_{\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{U}, I)} \{p\} \\ \simeq& \left(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{U}, X) \times \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{U}, I) \right) \times_{\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{U}, I)} \{p\} \end{align*}

here we have used that mapping anima functor preserves limits 1 -

which shows that

\begin{align*} \left(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{U}, X) \times \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{U}, I) \right) \times_{\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{U}, I)} \{p\} \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{U}, X) \times \left( \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{U}, I) \times_{\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{U}, I)} \{p\} \right) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{U}, X) \times \{p\} \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{U}, X) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(\vert \mathcal{U} \vert, X) \\ \end{align*}

where we have used

  1. limits commute with limits
  2. square with parallel equivalences is a pullback
  3. product with terminal object is canonically equivalent
  4. the adjunction of geometric realization
