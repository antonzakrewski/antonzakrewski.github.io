noetherian ring and finite algebraically independent subset

Proposition

Let $A$ be a noetherian ring, $\{a_1,...,a_n\} \subseteq A$ be a finite subset of algebraically independent elements. Then there exists a minimal prime ideal $\mathfrak{p}$ such that

\begin{align*} \{a_1 + \mathfrak{p}_i,...,a_n + \mathfrak{p}_i \} \in A/\mathfrak{p}_i \end{align*}

is algebraically independent

Proof

using finite amount of minimal prime ideals for a noetherian ring let $\mathfrak{p}_1,...,\mathfrak{p}_r$ be the minimal prime ideals. Suppose the set $\{a_1 + \mathfrak{p}_i,...,a_n + \mathfrak{p}_i \}$ is algebraically independent for each $i \in \{1,...,r\}$ Then there exist polynomials $0 \neq f_i \in K[X_1,...,X_n]$ with $f_i(a_1,...,a_n) \in \mathfrak{p}_i$.

Consider

\begin{align*} a \coloneqq \prod_{i=1}^r f_i(a_1,...,a_n) \in \prod_{i=1}^r \mathfrak{p}_i \subseteq \bigcap_{i=1}^r \mathfrak{p}_i = \mathcal{N}A \end{align*}

(cf. nilradical as intersection of minimal prime ideals, product of ideals as subset of the intersection)

Then $a$ is nilpotent and there exists a $k > 0$ such that $a^k = 0$. Let

\begin{align*} f = \prod_{i=1}^r f_i^k \end{align*}

with $f \neq 0$ as $K[X_1,...,X_n]$ is an Integral domain and $f_i \neq 0$.

But then $f(a_1,...,a_n) = a^k = 0$ which contradicts the algebraic independence of $\{a_1,...,a_n\}$.
