cofinal infinity functor

Definition

Let $f: K \rightarrow L$ be a morphism of simplicial sets. Then $f$ is said to be cofinal if for all infinity functors $p: \mathcal{L} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ where $\mathcal{C}$ is an infinity category, the induced map

\begin{align*} f: \mathcal{C}^{p/} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{pf/} \end{align*}

is a joyal equivalence
