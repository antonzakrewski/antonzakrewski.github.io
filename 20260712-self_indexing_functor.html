self indexing functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category with pullbacks. Then the self indexing functor is the straightening of the target functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ar}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

or in other words, the functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat} \\ X \mapsto& \mathcal{C}_{/X} \\ (f: Y \rightarrow X) \mapsto& (f^*: \mathcal{C}_{/X} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{/Y}) \end{align*}

where $f^*$ maps $U$ to $U \times_{X} Y$
