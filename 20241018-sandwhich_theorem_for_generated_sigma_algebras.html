sandwhich theorem for generated sigma algebras

Proposition

Let $X$ be a set, $\mathcal{A} \subseteq \mathcal{B} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)$ family of sets Suppose $\mathcal{B} \subseteq \sigma(\mathcal{A})$

Proof

follows from:

  • generated sigma algebra idempotent
  • generated sigma algebra and subset

where

\begin{align*} \sigma(\mathcal{B}) \subseteq \sigma(\sigma(\mathcal{A})) = \sigma(\mathcal{A}) \end{align*}
