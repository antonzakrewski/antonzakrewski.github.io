finite product in the category of propositions as conjunction

Proposition

Given the category of propositions $\mathrm{Prop}$ and Propositions $A_1,...,A_n$, the categorical product is the conjunction

\begin{align*} A_1 \land ... \land A_n \end{align*}

\begin{align*} A_1 \land ... \land A_n \vdash A_i \end{align*}
