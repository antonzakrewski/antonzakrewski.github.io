quotient of an anima with homology-identity is simple and universal cover

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group, $X$ a simply connected homology-identity space and $\mathrm{BG} \rightarrow \{X\}$ a group action. TFAE:

  1. $X/G$ is a simple anima
  2. $G$ acts trivially on the homology of $X$ for the fiber sequence $\tilde{X} \rightarrow X \rightarrow B\pi_1(X)$
