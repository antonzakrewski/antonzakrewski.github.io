isomorphism on integral cohomology and general cohomology

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a morphism between anima which induces an isomorphism on singular cohomology with $\mathbb{Z}$ coefficients. Then this induces an isomorphism on arbitrary cohomology.

Proof

corollary of:

  • integral cohomology isomorphism induces homology isomorphism
  • isomorphism in integral homology and isomorphism on general cohomology
