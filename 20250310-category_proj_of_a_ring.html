category Proj of a Ring

Definition

Let $R$ be a ring. Then the category Proj is defined as groupoid core of the subcategory of $\mathrm{RMod}$ containing finitely generated projective modules as objects.

Note that it inherits a symmetric monoidal category from the cartesian monoidal structure
