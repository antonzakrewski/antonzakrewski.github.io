infinity sifted category as weakly contractible category

Proposition

Let $K$ be an infinity sifted simplicial set. Then $K$ is weakly contractible.

Proof

Let $x \in K$, which exists as $K$ is nonempty. Then we have to show that

\begin{align*} \pi_n(K,x) = \{*\} \end{align*}

Consider the diagonal morphism in category sSet

\begin{align*} K \rightarrow K \times K \end{align*}

It is cofinal, hence by cofinal functor as weak equivalence we get

\begin{align*} \pi_n(K,x) \simeq& \pi_n(K \times K,(x,x)) \\ \simeq& \pi_n(K,x) \times \pi_n(K,x) \end{align*}

with $\pi_n(K,x) \neq \emptyset$, hence

\begin{align*} \pi_n(K,x) = \{*\} \end{align*}
