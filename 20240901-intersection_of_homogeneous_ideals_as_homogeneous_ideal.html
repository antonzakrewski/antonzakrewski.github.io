intersection of homogeneous ideals as homogeneous ideal

Proposition

Let $M$ be a monoid and $R = \bigoplus_{m \in m} R_m$ a graded ring. Suppose $\mathfrak{a}_i$ are homogeneous ideals, then so is the Intersection

\begin{align*} \bigcap_{i} \mathfrak{a}_i \end{align*}

an homogeneous ideal

Proof

using homogeneous ideal as additive prime ideal

Let $m \in \bigcap \mathfrak{a}_i$ with $m = \sum p_{m_i}$ for $p_{m_i} \in R_{m_i}$ and $m_i \neq m_j$ for $i \neq j$. Then by assumption of $\mathfrak{a}_i$ being homogeneous, we conclude that $p_{m_i} \in \mathfrak{a}_i$. Therefore $\mathfrak{p}_{m_i} \in \bigcap_{i} \mathfrak{a}_i$ showing that the intersection is also homogeneous
