kappa filtered colimits commute with kappa small limits in Cat Cat

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal. Then filtered colimits commute with finite limits in category Cat

Proof

follows from:

  • kappa filtered colimits and kappa small limits commute in Anima
  • category Cat is equivalent to complete segal anima and filtered colimits in complete segal anima are computed underlying, just as limits by some left bousfield localization argument.
