epimorphisms mirrored by pushouts

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $A,B,C \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and

a pushout where

\begin{align*} f: C \rightarrowtail A \end{align*}

is an epimorphism

Then so is

\begin{align*} \pi_B: A \times_C B \rightarrow B \end{align*}
