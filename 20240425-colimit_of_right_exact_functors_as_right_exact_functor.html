colimit of right exact functors as right exact functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, $\mathcal{F}_i: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ right exact functors where $\mathcal{B}$ is (sufficiently) cocomplete Then the colimit

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_I(\mathcal{F}_i) \end{align*}

is also a right exact functor

Proof

  • colimit of functors for a cocomplete codomain
  • colimits commute with colimits
