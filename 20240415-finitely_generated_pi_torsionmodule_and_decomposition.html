finitely generated pi torsionmodule and decomposition

Proposition

Let $R$ be a principal ideal domain, $M$ a finitely generated module and $T_p(M)$ a finitely generated pi-torsionmodule. Then

\begin{align*} T_p(M) \cong \bigoplus_{j = 1}^s R/(p^{n_j}) \end{align*}

Proof

Let $\{m_1,...,m_s\}$ be a minimal set of generators of $T_p(M)$a. For $s = 1$ we get $T_p(M) \cong m_1 R$. Furthermore there exists a minimal $n \geq 1$ such that

\begin{align*} p^{n} m_1 = 0 \end{align*}

hence

\begin{align*} R m_1 \cong R/(p^n) \end{align*}

Let $s \geq 2$, then there exists an $m_i$ with maximal $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $p^n \cdot m_i \neq 0$. Then it follows by maximality that $p^{n+1} m_i = 0, p^{n+1} m_j = 0$ w.l.o.g. let $m_i = m_1$
