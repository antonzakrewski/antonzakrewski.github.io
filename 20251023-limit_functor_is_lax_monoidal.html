limit functor is lax monoidal

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a symmetric monoidal category, $X$ any category Then the limit functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim} \mathrm{Fun}(X,\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

is lax with respect to the pointwise monoidal structure

Proof

follows from

  • lax right adjoint and oplax left adjoint

where by construction of the pointwise monoidal structure the functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathcal{C}) c \mapsto \mathrm{const}_c \end{align*}

is a monoidal functor
