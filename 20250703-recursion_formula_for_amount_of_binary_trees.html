recursion formula for the catalan number

Proposition

Let $B_n = \vert \mathcal{B}_{n} \vert$ be the cardinality of the set of binary trees. Then

\begin{align*} B_0 =& 1\\ B_{n} =& \sum_{k=1}^n B_{k-1} \cdot B_{n-k} \end{align*}

note that we consider the order.

Proof

$B_0$

follows from the empty binary tree

$B_n$

then we get a partition of $\mathcal{B}_n$ into $\mathcal{B}_{k-1} \times \mathcal{B}_{n-k}$ where the first factor is the binary tree on the left and the second on the right. Thus

\begin{align*} B_n =& \vert \mathcal{B}_n \vert \\ =& \vert \coprod_{k=1}^n B_{k-1} \times \mathcal{B}_{n-k} \vert \\ =& \sum_{k=1}^n B_{k-1} \cdot B_{n-k} \end{align*}
