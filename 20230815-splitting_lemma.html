Splitting Lemma

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an additive category and

be a short exact sequence.

TFAE:

  1. there exists a split $\iota: A_3 \rightarrow A_2$
  2. there exists a split $\pi: A_2 \rightarrow A_1$
  3. $A_2 \cong A_1 \oplus A_3$ such that $\iota'(a_1) = (a_1,0)$ and $\psi'(a_1,a_3) = a_3$ and that following diagram commutes #+OPTIONS: tex:dvisvgm

Proof

1) $\implies$ 3)

We want to define a map $A_2 \rightarrow A_1 \oplus A_3$ which becomes (in hindsight) an isomorphism For this consider the endomorphism $\pi := \mathrm{id} - \iota \circ f$: It restricts to $A_1$ -- the fiber -- as $f(\mathrm{id} - \iota \circ f) = f - (f \circ \iota) f = f - f = 0$. Therefore define $A_2 \xrightarrow{(\pi ,f)} A_1 \oplus A_3$ and $A_1 \oplus A_3 \xrightarrow{(g,\iota)} A_2$. Now it remains to calculate that 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
0\rar  & A_1 \rar \arrow[equal, ]{d}{}& A_2 \dar{(\pi,f)} \rar & A_3 \arrow[equal, ]{d}{} \rar & 0\\
0 \rar & A_1 \rar \arrow[equal, ]{d}{} & A_1 \oplus A_3 \rar \dar{(g, \iota)}& A_3 \arrow[equal, ]{d}{} \rar & 0  \\
0\rar  & A_1 \rar & A_2 \dar{\cong} \rar & A_3  \rar & 0\\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

is a commutative diagram where the middle maps are isomorphisms

2) $\implies$ 3)

dual to 1) $\implies$ 3)

3) $\implies$ 1), 3) $\implies$ 2)

follows from the retraction of the $A_1 \hookrightarrow A_1 \oplus A_3 \twoheadrightarrow A_3$
