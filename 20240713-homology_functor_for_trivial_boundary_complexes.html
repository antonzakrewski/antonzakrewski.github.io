homology functor for trivial boundary complexes

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes, $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})^{\mathrm{triv}}$ the full subcategory of trivial boundary complexes Then the homology functor

\begin{align*} H_*: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \end{align*}

is the identity functor

Proof

a)

  • homology of a trivial boundary complex

b)

follows from the definition, where for

\begin{align*} \partial_n^A: A_n \rightarrow& A_{n-1} \\ \partial_n^B: B_n \rightarrow& B_{n-1} \\ \end{align*}
\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{ker}(\partial_n^A)/\mathrm{im}(\partial_{n+1}^A) \arrow[]{r}{H_n(f)} \arrow[]{d}{\simeq}& \mathrm{ker}(\partial_n^B)/\mathrm{im}(\partial_{n+1}^B) \arrow[]{d}{\simeqd}\\
A_n/0 \arrow[]{d}{\simeq} & B_n/0 \arrow[]{d}{} \arrow[]{d}{\simeq}\\
A_n \arrow[]{r}{f}& B_n
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
