2-truncation of the underlying anima of a commutative group

Proposition

Let $X \in \mathrm{CGrp}(\mathrm{An})$ (or even only $\mathbb{E}_2$ group ?). Then the 2-truncation of the underlying anima is given by

\begin{align*} \tau_{\leq 2} X \simeq \mathrm{B}\pi_1(X) \times K(\pi_2(X), 2) \end{align*}
