big mapping space

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathcal{K}_1, \mathcal{K}_2$ be simplicial sets and $\mathcal{F}_i: \mathcal{K}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ morphisms of simplicial sets Then the big mapping space is defined as 1-categorical pullback

where the right vertical map is given by precomposition with

\begin{align*} \mathcal{K}_1 \coprod \mathcal{K}_2 \hookrightarrow K \star \Delta^0 \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{K}_1 \coprod \mathcal{K}_2, -) \cong \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{K}_1, -) \times \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{K}_2, -) \end{align*}

note: Define

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x, \mathcal{F}_2) \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{F}_1, x) \end{align*}

via the corresponding map by yoneda
