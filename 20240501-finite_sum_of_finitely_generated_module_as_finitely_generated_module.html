finite sum of finitely generated module as finitely generated module

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $M,N$ finitely generated modules over $R$. Then the direct sum $M \oplus N$ is also finitely generated.

Proof

By assumption there exist surjections.

\begin{align*} \varphi: \bigoplus R^n \twoheadrightarrow M \\ \psi: \bigoplus R^m \twoheadrightarrow N \\ \end{align*}

hence

\begin{align*} \varphi \oplus \psi: \bigoplus R^{n + m} \twoheadrightarrow M \oplus N \end{align*}

is a surjection (cf. colimit functor preserves epimorphisms)
