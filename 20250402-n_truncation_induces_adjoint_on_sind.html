n-truncation induces adjoint on sInd

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category. Then <postcomposition> with n-truncation $\tau_{\leq n}$ induces an infinity right adjoint

\begin{align*} \tau_{\leq n}_*: \mathrm{sInd} \rightarrow \mathrm{sInd}_{\leq n} \end{align*}
