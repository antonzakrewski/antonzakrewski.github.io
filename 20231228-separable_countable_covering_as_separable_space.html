separable, countable covering as separable space

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $U_i$ a countable covering of $X$, such that $U_i$ is separable. Then $X$ is also separable

Proof

uses axiom of countable choice (choosing dense subsets)

By assumption, for each $U_i$ there exists a dense, countable set. By countability of a countable union of countable sets, the union of those is also countable

Furthermore, there exists a canonical continuous map
