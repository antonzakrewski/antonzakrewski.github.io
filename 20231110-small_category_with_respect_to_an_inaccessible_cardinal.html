small category with respect to an inaccessible cardinal

Definition

Let $\kappa$ be an inaccessible cardinal and $\mathcal{C}$ a small category. then $\mathcal{C}$ is said to be small with respect to $\kappa$, if the cardinality of $\mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,B)$ for $A,B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ is smaller then $\kappa$
