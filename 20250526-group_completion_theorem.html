group completion theorem for homology and commutative monoids

Proposition

Let $M \in \mathrm{CMon}(\mathrm{An})$ and $E \in \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Sp})$ a commutative ring spectrum. Then the map $M \rightarrow M^{\mathrm{grp}}$ induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} E[M][\pi_0(M)^{-1}] \rightarrow E[M^{\mathrm{grp}}] \end{align*}

of commutative R-Algebras

Here $\pi_0(M)$ denotes the image of the stable hurewicz homomorphism

\begin{align*} \pi_0(M) \rightarrow& E[M] \end{align*}

  • S-localization of an R-Algebra spectrum
  • here we localize the ring $E[M]$
