left homotopy as equivalence relation from cofibrant objects

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}, \mathrm{Cof}, \mathrm{Fib})$ be a model category, $A \in \mathcal{C}$ a cofibrant object. Then left homotopy is an equivalence relation on $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,X)$ for each $X \in \mathcal{C}$
