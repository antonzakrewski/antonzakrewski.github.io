free R-module spectrum functor is lax monoidal for spectra

Proposition

The free R-module spectrum functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Sp} \\ X \mapsto R[X] \end{align*}

is lax monoidal functor for a $\mathbb{E}_{\infty}$-ring spectrum

Proof

use:

  • lax right adjoint and oplax left adjoint
