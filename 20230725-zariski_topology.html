Zariski topology on Spec

Definition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ the zariski spectrum. Then the Zariski topology is defined by the closed sets

\begin{align*} \{V(T) \vert T \subseteq A\} \end{align*}
