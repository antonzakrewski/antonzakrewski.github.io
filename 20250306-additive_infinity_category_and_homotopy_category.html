additive infinity category and homotopy category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category admitting finite products & finite coproducts

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ is an additive infinity category
  2. the homotopy category is an additive category

Proof

follows from:

  • preadditive infinity category and homotopy category

where morphisms are equivalences if and only if they are isomorphisms in the homotopy category
