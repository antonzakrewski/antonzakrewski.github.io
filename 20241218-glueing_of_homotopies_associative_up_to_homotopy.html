glueing of homotopies associative up to homotopy

Proposition

Let

\begin{align*} f_1,f_2,f_3,f_4: A \rightarrow B \end{align*}

be maps and

\begin{align*} H_1: f_1 \leadsto& f_2 \\ H_2: f_2 \leadsto& f_3 \\ H_3: f_3 \leadsto& f_4 \end{align*}

be homotopies

Then glueing of homotopies

\begin{align*} (H_1 \star H_2) \star H_3 \simeq H_1 \star (H_2) \star H_3) \end{align*}

is associative up to canonical homotopy

Proof
