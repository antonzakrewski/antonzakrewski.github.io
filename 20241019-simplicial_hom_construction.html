simplicial hom construction

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{F}: \Delta^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a simplicial object. Then the simplicial hom construction assigns to an object $C \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ the simplicial set

\begin{align*} C^{\mathcal{F}}_n \coloneqq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(C, \mathcal{F}(n)) \end{align*}

with the degeneracy and face maps given by functorality of $\mathrm{Hom}_{}(C,-)$
