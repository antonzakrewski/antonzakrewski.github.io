diagonal morphism natural transformation

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $I$ a set the diagonal morphism is defined as natural transformation is defined as natural transformation

\begin{align*} \mathcal{Y} \Rightarrow \prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{Y} \end{align*}

in the presheaf category

in particular if $I$ shaped products exists, this restricts to a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \mathcal{Y} \Rightarrow \prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{Y} \end{align*}
