fiber sequence of mapping anima, pointed anima and target splits for a group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a connected anima valued group with basepoint $e \in G$, $X \in \mathrm{An}_*$ a pointed anima. Then the fiber sequence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,G) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,G) \rightarrow G \end{align*}

is left split fiber sequence in the category of Anima

Proof

consider the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,G) \xrightarrow{\mathrm{id}, \mathrm{ev}_{x}}& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,G) \times G \\ \xrightarrow{\mathrm{id}, t_*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,G) \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,G) \\ \xrightarrow{- \cdot -}& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,G) \end{align*}

where $t_*$ is given by $G \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,G)$

Then the composition

  1. $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,G) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,G) \rightarrow G$ is nullhomotopic, so in particular lifts to a map of the fiber $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,G) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,G)$
  2. the composition $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,G) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,G) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,G)$ is given by the canonical forgetful map, so in particular lifts to the identity.
