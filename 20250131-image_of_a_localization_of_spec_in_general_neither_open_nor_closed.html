image of a localization of Spec in general neither open nor closed

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring

Proof

Consider

\begin{align*} A =& \mathbb{Z} \\ S =& \mathbb{Z} \setminus (p) \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Spec}(S^{-1}A) = \{(0), (p)\} \end{align*}

which is neither open nor closed in $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$ since $(0)$ is a generic point
