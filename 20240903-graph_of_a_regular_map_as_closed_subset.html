graph of a regular map as closed subset

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a regular map between quasi-projective varieties Then the graph is a closed subset

Proof

By assumption $Y \subseteq \mathbb{P}_K^n$. Hence we may consider the composition

and note, that the the graph

\begin{align*} \Gamma_f = \Gamma_f \cap (X \times Y) \end{align*}

thus w.l.o.g. we may assume that $Y = \mathbb{P}_K^n$.

Consider the identity on $\mathbb{P}_K^n$.

\begin{align*} (f,id): X \times \mathbb{P}_k^n \rightarrow \mathbb{P}_k^n \times \mathbb{P}_k^{n} \end{align*}

is a regular morphism with

\begin{align*} (f, \mathrm{id})^{-1}(\Gamma_{\mathrm{id}}) = (f, \mathrm{id})^{-1}((Q,Q) \vert Q \in \mathbb{P}_k^n \end{align*}
