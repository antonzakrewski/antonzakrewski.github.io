free module is not a categorical property

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category of modules over a monoid Then being a free module is not a categorical property

Proof

follows from:

  • morita equivalence of a ring with the matrix ring

so e.g. choose $M_2(\mathbb{F}_2)$ and $\mathbb{F}_2$. Then $\mathbb{F}_2$ is a free $\mathbb{F}_2$ module, but not free as $M_2(\mathbb{F}_2)$ module.
