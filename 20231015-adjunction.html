adjunction in 2-cat

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{L} \dashv \mathcal{R}$ adjoints. Then the natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \tau: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(L(-), -) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(-, \mathcal{R}(-)) \end{align*}

is said to be the adjunction

Note that by stating definitions we get for

\begin{align*} f_C: C' \rightarrow& C \\ f_D: D \rightarrow& D' \end{align*}

and $g: \mathcal{L}(C) \rightarrow D$ commuting diagrams

where for $f: \mathcal{L}(A) \rightarrow B$

\begin{align*} \tau(f) \end{align*}

is the short notation for

\begin{align*} \tau(A,B)(f) \end{align*}

and analogously for $\tau^{-1}$

by taking definitions
