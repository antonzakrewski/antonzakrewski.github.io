limit of additive categories and additive functors is additive

Proposition

Let $I: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}$ be a diagram consisting of additive categories and additive functors Then the limit category is also additive.

Proof

follows from limit of additive categories and additive functors is additive where being an additive category is characterized by existence of finite (co-)products and an morphism being an equivalence.
