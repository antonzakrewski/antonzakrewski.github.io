root of unities and separable Polynomial

Proposition

Let $\overline{K}$ be an algebraically closed field, $X^n - 1$ be a polynomial and $\mathrm{char}(\overline{K}) \not\mid n$. Then $X^n - 1$ is a separable polynomial

Proof

using algebraische Ableitung eines Polynoms und mehrfache Nullstelle, we get

\begin{align*} f' =& n \cdot X^{n-1} \end{align*}

where the only root of $f'$ is $0$, hence $f$ is separable.
