right mapping space as infinity groupoid

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $x,y \in \mathcal{C}_0$ objects. Then the right mapping space is an infinity-groupoid

Proof

follows from:

  • forgetful functor from the infinity slice category as right fibration
  • left fibration mirrored under pullback
  • kan complex and terminal morphism as left/right/kan fibration
  • infinity-groupoid and Kan complex
