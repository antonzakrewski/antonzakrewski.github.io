filtered colimit of injective modules over a noetherian ring is injective

Proposition

Let $A$ be a noetherian ring and $\mathrm{colim} M_i$ a filtered colimit consisting of injective modules. Then $\mathrm{colim} M_i$ is also injective.

Proof

We need to show Baer's criterion, so let $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ an ideal. Then it suffices to show that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{LMod}_{A}}(A,\mathrm{colim} M_i ) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{LMod}_{A}}(\mathfrak{a},\mathrm{colim}M_i) \end{align*}

is an epimorphism.
