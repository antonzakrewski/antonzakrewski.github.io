hamiltonian and product with interval as hamiltonian

Proposition

Let $G$ be a hamiltonian graph and $G \times \Delta^1$ the product with the interval. Then $G\times \Delta^1$ is also hamiltonian

Proof

find a hamiltonian cycle $C_n \subseteq G$. Denote this as $c_1,...,c_n$. Then consider

\begin{align*} (c_1,0), (c_1,1),(c_2,1)...,(c_n,1),(c_n,0),(c_{n-1},0),...,(c_1,0) \end{align*}

which is a $C_{2n}$
