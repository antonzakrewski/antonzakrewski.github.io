monomorphisms in PrL stable are exactly fully faithful functors

Proposition

The class of monomorphisms in category PrL stable is precisely the class of fully faithful functors

Proof

monomorphisms are fully faithful

Recall that the forgetful functor $\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\mathrm{st}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}$ preserves limits, hence monomorphisms in $\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\mathrm{st}}$ are monomorphisms in $\mathrm{Cat}$ (cf. limit preserving functor preserves monomorphisms, monomorphisms in category Cat). Hence for any $x,y \in \mathcal{C}$ and any monomorphism $\mathcal{F}: x \rightarrow y$, the map $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(x), \mathcal{F}(y))$ is an inclusion of connected components, i.e. an isomorphism on $\pi_1$ and above. So it remains to show that it is also a $\pi_0$ isomorphism.

fully faithful implies monomorphisms

Suppose $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is a fully faithful functor, then the functor $\mathcal{C} \times_{\mathcal{D}} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is also fully faithful (cf. limits in PrL are computed in Cat & fully faithful functors under pullbacks). Moreover, the functor $\mathcal{C} \times_{\mathcal{D}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is essentially surjective (cf. relative diagonal is split mono)
