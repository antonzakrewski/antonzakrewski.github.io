dualizable stable category and Ab6

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable presentable category. TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ is an Ab6 category
  2. $\mathcal{C}$ is dualizable

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

use that the colimit functor $\mathrm{colim}: \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is both a left adjoint & right adjoint, hence preserves limits and colimits. Moreover, we can lift filtered colimits and products from $\mathcal{C}$ to $\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C})$ via the yoneda embedding

Now in $\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C})$ satisfies Ab6, hence so does $\mathcal{C}$.

2) $\implies$ 1)
