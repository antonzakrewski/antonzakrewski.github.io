degree under the induced ring homomorphism for the polynomial rings

Proposition

Let \(A,B\) be rings, $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ a ring-homomorphism and $\varphi^X: A[X] \rightarrow B[X]$ the induced ring homomorphism for the polynomial rings then for $f \in A[X]$ and $\varphi^X(f)$ it follows that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{deg}(\varphi^X(f)) \leq& \mathrm{deg}(f) \end{align*}

Proof

Let $\varphi^X(f) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \alpha_i X^i$, then by construction, there exists an $\beta_i \in A$ such that

\begin{align*} \varphi^X(\beta_n X^n) =& \alpha_n X^n \end{align*}

Hence $\mathrm{deg}(f) \geq n$, as

\begin{align*} f = \alpha_n X^n + ... \end{align*}
