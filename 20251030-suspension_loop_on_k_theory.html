suspension/loop on k-theory

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category and $\Sigma: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ or $\Omega: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ the suspension resp. loop space functor. Then

\begin{align*} K_*(\Sigma) = K_*(\Omega) = - \mathrm{id}_{K(\mathcal{C})} \end{align*}

Proof

consider the bifiber sequence

which shows

\begin{align*} [X] + [\Sigma X] = 0 \end{align*}
