n-homotopy category functor

Definition / Proposition

the inclusion of $\infty,1$ categories $\mathrm{Cat}_{(n,1)} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty,1}$ admits a left adjoint, which is given by

  1. $h_n \mathcal{C}^{\simeq} \simeq \tau_{\leq n} \mathcal{C}^{\simeq}$
  2. $\mathrm{Hom}_{h_n \mathcal{C}}([x],[y]) = \tau_{\leq n-1} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y)$
