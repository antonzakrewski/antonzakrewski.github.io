mapping spectrum for suspension spectra are stable maps

Proposition

Let $X$ be a compact, pointed anima and $Y$ any anima. Then the mapping spectrum of the suspension spectra is given by Then we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[X], \mathbb{S}[Y]) \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_n \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\Sigma^n X, \Sigma^n Y) \end{align*}

Proof

using the explicit description of spectrification and forgetting we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[X], \mathbb{S}[Y]) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X, \Omega^{\infty} \mathbb{S}[Y]) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X, \mathrm{colim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \Omega^n \Sigma^n Y) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X, \Omega^n \Sigma^n Y) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(\Sigma^n X, \Sigma^n Y) \\ \end{align*}

where we have used

  1. the adjunction
  2. the explicit description of spectrification and forgetting
  3. compactness of $X$
