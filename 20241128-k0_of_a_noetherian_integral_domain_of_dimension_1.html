k0 of a noetherian integral domain of dimension 1

Proposition

Let $R$ be a noetherian Integral domain with dimension $1$ Then

\begin{align*} (\mathrm{det} \oplus \mathrm{rk}): K_0(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{Pic}(R) \oplus \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

is a welldefined isomorphism

Proof

welldefined, i.e. $H_0(R) = \mathbb{Z}$

As $R$ is an integral domain, this follows from

  1. zariski spectrum of an integral domain as connected space
  2. connected spectrum and constant rank

surjective

  • short exact sequence of k0 and picard
  • exact sequence and epimorphisms

injective

using minimal kernel and monomorphism

Let $K \in \mathrm{ker}(\mathrm{det} \oplus \mathrm{rk})$. Then $K = [P] - [Q]$ (cf. ?) with

  1. $\mathrm{rk}(P) = \mathrm{rk}(Q)$
  2. $\mathrm{det}(P) \cong \mathrm{det}(Q)$

It remains to show that $P \cong Q$

$\mathrm{rk}(P) = \mathrm{rk}(Q) = 0$

Then $P = Q = 0$ (cf. ??)

$\mathrm{rk}(P) = \mathrm{rk}(Q) = 1$

Let $\mathrm{rk}(P) = 1$, then $P = \mathrm{det}(P) \cong \mathrm{det}(Q) \cong Q$

$\mathrm{rk}(P) = \mathrm{rk}(Q) = n > 1$

By Serre-Bass we may concldue that

\begin{align*} P \cong L \otimes R^{n-1} \end{align*}

for some algebraic line bundle $L$

This shows that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{det}(P) \cong& \mathrm{det}(L \oplus R^{n-1}) \\ \cong& \mathrm{det}(L) \otimes_R \mathrm{det}(R^{n-1}) \\ \cong& L \otimes R \cong& L \end{align*}

Analogously we conclude that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{det}(Q) \cong L' \end{align*}

for some algebraic line bundle.

Thus $L \cong \mathrm{det}(P) \cong \mathrm{det}(Q) \cong L'$

Furthermore

\begin{align*} [P] = [L \oplus R^{n-1}] = [L' \oplus R^{n-1}] = [Q] \end{align*}

which shows that

\begin{align*} [P] - [Q] = 0 \end{align*}
