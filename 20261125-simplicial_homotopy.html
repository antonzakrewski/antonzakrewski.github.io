simplicial homotopy

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $f,g: X \rightarrow Y$ a morphism in $\mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{C})$. Then a simplicial homotopy from $f$ to $g$ is a map

\begin{align*} h: (\pi^{\mathrm{op}})^*(X) \rightarrow (\pi^{\mathrm{op}})^*(Y) \end{align*}

such that $\iota_0^*(h) = f$ and $\iota_1^*(h) = g$.
