animation of a category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a \(1\)-category which is cocomplete and generated under colimits by $\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}}$ (cf. 1-sfp object). Then the animation of $\mathcal{C}$ is defined as (\(\infty\)-version) sInd of an infinity category
