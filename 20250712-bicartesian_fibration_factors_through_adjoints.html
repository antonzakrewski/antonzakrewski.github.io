unstraightening of bicartesian fibration factors through adjoints

Proposition

Let $p: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a cocartesian fibration. TFAE:

  1. bicartesian fibration.
  2. the cocartesian unstraightening factors through CatL
  3. the cartesian unstraightening factors through CatR

think about the anti-equivalence of CatL and CatR

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

corollary of:

  • globally cartesian fibration is locally cartesian
  • globally cocartesian fibration is locally cocartesian
  • adjunction and bicartesian fibration

2) $\implies$ 3)

  • cartesian fibration and composition of locally cartesian lifts
  • cocartesian fibration and composition of locally cocartesian lifts
  • right adjoints compose & left adjoints compose

3) $\implies$ 1)
