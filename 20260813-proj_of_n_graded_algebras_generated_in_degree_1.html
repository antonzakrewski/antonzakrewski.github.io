Proj of N-graded algebras generated in degree 1

Definition

Let $k$ be a commutative ring, $A$ a \(\mathbb{N}\)-graded algebra. Then we define

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Proj}(A): \mathrm{CAlg}_k \rightarrow& \mathrm{Set} \\ R \mapsto& \{\textrm{ quotient lines } A_1 \otimes_k R \twoheadrightarrow L, \textrm{ such that } \mathrm{Sym}_k(A_1) \rightarrow \mathrm{Sym}_R(L) \textrm{ factors through } A \} \end{align*}
