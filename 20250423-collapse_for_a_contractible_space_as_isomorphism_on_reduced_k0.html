collapse for a contractible space as isomorphism on reduced k0

Proposition

Let $X$ be a compact hausdorff space, $A \subseteq X$ contractible space. Then reduced topological K_0

\begin{align*} \tilde{K}_{\mathbb{K}}(X/A) \rightarrow \tilde{K}_{\mathbb{K}}(X) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism
