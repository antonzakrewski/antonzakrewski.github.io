currying of limit preserving functors into cartesian commutative monoids

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be complete infinity categories Then there exists a canonical equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Fun}^{R}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{R}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{D})) \end{align*}
