colimit of yoneda embedding as constant presheaf

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small infinity category, $\upgamma_{\mathcal{C}}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ the infinity yoneda embedding. Then the colimit is a constant presheaf

\begin{align*} \mathrm{const}(*) \end{align*}

Proof

Let $P \in \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ be any infinity presheaf. note that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(\mathrm{colim} \upgamma_C(c), P) \simeq& \mathrm{lim}_{\mathcal{C}} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(\upgamma_{\mathcal{C}}(c), P) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{lim}_{\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}}(P(c) \end{align*}
