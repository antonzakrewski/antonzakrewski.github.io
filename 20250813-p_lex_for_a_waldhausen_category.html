P_lex for a waldhausen category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small waldhausen category, then define

\begin{align*} \mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{lex}}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

as full subcategory containing presheaves $\mathcal{F}$ which are

  1. additive, i.e. map finite coproducts to finite products
  2. each cofiber of an inclusions

gets mapped to a fiber $\mathcal{F}(\mathrm{cofib}(\iota)) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(y) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(x)$d
