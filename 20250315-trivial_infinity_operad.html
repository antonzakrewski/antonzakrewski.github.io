trivial infinity operad

Definition

The trivial infinity operad is defined as inclusion of the nerve inclusion

\begin{align*} N(\mathrm{Triv}) \rightarrow N(\mathrm{Fin}_*) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Triv} \subseteq \mathrm{Fin}_*$ is the wide subcategory containing inert morphisms in Fin*

Remark

This is the operad, which contains only the identity operation
