basis of a final topology

Proposition

Let $X$ be a set, $(X_i,\mathcal{T}_i)$ be a class of topological spaces and $f_i: X_i \rightarrow X$ be a class of set-theoretic maps Then for the final topology

\begin{align*} \{U \subseteq X \vert \forall f_i : f_i^{-1}[U] \in \mathcal{T}_i\} \end{align*}
