coordinate ring functor essentially surjective

Proposition

the coordinate ring functor from the category of algebraic sets $\mathrm{AlgSet}$ to the category of affine algebras $\mathrm{AffAlg}$ is essentially surjective

Proof

Let $B$ be an affine k-algebra. Then as $B$ is finite type, there exists a algebraic epimorphism of A-Algebras

\begin{align*} \varphi: K[X_1,...,X_n] \twoheadrightarrow B \end{align*}

Then it follows that the kernel is a radical ideal (cf. radical ideal and reduced quotient ring).
