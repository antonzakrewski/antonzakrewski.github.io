finite free cocompletion

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category Then $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{fin}} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ is defined as full subcategory of the infinity presheaf category

  1. containing $\mathcal{C}$ under the infinity yoneda embedding
  2. which is also finitely cocomplete
