cofibration and retraction

Proposition

Let $X,A$ be topological spaces.

TFAE:

  1. $\iota: A \rightarrow X$ is a cofibration
  2. there exists a Retraction $R: X \times [0,1] \rightarrow A \times [0,1] \cup X \times \{0\} \subseteq X \times [0,1]$
  1. there exists a Retraction $R: X \times [0,1] \rightarrow A \times [0,1] \coprod_{A \times \{0\}} X \times \{0\} \subseteq X \times [0,1]$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let

\begin{align*} H: A \times [0,1] \rightarrow A \times [0,1] \cup X \times \{0\} \end{align*}

be the inclusion.

Then we can, by Homotopy Extension Property, extend $\mathrm{id}_{A} = H(-,0)$ to $\mathrm{id}_{X}: X \rightarrow X \times \{0\}$ and thus get a homotopy

\begin{align*} \overline{H}: X \times [0,1] \rightarrow& A \times [0,1] \cup X \times \{0\} \end{align*}

which by construction is our desired retraction

2) $\implies$ 3)

follows from the identity map and retractable product with interval homeomorphic to pushout

3) $\implies$ 1)

Suppose there exist a map $f: X \rightarrow Y$ and a homotopy

\begin{align*} H: A \times [0,1] \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

Then by universal property of a pushout $A \times [0,1] \coprod_{A \times \{0\}} X \times \{0\}$, there exists a continuous map

\begin{align*} H': A \times [0,1] \coprod_{A \times \{0\}} X \times \{0\} \rightarrow& Y \\ (a,t) \mapsto& H(a,t) \\ (x,0) \mapsto& f(x) \end{align*}

Thus we can precompose with the Retraction and get a continuous map

\begin{align*} H' \circ R: X \times [0,1] \rightarrow& Y \end{align*}
