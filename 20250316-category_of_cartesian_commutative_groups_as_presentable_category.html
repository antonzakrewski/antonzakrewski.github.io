category of cartesian commutative groups as presentable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an presentable infinity category Then so is the category of cartesian commutative groups

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from functor category with generalized segal condition for a small collection as presentable category for

  1. the segal maps (cf. category of cartesian commutative monoids as presentable category)
  2. the shear map
\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}( \langle 2\rangle) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}( \langle 1\rangle) \times \mathcal{F}( \langle 1\rangle) \end{align*}
