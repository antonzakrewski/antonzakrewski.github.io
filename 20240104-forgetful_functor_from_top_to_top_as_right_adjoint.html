forgetful functor from top* to top as right adjoint

Proposition

Given category Top* and category Top, the forgetful functor from top* to top is a right adjoint to the topological pointification functor

Proof(sketch)

Given by

\begin{align*} \eta: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{Top}}(X,\mathrm{Forget}(Y,y)) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Top}_*}((X \coprod *,*), (Y,y)) f \mapsto (f') \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} f': (X \coprod *,*) \rightarrow& (Y,y) \\ * \mapsto& y \\ x \mapsto& f(x) \end{align*}
