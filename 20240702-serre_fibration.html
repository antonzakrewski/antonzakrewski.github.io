serre fibration

Definition

Let $(X,\mathcal{T}_X)$ and $(Y,\mathcal{T}_Y)$ be topological spaces and $p: X \rightarrow Y$ a continuous map Then $p$ is said to be a serre fibration if for all $n \geq 0$ $p$ has the right lifting property against

\begin{align*} \mathrm{inc}: D^n \times \{0\} \rightarrow D^n \times [0,1] \end{align*}
