image of a map of vector bundles

Definition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $E_1,E_2 \in \mathrm{Vect}(X)$ topological vector bundles and $f: E_1 \rightarrow E_2$ a morphism of locally constant rank. Then the image is defined as subbundle of a topological vector bundle

\begin{align*} \{f(x) \vert x \in E_1\} \end{align*}
