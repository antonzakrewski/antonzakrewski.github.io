semiorthogonal decomposition of the category of pairs

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{B} \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ be a abelian full subcategory which is closed under subobjects and quotient objects Then for the quasi-abelian category of pairs $E_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}}$ there exists a sequence

where

  1. $\iota$ is a right adjoint
  2. $\pi$ is a left adjoint

In particular it induces a semiorthogonal decomposition

Proof

a)

Consider

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathcal{B} \rightarrow& E_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}} \\ b \mapsto& (0 \rightarrow b) \\ \pi: E_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}) \rightarrow \mathcal{B} \\ (b \rightarrow a) \mapsto& a/b \end{align*}

and observe that it is an adjoint

b)

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ev}_0: E_{\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B}} \rightarrow& \mathcal{B} (b \rightarrow a) \mapsto& b \tilde{\Delta}: \mathcal{B} \rightarrow E_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}} b \mapsto& (b = b) \end{align*}

and observe that $\tilde{\Delta}$ is the right kan extension.
