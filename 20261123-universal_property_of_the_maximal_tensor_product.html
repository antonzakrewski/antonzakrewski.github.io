universal property of the maximal tensor product

Proposition

For every $C^*$ Algebra, we haev

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}(A \otimes_{\mathrm{max}} B, D) \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{*\mathrm{Alg}}(A \otimes^{\mathrm{alg}} B, D) \\ \subseteq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Set}}(A \time sB, D) \end{align*}

Proof
