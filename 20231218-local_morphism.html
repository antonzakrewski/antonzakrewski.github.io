local morphism

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a locally small category, $f: X \rightarrow Y \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y)$ a morphism and $S \subseteq \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$ a collection of morphism.

Then $f$ is said to be \(S\)-local, if for each \(S\)-local object $C \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ the induced morphism from the contravariant hom-functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(f,C): \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y,C) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,C) \end{align*}
