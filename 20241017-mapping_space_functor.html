mapping space functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category Then the mapping space functor is defined as infinity functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,-): \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Spc} \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Spc}$ is the category Spc
