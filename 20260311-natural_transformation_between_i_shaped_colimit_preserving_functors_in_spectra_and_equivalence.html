natural transformation between I-shaped colimit preserving functors in spectra and equivalence

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be cocomplete categories, $\mathcal{K}$ be a collection of categories and $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ \(\mathcal{K}\)-shaped colimit preserving functors. Let $\alpha: \mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ be natural transformation and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{E} = \{X \in \mathrm{Sp} \vert \alpha(X) \textrm{ is equivalence}\} \end{align*}

Then $\mathcal{E}$ is closed under $\mathcal{K}$ shaped colimits.

Proof

Let $I: K \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a diagram for $K\in \mathcal{K}$ and $\mathrm{colim}_{K} I \in \mathcal{C}$. Then it follows from uniqueness, that $\alpha_{\mathrm{colim}_K I}$ identifies with $\mathrm{colim}_K \alpha(I)$.
