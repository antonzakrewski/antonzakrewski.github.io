Mapping Space inverting some morphisms as functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $W \subseteq \mathcal{C}_1$ a subset of 1-morphisms. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{W}(\mathcal{C},-): \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty} \rightarrow \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

is an infinity functor

Proof
