cofinal map of simplicial sets

Definition

Let $S,T$ be simplicial sets and $p: S \rightarrow T$ a morphism of simplicial sets Then $p$ is said to be cofinal, if for any right fibration the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{T}(T,X) \rightarrow \mathrm{Map}_{T}(S,X) \end{align*}

is a homotopy equivalence
