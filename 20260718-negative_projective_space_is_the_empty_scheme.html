Proposition

The functor $\mathbb{P}_k^{-1} \coloneqq \mathbb{P}_k^{\emptyset}$ is the empty scheme.

Proof

observe that any quotient of the zero module is the zero module. moreover vector bundle of constant rank 0 is trivial shows it is not a line bundle over a nonzero ring. It is trivially invertible as module over the zero ring.
