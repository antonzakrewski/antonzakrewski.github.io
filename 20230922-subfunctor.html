Subfunctor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $F: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ a contravariant functor A Subfunctor is a contravariant functor such that $G: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$

  1. for $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ it follows, that $G(A) \subseteq F(A)$
  2. for $f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,B)$ it follows, that $G(f) = F(f)_{\vert G(B)}$ (see: restriction)
