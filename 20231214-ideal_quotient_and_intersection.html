ideal quotient and intersection

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a}_i, \mathfrak{b} \subseteq A$ be ideals.

Then it holds for the ideal quotient

\begin{align*} ( \left(\bigcap_{i \in I} \mathfrak{a}_i \right) : \mathfrak{b}) = \bigcap_{i \in I} (\mathfrak{a}_i : \mathfrak{b}) \end{align*}

Proof
