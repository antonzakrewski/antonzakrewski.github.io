character of a finite dimensional representation

Definition

Let $V$ be a finite dimension \(A\) representation Then the character is defined as

\begin{align*} \chi_V: A \rightarrow& k \\ a \mapsto& \chi_v(a) = \mathrm{tr}(p(a)) \end{align*}

trace is independent of basis

Remark

Now $[A,A]$ is the span by ab - ba$ for $a,b \in A$. Then $\chi_{V}([A,A]) = 0$ since traces are invariant under cyclic permutation (cf. trace commutes with cyclic permutations). So we typically view $\chi_V$ as function $\chi_{V}: A/[A,A] $\rightarrow k$.
