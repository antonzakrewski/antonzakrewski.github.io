3-functor formalism

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{E})$ be a geometric setup. Then a 3-functor formalism is a lax symmetric monoidal infinity functor

\begin{align*} D: \mathrm{Corr}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{E}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty} \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Corr}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{E})$ is considered with the canonical symmetric monoidal structure and $\mathrm{cat}_{\infty}$ with the cartesian monoidal structure. see:

  • category of correspondences
