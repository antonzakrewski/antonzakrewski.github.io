ring of continuous functions

Definition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $\mathbb{K} \in \{\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C} \}$ a topological ring Then the ring of continuous functions is defined

\begin{align*} C_{\mathbb{K}}(X) \end{align*}
