points of the presheaf category are pro objects

Proposition

Let $T = \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ be a presheaf category Then the points are given by $\mathrm{Pro}(\mathcal{C})^{\mathrm{op}}$.

see:

  • category of pro-objects

Proof

we compute

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Pt}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})) \simeq& \mathrm{Geom}(\mathrm{An}, \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Geom}(\mathrm{An}, \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}, \mathrm{lex}}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{An})) \\ \subseteq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}), \mathrm{An}) \end{align*}

where we have 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}, \mathrm{lex}}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}), \mathrm{An}) \arrow[hookrightarrow, ]{r}{} \dar{\siemq} & \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}), \mathrm{An}) \dar{\simeq} \\
\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{lex}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{An}) \arrow[hookrightarrow, ]{r}{}& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{An})
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
