total stiefel whitney class

Definition

Let $\pi: E \rightarrow B$ be a spherical fibration Then the total stiefel whitney class is defined as

\begin{align*} \sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} w_n(\pi) \in H^*(\mathrm{Th}(\pi),\mathbb{F}_2)^{^} \end{align*}

in the completed cohomology ring
