generated sigma algebra and subset

Proposition

Let $X$ be a set, $\mathcal{A} \subseteq \mathcal{B} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)$ family of sets Then the generated sigma algebra

\begin{align*} \sigma(\mathcal{A}) \subseteq \sigma(\mathcal{B}) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from the characterization of $\sigma(\mathcal{A})$ as smallest sigma algebra containing $\mathcal{A}$, where $\mathcal{B} \subseteq \sigma(\mathcal{A})$
