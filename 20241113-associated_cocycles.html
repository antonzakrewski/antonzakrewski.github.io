associated cocycles to a principal g-bundles

Proposition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a principal g-bundle and $U_i$ a trivializing cover. Then there exist \(G\)-equivariant homeomorph

\begin{align*} \mathrm{triv}^i: U_{i} \times G \cong p^{-1}[U_{i}] \end{align*}

Denote by $U_{i,j} \coloneqq U_i \cap U_j$. Then as these \(G\)-equivariant homeomorphisms are compatible with $p: E \rightarrow B$, there exist \(G\)-equivariant homeomorphisms

Let $s_{i,j}: U_{i,j} \rightarrow G$ be the map corresponding to $\mathrm{triv}^j_{\vert U_{i,j}}^{-1} \circ \mathrm{triv}^i_{\vert U_{i,j}}$:

  1. an element $(u,g)$ maps to some $(u,g')$ since both $\mathrm{triv}^i$ are compatible with $p$
  2. $\mathrm{triv}^i_{\vert U_{i,j}}, \mathrm{triv}^i_{\vert U_{i,j}}$ are \(G\)-equivariant, hence $(u,g) = g \cdot (u,1)$ or the map $U_{i,j} \times G \rightarrow U_{i,j} \times G$ is determined by mapping $(u,1) \mapsto (u,g)$ for some $g \in G$

Then $s_{i,j}$ satisfy the cocycle conditions, i.e. for arbitrary $u \in U_{i,j}$

  1. $s_{i,i}(u) = 1$
  2. $s_{i,j}(u) = s_{i,j}(u)^{-1}$

and for arbitrary $u \in U_{i} \cap U_j \cap U_k$

  1. $s_{i,k}(u) = s_{j,k}(u) \cdot s_{i,j}(u)$

Proof
