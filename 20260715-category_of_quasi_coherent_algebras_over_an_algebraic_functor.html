category of quasi-coherent algebras over an algebraic functor

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor. Then the category of quasi-coherent lines $\mathrm{Mod}_X$ is defined as image of the functor of quasi-coherent objects for the functor from commutative rings to commutative Algebras in Ab via extension of scalars

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ext}: \mathrm{CAlg}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat} \end{align*}

i.e. $\mathcal{C} = \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{op}}$

Proof

follows from:

  • some monodial functor argument
  • lax monoidal infinity functor induces functor on commutative algebra objects
  • extension of scalars is symmetric monoidal
