R-local equivalence between R-local anima is an equivalence

Proposition

Let $R$ be an spectrum and $X,Y$ be a R-local Anima and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a map. TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an R-local equivalence
  2. $f$ is an equivalence

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
