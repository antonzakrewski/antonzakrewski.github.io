bousfield localization of a cartesian closed category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a cartesian closed category and $S \subseteq \mathcal{D}$ a collection of morphisms. Let $\mathcal{C} = \mathcal{D}[S^{-1}} \subseteq \mathcal{D}$ be a left bousfield localization. Suppose that $S$ is closed under $- \times c$ for $c \in \mathcal{C}$.

Then $\mathcal{C}$ is also cartesian closed:
