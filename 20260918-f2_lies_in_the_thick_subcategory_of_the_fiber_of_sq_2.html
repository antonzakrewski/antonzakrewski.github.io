f2 lies in the thick subcategory of the fiber of Sq^2

Proposition

Let $Y$ be the fiber of $\mathrm{Sq}^2: \mathbb{F}_2 \rightarrow \Sigma^2 \mathbb{F}_2$. Then $\mathbb{F}_2$ lies in the thick stable subcategory generated by $Y$.

Proof

observe that $\mathrm{Sq}^2$ is a nilpotent self-map, since $(\mathrm{Sq}^2)^4 \simeq 0$: This follows from $\mathrm{Sq}^2 \mathrm{Sq}^2 \simeq \mathrm{Sq}^3 \mathrm{Sq}^1$ and $\mathrm{Sq}^1 \mathrm{Sq}^3 \simeq 0$. Hence

\begin{align*} (\mathrm{Sq}^2)^4 \simeq& \mathrm{Sq}^3 (\mathrm{Sq}^1 \mathrm{Sq}^3) \mathrm{Sq}^1 \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Sq}^3 \cdot 0 \cdot \mathrm{Sq}^1 \\ \simeq& 0 \end{align*}

Now thick subcategory of the fiber/cofiber of a nilpotent self-map contains X shows this claim for $\mathbb{F}_2 = X$ and $Y = \mathrm{fib}(\mathrm{Sq}^2)$.
