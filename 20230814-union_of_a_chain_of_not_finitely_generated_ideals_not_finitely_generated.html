union of a chain of non-finitely generated ideals non-finitely generated

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $\mathfrak{a}_1 \subseteq \mathfrak{a}_2 ...$ a chain of ideals, such that $\mathfrak{a}_1,....$ are not finitely generated ideals. Then $\mathfrak{m} \coloneqq \bigcup_{i \in \mathbb{N}} \mathfrak{a}_i$ is also not finitely generated

Proof

$\mathfrak{m}$ is a welldefined ideal, since Vereinigung von einer Kette von Idealen als Ideal.

Suppose $\mathfrak{m}$ is finitely generated, then there exist $a_1,...,a_n \in \mathfrak{m}$ such that $(a_1,...,a_n) = \mathfrak{m}$. Furthermore, there exists an $m \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $a_1,...,a_n \in \mathfrak{a}_m$. Hence $\mathfrak{m} = (a_1,...,a_n) \subseteq \mathfrak{a}_m \subseteq \mathfrak{m}$, contradiction to the assumption that each $\mathfrak{a}_i$ is not finitely generated.

Therefore $\mathfrak{m}$ is not finitely generated.
