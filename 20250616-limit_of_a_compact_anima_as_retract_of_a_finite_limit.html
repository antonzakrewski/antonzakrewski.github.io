limit of a compact anima as retract of a finite limit

Proposition

Let $X$ a compact anima and $\mathcal{F}: X \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a diagram where $\mathcal{C}$ admits (sufficiently many) limits. Then the limit - if it exists - is a retract of a finite limit.

proof

Follows as there exists a finite anima $X_{f}$ and an retraction $X \rightarrow X_f \rightarrow X$.

Then apply retract of indexing categories and limits
