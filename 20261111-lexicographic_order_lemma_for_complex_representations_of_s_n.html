lexicographic order lemma for complex representations of S_n

Proposition

Let $\lambda > \mu$. Then

\begin{align*} \alpha_{\lambda} \mathbb{C}[S_n] \beta_{\mu} = 0 \end{align*}

if $\lambda > \mu$

Proof

it suffices to show $\alpha_{\lambda} g \beta_{\mu} = 0$ for all $g \in S_n$. Fix $g$ and write $T = T_{\lambda}, T' = g T_{\mu}$. As in linear functional lemma for complex representations of S_n we wil find $t = (rs) \in P_{\lambda}$ and $g^{-1} t g \in Q_{\mu}$.

Similarly, this is equivalent to finding $(rs)$ such that $r_is$ same row of $T$ and the same column of $T'$.

  3. $\lambda_1 < \mu_1$
