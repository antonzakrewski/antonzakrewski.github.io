n-truncated object in spectra is trivial

Proposition

The only n-truncated object in the category of Spectra is the zero object

Proof

Let $X$ be an n-truncated spectrum, then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}(\mathbb{S}[k],X) \cong * \end{align*}

so in particular $\pi_k(X) \cong \pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbb{S}[k],X)) \cong 0$ is trivial for $k \in \mathbb{Z}$. Hence by whitehead theorem for spectra the initial map $0 \rightarrow X$ is an equivalence
