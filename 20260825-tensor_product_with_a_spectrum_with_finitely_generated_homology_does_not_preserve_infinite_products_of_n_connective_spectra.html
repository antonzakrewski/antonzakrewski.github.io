tensor product with a spectrum with finitely generated homology does not preserve infinite products of n-connective spectra

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum such that $H_*(X,\mathbb{Z})$ is degreewise finitely generated. Then $X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} -$ does not preserve infinite products of $n$ connective spectra.

Proof

should be something like infinite product and infinite coproduct do not commute in the category of modules.
