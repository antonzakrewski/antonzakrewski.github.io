reedy's lemma

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}, \mathrm{Cof}, \mathrm{Fib})$ be a model category Given a pullback

where

  1. $p$ is a fibration
  2. $f$ is a weak equivalence
  3. $C,A$ are fibrant objects

Then the upper map

\begin{align*} C \times_A B \rightarrow B \end{align*}

is also a weak equivalence
