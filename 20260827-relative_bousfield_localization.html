relative bousfield localization

Definition / Proposition

Let $E,F$ be spectra such that $\langle F\rangle \leq \langle E\rangle$ or equivalently $\mathrm{Sp}_{F} \subseteq \mathrm{Sp}_{E}$. Then the inclusion $\mathrm{Sp}_{F} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Sp}_{E}$ admits a symmetric monoidal left adjoint $\mathcal{L}_{F}^{E}: \mathrm{Sp}_{E} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}_{F}$
