Steenrod Algebra as finite graded Algebra

Proposition

The Steenrod Algebra is a finite graded F2-Algebra.

Proof

follows as for each degree $n \in \mathbb{N}$ there only exist finitely many combinations

\begin{align*} \prod \mathrm{Sq}^{n_i} \end{align*}

with $\sum n_i = n$ and $n_i \geq 1$ (since $\mathrm{Sq}^0 = 1$)
