schur lemma over algebraically closed fields

Proposition

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field and $V$ a finite dimensional simple A-representation of an K-Algebra $A$. Then every endomorphism of $V$ is given by multiplication with a scalar $\alpha \in k$.

In other words, $\mathrm{End}(V) = k$.

Proof

Let $f: V \rightarrow V$ be an endomorphism and pick an eigenvalue $\lambda$ of $f$. Then $f - \lambda \mathrm{id}: V \rightarrow V$ is a new homomorphism of $V$ with kernel containing an eigenvector $\lambda$

Now the eigenspace is a subrepresentation: The map given by multiplication with $\lambda$ is an $A$ linear map, hence the eigenspace is the kernel of the sum of two $A$ linear maps.

Now $V$ is simple, hence we get that $f - \lambda \mathrm{id} = 0$ or that $f = \lambda \mathrm{id}$.
