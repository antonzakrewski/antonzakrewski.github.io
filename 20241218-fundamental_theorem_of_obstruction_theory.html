fundamental theorem of obstruction theory for simple maps

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a simple fibration, $A,B$ CW-Complexes

  1. $\varphi_{1,A}^{*}(\theta_1)$ is defined provided adtmits a lift on $\pi_1$
  2. if $\varphi_{n,A}^*(\theta_n)$ is defined and vanishes, then $\varphi_{n,A}^*(\theta_{n+1})$ is defined
  3. if $\varphi_{n,A}^*(\theta_n)$ is defined and vanishes, then a lift up to compatible homotopy exists.

Proof
