explicit description of an arbitrary product of c star algebras

Proposition

Let $A_i$ be a collection of C star algebras. Then the product $\prod A_i$ is given by

\begin{align*} \prod_{i \in I}^{\mathrm{C}^*\mathrm{Alg}} A_i \cong \{(a_i)_{i \in I} \vert \mathrm{sup} \| a_i \|_{\mathrm{max}, A} < \infty\} \end{align*}

with the maximal seminorm $\| (a,i) \|_{\mathrm{max}} = \mathrm{sup} \| a_i \|_{\mathrm{max}}$.

Proof

using the adjunctions (cf. Bd infinity functor from star algebras & inclusion of pre c star algebras creates limits). Here we have that the limit is given by

\begin{align*} \prod_{i \in i}^{\mathrm{C}^*\mathrm{Alg}} A_i \cong \mathrm{Bd}^{\infty}(\prod_{i \in I}^{\mathrm{*}-Alg} A_i) \end{align*}

which is a subset of the set theoretic product $\prod A_i$.

Now we have projections $\prod_{i \in i}^{\mathrm{C}^*\mathrm{Alg}} A_i \rightarrow A_i$ which implies

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sup}_{i \in I} \| a_i \|_{A} \leq \| (a_i) \|_{\mathrm{max}, \prod} < \infty \end{align*}

Now maximal norm estimate for a banach star algebra with C-star identity implies the other direction.
