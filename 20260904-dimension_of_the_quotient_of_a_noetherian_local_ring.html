dimension of the quotient of a noetherian local ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be a noetherian local ring of dimension $d$ and let $f_1,...,f_m \in \mathfrak{m}$. TFAE:

  1. the local ring $R/(f_1,...,f_n)$ has dimension $d-n$
  2. the sequence $(f_1,...,f_n)$ can be completed to a system of parameters

Proof
