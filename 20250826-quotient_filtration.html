quotient filtration

Definition

Let $R = R_0$ be a ring and $\mathrm{GrRMod}$ the category of graded modules and $M$ a graded module. Let $F_s$ be a filtration.

Let $K \hookrightarrow \mathrm{colim} F_s$ be a Subobject. Then the quotient filtration is defined as

\begin{align*} F_s/(K \cap F_s) \end{align*}

where we define

\begin{align*} K \cap F_s = K \times_{\mathrm{colim} F_s} F_s \end{align*}
