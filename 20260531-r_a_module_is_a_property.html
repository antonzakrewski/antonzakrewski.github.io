R/a module in abelian groups is a property

Proposition

Let $R$ be an ordinary ring, $M$ a left r-module and $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq R$ an ideal TFAE:

  1. $M$ admits a unique $R/\mathfrak{a}$ module structure, such that restriction of scalars along $R \rightarrow R/\mathfrak{a}$ of $M$ is isomorphic to $M$
  2. $\mathfrak{a}M = 0$.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows as multiplication with $a$ on $M$ is then equivalent to multiplication with $[a] = 0$.

2) $\implies$ 1)

Recall that an \(R\)-module structure is precisely given by a ring-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: R \rightarrow \mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M) \end{align*}

Let $a \in \mathfrak{a}$, then $\mathfrak{a}M = 0$ implies that $a \cdot m = 0$ for all $m\in M$ or that $\varphi(a)$ is the zero element. Hence Homomorphiesatz für Ringe
