realization and edgewise subdivision of a simplicial Anima

Proposition

There exists a canonical equivalence

\begin{align*} \vert - \vert \simeq \vert \mathrm{sd}^*(-) \vert \end{align*}

as functors $\mathrm{sAn} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$

Proof

consider the infinity yoneda embedding

\begin{align*} \Delta \rightarrow \mathrm{PSh}(\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An}) \simeq \mathrm{sAn} \end{align*}

Then by infinity presheaf category arises by freely adding colimits we get an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}}(\mathrm{sAn}, \mathrm{An}) \simeq \end{align*}

Hence we will show that

  1. both $\vert - \vert$ and $\vert \mathrm{sd}^* (-) \vert$ preserve colimits
  2. both functors are pointwise contractible restricted on the essential image of the yoneda embedding
\begin{align*} \vert \mathrm{sd}^*(\Delta^n) \vert \simeq& * \simeq& \vert \Delta^n \vert \end{align*}

Then both functors are equivalent to the constant functor, since they are terminal objects (cf. pointwise calculation of limits, terminal object in an infinity functor category ).
