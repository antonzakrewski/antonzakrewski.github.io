additive functor from RMod induces module structure for an R,R bimodule

Proposition

Let \(R,S\) be rings, $\mathrm{RMod}, \mathrm{SMod}$ the category of modules. Then an additive functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{RMod} \rightarrow \mathrm{SMod} \end{align*}

induces a \(S,R\)-bimodule structure on $\mathcal{F}(M)$ for an \(R,R\)-bimodule $M \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathrm{ModR})$

Proof

By Note that the right \(R\)-module structure on $M$ is given by a ring-homomorphism

\begin{align*} R^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{End}_{\mathrm{RMod}}(M) \end{align*}

(cf. bimodule as ring homomorphism into endomorphism ring)

Then an additive functor as ring homomorphism on endomorphism rings, hence we get

\begin{align*} R^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{End}_{\mathrm{RMod}}(M) \rightarrow \mathrm{End}_{\mathrm{SMod}}(M) \end{align*}
