subrepresentation of a group

Definition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{BG} \rightarrow \mathrm{Vect}_K$ be a representation of a group. Then a subrepresentation is given by another representation $\mathcal{G}$ together with a G-linear map $\mathcal{G} \rightarrow \mathcal{F}$ which is a monomorphism
