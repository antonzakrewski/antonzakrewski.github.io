hom out of the tensor unit as lax monoidal functor

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a monoidal category. Then the hom-functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(\mathds{1}, -) \end{align*}

is a lax monoidal functor

Proof

Define

\begin{align*} \mathrm{id}: * \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(\mathds{1}, \mathds{1}) \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(\mathds{1}, A) \times \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(\mathds{1}, B) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(\mathds{1}, A \otimes B) (f,g) \mapsto (f \otimes g: 1 \simeq 1 \otimes 1 \rightarrow A \otimes B) \end{align*}
