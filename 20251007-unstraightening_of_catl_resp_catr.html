unstraightening of CatL resp. CatR

Proposition

The straightening-unstraightening restricts to an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Cat}^L) \simeq \mathrm{Bicart}(\mathcal{C}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Cat}^R) \end{align*}

Proof

follows as

  • unstraightening of bicartesian fibration factors through adjoints
