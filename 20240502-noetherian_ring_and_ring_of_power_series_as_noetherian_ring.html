noetherian ring and ring of power series as noetherian ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $A [ [ X ] ]$ the power series ring. TFAE:

  1. $A$ is noetherian
  2. $A [[ X] ]$ is noetherian

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows analogously to the Hilbert's basis theorem

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from the algebrabic ring-epimorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: A[ [X ]] \rightarrow& A \\ (a_i) \mapsto& a_1 \end{align*}

and image of a noetherian ring as noetherian ring
