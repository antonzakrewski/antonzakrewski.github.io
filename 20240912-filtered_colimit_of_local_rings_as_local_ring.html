filtered colimit of local rings as local ring

Proposition

Let $I: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathrm{CRing}$ be a filtered diagram of local rings Then the colimit $R \coloneqq \mathrm{colim} R_i$ in category CRing is also a local ring

Proof

using filtered colimit of rings and maximal ideal and non-unit it suffices to show that

\begin{align*} R \setminus R^{\times} \end{align*}

is an ideal

using:

  • element in a filtered colimit of commutative rings
  • unit in a filtered colimit of commutative rings

additive

Let $r_1,r_2 \in R \setminus R^{\times}$. w.l.o.g. we may assume that $r_1,r_2 \in R_j$ for sufficiently large $j$.

Furthermore there does not exists a $R_k$ where $r_1,r_2$ are units. (cf. unit in a filtered colimit)

Then it follows that $r_1 + r_2 \in R_j$ is not a unit: Otherwise there would be an $R_k$ where neither $r_1,r_2$ are units but their sum. This is a contradiction to $R_k$ being a local ring

multiplicative

non units closed under multiplication

nonzero

Note that $(1,0,....,)$ is always at each $R_k$ nonzero.
