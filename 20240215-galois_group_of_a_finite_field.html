galois group of a finite field

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{F}_q, \mathbb{F}_{q^n}$ be finite fields. Then the galois group is a cyclic group

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{F}_{q^n}, \mathbb{F}_{q}) \cong \mathbb{Z}/ n \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}
