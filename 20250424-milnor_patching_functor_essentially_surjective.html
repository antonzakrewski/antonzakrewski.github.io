milnor patching functor essentially surjective

Proposition

the milnor patching functor for a given milnor square of rings is essentially surjective

Proof

Let $P$ be a finitely generated projective module Then define

\begin{align*} P_1 \coloneqq P \otimes_R R_1 \\ P_2 \coloneqq P \otimes_R R_2 \\ \end{align*}

with

\begin{align*} (P_1 \otimes_R R_1) \otimes_{R_1} S \end{align*}

It follows that $M(P_1,P_2,\alpha) \cong P$
