operad of a family of subsets

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{U} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)$ be a full subcategory. Then the operad $\mathcal{U}^{\otimes}$ is defined via the operad induced by

\begin{align*} \mathcal{U}^{\otimes}((U_1,...,U_n),U) = \begin{cases} * & \mbox{if } \coprod_{i=1}^n U_i \subseteq U, \mathrm{ disjoint} \\ \emptyset & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
