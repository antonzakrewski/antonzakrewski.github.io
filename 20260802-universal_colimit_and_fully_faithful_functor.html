universal colimit and fully faithful functor

Proposition

Let $T$ be a category with pullbacks and \(I\)-indexed colimits. TFAE:

  1. $I$ indexed colimits are universal
  2. for every diagram $X_*: I \rightarrow T$, the counit of the adjunction $T_{/X} \rightarrow \lim_{i} T_{/X_i}$ is an equivalence (cf. descent adjunction)
  3. for every diagram $X_*: I \rightarrow T$, the functor $T_{/X} \rightarrow \lim_{i} T_{/X_i}$ is fully faithful

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 3)

  • fully faithful infinity right adjoint and counit as equivalence

3) $\implies$ 1)
