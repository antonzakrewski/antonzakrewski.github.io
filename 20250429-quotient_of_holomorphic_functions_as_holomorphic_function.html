quotient of holomorphic functions as holomorphic function

Proposition

Let $\Sigma \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be open and $f,g: \Sigma \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ be holomorphic functions. Suppose $g(z_0) \neq 0$ for $z_0 \in \mathbb{C}$, then

\begin{align*} \frac{f}{g} \end{align*}

is also holomorphic with derivative

\begin{align*} \left(\frac{f}{g}\right)'(z_0) = \frac{f'(z_0) g(z_0)-f(z_0)g'(z_0)}{g(z_0)^2} \end{align*}

Proof

Assume w.l.o.g. that $0 \not\in g[\Sigma]$ after taking a sufficiently small open ball around $z_0$. Then

\begin{align*} \frac{f(z_0 + h)}{g(z_0 + h)} - \frac{f(z_0)}{g(z_0)} =& \frac{f(z_0 + h) g(z_0) - f(z_0) g(z_0 + h)}{g(z_0 + h) g(z_0)} \end{align*}

Then let

\begin{align*} \gamma_f(h) \coloneqq& f'(z_0) h - f(z_0 + h) \\ \gamma_g(h) \coloneqq& g'(z_0) h - g(z_0 + h) \end{align*}

in particular it follows that $\gamma_f(h) \rightarrow 0$ for $h \rightarrow 0$.

Then

\begin{align*} \frac{f(z_0 + h) g(z_0) - f(z_0) g(z_0 + h)}{g(z_0 + h) g(z_0)} =& \frac{f(z_0) + h f'(z_0) + h\gamma_f(h)) g(z_0) - f(z_0) ( g(z_0) + g'(z_0) h + h \gamma_g(h))}{gtz_0 + h) g(z_0)} =& \frac{(hf'(z_0) + h\gamma(h)) g(z_0) - f(z_0)((g'(z_0) h + h \gamma_g(h))}{g(z_0 + h) g(z_0)} \end{align*}

with

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_{h \rightarrow 0} \frac{(hf'(z_0) + h\gamma(h)) g(z_0) - f(z_0)((g'(z_0) h + h \gamma_g(h))}{g(z_0 + h) g(z_0)} \cdot \frac{1}{h} =& \frac{f'(z_0) g(z_0) - f(z_0) g'(z_0)}{g(z_0)^2} \end{align*}
