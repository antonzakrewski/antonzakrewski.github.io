flat module spectrum and tensor product is t-exact

Proposition

Let $M$ be a module spectrum over a ring spectrum $E$ . TFAE:

  1. $M$ is a flat module spectrum
  2. the tensor product $M \otimes_{R} -: \mathrm{ModR} \rightarrow& \mathrm{ModR}$ is t-exact

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
