tensor product functor of CAlg

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathrm{CAlg}_A$ the category CAlg_A. Then the tensor product of A-Algebras functor restricts to $\mathrm{CAlg}_A \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Alg}_A$

Proof

follows from

\begin{align*} (\alpha_1 \otimes_A \beta_1) \cdot (\alpha_2 \otimes_A \beta_2) =& (\alpha_1 \cdot \alpha_2 \otimes_A \beta_1 \beta_2) \\ =& (\alpha_2 \otimes_A \beta_2) \cdot (\alpha_1 \otimes_A \beta_1) \end{align*}

and extending to arbitrary elements
