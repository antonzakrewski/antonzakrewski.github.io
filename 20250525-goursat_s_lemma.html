goursat's lemma

Proposition

Let $\Omega \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be open and $f: \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ be holomorphic. Let $T: \Delta^2 \rightarrow \Omega$ be a triangle, then

\begin{align*} \int_{\partial \Delta^2} f(z) \mathrm{d}z = 0 \end{align*}
