left adjoint of continuous right adjoint preserves compact objects for accessible categories

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal, $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be kappa accessible categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be an adjoints $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$. TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ preserves kappa compact objects
  2. $\mathcal{G}$ is kappa continuous in the sense that it preserves kappa filtered colimits

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $I \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a diagram. We want to show that the canonical map $\mathrm{colim}_{I} \mathcal{G}(D_i) \rightarrow \mathcal{G}(\mathrm{colim}_{I} D_i)$ is an equivalence. Now a kappa presentable category admits a set of kappa compact generators $\{C_j\} \subseteq \mathcal{C}$, so it suffices to show that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(C_j, \mathrm{colim}_{I} \mathcal{G}(D_i)) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(C_j, \mathcal{G}(\mathrm{colim}_{I} D_i)) \end{align*}

is an equivalence

But we may use that $C_i$ is $\kappa$ compact so we get natural equivalences

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(C_j, \mathrm{colim}_{I} \mathcal{G}(D_i)) \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{I} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(C_j, \mathcal{G}(D_i)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{I} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(C_j), D_i) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(C_j), \mathrm{colim}_{I} D_i) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(C_j,\mathcal{G}(\mathrm{colim}_{I} D_i)) \end{align*}

where we have used that $\mathcal{F}(C_j)$ is compact.

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from: left adjoint of a continuous right adjoint preserves compact objects
