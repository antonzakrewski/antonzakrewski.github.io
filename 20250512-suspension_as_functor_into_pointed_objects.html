suspension as functor into pointed objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category with terminal object and (enough finite) colimits Then the suspension functor factors as 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{C} \arrow[]{rd}{} \rar{} & & \mathcal{C} \\
& \mathcal{C}_{*/} \arrow[]{rd}{} & \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Proof

Note that the suspension is given as pushout of $\Lambda_{0}^2$ (left horn).

Thus choose (w.l.o.g.) the vertex $1$ to denote the induced map

\begin{align*} * \rightarrow \Sigma X \end{align*}

as basepoint ("choose the north or south pole as basepoint")
