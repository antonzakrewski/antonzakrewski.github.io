projective solution functor

Definition

Let $\Sigma = (f_j)_{j \in J}$ be a system of \(J\)-homogeneous polynomial equations in $I$ variables over $k$. Then its solution functor $h \mathrm{Sol}_{\Sigma}: \mathrm{CAlg}_k \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ is the projective vanishing locus of $\{f_j \vert j \in J\}$ in $\mathbb{P}_k^I$
