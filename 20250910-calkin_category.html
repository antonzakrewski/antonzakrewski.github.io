continuous calkin category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable stable (pointed) category. Then the calkin category is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Calk}(\mathcal{C}) = \mathrm{fib}(\mathrm{colim}: \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
