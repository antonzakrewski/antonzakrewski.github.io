morera theorem

Proposition

Let $D \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be a disk and $f: D \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ a continuous map such that for any triangle $T \subseteq D$

\begin{align*} \int_{T} f(z) \mathrm{d} z = 0 \end{align*}

Then $f$ is holomorphic

Proof

follows from:

  • existence of primitives and integral of triangles
  • cauchy integral formula
