spaces with same homotopy groups in general not weakly equivalent

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be path connected topological spaces such that

\begin{align*} \pi_n(X) \cong \pi_n(Y) \end{align*}

are isomorphic as groups

Then $X \not\simeq Y$ are not weakly homotopy equivalent

Proof

consider

\begin{align*} X =& \mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^2 \times S^3 \\ Y =& \mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^3 \times S^2 \\ \end{align*}

\begin{align*} \pi_n(X) \cong& \pi_n(S^2) \times \pi_n(S^3) \\ \pi_n(Y) \cong& \pi_n(S^3) \times \pi_n(S^2) \\ \end{align*}

where we used

\begin{align*} \pi_n(\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^k) \cong& \pi_n(S^k) \end{align*}

for $n > 1$.

\begin{align*} \pi_1(\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^l) = \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}
