slice category functor admits left adjoint on functors with colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a category and $\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}/\mathcal{D}$ the slice category. Let $\mathcal{T} \subseteq \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}/\mathcal{D}$ be the full subcategory containing functors which admit colimits

Then the slice category functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{D}_{/(-)}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}/\mathcal{D} \\ y \mapsto& ( \mathcal{D}_{/y} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

restricts to $\mathcal{T}$ and admits a left adjoint 

\begin{align*}
\begin{tikzcd} \mathcal{T} \arrow[r, shift left=1ex, "\mathrm{colim}"{name=F}] & \mathcal{D}\arrow[l, shift left=.5ex, "\mathcal{D}_{/(-)}"{name=G}] \arrow[phantom, from=G, to=F, , "\scriptscriptstyle\boldsymbol{\bot}"] \end{tikzcd}
\end{align*}

Proof

restriction

follows from coinitial

  • inclusion of a terminal object is coinitial

adjoint

using pointwise construction of left adjoints: We want to show

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}/\mathcal{D}}(I \xrightarrow{\alpha} \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{D}_{/y} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}) \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathrm{colim} \alpha, y) \end{align*}

For that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}/\mathcal{D}}(I \xrightarrow{\alpha} \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{D}_{/y} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}) \simeq& \{\alpha\} \times_{\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}}(I, \mathcal{D}}(I, \mathrm{Ar}(\mathcal{D}) \times_{f,\mathcal{D}} \{y\}) \\ \simeq& \{\alpha\} \times_{\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}}(I, \mathcal{D})} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}}(I \times \Delta^1, \mathcal{D}) \times_{\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}}(I, \mathcal{D})} \{\mathrm{const}(y)\}\\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Fun}(I, \mathcal{D})}(\alpha, \mathrm{const}(y)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathrm{colim} \alpha, y) \end{align*}
