commutativity of the scalar of an elementary tensor

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $M,N$ \(R\)-modules and $M \otimes_R N$ the tensor product. For an elementary tensor $m \otimes n$ we get

\begin{align*} (r \cdot m) \otimes n =& r \cdot (m \otimes n) \\ =& (m \otimes r \cdot n) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from the construction with $\iota$ as bilinear map. 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
M \times N \arrow[hookrightarrow, ]{r}{\iota} M \otimes_R N
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
