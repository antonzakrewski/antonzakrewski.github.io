n-truncated presheaf and pointwise n-truncated

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ the infinity presheaf category. Suppose $\overline{\mathcal{P}} \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ is a full subcategory which contains the essential image of the infinity yoneda embedding.

Let $\mathcal{F} \in \overline{\mathcal{P}}$ be an object.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is an n-truncated object
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ as infinity functor restricts to n-truncated anima # i.e. infinity factors

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

n-truncated presheaves and testing against representables

2) $\implies$ 1)

using truncated objects in a full subcategory it suffices to show that $\mathcal{F}$ is \(n\)-truncated in the presheaf category Let $\mathcal{G}$ be any other presheaf.

Then by infinity contravariant co-yoneda lemma

\begin{align*} \mathcal{G} \simeq \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,c_i) \end{align*}

for some diagram of representables.

Thus

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(\mathcal{G}, \mathcal{F}) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(\mathrm{colim} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,c_i), \mathcal{F}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,c_i), \mathcal{F}) \simeq& \mathrm{lim} \mathcal{F}(c_i) \end{align*}

where the n-truncated objects are closed under limits
