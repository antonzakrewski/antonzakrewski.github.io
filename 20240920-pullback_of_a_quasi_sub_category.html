pullback of a infinity subcategory

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathcal{C}' \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$ asub quasicategory Then the diagram

is a pullback

Proof

using simplex in a sub quasicategory, limit of simplicial sets and standard simplex:

Given $\Delta^n$

Then $\Delta^n \rightarrow N(h \mathcal{C}')$ witnesses, that each \(1\)-simplex of the spine

\begin{align*} I^n \rightarrow \Delta^n \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

lies inside $\mathcal{C}'$

Hence $\Delta^n$ lifts to $\mathcal{C}'$
