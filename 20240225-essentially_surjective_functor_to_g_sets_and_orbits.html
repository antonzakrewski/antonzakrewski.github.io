essentially surjective functor to G-Sets and orbits

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group, $\mathrm{G-Sets}$ the Category of G-Sets and $\mathcal{C}$ a category admitting coproducts. Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{G-Sets}$ be a functor preserving coproducts.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is essentially surjective
  2. for each subgroup $H \subseteq G$, the quotient is in the image

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

special case

2) $\implies$ 1)
