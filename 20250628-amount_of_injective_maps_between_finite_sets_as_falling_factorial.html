amount of injective maps between finite sets as falling factorial

Proposition

Let $n,k \in \mathbb{N}$. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Inj}( \{1,...,k\}, \{1,...,n\}) =& n^{\underline{k}} \\ =& \prod_{i=0}^{k-1} n - i \end{align*}

is given by the fallende Faktorielle

Proof

Proof by induction

induction basis $k = 0,k=1$

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Inj}( \emptyset, \{1,...,n\}) \cong& \{\emptyset\} \\ \mathrm{Inj}( \{1\}, \{1,...,n\}) \cong& \{1,...,n\} \end{align*}

induction step

Note that

\begin{align*} \coprod_{a \in \{1,...,n\}} \mathrm{Inj}( \{1,...,k-1\}, \{1,...,n\}\setminus \{a\}) \cong \mathrm{Inj}( \{1,...,k\}, \{1,...,n\}) (f,a) \mapsto& f: i \mapsto \begin{cases} a & \mbox{if } i = k \\ f(i) \end{cases} \end{align*}

here by assumption

\begin{align*} \vert \mathrm{Inf}( \{1,...,k-1\}, \{1,...,n\}\setminus \{a\}) \vert = (n-1)^{\underline{k-1}} \end{align*}

and therefore

\begin{align*} \vert \mathrm{Inj}( \{1,...,k\}, \{1,...,n\}) \vert =& n \cdot (n-1)^{\underline{k-1}} \\ =& n^{\underline{k}} \end{align*}
