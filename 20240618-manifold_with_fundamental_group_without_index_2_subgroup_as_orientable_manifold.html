manifold with pi_1 without index 2 subgroup is orientable

Proposition

Let \(M\) be a manifold such that the fundamental group contains no index $2$ subgroup Then $M$ is orientable

Proof

Suppose $M$ is not orientable, then the orientation character $\omega_{M}$ is nontrivial (cf. orientable manifold and trivial orientation character) Therefore

\begin{align*} \omega_{X}: \pi_{1}(X,x) \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

is not the zero morphism, hence surjective.

But then the kernel is an index 2 subgroup, as $\vert \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} \vert = 2$
