morita equivalence in PrL

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be presentable categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a morphism in $\mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_n\)}(\mathrm{Pr}^L)$ of E_n monoids Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be a morita equivalence in $\mathrm{Pr}^L$ if the induced functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}^*: \mathrm{Mod}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathrm{Pr^L}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathrm{Pr}^L) \end{align*}

is an equivalence
