dimension of a topological space

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space. Then the dimension $d \coloneqq \mathrm{dim}(X)$ of $X$ is defined as supremum in $\mathbb{N}_0 \sqcup \{\infty\}$ of length of proper chains of closed irreducible topological spaces

\begin{align*} \emptyset \subsetneq A_0 \subsetneq ... \subsetneq A_d \subseteq X \end{align*}
