postcomposition with a kan fibration as kan fibration

Proposition

Let $B$ be a simplicial set, $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a kan fibration Then postcomposition for the internal hom

\begin{align*} f_*: \mathrm{Hom}(B,X) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}(B,Y) \end{align*}

is a kan fibration

Proof

follows from:

  • covariant inner hom as pullback hom
  • inner hom of a monomorphism and kan fibration
