Moore space

Definition

Let $A$ be abelian group, then the \(n\)-th moore space is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{M}(A,n) = \mathrm{Cone}(f) \end{align*}

where $f$ is the map

\begin{align*} f\bigvee_{i \in I} S^n \rightarrow \bigvee_{\partial} S^n \end{align*}

corresponding to a presentation in Category Ab (cf. presentation of a group
