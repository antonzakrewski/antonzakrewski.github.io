localization of a graded ring

Proposition

Let $\Gamma$ be a commutative monoid and $A$ a $\Gamma$ graded ring. Let $S \subseteq A$ be a subset of homogeneous elements whose degree in $\Gamma$ is invertible. Then the localization $A[S^{-1}]$ inherits a canonical $\Gamma$ grading, such that $A \rightarrow A[S^{-1}]$ is a graded map.
