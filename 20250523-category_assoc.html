category assoc

Definition

The category $\mathbb{Assoc}$ is defined as 1 category containing

  1. finite pointed sets $<n>$ as objects
  2. pointed maps $\alpha: <n> \rightarrow <m>$ and a total order of $a^{-1}[j]$ for $j \neq *$
  3. composition given by composition of the maps and lexicographic ordering, i.e. for $i,l \in \beta^{-1}[j], i < l$ we get $a^{-1}[i] < a^{-1}[l}$ as induced order.
