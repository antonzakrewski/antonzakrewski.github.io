internal hom out of the initial object as terminal object

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a closed monoidal category. Then the internal hom out of the initial object is the terminal object

Proof

Let $X,Y$ be arbitrary objects. Then as the tensor product preserves initial objects, we get

\begin{align*} X \otimes \emptyset = \emptyset \end{align*}

Thus

\begin{align*} \{1\} \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\emptyset, Y) \\ =& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X \otimes \emptyset, Y) \\ \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\emptyset,Y)) \end{align*}

where the terminal object is characterized by the universal property, that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,1) = \{1\} \end{align*}
