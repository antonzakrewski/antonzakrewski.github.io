homotopy category and 2 skeleton

Proposition

Let $X$ be a simplicial set. Then the inclusion of the 2-skeleton

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathrm{Sk}_2(X) \rightarrow X \end{align*}

is an isomorphism after applying the homotopy category functor

Proof

corollary of:

  • skeleton functor idempotent
  • isomorphism on 2 skeleton and isomorphism on homotopy category
