infinity twisted arrow category as infinity category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category. Then the infinity twisted arrow category is also an infinity category.

Proof

follows from:

  • composition of inner fibrations as inner fibration
  • right fibration as inner fibration
  • forgetful functor from infinity twisted arrow to the product as right fibration
  • quasicategory and terminal morphism as inner fibration
