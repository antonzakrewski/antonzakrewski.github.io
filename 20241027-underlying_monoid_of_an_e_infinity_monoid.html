underlying Monoid of a cartesian commutative Monoid

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $X: \Gamma \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a cartesian commutative Monoid. Then the underlying commutative monoid is defined as functor

\begin{align*} X \circ \mathrm{Cut}: \Delta \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}
