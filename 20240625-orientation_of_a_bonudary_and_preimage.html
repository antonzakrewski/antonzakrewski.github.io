orientation of a bonudary and preimage

Proposition

Let $W$ be a \(n+1\)-dimensional compact topological manifold with boundary and $R$ a ring Suppose $\sigma_W \in H_n(\partial W;R)$ be a R-Orientation and $s \in H_{n+1}(W, \partial W;R)$ such that

\begin{align*} \partial s = \sigma_{\partial W} \end{align*}

Then $s$ is an R-Orientation on the connected components of $W$ which have nonempty boundary

Proof
