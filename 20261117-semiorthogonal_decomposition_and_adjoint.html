semiorthogonal decomposition and adjoint

Proposition

Let $\iota_1: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ and $\iota_2: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ be strongly continuous functors between stable presentable categories. TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{E} = \langle \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}\rangle$

2) a) $\iota_1$ (resp. $\iota_2$) has a left (strongly continuous right) adjoint. b) $\iota_1^{L} \iota_1 \simeq \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{C}$, $\iota_{2}^R \iota_2 \simeq \iota_{\mathcal{D}}$. c) $\iota_1^L \iota_2 = 0$ d) there exists a cofiber sequence $\iota_2 \iota^R \rightarrow \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{E}} \rightarrow \iota_1 \iota_1^L$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

a) b) is fully faithfulness c) $\mathrm{Hom}(\iota_1^L \iota_2 d, -)\mathrm{Hom}(\iota_2 d, \iota_1 -) = 0$ implies that $\iota_1^L \iota_2(-) = 0$. d) $\iota_2 \iota_2^R \rightarrow \mathrm{id} \rightarrow \mathrm{cofib}(\epsilon)$

2) $\implies$ 1)
