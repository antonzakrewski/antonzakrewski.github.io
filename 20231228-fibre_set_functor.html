topological fibre Set functor

Definition

Let $B$ be a topological space, $b \in B$ and $p: E \rightarrow B$ a covering map. Then the topological fiber set functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fib}_b: \mathrm{Cov}(B) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Set} \\ (p: E \rightarrow B) \mapsto& \mathrm{Fib}_b^p \\ \left(f: (p: E \rightarrow B) \rightarrow (p': E' \rightarrow B') \right) \mapsto& f: \mathrm{Fib}_b^p \mapsto \mathrm{Fib}_b^{p'} \end{align*}
