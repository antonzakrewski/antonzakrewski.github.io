generated sieve

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a locally small category, $A_i,B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and $f_i: A_i \rightarrow B \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,B)$ a family of morphisms. The generated sieve is defined by

\begin{align*} S(Y) = \{f: C \rightarrow X \vert f \text{ factors through some } f_i\} \end{align*}

or equivalently it is the image of the map

\begin{align*} \coprod よ(Y_i) \rightarrow よ(X) \end{align*}
