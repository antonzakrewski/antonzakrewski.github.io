pi_1 of a group as abelian group

Proposition

Let $G$ be an anima valued group Then $\pi_1(G)$ is an abelian group.

Proof

general case

consider the equivalence of anima valued groups and connected pointed anima. Then in particular

\begin{align*} \Omega BG \simeq G \end{align*}

as anima.

But

\begin{align*} \pi_1(G,g) =& \pi_1(\Omega BG, \mathrm{const}_g) \\ =& \pi_2(BG) \end{align*}

where the RHS is abelian, since it is a higher homotopy group .

commutative case

  • recognition principle for connective spectra and commutative groups
  • where spectra are infinite loop spaces (cf.0infinite loop functor out of spectra )
