classical affine line as cogenerator in the category of affine varieties

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathfrak{Aff}$ the category of affine varieties. Then $A[X]$ is a cogenerator

Proof

Suppose $f,g: X \rightarrow Y \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^n$ is are different morphisms Then there exists an $x \in X$ with $f(x) \neq g(x)$ or that $f(x)_l \neq g(x)_l$ for some $l \in \{1,...,n\}$

Then consider $\pi_l: Y \rightarrow \mathbb{A}[X]$

It follows that

\begin{align*} \pi_l \circ g \neq \pi_l \circ f \end{align*}

as $(\pi_l \circ g)(x) \neq (\pi_l \circ f)(x)$
