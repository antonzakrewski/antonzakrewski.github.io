Q local spectrum is rational

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum TFAE:

  1. $X$ is $\mathbb{Q}$ local spectrum
  2. $X$ is a rational spectrum

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

we need to show that multiplication with $p$ is an equivalence so we may use the cofiber sequence $\mathbb{S} \xrightarrow{\cdot p} \mathbb{S} \rightarrow \mathbb{S}/p$ and apply $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(-,X)$ so that we get

This shows that the fiber vanishes, as $\mathbb{S}/p$ is $\mathbb{Q}$ acyclic. Hence multiplication with $p$ is an equivalence

2) $\implies$ 1)

suppose $A$ is $\mathbb{Q}$ acyclic and $X$ is rational. Then

\begin{align*} 0 \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(0, X) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(A \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Q}, X) \\ \simeq& \lim_{\mathbb{N}} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(A, X) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(A, X) \end{align*}

where we have used that

  1. filtered category is weakly contractible
  2. limit of a diagram factoring through the groupoid from a weakly contractible category
