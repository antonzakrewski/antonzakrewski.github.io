E_n monoidal category with coproduct preserving tensor product as E_n semiring

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an E_n monoidal category with coproducts such that the monoidal product

\begin{align*} \otimes: \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

preserves coproduct in each variable.

Then

\begin{align*} (\mathcal{C}, \coprod, \otimes) \end{align*}

is canonically an object oj

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Rig}_{E_n}(\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}) \end{align*}

proof

Consider Cat infinity Sigma

Then there exists a fully faithful inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}^{\Sigma} \rightarrow \mathrm{SymMonCat}_{\infty} \end{align*}
