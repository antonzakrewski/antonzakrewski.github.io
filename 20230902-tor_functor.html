Tor Functor

Definition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{R-Mod}, \mathrm{Mod-R}$ the category RMod (of left resp. right modules). Then the tor functor $(\mathrm{Tor}_i^{R}(-,-))$ is defined as left derived functor of

\begin{align*} - \otimes_R -: \mathrm{R-Mod} \otimes \mathrm{Mod-R} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab} \end{align*}
