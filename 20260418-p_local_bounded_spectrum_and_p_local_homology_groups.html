R-local, bounded below spectrum and p-local homology groups

Proposition

Let $R = \mathbb{Z}[p^{-1} \vert p \in P] \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ be a subring and $X$ be a bounded below spectrum. TFAE:

  1. $X$ is an R-local spectrum
  2. $H_*(X,\mathbb{Z})$ is an \(R\)-local abelian group

Proof

recall that $X$ is R-local if and only if multiplication by $p$ is an equivalence for $p$ in $P$. But $- \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z}$ is an additive functor, so in particular preserves multiplication by $p$. Hence this follows from the stable hurewicz theorem
