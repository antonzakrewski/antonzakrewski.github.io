point local property of a module

Definition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod Let $\Pi$ be a property of a ring $M$

Then $\Pi$ is said to be a point local property, if

TFAE:

  1. $M$ has the property $\Pi$
  2. the property is a local property
  3. for each maximal ideal $\mathfrak{p}$, the localization at a prime ideal $M_{\mathfrak{p}}$ has the property $\Pi$
