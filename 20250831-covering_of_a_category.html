covering of a category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an essentially small category, then a covering consists for each object $X\in \mathcal{C}$ of a collection of covering families $\mathcal{X} = \{X_i \rightarrow X\}_{i \in I}$ such that

  1. each isomorphism $f: X' \rightarrow X$ is a covering family
  2. for every covering family $\mathcal{X} = \{X_i \rightarrow X\}_{i \in I}$ and every map $Y \rightarrow X$ the pullbacks $Y_i = Y \times_{X} X_i$ exist and the collection $\mathcal{Y} = Y \times_{X} \mathcal{X} = \{Y_i \rightarrow Y\}_{i \in I}$ is a covering family
  3. given a covering family $\mathcal{X} = \{X_i \rightarrow X\}$ and for each $i \in I$ another covering family $\mathcal{Y}_} = \{Y_{i,j} \rightarrow X_i\}_{j \in J(i)}$, then the collection $\{Y_{i,j} \rightarrow X\}_{i \in I, j \in J(i)}$ forms another covering family of $X$
