normalizer of a normalizer of a sylow group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a finite group and $P \subseteq G$ a p-sylow group. Then it holds for the Normalizer

\begin{align*} N_G(N_G(P)) = N_G(P) \end{align*}

Proof

$\supseteq$

follows from definitions

$\subseteq$

corollary of:

  • transitivity of normal subgroups for a sylow subgroup
