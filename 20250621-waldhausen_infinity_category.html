waldhausen infinity category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a pointed infinity category. Then a waldhasuen structure is given by a subcategory $\mathrm{in}(\mathcal{W})$ satisfying

  1. for all $x \in \mathcal{W}$ the initilal map $0 \rightarrow x \in \mathrm{in}(\mathcal{W})$
  2. each equivalence is an ingressive
  3. the class $\mathrm{in} \mathcal{W}$ is closed under pushouts.
