precomposition with a continuous functor induces contravariant functor on sheaves

Definition

Let $\mathrm{Site}$ be the category of sites and $\mathcal{V}$ another category. Then there exists a functor

\begin{align*} ()^*: \mathrm{Site}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat} \\ \mathcal{C} \mapsto& \mathrm{Sh}_{\mathcal{V}}(\mathcal{C}) \\ (\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D})| \mapsto& (\mathcal{F}^{ \rightarrow}: \mathrm{Sh}_{\mathcal{V}}(\mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Sh}_{\mathcal{V}}(\mathcal{C})) \end{align*}

for a Category Cat (up to size issues)
