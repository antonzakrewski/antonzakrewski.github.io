discriminant

Definition

Let $K$ be a field, $f \in K[X]$ a polynomial and $L/K$ a splitting field of $f$. Suppose $\alpha_1,...,\alpha_n$ are roots of $f$, then the discriminant is defined as

\begin{align*} \Delta_f \coloneqq \prod_{i < j} (\alpha_i - \alpha_j)^2 \end{align*}
