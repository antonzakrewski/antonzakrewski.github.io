group action as group homomorphism

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group, $X$ a set and $\varphi: G \times X \rightarrow X$ a map

TFAE:

  1. currying $\varphi$ induces a group-homomorphism
\begin{align*} \varphi': M \rightarrow& \mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{Set}}(X,X) \\ g \mapsto& (x \mapsto gx) \end{align*}
  1. $\varphi$ is a group action

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
