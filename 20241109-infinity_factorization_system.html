infinity factorization system

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category. Then a pair $(S_L,S_R)$ of collections of morphisms of $\mathcal{C}$ is said to be a factorization system, if

  1. $S_l,S_R$ are stable under <retracts>
  2. each morphism of $S_L$ is left orthogonal to each morphism of $S_R$
  3. for every morphism $h: X \rightarrow Z$ in $\mathcal{C}$ there exists a commutative diagram

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category Then a factorization system is given by two wide subcategories $\mathcal{C}_{L}, \mathcal{C}_R$ such that the composition map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}([1], \mathcal{C}_L) \times_{\mathcal{C}^{\cong}} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}([1], \mathcal{C}_R) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}([1], \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is an equivalence
