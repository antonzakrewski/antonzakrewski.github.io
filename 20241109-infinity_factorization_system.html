factorization system

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category Then a factorization system is given by two wide subcategories $\mathcal{C}_{L}, \mathcal{C}_R$ such that the composition map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}([1], \mathcal{C}_L) \times_{\mathcal{C}^{\cong}} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}([1], \mathcal{C}_R) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}}([1], \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is an equivalence

Definition

A factorization ssytem of a category $\mathcal{C}$ constists of 2 clasess of maps $L$ and $R$ such that

  1. both are closed under compositions
  2. every map $f$ in $\mathcal{C}$ factors uniquely as $f = r \circ l$ for $l \in L, r \in R$
