zariski local nature of closed immersions

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $u: X \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(R)$ be a map of algebraic functors Suppose that $X$ satisfies zariski descent and let $(f_i)$ generate the unit ideal in $R$. Let $u_i: X \times_{\mathrm{Spec}(R)} D(f_i) \rightarrow D(f_i)$ be the base change of $u$ to $D(f_i)$.

Suppose that each morphism $u_i$ is a closed immersion, then so is $u$

Proof
