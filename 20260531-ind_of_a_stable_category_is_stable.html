Ind kappa of a stable category is stable

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category and $\kappa$ a regular cardinal. Then $\mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C})$ is also stable.

Proof

special case $\mathcal{C}$ admits $\kappa$ small colimits

Here we may use

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa} \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\kappa-\mathrm{rex}}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\kappa-\mathrm{rex}}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Sp}(\mathrm{An})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\kappa-\mathrm{rex}}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An}) \\ \end{align*}

where the RHS is a stable category
