catanesque formula steenrod operation for the cone as module

Proposition

Let $f: Y \rightarrow X$ a continuous map Then the reduced singular cohomology of the mapping cone $H^*(C(f); \mathbb{F}_2)$ regarded \(H^*(X;\mathbb{F}_2)\)-module satisfies

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}^{n}(a \cdot x) =& \sum_{i=0}^n \mathrm{Sq}^i(a) \cdot \mathrm{Sq}^{n-i}(x) \end{align*}

for the steenrod operation $\mathrm{Sq}^(-)$ and $x \in H^*(X;\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z})$ resp. $a \in \tilde{H}^*(C(f),\mathbb{F}_2)$

Proof

note that by definition

\begin{align*} a \cdot x = \overline{\Delta}^* a \vee x \end{align*}

for the map in reduced cohomology of a mapping cone as module

Then by naturality of the steenrod operation

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}^n(a \cdot x) =& \mathrm{Sq}^n(\overline{\Delta}^*(a \wedge x)) \\ =& \overline{\Delta}^*(\mathrm{Sq}^n(a \wedge x) \\ =& \overline{\Delta}^*(\sum_{i=0}^n \mathrm{Sq}^i(a) \wedge \mathrm{Sq}^{n-i}(x)) \\ \end{align*}
\begin{align*} \overline{\Delta}^*(\sum_{i=0}^n \mathrm{Sq}^i(a) \wedge \mathrm{Sq}^{n-i}(x)) =& \sum_{i=0}^n \mathrm{Sq}^i(x) \cdot \mathrm{Sq}^{n-i}(x) \end{align*}

where applying the Cartan formula of steenrod operations shows the second equality
