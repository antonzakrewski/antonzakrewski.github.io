product of Eilenberg-MacLane anima is an Eilenberg-MacLane anima

Proposition

Let $A_i$ be abelian groups. then there exists a canonical equivalence

\begin{align*} \prod_{i \in I} K(A_i,n) \simeq K(\prod_{i \in I} A_i,n) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from uniqueness of Eilenberg-MacLane spaces and the canonical identification

\begin{align*} \pi_n(\prod X_i) \cong \prod_i(\pi_i(X))$ \end{align*}

(cf. homotopy group functor preserves products).
