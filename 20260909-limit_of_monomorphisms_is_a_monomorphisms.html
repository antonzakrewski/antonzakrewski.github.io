limit of monomorphisms is a monomorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category with enough limits and $I$ an indexing category. suppose that $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}: I \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ are diagrams and $\eta: \mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ is a natural transformation which consists pointwise of monomorphism Then so is the induced morphism $\lim \eta: \lim \mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \lim \mathcal{G}$.

Proof

use that:

  • limits commute with limits
  • monomorphisms in functor categories
  • limit preserving functor preserves monomorphisms
