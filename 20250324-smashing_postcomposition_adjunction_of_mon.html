smashing postcomposition adjunction of Mon

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a infinity left adjoint between presentable infinity categories and right adjoint $\mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ Then the smashing localization and postcomposition with the right adjoint (cf. infinity right adjoint preserves limits) induces an adjunction

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{L}) \dashv \mathcal{R}^{*} \end{align*}
