coresolving exact subcategory

Definition

Let $\iota: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ be a exact infinity subcategory. Then $\iota$ is said to be coresolving, if

\begin{align*} \iota^{\mathrm{op}}: \mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}

is a resolving exact subcategory
