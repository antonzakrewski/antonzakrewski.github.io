riesz-representation for L^p

Proposition

Proof

$(X, \mathcal{A})$ sigma endlich

Let $l \in (L^{1}(X))^*$. Assume that $X = \bigcup_{n \in \mathbb{N}} X_n$ with $\mu(X_n) < \infty$. In particular, we get $l_{\vert X_n} \in (L^1(X_n))^*$ by defining $l_{\vert X_n}(g) = l(g')$ after defining

\begin{align*} g': x \mapsto \begin{cases} g(x) & \mbox{if } x \in X_n \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Now for each $X_n$, there exists an $f_n \in L^{\infty}(X_n)$ such that $l_{f_n}(g) = \int_{X_n} \mathrm{d} \mu g f_n$ for any $g \in L^{1}(X_n)$.

We define

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow& \overline{\mathbb{K}} \\ x \mapsto& f_n(x_n) & \mbox{if } x \in X_n \setminus X_{n-1} \end{align*}

and claim that it is essentially bounded: Suppose not, then for each $n >> 0$, there exists an $Y_{n} \subseteq X$ with $\mu(Y_n) > 0$ and $\vert f_{\vert Y_n} \vert > n$. Moreover, we may assume without loss of generality that $Y_n \subseteq X_m$ for some $m$, as otherwise $\mu(Y_n) = \mu(Y_n \cap X) = \sum_{m \in \mathbb{N}} \mu(Y_n \cap X_{m} \setminus X_{m-1})$. Then we would get

\begin{align*} \| l(\chi_{Y_n}) \| =& \| l_{\vert X_m}(\chi_{Y_n}) \| \\ =& \int_{X_m} \mathrm{d}\mu \vert f \cdot \chi_{Y_n} \vert \\ =& \int_{Y_n} \mathrm{d}\mu \vert f \vert \\ \leq& \mu(Y_m) \cdot n \\ \leq& \| \chi_{Y_m} \|_1 \cdot n \\ \end{align*}

for all $n$ and hence $\| l \| \geq \frac{\| l(\chi_{Y_m})}{\| \chi_{Y_m} \|_1} \geq n$ for all $n$, contradiction to $\| l \| < \infty$.

Let $g \in L^1(X)$, then define $g_n = g \cdot \chi_{X_n \setminus X_{n-1}}$ and observe that $g = \sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} g_n$. Hence as $l$ is continuous, we can write

\begin{align*} l(g) =& l(\sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} g_n) \\ =& \sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} l(g_n) \\ =& \sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} l_{\vert X_n} (g_n) \\ =& \sum_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \int_{X_n} \mathrm{d}\mu \vert f_n \cdot g \cdot \chi_{X_n \setminus X_{n-1}} \vert \\ =& \int_{X} \mathrm{d}\mu \vert f \cdot g \vert \\ \end{align*}

where the last step uses Beppo-Levi.
