E_n monoids with respect to day convolution are E_n lax monoidal functors

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}$ be E_n monoidal categories.

Then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Alg}(\mathrm{Fun}^{\textrm{day}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathbb{E}_n, \textrm{ lax}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}
