tensor functor of a monoidal category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a monoidal category category. then the tensor functor is a designated functor

\begin{align*} \otimes: \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

satisfying

  1. triangle identity of a monoidal category
  2. pentagon identity
