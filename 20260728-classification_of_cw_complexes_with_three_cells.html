classification of CW complexes with three cells

Proposition

Let $k,n \in \mathbb{N}$, then depending on $k$ and $n$ there exist the following CW-Complexes with cells in dimension $0,n,n+k$:

  1. exactly $S^n \vee S^n$ if $k = 0$
  2. exactly $\mathbb{N}_0$ many, namely $M(\mathbb{Z}/l \mathbb{Z}, n)$ for $l \in \mathbb{N}_0$ if $k = 1$
  3. exactly $\pi_{n+k-1}(S^n)_{C_2}$ many in either case, where this denotes the set-theoretic quotient of $\pi_{n+k-1}(S^n)$ modulo the sign action (in particular, not the group theoretic quotient)
