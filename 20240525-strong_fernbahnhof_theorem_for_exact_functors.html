strong Fernbahnhof theorem for exact functors

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}), \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{B})$ the category of chain complexes and $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{B})$ an exact functor, which preserves trivial boundary chain complexes Then for the homology chain complex functor, there exists a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}H_*(-) \cong H_*(\mathcal{F}(-)) \end{align*}

of functors

Note that $\mathcal{F}$ may be induced by an exact functor $\mathcal{F}': \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ (cf. exact functor induces exact functor on chain complexes)

Proof

objects

follows, as $\mathcal{F}^*$ resp. $\mathcal{F}$ preserve kernels / cokernels and hence commute with the homology functor

morphism

follows from:

  • isomorphism of chain complexes and isomorphism of points
  • strong FHHF for left exact functors
