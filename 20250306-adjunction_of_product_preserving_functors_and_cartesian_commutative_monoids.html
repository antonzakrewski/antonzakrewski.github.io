currying of product preserving functors into cartesian commutative monoids

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories admitting finite products. Then there exists a canonical equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{D})) \end{align*}

see:

  • infinity functor category
  • category of cartesian commutative monoids
  • category of finite product preserving functors

Proof

follows from currying of functors preserving limits / colimits & and the segal condition for gamma objects as coproduct to product:

Here

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D})) \end{align*}

is precisely equivalent to

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\Gamma, \prod}(\mathrm{Fin}_* \times \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

consisting of Functors which preserve finite products in the second argument and where

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(-,C) \end{align*}

is a reduced functor satisfying the segal condition

The last condition follows from limit in an infinity functor category, where we may compute them pointwise

Analogously we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{D})) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod, \Gamma}(N(\mathrm{Fin}_*) \times \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}
