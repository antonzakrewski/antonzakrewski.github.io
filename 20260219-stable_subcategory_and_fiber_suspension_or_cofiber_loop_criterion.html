stable subcategory and fiber/suspension or cofiber/loop criterion

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category and $\mathcal{C}_0 \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ a subcategory which contais the zero object TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}_0$ is a stable subcategory
  2. $\mathcal{C}_0$ is closed under suspensions and fibers
  3. $\mathcal{C}_0$ is closed under loops and cofibers

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

special case

2) $\implies$ 1)

We need to show that $\mathcal{C}_0$ is closed under cofibers So let $X \rightarrow Y \rightarrow Z$ be a cofiber sequence in $\mathcal{C}$ with $X,Y \in \mathcal{C}_0$. Then in particular $Z \rightarrow \Sigma X \rightarrow \Sigma Y$ is a fiber sequence with $\Sigma X, \Sigma Y \in \mathcal{C}_0$ so we may conclude that $Z \in \mathcal{C}_0$

1) $\implies$ 3)

special case

3) $\implies$ 1)

dual to $2) \implies 1)$
