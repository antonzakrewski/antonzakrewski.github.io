dimension of an irreducible subpace in general not the dimension of the closure

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space, $A \subseteq X$ an irreducible subspace. Then the dimension of $A$ is in general not the dimension of the closure $\overline{A}$.d

Proof

Consider the Sierpinski-Space $\{1,2\}$ where $\{1\}$ is closed. Then $\{2\}$ is irreducible, has dimension $0$. But the closure is given by $\{1,2\}$, which is also irreducible (cf. sierpinski-space as irreducible space) but has dimension $1$ since $\{1\} \subseteq \{1,2\}$ is a chain of length $1$
