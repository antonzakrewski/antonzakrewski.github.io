1 categorical equivalence and nerve as joyal equivalence

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ functor

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is a equivalence
  2. the nerve $N(\mathcal{F})$ is a joyal equivalence

Proof

follows from

  • nerve homotopy category adjunction and naturally equivalent functors
  • homotopy category of a nerve canonically isomorphic
