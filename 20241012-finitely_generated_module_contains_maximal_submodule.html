finitely generated module contains maximal submodule

Proposition

Let \(A\) be a ring, $M \neq 0$ a finitely generated \(A\)-module. Then there exists a maximal proper submodule $N$

Proof

follows from: Zorn's lemma

consider generators $m_1,...,m_n$. Then by union of a chain of submodules as module it suffices to show, that the union of a chain of proper submodules is itself proper. Assume the union is not a proper module, then choosing a sufficient $l >> 0$ with $m_1,...,m_n \in M_l$ provides a contradiction.
