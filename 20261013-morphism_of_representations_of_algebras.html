morphism of representations of algebras

Definition

Let $A$ be an R-Algebra and $(M, \varphi),(N, \psi)$ be representations of $A$. Then a morphism of the representations is an \(R\)-linear map

\begin{align*} f: V \rightarrow W \end{align*}

such that

\begin{align*} \psi(a) (f(v)) = f(\varphi(a)(v)) \end{align*}

or with abuse of notation,

\begin{align*} a \cdot f(v) = f(a \cdot v) \end{align*}
