augmentation map of a group ring

Definition / Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $G$ a group and $R[G]$ the group ring Then the augmentation map is defined as morphism of A-Algebras

\begin{align*} \varphi: R[G] \rightarrow& R \\ r \cdot g \mapsto& r \end{align*}

where $R[G], R$ are seen as Algebras over $R[G]$ via.

Proof

it remains to show that $\varphi$ is a welldefined ring-homomorphism

unit

follows as

\begin{align*} \varphi(1e) = 1 \end{align*}

additive

follows

\begin{align*} \varphi(rg + r'g) =& \varphi((r + r')g) \\ =& r + r' \\ =& \varphi(rg) + \varphi(r' g) \end{align*}

and definition

multiplicative

w.l.o.g. for generators $r \cdot g$ as the general case follows from distrubitivity

\begin{align*} \varphi(rg \cdot r'g') =& \varphi((r \cdot r') \cdot (g \cdot g')) \\ =& r \cdot r' \\ =& \varphi(rg) \cdot \varphi(r' \cdot g') \end{align*}
