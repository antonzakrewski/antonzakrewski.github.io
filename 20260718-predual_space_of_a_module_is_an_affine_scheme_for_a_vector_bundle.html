predual space of a module is an affine scheme for a vector bundle

Proposition

Let $k$ be a commutative ring, $M$ an vector bundle. Then the predual of $M$ is an affine scheme

Proof

follows as there exists an isomorphism $(M^{\vee})^{\vee} \simeq M$ and hence a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{k}(\mathrm{Hom}_{k}(M,k),k) \otimes_{k} R \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{k}(\mathrm{Hom}_{k}(M,k),k \otimes_{k} R) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{k}(\mathrm{Hom}_{k}(M,k), R) \\ \simeq& \mathbb{A}^{\vee}(M^{\vee})(R) \end{align*}

where we have used that $\mathrm{Hom}_{k}(M,k)$ is dualizable (cf. internal hom out of a dualizable object) and that $\mathbb{A}^{\vee}(-)$ is an affine scheme.
