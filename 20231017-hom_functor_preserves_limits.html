covariant hom-functor preserves small limits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a locally small category Then the covariant hom functor preserves small limits.

Proof

Let $J: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a diagram 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
& \mathrm{lim}(J) \arrow[swap]{ld}{\pi_i} \arrow[]{rd}{\pi_j} & \\
A_i \arrow[]{r}{f} & & A_j \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

then consider for $X \in \mathcal{C}$ the diagram

Suppose $Y$ is a set with morphism

\begin{align*} h_i: Y \rightarrow& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,A_i) \\ h_j: Y \rightarrow& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,A_j) \end{align*}

Then each $y \in Y$ defines morphismss

\begin{align*} h_i(y) \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,A_i) \end{align*}

where by assumption

\begin{align*} f^{ \leftarrow}(h_i(y)) = h_j(y) \end{align*}

Thus by universal property, there exists a unique morphism $y: X \rightarrow \mathrm{lim}(J)$

Thus let

\begin{align*} h_l: Y \rightarrow& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, \mathrm{lim}(J)) \\ y \mapsto& h_y \end{align*}

Then this respects the diagram, as

\begin{align*} h_i(y) = \pi_i \circ h_y \end{align*}
