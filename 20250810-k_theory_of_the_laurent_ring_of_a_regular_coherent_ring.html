connective K-spaces of the laurent ring of a regular coherent ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be a left regular coherent ring. Then

\begin{align*} K(R[t,t^{-1}]) \simeq& K(R) \oplus \Sigma K(R) \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of:
