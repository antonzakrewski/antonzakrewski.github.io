normed space with schauder basis is separable

Proposition

Let $X$ be a normed vector space with schauder basis. Then $X$ is separable.

Proof

define $A$ to be the set of $\mathbb{Q} + \mathrm{i} \mathbb{Q}$-linear combinations. It is dense by definition of a schauder basis and it is countable (cf. countability of a countable union of countable sets).
