mapping anima as module for a commutative ring spectrum

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring spectrum. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(R)}(-,-): \mathrm{Mod}(R)^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathrm{Mod}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

lifts canonically to a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(R)}(-,-): \mathrm{Mod}(R)^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathrm{Mod}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}(R) \end{align*}

compatible with mapping anima of a stable infinity category as spectrum

Proof
