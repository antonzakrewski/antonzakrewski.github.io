hom in finite spectra are stable maps

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be anima, then $\pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[X], \mathbb{S}[Y])$ consists of homotopy classes of maps up to stabilization.

Proof

follows from:

  1. category of finite spectra is sequential colimit
  2. Hom of a filtered colimit of categories
