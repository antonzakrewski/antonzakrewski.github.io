dualizable objects in the derived category of a commutative ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $\mathcal{D}(R)$ the derived category of a ring. Then the dualizable objects are precisely the compact objects.

Proof

Observe that the monoidal unit $R$ is dualizable. Hence subcategory of dualizable objects is a thick stable subcateogry as well as compact object in the derived category of a ring shows that all compact objects are dualizable.
