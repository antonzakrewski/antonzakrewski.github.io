Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal, $K$ be a kappa-small simplicial set and $\mathcal{C}$ an infinity category. Suppose $\mathcal{F} \in \mathrm{Fun}(K, \mathcal{C})$ is a functor, which is objectwise kappa compact

Then $\mathcal{F}$ is \(\kappa\)-compact in $\mathrm{Fun}(K, \mathcal{C})$ infinity functor category
