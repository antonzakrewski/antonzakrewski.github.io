precomposition with homotopic maps for the mapping spaces

Proposition

Let $X,Y,Z$ be topological spaces, $f,f': X \rightarrow Y$ be homotopic continuous maps and $\mathrm{Map}(X,Z), \mathrm{Map}(Y,Z)$ the mapping spaces with the compact open topology Then the precomposition maps

\begin{align*} f^{ \rightarrow }, f'^{ \rightarrow}: \mathrm{Map}(Y,Z) \rightarrow \mathrm{Map}(X,Z) \end{align*}

are homotopic

Proof

Choose a Homotopy $H: [0,1] \times X \rightarrow Y$ from $f$ to $f'$

Then since precomposition for the compact open topology is continuous, we get

\begin{align*} (H^{ \leftarrow}, \mathrm{id}_{[0,1]}): \mathrm{Map}(X', Y) \times [0,1] \rightarrow& \mathrm{Map}([0,1] \times X,Y) \times [0,1] \end{align*}

Then since $[0,1]$ is hausdorff, currying is continuous

\begin{align*} (\mathrm{curry}, \mathrm{id}_{[0,1]}): \mathrm{Map}([0,1] \times X,Y) \times [0,1] \rightarrow& \mathrm{Map}([0,1], \mathrm{Map}(X,Y)) \times [0,1] \end{align*}

at last, because $[0,1]$ is locally compact, the evaluation map is continuous

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ev}: \mathrm{Map}([0,1], \mathrm{Map}(X,Y)) \times [0,1] \rightarrow \mathrm{Map}(X,Y) \end{align*}
