coordinate ring of an integral scheme

Proposition

Let $X$ be an integral scheme and $O$ an open subscheme and $U_i \subseteq O$ an open covering. Then there exists an equality

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O}_{X}(O) = \bigcap_{U_i} \mathcal{O}_{X}(U_i) = \bigcap_{x \in O} \mathcal{O}_{X,x} \subseteq K(X) \end{align*}

Now assume that we have an element $s \in \bigcap_{x \in O} \mathcal{O}_{X,x}$, then for each $x_i \in O$ we may choose an $s_{x_i}$ which represents $s$ in $K(X)$. In particular, since $s_{x_i} = s = s_{x_j}$ in $K(X)$ for $x_i,x_j \in O$ we have that all sections agree on the generic point, hence agree on all possible intersections (cf. restriction in an integral scheme is a monomorphism).
