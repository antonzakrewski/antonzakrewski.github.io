left exact functor between abelian categories preserves monomorphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ a left exact functor Then $\mathcal{F}$ preserves monomorphisms
