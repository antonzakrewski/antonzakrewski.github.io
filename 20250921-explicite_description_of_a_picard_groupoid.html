explicit description of a picard groupoid

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \otimes$ be a symmetric monoidal category. Then the picard groupoid is given by the disjoint union of

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Pic}(\mathcal{C}) \simeq \coprod_{\mathrm{inv.}\,\mathrm{ob}} \mathrm{BAut}(\mathbbm{1}) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from connected components of an anima valued group equivalent where the connected components are precisely the invertible objects. Hence it suffices to look at the unit - which is always invertible - to determine the higher structure
