finite union of vanishing loci on integral domains

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor and $F_i$ a finite family of subsets of $\mathcal{O}(X)$ and $A$ an Integral domain. Then

\begin{align*} (V(F_1) \cup ... \cup V(F_n))(A) = V(F_1 \cdot ... \cdot F_n)(A) \end{align*}

where the intersection is either the categorical colimit or the pointwise intersection.

Proof

Suppose that $x \in V(F_1 \cdot ... \cdot F_n)(A)$, then all $f_1 \cdot ... f_n$ with $f_i \in F_i$ evaluate as zero on $x$. Now assume that $f \not\in (V(F_1) \cup ... \cup V(F_n))(A)$, then there exist functions $f_i \in F_i$ such that $f_i(x) \neq 0$. But as $A$ is an integral domain, this would imply $(f_1 \cdot ... \cdot f_n)(x) \neq 0$, contradiction.
