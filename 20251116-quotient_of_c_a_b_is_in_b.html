quotient of C_A,B is in B

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{B} \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ be an abelian full subcategory closed under quotient objects. Let $(Y \rightarrow X) \in \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}}$ be an object (cf. abelian category C_A,B) . Then the quotient $X/Y$ lies in $\mathcal{B}$.

Proof

By assumption there exists a square

where the left vertical morphism is an epimorphism.

Hence the morphism factors through 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\iota(\tilde{j}(X)) \rar{\iota(v)} \arrow[twoheadrightarrow, ]{d}{} & \iota(Y) \dar \\
& \mathrm{im}(\iota(Y) \rightarrow X) \dar \\
\iota(j(X)) \rar \arrow[]{ru}{}& X 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

so $X/\iota(Y)$ is a quotient of $X/\iota(j(X))$.

But $X/\iota(j(X)) = \iota(\iota^L(X))$ where $\iota^L(X) \in \mathrm{Pro}(\mathcal{B})$.
