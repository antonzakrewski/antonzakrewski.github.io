product and standard simplex preserving functor induces map on internal hom of sSet

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{sSet} \rightarrow \mathrm{sSet}$ be a functor which

  1. preserves products
  2. preserves standard simplices in a natural way, i.e. $\mathcal{F}(\Delta^n) \simeq \Delta^n$, compatible with all maps.

Then there exists an induced map

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(-,-) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathcal{F}(-), \mathcal{F}(-)) \end{align*}

Proof

note that we have

\begin{align*} n \mapsto \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(- \times \Delta^n, -) \mapsto \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\mathcal{F}(- \times \Delta^n), \mathcal{F}(-)) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\mathcal{F}(-) \times \mathcal{F}(\Delta^n), \mathcal{F}(-)) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\mathcal{F}(-) \times \Delta^n, \mathcal{F}(-)) \end{align*}
