strongly convergent spectral sequence and serre class

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a serre class and $E_{*,*}^*, M_n$ a strongly convergent spectral sequence which converges against some $M$ . Assume that there exists some \(r\)-th page where $E_{p,q}^r \in \mathcal{C}$, then $M_n \in \mathcal{C}$ for $n \in \mathbb{N}$

Proof

Note that the $E^r$ page is given by a

  1. taking some subobject (the kernel)
  2. then taking some quotient (the quotient)

then we get a finite filtration of $(M_n)_l$, i.e. 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
0 \rar & (M_{n})_{l_0} \rar \dar & (M_{n})_{l_0+1} \dar & ... & M_{n}
& E^{\infty}_{?,?} & E^{\infty}_{?,?} & ...
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

where the bottom part lies in $\mathcal{C}$. Hence by induction $(M_n)_{l_0 + i} \in \mathcal{C}$ and since our filtration is finite eventually $M_n \in \mathcal{C}$
