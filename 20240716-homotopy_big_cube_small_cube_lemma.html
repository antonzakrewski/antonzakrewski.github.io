Proposition

Let $g_0: I^l \rightarrow Z$ be a continuous map and $Y \subseteq Z$ a subset. Then defined

\begin{align*} S^l_p \coloneqq \{x \in I^l \vert x_i < \frac{1}{2} \mathrm{ for at least } p \mathrm{ indices } i\} \\ B^l_p \coloneqq \{x \in I^l \vert x_i > \frac{1}{2} \mathrm{ for at least } p \mathrm{ indices } i\} \\ \end{align*}

Suppose $l \geq p$.

a)

If the intersection of $g_0^{-1}[Y]$ with each face of $I^l$ is contained in $S_{p}^{l-1}$. Then $g_0$ is homotopic relative to $\partial I^n$ to a map

\begin{align*} g_1: I^l \rightarrow Z \end{align*}

such that $g_1^{-1}(Y) \subseteq S_p^l$

b)

Analogously the assertian holds for $B_p^l$ in place of $S_p^l$

Note that that each face is homeomorphic to $I^{l-1}$

Proof

a)

Let $x \in g_1^{-1}(Y)$. If $x \in S_l^l$, then all is good because $l \geq p$,.

So assume there exists an $i$ with $x_i > \frac{1}{2}$

Note that $h(x) \in \partial I^l \cap g_0^{-1}(Y)$

Then by aassumption this implies, that at least $p$ coordinates $h(x)_i$ satisfy

By construction

\begin{align*} h(x) = z + \lambda(x - z) \end{align*}

with $\lambda \geq 1$

So fro at least $p$ many $i$ we have

\begin{align*} h(x)_i = \frac{1}{4} + \lambda(x_i - \frac{1}{4}) < \frac{1}{2} \end{align*}

showing that

\begin{align*} \lambda(x_i - \frac{1}{4}) <& \frac{1}{4} \\ x_i - \frac{1}{4} <& \frac{1}{4} x_i < \frac{1}{2} \end{align*}
