multiplication of irreducible and unit

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $u \in A^{\times}$ a unit and $p \in A$ irreducible. then $u \cdot p$ is irreducible

Proof

Suppose there exist $a,b \in A$ such that

\begin{align*} a \cdot b = u \cdot p \end{align*}

then we get

\begin{align*} a \cdot (b \cdot u^{-1}) = p \end{align*}

and by irreducibility of $p$ we conclude, that w.l.o.g. $b \cdot u^{-1}$ is a unit. Then $b$ is also a unit with $u \cdot (b \cdot u^{-1})^{-1}$ as inverse
