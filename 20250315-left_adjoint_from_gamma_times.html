left adjoint from gamma times to Fin*

Definition / Proposition

Consider the forgetful functor from Gamma times to Fin*

\begin{align*} \Gamma^{\times} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fin}_* \end{align*}

Then the left adjoint is given by

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}(\langle n\rangle) = (\langle n\rangle, \langle n\rangle^{\circ}) \end{align*}
