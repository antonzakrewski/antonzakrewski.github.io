2 out of three for homology of a simple fiber sequence and derived serre ring

Proposition

Let $F \rightarrow E \rightarrow B$ be a simple fiber sequence and $\mathcal{C}$ a derived serre ring. If two out of three from $\tilde{H}_*(B), \tilde{H}_*(E), \tilde{H}_*(F)$ lie degreewise in $\mathcal{C}$, then so does the third.

Proof

use the homological serre spectral sequence By assumption we get constant coefficients.

$\tilde{H}_*(F), \tilde{H}_*(B)$ lie in $\mathcal{C}$

follows from homological serre spectral sequence for a simple map remains inside a serre class

$\tilde{H}_*(F), \tilde{H}_*(E)$ lie in $\mathcal{C}$

By assumption we have a finite filtration of $\tilde{H}_p(E)$, i.e. in particular subobjects of an object in $\mathcal{C}$. Since the serre spectral sequence is strongly converging, it must be degreewise eventually constant.

$\tilde{H}_*(B), \tilde{H}_*(E)$ lie in $\mathcal{C}$
