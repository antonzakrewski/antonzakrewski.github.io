continuous surjection from a compact hausdorff space to a T1 space contains minimal compact subset which surjects

Proposition

Let $f: X \twoheadrightarrow Y$ be a surjective continuous map from a compact hausdorff space to a T1 space. Then there exists a minimal closed subset $A\subseteq X$ such that $f[A] = Y$, i.e. for $B \subsetneq A$ closed and proper we get that $f[B] \neq Y$

Proof

consider the partially ordered set of closed subsets $C \subseteq X$ with $f[C] = Y$ with inclusion as relation. Note that it is nonempty, as $X$ suffices. We claim that it contains a minimal element: using Zorn's lemma we want to show that a chain of closed subsets satisfies the conditions above. Let $X_i$ be such a chain. Then the intersection $\bigcap X_i$ will be closed and compact. We claim that

\begin{align*} f[ \bigcap X_i] = Y \end{align*}

So let $y \in Y$ be arbitrary, then $f^{-1}[ \{y\}]$ will be closed and compact, since $\{y\}$ is closed by assumption ($Y$ is T1). then $X_i \cap f^{-1}[ \{y\}]$ will also be nonempty, closed and compact (since $y \in f[X_i] = Y$). In particular $(\bigcap X_i) \cap f^{-1}[ \{y\}] = \bigcap (X_i \cap f^{-1}[ \{y\}]) \neq \emptyset$ by compact space and nonempty finite intersection imply nonempty intersection and contraposition.
