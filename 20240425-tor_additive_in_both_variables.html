tor additive in both variables

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod Then the Tor Functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tor}_n: \mathrm{RMod} \times \mathrm{ModR} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab} \end{align*}

is an additive functor in both variables

Proof

follows from the

  • left derived functor as additive functor
