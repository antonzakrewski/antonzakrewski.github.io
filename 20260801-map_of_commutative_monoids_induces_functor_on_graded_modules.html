map of commutative monoids induces functor on graded modules

Proposition

Let $f: M \rightarrow N$ be a monoid-homomorphism between commutative monoids Then this induces a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{N-grMod}_{A} \rightarrow& \mathrm{M-grMod}_{f^*(A)} \end{align*}
