sign automorphism and specht modules

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{sgn}: \mathbb{C}[S_n] \rightarrow \mathbb{C}[S_n]$ be the sign automorphism of the S_n group ring. Then this maps specht module $V_{\lambda}$ to the specht module of the conjugate partition

Proof

Recall that

\begin{align*} V_{\lambda} =& \mathbb{C}[S_n] c_{\lambda} \\ =& \mathbb{C}[S_n] (\frac{1}{\vert P_{\lambda} \vert } \sum_{g \in P_{\lambda}} g \cdot \frac{1}{\vert Q_{\lambda} \vert } \sum_{g \in Q_{\lambda}} (-1)^{\mathrm{sgn}(g)} g) \end{align*}

and applying generated module and homomorphism gives

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sgn}(V_{\lambda}) =& \mathrm{sgn}(\mathbb{C}[S_n] c_{\lambda}) \\ =& \mathbb{C}[S_n] \mathrm{sgn}(c_{\lambda}) \\ =& \mathbb{C}[S_n] \mathrm{sgn}(\frac{1}{\vert P_{\lambda} \vert} \sum_{g \in P_{\lambda}} g \cdot \frac{1}{\vert Q_{\lambda} \vert } \sum_{g \in Q_{\lambda}} (-1)^{\mathrm{sgn}(g)} g)) \\ =& \mathbb{C}[S_n] \frac{1}{\vert P_{\lambda} \vert} \sum_{g \in P_{\lambda}} \mathrm{sgn}(g) \cdot \frac{1}{\vert Q_{\lambda} \vert } \sum_{g \in Q_{\lambda}} \mathrm{sgn}((-1)^{\mathrm{sgn}(g)} g) \\ =& \mathbb{C}[S_n] \frac{1}{\vert P_{\lambda} \vert} \sum_{g \in P_{\lambda}} (-1)^{\mathrm{sgn}(g)} g \frac{1}{\vert Q_{\lambda} \vert } \sum_{g \in Q_{\lambda}} g) \\ =& \mathbb{C}[S_n] \frac{1}{\vert Q_{\lambda^*} \vert} \sum_{g \in Q_{\lambda^*}} \frac{1}{ \vert P_{\lambda^*} \vert }(-1)^{\mathrm{sgn}(g)} g \cdot \frac{1}{\vert P_{\lambda^*} \vert } \sum_{g \in P_{\lambda}} g) \end{align*}

Now use specht module and order to conclude that

\begin{align*} \mathbb{C}[S_n] \frac{1}{\vert Q_{\lambda^*} \vert} \sum_{g \in Q_{\lambda^*}} \frac{1}{ \vert P_{\lambda^*} \vert }(-1)^{\mathrm{sgn}(g)} g \cdot \frac{1}{\vert P_{\lambda^*} \vert } \sum_{g \in P_{\lambda}} g) =& \mathbb{C}[S_n] c_{\lambda^*} \end{align*}
