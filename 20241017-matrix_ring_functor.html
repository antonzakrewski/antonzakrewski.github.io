matrix ring functor

Definition

Given category Ring, the matrix ring functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{M}_n(-): \mathrm{Ring} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ring} \\ R \mapsto& \mathrm{M}_n(R) \\ (f: R \rightarrow S) \mapsto f^*: M_n(R) \rightarrow M_n(S) \end{align*}

where

  • ring homomorphism induces ring homomorphism between matrix rings
