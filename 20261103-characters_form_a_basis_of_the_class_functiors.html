characters form a basis of the class functions

Proposition

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field with $\mathrm{char}(k) \not\mid \vert G \vert$. Then $\chi_{V}$ for $V$ a simple representation of $K[G]$ give a basis of $CK[G]$

Proof

corollary of:

  • maschkes theorem about semisimple group rings
  • class functions are equivalent to the dual space of the cocenter
  • characters form a basis of the dual of the quotient modulo the commutator
