inverse image sheaf functor

Definition / Proposition

Let $(X,\mathcal{T}_X)$ and $(Y,\mathcal{T}_Y)$ be topological spaces and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a continuous map. Given a (sufficiently) bicomplete category $\mathcal{C}$ the \(\mathcal{C}\)-valued category of sheaves $\mathrm{Sh}(X), \mathrm{Sh}(Y)$, the inverse image sheaf functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} f^{-1}: \mathrm{Sh}(Y) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Sh}(X) \\ \mathcal{F} \mapsto& \mathrm{Sheaf}( \begin{cases} U \mapsto& \mathrm{colim}_{V \subseteq f[U]} \mathcal{F}(V))) \iota^{\mathrm{op}} U \rightarrow U': \mapsto& (\iota': \mathrm{colim}_{V \subseteq f[U]} \mathcal{F}(V) \rightarrow \mathrm{colim}_{V \subseteq f[U'] \mathcal{F}(V)) \end{cases} (\eta: \mathcal{F}_1 \rightarrow \mathcal{F}_2) \mapsto& \eta' \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Sheaf}$ is the sheafification and $f^{-1}\mathcal{F}$ is the inverse image sheaf

Proof

functorality

Here functorality of both $f^{-1}(\mathcal{F}): \mathrm{Op}(X)^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ and $f^{-1}: \mathrm{Sh}(Y) \rightarrow \mathrm{Sh}(X)$ is given analogously to the colim functor, where the identity resp. the composition is a morphism ensuring commutativity and hence unique by universal property
