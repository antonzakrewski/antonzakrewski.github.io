kernel of a localization

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $M$ an \(R\)-module and $S \subseteq R$ a multiplicatively closed subset. For the localization $S^{-1}M$ and the canonical homomorphism

\begin{align*} \iota: M \rightarrow S^{-1}M \end{align*}

the kernel is

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ker}(\iota) = \{m \in M \vert \exists s \in S : s \cdot m = 0\} \end{align*}

Proof
