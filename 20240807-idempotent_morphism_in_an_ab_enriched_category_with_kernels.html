idempotent morphism in an ab-enriched category with kernels

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an Ab-enriched category, $f$ an idempotent morphism, such that the kernel $\mathrm{ker}(f), \mathrm{ker}(1 -f)$ exist. Then there exists a decomposition

for an isomorphism $g: A \rightarrow \mathrm{ker}(f) \oplus \mathrm{ker}(1 - f)$

Proof

Note that

\begin{align*} f \circ (1 - f) =& 0 \\ (1 - f) \circ f =& 0 \end{align*}

Therefore we get diagrams 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
A \arrow[]{rd}{\pi_{1 -f}} \arrow[]{r}{f}& A \arrow[]{r}{1 - f} & A
& \mathrm{ker}(1 - f) \arrow[]{ru}{\iota_{1 - f}}& \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
A \arrow[]{rd}{\pi_f} \arrow[]{r}{(1 - f)}& A \arrow[]{r}{f} & A
& \mathrm{ker}(f) \arrow[]{ru}{\iota_{f}}& \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Then we get

as an isomorphism, as

\begin{align*} \iota_f \coprod \iota_{1 - f} \circ \pi_{f} \times \pi_{1 - f} =& \iota_f \circ \pi_f + \iota_{1 - f} \circ \pi_{1 - f} \\ =& \mathrm{id} - f + f \\ =& \mathrm{id} \end{align*}

Furhtermore by construction we have

\begin{align*} \pi_{1 - f} \circ \iota_{1 - f} = f \end{align*}

hence the diagram

commutes.
