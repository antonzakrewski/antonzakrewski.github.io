cotangent module and open immersions

Proposition

Let $j: U \hookrightarrow Y$ be an open immersion and $f: Y \rightarrow X$ a map of algebraic functors admitting a cotangent module Then $f \circ j$ admits a ctoangent module and the map $f^*(\Omega_f) \rightarrow \Omega_{f \circ j}$ is an isomorphism

Proof
