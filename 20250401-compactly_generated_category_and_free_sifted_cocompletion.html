compactly generated category and free sifted cocompletion

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a cocomplete infinity category and $S$ a set of compact projective <generators>. Let $\mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathcal{C}^0$ be a skeleton for $\mathcal{C}^0$, where $\mathcal{C}^0$ is the full infinity subcategory of finite infinity coproducts of $S$.

Then the induced functor

\begin{align*} P_{\Sigma}(\mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

on free infinity sifted cocompletion is an equivalence

Proof
