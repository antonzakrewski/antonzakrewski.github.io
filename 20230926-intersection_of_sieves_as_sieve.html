intersection of sieves

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category, $U \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and $S_i$ sieves on $U$. Then the intersection $\bigcap_{i \in I} S_i$ is defined through

\begin{align*} (\bigcap_{i \in I} S_i)(A) = \bigcap_{i \in I} S_i(A) \end{align*}

for $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$

Proof
