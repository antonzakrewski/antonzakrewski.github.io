finite free R-Modules equivalent to opposite category

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. Then the category of category of finite free modules is equivalent to its opposite category via.

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{FinFreeRMod}}(-,R): \mathrm{FinFreeRMod}^{op} \rightarrow \mathrm{FinFreeRMod} \end{align*}

Proof

essentially surjective

follows as

\begin{align*} R \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{FinFreeRMod}}(R,R) \\ R^n \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{FinFreeRMod}}(R^n,R) \end{align*}

fully faithful

We reduce it to the case

\begin{align*} R \rightarrow R \end{align*}

by writing

\begin{align*} R^n \simeq$ \coprod_{i = 1}^n R \\ R^n \simeq$ \prod_{i = 1}^n R \\ \end{align*}

Let $\varphi: R \rightarrow R$ be a morphism, corresponding to $r \in R$. Then (one can check that) precomposition with $\varphi$ corresponds to $r\in R \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{FinFreeRMod}}(R,R)$
