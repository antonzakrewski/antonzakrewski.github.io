contravariant infinity hom functor

Definition

Let $\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}$ be the infinity category of infinity categories. Then for an infinity category $\mathcal{C}$ the contravariant infinity hom functor is defined as infinity functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(-, \mathcal{C}): \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty} \\ \end{align*}
