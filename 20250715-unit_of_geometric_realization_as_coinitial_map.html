unit of geometric realization as coinitial map

Proposition

The unit of the geometric realizations is a coinitial map

Proof

follows as its opposite is given by

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \vert \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \vert \simeq \vert \mathcal{C} \vert \simeq \vert \mathcal{C} \vert^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}

so apply unit of geometric realization as cofinal map
