tensoring with dualizable object and left/right adjoint in a symmetric monoidal category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a symmetric monoidal category and $X$ an object.

TFAE:

  1. $X$ is a dualizable object.
  2. $- \otimes X$ is a right adjoint with left adjoint $X^{\vee} \otimes -$ for some $X^{\vee}$ and the unit / counit are $\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathcal{C}}$ linear.
