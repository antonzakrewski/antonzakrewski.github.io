stable envelope of a presentable fibration

Definition

Let $p: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a presentable fibration. Then a stable envelope is a presentable fibration with a morphism of fibrations 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{E} \arrow[]{rd}{} \arrow[]{rr}{g}& &  \arrow[]{ld}{}\mathcal{D}\\
\mathcal{C} \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

such that $\mathcal{E}$ is the fiberwise stabilization of $\mathcal{D}$ over $\mathcal{C}$ in the sense: Let $\mathrm{RFun}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,-)$ denote the full subcategory of relative right adjoints over $\mathcal{C}$

Let $p:\mathcal{E}' \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a stable presentable fibration, then postcomposition with $g: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{RFun}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{E}', \mathcal{E}) \rightarrow \mathrm{RFun}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{E}', \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}
