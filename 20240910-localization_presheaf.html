localization presheaf

Definition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ the zariski spectrum Then the localization presheaf is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{local}: \mathrm{Op}(\mathrm{Spec}(A))^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{CRing} \\ U \mapsto& (A \setminus \bigcup_{\mathfrak{p} \in U} \mathfrak{p})^{-1} A \end{align*}
