homotopy category functor preserves adjunctions

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ infinity functors and $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$ an infinity adjunction . Then the infinity homotopy category functor preserves this adjunction, i.e.

\begin{align*} \mathrm{hocat}(\mathcal{F}) \dashv \mathrm{hocat}(\mathcal{G}) \end{align*}

Proof

  • infinity adjunction and triangle identities
  • Adjunction and triangle identity
  • where the: homotopy category functor preserves natural transformations
