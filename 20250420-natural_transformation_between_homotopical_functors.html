natural transformation between homotopical functors induces natural transformation

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}$ be homotopical categories and $f_1,f_2: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ homotopical functors Suppose $\eta: f_1 \rightarrow f_2$ is a natural transformation.

Then there exists a naturl transformation

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ho}(\eta): \mathrm{ho}(f_1) \rightarrow \mathrm{ho}(f_2) \end{align*}

Proof

follows as a natural transformation is equivalently given by a functor

\begin{align*} \eta: \mathcal{C} \times \Delta^1 \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}
