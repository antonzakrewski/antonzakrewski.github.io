torsion pair closed under extension

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{F}) \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ be a torsion pair of an abelian category. Then $\mathcal{T}$ resp. $\mathcal{F}$ are closed under extensions

Proof

follows from the essential image of a torsion pair and applying the covariant hom-functor as left-exact functor
