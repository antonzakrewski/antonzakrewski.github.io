precomposition with a morphism as injection on hom sets and epimorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a locally small category, $A,B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,-), \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(B,-)$ be the contravariant hom-functor and $f: A \rightarrow B$ a morphism TFAE:

  1. each components of the natural transformation $f^{\rightarrow}: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,A) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,B)$ is injective
  2. $f$ is an epimorphism
