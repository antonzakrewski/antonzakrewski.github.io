stable k-stem

Definition

Let $k \in \mathbb{Z}$, then the stable \(k\)-stem is defined as sequential colimit

\begin{align*} \pi_{k}(\mathbb{S}) \coloneqq \mathrm{colim}( \pi_k(S^0) \rightarrow \pi_{k+1}(S^1) ...) \end{align*}

of the homotopy groups

under the

  1. freudenthal suspension map for homotopy groups
  2. for the identification $\Sigma S^n \cong S^{n+1}$
