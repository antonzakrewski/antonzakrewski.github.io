cogenerator with products implies monomorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $I$ an cogenerator, $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ an object, such that the products $\prod_{\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(A,I)}$ exists. Then there exists a canonical monomorphism

\begin{align*} \iota: A \rightarrow \prod_{\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,I) I \end{align*}

Proof

Let 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
& & I_{f: A \rightarrow I} \\
A \arrow[]{rru}{} \arrow[dashed]{r}{} & \prod_{\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,I) I \arrow[]{ru}{} & 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Suppose there exist parallel morphisms $f \neq g$.

Then by assumption of $I$ being a cogenerator there exists a morphism

\begin{align*} h: A \rightarrow I \end{align*}

such that

\begin{align*} h \circ g \neq h \circ f \end{align*}

Therefore

does not commute, hence

\begin{align*} \iota \circ g \neq \iota \circ f \end{align*}
