anima is postnikov complete

Proposition

The category of Anima is postnikov complete category.

Proof

fully faithful

We need to show that the canonical map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,Y) \rightarrow \lim_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}_{\leq n}}(\tau_{\leq n} X, \tau_{\leq n} Y) \end{align*}

is an equivalence

For that we may notice that the n-truncation functor is a left adjoint, hence there exists a natural equivalence $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}_{\leq n}}(\tau_{\leq n} X, \tau_{\leq n} Y) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}_{\leq n}}(X, \tau_{\leq n} Y)$. Furthermore there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \lim_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}_{\leq n}}(X, \tau_{\leq n} Y) \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}_{\leq n}}(X, \lim_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \tau_{\leq n} Y) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}_{\leq n}}(X, Y) \end{align*}

where we have used the equivalence $\lim_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \tau_{\leq n} Y$

essentially surjective - wonky

Let $(X_n) \in \lim_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathrm{An}_{\leq n}$ be an object. Then there exist equivalences $\tau_{\leq m} X_n \simeq X_{\mathrm{min}(n,m)}$.

Moreover, we may take the limit and some sequential limit of anima with eventually constant homotopy groups shows that $\lim_{n} X_n$ has $\tau_{\leq m} (\lim_{n} X_n) \simeq X_m$, i.e. $\lim_n X_n$ maps to $X_n$.
