spectral radius of bounded endomorphism

Definition

Let $T \in \mathrm{BL}(X,X)$ be a bounded operator of a banach space. Then the spectral radius is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sup}_{\lambda \in \mathrm{spec}(T)} \vert \lambda \vert \end{align*}

Remark

  • spectrum is bounded by operator norm
