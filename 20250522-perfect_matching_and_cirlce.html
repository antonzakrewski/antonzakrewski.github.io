perfect matching and circle

Proposition

Let $M_1,M_2$ be perfect matchings of a Graph $G$. Suppose $\gamma: C_n \rightarrow M_1 \cup M_2$ is a circle.

Then the symmetric difference is a perfect matching $M = M_1 \Delta C$.

Proof

Note that $C$ contains an even amount of vertices since $M_1$ and $M_2$ edges appear alternating. Then $M_1 \setminus C$ is a perfect matching of $G \setminus C$. Furthermore $C \setminus M_1$ is a perfect matching of $C$.

Thus the disjoint union of those perfect matchings is a perfect matching of the disjoint union.
