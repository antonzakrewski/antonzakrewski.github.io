functor mapping commutative rings to modules in Ab via extension of scalars

Proposition

There exists a $(2,1)$-categorical functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mod}^*: \mathrm{CRing} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat} \\ R \mapsto& \mathrm{Mod}_R \\ (f: R \rightarrow S) \mapsto& (S \otimes_R -: \mathrm{Mod}_R \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}_S) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • anti-equivalence of CatL and CatR
  • functor from commutative rings to modules in Ab for restriction of scalars
