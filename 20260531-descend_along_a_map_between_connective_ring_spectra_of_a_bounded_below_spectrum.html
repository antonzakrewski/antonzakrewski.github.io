descend along a map between connective ring spectra of a bounded below spectrum

Proposition

Let $R,S$ be connective E_infinity-ring spectra, $\varphi: S \rightarrow R$ a map of ring spectra and $M$ an $S$ module spectrum such that

  1. $\pi_0(S) \rightarrow \pi_0(R)$ is a surjection
  2. $\pi_0(S)/p \rightarrow \pi_0(R)/p$ is an isomorphism
  3. $M$ is a bounded below $S$ spectrum
  4. $M$ is a bounded p-torsion spectrum

Then $M$ descends alongs $\varphi: S \rightarrow R$.

Proof

We first show that any bounded below and bounded above, bounded p-torsion spectrum satisfies descend. For that it suffices to show that $\coprod \mathbb{F}_p$ satisfies descend (cf. bounded below and bounded above, bounded p-torsion spectrum lies in the generated subcategory of Fp, subcategory of spectra with descend is a stable subcategory).

general case

Let $F(-) \coloneqq \mathrm{fib}(- \Rightarrow \lim_{\Delta}(R^{\otimes_{S} \bullet + 1} \otimes_{S} M)$ Then a module $M$ satisfies descend if and only if $F(M)$ vanishes and $F(-)$ is an exact functor.

Let $M$ be bounded below, bounded p-torsion. Observe that there exists a fiber sequence

\begin{align*} \tau_{\geq n} M \rightarrow M \rightarrow \tau_{\leq n-1}(M) \end{align*}

and moreover also a fiber sequence

\begin{align*} F(\tau_{\geq n} M) \rightarrow F(M) \rightarrow F(\tau_{\leq n-1}(M)) \end{align*}

where the RHS vanishes since $\tau_{\leq n-1}(M)$ is bounded. In particular, $F(\tau_{\geq n} M) \rightarrow F(M)$ is an equivalence.
