Z-linear map of spectra in general not Fp linear

Proposition

Let $A,B$ be $\mathbb{F}_p$ module spectra and $f: A \rightarrow B$ a \(\mathbb{Z}\)-linear map. Then $f$ is in general not $\mathbb{F}_p$ linear.

Proof

consider the short exact sequence

\begin{align*} \mathbb{F}_p \rightarrow \mathbb{F}_{p^2} \rightarrow \mathbb{F}_p \end{align*}

of abelian groups.

This induces a map

\begin{align*} \mathbb{F}_p \rightarrow \mathbb{F}_p[1] \end{align*}

with fiber $\mathbb{F}_{p^2}$ Moreover, the forgetful functor $\mathrm{Mod}(\mathbb{F}_p) \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}(\mathbb{Z})$ preserves limits / is exact (cf. infinite extension of scalars/ infinite co-extension of scalars).

But $\mathbb{F}_{p^2}$ is not a module spectrum over $\mathbb{F}_p$ (e.g. as $\pi_*(-)$ is not a graded module over $\pi_0(R) = \mathbb{F}_p$).
