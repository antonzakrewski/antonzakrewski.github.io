cosimplicial hom construction

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{F}: \Delta \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a cosimplicial object. Then the cosimplicial hom construction assigns to an object $C \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ the simplicial set

\begin{align*} C^{\mathcal{F}}_n \coloneqq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{F}(n), C) \end{align*}

with the degeneracy and face maps given by functorality of $\mathrm{Hom}_{}(-, C)$

Note that it is also a functor

\begin{align*} (-)^{\mathcal{F}}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{sSet} \end{align*}
