coinitial approximation of a kappa filtered diagram by smaller filtered colimits

Proposition

Let $\kappa, \lambda$ be a regular cardinals such that $\kappa > \lambda$. Let $I: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a kappa filtered diagram into a category with (enough) colimits, then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{I} X_i \simeq \mathrm{colim}_{J \subseteq I, \vert J \vert \leq \lambda, J \textrm{ filtered}} \colim_{J} \end{align*}
