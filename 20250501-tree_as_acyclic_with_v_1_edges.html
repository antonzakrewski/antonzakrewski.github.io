tree as acyclic graph with V - 1 edges

Proposition

Let $G = (V,E)$ be a graph.

TFAE:

  1. $G$ contains $\vert V \vert - 1$ edges and is acyclic graph
  2. $G$ is a tree

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Assume $G$ is not connected. Then it is a forest and hence can be connected to a tree $T$ with $V_G = V_T$ Note that by construction

\begin{align*} E_G < E_T \end{align*}

But by 2) $\implies$ 1) we get

\begin{align*} V_G < V_T = \vert V \vert - 1 \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)

Proof by induction, induct on $\vert V \vert$

induction basis

follows for the unique tree with $1$ edge

induction step

assume $\vert V \vert \geq 2$. Then $G$ contains at least two leafs so choose one $v$ and remove it to get the new tree $T \coloneqq G \setminus \{v\}$. (cf. tree minus leaf as tree). Note that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{E}_G = \mathrm{E}_T + 1 \end{align*}

and by induction $\mathrm{E}_T = \vert V \setminus \{v\}\vert - 1$ which shows

\begin{align*} \mathrm{E}_G = \vert V \vert - 1 \end{align*}
