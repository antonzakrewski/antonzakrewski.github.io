colimit of bifiber sequences in PrL stable is a bifiber sequence up to fully faithfullness

Proposition

Let $A_i \rightarrow{\mathcal{F}_i} B_i \xrightarrow{\mathcal{L}_{i}} C_i$ be a diagram of bifiber sequences in category PrL stable. Then $\mathrm{im}(\mathrm{colim} A_i \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} B_i) \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} B_i \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} C_i$ is a bifiber sequence.

Proof

Observe that $\mathrm{colim} A_i \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} B_i \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} C_i$ is a quotient sequence, since colimits commute with colimits. Let $\mathcal{A} = \mathrm{colim} A_i$ and similarly for $\mathcal{B}, \mathcal{C}$. So it remains to show that $A_i \rightarrow B_i$ is essentially surjective on the fiber: Let $\mathcal{L} \dashv \mathcal{R}, \mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$ be the adjoints after taking the colimit Let $x \in \mathcal{B}$ lie in $\mathrm{fib}(\mathcal{L})$, then it suffices to show that $\mathcal{F} \mathcal{G}(x) \rightarrow x$ is an equivalence. But this is an equivalence if and only if the cofiber is trivial and the cofiber of this map identifies with $\mathcal{LR}(x) \simeq 0$.
