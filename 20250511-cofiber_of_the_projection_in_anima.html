cofiber of the projection in Anima

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be a anima and $\mathrm{pr}_X: X \times Y \rightarrow X$ the projection. Then the cofiber is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{cofib}(\mathrm{pr_X}) \simeq& X_+ \wedge \Sigma Y \\ \simeq& \Sigma(X \wedge Y) \vee \Sigma Y \end{align*}

where $X_+$ is the pointification and $\wedge$ the smash product of pointed anima.
