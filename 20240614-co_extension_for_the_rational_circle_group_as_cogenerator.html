co-extension for the rational circle group as cogenerator

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod Then the co-extension of scalars of the rational circle group

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(R,\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

is a cogenerator

Proof

corollary of:

  • restriction of scalars as faithful functor
  • right adjoint of a faithful left adjoint preserves cogenerator
  • co-extension of scalars as right adjoint
