p-completion mod p

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum. Then $X_{p}^{\wedge}/p^n \simeq X/p^n$.

Proof

recall that p-completion of spectra is exact, hence by exact functor between stable categories preserves mod p

\begin{align*} (X_{p}^{\wedge})/p^n \simeq (X/p^n)_p^{\wedge} \end{align*}

so it suffices to recall that $X/p^n$ is \(p\)-complete (cf. mod p^n of an object is p-complete in a stable category), which shows

\begin{align*} (X_{p}^{\wedge})/p^n \simeq (X/p^n)_p^{\wedge} \simeq X/p^n \end{align*}

(cf. p-completion of a spectrum is p-complete).
