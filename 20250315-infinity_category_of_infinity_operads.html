infinity category of infinity operads

Definition

The infinity category of infinity operads is defined as homotopy coherent nerve of the kan enriched category containing

  1. (small) infinity operads as objects
  2. $\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{O}')^{\simeq} = \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}, \mathcal{O}'^{\otimes})$ as hom object
