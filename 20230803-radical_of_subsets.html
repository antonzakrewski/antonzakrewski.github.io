radical of subsets

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq \mathfrak{b}$ ideals. Then

\begin{align*} \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}} \subseteq \sqrt{\mathfrak{b}} \end{align*}

Proof

Suppose $a \in \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}}$, then there exists an $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $a^n \in \mathfrak{a}$. Hence $a^n \in \mathfrak{b}$ and therefore $a \in \sqrt{\mathfrak{b}}$
