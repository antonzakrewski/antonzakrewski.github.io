steenrod operation in RP infinity

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty} \cong \mathbb{F}_2[\alpha]$ with $\mathrm{deg}(\alpha) = 1$ be the infinite real projective space. Then the steenrod operations are given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}^i(\alpha^l) = \binom{l}{i} \alpha^{i+l} \end{align*}

Proof

Proof by induction

induction basis: $i$ arbitrary, $l = 0$

note that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}^i(\alpha^0) = \binom{0}{i} \alpha^i \end{align*}

holds by triviality of steenrod operation

induction step $i,l \Rightarrow i,l+1$

Rekursionsformel (Binomialkoeffizient) Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}^i(\alpha^{l+1}) =& \mathrm{Sq}^i(\alpha^l) \cdot \mathrm{Sq}^0(\alpha) + \mathrm{Sq}^{i-1}(\alpha^l) \cdot \mathrm{Sq}^{1}(\alpha) \\ =& \mathrm{Sq}^i(\alpha^l) \cdot 1 \cdot \alpha + \mathrm{Sq}^{i-1}(\alpha^l) \cdot 1 \cdot \alpha^2 \\ =& \binom{l}{i} \alpha^{l+i+1} + \binom{l}{i-1} \alpha^{i-1+l+2} \\ =& \binom{l+1}{i} \alpha^{l+i+i} \end{align*}

where we use $\mathrm{Sq}^{i}(\alpha) = 0$ for $i \geq 2$ (cf. triviality of steenrod operation) and Cartan formula of steenrod operations for the cup product
