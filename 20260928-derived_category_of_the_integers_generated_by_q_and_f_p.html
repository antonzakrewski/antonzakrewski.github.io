derived category of the integers generated by Q and F_p under colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C} \subseteq \mathcal{D}(\mathbb{Z})$ be the full localizing subcategory containing $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{F}_p$. Then $\mathcal{C} = \mathcal{D}(\mathbb{Z})$.

Proof

Observe that it suffices to show that $\mathbb{Z} \in \mathcal{C}$. Here we have $\mathbb{Z} = \mathrm{fib}(\mathbb{Q} \rightarrow \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})$ where $\mathbb{Q} \in \mathcal{C}$ so it remains to show $\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z} \in \mathcal{C}$. Moreover, we can inductively build $\mathbb{Z}/p^n$ as extension of $\mathbb{Z}/p \mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{Z}/p^{n-1} \mathbb{Z}$. Furthermore, $\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$ is the filtered colimit of the finite torsion subgroups, namely sums of $\mathbb{Z}/p_i^{n_i}$.
