normal bundle of an embedding of manifolds

Definition

Let $\iota: M \rightarrow W$ be an embedding of Manifolds. Let $T(M,W): M \rightarrow \mathrm{BTop}(w;m)$ be the relative topological tangent bundle.

A normal bundle of the embedding consists of a map

\begin{align*} \nu(i): M \rightarrow \mathrm{BTop}(w-m) \end{align*}

and a homotopy between the composition 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
M  \arrow[equal, ]{d}{} \rar{\nu(i),?} & \mathrm{BTop}(w-m) \times \mathrm{BTop}(m) \rar{} & \mathrm{BTop}(w;m) \arrow[equal, ]{d}{}\\
M \rar{T(w;m)}  && \mathrm{BTop}(w;m) 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
