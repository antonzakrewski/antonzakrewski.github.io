Proposition

The subcategory of the category of smooth vector bundles containing maps which have locally constant rank is idempotent complete category.

Proof

Let $e: (p: E \rightarrow B) \rightarrow (p: E \rightarrow B)$ be an idempotent morphism Then there exists an splitting in $\mathrm{Ar}(\mathrm{sMfd}) \supseteq \mathrm{sVB}$ (cf. functor category into an idempotent complete categories is idempotent complete, category of smooth manifolds is idempotent complete). Now let $p': E' \rightarrow B'$ be the idempotent splitting in $\mathrm{Ar}(\mathrm{sMfd}$.

We need to show that $p': E' \rightarrow B'$ admits a vector bundle structure such that $p \rightarrow p' \rightarrow p$ is fiberwise linear and of locally constant rank. So let $b' \in B'$, then it follows that there exists a neighbourhood $U \subseteq B$ of $b' \in B' \subseteq B$ such that the map on the fibers has constant rank. So in particular the preimage of $p'^{-1}[\tilde{b}] \simeq e[\tilde{b} \times \mathbb{R}^m] \simeq \mathbb{R}^n$ for $n = \mathrm{rank}$ and $\tilde{b} \in U \cap B'$.
