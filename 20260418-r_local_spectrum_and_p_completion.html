R-local spectrum and p-completion

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum, $p$ a prime and $R = \mathbb{Z}[p^{-1}]$ TFAE:

  1. $X$ is an R-local spectrum
  2. the p-completion $X_{p}^{\wedge}$ is the zero object

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

observe that $X/p^{n}$ is given by the zero object as it is the cofiber of the equivalence $X \xrightarrow{\cdot p^n} X$. Hence $X_{p}^{\wedge}$ is the limit of the zero object, i.e., in particular also the zero object (cf. infinity limit of terminal objects as terminal object).

2) $\implies$ 1)

This follows from p-completion mod p: To show that $X$ is $R$-local, it suffices to show that $X/p \simeq 0$. But

\begin{align*} X/p \simeq& (X_p^{\wedge})/p \\ \simeq& 0/p \\ \simeq& 0 \end{align*}

which shows the statement.
