underlying infinity category of a model category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a model category. Then the underlying infinity category of $\mathcal{A}$ is defined up to equivalence as

\begin{align*} N(A^c)[W^{-1}] \end{align*}

where

  1. $N(-)$ is the nerve
  2. $A^c$ is the category of cofibrant objects
  3. $(-)[W^{-1}]$ denotes the dwyer-kan localization at the weak equivalence
