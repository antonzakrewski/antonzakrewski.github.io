semifunctor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be semicategories. A semifunctor is a map

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \\ \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{D}) \\ \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,B) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(A), \mathcal{F}(B)) \end{align*}

compatible with the composition, i.e.

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(f \circ g) = \mathcal{F}(f) \circ \mathcal{F}(g) \end{align*}
