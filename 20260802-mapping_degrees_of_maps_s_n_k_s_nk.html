mapping degrees of maps (S^n)^k to S^nk

Proposition

Let $n,k \geq 1$, then the degrees $D((S^{n})^k), S^{n \cdot k})$ is given by $\mathbb{Z}$.

Proof

Observe that degree of maps from S^n to S^n & degree of a composites show that if we can realize a map with degree $\pm 1$, then we can realize any degre. So consider the cofiber sequence

which is once rotated from the cw structure $S^{nk - 1} \rightarrow \bigvee_{k} S^n \rightarrow \prod_{k} S^n$.

Then the long exact sequence and homology / long exact sequence of a pair of singular cohomology shows that the map is an isomorphism on top degree, hence has degre $\pm 1$.
