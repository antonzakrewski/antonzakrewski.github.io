infinite suspension module of a compact anima as dualizable object

Proposition

Let $X$ be a compact anima, $R[X]$ the suspension module of an anima. Then $R[X]$ is a dualizable object.

Proof

Note that $R$ is compact (cf. object as compact object in the category of modules) and

\begin{align*} R[-]: \mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}(R) \end{align*}

is uniquely defined as colimit preserving functor extending $* \mapsto R$.

Since the forgetful functor - which is the right adjoint - $\mathrm{Mod}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ (forgetful functor from module spectra to anima preserves filtered colimits)

Hence $R[X]$ is also a compact object (alternatively: write $R[X]$ as compact colimit, compact colimits of compact objects is again compact)
