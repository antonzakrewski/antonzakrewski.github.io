category of finite abelian p-groups as abelian category

Proposition

The category of finite abelian p-groups is an abelian category

Proof

exact abelian subcategory and closed under kernel and cokernel

biproduct

  • finite product of p-groups as p-group

kernel

  • subgroup of a p-group as p-group

cokernel

  • quotient group of a p-group as p-group
