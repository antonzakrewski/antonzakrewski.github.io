cosaturated set

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\Sigma \subseteq \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$ a subcollection of morphisms. Then $\Sigma$ is called cosaturated if it is closed under

  1. pullbacks in $\mathcal{C}$
  2. sequential limits in $\mathcal{C}$
  3. products in $\mathcal{C}$
  4. sections in the arrow category $\mathrm{Fun}([1],\mathcal{C})$
