monomial ideal

Definition

Let \(A\) be a ring and $A[X_1,...,X_n]$ the polynomial ring and $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A[X_1,...,X_n]$ an ideal. Then $\mathfrak{a}$ is said to be monomial, if there exist monomials $x^\alpha_i$ such that

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a} = \langle x^{\alpha_i}\rangle \end{align*}
