subcategory of the homotopy category determines sub quasi category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathcal{D} \hookrightarrow h \mathcal{C}$ a subcategory of the homotopy category Then for the pullback

$\mathcal{C}'$ is a sub quasicategory with

  1. $\mathcal{C}'_0 = \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{D})$
  2. $f \in \mathcal{C}'_1 \Leftrightarrow [f] \in \mathcal{D}$
