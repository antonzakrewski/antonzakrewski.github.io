universal line bundle

Definition

Let $\mathbb{K} \in {\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}, \mathbb{H}}$. Then consider the classification of topological line bundles, where $\mathbb{K}$ line bundles are classified by

\begin{align*} [-, \mathbb{KP}^{\infty}] \end{align*}

Thus define the universal line bundle as line bundle corresponding to

\begin{align*} \mathbb{KP}^{\infty} \rightarrow \mathbb{KP}^{\infty} \end{align*}

.
