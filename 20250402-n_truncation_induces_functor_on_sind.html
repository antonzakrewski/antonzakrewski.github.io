n-truncation induces functor on sInd

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category. Then <postcomposition> with n-truncation $\tau_{\leq n}$ induces an infinity functor

\begin{align*} \tau_{\leq n}_*: \mathrm{sInd}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{sInd}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

Proof

  • n-truncation as right adjoint
  • right adjoint preserves limits
  • free infinity sifted cocompletion as finite product preserving functors
