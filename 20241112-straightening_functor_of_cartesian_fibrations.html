straightening functor of cartesian fibrations

Definition

The straightening functor for cartesian fibrations is defined as infinity functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{straight}_{\mathrm{Cart}}: \mathrm{Cart}(B) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}) \end{align*}
