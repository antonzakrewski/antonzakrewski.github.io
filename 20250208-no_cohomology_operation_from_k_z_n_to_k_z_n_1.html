no nontrivial cohomology operation from K(Z,n) to K(Z,n+1)

Proposition

There exist no nontrivial cohomology operations

\begin{align*} H^n(-,\mathbb{Z}) \rightarrow H^{n+1}(-,M) \end{align*}

for an abelian group $M$

Proof

using classification of set valued natural transformation between cohomology it suffices to show that

\begin{align*} H^{n+1}(K(\mathbb{Z},n),M) = 0 \end{align*}

and existence of eilenberg-maclane spaces as construction via moore spaces.

Then using the universal coefficient theorem for singular homology we get

\begin{align*} H^{n+1}(K(\mathbb{Z},n),M) = \mathrm{Hom}(H_{n+1}(K(\mathbb{Z},n),M) \oplus \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathbb{Z}}(H^{n}(K(\mathbb{Z},n)),M) \end{align*}

here

\begin{align*} H^n(K(\mathbb{Z},n)) \cong& \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathbb{Z}}(H_{n-1}(\mathbb{Z}),M) \oplus \mathrm{Hom}(H_n(K(\mathbb{Z},n),\mathbb{Z}) \\ \cong& \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathbb{Z}}(0,M) \oplus \mathrm{Hom}(\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Z}) \\ \cong& 0 \oplus \mathbb{Z} \\ \cong& \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

by covariant ext for a fixed projective module & cellular homology
