Quotient of the nilradical as reduced ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $A_{\mathrm{red}}$ the quotient ring for the nilradical. Then $A_\mathrm{red}$ is a reduced ring

Proof

Let $a \in A_{\mathrm{red}}$ such that $a^n = 0$. w.l.o.g. we may assume that $n = 2$ (see: reduced ring and square)

By construction

\begin{align*} A_{\mathrm{red}} = A / \mathcal{N}_A \end{align*}

we get

\begin{align*} (a + \mathcal{N}_A) \cdot (a \cdot \mathcal{N}_A) =& a^2 + \mathcal{N}_A \\ =& 0 + \mathcal{N}_A \end{align*}

Then we get, that $[a^2] = 0$ and by definition of $\mathcal{N}_A$ also, that $a \in \mathcal{N}_A$. Hence $a = 0 \in A_{\mathrm{red}}$
