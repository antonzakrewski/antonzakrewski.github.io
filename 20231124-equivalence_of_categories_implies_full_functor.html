equivalence of categories implies full functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an equivalence Then $\mathcal{F}$ is a full functor

Proof

By assumption, there exists a functor $\mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ and natural isomorphisms $\eta: \mathcal{F} \mathcal{G} \Rightarrow \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{D}}, \epsilon: \mathcal{G} \circ \mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{C}}$.
