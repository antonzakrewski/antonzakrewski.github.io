day convolution

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be symmetric monoidal categories where

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ is a small category
  2. $\mathcal{D}$ is cocomplete
  3. the tensor product commutes with colimits in both variables

Then there exists a symmetric monoidal structure on the functor category $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ with the property that for

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{lax}}(\mathcal{E}, \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})^{\otimes}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{lax}}(\mathcal{E} \times \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

where $\mathcal{E} \times \mathcal{C}$ is equipped with the pointwise symmetric monoidal structure.
