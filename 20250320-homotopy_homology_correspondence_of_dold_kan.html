homotopy - homology correspondence of dold kan

Proposition

Let

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sAb} \simeq \mathrm{Ch}_{\geq 0} \end{align*}

be the dold-kan correspondence.

Then

  1. the \(n\)-th simplicial homotopy group functor
  2. the \(n\)-th homology functor

coincide up (to natural isomorphism)

Proof
