left orthogonal for a stable subcategory

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A} \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ be a stable subcategory. Then the left orthogonal is defined as stable subcategory

\begin{align*} ^{\perp}\mathcal{A} \coloneqq \{c \in \mathcal{C} \vert \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(c,a) \simeq * \forall a \in \mathcal{A}\} \end{align*}
