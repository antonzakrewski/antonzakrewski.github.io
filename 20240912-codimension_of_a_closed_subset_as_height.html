codimension of a closed subset of an affine scheme as height

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ the affine scheme and $\mathfrak{p}$ a prime ideal Then the codimension of

\begin{align*} \mathrm{codim}(\mathcal{V}(\mathfrak{p}), \mathrm{Spec}(A)) =& \mathrm{dim}(A_{\mathfrak{p}}) \\ =& \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{p}) \end{align*}
