cohomology of the loop space of the quaternionic projective space

Proposition

Let $n \geq 2$. Then the cohomology of $\Omega \mathbb{H}\mathbb{P}^n$ is given additively by 

\begin{align*}
\mathbb{Z}[x_{m(4n+2)}, x_{m(4n+2) + 3}]
\end{align*
with the ring structure given by 
1) $x_{m(4n+2)} \cdot x_{3} = x_{m(4n+2)+3}$
2) $x_{m(4n+2)} \cdot x_{m'(4n+2)} = \binom{m+m'}{m} x_{(m+m')(4n+2)}$
* Proof
