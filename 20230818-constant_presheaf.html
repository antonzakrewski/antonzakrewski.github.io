constant presheaf on a topological space

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space and $A$ a set. Then the constant presheaf with value $A$ is the presheaf $\mathcal{F}$ on $X$ such that for any nonempty, open sets $U \subseteq V \subseteq X$

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(U) =& A \\ \mathcal{F}(\varphi_{V}) =& \mathrm{id}_{A} \end{align*}

Furthermore, $F(\emptyset) \coloneqq \empytset$ and thus $f: \empytset \rightarrow A$
