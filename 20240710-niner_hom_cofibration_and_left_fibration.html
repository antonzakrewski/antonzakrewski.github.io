inner hom, cofibration and left fibration

Proposition

Let $\iota: A \rightarrow B$ be a cofibration of simplicial sets, $p: X \rightarrow Y$ a left fibration. Then for the inner hom of simplicial Sets the induced map

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(B,X) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(A,X) \times_{\mathcal{F}(A,Y)} \mathcal{F}(B,Y) \end{align*}

is a left fibration

Proof
