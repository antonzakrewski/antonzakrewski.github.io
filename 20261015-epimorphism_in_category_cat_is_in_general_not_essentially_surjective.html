epimorphism in category cat is in general not essentially surjective

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an epimorphism in category Cat. Then it is in general not essentially surjective

Proof

any nontrivial idempotent completions suffices: Let $\mathrm{Idem}$ be the category Idem, then the idempotent completion $\mathrm{Idem} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ret}$ is not essentially surjective

But idempotent completions are epimorphisms in Cat
