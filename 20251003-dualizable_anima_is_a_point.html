Proposition

The only dualizable anima with respect to the cartesian monoidal structure is the singleton.

Proof

follows from:

  • monoidal unit is dualizable

b)

Observe that

  1. $X \times \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,*) \simeq X \rightarrow *$ is the terminal morphisms.
  2. $* \rightarrow X \times \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,*) \simeq X$ are both nullhomotopic

Then we may identify the composition

as nullhomotopic maps. This shows that a nullhomotopic map is an equivalence or $X \simeq *$.
