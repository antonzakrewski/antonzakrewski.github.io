quotient of a normed vector space for a closed subspace is a normed vector space

Proposition

Let $(X, \| \cdot \|)$ be a normed vector space and $Z \subseteq X$ a closed subspace Then $X/Z$ is also a normed vector space such that

  1. $\| x + Z \| = \mathrm{inf}_{z \in Z} \| x + z \|$,
  2. $\pi: X \rightarrow X/Z$ has Operatornorm $\| \pi \| = 1$ if $Z \neq X$ and $0$ else.

Proof

well-defined

triangle etc.

non-degeneracy

Let $[x]$ be arbitrary with $\| [x] \| = 0$. Then choose a sequence $x_n$ with $\| x_n \| \rightarrow 0$ and $x_n \in [x]$. Now $\{ x + A\}$ is a closed subspace since $A$ is and $0 \in \{x + A\}$ implies that $[x] = [0]$.

operator-norm

$Z \neq X$

$\| \pi \| \leq 1$

observe that $\| x \| = \| x + 0 \| \geq \mathrm{inf}_{z \in Z} \| x + z \|$.

$\| \pi \| \geq 1$

Let $x + Z$ be a nonzero element in $X/Z$, w.l.o.g. with $\| x + Z \| = \mathrm{inf}_{z \in Z} \| x + z \|$. Then choose a sequence $z_n$ with $\| x + z_n \| \nearrow 1$.

Then

\begin{align*} \| \cdot \| =& \mathrm{sup}_{x' \in X} \frac{\| x' + Z \|}{\| x' \|} \\ \geq& \lim \frac{\| x + Z \|}{\| x + z_n\|} \\ =& 1 \end{align*}

$Z = X$

follows immediately as $X/Z$ is the zero space
