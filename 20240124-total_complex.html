sum total complex

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes and $\mathrm{Ch_*(Ch*}(\mathcal{A}))$ the category of double complexes. Suppose $C_{*,*} \in \mathrm{Ch_*(Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}))$ is a double complex, then the associated total complex is defined

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tot}^{\oplus}(C)_n = \bigoplus__{k + l = n} C_{k,l} \end{align*}

with differentials

\begin{align*} \partial^{\mathrm{Tot}} \coloneqq \partial^{C}_{\mathrm{vert}} + (-1)^{\mathrm{vertical degree}} \partial^{C}_{\mathrm{hor}} \end{align*}
