singular homological degree of an homeomorphism

Proposition

Given the n-sphere an homeomorphism $f: S^n \rightarrow S^n$ the singular homological degree is

\begin{align*} \mathrm{deg}(f) = \pm 1 \end{align*}

with

\begin{align*} \mathrm{deg}(f) = \mathrm{deg}(f^{-1}) \end{align*}
