mapping anima and suspension/loop space for a pointed category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a pointed category Then the mapping space functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

sends

  1. suspensions in the first variable to loop spaces in Anima
  2. loop spaces in the second variable to loop spaces in Anima

Proof

  • mapping anima functor preserves limits
