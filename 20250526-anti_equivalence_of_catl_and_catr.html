anti-equivalence of CatL and CatR

Proposition

There exists an anti-equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cat}^{L} \cong (\mathrm{Cat}^{R})^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}

which is given by

  1. the identity on objects
  2. taking a right adjoint of a left adjoint

Proof

After appealing to large cardinals we may regard both $\mathrm{Cat}^L, \mathrm{Cat}^R$ as categories in $\mathrm{Cat}$ We will show that there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}}(-,\mathrm{Cat}^L)) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}}(-,\mathrm{Cat}^R^{\mathrm{op}}) \end{align*}

which is given by postcomposition with the functor described above.

We can identify

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}}(-, \mathrm{Cat}^L) \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(-, \mathrm{Cat}^L)^{\simeq} \\ \simeq& (\mathrm{Bicart}(-))^{\simeq} \end{align*}

as groupoid core via the cocartesian unstraightening (cf. unstraightening of bicartesian fibration factors through adjoints).

But using cartesian unstraightening we also get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Bicart}(-)^{\simeq} \simeq& (\mathrm{Fun}((-)^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Cat}^R))^{\simeq} \\ \simeq& (\mathrm{Fun}(-, (\mathrm{Cat}^R)^{\mathrm{op}})^{\mathrm{op}})^{\simeq} \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(-, (\mathrm{Cat}^R)^{\mathrm{op}}))^{\simeq} \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Cat}}(-, (\mathrm{Cat}^R)^{\mathrm{op}})) \\ \end{align*}
