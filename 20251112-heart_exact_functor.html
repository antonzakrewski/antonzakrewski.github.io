heart-exact functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}$ be stable categories with heart structures. Then a heart exact functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is an exact functor such that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(\mathcal{C}_{\geq 0}) \subseteq \mathcal{D}_{\geq 0} \mathcal{F}(\mathcal{C}_{\leq 0}) \subseteq \mathcal{D}_{\leq 0} \end{align*}
