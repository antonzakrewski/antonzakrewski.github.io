cardinality of products

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be sets. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{card}(X \times Y) = \begin{cases} \mathrm{card}(\emptyset) & \mbox{if } X = \emptyset \textrm{ or } Y = \emptyset \\ \mathrm{card}(X) \cdot \mathrm{card}(Y) & \mbox{if } X,Y \textrm{ finite} \\ \mathrm{max}(\mathrm{card}(X), \mathrm{card}(Y)) & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof
