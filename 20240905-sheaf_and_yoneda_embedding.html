sheaf and yoneda embedding

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a site and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a presheaf for another category $\mathcal{D}$ TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is a sheaf
  2. the functor $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(d, \mathcal{F}(-))$ is a sheaf for each $d \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{D})$

Proof

using the equalizer condition
