sum of two tangent bundles of spheres is trivial

Proposition

Let $S^n$ be the n-sphere, then $TS^n \oplus TS^n$ is the trivial vector bundle of rank $2n$.

Proof

$n =1$

then the tangent bundle is trivial

  • lie group is parallelizable

$n \geq 2$

consider the map $\mathrm{BO}(2n) \rightarrow \mathrm{BO}$, which is $2n-1$ connected. In particular postcomposition is an isomorphism $[S^n, \mathrm{BO}(2n)] \cong [S^n, \mathrm{BO}]$. So it suffices to show that $TS^n \oplus TS^n$ is stably trivial. But the tangent bundle of the sphere is stably trivial so in particular the sum of stably trivial vector bundles is trivial.
