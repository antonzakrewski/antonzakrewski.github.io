relative singular chain complex functor preserves homotopies

Proposition

Let $f,g: (X,A) \rightarrow (Y,B)$ be a homotopic maps of pairs. Then for the relative singular chain complex functor

\begin{align*} f,g: \mathrm{C}^{\mathrm{sing}}(X,A) \rightarrow C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y,B) \end{align*}

are chain homotopic maps.

Proof

by natural transformation of singular chains and product with the interval
