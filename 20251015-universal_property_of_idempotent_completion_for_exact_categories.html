universal property of idempotent completion for exact categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an exact category, $\mathcal{C}^{\sharp}$ the idempotent completion and $\mathcal{D}$ any idempotent complete category. Then the restriction $\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\sharp}$ induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{ex}}(\mathcal{C}^{\sharp}, \mathcal{D}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{ex}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

with respect to the canonical exact structure

Proof
