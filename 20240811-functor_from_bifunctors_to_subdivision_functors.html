functor from bifunctors to subdivision functors

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ the functor category of bifunctors and $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{§}, \mathcal{D})$ the functor category from the subdivision category. Then there exists a functor

\begin{align*} (-)^§: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{§}, \mathcal{D}) \\ \mathcal{S} \mapsto& \mathcal{S}^{§} \\ \eta \mapsto& \eta^{§} \end{align*}

Proof

$\mathcal{S}^§$

define:

\begin{align*} \mathcal{S}^§: \mathcal{C}^§ \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \\ b^§ \mapsto& \mathcal{S}(b,b) \\ f_{b \rightarrow c}^§ \mapsto& \mathcal{S}(b,c) f_b: b^§ \rightarrow f^§ \mapsto& \mathcal{S}(\mathrm{id}, f): \mathcal{S}(b,b) \rightarrow \mathcal{S}(b,c) \\ f_c: c^§ \rightarrow f^§ \mapsto& \mathcal{S}(f, \mathrm{id}): \mathcal{S}(c,c) \rightarrow \mathcal{S}(b,c) \end{align*}
