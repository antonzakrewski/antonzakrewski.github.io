morphism of extensions

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an abelian category, $A,B, \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ objects and $E_1,E_2$ Extensions

A morphism of Extensions is defined as morphism $f: E_1 \rightarrow E_2$ such that following diagram commutes 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
0 \arrow[]{r}{} & A \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{\mathrm{id}} & E_1 \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{f} } & B \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{\mathrm{id}} & 0 \\
0 \arrow[]{r}{} & A \arrow[]{r}{} & E_2 \arrow[]{r}{} & B \arrow[]{r}{} & 0
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
