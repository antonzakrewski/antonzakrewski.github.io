essential image of a functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an infinity functor Then the essential image is defined as full sub infinity category genereted by objects $d \in \mathcal{D}_0$ such that there exists an object $c \in \mathcal{C}$ and an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(c) \simeq d \end{align*}

in $\mathcal{D}_1$
