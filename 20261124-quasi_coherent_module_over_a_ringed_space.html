quasi-coherent module over a ringed space

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{O}_{X})$ be a ringed space. Then a $\mathcal{O}_{X}$ module $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be quasi-coherent, if for each $x \in X$ there exists an open neighborhood $U$ such that $\mathcal{F}_{\vert U}$ is the quotient

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O}_{X}^{(J)}_{\vert U} \rightarrow \mathcal{O}_{X}^{(I)}_{\vert U} \rightarrow \mathcal{F}_{\vert U} \rightarrow 0 \end{align*}
