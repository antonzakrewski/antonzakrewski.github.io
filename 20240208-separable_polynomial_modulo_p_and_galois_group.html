separable polynomial modulo p and galois group

Proposition

Let $A$ be a UFD and $\mathfrak{p} \subseteq A$ a prime ideal. Suppose $f \in A[X]$ and $\overline{f} \in (A/\mathfrak{p})[X]$ are separable polynomials. Then it holds for the galois group

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Gal}(\overline{f}) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Gal}(f) \end{align*}
