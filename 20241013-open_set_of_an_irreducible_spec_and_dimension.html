open set of an irreducible spec and dimension_

Proposition

Let $A$ be an Integral domain,

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Spec}(A) \end{align*}

the zariski spectrum and $O \subseteq \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ an open subset

Then in general

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(O) \neq \mathrm{dim}(A) \end{align*}

Proof

Consider $\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}$. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}) = \{(p), (0)\} \end{align*}

with the Sierpinski topology

It follows that $(p)$ is open and irreducible.

But

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}\{(p), (0)\} =& 2 \\ \mathrm{dim}((p)) =& 1 \end{align*}
