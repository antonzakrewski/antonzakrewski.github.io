continuous kappa calkin category

Definition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal. The continuous $\kappa$ calkin category functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cal}_{\kappa}^{\textrm{contin}}: \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{perf}} \\ \mathcal{C} \mapsto (\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\kappa})/\widehat{よ}(\mathcal{C}))^{\omega} \end{align*}
