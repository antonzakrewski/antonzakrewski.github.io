unit of a product of rings

Proposition

Let $R_i$ be rings and $\prod R_i$ the direct product of rings. Suppose $(r_i) \in \prod R_i$

TFAE:

  1. each $r_i$ is a unit
  2. $(r_i)$ is a unit

Proof

Note that $1 = (1) \in \prod R_i$.

1) $\implies$ 2)

Thus $(r_i^{-1}) = (r_i)^{-1}$

2) $\implies$ 1)

Thus there exists an inverse $(r_i)^{-1} = (s_i)$. therefore $r_i \cdot s_i = 1$.
