primary ideal and quotient

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a} \subsetneq \mathfrak{p}$ an ideal

TFAE:

  1. $\mathfrak{p}$ is a primary
  2. $\mathfrak{p}/\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A/\mathfrak{a}$ is primary

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Take representatives: Given $[x],[y]$ such that $[x] \cdot [y] \in \mathfrak{p}/\mathfrak{a}$ we conclude that

\begin{align*} x \cdot y \in \mathfrak{p} + \mathfrak{a} = \mathfrak{p} \end{align*}

Hence $x \in \mathfrak{p}$ or $y^n \in \mathfrak{p}$ for some $n$ showing

\begin{align*} [x] \in& \mathfrak{p}/\mathfrak{a} \\ [y]^n \in& \mathfrak{p}/\mathfrak{a} \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)
