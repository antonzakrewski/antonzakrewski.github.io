fibrant replacement of an object

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}, \mathrm{Cof}, \mathrm{Fib})$ be a model category Then for $X \in \mathcal{C}$ a fibrant replacement is defined as fibrant object $RX$ with a weak equivalence

\begin{align*} X \rightarrow RX \end{align*}

Existence

Note that there always exists one, since we may factorize 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
X \arrow[]{rr}{} \arrow[rightarrowtail, ]{rd}{} & & 1 \\
& RX \arrow[dashed]{rd}{\simeq}& 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
