affine covering homeomorphic to affine space

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{P}K^n$ the projective space equipped with the zariski topology on the projective space. Then for the affine covering of a projective space, there exists the homeomorphism

\begin{align*} U_i \cong \mathbb{A}^n \end{align*}

where $\mathbb{A}^n$ is the affine space with the zariski topology on an affine space

Proof

\begin{align*} [\alpha_0,...,\alpha_n] \mapsto& ( \frac{\alpha_0}{\alpha_i},..., \frac{\alpha_n}{\alpha_i}) [b_0,...,b_{i-1},1,...,b_{n-1}] \mapsfrom& (b_0,...,b_{n-1}) \end{align*}
