dimension of a projective variety and homogeneous coordinate ring

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ projective varietyK Then the dimension of $X$ is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(Y) = \mathrm{dim}(\mathcal{O}(X)) - 1 \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{dim}(\mathcal{O}(X))$ is the dimension of the homogeneous coordinate ring

Proof
