poincare duality for a compact manifold with boundary

Proposition

Let $W$ be a \(n+1\)-dimensional compact topological manifold with boundary and $\sigma_W \in H_n(W, \partial W;R)$ be an element corresponding to an R-Orientation for a ring $R$ Then the poincare map

\begin{align*} \cap \sigma_W : H^p(W, \partial(W);M) \rightarrow& H_{n+1-p}(W;M) \\ \cap \sigma_{W}: H^p(W;M) \rightarrow& H_{n+1-p}(W, \partial W;M) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism for every \(R\)-module $M$

Proof

a)

Take an element in the cohomology of a space with compact support

commutes by <naturality of the cup product>

b)

follows from

  1. Five-Lemma
  2. poincare induces map for long exact sequence
  3. poincare duality for $\partial W$ as manifold (cf. orientation of a manifold induces orientation of the boundary)
