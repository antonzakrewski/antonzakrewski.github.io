irreducible element and division by prime

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $a \in A$ an irreducible element.

TFAE:

  1. $a$ is prime
  2. there exists a prime element $p$ such that
\begin{align*} p \mid a \end{align*}

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from

\begin{align*} a \mid a \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)

Note that

\begin{align*} p \mid a \end{align*}

implies that there exists an element $\beta \in A$ such that

\begin{align*} p \cdot \beta = a \end{align*}
