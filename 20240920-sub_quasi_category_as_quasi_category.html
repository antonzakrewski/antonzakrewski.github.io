sub quasi category as quasi category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathcal{C}' \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a sub quasicategory. Then $\mathcal{C}'$ itself is a quasi category

Proof

follows from:

  • spine inclusion factors through inner horn inclusion
  • simplex in a sub quasicategory
