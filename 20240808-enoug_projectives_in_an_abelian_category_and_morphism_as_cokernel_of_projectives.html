enough projectives in an abelian category and morphism as zeroth homology of a projective resolution

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category admitting enough projectives Suppose $f: A \rightarrow B$ is a morphism.

Then there exists a projective resolution

\begin{align*} P_* \rightarrow& A \\ Q_* \rightarrow& B \end{align*}

and a morphism

\begin{align*} f_*: P_* \rightarrow Q_* \end{align*}

such that

\begin{align*} H_0(P_* \rightarrow Q_*) = f \end{align*}

Proof

using existence of a projective resolution unique up to homotopy equivalence for a category admitting enough projectives and extension of a chain map we get a commutative diagram

it follows that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{coker}(P_1 \rightarrow P_0) \cong& A \\ \mathrm{coker}(Q_1 \rightarrow Q_0) \cong& B \\ \end{align*}

and furthermore $f: A \rightarrow B$ satisfies is a solution to the induced morphism by the universal property.
