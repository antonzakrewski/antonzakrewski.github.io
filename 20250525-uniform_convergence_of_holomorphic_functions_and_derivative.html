uniform convergence of holomorphic functions and derivative

Proposition

Let $f_n: \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ be a Sequence of holomorphic function which converges on each compact subset $K \subseteq \Omega$ uniformly against $f$. Then for the derivative

\begin{align*} f_n^{(k)} \rightarrow f^{(k)} \end{align*}

converges uniformly on each compact $K \subseteq \Omega$

Proof
