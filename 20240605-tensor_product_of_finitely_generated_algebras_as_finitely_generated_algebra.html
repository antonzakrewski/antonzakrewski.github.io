tensor product of finite type algebras as finite type algebra

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $B,C$ finite type A-Algebra. Then the tensor product of A-algebras is also a finite type algebra

Proof

Note that an \(A\)-Algebra $B$ is of finite type, if the morphism of algebras

\begin{align*} A[X_1,...,X_n] \twoheadrightarrow B \end{align*}

is surjective

Furtermore note that the underlying set of the tensor product of algebras is given by the tensor product of modules, and

\begin{align*} A[X_1,...,X_n] \otimes_A A[Y_1,...,Y_m] \cong A[X_1,...,X_n,Y_1,...,Y_m] \end{align*}

is the free algebra in $n + m$ generators.

Then as the tensor product of modules is a right exact, hence preserves epimorphism

thus we get an epimorphism

\begin{align*} A[X_1,...,X_n,Y_1,...,Y_m] \twoheadrightarrow B \otimes_A C \end{align*}
