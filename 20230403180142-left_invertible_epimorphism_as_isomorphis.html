monic split epi as isomorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $f$ a monomorphism and split epi. Then $f$ is an isomorphism

Proof

1-categorical case

By assumption, there exists a right-inverse $f^{-1} \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(B,A)$ such that $f \circ f^{-1} = \mathrm{id}_{B}$. Furthermore

\begin{align*} \mathrm{id}_{B} \circ f =& f \circ f^{-1} \circ f \\ (f \circ f^{-1}) \circ f =& \mathrm{id}_{A} \circ f \end{align*}

and since $f$ is monic, it follows that

\begin{align*} f \circ f^{-1}) = \mathrm{id}_{A} \end{align*}

and thus $f$ is an iso

bad proof

  • split epis are functors $\mathrm{Ret} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$.
  • projection to the homotopy category preserves monomorphisms / epimorphisms
  • equivalences can be detected on the homotopy category
