prime ideal

Definition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $\mathfrak{p} \subsetneq R$ a strict ideal. Then $\mathfrak{p}$ is a prime ideal, if for $a,b \in R$ with $a \cdot b \in R$

\begin{align*} a \in \mathfrak{p} \lor b \in \mathfrak{p} \end{align*}
