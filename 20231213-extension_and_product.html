extension and product

Proposition

Let $A,B$ be a commutative ring, $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ be a ring-homomorphism, and $\mathfrak{a}_1,\mathfrak{a}_2 \subseteq A$ ideals.

Then it holds for the extension and product

\begin{align*} (\mathfrak{a}_1 \cdot \mathfrak{a}_2)^e = \mathfrak{a}_1^e \cdot \mathfrak{a}_2^e \end{align*}

Proof

Let $\alpha \in (\mathfrak{a}_1 \cdot \mathfrak{a}_2)^e$ then we may assume that

\begin{align*} \alpha = \varphi(a_1 \cdot a_2) \end{align*}

for $a_1 \in \mathfrak{a}_1, a_2 \in \mathfrak{a}_2$

Thus

\begin{align*} \alpha =& \varphi(a_1 \cdot a_2) \\ =& \varphi(a_1) \cdot \varphi(a_2) \in& \mathfrak{a}_1^{e} \cdot \mathfrak{a}_2^{e} \end{align*}

The other direction follows backwards
