hom of a low dimensional cw complex into the real projective space

Proposition

Let $A$ be a CW-Complex with cells in dimension $\leq n-1$, then there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}}(A, \mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^n)) \cong H^1(A,\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}
