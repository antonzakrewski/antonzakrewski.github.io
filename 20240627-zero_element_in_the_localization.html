zero element in the localization

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $S \subseteq A$ a multiplicatively closed subset. Let $a \in A$.

TFAE:

  1. $a \cdot s = 0$ for some $s \in S$
  2. $\frac{a}{s'} = 0$ for any $s' \in S$
  3. $\frac{a}{1} = 0$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from

\begin{align*} \frac{a}{s'} = \frac{0}{s} \end{align*}

as $s \cdot a = 0 \cdot s'$

2) $\implies$ 3)

special case

3) $\implies$ 1)

Note that

\begin{align*} \frac{a}{1} = \frac{0}{1} \Leftrightarrow \exists s \in S: s \cdot (a \cdot 1) = s \cdot (0 \cdot 1) = s \cdot a = 0 \end{align*}
