vanishing commutator and commuting flows

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be smooth vector fields on a smooth manifold $M$ and $\psi^X,\psi^$ their smooth flow. TFAE:

  1. $[X,Y] = 0$
  2. $\psi_t^X \circ \psi_S^Y = \psi_S^Y \circ \psi_t^X$ for all $s,t$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

\begin{align*} 0 =& [X,Y] \\ =& \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}_{\vert t = t_0} \psi_{-t}^X Y = \\ =& \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}_{\vert t = t_0} \psi_{-t_0} \psi_{-(t-t_0)} Y =& \psi_{-t} ( \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}s}_{\vert s = 0} \psi_{-s}^X Y) \\ =& 0 \end{align*}

since $Y = \psi_{-t*}^X Y$ is constant

2) $\implies$ 1)
