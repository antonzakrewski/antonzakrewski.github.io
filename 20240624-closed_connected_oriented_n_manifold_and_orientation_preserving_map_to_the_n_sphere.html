closed connected oriented n-manifold and map to the n-sphere

Proposition

Let \(M\) be a closed connected \(n\)-dimensional manifold and $\sigma_X \in H_n(X)$ an orientation Then there exists a continuous map

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow S^n \end{align*}

such that

\begin{align*} f_*(\sigma_X}) \in H_n(S^n) \end{align*}

is a generator of $H_n(S^n)$

Proof

Pick a point $x \in X$ and let $U$ be a chart. Then using one point compactification of a subspace as collapse we get for the topological collapse

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow X/(X \setminus U) \cong (U)^+ \cong (\mathbb{R}^n)^+ \cong S^n \end{align*}

Then 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
H_n(X) \arrow[]{r}{f_*} \arrow[]{d}{} & H_n(S^n) \arrow[]{d}{} \arrow[]{d}{}\\
H_n(X, X \setminus \{x\}) \arrow[swap]{r}{\cong}\arrow[]{r}{f_*}}& H_n(S^n, S^n \setminus f(x)) \\
H_n(U, U \setminus \{x\}) \arrow[]{r}{f_*}\arrow[swap]{r}{\cong}\arrow[]{u}{\cong} & H_n(f[U], f[U] \setminus f(u)) \arrow[]{u}{\cong}\\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

where the bottom isomorphism follows as $f$ is a homeomorphism and the vertical are isomorphisms by relative singular homology and excision theorem

note that 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
H_n(X) \arrow[]{r}{f_*} \arrow[dashed]{d}{} & H_n(S^n) \arrow[]{d}{} \arrow[]{d}{}\\
H_n(X, X \setminus \{x\}) \arrow[swap]{r}{\cong}\arrow[]{r}{f_*}}& H_n(S^n, S^n \setminus f(x)) \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

the dashed arrow maps by definition of an orientation a generator to a generator and the right vertical arrow is an isomorphism, as $S^n$ is a orientable, closed and connected (cf. ?)

Hence a generator maps
