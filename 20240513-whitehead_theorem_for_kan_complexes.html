whitehead theorem for kan complexes

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be Kan complexes, $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a morphism of simplicial sets.a

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a simplicial homotopy equivalence
  2. $f$ induces a Bijection on pi_0 of a simplicial set and isomorphism $\pi_n(X,x) \rightarrow \pi_n(Y,f(x))$ for each $x \in X$ and $n \geq 1$ (cf. homotopy group of kan complex)

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)

& \pi_n(Y,y) 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

commutes. As $f^{ \rightarrow}$ is a Bijection

Using two out of three for isomorphisms we conclude that $p^{ \rightarrow}$ is a bijection
