hopf algebra and shear maps

Proposition

Let $C \in \mathrm{BiAlg}(\mathcal{C})$ be a bialgebra. TFAE:

  1. the left shear map of a bialgebra is an equivalence
  2. the right shear map of a bialgebra is an equivalence
  3. $C$ can be upgraded to a Hopf Algebra
