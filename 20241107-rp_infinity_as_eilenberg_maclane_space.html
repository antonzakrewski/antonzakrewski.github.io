RP infinity as Eilenberg-MacLane Space

Proposition

The infinite real projective space is an Eilenberg-MacLane space for $K(\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z},1)$

Proof

follows from the fiber sequence in top

\begin{align*} S^0 \rightarrow S^{\infty} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty} \end{align*}

where $S^{\infty}$ is contractible space (cf. infinite sphere as contractible space).

Hence $\pi_1(\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}) \cong \pi_0(S^0)$ as sets. Thus by classification of classification of groups of order p this has to be $\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}$
