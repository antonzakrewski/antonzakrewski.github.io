homotopy group of a sphere as abelian group

Proposition

Let $S^n$ be the n-sphere. Then the \(i\)-th homotopy group of $S^n$ is abelian

\begin{align*} \pi_i(S^n) \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of:

  • $i = 1$ smaller homotopy group of a sphere
  • $i \geq 2$ commutativity of a higher homotopy group
