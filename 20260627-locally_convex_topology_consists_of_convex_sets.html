locally convex topology consists of convex sets

Proposition

The subbasis $U_{i,r}$ for a Seminorm $p_i: V \rightarrow \mathbb{K}$ is a convex set.

Proof

let $y_1,y_2 \in U_{i,r}$ and $\lambda \in [0,1]$. Then we need to show $\lambda y_1 + (1 - \lambda) y_2 \in U_{i,r}$ or $p_i(\lambda y_1 + (1 - \lambda) y_2) < r$

\begin{align*} p_i(\lambda y_i + (1 - \lambda) y_2) \leq& p_i(\lambda y_1) + p_i((1 - \lambda) y_2 \\ =& \lambda p_i(y_1) + (1 - \lambda) p_i(y_2) \end{align*}

where $p_i(y_1), p_i(y_2) < r$ shows $p_i(\lambda y_i + (1 - \lambda) y_2) < r$.
