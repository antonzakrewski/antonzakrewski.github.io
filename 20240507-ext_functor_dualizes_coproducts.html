contravariant ext functor dualizes coproducts

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an Ab4 opp abelian category. Then it follows that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{A}}^n(\coprod -,-) \cong \prod \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{A}}^n(-,-) \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of

  • contravariant ab-hom functor dualizes colimits
  • right derived functor from an ab4 category commuting with coproducts commutes with coproducts
