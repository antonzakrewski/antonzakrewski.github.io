thom prespectrum

Definition

Let $\xi: B \rightarrow \mathrm{BG}$ be a stable spherical fibration. Then the thom prespectrum is defined as

\begin{align*} (\mathrm{Th}(\xi_n))_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \end{align*}

for

\begin{align*} \xi_n: B \times_{\mathrm{BG}} \mathrm{BG}(n) \end{align*}

with the canonical induced map

\begin{align*} B \times_{\mathrm{BG}} \mathrm{BG}(n) \rightarrow B \times_{\mathrm{BG}} \mathrm{BG}(n+1) \end{align*}

Proof

Let $B_n \coloneqq B \times_{\mathrm{BG}} \mathrm{BG}(n)$ note that for each $n \in \mathbb{N}$ we get a diagram

so in particular a commutative diagram

Here the map $B_n \rightarrow \mathrm{BG}(n) \rightarrow \mathrm{BG}(n+1)$ classifies the suspension of the spherical fibration, so we get by glueing lemma for infinity pullbacks

now we use thom space of the sum with a trivial spherical fibration and $\Sigma \xi_n \coloneqq \xi_n \oplus \sigma^1$ to get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Th}(\Sigma \xi_n) \simeq& \mathrm{Th}(\xi_n \oplus \sigma^1) \\ \simeq& \Sigma \mathrm{Th}(\xi_n) \end{align*}

and an induced map $\Sigma \mathrm{Th}(\xi_n) \rightarrow \mathrm{Th}(\xi_{n+1})$ which by adjunction then induces a map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Th}(\xi_n) \rightarrow \Sigma \mathrm{Th}(\xi_{n+1}) \end{align*}
