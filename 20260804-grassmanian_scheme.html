grassmanian scheme over a module

Definition

Let $k$ be a commutative ring, $M$ a \(k\)-module and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then the rank n grassmanian scheme of $M$ is the algebraic k-functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Gr}_{n}(M)(R) = \{\textrm{ quotient space of } M \otimes_{k} R \textrm{ of canstant rank n}\} \end{align*}
