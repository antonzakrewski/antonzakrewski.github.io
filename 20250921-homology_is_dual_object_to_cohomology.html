homology is dual object to cohomology

Proposition

Let $X$ be an anima and $E$ a $\mathbb{E}_{\infty}$-ring spectrum (I think \(\mathbb{E}_1\) should suffice) Then the associated homology theory of a spectrum is the dualizable object with dual object given by the associated cohomology theory of a spectrum.

Proof

follows from working in $\mathrm{Mod}(E)$ to get $E[X]$ and $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(E)}(E[X],E)$ as homology / cohomology
