degree of maps between tori/torus

Proposition

Let $m \cdot n = k \cdot l$ for natural numbers $m,n,k,l$. Then

\begin{align*} D((S^m)^n, (S^k)^l) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } m \mid k \\ 0 & \mbox{if } m < k \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

a)

use: degrees of maps between products of closed manifolds, which shows that $D((S^m)^n, (S^k)^l) \supseteq D((S^m)^{\frac{k}{m}}, S^k)^l = \mathbb{Z} \cdot \mathbb{Z} ... \mathbb{Z} = \mathbb{Z}$ (cf. mapping degrees of maps (S^n)^k -> S^nk)

b)

Suppose that $m \not\mid k$. Hence in particular consider $e_1,...e_l \in H^k((S^k)^l)$, which generate the cohomology of $(S^k)^l$. But the image of $e_1,...,e_l$ is zero for degree reasons: $(S^m)^n$ has integral homology only in degrees $d$ with $m \mid d$, so in particular not in degree $k$. This shows that the generator $\pm \prod_{i=1}^l e_i$ maps to zero.
