uniformly bounded sequence of functions

Definition

Let $f_n: X \rightarrow \mathbb{V}$ be a sequence of continuous maps into a normed vector space. Then $f_n$ is said to be uniformly bounded if there exists an $\varepsilon > 0$ such that

\begin{align*} f_n: X \rightarrow B_0(\varepsilon) \end{align*}

restricts for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$
