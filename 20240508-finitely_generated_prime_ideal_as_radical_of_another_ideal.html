finitely generated prime ideal as radical of another ideal

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{p} \subseteq A$ a finitely generated prime ideal and $\mathfrak{a}$ another ideal. TFAE:

  1. $\sqrt{\mathfrak{a}} = \mathfrak{p}$
  2. there exists an $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $\mathfrak{p}^n \subseteq \mathfrak{a} \subseteq \mathfrak{p}$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

corollary of:

  • finitely generated radical and finite power

2) $\implies$ 1)

corollary of:

  • power of a prime ideal as subset and radical
