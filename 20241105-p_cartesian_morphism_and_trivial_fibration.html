p-cartesian morphism and trivial fibration

Proposition

Let $p: X \rightarrow Y$ be an inner fibration and $f: x \rightarrow y$ a \(1\)-simplex in $Y$. TFAE:

  1. $f$ is p-cartesian
  2. the functor
\begin{align*} X_{/f} \rightarrow X_{/y} \times_{X/p(y)} Y_{/pf} \end{align*}

Proof

follows

  • bijection between lifting problems of join slice adjunction

where a lifting problem

Note that the

  • left coslice join functor / right coslice join functor
  • left slice functor for a simplicial set / right slice functor for a simplicial set

ensures that

\begin{align*} \Delta^1 = \Delta^{n-1,n} \rightarrow X \end{align*}

is given by $p(f)$
