nilpotent group and nilpotent conjugation

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group

TFAE:

  1. $G$ is a nilpotent group
  2. the conjugation is a nilpotent action on a group

Proof

Note that we may reuse the tower $1 = G_0 \triangelefteq G_1 \trianglelefteq ... \trianglelefteq G_n = G$

Here the conjugation group action on a normal subgroup ensures that each $G_i$ is closed under the group action of $G$
