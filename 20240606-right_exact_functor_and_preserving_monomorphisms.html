right exact functor and preserving monomorphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ a right exact functor

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ preserves monomorphisms
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ is exact

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from

  • half exact functor preserving monomorphisms as left exact functor

2) $\implies$ 1)

see

  • left exact functor between abelian categories preserves monomorphisms
