infinite extension of scalars is symmetric monoidal

Proposition

Let $R,S$ be $\mathbb{E}_{\infty}$-ring spectra and $R \rightarrow S$ a map. Then the extension of scalars functor is symmetric monoidal

\begin{align*} - \otimes_{R} S \mathrm{Mod}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}(S) \\ \end{align*}

Proof
