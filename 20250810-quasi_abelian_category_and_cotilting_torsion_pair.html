quasi-abelian category and cotilting torsion pair

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{E}$ be a small additive 1-category.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{E}$ is quasi-abelian category
  2. there exists a cotilting torsion pair $\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{F}$ in a small abelian category $\mathcal{A}$ with an equivalence
\begin{align*} \mathcal{E} \simeq \mathcal{F} \end{align*}

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)

cotilting torsion pair is quasiabelian
