set with left lifting property closed under retracts

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{S} \subseteq \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$ a subcollection of morphisms

\begin{align*} \chi_{L}(\mathcal{S}) \end{align*}

the set of morphisms with the set with the left lifting property

Then $\chi_L(\mathcal{S})$ is a closed under retract of a morphisms

Proof

Suppose $A $\rightarrow B$ is a retract of $A' \rightarrow B'$ and $A' \rightarrow B' \in \chi_L( \mathcal{S})$. Then we get for $X \rightarrow Y \in \mathcal{S}$ a diagram

and by assumption a lift

Note that each square / triangle commutes by construction

Then the composition gives a lift
