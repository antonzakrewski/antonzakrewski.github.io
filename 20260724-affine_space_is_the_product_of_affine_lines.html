products of affine spaces is again an affine space

Proposition

Let $k$ be a commutative ring and let $N_i$ be sets, then $\prod_{i \in I} \mathbb{A}^{N_i} \cong \mathbb{A}^{\coprod N_i}$ are isomorphic.

Proof

follows as the coproduct of $k[x_n \vert n \in N_i]$ in category CAlg_k is given by $k[x_n \vert n \in \coprod N_i]$. e.g. use some free commutative algebra functor argument.
