cocartesian fibration of infinity operads

Definition

Let $\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}$ be an infinity operad Then a morphism of simplicial sets $p: \mathcal{C}^{\otimes} \rightarrow \mathcal{O}^{\otimes}$ is said to be a cocartesian fibration of infinity operads, if

  1. it is a cocartesian fibration
  2. the composition
\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}^{\otimes} \rightarrow \mathcal{O}^{\otimes} \rightarrow N(\mathrm{Fin}_*) \end{align*}

exhibits $p$ as operad
