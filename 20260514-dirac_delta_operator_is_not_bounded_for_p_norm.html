dirac-delta operator is not bounded for p-norm

Proposition

The dirac-delta operator $\delta_x: C([0,1]) \rightarrow \mathbb{K}$ is not bounded for the p-Norm

Proof

Consider the function

\begin{align*} f_n: [0,1] \rightarrow \mathbb{K} \\ x \mapsto \mathrm{max}(0,n-2n^2x) \end{align*}

then $\int_{0}^x f_n \mathrm{d}x \leq ?$ but the dirac delta $\delta_0$ is not bounded.
