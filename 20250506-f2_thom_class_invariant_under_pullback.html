F2 thom class invariant under pullback

Proposition

Let

be a pullback diagram of oriented spherical fibrations.

Then the F2 thom classes are compatible

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Th}(f)^*(u(\pi)) = u(\pi') \in H^d(\mathrm{Th}(\pi'), \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

with the induced map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Th}(f): \mathrm{Th}(\pi') \rightarrow \mathrm{Th}(\pi) \end{align*}

(cf. pullback diagram induces map between thom spaces)
