r-th page of a spectral sequence

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category. Then an \(r\)-th page $E_r$ is given by a family of objects $E_r^{p,q}$ for $p,q \in \mathbb{Z}$ together with differentials (i.e. morphisms)

\begin{align*} d_r^{p,q}: E_r^{p,q} \rightarrow E_{r}^{p+r,q-r+1} \end{align*}

which satisfy $d_r \circ d_r = 0$
