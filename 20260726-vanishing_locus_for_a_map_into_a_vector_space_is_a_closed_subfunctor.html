vanishing locus for a map into a vector space is a closed subfunctor

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $f: M \rightarrow N$ a map of \(A\)-modules. Suppose that $N$ is a vector bundle, then the vanishing locus $V(f) \subseteq \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ is a closed subfunctor

Proof

For all $x \in M$ let $f(x): R \xrightarrow{x} M \xrightarrow{f} N$. Then $V(f) = \bigcap_{x \in M} V(f(x))$ and hence it suffices to assume that $M = R$ since arbitrary intersections of closed subfunctors are closed (cf. intersection of vanishing loci).
