saturated collection of morphisms in a presentable category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category. Then a collection $S$ of morphisms is said to be saturated if

  1. $S \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1,\mathcal{C})$ is <closed under small colimits>
  2. $S$ contains all equivalences
  3. $S$ is stable under composition
