compact object in anima valued E_n groups and compact n-fold delooping

Proposition

Let $X \in \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_n}(\mathrm{An})$ be an E_n group. TFAE:

  1. $X$ is a compact object,
  2. $B^n X$ is a compact anima

Proof

recall the recognition principle for anima valued E_n groups, which shows that $X$ is a compact object in $\mathrm{Grp}_{\mathrm{E}_n}(\mathrm{An})$ if and only if $B^n X$ is compact in $\mathrm{An}_{\geq n,*}$. So it suffices to show that $X$ is compact in $\mathrm{An}_{\geq n,*}$ if and only if it is compact in $\mathrm{An}$:

$X$ compact

Let $\mathrm{An}^{\mathrm{fin}_{\geq n,*}$ be the full subcategory of $\mathrm{An}_{\geq n,*}$ containing finite anima. Then the same argument as in spectrum is filtered colimit of finite spectra shows that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{\mathrm{An}^{\mathrm{fin}_{\geq n,*}} X_i \rightarrow X \end{align*}

is an equivalence on $\pi_n$ and above and furthermore trivially on $\pi_{0},...,\pi_{n-1}$ since each $X_i$ and $X$ is $n$-connected (todo: indices) and the colimit is filtered.

Hence compact object as filtered colimit and retract shows that $X$ is a retract of a finite (and n-connected) anima and hence compact (cf. compact anima as retract of finite anima).

$X$ finite

Observe that $\mathrm{An}_{\geq n,*} \subseteq \mathrm{An}_{*} \mathrm{An}$ is closed under filtered colimits (cf. n-connected colimit of n-connected anima as n-connected anima,pointed anima closed under weakly contractible colimits).

Hence the argument of compact objects in pointed anima are compact anima shows the claim.
