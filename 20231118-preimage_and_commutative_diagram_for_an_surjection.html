preimage and commutative diagram for an surjection

Proposition

Let $A,B,C,D$ be sets and 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
A \arrow[twoheadrightarrow, ]{d}{g} \arrow[]{rd}{h} 
C \arrow[]{r}{f} & D
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

be a commutative diagram for maps $f,g,h$, where $g$ is an surjection. Then for $U \subseteq D$ and the preimage, it holds

\begin{align*} f^{-1}[U] = g^{-1}[h^{-1}[U]] \end{align*}

Proof
