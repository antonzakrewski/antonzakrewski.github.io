limit of a colimit of indexing categories

Proposition

Let $I: J \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}$ be a diagram and let $\mathcal{I} = \mathrm{colim}_I$ be the colimit of $I$ in $\mathrm{Cat}$ category Cat. Let $\mathcal{C}$ be another category and let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a functor.

Suppose

  1. each restriction $I_j \rightarrow \mathcal{I} \xrightarrow{\mathcal{F}} \mathcal{C}$ admits a limit
  2. the resulting diagram
\begin{align*} J \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ j \mapsto& \mathrm{lim}_{I_j} \end{align*}

admits a limits

Then $\mathcal{F}$ also admits a limits which is given by $\mathrm{lim} \mathrm{lim}_{I_j}$
