bounded below and bounded above, bounded p-torsion spectrum lies in the generated subcategory of Fp vector spaces

Proposition

Let $X$ be a bounded below, bounded above, bounded p-torsion spectrum. Then $X$ lies in the smallest stable subcategory in spectra generated by the subcategory of $\mathbb{F}_p$ vector spaces $\mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb{F}_p} \subseteq \mathrm{Ab} = \mathrm{Sp}^{\heartsuit}$.
