restriction of scalars fully faithful for an epimorphism

Proposition

Let \(A,B\) be commutative rings, $f: A \rightarrow B$ an epimorphism in category Ring. Then the restriction of scalars functor is fully faithful

Proof

a)

restriction of scalars as faithful functor

b)

Let $M,N$ be \(B\)-modules and

\begin{align*} f: M \rightarrow N \end{align*}

an \(A\)-linear map of $M,N$ regarded as \(A\)-modules after restriction of scalars.

Fix an $m \in M$, then consider the map

\begin{align*} \varphi: B \otimes_A B \rightarrow& N b \otimes b' \mapsto& b \cdot g(b'm) \end{align*}

This is welldefined as $g$ is A linear in both $b,b'$

By symmetry of the tensor product for a ring epimorphism it follows that

\begin{align*} b \cdot g(m) =& \varphi(b \otimes 1) \\ =& \varphi(1 \otimes b) \h =& g(bm) \end{align*}
