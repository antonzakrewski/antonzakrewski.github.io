group completion of anima valued monoids as loop space of the geometric realization

Proposition

Let $M \in \mathrm{Mon}(\mathrm{An})$ be an anima valued monoid.

Then the infinite group completion is equivalent to

\begin{align*} \Omega \vert \mathrm{B}M \vert \simeq M^{\mathrm{gp}} \end{align*}

Proof

we compute the universal property of the adjoint object using the universal properties of the adjunction

  1. total dwyer-kan localization as left adjoint

and

  1. anima valued groups and connected pointed anima

we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{Grp}(\mathrm{An})}(\Omega \vert \mathrm{B}M \vert, G) \simeq& \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(\vert \mathrm{B}M \vert, BG) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Map}_{(\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty})_*}(\mathrm{B}M, BG) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Map}_{(\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty})_*}(\mathrm{B}M, BG) \\ \end{align*}

where the last step uses that the delooping of anima valued monoids is fully faithful
