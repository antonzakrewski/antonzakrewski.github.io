1-categorical right adjoint object

Definition

Dual to left adjoint object:

Explicite

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor and $d \in \mathcal{D}$. Then a right adjoint object for is given by an object $\mathcal{G}(d)\in \mathcal{C}$ together with a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{F})(-),d) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,\mathcal{G}(d)) \end{align*}
