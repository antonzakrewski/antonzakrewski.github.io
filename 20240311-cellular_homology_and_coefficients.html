Proposition

Let $X$ be a CW-Complex and $M$ an abelian group Then we have for the cellular chain complex, that

\begin{align*} C_{*}^{\mathrm{cell}}(X,M) \cong C_{*}^{\mathrm{cell}}(X, \mathbb{Z}) \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} M \end{align*}
