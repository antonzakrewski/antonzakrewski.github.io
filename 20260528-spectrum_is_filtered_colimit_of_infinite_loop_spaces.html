spectrum is filtered colimit of suspensions of infinite loop spaces

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum, then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \Omega^n \tilde{\mathbb{S}}[\Omega^{\infty} \Sigma^n X] \rightarrow X \end{align*}

Proof

Recall that homotopy group functor of spectra preserves filtered colimits and equivalences of spectra can be detected on homotopy groups (cf. whitehead theorem for spectra). So let $\pi_m$ be arbitrary. Then we get

\begin{align*} \pi_m(\mathrm{colim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \Omega^n \tilde{\mathbb{S}}[\Omega^{\infty} \Sigma^n X]) \cong \mathrm{colim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \pi_m(\Omega^n \tilde{\mathbb{S}}[\Omega^{\infty} \Sigma^n X]) \\ \cong \mathrm{colim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \pi_{m+n}(\tilde{\mathbb{S}}[\Omega^{\infty} \Sigma^n X]) \\ \cong \mathrm{colim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \pi_{m+n}^{\mathrm{st}}(\Omega^{\infty} \Sigma^n X) \end{align*}
