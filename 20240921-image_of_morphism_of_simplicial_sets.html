image of morphism of simplicial sets

Definition / Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be simplicial sets and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a morphism of simplicial sets Then the image is given by the simplicial subset

\begin{align*} \mathrm{im}(f)_n = \{y \in Y_n \vert \exists x \in X_n : f_n(x) = y\} \end{align*}

Proof
