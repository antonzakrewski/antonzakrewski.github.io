equivalence creates limits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ part of an equivalence. Then $\mathcal{F}$ creates limits

Proof

corollary of:

  • adjointification of an equivalence
  • equivalence of categories implies faithful functor + equivalence of categories implies full functor
  • fully faithful functor reflects limits
  • right adjoint preserves limits
