dimension of a quotient finite type k-algebra

Proposition

Let $k$ be a field, $A$ a finite type K-Algebra. Suppose $\mathfrak{p}$ is a prime ideal.

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{p}) + \mathrm{dim}(A/\mathfrak{p}) = \mathrm{dim}(A) \end{align*}

Proof

domain

w.l.o.g. we ma assume that $A$ is an Integral domain

reduction

minimal prime ideal Consider the case of $\mathfrak{p}$ as minimal prime ideal.

consider a maximal chain

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{p}_0 \subsetneq ... \subsetneq \mathfrak{p}_n \end{align*}

Then the statement says

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(A/\mathfrak{p}) =& \mathrm{dim}(A) =& n \end{align*}

Proof by induction

induction basis

Let $n = 0$. Then $\mathfrak{p}_0 = (0)$ is a maximal ideal, hence $A$ is a field with domain $\mathrm{dim}(A) = 0$

induction step

correspondence theorem for prime ideals and quotient ring Note that

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{p}_1/\mathfrak{p}_1 \subsetneq ... \subsetneq \mathfrak{p}_n/\mathfrak{p}_1 \subsetneq A/\mathfrak{p}_1 \end{align*}

is a maximal chain
