epimorphism locus of a map of A-modules

Definition

Let $f: M \rightarrow N$ be a map of \(A\)-modules for a commutative ring $A$ Then the epimorphism locus of $f$ is defined as locus for the property of being an epimorphism, i.e.

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Epi}(f; A)(R) = \{\varphi: A \rightarrow R \vert R \textrm{ linear, } M \otimes_{A} R \xrightarrow{f \otimes_{A} R} N \otimes_{A} R \textrm{ is an epimorphism}\} \end{align*}
