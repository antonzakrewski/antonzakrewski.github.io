p-primary ideal

Definition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $\mathfrak{q} \subseteq A$ a primary ideal. Suppose $\mathfrak{p}$ is a prime ideal with $\mathfrak{p} \supseteq \mathfrak{q}$.

Then $\mathfrak{q}$ is said to be $\mathfrak{p}$ primary if

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{p} = \sqrt{\mathfrak{q}} \end{align*}
