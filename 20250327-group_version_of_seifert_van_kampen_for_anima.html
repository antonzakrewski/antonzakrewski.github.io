group version of seifert van kampen for anima

Proposition

Let $A,B,C$ be connected anima and

a pushout

Let $a \in A$ be a point and $b \in B, c \in C, d \in D$ the respective image of $a$, i.e. w.l.o.g. assume that this is a diagram in category of pointed anima $\mathrm{An}_*$

Then the fundamental group functor of anima preserves this pushout, i.e.

\begin{align*} \pi_1(B,b) \star_{\pi_1(A,a)} \pi_1(C,c) \rightarrow \pi_1(D,d) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism

Proof
