fully faithfulness criterion for Ind kappa

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category, $\kappa$ a regular cardinal and $\mathcal{D}$ a category which admits kappa filtered colimits. Let $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor and $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be the induced functor via Ind Kappa arises freely by adding kappa filtered colimits.

Suppose $f$ is fully faithful and its essential image consists of kappa compact object. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is also fully faithful
