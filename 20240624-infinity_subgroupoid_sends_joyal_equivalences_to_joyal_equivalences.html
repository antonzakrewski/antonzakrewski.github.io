infinity groupoid core sends joyal equivalences to joyal equivalences

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{sSet}$ be the category sSet, $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a joyal equivalence between infinity categories. Then the induced map on maximal infinity-subgroupoids

\begin{align*} f^{\cong}: \mathcal{C}^{\cong} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}^{\cong} \end{align*}

is also a joyal equivalence

Proof

by equivalence in cat infinity and equivalence in functor category there exists an appropriate \(2\)-simplex

\begin{align*} \Delta^2 \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

By ??? the groupoid core gives a restriction map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\simeq}, \mathcal{D}^{\simeq}) \end{align*}

which then results in an appropriate \(2\)-simplex

\begin{align*} \Delta^2 \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\simeq}, \mathcal{D}^{\simeq}) \end{align*}
