finite subsets as ring of sets

Proposition

Let $X$ be a set, $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{fin}}(X) \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)$ the family of finite subsets Then $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{fin}}(X)$ is a ring of sets

Proof

  1. $\emptyset$ is finite
  2. the finite union of finite sets is finite
  3. the complement $A \setminus B$ for $A,B$ is finite, as $A \setminus B \subseteq A$
