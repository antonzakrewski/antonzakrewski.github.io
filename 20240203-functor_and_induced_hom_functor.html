postcomposition of a functor for the hom functor

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be a locally small categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor Then the postcomposition of $\mathcal{F}$ with the hom-functor is defined

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}^{ \leftarrow} \mathrm{Hom}(-,-): \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Set} \\ (A,B) \mapsto& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(A), \mathcal{F}(B)) \\ (f,g) \mapsto& \mathcal{F}(f)^{ \rightarrow} \mathcal{F}(g)^{ \leftarrow} \\ \end{align*}
