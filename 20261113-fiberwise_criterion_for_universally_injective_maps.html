fiberwise criterion for universally injective maps

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a morphism of schemes TFAE:

  1. $f$ is universally injective morphism of schemes
  2. for each algebraically closed field $k$ and each geometric point $\mathrm{spec}(k) \rightarrow Y$, the map $X \times_{Y} \mathrm{Spec}(k) \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(k)$ is an injective map.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

special case

2) $\implies$ 1)
