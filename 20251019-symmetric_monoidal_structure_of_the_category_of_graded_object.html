symmetric monoidal structure of the category of graded objects

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a (nice ?) symmetric monoidal category, then the category of graded objects admits a symmetric moonidal structure

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Gr}(\mathcal{C}) \coloneqq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathbb{Z}^{\delta}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

via the day convolution

here $\mathbb{Z}^{\delta}$ is the discrete category and the symmetric monoidal structure on $\mathbb{Z}^{\delta}$ is given by the sum symmetric monoidal structure of the integers (after passing to the groupoid core if necessary).
