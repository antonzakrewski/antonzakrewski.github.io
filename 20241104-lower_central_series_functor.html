lower central series functor

Definition

Given category Group, the lower central series functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lower}: \mathrm{Grp} \rightarrow& \prod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathrm{Grp} \\ G \mapsto& (\Gamma^n(G))_n \\ (f: G \rightarrow H) \mapsto& f_{\vert \Gamma^n(G)}: \Gamma^n(G) \rightarrow \Gamma^n(H) \end{align*}

here the restriction is welldefined, which can be shown inductively
