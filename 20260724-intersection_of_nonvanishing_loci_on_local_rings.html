intersection of nonvanishing loci on local rings

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic k-functor, $A$ a local ring and $F_i$ subsets of $\mathcal{O}(X)$. Then

\begin{align*} (\bigcup_{i \in I} D(F_i))(A) = D(\bigcup_{i \in I} F_i)(A) \end{align*}

where the union is either the categorical colimit or the pointwise union

Proof

Let $A$ be arbitrary and $x \in (D(\bigcup_{i \in I} F_i)(A)$. Then $(f(x))_{f \in \bigcup_{i \in I} F_i}$ generates the unit ideal. But as $A$ is a local ring, this implies that $f(x)$ must be a unit for some $f$ - otherwise it would be contained in the maximal ideal, which consists precisely of the non units (cf. maximal ideal and non-unit).
