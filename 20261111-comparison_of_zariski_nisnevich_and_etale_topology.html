comparison of zariski, nisnevich and etale topology

Proposition

then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Zar} < \mathrm{Nis} < \mathrm{et} \end{align*}

with strict inclusions.

Proof

Assume that $k$ is a $\mathbb{Z}[\frac{1}{2}]$ algebra.

a)

Consider $\mathbb{G}_m \setminus \{-1\} \xrightarrow{\cdot 2} \mathbb{A}^1$ and $\mathbb{A}^1 \setminus \{1\} \hookrightarrow \mathbb{A}^1$. Then this is a nisnevich cover (cf. etale neighbourhood induces nisnevich cover) but not a zariski cover, since $\mathbb{G}_m \setminus \{-1\} \xrightarrow{\cdot 2} \mathbb{A}^1$ has no section on any open neighbourhood of $1 \in \mathbb{A}^1$. This follows as

\begin{align*} k[x]_{(x-1)} \rightarrow& k[x]_{(x-1)} \\ x \mapsto x^{2} \end{align*}

does not have a retraction

b)

consider $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{R})$, which is an etale cover, but not a nisnevich cover.

Alternatively, consider $a \in k^{\times}$ and $\mathbb{G}_{m} \xrightarrow{2}\mathbb{A}^1$ and $\mathbb{A}^1 \setminus \{a\} \hookrightarrow \mathbb{A}^1$ which is an etale covering of $\mathbb{A}^1$. But this is a nisnevich cover if and only if $a$ has a square root in every residue field of $k$.
