fp splitting lemma for a qcqs scheme

Proposition

Let $f: Y \rightarrow X$ be a morphisms of locally finite presentation into a qcqs scheme. TFAE:

  1. for all $x \in X$, there exists an $y \in Y$ over $x$ with $\kappa(x) \rightarrow \kappa(y)$
  2. there exists finitely presented closed subschemes $\emptyset = Z_n \hookrightarrow Z_{n-1} \hookrightarrow ... \hookrightarrow Z_0 = 0$ such that $Y \rightarrow X$ has a section over $Z_{i-1} \setminus Z_i$.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $P \subesteq \mathrm{closed}(X)$ be the sub poset of closed subschemes $Z \subseteq X$ such that $Y \times_{X} Z \rightarrow Z$ does not satisfy 2). Then if $Y \rightarrow X$ is lfp, then $P$ is closde under cofiltered limits: let $Z = \bigcap_{\alpha} Z_{\alpha}$ be some cofiltered intersection (cf. todo: noetherian approximation via Pro objects).

Then using Zorn's lemma, to show that it is empty, it remains to show that it does not contain a minimal element.

Let $x \in X$ be a point with codimension $0$. Then if $Y \rightarrow X$ lfp has a section over $\kappa(x)$, then there exists a $Z_2 \hookrightarrow Z_1 \hookrightarrow Z_0 = X$ morphisms finite presentation where $f$ has a section over $Z_0 \setminus Z_1$ and $Z_1 \z Z_2$ and $x \not\in Z_2$. hence $Z_2 \in P$.
