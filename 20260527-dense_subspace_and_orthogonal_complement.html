dense subspace and orthogonal complement

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{H}$ be a hilbert space and $M$ a linear subspace. TFAE:

  1. $M$ is dense
  2. $M^{\bot} = \{0\}$ is trivial

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from orthogonal complement of the closure of the generated subspace

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from the projection theorem: For any $x \in X$, there exists a unique sum $m + n = x$ for $m \in \overline{M}$ and $n \in \overline{M}^{\bot} \subseteq M^{\bot} = \{0\}$. Hence $x \in \overline{M}$ or $X = M$.
