rational circle group is a cogenerator

Proposition

The rational circle group is a cogenerator of Category Ab for

\begin{align*} \varphi: M \rightarrow& \prod_{\varphi: M \rightarrow \mathbb{Q}/ \mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Q}/ \mathbb{Z} \\ m \mapsto& (\varphi(m)) \end{align*}

Proof

Let $m \in M \setminus \{0\}$. Then it remains to show that there exists a nontrivial morphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: M \rightarrow \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

Then by injectivity of $\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$ (cf. rational circle group)

Therefore it suffices to construct a nontrivial group-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: \langle m\rangle \rightarrow \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

using the classification of finitely generated abelian groups we have two cases

\begin{align*} \langle m\rangle \cong& \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} \\ \mathbb{Z}/m \mathbb{Z} \end{cases} \end{align*}

In the first case, use the universal property of a ring as module, in the second case use

\begin{align*} \varphi: \mathbb{Z}/m \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow& \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z} \\ 1 \mapsto& \frac{1}{m} \end{align*}

where $\frac{1}{m}$ has the desired torsion.
