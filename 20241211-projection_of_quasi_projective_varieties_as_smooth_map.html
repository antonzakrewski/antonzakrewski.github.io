projection of nonsingular quasi projective varieties as smooth map

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X,Y \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ be nonsingular quasi-projective varieties, $P \in X$ a point. Then the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{pr}: X \times Y \rightarrow X (x,y) \mapsto x \end{align*}

is a smooth map

Proof

$Y = \emptyset$

  • vacuous truth

$Y \neq \emptyset$

Choose a point $y \in Y$. Then consider the inclusion

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow& X \times Y \\ x \mapsto& (x,y) \end{align*}

\begin{align*} \mathrm{pr} \circ \iota = \mathrm{id} \end{align*}
