projection from the mapping torus to S^1

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $f: X \rightarrow X$ a homeomorphism and $T_f$ the mapping torus. Then there exists a continuous map

\begin{align*} \pi_{S^1}: T_f \rightarrow& S^1 \\ [x,r] \mapsto& e^{2 \pi r} \end{align*}

Proof

welldefined

Suppose $[x,r] = [x',r']$. Then by assumption, $r - r' = \pm 1$ and hence

\begin{align*} e^{2 \pi r} =& e^{2\pi (\pm 1)} \cdot e^{2 \pi r'} \\ =& 1 \cdot e^{2 \pi r'} \\ =& e^{2 \pi r'} \\ \end{align*}

continuous

By definition of a quotient topology as final topology, a map $\pi_S$ is continuous if and only if

\begin{align*} \pi_S \circ \pi: T \times [0,1] \rightarrow S^1 \end{align*}

is continuous

but $e^{2 \pi r}$ is continuous
