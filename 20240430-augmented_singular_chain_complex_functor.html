augmented singular chain complex functor

Proposition

Given category Top and an abelian group $M$, the augmented singular chain complex functor is defined as functor to the category of augmented chain complexes

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(-;M) \mathrm{Top} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ch_*}^M(\mathcal{A}) \\ X \mapsto& \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(X;M) \\ (f: X \rightarrow Y) \mapsto& \mathrm{Sing}(f): \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(X;M) \rightarrow \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(Y;M) \end{align*}

to the augmented singular chain complex with coefficients where $f: X \rightarrow Y$
