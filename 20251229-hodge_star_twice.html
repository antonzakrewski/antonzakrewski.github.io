hodge star twice of a pseudo riemannian manifold

Proposition

Let $M$ be a pseudo riemannian manifold and $\alpha \in \Omega^{k}(M)$. Then

\begin{align*} \mbox{}* * (\alpha) =& (-1)^{k(n-k)} \frac{\mathrm{det}(g)}{\vert \mathrm{det}(g) \vert} \alpha \end{align*}

In particular if $M$ is even riemannian, then this simplifies to

\begin{align*} \mbox{} * * \alpha = (-1)^{k(n-k)} \alpha \end{align*}

Proof
