contravariant identity functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. Then the contravariant identity functor is defined as contravariant functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Id}^{\mathrm{op}}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C}^\mathrm{op} A \mapsto& A \\ f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,B) \mapsto& f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(B,A) \end{align*}
