kompakte Mannigfaltigkeit ohne globaler Karte

Proposition

Let $K$ be a closed topological manifold, then there does not exist a global chart.

Proof

Assume there exists a global chart $K \cong U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ where $U$ is open. Then $U$ is compact, hence also closed. As $\mathbb{R}^n$ is connected, it follows that $U = \emptyset$.
