Proposition

The polynomial ring functor is not an affine scheme

Proof

recall that representable functors preserve limits, so in particular also infinite products. But consider $\prod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} R$ Then $(x,x^2,x^3,...)$ is an element in $\prod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} R[X]$ but not one in $(\prod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} R)[X]$
