monomorphisms in category Cat

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is a monomorphism in category Cat
  2. $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}}([n], \mathcal{F})$ is a monomorphism in anima
  3. $\mathcal{F}$ is a monomorphism on the groupoid core and $\mathrm{Ar}(\mathcal{C})^{\simeq} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ar}(\mathcal{D})^{\simeq}$ is a monomorphism, that is a monomorphism in the category of Anima (cf. monomorphisms in Anima).
  4. $\mathcal{F}$ is a monomorphism on $\mathrm{Ar}(\mathcal{C})^{\simeq}$
  5. $\mathcal{F}$ is a monomorphism on the groudoid core (that is, an injection on $\pi_0$ and isomorphism on all higher homotopy groups) and a faithful functor, that it $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(f(x),f(y))$ is a monomorphism in anima
