n-th homology of a CW complex without n-cell

Proposition

Let $(X, \emptyset, \mathrm{SK}_n(X))$ be a CW-Complex such that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sk}(X)_n = \mathrm{Sk}(X)_{n-1} \end{align*}

Then it holds for the singular homology functor

\begin{align*} H_n(X;M) = 0 \end{align*}
