colimits in a left bousfield localization

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_0 \xhookrightarrow{\iota} \mathcal{C}$ be a left Bousfield localization and $I: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_0$ a diagram in $\mathcal{C}_0$. Suppose that the colimit of $I$ exists in $\mathcal{C}$, then it also exists in $\mathcal{C}$ and is given by $\mathcal{L}(\mathrm{colim}_{\mathcal{I}} \iota \circ I)$ for the left adjoint $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_0$.
