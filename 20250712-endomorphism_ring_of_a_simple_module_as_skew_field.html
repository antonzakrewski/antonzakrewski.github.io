endomorphism ring of a simple object is a skew field

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $S$ a simple object. Then the endomorphism ring is a skew field.

Proof

Let $f: S \rightarrow S$ be an endomorphism with $f \neq 0$. Then $\mathrm{ker}(f) = 0$: since otherwise $\mathrm{ker}(f) = S$ (isomorphic kernel and zero morphism) would imply $f = 0$ or $\mathrm{ker}(f)$ would be a subobject. Using simple object and quotients in an abelian category shows $\mathrm{coker}(f) = 0$ by some argument of the kind cokernel of a zero morphism

By abelian category as balanced category we are done (and possibly kernel and monomorphism in pointed category / cokernel and epimorphism in an ab-enriched, pointed category).
