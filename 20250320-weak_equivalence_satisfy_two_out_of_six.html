weak equivalences in a model category satisfy two out of six

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}, \mathrm{Cof}, \mathrm{Fib})$ be a model category Then $\mathcal{W}$ satisfies two out of six

Proof

follows from:

  • weak equivalence and isomorphism in the homotopy category
  • isomorphisms satisfy two out of six
