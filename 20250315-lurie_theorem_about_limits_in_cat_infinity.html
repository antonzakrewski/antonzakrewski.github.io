Lurie theorem about limits in Cat infinity

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: I \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}$ be an infinity functor where $I$ is an infinity category Then the limit is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_{I} \mathcal{F} \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}_{I}^{\mathrm{cocart}}\mathrm{Un}(\mathcal{F})) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Fun}_I^{\mathrm{cocart}}(I, \mathcal{U}$ is the functor category of sections, which map morphisms in $I$ to p-cocartesian maps: see: functor category of cocartesian preserving sections

Proof
