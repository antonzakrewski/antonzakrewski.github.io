regular morphism induces a vectorspace homomorphism between tangent spaces

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X,Y \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ quasi-projective varieties. Then a regular map $f: X \rightarrow Y$ induces a vectorspace-homomorphism on the tangent space

\begin{align*} T_p(X) \rightarrow T_p(Y) \end{align*}

Proof
