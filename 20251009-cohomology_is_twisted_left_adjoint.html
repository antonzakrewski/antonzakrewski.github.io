cohomology is left adjoint into opposite category

Proposition

Let $E\in \mathrm{Sp}$ be a spectrum Then the cohomology functor is a left adjoint

\begin{align*} E^*(-): \mathrm{An} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{op}} \\ \end{align*}

into the opposite category
