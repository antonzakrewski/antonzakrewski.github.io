Weyl algebra

Definition

Let $k$ be a field. Then the weyl algebra is the algebra defined by the operation

\begin{align*} W = k\langle x,y\rangle / \langle xy - yx + 1\rangle \end{align*}

Remark

if you are in $\mathrm{char}(k) = 0$, then think of $x$ as multiplication by $t$ and $y$ by $\frac{\partial}{\partial t}$ on $k[t]$
