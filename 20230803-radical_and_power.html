radical and power

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq R$ a ideal. Then for $n \in \mathbb{N}^+$

\begin{align*} \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}} = \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}^n} \end{align*}

Proof

a)

corollary of:

  • radical of subsets
  • power of ideals as subset

b)

Suppose $a \in \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}}$, then there exists an $m \in \mathbb{N}$ such that

\begin{align*} a^m \in \mathfrak{a} \end{align*}

Then $a^{m \cdot n} = (a^m)^n \in \mathfrak{a}^n$ and hence $a \in \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}^n}
