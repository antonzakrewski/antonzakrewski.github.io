moebius strip

Definition

One can define the moebius strip as quotient topology of $\mathbb{R}^2$ with respect to

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Diff}(\mathbb{R}^2) \\ k \mapsto& (x + k, (-1)^k y) \end{align*}
