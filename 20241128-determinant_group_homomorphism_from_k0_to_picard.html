determinant group epimorphism from k0 to picard

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, then the determinant map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{det}: K_0(R) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Pic}(R) \\ [P] \mapsto& [\mathrm{det}(P)] \end{align*}

is a group-epimorphism

Proof

welldefined

  • functor induces map on isomorphism classes
  • exterior power functor
  • rank invariant under iso ?

surjective

Given an algebraic line bundle $L$, we may take

\begin{align*} [L] \mapsto [\mathrm{det}(L)] = [L] = 0 \end{align*}

monoid homomorphism

It suffices to show, that this restricts to a monoid-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Proj}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{Pic}(R) \end{align*}

Given $P,Q \in \mathrm{Proj}(R)$ with

\begin{align*} \Lambda_R^{m+n}(P \oplus Q) \cong \bigoplus_{j=1}^{m + n} ( \Lambda_j^R P \otimes_R \Lambda_R^{m+n-j} Q) \\ =& \Lambda_R^m P \otimes \Lambda_R^n \end{align*}
