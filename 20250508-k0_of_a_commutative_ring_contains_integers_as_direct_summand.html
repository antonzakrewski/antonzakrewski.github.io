k0 of a commutative ring contains integers as direct summand

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. Then the K0 contains $\mathbb{Z}$ as direct summand

Proof

Choose a maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$ and a map

\begin{align*} R \rightarrow R/\mathfrak{m} \end{align*}

Then this induces a map

\begin{align*} K_0(R) \rightarrow K_0(R/\mathfrak{m}) \cong \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

in particular it is an epimorphism: Let $[R/\mathfrak{m}] \in K_0(R/\mathfrak{m})$ denote a (even the canonical) generator. Then $[R]$ as the equivalence class of a free module of rank 1 maps to $[R/\mathfrak{m}]$
