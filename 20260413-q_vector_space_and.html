Q-Vector space is characterized by vanishing Fp Tor and tensor product

Proposition

Let $M$ be an abelian group TFAE:

  1. $M$ is a $\mathbb{Q}$ vector space
  2. $\mathrm{Tor}_1^{\mathbb{Z}}(M,\mathbb{Q})$ and $M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Q}$ vanish

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Then we consider the short exact sequence $\mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\cdot p} \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{F}_p$ and the associated long exact sequence of the left derived functor $\mathrm{Tor}_*^{\mathbb{Z}}(-, M)$. 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{Tor}_1^{\mathbb{Z}, M) & \mathrm{Tor}_1^{\mathbb{F}_p, M) \rar & \mathbb{Z} \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} M \rar & \mathbb{Z} \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} M \rar & \mathbb{F}_p \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} M & 0\\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

As \mathrm{Tor}_1^{\mathbb{Z}, M)

is an isomorphism and $\mathrm{Tor}_1^{\mathbb{Z}, M)$ vanishes ($\mathbb{Z}$ is free/projective/flat) it follows that $\mathrm{Tor}_1^{\mathbb{Z}}(M,\mathbb{Q})$ and $M \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Q}$ vanish

2) $\implies$ 1)

we need to show that $M$ is uniquely divisible (cf. Q-Vector space is uniquely divisible abelian group), so fix a prime $p$ and we need to show that

is an isomorphism Then we consider the short exact sequence $\mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\cdot p} \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{F}_p$ and the associated long exact sequence of the left derived functor $\mathrm{Tor}_*^{\mathbb{Z}}(-, M)$. 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{Tor}_1^{\mathbb{F}_p, M) \rar & \mathbb{Z} \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} M \rar & \mathbb{Z} \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} M \rar & \mathbb{F}_p \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} M \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Hence if $\mathrm{Tor}_1^{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{F}_p, M), \mathbb{F}_p \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} M$ vanish, then the map $M \xrightarrow{\cdot p} M$ is an isomorphism. I.e., $M$ is uniquely \(p\)-divisible.
