infinity equivalence is an adjoint

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathcal{D} \\ \mathcal{F}^{-1}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ \end{align*}

be joyal equivalence

Then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{F}^{-1} \end{align*}

form an infinity adjunction

Proof

For $X \in \mathcal{C}, Y \in \mathcal{D}$ consider

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, \mathcal{F}^{-1}(Y)) \overset{\mathcal{F}}{\simeq}& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{F}(X), \mathcal{F}(\mathcal{F}^{-1}(Y)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{F}(X), Y) \end{align*}

where the latter isomorphism follows from fully faithfulness
