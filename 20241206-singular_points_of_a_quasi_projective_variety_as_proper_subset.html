singular points of a quasi-projective variety is a proper subset

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ a quasi-projective variety. Then the set of singular points is a proper subset.

Proof

Then $X \sim X'$ where $X' \subsete $\mathbb{P}_k^{d+1}$ is a projective hyperplane (cf. todo)

Since $X \sim X'$ there exist nonempty open subset $U \subseteq X, $V \subseteq X'$ with $U \cong V$. Let $\emptyset \neq X' \setminus X^{\mathrm{sing}}$, i.e.. affine.

Then $\emptyset \neq V \cap (X \setminus X^{\mathrm{sing}}) \subseteq V$
