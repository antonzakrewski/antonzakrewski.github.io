equivalence of opposite LCH_+ and nonunital, commutative C-star Algebras

Proposition

The functor

\begin{align*} C_0(-): \mathrm{LCH}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{C^*}^{\mathrm{Alg}} \end{align*}

is an equivalence compatible with 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{CH}^{\mathrm{op}} \arrow[hookrightarrow, ]{r}{} \dar{\simeq} & \mathrm{LCH}^{\mathrm{op}} \dar{\simeq} \\
C^*^{\textrm{comm, unital}} \arrow[hookrightarrow, ]{r}{} & C^*^{\textrm{comm}} \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

see:

  • C_0 of a locally compact hausdorff space
  • C_0 of a locally compact hausdorff space is a C-star algebra
