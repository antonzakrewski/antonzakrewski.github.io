composite of abelian field extensions is an abelian field extension

Proposition

Let $L_i/K$ be a abelian field extensions, then so is the composite field extension $\prod L_i/K$.

Proof

observe that this is a galois field extension (cf. composite of galois extensions is a galois extension) Moreover, beidseitig galoisscher Zwischenkörper induziert Gruppenepimorphismus der Galoisgruppen induces a group-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Gal}(\prod L_i/K) \rightarrow \mathrm{Gal}(L_i/K) \end{align*}

we claim that the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Gal}(L_1L_2/K) \rightarrow \prod_{i} \mathrm{Gal}(L_i/K) \end{align*}

is a monomorphism where the RHS is an abelian group:

Let $\varphi$ be an automorphism of $\prod L_i$ over $K$, then it is uniquely determined by $\varphi_{\vert L_i}: L_i \rightarrow L_i$ for $i \in I$, as $\prod L_i$ is given by finite sums and finite products of elements from $\bigcup L_i$. This implies that the restriction map is a monomorphism, hence $\mathrm{Gal}(\prod L_i/K)$ is a subgroup of an abelian group, hence itself abelian.
