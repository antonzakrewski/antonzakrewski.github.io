Young's inequality for L^p,L^q,L^r

Proposition

Let $p,q,r \in [1,\infty]$ such that $\frac{1}{p} + \frac{1}{q} + \frac{1}{r} = 2$. Let $f \in L^p(\mathbb{R}^d), g \in L^q(\mathbb{R}^d), h \in L^r(\mathbb{R}^d)$.

Then

\begin{align*} I \coloneqq \vert \int_{\mathbb{R}^d \times \mathbb{R}^d} \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d}y f(x) g(x - y) h (y) \vert \leq \int_{\mathbb{R}^d \times \mathbb{R}^d} \mathrm{d}x \mathrm{d}y \vert f(x) \vert g(x - y) \vert \vert h(y) \vert \leq \| f \|_p \| g \|_q \| h \|_r \end{align*}

Proof
