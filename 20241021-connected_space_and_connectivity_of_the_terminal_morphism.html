connected space and connectivity of the terminal morphism

Proposition

Let $(X,x_0)$ be a based space

TFAE:

  1. $X$ is an d-connected space
  2. the terminal morphism $X \rightarrow *$ is a (d+1)-connected map

Proof

follows from

  • d-connected map and homotopy group

where one notices that

\begin{align*} \pi_{d+1}(X,x_0) \rightarrow \pi_{d+1}(X,x_0) = \{*\} \end{align*}

trivially surjects and furthermore the singleton is uniquely determined
