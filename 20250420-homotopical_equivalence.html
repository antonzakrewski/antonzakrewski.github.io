homotopical equivalence

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}$ be homotopical categories and $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a homotopical functor. Then $f$ is said to be a homotopical equivalence, if there exists a homotopical functor $g: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ such tat the compositions

\begin{align*} g \circ f, f \circ g \end{align*}

are naturally weakly equivalent functors to the respective identity functors.
