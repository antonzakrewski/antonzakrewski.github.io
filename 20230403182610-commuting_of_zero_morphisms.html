commuting of zero morphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $A,B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and $0$ an zero object. Then for morphisms $f: A \rightarrow B$ the following diagrams commute.

Proof

follows from the Uniqueness of a zero morphism by composing

\begin{align*} i_A \circ f \circ \mathrm{id}_{0}: 0 \rightarrow& B \\ i_B \circ \mathrm{id}_{0}: 0 \rightarrow& B \end{align*}
