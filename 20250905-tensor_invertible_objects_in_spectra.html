tensor invertible objects in spectra are shifts of the sphere spectrum

Proposition

The invertible spectra in $\mathrm{Sp}, \otimes$ with the usual tensor product of spectra are precisely shifts of the sphere spectrum.

Proof

a) shift sphere spectrum is invertible

follows as the tensor product of spectra is exact in both variables, hence

\begin{align*} \mathbb{S}[n] \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{S}[-n] \simeq& (\mathbb{S} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{S}[-n])[n] \\ \simeq& (\mathbb{S} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{S})[-n][n] \\ \simeq& \mathbb{S} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{S} \\ \simeq& \mathbb{S} \end{align*}

b)

Let $X$ be a spectrum. Now dualizable spectrum is connective and invertible object is dualizable implies that $X$ is connective spectrum. Observe that monoidal functor induces functor on Picard groupoid and infinite extension of scalars is symmetric monoidal. Hence it suffices to see that invertible objects in $\mathcal{D}(\mathbb{Z})$ are precisely the shifts of $\mathbb{Z}$ (cf. picard of the derived category of the integers, connective, stable homology sphere is the shifted sphere spectrum).
