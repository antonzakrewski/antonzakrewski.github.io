sum of grouphomomorphisms

Definition / Proposition

Let $G,G'$ be abelian groups and $f_1,f_2: G \rightarrow G'$ be grouphomomorphisms. Then the sum of $f_1, f_2$, defined by: $(f_1 + f_2)(g) = f_1(g) + f_2(g)$ is a grouphomomorphism.

Proof

Suppose $g_1,g_2 \in G$:

\begin{align*} (f_1 + f_2)(g_1 + g_2) =& f_1(g_1 + g_2) + f_2(g_1 + g_2) \\ =& f_1(g_1) + f_1(g_2) + f_2(g_1) + f_2(g_2) \\ =& f_1(g_1) + f_2(g_1) + f_1(g_2) + f_2(g_2) \\ =& (f_1 + f_2)(g_1) + (f_1 + f_2)(g_2) \end{align*}
