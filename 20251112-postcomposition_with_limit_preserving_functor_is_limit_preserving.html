postcomposition with limit preserving functor is limit preserving_

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a limit preserving functor between complete categories and $K$ a small category. Then postcomposition induces a limit preserving functor on functor categories

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_*: \mathrm{Fun}(K, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(K, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}
