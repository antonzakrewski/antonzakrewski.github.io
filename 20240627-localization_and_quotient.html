localization and quotient

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod Suppose $M$ is an \(R\)-module, $N \subseteq M$ submodules Then

\begin{align*} S^{-1}(M/N) = S^{-1}(M)/S^{-1}(N) \end{align*}

is the quotient module

Proof

follows from

  • localization as exact functor
  • quotient modulo image as cokernel
