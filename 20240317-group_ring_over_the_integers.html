group ring of the integers

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $\mathbb{Z}$ the integers as abelian group. Then the group ring $R \mathbb{Z}$ is canonically group-isomorphic to the polynomial ring

\begin{align*} R \mathbb{Z} \cong R[X] \end{align*}

Proof

Consider the morphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: R \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow& R[X] \\ r n \mapsto& r \cdot X^n \end{align*}
