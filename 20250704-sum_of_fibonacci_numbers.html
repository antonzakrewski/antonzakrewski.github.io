sum of fibonacci numbers

Proposition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then

\begin{align*} \sum_{i=0}^n F_i = F_{n+2} - 1 \end{align*}

Proof

Proof by induction

induction basis

follows as $F_0 = 0 = 1 - 1 = F_2 - 1$

induction step

Then

\begin{align*} \sum_{i=0}^{n+1} F_i =& F_{n+2} + F_{n+1} - 1 \\ =& F_{n+3} - 1 \end{align*}
