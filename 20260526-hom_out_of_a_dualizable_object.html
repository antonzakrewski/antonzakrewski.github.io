internal hom out of a dualizable object

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a closed symmetric monoidal category and $X$ a dualizable object. Then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Y) \simeq \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,\mathbbm{1}) \otimes Y \end{align*}

Proof

Recall that tensoring with dualizable -- here $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X, \mathbbm{1})$ -- is a right adjoint with left adjoint given by $- \otimes X$, so we have a natural equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,\mathbbm{1}) \otimes Y) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(- \otimes X, Y) \end{align*}

but we also have a natural equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Y)) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(- \otimes X, Y) \end{align*}

since $\mathcal{C}$ is a closed monoidal category.

Now yoneda shows that both represented functors are equivalent, so in particular also their representing objects, that is $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Y) \simeq \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,\mathbbm{1}) \otimes Y$.
