hermitian metric

Definition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a complex vector bundle, then a hermitian metric is smooth section

\begin{align*} h: B \rightarrow (E \otimes \overline{E})^* \end{align*}

such that for every point $p\in M$ the linear transformation $h_p(-,-)$ is a hermitian sesquilinearform, i.e.

  1. $h_p(x,x) > 0$
  2. $h_p(x,\overline{y}) = \overline{h_p(y,\overline{x})}$
