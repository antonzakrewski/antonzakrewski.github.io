strong join of topological spaces

Definition

Let $X_i$ be topological spaces $\star_I X_i$ the set of the join of topological spaces.

Then the strong topology

\begin{align*} (t_i,x_i) \sim (s_i,y_i) :\Leftrightarrow \forall i \in I t_i = s_i , x_i = y_i \textrm{ for all } i \in I \textrm{ such that } t_i = s_i \neq 0 \end{align*}

with the coarsest topology, such that

  1. time projection of the join
  2. partial projection of the join

are continuous (on the subspace)
