recursion formula for amount of spanning trees for a multigraph

Proposition

Let $G = (V,E)$ be a undirected multigraph and $e \in E$. Then the amount of Spanning Trees $\tau(G)$ is given by

\begin{align*} \tau(G) = \tau(G \setminus \{e\}) + \tau(G / e) \end{align*}

Proof

denote

\begin{align*} \tau_{e^+}(G) \coloneqq \mathrm{amount of spanning trees with } e \\ \tau_{e^-}(G) \coloneqq \mathrm{amount of spanning trees without } e \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} \tau(G) = \tau_{e^+}(G) + \tau_{e^-}(G) \end{align*}

and furthermore

\begin{align*} \tau_{e^{-}}(G) = \tau(G \setminus \{e\}) \end{align*}

Hence it suffices to show that there exists a bijection between spannig trees $\tau(G / \{e\}$ and $\tau_{e^+}(G)$

This is given by mapping a spanning tree $T$ of $G$ containing $e$ to $T/e$:
