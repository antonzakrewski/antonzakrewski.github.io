interval is a cogenerator of complete regalur spaces

Proposition

The interval $[0,1]$ is a cogenerator of the full subcategory of complete regular spaces. In particular it is one in the category CompHaus.

Proof

follows from the definition Let $f,g: A \rightarrow B$ be different continuous maps, then there exists an $a\in A$ such that $f(a) \neq g(a)$. Then any extension of

\begin{align*} \{f(a),g(a)\} \rightarrow& [0,1] f(a) \mapsto& 0 \\ g(a) \mapsto& 1 \end{align*}

suffices, where $f(a), g(a)$ are closed (since $B$ is complete regular) - note that there exists one, since $B$ is complete regular
