trivial fibration for a p-cartesian morphism and pullbacks

Proposition

Let $p: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be an isofibration and $f: \Delta^1 \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ be a p-cartesian morphism $x \rightarrow y$. Then for all $z \in \mathcal{E}$ the map

\begin{align*} \mathcal{E}^{/f} \times_{\mathcal{E}} \{z\} & \mathcal{E}^{/y} \times_{\mathcal{C}^{/p(y)}} \mathcal{C}^{/pf} \times_{\mathcal{E}} \{e\} \end{align*}

is a trivial fibration

Proof

Take 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{E}^{/f} \times_{\mathcal{E}} \{z\} \rar \dar & \mathcal{E}^{/f} \dar \\
\mathcal{E}^{/y} \times \mathcal{C}^{/pf} \times \{z\} \rar \dar & \dar \mathcal{E}^{/y} \times_{\mathcal{C}^{/p(y)} \\
\Delta^0 \rar & \mathcal{E}
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
