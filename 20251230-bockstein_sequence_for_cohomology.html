bockstein sequence for cohomology

Definition

Let $A \rightarrow B \rightarrow C$ be a fiber sequence in category of Spectra and $X$ any spectrum Then there exists a long exact sequence

\begin{align*} \pi_{-n+1}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[X], C)) \rightarrow \pi_{-n}( \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[X], A)) \rightarrow \pi_n(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[X], B)) \rightarrow \pi_{-n}( \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[X], C)) \rightarrow \pi_{-n-1}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[X],A)) \end{align*}

proof

  • long exact sequence homotopy groups for a spectrum
  • $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(-,-) is exact infinity functor (cf. mapping spectrum functor preserves limits / mapping spectrum functor is exact in both variables)
