coexact puppe sequence of a connected cw complex

Proposition

Let $(X, \emptyset, \mathrm{SK}_n(X))$ be a connected CW-Complex. Then there exists a coexact puppe sequence

\begin{align*} \bigvee_J S^n & \mathrm{Sk}_n & \mathrm{Sk}_{n+1} & \bigvee_{J} S^{n+1} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • connected cw complex as pushout of wedges up to homotopy
