retract of a free monoid as free monoid

Proposition

Let $N \hookrightarrow M$ be a retract of a free monoid. Then $N$ is also free

Proof

note that $N$ is projective by retract of a projective object as projective object. Let $I$ be the set of irreducible elements.

Then it is a generator (cf. irreducible elements generate submonoid of a free monoid). Hence consider

\begin{align*} \varphi: \mathrm{Free}(I) \twoheadrightarrow N \end{align*}

which splits (cf. epimorphism onto a projective splits).

We will show that the split mono $\varphi^{-1}$ is an epimorphism: It suffices to show that each generator lies in the image of $\varphi^{-1}$.

So let $i \in I$ be a generator which maps to $n_i$ under $\varphi$. Since $n_i$ is irreducible, it follows that any preimage of $n_i$ - in particular $\varphi(n_i)$ - is irreducible (preimage of an irreducible element as irreducible element). But the only irreducible elements in $\mathrm{Free}(I)$ are the generators.

Hence $\varphi(n_i)$ has to be $i$ since it is the only generator of $\mathrm{Free}(I)$ which maps to $n_i$
