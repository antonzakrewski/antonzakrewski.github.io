left bousfield localization of a stable category induces a orthogonal category

Proposition

Let $\iota:\mathcal{A} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a left bousfield localization. Then there exists functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ with

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\iota(-),\mathcal{F}(-)) \simeq * \end{align*}

Proof

For $c \in \mathcal{C}$ let $\eta: c \rightarrow \iota(\mathcal{L}(c))$ be the unit Then consider the cofiber $c \rightarrow \iota(\mathcal{L}(c)) \rightarrow \overline{c}$. Let $\tilde{a}\in \mathcal{A}$, then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{A}}(\overline{c}, \iota(\tilde{a})) \simeq \mathrm{fib}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\iota(\mathcal{L}(c)),\iota(\tilde{a})) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(c, \iota(\tilde{a}))) \end{align*}

but precomposition with the unit induces an equivalence on mapping anima $\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,\iota(-))$, so the fiber above is given by $0$
