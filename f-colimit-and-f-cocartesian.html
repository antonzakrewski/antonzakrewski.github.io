f-colimit and f-cocartesian

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an inner fibration Let $e: \Delta^1 = (\Delta^0)^{\triangleleft} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a morphism.

TFAE:

  1. $e$ is a f-colimit
  2. $e$ is \(\mathcal{F}\)-cocartesian

Proof
