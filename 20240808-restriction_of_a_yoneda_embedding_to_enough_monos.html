restriction of a contravariant yoneda embedding to enough monos

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category, $\mathcal{D}$ a (full subcategory ?) subcategory such that for each object $c \in \mathcal{C}$ there exists a monomorphism

\begin{align*} c \hookrightarrow d \end{align*}

for $d \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{D})$

Then the restriction of the contravariant yoneda embedding

is a faithful functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathrm{Set}) \end{align*}

Proof
