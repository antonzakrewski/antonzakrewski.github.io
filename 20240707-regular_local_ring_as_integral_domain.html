regular local ring as integral domain

Proposition

Let $(A, \mathfrak{m})$ be a regular local ring. Then $A$ is an Integral domain

Proof

Note that the dimension is given by $\mathrm{dim}(A) < \infty$ (cf. noetherian local ring as finite dimensional ring) Proof by induction

induction basis

Let $\mathrm{dim}(A) = 0$. Then by assumption $\mathrm{dim}_{A/\mathfrak{m}}(\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2) = 0$ showing that $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2 = 0$ Therefore

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{m} = \mathfrak{m}^2 \end{align*}

and by Nakayama's Lemma for local rings and $\mathfrak{m}$ (note that $\mathfrak{m}$ is a finitely generated module, as $A$ is a noetherian ring) we conclude that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{m}) = 0 \end{align*}

Hence $(0) = \mathfrak{m}$ is a prime ideal (cf. maximal ideal as prime ideal) showing that $A$ is an Integral domain (cf. )
