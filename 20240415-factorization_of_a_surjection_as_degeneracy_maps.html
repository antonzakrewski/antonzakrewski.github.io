factorization of a surjection as degeneracy maps in a simplex category

Proposition

Let $\Delta$ be the simplex category and $f: [k+n] \twoheadrightarrow [k]$ a surjection. Then there exist degeneracy maps $\sigma_i$ such that

\begin{align*} \sigma_i \circ \sigma_{i'} ... \circ \sigma_{i''} = f \end{align*}
