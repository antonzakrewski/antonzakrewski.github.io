precomposition with a bousfield localization induces localization on functor categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be infinity categories. Then a left bousfield resp. right bousfield localization $\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{D}$ induces a right / resp. left bousfield localization

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C},\mathcal{E}) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D},\mathcal{E}) \end{align*}

Proof

  • postcomposition with infinity adjoints as infinity adjoint
  • fully faithful infinity right adjoint and counit as equivalence / fully faithful infinity left adjoint and unit as equivalence
