S-local objects for a presentable category as left bousfield localization

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable category, $S \subseteq \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$ a small collection. Then the collection of S-local objects $\mathcal{L}_S(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ is also presentable and a left bousfield localization

Proof
