hilbert adjoint functor is semilinear and isometric on Endomorphisms

Proposition

Let $(-)^*: \mathrm{Hlb} \rightarrow \mathrm{Hlb}$ be the hilbert adjoint functor. Then upon enrichment, the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{BL}(\mathcal{H}) \rightarrow \mathrm{BL}(\mathcal{H}) \end{align*}

is a semi-linear isometry.
