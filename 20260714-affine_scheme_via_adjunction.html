affine scheme via adjunction

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $x \in \mathcal{C}$ be an object and $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{CAlg}_k$ a left adjoint with right adjoint $\mathcal{R}$ for a commutative ring $k$ . Then the functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,\mathcal{R}(-)) \end{align*}

is an affine scheme
