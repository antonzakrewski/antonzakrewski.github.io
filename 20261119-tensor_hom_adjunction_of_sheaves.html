tensor hom adjunction of sheaves

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $\varphi: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ be a mrophism of sheaves of rings on $X$ and let $\mathcal{F} \in \mathrm{Mod}_{\mathcal{A}}, \mathcal{G}, \mathcal{H} \in \mathrm{Mod}_{\mathcal{B}}$. Then there exists a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathcal{Hom}_{\mathcal{B}}(\mathcal{H} \otimes_{\mathcal{A}} \mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}) \cong \mathcal{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(\mathcal{F}, \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{B}}(\mathcal{H}, \mathcal{G}) \end{align*}

Proof
