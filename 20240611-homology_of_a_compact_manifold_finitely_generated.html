homology of a compact manifold finitely generated

Proposition

Let \(M\) be an \(n\)-dimensional compact manifold. Then $X$ is degreewise finitely generated

Proof

using

  1. closed topological manifold as subset of R^m
  2. manifold as locally contractible space
  3. retract of cw complexes, and weakly locally contractible
  4. retract of a homologically finite space
  5. finite cw complex as homologically finite space
