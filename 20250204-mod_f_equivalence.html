mod-F-equivalence

Definition

Let

be an exact functor in cat stable.

Let $\langle F\rangle$ be the smallest full sub infinity category containing the essential image and which is closed under infinity retracts

Then a morphism $f: d \rightarrow d'$ is said to be a \(\mathrm{mod}\)-F-equivalence, if

\begin{align*} \mathrm{fib}(f) \in \langle F\rangle \end{align*}
