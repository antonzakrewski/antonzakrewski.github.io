degree of maps and index of pi_1

Proposition

Let $f: M \rightarrow N$ be a map between closed orientable manifolds. Then the degree $d$ of $f$ divides the index $[\pi_1(N) : f[\pi_1(M)]]$ or equivalently the cardinality of the $\pi_1(N)$-set $\pi_1(N) / f[\pi_1(M)]$

Proof

observe that covering theory shows that the map lifts to an $\pi_1(N) / f[\pi_1(M)]$ sheeted covering $N'$

now the statement about the degree of a composite of maps of closed manifolds implies that the degree of $f$ is a multiple of the degree of the covering map, which has the amount of sheets as degree (cf. degree of an n-sheeted covering for a closed orientable manifold).
