sInd_1 as 0-truncated objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small \(1\) category. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sInd}_1(\mathcal{C}) \simeq \tau_{\leq 0}(\mathrm{sInd}_\infty(\mathcal{C})) \end{align*}

are equivalent

see:

  • n-truncated object
  • sInd_1 as full subcategory of sInd
  • sInd_1 of a 1-category
  • sInd of an infinity category

Proof

note that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sInd}_1(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathrm{sInd}_{\infty}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is an full sub infinity category (cf. sInd_1 as full subcategory of sInd)

Hence it suffices to show that the essential image contains precisely the \(0\)-truncated objects:

By n-truncated presheaf and pointwise n-truncated we conclude that $\mathcal{F} \in \tau_{\leq 0}(\mathrm{An}(\mathcal{C}))$ if and only if it is pointwise \(0\)-truncated, or equivalent to a functor which factors through $\mathrm{Set}$: To get the equivalence, apply the infinity unit which is objectwise an equivalence, hence a natural equivalence (cf. natural equivalence between infinity functors and objectwise equivalence)

Thus the map

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \simeq \mathrm{sInd}_1(\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}}) \rightarrow \tau_{\leq 0}(\mathrm{An}(\mathcal{C})) \end{align*}

is essentially surjective functor and fully faithful functor, hence a joyal equivalence (cf. joyal equivalence as fully faithful essentially surjective functor)
