projective quotient and inclusion as surjection on hom sets

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, $A,B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ objects, $f: A \rightarrow B$ a monomorphism such that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{coker}(f) \cong A/B \end{align*}

is a projective object

Then precomposition with $f$ induces a surjection on hom-sets

\begin{align*} f^{ \rightarrow}: \mathrm{Hom}_{A}(M,L) \twoheadrightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{A}(N,L) \end{align*}

Proof

consider the short exact sequence

using epimorphism onto a projective splits and the Splitting Lemma we conclude that $f$ is split mono

Therefore it is split epi in the opposite category

Since functor preserve split epis we conclude that

\begin{align*} f^{ \rightarrow}: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(M,L) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(N,L) \end{align*}

is an epimorphism in set, hence surjective
