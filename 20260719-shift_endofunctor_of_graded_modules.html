shift endofunctor of graded modules

Proposition

Let $A$ be a $\Gamma$ graded ring, $\mathrm{grMod}_{A}$ the category of graded modules. Then for $\gamma \in \Gamma$, the $\gamma$ shift functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{grMod}_{A} \rightarrow& \mathrm{grMod}_{A} \bigoplus_{\delta \in \Gamma} M_{\delta} \mapsto& \bigoplus_{\delta \in \Gamma} M_{\delta + \gamma} \end{align*}

Proof
