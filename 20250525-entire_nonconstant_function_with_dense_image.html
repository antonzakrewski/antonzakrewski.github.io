entire nonconstant function with dense image

Proposition

Let $f$ be a nonconstant, entire function. Then

\begin{align*} \{f(z) : z \in \mathbb{C} \} \end{align*}

is dense in $\mathbb{C}$.

Proof

Assume the map is not dense. Then consider there exists an $w \in \mathbb{C}$ and $\varepsilon > 0$ such that $\vert f(z) - w \vert \geq \varepsilon$. Then

\begin{align*} z \mapsto \frac{1}{f(z) - w} \leq \frac{1}{\varepsilon} \end{align*}

is bounded and entire, so constant.

Hence $f(z) - w$ or $f(z)$ is constant
