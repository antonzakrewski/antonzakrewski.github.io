Mul of an infinity operad

Definition

Let $\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}$ be an infinity operad and $X_1,...,X_n,Y \in \mathcal{O}$ be objects. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mul}_{\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}}((X_1,...,X_n),Y) \end{align*}

is defined as full sub infinity groupoid of

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}}((X_1,...,X_n),Y) \end{align*}

containing maps $f: (X_1,...,X_n \rightarrow Y$ which map to the active morphism

\begin{align*} \beta: \langle n\rangle \rightarrow& \langle 1\rangle \\ i \mapsto& \begin{cases} * & \mbox{if } i = * \\ 1 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Here

\begin{align*} (X_1,...,X_n) \in \mathcal{O} \end{align*}

is an object equivalent to $(X_1,...,X_n) \in \mathcal{O}^n$ under the induced equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O}^{\otimes}_n \simeq \mathcal{O}^n \end{align*}

Note that this is given as pullback
