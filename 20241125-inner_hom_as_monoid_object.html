internal hom as monoid object

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a closed monoidal category. Then for an object $X \in \mathcal{V}$ the internal hom $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,X)$ is a monoid object via

  1. the unit given by
\begin{align*} * \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(M,M) \end{align*}

adjoint to

\begin{align*} \mathrm{id}: M \rightarrow M \end{align*}
  1. multiplication given by the composition of morphisms
\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(M,M) \otimes \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(M,M) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(M,M) \end{align*}
