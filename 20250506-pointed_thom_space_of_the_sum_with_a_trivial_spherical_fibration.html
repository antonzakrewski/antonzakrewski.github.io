pointed thom space of the sum with a trivial spherical fibration

Proposition

Let $\pi: E \rightarrow B$ be a pointed spherical fibration and $e^k: S^k \times E \rightarrow E$ the trivial pointed spherical fibration. Then for the direct sum of pointed spherical fibrations $\pi \oplus e^k$ the pointed Thom space is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Th}_*(\pi \oplus e^{k}) \simeq S^k \wedge \mathrm{Th}_*(\pi) \end{align*}

Proof
