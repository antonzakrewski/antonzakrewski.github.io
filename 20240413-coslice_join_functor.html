right coslice join functor

Definition

Let $X$ be a simplicial set. Then the right slice join functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} X \star -: \mathrm{sSet} \rightarrow& X / \mathrm{sSet} \\ Y \mapsto& (\iota: X \rightarrow X \star Y) \\\ (f: Y \rightarrow Y') \mapsto& (X \star f: X \star Y \rightarrow X \star Y') \end{align*}

where

  1. $X \star Y$ is the join of a simplicial set
  2. $\iota: X \rightarrow X \star Y$ is the left inclusion into the join of simplicial sets
  3. $X \star f$ is the right induced morphism between joins

see: coslice category

Note that $X \star f$ is by construction functorial
