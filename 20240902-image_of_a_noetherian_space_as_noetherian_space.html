image of a noetherian space as noetherian space

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be topological spaces, $X$ be a noetherian space and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a continuous map. Then the image $f[X]$ is also a noetherian space

Proof

Let $Z_1 \supseteq ...$ be a chain of closed sets in $f[X]$. Then it follows that $f^{-1}[Z_i]$ is also a chain of closed sets.

This stabilizes as $X$ is noetherian. Hence $Z_i$ stabilizes.
