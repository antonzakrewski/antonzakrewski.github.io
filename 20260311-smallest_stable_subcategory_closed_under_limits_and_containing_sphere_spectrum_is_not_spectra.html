smallest stable subcategory closed under limits and containing sphere spectrum is not spectra

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be the smallest stable subcategory of $\mathrm{Sp}$ which

  1. is closed under limits
  2. contains $\mathbb{S}$.

Then $\mathcal{C} \neq \mathrm{Sp}$.
