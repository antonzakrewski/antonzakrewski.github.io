functor preserving finite colimits preserves compact colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an infinity functor. Suppose $\mathcal{F}$ preserves finite colimits.

Then $\mathcal{F}$ also preserves compact colimit.

Proof

  • compact anima as retract of finite anima
  • precomposition with a functor induces map on colimits
  • some absolute colimit argument
