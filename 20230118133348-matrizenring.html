matrix ring

Definition

Let $R$ be a ring, then the matrix ring is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{End}_{\mathrm{Mod}_R}(R^n) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{RMod}$ is the category of right modules.

see:

  • endomorphism set of an ab-enriched category as ring

Remark

I think it is important to keep track that these are endomorphisms of right modules, otherwise we have

\begin{align*} \mathrm{End}_{{}_{R}\mathrm{Mod}}(R^n) \cong \mathrm{Mat}_{n}(R^{\mathrm{op}}) \end{align*}

under the usual convention of matrix multiplication.
