left derived functor of an infinity functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, where $\mathcal{D}$ admits infinity sifted colimits and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an infinity functor Then the left derived functor is defined as extension

\begin{align*} \mathcal{P}_{\Sigma}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}
