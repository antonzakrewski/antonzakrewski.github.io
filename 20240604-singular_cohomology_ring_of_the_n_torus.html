singular cohomology ring of the n-torus

Proposition

Let $T^n$ be the n-torus. Then the singular cohomology is given by

\begin{align*} H^*(T^n) \cong \Lambda_{\mathbb{Z}}(x_1,...,x_n) \end{align*}

with $\vert x_i \vert = 1$
