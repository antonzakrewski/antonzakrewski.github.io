wu class

Definition

Let $\Chi$ denote the antipode of the steenrod algebra, $\pi: E \rightarrow B$ be a spherical fibration. Then the \(n\)-th wu class $v_n$ is defined as unique class satisfying

\begin{align*} v_n(\pi) \cdot u = \Chi(\mathrm{Sq}^n)(u) \in H^{d+n}(\mathrm{Th}(\pi), \mathbb{F}_2) \end{align*}
