yoneda embedding preserves and detects group objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category with finite products. Then the yoneda embedding induces fully faithful functors

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Grp}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Grp}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})) \\ \mathrm{CGrp}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{CGrp}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})) \\ \end{align*}

whose essential image consist of the functors, which are representable underlying.
