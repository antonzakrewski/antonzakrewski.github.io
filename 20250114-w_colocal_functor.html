W-colocal functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be \(W\)-colocal, if there exists a factorization by

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F} = q \circ \iota \end{align*}

where

  1. $q$ is a left fibration
  2. $\iota$ is cofinal

not dual to W-local
