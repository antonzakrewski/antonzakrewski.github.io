Jacobson space

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space and $Y = \{x_0 \in X \vert \{x_0\} \text{ is closed}\}$ be the set of closed points. Then $X$ is said to be a jacobson space, if for each closed set $Z \subseteq X$ it holds

\begin{align*} \overline{Z \cap Y} = Z \end{align*}
