module structure equivalently given by ring homomorphism to the endomorphism ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a ring and $M$ an abelian group.

TFAE:

  1. $M$ is a left \(A\)-module
  2. there exists a ring-homomorphism $\varphi: A \rightarrow \mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M)$ where $\mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M)$ is the endomorphism ring for $M$ regarded as \(\mathbb{Z}\)-module (cf.abelian group as module over the integers)

For a right module structure this is given by the opposite ring

\begin{align*} A^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M) \end{align*}
