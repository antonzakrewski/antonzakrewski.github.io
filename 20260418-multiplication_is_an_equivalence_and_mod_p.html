multiplication is an equivalence and mod p is trivial in a stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category, $X$ in $\mathcal{C}$ an object and $n$ a natural number. TFAE:

  1. multiplication by $n$ is an equivalence
  2. $X/n$ is the zero object

Proof

follows from the bicartesian square

and square with parallel equivalences is bicartesian
