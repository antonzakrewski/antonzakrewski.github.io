thom space of the tangent bundle of the sphere

Proposition

Let $\pi: TS^n \rightarrow S^n$ be the tangential bundle. Then the Thom Space is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Th}(\pi) \simeq& S^n \times S^n/\Delta \end{align*}

Proof

using pointed thom space of the suspension equivalent to thom space & stabilization of the tangent bundle of the sphere we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Th}(\pi) \simeq \mathrm{Th}_*(\Sigma \pi)\\ \simeq& \mathrm{Th}_*(e^k) \end{align*}

as pointed Thom space of the trivial bundle.
