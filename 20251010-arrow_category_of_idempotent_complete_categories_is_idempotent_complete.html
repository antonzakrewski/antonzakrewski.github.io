functor category into an idempotent complete categories is idempotent complete

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an idempotent complete category and $I$ an indexing category. Then the functor category $\mathrm{Fun}(I, \mathcal{C})$ is also idempotent complete

Proof

use:

  • pointwise calculation of limits
  • split idempotent and limit / colimit
