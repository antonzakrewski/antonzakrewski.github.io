stalk of an integral scheme is integral

Proposition

The stalks of an integral scheme is an Integral domain

Proof

We use the characterization, that $\mathcal{O}_{X}(U)$ is an integral domain for $U \subseteq X$. Then the stalk is a filtered colimit of an integral domain, hence also an integral domain (cf. integral domains closed under filtered colimits).

alternative

Alternatively, one can identify the stalks as subrings of the function field.
