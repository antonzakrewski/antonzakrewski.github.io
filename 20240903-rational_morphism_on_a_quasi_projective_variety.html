rational morphism on a quasi-projective variety

Definition

Let $X$ be a quasi-projective variety and $Y$ a quasi-projective algebraic set Then a rational morphism $f: X \rightarrow Y$ is the equivalence class of regular maps

\begin{align*} f_{\vert U}: U \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

on nonempty open subsets $U \subseteq Y$.
