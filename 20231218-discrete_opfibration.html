discrete opfibration

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor.

Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be a discrete opfibration, if the opposite functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{op}}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}

is a discrete fibration
