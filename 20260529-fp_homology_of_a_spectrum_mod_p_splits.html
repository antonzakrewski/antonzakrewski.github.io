Fp homology of a spectrum mod p splits

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum, $X/p$ the mod q spectrum. Then $X/p \otimes \mathbb{F}_p \simeq X \otimes \mathbb{F}_p \oplus (X \otimes \mathbb{F}_p)[1]$.

Proof

observe that there exists a fiber sequence $X \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X \rightarrow X/q$. Then we may tensor with $\mathbb{F}_p$ and observe that multiplication with $p$ is nullhomotopic on $\mathbb{F}_p$. Hence apply cofiber of a nullhomotopic map.
