hom-set into a bimodule with right module structure

Proposition

Let $R,S$ be rings, $M,N$ left \(R\)-module and $N$ a right \(S\)-module. Then the hom-set

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{R-Mod}}(M,N) \end{align*}

admits a natural left-\(S\) module structure with

\begin{align*} f(-) \cdot s \coloneqq (m \mapsto f(m) \cdot s) \end{align*}

Proof

analogously to hom-set out of a bimodule with module structure
