S^1 wedge S^n not nilpotent or simple

Proposition

The anima $S^1 \wedge S^n$ is not nilpotent

Proof

It has finitely generated homology, but $\pi_2(S^1 \wedge S^2) \cong \bigoplus_{\mathbb{N}} \mathbb{Z}$ is highly non finitely generated. Therefore by by contraposition and the fundamental theorem of serre class theory (cf. finitely generated abelian groups is a derived serre ring) it can't be simple (or even nilpotent)
