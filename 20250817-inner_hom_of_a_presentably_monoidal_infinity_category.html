inner hom of a presentably monoidal category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentably monoidal category. Then the tensor product admits an internal hom bifunctor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ (X,Y) \mapsto \underline{\mathrm{map}}(X,Y) \end{align*}

Proof

using left adjoint functor theorem for presentable categories we may conclude that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ Y \mapsto& Y \otimes X \end{align*}

admits a left adjoint for arbitrary $X$ (since $\mathcal{C}$ is presentable and $- \otimes -$ preserves colimits)

Thus we get $\underline{\mathrm{map}}(X,-): \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C}$.

By currying of $- \otimes -: \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ we get a functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{C}) \\ X \mapsto& (Y \mapsto X \otimes Y) \end{align*}

which by the argument above restricts to FunLAdj. Using opposite FunRAdj equivalent to FunLAdj we get a functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{C}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{RAdj}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{C})^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}

and then using opposite infinity functor category we get a functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}})\\ \end{align*}

and then currying yields

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}

Then applying the opposite infinity category functor we get

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}
