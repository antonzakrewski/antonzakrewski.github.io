join of spherical fibrations

Definition

Let $\pi_i: B_i \rightarrow BF(d_i)$ be spherical fibrations for $i \in \{1,2\}$. Then the join product is defined as composition

\begin{align*} \pi_i \wedge \pi_2: B_1 \times B_2 \rightarrow BF(d_1) \times BF(d_2) \rightarrow BF(d_1 + d_2) \end{align*}

where the latter map is given by

  1. <B functor preserves products>
  2. join homomorphism of F(d)
