associated spectral sequence converges against colimit

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category equipped with a t-structure compatible with sequential colimits. Let $X: \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is a filtered object with $X(n) =0$ for $n << 0$. Then the induced spectral sequence converges

\begin{align*} E_{r}^{p,q} \Rightarrow \pi_{p+q} \mathrm{colim}(X) \end{align*}
