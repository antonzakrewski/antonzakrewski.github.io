exact infinity functor as additive functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be exact infinity categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an exact infinity functor. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is an additive infinity functor

Proof

a) reduced infinity functor

follows from the definition

b) additive

Let $X,Y\in \mathcal{C}$, then $X \oplus Y$ is characterized as the pushout

But $0 \hookrightarrow Y$ is an inclusion. Therefore by exact infinity functor preserves pushouts along an inclusion we get the pushout
