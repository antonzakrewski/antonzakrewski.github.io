canonical morphism between commuting limit and colimit

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $J: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{I}: I \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ diagrams, such that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{I} \mathrm{lim}_J, \mathrm{lim}_J \mathrm{colim}_I \end{align*}

exist.

Then there exists a canonical morphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{I} \mathrm{lim}_J \rightarrow \mathrm{lim}_J \mathrm{colim}_I \end{align*}

induced by the universal property

Proof
