closed operator in general not bounded

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a closed operator from a normed vector space $Y \subseteq Y'$ to a banach space. Then $f$ is in general not bounded operator.

Proof

consider $X = C^1([0,1])$ and $Y' = Y= C([0,1])$ with the supremumsnorm. Then consider

\begin{align*} \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}: C^1([0,1]) \rightarrow& C([0,1]) \\ f \mapsto& f' \end{align*}

Then $T \coloneqq \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}$ is not bounded (cf. differentiation operator is not bounded).

So it remains to show tha $T$ is closed: Let $f_n \in C^1([0,1])$ be a sequence such that

  1. $\exists g \in C([0,1])$ with $\| f_n - g \|_{\infty} \xrightarrow{n \rightarrow \infty} 0$
  2. $\exists h \in C([0,1])$ with $\| f_n' - h \|_{\infty} \xrightarrow{n \rightarrow \infty} 0$
