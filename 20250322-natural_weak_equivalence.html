natural weak equivalence

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}, \mathrm{Cof}, \mathrm{Fib})$ be a model category and $\mathcal{D}$ another category. Let $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be functors and $\eta: \mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ a natural transformation Then $\eta$ is said to be a natural weak equivalence, if for each object $d \in \mathcal{D}$ the morphism

\begin{align*} \eta(d): \mathcal{F}(d) \rightarrow \mathcal{G}(d) \end{align*}

is a weak equivalence
