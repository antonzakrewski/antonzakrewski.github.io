surjective und split epi in ZFC

Proposition

Assume the Axiom of choice. Let $X,Y$ be sets and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a map

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is surjective
  2. $f$ is split epi i.e. there exists a right inverse $g: Y \rightarrow X$ such that
\begin{align*} f \circ g = \mathrm{id}_Y \end{align*}

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Note that for each $y \in Y$ the fibre $f^{-1}[ \{y\}]$ is nonempty Hence we may choose for each $y \in Y$ an element $x_y \in f^{-1}[ \{y\}]$. Then define

\begin{align*} g: Y \rightarrow& X \\ y \mapsto& x_y \end{align*}

This $g$ then provides a right inverse

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $y \in Y$. Then $g(y)$ is a preimage of $y$ since

\begin{align*} f(g(y)) =& (f \circ g)(y) \\ =& \mathrm{id}(y) \\ =& y \end{align*}
