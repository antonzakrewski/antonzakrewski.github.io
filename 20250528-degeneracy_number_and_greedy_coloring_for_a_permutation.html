degeneracy number and greedy coloring for a permutation

Proposition

Let $G = ([n],E)$ be a graph.

TFAE:

  1. $G$ is a k degenerate graph
  2. there exists a Permutation $\pi \in S_n$ such that the greedy chromatic number for a permutation is at most $k$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Proof by induction, number of vertices

induction basis $[n] = [1]$

then the only graph is $*$

induction step

then choose an element with degree at most $k$ - w.l.o.g. the vertex $n \in [n]$. Then the subgraph $[n-1]$ is still k degenerate, so by induction hypothesis there exists a permutation $\pi$. Then adding $n$ as the identity suffices.

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $H$ be a subgraph, $\pi$ such a permutation. Let $l = \mathrm{max}(i \in H, \pi(i) \in H)$. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{deg}_H(l) \leq& \mathrm{deg}_{ \{\pi(1),...,\pi(l)\}}(\pi(l)) \\ \leq& k \end{align*}
