characterization of f-related smooth vector fields

Proposition

Let $\phi: M \rightarrow N$ be a smooth map and $X \in \mathfrak{X}(M), Y\in \mathfrak{X}(M)$. TFAE:

  1. $X,Y$ are \(\psi\)-related
  2. $\psi^*(Yf) = X(\psi^* f)$ for all $f \in C^{\infty}(N)$, i.e.

Proof

\begin{align*} X_p(\psi^*(f)) =& D(f \circ \psi) \\ =& Df(D\psi (X_p)) \\ =& Df(Y_{\psi(p)} \\ =& (Y f)_{\psi(p)} \\ =& (\psi^*(Y(f))_p \end{align*}

then $Y$ is uniquely determined by this equality and above says that $(D \psi)(X) = Y_{\psi(p)}$ by the above choice
