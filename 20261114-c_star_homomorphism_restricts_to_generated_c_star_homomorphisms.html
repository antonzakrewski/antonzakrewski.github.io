c star homomorphism restricts to generated c star homomorphisms

Proposition

Let $f: A \rightarrow B$ be a map of C star algebras and $a_i \in A$ a collection of elements. Then there exists a map

\begin{align*} C(a_i) \rightarrow C(f(a_i)) \end{align*}

where $C(a_i)$ is the generated, closed subalgebra.
