free A-Algebra

Definition

Let $X$ be a set and $R$ a ring Then the free algebra $\mathrm{freeAlg}_R(X)$ over $X$ is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{freeAlg}(X) = \bigoplus_{w \in X^{*}} Rw \end{align*}

where $X^{*}$ is the kleene star (or free monoid) and $\bigoplus$ the categorical coproduct in the category RMod
