bijection between idempotent sections and clopen sets of a locally ringed space

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{O}_X$ be a locally ringed space. Then there exists a bijection between idempotent elements

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Idem}(\mathcal{O}_{X}(X)) \cong \{Z \subseteq X \vert \mathrm{ open and closed}\} e \mapsto& X_e \\ Z \mapsto& e_Z \\ \end{align*}

given by

  1. nonzeros in the residue field of a section of a locally ringed space as open subset
  2. the construction in clopen subset of a ringed space induces idempotent element in the section

Proof

using: monic split epi as isomorphism

a)

Note that

\begin{align*} Z \mapsto e_Z \mapsto Z \end{align*}

is by construction a one sided inverse (after getting the right $\pm 1$)

b)

We show that

\begin{align*} e = X_{e} \end{align*}

is injective by showing that $e,e'$ with $X_{e} = X_{e'}$ have the same germ for $x \in X$.

By assumption

\begin{align*} e(x), e'(x) \neq 0 \end{align*}

with $e(x), e'(x)$ as idempotent elements. By idempotent elements in a local ring we may conclude that $e(x) = 1 = e'(x)$.

Hence the germs coincide
