zariski spectrum not connected and nontrivial idempotent element

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ the zariski spectrum with the Zariski topology Suppose $\alpha \in A \setminus \{0,1\}$ is idempotent, then it follows that $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ is not connected

Proof

Consider

\begin{align*} V(\alpha), V(1 - \alpha) \end{align*}

Then it follows that $(\alpha), (1 - \alpha)$ are coprime, hence

\begin{align*} V(\alpha) \cap V(1 - \alpha) = \emptyset \end{align*}

Furthermore

\begin{align*} (1 - \alpha) \cdot \alpha = 0 \end{align*}

showing that each prime ideal $\mathfrak{p}$ contains one of $1 - \alpha, \alpha$ and hence is inside one of $V(\alpha), V(1 - \alpha)$
