weiss topology for two

Proposition

The kappa-weiss topology for $\kappa = 2$ is the canonical grothendieck topology on Open(X) except that the only covering sieve of $\emptyset$ is the representable presheaf, i.e. the empty subfunctor is not a covering sieve of $\emptyset$.d

Proof

Let $R \subseteq U$ be a sieve Then any singleton of $U$ is contained in some open element of $R$, hence the elemenst of $R$ is precisely an open cover of $U$.
