k0 of an exact category

Definition / Proposition

Assume some large cardinal axioms. Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an exact category.

Then $K_0$ is defined as free abelian group generated by the objects of $\mathcal{A}$ modulo the relation

\begin{align*} B \sim A + C \end{align*}

for an exact sequence

Proof

By definition

\begin{align*} K_0(\mathcal{E}) \simeq& \pi_0( \Omega \vert \mathrm{Span}(\mathcal{E}) \vert) \\ \simeq& \pi_1( \vert \mathrm{Span}(\mathcal{E}) \vert \\ \simeq& \pi_1( \vert S(\mathcal{E})^{\simeq} \vert) \end{align*}

where $S(\mathcal{E})^{\simeq}$ is the simplicial anima with $S_0(\mathcal{E})^{\mathrm{\simeq}} = *$

Hence we use pi_1 of a realization of a simplicial Anima with contractible zero simplices
