equivalence between the category of affine varieties and dominant rational maps and finitely generated field extension

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathrm{AffVar}$ the category of affine varieties and dominant maps and $\mathrm{FinField}_K$ the category of finite field extensions. Then the function field functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{FunField}: \mathrm{AffVar} \rightarrow \mathrm{FinField}_K \\ \end{align*}

is an equivalence

Proof

adjoint equivalence of categories and fully faithful and essentially surjective functor

essentially surjective

  • finitely generated field extension as function field of an affine variety

fully faithful

  • contravariant functor from affine varieties to the function fields as fully faithfull functor
