musical isomorphism of a symplectic form

Definition

Let $(M,\omega)$ be a symplectic manifold. Then $\omega$ induce isomorphisms

\begin{align*} \omega^{\flat}: TM \rightarrow T^*M \\ \omega^{\sharp}: T^*M \rightarrow TM \end{align*}

which is fiberwise given by

\begin{align*} TM_p \rightarrow T^*M_p \\ v \mapsto \omega_p(v, -) \end{align*}
