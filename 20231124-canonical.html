canonical

prefered meaning

  • functorial or natural

possible

rather mathematical

  1. construction without choice
  2. functorial, i.e. assigning some morphisms and making them (globally) commute
  3. constructive example, i.e. only finite choice

rather informal

  1. usual, socially established choice
  2. "I like it a lot"

see:

  • natural transformation as natural transformation as functor from the product with the interval category
  • or n-morphism

example

  • e.g. $\frac{1}{2}$ would be canonical, but not $\frac{24}{48}$.
