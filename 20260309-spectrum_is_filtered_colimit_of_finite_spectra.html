spectrum is filtered colimit of finite spectra

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum Then $X$ is the filtered colimit of finite spectra.

Proof

Let $\mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{fin}}_{/X}$ be the pullback

Then $\mathrm{Sp}_{/X}^{\mathrm{fin}}$ admits finite colimits, as colimits in $\mathrm{Sp}_{/X}$ are computed underlying and $\mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{fin}}$ is by definition closed under finite colimits. Hence it is in particular filtered (cf. category with kappa small colimits is kappa filtered).

Then we claim that $\mathrm{colim}_{V \in \mathrm{Sp}_{/X}} V \rightarrow X$ is an equivalence: We need to show that the map is an equivalence upon applying $\pi_*$ (cf. whitehead theorem for spectra). we show that it is surjective and injective:

Recall that $\pi_n(-)$ preserves filtered colimits (cf. homotopy group functor of spectra preserves filtered colimits).

surjective

Let $x \in \pi_n(X)$, then it is in particular represented by a map $\mathbb{S}[n] \xrightarrow{x} X$. In particular, the generator $\pi_n(\mathbb{S}[n]) \rightarrow \mathrm{colim}_{V \in \mathrm{Sp}_{/X}} \pi_n(V) \rightarrow X$

injective

Let $y$ be an element in $\mathrm{colim}_{V \in \mathrm{Sp}_{/X}} \pi_n(V)$ which maps to $0$. Then in particular it is represented by a map $\mathbb{S}[n] \rightarrow V \rightarrow \mathrm{colim}_{V \in \mathrm{Sp}_{/X}} V \rightarrow X$ such that the composite is nullhomotopic. Let $C = \mathrm{cofib}(\mathbb{S}[n] \rightarrow V)$ be the cofiber, then the map $\mathbb{S}[n] \rightarrow V \rightarrow X$ factors through $C$. In particular, $y$ is also represented by the composite above, which is nullhomotopic.
