dimension of an open cover

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space, $U_i \subseteq X$ an open cover. Then the dimension of $X$ is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(X) = \mathrm{sup}(\mathrm{dim}(U_i)) \end{align*}

Proof

a)

Note that $\mathrm{dim}(X) \geq \mathrm{sup}(\mathrm{dim}(U_i))$ by dimension of a subspace

b)

Assume that there exists a chain of closed irreducible subspaces $Z_0 \subsetneq ... \subsetneq Z_n$. Assume that $U_i$ is an open cover, w.l.o.g. we may choose an $U$ such that $U \cap Z_0 \neq \emptyset$.

Then $U \cap Z_i$ is irreducible (cf. open subset of an irreducible space as irreducible space).

Furthermore assume that $U \cap Z_i$ is not a chain of length $n$. Then there exists an $i$ such that

\begin{align*} U \cap Z_i = U \cap Z_{i+1} \end{align*}

Then we may take the closure and get

\begin{align*} \overline{U \cap Z_i} \subseteq \overline{Z_i} = Z_i \end{align*}

(cf. Closure of a Subset) but also by irreducible space and dense open set it follows that $U \cap Z_{i+1}$ is dense in $Z_{i+1}$, since $U \cap Z_{i+1} \supseteq U \cap Z_{0} \neq \emptyset$.

Thus $\overline{U \cap Z_i} = \overline{U \cap Z_{i+1}} = Z_{i+1}$

But this is a contradiction, since $Z_{i} \subsetneq Z_{i+1}$
