torsion pair of an abelian category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category. Then a torsion pair si given by full subcategories $\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{F} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{A}$ such that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{F}) = 0 \end{align*}

and such that for any $X \in \mathcal{A}$ there exists a short exact sequence

with $T \in \mathcal{T}, F \in \mathcal{F}$
