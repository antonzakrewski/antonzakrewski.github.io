direct sum of groups

Definition

Let $G_i$ be groups, then the direct sum of $G_i$ is defined as the set

\begin{align*} \{ (g_i) \in \prod_{i \in I} G_i \vert g_i = 1 \text{ almost all }i \in I\} \end{align*}

with componentwise operation
