compact space is not a compact anima

Proposition

Let $X$ be a compact space. Then $X$ is in general not a compact anima.

Proof

Let $X$ be the hawaiian earring, which is a closed subset of (some scaled version of) $D^2$ hence compact. But $H_1(X) \simeq \pi_1(X)^{\mathrm{ab}}$ is infinitely generated, contradiction to compact anima has finitely generated homology
