non simple moore space in general not unique

Proposition

Let $M(A,n), M(A,n)'$ be a Moore spaces, where $M(A,n)$ is not simple. Then in general $M(A,n) \not\simeq M(A,n)'$

Proof

choose a nontrivial acyclic space $X$ and a moore space $M(A,n)$. Then $M(A,n) \wedge X$ is a moore space, but e.g. $\pi_1(-)$ differs.
