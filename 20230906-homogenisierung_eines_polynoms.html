homogenization of a polynomial

Definition

Let $I$ be a set (not containing $0$) and $f \in k[x_i \vert i \in I]$ a polynomial. Then the homogenization of $f$ is the homogeneous polynomial

\begin{align*} f^h = x_0^d f(\frac{x_i}{x_0}) \in k[x_0, x_i \vert i \in I] \end{align*}

where $d$ is the maximal degree of a monoimal with nonzero coefficient in $f$
