filtered category and regular cardinal

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a kappa filtered category for an infinite nonregular cardinal $\kappa$. Then it is also $\kappa + 1$ filtered for the successor cardinal (cf. successor cardinal as regular cardinal)

Proof

Let $U$ be a colllection of objects with cardinality less than $\kappa + 1$. Then by construction $U$ has at most $\kappa$ many objects.

By assumption there exists a union

\begin{align*} \bigcup_{i \in I} U_i \end{align*}

with cardinality $\kappa$, where $U_i,I$ each have less than $\kappa$ many objects Choose an inclusion $\iota: U \hookrightarrow \bigcup_{i \in I} U_i$ Choose an upper bound (name ?)$u_i$ of each $U_i$, which can be done by $\kappa$ filteredness

Then we can choose an upper bound for the collection of $u_i$, which is also an upper bound of each $U_i$
