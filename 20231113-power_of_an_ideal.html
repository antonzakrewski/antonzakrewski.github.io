power of an ideal

Definition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{q}$ an ideal. Then the \(n\)-th power is defined inductively

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{q}^0 \coloneqq A \\ \mathfrak{q}^{n+1} \coloneqq \mathfrak{q}^n \cdot \mathfrak{q} \end{align*}

see: product of ideals

Note, that by convention, $\mathfrak{a}^0 = A$
