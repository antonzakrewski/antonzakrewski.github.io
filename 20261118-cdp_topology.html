cdp topology

Definition

The $\mathrm{cdp}$ topology is the topology generated by families of proper morphisms $(Y_i \rightarrow X)_{i \in I}$ such that for every $x \in X$ there exists an $i \in I$ and $y \in Y_i$ over $X$ such that $\kappa(x) \xrightarrow{\simeq} \kappa(y)$.

Remark

scheme is in general not a sheaf for the cdp topology.
