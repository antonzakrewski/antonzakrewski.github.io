triviality of stiefel-whitney class

Proposition

Let $\pi: E \rightarrow B$ be a spherical fibration. Then the \(n\)-th stiefel whitney class $w_n$ vanishes for $n > \mathrm{rank}(\pi)$

Proof

\begin{align*} w_n(\pi) \cdot u(pi) = \mathrm{Sq}^n(u(\pi)) \end{align*}

by triviality of steenrod operation
