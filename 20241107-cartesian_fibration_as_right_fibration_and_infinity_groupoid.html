cartesian fibration as right fibration and infinity groupoid

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{E}, \mathcal{C}$ be infinity categories and $p: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a cartesian fibration. TFAE:

  1. $p$ is a right fibration
  2. for each $x \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ the fibre $\mathcal{E}_x = \mathcal{E} \times_{\mathcal{C}} \{x\}$ is an infinity-groupoid
