line bundles in sheaves of modules are invertible objects

Proposition

Let $X$ be a ringed space and $\mathcal{L}$ a line bundle. Then $\mathcal{L}$ is an invertible object for the tensor product.

Proof

We want to show that the evaluation map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ev}: \mathcal{H}om(\mathcal{L}, \mathcal{O}_{X}) \otimes \mathcal{L} \rightarrow \mathcal{O}_{X} \end{align*}

is an isomorphism. Here the evaluation map is the counit.

So choose a covering $U_i$ of $X$ such that $\mathcal{L}_{\vert U_i} \cong \mathcal{O}_{U_i}$. Then under the symmetric monoidal restriction functor, the restriction of $\mathrm{ev}_{\mathcal{L}}$ corresponds to $\mathrm{ev}_{\mathcal{L}_{\vert U}}$ which follows e.g. from the explicit descripiton of the adjunction and the fact that restriction functor is closed monoidal for sheaves of modules.

But the evaluation map is an isomorphism for the monoidal unit (e.g. use that $- \otimes \mathbbm{1}$ is an equivalence, so both left and right adjoint are fully faithful +fully faithful right adjoint and counit as isomorphism). Hence the map $\mathrm{ev}_{\mathcal{L}}$ is an isomorphism when restricted on an open cover and therefore by the sheaf condition an isomorphism.
