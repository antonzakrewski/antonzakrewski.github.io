stabilization of additive categories

Definition / Proposition

The forgetful functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}^{\mathrm{st}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}^{\mathrm{add}} \end{align*}

admits a left adjoint.

Proof

  • pointwise construction of left adjoints

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an additive category Note that the yoneda embedding preserves limits, hence by product preserving functor out of an additive category lifts to commutative groups, hence we get a fully faithful inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathcal{A} \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Sp}_{\geq 0}) \end{align*}

where we implicitly use recognition principle for connective spectra and commutative groups.

Then define $\mathrm{St}(\mathcal{A})$ as smallest stable subcategory containing the image of the yoneda embedding.
