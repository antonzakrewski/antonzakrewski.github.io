Proof

consider $l_0$ (l0 space of sequences) with the Supremumsnorm. Then consider the map

\begin{align*} T: (x_n) \rightarrow ((1 - \frac{1}{n+1}) x_n) \end{align*}

it is linear, since it is the (categorical) product of linear maps and we claim that its Operatornorm is $1$:

\begin{align*} \frac{\|T(e_i) \| }{\| e_i \|} = \frac{(1 - \frac{1}{n+1}}{1} \xrightarrow{n \rightarrow \infty} 1 \end{align*}

where $e_i \coloneqq (\delta_{i,n})_n$, in other words $\| T \| \geq 1$. But we claim that $\frac{\|T(x_n) \| }{\| x_n \|} < 1$ for each $x \in l_0 \setminus \{0\}$:

Let $x \neq 0$ and w.l.o.g. assume that $x_m \neq 0$. Then

\begin{align} \| T(x) \| =& \sum_{n \neq m} (1 - \frac{1}{n+1}) \vert x_n \vert + \frac{1}{m+1} \vert x_m \vert \\ \leq& \sum_{n \neq m} \vert x_n \vert + \frac{1}{m+1} \vert x_m \vert \\ <& \sum_{n \neq m} \vert x_n \vert + \vert x_m \vert \\ =& \| x \| \end{align}

which shows $\| T \| \leq 1$ and more importantly, that there does not exist an $x$ such that $\| T(x) \| = \frac{\| T \|}{\| x \|}$
