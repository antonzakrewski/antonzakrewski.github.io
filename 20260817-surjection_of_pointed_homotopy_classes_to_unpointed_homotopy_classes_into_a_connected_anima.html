surjection of pointed homotopy classes to unpointed homotopy classes into a connected anima

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be pointed anima, such that $Y$ is also connected. Then the forgetful map

\begin{align*} \pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,Y)) \rightarrow \pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,Y)) \end{align*}

is a surjection

Proof

use the fiber sequence of mapping anima, pointed mapping anima and target $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,Y) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,Y) \rightarrow Y$ and the resulting exact sequence of sets $\pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,Y)) \rightarrow \pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,Y)) \rightarrow *$. (cf. exact sequence of pointed sets or so (at least chris topo 2, 5.2.7 3) shows this)
