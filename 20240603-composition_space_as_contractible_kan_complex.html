composition space as contractible kan complex

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $f,g \in \mathcal{C}_1$ two \(1\)-simplices.

Then the Composition space

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Comp}_{\mathcal{C}}(f,g) \end{align*}

is a contractible Kan complex
