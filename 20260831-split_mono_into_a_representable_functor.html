split epi out of a representable functor in an idempotent complete category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an idempotent complete category and $f: よ(x) \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal{F}$ a split epi in the presheaf category out of a representable. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is also a representable functor.

Proof

Observe that this split is characterized by an idempotent morphism $\eta: よ(x) \rightarrow よ(x)$, which comes from an idempotent morphism $\eta: x \rightarrow x$. By assumption, this idempotent splits, i.e. there exists an $f': x \twoheadrightarrow y$ (and a split mono), which exhibits $y$ as (absolute) limit. Then the yoneda embedding preserves this limit (either because it is absolute or because the yoneda embedding preserves limits) and $f: よ(x) \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal{F}$ is similarly also a limit. In other words, $\mathcal{F} \siemq よ(y)$.
