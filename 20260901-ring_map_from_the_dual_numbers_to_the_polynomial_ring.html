ring map from the dual numbers to the polynomial ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, then the map

\begin{align*} \varphi: R[\varepsilon] \rightarrow& R[\varepsilon][t] \\ a + u \varepsilon \mapsto&a + u \varepsilon t \end{align*}

is a ring-homomorphism

Proof

observe that it is \(R\)-linear. Let $a + u \varepsilon, a' + u' \varepsilon \in R[\varepsilon]$. Then

\begin{align*} \varphi(a + u \varepsilon) \cdot \varphi(a' + u' \varepsilon) =& (a + u \varepsilon t) \cdot (a' + u' \varepsilon t) \\ =& a a' + (au' + a'u \varepsilon)t \\ =& \varphi(a a' + (au' + a'u \varepsilon)) \\ =& \varphi((a + u \varepsilon) \cdot (a' + u' \varepsilon)) \end{align*}
