destabilization of a spherical fibration

Definition

Let $\pi: E \rightarrow B$ a spherical fibration. Then a destabilizaiton of $\pi$ is another spherical fibration $\pi': E' \rightarrow B$ where

\begin{align*} \sigma^k(\pi') \cong \pi \end{align*}
