biadditive functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}, \mathcal{C}$ be additive categories and $\mathcal{A} \times \mathcal{B}$ the product category Then a bifunctor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \times \mathcal{B} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

is said to be biadditive, if for each object $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{A}), B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{B})$ the functors

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(A,-), \mathcal{F}(-,B) \end{align*}

are additive functors
