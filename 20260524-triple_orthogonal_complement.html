triple orthogonal complement

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{H}$ be a prehilbert space and $M \subseteq \mathcal{H}$. Then $M^{\bot} = ((M^{\bot})^{\bot})^{\bot}$.

Proof

$\subseteq$

follows from double orthogonal complement for setting $N = M^{\bot}$ and $N^{\bot} \subseteq (N^{\bot})^{\bot}$.

$\supseteq$

Let $m \in M^{\bot}$ be fixed, then $\langle m,n\rangle = 0$ for each $n \in (M^{\bot})^{\bot}$ . This shows $m \in ((M^{\bot})^{\bot})^{\bot}$.
