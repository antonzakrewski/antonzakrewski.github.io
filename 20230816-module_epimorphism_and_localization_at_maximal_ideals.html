module epimorphism and localization at maximal ideals

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $M,N$ an \(R\)-modules. Suppose $\varphi: M \rightarrow N$ is a module homomorphism. TFAE:

  1. $\varphi$ is a epimorphism
  2. $\varphi_{\mathfrak{p}}: M_{\mathfrak{p}} \rightarrow N_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is a epimorphism for $\mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm{Spec}(R)$
  3. $\varphi_{\mathfrak{m}}: M_{\mathfrak{m}} \rightarrow N_{\mathfrak{m}}$ is a epimorphism for $\mathfrak{m} \in \mathrm{Spec}_{\mathrm{max}}(R)$
