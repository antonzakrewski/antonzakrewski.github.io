mollifier lemma for convolution

Proposition

Let $p \in [1, \infty]$, $\Omega \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$ open and $f \in L_c^p(\Omega)$ be a compactly supported function. Let $j \in C_c^{\infty}(\Omega)$ with $\int_{\Omega} \mathrm{d}x \vert j \vert = \| j \|_1 = 1, j \geq 0$. Then there exists an $\varepsilon_0$ such that for all $\varepsilon \in (0, \varepsilon_0)$, $f_{\varepsilon} \coloneqq j_{\varepsilon} \star f$ lies in $C_{c}^{\infty}(\Omega)$ and

\begin{align*} \| f_{\varepsilon} \|_p \leq \| f \|_p \end{align*}

Proof

Recall that $f_{\varepsilon}(x) = \int_{\Omega} \mathrm{d} y j_{\varepsilon}(x - y) f(y)$ Now write $f_{\varepsilon} = f \star j_{\varepsilon}$ (cf. convolution is commutative) and let $r = p, q = 1$.

The convolution estimate in L^p says that $\| f_{\varepsilon} \|_p = \| f \star j_{\varepsilon} \|_p \leq \|f \|_{p} \cdot \| j_{\varepsilon} \|_q = \| f \star j_{\varepsilon} \|_p \leq \|f \|_{p} \cdot 1 = \| f \star j_{\varepsilon} \|_p \leq \|f \|_{p}$.
