presheaf cokernel

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a category, such that cokernels exist, $\mathcal{F}_1,\mathcal{F}_2$ A-valued presheaf on a topological space $(X, \mathcal{T}_X)$ and $\varphi: \mathcal{F}_1 \rightarrow \mathcal{F}_2$ a morphism of sheafs Then the presheaf cokernel of $\varphi$ is defined as presheaf with

\begin{align*} \mathrm{coker}(\varphi)(U) \coloneqq \mathrm{coker(\varphi_U) \end{align*}
