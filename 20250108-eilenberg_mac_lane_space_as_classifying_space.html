eilenberg mac lane space as classifying space

Proposition

Let $G$ be an abelian group, $K(G,1)$ the Eilenberg-MacLane space. Then $K(G,1)$ together with the universal cover is a classifying space of $G$ regarded as discrete topological group.

Proof

  • universal cover and homotopy groups
  • weakly contractible CW complex as contractible cw complex
  • weakly contractible principal g-bundle as universal bundle on CW Complexes
