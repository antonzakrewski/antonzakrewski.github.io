Proposition

Let $X \cong \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ be an affine scheme. Then it is quasi-separated

Proof

Let $D(\mathfrak{a}) = \bigcup_{f \in \mathfrak{a}} D(f) \subseteq \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ be open Then it is compact if and only if $D(\mathfrak{a}) = \bigcup_{i=1}^n D(f_i) = D((f_1,...,f_n))$ i.e. finitely generated radical ideal.
