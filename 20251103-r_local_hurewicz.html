R-local hurewicz

Proposition

Let $R\subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ be a subring and $X$ a connected simple Anima. Suppose $\pi_*(X) \otimes R = 0$ for $1 \leq * \leq n-1$ and $n \geq 2$.

Then

  1. $H_*(X;R) = 0$ for $1 \leq * \leq n-1$
  2. $\pi_n(X) \otimes R \rightarrow H_n(X;R)$
  3. $\pi_{n+1}(X) \otimes R \twoheadrightarrow H_{n+1}(X;R)$

Proof

consider the R-localization of Anima $X \rightarrow X_R$ which has the property

\begin{align*} \pi_*(X_R) = \pi_*(X) \otimes R = 0 \end{align*}
