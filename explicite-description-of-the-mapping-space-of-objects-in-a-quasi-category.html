explicit description of the mapping space of objects in a quasicategory

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $x,y \in \mathcal{C}_0$.

Then the mapping space $\mathcal{C}(x,y)$ is given by elements

\begin{align*} f \in \mathcal{C}(x,y)_n \end{align*}

with

\begin{align*} f: \Delta^n \times \Delta^1 \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

such that

  1. $\Delta^n \times \{0\} \rightarrow \Delta^n \times \Delta^1 \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is given by $x$ and
  2. $\Delta^n \times \{1\} \rightarrow \Delta^n \times \Delta^1 \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is given by $y$
  3. each edge of $\Delta^n \times \{0\}, \Delta^n \times \{1\}$ is the identity
