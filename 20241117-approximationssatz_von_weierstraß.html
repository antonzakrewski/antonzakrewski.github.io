Approximationssatz von Stone-Weierstraß für kompakte räume

Proposition

Let $X$ be compact and $B \subseteq C(X, \mathbb{K})$ a subalgebra with the following properties:

  1. $1 \in B$, i.e., the multiplicative unit
  2. $B$ separates points
  3. $C(X, \mathbb{K})$ is closed under complex conjugation (necessary/nontrivial if $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{C}$)

Then $B$ is dense with respect to the supremems norm

Proof
