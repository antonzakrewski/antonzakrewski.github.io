functor from an abelian category to Ab as conservative functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a small abelian category and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab} \end{align*}

be the exact functor from abelian category to Ab Then $\mathcal{F}$ is conservative.

Proof

corollary of:

  • forgetful functor from RMod to Ab as conservative functor
  • composition of conservative functors as conservative functor
  • Mitchell's embedding theorem
  • fully faithful functor as conservative functor
