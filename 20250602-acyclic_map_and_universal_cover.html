acyclic map and universal cover

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a morphism of anima.

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an acyclic map
  2. for the universal covers $\tilde{Y} \rightarrow Y, \tilde{X} \rightarrow X$ the map
\begin{align*} \tilde{f}: \tilde{Y} \rightarrow \tilde{X} \end{align*}

is an isomorphism on singular homology.

universal cover functor

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
