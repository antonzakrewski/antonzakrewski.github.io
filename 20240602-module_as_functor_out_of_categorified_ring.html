module as functor out of categorified ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring. Then there exists a correspondence between

  1. \(R\)-modules $M$
  2. functors $\mathrm{Fun}(BR, \mathrm{Ab})$ where $BR$ is the categorification of a ring and $\mathrm{Ab}$ is Category Ab
