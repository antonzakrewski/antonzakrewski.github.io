reedy cofibrant cosimplical sSet

Definition

Let $X: \Delta \rightarrow \mathrm{sSet}$ be a cosimplicial simplicial Set. Then $X$ is said to be reedy cofibrant, if the realization functor

\begin{align*} \| \cdot \|_X \end{align*}

takes the boundary inclusion

\begin{align*} \partial \Delta^n \rightarrow \Delta^n \end{align*}

to a cofibration
