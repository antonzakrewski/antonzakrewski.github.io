suspension of an acyclic space is contractible

Proposition

Let $X$ be an acyclic space. Then $\Sigma X$ is contractible

Proof

Observe that $X$ is connected (cf. acyclic space is connected). Hence $\Sigma X$ is simply connected (cf. pi_1 of a suspension of a path connected space).

Moreover, $\tilde{H}_*(X) \simeq \tilde{H}_{*+1}(\Sigma X)$ so $\Sigma X$ is simply connected and acyclic. Therefore nilpotent, acyclic space is contractible applies.
