epimorphisms are equivalences in a stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category, then any epimorphism is an equivalence

Proof

Let $A \twoheadrightarrow B$ be an epimorphism, then 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
A \rar \dar & B \dar{\id} \\
B \rar & B
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

is a pushout square by assumption. Hence also a pullback and by square with parallel equivalences is bicartesian implies that $A \rightarrow B$ is equivalent to $B \simeq B$, hence also an isomorphism.
