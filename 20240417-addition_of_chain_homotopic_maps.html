addition of chain homotopic maps

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes. Suppose $X_*, Y_*$ are chain complexes and $f_1,f_2, g_1,g_2: X_* \rightarrow Y_*$ are chain maps such that

\begin{align*} f_1 \sim f_2 \\ g_1 \sim g_2 \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} f_1 + g_1 \sim f_2 + g_2 \end{align*}

Proof

Consider $h_n^g, h_n^f$ as chain homotopies. Then let $H \coloneqq h_n^g + h_n^f$ and thus

\begin{align*} d_{n+1} \circ H_n + H_{n-1} \circ d_n =& d_{n+1} \circ (h_n^g + h_n^f) + (h_{n-1}^g + h_{n-1}^f) d_n \\ =& d_{n+1} h_n^g + d_{n+1} h_n^f + h_{n-1}^g d_n + h_{n-1}^f d_n \\ =& \left(d_{n+1} h_n^g + h_{n-1}^g d_n \right) + \left(d_{n+1} h_n^f + h_{n-1}^f d_n \right) \\ =& (g_1 - g_2) + (f_1 - f_2) \\ =& (g_1 + f_1) - (g_2 + f_2) \end{align*}
