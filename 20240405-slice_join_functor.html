left coslice join functor

Definition

Let $X$ be a simplicial set. Then the left coslice join functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} - \star X: \mathrm{sSet} \rightarrow& X / \mathrm{sSet} \\ Y \mapsto& (\iota: X \rightarrow Y \star X) \\ (f: Y \rightarrow Y') \mapsto& (f: Y \star X \rightarrow Y' \star X) \end{align*}

where

  1. $X \star Y$ is the join of a simplicial set
  2. $\iota: X \rightarrow X \star Y$ is the right inclusion into the join of simplicial sets
  3. $f$ is the left induced morphism between joins
