Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a dualizable category, then the essential image of the functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{cofib}( \widehat{よ} \Rightarrow よ): \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is in general not equivalent to the continuous calkin category.

Proof

Let $R$ be a commutative ring with $K_{-1}(R) \neq 0$. Suppose $\mathcal{D}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{Calk}^{\mathrm{contin}}_{\kappa}$ were essentially surjective. Then $K_0(\mathcal{D}(R))$ vanishes by the eilenberg's swindle and $K_0(\mathcal{D}(R)) \rightarrow K_0(\mathrm{Calk}^{\mathrm{contin}}_{\kappa})(\mathcal{D}(R))$ is surjective, since $\mathcal{D}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{Calk}^{\mathrm{contin}}_{\kappa})(\mathcal{D}(R)$ is essentially surjective.
