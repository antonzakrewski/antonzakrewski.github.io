compact limit in a stable infinity category commutes with colimits

Proposition

Let $X$ be a compact anima and $\mathcal{C}$ a stable infinity category. Assume that $\mathcal{C}$ admits $X$ shaped limits

Then right kan extension

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}(-) = r_*: \mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

preserves arbitrary (!) colimit
