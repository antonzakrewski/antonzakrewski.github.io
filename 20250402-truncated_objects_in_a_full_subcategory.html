truncated objects in a full subcategory

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $\mathcal{D}_{\leq n} \subseteq \mathcal{C}_{\leq n}$ be the subcategory of n-truncated objects Let $x \in \mathcal{C}$ be an object with $x \in \mathcal{D}_{\leq n}$.

Then $x \in \mathcal{C}_{\leq n}$ is also \(n\)-truncated in $\mathcal{C}$.
