symmetric monoidal structure of quasi-coherent modules

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor, then the category of quasi-coherent modules admits a canonical symmetric monoidal structure

Proof

follows from

  • extension of scalars is symmetric monoidal, hence the functor $\mathrm{CRing}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{op}}$ lifts to $\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}^L)^{\mathrm{op}}$.

now the forgetful functor from CAlg preserves limits.
