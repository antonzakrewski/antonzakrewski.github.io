functor category of cocartesian preserving sections

Definition

Let $p: \mathcal{U} \rightarrow I$ be a functor Then the functor category of cocartesian preserving sections is defined as full subcategory of the functor category of sections

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}_{I}^{\mathrm{cocart}}(I, \mathcal{U}) \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}_I(I, \mathcal{U}) \end{align*}

containing functors, which map morphisms in $I$ to p-cocartesian morphisms.

note that this category may be nonempty
