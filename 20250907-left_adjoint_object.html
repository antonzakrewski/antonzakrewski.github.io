left adjoint object

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a functor. Let $x \in \mathcal{C}$. Then $y \in \mathcal{D}$ is said to be a left adjoint object to $x$ under $\mathcal{G}$ if there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(y,-) \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x, \mathcal{G}(-)) \end{align*}
