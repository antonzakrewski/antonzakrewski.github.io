Fernbahnhof theorem for exact functors

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes. Suppose $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{A}$ is an exact functor and $\mathcal{F}^*$ the induced functor on chain complexes (cf. exact functor as additive functor)

Then $\mathcal{F}$ commutes with the homology functor

Proof

follows, as $\mathcal{F}^*$ resp. $\mathcal{F}$ preserve kernels / cokernels and hence commute with the homology functor
