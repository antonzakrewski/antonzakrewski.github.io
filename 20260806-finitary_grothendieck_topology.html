kappa finitary grothendieck topology

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \tau)$ be a site and $\kappa$ a regular cardinal. Then it is called a $\kappa$ finitary grothendieck topology, if there exists a Grothendieck pretopology $\pi$ such that the coverings in $\pi$ are all $\kappa$ small and the generated topology is $\tau$.
