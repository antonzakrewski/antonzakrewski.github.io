base change functor is a right adjoint

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a finitely complete category, $X,Y$ objects and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a map. Then the base change functor is a right adjoint.

Remark

for $Y = *$, this is then a functor $\mathcal{C}_{/*} \simeq \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{X}$
