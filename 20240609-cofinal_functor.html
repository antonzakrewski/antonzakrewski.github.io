1-initial functor

Definition

An initial functor is a functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ such that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{op}}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}

is a final functor

Explicite

If for every object $d \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{D})$ the comma category $\mathcal{F} / d$ is
