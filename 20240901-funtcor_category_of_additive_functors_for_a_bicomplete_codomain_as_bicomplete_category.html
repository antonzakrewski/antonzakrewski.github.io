funtcor category of additive functors for a bicomplete codomain as bicomplete category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be Ab-enriched categories, where $\mathcal{B}$ is a bicomplete category Then the functor category of additive functors

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}) \end{align*}

is also bicomplete

Proof

  • functor category of additive functors for a cocomplete codomain as cocomplete category
  • functor category of additive functors for a complete codomain as complete category
