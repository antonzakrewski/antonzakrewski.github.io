universal cohomological delta functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, $\{\mathcal{RF}^n\}_n$ a cohomological delta functor. then $\{\mathcal{RF}^n\}$ is said to be universal, if for any other cohomological delta functor $\{\mathcal{G}^n\}$ every natural transformation

\begin{align*} \mathcal{RF}^0 \rightarrow \mathcal{G}^0 \end{align*}

ehtends uniquely to a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \mathcal{RF}^n \rightarrow \mathcal{G}^n \end{align*}
