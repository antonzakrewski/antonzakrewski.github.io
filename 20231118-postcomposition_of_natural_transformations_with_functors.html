right whiskering

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be categories, $\mathcal{G}_1,\mathcal{G}_2: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ be functors.

Then for a natural transformation $\eta: \mathcal{G}_1 \rightarrow \mathcal{G}_2$, right whiskering of $\eta$ with $\mathcal{F}$ is also canonically a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}^{ \leftarrow} (\eta): \mathcal{F} \circ \mathcal{G}_1 \rightarrow& \mathcal{F} \circ \mathcal{G}_2 \\ A \mapsto& \mathcal{F}(\eta(A)) \end{align*}
