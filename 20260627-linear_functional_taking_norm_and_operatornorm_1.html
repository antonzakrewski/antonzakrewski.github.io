Proposition

Let $X$ be a normed vector space and $x \in X$ with $x \neq 0$. Then there exists an $f \in X^*$ with $f(x) = \| x \|$ and $\| f\|_{X^*} = 1$.

Proof

follows from the bounded linear extension theorem: observe that $\mathrm{span}(x) \cong \mathbb{K}$ and consider the linear map

\begin{align*} f: \mathrm{span}(x) \rightarrow& \mathbb{K} \\ # \lambda \cdot x \mapsto \lambda \| x \| \end{align*}

which has norm $1$ now bounded linear extension theorem
