functor reflecting colimits

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to reflect colimits of a shape $\mathcal{J}$ if

  1. for a diagram $J: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ and an object $C \in \mathcal{C}$
  2. a natural transformation $\eta: J \Rightarrow \mathrm{const}_{C}$
  3. the induced diagram $\mathcal{F} \circ J: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ and the induced natural transformation by right whiskering $\mathcal{F}(\eta)$ are a colimit

Then so was $J: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ with $\eta$
