weak semiorthogonal decomposition closed under retracts

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category with a weak semiorthogonal decomposition $\mathcal{A} \perp \mathcal{B}$. Then both $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ are closed under retracts

Proof

corollary of:

  • weak semiorthogonal decomposition is determined by one half
  • weak semiorthogonal decomposition closed under retracts
