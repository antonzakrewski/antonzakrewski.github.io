isolated prime ideal and localization

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ an ideal,

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a} = \bigcap_{i=1}^n \mathfrak{q}_i \end{align*}

be a minimal primary decomposition.

Suppose $\mathfrak{p}_i \coloneqq \sqrt{\mathfrak{q}_i}$ is an isolated prime ideal, then

\begin{align*} (\mathfrak{a}^{e})^c = \mathfrak{q}_i \end{align*}

for the localization

\begin{align*} \iota: A \rightarrow A_{\mathfrak{p}_i} \end{align*}
