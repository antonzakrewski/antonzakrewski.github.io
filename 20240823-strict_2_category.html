strict 2-category

Definition

a strict \(2\)-category is a category enriched over Category Cat with the cartesian closed monoidal structure

Explicite

A strict \(2\) category $\mathcal{C}$ contains

  1. an ordinary category $\mathcal{C}_{\vert 1}$, i.e. objects and morphisms
  2. for objects $x,y \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ an ordinary category $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y)$ where a) the objects are the \(1\)-morphisms given by $\mathcal{C}_1$ b) the morphisms in $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y)$ are the \(2\)-morphisms
  3. composition in $\mathcal{C}_{\vert 1}$ is a functor, i.e.
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(y,z) \times \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,z) \end{align*}
