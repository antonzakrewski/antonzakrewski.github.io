diagonal functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, then the diagonal functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \Delta: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C} \\ f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,B) \mapsto& (f,f) \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C}}((A,A),(B,B)) \\ A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C}) \mapsto (A,A) \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
