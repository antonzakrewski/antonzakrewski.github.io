sheaves on a finitary topology are closed under filtered colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $\tau$ a finitary grothendieck topology. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sh}_{\tau}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

is closed under $\kappa$-filtered colimits

Proof

this follows as filtered colimits commute with finite limits in Set: The descent condition for the grothendieck topology associated to a pretopology shows that this is only a condition
