coinitial infinity functor

Definition

Let $f: K \rightarrow L$ be a morphism of simplicial sets. Then $f$ is said to be coinitial, if for all infinity category $\mathcal{C}$ and infinity functor $p: L \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ the induced map on <>

\begin{align*} f: \mathcal{C}^{/p} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{/pf} \end{align*}

is a joyal equivalence

Then $f$ is said to be cofinal if for all left fibration $p: X \rightarrow Y$ the induced morphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}_{\mathrm{id}}(L,X) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}_f(K,X) \end{align*}

is a joyal equivalence
