continuous maps with supremumsmetric is complete

Proposition

Let $X$ be a compact hausdorff space and $C(X)$ the space of continuous maps with the supremumsmetric. Then $C(X)$ is a complete metric space.

Proof

follows from:

  • Satz vom Minimum und Maximum, i.e., $C(X) = C_b(X)$.
  • bounded continuous functions are a complete metric space
