postcomposition with a bousfield localization induces localization on functor categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $K$ a simplicial set Then a left bousfield resp. right bousfield localization $\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{D}$ induces a left / resp. right bousfield localization

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(K,\mathcal{C}) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(K,\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

Proof

  • precomposition with an infinity adjoint as adjoint on functor categories
  • fully faithful infinity right adjoint and counit as equivalence / fully faithful infinity left adjoint and unit as equivalence
