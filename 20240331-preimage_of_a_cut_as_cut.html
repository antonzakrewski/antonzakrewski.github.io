Proposition

Let $J,J'$ be strict total ordered sets and $\varphi: J \rightarrow J'$ a monotone map. Suppose $(J_1',J_2') \in \mathrm{Cut}(J')$ is a cut, then so is

\begin{align*} (\varphi^{-1}[J_1'], \varphi^{-1}[J_2']) \in \mathrm{Cut}(J) \end{align*}

Proof
