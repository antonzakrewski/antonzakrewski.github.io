Homology of a chopped short exact sequence

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an additive category and 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
0 \arrow[]{r}{} & A \arrow[]{r}{f} & B \arrow[]{r}{g} & C \arrow[]{r}{} 0
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

a short exact sequence

Then it follows for the homology that

is also short exact

In particular,

\begin{align*} H(0 \rightarrow B \rightarrow C) \cong& A \\ H(A \rightarrow B \rightarrow 0) \cong& B \end{align*}

up to unique isomorphism

Proof

follows from

\begin{align*} H(0 \rightarrow B \rightarrow C) =& \mathrm{ker}(g)/\mathrm{im}(0) \\ \cong& \mathrm{ker}(g) \end{align*}

(see: image of a zero morphism, quotient with a zero object) resp.

\begin{align*} H(A \rightarrow B \rightarrow 0) = \mathrm{ker}(0)/\mathrm{im}(f) \\ =& B/\mathrm{im}(f) \\ \cong& \mathrm{coker}(f) \end{align*}

(see: isomorphic kernel and zero morphism, exactification of a homomorphism)

The isomorphism follows from universal property unique up to unique isomorphism
