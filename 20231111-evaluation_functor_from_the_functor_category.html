evaluation functor from the functor category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ the functor category and $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ an object. Then the evaluation functor $\mathrm{ev}_{A}: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ev}_A: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow& \mathcal{D} \\ (\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}) \mapsto& \mathcal{F}(A) \\ (\eta: \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{G}) \mapsto& \eta(A): \mathcal{F}(A) \rightarrow \mathcal{G}(A) \end{align*}

Proof

identity

for an object $\mathcal{F} \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}))$, the identity morphism is the identity natural transformation and hence by definition it holds, that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ev}_A(\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{F}} = \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{F}(A)}: \mathcal{F}(A) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(A) \end{align*}

commuting

follows from composition of natural transformations as natural transformation

where $\eta' \circ \eta$ is defined as

\begin{align*} (\eta' \circ \eta)(A) = \eta'(A) \circ \eta(A) \end{align*}
