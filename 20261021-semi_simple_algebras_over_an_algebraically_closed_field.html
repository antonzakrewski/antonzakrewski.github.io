characterization semi-simple algebras over an algebraically closed field

Proposition

Let $A$ be a finite dimension K-Algebra TFAE:

  1. $A$ is semi-simple algebra
  2. $\mathrm{dim}(A) = \sum_{V_i} n_i^2$
  3. $A \cong \bigoplus \mathrm{Mat}_{n_i}(k)$
  4. every finite dimensional representation of $A$ is semi-simple representation
  5. the regular representation of $A$ is semi-simple

Proof

  • schur lemma over algebraically closed fields
  • characterization of semi-simple algebras
