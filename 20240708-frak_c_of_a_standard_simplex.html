coherent unnerving of a standard simplex

Definition

Let $\mathfrak{c}(\Delta^n)$ be the cosimplicial 2-category of simplices, then the coherent unnerving is defined as

\begin{align*} N_*(\mathfrak{c}(\Delta^n)) \in \mathrm{Cat}_{\Delta} \end{align*}

for lax monoidal functor induces a 2-functor on category of of enriched functors
