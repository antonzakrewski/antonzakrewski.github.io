cofibration in the projective model structure as pointwise cofibration

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}, \mathrm{Cof}, \mathrm{Fib})$ be a model category, and $\mathrm{Fun}(I, \mathcal{C})_{\mathrm{proj}}$ the projective model structure. Then any cofibration in the projective model structure is also pointwise a cofibration
