monoidal functor induces functor on categories of monoid objects

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}}), (\mathcal{W}, \otimes_{\mathcal{W}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{W}})$ be monoidal categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{V} \rightarrow \mathcal{W}$ a monoidal functor Then $\mathcal{F}$ induces a functor on the categories of monoid objects
