infinite extension of scalars

Definition

Let $f: R \rightarrow S$ be a morphism of ring spectrum. Then the extension of scalars functor $\otimes_{R} S$ is defined as infinity left adjoint to the infinite restriction of scalars.

Proof

  • infinity right adjoint functor theorem for presentable categories
  • category of module spectra as presentable category
  • infinite restriction of scalars preserves limits & infinite restriction of scalars preserves colimits
