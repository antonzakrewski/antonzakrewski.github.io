open euclidean set covered by a countable subcover

Proposition

Let $O \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$ be an open subset of the euclidean space and $U_i$ an open cover Then there exists a countable subcover.

Proof

by open euclidean subset as cw complex with cells smaller than dimension epsilon

\begin{align*} O = \bigcup_{n \in \mathbb{N}} K_n \end{align*}

is given as union of countably many compact spaces.

Then for each $K_n$ we may choose a finite subcover. The union of those subcovers results in a countable cover of $O$
