Cocone

Definition

A Cocone is a cone in the opposite category

Explicite

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $J: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a diagram. A cocone from $N$ to $J$ is an object $N \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and a class of morphisms

\begin{align*} \varphi_X: J(X) \rightarrow N \end{align*}
