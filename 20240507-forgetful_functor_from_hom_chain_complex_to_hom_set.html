forgetful functor from hom chain complex to hom set

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, then the forgetful functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Set}(\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(-,-)): \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{Ab})^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{Ab}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} (X,Y) \mapsto \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Y)_0 = \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})}(X,Y) \end{align*}
