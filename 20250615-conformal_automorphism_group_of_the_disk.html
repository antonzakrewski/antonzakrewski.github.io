conformal automorphism group of the disk

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{D}$ be the open unit ball, then the conformal automorphism group is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{D}) = \{ e^{\mathrm{i} \theta} \psi_{\alpha} : \theta \in \mathbb{R}, \alpha \in \mathbb{D}\} \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} \psi_{\alpha}(z) = \frac{\alpha - z }{1 - \overline{a}z} \end{align*}

Proof

$\subseteq$

  • blaschke Abbildung als involution
  • blaschke abbildung
  • multiplication with a rotation is also a conformal map

$\supseteq$

Let $f \in \mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{D})$ be a conformal map. Let $0 \coloneqq f^{-1}\{0\}$ and

\begin{align*} g \coloneqq f \circ \psi_{\alpha} \end{align*}

Then it follows that $\psi_{\alpha}(0) =& \alpha$ and $g(0) = 0$. So by schwarz lemma

\begin{align*} \vert g(z) \vert \leq \vert z \vert \end{align*}

for all $z \in \mathbb{D}$

moreover $g(0) = 0$ implies $0 = g^{-1}(0)$ so using again the schwarz lemma we get

\begin{align*} \vert g^{-1}(w) \vert \leq \vert w \vert \end{align*}

for all $w \in \mathbb{D}$, i.e.

\begin{align*} \vert z \vert \leq \vert g(z) \vert \end{align*}

Hence by the uniqueness part of the schwarz lemma we get $g(z) = e^{\mathrm{i} \theta} z$ for some $\theta \in \mathbb{R}$ and all $z \in \mathbb{D}$, i.e.

\begin{align*} f(\psi_\alpha(z)) = e^{\mathrm{i} \theta} z \end{align*}

Now using blaschke Abbildung als involution we get

\begin{align*} f(z) =& f(\psi_\alpha(z)^2) \\ =& g \circ \psi_\alpha(z) \\ =& e^{i \theta} \psi_\alpha(z) \end{align*}
