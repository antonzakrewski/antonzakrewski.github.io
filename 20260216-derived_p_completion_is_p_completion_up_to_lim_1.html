E_p of the derived p-completion is p-completion up to lim^1

Proposition

Let $A$ be an abelian group, then there exists a short exact sequence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}^1(A_n) \rightarrow \mathbb{E}_p(A) \rightarrow \widehat{A}_p \end{align*}

where $A_n = \mathrm{ker}(A \rightarrow A/p^n A)$.

see:

  • lim-lim1 for abelian groups
  • E_p of an abelian group
