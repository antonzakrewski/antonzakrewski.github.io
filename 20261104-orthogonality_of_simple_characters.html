orthogonality of simple characters

Proposition

Let $V,W$ be simple representations of a finite group $G$. Then

\begin{align*} \langle \chi_{V}, \chi_{W} \rangle = \begin{cases} 1 & \mbox{if } V \cong W \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from schur lemma over algebraically closed fields & schur lemma. here for simple $\mathrm{Hom}_{G}(V,W) \cong \mathbb{C}$ if $V$ and $W$ are isomorphic (they are always scalars).
