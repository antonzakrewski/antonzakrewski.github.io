zariski sheaf maps coproducts in Aff to coproducts

Proposition

Let $X: \mathrm{CAlg}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ be a zariski sheaf. Then $X$ maps coproducts in $\mathrm{CAlg}^{\mathrm{op}}$ to products in category set.

\begin{align*} X(R \times S) \cong& X(R) \times X(S) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • codisjoint bicartesian square in CRing
  • descent along a sieve generated by two subobjects

where $(R \rightarrow R \times S, S \rightarrow R \times S$ gene
