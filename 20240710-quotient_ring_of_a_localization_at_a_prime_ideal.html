quotient ring of a localization at a maximal ideal

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{p}$ a maximal ideal. Then the quotient ring of the localization of a ring at a prime ideal $A_{\mathfrak{p}}/(\mathfrak{p})$ is given by

\begin{align*} A/\mathfrak{p} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from

\begin{align*} \varphi: A_{(\mathfrak{p})}/\mathfrak{p} \rightarrow A/\mathfrak{p} \frac{\alpha}{\beta} \mapsto \alpha \cdot \beta^{-1} \end{align*}

where by assumption $\beta \not\in \mathfrak{p}$
