equivalences of a colimit preserving functor between presentable categories is of small generation

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a colimit preserving functor between presentable categories Then the class of morphisms in $\mathcal{C}$ which get send to equivalences in $\mathcal{D}$ is of <small generation>

Proof

Let $\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{D}} \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{D})$ be the full subcategory containing equivalences as arrows. It is equivalent to $\mathcal{D}$ (cf. arrow category of equivalences is equivalent to the original category). so consider the pullback

here both legs preserves colimits:

  • postcomposition with colimit preserving functor is colimit preserving
  • constant infinity diagram functor preserves colimits

and all three categories are presentable (c.f functor category into a presentable category is presentable) So use that the forgetful functor from PrL to Cat preserves limits, i.e. the pullback in Cat infinity is presentable

Now the functor $\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{D}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{D})$ is fully faithful (since it is a full subcategory)

Since pullback reflects fully faithful functors we may identify $\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{C}} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{C})$ as full subcategory.

Here the objects consist of those morphisms $c \rightarrow c'$ which after applying $\mathcal{F}$ restrict to $\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{D}}$, i.e. morphisms which are equivalences after applying $\mathcal{F}$.

In particular $\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{C}}$ is presentable, so there exists a small set of kappa compact objects which generate $\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{C}}$

Since the inclusion $\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{C}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{C})$ is one of PrL, so we may use that fully faithful functor reflect colimits to conclude that we may generate $\mathcal{F}^{-1}(S)$ by colimits in $\mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{C})$ via a small set.
