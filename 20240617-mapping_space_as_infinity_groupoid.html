mapping anima as infinity groupoid

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category. Then for $x,y \in \mathcal{C}_0$ the mapping space

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \end{align*}

is an infinity-groupoid

Proof

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}^{\Delta^1} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\partial \Delta^1} \end{align*}

is an inner fibration and conservative functor (cf. precomposition with a bijection on zero simplices as conservative infinity functor + precomposition with a monomorphism as inner fibration for a functor category into an infinity category)

Hence by conservative inner fibrations closed under pullbacks we conclude that the terminal morphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \rightarrow * \end{align*}

is a conservative innerfibration, thus especially also conservative.

Therefore $\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y)$ is an infinity groupoid (cf. infinity groupoid and conservative terminal morphism)
