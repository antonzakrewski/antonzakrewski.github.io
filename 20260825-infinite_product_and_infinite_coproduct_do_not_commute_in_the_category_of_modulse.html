infinite product and infinite coproduct do not commute in the category of modules

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring / $\mathbb{E}_{\infty}$-ring spectrum and $\mathrm{Mod}_R$ the category RMod / category of module spectra. Then the infinite product and infinite coproduct do not commute in general.

Proof

consider $R = \mathbb{Z}$, then the map

\begin{align*} \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \prod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \prod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

is not surjective: An element in $\bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \prod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{Z}$ is given by $m$ many infinite tuples of integers. This gets mapped to the infinite tuple consisting of $m$ many integers. In particular, for any element $(z_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}, z_n \in \bigoplus_{m \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{Z}$ in the image of the map, we get that each $z_n$ lies in $\bigoplus_{n \leq m} \mathbb{Z}$ for some $m$.

Hence the element $(k_n)$ for $k_n = (0,1,...,n) \in \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z}$ does not lie in the image.
