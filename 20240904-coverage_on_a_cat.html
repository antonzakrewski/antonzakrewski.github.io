strong coverage on a category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. Then a coverage on $\mathcal{C}$ is a family $\mathrm{Cov}(\mathcal{C})$ of covering families for each object $U \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ such that

  1. for an isomorphism $f: V \rightarrow U$ the singleton $\{f: V \rightarrow U\} \in \mathrm{Cov}(\mathcal{C})$
  2. if $\{U_i \rightarrow U\} \in \mathrm{Cov}(\mathcal{C})$ and $\{V_{i,j} \rightarrow U_i\} \in \mathrm{Cov}(\mathcal{C})$, then $\{V_{i,j} \rightarrow U\}_{i,j} \in \mathrm{Cov}(\mathcal{C})$
  3. if $\{U_i \rightarrow U\} \in \mathcal{C}$ and $V \rightarrow U$ is a morphism, then the pullback $U_i \times_{U} V$ exists and
\begin{align*} \{U_i \times V \rightarrow V\}_{i \in I} \in \mathrm{Cov}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
