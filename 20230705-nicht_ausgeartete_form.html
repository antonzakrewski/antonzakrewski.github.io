non degenerate form

Definition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $M,N,L \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathrm{RMod})$ \(R\)-modules and

\begin{align*} \varphi: M \times N \rightarrow L \end{align*}

a bilinear map. Then $\varphi$ is said to be non-degenerate, if

\begin{align*} \varphi^M: M^{\mathrm{tf}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{RMod}}(N,L) \\ m \mapsto \varphi(m,-) \\ \varphi^N: N^{\mathrm{tf}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{RMod}}(M,L) \\ n \mapsto \varphi(-,n) \\ \end{align*}

is an isomorphism , where $()^{\mathrm{tf}}$ denotes the torsion quotient
