contractible kan complex and trivial fibration

Proposition

Let $X$ be a simplicial set. TFAE:

  1. $X$ is contractible Kan complex
  2. the terminal morphism $X \rightarrow \Delta^0$ is a trivial fibration

Proof

follows from:

  • kan complex and terminal morphism as kan fibration
  • trivial fibration as kan fibration
  • trivial fibration between kan complexes and homotopy equivalent kan fibration
