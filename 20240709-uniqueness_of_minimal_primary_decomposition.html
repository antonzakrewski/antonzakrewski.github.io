uniqueness of minimal primary decomposition

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a} \subsetneq A$ a strict ideal and

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a} = \bigcap_{i=1}^r \mathfrak{q}_i \end{align*}

a minimal primary decomposition with

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{p}_i \coloneqq \sqrt{\mathfrak{q}_i} \end{align*}

Then the AP is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{AP}(\mathfrak{a}) = \{\mathfrak{p}_i\} \end{align*}

Proof

$\subseteq$

Let $\mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm{AP}(\mathfrak{a})$, i.e. $\mathfrak{p} = \sqrt{(\mathfrak{a} : a)}$ for some $a \in A$. Then it remains to show that $\mathfrak{p} = \mathfrak{p}_i = \sqrt{\mathfrak{q}_i}$, where $\mathfrak{a} = \bigcap \mathfrak{q}_i$

Then we get

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{p} = \sqrt{(\mathfrak{a} : a)} =& \sqrt{ ((\bigcap \mathfrak{q}_i) : a)} \\ =& \sqrt{ \bigcap (\mathfrak{q}_i : a)} \\ =& \bigcap \sqrt{(\mathfrak{q}_i : a)} \end{align*}

By radical of a modul quotient for a primary ideal we get an intersection of some $\mathfrak{p}_i$ with $A$. Note that not all factors $\sqrt{(\mathfrak{q}_i : a)}$ are $A$, as $\mathfrak{p} \subsetneq A$. Hence there exists some factor $\mathfrak{q}_i$ with $a \not\in \mathfrak{q}_i$

Using the prime avoidance theorem we conclude that

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{p} \subseteq \sqrt{\mathfrak{(q_i : a)}} \end{align*}

for some $i$
