schauder basis of convergent nullsequences

Proposition

Let $\{x_n \vert \lim_{n \rightarrow \infty} x = 0\}$ be the space of null sequence with the Supremumsnorm. Then $\{e_n\}$ is a schauder basis.

Proof

Let $x$ be a null sequence and $\varepsilon > 0$. We define $\tau_{\leq n} x = (x_1,x_2,...,x_n,0,0,...)$ as truncation. Then $\tau_{\leq n} x = \sum_{i=1}^n x_i e_i$, i.e., we choose $x_n$ as coefficients. Observe that these $x_n$ are constant over all $\tau_{\leq m}$ for $m \geq n$.
