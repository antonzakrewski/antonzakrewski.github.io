Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a compactly generated category. Then it is compactly assembled category.

Proof

By assumption, $\mathcal{C} \cong \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}_0)$ for some small category. Then $\widehat{よ}$ is equivalently described as applying $\mathrm{Ind}(-)$ to $よ: \mathcal{C}_0 \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}_0)$.
