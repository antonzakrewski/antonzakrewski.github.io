category of infinity sheaves on a topological space

Definition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $\mathcal{C}$ be an bicomplete infinity category. Then the category of infinity sheaves on $X$ is defined as full sub infinity category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Op}(X)^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

satisfying the usual sheaf condition
