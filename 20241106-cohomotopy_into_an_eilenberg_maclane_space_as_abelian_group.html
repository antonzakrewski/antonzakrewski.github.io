hom into an Eilenberg-MacLane space as abelian group

Proposition

Let $K(A,n)$ be the Eilenberg-Maclane space for an abelian group $A$ and $n \geq 1$. Then for a topological space $X$ the unpointed maps up to homotopy

\begin{align*} [X, K(A,n)] \end{align*}

admits an abelian group structure, natural with respect to $X$.

Proof

We have seen

\begin{align*} K(A,n) \cong \Omega\Omega(K(A,n+2)) K(A,n) \cong \Omega(K(A,n+1)) \end{align*}
  • Loop space of an Eilenberg-MacLane Space
  • functor preserving limits preserves group objects

Then this is an based loop space as group in hTop*

by categorical eckmann-hilton arguments this is also an abelian group for
