equivalence and p-cocartesian

Proposition

Let $p: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be an inner fibration between infinity categories and $f: \Delta^1 \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a \(1\)-simplex TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an equivalence
  2. $f$ is p-cocartesian and $p(f)$ is an equivalence
