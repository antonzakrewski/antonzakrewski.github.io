s-periodic algebra and dual

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $L$ a line, $s \in L$ and $M$ an s-periodic module . Then the map $L^{\vee} \rightarrow R^{\vee} \cong R$ induces an isomorphism

\begin{align*} M \otimes_{R} L^{\vee} \rightarrow M \otimes_{R} R^{\vee} \cong M \end{align*}
