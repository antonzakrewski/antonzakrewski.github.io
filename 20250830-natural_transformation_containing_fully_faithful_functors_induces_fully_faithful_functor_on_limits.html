limit of fully faithful functors is fully faithful

Proposition

Let $I$ be an indexing category, $\mathcal{F}_1, \mathcal{F}_2: I \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}$ be functors and $\alpha: \mathcal{F}_1 \rightarrow \mathcal{F}_2$ a natural transformation consisting of pointwise fully faithful functors Then the induced map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}(\alpha): \mathrm{lim} \mathcal{F}_1 \rightarrow \mathrm{lim} \mathcal{F}_2 \end{align*}

is also fully faithful

Proof

follows from:

  • mapping anima in the limit of categories
  • limit functor
