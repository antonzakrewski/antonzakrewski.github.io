compact subset of an open subset of a proper metric space as subset of a countable, compact family

Proposition

Let $\Omega \subseteq M$ be an open subset of a proper metric space $M$. Then there exists countably many compact subsets $K_l \subseteq \Omega$ such that any compact subset $K \subseteq \Omega$ lies in some $K_l$

Proof

Let

\begin{align*} K_{l} \coloneqq \{x\in \Omega : \vert x \vert \leq l, d(x,\Omega^{c}) \geq \frac{1}{l}\} \end{align*}

which is closed and bounded, hence compact as $\mathbb{C}$ is a proper metric space, where $l \in \{ \frac{1}{n}, n \in \mathbb{N}^+ \}$.

Then any compact subset $K$ is closed (cf. closedness of compact sets in Hausdorff spaces) and bounded (cf. Beschränktheit einer kompakten Menge). Now for $x \in K$ we get $d(K, \Omega^{c}) > 0$ by positiver Abstand zu einer Abgeschlosenen Menge.
