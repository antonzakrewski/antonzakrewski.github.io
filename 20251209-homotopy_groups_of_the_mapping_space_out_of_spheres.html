homotopy groups of the mapping space out of a coproduct of spheres

Proposition

Let $X$ be any anima, then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \pi_i(\mathrm{map}(\coprod S^n, X)) \cong \prod \pi_i(X) \times \pi_{i+n}(X) \end{align*}

for $i \geq 2$

Proof

follows from

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}(\coprod S^n, X) \cong \prod \mathrm{map}(S^n,X) \end{align*}

and homotopy group functor preserves products.
