jacobson radical of the quotient to the jacobson radical as zero ideal

Proposition

Let \(A\) be a ring, $\mathcal{J}(A)$ the Jacobson radical. Then the jacobson radical of the quotient ring

\begin{align*} A/\mathcal{J}(A) \end{align*}

is the zero ideal

\begin{align*} \mathcal{J}(A/\mathcal{J}(A) \end{align*}

Proof
