contravariant inner hom as pullback hom

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a closed monoidal category with a terminal object,, then for $X \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and $f: A \rightarrow B$ the morphism

\begin{align*} f^{*}: \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(B,X) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(A,X) \end{align*}

is given by a pullback hom of two morphisms, namely

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(B,X) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(A,X) \times_{\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(A,1)} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(B,1) \end{align*}

where $1$ is the terminal object and

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow Y = 1 \end{align*}

is the terminal morphism

Proof

  • internal hom into a terminal object as terminal object
  • product with the terminal object canonically iso
  • pullback and terminal objects induce finite products ~ ish
