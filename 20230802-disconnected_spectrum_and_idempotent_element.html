disconnected spectrum and idempotent element

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ the zariski spectrum with the zariski topology such that $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ is disconnected. Then there exists an $e \in A \setminus \{0,1\}$ such that

\begin{align*} e^2 = e \end{align*}

Proof
