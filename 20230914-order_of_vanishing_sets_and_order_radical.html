order of vanishing sets and order radical

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a}, \mathfrak{b} \subseteq A$ be (w.l.o.g.) ideals Then it holds for the Vanishing set

\begin{align*} V(\mathfrak{a}) \subseteq V(\mathfrak{b}) \Leftrightarrow \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}} \supseteq \sqrt{\mathfrak{b}} \end{align*}
