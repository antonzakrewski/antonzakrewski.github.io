frobenius formula for ordinary representations

Proposition

Pick a representative $x_{\sigma} \in G$ for every right coset $\sigma \in H \ G$ Let $H \subseteq G$ be a subgroup of a finite group Let $V$ be an \(H\)-representation. Let $\chi$ be the character of $\mathrm{Ind}_{H}^G(V)$.

Then

\begin{align*} \chi(g) = \sum_{\sigma \vert x_{\sigma} g x_{\sigma}^{-1} \in H } \chi_{V}(x_{\sigma} g x_{\sigma}^{-1}) \end{align*}

Proof
