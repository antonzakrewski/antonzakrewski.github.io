affine coordinate ring of a standard open of the affine space as localization

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be an affine variety and $D(f) \subseteq X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^n$ a standard open Then the coordinate ring of an quasi-affine variety at $D(f)$ is given as localization

\begin{align*} \mathcal{A}(D(f)) = \mathcal{A}(X)[\frac{1}{f}] \end{align*}

Proof
