thick subcategory of the fiber/cofiber of a nilpotent self-map contains X

Proposition

let $f: \Sigma^d X \rightarrow X$ be a nilpotent self-map of degree $d$. Then $X$ lies in the thick subcategory of $\mathrm{cofib}(f)$.

Proof

Recall that for any $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\mathrm{cofib}(f^n)$ lies in $\mathrm{Thick}(\mathrm{cofbi}(f^n)$ (cf. cofiber of the power of a self-map lies in the thick subcategory). Now by assumption, there exists an $n$ such that $f^n$ is the zero morphism. Then $\mathrm{cofib}(f^n) \simeq \mathrm{cofib}(0) \simeq \Sigma^{d+1} X \oplus \Sigma^{-(n-1)d} X$ (up to correct shifts) and hence $\Sigma^{d+1} X$ is a retract of an element lying in $\mathrm{Thick}(\mathrm{cofib}(f))$. (cf. cofiber of a nullhomotopic map)
