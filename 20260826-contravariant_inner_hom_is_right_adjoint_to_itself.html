contravariant inner hom is right adjoint to itself

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a closed symmetric monoidal category and $X \in \mathcal{C}$ an object Then the functor $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(,-X): \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is a right adjoint to $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(-,X)^{\mathrm{op}}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}

Proof

follows from

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(B,X)) \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A \otimes B,X) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(B \otimes A,X) \\ \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(B,\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(A,X)) \end{align*}
