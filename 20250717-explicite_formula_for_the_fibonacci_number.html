explicit formula for the fibonacci number

Proposition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then

\begin{align*} F_n = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}( q^n - \widehat{q}^n) \end{align*}

for

\begin{align*} q =& \frac{1+ \sqrt{5}}{2} \\ \widehat{q} =& \frac{1 - \sqrt{5}}{2} \\ \end{align*}

Proof

Let $F = \sum_{n \geq 0}F_n X^n$. Then by definition

\begin{align*} F =& X + \sum_{n\geq 2} (F_{n-1} + F_n-2) X^n\\ =& X + \sum_{n\geq 2} F_{n-1} X^n + \sum_{n \geq 2} F_{n-2} X^n \\ =& X + X \cdot \sum_{n \geq 2} F_{n-1} X^{n-1} + X^2 \sum_{n\geq 2} F_{n-2} X^{n-2} =& X + X \cdot F + X^2 F \end{align*}

Thus

\begin{align*} F = X + XF + X^2 F \end{align*}

where we use $F_0 = 0$ or

\begin{align*} (1 - X - X^2) F = X \end{align*}

or

\begin{align*} F = X \cdot (1 - X - X^2)^{-1} \end{align*}

where the inverse exists by units in the ring of formal power series .

Now the solutions of $1 - z - z^2 = 0$ in $\mathbb{C}$ are given by $-\varhphi, - \widehat{\varhphi}$ or

\begin{align*} 1 - z - z^2 =& - (z + \varhphi) \cdot (z + \widehat{\varhphi}) \end{align*}

Hence

\begin{align*} 1- X - X^2 = - (X + \varhphi)(X + \widehat{\varhphi}) \end{align*}

or

\begin{align*} 1 - X - X^2 =& -\frac{1}{\varhphi \cdot \widehat{\varhphi}} (1 + \frac{X}{\varhphi}) (1 + \frac{X}{\widehat{\varhphi}}) \\ =& (1 + \frac{X}{\varhphi}) (1 + \frac{X}{\widehat{\varhphi}}) \\ \end{align*}

where we use $\frac{1}{\varphi \cdot \widehat{\varphi}} = 1$

Now we get

\begin{align*} \frac{1}{\varphi} = - \widehat{\varphi} \end{align*}

and therefore

\begin{align*} 1 - X - X^2 =& (1 - \varphi X) (1 - \widehat{\varphi}X}) \\ \end{align*}

Now this amounts to

\begin{align*} (1 - \widehat{\varphi} X) (1 - \varphi X) \cdot F = X \end{align*}

using formal binomial coefficient theorem for power series ring we

now

\begin{align*} \sqrt{5} =& \varphi - \widehat{\varphi} \\ =& \varphi \cdot (1 - \widehat{\varphi}) - \widehat{\varphi} (1 - \varphi X) \end{align*}

therefore

\begin{align*} \sqrt{5} =& (1- \widehat{\varphi}X)^{-1} (1 - \varphi X)^{-1} \\ =& \varphi ( 1 - \varphi X)^{-1} - \widehat{\varphi} (1 - \widehat{\varphi} X)^{-1} \end{align*}

this shows

\begin{align*} (1 - X - X^2) F =& X \\ (1 - \widehat{\varphi}X)(1 - \varphi X) F =& X \\ F = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} X \cdot ( \varphi(1 - \varphi X)^{-1} - \widehat{\varphi}(1 - \widehat{\varphi} X^{-1})) \end{align*}

now by <kosta thm 3.15> we get

\begin{align*} F_n =& \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} (\varphi \cdot \varphi^{n-1} - \widehat{\varphi} \cdot \widehat{\varphi}^{n-1}) \\ =& \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} (\varphi - \widehat{\varphi} ) \\ \end{align*}
