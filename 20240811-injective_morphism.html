injective morphism_

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $I \subseteq \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$ a subcollection of morphisms Then $f \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$ is said to be an \(I\)-injective morphism, if

\begin{align*} f \in \chi_{R}(i) \end{align*}

is in the set with right lifting property
