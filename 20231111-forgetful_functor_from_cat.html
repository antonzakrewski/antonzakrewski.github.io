forgetful functor from Cat

Definition

The forgetful functor from Category Cat to category set is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{forget}: \mathrm{Cat} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Set} \\ \mathcal{C} \mapsto& \{\mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C}), \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})\} \\ (\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}) \mapsto& f \begin{cases} \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C}) \ni A \mapsto& \mathcal{F}(A) \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{D})\\ \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,B) \ni f \mapsto& \mathcal{F}(f) \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{D}) \end{cases} \end{align*}
