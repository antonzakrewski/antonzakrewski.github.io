hom chain complex and chain homotopy as 1-chain

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod})$ be the category of chain complexes Suppose $C,D,E$ are chain complexes, $\varphi_1, \varphi_2: C \rightarrow D$ chain maps with a chain homotopy $h: \varphi_1 \cong \varphi_2$.

Then precomposition with $h$ is an element of the hom chain complex

\begin{align*} h^{ \rightarrow} \in \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(D,E), \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(C,E))_1 \end{align*}

Proof
