homogenization of an ideal

Definition

Let $K$ be a field, $K[X_1,...,X_n]$ the polynomial ring and $I \subseteq K[X_1,...,X_n]$ an ideal. Then the <homogenization> $I^h$ is defined as

\begin{align*} I^H \coloneqq \langle f^H \vert f \in I\rangle \subseteq K[X_0,...,X_n] \end{align*}

where $f^H$ is the <Homogenisierung eines Polynoms>
