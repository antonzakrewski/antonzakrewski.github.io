nilpotent action on a group

Definition

Let $G,H$ be groups, $\varphi: G \rightarrow \mathrm{Aut}(H)$ a group action Then $\varphi$ is said to be nilpotent, if there exists a normal series $H \trianglelefteq H_1 \trianglelefteq H_1 \trianglelefteq ... \trianglelefteq H_n = 0$ such that

  1. each $H_i$ is closed under the action, i.e. $\varphi[H_i] \subseteq H_i$
  2. the induced action on the quotient group $H_n/H_{n+1}$ is a trivial action
