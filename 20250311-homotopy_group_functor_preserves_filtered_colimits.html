homotopy group functor preserves filtered colimits

Proposition

The homotopy group functor of anima

\begin{align*} \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(S^n,-)) \end{align*}

preserves filtered infinity colimits.

Proof

follows from:

  • pointed anima n-sphere as compact object
  • pi_0 as right adjoint
  • infinity left adjoint functor theorem for presentable categories
