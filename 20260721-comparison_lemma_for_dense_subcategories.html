comparison lemma for dense subcategories

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \tau)$ be a site and $\mathcal{D}$ a dense subcategory. Then the restriction functor induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sh}_{\tau}(\mathcal{C}) \simeq \mathrm{Sh}_{\tau \vert \mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}
