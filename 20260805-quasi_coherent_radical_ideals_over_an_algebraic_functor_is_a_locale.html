quasi-coherent radical ideals over an algebraic functor is a locale

Proposition

The quasi-coherent radical ideals functor restricts to a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}, \mathrm{Set}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Loc} \end{align*}

Proof

observe that the pointwise left kan extension agrees for

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Aff} \rightarrow \mathrm{Loc} \rightarrow \mathrm{Pos}^{\mathrm{op}} \subseteq \mathrm{Cat} \end{align*}

and colimits in locales are computed in Pos^op or Cat^op.
