principal g-action as free action

Proposition

Let $B$ be a topological space, $G$ a topological group and $\cdot: B \times G \rightarrow B$ a principal action. Then a $\cdot$ is a free group action.

Proof

using restriction of a homeomorphism as homeomorphism for the

\begin{align*} U_i \times G \cong p^{-1}[U_i] \end{align*}

shows that

\begin{align*} \{e\} \times G \cong p^{-1}[ \{e\}] \end{align*}

since the map $U_i \times G \rightarrow p^{-1}[U_i]$ is over $B$

Here by \(G\)-equivariantness this map is given by

\begin{align*} (e,g) \mapsto e \cdot g \end{align*}

for a fibre $e$ which is chosen by the homeomorphism $\pi^{-1}[U_i] \cong U_i \times G$ (as preimage of $(e,1)$)

Since the homeo is injective, the action is free
