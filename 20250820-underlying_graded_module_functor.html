underlying graded module functor from module spectra

Definition

Let $\mathrm{Mod}(R)$ the category of module spectra over an $E_n$ ring spectrum $R$. Then the underlying graded module functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \pi_*: \mathrm{Mod}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{GrRMod} \end{align*}

given by lax monoidal functor induces functor on Algebras & homotopy group functor from spectra to graded abelian groups is lax monoidal where

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mod}(R) =& \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathrm{LMod}}(\mathrm{Sp}) \times_{\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathrm{E}_n}(\mathrm{Sp})} * \\ \pi_*(R)\mathrm{GrMod} =& \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathrm{LMod}}(\mathrm{GrAb}) \times_{\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathrm{E}_n}(\mathrm{GrAb})} * \end{align*}
