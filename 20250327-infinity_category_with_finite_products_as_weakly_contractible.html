infinity category with finite products is weakly contractible

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a nonempty infinity category with finite products. Then $\mathcal{C}$ is weakly contractible

Proof

Choose an object $X \in \mathcal{C}$. Then consider the endofunctor

\begin{align*} X \times -: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

Then the projection gives rise to natural transformations

\begin{align*} \mathrm{pr}_1: X \times - \Rightarrow& \mathrm{const}_X \\ \mathrm{pr}_2: X \times - \Rightarrow& \mathrm{id} \end{align*}

and thus use that geometric realization from Cat infinity sends

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}\times (\Delta^1 \wedge \Delta^1) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

which shows that $\vert\mathrm{pr}_1 \vert, \vert \mathrm{const}_X \vert$ are homotopic once geometrically realized.
