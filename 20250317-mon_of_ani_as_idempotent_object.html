Mon of Ani as idempotent object in PrL

Proposition

The Anima-valued category of cartesian commutative monoids of $\mathrm{Grp}(\mathrm{An})$ is an idempotent object in the category PrL as closed symmetric monoidal category

Proof

follows from

  • tensor product in PrL and Mon
  • Anima as unit of PrL
