path inside the mapping space of an infinity category as homotopy

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category and $f,g: x \rightarrow y$ morphisms

TFAE:

  1. the maps $f,g$ are <homotopic>
  2. there exists an \(1\)-simplex.
\begin{align*} \Delta^1 \rightarrow \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \end{align*}

where the edges restrict to $f,g$
