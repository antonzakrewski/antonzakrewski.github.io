lax monoidal transformation

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}}), (\mathcal{W}, \otimes_{\mathcal{W}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{W}})$ be monoidal categories. Suppose $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{V} \rightarrow \mathcal{W}$ are lax monoidal functor, then a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \tau: \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{G} \end{align*}

is said to be lax monoidal, if for $X,Y \in \mathcal{V}$ the following diagram commutes 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{F}(X) \otimes \mathcal{F}(Y) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & \mathcal{F}(X \otimes Y) \arrow[]{d}{}\\
\mathcal{G}(X) \otimes \mathcal{G}(Y) \arrow[]{5}{}& \mathcal{G}(X \otimes Y) \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

where $\mathcal{F}(X) \otimes \mathcal{F}(Y) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(X \otimes Y)$ is the natural transformation of $\mathcal{F}$
