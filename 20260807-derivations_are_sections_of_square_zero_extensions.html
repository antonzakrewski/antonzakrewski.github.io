derivations are sections of square-zero extensions

Proposition

Let $f: R \rightarrow S$ be a ring-homomorphism and $M$ an \(S\)-module Then there exists a Bijection

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Der}_{R}(S,M) \simeq& \{R-\textrm{algebra sections of} S \oplus M \twoheadrightarrow S\} \\ \delta \mapsto& (\mathrm{id}_S, \delta) \end{align*}

Proof

observe that this maps derivations to sections of R-algebras and it has an one-sided inverse map ($\mathrm{id}_{S}, \delta) \mapsto \delta$): For any section $\iota: S \rightarrow S \oplus M$, we have $\iota(s_1 \cdot s_2) = (s_1, \delta(s_1)) \cdot (s_2, \delta(s_2)) = (s_1 s_2, s_1 \partial(s_2) + s_2 \partial(s_1))$ since $\partial(s_1) \cdot \partial(s_2) = 0$ vanishes.

Moreover, the first term hsa to be fixed, so we can reconstruct a section (which is a \(R\)-linear map with extra compatibilities) from the second component, i.e. the derivation.
