smooth map at a point of a nonsingular quasi-projective variety

Definition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X,Y \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ quasi-projective varieties Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a regular maps.

Then $f$ is said to be smooth at $P \in X$, if the regular morphism induces a vectorspace homomorphism between tangent spaces is a vectorspace-epimorphism
