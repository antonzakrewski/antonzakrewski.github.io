singular cohomology for an injective abelian group

Proposition

Let $I$ be an injective abelian group then the singular cohomology functor with coefficients in $K$ is given by

\begin{align*} H^*(-,I) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(H_*(-), I) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{RMod}}(-,I)$ is the hom-functor.

Proof

follows from the universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology where

\begin{align*} H^*(-,K) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(H_*(-), I) \oplus \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathbb{Z}}^{*}(-,I)[1] \end{align*}
