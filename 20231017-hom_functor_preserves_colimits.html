contravariant hom-functor dualizes small colimits to limits

Proposition

The contravariant hom-functor dualizes small limits to colimits, i.e.

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathrm{colim}_{\mathcal{I}} A_i, B) \cong \mathrm{lim}_{\mathcal{I}} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A_i,B_i) \end{align*}

are unique up to unique isomorphism

Proof

a)

definition of limit / colimit

b)

follows from

  • covariant hom-functor preserves small limits

where

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathrm{colim}_{\mathcal{I}} A_i, B) =& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}}(B,\mathrm{lim}_{\mathcal{I}} A_i) \\ \cong& \mathrm{lim}_{\mathcal{I}} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}}(B,A_i) \\ =& \mathrm{lim}_{\mathcal{I}} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A_i,B) \\ \end{align*}
