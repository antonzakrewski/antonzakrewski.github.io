evaluation homomorphism of a non integral element in general not injective

Proposition

Let $A \hookrightarrow B$ be Integral domains, where $A$ is a subring. Suppose $\beta \in B$ is not integral over $B$.

Then the evaluation homomorphism

\begin{align*} A[X] \rightarrow B \end{align*}

is in general not a monomorphism

Proof

consider $A = \mathbb{Z}, B l \mathbb{Q}$. Then $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ is not integral element over $\mathbb{Z}$

non integral

suppose there exists an equation

\begin{align*} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}^n} + \sum_{i=1}^n \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}^{n-i}} =& 0 \end{align*}

then multiplication by $\sqrt{2}^-$ results in

\begin{align*} (1 + 2 a_2 + 4 a_4 + ... ) + \sqrt{2} (a_1 + 2a_3 + ...) = 0 \end{align*}

As $1, $\sqrt{2}$ are linearly independent over \mathbb{Z}q we conclude that

\begin{align*} (1) + 2 (a_1 + 2 a_4 ...) = 0 \end{align*}

But the left side is odd, the right side is even, contradiction

zero

Note that $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ is a zero of

\begin{align*} 2 x^2 - 1 \end{align*}
