projective n-space over an algebraically closed field

Definition

Let $F$ be an algebraically closed field, then the projective \(n\)-space is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathbb{R}^{n+1}/\sim \end{align*}

with

\begin{align*} (a_0,...,a_n) \sim (b_0,...,b_n) :\Leftrightarrow \exists \lambda \in F : \lambda a_i = b_i \forall i \in I \end{align*}
