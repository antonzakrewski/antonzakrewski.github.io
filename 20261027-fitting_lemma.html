fitting lemma of a finite dimensional indemposable representation

Proposition

Let $V$ be a finite dimensional indecomposable representation.

  1. Then any endomorphism $\theta: V \rightarrow V$ is either an isomorphism or nilpotent
  2. suppose that $\theta_i: V \rightarrow V$ is nilpotent, then $\theta = \sum_{i} \theta_i$ is again nilpotent.

Proof

1)

By the Pigeonhole principle, there exists some $N$ such that $\mathrm{ker}(\theta^N)$ and $\mathrm{im}(\theta^N)$ stabilize. Then we claim $V = \mathrm{ker}(\theta^N) \oplus \mathrm{im}(\theta^N)$.

using the rank theorem it suffices to show $\mathrm{ker}(\theta^N) \cap \mathrm{im}(\theta^N) = \{0\}$ Let $v $ lie in the intersection. Then it is of the form $\theta^n(v_0)$ and $\theta^{N} \theta^{N}(v) = \theta^{2N}(v) = 0$. Since $\mathrm{ker}(\theta^2N) = \mathrm{ker}(\theta^N)$ this implies that $v \in \mathrm{ker}(\theta^N)$ or $u = \theta^N(v) = 0$.
