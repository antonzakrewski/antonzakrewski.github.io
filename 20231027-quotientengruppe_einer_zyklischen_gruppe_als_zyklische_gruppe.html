quotientengruppe einer zyklischen Gruppe als zyklische Gruppe

Proposition

Let $G$ be a cyclic group, $N \trianglelefteq G$ a normal subgroup. Then the quotient group $G/N$ is also a cyclic group

Proof

By assumption, there exists an $g \in G$ such that for each $g' \in G$ there exists an $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that

\begin{align*} g^n = g' \end{align*}

Hence for each $g'N$, it follows, that

\begin{align*} g'N =& (g^n)N \\ =& (gN)^n \end{align*}

and therefore, $gN$ generates $G/N$
