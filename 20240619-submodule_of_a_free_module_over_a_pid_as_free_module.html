submodule of a free module over a pid as free module

Proposition

Let $A$ be a principal ideal domain, $\oplus_{i \in I} A$ a free module and $N \hookrightarrow \oplus_{i \in I} A$ a submodule Then $N$ is also free

Proof

Let $<$ be a well order on $\oplus_{i \in I} A$ and $\mathrm{pr}_i: \oplus_{i \in I} A \rightarrow A$ the projection to the \(i\)-th component. Let $F_j$ be the submodule $\oplus_{i \leq j} A$ and $N_j = N \cap F_j$.

Then $\mathrm{pr}_j(U_j)$ is a submodule of $A$ or equivalently an ideal and hence of the form $A \alpha_i$ for some $\alpha_i \in a$. Choose $u_j \in N_j$ with $\mathrm{pr}_j(u_j) = \alpha_j$. If $\alpha_j=0$, then choose exactly $u_j = 0$.

We claim that $\{u_j \neq 0\}$ is a basis:

generates

Let $v = \sum_{n=1}^k \beta_{n} e_{j_n} \in N$ with $j_1 < j_2 ... < j_n$. Then $\mathrm{pr}_{j_n}(v) \in \mathrm{pr}_{j_n}(N_j)$ and hence of the form $\gamma_{j_n} \alpha_{j_n}$. It follows that $v - \gamma_{j_n} u_{j_n} \in N_j$ with $\mathrm{pr}_{j_n}(v - \gamma_{j_n} u_{j_n}) = 0$. Therefore (by well-ordered set has only finite descending sequence) we get that $v - \gamma_{j_n} u_{j_n}$ lies in some $N_{j_1}$ and repeating this shows that we eventually -- after finitely many steps -- have to land in $N_{1}$ for the first

linearly independent

Assume that $0 = \sum_{n=1}^k \beta_n \alpha_{j_n}$ with $j_1 < j_2 ... < j_n$. Then $0 = \mathrm{pr}_{j_n}(0) = \mathrm{pr}_{j_n}(\sum_{n=1}^j \beta_n \alpha_{j_n}) = \beta_n \alpha_{j_n}$. As $u_{j_{n}} \neq 0$, this shows that $\beta_n = 0$ and hence $0 = \sum_{n = 1}^{k-1} \beta_n \alpha_{j_n}$. Hence by induction, $\beta_1,...,\beta_n = 0$.
