multiplicative basis of disks for a manifold

Proposition

Let

\begin{align*} \mathcal{B} = \{\mathrm{ embedded multidisks} U \subseteq X\} \subseteq \mathrm{Open}(X) \end{align*}

where a multidisk is an open subspace abstractly isomorphic to $\coprod D^n$

Then $\mathcal{B}$ is a multiplicative basis of disks for a manifold (but rarely factorizing basis of a subposet of Open(X))

Proof
