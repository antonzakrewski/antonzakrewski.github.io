integral homology ring of the loop space of the quaternionic projective space

Proposition

Let $n\geq 2$. Then the homology ring of $\Omega \mathbb{H}P^n$ is given by

\begin{align*} \Lambda(y_3) \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z}[x_{4n+3}] \end{align*}

Proof

use the fiber sequence $S^{4n+3} \rightarrow \mathbb{H}\mathbb{P}^n \rightarrow \mathrm{B}S^3$ and then the fiber sequence of anima valued groups $\Omega S^{4n+3} \rightarrow \Omega \mathbb{H}\mathbb{P}^{n} \rightarrow S^3$. Then
