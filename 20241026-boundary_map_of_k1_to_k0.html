boundary map from K1 to K0 of rings

Definition

Let $R$ be a ring, $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq R$ an ideal Then the boundary map of $K_1$ and $K_0$ is defined as

\begin{align*} \partial: K_1(R/\mathfrak{a}) \rightarrow& K_0(R, \mathfrak{a}) \\ [\overline{A}] \mapsto& [(R^n,R^n,\overline{A})] - [n] \end{align*}

where

  1. $K_1(R/\mathfrak{a}) \cong \mathrm{Gl}(R/\mathfrak{a})/E(R/\mathfrak{a})$ is canonically identified (cf. whitehead theorem for k1 of a ring)
  2. $[n]$ denotes the isomorphism class of the free module with rank $n$
  3. $[(R^n,R^n, \overline{A})]$ denotes the clutching construction for the pullback

i.e.

\begin{align*} [(R^n,R^n,\overline{A})] = \{(x,y) \in R^n \times R^n \vert \overline{A}(\overline{x}) = \overline{y}\} \end{align*}
