Filterbasis eines Mengenfilters

Definition

Let $X$ be a set and $\mathcal{S} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)$ be a system of sets such that a finite intersection $\bigcap_{i =1}^n S_i \neq \emptyset$ is nonempty. Then $\mathcal{S}$ is a filterbasis for the setfilter

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F} \coloneqq \{A \subseteq X \vert \exists S_1,...,S_n : \bigcap_{i=1}^n S_i \subseteq A\} \end{align*}
