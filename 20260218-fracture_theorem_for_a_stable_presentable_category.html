Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a stable presentable category, $I$ a small set and for every $i \in I$ let $\mathcal{L}_i: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an exact localization of an exact category. Let that $\mathcal{L}': \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is another exact localization functor and $\alpha_i: \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{D}} \rightarrow \mathcal{L}_i, \alpha': \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{D}} \rightarrow \mathcal{L}'$ be the unit transformations.
