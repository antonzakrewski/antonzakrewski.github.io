free category functor

Definition / Proposition

The free category functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{FreeCat}: \mathrm{Set} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat} \\ X \mapsto& X \\ (f: X \rightarrow Y) \mapsto (f: X \rightarrow Y) \end{align*}

where $X$ is regarded as category containing only identity morphisms and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ the mapping of $f$

Proof

Note that a functor from a discrete category is the same datum as a map on the underlying objects.
