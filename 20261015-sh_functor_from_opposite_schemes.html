SH functor from opposite schemes

Definition / Proposition

There exists a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{SH}(-): \mathrm{Sch}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Pr}^L X \mapsto \mathrm{SH}(X) \end{align*}

Proof

Remark

This is an analogue of the functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Top}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Pr}^L \\ X \mapsto& \mathrm{Shv}(X, \mathrm{Sp}) \end{align*}
