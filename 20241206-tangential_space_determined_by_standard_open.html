tangential space determined by standard open for an affine variety

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be an affine variety. Let $P \in X$ be a point, $f \in A(X)$ with $f(P) \neq 0$ and $D(f)$ the <standard open>.

Then the inclusion

\begin{align*} D(f) \rightarrow X \end{align*}

induces an isomorphism on the tangent space of a point of an affine algebraic set

\begin{align*} T_P(D(f)) \cong T_P(X) \end{align*}

note that $D(f)$ is standard open of an affine variety as affine variety up to isomorphism

Proof
