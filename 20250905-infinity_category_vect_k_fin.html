infinity category Vect_K^fin

Definition

Let $\mathbb{K} \in \{\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}, \mathbb{H}\}$, then the infinity category $\mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb{K}}^\mathrm{fin}$ is defined as underlying infinity category of the topologically enriched Vect_K^fin
