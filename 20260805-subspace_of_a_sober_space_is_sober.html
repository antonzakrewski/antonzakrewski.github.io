open subspace of a sober space is sober

Proposition

Let $X$ be a sober space and $U \subseteq X$ an open subspace. Then $U$ is sober.

Proof

Let $A \subseteq U$ be irreducible and closed in $U$, in particular nonempty. Then $\overline{A}$ is also irreducible (cf. irreducible subspace and closure) and hence it is given by the closure of a point $\{a\}$. Now suppose $u \not\in A$, then $\overline{a} \subseteq X \setminus U$ and hence $\overline{a} \neq A$, contradiction to our assumption. Thus $A$ is the closure of a point.

Moreover, if $a' \in A$ with $\overline{a'} = A$ in $U$, then $\overline{a'} = \overline{A}$ in $X$, hence $a'$ coincides with $a$.
