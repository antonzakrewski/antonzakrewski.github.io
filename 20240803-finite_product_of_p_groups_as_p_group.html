finite product of p-groups as p-group

Proposition

Let $G_i$ be p-groups for $i \in \{1,...,n\}$. Then the direct product

\begin{align*} \prod G_i \end{align*}

is also a p-group

Proof

for $(g_1,...,g_n)$ choose a sufficiently large $m$ - e.g. $\mathrm{max}(n_i)$ - such that

\begin{align*} g_i^{p^m} = 1 \end{align*}

for all $i$.

Hence

\begin{align*} (g_1,...,g_n)^{p^m} =& (1,...,1) \\ =& 1 \end{align*}
