semiorthogonal decomposition of a presentable, stable category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable stable category. Then a semiorthogonal decomposition is a collection of categories $\mathcal{C}_i$ such that

  1. the inclusion $\mathcal{C}_i \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is strongly continuous,
  2. for all $i > j$, we have $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{C}_i, \mathcal{C}_j) = 0$
  3. $\mathcal{C}$ is generated by the essential images of $\mathcal{C}_i$ under colimits

We also write $\langle C_i\rangle$
