stalkwise characterization of epimorphisms

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space and $\eta: \mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ a natural transformation between sheaves on $X$.

TFAE:

  1. $\varphi_{X}: \mathcal{F}_{x} \rightarrow \mathcal{G}_{x}$ is surjective
  2. for all $U$ open and $t \in \mathcal{G}(U)$, there exists an open cover $U = \bigcup U_i$ and $s_i \in \mathcal{F}(U_i)$ such that $\varphi_{\vert U_i} = t_{\vert U_i}$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $t \in \mathcal{G}(U)$ be a arbitrary, then choose preimages $s_{x}$ of $t$ for all $x \in X$. Then choose representatives $(s_{x, V_x})$ of $s_{x}$, where we may assume - after restricting if necessary - that $V_x \subseteq U$. Then $\varphi_{\vert V_x}(s_{x,V_x})_{x} = t_{x}$ by construction, so there exists an open $U_x \subseteq V_i$ such that $\varphi_{\vert U_x}(s_{x, V_x}) = t_{\vert U_x}$. In particular, we may take $U_{x} \subseteq V_x \subseteq X$ for $x \in U$ as open cover (cf. self indexing trick).

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $t_x \in \mathcal{G}_x$ be represented by a germ $(t_{V},V) \in \mathcal{G}(V)$ for $V \subseteq X$ open. Then choose an open cover $V_i$ of $U$ with germs $s_i$ such that $\varphi_{\vert U_i}(s_{\vert V_i}) = t_{\vert V_i}$ and let $x \in V_j$, which exists, since it is an open cover of $V \ni x$. Then the germ of $(s_{\vert V_i})_x$ maps to $t_x$, by naturality of the unit transformation
