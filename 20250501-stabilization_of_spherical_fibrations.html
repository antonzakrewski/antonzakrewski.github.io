stabilization of spherical fibrations

Definition

The stabilization of spherical fibrations is via postcomposition with

\begin{align*} \sigma^i: BF(d) \simeq BF(d) \times \Delta^0 \rightarrow BF(d) \times BF(i) \rightarrow BF(d + i) \end{align*}

where $\Delta^0 \rightarrow BG(i)$ is the terminal spherical fibration $S^{i} \rightarrow *$ and join homomorphism of F(d)
