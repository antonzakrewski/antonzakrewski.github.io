pure dimension of an affine variety, height as 1 and generated by single element

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $A \cong K[X_1,...,X_n]/\mathfrak{a}$ an affine k-Algebra and $X \coloneqq \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ the zariski spectrum

TFAE:

  1. $X$ has pure dimension \(n-1\)
  2. $\mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{a}) = 1$ for the height
  3. $\sqrt{\mathfrak{a}} = (g)$ for some non-constant polynomial $g \in k[X_1,...,X_n]$

Proof

prelim

Note that $A$ is a noetherian ring (cf. Hilbert's basis theorem, quotient ring of a noetherian ring as noetherian ring). Therefore there only exist finite amount of minimal prime ideals for a noetherian ring. Let

\begin{align*} T_{\mathfrak{a}}: = \{\mathrm{minimal prime ideals in } K[X_1,...,X_n] \mathrm{ containing } \mathfrak{a}\} \rightarrow& A \\ \mathfrak{p} \mapsto& \overline{\mathfrak{p}} = \mathfrak{p} + \mathfrak{a} \end{align*}

using the correspondence theorem for prime ideals and quotient ring we conclude that $T_{\alpha}$ is finite

1) $\implies$ 2)

Note that $\mathfrak{a} \subsesteq K[X_1,...,X_n]$ is a strict ideal (cf. algebraic set with pure dimension n-1 and strict ideal - todo) If $\mathfrak{p} \in T_{\mathfrak{a}}$, then using the 2. Isomorphiesatz (Modul) we get

\begin{align*} K[X_1,...,X_n]/\mathfrak{p} \cong& (K[X_1,...,X_n]/\mathfrak{a})/(\mathfrak{p}/\mathfrak{a}) \\ \cong& A/\mathfrak{p} \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(K[X_1,...,X_n]/\mathfrak{p}) = \mathrm{dim}(A/\overline{\mathfrak{p}}) = n - 1 \end{align*}

where the first equality follows by the isomorphism and the latter follows by assumption of pure dimension $n -1$ (dimension of an irreducible component and quotient ring - todo) In particular we concldue that $0 \neq \mathfrak{p}$ and thus $\mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{p}) \geq 1$

Assume $\mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{a}) => 1$. Then $\mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{p}) \geq 2$ (?) and therefore

\begin{align*} 2 + (n-1) \leq& \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{p}) + \mathrm{dim}(K[X_1,...,X_n]/\mathfrak{p}) \\ \leq& \mathrm{dim}(K[X_1,...,X_n]) = n \end{align*}

where the second $\leq$ follows from height of a prime ideal and quotient.

This would imply

\begin{align*} n + 1 \leq n \end{align*}

contradiction

2) $\implies$ 3)

Let $\mathfrak{p} \in T_{\mathfrak{a}}$ then

\begin{align*} 1 = \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{p}) \leq \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{p}) = 1 \end{align*}

where the first equality follows from assumption, the $\leq$ follows from $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq \mathfrak{p}$ and the last equality follows from $\mathfrak{p}$

and by <8.3> we conclude that

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{p} = (g_{\mathfrak{p}}) \end{align*}

for some irreducible $g_\mathfrak{p} \in K[X_1,...,X_n]$

Denote

\begin{align*} g \coloneqq \prod_{\mathfrak{p} \in T_{\alpha}} g_{\mathfrak{p}} \end{align*}

Then the radical is given by intersection of prime ideals as radical ideal

\begin{align*} \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}} =& \bigcup \{\mathfrak{p} \vert \mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm{Spec}(A), \mathfrak{a} \subseteq \mathfrak{p} \} \\ =& \bigcup_{\mathfrak{p} \in T_{\mathfrak{a}}} \mathfrak{p} \\ =& \bigcup_{\mathfrak{p} \in T_{\mathfrak{a}}} (g_{\mathfrak{p}}) \\ =& (\mathfrak{g} \end{align*}

product of ideals as intersection of ideals for a UFD - TODO

3) $\implies$ 1)

Assume that the radical is a principal ideal for a non constant $g$. Then take a factorization

\begin{align*} g = g_1 \cdot g_r \end{align*}

where $g_i$ is prime (cf. Polynomial ring over a UFD as UFD, field as ufd)

Then $g_i \not\mid g_j$ for $i \neq j$, as eahc $g_i$ has multiplicity of $1$

If $g = g_1^2$ then $(g) \subseteq (g_1)$ but $(g) = \sqrt{(g)} = (g_1)$

Let $p_i = (g_i)$ be the corresponding prime ideals (cf. Primelement und erzeugte Primideal)

Then

\begin{align*} \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}} =& (g) \\ =& \bigcap_{i=1}^r (g_i) \\ =& \bigcap_{i=1}^r p_i \end{align*}

showing $T_{\alpha} = \{\mathfrak{p}_1,...,\mathfrak{p}_r\}$

We need to show

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(K[X_1,...,X_n]/\mathfrak{p}_i) = n - 1 \end{align*}

for all $i$:

a)

As $0 \neq \mathfrak{p}$ this implias $\mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{p}_i) \geq 1$ and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{p}_i) + \mathrm{dim}(K[X_1,...,X_n]/\mathfrak{p}_i) \leq \mathrm{dim}(K[X_1,...,X_n]) = n \end{align*}

showing

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(K[X_1,...,X_n]/\mathfrak{p}_i) \leq n - 1 \end{align*}

b

by dimension of a finitely generated k-algebra as transcendence degree it suffices to show that there exists a set of $n-1$ algebraically independent elements in $K[X_1,...,X_n]/\mathfrak{p}$

Since $\mathfrak{p}_i = (g_i)$ we may conclude that $g_i$ is not a unit resp. has a non trivial \(X_j\)-component. w.l.o.g. after renumbering we may assume that $j = n$

In particular, $g_i$ as an element of

\begin{align*} (K[X_1,...,X_{n-1}])[X_n] \end{align*}

as positive degree

Then consider the quotient map

\begin{align*} \pi: K[X_1,...,X_n] \rightarrow K[X_1,...,X_n]/(g_i) \end{align*}

Assume

\begin{align*} \{\pi(X_1),...,\pi(X_n)\} \end{align*}

is algebraically dependent in $K[X_1,...,X_n]/(g_i)$

Then there is a nonzero polynomial $f \in K[X_1,...,X_{n-1}]$, wlog irreducible, such that

\begin{align*} f(\pi(X_1),...,\pi(X_n)) = 0 \end{align*}

$f$ as an element of $K[X_1,...,X_n]$ has $\pi(f) = 0$. This shows that $f \in \mathrm{ker}(\pi) = (g_i)$ resp.

\begin{align*} c \cdot g_i = f \end{align*}

for some $c \in K^{\times}$.

Therefore

\begin{align*} 0 = \mathrm{deg}_{X_n}(f) = \mathrm{deg}_{X_n}(c) + \mathrm{deg}_{X_n}(g_1) > 0 \end{align*}

contradiction
