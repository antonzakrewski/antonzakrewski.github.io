tensor of the zero module

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $M$ an \(R\)-module and $0$ the zero module. Then the tensor product

\begin{align*} M \otimes_R 0 = 0 \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of:

  • colimit of the empty diagram as initial object
  • tensor functor over a commutative ring as left adjoint
  • left adjoint preserves colimits
