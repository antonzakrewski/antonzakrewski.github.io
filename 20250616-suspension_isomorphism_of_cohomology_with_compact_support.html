suspension isomorphism of cohomology with compact support

Proposition

There exists an isomorphism of

\begin{align*} H^n_c(X) \simeq H_c^{n+1}(X \times \mathbb{R}) \end{align*}

cohomology of a space with compact support

Proof

Let $\mathcal{K}_X, \mathcal{K}_{X \times \mathbb{R}}$ be the directed posets of compact subspaces. Then we will replace cofinally

\begin{align*} \{A \times [-n,n] \vert \mathcal{K}_X, n \in \mathbb{N} \}\rightarrow \mathcal{K}_{X \times \mathbb{R}} \end{align*}

Then consider

\begin{align*} A =& X \times (-\infty,n] \\ B =& X \times [-n, \infty) \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} C =& X \times (-\infty,-n] \cup (X \setminus K) \times (-\infty, n] \\ D =& X \times [n, \infty) \cup (X \setminus K) \times [-n,\infty) \\ \end{align*}

Then we get homotopies (even of pairs)

\begin{align*} (A, C) \simeq& (X,X) \\ (B, D) \simeq& (X,X) \end{align*}

but also

\begin{align*} A \cup B =& X \times \mathbb{R} \\ C \cup D =& X \times \mathbb{R} \setminus (K \times [-n, n]) \end{align*}

Thus using relative mayer vietoris for singular cohomology we get an isomorphism

\begin{align*} H^{n+1}(\tilde{X}, \tilde{Y}) \simeq H^n(X, K) \end{align*}
