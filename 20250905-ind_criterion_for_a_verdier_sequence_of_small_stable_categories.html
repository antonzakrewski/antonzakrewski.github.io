Ind criterion for a verdier sequence of small, stable categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ be a (fiber ? ) sequence of small stable categories. TFAE:

  1. the sequence above is a verdier sequence
  2. the following three conditions hold: a) $\mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ is essentially surjective b) the right adjoint of $\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{E})$ is fully faithful c) the kernel of the functor $\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{E})$ is compactly generated

Proof
