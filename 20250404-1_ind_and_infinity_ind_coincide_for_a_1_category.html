1-Ind and infinity Ind coincide for a 1-category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a \(1\) category

Then

  1. $\mathrm{Ind}_{\infty} \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An})$
  2. $\mathrm{Ind}_{1} \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Set}$

coincide

Proof

n-truncated anima closed under filtered colimits n-truncated presheaf and pointwise n-truncated pointwise calculation of colimits
