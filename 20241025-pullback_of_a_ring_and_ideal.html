explicit description of a pullback of a ring and ideal

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq R$ an ideal Then the pullback $R \times_{R/\mathfrak{a}} R$ is given by

\begin{align*} \{(a,b) \vert a - b \in \mathfrak{a}\} \subseteq R \times R \end{align*}
