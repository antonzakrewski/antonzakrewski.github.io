cotangent module of a map of algebraic functors

Definition

Let $f: Y \rightarrow X$ be a map of algebraic functors Then $f$ admits a cotangent module $\Omega_f \in \mathrm{Mod}_{Y}$ if the following conditions hold:

  1. for every commutative ring $R$ and every R-point $y: \mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow Y$, the functor $\mathrm{Mod}_{R} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ sending $M$ to the set of lifts

is representable functor

  1. for every ring map $\varphi: R \rightarrow S$ and every \(R\)-point $y: \mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow Y$, the canonical \(S\)-linear map $\Omega_{f}(y) \otimes_{R} S \rightarrow \Omega_{f}(\varphi^{*}(y))$.
