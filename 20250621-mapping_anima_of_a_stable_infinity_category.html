mapping anima of a stable infinity category as spectrum

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,-): \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

lifts canonically to a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,-): \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp} \end{align*}

Proof

informal

Let $X,Y \in \mathcal{C}$.

Then consider the infinite loop structure

\begin{align*} [n] \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, \Sigma^n Y) \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} \Omega \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, \Sigma^n Y) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, \Omega \Sigma^n Y) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, \Sigma^{n-1} Y) \end{align*}

where suspension as equivalence in a stable category
