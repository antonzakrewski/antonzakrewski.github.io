algebraic jacobian determinant

Definition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{A}_K^n$ the affine space and

\begin{align*} f: \mathbb{A}_K^n \rightarrow \mathbb{A}_K^n \end{align*}

a rational map

Then the jacobian determinant is defined as determinant

\begin{align*} \mathrm{det}( \begin{pmatrix} \frac{\partial f_1}{\partial x_1} & ... & \frac{\partial f_1}{\partial x_n} \\ \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ \frac{\partial f_n}{\partial x_1 }& ... & \frac{\partial f_n}{\partial x_n} \\ \end{pmatrix} \end{align*}

where $\frac{\partial f_i}{\partial x_j}$ is the formal derivative of a rational map
