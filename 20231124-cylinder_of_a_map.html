mapping cylinder

Definition

Let $(X,Y)$ be topological spaces and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a continuous map.

Then the mapping cylinder $\mathrm{Cyl}(f)$ is defined as pushout

where

\begin{align*} \iota: X \rightarrow& X \times [0,1] \\ x \mapsto& (x,0) \end{align*}

Explicite

The mapping cylinder is given as quotient topology

\begin{align*} (X \sim [0,1] \coprod Y)/\sim \end{align*}

where $\sim$ is the smallest equivalence relation such that $f(x) \sim (x,0)$
