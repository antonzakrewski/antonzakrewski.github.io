Ext(Q,Z) is the additive group of real numbers

Proposition

there exists a canonical isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ext}_{1}^{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{Z}) \simeq& (\mathbb{R},+) \end{align*}
