semiorthogonal gluing of stable categories and continuous functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an accessible functor between dualizable categories. Let $\mathcal{E} = \mathcal{C} \oplus_{\mathcal{F}} \mathcal{D}$ and $\iota_1: \mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{E}, \iota_2: \mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{E}$ be the inclusions TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is continuous
  2. $\iota_1, \iota_2$ are strongly continuous and $\mathcal{E}$ is dualizable
