coproduct and epimorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $A_i, B,C$ be objects, $\coprod A_i \coprod B$ a coproduct, $f_i: A_i \rightarrow C, g: B \twoheadrightarrow C$ morphisms where $g$ is an epimorphism Then the induced morphism

\begin{align*} \coprod f_i \sqcup g: \coprod A_i \sqcup B \rightarrow C \end{align*}

is also an epimorphism

Proof

follows from precomposition as epimorphism implies epimorphism and the factorization
