acyclic spectra for the localized sphere

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum and $P$ a collection of primes TFAE:

  1. $X$ is $\mathbb{S}[P^{-1}]$ acyclic
  2. each element in $\pi_*(X)$ is p-primary torsion

Proof

follows from:

  • explicit description of filtered colimtis in $\mathrm{Ab}$
  • homotopy group functor preserves filtered colimits
  • R-localization is objectwise sequential colimit
  • $X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{S}[P^{-1}] \simeq \mathrm{colim} X \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X \xrightarrow{\cdot p} ...$
