projective object exact bifunctor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}, \mathcal{C}$ be abelian categories and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \times \mathcal{B} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

a bifunctor

Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be a projective exact bifunctor, if for each projective object $P_A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{A}), P_B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{B})$

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(P_A,-): \mathcal{B} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ \mathcal{F}(-,P_B): \mathcal{A} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \end{align*}
