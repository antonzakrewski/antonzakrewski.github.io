zariski locale functor

Definition

The zariski locale functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{CRing}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Loc} \\ R \mapsto& \mathrm{Rad}_{R} (f: R \rightarrow S) \mapsto& S \otimes_{R} - \end{align*}

where a ring-homomorphism $f: R \rightarrow S$ induces the following map on posets:

\begin{align*} S \otimes_{R} - \mathrm{Rad}_{R} \rightarrow \mathrm{Rad}_S I \mapsto& \sqrt{SI} \end{align*}

which is then regarded morphism in $\mathrm{Loc}^{\mathrm{op}}$.

Remark

This is the restriction of the functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{CRing} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Pos} R \mapsto& \mathrm{Rad}_R \end{align*}
