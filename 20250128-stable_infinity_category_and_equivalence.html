stable infinity category and equivalence from Spectra

Proposition

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ is a stable infinity category
  2. the map
\begin{align*} \Omega^{\infty}: \mathrm{Spc} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

is an equivalence

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Assume $\mathcal{C}$ is stable. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Exc}_*(\mathrm{Spc}^{\mathrm{fin}}_*, \mathcal{C}) \simeq& \mathrm{Exc}'(\mathrm{Spc}^{\mathrm{fin}}, \mathcal{C}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{Rex}}(\mathrm{Spc}^{\mathrm{fin}}, \mathcal{C}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(*,\mathcal{C}) \\ \simeq& \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

which is given by evaluation functor from Spectra at S^0

2) $\implies$ 1)
