monomial steenrod operation as sum of admissable monomial steenrod operations

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Sq}^I$ be amonomial steenrod operation. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}^I = \mathrm{Sq}^{I'} \end{align*}

where the RHS is an admissible monomial steenrod operation

Proof

follows from inductively applying the Adem relation of steenrod operations
