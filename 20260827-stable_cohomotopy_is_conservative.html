stable cohomotopy is not conservative

Proposition

The functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(-,\mathbb{S}): \mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp} \end{align*}

is not a conservative functor

Proof

stable miller's theorem implies $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{F}_p, \mathbb{S}) \simeq 0$, so $\mathbb{F}_p \rightarrow 0$ induces an equivalence after applying $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(-,\mathbb{S})$. But this is of course not an equivalence
