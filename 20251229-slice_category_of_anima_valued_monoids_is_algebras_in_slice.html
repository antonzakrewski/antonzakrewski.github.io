slice category of anima valued monoids is algebras in slice

Proposition

Let $M$ be an anima valued monoid, then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}(\mathrm{An})_{/M} \simeq \mathrm{Alg}(\mathrm{An}_{/M}) \end{align*}

which lies over $\mathrm{An}_{/M}$, i.e. 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{Mon}(\mathrm{An})_{/M} \arrow[]{ru}{} \arrow[]{rr}{}& & \mathrm{Alg}(\mathrm{AN}_{/M}) \arrow[]{ld}{}\\
& M & \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

here $\mathrm{An}_{/M}$ is equipped with the inherited a monoidal structure

Proof
