joyal equivalence between infinity categories

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{d}$ be infinity categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be a Joyal equivalence, if the corresponding \(1\)-simplex in $\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}$, the category of infinity categories is an equivalence between infinity categories

Explicite

There exists a \(2\)-simplex

\begin{align*} \sigma: \Delta^2 \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty} \end{align*}

such that

\begin{align*} \sigma_{\vert \Delta^{0 \rightarrow 1}} =& f \\ \sigma_{\vert \Delta^{0 \rightarrow 2}} =& \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{C}} \end{align*}

(i.e. exists $g$ such that $fg \rightarrow \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{D}}, gf \rightarrow \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{C}} $ is an equivalence ("homotopy equivalence, but ")
