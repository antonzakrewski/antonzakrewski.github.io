vitali theorem

Proposition

Using the Axiom of choice, there does not exist a map

\begin{align*} \mu: \mathcal{P}(\mathbb{R}^n) \rightarrow [0, \infty] \end{align*}

which is

  1. invariant under isometries
  2. sigma additive function
  3. normed, i.e. $\mu([0,1]^d) = 1$
