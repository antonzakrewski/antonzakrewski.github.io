exact infinity subcategory

Definition

Let $\mathcal{E}$ be a exact infinity category and $\iota: \mathcal{A} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{E}$ a full additive infinity subcategory. Then $\mathcal{A}$ is said to an exact subcategory if it is equipped with an exact structure such that

  1. $\iota$ is an exact infinity functor where an injection/projection is an injection/projection in $\mathcal{A}$ if and only if it is one in $\mathcal{E}$
  2. $\mathcal{A}$ is closed under extensions in $\mathcal{E}$
