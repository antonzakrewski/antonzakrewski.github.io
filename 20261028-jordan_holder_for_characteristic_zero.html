jordan hölder for characteristic zero

Proposition

Let $A$ be a K-Algebra over characteristic zero and $V$ a finite dimensional representation Let $0 = V_0 \subseteq V_1 \subseteq ... \subseteq v_n = V$ and $0 = V_0' \subseteq V_1' \subseteq ... \subseteq V_m' = V$ be two filtrations of $V$.

Thne $n = m$ and there exists a Permutation $\sigma$ such that

\begin{align*} W_i \cong W_{\sigma(i)}' \end{align*}

for $W_i \cong V_{i}/V_{i-1}, \tilde{W}_i' = V_{i}'/V_{i-1}'$

Proof

using additivity of characters and induction shows that

\begin{align*} \chi_{V} = \sum_{i} \chi_{W_i} = \sum_{i'} \chi_{\tilde{W}_i} \end{align*}

or

\begin{align*} 0 =& \sum_{[W_i]} #_{W_i's} \chi_{W_i} - \sum_{[\tilde{W}_i]'} #_{\tilde{W}_{i}'s} \chi_{\tilde{W}_{i'}} \\ =& \sum_{[V] \textrm{ simple}} #_{W_i's \cong V} - #_{\tilde{W}_i's \cong V} \chi_{V} \end{align*}

which with linear independence implies implies that $#_{W_i's \cong V} = #_{\tilde{W}_i's \cong V}$ for all simple representation $V$.
