infinity slice category of an accessible category as accessible category

Proposition

Let $K$ be a small simplicial set and $\mathcal{C}$ a accessible infinity category and $p: K \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a morphism of simplicial sets Then the left slice

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}_{p/} \end{align*}

is also accessible

Proof
