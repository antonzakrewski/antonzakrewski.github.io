brown comenetz dual for a sum of injective groups

Proposition

Let $D_i$ be a set of injective abelian groups. Then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} I_{\bigoplus_{i \in I} D_i} \rightarrow \bigoplus_{i \in I} I_{ D_i} \end{align*}

for brown comenetz duals.

Proof

Observe that $\bigoplus D_i$ is also an injective group (cf. injective abelian groups are closed under colimits). The inclusions $D_i \rightarrow \bigoplus_{i \in I} D_i$ induce maps $I_{D_i} \rightarrow I_{\bigoplus_{i \in I} D_i}$ (cf. map of injective groups induces a map on brown-comenetz duals) for each $i \in I$ and hence in particular a map

\begin{align*} \bigoplus_{i \in I} I_{D_i} \rightarrow I_{\bigoplus_{i \in I} D_i} \end{align*}

we need to show that it induces an isomorphism on all homotopy groups, that is it suffices to show that

\begin{align*} \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[n],\bigoplus_{i \in I} I_{D_i})) \rightarrow \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[n],I_{\bigoplus_{i \in I} D_i})) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism for all $n$.

where we have used that

  1. $\mathbb{S}[n]$ is a compact spectrum and homotopy group functor from spectra preserves arbitrary sums.
  2. $\pi_n(\mathbb{S})$ is finitely generated, hence compact

This diagram shows that the map

\begin{align*} \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[n],\bigoplus_{i \in I} I_{D_i})) \rightarrow \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[n],I_{\bigoplus_{i \in I} D_i})) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism.
