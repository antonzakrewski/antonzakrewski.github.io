cartesian monoid is canonically a bimonoid

Proposition

Let $M \in \mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{C})$ be a cartesian Monoid. Then it is canonically a bimonoid.

Proof

sketchy

we need to show that the diagonal and the projections are maps of monoids. But the forgetful functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

preserves products (and $\mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{C})$ admits products - todo) , so in particular the diagonal and projection lift to maps of monoids.
