homotopy category of the dg nerve and dg homotopy category

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathcal{C}$ be a dg category. Then the homotopy category of the dg nerve is canonically isomorphic to the dg homotopy category

\begin{align*} h N_{\mathrm{dg}}(\mathcal{C}) \cong& h_{\mathrm{dg}}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

Proof
