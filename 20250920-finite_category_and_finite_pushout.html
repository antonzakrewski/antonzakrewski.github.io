finite category and finite cw-structure

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ is a finite category
  2. $\mathcal{C}$ can be written in finitely many steps as pushout

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

observe that $\partial \Delta^n, \Delta^n$ are finite categories.

2) $\implies$ 1)
