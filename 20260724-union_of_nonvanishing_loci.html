union of nonvanishing loci

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor and $F_i$ subsets of $\mathcal{O}(X)$. Then

\begin{align*} \bigcup_{i \in I} D(F_i) \subseteq D(\bigcup_{i \in I} F_i) \end{align*}

where the union is either the categorical colimit or the pointwise union

Proof

Let $x \in D(F_i)(R)$, then $(f_(x))_{f \in F_i}$ generates the unit ideal. So in particular $(f_(x))_{f \in \bigcup F_i}$ generates the unit ideal.
