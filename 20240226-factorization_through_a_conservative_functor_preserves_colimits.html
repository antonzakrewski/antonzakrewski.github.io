Suppose that $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{H}$ preserve colimits of a shape $\mathcal{J}$ and that $\mathcal{H}$ is conservative

Then $\mathcal{G}$ also preserves colimits of this shape

Proof

Let $\mathrm{colim}$ be a colimit of this shape, then we conclude, that

\begin{align*} H(\mathrm{colim}(\mathcal{G})) \cong& \mathrm{colim}(\mathcal{HG}) \\ \cong& \mathrm{colim}(\mathcal{F}) \\ \cong& \mathcal{F}(\mathrm{colim}) \\ \cong& \mathcal{HG}(\mathrm{colim}) \end{align*}

and hence since $\mathcal{H}$ is conservative, it follows, that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}(\mathcal{G}) \cong \mathcal{G}(\mathrm{colim}) \end{align*}
