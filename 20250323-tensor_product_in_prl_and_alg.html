tensor product in PrL and Alg

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a closed symmetric monoidal presentable infinity category and $\mathcal{C}$ a presentable cartesian closed monoidal infinity category and $\mathcal{O}$ an infinity operad Then there exists a canonical equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{D}) \otimes \mathcal{C} \simeq \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{C} \otimes \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

see:

  • category PrL as closed symmetric monoidal category

Proof

a)

consider

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Rig}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{C}) \simeq& \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Mon}_{E_{\infty}}(\mathcal{C})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Mon}_{E_{\infty}}(\mathrm{An}) \otimes \mathcal{C}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Mon}_{E_{\infty}}(\mathrm{An})) \otimes \mathrm{An} \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Rig}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{C}) \otimes \mathrm{An} \\ \end{align*}

b)

Note that by presentable cartesian infinity category as sbucategory of a presheaf category we may identify

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}^{\kappa}) \end{align*}

assume that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \simeq \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}_0) \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{D}) \otimes \mathcal{C} \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}},\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{D})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}_0),\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{D})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}_0,\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{D})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}_0,\mathcal{D})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}_0),\mathcal{D})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{C} \otimes \mathcal{D}) \\ \end{align*}
