Proposition

Proof

Choose $(13), (1234)$ Then $\mathrm{ord}(13) = 2, \mathrm{ord}(1234) = 4$ and furthermore

\begin{align*} (13)(1234)(13)(1234) = \mathrm{id} \end{align*}

Hence by fundamental theorem of group homomorphism

it is surjective by factorization, hence it suffices to show, that $\vert U_2 \vert \geq 8$.

by Lagrange $4 \mid \vert U_2 \vert$ and with

\begin{align*} (1234)^2 =& (13)(24) \\ (1234)^3 =& (14)(23) \end{align*}

it follows, that $\vert U_2 \vert \neq 4$.
