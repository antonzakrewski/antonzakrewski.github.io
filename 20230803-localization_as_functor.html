localization functor for modules

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $S \subseteq R$ multiplicatively closed and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category of modules over $R$. Then the map

\begin{align*} F: \mathrm{RMod} \rightarrow& \mathrm{S^{-1}RMod} \\ \mapsto& S^{-1}M \\ (f: M \rightarrow N) \mapsto& (S^{-1}f: S^{-1}M \rightarrow S^{-1}N) \end{align*}
