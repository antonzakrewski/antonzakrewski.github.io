union of sieves

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category, $U \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and $S_i$ sieves on $U$. Then the union $\bigcap_{i \in I} S_i$ is defined through

\begin{align*} (\bigcup_{i \in I} S_i)(A) = \bigcup_{i \in I} S_i(A) \end{align*}

for $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$
