shifting of a graded module

Definition / Proposition

Let (R = \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0} R_n) be a graded ring and $M = \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0} a graded module Then the shifting of $M$ by $m \in M$ is defined as graded module

\begin{align*} \bigoplus_{n = m}^{\infty} M_n \end{align*}
