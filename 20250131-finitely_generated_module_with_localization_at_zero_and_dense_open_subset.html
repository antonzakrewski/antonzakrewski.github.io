finitely generated module with localization at zero and dense open subset

Proposition

Let $A$ be a Integral domain and $(0) \in \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ be the generic point. Suppose $M$ is a finitely generated module with $M_{(0)} = 0$.

Then there exists an open dense subset $U \subseteq \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ with

\begin{align*} M_{\mathfrak{p}} = 0 \end{align*}

for all $\mathfrak{p} \in U$

Proof

Choose finitely many generators $x_1,...,x_n$ in $A$. Then

\begin{align*} \frac{x_i}{1} = 0 \in M_{(0)} \end{align*}

hence there exists $\alpha_i \in A \setminus (0) = A \setminus \{0\}$ with

\begin{align*} \alpha_i \cdot (\frac{0}{1} - \frac{x_i}{1}) = 0 \end{align*}

in $M$ or $\alpha_i \cdot x_i = 0$.

Then consider

\begin{align*} \alpha \coloneqq \prod_{i=1}^n \alpha_i \in A \end{align*}

note that $\alpha \neq 0$ since each factor $\alpha_i$ is nonzero and $A$ is an integral domain.

Then consider

\begin{align*} D(\alpha) \subseteq \mathrm{Spec}(A) \end{align*}

it is open by construction of the standard opens of the zariski topology

dense

it suffices to show that the generic point $(0) \in D(\alpha)$ (cf. Closure of a Subset). But this holds as

\begin{align*} (\alpha) \subsetneq (0) = \{0\} \end{align*}

since $\alpha \neq 0$

zero

Let $x_i$ be a generator of $M$, $\mathfrak{p} \in D(\alpha)$. Then $\alpha \in A \setminus \mathfrak{p}$.

\begin{align*} 1 \cdot \frac{x_i}{1} =& \frac{1}{\alpha} \cdot \frac{\alpha}{1} \frac{x_i}{1} \\ =& \frac{1}{\alpha} \cdot 0 \\ =& 0 \end{align*}

since

\begin{align*} \alpha \cdot x_i =& \prod_{j \neq i} \alpha_j \cdot (\alpha_i \cdot x_i) \\ =& 0 \end{align*}
