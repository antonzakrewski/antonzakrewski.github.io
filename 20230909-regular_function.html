regular function

Definition

quasi-affine

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $Y \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ a quasi-affine variety. A set theoretic function $f: Y \rightarrow K$ is said to be regular, if it is regular at each Point $p \in Y$

quasi-projective

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $O \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ a quasi-projective variety A function $f: Y \rightarrow K$ is said to be regular, if it is regular at a each point $p \in Y$
