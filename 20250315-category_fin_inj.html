category Fin* inj

Definition

The category $\mathrm{Fin}_*^{\mathrm{inj}}$ is defined as wide subcategory of category Fin* containing maps

\begin{align*} f: <m> \rightarrow <n> \end{align*}

such that

\begin{align*} f_{\vert \langle m\rangle^{\circ}}: \langle m\rangle^{\circ} \rightarrow <n> \end{align*}

is injective

note that using the equivalence to Fin partial those maps correspond to injective partial maps
