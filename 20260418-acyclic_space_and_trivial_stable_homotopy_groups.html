acyclic space and trivial stable homotopy groups

Proposition

Let $X$ be an anima TFAE:

  1. $X$ is an acyclic space
  2. $X$ has trivial stable homotopy groups

Proof

observe that reduced homology is given by the homotopy groups of $\mathbb{Z} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \tilde{\mathbb{S}}[X]$. then this statement follows from the stable hurewicz theorem.
