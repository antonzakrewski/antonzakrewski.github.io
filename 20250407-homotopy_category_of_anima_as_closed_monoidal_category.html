homotopy category of anima as closed monoidal category

Proposition

The homotopy category of anima is a cartesian closed category via

  1. projection to the homotopy category preserves products
  2. homotopy mapping space of Anima

Proof

We have to show that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{hAnima}}(X \times_{\mathrm{hAnima}} Y, Z) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{hAnima}}(X, \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{Anima}}(Y,Z)) \end{align*}

For that we notice that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{Anima}}(X \times_{\mathrm{Anima}} Y,Z) \simeq \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{Anima}}(X, \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{Anima}}(Y,Z)) \end{align*}

and the projection to the homotopy category is a functor, hence sends equivalence to equivalence (resp. equivalence to isomorphism). Furthermore both products coincide by 1)
