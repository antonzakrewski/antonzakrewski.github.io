precomposition with a functor preserves colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a cocomplete category, $p: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor. Then precomposition on functor categories

\begin{align*} p^*: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{E}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

preserves colimits

Remark

I think that $\mathcal{C}$ does not always have to be cocomplete, e.g. it suffices that $\mathcal{C}$ admits all necessary colimits for the left kan extension formula I think one has to have some assumptions on $\mathcal{C}$ since not every colimit is computed pointwise
