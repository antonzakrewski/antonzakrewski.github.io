bousfield class of a kappa compact spectrum is kappa compactly generated

Proposition

Let $E$ be a $\kappa$ compact spectrum for $\kappa$ uncountable, then the bousfield class is a $\kappa$ presentable category

Proof

follows from:

  • bousfield class is a localizing subcategory, hence closed under colimits in $\mathrm{Sp}$
  • E-acyclic spectrum is a kappa filtered colimit of kappa compact E-acyclic spectra, hence the collection of $E$ acyclic and $\kappa$ compact objects forms a subcategory $\mathcal{C}$ such that $\langle E\rangle \simeq \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C})$.
