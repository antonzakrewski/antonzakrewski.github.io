rational, compact filtration lemma in a dualizable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C} \in \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}}$ be a actegory. Then for all $x \in \mathcal{C}^{\omega_1}$ there exists a map $\varphi: (\mathbb{Q}, \leq) \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ such that

  1. $\mathrm{colim}_{a \in \mathbb{Q}} \varphi(a) \simeq x$
  2. for all $a \in \mathbb{Q}$, we have $\mathrm{colim}_{b < a} \varphi(b) \simeq \varphi(a)$
  3. for all $a < b$ we have $\varphi(a) \rightarrow \varphi(b)$ is compact.

Proof

define a functor $\mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ with the compact property. inductively define on $\mathbb{Z}[\frac{1}{2}] = \bigcup \frac{a}{2^n}$ where $x_1 \rightarrow x_2 \rightarrow ...$ where each $x_i \rightarrow x_{i+1}$ is compact.
