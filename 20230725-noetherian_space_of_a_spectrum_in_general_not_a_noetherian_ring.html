noetherian space of a spectrum in general not a noetherian ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ the zariski spectrum with the Zariski topology, such that $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ is a Noetherian topological space. Then $A$ is not in general noetherian

Proof

Let $K$ be a field, $K[X_1,X_2,...]$ the polynomial ring in countably many variables. Suppose $A \coloneqq K[X_1,X_2,...]/(X_1^2,X_2^2,...)$. Then $I \coloneqq (X_1,X_2,...)$ is not finitely generated, so $A$ is not noetherian.

Nonetheless, $I$ is the only prime ideal, hence by definition $\mathrm{Spec}(A) = \{I\}$.
