suspension of a manifold in general not a manifold

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological manifold. Then the suspension is in general not

Proof

consider $X = \{1,2,3\}$. Then the suspension of $X$ is given by a bouquet $S^1 \vee S^1$.

alternatively

consider $S^1 \times S^1$. Then the suspension $\Sigma S^1 \times S^1$ is still orientable (e.g. manifold with pi_1 without index 2 subgroup is orientable). But it is not a poincare duality complex (cf. closed manifold as poincare duality complex).
