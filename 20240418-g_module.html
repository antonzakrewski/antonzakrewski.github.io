G-modules

Definition

Let $G$ be a group and $M$ an abelian group. Then a \(G\)-Module consists of $M$ and a group-homomorphism

\begin{align*} G \rightarrow \mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(M) \end{align*}

Alternatively

Let $G$ be group, $BG$ the categorification of a group. Then a \(G\)-module is a functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: BG \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab} \end{align*}
