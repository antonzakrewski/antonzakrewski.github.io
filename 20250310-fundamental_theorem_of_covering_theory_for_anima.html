fundamental theorem of covering theory for anima

Proposition

Let $X$ be an anima, $\mathrm{Cov}(X)$ the category of strict coverings. Then there exists a joyal equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cov}(X) \cong N(\mathrm{Fun}(\pi_{\leq 1} \vert X \vert, \mathrm{Set})) \end{align*}

between the nerve of the 1-categorical functor category

In particular we get an natural equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ho}(\mathrm{Cov}(X)) \cong& \mathrm{Fun}(\pi_{\leq 1} \vert X \vert, \mathrm{Set}) \end{align*}

Proof
