orientation of a closed manifold

Proposition

Let $M$ be an \(n\)-dimensional closed topological manifold and $R$ a ring. Then an orientation is precisely given by an element

\begin{align*} \sigma_X \in H_n(X;R) \end{align*}

which maps to a generator

\begin{align*} H_n(X, X \setminus \{x\};R) \end{align*}

for each $x \in X$

Proof

follows as $\mathcal{K}_{X}^{\mathrm{op}}$ has a minimal element, namely $X$. Therefore an element in the limit is given by an element in

\begin{align*} H_n(X, X \setminus X;R) = H_n(X;R) \end{align*}
