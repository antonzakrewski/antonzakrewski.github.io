amount of path graphs up to isomorphism

Proposition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $V = \{1,...,n\}$ Then there exists $\frac{n!}{2}$ many Graphs isomorphic to the path graph $P_n$

Proof

Note that a Path graph has precisely 2 Automorphisms: the identity and flipping the endpoints. Here we may consider the amount of permutations $\sigma \in S_n$ which determine a path graph via $E = \{\{\sigma(i), \sigma{i+1} \}\}$.

But then the path graphs of $\sigma$ and $n + 1 - \sigma$ are the same, so we have to divide by 2
