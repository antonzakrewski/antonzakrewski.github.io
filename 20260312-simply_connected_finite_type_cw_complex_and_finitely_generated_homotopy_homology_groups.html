simply connected, finite type cw complex and finitely generated homotopy/homology groups

Proposition

Let $X$ be a simply connected anima TFAE:

  1. $X$ is finite type cw complex
  2. $H_n(X, \mathbb{Z})$ is finitely generated for each $n$
  3. $\pi_n(X, \mathbb{Z})$ is finitely generated for each $n$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows similarly to finite type spectrum and finitely generated homotopy and finitely generated homology

2) $\implies$ 3)

follows from fundamental theorem of serre class theory

3) $\implies$ 1)

follows from CW-Approximation of Anima where simply connectedness & serre class theory ensures, that only finitely many cells have to be attached
