cartan formula of stiefel-whitney classes

Proposition

Let $\pi: E \rightarrow B, \pi': E' \rightarrow B'$ be spherical fibrations

Then

\begin{align*} w(\pi \star \pi') = w(\pi) \times w(\pi') \in H^*(B \times B'; \mathbb{F}_2)^{^} \cong H^*(B; \mathbb{F}_2) \times H^*(B'; \mathbb{F}_2) \end{align*}

see:

  • join of spherical fibrations
  • total stiefel whitney class

Proof
