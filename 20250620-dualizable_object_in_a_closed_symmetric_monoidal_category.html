dualizable object in a closed symmetric monoidal category is equivalent to internal hom

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a closed symmetric monoidal infinity category and $X \in \mathcal{C}$ an object. Then the dualizing object of $X$ - if it exists - is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}(X,1) \end{align*}
