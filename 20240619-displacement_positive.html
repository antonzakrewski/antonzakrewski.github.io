displacement strictly positive

Proposition

Let $X$ be a compact metric space and $f: X \rightarrow X$ a continuous map without a fixed point. Then the displacement of $f$ is strictly positive

\begin{align*} \delta(f) \coloneqq \mathrm{inf}_{x \in X}(d(x,f(x)) \end{align*}
