universal element of a functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a locally small category and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} \end{align*}

be a functor.

Then a universal element is a pair $(X,x)$ where $X \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ is an object and $x \in \mathcal{F}(X)$ an element, such that for each $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and $a \in \mathcal{F}(A)$ there exists a unique morphism

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow A \end{align*}

such that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(f)(x) = a \end{align*}
