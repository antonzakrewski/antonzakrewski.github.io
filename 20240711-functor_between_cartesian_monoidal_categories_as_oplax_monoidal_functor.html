functor between cartesian monoidal categories as oplax monoidal functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}$ be cartesian monoidal categories and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

a functor

Then $\mathcal{F}$ is canonically $\mathcal{V}$ oplax monoidal functor

Proof

follows from induced morphism between limits and functor
