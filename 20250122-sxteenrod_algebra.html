F2 Steenrod Algebra

Definition

The steenrod Algebra is defined as free F2-Algebra on the set of the \(\mathbb{F}_2\)-vector space with a basis given by $\mathrm{Sq}^n$ for $n \in \mathbb{N}^0$ modulo the ideal generated by

  1. $1 + \mathrm{Sq}^0$, i.e. making $\mathrm{Sq}^0$ the unit
  2. for all $i < 2j$ the element $R(i,j) = \mathrm{Sq}^i \mathrm{Sq}^j + \sum_{k=0}^{\mathrm{floor}[\frac{i}{2}]} \binom{j - k - 1}{i-2k} \mathrm{Sq}^{i+j-k} \mathrm{Sq}^k$, i.e. imposing the Adem relation of steenrod operations

Note that we are an $\mathbb{F}_2$ Algebra, hence $+ = -$
