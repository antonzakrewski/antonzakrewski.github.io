projective coordinates of the projective space

Definition

Let $k$ be a commutative ring, $R$ a K-Algebra and $a_0,...,a_n \in R$ with $(a_i)= R$. Then we denote by $[a_0 : ... : a_n]$ the quotient line of $R^{n+1}$ given by

\begin{align*} (a_0,...,a_n): R^{n+1} \twoheadrightarrow R \end{align*}

Remark

if $R$ is a field, then this is precisely the standard definition given by $[a_0 : ... : a_n]$ with at least one $a_i \neq 0$.
