Hom in a filtered colimit of categories

Proposition

Let $I: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}$ be a filtered diagram, $\mathrm{colim}_{I} \mathcal{C}_i$ the filtered colimit. Then the Hom is the filtered colimit

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{colim}_{i} \mathcal{C}_i}([x_i],[y_i]) \simeq \mathrm{colim}_{j \in I_{i/}} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_j}(\pi_j(x_i), \pi_j(y_i)) \end{align*}

where $\pi_j$ is the projection.
