subspace of a separable metric space is separable

Proposition

Let $(M,d)$ be a separabler metric space and $S \subseteq M$ a subspace. Then $S$ is also separable.

Proof

Let $X = \{x_n \vert n \geq 1\}$ be a countable dense subset. Then consider $\{(x_n, B_{\frac{1}{m}}(x_n) \vert n,m \in \mathbb{N}^+\}$, which is also countable. Then for each $(x_n, B_{\frac{1}{m}}(x_n))$ choose an $s_{n,m} \in B_{\frac{1}{m}}(x_n) \cap S$ if it exists. Then $\{s_{n,m}\}$ is countable and we claim that it is also dense: Let $s \in S$ be arbitrary and $U$ a neighborhood. Choose an $B_{\frac{1}{l}}(s) \subseteq U$ for $l >> 0$, then there exists an $x_{l'} \in B_{\frac{1}{2l}}(s)$ since $\{x_n\}$ is dense. Moreover, $s \in B_{\frac{1}{2l}}(x_{l'})$, so there exists some $s_{l',l}$. Then it follows from the triangle inequality, that

\begin{align*} d(s_{l',l},s) \leq d(s_{l',l},x_{l'}) + d(x_{l'}, s) < \frac{1}{l} \end{align*}

so $s_{l',l'} \in U$.
