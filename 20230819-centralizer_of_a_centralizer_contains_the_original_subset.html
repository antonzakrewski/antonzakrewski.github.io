centralizer of a centralizer contains the original subset

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group and $S \subseteq G$ a subset. Then for the centralizer it holds,

\begin{align*} S \subseteq C_G(C_G(S)) \end{align*}

Proof

let $s \in S$. Then by construction, for any $g \in C_G(S)$ it holds, that

\begin{align*} g \cdot s = s \cdot g \end{align*}

Hence $s \in C_G(C_G(S))$
