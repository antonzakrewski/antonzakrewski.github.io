amount of circle graphs up to isomorphism

Proposition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $V = \{1,...,n\}$ Then there exists $\frac{n!}{n} = (n-1)!$ many Graphs isomorphic to the path graph $P_n$

Proof

Here we may consider the amount of permutations $\sigma \in S_n$ which determine a circle graph via $E = \{\{\sigma(i), \sigma{i+1} \} \vert i \} \cup \{ \{\sigma(1), \sigma(n)\}\}$.

But then the circle graphs of $\sigma$ and $i + 1 - \sigma$ for $i \in \{1,...,n\}$ are the same, so we have to divide by n
