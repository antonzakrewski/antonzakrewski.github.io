p-cocartesian-liftable morphism induces functor on fibres

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $p: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a inner fibration and $f: d \rightarrow d' \in \mathcal{D}_1$ a morphism, which admits a p-cocartesian lift. Then this induces an infinity functor

\begin{align*} f: \mathcal{C}_d \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{d'} \end{align*}
