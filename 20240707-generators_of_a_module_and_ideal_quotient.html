generators of a module and ideal quotient

Proposition

Let \(A\) be a ring, $M$ a finitely generated \(R\)-module and $\mathfrak{a}$ an ideal. Then

\begin{align*} M/\mathfrak{a} = M \otimes_{A} A/\mathfrak{a} \end{align*}

is a finitely generated $A/\mathfrak{a}$ module.

Proof

follows from staring at representatives and welldefinedness
