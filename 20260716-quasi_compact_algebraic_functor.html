quasi-compact algebraic functor

Definition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor Then $X$ is said to be quasi-compact if, for every Zariski-local epimorphism $\coprod_{i \in I} Y_i \rightarrow X$, there exists a finite subset $J \subseteq$ such that $\coprod_{i \in J} Y_i \rightarrow X$ is a Zariski-local epimorphism
