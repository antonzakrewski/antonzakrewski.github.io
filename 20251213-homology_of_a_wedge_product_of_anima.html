reduced homology of a wedge product of anima

Proposition

Let $(X_i,x_i)$ be anima, then the reduced homology is given by

\begin{align*} \tilde{\mathbb{S}[\bigvee_{i \in I} X_i]} \cong \bigoplus_{i \in I} \tilde{\mathbb{S}}[X] \end{align*}

Proof

follows as

  • homology commutes with filtered colimits
  • homology preserves coproducts
