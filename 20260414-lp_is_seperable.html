Lp for complex valued sequences is separable

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{C}^{\mathbb{N}}$ be the space of compelx valued sequences with the <lp norm>. Then this space is separable

Proof

Let $M_n = \{(x_1,...,x_n,0,0,...) \vert x_1,...,x_n \in \mathbb{Q} + i \mathbb{Q}\}$. Then $M_n$ is countable (cf. Abzählbarkeit des endlichen kartesischen Produkts einer abzählbaren Menge) and moreover $M \coloneqq \bigcup_{n \in \mathbb{N}} M_n$ is also countable (cf. countability of a countable union of countable sets). We claim that $M$ is dense

Let $y \in l^p$ and $\varepsilon > 0$. Observe that $\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} \vert y_j \vert^{p}$ converges, so there exists some $N \in \mathbb{N}$ such that

\begin{align*} \sum_{j = N + 1}^{\infty} \vert y_j \vert^p < \frac{\varepsilon^p}{2} \end{align*}

and as $\mathbb{Q} + i \mathbb{Q}$ is dense in $\mathbb{C}$, there exists some $x$ in M_N$ such that $\sum_{j=1}^N $\vert x_j - y_j \vert < \frac{\varepsilon^p}{2}$. Hence

\begin{align*} d_p(y,x))^p = \| x - y\|_{p}^p < \varepsilon^p \end{align*}
