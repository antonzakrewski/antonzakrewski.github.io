ktop spectrum

Definition

The $\mathrm{ktop}$ spectrum is defined as commutative group completion of symmetric monoidal structure of Euc (resp. their groupoid core)

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ktop} = (\coprod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathrm{BTop}(n))^{\simeq} \end{align*}
