n-th power functor on connective spectra detects zero objects

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Sp}_{\geq}$ the category of connective spectra. Then the functor

\begin{align*} (-)^{\otimes n}: \mathrm{Sp}_{\geq} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Sp}_{\geq} \\ X \mapsto& X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} ... \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} X \end{align*}

detects the zero object

Proof

observe that $- \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z}$ is a symmetric monoidal functor, hence the following diagram commutes

Moreover, $- \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z}$ is a conservative functor (cf. stable hurewicz theorem), hence detects the zero object (cf. conservative, reduced functor detects terminal object)

hence this question reduces to power functor on Z-modules detects zero objects.
