projective algebraic set

Definition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ a subset Then $X$ is said to be a projective algebraic set, if there exists a homogeneous ideal $T \subseteq K[X_1,...,X_n]$ such that

\begin{align*} X = Z(T) \end{align*}
