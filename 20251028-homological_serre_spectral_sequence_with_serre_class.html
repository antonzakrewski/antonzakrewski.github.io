homological serre spectral sequence for a simple map remains inside a serre class

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a serre class and $F \rightarrow E \rightarrow B$ a simple map between anima. Assume that $\tilde{H}(F, \mathbb{Z}), \tilde{H}(B, \mathbb{Z}) \in \mathcal{C}$.

Then it follows for the homology serre spectral sequence that $E_{p,q}^2 \in \mathcal{C}$ for any $p,q \in \mathbb{Z}$.

In particular $\tilde{H}(E,\mathbb{Z}) \in \mathcal{C}$.
