K-equivalence of c-star algebras

Definition

Let $\mathbb{K}$. Then a morphism $f: A \rightarrow B$ is a $\mathbb{K}$ equivalence if

\begin{align*} f \otimes \mathrm{id}_{\mathbb{K}: A \otimes \mathbb{K} \rightarrow B \otimes \mathbb{K} \end{align*}

is a homotopy equivalence in the top-enriched category of category of nonunital C-star algebras
