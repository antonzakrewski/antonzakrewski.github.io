Proposition

Let $\mathbb{Z}[p^{-1}]/\mathbb{Z}$ be the p-prüfer group. Then the ordinary p-completion is the zero object

Proof

Observe that

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Z}[p^{-1}]/\mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\cdot p^n} \mathbb{Z}[p^{-1}]/\mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

is an epimorphism for $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Hence - ordinary - $\mathbb{Z}[p^{-1}]/\mathbb{Z}/p^n$ vanishes.
