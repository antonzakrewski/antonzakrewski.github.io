pointed maps up to homotopy between classifying spaces of discrete groups

Proposition

Let $G,H$ be discrete topological groups, then

\begin{align*} \pi_0(-) \end{align*}

induces a Bijection on pointed sets

\begin{align*} [BG, BH]_* \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Grp}}(G,H) \end{align*}

Proof

Choose a presentation

i.e. exact at $F_I$

Let $X(G)$ be a presentation complex of $G$. Then we may build $BG$ by attaching cells of dimension $\geq 3$ to kill non-zero elements in higher homotopy groups.

Using the coexact puppe sequence we may conclude that

\begin{align*} [BG, BH]_* \rightarrow [X(G), BH] \end{align*}

is a Bijection
