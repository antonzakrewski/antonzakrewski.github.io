Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, then the canonical map $R \rightarrow \prod_{\mathfrak{m}} R_{\mathfrak{m}}$ is a monomorphism

Proof

Let $r \in R \setminus R^{\times}$, then there exists a maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$ with $r \in \mathfrak{m}$. Hence $R \rightarrow \prod_{\mathfrak{m}} R_{\mathfrak{m}} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{pr}_{R_{\mathfrak{m}}}} R_{\mathfrak{m}}$ todo
