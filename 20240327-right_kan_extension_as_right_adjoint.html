right Kan extension as right adjoint

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be categories and $p: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$. Then the left kan extension of $p$ is defined, if it exists as right adjoint

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ran}_p: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{E}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}) \end{align*}

to the left adjoint

\begin{align*} p^{ \rightarrow}: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{E}) \end{align*}

Proof
