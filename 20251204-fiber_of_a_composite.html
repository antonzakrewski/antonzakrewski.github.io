fiber of a composite is a pullback

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a pointed category and $X \xrightarrow{f} Y \xrightarrow{g} Z$ be a composition Then there exists a canonical equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{fib}(g \circ f) \simeq \mathrm{fib}(g) \times_{Y} X \end{align*}

Proof

follows from the glueing lemma for pullbacks
