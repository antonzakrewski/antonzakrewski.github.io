cat dual stable is complete

Proposition

The category $\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}}^{\mathrm{dual}}$ is complete .

Proof

Let $\mathcal{C}: I \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}}^{\mathrm{dual}}$ and $\mathcal{D}$ hin $\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}}$.

Then we want to show that

\begin{align*} \lim_{I} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}}^{\mathrm{dual}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is representable.

So 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\lim_{I} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\kappa})) \lar \dar & \lim_{I} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}}^{\mathrm{dual}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C}) \dar \\
\lim_{I} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathrm{Ind}(\mathrm{Calk}^{\mathrm{cont}}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C})) & \lar * 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

is a pullback diagram. 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathrm{Ind}(\lim \mathcal{C}^{\kappa}))& \lar{\simeq} \lim_{I} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\kappa})) \lar \dar & \lim_{I} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}}^{\mathrm{dual}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C}) \dar \\
& \lim_{I} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathrm{Ind}(\mathrm{Calk}^{\mathrm{cont}}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C})) & \lar 0 \dar  \\
& \prod_{I} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathrm{Ind}(\mathrm{Calk}_{\kapap}^{\mathrm{cont}}, \mathcal{C}) & \lar 0  
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

So we are interseted in the fiber of the map $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}}^{\mathrm{dual}}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathrm{Ind}(\lim_{I} \mathcal{C}^{\kappa})) \rightarrow \lim_{I} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}}^{\mathrm{dual}}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C})$.

  • Image of a strongly continuous functor out of a dualizable stable category is dualizable

imply that $\lim^{\mathrm{dual}} \mathcal{C}$ is the dualizable fiber of $\mathrm{Ind}(\lim_{I} \mathcal{C}^{\kapap}) \rightarrow \prod_{i \in I} \mathrm{Ind}(\mathrm{Calk}_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{cont}} \mathcal{C}_i)$
