Proposition

Being a quasi-compact morphism is local on the base

Proof

down to open subschemes

follows from glueing lemma for pullbacks:

if $A$ is an open quasi-compact subscheme,

from local to global

suppose there exists an open cover such that $U_i \times_{X} Y \rightarrow U_i$ is quasi-compact. Then let $A \subseteq X$ be an open, quasi-compact subscheme. In particular, $\vert A \vert$ is compact and is covered by $U_i$.

Therefore there exist finitely many $U_1,...,U_n$ such that $A \subseteq U_1,...,U_n$. Hence since preimage commute with everything, we have that $f^{-1}[(\bigcup_{i=1}^n U_i) \cap A] = \bigcup_{i=1}^n f^{-1}[U_i \cap A]$ is the union of finitely many compact subspaces, hence compact.
