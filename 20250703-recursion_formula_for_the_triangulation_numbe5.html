triangulation number as catalan number

Proposition

The triangulation number is given by the catalan number up to index shift, i.e.

\begin{align*} T_n = B_{n-2} \end{align*}

Proof

we show that it satisfies the recursion formula for amount of binary trees:

For $T_2,T_3 = 1$ we can show this by hand resp. convention.

Let $n \geq 3$. Then choose a numbering of the knots.

Let $A_k$ be the number of triangulations with $1-2-k$ as triangle. Then

\begin{align*} T_n = \sum_{k=3}^n A_k \end{align*}

But each triangulation of $A_k$ is given by a triangulation of $2,...,k$ and $1,k,k+1,...n$. Thus

\begin{align*} A_k = T_{k-1} \cdot T_{n-k-2} \end{align*}

which shows

\begin{align*} T_n = \sum_{k=3}^n T_{k-1} \cdot T_{n-k+2} \end{align*}
