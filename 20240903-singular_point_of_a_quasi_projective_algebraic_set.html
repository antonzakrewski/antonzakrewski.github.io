singular point of a quasi-projective algebraic set

Definition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ a quasi-projective algebraic set.

Then a point $P \in X$ is said to be singular, if it is not nonsingular

i.e. a) lies in two irreducible components or b) $\mathrm{dim} T_p(X_i) \neq \mathrm{dim}(X_i)$ for $P \in X_i$
