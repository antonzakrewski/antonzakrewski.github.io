montel theorem

Proposition

Let $\Omega \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be open and $f_n$ a sequence of holomorphic functions that is uniformly bounded on compact subsets Then there exists a subsequence $f_{n_k}$ that converges uniformly on compact subsets of $\Omega$ to a holomorphic function on $\Omega$

Proof

reduction to single compact subset

Suppose that for any compact subset there exists a uniformly convergent sequence. Then consider

\begin{align*} K_{l} \coloneqq \{x\in \Omega : \vert x \vert \leq l, d(x,\Omega^{c}) \geq \frac{1}{l}\} \end{align*}

which is closed and bounded, hence compact as $\mathbb{C}$ is a proper metric space.

where $l \in \{ \frac{1}{n}, n \in \mathbb{N}^+ \}$.

Then iteratively choose elements of subsequences such that we get a single subsequence which converges uniformly on each $K_l$ see: compact subset of a proper metric space as subset of a countable, compact family

In other symbols

\begin{align*} \forall \varepsilon > 0 \exists \delta > 0 \forall n \forall x,y \in K : \vert x - y \vert \leq \delta \implies \vert f_n(x) - f_n(y) \vert \leq \varepsilon \end{align*}

Let $d \coloneqq \mathrm{dist}(K, \Omega^c) > 0$ (cf. Abstand zwischen einer abgeschlossenen und kompakten Menge).

Let $x,y \in K$ with $\vert x - y \vert \leq d$. Then for any $t \in [0,1]$ we have

\begin{align*} d(tx + (1 - t)y, K) \leq \mathrm{min}(\vert tx + (1 - t) y - x \vert, \vert tx + (1 - t)y - y \vert) = \mathrm{min}(t,1-t) \vertx - y \vert \leq \frac{d}{w} \end{align*}

and thus

\begin{align*} \vert f_n(x) - f_n(y) \vert =& \vert \int_{0}^1 (x - y) f'_n(tx + (1 - t)y) \vert \mathrm{d}t \vert \\ \leq& \mathrm{sup}_{x \in K_{\frac{d}{w}}} \vert f'_n(z) \vert \cdot \vert x - y \vert \end{align*}

where $K_{\varepsilon} \coloneqq \{z \in \mathbb{C} : d(z,K) \leq \varepsilon \}
