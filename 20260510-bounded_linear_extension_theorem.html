bounded linear extension theorem

Proposition

Let $X$ be a normed vector space, $X_0 \subseteq X$ a linear subspace and $Y$ a banach space. Let $T: X_0 \rightarrow Y$ be a bounded operator such that $X_0 \subseteq X$ is dense.

Then there exists a unique bounded linear extension

\begin{align*} \tilde{T}: X \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

with $\| \tilde{T} \| = \| T \|$.

\begin{align*} \| T \| = \mathrm{sup}_{x \in X_0 \setminus \{0\}} \frac{\| T x \|}{\| x \|} = \mathrm{sup}_{x \in X_0 \setminus \{0\}} \frac{\| \tilde{T} x \|}{\| x \|} \geq \mathrm{sup}_{x \in X \setminus \{0\}} \frac{\| T x \|}{\| x \|} = \| \tilde{T} \| \end{align*}

So it remains to show $\| T \| \geq \| \tilde{T} \|$. Now let $\varepsilon > 0$, then we it suffices to show that $\| T \| \geq \| \tilde{T} \| - \varepsilon$. By assumption, there exists an $x \in X$ with $\frac{\| \tilde{T} x \|}{\| x \|} > \| \tilde{T} \| - \varepsilon$. Moreover, choose a convergent sequence $x_n \rightarrow x$ with $x_n \in X_0$. We may assume that $x \neq 0$ and $x_n \neq 0$ for almost all $n$.

Then this follows as

\begin{align*} X_0 \rightarrow& \mathbb{R} \\ x \mapsto \frac{\| T(x) \|}{\| x \|} = \frac{\| \tilde{T}(x) \|}{\| x \|} \end{align*}

is continuous (in particular sequentially continuous).

So $\frac{\| T(x_n) \|}{\| x_n \|} \rightarrow \frac{\| \tilde{T}(x) \|}{\| x \|} > \| \tilde{T} \| - \varepsilon$, so in particular $\| T \| \geq \| \tilde{T} \| - \varepsilon$.
