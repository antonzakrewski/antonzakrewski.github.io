sheaf of rational functions

Definition

Let $X$ be a scheme. Then the sheaf of rational functions $\mathcal{K}_{X}$ on $\vert X \vert$ is the sheafification of the ring-valued presheaf

\begin{align*} U \rightarrow \mathcal{O}(U)[S_{U}^{-1}] \end{align*}

where $S_{U} \subseteq \mathcal{O}(U)$ is the multiplicative subset of all functions $f$ such that $f_{\vert V}$ is not a zero divisor in $\mathcal{O}(V)$ for all opens $V \subseteq U$.
