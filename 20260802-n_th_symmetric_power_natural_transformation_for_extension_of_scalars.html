n-th symmetric power natural transformation for extension of scalars

Proposition

The n-th symmetric power functor is a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sym}_(-}^n: \mathrm{Mod}_* \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}_* \end{align*}

Proof

use:

  • extension of scalars is symmetric monoidal functor
