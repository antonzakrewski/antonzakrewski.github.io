shortification of an exact sequence

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and

a sequence exact at $B$ and $C$

Then the sequnce

is also exact

Proof

Mitchell's embedding theorem

injectivity of $\iota$ follows from $\mathrm{ker}(f_2) = \mathrm{im}(f_1)$, (?)

Surjectivity of $f_3$ follows from construction.

exactness at $C$:

Since the diagram commutes

\begin{align*} B \arrow[swap]{rdd}{0} \arrow[swap]{rd}{f_2} \arrow[]{r}{\pi} & B/\mathrm{ker}(f_2) = A/\mathrm{im}(f_1) \arrow[]{d}{f_2'} \arrow[bend right]{dd}{0}\\ & C \arrow[]{d}{f_3'} \\ & \mathrm{im}(f_3) \\ \end{align*}

we get by surjectivity of $\pi$, that

\begin{align*} f_3 \circ f_2' = 0 \end{align*}

hence $\mathrm{im}(f_2') \subseteq \mathrm{ker}(f_3)$

Furthermore, for $c \in \mathrm{ker}(f_3)$, there exists an $b \in B$ such that $f_2(b) = c$ Taking $[b] \in B/\mathrm{ker}(f_2)$ gives

\begin{align*} f_3'([b]) = f_3(b) = c \end{align*}
