category of nonunital star algebras is bicomplete

Proposition

The category of nonunital star algebras is bicomplete.

Proof

limit

using the free nonunital star algebra functor, we can observe that the if the limit exists, then the set underlying set agrees with the limit in $\mathrm{*Alg}^{\mathrm{nu}$. Here we can invoke the usual formula for limits of sets (cf. todo) and since each transition map preserves all operations, the underlying set inherits a pointwise *-algebra. Hence the underlying set admits a star algebra structure and the projections also are maps of star algebras By a similar argument, this also satisfies the universal property.

colimit

coproduct

Let $A_i $ be a family, then we claim that the underlying nonunital, noncommutative $\mathbb{C}$ algebra is the noncommutative, nonunital free coproduct,i.e. the free nonunital star algebra

\begin{align*} \coprod A_i = \mathrm{free}(\coprod_{i} \mathcal{U}(A_i))/I \end{align*}

modulo the ideal generated by $[i,a + b] = [i,a] + [i,b], [i,a^*] = [i,a]^*, [i,ab] = [i,a] \cdot [i,b], [i, \lambda a] = \lambda [i,a]$ Here $\mathcal{U}(A_i)$ is the underlying set

Now the inclusions $A_i \rightarrow \coprod A_i$ is a map of star algebras and it satisfies the universal property.

general colimit

Let $A: I \rightarrow \mathrm{*Alg}^{\mathrm{nu}}$ be a diagram. then we claim that $\mathrm{colim}_{I} A \cong (\coprod_{i \in I} A(i)/J$ for

\begin{align*} J = \langle [j,A(u)a] = [i,a] \vert u: i \rightarrow j\rangle \end{align*}

(here $u$ is a transition map).
