trivial Hom into Q/Z in abelian groups

Proposition

Let $A$ be an abelian group. TFAE:

  1. $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(A, \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})$ is the zero object
  2. $A$ is the zero object

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Proof by contraposition Let $A$ be a nonzero group. Then it suffices to show that $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(A, \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})$ is nonzero. So let $a$ be a nonzero element in $A$, then choose a nonzero map $\langle a\rangle \rightarrow \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$ (e.g. pontrjagin duality for finite abelian groups).
