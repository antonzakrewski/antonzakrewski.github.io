singular homology of a complex projective space

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n$ be the complex projective space Then the singular homology is given by

\begin{align*} H_k(\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } k \equiv 0 \mod{2}, 0 \leq \frac{k}{2} \leq n \\ 0 & \mbox{if } k > 2n \\ 0 & \mbox{if } k \equiv 1 \mod{2} \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof
