idempotent completion functor

Definition

Let $\mathrm{Cat}$ be the category of Categories and $\mathrm{Cat}_{\natural}$ the category of idempotent complete categories. Then the idempotent completion functor is defined as left adjoint to the fully faithful inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cat}_{\natural} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Cat} \end{align*}
