q-weyl algebra does not admit finite dimensional representations for non roots of unities

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $q \neq 0$ an element, which is not a root of unity. Then the q-Weyl algebra does not admit finite dimensional representations.

Proof

Suppose $V$ is a finite dimensional representation, then $x$ and $y$ act by automorphisms. So we can look at the determinant and get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{det}(xy) = \mathrm{det}(q^{\pm 1} yx) \\ =& q^{\pm n} \mathrm{det}(xy) \end{align*}

which implies that $q^n = 1$
