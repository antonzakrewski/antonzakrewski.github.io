Proposition

Let $n \geq 2$ and $G_1,G_2$ be abelian groups Then there exists a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \pi_n(-,G_1) \times \pi_n(-,G_2) \times \cong \pi_n(-, G_1 \oplus G_2) \end{align*}

Proof

follows as $M(G_1,n) \vee M(G_2,n)$ is a simply connected Moore space of the form $M(G_1 \oplus G_2,n)$ which is unique
