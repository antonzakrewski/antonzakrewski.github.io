universal property of animation

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a \(1\)-category which is cocomplete and generated under colimits by $\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}}$ (cf. 1-sfp object) and $\mathcal{A}$ an infinity category which admits infinity sifted colimits. Then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{sifted}}(\mathrm{Ani}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{A}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}}, \mathcal{A}) \end{align*}

where

  1. $\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}}$ denotes the full subcategory of 1-sfp objects
  2. $\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{sifted}}(-,-)$ denotes the infinity sifted colimit preserving functors
