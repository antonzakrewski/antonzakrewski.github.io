cancelling contraction and extension

Proposition

Let $f: A \rightarrow B$ be a ring-homomorphism for rings $A,B$. Then we get for the contraction and extension of an ideal $\mathfrak{b} \subseteq B$

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{b}^{c} = \mathfrak{b}^{cec} \end{align*}

Proof

$\subseteq$

follows as

\begin{align*} \varphi^{-1}[\varphi[\varphi^{-1}[\mathfrak{b}]]] =& \varphi^{-1}[\mathfrak{b}] \\ \subseteq& \varphi^{-1}[(\mathfrak{b}^{c})^{e}] \end{align*}

by Cancelling Preimage and Image of Subsets and extension of the contraction as a subset

$\supseteq$

follows from preimage and subsets, where by contraction of the extension as superset

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{b} \supseteq \mathfrak{b}^{ec} \end{align*}
