limit of chain complexes

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a pointed category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{C})$ the category of chain complexes. Suppose there exists a diagram $J: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{C})$ and all limits $\mathrm{lim}_{J_n}$, then a limit of the chain complexes is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_{J} = (... \rightarrow \mathrm{lim}_{J_n} \rightarrow \mathrm{lim}_{J_n} \rightarrow ... ) \end{align*}
