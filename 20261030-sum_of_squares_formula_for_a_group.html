sum of squares formula for a group

Proposition

Let $G$ a finite group $G$ and $k$ a field with $\vert G \vert \not\mid \mathrm{char}(k)$. Let $\{[V_i]\}$ be the set of isomorphism classes of finite dimensional simple representations

Then

\begin{align*} \vert G \vert = \sum_{i} \mathrm{dim}_{k}(V_i)^2 \end{align*}

Proof
