fiber of a split mono

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a finitely complete pointed category and $f: A \hookrightarrbw B$ a split mono. Let $g: B \twoheadrightarrow A$ be a one sided inverse. Then the fiber of $A \hookrightarrow B$ is given by $\Omega \mathrm{fib}(g)$.
