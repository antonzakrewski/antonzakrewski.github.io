categorification of commutative Algebras in a presentably symmetric monoidal category

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C} \in \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\kappa})$ be kappa presentable, Then the categorification functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Mod}_{C}(\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\kappa})) \\ A \mapsto& \mathrm{Mod}_{A}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

e.g.

If we take $\mathcal{C} = \mathrm{Sp}$, then this amounts to mapping a commutative ring spectrum $A$ to the derived category $\mathcal{D}(A)$ with its closed symmetric monoidal structure.
