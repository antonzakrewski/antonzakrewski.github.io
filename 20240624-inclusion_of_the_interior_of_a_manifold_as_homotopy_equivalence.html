inclusion of the interior of a manifold as homotopy equivalence

Proposition

Let $W$ be a \(n+1\)-dimensional topological manifold with boundary. Then the inclusion of the interior

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathrm{int}(W) \hookrightarrow W \end{align*}

is a Homotopy equivalence

Proof

follows from existence of collar neighbourhoods

Choose a collar neihgbourhood $V \simeq \partial W \times [0,1)$ of $\partial W$. Then one can define a homotopy inverse given by

\begin{align*} \pi: W \rightarrow& \mathrm{int}(W) \\ \begin{cases} (w,t) \mapsto& (w,\frac{1}{2} t) \\ w' \mapsto& w' & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
