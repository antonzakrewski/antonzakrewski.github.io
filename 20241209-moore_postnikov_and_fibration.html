Moore-Postnikov and fibration

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a continuous map between pathconnected spaces. Let $X \rightarrow Z_n \rightarrow Y$ be a Moore-Postnikov stage.

Then we may w.l.o.g. assume that $Z_n \rightarrow Y$ is a serre fibration

Proof
