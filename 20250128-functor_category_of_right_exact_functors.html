functor category of right exact functors

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be infinity categories where $\mathcal{D}$ is finitely cocomplete. Then define

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{RightExact}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}) \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}) \end{align*}

to be the full sub infinity category spanned by infinity functors preserving finite infinity colimit.
