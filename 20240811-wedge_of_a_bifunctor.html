wedge of a bifunctor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a bifunctor Then a wedge is defined as object $e \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{D})$ and morphisms

\begin{align*} \theta_c: e \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(c,c) \end{align*}
