naturally isomorphic functors and preserving epimorphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}_1,\mathcal{F}_2: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ functors such that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_1 \cong \mathcal{F}_2 \end{align*}

are naturally isomorphic

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}_1$ preserves epimorphisms
  2. $\mathcal{F}_2$ preserves epimorphisms

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)
