degreewise split exact sequence

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes. A sequence of chain complexes

is said to be degreewise split exact if $\iota$ is a degreewise split mono chain map or degreewise split epi chain map
