faithful functor reflects monomorphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a faithful functor. Then $\mathcal{F}$ reflects monomorphisms

Proof

Suppose $\mathcal{F}(f) \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{D})$ is a monomorphism. Suppose that there exist $g,h \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$, such that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(f \circ g) = \mathcal{F}(f \circ h) \end{align*}

then by functorality

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(f) \circ \mathcal{F}(g) = \mathcal{F}(f) \circ \mathcal{F}(h) \end{align*}

and since $\mathcal{F}(f)$ is a monomorphism, we conclude, that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(g) = \mathcal{F}(h) \end{align*}

hence by faithfulness,

\begin{align*} g = h \end{align*}
