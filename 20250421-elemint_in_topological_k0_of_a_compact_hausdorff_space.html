element in topological k0 of a compact hausdorff space

Proposition

Let $X$ be compact hausdorff and $[E_1] - [E_2] \in K_0(X)$. Then there exists a bundle $E'$ with

\begin{align*} [E_1] - [E_2] = [E''] - n \cdot 1 \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • vector bundle over a locally compact hausdorff space as direct summand of a trivial bundle

where

\begin{align*} [E_2] = n - [E_3] \end{align*}

by choosing $n = [E_2 \oplus E_3]

Therefore

\begin{align*} [E_1] - [E_2] =& [E_1] - n + [E_3] \\ =& [E_1 \oplus E_3] - n \end{align*}

which shows the claim for $E'' =E_3$
