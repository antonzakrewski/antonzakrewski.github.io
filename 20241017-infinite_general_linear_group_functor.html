infinite general linear group functor

Definition

the infinite general linear group functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{GL}: \mathrm{Ring} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Grp} \\ R \mapsto& \mathrm{GL}(R) \\ (f: R \rightarrow S) \mapsto& (\mathrm{GL}(f): \mathrm{GL}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{GL}(S)) \end{align*}
