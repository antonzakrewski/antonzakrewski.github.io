vector bundle over a finite dimensional cw complex and decomposition

Proposition

Let $X$ be a connected CW-Complex with $\mathrm{dim}(X) = n$ (cf. dimension of a CW-Complex). Let $E$ be a topological vector bundle with $\mathrm{dim}(E) > n$ then there exists another vector bundle $E'$ and

\begin{align*} E = E' \oplus E^{\mathrm{dim}(E') - n} \end{align*}

Proof

choose a section $s: E \rightarrow X$ which is globally nontrivial (cf. high dimensional vector bundle over a cw complex admits globally nontrivial section) using section of a vector bundle as morphism from 1-dimensional trivial bundle

\begin{align*} X \times \mathbb{K} \rightarrow X \end{align*}

this corresponds to a bundle map of locally constant rank.

In particular the image of a map of vector bundles is a vector bundle and by subbundle for a paracompact space as direct summand we get

\begin{align*} E \simeq E^{\bot} \oplus \mathrm{im}(s) \end{align*}

Here

\begin{align*} s: X \times \mathbb{K} \rightarrow \mathrm{im}(f) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism of bundles, hence $\mathrm{im}(f)$ is a trivial bundle of rank 1.

Thus

\begin{align*} E \simeq& E^{\bot} \oplus \mathrm{im}(s) \\ \simeq& E^{\bot} \oplus E^1 \end{align*}

for $E' \coloneqq E^{\bot}$

by repeated splitting, we get

\begin{align*} E \simeq E' \oplus E^{\mathrm{dim}(E) - n} \end{align*}
