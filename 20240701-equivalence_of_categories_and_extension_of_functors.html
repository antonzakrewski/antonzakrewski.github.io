equivalence of categories and extension of functors

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an equivalence. Suppose $\mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ is a functor, then there exists an extension in the sense that for a full subcategory $\overline{\mathcal{C}}$ such that the restriction

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_{\overline{\mathcal{C}}}: \overline{\mathcal{C}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

is object-injective functor (e.g. a skeleton of a category)

commutes

Proof

using adjoint equivalence of categories and fully faithful and essentially surjective functor:

For each object $D \in \mathcal{D}$ choose by essentially surjectivity an object $C_{D}$ such that $\mathcal{F}(C_{D}) \simeq D$. Then choose a fixed isomorphism $f_{D}: \mathcal{F}(C_{D}) \simeq C$

If $D$ is in the Image of $\mathcal{F}$ we may w.l.o.g. choose $\mathrm{id}: \mathcal{F}(C_D) = D$ for some $C_D$

Then define

\begin{align*} \mathcal{G}': \mathcal{D} \rightarrow& \mathcal{E} \\ D \mapsto& \mathcal{G}(C_D) \\ (g: D_1 \rightarrow D_2) \mapsto& \mathcal{G}(\overline{g}): \mathcal{G}(C_{D_1}) \rightarrow \mathcal{G}(C_{D_2}) \end{align*}

where $\overline{g}$ is defined by the unique morhpism $\overline{g}: \mathcal{F}(C_{D_1}) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(C_{D_2})$ such that

commutes (note that $\mathcal{F}$ is fully faithful)
