product with infinite intersection of an ideal

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ an ideal. Then

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a} \cdot (\bigcap_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathfrak{a}^n) =& (\bigcap_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathfrak{a}^n) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from definitions and logic
