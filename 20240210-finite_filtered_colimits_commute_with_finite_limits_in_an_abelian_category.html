finite filtered colimits commute with finite limits in an abelian category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{colim} \mathrm{lim}$ a

  1. finite colimit filtered colimit and
  2. finite limit

Then both of them commute

Proof

corollary of:

  • Mitchell's embedding theorem, which preserves finite limits and colimits

using

  • filtered colimits commute with finite limits in RMod they commute in $\mathrm{RMod}$

by

  • fully faithful functor reflects limits / fully faithful functor reflects colimits
