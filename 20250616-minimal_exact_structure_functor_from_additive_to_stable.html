minimal exact structure functor from additive to exact categories

Definition / Proposition

The minimal exact structure functor is defined as infinity functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cat}^{\prod} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{Ex}}\\ \mathcal{C} \mapsto& (\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{min}) \mathcal{F} \mapsto& \mathcal{F} \end{align*}

Proof

using minimal exact structure of an additive category we know that $(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{min})$ is a welldefined exact structure. Thus it suffices to show that an additive $\mathcal{F}$ is a welldefined exact functor
