Cartan Eilenberg about Tor and Ext

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring of projective dimension $\leq 1$. Then there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ext}_R^1(A, \mathrm{Ext}_R^1(B,C)) \cong& \mathrm{Ext}_R^1(\mathrm{Tor}_1^R(A \otimes_{R} B), C) \end{align*}

or

\begin{align*} \pi_{-1}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(A, \pi_{-1}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(B,C)))) \cong& \pi_{-1}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(\pi_{1}(A \otimes B) C)) \end{align*}

Proof

Recall that there exists a derived tensor hom adjunction

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(A, \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(B,C)) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(A \otimes B,C) \end{align*}

and hence also an isomorphism (after shifting)

\begin{align*} \pi_{-1}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(A, \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(B,C)[1])) \simeq \pi_{-1}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}((A \otimes B)[-1],C)) \end{align*}

Since $R$ has projective dimension $\leq 1$, this implies that

  1. $A \otimes -$ loses only one degree of coconnectivity
  2. $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(A,-)$ loses only one degree of connectivity

Furthermore, the we have fiber sequences

  1. $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(B,C)[1] \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(B,C)[1] \rightarrow \tau_{\leq 0}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}(R)}(B,C)[1]) \cong \mathrm{Ext}_R^1(B,C)$
  2. $\mathrm{Tor}_1^R(A,B) \cong \tau_{\geq 0}(A \otimes B) \rightarrow (A \otimes B)[-1] \rightarrow (A \otimes^{\heartsuit} B)[-1]$

Now we may pass to the mapping spectra
