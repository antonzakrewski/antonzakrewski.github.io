right exact functor from R-Mod determined by free modules

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod and $\mathcal{A}$ an abelian category Then a right exact functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{RMod} \rightarrow \mathcal{A} \end{align*}

is determined by the class of free modules

Proof

analogously to right exact functor determined by projective objects where the free modules provide free resolutions (and hence projective resolutionswa
