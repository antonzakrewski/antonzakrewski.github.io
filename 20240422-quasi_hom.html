infinity hom of a infinity category

Definition

Let $X$ be a quasicategory, $x,y \in X_0$ objects. Then the quasi hom $\mathrm{Hom}_{X}(x,y)$ is defined as pullback 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
|[label={[label distance=-2mm]-45:\lrcorner}]|
\mathrm{Hom}_{X}(x,y) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\Delta^1, X)} \arrow[]{d}{(\mathrm{ev}_0, \mathrm{ev}_1)}\\
\Delta^0 \arrow[]{r}{(x,y)} & X_0 \times X_0 \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
