Proposition

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
1 \otimes (x \otimes y) \arrow[]{rr}{\alpha_{1,x,y}} \arrow[]{d}{1 \otimes \lambda_{x,y}} & & (1 \otimes x) \otimes y \arrow[]{ld}{(\lambda_x \otimes y)} \\
& (x \otimes y) &  \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
