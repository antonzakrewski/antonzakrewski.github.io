odd cup square is a 2-torsion element

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $H^*(-)$ the singular cohomology functor and $H^*(X)$ the graded-commutative ring. Then for a homogeneous element $\alpha \in H^{2n + 1}(X)$

\begin{align*} 2 \cdot \alpha^2 = 0 \end{align*}
