etale map between commutative rings

Definition

Let $f: A \rightarrow B$ be a ring-homomorphism between commutative rings then $f$ is said to be etale, if the following three conditions are satisfied

  1. $B$ is a finitely presented A-Algebra
  2. $f$ is a flat ring morphism
  3. the multiplication map
\begin{align*} p: B \otimes_{A} B \rightarrow B \end{align*}

is the projection onto a summand
