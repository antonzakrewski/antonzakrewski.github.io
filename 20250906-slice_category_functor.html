slice category functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, then the slice category functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}_{/(-)}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat}_\infty\\ x \mapsto& \mathcal{C}_{/x} \\ (f: x \rightarrow y) \mapsto& (\mathcal{C}_{/x} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{/y}) \\ \end{align*}
