symmetric monoidal localization induces monoidal structure

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{L}: \mathrm{Pr}^{L} \rightarrow \mathrm{Pr}^L$ be a symmetric monoidal localization. Let $\mathcal{C} \in \mathrm{Pr}^{L}$ be a closed symmetric monoidal infinity functor

Then $\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{C})$ admits a unique symmetric monoidal structure, such that the localization

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{L}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is symmetric monoidal

Proof
