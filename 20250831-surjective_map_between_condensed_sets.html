surjective map between condensed sets

Definition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a morphism of condensed sets. Then $f$ is said to be surjective, if for all $T\}n \mathrm{tdCH}$ and $y \in Y(T)$ there exists a cover $\mathcal{T} = \{T_i \rightarrow T\}_{i \in I}$ of $T$ such that each $y_{i} = p^*_{i}(y) \in Y(T_i)$ lifts to an element $x_i \in X(T_i)
