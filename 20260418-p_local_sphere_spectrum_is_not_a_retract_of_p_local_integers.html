p-localized sphere spectrum is not a retract of p-localized integers

Proposition

Let $p$ be a prime, then $\mathbb{S}_{(p)}$ is not a retract of $H \mathbb{Z}_{(p)}$.

Proof

observe that the $p$ local homotopy groups of $\mathbb{S}$ are not trivial (cf. some p-local stable homotopy groups) This shows the desired claim
