functor from Top to the category of totally disconnected space

Proposition

Given category Top, there exists a covariant functor to the category of totally disconnected spaces

\begin{align*} \mathrm{\pi}: \mathrm{Top} \rightarrow& \mathrm{td-spaces} \\ X \mapsto& \pi(X) \\ (f: X \rightarrow Y) \mapsto& (f': \pi(x) \mapsto \pi(f(x))) \end{align*}

where $\pi(X)$ is equipped with the quotient topology
