Kurvenintegral und Orientierung

Proposition

Let $\gamma: [0,1]$ be a piecewise c^1 curve, $\gamma^{-1}$ the same curve but with different orientation, i.e. $\gamma^{-1} \coloneqq \gamma(b - t \cdot a)$. Then

\begin{align*} \int_{\gamma^{-1}} f(z) \mathrm{d}z =& - \int_{\gamma}(f(z) \mathrm{d}z \end{align*}

Proof

w.l.o.g. assume that $\gamma$ is $C^1$, the general case follows from splitting $\gamma$ into parts.
