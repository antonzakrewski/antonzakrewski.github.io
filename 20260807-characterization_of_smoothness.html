characterization of smoothness

Proposition

Let $f: Y \rightarrow X$ be morphisms of locally finite presentation TFAE:

  1. (Jacobian criterion) $f$ is a smooth morphism
  2. (infinitesimal lifting porperty) $f$ is formally smooth map
  3. (cotangent complex) The <cotangent bundle> $\Omega_f \in \mathrm{Mod}_{Y}$ is an quasi-coherent vector bundle and the conormal module $N_{f} \in \mathrm{Mod}_{Y}$ is zero

Proof
