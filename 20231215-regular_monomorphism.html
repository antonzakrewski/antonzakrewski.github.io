regular monomorphism

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $B,C \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ objects.

A regular monomorphism is a morphism

\begin{align*} f: B \rightarrow C \end{align*}

such that $f$ with $B$ is a equalizer, i.e. there exist some $A$ and morphisms $g_1,g_2: A \rightarrow B$ such that 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
A \arrow[bend left]{r}{g_1} \arrow[bend right]{r}{g_2} & B \arrow[]{f}{} & C
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

is universal
