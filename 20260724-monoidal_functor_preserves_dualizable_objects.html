monoidal functor preserves dualizable objects

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}}), (\mathcal{W}, \otimes_{\mathcal{W}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{W}})$ be monoidal categories. Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{V} \rightarrow \mathcal{W}$ be a monoidal functor. Then $\mathcal{F}$ preserves dualizable objects and their duals.
