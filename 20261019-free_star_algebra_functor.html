free nonunital star algebra functor

Definition / Proposition

There exists a left adjoint to the forgetful functor $\mathrm{*Alg}^{\mathrm{nu}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$.

Proof

using pointwise construction of left adjoints:

Let $S$ be a set. Then consider the free nonunital $\mathbb{C}$ algebra generated by $\{s, s^* \vert s \in S\}$ (todo: existence of a free nonunital algebra). Define the soon to be antinvolution by $s \mapsto s^*$ and $s^* \mapsto s$ and extend it antilinearly and anticommutative.
