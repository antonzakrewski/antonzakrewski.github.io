span category functor from Cat Ex

Definition

There exists an infinity functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{Ex}}_{\infty} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}\\ \mathcal{C} \mapsto& \mathrm{Span}(\mathcal{E}) \\ \mathcal{F} \mapsto& \mathrm{Span}(\mathcal{F}) \end{align*}

where $\mathcal{F}$ is applied

  1. on objects
  2. on spans
