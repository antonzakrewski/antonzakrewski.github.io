Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category equipped with a bounded t-structure. Then the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{St}(\mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

induces an equivalence on connective k-spectra 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
K(\mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit} \dar{\simeq} & \\
K(\mathcal{C}) \rar{\simeq} &   K(\mathcal{C}^{\heart}) \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

where $\mathcal{C}^{\heart}$ is viewed as abelian category (cf. heart as abelian category)
