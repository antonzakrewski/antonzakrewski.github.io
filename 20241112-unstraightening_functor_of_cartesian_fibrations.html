unstraightening functor of cartesian fibrations

Definition

The unstraightening functor for cartesian fibrations is defined as infinity functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{unstraight}_{\mathrm{Cart}}: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Cart}(B) \end{align*}
