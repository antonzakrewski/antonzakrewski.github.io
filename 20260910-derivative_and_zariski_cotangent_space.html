Observe that the zariski cotangent space $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$ is generated by $(x_1,...,x_n) = \mathfrak{m}$ with the relations $x_i x_j$ and $f_i$. Furthermore we have that $\mathfrak{m} \in V(f_1,...,f_r)$ or equivalently that $f_1,...,f_r$ does not have any constant terms. Let $f_i = \sum_{j=1}^n \alpha_{i,j} x_j + \textrm{higher order terms}$, then the ideal $f_i + \mathfrak{m}^2$ is equivalently generated by $(\sum_{i=1} \alpha_{i,j} x_j) + \mathfrak{m}^2$ by subtracting/adding higher order terms.

Now we can identify $x_i$ with a base vector $e_i$ and the quotient by $f_i$ can then be identified by the cokernel above.
