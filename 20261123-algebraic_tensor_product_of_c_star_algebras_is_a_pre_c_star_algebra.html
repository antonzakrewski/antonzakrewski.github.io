algebraic tensor product of C-star algebras is a pre C star algebra with a proper norm

Proposition

Let $A,B \in \mathrm{C}^*\mathrm{Alg}^{\mathrm{nu}}$. Then the algebraic tensor product is a pre C star algebra where the maximal seminorm is a Norm with

\begin{align*} \| a \otimes b \|_{\mathrm{max}} = \| a \| \| b \| \end{align*}

Proof

bounded max norm

Let $\rho: A \otimes^{\mathrm{alg}} B \rightarrow B(H)$ be a representaiton. Then let $C \subseteq B(H)$ be the closed subalgebra, generated by the image of $\rho$.

Then the algebraic tensor product lemma for c star algebras implies the claim.

elementary tensor

Let $\pi_{A}: A $\hookrightarrow \mathbb{B}(\mathbb{H}), $\pi_{B}: B $\hookrightarrow \mathbb{B}(\mathbb{K})$ be closed subalgebra inclusions (cf. gelfand naimark).

Then we claim that

\begin{align*} \rho: A \otimes^{\mathrm{alg}} B \rightarrow \mathbb{B}(\mathbb{H} \otimes \mathbb{K}) \end{align*}

is injective:

Now let $\rho(\sum a_i \otimes b_i)) = 0$ for $a_i$ linearly independent. then $E = \langle \pi_A(a_i) \rangle \subseteq \mathbb{B}(\mathbb{H})$, which is a fintie dimensional subspace.

Now consider

\begin{align*} 0 =& \langle \sum_{i} \rho(a_i \otimes b_i) (h_0 \otimes k_0) \rangle \\ \end{align*}

From the injectivity, we get that no element has nonzero norm.

Moreover, this implies that

\begin{align*} \| \rho(a \otimes b) \| =& \| \pi_A(a) \otimes \pi_B(b) \| \\ =& \| \pi_A(a) \| \| \pi_B(b) \| \\ =& \| a \| \| b \| \end{align*}

where we can optimize $\| \pi(a) \otimes \sigma(b) \|$ separately and $\pi_A,\pi_B$ are isomteric.
