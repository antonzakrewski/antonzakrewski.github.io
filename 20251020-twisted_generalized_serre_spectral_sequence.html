twisted homological serre spectral sequence

Definition / Proposition

Let $F \xrightarrow{\iota} E \rightarrow B$ be a fiber sequence in anima and $E \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}$ a functor. Assume that $\mathrm{colim}_E \varphi$ lands in bounded below spectra.

Then there exists a spectral sequence - called the twisted, homological generalized serre spectral sequence - with

  1. $E_{p,q}^2 = H_p(B; \pi_q(\mathrm{colim}_F \varphi_i))$ (after reindexing)
  2. abbuting to $\pi_{*}(\mathrm{colim}_E \varphi)$ with filtration $\mathrm{colim}_B \tau_{\geq *} \mathrm{colim}_F \varphi \iota$

Proof

use the filtered spectrum

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_B \tau_{\geq *} (\mathrm{colim}_{F} \varphi \iota) = \tau_{\geq *} \mathrm{colim}_B \mathrm{colim}_F \varphi \iota \end{align*}

Then we want to use the theorem about spectral sequence of a filtered spectrum:

  1. the identity above follows from upper t-subcategory closed under colimits
  2. here we have the computation
\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_B \mathrm{colim}_F \varphi \iota \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_E \varphi \end{align*}

so the colimit of the filtered spectrum is given by $\mathrm{colim}_E \varphi$

  1. before indexing we have
\begin{align*} E^1_{s,t} =& \pi_{s + t} \mathrm{gr}^{-s}( \mathrm{colim}_E \varphi) \\ \simeq& \pi_{s + t} \mathrm{gr}^{-s}( \mathrm{colim}_B \mathrm{colim}_F \varphi \iota) \\ \simeq& \pi_{s+t} \mathrm{colim}_B \mathrm{gr}^{-s}( \mathrm{colim}_F \varphi \iota ) \\ \simeq& \pi_{s+t} \mathrm{colim}_B \Sigma^{-s} \pi_{-s} \mathrm{colim}_F \varphi \iota \\ \simeq& \pi_{2s+t} \mathrm{colim}_B \pi_{-s} \mathrm{colim}_F \varphi \iota \\ \end{align*}

where we use that underlying graded object functor preserves limits and colimits for a stable category Now reindex $p = 2s + t, q = -s$ to get

\begin{align*} \pi_{s+t} \mathrm{colim}_B \Sigma^{-s} \pi_{-s} \mathrm{colim}_F \varphi \iota \simeq& \pi_{p} \mathrm{colim}_B \pi_{q} \mathrm{colim}_F \varphi \iota \end{align*}
