currying of mapping spaces bijective for locally compact spaces

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T}_X)$, $(Y, \mathcal{T}_Y)$ and $(Z, \mathcal{T}_Z)$ be topological spaces, such that $Y$ is locally compact Then for the mapping spaces equipped with the compact open topology, Currying

\begin{align*} \mathrm{curry}: \mathrm{Map}(X \times Y, Z) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Map}(X, \mathrm{Map}(Y,Z)) \\ f \mapsto& (\widehat{f}: x \mapsto (f_x: y \mapsto f(x,y))) \end{align*}

is bijective

Proof

injective

since currying inside category set is an isomorphism, we conclude, that it is injective here.

surjective

Let $\widehat{f} \in \mathrm{Map}(X, \mathrm{Map}(Y,Z))$. Then $\widehat{f}q is continuous and furthermore for

\begin{align*} f \coloneqq \mathrm{ev} \circ (\widehat{f}, \mathrm{id}) \end{align*}

or

Here $f$ is continuous as composition of continuous maps (cf. evaluation map from a locally compact as continuous map)

Furthermore, by construction (since they agree on all values)

\begin{align*} \mathrm{curry}(f) = \widehat{f} \end{align*}
