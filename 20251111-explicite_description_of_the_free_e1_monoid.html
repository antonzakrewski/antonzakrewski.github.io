explicit description of the free E1 Algebra

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a cocomplete symmetric monoidal category where the tensor product preserves colimits in both variables. Then the forgetful functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbb{E}_?}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

admits a left adjoint which takes an object $V \in \mathcal{C}$ to a

\begin{align*} \coprod_{k \geq 0} V^{\otimes k} \end{align*}

whose multiplication is given by the

\begin{align*} V^{\otimes k} \otimes V^{\otimes k'} \rightarrow V^{\otimes k + k'} \end{align*}

Proof
