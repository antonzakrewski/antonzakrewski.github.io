finite product preserving functors into a stable category as stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be any category and $\mathcal{S}$ a stable infinity category Then the category of finite product preserving functors

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{S}) \end{align*}

is a stable infinity categories

Proof

follows as:

  • constant zero: check
  • pointwise calculation of colimits / pointwise calculation of limits
  • argue that finite products are an exact functor
