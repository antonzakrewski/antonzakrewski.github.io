K-Space functor from Cat perf

Definition

The K-space functor from category Cat Perf is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathbb{K}(-): \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{perf}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Sp} \\ \mathcal{A} \mapsto& \mathrm{colim}_{\mathbb{N}} \Omega^n K(\Sigma_{\kappa}^n(\mathcal{A})) \end{align*}

for a choice of uncountable regular cardinal
