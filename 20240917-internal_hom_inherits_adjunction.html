internal hom inherits adjunction

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a closed monoidal category. Then there exists a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X \otimes Y, Z) \cong \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(Y,Z)) \end{align*}

Proof

using isomorphism between contravariant hom-sets and objects we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(-, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X \otimes Y, Z)) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(- \otimes (X\otimes Y), Z) \\ \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}((- \otimes X) \otimes Y, Z) \\ \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(- \otimes X, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(Y,Z)) \\ \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(-, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(Y,Z))) \end{align*}

where

  1. the first isomorphism is given by the adjunction
  2. the second given by associativity of the tensor functor
  3. the third and fourth are given by the adjunction
