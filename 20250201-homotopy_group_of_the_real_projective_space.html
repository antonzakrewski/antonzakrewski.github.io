homotopy group of the real projective space

Proposition

The homotopy groups of the real projective space are given by

\begin{align*} \pi_i(\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^n) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } i = n \\ \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } i = 1 \\ 0 & & \mbox{if } i \in \{2,...,n-1\} \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

for $n \geq 2$

Proof

follows from the fiber sequence in top

\begin{align*} S^0 \rightarrow S^{n} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{n} \end{align*}
