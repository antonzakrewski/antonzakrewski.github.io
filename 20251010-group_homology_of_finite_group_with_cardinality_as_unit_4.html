group homology of finite group with cardinality as unit

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $G$ a group with $\vert G \vert \in R^{\times}$. Then the group homology

\begin{align*} H^*(G,R) \simeq R \end{align*}

is trivial

Proof

follows from:

  • ring as projective module for a finite group ring

so

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tor}^{R[G]}(G,R) = G \otimes_{R[G]} R \end{align*}
