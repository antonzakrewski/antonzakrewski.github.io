triangle identity for a right adjoint object

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor and $d \in \mathcal{D}$ an object. TFAE:

  1. there exists a right adjoint object $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(-), d) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, \mathcal{R}(d))$
  2. there exists an object $\mathcal{R}(d)$ together with a morphism (called counit) $\epsilon(d): \mathcal{L}(\mathcal{R}(d)) \rightarrow d$ such that for every object $c \in \mathcal{C}$ and every morphism $h: \mathcal{L}(c) \rightarrow d$ in $\mathcal{D}$ there exists a unique morphism $f: c \rightarrow \mathcal{R}(d)$ making the left diagram commute
