extension of a maximal ideal is a maximal ideal for an integral ring extension

Proposition

Let $\iota: A \hookrightarrow B$ be a integral extension, $\mathfrak{m} \subseteq A$ an ideal.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathfrak{m}$ is a maximal ideal
  2. the extension $\iota[\mathfrak{m}]$ is a maximal ideal

Proof

By the lying over theorem there exists a maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}' \subseteq B$ such that $\iota^{-1}[\mathfrak{m}'] = \mathfrak{m}$ Then it follows that

\begin{align*} \iota[\mathfrak{m}] \subseteq \mathfrak{m}' \end{align*}

with

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{m} \subseteq \iota^{-1}[\iota[\mathfrak{m}]] \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} \iota^{-1}[\iota[\mathfrak{m}]] \subseteq \iota^{-1}[\mathfrak{m}'] = \mathfrak{m} \end{align*}

or

\begin{align*} \iota^{-1}[\iota[\mathfrak{m}]] \end{align*}

by maximal ideal and integral extension it follows that $\iota[\mathfrak{m}]$ is maximal or

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{m} = \mathfrak{m}' \end{align*}
