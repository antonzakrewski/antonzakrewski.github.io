rank of a finitely generated projective module over a commutative ring

Definition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ a prime ideal and $M$ a finitely generated module Then the rank of $M$ at $\mathfrak{p}$ is defined as dimension

\begin{align*} k(\mathfrak{p}) \otimes M \end{align*}

where $k(\mathfrak{p})$ is the residue field at a prime ideal
