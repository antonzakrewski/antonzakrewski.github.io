characterization of compact objects in modules over a connective ring spectrum

Proposition

Let $S$ be a connective E_infinity-ring spectrum and $M \in \mathrm{LMod}_{S}(\mathrm{Sp})$. TFAE:

  1. $M$ is a compact object
  2. $M$ is a retract of an $M'$, where $M'$ admits a finite filtration with associated graded of the form $\bigoplus_{\Gamma_i} \Sigma^i S$ for a finite set $\Gamma_i$.
  3. $M$ admits a finite filtration where each associated graded is a retract of a finite sum of copies $\Sigma^i S$.
  4. $M$ lies in the thick stable subcategory generated by $S$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 3)

3) $\implies$ 4)

4) $\implies$ 1)

follows as

  1. $S$ is a compact object (cf. ring spectrum as compact object in its category of module spectra).
  2. compact objects in a stable category is a thick subcategory
