orbital collapse as g-set

Definition / Proposition

Let $G$ be a group, $X$ a set and $\varphi: G \times X \rightarrow X$ a group action. Then for the set of orbits $X/G$ there exists the canonical group action

\begin{align*} \varphi': G \times X/G \rightarrow X/G \\ (g, [x]) \mapsto& [g \cdot x] \end{align*}

which is the trivial group action
