affine coordinate ring and homogeneous coordinate ring

Proposition

Let $Y \subseteq \mathbb{P}_k^n$ be a projective variety over an algebraically closed field $k$ with homogeneous coordinate ring $S(Y)$ and $U_i \cong \mathbb{A}_K^n$ with $U_i \subseteq \mathbb{P}_k^n$ be part of the affine covering Let $Y_i \cong \varphi_i(Y \cap U_i) \subseteq \mathbb{A}_k^n$ be the affine variety with affine coordinate ring $A(Y_i)$.

Then

\begin{align*} A(Y_i) \cong S(Y)_{(X_i)} \end{align*}

is the localization along $X_i$

Proof

$Y_i = \emptyset$

Then it follows that $Y \subseteq Z(X_i)$. Applying the homogeneous coordinate ring functor for $Y \hookrightarrow Z(X_i)$ as identity gives an algebra-homomorphism

\begin{align*} k[X_1,...,X_n]/X_i \rightarrow S(Y) \end{align*}

Then localization at $X_i$ gives

\begin{align*} (k[X_1,...,X_n]/X_i)[X_i^{-1}] = 0 \end{align*}

and thus especially also, since $X_i = 0$ in $S(Y)$ that $S(Y)[X_i^{-1}] = 0$

Furthermore $A(Y_i) = 0$ holds, hence $A(Y_i) = S(Y)_{X_i}$

$Y_i \neq \emptyset$

w.l.o.g. assume that $i = 0$ Let $A = k[T_1,...,T_n]$ and $S = k[X_0,...,X_n]$. Then each element in $S_{(X_0)}$ is given by $G/X_{0}^e$ for some $e \geq 0$ and $G \in S$ the homogeneous localization along a homogeneous element
