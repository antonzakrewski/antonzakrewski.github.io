compact endomorphisms for a separable hilbert space is the closure of finite rank, bounded operators

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{H}$ be a separable Hilbert space. Then

\begin{align*} \{T \in \mathrm{BL}(\mathcal{H}) : \textrm{ compact}\} = \overline{ \{T \in \mathrm{BL}(\mathcal{H}) : T \textrm{ has finite rank}\}}^{\| \cdot \|} \end{align*}

agree.

In particular, the right hand side converges in the uniform topology.

Proof

$\subseteq$

follows from:

  • bounded, finite rank operator is compact
  • compact operators closed under uniform convergence

$\subseteq$

w.l.o.g. assume that $\mathrm{dim}(\mathcal{H}) = \infty$, otherwise both sets agree (even before taking closures). Let $\psi_i$ be an orthonormal basis in a hilbert space (cf. separable hilbert space and countable orthonormal basis).

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$, consider $P_n \coloneqq \sum_{j=1}^n \langle \psi_j, -\rangle \psi_j$. Then this converges in the strong operator topology to $\mathbbm{1}$ (cf. thm 2.49 series in a hilbert space ?)

Let $R_n \coloneqq T P_n$, then the rank of $R_n$ is bounded by $n$, so in particular each $R_n$ is a finite rank operator. Here $R_n \rightarrow T$ converges in the strong topology (cf. <postcomposition with bounded operator is continuous for strong topology>)

Now $\| T - R_n \|= \mathrm{sup}_{\psi \in \mathcal{H}, \| \psi \| = 1} \| (T - R_n)(\psi) \| \leq \mathrm{sup}_{\varphi \in \mathrm{rank}(\mathbbm{1} - P_n, \| \varphi \| \leq 1)} \ T \varphi \| \eqqcolon \lambda_n$. Now we claim that $\lambda_n \rightarrow 0$ converges to zero.
