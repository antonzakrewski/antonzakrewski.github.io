cartesian fibration as conservative morphism and right fibration

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a cartesian fibration

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is a conservative functor
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ is a right fibration

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Assuem that $\mathcal{F}$ is conservative, then it suffices to show that the fiber over each object $x \in \mathcal{C}$ is an infinity groupoid (cf. cartesian fibration as right fibration and infinity groupoid). Each morphism in the fibre is send to the identity morphism, hence an equivalence Thus as $\mathcal{F}$ is conservative, we may find a lift

2) $\implies$ 1)
