limit functor in the module category as corepresentable functor

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring spectrum resp. commutative ring. Then for any anima $X$ the infinity functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_X(-): \mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{Mod}(R)) \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}(R) \end{align*}

is a corepresentable functor, i.e.

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_{X}(-) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(R)}(\mathrm{const}(R), -) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_X(-) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{[X,\mathrm{Mod}(R)]}(R, \mathrm{lim}_X(-)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{[X, \mathrm{Mod}(R)]}(\mathrm{const}(R), -) \end{align*}

where the first step uses (?) and the second step uses the infinity adjunction of a limit
