poset of grothendieck topologies is bicomplete

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $(X, \leq)$ the partially ordered set of grothendieck topologies. Then $X$ is bicomplete (partially ordered set as category)

Proof

follows from:

  • intersection of grothendieck topologies is a grothendieck topology
  • partially ordered set admitting infima admits suprema
