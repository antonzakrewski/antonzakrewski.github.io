associated prime ideal

Definition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $M$ an module and $\mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ an ideal (note that it will be always prime) Then $\mathfrak{p}$ is said to be associated to $M$, if there exists an prime submodule $N$ such that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ann}(N) = \mathfrak{p} \end{align*}
