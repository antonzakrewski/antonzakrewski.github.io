convergence of a spectral sequence

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $E = (E_{r}^{p,q})$ a spectral sequence. Then $E$ converges against an \(\mathbb{Z}\)-graded object $E^n$ if

  1. for all $p,q \in \mathbb{Z}$ there exists an $r$ such that $d_r^{p,q} = 0$, in particular we get epimorphisms $E_r^{p,q} \rightarrow E_{r+1}^{p,q}$ (cf. homology the r-th page)
  2. Let $E_{\infty} \coloneqq \mathrm{colim}_{r >> 0} E_r^{p,q}$
  3. there exists a filtration $... \hookrightarrow E^n_p \hookrightarrow E^n_{p-1} \hookrightarrow ...$ of $E_n$ where $\mathrm{colim} E^n_p = E_n$ and $E^n_p = 0$ for $p>> 0$
  4. there exist chosen isomorphisms of $E^n_{p}/E^{n}_{p-1} \cong E_{\infty}^{p,n-p}$
