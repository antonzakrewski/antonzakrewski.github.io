covering space of a locally euclidean space

Proposition

Let $p: E \twoheadrightarrow B$ be a surjective covering map

TFAE:

  1. $E$ is locally euclidean
  2. $B$ is locally euclidean

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $b \in B$. Then choose a open neighbourhood $U$ such that

\begin{align*} p^{-1}[U] \cong U \times F^{\Delta} \end{align*}

Choose a fibre $(b,f) \in p^{-p}[b]$ Then there exists an open set $V \cong \mathbb{R}^n$. Then restriction to the open set

\begin{align*} \overline{U} \coloneqq V \cap (U \times \{f\}) \end{align*}

gives an isomorphism $\overline{U} \cong \textrm{ some open subset of } \mathbb{R}^n$. Restricting to some open subset further gives an isomorphism $\cong B_{\varepsilon}(0)$

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $e \in E$. then there exists an neighbourhood $U$ of $p(e)$ such that

\begin{align*} p^{-1}[U] \cong U \times F^{\delta} \end{align*}

Furthermore there exists an neighbourhood $V \cong \mathbb{R}^n$. Then choosing a subset $\overline{U} \cong \mathbb{R}^n$ of $U \cap V$ results in

\begin{align*} p^{-1}[\overline{U}] \cong (\overline{U}) \times F^{\delta} \cong \mathbb{R}^n \times F^{\delta} \end{align*}

Thus each copy of $\overline{U}$ in $\overline{U} \times F^{\delta}$ is both open and locally euclidean
