coslice category of a cocomplete category as cocomplete category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a (sufficiently)1 cocomplete category, $X \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ an object Then the coslice category $X / \mathcal{C}$ is also cocomplete.
