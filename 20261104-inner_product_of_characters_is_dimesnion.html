inner product of same characters is dimension

Proposition

Let $V,W$ be two finite dimensional representations of a finite group $G$. Then

\begin{align*} \langle \chi_{V}, \chi_{W}\rangle = \mathrm{dim} \mathrm{Hom}_{G}(W,V) \end{align*}

for the convention

\begin{align*} \langle f,f\rangle = \frac{1}{\vert G \vert} \sum_{g \in G} f(g) \overline{f(g)} \end{align*}

Proof

We have

\begin{align*} \langle \chi_{V} \chi_{W}\rangle =& \frac{1}{\vert G \vert} \sum_{g \in G} \chi_{V}(g) \cdot \overline{\chi_{W}(g)} \\ =& \frac{1}{\vert G \vert} \sum_{g \in G} \chi_{V}(g) \cdot \chi_{W^*}(G) \\ =& \frac{1}{\vert G \vert} \sum_{g \in G} \chi_{V \otimes W^*}(G) \\ =& \frac{1}{\vert G \vert} \chi_{\mathrm{Hom}_{k}(W,V)}(g) \\ =& \mathrm{Tr}(P) \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} P: \mathrm{Hom}_{k}(V,W) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{k}(V,W) f \mapsto \frac{1}{\vert G \vert} g \cdot f \end{align*}
