cotangent module of a polynomial R-algebra

Proposition

Let $R[x_i \vert i \in I]$ be a polynomial algebra. Then the cotangent module $\Omega_{R[x_i]/R}$ is the free \(R\)-module $R[x_i]$.

Proof

a)

follows from the presentation of the cotangent module

b) as lemma

Recall that a derivation is given by a section $R[x_i] \hookrightarrow R[x_i] \oplus M$. Now it is still an R-Algebra morphism from a free R-algebra, so it is given by mapping $x_i \mapsto (s,m)$ where $s_i \in R[x_i]$ and $m_i \in M$. But for this map to be a section, we have to assume that $s_i = x_i$ and therefore this is equivalent to choosing $m_i$
