embedding of a torsion free group into a sum of Q

Proposition

Let $G$ be a torsion-free abelian group, then there exists an group-monomorphism

\begin{align*} G \rightarrow \bigoplus \mathbb{Q} \end{align*}

Proof

e.g. consider

\begin{align*} G \rightarrow G \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Q} \cong \bigoplus \mathbb{Q} \end{align*}

where the RHS is a $\mathbb{Q}$ vector space, hence decomposes as $\bigoplus \mathbb{Q}$.
