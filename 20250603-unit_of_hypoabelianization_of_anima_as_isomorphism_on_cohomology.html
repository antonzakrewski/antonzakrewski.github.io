unit of hypoabelianization of anima is equivalence on cohomology for arbitrary spectrum

Proposition

Let $X$ be an anima and $X \rightarrow X^+$ the hypoabelianization adjunction on anima. Then for any spectrum $E$ the induced map

\begin{align*} E^*X^+ \rightarrow E^*X \end{align*}

is an equivalence

Proof

By definition $E^*(-) \coloneqq \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[-],E)$ which by adjunction simplifies to $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,\Omega^{\infty} E)$ where $\Omega^{\infty} E$ is the forgetful functor. But $\Omega^{\infty} E$ is a hypoabelian anima, since homotopy group of a spectrum is abelian, so in particular hypoabelian.
