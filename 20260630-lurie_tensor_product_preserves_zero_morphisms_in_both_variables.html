lurie tensor product preserves zero morphisms in both variables

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_1, \mathcal{C}_2, \mathcal{D}_1, \mathcal{D}_2$ be presentable categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}_1 \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_2$ any left adjoint. Then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}_1 \otimes \mathcal{D}_1 \xrightarrow{\mathcal{F} \otimes 0} \mathcal{C}_2 \otimes \mathcal{D}_2 \end{align*}

is equivalent to the zero morphism in $\mathrm{Pr^L}$ (cf. PrL is a pointed category).

Proof

this uses the explicit description of the lurie tensor product as FunRAdj, where any zero morphism factors through $*$ and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{R}}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, *) \simeq * \end{align*}

so $\mathcal{C}_1 \otimes * \simeq *$ is also the zero object.
