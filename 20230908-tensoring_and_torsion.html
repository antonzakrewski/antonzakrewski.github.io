tensoring with a localization and torsion

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a Ring and $M$ an \(R\)-module with a ringelement $\alpha \in \mathrm{Ann}_{R}(M)$ (cf. Annihilator). Then for a multiplicatively closed set $S \subseteq R$ with $\alpha \in S$, it follows, that

\begin{align*} M \otimes_{R} S^{-1}R = 0 \end{align*}

Proof

follows from

\begin{align*} m \otimes \frac{1}{1} =& m \otimes \frac{\alpha}{\alpha} \\ =& \alpha m \otimes \frac{1}{\alpha} \\ =& 0 \otimes \frac{1}{\alpha} \\ =& 0 \end{align*}
