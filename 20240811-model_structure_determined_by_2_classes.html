model structure determined by 2 classes

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}, \mathrm{Cof}, \mathrm{Fib})$ be a model category. Then the model structure is determined by 2 out of

  1. $\mathcal{W}$
  2. $\mathrm{Cof}$
  3. $\mathrm{Fib}$

Proof

1 + 2

follows from:

  • weak factorization system determined by projective morphisms

where $\mathcal{W} \cap \mathrm{Cof}$ are the projective morphisms

1 + 3

follows from

  • weak factorization system determined by injective morphisms

where $\mathcal{W} \cap \mathrm{Fib}$ are the projective morphisms
