homotopy category of Anima equivalent to hCWC

Proposition

the homotopy category of anima is equivalent to category hCWC

Proof

follows from the

  • quillen equivalence between sSet and Top
  • quillen equivalence induces equivalence on homotopy categories

where

  • $hCWC$ is precisely the homotopy category of the quillen model structure on top
  • hCWC as homotopy category of quillen model structure
