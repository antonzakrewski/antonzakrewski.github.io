constant diagram

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{I}$ an index category. Then the constant functor for $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \underline{A}: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \\ I \mapsto& A \\ (\alpha: I \rightarrow J) \mapsto& \mathrm{id}_{A} \end{align*}
