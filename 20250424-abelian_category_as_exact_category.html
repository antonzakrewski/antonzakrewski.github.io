exact structure of an abelian category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category. Then $\mathcal{A}$ is canonically an exact infinity category for

  1. monomorphisms as inclusions
  2. epimorphisms as projections

where exact sequences are given by short exact sequence

note that this exact structure is in general different than the exact structure of the minimal exact structure of an additive category
