transgressive pairs is a submodule

Proposition

Let $E'$ be a spectral sequence of \(R\)-modules. Then the subset of transgressive pairs forms a submodule

Proof

follows as it is given by the intersection of subsets of $E_2^{0,p} \times E_2^{p,0}$ given by

  1. $\mathrm{ker}(d_r,0)$ for $r \leq n$
  2. $\mathrm{ker}(d_{n+1} - \mathrm{id})$
