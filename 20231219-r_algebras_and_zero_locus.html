R-Algebras and zero locus

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $P_1,...,P_r \in $R[X_1,...,X_n]$ polynomials. Let

\begin{align*} A \coloneqq R[X_1,...,X_n]/(P_1,...,P_r) \end{align*}

be the quotient ring for the generated Ideal $(P_1,...,P_r)$ and $B$ a commutative R-Algebra.

Then for the zero locus

\begin{align*} Z(P_1,...,P_r)(B) \end{align*}

there exists a natural bijection

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{R-Alg}(A,B) \cong Z(P_1,...,P_r)(B) \end{align*}

Proof
