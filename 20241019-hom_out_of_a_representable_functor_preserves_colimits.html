hom out of a representable functor preserves colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category, $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ the category of presheaves Then for a contravariant representable functor the hom-functor preserves small colimits

Proof

follows from the contravariant Yoneda Lemma, as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,X), \mathrm{colim} \mathcal{F}(-)) \cong& \mathrm{colim} \mathcal{F}(X) \\ \cong& \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,X), \mathcal{F}(-)) \end{align*}
