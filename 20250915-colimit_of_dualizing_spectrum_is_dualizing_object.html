colimit of dualizing spectrum is dualizing object

Proposition

Let $X$ be a compact anima, then the dual object of $R[X]$ is is given by the colimit of the R-dualizing spectrum.

Proof

note that $R[X] = r_! r^*(X)$ is defined as left kan extension of the constant functor. Then we may apply the adjunction of kan extensions and the definition of the dualizing object to get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}(R[X], R) \simeq& \mathrm{map}(r_!r^*(R), R) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_X(r^*(R), r^*(R)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}(R, r_*r^*(R)) \\ \simeq& r_*r^*(r) \\ \simeq& r_!(D_X^R) \end{align*}
