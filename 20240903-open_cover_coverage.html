open cover coverage

Definition / Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space. Then the open cover coverage is defined as site wtih

  1. open subsets as objects
  2. inclusion $U \subseteq V$ as morphism $U \rightarrow V$
  3. for $V$ the collection of sieves is given by the set of open covers, i.e.
\begin{align*} \{ \mathcal{O} = \{U_i \rightarrow V \} \vert \bigcup_{U_i \in \mathcal{O}} U_i = V\} \end{align*}

Proof

a) an open is covered by itself b) given opens $U_i$ and a covering of each $U_i$, this becomes a cover of $\bigcup U_i$ c) given a cover $\bigcup U_i$ and an open subset $V \subseteq \bigcup U_i$, then $\bigcup (U_i \cap V)$ is an open cover of $V$
