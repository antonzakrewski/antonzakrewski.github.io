noetherian module and finitely generated submodules

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $M$ an \(R\)-module

TFAE:

  1. $M$ is a Noetherian module
  2. each submodule is finitely generated

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

  • submodule of a noetherian module as noetherian module
  • noetherian module as finitely generated module

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $N_i \subseteq N_{i+1}$ be a ascending chain of submodules. Then consider the union as submodule

By assumption it is finitely generated, hence there exists generators $n_1,...,n_n$. But then taking an $i$ - e.g. $\mathrm{max} \{\mathrm{minimum j} \in \mathbb{N} \vert n_i \in N_j\}$ - such that

\begin{align*} n_j \in N_i \end{align*}

shows that $N_i = \bigcup N_i$ or $N_i = N_{i+1} = ...$
