convolution in L^p

Proposition

Let $1 \leq \frac{1}{p} + \frac{1}{q} \leq 2$ and $f \in L^p(\mathbb{R}^d), g \in L^q(\mathbb{R}^d)$. Then the convolution is defined as map

\begin{align*} (f \star g): \mathbb{R}^d \rightarrow& \overline{\mathbb{R}} \\ x \mapsto \int_{\mathbb{R}^d} \mathrm{d}y f(x - y) g(y) \end{align*}

with $(f \star g)(x) < \infty$ almost everywhere.

Proof
