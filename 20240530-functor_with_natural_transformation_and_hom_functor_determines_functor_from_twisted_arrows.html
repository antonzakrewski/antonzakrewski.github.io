endofunctor with natural transformation and hom functor of coslice determines functor from twisted arrows

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathrm{Tw}(\mathcal{C})$ the twisted arrow category, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a functor with a natural transformation $\eta: \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{C}} \rightarrow \mathcal{F}$ Let $\mathrm{Hom}_{A / \mathcal{C}}(-,-)$ be the hom-functor of the coslice category $A/ \mathcal{C}$ for an object $A$.

Then there exists a covariant functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{Tw}, \eta}: \mathrm{Tw}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Set} \\ (p: A \rightarrow B) \mapsto& \mathrm{Hom}_{A / \mathcal{C}}((\eta(A): A \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(A)),(p: A \rightarrow B)) \\ (\alpha,\beta) \mapsto& (\alpha^{ \rightarrow}, \beta^{ \leftarrow}) \end{align*}
