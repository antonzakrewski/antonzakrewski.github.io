reduced ring in general not an integral domain

Proposition

Let $R$ be a reduced ring, then $R$ is in general not an integral domain

Proof

let $\mathbb{Z} / 6 \mathbb{Z} \cong \mathbb{Z} /2 \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z} /3 \mathbb{Z}$, then it is reduced (cf. general chinese remainder theorem, field as reduced ring, product of reduced rings), but $2,3$ are zero divisors
