conservative functor between quasicategories and homotopy category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is conservative functor
  2. $h \mathcal{F}$ is a conservative functor

Proof

follows from:

  • universal property of the classifier nerve
  • universal property of the isomorphism classifier
  • conservative functor and RLP
  • conservative functor between quasicategories and RLP
