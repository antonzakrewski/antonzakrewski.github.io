complete variety and closed projection for affine varieties

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ a quasi-projective variety

TFAE:

  1. $X$ is a complete quasi-projective variety
  2. for each affine variety $Y \subseteq \mathbb{A}_k^n$, the projection
\begin{align*} \mathrm{pr}: X \times Y \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

is a closed map

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

special case

2) $\implies$ 1)
