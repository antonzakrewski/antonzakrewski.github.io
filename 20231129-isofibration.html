isofibration

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories

A functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is called an isofibration, if for objects $X_1 \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C}), X_0 \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{D})$ and an isomorphism $\alpha: \mathcal{F}_1(X_1) \rightarrow X_0$ there exists a lift, i.e some object $Y_0 \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and an isomorphism $\overline{\alpha}: X_1 \rightarrow Y_0$
