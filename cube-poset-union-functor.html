cube poset union functor

Definition / Proposition

Let $\alpha: [n] \rightarrow [m]$ be a morphism of the finite linear orders. Then there exists a functor on the cube posets

\begin{align*} \alpha: P_{i,j} \rightarrow& P_{\alpha(i), \alpha(j)} S \mapsto& \alpha[S] \end{align*}
