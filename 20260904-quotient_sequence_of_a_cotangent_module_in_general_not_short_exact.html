quotient sequence of a cotangent module in general not short exact

Proposition

The quotient sequence of cotangent module is in general not a short exact sequence

Proof

consider the sequence $k \rightarrow k[x] \twoheadrightarrow k[x]/(x^2) = R$. Then the presentation of the cotangent module implies $\Omega_f = k, \Omega_{gf} = R[dx]/(2x dx)$ and hence there exists an exact sequence

(cf. quotient sequence) where $d$ is the universal derivation

This map is not injective, since $\mathrm{d}(x^3) = 3x^2 \mathrm{d}x = 0$.
