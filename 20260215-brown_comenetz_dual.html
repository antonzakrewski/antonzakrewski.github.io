brown comenetz dual of a spectrum

Definition

Let $E$ be a spectrum. Then the brown comenetz dual to $E$ is defined as representing spectrum for the cohomology

\begin{align*} X \mapsto \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(E_{-*}(X), \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}
