functor from modules over a ring to modules over the ringed space

Definition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $S = \mathrm{Spec}(A)$. Then the functor

\begin{align*} (-): \mathrm{Mod}_{A} \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}_{\mathcal{O}_{S}} \end{align*}

is defined as extension of scalars for the map $a: (S, \mathcal{O}_{S}) \rightarrow (*, A)$
