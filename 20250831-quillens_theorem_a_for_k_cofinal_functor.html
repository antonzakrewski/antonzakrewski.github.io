quillens theorem A for k-cofinal functors

Proposition

Let $i: K \rightarrow L$ be a functor between infinity categories and assume that for all objects $x \in L$ the slice $K_{/x}$ is k-connected. Then for every (k,1)-category $\mathcal{E}$ and every diagram $F: L \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_{K} F \iota \rightarrow \mathrm{lim}_{L} F \end{align*}

is an equivalence

Proof
