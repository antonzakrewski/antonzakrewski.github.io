augmented product singular chain complex bifunctor

Definition

Given category Top and an abelian group $M$, the augmented singular chain complex functor is defined as functor to the category of augmented chain complexes

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(- \times -;M) \mathrm{Top} \times \mathrm{Top} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ch_*}^M(\mathcal{A}) \\ (X,Y) \mapsto& \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(X \times Y;M) \\ (f_1,f_2: (X,Y) \rightarrow (X',Y')) \mapsto& \mathrm{Sing}(f_1,f_2): \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(X \times Y;M) \rightarrow \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(X' \times Y';M) \end{align*}

to the augmented singular chain complex with coefficients where $f: X \rightarrow Y$
