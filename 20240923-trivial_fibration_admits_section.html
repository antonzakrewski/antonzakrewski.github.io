trivial fibration as split epi

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a trivial fibration Then $f$ admits a section, i.e. a morphism $g: Y \rightarrow X$ such that

\begin{align*} f \circ g = \mathrm{id}_Y \end{align*}

Proof

using postcomposition with a trivial fibration as trivial fibration we get the trivial fibration

\begin{align*} f_*: \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(Y,X) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(Y,Y) \end{align*}
