hodge star operation

Definition

Let $M$ be a riemannian manifold with canonical volume form $\mathrm{vol}$ Then the hodge star operatino is defined by the multilinear map

\begin{align*} \mbox *: \Omega^k \rightarrow& \Omega^{n-k} \\ (e^{i_1} \wedge ... \wedge e^{i_n}) \mapsto& \partial^{\iota_1 \iota_n} ... \partial^{\iota^k \iota_n} \iota_{e_{j_k}} ... i_{e_{j_n}} \mathrm{vol} \end{align*}
