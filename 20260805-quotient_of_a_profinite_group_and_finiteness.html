quotient of a profinite group and finiteness

Proposition

Let $G$ be a profinite group and $N$ a normal subgroup. TFAE:

  1. $G/N$ is finite
  2. $N$ is an open subgroup

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Observe that there exists a canonical continuous group-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \iota: \lim_{G/H} G/H \rightarrow \prod_{G/H} G/H \end{align*}

where $G/H$ ranges over all subgroups with finite index.

In particular, $G/N$ is also of the form. Hence consider the open subset $\{0\} \times \prod_{G/H, H \neq N} G/H \subseteq \prod_{G/H} G/H$, where the preimage of this subset consists precisely of all elements $g \in G$ such that $g = 0$ in $G/N$ or $g \in N$. Moreover, continuous preimages of open subsets are open again.

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $N$ be open, then it is the finite intersection of subsets $G \cap (U_{H'} \times \prod_{G/H, H \neq H'} G/H)$ for open subsets $U_{H'} \subseteq G/H'$. Hence it is contained in the finite intersection of subsets $G \cap ( \{0\} \times \prod_{G/H, H \neq H'} G/H)$ and for a finite intersection, we have a monomorphism

\begin{align*} G/(N_1 \cap N_2) \hookrightarrow G/N_1 \times G/N_2 \end{align*}

so it suffices to show that $G/N_1$ is finite for $N_1 = G \cap \{0\} \times \prod_{G/H, H \neq H'} G/H)$. But here $G/N_1 \subseteq G/H'$, where $G/H'$ is finite by assumption.
