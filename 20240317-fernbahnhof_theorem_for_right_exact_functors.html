Fernbahnhof theorem for right exact functors

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}), \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{B})$ the category of chain complexes and $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{B})$ a right exact functor. Then for the \(n\)-th component of the homology chain complexes, there exists a natural transformation

\begin{align*} (\mathcal{F}H_*(-))_n \rightarrow (H_*(\mathcal{F}(-)))_n \end{align*}

of functors

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow \mathcal{B} \end{align*}

Note that $\mathcal{F}$ may be induced by a right exact functor $\mathcal{F}': \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ (cf. right exact functor induces right exact functor on chain complexes)
