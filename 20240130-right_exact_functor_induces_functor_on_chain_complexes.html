right exact functor induces functor on chain complexes

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be additive categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ be a right exact functor Then $\mathcal{F}$ induces a functor on the categories of chain complexes

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_*: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{B}) \end{align*}
