monoidal functor induces functor on Picard groupoid

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor between (symmetric) monoidal categories. Then $\mathcal{F}$ induces a functor on the picard groupoid of a symmetric monoidal category

Proof

follows as $\mathcal{F}$ induces a functor on the groupoid core and maps invertible objects to invertible objects.
