singular cohomology ring of the complex projective space

Proposition

Let $n \geq 1$, then the singular cohomology of the complex projective space $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n$ is given by

\begin{align*} H^*(\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n) \cong P_{\mathbb{Z}}(c)/(c^{n+1}) \end{align*}

Proof

follows by analagous arguments as in singular cohomology ring of the real projective space
