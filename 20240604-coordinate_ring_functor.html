coordinate ring functor from affine algebraic sets

Definition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathrm{AffAlg}$ the category of affine algebras and $\mathrm{AlgSet}$ the category of algebraic sets.

Then the coordinate ring functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O}: \mathrm{AlgSet} \rightarrow& \mathrm{AffAlg}^{\mathrm{op}} \\ X \mapsto& \mathcal{O}(X) \\ (f: X \rightarrow Y) \mapsto& (f^*: \mathcal{O}(Y) \rightarrow \mathcal{O}(X)) \end{align*}

where $f^*$ is the precomposition for the coordinate ring as ring of morphisms
