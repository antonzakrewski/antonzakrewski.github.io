homology functor for an ab5 category commutes with filtered colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an Ab5 category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes Then the homology functor commutes with filtered colimits, i.e.

\begin{align*} H_*(\mathrm{colim}_i C_*^i) \cong \mathrm{colim} H_*(C_*^i) \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of:

  • FHHF for exact
  • where $\mathrm{colim}$ is an exact functor
