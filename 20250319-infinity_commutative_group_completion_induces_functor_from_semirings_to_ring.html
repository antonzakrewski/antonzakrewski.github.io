infinity commutative group completion induces functor from semirings to ring

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable closed symmetric monoidal infinity category. Then the infinite commutative group completion functor induces a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Rig}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ring}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • infinite commutative group completion for a presentable infinity category
  • <group completion is symmetric monoidal>
