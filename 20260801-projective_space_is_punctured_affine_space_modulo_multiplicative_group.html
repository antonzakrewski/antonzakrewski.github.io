projective space is punctured affine space modulo multiplicative group

Proposition

Let $I$ be a set, then the map from punctured affine space to projective space induces an injective map

\begin{align*} (\mathbb{A}^I \setminus \{0\})/R^{\times} \hookrightarrow& \mathbb{P}^{I}(R) \end{align*}

where $R^{\times}$ acts on $(\mathbb{A}^{I} \setminus \{0\})(R)$ by scalar multiplication

see:

  • action of G_m on affine space

Proof

Observe that two affine lines

\begin{align*} (a_i): R^I \twoheadrightarrow& R \\ (b_i): R^I \twoheadrightarrow& R \\ \end{align*}

are equivalent as affine lines if and only if there exists a commutative diagram 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
& R \dar[dashed]{\cong}\\
R^I \arrow[]{ru}{(a_i)} \arrow[]{rd}{(b_i)} & \\
& R
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

where an isomorphism $R \cong R$ is classified by multiplication with a unit.
