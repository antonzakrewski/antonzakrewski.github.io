cofinal functor and weakly contractible right slice

Definition

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be an infinity category and $\mathcal{C}$ a simplicial set and $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a morphism of simplicial sets

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is cofinal
  2. for all $d \in \mathcal{D}_0$ the right slice $\mathcal{C}_{d/}$ is weaklycontractible
