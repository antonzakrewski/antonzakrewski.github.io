category of Fields of an algebraic functor

Definition

Let $X$ be an affine scheme Then the category $\mathrm{Field}_{X}$ is defined as full subcategory of the category of elements containing fields $k \rightarrow X$ as objects.

Remark

  • unstraightening after precomposing with the inclusion $\mathrm{Field} \rightarrow \mathrm{CAlg}$
