relative singular chain complex functor

Definition / Proposition

Given the category of topological pairs, the relative singular chain complex functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{C}^{\mathrm{sing}}(-,-): \mathrm{TopPairs} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{Ab}) \\ (X,A) \mapsto& \mathrm{C}^{\mathrm{sing}}(X,A) \\ (f: (X,A) \rightarrow (Y,B)) \mapsto& \left(f': C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X,A) \rightarrow C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y,B) \right) \end{align*}
