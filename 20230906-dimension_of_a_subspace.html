dimension of a subspace

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space and $Y \subseteq X$. Then for the dimension, it holds, that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(Y) \leq \mathrm{dim}(X) \end{align*}

Proof

Let $Z_1 \subestneq ... \subsetneq Z_n$ be a chain of closed, irreducible subspaces in $Y$ Then consider the closure

\begin{align*} \overline{Z_1} \subset ... \subset \overline{Z_n} \end{align*}

by irreducible subspace and closure each $\overline{Z_i}$ is closed.

Furthermore $\overline{Z_i} \subsetneq \overline{Z_{i+1}}$ is a proper subset: By assumption $Z_i \setminus Z_{i+1} \neq \emptyset$, hence there exists an $z \in Z_i \setminus Z_{i+1}$. Thus there also exists in $Y$ a closed subset $A$ such that $Y \cap A = Z_i$, hence containing $Z_i$ but not $z$, Therefore $z \not\in \overline{Z_i}, z \in \overline{Z_{i+1}}$
