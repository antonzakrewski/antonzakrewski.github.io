Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category

TFAE:

  1. there exists an E_1 Ring $R$ and $\mathcal{C} \simeq \mathrm{RMod}_R$ (cf. infinity category of modules over an E_1 Ring)
  2. $\mathcal{C}$ is presentable and there exists a compact projective object $C$ in $\mathcal{C}$ which generates $\mathcal{C}$ in the following sense: if $D$ in $\mathcal{C}$ is an object such that $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(C,D)$ is the zero spectrum, then $D$ is the zero object in $\mathcal{C}$.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
