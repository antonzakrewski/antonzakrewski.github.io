hom kan complex of a kan enriched category equivalent to the mapping space of the homotopy coherent nerve

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a kan enriched category, $N^{\mathrm{hc}}(\mathcal{C})$ the homotopy coherent nerve. Then for objects $x,y \in \mathcal{C}$

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{N^{\mathrm{hc}}(\mathcal{C})}(x,y) \cong \mathrm{Hom}(x,y) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Hom}(x,y)$ is the enriched hom

Proof
