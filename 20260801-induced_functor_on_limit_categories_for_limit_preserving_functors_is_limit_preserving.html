induced functor on limit categories for limit preserving functors is limit preserving

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{K}$ be a collection of categories, $I_1,I_2: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}$ be diagrams consisting of categories which admit $\mathcal{K}$ shaped limits and functors, which preserve $\mathcal{K}$ shaped limits. Suppose that $\eta: I_1 \rightarrow I_2$ also consists of $\mathcal{K}$ limit preserving functors.

Then $\lim \eta: \lim_{I_1} \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \lim_{I_2} \mathcal{D}_i$ also preserves limits.
