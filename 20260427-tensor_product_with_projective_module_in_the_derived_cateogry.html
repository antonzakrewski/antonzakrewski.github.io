tensor product with projective module in the derived category

Proposition

Let $R$ be a (commutative ring) ring, $\mathcal{D}(R)$ the derived category of a ring. Let $M,P$ be a ordinary modules, where $P$ is a projective module Then the derived tensor product $M \otimes_{R} P$ coincides with the ordinary tensor product.

Proof

Let $\coprod R \twoheadrightarrow P$ be a retraction. Then we have a split exact sequence

\begin{align*} P' \rar & \coprod R \rar & P \end{align*}

and $\coprod R \otimes_{R} P$ is a retract of $\coprod R \otimes_{R} M$, where the RHS is concentrated in a single degree, since $R$ is the monoidal unit and $- \otimes_{R} -$ preserves coproducts in both variables. Hence $M \otimes_R P$ is also concentrated in a single degree. Similarly, $P' \otimes_{R} M$ is also concentrated in a single degree, as $P'$ is again a projective module.
