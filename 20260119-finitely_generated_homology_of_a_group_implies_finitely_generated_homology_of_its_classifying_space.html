finitely generated homology of a group implies finitely generated homology of its classifying space

Proposition

Let $G$ be an anima valued group and $\mathrm{BG}$ the delooping of anima valued groups. Suppose $\pi_0(G)$ is abelian and finite TFAE:

  1. $H_*(\mathrm{BG})$ is degreewise finitely generated group
  2. $\pi_*(\mathrm{BG})$ is degreewise finitely generated
  3. $\pi_*(G)$ is degreewise finitely generated
  4. $H_*(G)$ is degreewise finitely generated

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2), 2) $\implies$ 1)

  • fundamental theorem of serre class theory

2) $\implies$ 3), 3) $\implies$ 2)

  • $G \simeq \Omega \mathrm{BG}$
  • where we may also require finite amount of connected componest

3) $\implies$ 4), 4) $\implies$ 3)

  • we may reduce it to the zeroth component
  • anima valued group is a simple space
  • fundamental theorem of serre class theory
  • $H_*(\coprod_{\pi_0(G)} G_0) \simeq \coprod_{\pi_0(G)} H_*( G_0)$
