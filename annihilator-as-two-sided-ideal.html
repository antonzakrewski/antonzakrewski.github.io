annihilator as two sided ideal

Proposition

Let \(A\) be a ring, $M$ an \(R\)-module. Then the Annihilator $\mathrm{Ann}(M)$ is a two sided ideal.

Proof

Let $r_1,r_2,r_3 \in R$ with $r_2 \in \mathrm{Ann}(M)$. Then for any $m \in M$ it follows that

\begin{align*} r_1 \cdot r_2 \cdot r_3 =& r_1 \cdot (r_2 \cdot (r_3 \cdot m)) =& \\ =& r_1 \cdot (r_2 \cdot m') \\ =& r_1 \cdot 0 \\ =& 0 \end{align*}

where $m' \coloneqq r_3 \cdot m$
