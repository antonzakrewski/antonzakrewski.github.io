noetherian ring and finitely generated projective resolution for a finitely generated module

Proposition

Let $A$ be a noetherian ring, $M$ a finitely generated module over $A$. Then there exists a projective resolution of finitely generated modules.

Proof

By assumption there exists an algebraic module-epimorphismz

\begin{align*} \varphi: \bigoplus_{i=1}^n A \rightarrow& M \\ \end{align*}

Then $\bigoplus_{i=1}^n$ is finitely presented (cf. finitely generated modules over a noetherian ring as finitely presented module).

Therefore $\mathrm{ker}(\varphi)$ is finitely generated and this resouliton can be iteratively constructed
