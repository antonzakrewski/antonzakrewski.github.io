f-colimit

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an inner fibration and $\overline{p}: K^{\triangleright} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a diagram for a simplicial set $K$ and the Then $\overline{1}$ is an \(f\)-colimit of $p_{\vert K}$ if the map

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}_{\overline{p}/} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{p/} \times_{\mathcal{D}_{fp/} \mathcal{D}_{f \overline{p}/} \end{align*}

is a trivial fibration
