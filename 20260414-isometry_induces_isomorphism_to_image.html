isometry induces isomorphism to image

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be an isometry between metric spaces Then it induces an isomorphism $f: X \rightarrow f[Y]$ in the category of metric spaces.

Proof

observe that an injective map is a bijection onto its image. moreover, the inverse map

\begin{align*} f^{-1}: f[Y] \rightarrow& X \\ f(y) \mapsto& y \end{align*}

is not only welldefined, but also 1-lipschitz continuous, hence in particular continuous (cf. Lipschitzstetigkeit als verschärfte Bedingung).
