tensor product of a commutative ring spectrum as r-dualizing spectrum

Proposition

Let $R \in \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Sp})$ be a commutative ring spectrum and $X$ a compact anima. Then the R-dualizing spectrum is given by

\begin{align*} R \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} D_X^{\mathbb{S}} \simeq B_X^{R} \end{align*}

Proof

follows as

\begin{align*} r_{!}^R(\mathcal{F} \otimes_R( R \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} D_X)) \simeq& r_{!}^R((\mathcal{F} \otimes_R R) \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} D_X)) \\ \simeq& r_!^R(\mathcal{F} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} D_X) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_X(\mathcal{F}\otimes_{\mathbb{S}} D_X) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{lim}_X \mathcal{F} \\ \simeq& r_* \in \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Mod}(R), \mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{Mod}(R))) \end{align*}

where we use associativity of the infinite tensor product of bimodules and the defining property of the dualizing spectrum
