invertible modules closed under tensor product

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $M_1,M_2$ invertible module. Then so is $M_1 \otimes_A M_2$.

Proof

follows from choosing inverses $N_1,N_2$ and

\begin{align*} M_1 \otimes_{A} M_2 \otimes_A N_2 \otimes_A N_1 \cong& M_1 \otimes_A A \otimes N_1 \\ \cong& M_1 \otimes_A N_1 \\ \cong& A \end{align*}
