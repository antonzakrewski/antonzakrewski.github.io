complete segal anima and constant simplicial anima

Proposition

Let $X$ be a segal anima.

TFAE:

  1. $X$ is a complete segal anima
  2. $X^{\times}$ is - up to equivalence - a constant functor

see:

  • core of a segal anima

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

we show that some map

\begin{align*} p_{i}: [1] \rightarrow [n] \end{align*}

induces equivalences.

This follows as by assumption 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
|[label={[label distance=-2mm]-45:\lrcorner}]|
X_{n}^{\times} \dar \lar & X_1^{\times} \dar[dashed] \\
X_{n-1}^{\times} \rar & X_0^{\times} \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

is defined as pullback, where by assumption the dashed map is an equivalence.

In particular we conclude that the left vertical map is an equivalence (cf. square with parallel equivalences as infinity pullback).

2) $\implies$ 1)

then the degeneracy is trivially

\begin{align*} X_0 \rightarrow X_0^{\times} \end{align*}

an equivalence, since the whole functor is an equivalence
