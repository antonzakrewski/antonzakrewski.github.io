weak convergence criterion

Proposition

Let $X$ be a normed vector spaceand $x_n$ a sequence. Then $x_n$ converges weakly to some $x$ if and only if the following two statements hold:

  1. $\mathrm{sup}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \| x_n \| < \infty$
  2. there exists an $F \subseteq X^*$ such that $\mathrm{span}(F)$ is dense with respect to the weak-topology and such that for every $f \in F$
\begin{align*} \lim_{n \rightarrow \infty} f(x_n) = f(x) \end{align*}

Proof

$\Rightarrow$

follows from:

  • supremum of norms of a weakly convergent sequence

and $F = X^*$

$\Leftarrow$

Let $\varepsilon > 0$ and $g \in X^*$. Then we need to show that $\vert g(x) - g(x_n) \vert < \varepsilon$ for almost all $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

Obserev that $\lim_{n \rightarrow \infty} f(x_n) = f(x)$ for $f \in \mathrm{span}(F)$ as taking linear combinations behaves well with converrgent sequences.

Let $K \coloneqq \frac{(\| x \| + \mathrm{sup}_{n \in \mathbb{N}})}{2} < \infty$.

Then as $\mathrm{span}(F)$ is dense in $X^*$, there exists an $f$ in $\mathrm{span}(F)$ such that $\|f - g \|_* < \frac{\varepsilon}{3 K}$.

For $f \in \mathrm{span}(F)$ there exists an $n_0$ such that for every $n \geq N$ $\vert f(x_n) - f(x) \vert < \frac{\varepsilon}{3}$.

Now for all $n \geq N$ we may estimate

\begin{align*} \vert g(x) - g(x_n) \vert \leq& \vert g(x) - f(x) \vert + \vert f(x) - f(x_n) \vert + \vert f(x_n) - g(x_n) \vert \\ \leq& \| g - f \|_* (\| x \| + \| x_n \|) + \vert f(x) - f(x_n) \vert \\ <& \frac{\varepsilon}{3 K} \cdot 2 K + \frac{\varepsilon}{3} \\ \leq& \varepsilon \end{align*}
