p-primary ideal and p as smallest prime ideal

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{p}$ a prime ideal and $\mathfrak{q}$ a primary ideal

TFAE:

  1. $\mathfrak{q}$ is p-primary ideal
  2. $\mathfrak{p}$ is the smallest prime ideal containing $\mathfrak{q}$

Proof

follows from:

  • radical of a primary ideal as prime ideal
  • radical of an ideal as intersection of prime ideals
