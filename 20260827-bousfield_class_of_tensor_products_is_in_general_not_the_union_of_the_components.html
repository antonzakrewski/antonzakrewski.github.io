bousfield class of tensor products is in general not the union of the components

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum, which is $E \otimes E'$ acyclic spectrum. Then it is in general neither $E$ onr $E'$ acyclic.

Proof

consider e.g. $E = \mathbb{F}_p$ and $E' = \mathbb{F}_q$ for $p \neq q$ or $E = \mathbb{Q}$ and $E' = \mathbb{F}_p$
