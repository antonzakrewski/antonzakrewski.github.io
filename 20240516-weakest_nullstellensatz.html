weakest nullstellensatz

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space. Suppose $\mathfrak{a} \subsetneq K[X_1,...,X_n]$ is a strict ideal then the zero locus is nonempty

\begin{align*} Z(\mathfrak{a}) \neq \emptyset \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of the weak Nullstellensatz and existence of a maximal ideal for a strict ideal
