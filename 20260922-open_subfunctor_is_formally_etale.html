open immersion is formally etale

Proposition

Let $O \rightarrow X$ be an open immersion. Then it is formally etale.

Proof

Let $\mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(R')$ be a first order thickening, then $\vert \mathrm{Spec}(R) \vert \rightarrow \vert \mathrm{Spec}(R') \vert$ is an isomorphism (cf. thickening of schemes induces isomorphism on underlying spaces). Now the subset criterion for elements in open subfunctors together with the commutative diagram implies the claim
