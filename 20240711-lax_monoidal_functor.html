lax monoidal functor

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}}), (\mathcal{W}, \otimes_{\mathcal{W}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{W}})$ be monoidal categories. Then a lax monoidal functor is defined as a functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{V} \rightarrow \mathcal{W} \end{align*}

together with

  1. a map $\mathds{1}_{\mathcal{W}} \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(\mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$
  2. a natural transformation
\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(-) \otimes_{\mathcal{W}} \mathcal{F}(-) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(- \otimes_{\mathcal{V}} -) \end{align*}
