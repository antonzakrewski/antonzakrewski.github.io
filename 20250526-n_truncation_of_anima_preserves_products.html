n-truncation of anima preserves products

Proposition

The n-truncation functor of anima preserves products.

Proof

follows from infinity homotopy group preserves products where

\begin{align*} \pi_n(X) \rightarrow \pi_n(\tau_l(X)) = \begin{cases} 0 & \mbox{if } n > l \\ \mathrm{id} & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
