integral along a closed curve and primitive function

Proposition

Let $\Omega \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be open, $\gamma: \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ be a closed piecewise c^1 curve and $f: \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ be a continuous map with primitive Function $F$. Then

\begin{align*} \int_{\gamma} f(z) \mathrm{d}z = 0 \end{align*}
