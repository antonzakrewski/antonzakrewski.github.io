category of cofiber sequences is equivalent to arrow category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a finitely cocomplete pointed category. Let $\mathrm{Cofib}(\mathcal{C}) = \mathrm{Fun}((\Delta^1)^{2}, \mathcal{C})$ be the full subcategory containing cofiber diagrams.

Then the cofiber functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ar}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Cofib}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is an equivalence with inverse given by restriction to the original morphism.

Proof

follows as left kan extension along a fully faithful functor is fully faithful and right kan extension along a fully faithful functor is fully faithful : Here the essential image is by definition $\mathrm{Cofib}(\mathcal{C})$.
