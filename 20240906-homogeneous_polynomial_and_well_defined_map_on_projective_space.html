homogeneous polynomial and well defined map on projective space

Proposition

Let $K$ be a algebraically closed field, $K[X_0,...,X_n]$ the polynomial ring and $f \in K[X_1,...,X_n]$ a polynomial. TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a homogeneous polynomial
  2. the map
\begin{align*} f: \mathbb{P}_k^n \rightarrow& \{0,1\} \\ [\alpha_0 : ... \alpha_n] \mapsto& \begin{cases} 0 & \mbox{if } f(\alpha_0,...,\alpha_n) = 0 \\ 1 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

is welldefined

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows as $(\lambda \cdot \alpha_0)^n = \lambda^n \cdot \alpha_0^n$ so it follows that

\begin{align*} f(\lambda \cdot (\alpha_0,...,\alpha_n)) = \lambda^n \cdot f(\alpha_0,...,\alpha_n) \end{align*}

for $\lambda \neq 0$ and as $K$ is a UFD the LHS is 0 if and only if $f(\alpha_0,...,\alpha_n)$ is zero

2) $\implies$ 1)
