pullback of a monomorphism is a monomorphism in a 1-category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $A,B,C \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and $f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,C), g \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(B,C)$ morphisms. Suppose $f$ is a monomorphism and $P$ a pullback, then $\pi_1 \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(P,B)$ is also a monomorphism.
