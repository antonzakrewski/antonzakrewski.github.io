affine space as noetherian space

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space. Then $\mathbb{A}_K^n$ is a noetherian topological space.

Proof

Sei $Y_1 \supseteq Y_2 .. $ eine Dann folgt durch das vanishing ideal

\begin{align*} I(Y_1) \subseteq I(Y_2) \subseteq ... \end{align*}

finite polynomial ring over a field as noetherian ring da noethersch ~> wird stationär.

\begin{align*} V(I(Y_l)) = Y_l \end{align*}

for welldefinedness:

  • Vanishing set and order reversing
  • Bijection of radical ideals and affine subvarieties in an algebraically closed field
