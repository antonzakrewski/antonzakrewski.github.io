direct product of groups as categorical product in Grp

Proposition

Let $G_i$ be groups, then the direct product of groups is the categorical product in category Group

Proof

Suppose there exists a group $H$ and group-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi_i: H \rightarrow G_i \end{align*}

Then we define

\begin{align*} \varphi: H \rightarrow \prod G_i h \mapsto (\varphi_i(h))_{i \in I} \end{align*}

This is a welldefined group homomorphism, as

\begin{align*} \varphi(h_1 + h_2) =& (\varphi_i(h_1 + h_2) \\ =& (\varphi_i(h_1) + \varphi_i(h_2)) \\ =& (\varphi_i(h_1)) + (\varphi_i(h_2)) \\ =& \varphi(h_1) + \varphi(h_2) \end{align*}

Note, that by construction

commutes

Furthermore, this map is unique, as for $h \in H$ each component of $\varphi(h)$ is determined by $\varphi_i(h)$
