poincare duality lemma for a chain of open subsets

Proposition

Let \(M\) be an \(n\)-dimensional manifold, $U_i$ be a countable chain of open sets such that

\begin{align*} M = \bigcup U_i \end{align*}

Suppose furthermore $\sigma_M \in H_n^{\mathrm{lf}}(M;R)$ is an R-Orientation such that

\begin{align*} - \cap \sigma_{U_i}: H^n_c(U_i;R) \rightarrow& H_{n-p}^{\mathrm{lf}}(U_i;R) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism

Then

\begin{align*} - \cap \sigma_{M}: H^n_c(M;R) \rightarrow& H_{n-p}^{\mathrm{lf}}(M;R) \end{align*}

is also an isomorphism

\begin{displaymath}

H^p_c(U_j) \arrow[]{r}{\cap o_{U_j} }& H_{n-p}(U_i}
\end{tikzcd}

commutes.

where by definition

\begin{align*} o_{U_i} = \mathrm{inc}^{#}(o_{U_j}) \end{align*}
