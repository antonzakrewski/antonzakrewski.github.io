category of almost modules

Definition / Proposition

Let $A$ be a unital associative ring, $I \subseteq A$ a two sided ideal such that $I^2 = I$.

Then the category of almost $A/I$ modules is defined as the serre quotient of the restriction of scalars functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mod}_{A/I} \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}_{A} \end{align*}

Proof
