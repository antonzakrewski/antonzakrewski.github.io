trivial tangent bundle on sphere induces h-space

Proposition

Proof

choose a basis $v_1,...,v_{n-1}$. Then $x,v_1(x),...,v_{n-1}(x)$ forms a basis of $\mathbb{R}^{n} \supseteq S^{n-1}$.

Using Gram-Schmidtsches Orthogonalisierungsverfahren we may assume w.l.o.g. tha $x,v_1(x),...,v_{n-1}(x)$ is an Orthonormalbasis with $v_{k}(e_1) = e_{k+1}$
