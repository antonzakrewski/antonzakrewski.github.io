some low homotopy groups of S/n

Proposition

Let $n$ Then

\begin{align*} \pi_0(\mathbb{S}/n) =& \mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z} \\ \pi_1(\mathbb{S}/n) =& \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } 2 \mid n \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from the long exact sequence in $\mathrm{Sp}$
