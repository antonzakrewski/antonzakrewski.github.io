inclusion of set to anima induces inclusion of groups / monoids

Proposition

The full subcategory inclusions

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Set} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

induce full subcategory inclusions

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}(\mathrm{Set}) \hookrightarrow& \mathrm{Mon}(\mathrm{An}) \\ \mathrm{CMon}(\mathrm{Set}) \hookrightarrow& \mathrm{CMon}(\mathrm{An}) \\ \mathrm{Grp}(\mathrm{Set}) \hookrightarrow& \mathrm{Grp}(\mathrm{An}) \\ \mathrm{CGrp}(\mathrm{Set}) \hookrightarrow& \mathrm{CGrp}(\mathrm{An}) \\ \end{align*}

of:

  • category of cartesian commutative groups
  • category of cartesian commutative monoids

  • product preserving functor induces functor on cartesian commutative monoids / product preserving functor induces functor on cartesian groups
