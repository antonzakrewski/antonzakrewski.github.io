torsion element under module homomorphisms

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $M,N$ \(R\)-modules and

\begin{align*} \varphi: M \rightarrow N \end{align*}

Suppose $m \in M$ is a torsion element. Then so is $\varphi(m)$

Proof

By assumption there exists an $r \in R$ such that

\begin{align*} r \cdot m = 0 \end{align*}

Thus

\begin{align*} r \cdot \varphi(m) =& \varphi(r \cdot m) \\ =& \varphi(0) \\ =& 0 \end{align*}
