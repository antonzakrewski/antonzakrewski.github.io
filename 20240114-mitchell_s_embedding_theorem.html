Mitchell's embedding theorem

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be small abelian category. Then there exists a ring $R$ and a fully faithful exact functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathrm{RMod} \end{align*}

for the category RMod for some ring $R$
