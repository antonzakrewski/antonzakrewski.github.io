quotient free group action of a finite group on a smooth manifold is a smooth manifold

Proposition

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold and a smooth group action $G \curvearrowright M$ of a finite group which acts freely Then the quotient can be equipped with a canonical smooth structure such that the quotient map

\begin{align*} \pi: M \rightarrow M/\sim \end{align*}

Let $[x],[y] \in M/\sim$. Then choose disjoint open neighbourhoods $U_x,U_y$ and disjoint open neighbourhoods $U_{f(x)}, U_{f(y)}$ in $M$ (since $M$ is hausdorff). Then since $f$ is a diffeo, it is in particular an open map, so $f[U_x],f[U_y]$ are open. Hence take the intersections

\begin{align*} (U_{f(x)} \cap f(U_x)) \cup (U_x \cap f[U_{f(x)}]), (U_{f(y)} \cap f(U_y)) \cup (U_y \cap f[U_{f(y)}]) \end{align*}

Both are open as finite intersection / union of opens and they are disjoint.
