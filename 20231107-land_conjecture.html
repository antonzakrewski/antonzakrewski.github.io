Land conjecture

Conjecture

Let $(X, \mathcal{T}_X)$, $(Y, \mathcal{T}_Y)$ and $(Z, \mathcal{T}_Z)$ be topological spaces. Suppose $X$ is Hausdorff and $Y$ locally regular compact. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}(X \times Y, Z) \cong \mathrm{Map}(X, \mathrm{Map}(Y,Z)) \end{align*}

are homeomorphic

Proof
