representing object

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a locally small category, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ a covariant resp. contravariant functor. Then a representing object is an $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ such that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F} \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,-) \\ \mathcal{F} \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,A) \end{align*}

depending wether $\mathcal{F}$ is co/contravariant
