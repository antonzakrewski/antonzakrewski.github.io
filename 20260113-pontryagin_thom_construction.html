Pontryagin-Thom construction

Definition / Construction

Let $\theta: B \rightarrow \mathrm{BO}$ be a map. Then there exists a map of abelian groups

\begin{align*} \Omega_*^{\theta} \rightarrow \pi_*(\mathrm{M}\theta) \end{align*}

construction sketch

use the thom spectrum functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An}_{/\mathrm{BO}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp} \end{align*}

where a d-dimensional \(\theta\)-manifold induces a map on thom spectra $\mathrm{M}_{\nu_M} \rightarrow \mathrm{M}\theta$ where $\nu_M$ is the stable tangent bundle Now use the Pontryagin-Thom collapse map (???) which is a map $\mathbb{S}^d \rightarrow M_{\nu_M}$ which witnesses that the underlying spherical fibration of $\nu_M$ is the dualizing spectrum.

By precomposition this gives a map

\begin{align*} \mathbb{S}^d \xrightarrow M{\nu_M} \xrightarrow M \theta \end{align*}

or an element in $\pi_d(M\theta)$.
