uniqueness of an axiomatically characterized derived category

Any two axiomatically characterized derived categories are equivalent

Proof

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(1,-):\mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}(HR) \end{align*}
