lefschetz number

Definition

Let $X$ be a finite cw-complex and $f: X \rightarrow X$ a continuous map Then the lefschetz number is defined as

\begin{align*} \Lambda(f) \coloneqq \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} (-1)^k \mathrm{tr}(H_k(f;\mathbb{Q})) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{tr}$ is the trace of $H_k(f;\mathbb{Q})$ regarded as vectorspace-endomorphism of finite \(\mathbb{Q}\)-vector spaces
