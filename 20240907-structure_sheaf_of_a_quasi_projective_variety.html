structure sheaf of a quasi-projective variety

Definition / Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ a quasi-projective variety Then the structure sheaf is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O}_X: \mathrm{Op}(X)^{\mathrm{opp}} \rightarrow \mathrm{CRing} \\ U \mapsto& \mathcal{O}_X(U) = (U \hookrightarrow O)^{\mathrm{op}} \mapsto& \mathcal{O}_X(O) \rightarrow \mathcal{O}_X(U) \end{align*}

where $\mathcal{O}_X(U)$ is the ring of regular functions of a quasi-projective variety / ring of regular functions of a projective variety / ring of regular functions and the morphism is given by restriction.
