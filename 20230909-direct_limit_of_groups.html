direct limit of groups

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Grp}$ be the category of groups and $F: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a filtered diagram. Then the direct limit of $F$ is

\begin{align*} G \coloneqq (\coprod_{i \in J} G_i)/~ \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} (g,i) \sim (h,j) :\Leftrightarrow \exists k \in J f_{ik}(g) = f_j{jk}(h) \end{align*}

and multiplication is defined

\begin{align*} [(g,i)][(h,j)] = [f_{ik}(g)f_{jk}(h)] \end{align*}

\begin{align*} [e_i] \cdot [g_j] =& [\varphi_{i,k}(e_i) \cdot \varphi_{j,k}(g_j)] \\ =& [e_i \cdot \varphi_{j,k}(g_j)] \\ =& [\varphi_{j,k}(g_j)] \\ =& [g_j] \end{align*}
