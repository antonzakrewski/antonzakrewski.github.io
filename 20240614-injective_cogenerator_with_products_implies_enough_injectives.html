injective cogenerator with products implies enough injectives

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $I$ an injective cogenerator, $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ an object, such that the products $\prod_{\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(A,I)}$ exists. Then there exists a canonical monomorphism

\begin{align*} \iota: A \rightarrow \prod_{\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,I) I \end{align*}

into the injective object

Proof

corollary of:

  • product of injective objects as injective object
  • cogenerator with products implies monomorphism
