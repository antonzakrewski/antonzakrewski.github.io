amount of trees with certain degrees

Definition

Let $(d_1,...,d_n) \in \mathbb{N}^n$ with $\sum_{i=1}^n d_i = 2(n-1)$. Let $\mathcal{T}_{n}(d_1,...,d_n)$ be the set of trees on $\{1,...,n\}$.

Then

\begin{align*} \vert \mathcal{T}_n(d_1,...,d_n) \vert = \frac{(n-2)!}{\prod_{i=1}^n (d_i - 1)!} \end{align*}
