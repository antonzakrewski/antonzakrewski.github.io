left exact localization of a presheaf category and sheaf category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{E} \subseteq \mathcal{P}^1(\mathcal{C})$ a left exact left bousfield localization for $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$. Let $\tau$ be the collection of sieves $R \subseteq よ(X)$ such that $\mathcal{L}(R) \simeq \mathcal{L}(よ(X))$. Then $\tau$ is topology

Proof

observe that it contains the maximal sieves. Moreover, if $R \subseteq よ(X)$ is a sieve, then we get 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{L}(f^*(R)) \rar \dar & \mathcal{L}(よ(Y)) \dar \\
\mathcal{L}(R) \rar & \mathcal{L}(よ(X)) \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

which implies that $f^*(R) \subseteq よ(Y)$ is a sieve for all $f: Y \rightarrow X$.

hence the description from functor from grothendieck topologies to subcategories admits a left adjoint shows that the collection is a topology.
