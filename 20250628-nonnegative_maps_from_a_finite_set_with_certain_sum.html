amount of multisets with a fixed cardinality

Proposition

Let $n,k\in \mathbb{N}$. Then

\begin{align*} \vert \{f: \{1,...,k\} \rightarrow \mathbb{N}_0 \vert \sum_{i=1}^k f(i) = n\} \vert =& \frac{n^{\overline{k}}}{k!} \\ =& \binom{n+k-1}{k} \end{align*}
