mapping spectrum between finite spectra is finite

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be finite spectra, then so is the mapping spectrum $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X,Y)$.

Proof

follows as $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(-,-)$ is exact in both variables and $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S},\mathbb{S}) \simeq \mathbb{S}$.
