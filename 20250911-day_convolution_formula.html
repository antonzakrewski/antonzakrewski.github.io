day convolution formula

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})^{\otimes}$ be the functor category equipped with the day convolution Let $\mathcal{F}_1,\mathcal{F}_2 \in \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$, then the day convolution is pointwise given by

\begin{align*} (\mathcal{F}_1 \otimes \mathcal{F}_2)(x) \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{(x_1 \otimes x_2) \rightarrow x} \mathcal{F}(x_1) \otimes_{\mathcal{D}} \mathcal{F}_2(x_2) \\ \end{align*}
