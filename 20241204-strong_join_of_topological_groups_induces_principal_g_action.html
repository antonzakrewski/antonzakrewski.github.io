strong join of topological groups induces principal G-action

Proposition

Let $G$ be a topological group, then the \(G\)-topological group action on $\star_\mathbb{N} G$ is a principal g-action

Proof

Let $t_n: \star_{\mathbb{N}} G \rightarrow [0,1]$ be \(G\)-equivariant

consider

\begin{align*} \overline{U}_n \coloneqq \overline{t_n}^{-1}[(0,1]] \subseteq (\star_{\mathbb{N}} G)/G \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} p: \star_{\mathbb{N}} G \rightarrow (\star_{\mathbb{N}} G)/G \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} U_n =& p_{n}^{-1}( \overline{U}_n) \\ =& t_n^{-1}[(0,1]] \end{align*}

which projects to $G$

Consider the map

\begin{align*} \varphi_n: U_n \rightarrow U_n \end{align*}

given by

\begin{align*} (t,g_i) \mapsto (t g_i g_n^{-1}) \end{align*}

where

Then

\begin{align*} \varphi_n( (t_i,g_i) \cdot h) =& \varphi_n( (t_i g_i h)) \\ =& (t_i g_i h \cdot h^{-1} g_n) =& \varphi(t_i g_i) \end{align*}
