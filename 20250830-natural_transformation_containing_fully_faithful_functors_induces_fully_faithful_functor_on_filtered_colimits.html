filtered colimit of fully faithful functors is fully faithful on colimits

Proposition

Let $I$ be a filtered indexing category, $\mathcal{F}_1, \mathcal{F}_2: I \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}$ be functors and $\alpha: \mathcal{F}_1 \rightarrow \mathcal{F}_2$ a natural transformation consisting of pointwise fully faithful functors Then the induced map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}(\alpha): \mathrm{colim} \mathcal{F}_1 \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} \mathcal{F}_2 \end{align*}

is also fully faithful

Proof

follows from:

  • single arrow is compact
  • groupoid core functor preserves filtered colimits
