orthogonal complement is closed

Proposition

Let $V$ be a prehilbert space and $A \subseteq V$ a subset Then the orthogonal complement $A^{\bot}$ is a closed subset

Proof

follows as $A^{\bot}$ it is the intersection $\bigcap_{a_i \in A} <a_i,->^{-1}[0]$ where $\{0\}$ is closed and therefore also the preimage
