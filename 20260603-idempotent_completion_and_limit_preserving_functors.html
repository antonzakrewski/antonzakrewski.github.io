idempotent completion and product preserving functors

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a cardinal, $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{C}^{\natural}$ the idempotent completion and $\mathcal{D}$ any idempotent complete category. Then the restriction $\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\natural}$ induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\kappa-\prod}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\kappa-\prod}(\mathcal{C}^{\natural}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

Proof

Recall that there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \iota^*: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\natural}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

so it suffices to show that any functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\natural} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ preserves $\kappa$ small products if and only if the restriction to $\mathcal{C}$ preserves products.

a) if

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\natural} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor, which preserves products when restricted to $\mathcal{C}$. Let $c_i$ be a collection of objects in $\mathcal{C}^{\natural}$, we need to show that $\mathcal{F}(\prod_{i \in I} c_i) \simeq \prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{F}(c_i)$ are canonically equivalent. We can find $c_i'$ in $\mathcal{C}$ such that each $c_i$ is a retract of $c_i'$. This shows that $\mathcal{F}(\prod_{i \in I} c_i)$ is a retract of $\mathcal{F}(\prod_{i \in I} c_i') \simeq \prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{F}(c_i')$ and retract of a limit diagram is a limit diagram.
