loop space of a classifying space weakly equivalent to the group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a topological group, $BG$ the classifying space for $p: EG \rightarrow BG$ a universal bundle with $EG$ contractible space. Then there exists a weak homotopy equivalence

\begin{align*} \Omega BG \cong G \end{align*}

Proof

follows from the puppe sequence + based loop space as reduced functor

where applying $\pi_n$ gives a long exact sequnce
