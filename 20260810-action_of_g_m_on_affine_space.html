action of G_m on affine space

Proposition

Let $I$ be a set. Then there exists an group action of multiplicative affine group scheme G_m on $\mathbb{A}^I_k$, which is pointwise given by

\begin{align*} \cdot: \mathbb{A}_k^I(R) \times G_m(R) \rightarrow& \mathbb{A}_k^I(R) \\ (a_i) \times r \mapsto& (r a_i) \end{align*}

which is a natural transformation

Proof

the natural transformation bit follows as ring homomorphisms and multiplication behave well.
