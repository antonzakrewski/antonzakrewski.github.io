improved zariski descent

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $L$ a line over $R$ and $s_i \in L$ generating $L$. Then there exists an equivalence 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{Mod}_{R} \rar & \prod_{i \in I} \mathrm{Mod}_{R_{s_i}} \arrow[shift right=1, swap]{r}{} \arrow[shift left=1]{r}{} & \rar \arrow[shift right=2, swap]{r}{} \arrow[shift left=2]{r}{}  \prod_{i,j \in I} \mathrm{Mod}_{R_{s_i s_j}} &    \prod_{i,j,k \in I} \mathrm{Mod}_{R_{s_i s_j s_k}
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Proof
