extension of a principal ideal as principal ideal

Proposition

Let $A,B$ be rings, $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ a ring-homomorphism and $(a) \subseteq A$ a principal ideal. Then the extension $(a)^{\mathfrak{e}}$ is also a principal ideal.

Proof

Note that $(a)^{\mathfrak{e}}$ is the ideal generated by $\varphi[(a)]$. Here $\varphi[(a)]$ is generated by $\varphi(a)$, as $\varphi(r \cdot a \cdot r') = \varphi(r) \cdot \varphi(a) \cdot \varphi(r')$
