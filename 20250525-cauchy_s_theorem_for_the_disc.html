cauchy's theorem for the disc

Proposition

Let $D \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be an open disk and $f: D \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ be holomorphic and $\gamma$ be a closed curve. Then

\begin{align*} \int_{\gamma} f(z) \mathrm{d} z = 0 \end{align*}
