product of serre fibrations as serre fibration

Proposition

Let $p_{\alpha}: X_{\alpha} \rightarrow Y_{\alpha}$ be a family of serre fibrations Then the product

\begin{align*} \prod p_{\alpha}: \prod X_{\alpha} \rightarrow Y_{\alpha} \end{align*}
