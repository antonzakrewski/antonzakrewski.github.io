k-suspension as k-connected space

Proposition

Let $(X,x)$ be a well pointed space. Then the \(k\)-suspension for $k \geq 1$ is d-connected

Proof

Proof by induction

induction basis

  • reduced suspension as 1 connected space

induction step

Note that a \(k\) suspension is also a \(k-1\)-suspension of $\Sigma X$ Then it remains to show that

\begin{align*} \pi_k(\Sigma^k X) = 0 \end{align*}

This follows from freudenthal suspension map for p-connected, well pointed spaces and induction, where

\begin{align*} 0 = \pi_{k-1}(\Sigma^{k-1} X) \rightarrow \pi_k(\Sigma^k X) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism, as $k - 1 \geq 1$
