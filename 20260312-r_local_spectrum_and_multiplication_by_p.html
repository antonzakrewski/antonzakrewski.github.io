R-local spectrum and multiplication by p

Proposition

Let $R = \mathbb{Z}[p^{-1} \vert p \in P] \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ be a subring and $X$ a spectrum TFAE:

  1. $X$ is an R-local spectrum
  2. multiplication by $p$ is an equivalence on $X$ for each $p$.

Proof

follows from explicit description of the R-localization functor
