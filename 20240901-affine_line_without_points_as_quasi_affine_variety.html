affine line without points as quasi affine variety

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{A}_K^1$ be the classical affine line, $\{x_1,...,x_n\} \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^1$ a finite subset. Then the complement $\mathbb{A}_K^1 \setminus \{x_1,...,x_n\}$ is an simple quasi-affine variety

Proof

  • open without closed as open subset
