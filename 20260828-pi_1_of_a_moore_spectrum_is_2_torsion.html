pi_1 of a moore spectrum is 2-torsion

Proposition

Let $A$ be an abelian group Then $\pi_1(\mathbb{S}_{A})$ is a $2$ torsion group.

Proof

choose a presentation

\begin{align*} \bigoplus \mathbb{Z} \hookrightarrow \bigoplus \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow B \end{align*}

(cf. abelian group admits free resolution of length 2).

Then we have a cofiber sequence

\begin{align*} \bigoplus \mathbb{S} \rightarrow \bigoplus \mathbb{S} \rightarrow \mathbb{S}_{A} \end{align*}

and in particular - using stable hurewicz theorem - shows for the long exact sequence 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\bigoplus \mathbb{Z}/ 2 \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \bigoplus \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \pi_2(\mathbb{S}_A} \rightarrow \bigoplus \mathbb{Z} \hookrightarrow \bigoplus \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow A
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

which shows that $\pi_1(\mathbb{S}_{A}$ is a quotient of $\pi_1(\bigoplus \mathbb{S}) \simeq \bigoplus \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}$.
