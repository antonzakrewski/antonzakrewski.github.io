Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be a normed vector spaces where $Y$ is a banach space. Then $\{A \in \mathrm{BL}(X,Y) \vert A \text{ is invertible}\}$ is an open subset of $\mathrm{BL}(X,Y)$.

Proof

\begin{align*} B_{ \frac{1}{2} \| A^{-1} \|}(A) \subseteq& \{A \in \mathrm{BL}(X,Y) \vert A \text{ is invertible}\} \end{align*}
