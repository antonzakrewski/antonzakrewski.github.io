mapping anima as quasicategory

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category and $\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y)$ the mapping space. Then $\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y)$ is also a quasicategory

Proof

follows, as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

is an inner fibration
