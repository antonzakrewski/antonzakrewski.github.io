cofibration as embedding

Proposition

Let $(X,A)$ be topological spaces, such that $\iota: A \rightarrow X$ is a cofibration. Then $\iota$ is an topological embedding

Proof

By mapping cylinder as embedding for a cofibration, the map

\begin{align*} g: \mathrm{Cyl}(i) \rightarrow& X \times [0,1] \end{align*}

defined by the universal property for the maps

\begin{align*} g_1: X \times \{0\} \rightarrow& X \times [0,1] \\ q(x,0) \mapsto& (x,0) \\ g_2: A \times [0,1] \rightarrow X \times [0,1] \\ (a,t) \mapsto& (\iota(a), t) \end{align*}

is an topological embedding

Thus by restriction of an embedding as embedding, the map

\begin{align*} g_{\vert A \times \{1\}}: A \times \{1\} \rightarrow X \times [0,1] \end{align*}

or equivalently, since $\mathrm{im}(g_{\vert A \times \{1\}}) \subseteq X \times \{1\}$ and $A \times \{1\} \cong A, X \times \{1\} \cong X$ (see: coproduct with the initial object canonically iso)

\begin{align*} g': A \rightarrow X \end{align*}

is an embedding (cf. restriction of the codomain of an embedding)
