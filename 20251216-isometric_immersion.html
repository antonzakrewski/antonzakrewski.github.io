isometric immersion

Definition

Let $M,N$ be riemannian manifolds and $f: M \rightarrow N$ be a diffeomorphism between the underlying smooth manifolds. Then $f$ is said to be an isometry, if for all $p \in M$ the induced morphism

\begin{align*} Tf_p: T_pM \rightarrow T_{f(p)}M \end{align*}

is unitary
