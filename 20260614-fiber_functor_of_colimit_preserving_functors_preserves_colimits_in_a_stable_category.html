fiber functor of colimit preserving functors preserves colimits in a stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category, $\mathcal{F}_1 \Rightarrow \mathcal{F}_2$ a natural transformation of \(I\)-shaped colimit preserving functors. Then the fiber functor also preserves \(I\)-shaped colimits.

Proof

follows from fiber functor preserves colimits for a stable category and composition of \(I\)-shaped colimit preserving functors is \(I\)-shaped colimit perserving.
