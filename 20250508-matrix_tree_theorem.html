Matrix Tree theorem

Proposition

Let $G = (V,E)$ be a undirected multigraph with $\vert V \vert \geq 2$. Then the amount of Spanning Trees is given by

\begin{align*} \tau(G) = \mathrm{det}((D - A)^{i,i}) \end{align*}

where we delete the \(i\)-th row and \(i\)-th column
