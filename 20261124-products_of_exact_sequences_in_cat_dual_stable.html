products of exact sequences in Cat dual stable

Proposition

Any prodcut of an exact sequence in category of stable dualizable categories is exact.

Proof

we have to show that $\prod^{\mathrm{dual}}$ preserves localizations, since taking products commutes with fibers. So let $\mathcal{F}_i: \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{D}_}$ be a family of localizations in $\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{dual}}_{\mathrm{st}}$.

we show that $\prod_{i}^{\mathrm{dual} \mathcal{F})^{\vee}$ in $\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\mathrm{st}}$ is fully faithful.

Now $(\prod_{i}^{\mathrm{dual}} \mathcal{D}_i)^{\vee} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\prod_{i} \mathcal{D}_i^{\kappa})^{\vee} \simeq \mathrm{Ind}(\prod \mathcal{D}_i^{\kappa, \mathrm{op}})$
