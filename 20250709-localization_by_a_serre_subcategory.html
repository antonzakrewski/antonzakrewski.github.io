localization by a serre subcategory

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathcal{B} \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ a serre subcategory. Then there exists an abelian category $\mathcal{A}/\mathcal{B}$ with an exact functor $\iota: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{A}/\mathcal{B}$ such that precomposition induce a fully faithful functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{ex}}(\mathcal{A}/\mathcal{B}, \mathcal{A}') \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{ex}}(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{A}') \end{align*}

with essential image given by functors $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{A}'$ such that $\mathcal{F}_{\vert \mathcal{B}} = 0$
