weak cartesian structure on an infinity operad

Definition

Let $p: \mathcal{C}^{\otimes} \rightarrow N(\mathrm{Fin}_*)$ be an infinity operad and $\pi: \mathcal{C}^{\otimes} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a lax cartesian structure on an infinity operad. Then $\pi$ is said to be a weak cartesian structure, if for every p-cocartesian active morphism $f \in \mathcal{C}^{\otimes}_1$ the induced morphism

\begin{align*} \pi(f) \in \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

is an equivalence
