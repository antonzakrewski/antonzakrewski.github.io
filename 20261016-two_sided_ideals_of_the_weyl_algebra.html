weyl algebra in characteristic zero is simple

Proposition

Let $k \langle x,y\rangle/ \langle yx - xy - 1\rangle$ be the Weyl algebra over a field in characteristic zero. Then the zero ideal is a maximal two sided ideal (and hence trivial).

Proof

Suppose there exists a nontrivial polynomial $f \in \mathfrak{a}$ a two sided ideal. Then $f = \sum \alpha_i x^{n_i} y^{m_i}$ and we have

\begin{align*} f y - yf =& \sum \alpha_i n_{i} x^{n_i -1} \cdot y^{m_i} \\ xf - fx =& \sum \alpha_i n_{i} x^{n_i} \cdot y^{m_i-1} \end{align*}

And hence we may differentiate a polynomial (in both variables) often enough to eventually get a constant term, which then generates the whole weyl algebra: In particular, the constant term can be made nonzero: for this choose the lexicographic monomial order with $x < y$ and then choose the polynomial with highest monomial order and differentiate this accordingly.
