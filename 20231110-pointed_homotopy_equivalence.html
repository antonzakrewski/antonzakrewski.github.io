pointed homotopy equivalence

Definition

Let $(X,x_0)$ and $(Y,y_0)$ be based spaces. A pointed homotopy equivalence is a Homotopy equivalence if there exist based maps $f: X \rightarrow Y$ and $g: Y \rightarrow X$ sucht hat

\begin{align*} f \circ g \sim& \mathrm{id}_{Y} \\ g \circ f \sim& \mathrm{id}_{Y} \end{align*}
