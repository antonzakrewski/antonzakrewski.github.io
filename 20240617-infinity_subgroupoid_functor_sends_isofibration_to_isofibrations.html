infinity subgroupoid functor sends isofibration to isofibrations

Proposition

Let $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an <inner fibration> / isofibration> between infinity categories Then the induced morphism

\begin{align*} f^{\cong}: \mathcal{C}^{\cong} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}^{\cong} \end{align*}

under the maximal infinity-subgroupoid functor and the subcategory inclusion is an isofibration

Proof

Consider the lifting problem 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\{0\} \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & \mathcal{C}^{\cong} \arrow[]{d}{} \arrow[]{r}{}& \mathcal{C} \arrow[]{d}{}\\
\Delta^1 \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[dashde]{ru}{}& \mathcal{D}^{\cong} \arrow[]{r}{} & \mathcal{D}\\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Then 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\{0\} \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & \mathcal{C}^{\cong} \arrow[]{d}{} \arrow[]{r}{}& \mathcal{C} \arrow[]{d}{}\\
\Delta^1 \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[dashed]{rru}{} \arrow[dashde]{ru}{}& \mathcal{D}^{\cong} \arrow[]{r}{} & \mathcal{D}\\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

has a solution, as $p$ is an isofibration.

As $p$ is an isofibration, there exists a factorization through $\mathcal{C}^{\cong}$
