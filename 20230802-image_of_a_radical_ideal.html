extension of a radical ideal

Proposition

Let $A,B$ be commutative rings, $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ an homomorphism and $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ an ideal. Then

\begin{align*} \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}}^{\mathfrak{e}} \subseteq \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}^{\mathfrak{e}}} \end{align*}

Proof

Let $x \in \sqrt{a}$, then there exists an $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $x^n \in \mathfrak{a}$ Furthermore

\begin{align*} f(x^n) = f(x)^n \in f[\mathfrak{a}] \end{align*}

and hence $f(x) \in \sqrt{f[\mathfrak{a}]}$
