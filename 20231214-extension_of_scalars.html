extension of scalars

Definition

Let $A,B$ be a rings and $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ a ring-homomorphism. Then the extension of scalars is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ext}: \mathrm{A-Mod} \rightarrow \mathrm{B-Mod} M \mapsto& B \otimes_A M (f: M \rightarrow N) \mapsto& (\mathrm{id}\otimes_A f: B \otimes_A M \rightarrow B \otimes_A N) \end{align*}

where $B$ is the simple restriction of scalars for a ringhomomorphism and $B \otimes_A M$ is the \(B\)-module obtained by regarding $B$ as \(B,A\)-module (cf. tensor product of bimodules)
