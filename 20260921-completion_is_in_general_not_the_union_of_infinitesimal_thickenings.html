Proof

Let $R = k[x_n]/x^n$, $X = \mathrm{Spec}(R)$ and $V(I) = R$ for the ideal $I = \langle x_n\rangle$. Now the underlying space of $V(I)$ is given by $\vert X \vert$ and hence $X_{V(I)}^{\wedge} = X$ (cf completion of an algebraic functor along a closed subset is largest subfunctor).

Now $I^n$ is the ideal of polynomials consisting of monomials of at least order $n$ (and the zero element). Moreover, $\mathrm{colim} V(I^n)$ is the underlying union (cf. filtered colimits are exact in presheaf categories) but we claim that $\mathrm{id}_R \not\in \mathrm{colim} V(I^n)(R)$. Indeed, for each $n \in \mathbb{N}$, there exists a nontrivial element, namely $x_{n+1}^{n}$ in $I^n$, which shows that $\mathrm{id}_{R} \not\in V(I^n)$.
