infinity functor category of a small and locally small category as locally small category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an essentially small infinity category and $\mathcal{D}$ a locally small infinity category. Then the infinity functor category $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ is also locally small.

Proof

we may assume that $\mathcal{C}$ is small by joyal equivalence of infinity categories and precomposition and mapping space functor, equivalence in an infinity category under functors.

Then given two functors $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ we may factor them through the small full infinity subcategory $\mathcal{D}^0$ containing $\mathcal{F}(c), \mathcal{G}(c)$ for $c \in \mathcal{C}$

Then any natural transformation between $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}$ also lies in

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}^0) \end{align*}

which is small by infinity functor category of small categories as small category
