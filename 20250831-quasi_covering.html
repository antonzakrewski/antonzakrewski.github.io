quasi-covering

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an essentially small category, then a quasi-covering consists for each object $X\in \mathcal{C}$ of a collection of covering families $\mathcal{X} = \{X_i \rightarrow X\}_{i \in I}$ such ahat

  • for every covering family $\mathcal{X} = \{X_i \rightarrow X\}_{i \in I}$ and every map $Y \rightarrow X$ the pullbacks $Y_i = Y \times_{X} X_i$ exist and the collection $\mathcal{Y} = Y \times_{X} \mathcal{X} = \{Y_i \rightarrow Y\}_{i \in I}$ is a covering family
