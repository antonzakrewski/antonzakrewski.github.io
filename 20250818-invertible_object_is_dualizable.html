Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \otimes, \mathbbm{1})$ be a monoidal category, and $X$ a invertible object. Then $X$ is right dualizable.

Proof

Let $X^{\vee} \coloneqq X^{-1}$ the inverse object. Then consider the equivalences

\begin{align*} \mathbbm{1} \rightarrow& X \otimes X^{\vee} \\ X^{\vee} \otimes X \rightarrow& \mathbbm{1} \end{align*}
