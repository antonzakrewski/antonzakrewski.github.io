homotopy group functor from spectra preserves arbitrary sums

Proposition

The homotopy group functor of spectra preserves coproducts

Proof

finite

note that stable infinity category as additive infinity category and spectra is stable. Hence $M \coprod N \simeq M \times N$ and thus

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}[n], M \coprod N) \simeq& \mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}[n], M \times N) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}[n], M) \times \mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}[n], N) \\ \end{align*}

where the pi_0 of Anima preserves products, which shows

\begin{align*} \pi_0(\mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}[n], M) \times \mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}[n], N)) \simeq& \pi_0(\mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}[n], M)) \times \pi_0(\mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}[n], N)) \end{align*}

arbitrary

  • use kappa filtered colimits and kappa small coproducts imply small coproduct
  • homotopy group functor of spectra preserves filtered colimits
