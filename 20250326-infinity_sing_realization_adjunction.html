infinity sing / realization adjunction

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an infinity functor. Suppose $\mathcal{D}$ is cocomplete

Then there exists an adjunction

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}: \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow& \mathcal{D} \\ \mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow& \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

where

is given as composition of infinity yoneda embedding and <precomposition> with $\mathcal{F}$ and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}: \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow& \mathcal{D} \\ \end{align*}

is given as unique colimit preserving extension of $\mathcal{F}$ (cf. infinity presheaf category arises by freely adding colimits)

Proof

Let $P$ be any presheaf with $P \simeq \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,c_i)$ and $d \in \mathcal{D}$. Then $\mathcal{R}(d) \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,\mathcal{F}(d))$ and using pointwise construction of an adjunction and infinity presheaf as colimit of representables and contravariant infinity yoneda lemma

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(P, \mathcal{R}(d)) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathrm{colim}_{I} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,c_i),\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(-),d)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{lim}_{I} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}( \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,c_i),\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(-),d)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{lim}_{I} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(c_i),\mathcal{F}(d)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathrm{colim}_I \mathcal{F}(c_i),d) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathrm{colim}_I \mathcal{L}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,c_i)),d) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}( \mathcal{L}(\mathrm{colim}_I \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,c_i)),d) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}( \mathcal{L}(P),d) \\ \end{align*}
