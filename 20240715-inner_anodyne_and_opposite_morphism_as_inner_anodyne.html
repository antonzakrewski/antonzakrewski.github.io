inner anodyne and opposite morphism is inner anodyne

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a morphism of simplicial sets

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is inner anodyne map
  2. the opposite morphism $f^{\mathrm{op}}$ is inner anodyne

Proof

a)

  • inner anodyne as saturation of inner horn inclusion
  • isomorphism preserves saturations
  • opposite of an inner horn as inner horn

b) alternative

  • lifting problem and isomorphism
