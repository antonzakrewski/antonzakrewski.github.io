kappa filtered colimits and kappa small coproducts imply arbitrary colimit

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal, $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, which admits

  1. kappa filtered infinity colimit
  2. kappa-small infinity coproducts

Then $\mathcal{C}$ is cocomplete
