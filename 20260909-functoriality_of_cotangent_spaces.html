functoriality of cotangent spaces

Proposition

Let <category of k-schemes over a field, pointed over geometric points>. Then the zariski cotangent space is a functor.

Proof

follows as a map of schemes $f: Y \rightarrow X$ induces a local ring homomorphism $f: (\mathcal{O}_{Y,p}, \mathfrak{m}) \rightarrow (\mathcal{O}_{X,f(p)}, \mathfrak{n})$. Then this induces a map $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2 \rightarrow \mathfrak{n}/\mathfrak{n}^2$ since everything restricts appropriately.
