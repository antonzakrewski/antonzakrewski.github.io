lower t-subcategory of a stable infinity category as left bousfield localization

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category equipped with a t-structure. Then the lower t-subcategory $\mathcal{C}_{\leq n}$ is an localization

Proof

w.l.o.g. assume that $n = -1$, the general case follows from $\Sigma, \Omega$ as equivalences (cf. endofunctor as localization and equivalence). Then by localization and objectwise localization it suffices to show that for each $X \in \mathcal{C}$ there exists a map

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow X'' \end{align*}

with $X'' \in \mathcal{C}_{-1}$ which induces an equivalence of mapping spaces for $Y \in \mathcal{C}_{\leq -1}$

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}_{\leq -1}}(X'',Y) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}_{\leq -1}}(X,Y) \end{align*}

Choose a fiber sequence

\begin{align*} X' \rightarrow X \rightarrow X'' \end{align*}

with $X' \in \mathcal{C}_{\geq 0}$ given by the t-structure and $X''$ as localization candidate.

Then we have to show that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{C}}^{k}(X'',Y) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{C}}^{k}(X,Y) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism for $k \leq 0$ (cf. whitehead theroem for stable infinity categories)

But then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{C}}^k(X',Y) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{h \mathcal{C}}(X'[-k], Y) \simeq 0 \end{align*}

since $Y \in \mathcal{C}_{\geq 0}$.

Thus we may invoke the long exact sequence of homotopy groups for anima to see that the map is an isomorphism.
