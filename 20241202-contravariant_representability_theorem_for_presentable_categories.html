contravariant representability theorem for presentable categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ an infinity functor

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is representable
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ preserves limits

Proof
