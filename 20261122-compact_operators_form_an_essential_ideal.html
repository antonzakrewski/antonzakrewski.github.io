compact operators form an essential ideal

Proposition

Let $H$ be a Hilbert space and let $\mathbb{K}(H) \subseteq \mathbb{B(H)}$ be the compact operators form a star ideal. Then it is an essential ideal

Proof

Let $A \in \mathbb{B(H)}$, then for eahc $x \in \mathbb{H}$ we can define the projection $P_x$ to $\langle x\rangle$. It is a compact operator and if $ P_x A = 0$ for all $x \in X$, then this implies that $\mathrm{im}(P) = 0$ or $P = 0$.
