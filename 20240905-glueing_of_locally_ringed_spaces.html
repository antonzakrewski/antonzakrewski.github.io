glueing of locally ringed spaces

Definition / Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $X_i$ an open cover and $\mathcal{O}_{X_i}$ locally ringed spaces. Suppose for $U_i \subseteq X_i \cap X_j$ it follows that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O}_{X_i}(U_i) \cong \mathcal{O}_{X_j}(U_i) \end{align*}

compatible with the restriction.

Then there exists a locally ringed space determined by

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O}_X(X_i) = \mathcal{O}_{X_i}(X_i) \end{align*}

or

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O}_X(U) = \mathrm{lim}(\prod_{i} \mathcal{O}_{X_i}(X_{i} \cap U) \rightarrow \prod_{i,j} \mathcal{O}_{X_i}(X_i \cap U_i \cap U_j)) \end{align*}
