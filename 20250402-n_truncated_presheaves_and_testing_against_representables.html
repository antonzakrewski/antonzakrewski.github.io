n-truncated presheaves and testing against representables

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ the infinity presheaf category. Suppose $\overline{\mathcal{P}} \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ is a full subcategory which contains the essential image of a subcategory $\mathcal{C}_0 \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ under the infinity yoneda embedding.

Let $\mathcal{F}$ be an n-truncated object. Then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(c_0) \end{align*}

is an n-truncated anima for $c_0 \in \mathcal{C}_0$

Proof

follows from:

  • contravariant infinity yoneda lemma

where

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\overline{\mathcal{P}}}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,c_0), \mathcal{F}) \simeq& \mathcal{F}(c_0) \end{align*}

is an \(n\)-truncated anima since $\mathcal{F}$ is \(n\)-truncated

note

if and only if requires knowledge about other presheafs and their colimit representation under yoneda :(
