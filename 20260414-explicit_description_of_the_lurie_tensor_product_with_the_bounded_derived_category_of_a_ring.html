explicit description of the lurie tensor product with the bounded derived category of a ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a ring, $\mathcal{D}(A)_{\geq 0}$ the connective part of the bounded derived category and $\mathcal{C}$ a presentable category. Then the lurie tensor product $\mathcal{D}_{\geq 0}(A) \otimes \mathcal{C}$ is equivalent to $\mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}( \{A^n\}, \mathcal{C})$
