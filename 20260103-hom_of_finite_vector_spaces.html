hom of finite vector spaces is isomorphic to tensor product

Proposition

Let $V_1,V_2$ be finite dimensional vector spaces, then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{K}(V_1,V_2) \cong V_1^* \oplus V_2 \end{align*}

Proof

pointwise

choose a basis of $V_1$ (thus we get in particular a dual basis for $V_1^$) and consider

\begin{align*} (f: V_1 \rightarrow V_2) \mapsto (\sum_{i=1}^n \partial_i \otimes f(e_i)) \end{align*}
