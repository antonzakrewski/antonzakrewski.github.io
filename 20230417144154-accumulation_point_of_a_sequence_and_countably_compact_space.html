accumulation point of a sequence and countably compact space

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space and $A \subseteq X$ a subset. Then following propositions are equivalent:

  • $A$ is countably compact
  • every sequence $A$ has an accumulation point
