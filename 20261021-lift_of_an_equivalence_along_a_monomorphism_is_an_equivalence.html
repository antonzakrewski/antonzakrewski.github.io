lift of an equivalence along a monomorphism is an equivalence

Proposition

Let

be a diagram, where $C \hookrightarrow B$ is a monomorphism Then all three maps are equivalence

Proof

By the two out of three principle for equivalences it suffices to show that $A \rightarrow C$ is an equivalence But for that consider the pullback squares 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
A \rar \dar & \dar \rar C & \dar C \\
A \arrow[bend right]{ll}{} \rar & C \rar & B 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Here both small squares are pullbacks, therefore so is the outer. Now $A \rightarrow B$ is an equivalence, hence so must be $A \rightarrow C$ (cf. square with parallel isos as pullback).
