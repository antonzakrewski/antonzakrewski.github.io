locality of a sheaf

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space and $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Op}(X) \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ a sheaf. Suppose $U \subseteq X$ is open, $U_i \subseteq U$ a cover of $U$ and $s,t \in \mathcal{F}(U)$ sections such that

\begin{align*} s_{\vert U_i} = t_{\vert U_i} \forall i \in I \end{align*}

Then $s = t$
