forgetful functor from the coslice category

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $X \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ an object and $\mathcal{C} \downarrow X$ the coslice category. Then there exists the canonical forgetful functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \downarrow X \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ (p: X \rightarrow A) \mapsto& A \\ (f: (X \rightarrow A) \mapsto& (X \rightarrow B)) \mapsto& (f: A \rightarrow B) \end{align*}
