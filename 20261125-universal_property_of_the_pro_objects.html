universal property of the pro objects

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. Then the category $\mathrm{Pro}(\mathcal{C})$ has the universal property that there exists a map $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Pro}(\mathcal{C})$ which induces the following equivalence for all categories $\mathcal{D}$ with cofiltered limits, there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\textrm{cofiltered, lim}}(\mathrm{Pro}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}
