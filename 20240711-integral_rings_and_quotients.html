integral rings and quotients

Proposition

Let $A \hookrightarrow B$ be an integral ring extension, $\mathfrak{b} \subseteq B$ an ideal. Let $\mathfrak{a} \coloneqq A \cap B \subseteq A$ be the intersection.

Then

\begin{align*} \frac{A}{\mathfrak{a}} \hookrightarrow \frac{B}{\mathfrak{b}} \end{align*}

is an integral extension

Proof
