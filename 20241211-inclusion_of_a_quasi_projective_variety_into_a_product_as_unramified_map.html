inclusion of a quasi projective variety into a product as unramified map

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X,Y \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ be nonsingular quasi-projective varieties, $P \in X$ a point. Then the map

\begin{align*} \iota: X \rightarrow& X \times Y \\ x \mapsto (x,y) \end{align*}

is a unramified

Proof

Consider the projection

\begin{align*} f: X \times Y \rightarrow& X \\ (x,y) \mapsto& x \end{align*}

It follows that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{pr} \circ \iota = \mathrm{id} \end{align*}

and hence by the chain rule for the formal derivative of a polynomial

\begin{align*} \mathrm{d}( \mathrm{pr} \circ \iota) =& (d \mathrm{pr})_{\iota(P)} \circ (\mathrm{d} \iota)_P \\ =& \mathrm{id}_{T_p(X)} \end{align*}

hence $\mathrm{d} \mathrm{inc}$ is split mono
