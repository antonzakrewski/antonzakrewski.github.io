extendable morphism of cartesian patterns

Proposition

a morphism of cartesian patterns $f: \mathcal{O} \rightarrow \mathcal{P}$ is said to be extendable if

  1. $f$ has unique lift of inerts
  2. themap
\begin{align*} \prod (p_i)_! \mathcal{O}_{/p}^{\mathrm{act}} \rightarrow \prod \mathcal{O}_{/p_i} \end{align*}

is cofinal

Proof
