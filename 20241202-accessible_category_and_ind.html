accessible category and Ind

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal and $\mathcal{C}$ an infinity category.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ is a \(\kappa\)-kappa accessible infinity category.
  2. $\mathcal{C} \simeq \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C}^{\kappa})$ where $\mathcal{C}^{\kappa}$ is the full infinity subcategory containing \(kappa\)-compact objects, where $\mathcal{C}^{\kappa}$ furthermore is essentially small.
  3. there exists a set of \(\kappa\)-compact objects such that for full subcategory $S$ containing those objects
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(S) \cong \mathcal{C} \end{align*}
  1. there exists a small infinity category $S$ such that
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(S) \cong \mathcal{C} \end{align*}
