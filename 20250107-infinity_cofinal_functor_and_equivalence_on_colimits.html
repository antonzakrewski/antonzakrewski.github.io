cofinal functor and limits

Proposition

Let $\alpha: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathcal{J}$ be a functor between categories.

TFAE:

  1. $\alpha$ cofinal
  2. for each category $\mathcal{E}$ and every diagram $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ the following two statements holds a) the functor $\mathcal{F}$ has a limit if and only if $\mathcal{F} \circ \alpha$ does b) if both of the limits exists, then the natural map (cf. precomposition with a functor induces map on limits)
\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim} \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathrm{lim} \mathcal{F} \circ \alpha \end{align*}

is an equivalence

Proof
