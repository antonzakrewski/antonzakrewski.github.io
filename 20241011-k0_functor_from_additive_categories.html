K0 functor from preabelian categories

Definition

Let $\mathrm{PreAb}$ the category containing (small) pre-abelian caegories as objects and additive functors as morphisms. Then there exists the $K_0$ functor

\begin{align*} K_0: \mathrm{Add} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ab} \\ \mathcal{C} \mapsto& K_0(\mathcal{C}) \\ (\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}) \mapsto& \mathcal{F}: P \mapsto \mathcal{F}(P) \\ \end{align*}

Proof

object:

  • k0 of an abelian category

morphism

Welldefined on elements follows from the fact, that relations $P + Q = M$ correspond to short exact sequences

which get preserved by $\mathcal{F}$

hence each $\mathcal{F}: K_0(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow K_0(\mathcal{D})$ is a welldefined group homomorphism.

Furthermore it is clearly functorial.
