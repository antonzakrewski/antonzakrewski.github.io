destabilization of a oriented spherical fibration

Definition

Let $\pi: E \rightarrow B$ a oriented spherical fibration Then a destabilizaiton of $\pi$ - if it exists - is another oriented spherical fibration $\pi': E' \rightarrow B$ where

\begin{align*} \sigma^k(\pi') \cong \pi \end{align*}
