zero locus of the vanishing ideal as superset

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^n$ a subset. Then

\begin{align*} X \subseteq V(I(X)) \end{align*}

see:

  • zero locus of an affine space
  • vanishing ideal

Proof

Let $x \in X$, then it follows by definition of $I(X)$ that $f(x) = 0$ for $f \in I(X)$. Hence $x \in V(I(X))$
