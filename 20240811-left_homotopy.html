left homotopy

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}, \mathrm{Cof}, \mathrm{Fib})$ be a model category,

\begin{align*} f,g: X \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

parallel morphisms and $\mathrm{Cyl}(X)$ a cylinder Then a left homotopy $\eta: f \Rightarrow_L g$ is a morphism

\begin{align*} \eta: \mathrm{Cyl}(X) \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

such that 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
& X \coprod X \arrow[]{d}{} & \\
X \arrow[]{ru}{\iota_1} \arrow[]{rd}{f}& \mathrm{Cyl}(X) \arrow[]{d}{\eta} & \arrow[]{lu}{\iota_2}X \arrow[]{ld}{g}
& Y & Y
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

commutes
