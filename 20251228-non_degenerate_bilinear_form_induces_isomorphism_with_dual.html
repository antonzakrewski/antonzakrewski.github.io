non degenerate bilinear form induces isomorphism with dual for a finite dimensional vector space

Proposition

Let $V$ be finite dimensional vector space and $\langle -, -\rangle$ a non degenerate, Bilinearform. Then there exists a canonical isomorphism

\begin{align*} V \rightarrow& V^* \\ v \mapsto \langle v, -\rangle \end{align*}

Proof

  • Äquivalenz von Isomorphismus, Monomorphismus und Epimorphismus für Vektorräume gleicher, endlicher Dimension
  • minimal kernel and monomorphism
