descent of adjunctions onto slice categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$ be an infinity adjunction for $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$, where $\mathcal{C}$ admits infinity pullbacks Let $c \in \mathcal{C}$, $\mathcal{C}_{/c}$ the infinity slice category.

Then $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$ descends onto an adjunction

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_{c}: \mathcal{C}_{c/} \rightarrow& \mathcal{D}_{\mathcal{F}(c)/} \\ \mathcal{G}_{\mathcal{F}(c)}: \mathcal{D}_{\mathcal{F}(c)/} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C}_{c/} \end{align*}

Proof
