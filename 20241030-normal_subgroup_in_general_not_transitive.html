normal subgroup in general not transitive

Proposition

Let $G_1 \subseteq G_2 \subseteq G_3$ be groups. Assume that $G_1 \trianglelefteq G_2, G_2 \trianglelefteq G_3$ are normal subgroups Then $G_1$ is in general not a normal subgroup of $G_3$
