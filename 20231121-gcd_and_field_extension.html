gcd and field extension

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $L/K$ a field extension and $f,g \in K[X]$ polynomials. Suppose

\begin{align*} d_K =& \mathrm{gcd}_{K[X]}(f,g) \\ d_L =& \mathrm{gcd}_{K[X]}(f,g) \end{align*}

are monic

Then

\begin{align*} d_K = d_L \end{align*}

Proof

Since $d_K \mid f,g$ in $K[X]$, we conclude by universal property of a gcd, that $d_K \mid d_L$. Furthermore by größter gemeinsamer Teiler in einem Hauptidealring als erzeuger des erzeugten Ideals, it holds that

\begin{align*} (d_K) = (f,g) \subseteq K[X] \end{align*}

Thus there exist polynomials $p,q \in K[X]$ with

\begin{align*} d_K = pf + qg \end{align*}

Therefore
