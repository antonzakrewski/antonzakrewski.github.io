prime ideal with height 1 as principal ideal in a ufd

Proposition

Let $R$ be a unique factorization domain, $\mathfrak{p}$ a prime ideal with height $1$. Then $\mathfrak{p}$ is a principal ideal

Proof

Note that $\mathfrak{p} \neq 0$ as $\mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{p}) \neq 0$ Let $a \neq 0, a \in \mathfrak{p}$. Take a prime decomposition

\begin{align*} a = \prod_{i=1}^n p_i \end{align*}

by applying primeness for $(\prod_{i=1}^{n-1} p_i) \cdot p_n$ we conclude that

\begin{align*} p_i \in \mathfrak{p} \end{align*}

for some $i$

Then $(p_i)$ is a prime ideal (cf. Primelement und erzeugte Primideal) with $(p_i) \subseteq \mathfrak{p}$. Since $\mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{p}) = 1$ and $R$ is a ufd we get

\begin{align*} (0) \subsetneq (a) \subseteq (\mathfrak{p}) \end{align*}

showing $(a) = \mathfrak{p}$
