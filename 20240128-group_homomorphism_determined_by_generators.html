group homomorphism determined by generators

Proposition

Let $G,H$ be groups and $S$ generators of $G$ Then a group-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: G \rightarrow H \end{align*}

is - if it exists - defined by $\varphi(s)$ for $s \in S$

Proof

Let $g \in G$, then there exist $s_1,...,s_n$ such that

\begin{align*} \prod s_i = g \end{align*}

hence

\begin{align*} \varphi(g) = \varphi(\prod s_i) \\ =& \prod \varphi(s_i) \end{align*}
