formal derivative of a rational map

Definition

Let $K$ be a field, $K(x_i)$ the field of rational functions, $f = \frac{g}{h} \in K(x)$ a rational function for polynomials $g,h \in K[X]$ Then the formal derivative is defined as

\begin{align*} \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i} = \frac{\partial_i g \cdot h - g \cdot \partial_i h }{h^2} \end{align*}

where $\partial_i$ is the formale Ableitungsfunktion eines Polynoms
