vanishing set of a polynomial

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space. Supopse $f \in K[X_1,...,X_n]$ is a polynomial then there

\begin{align*} \mathcal{V}(f) = \mathcal{V}(f_1) \cup ... \cup \mathcal{V}(f_n) \end{align*}

for irreducible polynomials $f_i \mid f$
