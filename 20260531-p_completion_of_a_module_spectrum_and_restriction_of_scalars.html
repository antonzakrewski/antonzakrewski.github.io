p-completion of a module spectrum and restriction of scalars

Proposition

Let $\varphi: R \rightarrow S$ be a map of ring spectra and $M$ a $S$ module spectrum. Then the p-completion functor of $M$ as $S$ module and as $R$ module spectrum coincide as $R$ module spectra.

Proof

use:

  • infinite restriction of scalars creates limits
  • infinite restriction of scalars creates colimits
