Mapping space inverting some morphisms

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $W \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ be a subset of morphisms. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{W}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

is defined as full sub infinity category of the mapping space containing infinity functors $f$ with

\begin{align*} f(W) \subseteq \mathcal{D}^{\simeq} \end{align*}

where the right side is the infinity groupoid core
