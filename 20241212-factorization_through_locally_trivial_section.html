factorization modulo pointwise trivial section

Proposition

Then there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \Gamma(\eta)/I_x\Gamma(y) \rightarrow E_x \end{align*}

Proof

surjective

Let $e \in E_x$ and $U \subseteq X$ be an open subset with $\theta: \eta^{-1}[U] \cong U \times \mathbb{K}^n$ Then $\theta$ is an iso on each fibre.

Find an $s \in \Gamma(\eta^{-1}(U))$ with $s(x) = e$.

Then there exists an open neighbourhood $V$ of $x$ and a global section $t \in \Gamma(E)$ with

\begin{align*} t_{\vert V} = s_{\vert V} \end{align*}

(cf. extension of a section )

It follows that

\begin{align*} t(x) = s(x) = e \end{align*}

inj.

Let $s \in \Gamma(\eta)$ with $s(x) = 0$ Let $s_1,...,s_k: U \rightarrow E$ be sections, such that

\begin{align*} \theta(s_i) = x \mapsto (x,e_i) \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} s_{\vert U} = \sum_{i} f_i s_i \end{align*}

for some $f \in C_{\mathbb{K}}(X)$

But $0 = s(x) = \sum f_i(0) s_i(0)$

Note that by construction $s_i$ are linearly independent, hence $f_i = 0$ or $f \in I_{k}(\Gamma(\eta))$
