connected cw complex as pushout of wedges up to homotopy

Proposition

Let $(X, \emptyset, \mathrm{SK}_n(X))$ be a connected CW-Complex. Then it is up to homotopy a pushout 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\bigvee S^{n-1} \rar \dar & \mathrm{Sk}_{n-1} \dar
\bigvee D^n \rar & \mathrm{Sk}_n
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Proof

follows as

\begin{align*} \bigvee S^{n} \rightarrow \bigvee D^n \simeq * \end{align*}

is a cofibration

thus the pushout is a homotopy pushout

Hence we may replace maps by homotopic maps (???). Since $\mathrm{Sk}_{n-1}$ is connceted, we may thus replace $S^{n-1} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sk}_{n-1}$ by a pointed map or

\begin{align*} \bigvee S^{n-1} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sk}_{n-1} \end{align*}

by explicit calculation in $\mathrm{Top}$

Thus $\mathrm{Sk}_n(X) \simeq \mathrm{Sk}_n(X)'$.
