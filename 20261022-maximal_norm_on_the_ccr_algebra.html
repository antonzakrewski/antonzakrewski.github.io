maximal seminorm on the CCR algebra

Proposition

Let $A$ be the CCR Algebra. Then the maximal norm on $A$ vanishes.

Proof

Observe that $pq^n - q^n p = [p,q^n] = \pm i (n-1) q^{n-1}$. Then let $\rho: A \rightarrow \mathcal{B}(H)$ be any representation. In particular, $\rho(p), \rho(q)$ are self-adjoint and we show that one of them must vanish. Here we may use estimate

\begin{align*} \| p q^n - q^n p \| \leq& 2 \cdot \| p \| \| q \|^n \\ \| \pm \mathrm{i} (n-1) q^{n-1} \| =& (n-1) \cdot \| q \|^{n-1} \end{align*}

Here norm of the power of a self-adjoint operator shows $\| q^n \| = \| q \|^n, \| q^{n-1} \| = \| q \|^{n-1}$.

Now this implies

\begin{align*} 2 \cdot \| p \| \cdot \| q \| \geq (n-1) \end{align*}

for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

Hence $\| p \| = 0$ or $\| q \| = 0$, so w.l.o.g. assume that $p = 0$. Now $\rho(p)$ lies in the center, which implies that $i \cdot \rho(1) = 0$ or $\rho(1) = 0$ since multiplication with $i$ is an isomorphism.
