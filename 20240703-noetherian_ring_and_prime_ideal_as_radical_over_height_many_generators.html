noetherian ring and prime ideal as minimal prime ideal over height many generators

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{p}$ an ideal with height $\mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{p}) = n$. Then there exist $a_1,...,a_n \in A$ such that $\mathfrak{p}$ is minimal prime ideal over $(a_1,...,a_n)$
