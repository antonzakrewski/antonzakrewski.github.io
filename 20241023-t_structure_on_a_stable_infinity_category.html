t-structure on a stable infinity category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category. Then a \(t\)-structure on $\mathcal{C}$ is a t-structure on the homotopy category

Denote tha \(t\)-subcategories as

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}_{\geq n} \\ \mathcal{C}_{\leq n} \end{align*}

which are the full sub infinity categories spannde by objects $h \mathcal{C}_{\geq n}, h \mathcal{C}_{\leq n}$
