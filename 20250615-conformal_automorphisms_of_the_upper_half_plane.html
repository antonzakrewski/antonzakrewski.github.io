conformal automorphisms of the upper half plane

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{H}$ denote the complex upper half plane. Then the conformal automorphism group is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{H}) = \{z \mapsto \frac{az + b}{cz + d} : a,b,c,d \}n \mathbb{R}, ad - bc = 1\} \end{align*}

Proof
