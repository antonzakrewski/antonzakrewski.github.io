faithful additive functor reflects zero morphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ be an additive functor Then $\mathcal{F}$ reflects the zero morphism

Proof

follows as

  • additive functor and zero morphism

hence $\mathcal{F}(0) = 0$. By faithfulness it follows for

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(f) = 0 = \mathcal{F}(0) \Rightarrow f = 0 \end{align*}
