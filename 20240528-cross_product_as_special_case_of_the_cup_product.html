cross product as special case of the cup product

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be topological spaces, $R$ a commutative ring and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{pr}_X: X \times Y \rightarrow& X \\ \mathrm{pr}_Y: X \times Y \rightarrow& Y \end{align*}

the projection from the product topology

Then for $\alpha \in H^p(X;R), \beta \in H^q(Y;R)$ the cross product is given by

\begin{align*} (\mathrm{pr}_X^* \alpha) \cup (\mathrm{pr}_Y^* \beta) =& \alpha \times \beta \end{align*}

for $\mathrm{pr}_X^*(\alpha) \in H^*(X \times Y;R)$

Proof

Note that by definition and cross product of singular cohomology as natural operation

\begin{align*} (\mathrm{pr}_X^* \alpha) \cup (\mathrm{pr}_Y^* \beta) =& \Delta^*(\mathrm{pr}_x^* \alpha \times_{\mathrm{cross}} \mathrm{pr}_Y^* \beta) \\ =& \Delta^*(\mathrm{pr}_x \times_{\mathrm{prod}} \mathrm{pr}_Y)^*( \alpha \times_{\mathrm{cross}} \beta) \\ =& (\Delta^*( \mathrm{pr}_x) \times_{\mathrm{prod}} \Delta^*(\mathrm{pr}_Y))( \alpha \times_{\mathrm{cross}} \beta) \\ =& ( \mathrm{pr}_X \Delta)^* \times_{\mathrm{prod}} (\mathrm{pr}_Y \Delta)^*( \alpha \times_{\mathrm{cross}} \beta) \\ =& ((\mathrm{id})^* \times_{\mathrm{prod}} (\mathrm{id})^*)(\alpha \times_{\mathrm{cross}} \beta) =& \alpha \times_{\mathrm{cross}} \beta \end{align*}
