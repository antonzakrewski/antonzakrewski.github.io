dimension of the tangent space of an affine variety and jacobi matrix

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be an affine variety. Then for $P \in X$, the dimension is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}_{k}(T_P(x)) = m - \mathrm{Rang}(J(P)) \end{align*}

where $J(P)$ is the jacobi Matrix of an affine variety evaluated at $P$

Proof

Note that by definition

\begin{align*} T_p(X) = \mathrm{ker}(J(P): k^m \rightarrow k^r) \end{align*}

hence this follows from Rangsatz
