commutator subgroup is a normal subgroup

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group and $G^{(0)}$ the commutator subgroup. Then $G^{(0)}$ is a normal subgroup.

Proof

subgroup

by definition

normal subgroup

Let $k \in G$ and $ghg^{-1}h^{-1} = [gh] \in G^{(1)}$. Then we get

\begin{align*} k \cdot ghg^{-1}h^{-1} \cdot k^{-1} =& kgk^{-1} \cdot k h k^{-1} \cdot kg^{-1}k^{-1} \cdot k h^{-1} k^{-1} \\ =& (kgk^{-1}) \cdot (khk^{-1}) \cdot (kgk^{-1})^{-1} \cdot (kgk^{-1})^{-1} \\ =& [kgk^{-1}, khk^{-1}] \end{align*}
