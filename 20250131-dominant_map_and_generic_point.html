dominant map and generic point

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be topological spaces and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a continuous map. Suppose $\eta \in X$ is a generic point.

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a dominant map
  2. $f(\eta)$ is a generic point

Proof

corollary of the sandwhichen vom Abschluss unter stetigen Funktionen

\begin{align*} \overline{f[\overline{A}]} = \overline{f[A]} \end{align*}

for $A = \{\eta\}$
