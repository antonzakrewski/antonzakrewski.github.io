c star algebra and closed subalgebra

Proposition

Let $A$ be a nonunital star algebra. TFAE:

  1. $A$ is a C star algebra
  2. $A$ is isomorphic as a star algebra to a closed subalgebra of $B(H)$ for some $H$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

for every $a \in A$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, there exists a representation $\rho_{a,n}(a): A \rightarrow B(H_{a,n})$ such that

\begin{align*} \| \rho_{a,n}(a) \| \geq \| a \| - \frac{1}{n} \end{align*}

Then let $H = \bigoplus H_{a,n}$ and define the diagonal rerpesentation $\rho: A \rightarrow B(H)$ given by $\rho(-) = (\rho_{a,n}(-))$ the diagnoal representation. Then the norm of this diagonal representation is given by $\| \rho(b) \| = \mathrm{sup}_{a,n} \rho_{a,n}(b) = \| b \|_{\mathrm{max}}$ where $\leq$ follows from the definition of the maximum norm.

2) $\implies$ 1)

then $A$ is a banach algebra with $C^*$ equality and by maximal norm estimate for a banach star algebra with C-star identity we have $\| - \|_{\mathrm{max}} = \| - \|_{B(H)}$.
