limit in the category of commutative monoids

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category admitting $I$ shaped limits, $\mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{C})$ the category of cartesian commutative monoids. Then $\mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{C})$ also admits $I$ shaped limits, which are computed pointwise.

Proof

a)

follows from:

  • infinity limits commute with infinity limits
  • pointwise calculation of limits

b)

  • functor category with generalized segal condition for a small collection as presentable category
