state lemma for c star algebras

Proposition

Let $A \neq 0$ and assume that $\varphi: A \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ is $\mathbb{C}$- linear and $\| \varphi \| = \varphi(1) = 1$. Then $\varphi$ is a state.

Moreover, for $b \in A^+$ there is a state $\varphi$ with $\varphi(b) = \| b \|$.

Proof

a)

we need to show that $\varphi(a^*) = \overline{\varphi(a)}$. Since $\varphi$ is linear, using a cartesian decomposition, it suffices to show that $\varphi(h) \in \mathbb{R}$ for self-adjoint elements $h$. Here we write

\begin{align*} \| 1 + \mathrm{i} t \varphi(h) \|^2 =& \| \varphi(1 + \mathrm{i} th) \|^2 \\ \leq& \| 1 + \mathrm{i} th\|^2 \\ =& \| 1 + t^2 h^2 \| \\ \leq& 1 + t^2 \| h|^2 \end{align*}

where we use $\varphi(1) = 1$, $\| \varphi \| = 1$ andthe the c star identity

But we also have

\begin{align*} \vert 1 + \mathrm{i} t \varphi(h) \vert = 1 - 2ty + t^2 \vert \varphi(h) \vert ^2 \end{align*}

for $\varphi(h) = x + \mathrm{i}y$. this shows $-2 ty \leq t^2 ( \| h \|^2 - \vert \varphi(h) \vert^2)$ for all $t$. As we can let $\vert t \vert < \varepsilon$, (for appropriate sign), this shows that $y = 0$.

b)

Lte $b > 0$, then

\begin{align*} \| 1 - \frac{b}{\| b \|} \| \leq& 1 \\ \vert 1 - \frac{\varphi(b)}{\| b \|} \vert \leq& 1 \end{align*}

where the second inequality follows from $\| \varphi \| \leq 1$

This shows $\varphi(b) \geq 0$.

Let $b$ be positive, then consider $C^*(1,b) \subseteq A$ where we can identify $C^*(1,b) \simeq C(\sigma(b))$ by Gelfand duality for CH. where $\| b \| \in \sigma(b)$.

where $\varphi$ comes from hahn-bach banach, satisfying $\| \varphi \| = 1$, $\varphi(1) = 1$, $\varphi(b) = \| b \|$.
