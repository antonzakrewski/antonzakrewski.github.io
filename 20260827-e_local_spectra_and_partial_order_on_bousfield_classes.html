E-local spectra and partial order on bousfield classes

Proposition

Let $E,F$ be spectra TFAE:

  1. $\langle E\rangle \leq \langle F\rangle$ or $\langle E\rangle \supseteq \langle F\rangle$.
  2. $\mathrm{Sp}_{E} \subseteq \mathrm{Sp}_{F}$ is a full subcategory

Proof

follows from the equivalent characterization of E-local spectrum, where a spectrum $X$ is \(E\)-local if and only if $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(A,X)$ is trivial for all E-acyclic spectra.
