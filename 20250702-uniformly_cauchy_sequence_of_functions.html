uniformly cauchy sequence of functions

Definition

Lte $f_n: M \rightarrow N$ be a sequence of continuous maps between metric spaces Then $f_n$ is said to be uniformly cauchy, if for any $\varepsilon > 0$ there exists an $N$ such that for any $n,n' \geq N$

\begin{align*} \vert f_n(x) - f_{n'}(x) \vert \leq \varepsilon \end{align*}

for any $x \in M$
