open subfunctor of a closed subfunctor and locally closed

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor and $U \subseteq Z \subseteq X$ be subfunctors. Suppose that $U \subseteq Z$ is an open subfunctor and $Z \subseteq X$ is a closed subfunctor. Then $U \subseteq X$ is a locally closed subfunctor.
