short exact sequence of k0 and picard

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, then there exists a short exact sequence in Category Ab

where

\begin{align*} \mathrm{SK}_0(R) \coloneqq \mathrm{ker}(\mathrm{det}_{\tilde{K}(R)}) \end{align*}

where $\tilde{K}(R)$ are the modules of constant rank $0$

see:

  • H0 of a commutative ring

Proof

consider the diagram

by the Four-Lemma of modules of epimorphisms it suffices to show that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{det}_{\vert \tilde{K}_{0}(R)}: \tilde{K}_0(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{Pic}(R) \end{align*}

is surjecticve

Let $[L] \in \mathrm{Pic}(R)$. Then consider

\begin{align*} [L] - [R] \in K_0(R) \end{align*}

Then it follows that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{rk}([L] - [R]) =& \mathrm{rk}([L]) - \mathrm{rk}([R]) \\ =& 1 - 1 \\ =& 0 \end{align*}

hence $[L] - [R] \in \tilde{K}_0(R)$

Furthermore

\begin{align*} \mathrm{det}(R) = \binom{1}{1} R \cong R \end{align*}

which shows that $R \in \mathrm{ker}(\mathrm{det})$

Thus

\begin{align*} \mathrm{det}([L] - [R]) =& \mathrm{det}([L]) - \mathrm{det}([R]) \\ =& \mathrm{det}([L]) - 0 \\ =& \mathrm{det}([L]) \end{align*}
