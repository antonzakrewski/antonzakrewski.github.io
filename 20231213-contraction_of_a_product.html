contraction and product

Proposition

Let $A,B$ be a commutative ring, $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ be a ring-homomorphism and $\mathfrak{b}_1,\mathfrak{b}_2 \subseteq B$ be ideals

Then it holds for the contraction and the product

\begin{align*} (\mathfrak{b}_1)^c \cdot (\mathfrak{b}_2)^c \subseteq (\mathfrak{b}_1 \cdot \mathfrak{b}_2)^c \end{align*}

Proof

Let $a \in (\mathfrak{b}_1)^c \cdot (\mathfrak{b}_2)^c$, then there exists $b_i \in \mathfrak{b}_i$ with

\begin{align*} \varphi(a) = \varphi(b_1) \cdot \varphi(b_2) \end{align*}

Therefore

\begin{align*} \varphi(a) = \varphi(b_1 \cdot b_2) \end{align*}

where $b_1 \cdot b_2 \in \mathfrak{b}_1 \cdot \mathfrak{b}_2$ showing

\begin{align*} a \in (\mathfrak{b}_1 \cdot \mathfrak{b}_2)^{c} \end{align*}
