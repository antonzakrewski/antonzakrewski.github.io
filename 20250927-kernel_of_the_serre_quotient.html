kernel of the serre quotient

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A} \subseteq \mathcal{B}$ be a serre subcategory, $\mathcal{B}/\mathcal{A}$ the serre quotient Then the kernel of $\mathcal{B} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}/\mathcal{A}$ in $\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{exact}}$ is given by the fully faithful, idempotent complete inclusion of $\mathcal{A}$

Proof

reduction from $\infty$ to $1$

limits in $\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{exact}}$ should be computed underlying in $\mathrm{Cat}$

  • $\mathrm{Cat}_{1} \subseteq \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}$ is closed under limits (which exist)

$\subseteq$

$\supseteq$

idempotent complete

  • split 1-idempotent and limit / colimit
  • inclusion is exact
