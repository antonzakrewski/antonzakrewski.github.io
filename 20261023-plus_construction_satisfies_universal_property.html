plus construction satisfies universal property

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category equipped with a grothendieck topology $\tau$. Let $\mathcal{F}$ be a presheaf. Then the plus construction satisfies the universal property of a sheafification (except that it may fail to be a sheaf).

Proof

Let $\mathcal{G}$ be a sheaf. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(\mathcal{F}^+, \mathcal{G}) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}) \end{align*}
