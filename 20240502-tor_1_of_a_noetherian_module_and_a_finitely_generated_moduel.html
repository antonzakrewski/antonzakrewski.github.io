tor 1 of a noetherian module and a noetherian module

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $N,M$ Noetherian modules Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tor}_1^A(N,M) \end{align*}

is also noetherian

Proof

Since $N$ is noetherian we may choose a finite presentation 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
P_2 \arrow[]{r}{} & A^{n} \arrow[twoheadrightarro]{r}{} & N \arrow[]{r}{} & 0
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Then applying $- \otimes_A M$ results in 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
P_2 \otimes_A M \arrow[]{r}{} & A^{n} \otimes_A M \arrow[]{d}{\cong} \arrow[twoheadrightarro]{r}{} & N \arrow[]{r}{} &  0 \\
& \bigotimes_{i=1}^n M & N & 0 \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

where $\bigotimes_{i=1}^n M$ is noetherian (cf. tensor product of finitely generated modules)

Therefore by homology of a noetherian module as noetherian module it follows that

\begin{align*} H(P_2 \otimes_A M \rightarrow A^n \otimes_A M \rightarrow 0) \cong \mathrm{Tor}_{1}^A(N,M) \end{align*}

is noetherian
