orientation inclusion map

Definition

Let \(M\) be an \(n\)-dimensional manifold, $A,B \subseteq M$ subspaces and $R$ a ring. Then the orientation inclusion map is defined as induced map

\begin{align*} \rho_{A,B}: H_n(M, M \setminus A;R) \rightarrow H_n(M, M \setminus A \cup M \setminus B);R) \end{align*}

for the inclusion of pairs of spaces

\begin{align*} (M,M \setminus A) \hookrightarrow (M, M \setminus A \cup M \setminus B) \end{align*}
