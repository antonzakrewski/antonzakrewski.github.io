colimit of open subsets weakly equivalent to homotopy colimit

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $U_i \subseteq X$ an open cover. Let

\begin{align*} I: \mathcal{P}(I)^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Top} \\ \{i_j\} \mapsto& \bicap_{i_j} U_{i_j} A \subseteq B \mapsto& \bigcap_{B} \subseteq \bigcap_{A} \\ \end{align*}

be the diagram which sends a subset to its intersection.

Then we get a weak homotopy equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{hocolim}_{I} \simeq \mathrm{colim}_{I} \cong X \end{align*}

where a topological space is of course the colimit of an open covering
