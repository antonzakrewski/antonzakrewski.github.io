exact functor preserves long exact sequences

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ a functor TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is exact
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ preserves long exact sequences

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Follows as exactness at $A_n$

is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ker}(\mathrm{coker}(d_{n+1}) \cong& \mathrm{im}(d_{n+1}) \\ \cong& \mathrm{ker}(d_{n}) \end{align*}

and as $\mathcal{F}$ preserves finite limits and colimits we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{im}(\mathcal{F}(d_{n+1}) \cong& \mathrm{ker}(\mathrm{coker})(\mathcal{F}(d_{n+1}) \\ \cong& \mathrm{ker}(\mathcal{F}(d_n)) \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from:

  • exact functor and exact sequence and the "long exact sequence"
