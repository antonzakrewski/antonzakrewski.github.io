kunneth formula for pairs of spaces

Proposition

Let $(X,A), (Y,B)$ be pairs of spaces such that

\begin{align*} (X \times Y, X \times B, Y \times A) \end{align*}

is an excisive triad, $R$ a hereditary ring and $M$ an \(R\)-module

Then there exists a short exact sequence

which also splits (albeit not naturally)

Proof

follows from

  • natural diagram of singular chains for two pairs of spaces

where we get chain homotopy equivalences

\begin{align*} C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X,A) \otimes C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y,B) \rightarrow ... \rightarrow C^{\mathrm{sing}}((X,A) \times_{p} (Y,B)) \end{align*}
  • natural diagram of singular chains of for two pairs of spaces with chain homotopy equivalences
  • natural diagram of singular chains for two pairs of spaces and excisive triad

and then applying the algebraic kunneth formula
