free module as faithfully flat module

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $R^{\kappa}$ a free \(R\)-module. Then $M$ is faithfully flat

Proof

Let $N$ be a module. Then as the tensor functor ais right exact functor it follows that

\begin{align*} R^{\kappa} \otimes_{R} N \cong& (R \otimes_R N)^{\kappa} \\ \cong& N^{\kappa} \end{align*}
