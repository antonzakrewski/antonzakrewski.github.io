ideal property of degree of maps of manifolds

Proposition

Let $M_1,M_2,M_3$ be closed orientable manifolds/ poincare duality complexexs and $D(-,-) \subseteq \mathbb{Z}$ the degree of maps of closed orientable manifolds functor. Then $D(M_1,M_2) \cdot D(M_2,M_3) \subseteq D(M_1,M_3)$.

Proof

Let $x_i \in M_i$ be the generatorrs and $M_1 \xrightarrow{f_1} M_2 \xrightarrow{f_2} M_3$ be maps. Suppose $d_i = \mathrm{deg}(f_i)$, then

\begin{align*} f_2(f_1(x_1)) =& f_2(d_1 x_2)) \\ =& d_2 d_1 x_3 \end{align*}

shows that $\mathrm{deg}(f_2 \circ f_1) = d_2 \cdot d_1$.
