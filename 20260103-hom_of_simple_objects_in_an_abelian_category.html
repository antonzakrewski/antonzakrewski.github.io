hom of simple objects in an abelian category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $X,Y$ be simple objects. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(X,Y) = \{0\} \cup \{f: X \rightarrow Y \vert \textrm{ Iso}\} \end{align*}
