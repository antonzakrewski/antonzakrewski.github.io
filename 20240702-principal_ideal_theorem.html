Let $A$ be a noetherian ring and $\mathfrak{a} = (a) \subsetneq A$ a strict principal ideal. Suppose $\mathfrak{p} \supseteq \mathfrak{a}$ is a minimal prime ideal, then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{p}) \leq 1 \end{align*}
