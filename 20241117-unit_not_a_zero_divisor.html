unit not a zero divisor

Proposition

Let \(A\) be a ring different from the zero ring and $\alpha \in A$ a unit Then $\alpha$ is not a zero divisor

Proof

Assume $\alpha \cdot \beta = \beta \cdot \alpha = 0$ for some $\beta \in A$. Then it remains to show that $\beta = 0$

By assumption there exists an inverse $\alpha^{-1} \in A$ and hence

\begin{align*} \alpha \cdot \beta =& 0 && \vert \alpha^{-1} \cdot 0 \\ \alpha^{-1} \cdot \alpha \cdot \beta =& \alpha^{-1} \cdot 0 \\ 1 \cdot \beta =& 0 \\ \beta =& 0 \end{align*}
