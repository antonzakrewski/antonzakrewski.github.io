quasi-abelian category of pairs

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories where $\mathcal{A} \subseteq \mathcal{B}$ is a full subcategory closed under subobjects and quotient

Then the quasi-abelian category of pairs $\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}}$ is defined as full subcategory of

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{B}) \end{align*}

where for $Y \rightarrow X$

  1. the map is a monomorphism
  2. $Y \in \mathcal{A}$
  3. $X/Y \in \mathcal{A}$
