fiber of a principal g-bundle as g-torsor

Proposition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a principal g-bundle for a topological group $G$. Then for $b \in B$, the fibre $p^{-1}[b]$ is a g-torsor (even noncanonically a topological group)

Proof

using restriction of a homeomorphism as homeomorphism for the

\begin{align*} U_i \times G \cong p^{-1}[U_i] \end{align*}

shows that

\begin{align*} \{e\} \times G \cong p^{-1}[ \{e\}] \end{align*}

since the map $U_i \times G \rightarrow p^{-1}[U_i]$ is over $B$
