functor category of additive functors as ab3 opp category for an ab3 opp category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a an Ab3 opp category, $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{A})$ the functor category for a small category $\mathcal{C}$. Then $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{A})$ is also Ab3 opp

Proof

  • functor category for a complete codomain as complete category
  • abelian valued functor category as abelian category
