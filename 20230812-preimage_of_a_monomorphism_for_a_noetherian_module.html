preimage of a monomorphism for a noetherian module

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a commutative ring and $M,N$ modules over $R$. Suppose $\varphi: M \rightarrowtail N$ is monomorphism and $N$ noetherian. Then $M$ is also noetherian

Proof

Let $S \subseteq M$ be a module, then $\varphi[S] \cong S$ and since $N$ is noetherian, $\varphi[S]$ is finitely generated. Therefore, since $\varphi_{\vert S}: S \rightarrow \varphi[S]$ is an isomorphism, $S$ is also finitely generated
