subtleties of definitions of manifolds

List

Apart from $M$ being locally euclidean & hausdorff one could either assume

  1. paracompact space
  2. secondcountable
  3. compact exhaustion

Then a) 2) $\iff$ 3) b) 2) $\implies$ 1) and 2) $\implies$ 1) c) 1) + countably many connected components $\implies$ 2),3)

Proof

a)

  • second countable, locally compact space admits compact exhaustion
  • manifold with compact exhaustion implies second countability

b)

  • manifold with compact exhaustion implies paracompactness

c)

  • paracompact manifold with countably many components implies second countability
