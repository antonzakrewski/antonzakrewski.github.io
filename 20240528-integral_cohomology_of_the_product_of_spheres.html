integral cohomology of the product of spheres

Proposition

then the singular cohomology is given by

\begin{align*} H^n(S^p \times S^q) \cong \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } n = 0 \\ \mathbb{Z} \oplus \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } p = n = q \\ \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } p = n \neq q, q = n \neq p \\ \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } n = p + q \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof
