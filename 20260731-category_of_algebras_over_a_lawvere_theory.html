category of algebras over a lawvere theory

Definition

Let $\mathcal{T}$ be a lawvere theory and $\mathcal{C}$ a category with finite products Then the category of algebras over $\mathcal{T}$ is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{T}}(\mathcal{C}) = \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

category of finite product preserving functors from $\mathcal{T}$ to $\mathcal{C}$.
