vector bundle is A1 homotopy equivalent

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{E}$ be a <finite locally free sheaf> on $X \in \mathrm{Sm}_{S}$. Then $p: \mathbb{V}(E) \rightarrow X$ is an A1 homotopy equivalence.

Proof

Consider the zero section $0: X \rightarrow \mathbb{V}(E)$. Then $p \circ 0 = \mathrm{id}$, i.e. it remains to show that $0 \circ p = \mathrm{id}$.

Here

\begin{align*} h: \mathbb{V}(E) \times \mathbb{A}^1 \rightarrow \mathbb{V}(E) (v,t) \mapsto t \cdot v \end{align*}

is a homotopy.
