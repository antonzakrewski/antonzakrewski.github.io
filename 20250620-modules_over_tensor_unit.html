modules over tensor unit

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \otimes, \mathbbm{1}$ be a symmetric monoidal category and $\mathbbm{1} \in \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{C})$ the tensor unit, which is canonically a commutative algebra. Then the <evaluation at underlying module>

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbbm{1}}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}
