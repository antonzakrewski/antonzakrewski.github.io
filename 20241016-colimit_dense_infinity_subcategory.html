colimit-dense infinity subcategory

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathcal{C}_0$ a sub infinity category. Then $\mathcal{C}_0$ is said to be colimit-dense, if each object $x \in \mathcal{C}$ is a infinity colimit of a infinity functor

\begin{align*} p: K \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_0 \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}
