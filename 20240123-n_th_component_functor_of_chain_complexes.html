n-th component functor of chain complexes

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be pointed category and $\mathrm{Ch}(\mathcal{A})$ the category of chain complexes. Then for $n \in \mathbb{N}$, the \(n\)-th component functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{n-comp}: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow& \mathcal{A} \\ (H_n) \mapsto& H_n (f: (H_n) \rightarrow (G_n)) \mapsto& f_n: H_n \rightarrow G_n \end{align*}
