short exact sequence in the heart is a bifiber sequence in a stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A} = \mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}$ be the heart of a stable category $\mathcal{C}$ equipped with a (nice ?) t-structure and $X \rightarrow Y \rightarrow Z$ a sequence. TFAE:

  1. the sequence above is a short exact sequence
  2. the sequence above is a fiber sequence in $\mathcal{C}$

Proof

Observe that $\mathrm{fib}(Y \rightarrow Z) \rightarrow Y \rightarrow Z$ is a fiber sequence, hence induces a long exact sequence in the heart for a t-structure. Now the long exact sequence shows, that the fiber lies in the heart (? should be true?) and is isomorphic to the kernel

Therefore, 1) and 2) are equivalent.
