left and right invariant basis property are equivalent

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring. TFAE:

  1. $R$ satisfies the left invariant basis property
  2. $R$ satisfies the right invariant basis property

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Choose $n,m$ and an isomorphism $R^n \rightarrow R^m, R^m \rightarrow R^n$. These are given by matrices $A = (a_{i,j}) \in R^{n \times m}, B = (b_{i,j}) \in R^{m \times n}$ satisfying

\begin{align*} (A \cdot_{\mathrm{left}} B)_{i,k} =& (\sum_{j} b_{j,k} a_{i,j}) \\ =& \partial_{i,k} \\ (B \cdot_{\mathrm{left}} A)_{i,k} =& (\sum_{j} a_{j,k} b_{i,j}) \\ =& \partial_{i,k} \\ \end{align*}

Set

\begin{align*} A' \coloneqq& (a_{i,j}') \\ B' \coloneqq& (b_{i,j}') \end{align*}

for

\begin{align*} a_{i,j}' =& b_{j,i} \\ b_{i,j}' =& a_{j,i} \end{align*}

Then we get

\begin{align*} (A' \cdot_{\mathrm{right}} B')_{i,k} =& (\sum_{j} a_{i,j}' b_{j,k}') \\ =& (\sum_{j} b_{j,i} a_{k,j}) \\ =& \partial_{k,i} \\ =& \partial_{i,k} \end{align*}

analogously for $B' \cdot_{\mathrm{right}} A'$
