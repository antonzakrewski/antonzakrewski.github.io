cartesian fibration between infinity categories as isofibration

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{E}$ be infinity categories, $p: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a cartesian fibration Then $p$ is an isofibration
