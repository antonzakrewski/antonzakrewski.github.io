reduction of lifting problems for a fully faithful right adjoint

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be functors where $\mathcal{L} \dashv \mathcal{R}$ are adjoints Suppose furthermore $\mathcal{R}$ is fully faithful

Then a lifting problem

reduces to a lifting problem

Proof

using fully faithful right adjoint and counit as isomorphism
