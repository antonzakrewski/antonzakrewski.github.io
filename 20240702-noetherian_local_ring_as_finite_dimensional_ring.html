noetherian local ring as finite dimensional ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a local noetherian ring. Then the dimension is finite $\mathrm{dim}(A) < \infty$

Proof

follows from the height theorem, as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{p}) \leq \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{m}) \end{align*}

by maximality of $\mathfrak{m}$ and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{m}) \leq r \end{align*}

for $\mathfrak{m} = (a_1,...,a_r)$, as $\mathfrak{m}$ is a finitely generated ideal since $A$ is noetherian (cf. noetherian ring and finite generation of prime ideals)
