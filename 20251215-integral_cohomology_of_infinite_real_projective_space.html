integral cohomology ring of infinite real projective space

Proposition

The integral cohomology of $\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}$ is given by

\begin{align*} H^*(\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}, \mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{F}_2[\alpha] \end{align*}

for $\vert \alpha \vert = 2$

Proof

a) additively

follows from:

  • singular homology of a real projective space
  • universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology

b) ring structure

cohomology is natural with respect to maps of rings. But $\mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}/2$ is a ring-homomorphism and we know the \(\mathbb{F}_2\)-cohomology of $\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}$ (cf. cohomology ring of infinite real projective space with coefficients in F_2)

So using the universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology - in particular naturality with respect to to modules.

Now $H_{n}(\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}, \mathbb{Z}) = 0, H_{n-1}(\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty},\mathbb{Z}) = \mathbb{F}_2, H_{n-1}(\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}, \mathbb{F}_2) = \mathbb{F}_2$ for $n$ even, so this short exact sequence becomes

So in particular the mod 2 reduction induces an isomorphism

\begin{align*} H^{2n}(\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}, \mathbb{Z}) \rightarrow H^{2n}(\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}, \mathbb{F}_2) \end{align*}

Hence in particular the ring structure of $H^*(\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}, \mathbb{Z})$ can be detected by $H^{2n}(\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}, \mathbb{F}_2) \cong \mathbb{F}_2[x]$
