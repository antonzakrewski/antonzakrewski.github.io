orientation reversing diffeomorphism and torsion in omega so

Proposition

Let $M$ be a closed smooth oriented manifold which admits a orientation reversing diffeomorphism $f: M \rightarrow M$. Then $M$ is a 2-torsion element in $\Omega_{\mathrm{dim}(M)}^{\mathrm{so}}$

Proof

we want to show that $[M] + [M] = [\emptyset]$ or in other words give a bordism $M \coprod M \sim \emptyset$. But then $M \times [0,1]$ has boundary $\partial (M \times [0,1] ) = M \coprod -M$ (where $-M$ is equipped with the opposite orientation) and applying $f$ gives $\partial M \times M = M \coprod -M \simeq (M \coprod f[-M]) \coprod \emptyset \simeq (M \coprod M) \coprod - \emptyset$
