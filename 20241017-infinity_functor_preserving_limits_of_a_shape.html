infinity functor preserving limits of a shape

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $K$ a simplicial set and $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an infinity functor. Then $f$ is said to preserve limits of the shape $K$, if any limit of a diagram $K \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is preserved
