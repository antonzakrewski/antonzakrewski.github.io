chromatic index of a complete graph

Proposition

The chromatic index of a complete graph $K_n$ is given by

\begin{align*} \chi'(K_n) = \begin{cases} n -1 & \mbox{if } n \mathrm{ even} \\ n & \mbox{if } n \mathrm{ odd} \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

consider vizing theorem. Now we will show that

  1. if $\chi'(K_n) = n-1$ then $n$ is even
  2. if $\chi'(K_n) = n$, then $n$ is odd

1)

Suppose it is given by $n-1$, then fix a color. Then each vertex contains precisely one edge of the chosen color. In particular those edges form a perfect matching, hence the amount of edges must be even.
