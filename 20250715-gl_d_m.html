GL(d,m)

Definition

Define $\mathrm{GL}(d,m)$ as subgroup / subspace of general linear group

\begin{align*} \mathrm{GL}(d,m) =& \{A: \mathbb{R}^d \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^d \vert A[\mathbb{R}^m] \subseteq A[\mathbb{R}^m]\} \subseteq& \mathrm{GL}(d) \end{align*}

note

one can also define equivalently

\begin{align*} \mathrm{GL}(d,m) = \{A: \mathbb{R}^d \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^d \vert A[\mathbb{R}^m] = A[\mathbb{R}^m]\} \end{align*}
