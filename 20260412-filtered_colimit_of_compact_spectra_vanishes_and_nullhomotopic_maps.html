filtered colimit of compact spectra vanishes and nullhomotopic maps

Proposition

Let $(X_i)_{I}$ be a filtered diagram consisting of compact spectra such that $\mathrm{colim}_{I} X_i$ is equivalent to $0$. Then for each $i \in I$ there exists some $j \in I$ such that $X_i \rightarrow X_j$ vanishes

Proof

Let $X_i$, then $\pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}(X_i,-)): \mathrm{Sp} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ preserves filtered colimits. Moreover $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X_i, \mathrm{colim}_I X_i) \simeq 0$ since $\mathrm{colim}_I X_i$ is the zero object.

Now by compact object and factorization through component there exists some $j$ such that $X_i \rightarrow \mathrm{colim}_I X_i$ factors through $X_j$. Moreover by the explicit description of the hom-set in the homotopy category $\pi_0(\mathrm{map}(X_i, \mathrm{colim}_I X_j) \simeq \mathrm{colim} \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X_i, X_j))$ so in particular the map $X_i \rightarrow X_j$ must become eventually nullhomotopic.
