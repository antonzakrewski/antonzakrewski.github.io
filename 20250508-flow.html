flow

Definition

Let $N = (G,s,t,c)$ be a network. Then a flow is given by a map

\begin{align*} f: E \rightarrow \mathbb{R}_0^+ \end{align*}

with

  1. $f(e) \leq c(e)$ for $e \in E$
  2. $f(\{x\},E) = f(E, \{x\})$ for $x \in V \setminus \{s,t\}$, i.e.
\begin{align*} \sum_{u \in N^b(v)} f(vu) = \sum_{u \in N^-(v)}(uv) \end{align*}
