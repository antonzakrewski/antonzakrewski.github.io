infinity groupoid core and E_n monoidal categories with coproduct preserving tensor product

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an E_n monoidal category such that $\otimes: \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ preserves coproducts in each variable. Then using the canonical semiring structure (cf. E_n monoidal category with coproduct preserving tensor product as E_n Semiring), the infinity groupoid core carries a canonical E_n semiring structure

Proof

using the

  • infinity right adjoint functor theorem for presentable categories
  • category of Anima as presentable category , infinity category of infinity categories as presentable category

we conclude that

\begin{align*} (-)^{\simeq}: \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty} \rightarrow \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

preserves limits and filtered infinity colimits

Hence

\begin{align*} (-)^{\simeq}: \mathrm{Mon}_{E_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Mon}_{E_{\infty}}(\mathrm{An}) \end{align*}

is <lax symmetric monoidal>

Thus we get a functor

\begin{align*} (-)^{\simeq}: \mathrm{Rig}_{E_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Rig}_{E_{\infty}}(\mathrm{An}) \end{align*}
