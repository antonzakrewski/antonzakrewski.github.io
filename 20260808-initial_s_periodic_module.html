s-periodic localization

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $L$ a line and $s \in L$. Let $\mathrm{Mod}_{R_s}$ be the category of s-periodic modules. Then the forgetful functor $\mathrm{Mod}_{R_s} \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}$ admits a left adjoint, which is objectwise given by

\begin{align*} M \xrightarrow{s} M \otimes_{R} L \xrightarrow{s} M \otimes_{R} L^{\otimes 2} \end{align*}
