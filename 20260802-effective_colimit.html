effective colimit

Definition

Let $T$ be a category with pullbacks and $\(I\)-indexed colimits. Then \(I\)-indexed colimits in $T$ are effective if, for every cartesian transformation $Y_* $\rightarrow X_*$ of functors $X_*, Y_*: I \rightarrow T$, the extended natural transformation $\overline{Y}* \rightarrow \overline{X}*$ is also a cartesian transformation.
