dimension zero noetherian local ring as field

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative noetherian commutative ring.

TFAE:

  1. $A$ has dimension $0$ and is a regular local ring
  2. $A$ is a field

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

regular local ring as integral domain, $\mathfrak{p}$ as prime ideal hence maximal ideal by reasons of dimension.

2) $\implies$ 1)

dimension of a field
