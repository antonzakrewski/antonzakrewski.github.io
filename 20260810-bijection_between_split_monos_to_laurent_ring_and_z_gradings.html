bijection between sections to laurent ring and Z-gradings

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, then there exists a bijection between

  1. sections $A \rightarrow A[u^{\pm 1}]$ for the epi classified by $u \mapsto 1$
  2. Z-gradings

where a $\mathbb{Z}$ grading induces the map on homogeneous elements

\begin{align*} A_d \rightarrow& A[u^{\pm 1}] \\ a \mapsto& a u^{\vert a \vert} \end{align*}

and a section gives a grading $f^{-1}[A u^d]$

Proof

observe that $A[u^{pm 1}]$ is a $\mathbb{Z}$ graded ring and hence any equations etc. can be checked in $A[u^{\pm 1}]$ furthermore, both constructions are bijective
