retract of an adjoint for an idempotent complete category

Proposition

Suppose there exists a functor $g: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ which is a retract of some $g': \mathcal{C}' \rightarrow \mathcal{D}'$. Suppose $g'$ has a left adjoint and $\mathcal{C}$ is idempotent complete, then $g$ has a left adjoint

In our case, $\mathcal{C}$ is idempotent complete.
