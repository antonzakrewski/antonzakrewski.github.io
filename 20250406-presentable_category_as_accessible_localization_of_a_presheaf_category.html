presentable category as accessible localization of a presheaf category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a \(1\) category.

TFAE:

  1. there exists a small infinity category $\mathcal{A}$ such that $\mathcal{C} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{A})$ is an accessible localization
  2. $\mathcal{C}$ is presentable

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
