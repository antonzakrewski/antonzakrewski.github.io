extension and sum of ideals

Proposition

Let $A,B$ be a commutative ring, $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ be a ring-homomorphism and $\mathfrak{a}_1,\mathfrak{a}_2 \subseteq A$ be ideals

Then it holds, that

\begin{align*} ( \mathfrak{a}_1 + \mathfrak{a}_2)^e = \mathfrak{a}_1^e + \mathfrak{a}_2^e \end{align*}
