smoothness criterion via the cotangent complex

Proposition

Let $f: Y \rightarrow X$ be a morphism of schemes

  1. $f$ is a smooth morphism
  2. $f$ is a morphisms of locally finite presentation, $\Omega_f$ is a vector bundle and $\mathcal{N}_f = 0$
