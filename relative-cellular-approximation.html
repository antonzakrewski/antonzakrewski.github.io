CW approximation of a pair

Proposition

Let $(X,A)$ be a pair of spaces. Then there exists a CW Pair $(X',A')$ and a based map

\begin{align*} \varphi: (X',A') \rightarrow (X,A) \end{align*}

such that

\begin{align*} \varphi: X' \rightarrow& X \\ \varphi_{\vert A}: A' \rightarrow& A \end{align*}

are weakly homotopy equivalent spaces

Proof
