Definition

Given category Top / and $G$ a topological group, then the principal \(G\)-bundle functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Bun}_G(-): \mathrm{Top}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Set \\ X \mapsto& \mathrm{Bun}_G(X) \\ (f: X \rightarrow Y) \mapsto& (\mathrm{Bun}_G(Y) \rightarrow \mathrm{Bun}_G(X)) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Bun}_{G}(X)$ is the and the latter is given by taking the pullback of a principal g-bundle as principle g-bundle

We also often restrict this functor to category hCWC (cf. category hCWC as full subcategory of hTop)
