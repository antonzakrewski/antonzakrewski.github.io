zariski local nature of immersions

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $u: X \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(R)$ a map of algebraic functors. Suppose that $X$ satisfies zariski descent and let $(f_i)$ be a family generating the unit ideal. Let $u_i: X \times_{\mathrm{Spec}(R)} D(f_i) \rightarrow D(f_i)$ be the pullback of $u$ along $D(f_i) \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(R)$ and suppose that each $u_i$ is an immersion. Then so is $u$.
