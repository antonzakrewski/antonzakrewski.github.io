module homomorphism modulo ideal surjective and power of ideal

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $M_1,M_2$ an \(R\)-module and $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq R$ an ideal. Let $\varphi: M_1 \rightarrow M_2$ be a module-homomorphism such that

\begin{align*} \varphi/\mathfrak{a}: M_1/\mathfrak{a}M_1 \rightarrow M_2/\mathfrak{a}M_2 \end{align*}

is an epimorphism

Then so is

\begin{align*} \varphi/\mathfrak{a}^2: M_1/\mathfrak{a}^n M_1 \rightarrow M_2/\mathfrak{a}^n M_2 \end{align*}

Proof
