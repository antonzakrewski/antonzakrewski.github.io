steinberg group

Definition

Let $R$ be a ring Then the steinberg group $\mathrm{St}(R)$ is defined by the presentation

  1. symbols $e_{i,j}(r)$ for $r \in R$ and $i \neq j$
  2. the steinberg relations given by
  • $e_{i,j}(r) e_{i,j}(r') = e_{i,j}(r + r')$
  • $[e_{i,j}(r),e_{j,k}(r')] = e_{i,k}(rr')$
  • $[e_{i,j}(r),e_{k,l}(r')] = 1$ if $i \neq l$ and $j \neq k$
