fully faithful infinity left adjoint and unit as equivalence

Proposition

Let $\begin{tikzcd} \mathcal{C} \arrow[r, shift left=1ex, "\mathcal{F}"{name=G}] & \mathcal{D}\arrow[l, shift left=.5ex, "\mathcal{G}"{name=F}] \arrow[phantom, from=F, to=G, , "\scriptscriptstyle\boldsymbol{\bot}"]. \end{tikzcd}$ be an adjunction TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is fully faithful
  2. the unit is an equivalence

Proof

Note that $\mathcal{F}$ is fully faithful if and only if the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,-) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(-), \mathcal{F}(-)) \end{align*}

is an equivalence.

where the vertical map is the adjunction equivalence (precomposed with $\mathcal{F}(-)$ in the second variable hence using two out of three for equivalences the top map is an equivalence iff the composition is an equivalence.
