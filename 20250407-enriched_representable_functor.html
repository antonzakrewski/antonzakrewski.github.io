1-enriched representable functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a closed monoidal category, $\mathcal{C}$ a category enriched over $\mathcal{D}$ and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

an enriched functor

Then $\mathcal{G}$ is said to be \(1\)-categorically enriched, if there exists an object $C \in \mathcal{C}$ and a map $\eta: 1_{\mathcal{D}} \rightarrow \mathcal{G}(C)$ such that the composition of

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,C) \cong& \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,C) \otimes 1_{\mathcal{D}} \\ \rightarrow& \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,C) \otimes G(C) \\ \rightarrow& \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{G}(X), \mathcal{G}(C)) \otimes \mathcal{G}(C) \rightarrow& \mathcal{G}(X) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism
