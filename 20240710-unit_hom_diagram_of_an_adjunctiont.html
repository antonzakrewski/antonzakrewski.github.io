unit hom diagram of an adjunction

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ adjoints. Then the diagram

commutes where

  1. $\eta: \mathrm{id} \rightarrow \mathcal{RL}$ is the unit

Proof

left

Let $f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y)$. Then let $\eta(-): \mathrm{id} \rightarrow \mathcal{RL}$ be the unit

then we get

resp. 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{L}(X) \arrow[]{r}{\mathcal{L}(f)} \arrow[swap]{rd}{\tau^{-1}(\eta(Y) \circ f)& \mathcal{L}(Y) \arrow[]{d}{\mathrm{id}}\\
& \mathcal{L}(Y)
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

this shows that

\begin{align*} \tau(\mathcal{L}(f)) =& \tau(\mathcal{L}(f) \circ \mathrm{id}) \\ =& \tau(\tau^{-1}(\eta(Y) \circ f)) \\ =& \eta(Y) \circ f \end{align*}

right
