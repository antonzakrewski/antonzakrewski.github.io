symmetric algebra is a cogroup

Proposition

The symmetric algebra admits a cogroup structure.

Proof

Use that the forgetful functor

\begin{align*} (\mathrm{CAlg}_{R}, \otimes) \rightarrow (\mathrm{Mod}_{R}, \oplus) \end{align*}

is a functor between cocartesian monoidal structures, hence a lax functor (cf. functor between cocartesian monoidal categories as lax monoidal functor). Then the left adjoint is an oplax monoidal functor and therefore preserves cogroup objects. But the monodial structure $\oplus$ is also the cartesian monoidal structure, so every object is canonically a object is a monoid in a cocartesian monoidal structure.
