density theorem for representations

Proposition

Let $V$ be a finite dimensional representation of an K-Algebra $A$ over an algebraically closed field $k$.

  1. If $V$ is simple, then the map
\begin{align*} A \rightarrow& \mathrm{End}_{D}(V) a \mapsto& a \cdot - \end{align*}

is surjective

  1. Let $V \cong \bigoplus V_i$ where $V_i$ are pairwise non isomorphic and simple.

Then the map $A \rightarrow \bigoplus \mathrm{End}_{D_i}(V_i)$ is surjective

Proof

special case of 2) for $n = 1$.
