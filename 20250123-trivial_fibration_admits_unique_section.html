trivial fibration admits unique section

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a trivial fibration. Then $f$ admits a section which is unique up to contractible Kan complex

Proof

using postcomposition with a trivial fibration as trivial fibration

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(Y,X) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(Y,Y) \end{align*}

is also a trivial fibration.

Then by trivial fibration with contractible fibres
