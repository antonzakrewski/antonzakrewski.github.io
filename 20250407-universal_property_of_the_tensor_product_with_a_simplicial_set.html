universal property of the tensor product with a simplicial set

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $X$ a simplicial set and $C \otimes X$ a tensor product Then there exists a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(C \otimes X,-) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}(\vert X \vert,\mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(C,-)) \end{align*}
