R-localization is objectwise sequential colimit

Proposition

Let $R = \mathbb{Z}[\frac{1}{p} \vert p \in P] \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ be a subring. Then the R-localization is objectwise the sequential colimit

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}_R(X) = \mathrm{colim}(X \xrightarrow{\cdot p_1} X \xrightarrow{\cdot p_1 \cdot p_2} \xrightarrow{\cdot p_1 \cdot p_2 \cdot p_3} ...) \end{align*}

Proof

First note that the strongly saturated closure is closed under transfinite composition, i.e. sequential colimits, so the map

\begin{align*} X \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} X \end{align*}

is an R-local equivalence.

So it suffices to show that $\mathrm{colim} X$ is R-local.
