even spectrum and even cw structure

Proposition

Let $X$ be a finite spectrum TFAE:

  1. $X$ is an even spectrum
  2. there exists a cw structure $\mathrm{Sk}_n$ such that $\mathrm{Sk}_{2n} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sk}_{2n+1}$ is an equivalence (todo: use some convention).

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

do some CW-Approximation argument, use some stable hurewicz theorem for an argument, that only even degrees are necessary (remark: we are simply connected)

2) $\implies$ 1)

use some induction + long exact sequence of homology argument.
