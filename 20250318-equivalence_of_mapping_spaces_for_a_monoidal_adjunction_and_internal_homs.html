equivalence of mapping spaces for a monoidal adjunction and internal homs

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be closed symmetric monoidal infinity categories and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathcal{D} \\ \mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ \end{align*}

a infinity monoidal adjunction.

Then there exists an natural equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(-), \underline{\mathrm{map}}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(-),-)) \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, \underline{\mathrm{map}}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,\mathcal{R}(-)) \end{align*}

Proof

informal

follows from

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(-, \mathcal{R}(\underline{\mathrm{map}}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(-),-))) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(-), \underline{\mathrm{map}}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(-),-) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(-) \otimes_{\mathcal{D}} \mathcal{L}(-), -) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(- \otimes_{\mathcal{C}} -), -) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(- \otimes_{\mathcal{C}} -, \mathcal{R}(-)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, \underline{\mathrm{map}}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, \mathcal{R}(-)) \\ \end{align*}
