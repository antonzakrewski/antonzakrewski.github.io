existence of a good cover for an open subset of the euclidean space

Proposition

Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ be an open subset. Then there exists a countable good cover

Proof

Choose a countable dense subset, e.g. $D \coloneqq U \cap \mathbb{Q}^n$. Then for $x \in D$ choose an $\varepsilon > 0$ such that

\begin{align*} B_{\varepsilon}(x) \subseteq U \end{align*}

Then $B_{\varepsilon}(x)$ forms a countable good cover, as the intersection of convex sets is convex
