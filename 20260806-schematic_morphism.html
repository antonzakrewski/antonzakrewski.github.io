schematic morphism

Definition

A schematic morphism is a representable morphism in $\mathcal{P}(\mathrm{Sch})$.

Remark

If one works inside $\mathcal{P}(\mathrm{Aff})$, then being schematic should just mean that we regard $\mathcal{P}(\mathrm{Aff}) \subseteq \mathcal{P}(\mathrm{Sch})$ for the left kan extension of the yoneda embedding
