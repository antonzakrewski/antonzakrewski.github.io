cross product as associative operation

Proposition

Let $X,Y,Z$ be topological spaces, $L,M,N$ \(R\)-modules over a commutative ring $R$ and $\alpha \in H^p(X;L), \beta \in H^q(Y;M), \gamma \in H^r(Z;N)$

Then the cross product of singular cohomology is associative, i.e.

\begin{align*} (\alpha \times \beta) \times \gamma = \alpha \times (\beta \times \gamma) \end{align*}
