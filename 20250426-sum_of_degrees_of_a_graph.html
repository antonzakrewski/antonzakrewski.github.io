sum of degrees of a graph

Proposition

Let $G = (V,E)$ be a graph. Then

\begin{align*} \sum_{v \in V} d_G(v) = 2 \vert E \vert \end{align*}

Proof

Proof by induction, induction on the amount of edges

induction basis, $\vert E \vert = 0$

Then the degree of a vertex is given by $0$, since it is not connected.

induction step

adding an edge adds precisely 1 to the two vertices it connects. hence we add $2$ to the total sum.
