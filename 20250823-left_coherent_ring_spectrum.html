left coherent ring spectrum

Definition

Let $R$ be an \(\mathbb{E}_1\)-ring spectrum. Then $R$ is said to be left coherent, if

  1. $R$ is connective
  2. $\pi_0(R)$ is a left coherent ring
  3. for each $n \geq 0$ (resp. $n \in \mathbb{Z}$) the homotopy groups are finitely presented as $\pi_0(R)$-modules
