homology of the collapse for a cofibered pair

Proposition

Let $(X,A)$ be a cofibred pair, then for $n \geq 0$ the map

\begin{align*} (X,A) \rightarrow (X/A, [A]) \end{align*}

induces an isomorphism on relative singular homology

\begin{align*} H_n(X,A) \cong H_n(X/A,[A]) \end{align*}
