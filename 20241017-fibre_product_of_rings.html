fibre product of rings with an ideal

Definition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ an ideal. Then the fibre product is defined as pullback

concretely it is the subring of $A \times A$ where

\begin{align*} (\alpha, \beta) \in D(A, \mathfrak{a}) \Leftrightarrow \alpha - \beta \in \mathfrak{a} \end{align*}
