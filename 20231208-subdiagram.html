subdiagram

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $I: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a diagram

Then a subdiagram is a diagram $J: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ such that there exists a faithful, object-injective functor

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{I} \end{align*}

and a factorization
