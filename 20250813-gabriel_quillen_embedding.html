gabriel-quillen embedding

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a waldhausen infinity category. TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ is exact
  2. the category $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{lex}}(\mathcal{C})$ is grothendieck prestable category
  3. there exists a small prestable category $\mathcal{D}$ and a fully faithful functor $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ which identifies $\mathcal{C}$ with an extension closed subcategory of $\mathcal{D}$
