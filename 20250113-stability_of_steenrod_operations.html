stability of steenrod operations

Proposition

Then for the suspension isomorphism $\sigma(-)$

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}^i(\sigma(x)) = \sigma(\mathrm{Sq}^i(x)) \end{align*}

Proof

consider

\begin{align*} \Phi: H^n(X, \mathbb{F}_2) \rightarrow H^{n+1}(X \wedge S^1, \mathbb{F}_2) \cong H^{n+1}(\Sigma X, \mathbb{F}_2) \end{align*}

consider hte composite

\begin{align*} \sigma^{-1} \circ \Phi \end{align*}

cf. homology of the wedge product with sphere canonically isomorphic to suspension

actual proof

Note that

\begin{align*} \sigma(x) = x \wedge \iota_1 \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}^k(\iota_1) = \begin{cases} \iota_1 & \mbox{if } k = 0 \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Then the Cartan formula of steenrod operations

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}^i(\sigma(x)) =& \mathrm{Sq}^{i}(x \wedge \iota_1) \\ =& \mathrm{Sq}^i(x) \wedge \iota_1 \\ =& ... =& \sigma(\mathrm{Sq}^i(x)) \end{align*}
