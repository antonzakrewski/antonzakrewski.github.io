group automorphism of a ring in general not generated by multiplication

Proposition

Let \(A\) be a ring and $f: A \rightarrow A$ a group-automorphism Then $f$ is in general not generated by multiplication with a unit

Proof

consider

\begin{align*} \mathbb{F}_2 \times \mathbb{F}_2 \end{align*}

Then the map

\begin{align*} \varphi: \mathbb{F}_2 \times \mathbb{F}_2 \rightarrow \mathbb{F}_2 \times \mathbb{F}_2 \\ (1,0) \mapsto& (0,1) \\ (0,1) \mapsto& (1,0) \\ \end{align*}

is a nontrivial group automorphism

But $\mathbb{F}_2 \times \mathbb{F}_2$ has only $(1,1)$ as unit
