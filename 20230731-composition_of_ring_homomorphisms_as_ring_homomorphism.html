composition of ring homomorphisms as ring homomorphism

Proposition

Let $A,B,C$ be rings and $\varphi: A \rightarrow B, \psi: B \rightarrow C$ be homomorphisms, then the composition

\begin{align*} \psi \circ \varphi: A \rightarrow C \end{align*}

is also a homomorphism

Proof

corollary of:

  • composition of monoid homomorphisms as monoid homomorphism
  • composition of group homomorphisms as group homomorphism
  • furthermore, the identity element is preserved
