formal group law functor

Definition

The formal group law functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{FGL}: \mathrm{CRing} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Set} \\ R \mapsto& \mathrm{FGL}(R) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{FGL}(R) \subseteq R[[x,y] ]$ is the set of \(R\)-valued formal group laws and any ring-homomorphism $g: R \rightarrow R'$ maps induces the map $g: R[ [ x,y]] \rightarrow R'[[x,y] ]$ of \(R\)-Algebras which restricts to the subset of formal group laws.
