embedding of a graph into r-complete bipartite graph

Proposition

Let $K_n^{(r)}$ be a complete r-bipartite graph and $H$ another Graph with $n = V(H)$. TFAE:

  1. $H \subseteq G$ is a subgraph
  2. $\chi(H) \leq r = \chi(K_n^r)$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from chromatic number of a subgraph

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let

\begin{align*} c: V(H) \rightarrow [r] \end{align*}

be a k-coloring of a graph.

Then any embedding of

\begin{align*} c^{-1}[i] \hookrightarrow V_i \end{align*}

does the job.
