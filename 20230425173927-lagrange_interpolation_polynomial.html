Lagrange Interpolation Polynomial

Definition

Suppose $F$ is a field and $x_i$ and $y_i$ are pairwise different for $i \in \{1,...,n\}$ Then the Lagrange Interpolation Polynomial for $(x_i,y_i)$ is defined as:

\begin{align*} f(x) \coloneqq \sum_{i=1}^{n} y_i \prod_{j \neq i} \frac{x - x_j}{x_i - x_j} \end{align*}
