characteristic and ring homomorphism from Z/p

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $n \in \mathbb{N}_0$. TFAE:

  1. there exists a ring-homomorphism $\mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow R$
  2. the characteristic satisfies: $\mathrm{char}(R) \mid n$.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

characteristic and ring homomorphism

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from the universal property of $\mathbb{Z}/p \mathbb{Z}$ as ring
