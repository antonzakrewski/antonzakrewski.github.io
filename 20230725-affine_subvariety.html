affine subvariety

Definition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space. Then an affine subvariety $V(\mathfrak{p})$ for a prime ideal $\mathfrak{p} \in \mathbb{A}_K^n$ is defined as

\begin{align*} V(\mathfrak{p}) =& \{(a_1,...,a_n) \in \mathbb{A}_{K}^n \vert f(a_1,...,a_n) = 0 \forall f \in \mathfrak{p}\} \end{align*}
