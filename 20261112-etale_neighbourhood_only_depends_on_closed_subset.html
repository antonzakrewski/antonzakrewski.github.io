etale neighbourhood only depends on closed subset

Proposition

Let $Z,Z' \hookrightarrow X$ be closed immersions with same underlying closed subset (cf. underlying space). Let $p: V \rightarrow X$ be an etale morphism TFAE:

  1. $p$ is an etale neighbourhood of $Z$
  2. $p$ is an etale neighbourhood of $Z$
