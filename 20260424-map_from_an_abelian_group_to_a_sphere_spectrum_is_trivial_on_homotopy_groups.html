map from an abelian group to a sphere spectrum is phantom

Proposition

Let $A$ be an abelian group and $\varphi: A \rightarrow \mathbb{S}$ a map of spectra Then $\varphi$ is a phantom map.

Proof

Assume that $\varphi$ is not phantom. Then there exists an $x \in \pi_*(A) = A$ with order $nm$ such that $m = \varphi(x) \neq 0$.

Then we may find a prime number $p$ which is coprime to $mn$. In particular, we may find a map $\mathbb{S} \rightarrow A$ which classifies the element $x$.
