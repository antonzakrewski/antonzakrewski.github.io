lie group is parallelizable

Proposition

Any lie group is parallizable.

Proof

Let $G$ be a lie group. Then for $e \in G$ choose an euclidean open and an isomorphism $TG_{e} \simeq U_e \times \mathbb{R}^n$. Then we want to construct a bundle map

\begin{align*} G \times \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow \mathrm{T}G \end{align*}

over $G$ which is fibrewise an isomorphism.

So by smooth tangent bundle of a product & smooth tangent bundle functor we get a map

\begin{align*} \cdot: \mathrm{T}G \times \mathrm{T}G \rightarrow \mathrm{T}G \end{align*}

Then we can compose with the zero section

which gives a map over $G$ which is smooth and fiberwise an isomorphism
