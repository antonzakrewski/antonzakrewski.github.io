riesz representation map

Proposition

Let $X$ be a Hilbert space. Then the map

\begin{align*} \mathcal{A}: X^* \rightarrow& X \\ l \mapsto y_l \end{align*}

defined by the Riesz-representation is a semi-linear, isometric bijection.

semi-linear

Let $l_1,l_2 \in X^*$ and $\alpha \in \mathbb{K}$. Let $l_j = \langle y_j,-\rangle$ for $j \in \{1,2\}$. Then

\begin{align*} \alpha \cdot l_1 + l_2 =& \alpha \cdot \langle y_1,-\rangle + \langle y_2,-\rangle \\ =& \langle \overline{\alpha} y_1 + y_2, -\rangle \end{align*}
