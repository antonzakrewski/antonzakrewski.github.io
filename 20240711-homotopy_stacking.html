homotopy stacking for a cw complex

Definition / Proposition

Suppose $X = \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{Sk}_n(X)$ is a CW-Complex, $Y$ a topological space. Then to give a homotopy

\begin{align*} H: [0,1] \times X \rightarrow& Y \end{align*}

from $f$ to $g$ it suffices to give a set theoretic map

\begin{align*} H: [0,1] \times X \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

such that

  1. $H_{n,a}: [0,1 - \frac{1}{2^{n+1}}] \times \mathrm{Sk}_n \rightarrow Y$
  2. $H_{n,b}: [1 - \frac{1}{2^{n+1}},1] \times \mathrm{Sk}_n \rightarrow Y$

are continuous - often the second part is even constant, i.e. moving the $n$ skeleton only up until $1 - \frac{1}{2^{n+1}}$.

Proof

follows from:

  • glueing lemma for a locally finite, closed covering
  • inductive homotopy out of a cw complex
