Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space with singular cohomology ring

\begin{align*} H^*(X; \mathbb{F}_2) = \mathbb{F}_2[u]/u^m \end{align*}

Then $\vert u \vert = 2^n$

Proof

By assumption $\mathrm{Sq}^{\vert u \vert}(u) = u^2 \neq 0$. Then we also have

\begin{align*} H^k(X, \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}) l 0 \end{align*}

for $\vert k \vert < k < 2 \vert u \vert$
