existence of a root of unity

Proposition

Let $F$ be an algebraically closed field, $U_n$ the set of roots of unities for $n \in \mathbb{N}^+$ with $\mathrm{char}(F) \not\mid n$ Then there exist $\varphi(n)$ primitive roots of unity, where $\varphi$ is the eulers phi function
