initial object as compact object

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal, $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\emptyset$ the initial object. Then $\emptyset$ is a kappa compact object.

Proof

follows as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\emptyset, -) = \mathrm{const}_{ \{*\}} \end{align*}

where the filtered colimit of singletons is the singleton
