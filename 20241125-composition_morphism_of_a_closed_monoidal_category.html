composition morphism of a closed monoidal category

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a closed monoidal category. Then the composition morphism is defined as morphism

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(Y,Z) \otimes_{\mathcal{V}} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Y) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,Z) \end{align*}

via the internal hom inherits adjunction and evaluation morphism
