preimage of a q-cartesian morphism and composition as qp-cartesian

Proposition

Let $p: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, q: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be inner fibrations between infinity categories Assume that $f: \Delta^1 \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ is a morphism such that $p(f)$ is \(q\)-cartesian

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is \(p\)-cartesian
  2. $f$ is \(qp\)-cartesian

\begin{align*} \mathcal{E}_{/f} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}_{/y} \times_{\mathcal{C}_{/qp(y)}} \mathcal{C}_{/qpf} \end{align*}
