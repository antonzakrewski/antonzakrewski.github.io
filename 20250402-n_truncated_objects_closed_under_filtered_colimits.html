n-truncated objects closed under filtered colimits in a category generated by compact objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, where each object is an (arbitrary) colimit of kappa compact objects. Then the n-truncated objects are closed under kappa filtered infinity colimit

Proof

Let $y_i \in \mathcal{C}$ be n-truncated and $x \in \mathcal{C}$ any object. Write $x \simeq \mathrm{colim}_{j \in J} x_j$ for compact $x_i$.

then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x, \mathrm{colim}_i y_i) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathrm{colim}_{j \in J} x_j, \mathrm{colim}_i y_i) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{lim}_{j \in J} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x_j, \mathrm{colim}_i y_i) \\ \end{align*}

using n-truncated anima closed under limits it suffices to show that $\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x_j, \mathrm{colim}_i y_i)$ is n-truncated. But as $x_j$ is compact, we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x_j, \mathrm{colim}_i y_i) \simeq \mathrm{colim}_i \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x_j,y_i) \end{align*}

hence by n-truncated anima closed under filtered colimits it suffices to note, that each $\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x_j,y_i)$ is n-truncated.
