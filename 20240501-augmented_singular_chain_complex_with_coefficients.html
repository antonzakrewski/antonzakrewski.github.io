augmented singular chain complex with coefficients

Definition

Let $X$ be a topological space, and $M$ an abelian group, then the \(M\) augmented singular chain complex is defined using the map generated by

\begin{align*} \epsilon: C_0^{\mathrm{sing}}(X,M) \cong M[X] \rightarrow& M \\ x \mapsto& 1 \end{align*}
