p-cartesian fibration and p-op-cocartesian morphim

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $p: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ be an inner fibration and $f \in \mathcal{E}_1$ a morphism. TFAE:

  1. $f$ is p-cartesian
  2. $f$ is p^op-cocartesian for the opposite simplicial set $p^{\mathrm{op}}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{op}}$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)
