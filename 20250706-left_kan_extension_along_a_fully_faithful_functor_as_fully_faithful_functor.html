left kan extension along a fully faithful functor is fully faithful

Proposition

Let $\iota: \mathcal{C}_0 \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a fully faithful and $\mathcal{D}$ another category with sufficiently many colimits for the left kan extension formula Then the left kan extension functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Lan}\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}_0, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

is also fully faithful

Proof

corollary of:

  • left kan extension along a fully faithful functor commutes up to natural equivalence
  • fully faithful infinity left adjoint and unit as equivalence
