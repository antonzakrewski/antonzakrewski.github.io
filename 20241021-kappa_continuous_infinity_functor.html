kappa continuous infinity functor

Definition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal and $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an infinity functor Then $f$ is said to be $\kappa$ continuous, if it preserves kappa filtered colimits

note: different than the 1 categorical definition
