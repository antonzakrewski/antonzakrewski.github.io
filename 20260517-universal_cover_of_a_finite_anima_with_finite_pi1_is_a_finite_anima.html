universal cover of a finite anima with finite pi_1 is a finite anima

Proposition

Let $X$ be a (connected) finite anima such that $\pi_1(X)$ is a finite group. Then the universal cover $\tilde{X}$ is also a finite anima.

Proof

b)

Consider the fiber sequence

\begin{align*} \pi_1(X) \rightarrow \tilde{X} \rightarrow X \end{align*}

and recall that the middle is the unstraightening or by Luries theorem about colimits in Anima $\tilde{X} \simeq \colim_{X} \pi_1(X)$. Now $X$ and the set $\pi_1(X)$ are by assumption finite anima, so $\tilde{X}$ is a finite colimit of finite anima, hence again a finite anima (cf. category of finite anima is finitely cocomplete).
