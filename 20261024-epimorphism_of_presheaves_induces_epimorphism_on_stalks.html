Proposition

Any epimorphism between sheaves on a topological space $X$ induces epimorphisms on the stalks

Proof

use:

  • pushout characterization of epimorphism / epimorphisms in set are precisely surjections.
  • colimits commute with colimits
  • surjective morphism of sheaves and covering
