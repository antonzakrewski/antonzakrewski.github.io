Schanuels Lemma for projective resolutions

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, $M$ an \(R\)-module. Suppose we have projective resolutions

(we may even assume that $P_n, Q_n$ are not projective).

Then we have an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \bigoplus_{\mathrm{even}}(P_i) \oplus \bigoplus_{\mathrm{odd}}(Q_i) \simeq \bigoplus_{\mathrm{odd}}(P_i) \oplus \bigoplus_{\mathrm{even}}(Q_i) \end{align*}

Proof
