indiscrete site

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. Then the indiscrete site is given by the covering

\begin{align*} \{f: V \rightarrow U \vert f \mathrm{ iso}\} \in \mathrm{Cov}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

Proof

  1. clear
  2. follows from composition of isomorphisms as isomorphism
  3. given
