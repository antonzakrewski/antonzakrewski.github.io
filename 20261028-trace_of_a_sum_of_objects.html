trace of a sum of objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a preadditive symmetric monoidal category and $V_1,..,V_n$ be objcets such that $V_1 \oplus ... \oplus V_n$ is also dualizable. Then we can identify the trace map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{tr}_{V_1 \oplus ... \oplus V_n}: \mathrm{End}(V_1 \oplus ... \oplus V_n) \rightarrow \mathbbm{1} \end{align*}

as the trace map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{End}(V_1 \oplus ... \oplus V_n) \xrightarrow{\prod p_i_* \iota^*} \bigoplus_{i = 1}^n \mathrm{End}(V_i) \rar & \mathbbm{1} \end{align*}
