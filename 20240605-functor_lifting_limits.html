functor lifting limits

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor and $I: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a diagram Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to lift the limit of $I$, if there exists an object $C \in \mathcal{C}$ together with a natural transformation $\eta: \mathrm{const}_{C} \rightarrow I$ such that that $\mathcal{F}$ and $\mathcal{F} \circ I$ (cf. right whiskering) is a limit of $\mathcal{F} \circ I$
