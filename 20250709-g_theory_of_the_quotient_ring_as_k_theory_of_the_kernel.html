G theory of the quotient ring as k theory of the kernel of Mod

Proposition

Let $R$ be a left coherent ring, $x \in R$ a central element. Let $\mathrm{Mod}_{x}^{\mathrm{fp}} = \mathrm{ker}(\mathrm{Mod}^{\mathrm{fp}}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}^{\mathrm{fp}}(R[\frac{1}{x}]))$.

Then there exists a fully faithful inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mod}^{\mathrm{fp}}(R/x) \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}_x^{\mathrm{fp}}(R) \end{align*}

given by restriction of scalars

Proof
