left invariant vector field on a lie group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a lie group and $X\in \mathfrak{X}(G)$ Then $X$ is said to be a left invariant, if for all $g, h \in G$

\begin{align*} D_h(l_g)(X_h)) = X_{gh} \end{align*}

where $D_h(l_g)$ is the derivative of the map

\begin{align*} l_g: G \rightarrow G h \mapsto g \cdot h \end{align*}

Proof
