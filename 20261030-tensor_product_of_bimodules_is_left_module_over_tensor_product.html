tensor product of modules is left module over tensor product

Proposition

Let $M_1,M_2$ be an \((A_1,k)\) and $(A_2,k)$ module over commutative rings $A_i,k$. Then $M_1 \otimes_{k} M_2$ is a $(A_1 \otimes A_2^{\mathrm{op}}$ module.

Proof
