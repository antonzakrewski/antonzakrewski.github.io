factorization of the square map of the steenrod operation

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space and $x \in H^n(X;\mathbb{F}_2) \cong [X, K((\mathbb{F}_2),n)]$ Then consider the diagram

as cup product of singular cohomology.

Then this admits a factorization

Proof

Then

\begin{align*} X \times E \Sigma_k \rightarrow (X \times E\Sigma_k)/\Sigma_k \end{align*}

is a covering map

similarly

\begin{align*} * \times E \Sigma_k \rightarrow (* \times E\Sigma_k)/\Sigma_k = (*)_{h \Sigma_k} \end{align*}

is a covering map

This

\begin{align*} C_{*}^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times E\Sigma_k, x \times E \Sigma_k, \mathbb{Z}) \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}[\Sigma_k]} \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow C_{*}^{\mathrm{sing}}(X_{h\Sigma_k}, *_{h\Sigma_k}) \rightarrow C^{\mathrm{sing}}_{*}((X,*)_{h\Sigma_k}) \end{align*}
