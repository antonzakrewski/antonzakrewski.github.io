opposite category functor is identity on Anima

Proposition

The opposite category functor restricts to the identity on Anima (up to contractible choice of homotopy of course)

Proof

Note that Anima as full subcategory is closed under colimits in category Cat, Therefore a colimit preserving functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Cat} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ restricts to a colimit preserving functor $\mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ using presheaf category arises by freely adding colimits this functor is then given by left kan extension along the point, so the restricted opposite category functor $()^{\mathrm{op}}: \mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}$ is then determined by the point. Of course $*^{\mathrm{op}} \simeq *$ so in particular the point maps to itself in $\mathrm{An}$ and hence as $\mathrm{An}$ is again closed under colimits this functor restricts to $\mathrm{An}$. Thus the opposite category functor restricts to an autoequivalence $\mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ which is then trivial (cf. autoequivalence in Anima )
