weak infinity yoneda lemma

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a morphism.

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an equivalence
  2. postcomposition is an equivalence
\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(Z,X) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(Z,Y) \end{align*}

for arbitrary $Z$

  1. precomposition is an equivalence
\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y,Z) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Z) \end{align*}

for arbitrary $Z$

  1. postcomposition induces equivalences $\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y,X) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y,Y), \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,X) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y)$
  2. precomposition induces equivalences $\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y,X) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,X), \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y,Y) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y)$

Proof

follows from

  • isomorphism between contravariant hom-sets and objects
  • isomorphism between covariant hom-sets and objects

where on can detect equivalences on the level of the infinity homotopy category (by definition of an equivalence)
