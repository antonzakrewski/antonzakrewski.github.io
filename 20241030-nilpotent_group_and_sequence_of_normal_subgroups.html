nilpotent group and sequence of normal subgroups

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group.

TFAE:

  1. $G$ is a nilpotent group
  2. there exists finitely many normal subgroups $N_i \trianglelefteq G$ such that
\begin{align*} 1 = N_1 \trianglelefteq ... \trianglelefteq N_n = G \end{align*}

with $N_{i+1}/N_{i}$ is an abelian group and $N_{i+1}/N_i \subseteq C(N_n/N_{i})$ lies inside the center

Proof
