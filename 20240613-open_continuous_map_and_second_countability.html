open continuous surjection and second countability

Proposition

Let $(X,\mathcal{T}_X)$ and $(Y,\mathcal{T}_Y)$ be topological spaces and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ an open, continuous surjective map Suppose $X$ is secondcountable Then so is $Y$

Proof

Let $\mathcal{B}$ be a countable basis. Let $y \in Y$ and $U$ an open neighbourhood. Then the preimage $p^{-1}[U]$ is open with a fibre $x \in p^{-1}[b]$

choose an open set $O \subseteq p^{-1}[U]$ with $x \in O$. Then $p[O] \subseteq p^{-1}[U]$ is open with $p(x) = y \in p[O]$.
