local noetherian ring and dimension as minimal amount of generators

Proposition

Let $A$ be a local noetherian ring. Then the dimension is given by the minimal amount of elemenst $a_1,...,a_r$ such that

\begin{align*} \sqrt{(a_1,...,a_r)} = \mathfrak{m} \end{align*}

Proof

using noetherian ring and prime ideal as minimal prime ideal over height many generators there exists $a_1,...,a_r = A$ such that

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{m} = \sqrt{(a_1,...,a_r)} \end{align*}

showing

\begin{align*} \mathrm{min generators over} \leq \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{m}) = \mathrm{dim}(A) \end{align*}

\begin{align*} (a_p,...,a_m) = (a_1,...,a_r) \end{align*}

\begin{align*} r = \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{m}) \leq m \end{align*}

i.e. $r = m$
