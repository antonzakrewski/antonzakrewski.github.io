tangent space of a point of a quasi-projective variety

Definition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ a quasi-projective variety and $P \in X$ a point. Let $N_p \subseteq \mathcal{O}_{X,P}$ be the maximal ideal in the ring of regular functions of $X$ at $P$.

Then the tangential space of $X$ at $P$ is defined as vector space

\begin{align*} T_p(X) =& \mathrm{Hom}_{k}(N_p/N_p^2, k) \\ =& (N_p/N_p^2)^{\vee} \end{align*}
