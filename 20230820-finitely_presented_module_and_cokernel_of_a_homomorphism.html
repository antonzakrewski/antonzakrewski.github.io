finitely presented module and cokernel of a homomorphism

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $M$ an \(R\)-module.

TFAE:

  1. $M$ is finitely presented module
  2. there exist free \(R\)-modules $A^m,A^n$ for $m,n \in \mathbb{N}$ and a homomorphism $\varphi: A^m \rightarrow A^n$ such that
\begin{align*} M \cong \mathrm{coker}(\varphi) \end{align*}

Proof

using the characterization as finitely presented module and exact sequence:

1) $\implies$ 2)

By Assumption there exists an exact sequence

and by Homomorphiesatz für Moduln, we conclude

\begin{align*} M =& \mathrm{ker}(0) \\ =& \mathrm{im}(\psi) \\ \cong& A^n/\mathrm{ker}(\psi) \\ =& A^n/\mathrm{im}(\varphi) \\ =& \mathrm{coker}(\varphi) \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let

be exact at $A^n$ (see: exactification of a homomorphism), then by assumption we get, that $M$ is finitely generated with $\pi: A^n \rightarrow A^n/\mathrm{im}(\varphi)$ and the kernel is also finitely generated because of $\mathrm{ker}(\pi) = \mathrm{im}(\varphi) \cong A^n/\mathrm{ker}(\varphi) = A^n/0$
