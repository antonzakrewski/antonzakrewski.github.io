correspondence theorem for primary ideal and quotients

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring. Then the map

\begin{align*} \pi_{\mathfrak{a}}^{-1}: \{\overline{\mathfrak{b}} \subseteq A \vert \overline{\mathfrak{b}} \text{ primary ideal such that } \mathfrak{a} \subseteq \overline{\mathfrak{b}}\} \rightarrow \{\mathfrak{b} \subseteq A/\mathfrak{a} \vert \mathfrak{b} \text{ primary ideal}\} \end{align*}

is an isomorphism between posets (see: Category Pos)
