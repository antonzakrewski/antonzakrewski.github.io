inclusion of BN into BZ is not a monomorphism

Proposition

The inclusion $B \mathbb{N} \rightarrow B \mathbb{Z}$ is not a monomorphism in category Cat

Proof

Suppose it were a monomorphism, then we would have a pullback diagram 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
B \mathbb{N} \rar \dar & B \mathbb{N} \dar \\
B \mathbb{N} \rar & B \mathbb{Z}$
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Now the groupoid core preserves limits, so we would get a pullback diagram

in the category of Anima, which is of course not the case, since $\Omega B $\mathbb{Z} \cong \mathbb{Z}$.
