borel-sigma algebra and closed sets

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space, $\mathcal{A}$ be the set of closed subsets. Then the borel-sigma algebra is given by

\begin{align*} \sigma(\mathcal{A}) \end{align*}

Proof

follows as $\sigma(\mathcal{A})$ is closed under complements and contains $\mathcal{A}$, so for an open $O \in \mathcal{T}$ it follows that

\begin{align*} X \setminus O \in \mathcal{A} \end{align*}

hence $X \setminus O \in \sigma(\mathcal{A})$ resp. $O \in \mathcal{A}$.

Thus $\sigma(\mathcal{A}) \supseteq \sigma(\mathcal{T}$

Repeating this argument for closed $C \in \mathcal{A}$ shows

\begin{align*} \sigma(\mathcal{A}) \subseteq \sigma(\mathcal{T} \end{align*}
