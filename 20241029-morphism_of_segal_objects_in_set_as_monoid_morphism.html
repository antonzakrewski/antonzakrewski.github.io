morphism of segal objects in Set as monoid morphism

Proposition

Given category set, there exists a correspondence between

  1. segal objects in the nerve $N(\mathrm{Set})$ with $X_0 \cong *$ and morphisms of segal objects
  2. monoid-homomorphism of the 1 categorical segal object as monoid object

Proof

using natural transformation and nerve a morphsim of segal objects corresponds to a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \eta: M \rightarrow N \end{align*}

of the underlying functors $M,N: \Delta^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$
