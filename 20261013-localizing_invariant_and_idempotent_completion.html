localizing invariant and idempotent completion

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{st}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a functor into a stable category $\mathcal{C}$ TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ takes any sequence $\mathcal{A} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{B} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}/\mathcal{A}$ to a fiber sequence, hwere $\mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ is fully faithful
  2. for all sequences $\mathcal{A} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{B} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$, such that the composite is nullhomotopic and $(\mathcal{B}/\mathcal{A})^{\sharp} \simeq \mathcal{C}^{\sharp}$ are equivalent up to idempotent completion, the resulting sequence $\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(\mathcal{B}) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(C)$ is a fiber sequence

Proof
