connected graph as tree and sum of degrees

Proposition

Let $G = (V,E)$ be a connected graph with $\vert V \vert \geq 2$. TFAE:

  1. $G$ is a tree
  2. the amount of leafs is given by
\begin{align*} \mathrm{leaf}(G) = 2 + \sum_{v \in V, d_G(v) \geq 3}(d(v) - 2) \end{align*}

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Proof by induction, number of vertices

induction basis

for $\vert V \vert = 2$ this is true as the only tree is given by $\Delta^1$.

induction step

assume that $\vert V \vert \geq 3$. choose a leaf $v$ (cf. tree minus leaf as tree). Then $G \setminus \{v\}$ is still a tree (cf. tree contains two leafs).

Let $v'$ be the vertex which was connected to $v$ and $G' \coloneqq G \setminus \{v\}$ If $v'$ is a leaf in $G'$ then $G$ contains as many leafs as $G'$ and $d_G(v') = 2$. If $v'$ is not a leaf in $G'$, then $G$ contains one leaf more that $G'$. In this case we get in particular $d_{T}(v') \geq 3$ and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{leaf}(G') + 1 =& 2 + \sum_{v \in V, d_G(v) \geq 3} ( d_G(v) - 2) \\ =& \mathrm{leaf}(G) \end{align*}

by making a case distinction for $d_G(v') = 3$ or $d_G(v') > 3$

2) $\implies$ 1)

Suppose $G$ is not a tree. Then it is not minimally connected. Hence remove edges until it is minimally connected. Denote the tree as $T$

By the formula above we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{leaf}(T) = 2 + \sum_{v \in V, d_T(v) \geq 3}(d_T(v) - 2) \end{align*}

But furthermore

\begin{align*} 1 \leq d_T(v) \leq d_G(v) \end{align*}

since $T$ is still connected, which shows

\begin{align*} \mathrm{leaf}(T) \geq& \mathrm{leaf}(G) \\ 2 + \sum_{v \in V, d_T(v) \geq 3}(d_T(v) - 2) \leq& 2 + \sum_{v \in V, d_G(v) \geq 3}(d_G(v) - 2) \end{align*}

Then choose a cycle $v_1 \rightarrow ... \rightarrow v_n \rightarrow v_1$ of $G$. Note that some edge $\{v_i,v_{i+1}\}$ of the cycle is missing in $T$ (otherwise it wouldn't be a tree anymore).

Then if $d_{T}(v_i) = 1$ we got a new leaf in $T$ showing

\begin{align*} \mathrm{leaf}(T) > \mathrm{leaf}(G) \end{align*}

otherwise $d_T(v_i) > 2$ we got $d_T(v_i) > 3$ and hence

\begin{align*} 2 + \sum_{v \in V, d_T(v) \geq 3}(d_T(v) - 2) <& 2 + \sum_{v \in V, d_G(v) \geq 3}(d_G(v) - 2) \end{align*}

in any case by comparing to the equality for $T$ (see 1) $\implies$ 2) this shows

\begin{align*} \mathrm{leaf}(G) < 2 + \sum_{v \in V, d_G(v) \geq 3}(d_G(v) - 2) \end{align*}
