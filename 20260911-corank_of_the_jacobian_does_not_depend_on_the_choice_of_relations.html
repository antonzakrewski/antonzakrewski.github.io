corank of the jacobian does not depend on the choice of relations

Proposition

Let $A \cong k[x_1,...,x_n]/(f_1,...,f_r)$ be a finitely generated k-Algebra over a field $k$ and $\mathfrak{p}$ a point in $A$. Let $q \in k[x_1,...,x_n]$ and $y - q = h$ for another variable $y$. Then the jacobian matrix of $(f_1,...,f_r)$ has the same corank at $\mathfrak{p}$ as the Jacobian matrix of $(f_1,...,f_r)$ with respect to $(x_1,...,x_n)$.
