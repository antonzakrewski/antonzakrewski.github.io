forgetful functor category from Arrows of group to the category of equivariant groups

Definition

Let $\mathrm{EqGrp}$ be the category of equivariant groups, then the forgetful functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{forget}: \mathrm{Ar}(\mathrm{Grp}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{EqGrp} \\ \end{align*}

which maps $(G \rightarrow H)$ to the pair $(G,H)$ together with the automorphism $G \rightarrow H \rightarrow \mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{Grp}}(H)$ where the latter is given by the conjugation.
