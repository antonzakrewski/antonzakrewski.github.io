small object

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $\kappa$ a cardinal Suppose $\mathcal{C}$ admits $\kappa$ sized filtered colimits.

Then $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ is said to be $\kappa$ small, if

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,-) \end{align*}

preserves $\kappa$ sized filtered colimits
