Proof

Let $\mathrm{lim}$ be a limit of this shape, then we conclude, that

\begin{align*} H(\mathrm{lim}(\mathcal{G})) \cong& \mathrm{lim}(\mathcal{HG}) \\ \cong& \mathrm{lim}(\mathcal{F}) \\ \cong& \mathcal{F}(\mathrm{lim}) \\ \cong& \mathcal{HG}(\mathrm{lim}) \end{align*}

and hence since $\mathcal{H}$ is conservative, it follows, that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}(\mathcal{G}) \cong \mathcal{G}(\mathrm{lim}) \end{align*}
