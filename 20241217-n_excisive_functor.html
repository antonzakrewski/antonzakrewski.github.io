n-excisive Functor between stable infinity categories

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be a stable infinity categories, then an infinity functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is said to be \(n\)-excivsive, if $\mathcal{F}$ takes strongly cocartesian n+1 cube to cocartesian n+1 cubes
