coarse space

Definition

A coarse space is defined as set $X$ with a collection of entourages $\mathcal{C}$ where

  1. $\Delta \in \mathcal{C}$
  2. $\mathcal{C}$ is closed under subsets in $\mathcal{P}(X \times X)$
  3. closed under finite unions
  4. closed under inverse of an entourage
  5. closed under composition of entourages
