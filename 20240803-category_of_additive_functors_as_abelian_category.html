category of additive functors as abelian category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an additive category and $\mathcal{A}$ an abelian category

Then the full subcategory of additive functors

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{add}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{A}) \end{align*}

is an abelian category

Proof

follows from additive functor and biproduct and colimit of functors for a cocomplete codomain / limit of functors for a complete domain
