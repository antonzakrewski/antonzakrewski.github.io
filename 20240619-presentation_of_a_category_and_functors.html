presentation of a category and functors

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category with a presentation $(\mathrm{Mor}_{P}, R)$. Then there exists a natural Bijection

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C},-) \cong \{\mathrm{maps between the objects, maps out of the generators compatible with the relations}\} \end{align*}
