local ring not a product of rings

Proposition

Let $R$ be a local ring. Then for commutative rings $R_1,R_2$ and the product

\begin{align*} R \cong R_1 \times R_2 \end{align*}

it follows, that w.l.o.g. $R_1 = R$ and $R_2 = 0$

Proof

Suppose $R_2 \neq 0$, then by construction, $(0,1)$ is an idempotent element. Since local rings dont contain idempotent elements, we conclude that $R \not\cong R_1 \times R_2$
