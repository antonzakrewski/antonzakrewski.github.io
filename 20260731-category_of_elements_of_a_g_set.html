category of elements of a G-Set

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group, $X$ a G-Set regarded as presheaf $X: \mathrm{BG}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$. Then the category of elements consists of

  1. objects are given by $(*,x)$ for $x \in X(*)$, i.e. elements in the G-set
  2. morphisms $(*,x) \rightarrow (*,x')$ are given by elements $g \in G \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{BG}}(*,*)$ such that $g x = y$ (here precomposition should correspond to left multiplication)
