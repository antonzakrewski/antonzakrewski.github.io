limit of additive functors as additive functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be additive categories and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}) \end{align*}

the functor category

Then the full subcategory of additive functors is closed under limits

Proof

corollary of:

  • additive functor and biproduct
  • limit of functors for a complete domain
  • limits commute with limits

as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}\mathcal{F}(A \oplus B)) =& \mathrm{lim}(\mathcal{F}(A) \oplus \mathcal{F}(B)) \\ =& \mathrm{lim}(\mathcal{F}(A)) \oplus \mathrm{lim}(\mathcal{F}(B)) \end{align*}
