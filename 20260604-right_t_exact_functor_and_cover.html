right t-exact functor and truncation

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be (nice ?) stable categories equipped with t-structures and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a right t-exact functor. Then for $n \leq m$ $\pi_n(\mathcal{F}(-)) \Rightarrow \pi_n(\mathcal{F}(\tau_{\leq m}(-)))$ is an equivalence

Proof

follows from the fiber sequence $\tau_{\geq n+1} - \Rightarrow (-) \Rightarrow \tau_{\leq n}$ and the long exact sequence in the heart for a t-structure
