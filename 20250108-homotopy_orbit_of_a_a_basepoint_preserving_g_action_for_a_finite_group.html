homotopy orbit of a a basepoint preserving G-action for a finite group

Definition

Let $(X,x)$ be a based space and \(G\) a finite discrete topological group. Assume that $(X,*)$ is equipped with a Group Action in category Top*.

Then the pointed homotopy orbit is defined as

\begin{align*} (X,*)_{hG} \coloneqq \mathrm{Cone}( \{*\}_{hG} \rightarrow X_{hG}) \end{align*}

where $X_{hG}$ is the homotopy orbit
