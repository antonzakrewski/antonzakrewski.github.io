cocartesification of an inner fibration

Proposition

Let $p: X \rightarrow Y$ be an inner fibration of infinity categories Then the cocartesification is defined as cocartesian fibration

\begin{align*} p': X' \rightarrow Y' \end{align*}

as pullback in category sSet

where $Y' \hookrightarrow Y$ is the inclusion of morphisms which admit p-cocartesian morphisms
