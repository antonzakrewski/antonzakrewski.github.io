multiplicative spectral sequence

Definition

A spectral sequence is called multiplicative if

  1. $F_*(M)$ is equipped with a filtered graded ring structure
  2. each page $E_{*,*}^r$ is equipped with a bigraded ring structure
  3. the differentials $d^r$ satisfy the leibniz rule, i.e.
\begin{align*} d^r(x \cdot y) = d^r(x) \cdot y + (-1)^{\vert X \vert} x \cdot d^r(y) \end{align*}
