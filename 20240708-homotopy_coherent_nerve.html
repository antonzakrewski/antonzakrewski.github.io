homotopy coherent nerve

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C} \in \mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{sSet}}$ be a simplicially enriched category (or kan enriched category) Then the homotopy coherent nerve is defined as simplicial set

\begin{align*} \underline{N}(\mathcal{C}): \Delta^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{sSet} \\ n \mapsto& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{sSet}}}(\mathfrak{C}[\Delta^n], \mathcal{C}) \\ d \mapsto& \mathfrak{C}[d]^* \end{align*}
