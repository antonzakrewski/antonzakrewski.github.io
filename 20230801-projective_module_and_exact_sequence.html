projective module and split exact sequence

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $P$ an \(R\)-module. TFAE:

  1. $P$ is a projective module
  2. for a module $M$ and epimorphism $f: M \rightarrow P$ there exists a split exact sequence

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

take $K = \mathrm{ker}(f)$ and use epimorphism onto a projective splits

2) $\implies$ 1)

splitting lemma for modules projective module and sum as free module
