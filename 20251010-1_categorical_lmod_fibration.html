1-categorical LMod fibration

Definition

The 1-categorical $\mathrm{LMod}$ fibration is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{LMod} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ring} \end{align*}

where

  1. $\mathrm{Ring}$ is the category Ring
  2. $\mathrm{LMod}$ contains pairs $(M,R)$ where $M$ is an \(R\)-module over a ring $R$
