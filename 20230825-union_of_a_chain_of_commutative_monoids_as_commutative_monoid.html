union of a chain of commutative monoids as commutative monoid

Proposition

Let $M_1 \subseteq W_2 ...$ be a chain of commutative monoids Then $M \coloneqq \bigcup_{n \in \mathbb{N}} M_n$ is also a commutative monoid.
