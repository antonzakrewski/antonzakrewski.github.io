constant diagram functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{I}$ an index category. Then the constant diagram functor is defined as functor from $\mathcal{C}$ to the functor category $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{I}, \mathcal{C})$ is defined as precomposition of $\mathcal{I} \rightarrow *$

\begin{align*} \Delta \mathcal{C}: \mathcal{C} \simeq \mathrm{Fun}(*,\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{I}, \mathcal{C}) \\ \end{align*}

where $\underline{A}$ is the constant diagram
