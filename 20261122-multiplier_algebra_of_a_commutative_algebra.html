L and R agree for multiplier algebra of a commutative algebra

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative C star algebra Then in the multiplier C^* algebra, $L = R$.

Proof

We need to show that $L(a) = R(a)$ for all $a$, so fix an $a$. by the <cancellation property>, it suffices to show that $L(a) b = R(a) b$ for all $b \in B$.

Here we have

\begin{align*} a \cdot L(b) =& L(b) \cdot a \\ =& L(b \cdot a) \\ =& L(a \cdot b) \\ =& l(a) \cdot b \end{align*}

as well as

\begin{align*} a \cdot L(b) =& R(a) \cdot b \end{align*}

which shows the claim.
