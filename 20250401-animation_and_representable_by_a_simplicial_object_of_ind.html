animation and simplicial object of Ind

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small infinity category with finite coproducts. Let $X \in \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ be an infinity presheaf

TFAE:

  1. $X \in P_{\Sigma}(\mathcal{C})$ lies in the free sifted infinity cocompletion
  2. there exists a infinity simplicial object
\begin{align*} U_*: \Delta^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}(U_*) \simeq X \end{align*}

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Note that $\mathcal{P}_{\Sigma}(\mathcal{C})$ contains

  1. $\mathcal{C}$

and is closed under infinity sifted colimits. Hence it contains

  1. $\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C})$, since it is closed under filtered colimits (cf. filtered category as sifted category)
  2. the colimit of $U_*$, since geometric realizations are infinity-sifted colimits

2) $\implies$ 1)
