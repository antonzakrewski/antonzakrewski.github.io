invertible module as projective module

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod Suppose $M$ is an invertible module, then $M$ is a projective module

Proof

Let $N$ be an \(R\)-module such that

\begin{align*} N \otimes_{A} M = A \end{align*}

Then take an epimorphism

\begin{align*} A^\kappa \twoheadrightarrow M \end{align*}

Then applying the tensor $\otimes N$ gives an epimorphism

\begin{align*} A^\kappa \otimes N \twoheadrightarrow M \otimes N \simeq A \end{align*}

as $A$ is projective, this epimorphism splits (cf. epimorphism onto a projective splits).

Hence we get a split morphism

\begin{align*} N^{\kappa} \twoheadrightarrow A \end{align*}

and applying the tensor functor $\otimes M$ gives a spilt morphism

\begin{align*} A^\kappa \simeq (N^{\kappa} \otimes M) \twoheadrightarrow A \otimes M \simeq M \end{align*}

By the Splitting Lemma this gives rise to

\begin{align*} A^{\kappa} \simeq M \oplus \mathrm{ker}(A^{\kappa} \rightarrow M) \end{align*}

showing that $M$ si projective
