vector bundle of constant rank 0 is trivial

Proposition

Let $M$ be an vector bundle of constant rank $0$ over a commutative ring different from the zero ring. Then $M$ is the zero module

Proof

observe that there exists $f_1,...,f_n$ such that $M_{f_i} = 0$ and $(f_i) = R^n$ (cf. vector bundle and locally free for finitely many elements). Then in particular, $n = 0$ as otherwise, $M$ would have somewhere nonzero rank.

But being a zero module is a zariski local property shows that $M$ is the zero object
