local extension of a section for a normal space

Proposition

Let $X$ be a normal space $p: E \rightarrow X$ a topological vector bundle Let $U \subseteq X$ be an open subset, $x \in U$ and $s: U \rightarrow E$ a section defined on $U$.

Then there exists an open subset $V \subseteq U$ with $x \in V$ and a global section $t$ which coincide on $V$, i.e.

\begin{align*} s_{\vert V} = t_{\vert V} \end{align*}

Proof
