forgetful functor from oriented pointed R^d fibre bundles to oriented spherical fibrations

Definition

The forgetful functor from the <oriented pointed $\mathbb{R}^d$ fibre bundles> to <oriented spherical fibrations> is given by postcomposition

\begin{align*} [X,\mathrm{STop}_0(d)] \rightarrow [X,\mathrm{SG}(d)] \end{align*}
