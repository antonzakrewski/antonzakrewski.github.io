additive functor from abelian groups preserves Q vector spaces

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Ab} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab}$ be an additive functor Then $\mathcal{F}$ restricts to a functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathbb{Q}-\mathrm{Vect} \rightarrow \mathbb{Q}-\mathrm{Vect}$.

Proof

Recall that $\mathbb{Q}$ vector spaces are precisely the uniquely divisible abelian gorups, i.e., abelian groups $V$ such that multiplication with $p$ is an isomorphism. But additive functor preserves multiplication and isomorphisms under functors
