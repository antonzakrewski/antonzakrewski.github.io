lax right adjoint and oplax left adjoint

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \otimes_{\mathcal{C}}), (\mathcal{D}, \otimes_{\mathcal{D}})$ be monoidal categories. Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be functors and $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$ an adjunction.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is oplax monoidal
  2. $\mathcal{G}$ is lax monoidal functor
