\begin{align*} \chi_2 \circ \varphi_3 (g_2) =& \varphi_3 \circ \psi_2 (g_2) \\ =& \varphi_3(g_2) \\ =& 0 \end{align*}

Suppose $g_2,g_2' \in G_2$, such that $\psi_2(g_2) = \psi_2(g_2') = g_3$. Since $\psi_2(g_2 - g_2') = 0$ and hence $g_2 - g_2' \in \mathrm{ker}(\psi_2)$, by injectivity of $\psi_1$ (cf. exact sequence and monomorphism) there exists a unique $\Delta g_1$ such that $\psi_1(\Delta g_1) = g_2 - g_2$. Let $\Delta h_1 \coloneqq \varphi_1(\Delta g_1) \in \mathrm{im}(\varphi_1)$ Furthermore, by assumption, there exist unique $h_1,h_1' \in H_1$ such that $\chi_1(h_1) = h_2, \chi_1(h_1') = h_2'$, where $h_2 \coloneqq \varphi_2(g_2),h_2' \coloneqq \varphi_2(g_2')$. Then by commutativity we get

\begin{align*} \chi_1(h_1 - h_1' - \Delta h_1) =& \chi_1(h_1) - \chi_1(h_1') - \chi_1(\Delta h_1) \\ =& h_2 - h_2' - \varphi_2 (\psi_1(\Delta g_1)) \\ =& h_2 - h_2' - \varphi_2(g_2 - g_2') \\ =& h_2 - h_2' - \varphi_2(g_2) + \varphi_2(g_2') \\ =& h_2 - h_2' - h_2 + h_2' \\ =& 0 \end{align*}

hence by the lemma of minimal kernel and monomorphism we get $h_1 - h_1' - \Delta h_1 = 0$ or $h_1 - h_1' = \Delta h_1 \in \mathrm{im}(\varphi_1)$ Therefore Suppose $h_1,h_2 \in H_1$ such that $h_1 + \mathrm{im}(\varphi_i) = h_2 + \mathrm{im}(\varphi_i)$. Then $h_1 - h_2 \in \mathrm{im}(\varphi_i)$ so there exists an $g \in G_i$ such that $\varphi(g) = h_1 - h_2$

Hence

\begin{align*} \chi_i \circ \varphi_{i} (g) =& \varphi_{i+1} \circ \psi_i(g) \\ \chi_i(h_1 - h_2) \in \mathrm{im}(\varphi_{i+1}) \end{align*}

and therefore

\begin{align*} \chi_i(h_1) + \mathrm{im}(\varphi_{i+1}) =& \chi_i(h_2) + \mathrm{im}(\varphi_{i+1}) \end{align*}
