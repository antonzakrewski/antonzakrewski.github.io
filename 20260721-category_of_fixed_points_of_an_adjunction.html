category of fixed points of an adjunction

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ functors and $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$ an adjunction Then the category of fixed points is defined as full subcategory of $\mathcal{C}$ containing fixed points.

Remark

it follows that $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{fix}}$ and $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{fix}}$ are canonically equivalent: observe that the adjunction restricts to fixed points and adjunctions restrict to full subcategories. Now use proof strategy: equivalence via adjunctions and conservative
