rational cohomology ring of the rational eilenberg maclane space is free

Proposition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $K(\mathbb{Q}, n)$ the rational Eilenberg-MacLane space. Then

\begin{align*} Q^{*}[K(\mathbb{Q}, n)] \cong \mathrm{free}(\mathbb{Q}[-n]) \end{align*}

is the free algebra in $\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Mod}\mathbb{Q})$ generated by $\mathbb{Q}[-n]$

Proof
