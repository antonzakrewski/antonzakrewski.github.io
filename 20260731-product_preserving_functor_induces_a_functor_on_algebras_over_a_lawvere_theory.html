finite product preserving functor induces a functor on algebras over a lawvere theory

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{T}$ be a lawvere theory, then any finite product preserving functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ categories with finite products induces a functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{T}}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{T}}(\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}
