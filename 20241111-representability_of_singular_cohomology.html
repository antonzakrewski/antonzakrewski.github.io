representability of singular cohomology

Proposition

Let $A$ be an abelian group, then the singular cohomology group functor

\begin{align*} H^n(-,A): \mathrm{hCWC} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} \end{align*}

is a contravariant representable functor

Proof
