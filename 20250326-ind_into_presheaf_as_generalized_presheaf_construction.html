Ind into presheaf as generalized presheaf construction

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\kappa$ a regular cardinal Then the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is a generalized presheaf construction

\begin{align*} \mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{R}}^K(\mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C})) \end{align*}

for

  1. $K$ as collection of small simplicial sets
  2. $\mathcal{R}$ is the collection of diagrams $K^{\triangle} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C})$ which are \(\kappa\)-filtered colimit diagrams.

Proof

using the universal property: Let $\mathcal{D}$ be any cocomplete category. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}}(\mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{R}}^K(\mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C})), \mathcal{D}) \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\kappa}(\mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D}) \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}
