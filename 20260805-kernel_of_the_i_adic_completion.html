kernel of the I-adic completion

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $I$ an ideal. Then the kernel of the map $R \rightarrow \widehat{R}_I$ is given by $\bigcap_{n} I^n$.

Proof

observe that the map $\widehat{R}_I \rightarrow \prod_{n} R/I^n$ is a monomorphism (e.g. by explicite description of the limit in category set). Then it suffices to observe that the kernel of the map

\begin{align*} R \rightarrow \prod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} R/I^n \end{align*}

is given by $\bigcap_{n \in \mathbb{N}} I^n$
