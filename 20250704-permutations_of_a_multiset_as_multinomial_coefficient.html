permutations of a multiset as multinomial coefficient

Proposition

Let $[n] \twoheadrightarrow \mathbb{N}^+$ be a multiset with $\| f \|_1 = k$ many elements. Then there exist

\begin{align*} \binom{k}{f(1),...,f(n)} \end{align*}

many permutations of the multiset.

Proof

note that there exist $k! = \binom{k}{1,...,1}$ many permutaions of $[k]$. Any such permutation induces a permutation of the multiset.
