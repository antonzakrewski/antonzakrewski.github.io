holomorphic integral for a parametrized integral

Proposition

Let $\Omega \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be open and $F: \Omega \times [a,b] \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ be continuous and assume that for all $s \in [a,b];q

\begin{align*} F(-,s) \end{align*}

is holomorphic

\begin{align*} f(z) \coloneqq \int_{a}^b F(z,s) \mathrm{d}s \end{align*}

is also holomorphic
