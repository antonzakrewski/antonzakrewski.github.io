coproduct of zariski-local epimorphisms

Proposition

Let $f_i: X_i \rightarrow Y_i$ be Zariski-local epimorphisms. Then so is $\coprod_{i \in I} f_i: \coprod_{i \in I} X_i \rightarrow \coprod_{i \in I} Y_i$.

Proof

$\mathcal{P}(\mathrm{Aff})$

follows from:

  • yoneda lemma
  • pointwise calculation of colimits in the presheaf category
