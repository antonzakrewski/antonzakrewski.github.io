currying of functors preserving limits / colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be infinity categories, $I,J$ collections of indexing infinity categories. Suppose $\mathcal{E}$ admits $I$ resp. $J$ shaped limits reps. colimits. Suppose $\mathrm{Fun}^{I}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Fun}^{J}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}))$ is the iterated category of limit preserving functors of a shape / category of colimit preserving functors of a shape

Then currying in cat infinity gives an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{I}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Fun}^{J}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E})) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{I,J}(\mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}) \end{align*}

of functors, which preserve $I$ shaped limits/colimits in the first variable and $J$ shaped limits/colimits in the second variable, i.e. regarded as

  1. $\mathcal{F}(-,D)$
  2. $\mathcal{F}(C,-)$

Proof

follows from

  • limit in an infinity functor category
  • colimit in an infinity functor category
