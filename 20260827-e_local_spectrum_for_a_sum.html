E-local spectrum for a sum

Proposition

Let $X,E_i$ be spectra TFAE:

  1. $X$ is $E_i$ local for some $i$
  2. $X$ is $\bigoplus_{i} E_i$ local

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Assume that $X$ is $E_i$ local and suppose that $A$ is $\bigoplus E_i$ acyclic, so in particular $E_i$ acyclic for $i$ (cf. bousfield class of a sum is the intersection). Then $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(A,X) = 0$ since $A$ is $E_i$ acyclic and $X$ is $E_i$ local.

2) $\implies$ 1)
