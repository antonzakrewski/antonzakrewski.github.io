fully faithful, limit preserving embedding of a stable category into a bicomplete stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category, then there exists a fully faithful, limit preserving functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Sp}) \end{align*}

Proof

use $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C})$ where the RHS is even presentable (cf. presentable category, Ind and compact generator) since $\mathcal{C}$ admits all finite limits / colimits.
