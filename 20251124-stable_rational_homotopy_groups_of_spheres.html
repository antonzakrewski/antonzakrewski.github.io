stable rational homotopy groups of spheres

Proposition

The stable, rational homotopy groups are given by

\begin{align*} \pi_*(\mathbb{S})_{\mathbb{Q}} =& \pi_*(\mathbb{S}) \otimes \mathbb{Q} \\ =& \begin{cases} \mathbb{Q} & \mbox{if } * = 0 \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof
