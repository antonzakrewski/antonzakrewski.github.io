plus construction for presheaves

Definition

Let $\mathcal{F} \in \mathcal{p}(\mathcal{C})$ be a presheaf on a small category $\mathcal{C}$ equipped with a grothendieck topology $\tau$. Then the plus consrtuction is pointwise defined as

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}^+(X) \coloneqq \mathrm{colim}_{R \hookrightarrow よ(X)} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(R, \mathcal{F}) \end{align*}
