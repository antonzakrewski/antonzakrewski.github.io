tubular neighbourhood

Definition

Let $\iota: M \rightarrow W$ be a embedding. Then a tubular neighbourhood is a mapping cylinder neighbourhood $M \hookrightarrow K \hookrightarrow W$ where the deformation retraction $r: K \rightarrow M$ is diffeomorphic to the projection

\begin{align*} D(\nu(i)) \rightarrow M \end{align*}

where $D(\nu(i))$ is the underlying disk bundle normal bundle
