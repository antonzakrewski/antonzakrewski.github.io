multiplier algebra of the compact operators

Proposition

Let $H$ be a nonzero Hilbert space, then the multiplier algebra of the compact operators is given by the bounded operators.

Proof

Let $e \in H$ be a vector with norm $1$. Then consdier the map

\begin{align*} \theta_{v_1,v_2}(-) = v_1\langle v_2,- \rangle \end{align*}

where \langle -,-\rangle$ is $\mathbb{C}$- linear in the second variable. Observe that $\theta_{v_1,v_2}(-)$ is a rank 1 operator with image $\mathrm{span}(v_1), hence compact.

Then for a double centralizer $(L,R)$ define

\begin{align*} T : H \rightarrow H v \mapsto L(\theta_{v,e}(-))(e) \end{align*}

we it is a linear map, since $v_1 \mapsto v_1 \cdot \langle e,-\rangle$ is a linear map and $L(-)$ preserves linear maps . Moreover, the map is bounded as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sup}_{ \| w \| = 1} T(w) =& L(\theta_{v_1,e}(w))(e) \end{align*}
