fibrant replacement functor

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}, \mathrm{Cof}, \mathrm{Fib})$ be a model category, then the fibrant replacement functor

\begin{align*} R: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \pi_{f} \mathcal{C} \\ X \mapsto& RX \\ (f: X \rightarrow Y) \mapsto& [f]: RX \rightarrow RY \end{align*}

is given by

  1. choosing a fibrant replacement of an object $X \simeq RX$
  2. choosing a fibrant extension of a morphism
