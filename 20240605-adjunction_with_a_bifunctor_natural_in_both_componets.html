adjunction with a bifunctor natural in both components

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{H}(-,-): \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor and $\mathcal{F}_1, \mathcal{F}_2: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ Suppose for each object $C_i$ there exists a functor $\mathcal{F}_{C_1}$ resp. $\mathcal{F}_{C_2}$ such that

\begin{align*} B(C_i, -) \dashv& \mathcal{F}_{C_i} \\ B(-,C_i) \dashv& \mathcal{G}_{C_i} \end{align*}

Then there exists natural isomorphisms

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}(B(-,-),-) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}(\mathrm{pr}_1(-,-), \mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{pr}_2}(-,-)(-)) \\ \cong& \mathrm{Hom}(\mathrm{pr}_2(-,-), \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{pr}_1}(-,-)(-)) \end{align*}

Proof

Let $C_1,C_2 \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and $D \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{D})$. Then there exists a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}(B(C_1,C_2),D) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}(C_2,\mathcal{F}_{C_1}(D)) \\ \mathrm{Hom}(B(C_1,C_2),D) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}(C_1,\mathcal{G}_{C_2}(D)) \end{align*}
