category gamma^times

Definition

The category $\Gamma^{\times}$ is defined as category containing

  1. ordered pairs $( \langle n\rangle, S)$ where $\langle n\rangle \in \mathrm{Fin}_{*}$ and $S \susbeteq \langle n\rangle^{\circ}$
  2. a morphism from $( \langle n\rangle, S)$ to $( \langle n'\rangle, S')$ consists of a map $\alpha: \langle n\rangle \rightarrow \langle n'\rangle$ in $\mathrm{Fin}_*$ such that $\alpha^{-1}[S'] \subseteq S$, i.e.
\begin{align*} \alpha[ \langle n\rangle \setminus S] \subseteq \langle n'\rangle \setminus S' \end{align*}
