polynomial ring in multiple variables not a PID

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $R[X,Y]$ the polynomial ring in two variables. Then $R[X,Y]$ is not a principal ideal domain

Proof

Consider the ideal $(X,Y)$. Suppose it has one generator $r \in R[X,Y]$, then $\mathrm{deg}_Y(r), \mathrm{deg}(r)_X = 0$ as $X \in (X,Y)$ resp. $Y \in (X,Y)$. Hence $r \in R$, but this is a contradiction, as $r \not\in (X,Y)$
