universal property of the isomorphism classifier

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{J}$ the isomorphism classifier. Then there exists a (semi-)canonical isomorphism of sets

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{J}, \mathcal{C}) \cong \mathrm{Iso}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Iso}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$ are the isomorphism of $\mathcal{C}$ given by evaluating at one predefined morphism of $\mathcal{J}$.
