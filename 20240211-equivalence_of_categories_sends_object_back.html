equivalence of categories sends object back

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be equivalent categories for $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$. Then for $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ it follows, that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{GF}(A) \cong A \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of:

  • definition of an equivalence
  • natural isomorphism and isomorphism on the objects
