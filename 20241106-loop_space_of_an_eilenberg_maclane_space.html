Loop space of an Eilenberg-MacLane Space as Eilenberg-MacLane Space

Proposition

Let $K(A,n)$ be the Eilenberg-Maclane space for an abelian group $A$ and $n \geq 1$. Then the loop space $\Omega(K(A,n)) = K(A,n+1)$ is an Eilenberg-MacLane Space

Proof

follows from the Eckman-Hilton duality for suspension / loop space
