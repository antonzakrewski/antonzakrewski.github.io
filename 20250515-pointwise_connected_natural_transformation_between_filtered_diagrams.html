pointwise n-connected natural transformation between filtered diagrams and colimit

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $I$ a kappa filtered infinity category and $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}: I \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ infinity functors. Let $\eta: \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ be a natural transformations which is pointwise n-connected morphism of anima, then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}(\eta): \mathrm{colim}(F) \rightarrow \mathrm{colim}(G) \end{align*}

is also n-connected
