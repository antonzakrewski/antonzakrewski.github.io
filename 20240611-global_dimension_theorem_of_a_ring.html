global dimension theorem of a ring

Proposition

Let \(A\) be a ring, then the following numbers are the same

  1. $\mathrm{sup} \mathrm{\mathrm{id}(B) : B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathrm{RMod})}$
  2. $\mathrm{sup} \mathrm{\mathrm{pd}(B) : B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathrm{RMod})}$
  3. $\mathrm{sup} \mathrm{\mathrm{pd}(R/I) : I \text{right ideal}}$
