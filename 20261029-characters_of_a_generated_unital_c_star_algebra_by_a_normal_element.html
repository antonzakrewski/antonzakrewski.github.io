characters of a generated unital c star algebra by a normal element to the spectrum

Proposition

Let $A$ be a unital C-star Algebra and $a \in A$ a normal element in a star algebra. Let $C^*(1,a)$ be the generated unital c star algebra (cf. generated unital c star algebra by a normal element is commutative).

Then the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Char}(C^*(1,a)) \rightarrow& \sigma_{A}(a) \\ \chi \mapsto \chi(a) \end{align*}

is a homeomorphism

Proof

Gelfand duality for CH implies

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Char}(C^*(1,a)) \cong& \sigma_{C^*(1,a)}(a) \\ \supseteq& \sigma_{A}(a) \end{align*}

So let $\lambda \in \sigam_{C^*(1,a)}(a)$ and assume that $\lambda \not\in \sigma_A}(a)$. Let $g_n \in C_b(\mathbb{C} )$ with $\| g_n\|_{\infty} = 1$ and $g_n(\lambda) = 1$ but $g_-(z) = 0$ if $\vert z - \lambda \vert \leq \frac{1}{n}$

Then consider

\begin{align*} \chi \mapsto g_n(\chi(a)) \end{align*}

in $\mathrm{Char}(C^*(1,a)$ which by gelfand $b_n \in C^*(1,a)$ $v_{C^*(1,a)}(b_n) = g_n$
