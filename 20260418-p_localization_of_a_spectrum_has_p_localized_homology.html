R-localization of a spectrum has R-localized homology

Proposition

Let $R = \mathbb{Z}[p^{-1} \vert p \in P] \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ be a subring and $X,E$ spectra. Then the \(E\)-homology of the R-localization $X[p^{-1}]$ is given by

\begin{align*} E_*[X[p^{-1}] \cong E_*[X][p^{-1}] \end{align*}

Proof

follows as:

  • additive, sequential colimit preserving functor and R-localization
  • R-localization for a stable category and homotopy objects
