homology of the klein bottle

Proposition

Let $K$ be the klein bottle. Then the singular homology of the klein bottle is given by

\begin{align*} H_n(K) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } n = 0 \\ \mathbb{Z} \oplus \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } n = 1 \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof
