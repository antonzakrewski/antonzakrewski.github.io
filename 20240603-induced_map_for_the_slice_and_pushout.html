induced map for the join and pushout

Proposition

Let $A,B,S,T$ be simplicial sets, $\iota: A \rightarrow B, g: S \rightarrow T$ be morphism of simplicial sets. Then there exists a canonical map

\begin{align*} \iota \widehat{\star} g: A \star T \coprod_{A \star S} B \star S \rightarrow B \star T \end{align*}
