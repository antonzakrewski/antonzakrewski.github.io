image of a left adjoint between stable presentable categories is the fiber

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be left adjoint between stable presentable categories. Then the image is the fiber of the functor $\mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}/\mathcal{C}$.

Proof

We have a cofiber sequence $\mathrm{Im}(\mathcal{F}) \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}/\mathcal{C}$ in $\mathrm{Pr^L}$ and hence a pullback

after passing to PrR. Now $0 \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is fully faithful, since it is the inclusion of the initial object and hence $\mathcal{D}/\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ identifies with the full subcategory

pass to $\mathrm{PrR}$ and use that the inclusion $0 \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is fully faithful
