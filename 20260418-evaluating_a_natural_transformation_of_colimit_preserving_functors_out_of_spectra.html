evaluating a natural transformation of colimit preserving functors out of spectra against sphere spectrum

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}: \mathrm{Sp} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be colimit preserving functors. Suppose $\alpha: \mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ is a natural transformation TFAE:

  1. $\alpha$ is a natural equivalence
  2. $\alpha(\mathbb{S})$ is an equivalence

Proof

corollary of:

  • natural equivalence between infinity functors and objectwise equivalence
  • natural transformation between I-shaped colimit preserving functors in spectra and equivalence
