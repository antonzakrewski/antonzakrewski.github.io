moebius bundle

Definition

The moebius bundle

\begin{align*} M \rightarrow S^1 \end{align*}

is defined as vector bundle as principal GL_n Bundle with the associated cocycles

\begin{align*} U_1 =& B_{\varepsilon}(-1) \\ U_2 =& B_{\varepsilon}(+1) \\ \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} U_1 \cap U_2 \rightarrow& \mathrm{GL}(\mathbb{R},n) \\ 1 \mapsto& 1 \\ -1 \mapsto& -1 \\ \end{align*}
