dimension in general not a point local property

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{m} \subseteq A$ an ideal. Then in general

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(A_\mathfrak{m}) \neq \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{m} \neq \mathrm{dim}(A) \end{align*}

Proof

Note that everything is a noetherian ring as

  1. localization of a noetherian ring as noetherian ring
  2. quotient ring of a noetherian ring as noetherian ring

Let $A = \mathbb{Z}_{(p)}[X]$ and $\mathfrak{m} = (pX - 1)$. Then it follows that

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Z}_{(p)}[X]/(pX - 1) \simeq& (\mathbb{Z} \setminus (p) \cup (p))^{-1} \mathbb{Z} \\ \simeq& \mathbb{Q} \end{align*}

showing that $(pX - 1)$ is maximal.

by the height theorem it has height less than $1$. As $(0) \subsetneq (pX - 1)$ we conclude that $\mathrm{ht}(pX - 1) = 1$

But then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(A) =& \mathrm{dim}(\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}) + 1 \\ =& 1 \end{align*}

(cf. dimension of a polynomial ring over a noetherian ring)

alternatively consider the chain

\begin{align*} (0) \subseteq (p) \subseteq (p,X) \end{align*}

Hence we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(A_\mathfrak{m}) \neq \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{m} \neq \mathrm{dim}(A) \\ 0 \neq 1 \neq 2 \end{align*}
