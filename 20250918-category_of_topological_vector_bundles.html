category of topological vector bundles_

Definition

The category of topological vector bundles is defined as (1,1)-Category containing

  1. topological vector bundles $p: E \rightarrow B$ as objects.
  2. morphisms are given by morphisms in the arrow category

where for $b' \in B'$ the induced map

\begin{align*} p'^{-1}[b'] \rightarrow p^{-1}[f(b)] \end{align*}

is a vectorspace-homomorphism
