injective image and intersection

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be sets and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ an injection.

Then for a family $A_i \subseteq X$, it holds for the intersection and image

\begin{align*} f[\bigcap_{i \in I} A_i] = \bigcap_{i \in I} f[A_i] \end{align*}

Proof

$\subseteq$

follows from image and intersection

$\supseteq$

Suppose $y \in \bigcap_{i \in I} f[A_i]$, then for $i \in I$ it holds, that $y \in f[A_i]$, hence there exists an $x_i \in A_i$ such that

\begin{align*} f(x_i) = y \end{align*}

then for $i,j$ it follows by injectivity, that

\begin{align*} x_i = x_j \end{align*}

Hence $x_i \in \bigcap_{i \in I} f[A_i]
