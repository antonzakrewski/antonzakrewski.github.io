reduced singular homology functor

Definition

for $n \in \mathbb{N}_0$, the reduced singular homology functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \tilde{H}_n: \mathrm{Top}_* \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ab} \\ X \rightarrow \tilde{H}_n(X) \end{align*}

where $\tilde{H}_n$ is the reduced singular homology
