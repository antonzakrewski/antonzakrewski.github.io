quotients of regular local rings

Proposition

Let $R$ be a regular local ring of dimension $d$ and let $f_1,...,f_n \in \mathfrak{m}$ TFAE:

  1. the local ring $R/(f_1,...,f_n)$ is regular of dimension $d - n$
  2. the images of $f_1,...,f_n$ in the zariski cotangent space $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$ are $k(\mathfrak{m})$-linearly independent.
