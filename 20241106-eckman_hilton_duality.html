Eckman-Hilton duality for suspension / loop space

Proposition

Given a convenient category of spaces there exists a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \pi_n(\Omega Y, \overline{y}) \cong \pi_{n+1}(Y,y) \end{align*}

induced by the based loop functor

Proof

follows from the

  • reduced suspension based loop adjunction

and

  • internal hom inherits adjunction

where there exists a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}((S^n,*), (\Omega Y, \overline{y})) \cong \mathrm{Map}((S^{n+1}, *), (Y,y)) \end{align*}

of topological spaces and hence by applying the path component functor a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \pi_0(\mathrm{Map}((S^n,*), (\Omega Y, \overline{y}))) \cong \pi_0(\mathrm{Map}((S^{n+1}, *), (Y,y))) \end{align*}

where the LHS is given by $\pi_n(\Omega Y, \overline{y})$ and the RHS is $\pi_{n+1}(Y,y)$ (by definition)
