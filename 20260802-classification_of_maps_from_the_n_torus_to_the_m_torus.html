classification of maps from the n-torus to the m-torus

Proposition

Let $T^n = (S^1)^{\times n}, T^m = (S^1)^{\times m}$ be the n-torus respectively the n-torus. Then

\begin{align*} \pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Top}}(T^n, T^m)) \simeq& \mathbb{Z}^{n \times m} \end{align*}

Proof

follows as product of Eilenberg-MacLane anima is an Eilenberg-MacLane anima and $S^1$ is an Eilenberg-MacLane space. Now it suffices to compute the cohomology

\begin{align*} H^1(T^n, \bigoplus_{j=1}^m \mathbb{Z}) \cong& \bigoplus_{j=1}^m H^1(T^n,\mathbb{Z}) \\ \cong& \bigoplus_{j=1}^m \bigoplus_{i=1}^n \mathbb{Z} \\ \cong \mathbb{Z}^{n \times m} \end{align*}
