simplicial homotopy group

Definition

Let $K$ be a Kan complex, $x \in X_0$ a 0 simplex. Then the \(n\)-th homotopy group is defined as

\begin{align*} \pi_0(X,x) \coloneqq \pi_0(\mathcal{F}(D^n, \partial D^n), (X,x)) \end{align*}
