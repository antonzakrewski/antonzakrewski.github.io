ring homomorphism as module homomorphism

Proposition

Let \(A,B\) be rings and $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ a ring-homomorphism. Then $\varphi$ is also a module-homomorphism for

  1. $A$ as ring as module
  2. $B$ as \(A\)-module after applying a restriction of scalars

Proof

definitions, as

\begin{align*} \varphi(a_1 \cdot a_2 + a_3) =& \varphi(a_1 \cdot a_2) + \varphi(a_3) \\ =& \varphi(a_1) \cdot \varphi(a_2) + \varphi(a_3) \\ =& (a_1)_{\varphi} \cdot \varphi(a_2) + \varphi(a_3) \end{align*}
