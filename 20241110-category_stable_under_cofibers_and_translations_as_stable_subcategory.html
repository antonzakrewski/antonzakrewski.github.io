category stable under cofibers and translations as stable subcategory

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category, $\mathcal{C}' \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ a full sub infinity category. Assume that $\mathcal{C}'$ is stable under cofiber and suspension.

Then $\mathcal{C}'$ is a stable subcategory

Proof

it suffices to show that $\mathcal{C}'$ is stable under fibers. Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a morphism. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{fib}(f) \cong \mathrm{cofib}(f)[-1] \end{align*}
