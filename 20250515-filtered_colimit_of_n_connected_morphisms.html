filtered colimit of n-connected morphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{I}: I \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ be a filtered diagram consisting of n-connected maps. Then each map $\mathcal{I}(i) \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} \mathcal{I}$ is also n-connceted.

Proof

follows as

  • $\pi_n$ preserves filtered colimits: homotopy group functor preserves filtered colimits
  • infinity colimit of a diagram with only equivalences from a weakly contractible simplicial set, in particular filtered category is weakly contractible.
  • colimit of epimorphisms in category set & forgetful functor from Ab to Set preserves filtered colimits / forgetful functor from Grp to Set preserves filtered colimits
