criterion for 1-functoriality of animation

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be cocomplete categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ be 1-sifted colimit preserving functors. Suppose that one of the following conditions holds

  1. $\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}})$ restricts to $\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{D}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}}) \subseteq \mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathrm{An}(\mathcal{D})$.
  2. $\mathrm{An}(\mathcal{G})(\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}}))$ restricts to $\mathcal{E}$

Then the natural transformation from 2-functoriality of animation

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An}(\mathcal{G}) \circ \mathrm{An}(\mathcal{F}) \Rightarrow \mathrm{An}(\mathcal{G} \circ \mathcal{F}) \end{align*}

is an equivalence

Proof

flat purity, 5.1.5
