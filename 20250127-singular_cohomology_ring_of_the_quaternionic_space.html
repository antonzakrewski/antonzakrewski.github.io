singular cohomology ring of the quaternionic space

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. Then

\begin{align*} H^*(\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n, R) \simeq R[X]/X^{n+1} \end{align*}

with $\vert X \vert = 4$

Proof

  • cellular homology
  • universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology
  • cup product pairing non degenerate
  • complex projective space as orientable manifold
  • quaternionic projective space as orientable space
