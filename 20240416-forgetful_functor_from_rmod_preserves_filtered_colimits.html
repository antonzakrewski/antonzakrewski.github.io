forgetful functor from RMod to Set preserves filtered colimits

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod Then the underlying-set functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Set}: \mathrm{RMod} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} \end{align*}

preserves filtered colimits

Proof

corollary of:

  • forgetful functor from RMod to Ab as left adjoint
  • forgetful functor from Ab to Set preserves filtered colimits
  • left adjoint preserves colimits
