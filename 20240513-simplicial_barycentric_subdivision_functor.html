simplicial barycentric subdivision functor

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathrm{sSet}$ be the category sSet and $\mathrm{sd}$ the simplicial subdivision functor Then the simplicial barycentric subdivision functor is defined a

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sd}(X) \coloneqq \mathrm{colim}_{\Delta^n \rightarrow X) \mathrm{sd}\Delta^n \end{align*}
