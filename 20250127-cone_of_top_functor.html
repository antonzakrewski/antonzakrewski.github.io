cone of Top functor

Proposition

The Cone of a topological space refines to a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{C}(-): \mathrm{Top} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Top}_* X \mapsto& C(X) \\ f \mapsto& C(f) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from the colim functor

where the basepoint is given by $* \rightarrow C(X)$ where the bottom square shows that the map $C(X) \rightarrow C(Y)$ is pointed.
