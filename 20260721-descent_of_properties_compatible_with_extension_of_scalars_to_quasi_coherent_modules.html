descent of properties compatible with extension of scalars to quasi-coherent modules

Proposition

Let $P$ be a property in a category of a modules, which is preserved under extension of scalars. Then we can define the property $P$ of quasi-coherent modules: Let $\mathrm{PMod}_R$ be the property of $\mathrm{Mod}_R$, we define

\begin{align*} \mathrm{PMod}\mathrm{Mod}_{X} = \lim_{\mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow X} \mathrm{Mod}_R \hookrightarrow \lim_{\mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow X} = \mathrm{Mod}_{X} \end{align*}

Proof

observe that the limit of fully faithful functors is fully faithful, where we have by assumption a natural transformation $\mathrm{PMod}_{-} \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}_{-}$ consisting of fully faithful functors.
