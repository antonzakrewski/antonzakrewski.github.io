reduced excisive functor and equivalence

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be pointed infinity categories where $\mathcal{C}$ is finitely cocomplete, $\mathcal{D}$ is finitely complete. Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a reduced infinity functor

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is an excisive infinity functor
  2. the loop suspension map is an equivalence
