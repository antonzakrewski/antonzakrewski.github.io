postcomposition of naturally isomorphic functors preserves isomorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be categories, $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}), \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{E})$ the functor categories, $\mathcal{F}_1,\mathcal{F}_2 \in \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}), \mathcal{G} \in \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{E})$ functors such that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_1 \cong \mathcal{F}_2 \end{align*}

are naturally isomorphic

Then so are

\begin{align*} \mathcal{G} \circ \mathcal{F}_1 \cong \mathcal{G} \cong \mathcal{F}_2 \end{align*}

Proof

  • postcomposition with a functor for functor categories
  • isomorphisms under functors
