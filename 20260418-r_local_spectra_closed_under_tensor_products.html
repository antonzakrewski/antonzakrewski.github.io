R-local spectra closed under tensor products

Proposition

Let $R = \mathbb{Z}[p^{-1} \vert p \in P] \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ be a subring and $X$ an R-local spectrum Then $X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} Y$ is also \(R\)-local for any arbitrary $Y$.

Proof

Let $p \in R^{\times}$, then multiplication by $p$ is equivalently given by multiplication by $p$ on $X$ (since $- \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} Y$ is an additive functor). Hence it is an equivalence
