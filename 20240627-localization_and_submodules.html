localization and submodules

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod Suppose $M$ is an \(R\)-module, $N_1,N_2 \subseteq M$ submodules Then

\begin{align*} S^{-1}(N_1 + N_2) = S^{-1}(N_1) + S^{-1}(N_2) \subseteq S^{-1}(N_i) \end{align*}

where $+$ denotes the generated module.

Proof

$S^{-1}(N_1 + N_2) \subseteq S^{-1}(N_1) + S^{-1}(N_2)$

Given an element $\frac{n_1 + n_2}{s} \in S^{-1}(N_1 + N_2)$ we may write it as

\begin{align*} \frac{n_1}{s} + \frac{n_2}{s} \end{align*}

$S^{-1}(N_1 + N_2) \supseteq S^{-1}(N_1) + S^{-1}(N_2)$

Given an element $\frac{n_1}{s_1} + \frac{n_2}{s_2}$ we may write it

\begin{align*} \frac{s_2 \cdot n_1 + s_1 \cdot n_2}{s_1 \cdot s_2} \end{align*}
