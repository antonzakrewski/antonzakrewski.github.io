dimension of a proper closed subspace of a finite dimensional, irreducible subspace strictly smaller

Proposition

Let $X$ be a finite dimensional, irreducible topological space. Then for a closed proper subset $A \subseteq X$ the dimension is strictly smaller

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(A) < \mathrm{dim}(X) \end{align*}

Proof

Let $A_1 \subseteq ... \subseteq A_n$ be a chain of irreducible closed subsets. Then $A_1 \subseteq ... \subseteq A_n \subseteq X$ is a chain of irreducible closed subsets in $X$. Note that $A_i$ is irreducible by definition and it is closed by closed subspace and closed sets
