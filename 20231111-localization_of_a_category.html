localization of a 1-category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $W \subseteq \mathrm{Mor}(C)$ a subclass of morphisms. Then a localization $\mathcal{C}[W^{-1}]$ is a category $\mathcal{C}[W^{-1}]$ with a functor

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}[W^{-1}] \end{align*}
