limit and morphism defined by component

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $I: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a diagram and $\lim_{I}$ the limit

Then a morphism $f: B \rightarrow \lim_{I}$ for an object $B$ is precisely and uniquely defined by morphisms $f_j: B \rightarrow A_j$ such that 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
& \arrow[]{ld}{f_i} B \arrow[]{rd}{f_j}& 
A_i  \arrow[]{rr}{} & & A_j \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

commutes
