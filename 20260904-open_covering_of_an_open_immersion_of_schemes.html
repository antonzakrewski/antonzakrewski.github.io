open covering of an open immersion of schemes

Proposition

Let $j: X \hookrightarrow Y$ be an open immersion of schemes Then there exists a diagram 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\coprod \mathrm{Spec}(S_j) \rar{j} \dar \rar & Y \dar
\coprod \mathrm{Spec}(R_i) \rar{j} & X
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

where the horizontal morphisms are affine open coverings and $\mathrm{Spec}(S_j) \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(R_i)$ is - for each component - also an open immersion.

Proof

follows from:

  • open covering of a morphism of schemes
  • left cancellation property of open immersions
