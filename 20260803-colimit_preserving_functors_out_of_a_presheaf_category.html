colimit preserving functors out of a presheaf category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a cocomplete category and $\mathcal{D}$ any small category, then there exists an equivalence of categories

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{D}), \mathcal{C}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
