left anodyne as saturation of left horn inclusions

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{sSet}$ be the category sSet Then the set of left anodyne map is precisely given by the saturated closurer of left horn inclusions

\begin{align*} \Lambda_j^n \rightarrow \Delta^n \end{align*}

for $j \neq 0, j \neq 1$

Proof

follows from:

  • standard simplex as compact object
  • simplicial subset of a compact simplicial set as compact simplicial set
  • saturated closure of morphisms of simplicial sets with compact domain
