R-Algebra structure of a free R-module over a commutative ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $M$ a free \(R\)-module. Then $M$ also is an R-Algebra

Proof

choose a basis $W$ of $M$. Then if $W$ is finite, consider

\begin{align*} R \hookrightarrow R[X]/X^{\vert W \vert} \end{align*}

else choose a set $\{X_i\}$ and a bijection

\begin{align*} \mathrm{FinSubset}( \{X_i\}) \cong W \end{align*}

and consider

\begin{align*} R \hookrightarrow R[X_i] \end{align*}

(cf. Mächtigkeit der Menge der endlichen Teilmengen)
