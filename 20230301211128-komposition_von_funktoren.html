functor composition

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be categories and $F: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ and $G: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ be functors. Then the composition $G \circ F$ defined by:

\begin{align*} (G \circ F)(A) =& G(F(A)) \\ (G \circ F)(f) =& G(F(f)) \end{align*}

for $A \in \mathcal{C}$ and $f \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C}$ is also a functor $G \circ F: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$
