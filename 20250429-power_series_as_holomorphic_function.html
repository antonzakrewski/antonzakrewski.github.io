power series as holomorphic function

Proposition

Let $\alpha_n \in \mathbb{C}$ for $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and

\begin{align*} f = \sum_{i = 0}^\infty \alpha_i x^i \end{align*}

be the power series.

Suppose $f$ regarded as function converges on some open $\Sigma \subseteq \mathbb{C}$. Then $f$ is holomorphic on $\Sigma$

Proof
