generating hypothesis

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a map of finite spectra which is weak phantom (i.e. $\pi_*(f) = 0$). Then $f$ is nullhomotopic.

Or in other words,

\begin{align*} \pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbb{S}}(X,Y)) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\pi_*(\mathbb{S})}(\pi_*(X), \pi_*(Y)) \end{align*}

is a monomorphism
