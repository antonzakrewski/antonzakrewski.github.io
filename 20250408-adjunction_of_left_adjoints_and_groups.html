adjunction of left adjoints and Groups

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be presentable infinity categories Then there exists a natural equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}}(\mathrm{Grp}_{\mathrm{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D}) \simeq \mathrm{coGrp}(\mathrm{Fun}^L(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})) \end{align*}

Proof

note that by equivalence of infinity subcategories of left and right adjoints

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}}(\mathrm{Grp}_{\mathrm{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{RAdj}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathrm{Grp}_{E_{\infty}}(\mathcal{C}))^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}

then using the adjunction of right adjoinst and Groups

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{RAdj}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathrm{Grp}_{E_{\infty}}(\mathcal{C}))^{\mathrm{op}} \simeq& \mathrm{Grp}_{E_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{RAdj}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C}))^{\mathrm{op}} \simeq& \mathrm{Grp}_{E_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})^{\mathrm{op}})^{\mathrm{op}} \simeq& \mathrm{coGrp}_{E_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

where the last part is the definition infinity category of cocartesian infinity cogroups
