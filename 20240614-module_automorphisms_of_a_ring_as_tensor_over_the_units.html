module automorphisms of a ring as torsor over the units

Proposition

Let \(A\) be a ring regarded as \(A\)-module. Then $\mathrm{Aut}(A)$ is an \(A^{\times}\)-torsor with

\begin{align*} A^{\times} \times \mathrm{Aut}(A) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Aut}(A) \times \mathrm{Aut}(A) \\ (\alpha, f) \mapsto& (\alpha \cdot f(-), f) \end{align*}

Proof

using the canonical isomorphism between module automorphisms of a ring and units

\begin{align*} \varphi: \mathrm{Aut}(A) \rightarrow A^{\times} \\ f \mapsto& f(1) \\ \end{align*}

we can define

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Aut}(A) \times \mathrm{Aut}(A) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Aut}(T) \times A^{\times} \\ (g,f) \mapsto& (\varphi(g \circ f^{-1}), f) \end{align*}
