sheaf co-yoneda

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category, $X\in \mathcal{C}$, $S \subseteq X$ a sieve and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ a presheaf Then there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(R) \coloneqq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(R, \mathcal{F}) \simeq \mathrm{lim}_{Y \rightarrow R} \mathcal{F}(Y) \end{align*}

Proof

use contravariant co-Yoneda Lemma

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{Y \rightarrow F} Y \simeq \mathcal{F}(-) \end{align*}

and contravariant hom-functor dualizes small colimits to limits + yoneda lemma to get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(R, \mathcal{F}) \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(\mathrm{colim}_{Y \rightarrow R} Y, \mathcal{F}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{lim}_{Y \rightarrow R} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(Y, \mathcal{F}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{lim}_{Y \rightarrow R} \mathcal{F}(Y) \\ \end{align*}
