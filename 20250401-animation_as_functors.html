animation as functors from 1-sfp preserving finite coproducts

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an animatable category. Assume that $\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}}$ is small, e.g. after enlarging universes.

Then $\mathrm{An}(\mathcal{C})$ consists precisely of the infinity functors

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: (\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}})^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

which preserves finite products (resp. takes coproducts to products, after accounting for the opposite category)

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An}(\mathcal{C}) = \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}((\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}})^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An}) \end{align*}
