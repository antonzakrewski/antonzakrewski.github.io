finitely generated submodule of a module and finite subset of a generating set

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $M$ an \(R\)-module. Suppose $N$ is a finitely generated submodule and $B$ a generating set of $M$. Then there exists a finite subset $B' \subseteq B$ such that

\begin{align*} M \subseteq \langle B'\rangle \subseteq \langle B\rangle = M \end{align*}

Proof

Suppose $x_1,...,x_k$ generate $M$, then each $x_i$ can be written as finite sum

\begin{align*} \sum r_{i,b} b \end{align*}

since $b$ generates $R[B]$

Let $B'$ be the union of those $b$ generating $x_i$

Then $\langle B'\rangle$ suffices
