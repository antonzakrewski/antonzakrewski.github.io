homology of an Eilenberg-MacLane space

Proposition

Let $K(A,n)$ be an Eilenberg-MacLane space for an abelian group Then

\begin{align*} H^l(K(A,n),M) = \begin{cases} 0 & \mbox{if } l < n \\ A \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} M & \mbox{if } l = n \\ \mathrm{nontrivial} & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

  1. absolute Hurewicz theorem
  2. universal coefficient theorem for singular homology
