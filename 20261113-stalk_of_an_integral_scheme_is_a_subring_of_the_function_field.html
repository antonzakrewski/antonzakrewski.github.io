stalk of an integral scheme is a subring of the function field

Proposition

Let $X$ be an integral scheme and $x \in X$ a geometric point. Then $\mathcal{O}_{X,x}$ is a subring of $K(X)$.

Proof

Let $\mathcal{U}_{x}$ and $\mathcal{U}_{\eta}$ be the set of open neighborhoods of $x$ and $\eta$ resp. Then $\mathcal{U}_{x} \subseteq \mathcal{U}_{\eta}$ as $\eta$ is a generic point. Recall that $\mathcal{O}_{X,x} = \mathrm{colim}_{O \in \mathcal{U}_{x}} \mathcal{O}(O)$, $\mathcal{O}_{X,x} = \mathrm{colim}_{U \in \mathcal{U}_{x}} \mathcal{O}(U)$. Then either an abstract argument (cf. filtered colimit of subobjects in anima) or an explicit computation implies that the canonical map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{O \in \mathcal{U}_{x}} \mathcal{O}(O) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{colim}_{U \in \mathcal{U}_{x}} \mathcal{O}(U) \end{align*}
