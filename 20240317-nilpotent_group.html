nilpotent group

Definition

Let $G$ be a group. Then $G$ is said to be nilpotent, if the sequence

\begin{align*} G_0 \coloneqq& G \\ G_i \coloneqq& [G_{i-1}, G] \end{align*}

is eventually the zero group $\{e\}$
