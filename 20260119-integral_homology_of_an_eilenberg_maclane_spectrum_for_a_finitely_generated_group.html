integral homology of an eilenberg maclane spectrum for a finitely generated group

Proposition

Let $A$ be a finitely generated group. Then $H_*(A,\mathbb{Z})$ is fintely generated.

Proof

recall that for $n >> 0$ the maps

\begin{align*} \Omega^n \widehat{\mathbb{S}}[K(A,n)] \rightarrow& A \\ \tilde{\mathbb{S}}[K(A,n)] \rightarrow& \Sigma^n A \end{align*}

are highly connected (cf. abelian group is colimit of shifts of infinite suspensions of Eilenberg MacLane spaces) so in particular an isomorphism on lower homotopy groups.

Now stable hurewicz theorem shows that

\begin{align*} \tilde{\mathbb{Z}}[K(A,n)] \rightarrow& \mathbb{Z}[ \Sigma^n A] \end{align*}

induces an isomorphism on low homtopoy groups.
