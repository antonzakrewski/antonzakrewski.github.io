morphism of C^* algebras restricts to an epimorphism on generated C^* algebras

Proposition

Let $f: A \rightarrow B$ be morphism between $C^*$ Algebras and $x_i$ elements in $A$. Then $f: C^*(x_i) \rightarrow C^*(f(x_i))$ is also an epimorphism.

Proof

Let $g,h: C^*(f(x_i)) \rightarrow C$ be two morphisms into a test $C^*$ Algebra, which agree when precomposed with $C^*(x_i)$. Then we need to show that $\mathrm{Eq}(f,g) = C^*(f(x_i)) = \{b \in C^*(f(x_i)) \vert f(b) = g(b)\}$: This follows as $\mathrm{Eq}(f,g) \subseteq B$ contains $f(x_i)$ and is a closed subalgebra (as it is closed in $C^*(f(x_i))$), hence must be equal to $C^*(f(x_i))$.
