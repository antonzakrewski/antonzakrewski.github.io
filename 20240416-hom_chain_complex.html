hom chain complex

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes.

Then the hom chain complex is defined as chain complex in Category Ab

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}(C,D)}_n \coloneqq \prod_{p \in \mathbb{Z}} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}^{\mathrm{Ab}}(C_p, D_{p+n}) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Hom}^{\mathrm{Ab}}_{\mathcal{A}}(-,-)$ is the ab-hom functor with differentials defined as

\begin{align*} d_n(\varphi_p) \coloneqq d_{p+n}^D \circ \varphi_p + (-1)^{n +1} \varphi_{p-1} \circ d_p^{C} \end{align*}

Proof

Let $(\varphi_p)_{p \in \mathbb{Z}}$ be an element. Then for $p \in \mathbb{Z}$ the derivative is given by

\begin{align*} d_{n-1} \circ d_{n} (\varphi_p) =& d_{n-1}(d^D \circ \varphi_p + (-1)^{n+1} \varphi_{p-1} \circ d^C) \\ =& d^{D} \circ d^D \circ \varphi_p + (-1)^{n} d^D \circ \varphi_p \circ d^C + (-1)^{n+1} \cdot d^D \varphi_{p-1} \circ d^C + (-1)^{n+1} \varphi_{p-1} \circ d^C \circ d^C \\ =& 0 + (-1)^{n}(d^D \circ \varphi_p \circ d^C - \cdot d^D \varphi_{p-1} \circ d^C) + 0 \\ =& 0 \end{align*}
