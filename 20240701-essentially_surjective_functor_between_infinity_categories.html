essentially surjective functor between infinity categories

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an infinity functor Then $f$ is said to be essentially surjective, if for all $d \in D_0$ there exists an $c \in \mathcal{C}_0$ such that

\begin{align*} fc \simeq d \end{align*}

are equivalent
