prism operator of a smooth manifold

Definition

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold Then the prism operator is defined as vectorspace-homomorphism

\begin{align*} P: \Omega^k([0,1] \times M) \rightarrow& \Omega^{k-1}(M) \\ \beta \mapsto (P \beta: (v_1,...,v_{k-1} \mapsto \int_{0}^1 \beta(\partial_t, V_1,...,V_{k-1}) \mathrm{d}t) \end{align*}

here

  1. $V_{j} \in T_{t,p}([0,1] \times M)$ denote the tangent vectors which project to $v_j \in T_pM$ under $[0,1] \times M \rightarrow M$
