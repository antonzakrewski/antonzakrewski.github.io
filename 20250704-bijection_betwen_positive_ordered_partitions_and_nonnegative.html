bijection betwen positive ordered partitions and nonnegative

Proposition

Let $n,k \in \mathbb{N}$. Then there exists an bijection between

  1. $(x_1,...,x_k) \in (\mathbb{N}_0)^k$ with $\sum_{i=1}^k x_i = n +k$
  2. $(x_1,...,x_k) \in (\mathbb{N}^+)^k$ with $\sum_{i=1}^k x_i = n$
