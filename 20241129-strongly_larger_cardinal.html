strongly larger regular cardinal

Definition

Let $\kappa,\tau$ be regular cardinals. Then $\kappa$ is strongly larger than $\tau$, or

\begin{align*} \tau << \kappa \end{align*}

if for cardinals $\tau_0 < \tau, \kappa_0 < \tau_0$ it holds that

\begin{align*} \kappa_0^{\tau_0} < \kappa \end{align*}
