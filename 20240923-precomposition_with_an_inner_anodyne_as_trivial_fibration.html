precomposition with an inner anodyne as trivial fibration for an infinity category

Proposition

Let $X,A,B$ be an infinity category, $f: A \rightarrow B$ an inner anodyne Then precomposition on the inner hom

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(B, X) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(A,X) \end{align*}

is a trivial fibration

Proof

corollary of:

  • precomposition with a monomorphism as inner fibration for a functor category into an infinity category
  • inner hom of a monomorphism and inner anodyne as trivial fibration
