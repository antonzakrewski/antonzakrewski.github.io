integral on a closed intervall is bounded operator

Proposition

Let $[a,b]$ be a closed intervall and $X = C([a,b])$ be equipped with the Supremumsnorm. Then

\begin{align*} \int_{a}^b - \mathrm{d}x: C([a,b]) \rightarrow& \mathbb{K} \\ f \mapsto& \int_{a}^b f \mathrm{d}x \end{align*}

is a bounded operator

Proof
