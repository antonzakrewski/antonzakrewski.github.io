pullback preserves open complement

Proposition

Let $Z \subseteq X$ be a closed subscheme with open complement $X \setminus Z$. Then for any map $f: Y \rightarrow X$, the pullback $(X \setminus Z) \times_{X} Y$ is the open complement of the closed subscheme $Y \times_{X} Z$.
