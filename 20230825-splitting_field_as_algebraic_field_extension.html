splitting field as algebraic field extension

Proposition

Let $F$ be a field, $S \subseteq F[X]$ a subset of polynomials, then the splitting field $L$ is an algebraic field extension

Proof

Let $C \subseteq L$ be the integral closure (cf. integral closure of a field extension as intermediate extension). Suppose $f \in S$, then by assumption $f$ splits in $L$ and hence $f = \alpha \cdot \prod (X - \alpha_i)$. Therefore $f(\alpha_i) = 0$ and hence $\alpha_i$ are integral elements.

By minimality of $L$, we conclude that $L = C$
