Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C} \in \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\kappa})$ be kappa presentable, The right adjoint to the categorification is a left adjoint is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathrm{Pr^L}_{\kappa})) \rightarrow& \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{C}) \\ \mathcal{D} \mapsto \mathrm{End}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathbbm{1}) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{End}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathbbm{1})$ is the enriched hom (cf. Motivation: V-module in PrL is a V-enriched category) of the tensor unit
