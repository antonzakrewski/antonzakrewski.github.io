collection of covering sieves

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an essentially small category and $X \in \mathcal{C}$. A collection of covering sieves $\mathrm{Cov}_{\mathcal{T}}(X) \subseteq \mathrm{Sieve}(X)$ is <subset> of the collection of sieves $\mathrm{Sieve}(X)$ such that

  1. Base change of a covering sieve
  2. local character of a covering sieve
  3. identity of a covering sieve
