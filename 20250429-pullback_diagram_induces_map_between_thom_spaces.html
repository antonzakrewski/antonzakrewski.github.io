pullback diagram induces map between thom spaces

Proposition

Let

be a pullback (or even just commutative diagram with spherical fibrations) in Anima, where $B',B$ are connected anima and $\pi$ is a spherical fibration.

Then there exists an induced map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Thom}(\pi) \rightarrow \mathrm{Thom}(\pi') \end{align*}

Proof

follows from the functorality of the cofiber functor and the window lemma for infinity pullbacks showing that the left square is a pullback

and hence $\pi'$ is a spherical fibration of rank $d-1$
