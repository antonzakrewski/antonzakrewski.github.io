underlying graded object functor preserves limits and colimits for a stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category Then the underlying graded object functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{gr}^*: \mathrm{Fil}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Gr}(\mathcal{C}) (X_*) \mapsto (\mathrm{cofib}(X_{n+1} \rightarrow X_{n}) \end{align*}

preserves limits and colimits

Proof

follows from:

  • cofiber functor preserves colimits
  • cofiber functor preserves limits for a stable category
