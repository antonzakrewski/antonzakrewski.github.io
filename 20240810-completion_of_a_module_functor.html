completion of a module functor

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring, $I \subseteq R$ an ideal and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod Then the completion functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \widehat{-}: \mathrm{RMod} \rightarrow& \mathrm{\widehat{R}Mod} \\ M \mapsto& \widehat{M} \\ f \mapsto& \widehat{f} \end{align*}
