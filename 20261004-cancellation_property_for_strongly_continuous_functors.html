cancellation property for strongly continuous functors

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ be a left adjoint between (stable ?) presentable categories and assume that $\mathcal{F}_i: \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ are strongly continuous functors, such that the the union of the images of $\mathcal{F}_i$ generates $\mathcal{D}$ and the composition $\mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ is strongly continuous. Then $\mathcal{G}^R: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ is also strongly continuous.

Proof

Let $\mathcal{F}_i^R: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_i$ be the right adjoints, then by assumption, $\mathcal{D} \rightarrow \prod \mathcal{C}_i$ is conservative (cf. image generating a presentable category under colimits and conservative adjoint) and colimit preserving ($\mathcal{F}_i$ is strongly continuous). Now we have to show that $\mathcal{G}^R: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is colimit preserving and there it suffices to observe that $\mathcal{E} \rightarrow \prod \mathcal{C}_i$ does preserve colimits and $\mathcal{D} \rightarrow \prod \mathcal{C}_i$ does detect colimits (cf. conservative functor reflects preserved colimits).
