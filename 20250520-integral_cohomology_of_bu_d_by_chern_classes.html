integral cohomology of BU(d) by chern classes

Proposition

There exist unique classes - the chern classes - $c_d \in H^{2d}(\mathrm{BU}; \mathbb{Z})$ whose restrictions to $H^{2d}(\mathrm{BU}(d); \mathbb{Z})$ are given by the euler class of the <universal complex bundle>. Furthermore the map

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Z}[c_1,...,c_d] \rightarrow H^*(\mathrm{BU}(d); \mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism on singular cohomology
