classification of prime ideals in the polynomial ring over a pid

Proposition

Let \(R\) be an principal ideal domain, $R[X]$ the polynomial ring. Then $\mathrm{Spec}(R)$ is given as union of

  1. $(0)$
  2. $(p)$ for a prime $p \in R$
  3. $(f)$ for an irreducible polynomial $f$
  4. $(p,f)$ for a prime $p \in R$ and a polynomial $f$ which is irreducible in $R/(p)[X]$

Proof
