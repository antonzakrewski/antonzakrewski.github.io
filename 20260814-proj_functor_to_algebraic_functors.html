Proj functor to algebraic functors

Definition

Let $\mathrm{CAlg}^{\mathbb{N}, \textrm{es}}$ be the category of ℕ-graded rings and eventually surjective maps. Then the $\mathrm{Proj}$ functor is defined on objects as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Proj}(-): \mathrm{CAlg}^{\mathbb{N}, \mathrm{es}, \mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}, \mathrm{Set}) \\ A \mapsto& \mathrm{Proj}(A) \end{align*}

For an eventually surjective map $a: A \rightarrow B$ of N-graded rings, we define a natural transformation $a: \mathrm{Proj}(A) \rightarrow \mathrm{Proj}(B)$ as follows: for $(L,\varphi) \in \mathrm{Proj}_d(B)$, there exist $b_1,...,b_r \in B_d$ whose images by $\varphi_d$ generate $L$. Then there exists some $n \geq 1$ such that $b_1^n, ...,b_r^n$ lies in the image of $a_{nd}: A_{nd} \otimes_{A_0} B_0 \rightarrow B_{nd}$ and $(L^{\otimes n}, \varphi^{(n)} \circ a^{(nd)})$ is an element in $\mathrm{Proj}_{nd}(A)$.

Hence precomposition with $\alpha$ defines a map $\mathrm{Proj}(a): \mathrm{Proj}(B) \rightarrow \mathrm{Proj}(B)$ on colimits.
