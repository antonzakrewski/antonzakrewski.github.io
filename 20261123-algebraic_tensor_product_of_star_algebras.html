algebraic tensor product of star algebras

Definition

Let $A,B \in *-\mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbb{C}}^{\mathrm{nu}}$. Then the

\begin{align*} A \otimes^{\mathrm{alg}}_{\mathbb{C}} B \end{align*}

is the usual tensor product for nonunital algebra, with the star structure

\begin{align*} (a \otimes b)^* = (a^* \otimes b^*) \end{align*}
