pullback of a cofinal map along a left fibration

Proposition

Let $p$ be a left fibration, $i$ be cofinal

then $j$ is also cofinal

Proof

a)

factor $i$ as right anodyne followed by trivial fibration

  • infinity cofinal right fibration as trivial fibration

b) saturatedness

the set of right anodyne, such that the pullback along any left fibration is cofinal, is saturated

c)

the maps

\begin{align*} \{1\} \times \Delta^n \cup \Delta^1 \times \partial \Delta^n \rightarrow \Delta^1 \times \Delta^n \end{align*}

lies in this

Do this by arguing about

\begin{align*} \{1\} \times K \rightarrow \Delta^n \times K \end{align*}

which is a right deformation retract.

Then the pullback along it is a right deformation retract
