Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be stable categories equipped with a bounded t-structure. Suppose that $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is a t-exact functor, which is fully faithful on the heart Then $\mathcal{F}$ is in general not fully faithful

Proof

consider the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathcal{D}^b(\mathbb{Z}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}^{b} \end{align*}
