extending local properties of rings to schemes

Proposition

Let $P$ be a local property of a ring. Then $P$ extends uniquely to a property on schemes, where we say that a scheme $X$ satisfies $P$ if and only if there exists an open cover with affine schemes, whose coordinate rings satisfy the property.
