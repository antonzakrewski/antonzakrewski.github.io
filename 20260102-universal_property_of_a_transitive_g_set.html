universal property of a transitive G-Set

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group, $X$ a transitive g-set. Then (after choosing an isomorphism $X \cong G/H$) there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{G-Set}}(G/H,Y) \cong& Y^H \end{align*}

Proof

a map $\varphi$ from $G/H$ is deterimned by $\varphi(1)$
