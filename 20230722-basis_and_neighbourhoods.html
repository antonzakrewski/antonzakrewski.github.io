basis and neighbourhoods

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space and $\mathcal{B} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)$. TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{B}$ is a basis
  2. for each open set $O \in \mathcal{T}$ and each $x \in O$ there exists an $O_x \in \mathcal{B}$ with $x \in O_x \subseteq O$

Proof
