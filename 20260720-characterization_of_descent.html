characterization of descent for a 1-category

Proposition

Let $R$ be a sieve on $X$ and let $(Y_i \rightarrow X)$ be a family of maps generating $R$. Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ be a 1-categorical presheaf. TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ satisfies descent
  2. the canonical map
\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(X) \rightarrow \lim_{Y \in \mathrm{El}(R)} \mathcal{F}(Y) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism

  1. the diagram
\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(X) \rightarrow \prod_{i \in I} \mathcal{F}(Y_i) \rightrightarrows \prod_{i,j \in I} \mathrm{Hom}(よ(Y_i) \times_{よ(X)} よ(Y_j), \mathcal{F}) \end{align*}

is an equalizer

should be only \((1,1)\)-categorical

Proof

here we use that $R$ is the colimit - either by presentation of sieves or by 1-presheaf category arises by freely adding colimits as colimit over its unstraightening. Then we can use that $R \cong \mathrm{colim}_{\textrm{something}} よ(Y_i)$ and use that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(X) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(X, \mathcal{F}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(R, \mathcal{F}) \end{align*}

is an iso

Remark

If the pullbacks in 3) exist, then we can write

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}(よ(Y_i) \times_{よ(X)} よ(Y_j), \mathcal{F}) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}(Y_i \times_{X}Y_j, \mathcal{F}) \\ \cong& \mathcal{F}(Y_i \times_{X}Y_j) \end{align*}
