tensor functor in general not left exact

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring, $K$ an \(R\)-module and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod. Then the tensoring functor is in general not left exact

Proof

Let $R = \mathbb{Z}$ and consider

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow& \mathbb{Z} \\ n \mapsto 2 \cdot n \end{align*}

Then tensoring with $\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}$ results in

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Z} \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow (a \otimes b) \mapsto& (2a, b) = 2 \cdot (a \otimes b) = (a \otimes 2b) = (a \otimes 0) = 0 \end{align*}

Furthermore,

\begin{align*} \vert \mathbb{Z} \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} \vert = 2 \end{align*}

as $1 \otimes 1 \neq 0$
