standard open for an an affine variety

Definition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be an affine variety. Then for an element in the coordinate ring $f \in \mathcal{A}(X)$ the standard open is deined as

\begin{align*} D(f) \coloneqq X \setminus Z(f) = \{P \in X \vert f(P) \neq 0\} \subseteq X \end{align*}
