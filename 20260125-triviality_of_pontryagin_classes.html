triviality of pontryagin classes

Proposition

Let $\pi: B \rightarrow \mathrm{BO}(k) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{BO}$ be a \(k\)-dimensional topological vector bundle. Then $p_i(\pi) = 0 \in H^{4i}(B,\mathbb{Z})$ for $i > \frac{k}{2}$

"pontryagin classes see homology up to double of the dimension of the vector bundle"
