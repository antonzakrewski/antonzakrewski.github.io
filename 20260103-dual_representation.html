dual representation

Definition

Let $\mathcal{F}: BG \rightarrow \mathrm{Vect}_K$ be a representation Then the dual representation is given by the composition

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}^*: \mathrm{BG} \xrightarrow{(-)^{-1}} \mathrm{BG}^{\mathrm{op}} \xrightarrow{\mathcal{F}} \mathrm{Vect}_K^{\mathrm{op}} \xrightarrow{(-)^*} \mathrm{Vect}_K \end{align*}

where the first map is given by the equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{BG}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{BG} g \mapsto g^{-1} \end{align*}
