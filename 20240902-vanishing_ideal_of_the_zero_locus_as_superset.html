vanishing ideal of the zero locus as superset

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq K[X_1,...,X_n]$ be a subset. Then

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a} \subseteq I(Z(\mathfrak{a})) \end{align*}

see:

  • zero locus of an affine space
  • vanishing ideal

Proof

Let $f \in \mathfrak{a}$. Then it follows by definition for each $x \in Z(\mathfrak{a})$ that $f(x) = 0$. Hence $f \in I(Z(\mathfrak{a}))$
