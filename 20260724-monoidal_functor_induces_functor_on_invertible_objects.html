monoidal functor induces functor on invertible objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor between (symmetric) monoidal categories. Then $\mathcal{F}$ induces a functor on the full subcategory of invertible objects

Proof

follows as $\mathcal{F}$ maps invertible objects to invertible objects
