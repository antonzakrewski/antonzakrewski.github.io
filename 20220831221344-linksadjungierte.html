left adjoint

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ resp. $\mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be functors Then $\mathcal{L}$ is said to be a left adjoint, if there exists an adjunction

\begin{align*} \varphi: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(L(-), -) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, \mathcal{R}(-)) \end{align*}
