pointwise construction of infinity right adjoints

Proposition

Let $g: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor between infinity categories. TFAE:

  1. $f \dashv g$ is part of an infinity adjunction
  2. the infinity functor
\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(d,g(-)) \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\overline{d},-) \end{align*}

is representable for each $d \in \mathcal{D}$, that is it admits objectwise a right adjoint object
