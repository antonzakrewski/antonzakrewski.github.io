nilpotent element in the power series ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $A[ [X]]$ the power series ring and $f = \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} \alpha_i X^i$ a nilpotent element. Then $\alpha_n$ is nilpotent for arbitrary $i \in \mathbb{N}$

Note that the converse does not hold (zu schnell wachsende minimale $n$ ?)

Proof

Suppose

\begin{align*} f^m = 0 \end{align*}

then we conclude, that for smallest $i \in \mathbb{N}_0$ with $\alpha_i = 0$, it holds

\begin{align*} \alpha_i^m = 0 \end{align*}

as the \(i \cdot m\)-th component is zero.
