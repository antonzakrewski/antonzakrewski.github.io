group of unit functor for Mon

Definition

Given category Mon and category Group, the group of unit functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Unit}: \mathrm{Mon} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Grp} \\ M \mapsto& M^{\times} \\ (\varphi: M \rightarrow N) \mapsto& \varphi_{\vert M^\times}: M^\times \rightarrow N^{\times} \end{align*}
