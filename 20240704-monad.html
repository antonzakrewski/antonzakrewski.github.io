1-monad

Definition

A monad is a monoid object in the category of endofunctors $\mathrm{End}(\mathcal{C})$ for a category $\mathcal{C}$

Explicite

using the strict monoidal structure on $\mathrm{End}(\mathcal{C})$

A monad is given by

  1. an endofunctor $T: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$
  2. natural transformations:

a) $\mathrm{id} \Rightarrow \mathcal{T}$ b) $\mathcal{T} \circ \mathcal{T} \Rightarrow \mathcal{T}$
