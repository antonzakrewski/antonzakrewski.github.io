osgoods lemma

Proposition

Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{C}^n$ be an open subset and $f: U \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ a map TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a multivariate holomorphic map between affine subsets
  2. for each coordinate $i \in \{1,...,n\}$ the composition $\mathbb{C} \times 0^{n-1} \xrightarrow{\textrm{i-th component}} \mathbb{C}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ is holomorphic

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows as the composition of holomorphic maps is holomorphic.
