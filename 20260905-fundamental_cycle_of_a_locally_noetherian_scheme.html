fundamental cycle of a locally noetherian scheme

Definition

Let $X$ be a locally noetherian scheme. The fundamental cycle of $X$ is the codimension $0$ cycle

\begin{align*} \vert X \vert = \sum_{x} \mathrm{length}(\mathcal{O}_{x,x}) \cdot x \in Z^0(\vert X \vert, \mathbb{N}) \end{align*}

Remark

Observe that $X$ has locally finite amonut of points of codimension $0$ (cf. finite amount of irreducible components of a noetherian scheme).
