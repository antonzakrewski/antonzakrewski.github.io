projection map of the mapping cylinder

Definition

Let $(X,\mathcal{T}_X)$ and $(Y,\mathcal{T}_Y)$ be topological spaces and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a continuous map. Then the projection from the mapping cylinder $\mathrm{Cyl}(f)$

\begin{align*} \mathrm{pr}: \mathrm{Cyl}(f) \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

is defined as pushout

where $f$ is the induced map from the definition of $\mathrm{Cyl}(f)$ as pushout
