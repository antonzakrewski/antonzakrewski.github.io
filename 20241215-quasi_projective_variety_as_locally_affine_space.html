quasi projective variety as locally affine space

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ a quasi-projective variety Then for each point $x \in \mathbb{P}_K^n$ there exists an open neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ and an isomorphism $U \cong Y \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^m$ (cf. affine space)
