infinity slice category of a presentable category as presentable category

Proposition

Let $K$ be a small simplicial set and $\mathcal{C}$ a presentable infinity category and $p: K \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a morphism of simplicial sets Then the right slice

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}_{p/} \end{align*}

is also presentable
