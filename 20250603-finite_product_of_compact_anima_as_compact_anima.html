finite product of compact anima as compact anima

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be compact anima. Then so is $X \times Y$

Proof

a)

reduction

Note that by compact anima as retract of finite anima there exists some finite anima $\overline{X}, \overline{Y}$ such that $X,Y$ are retracts. In particular $X \times Y$ is a retract of $\overline{X} \times \overline{Y}$

finite anima

so w.l.o.g. assume that $X,Y$ are finite.

Then

\begin{align*} X \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{X} * \\ Y \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{Y} * \\ \end{align*}

where $X,Y$ are finite simplicial set Hence

\begin{align*} X \times Y \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{X \times Y} * \end{align*}

where $X\times Y$ is also a finite simplicial set (cf. finite product of finite simplicial sets as finite simplicial set)

b)

using the anima as cartesian closed category we get for a filtered colimit $\mathrm{colim} I(-)$

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X \times Y, \mathrm{colim} I(-)) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X, \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(Y,\mathrm{colim} I(-))) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X, \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(Y, I(-))) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X, \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(Y, I(-))) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X \times Y, I(-)) \end{align*}
