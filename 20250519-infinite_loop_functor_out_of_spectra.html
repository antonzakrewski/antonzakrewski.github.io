infinite loop functor out of spectra

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category. Then the infinite loop functor

\begin{align*} \Omega^{\infty}: \mathrm{Sp}(\mathcal{C}_*) \rightarrow& \mathcal{C}_* \\ \Omega^{\infty}: \mathrm{Sp}(\mathcal{C}_*) \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ \end{align*}

is defined as evaluation functor at $S^0$ when regarding $\mathrm{Sp}(\mathcal{C}_*)$ as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{red}, \mathrm{exc}}(\mathrm{FinAni}_*, \mathcal{C}_*) \end{align*}

remark

one is defined as forgetting to pointed objects; the other to just $\mathcal{C}$
