category of quasi-coherent modules over an algebraic functor

Definition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor. Then the category of quasi-coherent modules $\mathrm{Mod}_X$ is defined as image of the functor of quasi-coherent objects for the restriction functor from commutative rings to modules in Ab

\begin{align*} \mathrm{res}: \mathrm{CAlg}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat} \end{align*}

i.e. $\mathcal{C} = \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{op}}$
