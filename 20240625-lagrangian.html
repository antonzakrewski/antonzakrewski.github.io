lagrangian of a bilinear form

Definition

Let $\varphi: V \otimes V \rightarrow K$ be a non degenerate symmetric bilinear form for $V$ a $2n$ dimensional vector space. Then $\varphi$ is said to admit a lagrangian, if there exists an \(n\)-dimensional subspace $L \subseteq V$ such that

\begin{align*} \varphi_{V \otimes V} = 0 \end{align*}
