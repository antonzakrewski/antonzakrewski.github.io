infinity cartesian monoidal structure via adjoint

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category which admits finite infinity product. Then consider the functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(-,\mathcal{C}) \times_{\mathcal{C}} \{*\}: \mathrm{Fin}_*^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty} \\ (I,*) \mapsto& \mathrm{Fun}(I,\mathcal{C}) \times_{\mathrm{Fun}(*,\mathcal{C})} \{*\} \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(I, \mathcal{C}) \\ \end{align*}

where $\{*\}$ is the terminal category.

Then using infinity right kan extension formula & adjoints restrict to full subcategories this is a functor which takes values in adjoints, i.e.

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(-, \mathcal{C}): \mathrm{Fin}_{*}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}^{L} \end{align*}

Then using the anti-equivalence of CatL and CatR this is equivalently given by a functor

\begin{align*} \prod: \mathrm{Fin}_* \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}^R \\ <n> \mapsto& \prod_{i=1}^n \mathcal{C} \\ \mbox{} * \mapsto& * \\ \end{align*}

which maps by inspection

\begin{align*} \prod_{<n>^{\circ}} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ ((c_1,...,c_n),*) \mapsto& \prod_{i=1}^n c_i \end{align*}

Proof
