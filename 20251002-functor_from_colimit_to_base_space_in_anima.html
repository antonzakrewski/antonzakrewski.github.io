functor from colimit to base space in anima

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ be a functor, then there exists a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim} f \rightarrow X \end{align*}

where we need to invert some morphisms in $\mathcal{C}$ - so in particular inverting all suffices.
