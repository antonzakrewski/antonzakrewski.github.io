spectrum of a ring as compact space

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ the zariski spectrum with the Zariski topology. Then $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ is a compact space.

Proof

Let $\mathcal{V}(\mathfrak{a}_i)$ be a family of closed sets such that

\begin{align*} \bigcap \mathcal{V}(\mathfrak{a}_i) = \emptyset \end{align*}

Then by compact space and intersection of closed sets it suffices to show that finitely many $\mathcal{V}(\mathfrak{a}_i)$ suffice.

Then by vanishing set of an intersection of ideals we get

\begin{align*} \mathcal{V}(A) =& \emptyset \\ =& \bigcap \mathcal{V}(\mathfrak{a}_i) \\ =& \mathcal{V}(\sum \mathfrak{a}_i) \end{align*}

Hence there exists a finite sum

\begin{align*} \sum \lambda_i \alpha_i \in \sum_{i=1}^n \mathfrak{a}_i \end{align*}

and hence

\begin{align*} \bigcap_{i=1}^n \mathcal{V}(\mathfrak{a}_i) = \emptyset \end{align*}
