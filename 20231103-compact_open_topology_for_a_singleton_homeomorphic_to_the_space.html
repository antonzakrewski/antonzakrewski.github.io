compact open topology for a singleton homeomorphic to the space

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space. Then the mapping space $\mathcal{C}(*,X)$ with the compact open topology for the singleton $*$ is homeomorphic to $X$ for

\begin{align*} \varphi: X \rightarrow& \mathcal{C}(*,X) \\ x \mapsto& (f: * \mapsto x) \end{align*}

Proof

Homeomorphismus als stetig abgeschlossene/offene Bijektion

welldefined

  • constant map is continuous

bijective

follows from the evident Inverse map

\begin{align*} \varphi^{-1}: (f) \mapsto f(*) \end{align*}

continuous

continuous map and subbasis Let $\mathcal{O}_{K,U} \subseteq \mathcal{C}(*,X)$ be open. Then it follows

\begin{align*} \varphi^{-1}[\mathcal{O}_{K,U}] =& \varphi^{-1}[\mathcal{O}_{*,U}] \\ =& \varphi^{-1}[\{f \in \mathcal{C}(*,X) \vert f(*) \in U\}] \\ =& \{x \in X \vert \varphi(x)(*) \in U \} \\ =& \{x \in X \vert x \in U \} =& U \end{align*}

open map

Suppose $O$ is open, then

\begin{align*} \varphi[O] =& \{f \in \mathcal{C}(*,X) \vert f(*) \in O\} \\ =& \mathcal{O}_{*, O} \end{align*}

hence $\varphi[O]$ is open
