mixed model structure

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a bicomplete 1-category and $(C_q,W_q,F_q)$ and $(C_h,W_h,F_h)$ be model structures with

\begin{align*} F_h \subseteq& F_q \\ W_h \subseteq& W_q \\ \end{align*}

and hence necessarily $C_q \subseteq C_h$

Then there exists a mixed model structure $(C_m,W_q,F_h)$ on $M$ where

  1. the fibrations are $F_h$
  2. the weak equivalences are $W_q$
  3. the cofibrations are given by a composition

as composition of

  1. \(q\)-cofibration $\iota$
  2. \(h\)-equivalence $h$

which is also an \(h\)-cofibration

Proof
