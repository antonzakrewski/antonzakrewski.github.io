restriction of a ring homomorphism to the image as ring homomorphism

Proposition

Let \(R,S\) be rings, $\varphi: M \rightarrow N$ a ring-homomorphism and $L \subseteq N$ a subring such that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{im}(\varphi) \subseteq L \end{align*}

Then the map

\begin{align*} \varphi': M \rightarrow L \\ m \mapsto& \varphi(m) \end{align*}

is a welldefined ring homomorphism

Proof

Properties of the ring homomorphism are inherited by $\varphi'$, hence it follows from the definitions
