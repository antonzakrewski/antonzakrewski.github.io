sixth homotopy group of the 3-sphere

Proposition

There exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \pi_6(S^3) \cong \mathbb{Z}/12 \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

Proof

using homology of the truncation of a sphere and the serre spectral sequence

\begin{align*} K(\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}, 5) \rightarrow \tau_{\leq 5} S^3 \rightarrow \tau_{\leq 4} S^3 \end{align*}
