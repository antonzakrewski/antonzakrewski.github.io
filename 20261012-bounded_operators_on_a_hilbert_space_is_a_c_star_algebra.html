bounded operators on a hilbert space is a C star algebra

Proposition

Let $H$ be a Hilbert space and $B(\mathcal{H})$ the space of bounded operators. Then $B(\mathcal{H})$ is a unital C-star Algebra for the antiinvolution given by taking the hilbert adjoint.

Riesz-representation for hilbert spaces

$C^*$ identity

we need to show that for any operator $a$, $\| a \cdot a^* \| = \| a \|$. For this recall that $\| a \| = \| a^* \|$ by riesz-rep.

Hence the operatorinequality shows $\| a \cdot a^* \| \leq \| a \| \cdot \| a^*\| = \| a \|^2$.

Furthermore, we have

\begin{align*} \| a \|^2 =& \mathrm{sup}_{\| x \| = 1} \langle ax, ax\rangle \\ =& \mathrm{sup}_{\| x \| = 1} \langle a^* a x, x\rangle \\ =& \mathrm{sup}_{\| x \| = 1} \| a^* a \| \cdot \langle x, x\rangle \\ =& \| a^* a \| \end{align*}
