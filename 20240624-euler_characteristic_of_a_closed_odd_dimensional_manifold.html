euler characteristic of a closed odd dimensional manifold vanishes

Proposition

Let \(M\) be a closed \(2n+1\)-dimensional manifold. Then the euler characteristic is $0$

Proof

Let $\sigma_M$ be the unique \(\mathbb{F}_2\)-orientation. Using euler characteristic and f2 betti numbers we get

\begin{align*} b_{p}(X;\mathbb{F}_2) = \mathrm{dim}_{\mathbb{F}_2}(H_p(X;\mathbb{F}_2) \end{align*}

and using the poincare duality gives

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}_{\mathbb{F}_2}(H_p(X;\mathbb{F}_2) =& \mathrm{dim}_{\mathbb{F}_2}(H^{n-p}(X;\mathbb{F}_2)) \\ \end{align*}

and universal coefficient theorem for singular homology

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}_{\mathbb{F}_2}(H^{n-p}(X;\mathbb{F}_2)) =& \mathrm{dim}_{\mathbb{F}_2}(H_{n-p}(X, \mathbb{F}_2) \\ =& b_{n-p}(X;\mathbb{F}_2) \end{align*}

Therefore

\begin{align*} \chi_{X} =& \sum_{p=0}^n (-1)^p b_{p}(X;\mathbb{F}_2) \\ =& \sum_{p=0}^{ \frac{n-1}{2} - 1} (-1)^p b_{p(X; \mathbb{F}_2) + \sum_{p = \frac{n-1}{2}}^n (-1)^p b_p(X;\mathbb{F}_2) \\ =& \sum_{p= \frac{n-1}{2} -1}^n (-1)^{n-p} b_{n-p}(X ; \mathbb{F}_2) + =& 0 \end{align*}
