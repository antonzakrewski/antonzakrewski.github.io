kappa small abelian groups is a derived serre ring

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal. Then the category of $\kappa$ small abelian groups is a derived serre ring.

Proof

  • kappa small abelian groups is a serre class
  • tensor product of kappa small modules is kappa small
  • tor of kappa small modules is kappa small
  • projective resolution of a kappa small module is kappa small
