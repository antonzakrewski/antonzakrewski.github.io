equivalence as injection in an exact infinity category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an exact infinity category and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ an equivalence. Then $f \in \mathrm{inj}(\mathcal{C})$ is an injection.

Proof

follows from the pushout (cf. square with parallel equivalences as infinity pushout)
