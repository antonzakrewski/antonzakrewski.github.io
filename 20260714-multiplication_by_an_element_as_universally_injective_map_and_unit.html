multiplication by an element as universally injective map and unit

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $f \in R$ an element. TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a unit
  2. multiplication by $f$ is a universally injective map

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

split mono is universally injective

2) $\implies$ 1)

Assume that multiplication by $f$ is universally injective. Then consider the commutative ring $R/(f)$ and the morphism

\begin{align*} \cdot f: R \otimes_{R} R/(f) \xrightarrow{\cdot f} R \otimes_{R} R/(f) \end{align*}

is an injection. This shows that $0 = 1$, since $1 \cdot f = 0 = 0 \cdot f$ and $\cdot f$ is injective, hence $1 = 0$. This shows that $R \otimes_{R} R/(f) \simeq 0$ is the zero ring (cf. zero as unit and zero ring).
