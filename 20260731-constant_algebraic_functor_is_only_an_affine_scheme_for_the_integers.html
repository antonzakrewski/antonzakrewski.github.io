constant algebraic functor is only an affine scheme for the integers

Proposition

The constant functor $\mathrm{CAlg} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ is only an affine scheme for $\mathrm{const}_* \simeq \mathrm{Spec}(0)$.

Proof

use that $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{CAlg}}(R,0) \cong *$ and $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{CAlg}}(0,-) \cong \mathrm{const}_*$
