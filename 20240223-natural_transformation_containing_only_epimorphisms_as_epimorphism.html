natural transformation containing only epimorphisms as epimorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ the functor category Suppose $\eta \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$ is a natural transformation, such that $\eta(A)$ is an epimorphism for all $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$

Then $\eta$ is an epimorphism in $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$
