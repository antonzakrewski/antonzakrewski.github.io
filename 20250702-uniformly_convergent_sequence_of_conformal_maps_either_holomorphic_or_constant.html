uniformly convergent sequence of conformal maps either holomorphic or constant

Proposition

Let $\Omega \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be open and connected and $f_n$ a sequence of conformal maps that uniformly converges on compact subsets of $\Omega$ to a function $f$. Then $f$ is either constant or also bijective (and hence conformal)

Proof

Assume $f$ is nonconstant. Let $z_0 \in J\Omega$ such that $f$ has a zero of multiplicity $k \geq 1$ at $z_0$. Then there exists an $\varepsilon' > 0$ such that for any $\varepsilon > 0$ with $\varepsilon < \varepsilon'$ there is an $N > 0$ such that for $n \geq N$ f_n$ has exactly $k$ zeros in $D_{\varepsilon}(z_0)$.
