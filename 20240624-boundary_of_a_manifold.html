boundary of a manifold with boundary

Definition

Let \(M\) be a \(n\)-dimensional topological manifold with boundary. Then the boundary $\partial M$ of $M$ is defined as set of points $m$ such that there exists a chart $U \overset{\varphi}{\cong} \mathbb{R}^n \times \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$ with $\varphi(m) \in \mathbb{R}^n \times [0]$
