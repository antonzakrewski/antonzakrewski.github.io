equivalent characterization of an S-local module spectrum

Proposition

Let $R$ be an (\mathbb{E}_1)-ring spectrum and $S \subseteq \pi_*(R)$ be a homogeneous subset satisfying the left ore condition. Let $M$ be a module spectrum

TFAE:

  1. $M$ is an S-local module spectrum
  2. for every $s \in S$ and $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ the mapping anima $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(R)}(R/Rs[n], N)$ is contractible, see: R/Rs of a module spectrum
  3. for each S-nilpotent module spectrum the mapping anima $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(R)}(M,N)$ is contractible

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 3)

3) $\implies$ 1)
