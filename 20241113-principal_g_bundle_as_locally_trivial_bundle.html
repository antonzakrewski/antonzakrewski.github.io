principal g-bundle as locally trivial bundle

Proposition

Let $G$ be a topological group $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a principal g-bundle. Then $p$ is locally a trivial principal g-bundle.

Proof

follows from the definition, as for any point $e \in E$ we may choose an open neighbourhood $U \ni p(e)$ and an \(G\)-equivariant isomorphism

\begin{align*} U \times G \cong p^{-1}[U] \end{align*}
