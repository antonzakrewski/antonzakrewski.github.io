map of spectral sequneces induces a map of transgressive pairs

Proposition

Let $E,E'$ be spectral sequences of \(R\)-modules and $\varphi: E \rightarrow E'$ a morphism of spectral sequences. Then $\varphi$ restricts to a group-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: \mathrm{trans}(E) \rightarrow \mathrm{trans}(E') \end{align*}
