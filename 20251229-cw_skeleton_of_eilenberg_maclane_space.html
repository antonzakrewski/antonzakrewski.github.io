CW skeleton of an eilenberg-maclane space as suspension of one lower

Proposition

Let $A$ be an abelian group and $K(A,n+1)$ the Eilenberg-MacLane space Then there exists a cw structure on $K(A,n+1)$ such that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sk}_{2n+1}(K(A,n+1)) = \Sigma K(A,n) \end{align*}

Proof

use freudenthal suspension theorem to get that the map

\begin{align*} K(A,n) \rightarrow \Omega \Sigma K(A,n) \end{align*}

is \(2n-1\)-connected map, in particular $\pi_{2n}(K(A,n)) = 0$.

So by cellular approximation type arguments we only have to attach \(2n+2\)-cells. This shows that $\mathrm{Sk}_{2n+1}(K(A,n+1))$ can be chosen to be $\Sigma K(A,n)$ (by the regular attaching argument)
