complex conjugation of a vector bundle and orientation reversing

Proposition

Let

\begin{align*} X \rightarrow \mathrm{B}U(n) \end{align*}

be a complex topological vector bundle.

Then the conjugation on the underlying real vector bundle of a complex vector bundle corresponds to

  1. the identity if $n$ is even
  2. the orientation reversing of a real vector bundle if $n$ is odd

Proof
