(pseudo-)inclusion of Delta to FinFree Groups

Definition

The pseudo inclusion of simplex category into the category of finitely generated free groups

\begin{align*} \Delta \rightarrow& \mathrm{FinFreeGrp} \\ [n] \mapsto& F_n \\ (f: [n] \rightarrow [m]) \rightarrow& (g_i \mapsto \begin{cases} 1 & \mbox{if } f(i) = f(0) \\ g_{f(i)} & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
