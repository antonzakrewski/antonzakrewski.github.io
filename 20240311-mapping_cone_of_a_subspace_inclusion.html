mapping cylinder of a subspace inclusion homotopy equivalent

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $\iota: A \rightarrow X$ a subspace. Then the mapping cylinder is homotopy equivalent to $X$, i.e.

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cyl}(\iota) \cong X \end{align*}

as homotopic maps of pairs

\begin{align*} \pi: (\mathrm{Cyl}(\iota), X) \rightarrow& (X,X) \\ \end{align*}

Proof

Consider the maps

\begin{align*} \iota: X \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cyl}(\iota) \\ x \mapsto& (x,1) \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} \pi: \mathrm{Cyl}(\iota) \rightarrow& X \\ [a,t] \mapsto& \iota(a) \\ [x,1] \mapsto& x \end{align*}
