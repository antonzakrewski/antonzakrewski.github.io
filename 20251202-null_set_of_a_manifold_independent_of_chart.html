null set of a manifold independent of atlas

Proposition

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold, $\mathcal{A}$ an Atlas and $A \subseteq M$ a subset Suppose $U_i$ is an open cover by charts such that for any $\varphi: \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow M$ in $\mathcal{A}$ the subset

\begin{align*} \varphi^{-1}[A] \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n \end{align*}

is nullset

Then $A$ is a nullset.

Proof

Let $\psi: \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow M$ be any chart. w.l.o.g. we may choose a countable cover of $M$ by charts $\varphi_i$ - so in particular a countable cover of $\mathrm{im}(\psi)$ given by $U_i \cap \mathrm{im}(\psi)$.

We want to show that $\psi^{-1}[U_i]$ is a nullset in $\mathbb{R}^n$ - if we manage to do, then $\psi^{-1}[M] = \bigcup \psi^{-1}[U_i]$ is a countable union of nullsets - so in particular also a nullset.
