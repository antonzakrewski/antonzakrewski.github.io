transposition of ferres diagram as bijection of unordered partitions

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{P}_{n}$ be the set of unordered partitions of $n$ with arbitrary many summands Then the Transposition of a ferres diagram

\begin{align*} \mathcal{P}_{n} \rightarrow \mathcal{P}_{n} p \mapsto p^{t} \end{align*}

is a Bijection
