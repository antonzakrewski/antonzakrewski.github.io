integral cohomology of U(n)

Proposition

There exists an isomorphism of rings

\begin{align*} H^*(\mathrm{SU}(n); \mathbb{Z}) \cong \Lambda_{\mathbb{Z}}[e_1,e_3,...,e_{2n-1}] \end{align*}

Proof

Proof by induction

induction basis

for $n = 1$, we get $U(n) \cong S^1$ and hence singular cohomology of a sphere shows the claim

induction step

Recall that there exists a fiber sequence

\begin{align*} U(n) \rightarrow U(n+1) \rightarrow S^{2n +1} \end{align*}

and hence apply the multiplicative cohomological serre spectral sequence
