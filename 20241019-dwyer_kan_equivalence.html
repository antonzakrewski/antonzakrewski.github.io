dwyer-kan equivalence

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be simplicial categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an enriched functor. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be dwyer-kan equivalence if

  1. the induced functor on homotopy categories is essentially surjective
  2. for each $x,y$ the map
\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_{x,y}: \mathcal{C}(x,y) \rightarrow D(\mathcal{F}(x), \mathcal{F}(y)) \end{align*}

is a weak equivalence
