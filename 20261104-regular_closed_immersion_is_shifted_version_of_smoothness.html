regular closed immersion is shifted version of smoothness

Proposition

Let $\iota:Z \hookrightarrow X$ be a <finitely presented> closed immersion TFAE:

  1. $\iota$ is a regular closed immersion
  2. $\mathcal{L}_{Z/X}[-1]$ is locally free
  3. $\mathcal{N}_{Z/X}$ is locally free and the $H_i(\mathcal{L}_{Z/X}) = 0$ for $i \geq 2$
