inner fibration induces kan fibration on right mapping space

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $p: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a inner fibration Let $x,y \in \mathcal{C}_0$, then the <induced map>

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(p(x), p(y)) \end{align*}

is a kan fibration on the right mapping space

Proof
