fully faithful inclusion of presentably monoidal categories into StRing

Proposition

The functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{CAlg}(1\mathrm{Pr}) \rightarrow \mathrm{StRing} = \mathrm{colim}_{\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\kappa}}(\mathrm{CAlg}(n\mathrm{Pr})) \end{align*}

given by the inclusion is fully faithful

i.e., presentably symmetric monoidal categories

Proof

follows from:

  1. category StRing is limit
  2. limit of fully faithful functors is fully faithful
  3. fully faithful inclusion of nPr into n+1Pr
