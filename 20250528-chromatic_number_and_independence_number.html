chromatic number and independence number

Proposition

Let $G = (V,E)$ be a graph. Then

\begin{align*} \chi(G) \geq \frac{\vert V \vert}{\alpha(G)} \end{align*}

see:

  • chromatic number of a graph
  • independence number of a graph

Proof

Let $\iota$ denote the optimal coloring follows as

\begin{align*} \iota^{-1}[1],...,\iota^{-1}[\chi(G)] \end{align*}

are independent by definition of a coloring with $\iota^{-1}[1] \leq \alpha(G)$
