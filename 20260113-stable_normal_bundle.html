stable smooth normal bundle

Definition

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold. Then the stable normal bundle $\nu_{M}$ is defined as composition

\begin{align*} M \xrightarrow{T^s(M)} \mathrm{BO} \xrightarrow{(-)^{-1}} \mathrm{BO} \end{align*}

where

  1. $T^s(M)$ is the stable tangent bundle
  2. $(-)^{-1}$ is the inverse of $\mathrm{BO}$
