tor functor commutes with tensor product

Proposition

Let \(R\) be (commutative) rings and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod and $M,N$ \(R\)-modules. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tor}_n^R(M \otimes N, -) \cong \mathrm{Tor}_n^R(M, N \otimes -) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from

  • associativity of a tensor product
  • natural isomorphism between right exact functors and isomorphism of left derived functors
