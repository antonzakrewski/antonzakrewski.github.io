infinity functor category with groupoid morphisms

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $S \subseteq \mathcal{C}_1$ a subcollection of \(1\)-morphisms. Then the infinity functor category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{S}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

is defined as full sub infinity category of $\mathrm{Fun}^{S}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ containing infinity functors

\begin{align*} f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

such that $f(s)$ is an equivalence in $\mathcal{D}$ for $s \in S$.
