homotopy groups of the tensor product of module spectra over an E_10ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be an E_1 ring spectrum, $\pi_*(R)$ the underlying graded ring of a ring spectrum and $M,N$ module spectra over $R$. Then the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tor}_0^{\pi_*(R)}(\pi_*(M),\pi_*(N)) \rightarrow \pi_*(M \otimes_{R} N) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism of graded abelian groups.
