exact functor between waldhausen infinity categories

Definition

Let $\mathcal{W}, \mathcal{W}'$ be waldhausen infinity categories. Then an exact functor from $\mathcal{W}$ to $\mathcal{W}'$ is defined as

  1. reduced infinity functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{W} \rightarrow \mathcal{W}'$
  2. which restricts
\begin{align*} \mathrm{in}(\mathcal{W}) \rightarrow \mathrm{in}(\mathcal{W}') \end{align*}
  1. and sends pushouts along ingressive morphisms to pushouts
