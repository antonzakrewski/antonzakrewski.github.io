acyclic space is a classifying space of a perfect group

Proposition

Let $X$ be an acyclic space. Then it is equivalent to $B\pi_1(G)$ for a perfect group $G$.

Proof

we need to show the following

  1. $X$ is connected
  2. $\pi_1(X)$ is a perfect group
  3. the higher homotopy groups of $X$ vanish.

Observe that $H_0(X,\mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ detects the amount of path components and hence $X$ is connected. Furthermore the first homology is the abelianization of pi_1 and vanishes, hence $\pi_1(X)$ is a perfect group

At last the map $X \rightarrow *$ is an acyclic map and hence induces an equivalence on universal covers $\tilde{X} \rightarrow *$ (cf. acyclic map and universal cover), which shows that $\pi_*(X) \cong *$ for $* \geq 2$.
