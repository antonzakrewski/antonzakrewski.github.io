action of relative GL on relative topological embedding space

Proposition

Let $M \hookrightarrow W$ be a smooth embedding of smooth manifolds with dimension $m$ resp. $w$ (and $m \leq w$). Then GL(d,m) acts on the relative smooth embedding space

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Emb}(\mathbb{R}^w;\mathbb{R}^m, W; M) \end{align*}

Proof
