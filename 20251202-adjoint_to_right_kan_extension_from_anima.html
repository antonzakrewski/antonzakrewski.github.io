adjoint to right kan extension from anima

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a cocomplete category and $X\in \mathcal{C}$ an object. Let

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_(-) X: \mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

be the right kan extension

Then it is a left adjoint $\mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}$ resp. a right adjoint $\mathrm{An}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$

with adjoint given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}}(X,-): \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{An} Y \mapsto \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}}(X,Y) \end{align*}
