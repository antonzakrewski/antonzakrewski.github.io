exact category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an additive category Then $\mathcal{A}$ is said to be exact, if it comes equipped with collection $E$ of exact sequences

if

  1. $E$ is closed under natural isomorphism
  2. $\iota$ is a kernel and $p$ is a cokernel - here $\iota$ is an admissible monomorphism and $p$ an admissible epimorphism
  3. a split exact sequence is exact, i.e.
  1. the pullback along an admissible epi $f: N' \rightarrow M'$ exists
  1. the pushout along an admissible mono $f: N \rightarrow M$ exists
