S_(p) acyclic spectrum and p-primary torison

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum and $P$ a collection of primes. TFAE:

  1. $X$ is $\mathbb{S}[P^{-1}]$-acyclic
  2. every element in $\pi_*(X)$ is $p_1^{n_1} \cdot ... p_m^{n_m}$ torsion for some $p_1,...,p_m \in P$

Proof

use:

  • stable homotopy groups preserves filtered colimits
