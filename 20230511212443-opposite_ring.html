opposite ring

Definition / Proposition

Let $(R,+,\cdot)$ a ring, then the opposite ring $R^{\mathrm{op}} = (R,+,*)$ is defined with:

\begin{align*} *: R \times R \rightarrow& R \\ (r,r') \mapsto r' \cdot r \end{align*}

Proof

distributive

\begin{align*} (r_1 + r_2) * (r_3 + r_4) =& (r_3 + r_4) \cdot (r_1 + r_2 \\ =& r_3r_1 + r_3r_2 + r_4r_1 + r_4r_2 \\ =& r_1 * r_3 + r_2 * r_3 + r_1 * r_4 + r_2 * r_4 \end{align*}
