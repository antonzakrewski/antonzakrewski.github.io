trace for a positive matrix is state

Proposition

Let $D \in M_n(\mathbb{C})$ be a positive hermitesche Matrix with $\mathrm{tr}(D) = 1$. then the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{tr}(D \cdot -): M_n(\mathbb{C}) \rightarrow \mathbb{C} \end{align*}

is a state.

Proof

linear

  • clear

$\mathrm{tr}(D \cdot E_n) = 1$

  • clear by assumption

positive

Let $A = a a^* = a^* a$ be a positive matrix. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{tr}(D \cdot A) =& \mathrm{tr}(D \cdot a \cdot a^*) \\ =& \sum_{i} \langle (D \cdot a \cdot a^*) e_i, e_i\rangle \\ =& \sum_{i} \langle D \cdot (a \cdot e_i), (a e_i)\rangle \\ \geq& 0 \end{align*}

where each term $\langle D \cdot (a \cdot e_i), (a e_i)\rangle$ is nonnegative, since $D$ is positive.
