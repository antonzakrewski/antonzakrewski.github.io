faithful representation of a group in general does not induce a faithful representation of the group ring

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group and $k$ a field Then a faithful representation of $G$ is in general not a faithful representation of $k[G]$ as algebra.

Proof

consider $G = C_2$ and $k$ of $\mathrm{char}(k) \neq 2$. Then consider the sing representation $C_2 \rightarrow \mathrm{GL}_1(k) \simeq k^{\times}$ which is injective since $\mathrm{char}(k) \neq 2$. But $k[C_2] \rightarrow \mathrm{GL}_1(k)$ can't be injective for dimension reasons: the LHS has dimension $2$ and the RHS has dimension $1$.
