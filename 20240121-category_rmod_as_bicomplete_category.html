Category RMod as bicomplete category

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod. Then $\mathrm{RMod}$ is bicomplete

Proof

corollary of:

  • category RMod as cocomplete category
  • category RMod as complete category

categorical coproduct

  • direct sum of modules
  • direct sum of modules as categorical coproduct

coequalizer

  • coequalizer of modules

alternatively

  • functor category for a bicomplete codomain as bicomplete category
  • RMod as functor category of additive functors
  • limit of additive functors as additive functor / colimit of additive functors as additive functor
