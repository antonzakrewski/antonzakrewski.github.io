fiber of coproduct into product is a phantom map of spectra

Proposition

Let $X_i$ be spectra for $i \in I$. Then the inclusion of the fiber

\begin{align*} \mathrm{fib} \rightarrow \coprod_{i \in I} X_i \rightarrow \prod_{i \in I} X_i \end{align*}

is a phantom map

Proof

Let $Y$ be a finite spectrum, in particular compact, and $Y \rightarrow \mathrm{fib}$ a map. Then by compact object and coproduct it follows that there exists some finite subset $J \subseteq I$

Then the composition $Y \rightarrow \prod_{i \in I} X_i$ is nullhomotopic and $\prod_{j \in J} X_j$ is a retract of $\prod_{i \in I \setminus J} X_i \oplus Y \arrow[dashed, bend left]{rru}{} \arrow[]{r}{} & \mathrm{fib} \rar & \coprod_{i \in I} X_i \rar & \prod_{i \in I} X_i \rar{\simeq} & (\prod_{i \in I \setminus J} X_i) \oplus (\bigoplus_{j \in J} X_j)$ so in particular $Y \rightarrow \prod_{j \in J} X_j \simeq \coprod_{j \in J} X_j$ is nullhomotopic. Hence so is $Y \rightarrow \coprod_{i \in I} X_i$.
