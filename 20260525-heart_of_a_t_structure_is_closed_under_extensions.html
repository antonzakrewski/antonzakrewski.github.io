heart of a t-structure is closed under extensions

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category with t-structure and $X,Z \in \mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}$ objects in the heart Then any extension $Y$ - or object in a fiber sequence $X \rightarrow Y \rightarrow Z$ - lies also in the heart.
