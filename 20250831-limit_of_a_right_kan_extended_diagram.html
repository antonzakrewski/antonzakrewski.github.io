limit of a right kan extended diagram

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be categories, where $\mathcal{E}$ admits enough limits. Suppose $\iota: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ are functors and let $\mathcal{G} \coloneqq \mathrm{Lan}_{\iota}(\mathcal{F})$ be the right kan extension of $\mathcal{F}$ along $\iota$.

Then there exists a canonical equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim} \mathcal{G} \simeq \mathrm{lim} \mathcal{F} \end{align*}
