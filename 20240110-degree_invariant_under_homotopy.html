singular homological degree invariant under homotopy

Proposition

Let $f,g: S^n \rightarrow S^n$ be homotopic maps on the n-sphere. Then it holds for the singular homological degree

\begin{align*} \mathrm{deg}(f) = \mathrm{deg}(g) \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of singular homology as homotopy invariant functor and definition
