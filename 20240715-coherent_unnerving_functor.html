standard cosimplicial category

Definition

the coherent unnerving functor is defined as functor from the simplex category to the category of simplicially enriched categories $\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{sSet}}$

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{C}: \Delta \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{sSet}} \\ n \mapsto& \mathfrak{C}[\Delta^n] \\ (\alpha: [n] \rightarrow [m]) \mapsto& \alpha \end{align*}

where

  1. $\mathfrak{C}[\Delta^n]$ is the coherent unnerving of a standard simplex
  2. the enriched natural transformations are given by the cube poset union

for $\alpha$ and $i,j \in [n]$ there exists a map

\begin{align*} \cup_{\alpha}: P_{i,j} \rightarrow P_{\alpha(i), \alpha(j)} \end{align*}

and then applying the nerve functor.

Note that the cube poset union has some form of functorality, as

\begin{align*} \cup_{\alpha} \circ \cup_{\beta} = \cup_{\alpha \circ \beta} \end{align*}

which follows from the definition
