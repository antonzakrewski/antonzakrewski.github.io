space of characters is a compact hausdorff space

Proposition

Let $A \in C^*-\mathrm{Alg}$ be a. then $\mathrm{Char}(A)$ is a compact hausdorff space.

Proof

First observe that any character $\chi: A \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ is also an element $\chi \in A^*$ since a maps of star algebras are contractive for the maximal seminorm, in particular it is contained in the unit ball of the dual space of the underlying banach space $A^*$. Now there exists a map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Char}(A) \rightarrow \prod_{a \in B_A(0,1)} \{\vert z \vert \leq 1\} \chi \mapsto (\chi(a))_{a \in B}} \end{align*}

This map is injective since we can scale. It is continuous (??) and the target is compact hausdorff (cf. product of hausdorff spaces as hausdorff space & Tychonoff's Theorem). Moreover, it identifies $\mathrm{Char}(A)$ as a subspace.

Here the image is a closed subspace, since multiplicativity & star relations are cut out by continuous relations.
