quasicoherent sheaf of modules

Definition

Let $(X,\mathcal{O}_X)$ be a ringed spaced and $\mathcal{F}$ a sheaf of modules. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be quasicoherent, if

  1. $(\mathcal{X}, \mathcal{O}_X)$ is a scheme
  2. there exists an open covering $O_i$ of $X$, such that the restriction of the sheaf $\mathcal{O}_x(O_i) \cong \mathrm{Spec}(A_i)$ for some ring $A_i$
  3. and for each $i \in I$ there exists an \(\mathcal{O}_X(O_i)\)-module $M_i$ with
\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_{\vert O_i} \cong \tilde{M}_i \end{align*}

where $\tilde{M}_i$ is the associated sheaf to a module
