irreducible, finitely dimensional topological space and maximal dimension

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a irreducible, finitely dimensional, closed topological space. Then for a closed subset $Y \subseteq X$

TFAE:

  1. $\mathrm{dim}(X) = \mathrm{dim}(Y)$
  2. $X = Y$

1) $\implies$ 2)

clear

2) $\implies$ 1)
