Picard group functor

Definition

The picard group functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Pic}(-): \mathrm{CRing} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ab} \\ R \mapsto& \mathrm{Pic}(R) \\ (f: R \rightarrow S) \mapsto& (S \otimes_R (-)) \end{align*}
