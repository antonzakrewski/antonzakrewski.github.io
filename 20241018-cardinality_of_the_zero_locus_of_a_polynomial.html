cardinality of the zero locus of a polynomial

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space, $f \in K[X_1,...,X_n]$ a nonconstant polynomial TFAE:

  1. the zero locus is finite
  2. $n = 1$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

assume $n \geq 2$ Then there exists a variable $X_i$ which occurs in $f$, w.l.o.g. $i = 1$. Then for all $(a_2,...,a_n) \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^{n-1}$ - infinitely many points - the polynomial $f(x_1,a_2,...,a_n) \in K[X]$ is a polynomial. Furhtermore it contains at least one solution, as $K$ is algebraically closed and $f$ has degree at least $1$.

2) $\implies$ 1)

  • finitely many roots
