calkin category functor

Definition

The calkin category functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Calk}(-): \mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}}^{\mathrm{dual}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{perf}} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{colim}_{\kappa} \mathrm{Calk}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

Remark

Recall that Ind preserves locally small categories and passing to adjoints in the verdier sequence shows that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Calk}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C}^{\kappa}) \subseteq \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
