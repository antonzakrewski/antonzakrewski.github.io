equalizer in CRing

Proposition

Let $R,S$ be commutative rings and

be ring-homomorphism

Then the equalizer exists and is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Eq}(\varphi, \psi) \coloneqq \{r \in R \vert \varphi(r) = \psi(r)\} \end{align*}

with the natural inclusion $\iota: \mathrm{Eq}(\varphi, \psi) \hookrightarrow R$

Proof

$0,1$

Note that $\varphi(1) = 1_S = \psi(1)$ and $\varphi(0) = 0_S = \psi(0)$

additive closed

Suppose $r_1,r_2 \in R$ such that $\varphi(r_i) = \psi(r_i)$. Then

\begin{align*} \varphi(r_1 + r_2) =& \varphi(r_1) + \varphi(r_2) \\ =& \psi(r_1) + \psi(r_2) \\ =& \psi(r_1 + r_2) \end{align*}

multiplicative closed

Suppose $r_1,r_2 \in R$ such that $\varphi(r_i) = \psi(r_i)$. Then it follows, that

\begin{align*} \varphi(r_1 \cdot r_2) =& \varphi(r_1) \cdot \varphi(r_2) \\ =& \psi(r_1) \cdot \psi(r_2) \\ =& \psi(r_1 \cdot r_2) \end{align*}

and hence $r_1 \cdot r_2 \in \mathrm{Eq}(\varphi, \psi)$
