left / right and exact functor between stable infinity categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{C}'$ be stable infinity categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an infinity functor

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is exact
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ is infinity right exact functor, i.e. preserves finite colimits
  3. $\mathcal{F}$ is infinity left exact functor, i.e. preserves finite limits

Proof - HA 1.1.4

use:

  • kappa right exact functor for a sufficiently cocomplete domain
  • kappa left exact functor for a sufficiently complete domain

where $\mathcal{C}$ is finitely bicomplete, i.e. in particular \(\omega_0\)-(co-)complete

1) $\implies$ 2)

  • infinity pushout with zero object and coproduct
  • <cofiber coequalizer>

2) $\implies$ 1)

special case, as fiber sequences are limits
