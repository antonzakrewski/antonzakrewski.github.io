proper map between quasi projective varieties

Definition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X,Y,S \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ quasi-projective varieties Then a regular map $f: X \rightarrow S$ is said to be proper, if for each regular map $g: Y \rightarrow S$

the map

\begin{align*} q: X \times_S Y \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

is a closed map
