adjunction and orthogonal morphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ an infinity functor and $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$ an infinity adjunction. Let $f \in \mathcal{C}$ be a morphism, $g \in \mathcal{D}$.

TFAE:

  1. $f \bot \mathcal{G}(g)$
  2. $\mathcal{F}(f) \bot \mathcal{G}(g)$
