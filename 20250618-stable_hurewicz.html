stable hurewicz theorem

Proposition

Let $X$ be an \(n-1\)-connected spectrum for $n \in \mathbb{N}^+$. Then the stable hurewicz homomorphism induces an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \pi_n(X) \rightarrow \pi_n(X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

Proof

$n = 0$

note that both

\begin{align*} \pi_0(- \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{S}), \pi_0(- \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

preserve colimits.

But connected spectra generated under colimits by the sphere spectrum (cf. connected spectra closed under colimits).

Thus it suffices to show that the map

\begin{align*} \mathbb{S} \rightarrow \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

induces an isomorphism on $\pi_0$.

this follows from the explicit description of the stable hurewicz homomorphism and Hurewicz Homomorphism and suspension.

\begin{align*} \pi_0(\mathbb{S} \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{S}) = \pi_0^{\mathrm{st}}(\mathbb{S}) \end{align*}

arbritrary $n$

Note that then

\begin{align*} X \simeq \Sigma^n \Omega^n X \end{align*}

where $\Omega^n X$ is a connceted spectrum.

Therefore use that

\begin{align*} \pi_n(X) \simeq& \pi_0(\Omega^n \Sigma^n \Omega^n X) \\ \simeq& \pi_0(\Omega^n X) \\ \simeq& \pi_0(\Omega^n X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z}) \\ \simeq& \pi_0(\Omega^n (X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z})) \\ \simeq& \pi_n(X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \mathbb{Z}) \\ \end{align*}

where tensor product in spectra preserves loop spaces
