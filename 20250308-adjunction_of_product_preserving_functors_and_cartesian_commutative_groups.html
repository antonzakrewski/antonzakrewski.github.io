currying of product preserving functors into cartesian commutative groups

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories admitting finite products. Then there exists a canonical equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{D})) \end{align*}

see:

  • infinity functor category
  • category of cartesian commutative groups
  • category of finite product preserving functors

Proof

using

  • Adjunction of product preserving functors and cartesian commutative monoids

we get equivalences

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{D})) \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\Gamma, \prod}(N(\mathrm{Fin}_*) \times \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D})) \end{align*}

Thus it suffices to argue, that the subcategory

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D})) \subseteq \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D})) \end{align*}

restricts precisely to

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{D})) \end{align*}

Here $\mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}))$ is precisely the subcategory, where the shear map is a natural equivalence. But using limit in an infinity functor category, we may compute the shear map1

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(<1>,-) \times \mathcal{F}(<1>,-) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(<1>,-) \times \mathcal{F}(<1>,-) \end{align*}

objectwise

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(<1>,C) \times \mathcal{F}(<1>,C) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(<1>,C) \times \mathcal{F}(<1>,C) \end{align*}

where the first is an equivalence if and only if the map for all objects is an equivalence (cf. natural equivalence between infinity functors and objectwise isomorphism)

Precisely if the shear map is an equivalence for all objects, then the functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F} \in \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{D})) \end{align*}

restricts to

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F} \in \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{D})) \end{align*}
