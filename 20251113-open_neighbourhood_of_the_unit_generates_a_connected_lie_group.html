open neighbourhood of the unit generates a connected lie group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a lie group and $U\subseteq G$ an open subset of the unit $1$. Then the group generated by $U$ contains the connected component of the unit or $G$.

Proof

We show that the generated group is both open and closed, hence contains the connected component of $1$ (in a locally connected space $G$)

open

Let $g \in \langle U\rangle$, then $g[U] \subseteq \langle U\rangle$ is an open neighbourhood (since multiplication with $g$ is an homeomorphism).

closed

Let $g_n\in \langle U\rangle$ be a sequence with $g_n \rightarrow g$. We want to show that $g \in \langle U\rangle$.

Then it follows that $g^{-1} \cdot g_{n} \rightarrow g^{-1} \gdot g = 1$ and hence there exists an $n$ with $g^{-1} \cdot g_n \in U$, which shows

\begin{align*} (g^{-1} \cdot g_n) \cdot g_n^{-1} = g^{-1} \in \langle U\rangle \end{align*}

or $g \in \langle U\rangle$
