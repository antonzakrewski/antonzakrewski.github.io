kappa filtered infinity colimit

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{I}$ be infinity categories and $\kappa$ a regular cardinal, Then a $\kappa$ filtered infinity category is a infinity colimit

\begin{align*} \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

If $\kappa$ is not specified, this usually means $\kappa = \omega_0$
