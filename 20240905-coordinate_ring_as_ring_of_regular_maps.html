affine coordinate ring as ring of morphisms

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be an algebraic set.d Then the coordinate ring is given by the ring of morphism of affine algebraic sets

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}(X,K) \end{align*}

Proof

Let

\begin{align*} K[X_1,...,X_n] \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}(X,K) \\ f \mapsto f_{\vert X} \end{align*}

be the ring-homomorphism

Note that it is an epimorphism, as each element in $\mathrm{Hom}(X,K)$ is given by a polynomial.

Furhtermore the kernel is given by the ideal of polynomials which vanish on $X$, i.e. $I(X)$
