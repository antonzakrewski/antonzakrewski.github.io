kappa filtered infinity category

Definition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal and $\mathcal{C}$ an infinity category. Then $\mathcal{C}$ is said to be \(\kappa\)-filtered if any infinity functor

\begin{align*} I \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

where $I$ is a kappa-small simplicial set admits a cone

\begin{align*} J \star \Delta^0 \rightarrow I \end{align*}
