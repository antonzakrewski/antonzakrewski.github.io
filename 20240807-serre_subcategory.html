serre subcategory

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category. A weak serre subcategory is a nonempty <abelian> full subcategory $\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{A}$ such that for every exact sequence

with $A,C \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{A})$ it follows that $B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$
