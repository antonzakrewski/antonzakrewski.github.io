pi_1 action on homotopy groups of an anima for a fiber sequence

Definition

Let $F \rightarrow E \rightarrow B$ be a fiber sequence. Let $E$ be an anima, then the $\pi_1(E,e)$ action on $\pi_n(E,e)$ is defined as composition

\begin{align*} E \rightarrow \{F,f\} \subseteq \mathrm{An}_* \xrightarrow{\pi_n(-)} \mathrm{Grp} \end{align*}
