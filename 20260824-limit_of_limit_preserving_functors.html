limit of limit preserving functors in the functor category is limit preserving

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{K}$ be a collection of categories, $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, where $\mathcal{D}$ admits $\mathcal{K}$ and $I$ shaped limits. Suppose that $I \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ is a diagram consisting pointwise of $\mathcal{K}$ limit preserving functors. Then the limit in $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ also preserves $\mathcal{K}$ shaped limits

Proof

  • pointwise calculation of limits
  • limits commute with limits
