uniquely p-divisible group and tensor product with integers

Proposition

Let $A \in \mathcal{D}(\mathbb{Z})$ be an object in the derived category. TFAE:

  1. $A$ is a uniquely p-divisible object
  2. $A \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{F}_p$ is zero
  3. $A \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}^{\heartsuit} \mathbb{F}_p$ and $\mathrm{Tor}_{\mathbb{Z}}^1(A, \mathbb{F}_p)$ are zero

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

use the cofiber sequence $\mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\cdot p} \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{F}_p$ and that the derived tensor product is an exact functor.

2) $\implies$ 3)

special case

3) $\implies$ 1)

use the cofiber sequence $\mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\cdot p} \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{F}_p$ and that the derived tensor product is an exact functor.

  • tor functor of for modules over a hereditary ring where $\mathbb{Z}$ is hereditary.
