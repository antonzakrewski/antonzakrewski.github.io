kernel of a ring epimorphism to an integral domain as prime ideal

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $S$ an Integral domain and $\varphi: R \rightarrow S$ a homomorphism. Then $\mathrm{ker}(\varphi)$ is a prime ideal.

Proof

Let $r_1 \cdot r_2 \in \mathrm{ker}(\varphi)$, then it follows that

\begin{align*} 0 =& \varphi(r_1 \cdot r_2) \\ =& \varphi(r_1) \cdot \varphi(r_2) \end{align*}

Since $S$ is integral, w.l.o.g. $\varphi(r_1) = 0$ or $r_1 \in \mathrm{ker}(\varphi)$
