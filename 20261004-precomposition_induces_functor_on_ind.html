precomposition induces functor on Ind

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $\iota: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a left exact functor (e.g. a subcategory closed under finite colimits). Then precomposition with $\iota$ induces a filtered colimit preserving functor $\iota^{*}: \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{D})$

Proof

follows as composition of right exact functors (namely on opposite categories) is right exact use pointwise calculation of colimits for the filtered colimit argument, since $\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An})$ is closed under filtered colimits.
