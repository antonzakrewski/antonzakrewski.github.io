cup product on a coproduct of spaces

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be topological space, $X \coprod Y$ the topological sum Then the cup product of singular cohomology is given by

\begin{align*} (x_1,y_1) \cup (x_2,y_2) = (x_1 \cup x_2, y_1 \cup y_2) \end{align*}

for

\begin{align*} H^*(X \coprod Y) = H^*(X) \times H^*(Y) \end{align*}

Proof
