postnikov weight structure

Definition / Proposition

The postnikov weight structure on category of finite spectra is defined as weight structure given by

  1. $\mathrm{Sp}_{w \geq n}$ given by n-connective spectra
  2. $\mathrm{Sp}_{w \leq n}$ is given by
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sp}_{w \leq n} = \{E : \mathbb{Z}_*(E) = 0 \forall * > 0, \mathbb{Z}_n(E) = \text{ free} \} \end{align*}
