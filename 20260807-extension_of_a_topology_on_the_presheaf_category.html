extension of a topology on the presheaf category

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \tau)$ be a site. Then the topology $\tau$ extends to a topology $\widehat{\tau}$ on $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ as follows: A sieve $R$ on a presheaf $\mathcal{F}$ is $\widehat{\tau}$ covering if, for any $よ(X) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}$, the sieve of all $Y \rightarrow X$ such that $よ(Y) \rightarrow よ(X) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}$ is in $R$ is $\tau$ covering.
