characterizations of an open covering of algebraic functors

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor and $U_i \subseteq X$ a family of open subfunctors. Let $K_i \in \mathrm{Rad}_{X}$ be the poset of quasi-coherent radical ideal of $U_i$ (cf. classification of open subfunctors of an algebraic functor). TFAE:

  1. $X = \bigvee_{i \in I} U_i$ in the partially ordered set $\mathrm{Open}(X)$.
  2. $\mathcal{O}_{X} = \bigvee_{i \in I} K_i$ in the poset $\mathrm{Rad}_{X}$ (cf. maximal quasi-coherent radical ideal)
  3. for every commutative ring $R$ and every $x: \mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow X$, the union $\bigcup_{i \in I} K_i(X)$ generates the unit ideal in $R$
  4. for every local ring, $X(R) = \bigcup_{i \in I} U_i(R)$
  5. for every field $k$, $X(k) = \bigcup_{i \in I} U_i(k)$
  6. the induced map $\bigcup_{i \in I} U_i \rightarrow X$ is a zariski-local epimorphism
  7. the induced map $\bigcup_{i \in I} U_i \rightarrow X$ is surjective.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

classification of open subfunctors of an algebraic functor which is a natural isomorphism between the partially ordered sets hence it is an equivalence after passing to limits (colimits in $\mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{op}}$).

2) $\implies$ 3)

follows from the pointwise characterization of colimits in $\mathrm{Rad}_{X}$.

3) $\implies$ 4)

4) $\implies$ 5)

special case

5) $\implies$ 6)

6) $\implies$ 7)

7) $\implies$ 1)
