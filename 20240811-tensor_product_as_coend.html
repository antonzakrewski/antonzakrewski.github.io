Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $M,N$ \(R\)-modules, where $M: R^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M)$ is a right module and $N: R \rightarrow \mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{Z}}(N)$ a left module Then the tensor

\begin{align*} M \otimes_R N \end{align*}

is a Coend:
