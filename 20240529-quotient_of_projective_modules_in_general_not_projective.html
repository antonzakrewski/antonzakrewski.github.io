quotient of projective modules in general not projective

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $M,N$ projective \(R\)-modules. Then the quotient module is in general not projective.

Proof

using projective module and sum as free module

Consider $R = \mathbb{Z}$ and

\begin{align*} M = \mathbb{Z}, N = 2 \mathbb{Z} \cong \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} M/\mathbb{Z} \cong \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

is not projective.
