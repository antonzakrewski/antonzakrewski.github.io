non-triviality of stiefel whitney class of the universal line bundle

Proposition

Let $\gamma: E \rightarrow \mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}$ be the spherical fibration underlying the universal line bundle (cf. forgetful functor from real vector bundles to spherical fibrations). Then the first stiefel whitney class is nontrivial, i.e.

\begin{align*} w_1(\gamma) = 1 \in H^1(\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}, \mathbb{F}_2) \cong \mathbb{F}_2 \end{align*}

Proof
