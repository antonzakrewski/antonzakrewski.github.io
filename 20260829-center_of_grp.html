center of Grp

Proposition

The center of category Group is trivial.

Proof

use:

  • $\mathrm{id}$ after forgetting (faithful functor) is represented by $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Grp}}(\mathbb{Z}, -)$.
  • hence $\mathrm{End}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Grp}, \mathrm{Grp})(\mathrm{id}) \subseteq \mathrm{End}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Grp}, \mathrm{Set})(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Grp}}(\mathbb{Z},-)) \subseteq \mathbb{Z}$.
  • use: everything except the element classified by $\cdot 0, \cdot 1$ is not a natural transformation.
