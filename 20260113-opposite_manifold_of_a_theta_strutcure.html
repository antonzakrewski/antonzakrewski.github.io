opposite manifold of a theta structure

Definition

Let $(M,f,h)$ be a theta-manifold. Then the opposite manifold is defined as

\begin{align*} (M,f,h)^{\mathrm{op}} \coloneqq (M,f,h^{\mathrm{op}}) \end{align*}

where $h \circ \mathrm{inv}$ is given by the homotopy which is obtained via adding the self homotopy corresponding to adding the homotopy $\mathrm{const}_{-1}: M \rightarrow O$ where $-1$ is the other component of $O$ and where we use the equivalence

\begin{align*} \Omega_{f} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(M, \mathrm{BO}) \simeq& \Omega_{\mathrm{const}_{e}} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(M, \mathrm{BO}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(M, \Omega_{e} \mathrm{BO}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(M, O) \end{align*}
