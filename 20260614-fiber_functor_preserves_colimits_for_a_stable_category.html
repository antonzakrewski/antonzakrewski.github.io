fiber functor preserves colimits for a stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category, then the fiber functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{fib}: \mathrm{Ar}(\mathcal{C}) \xrightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

preserves colimits
