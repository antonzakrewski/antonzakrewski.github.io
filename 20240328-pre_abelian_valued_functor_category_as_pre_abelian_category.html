pre-abelian valued functor category as pre-abelian category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category and $\mathcal{A}$ an pre-abelian category Then the functor category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{A}) \end{align*}

is also pre-abelian

Proof

pre-additive

additive-valued functor category as additive category_

cokernels

follows from:

  • colimit of functors for a cocomplete codomain

kernels

follows from

  • limit of functors for a complete domain
