n-truncation functor as left adjoint

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category, $n \geq -2$. Then the \(n\)-truncation functor

\begin{align*} \tau_{\leq n}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \tau_{\leq n} \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

is defined as left adjoint to the inclusion

\begin{align*} \iota: \tau_{\leq n}\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}
