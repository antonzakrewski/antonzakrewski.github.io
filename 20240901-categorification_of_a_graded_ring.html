categorification of a graded ring

Definition / Proposition

Let \(R = \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0} R_n\) be a graded ring and $M = \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0$ a graded module.

Then $R$ is equivalently described by an Ab-enriched category with

  1. elements $m \in M$ as objects
  2. $\mathrm{Hom}(m,n) = r \in \bigcup_{m \in M} R_m$ such that r \cdot R_m \subseteq R_n$
  3. fully faithful additive endofunctors translation functors
\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}_m: M \rightarrow M \end{align*}

such that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}_{m} \circ \mathcal{L}_n = \mathcal{L}_{m \cdot n} \end{align*}

and

Proof

ring structure

Define

\begin{align*} R \coloneqq \bigoplus_{m \in M} R_m \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} r_i \cdot r_j \coloneqq \mathcal{L}_j(r_i) \circ r_j \end{align*}

unit

Let $1 = \mathrm{id} \in \mathrm{End}(1,1)$. Then

\begin{align*} r \cdot 1 =& \mathcal{L}_{1}(r) \circ \mathrm{id} \\ =& r \cric \end{align*}

additive

compatibly graded

Let

\begin{align*} R_m \coloneqq \mathrm{Hom}(1,m) \end{align*}

Then define

\begin{align*} R \coloneqq \bigoplus_{m \in M} R_m \end{align*}

with the multiplication defined on components

\begin{align*} r_m \cdot r_n = \mathcal{L}_n(r_m) \circ r_n \end{align*}

for the translation functor $\mathcal{L}_n$

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}_2/\mathcal{A}_1}(x,y) \cong \varinjlim_{\substack{x' \subseteq x, y' \subseteq y \\ x',y/y' \in \mathcal{A}_1}}\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}_2}(x',y/y') \\ \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}_2/\mathcal{A}_1}(x,y) \cong \mathrm{colim}\limits_{\substack{x' \subseteq x, y' \subseteq y \\ x',y/y' \in \mathcal{A}_1}}\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}_2}(x',y/y') \end{align*}
