product with interval category as left adjoint

Proposition

Given Category Cat, the functor

\begin{align*} (-) \times \Delta^1 \mathrm{Cat} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat} \\ \mathcal{C} \mapsto (\mathcal{C} \times \Delta^1) \end{align*}

is a left adjoint to the functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, -): \mathrm{Cat} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat} \\ \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{C} \times \Delta^1) \end{align*}

Proof

We have to show, that there exists a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}}((-) \times \Delta^1, (-)) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}}((-), \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, -)) \end{align*}

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, then for a functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \times \Delta^1 \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

we define

\begin{align*} \varphi(\mathcal{F}): \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{D}) \\ C \mapsto& \begin{cases} 0 \mapsto \mathcal{F}(C,0) \\ 1 \mapsto \mathcal{F}(C,1) \\ 0 \rightarrow 1 \mapsto \left(\mathcal{F}(\rightarrow): \mathcal{F}(C,0) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(C,1) \right) \end{cases} \\ (f: A \rightarrow B) \mapsto& \begin{cases} \mathcal{F}(f,\mathrm{id}_{0}): \mathcal{F}(A,0) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(B,0) \\ \mathcal{F}(f, \mathrm{id}_{1}): \mathcal{F}(A,1) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(B,1) \end{cases} \end{align*}

and for a functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

we define

\begin{align*} \varphi^{-1}(\mathcal{G}): \mathcal{C} \times \Delta^1 \rightarrow& \mathcal{D} \\ (C,0) \mapsto& \mathcal{G}(C)(0) \\ (C,1) \mapsto& \mathcal{G}(C)(1) \\ (f, \mathrm{id}_{n}): (A,n) \rightarrow (B,n) \mapsto& \mathcal{G}(f)(\mathrm{id}_{n}): \mathcal{G}(A)(n) \rightarrow \mathcal{G}(B)(n) \\ (f, 0 \rightarrow 1): (A,0) \rightarrow (B,1) \mapsto& \mathcal{G}(f)(1) \circ \mathcal{G}(A)(: \mathcal{G}(A)(0) \rightarrow \mathcal{G}(B)(1) \end{align*}
