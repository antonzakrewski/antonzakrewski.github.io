R-Orientation at a subspace

Definition

Let \(M\) be an \(n\)-dimensional manifold and $A \subseteq X$ a subset. Then an \(R\)-orientation of $X$ at $A$ is an element

\begin{align*} \sigma_{X,A} \in H_n(X, X \setminus A, M) \end{align*}

such that for each $x \in A$ and the natural transformation

\begin{align*} \rho_{x}: H_n(X, X \setminus A; R) \rightarrow H_n(X, X \setminus \{x\}; R) \end{align*}
\begin{align*} \rho_x(\sigma_{X,A}) \end{align*}

is a generator
