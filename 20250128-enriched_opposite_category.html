enriched opposite category

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a symmetric monoidal monoidal category and $\mathcal{C}$ an (\mathcal{V})-enriched category. Then the enriched opposite category is defined as enriched category containing

  1. $\mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}) = \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$
  2. $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}(a,b) = \mathcal{C}(b,a)$
