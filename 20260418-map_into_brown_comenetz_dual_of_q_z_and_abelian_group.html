map into brown comenetz dual of Q/Z and abelian group

Proposition

Let $A$ be an abelian group and $I_{\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}}$ brown comenetz dual of Q/Z . Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}(A, I_{\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}}) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(A, \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

is an Eilenberg-MacLane spectrum

Proof

follows from the defining feature

\begin{align*} \pi_n(\mathrm{map}(A, I_{\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}})) \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\pi_{-n}(A), \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

where the RHS vanishes if $n \neq 0$.
