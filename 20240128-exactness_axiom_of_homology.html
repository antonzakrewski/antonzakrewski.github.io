exactness Axiom of Homology

Definition

Let $(X,A)$ be a pair of spaces, then the homology functor induces a long exact sequence 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
... \arrow[]{r}{} & H_n(A) \arrow[]{r}{H_n(\iota)}& H_{n(X) \arrow[]{r}{H_n(j)} & H_{n}(X,A) \arrow[]{r}{\partial_n}& H_{n-1}(A) \arrow[]{r}{}& ...
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

where

\begin{align*} \iota: (A,\emptyset) \rightarrow& (X, \emptyset) \\ j: (X,\emptyset) \rightarrow& (X,A) \end{align*}
