iterated hopf map

Definition

the the iterated hopf maps are defined as appropriate composition of suspensions of the hopf map

\begin{align*} \eta^n =& \eta (\Sigma \eta) ... (\Sigma^{n-1} \eta): S^{2+n} \rightarrow S^2 \end{align*}
\begin{align*} \nu^n =& \nu (\Sigma^3 \nu) ... (\Sigma^{3(n-1)} \nu): S^{4+3n} \rightarrow S^4 \end{align*}
\begin{align*} \sigma^n =& \sigma (\Sigma^7 \sigma) ... (\Sigma^{7(n-1)} \sigma): S^{8+7n} \rightarrow S^8 \end{align*}
