isomorphism classes of objects in a filtered colimit

Proposition

Let $I \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}$ be a diagram. Then the isomorphism classes of objects is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ob}(\mathrm{colim} \mathcal{C}_I) \cong \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C}_I) \end{align*}

Proof

follows as:

  • pi_0 preserves colimits
  • groupoid core functor preserves filtered colimits
