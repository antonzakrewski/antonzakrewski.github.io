t-structure of S-nilpotent and S-local module spectra

Proposition

Let $R$ be an (\mathbb{E}_1)-ring spectrum and $S \subseteq \pi_*(R)$ be a homogeneous subset satisfying the left ore condition. Then there exists a t-structure on the category of module spectra given by $(\mathrm{LMod}_R^{\mathrm{S}-nil}, \mathrm{LMod}_R^{S-\mathrm{loc}})$

Proof

follows from equivalent characterization of an S-local module spectrum
