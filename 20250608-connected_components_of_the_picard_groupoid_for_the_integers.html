connected components of the picard groupoid for the integers

Proposition

The picard groupoid $\mathrm{Pic}(\mathbb{Z})$ contains precisely $\mathbb{Z}$ many components, classified by

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow& \pi_0(\mathrm{Pic}(\mathbb{Z})) 1 \mapsto& \Sigma \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}
