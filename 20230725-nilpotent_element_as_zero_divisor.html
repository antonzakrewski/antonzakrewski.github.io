nilpotent element as zero divisor

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $s \in R$ nilpotent. Then $s$ is a zero divisor

Proof

By Assumption, there exists an $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that

\begin{align*} s^n = 0 \end{align*}

therefore

\begin{align*} s^{n-1} \cdot s = 0 \end{align*}

and $s$ is a zero divisor
