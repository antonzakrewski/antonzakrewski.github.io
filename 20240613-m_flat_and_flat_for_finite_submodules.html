m-flat and flat for finitely generated submodules

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $N,M$ \(R\)-modules.

TFAE:

  1. $N$ is M-flat
  2. $N$ is \(M')\)-flat for finitely generated submodules $M' = \langle m_1,...,m_n\rangle$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from:

  • M-flat for a submodule

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from:

  • M-flat and flat for cyclic submodules
