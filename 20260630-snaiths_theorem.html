snaiths theorem for KU

Proposition

There exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{KU} \simeq \mathbb{S}[\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}][\beta^{-1}] \end{align*}

where the LHS is KU and the RHS is the suspension spectrum of BU and the localization of a generator $\beta \in \pi_2(\mathbb{S}[\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}])$ (cf. low stable homotopy of CP^n).

Proof
