torsion element coprime to p is p-divisible

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group, $g \in G$ a torsion element for some $n$. Suppose $n$ is coprime to $p$, then $g$ is p-divisible element

Proof

consider the map

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow G 1 \mapsto g \end{align*}

Now $1 \in \mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z}$ is a p-divisible element, so this claim follows from homomorphism and divisible element
