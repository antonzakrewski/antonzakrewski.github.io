galois group of a simple field extension with a primitive root of unity

Proposition

Let $\zeta \in \overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ be a root of unity, $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta)/\mathbb{Q}$ the galois extension of a primitive root. Then the galois group is

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Gal}( \mathbb{Q}(\zeta)/\mathbb{Q} ) =& \mathbb{Z}/ n \mathbb{Z})^{\times} \end{align*}

Proof
