image of a von neumann regular ring as von neumann regular ring

Proposition

Let \(R,R'\) be a rings, $\varphi: R \rightarrow R'$ a homomorphism and $R$ a von Neumann regular ring

Proof

corollary of:

  • Homomorphiesatz für Ringe
  • quotient ring of a von neumann regular ring as von neumann regular ring
\begin{align*} R/\mathrm{ker}(\varphi) \cong \mathrm{im}(\varphi) \subseteq R' \end{align*}
