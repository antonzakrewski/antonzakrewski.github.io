presentably symmetric monoidal infinity category

Definition

A presenbably symmetric monoidal infinity category is an E infinity algebra in the category PrL as closed symmetric monoidal category

Alternatively

A closed symmetric monoidal infinity category where

  1. the underlying infinity category is presentable
  2. the tensor product preserves colimits in both variables

note that presentability means, that $- \otimes x$ admits a infinity right adjoint
