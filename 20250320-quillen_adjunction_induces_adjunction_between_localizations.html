quillen adjunction induces adjunction between infinity localizations

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be model categories and $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$ a quillen adjunction for $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$. Then this induces an infinity adjunction

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}[W^{-1}] \dashv \mathcal{G}[W^{-1}] \end{align*}

Proof
