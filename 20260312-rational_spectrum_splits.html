rational spectrum splits

Proposition

Let $X$ be a rational spectrum, then

\begin{align*} \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \pi_n(X) \rightarrow X \end{align*}

is an equivalence

Proof

Observe that $\pi_n(X)$ admits a $\mathbb{Q}$ basis, i.e.

\begin{align*} \pi_n(X) \simeq& \bigoplus \mathbb{Q} \\ \simeq& \bigoplus \mathbb{S}_{\mathbb{Q}} \end{align*}

Hence by rational homotopy group and isomorphism of a spectrum there exist maps

\begin{align*} \bigoplus_i \mathbb{S}_{\mathbb{Q}} \simeq \pi_n(X) \rightarrow X \end{align*}

Moreover $\mathbb{S}_{\mathbb{Q}}$ has only only nontrivial $\pi_0$, so the map

\begin{align*} \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \pi_n(X) \rightarrow X \end{align*}

is an isomorphism on homotopy groups, hence an isomorphism of spectra (cf. whitehead theorem for spectra)
