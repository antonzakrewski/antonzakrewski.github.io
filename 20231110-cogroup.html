cogroup

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category A cogroup is a group object in the opposite category.

Explicite

Let $G \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ be an object and $\emptyset$ the initial object Then $G$ is said to be a cogroup if there exists

  1. a counit
\begin{align*} \eta: G \rightarrow \emptyset \end{align*}
  1. a comultiplication
\begin{align*} \mu: G \rightarrow G \coprod G \end{align*}
