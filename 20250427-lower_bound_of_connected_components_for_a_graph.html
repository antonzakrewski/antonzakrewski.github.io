lower bound of connected components for a graph

Proposition

Let $G = (V,E)$ be a graph. Then $G$ has at least $\vert V \vert - \vert E \vert$ many connected components

Proof

Proof by induction

induction basis

assume $\vert E \vert = 0$, then the graph has precisely $\vert V \vert$ many connected components.

induction step

add an edge to the graph. If the edge connects two vertices from different compenents, then we loose exactly one connected component. otherwise we don't lose any connected component. In both cases

\begin{align*} \pi_0(G) \geq& \vert V \vert - \vert E \vert + 1 \end{align*}

where $E$ are the edges before adding the new edge.
