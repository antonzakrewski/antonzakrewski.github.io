coequalizer of a group action and the projection as orbital collapse

Proposition

Let $\triangle: G \times X \rightarrow X$ be a topological group action for a topological group $G$ and a topological space $X$ Then the coequalizer in category Top for

\begin{align*} \pi(g,x) =& x \\ \triangle(g,x) =& g \cdot x \end{align*}

is the orbital collapse

\begin{align*} X/\sim \end{align*}

for $\sim$ as generated equivalence relation by $g \cdot x \sim x$

Proof

Let $Y$ be a topological space and $h: X \rightarrow Y$ a morphism such that

Let

\begin{align*} h: X/\sim \rightarrow& Y [x] \mapsto& h(x) \end{align*}

it is welldefined, as $[x] = [x']$ is equivalent to $\exists g \in G : g \cdot x = x'$. Furthermore, by assumption

\begin{align*} (g,x) x =& (g,x) \\ h(\pi(g,x)) =& h(g \cdot x) \\ h(x) =& h(x') \end{align*}

Furthermore, the map is continuous by definition of a final topology
