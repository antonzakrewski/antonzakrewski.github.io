power series ring and noetherian ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $A[ [X_1,...,X_n]]$ the power series ring in finitely many variables.

TFAE:

  1. $A$ is noetherian
  2. $A[ [ X_1,..., X_n]]$ is noetherian

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

similar to Hilbert's basis theorem - todo

https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0306

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from

\begin{align*} A[ [X_1,..., X_n]] \twoheadrightarrow A[ [X_1,..., X_n] ]/\mathfrak{a} \cong A \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a} = \{\sum \alpha_i \prod X_i^{\nu_i} \vert \sum \nu_i \geq 1 \} \end{align*}
