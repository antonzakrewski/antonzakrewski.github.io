nirgends dicht und Komplement enthält offene dichte Menge

Satz

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space and $A \subseteq X$ a subspace. TFAE:

  1. $A$ is nowhere dense
  2. the interior of $X \setminus A$ is dense in $X$

Beweis

Recall that $\overline{B} = X \setminus \mathrm{int}(X \setminus B), X \setminus \overline{B} = \mathrm{int}(X \setminus B)$ for $B \subseteq X$.(cf. Abschluss und Innere des Komplements).

1) $\implies$ 2)

Then it follows

\begin{align*} \emptyset =& \mathrm{int}(\overline{A}) \\ =& X \setminus \overline{X \setminus \overline{A}} \end{align*}

so $\overline{X \setminus \overline{A}} = X$ and hence $X \setminus \overline{A} \subseteq X \setminus A$ is both open and dense.

2) $\implies$ 1)

Assume that the interior is dense, then

\begin{align*} X =& \mathrm{int}(\overline{A}) \\ =& X \setminus \overline{X \setminus \overline{A}} \\ =& X \setminus \mathrm{int}(\overline{A}) \end{align*}

which shows $\mathrm{int}(\overline{A}) = \emptyset$.
