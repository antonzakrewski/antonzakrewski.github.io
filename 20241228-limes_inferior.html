limes inferior

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $A_n$ be objects for $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then the limes inferior is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathrm{lim}_{m \geq n} A_m =& \mathrm{colim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \prod_{m \geq n} A_m \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} \prod_{m \geq n} A_m \rightarrow \prod_{m \geq n +1} A_m \end{align*}

is the induced map by the universal property - "forgetting the \(n\)-th component"
