contravariant yoneda embedding

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a locally small category, then the contravariant yoneda embedding is defined as contravariant functor

\begin{align*} よ: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Set}) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from applying the covariant yoneda embedding $\mathcal{C} = \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}$
