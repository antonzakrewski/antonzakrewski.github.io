coproduct of metrizable spaces as metrizable space

Proposition

The coproduct of metrizable spaces in category Top is again metrizable.

Proof

Consider $\coprod_{i \in I} M_i$ for some collection of metrizable spaces $M_i$. choose metrics which are bounded by a fixed constant, w.l.o.g. $1$ (cf. existence of a topologically equivalent, bounded metric for a metric space). Then for each $M_i$ choose a basepoint $m_i$.

Then define

\begin{align*} d(x,y) \coloneqq \begin{cases} d_i(x,y) & \mbox{if } x,y \in M_i \\ d_i(x,m_i) + d_j(y,m_j) + 2 & \mbox{if } x \in M_i, y \in M_j \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

It can be shown that this defines a topologically equivalent metric.
