elements may not survive in multiplicative spectral sequence, but their product

Proposition

Suppose we have a multiplicative spectral sequence and under the internal multiplication of the $E^2$ page we get for $x \in E^2_{p,q}, y \in E^2_{p',q'}$ that $x \cdot y \in E^2_{p+p',q + q'}$ survives to the $E^3$ page. Then neither $x$ nor $y$ themself need to survive to the $E^3$ page

Proof
