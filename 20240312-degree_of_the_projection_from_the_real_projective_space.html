degree of the projection from the real projective space

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^n$ be the real projective space. Then the degree of the projection

\begin{align*} \pi: S^n \rightarrow \mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^n/ \mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{n-1} \cong S^n \end{align*}

is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{deg}(\pi) = 1 + (-1)^{n+1} \end{align*}

Proof

Consider the degre and local degree

Then we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{deg}(f) =& \mathrm{deg}_N(f) + \mathrm{deg}_S(f) \\ =& \mathrm{deg}_N(f) + \mathrm{deg}_S(f \circ (- \mathrm{id}_{S^n}) ) \end{align*}

applying the multiplicativity of the local degree results in

\begin{align*} # \mathrm{deg}_N(f) + \mathrm{deg}_S(f \circ (- \mathrm{id}_{S^n}) ) \end{align*}
