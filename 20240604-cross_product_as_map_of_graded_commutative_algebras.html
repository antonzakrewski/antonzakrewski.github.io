cross product as map of graded-commutative Algebras

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring (?), $H^*(-;R)$ the singular cohomology algebra functor. Then the cross product of singular cohomology

\begin{align*} \times: H^*(X;R) \otimes_R H^*(Y;R) \rightarrow H^*(X \times Y;R) \end{align*}

is a morphism of graded Algebras

Proof

Let $\alpha_1 \in H^p(X;R), \alpha_2 \in H^{p'}(X;R), \beta_1 \in H^q(Y;R), \beta_2 \in H^{q'}(Y;R)$ Then

\begin{align*} (-1)^{\vert \alpha_{2} \vert \vert \beta_1 \vert} (\alpha_1 \cup \alpha_2) \times (\beta_1 \cup e_2) =& (-1)^{\vert \alpha_2 \vert \vert \beta_1 \vert} \Delta^*_X(\alpha_1 \times \alpha_2) \times \Delta^*_Y(\beta_1 \times \beta_2) \\ =& (-1)^{\vert \alpha_2 \vert \vert \beta_1 \vert } (\Delta_X \times \Delta_Y)^* (\alpha_1 \times \alpha_2 \times \beta_1 \times \beta_2) \\ =& (-1)^{\vert \alpha_2 \vert \vert \beta_1 \vert} (\Delta_X \times \Delta_Y)^* (\alpha_1 \times (-1)^{\vert \alpha_2 \vert \vert \beta_1 \vert} t^*(\beta_1 \times \alpha_2) \times \beta_2) \\ =& (\Delta_X^* \times \Delta_Y^*) (\mathrm{id}_{X} \times t \times \mathrm{id}_{Y}) (\alpha_1 \times \beta_1 \times \alpha_2 \times \beta_2) \\ =& \Delta_{X \times Y} (\alpha_1 \times \beta_1 \times \alpha_2 \times \beta_2) \\ =& (\alpha_1 \times \beta_1) \cup (\alpha_2 \times \beta_2) \end{align*}
