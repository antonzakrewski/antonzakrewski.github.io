surjection of a complete variety as complete variety

Proposition

Let $Y$ a quasi-projective variety, $X$ a complete quasi-projective variety and $f: X \twoheadrightarrow Y$ a surjective regular map Then $Y$ is also a complete variety

Proof

Let $A \subseteq Y \times Z$ be closed. Then so is $(f,\circ)^{-1}[A]$ by continuity of $f$
