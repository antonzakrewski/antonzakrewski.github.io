right bousfield localization

Definition

A right bousfield localization is a dwyer-kan localization $\mathcal{R}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ which is a infinity right adjoint to some fully faithful $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$

Note that we may often

\begin{align*} \mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathcal{C} \end{align*}
