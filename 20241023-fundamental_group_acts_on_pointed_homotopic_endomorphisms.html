pi_1 for a fibration acts on pointed homotopic endomorphisms

Proposition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a serre fibration, $e \in E$ and $E_{p(e}}$ the fibre. Then the fundamental group acts on the automorphism group of $E_{p(e)}$ in Category hTop*

Proof

follows from:

  • fiber functor to pointed hTop
  • group action as functor
