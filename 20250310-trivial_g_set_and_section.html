trivial group and G-Sets with section

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group.

TFAE:

  1. $G = 1$ is the trivial group
  2. each G-Set $G \rightarrow X$ admits a G-equivariant section
  3. each G-Set splits into a disjoint union of copies of $G$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 3)

3) $\implies$ 1)
