Artinian ring and finite amount of maximal ideals

Proposition

Let $A$ be an Artinian ring, then $\mathrm{Spec}_{\mathrm{max}}(R)$ is finite

Proof

Suppose there exist infinitely many maximal ideals $\mathfrak{m}_i$. Let $\mathfrak{m}_n$ for $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ be countably, pairwise different many of those. Then

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{m}_1 \supseteq \mathfrak{m}_1 \cap \mathfrak{m}_2 \supseteq ... \end{align*}

is a Chain.

Furthermore, $\bigcap_{i=1}^n \mathfrak{m}_i \neq \{0\}$ (see: finite intersection of maximal ideals as zero ideal and finite amount of maximal ideals) and $\mathfrak{m}_i \not\subseteq \mathfrak{m}_j$ for $i \neq j$, we get a chain, that doesn't stabilize (cf. prime avoidance for the intersection of ideals) Contradiction
