poset of radical ideals is a locale

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. Then the partially ordered set of radical ideals is a locale

Proof

supremum

Let $K_i$ be radical ideals, then the supremum is the radical ideal generated by $\bigcup K_i$.

infimum

observe that the intersection of radical/prime ideals is radical ideal (although suprema suffice for existence).

distributivity

Let $K, L_i$ be radical ideals Then we need to show that

\begin{align*} K \cap \sqrt{\sum L_i} = \sqrt{\sum_{i} (K \cap L)} \end{align*}

but this follows from $K \cap L = \sqrt{K L}$ (cf. radical of the product of ideals and intersection) - i.e. and product and sum of ideals distribute
