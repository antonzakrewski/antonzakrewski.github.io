dual vector space functor of finite vect as monoidal functor

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathrm{Vect}_{K, \mathrm{fin}}$ the category of finite vector spaces. Then the internal hom is a monoidal functor

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(,K): \mathrm{Vect}_{K,\mathrm{fin}}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Vect}_{K,\mathrm{fin}} \end{align*}
