commutative tensor functor of modules

Definition / Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $M$ an \(R\)-module. Then the tensoring functor is defined as covariant functor for the category of Modules $\mathrm{RMod}$

\begin{align*} M \otimes_R -: \mathrm{RMod} \rightarrow& \mathrm{RMod} \\ N \mapsto& M \otimes_R N \\ (f: N_1 \rightarrow N_2) \mapsto& (\mathrm{id} \otimes_R f: M \otimes_R N_1 \rightarrow M \otimes_R N_2) \end{align*}
