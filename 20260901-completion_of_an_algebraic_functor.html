completion of an algebraic functor along a closed subset

Definition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor and $Z \subseteq \vert X \vert$ a closed subset. Let $K = \vert X \vert \setminus Z$ be the open complement and $I$ the quasi-coherent radical ideal classifying the open subfunctor.

The completion $X_{K}^{\wedge}$ of $X$ along $K$ is the algebraic functor

\begin{align*} X^{\wedge}_{K}(R) = \{x \in X(R) \vert I(x) = \sqrt{0}\} \end{align*}

Remark

It is in general not a scheme or a closed subfunctor.
