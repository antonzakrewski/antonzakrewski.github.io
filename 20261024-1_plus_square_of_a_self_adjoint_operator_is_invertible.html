1 plus square of a self adjoint operator is bounded below

Proposition

Let $T$ be a self-adjoint operator. Then $(1 + T^2)$ is bounded below by $1$.

Proof

follows from

\begin{align*} \langle (1 + T^2) v, (1 + T^2) v \rangle =& \langle v,v\rangle + \langle T^2 v, T^2 v\rangle + \langle v, T^2 v\rangle + \langle T^2 v, v\rangle \\ =& \| v \|^2 + \| T^2 v\|^2 + 2 \| T v \|^2 \\ \geq& \| v \|^2 \end{align*}
