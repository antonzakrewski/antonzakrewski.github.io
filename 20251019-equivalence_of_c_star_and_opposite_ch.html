equivalence of commutative, unital C-star Algebras and opposite CH

Proposition

The functor

\begin{align*} C_0(-): \mathrm{CH}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow C^*\mathrm{Alg}^{\mathrm{un}, \mathrm{comm}} X \mapsto C_0(X) \end{align*}

is an equivalence of categories with inverse given by $\mathrm{Hom}_{C^*-\mathrm{Alg}}(-, \mathbb{C})$ together with the pointwise convergence topology.

Proof
