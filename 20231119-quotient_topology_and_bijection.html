quotient map and bijection

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space, $\pi: X \rightarrow Y$ and $\pi': X \rightarrow Y'$ be quotient maps. Suppose $\varphi: Y \rightarrow Y'$ is a Bijection, such that 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
X \arrow[phantom, near start]{dr}{\circlearrowleft}  \arrow[]{r}{\pi} \arrow[swap]{d}{\pi'} & Y \\
 Y' \arrow[leftrightarrow, swap]{ru}{\varphi} & \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

commutes. Then $\varphi$ is a homeomorphism

Proof

By assumption,

\begin{align*} \varphi \circ \pi =& \pi' \\ \varphi^{-1} \circ \pi' =& \pi \end{align*}

where $\pi,\pi'$ are continuous. Hence we conclude by universal property of a quotient map, that $\varphi,\varphi^{-1}$ are continuous, hence a homeomorphism
