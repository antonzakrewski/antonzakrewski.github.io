lie quotient by a closed, covering like acting subgroup

Proposition

Let $G$ be a lie group and $H$ a subgroup which acts via conjugation covering-like. Then the lie quotient $G/H$ is also a lie group and the projection

\begin{align*} \pi: G \rightarrow G/H \end{align*}

is a local diffeomorphism

Proof

local diffeo

  • quotient of smooth covering like group action from a finite group on a smooth manifold is a smooth manifold
  • quotient of a covering like, free group action from a finite group on a manifold is a topological manifold
