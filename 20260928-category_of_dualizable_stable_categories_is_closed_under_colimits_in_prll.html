Proposition

The full subcategory $\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}}^{\mathrm{dual}} \subseteq \mathrm{Pr}^{LL}_{\mathrm{st}}$ is closed under colimits

Proof

Let $I \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}}^{\mathrm{dual}}$ be a diagram. Then let $\mathrm{colim}_{I} \mathcal{C}_i = \mathcal{C}$ be the colimit in $\mathrm{Pr}^{LL}_{\mathrm{st}}$ (cf. inclusion of PrLL stable into PrL stable preserves colimits) and we need to show that this colimit is also dualizable.

Here each functor $\mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is strongly continuous and their images generate the target under colimits: For this it suffices to show that the image of $\coprod \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ generates the target or that the right adjoint is conservative (cf. image generating a presentable category under colimits and conservative adjoint). For this we pass to $\mathrm{Pr}^{R}$ and have $\lim \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \prod \mathcal{C}_i$, which is conservative (cf. forgetful functor from the limit of categories to the product is conservative) and the,

Remark

coproducts in $\prod \mathcal{C}_i$ are computed pointwise, so both the generated subcategory from the images of $\mathcal{C}_i$ and $\prod \mathcal{C}_i$ agree.
