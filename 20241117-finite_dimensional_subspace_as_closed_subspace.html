finite dimensional subspace of a normed space is closed and complete subspace

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{H}$ be a normed vector space / Hilbert space, $U \subseteq \mathcal{H}$ a finite dimensional subspace. Then $U$ is closed

Proof

using Charakterisierung einer abgeschlossene Menge durch konvergente Folgen in einem metrischen Raum:

suppose $u_n$ is a sequence in $U$ with $u_n \rightarrow u$.

It remains to show that $u \in U$

using Gram-Schmidt we may find an orthonormal basis in $U$. Then

\begin{align*} u_n = \sum \alpha_{i,n} e_i \end{align*}

for some $\alpha_{i,n} \in \mathbb{K}$ In particular $u_n$ is Cauchy (cf. konvergente Folge als Cauchy-Folge) by Cauchy-Folge in einem Produktraum und Cauchy-Folge der einzelnen Räume we may conclude that each $\alpha_{i,n}$ is cauchy. Since $\mathbb{K}$ is complete each $\alpha_{i,n}$ converges against some $\alpha_i$ Thus by Konvergenz in einem Produktraum it follows that

\begin{align*} u_n \rightarrow \sum \alpha_i e_i \end{align*}
