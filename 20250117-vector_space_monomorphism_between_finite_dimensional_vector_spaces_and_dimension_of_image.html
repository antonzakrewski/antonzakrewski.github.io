vector space monomorphism between finite dimensional vector spaces and dimension of image

Proposition

Let $f: V \rightarrow W$ be a vectorspace-homomorphism between finite dimensional vector spaces TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a vectorspace-monomorphism
  2. $\mathrm{dim}(V) = \mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{im}(f))$
  3. $\mathrm{dim}(V) \leq \mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{im}(f))$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows fromand the rank theorem

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(V) =& \mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{ker}(f)) + \mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{im}(f)) \\ =& \mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{ker}(f)) + \mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{im}(f)) \\ =& 0 + \mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{im}(f)) \\ =& \mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{im}(f)) \end{align*}

where we have used minimal kernel and monomorphism

2) $\implies$ 3)

special case

3) $\implies$ 1)

using minimal kernel and monomorphism it suffices to show that $\mathrm{ker}(f) = 0$ or equivalently $\mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{ker}(f)) = 0$ The rank theorem tells us

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(V) =& \mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{ker}(f)) + \mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{im}(f)) \\ \end{align*}

or

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(V) - \mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{im}(f)) = \mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{ker}(f)) \end{align*}

our assumptions tell us that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(V) - \mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{im}(f)) \leq 0 \end{align*}

hence $\mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{ker}(f)) \leq 0$ which implies $\mathrm{dim}(\mathrm{ker}(f)) = 0$ since the dimension of a vector space is always non-negative
