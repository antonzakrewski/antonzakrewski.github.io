1-categorical pullback is full subcategory of the comma category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be categories and

a cospan Then the \((1,1)\)-categorical pullback is a full subcategory of the comma category where the projections

\begin{align*} \mathrm{pr}_{\mathcal{C}}: \mathcal{C} \times_{\mathcal{D}} \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \\ \mathrm{pr}_{\mathcal{E}}: \mathcal{C} \times_{\mathcal{D}} \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{E} \\ \end{align*}

are exact

Proof
