colimit of a simplicial set as functor

Proposition

Let $X: \Delta^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ be a simplicial set. Then the colimit is given by the set of non degenerate simplices and the coprojections is the map

\begin{align*} X_n \rightarrow X_{\mathrm{non degenerate}} \end{align*}

assigning to each \(n\)-simplex the non degenerate simplex
