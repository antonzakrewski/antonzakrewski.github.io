tensor product of chain complexes of modules

Definition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod. Suppose $C$ is a chain complex of right \(R\)-modules and $D$ a chain complex of left \(R\)-Modules.

Then the tensor product $C \otimes_R D$ is defined as

\begin{align*} (C \otimes_R D)_n \coloneqq \bigoplus_{p + q = n} C_p \otimes_R D_q \end{align*}

with differentials defined on elemantery tensors

\begin{align*} d_n^{C \otimes_R D}(x \otimes y) \coloneqq d^C(x) \otimes y + (-1)^{\vert x \vert} \otimes d^{D}(y) \end{align*}
