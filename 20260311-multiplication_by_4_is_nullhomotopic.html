multiplication by 2n is nullhomotopic

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category, $X$ an object. Then the map $\cdot 2n: X/n \rightarrow X/n$ is nullhomotopic.

Proof

$\mathrm{Sp}$

a) $X = \mathbb{S}$

w.l.o.g. we may assume that $n$ is even (cf. multiplication by odd n is nullhomotopic) Consider the sequence $\mathbb{S} \xrightarrow{\cdot n} \mathbb{S} \rightarrow \mathbb{S}/n$ and apply $\mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(-, \mathbb{S}/n)$ to get an exact sequence

In particular this gives a long exact sequence on homotopy groups, which evaluates as (cf. some low homotopy groups of S/n)

hence $\pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}/n, \mathbb{S}/n))$ is an extension of $\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z}$ (here the even case is more subtle). Using short exact sequence and torsion it follows that each element in $\pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}/n, \mathbb{S}/n))$ is $2n$ torsion, so $\cdot 2n = 2n \cdot \mathrm{id} = 0$ (cf. multiplication by n is n times identity in Hom).

b) general $X$

follows as $X/n \simeq X \otimes \mathbb{S}/n$.
