fiber of the relative diagonal map

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a finitely complete pointed category (e.g. category of pointed anima) and $f \colon X \rightarrow Y$ be map. Then there exist a canonical equivalence

\begin{align*} \Omega \mathrm{Fib}(f) \simeq \mathrm{Fib}(\Delta: X \rightarrow X \times_{Y} X) \end{align*}

Proof

observe that $\Delta: X \rightarrow X \times_{Y} X$ is a split mono since the composition

\begin{align*} X \xrightarrow{\Delta} X \times_{Y} X \xrightarrow{\mathrm{pr}_1} X \end{align*}

is by construction the identity.

Moreover, the fiber of $X \times_{Y} X \xrightarrow{\mathrm{pr}_1} X$ is given by the $\mathrm{fib}(f)$: For this it is suffices to recall that

is a pullback by definition and hence both vertical fibers agree (cf. infinity pullback and fiber).

Now fiber of a split mono shows that the fiber of $\Delta \colon X \rightarrow X \times_{Y} X$ is given by $\Omega(\mathrm{fib}(X \times_{Y} X \rightarrow X) \cong \Omega (\mathrm{fib}(f))$
