mapification of a polynomial

Definition

Let $R$ be a ring, $R[X_i]$ the polynomial ring, $A$ an R-Algebra for $\psi: R \rightarrow A$. Then the mapification of $R[X_i]$ is defined as map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}: R[X_i] \rightarrow \mathrm{Maps}(A^i,R) f \mapsto ((a_i) \mapsto \varphi_{(\alpha_i)}(f)) \end{align*}

for the evaluation homomorphism $\varphi_{(\alpha_i)}$
