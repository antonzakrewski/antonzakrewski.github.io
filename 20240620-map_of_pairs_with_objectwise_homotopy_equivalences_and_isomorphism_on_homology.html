map of pairs with objectwise homotopy equivalences and isomorphism on homology

Proposition

Let $(X,A),(Y,B)$ be pairs of spaces and $f: (X,A) \rightarrow (Y,B)$ a map of pairs such that

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow& Y \\ f_{\vert A}: A \rightarrow& B \end{align*}

are homotopy equivalences

Then

\begin{align*} f: (X,A) \rightarrow (Y,B) \end{align*}

induces an isomorphism on singular homology

Proof

follows from

  • Five-Lemma
  • long exact sequence of homology of a pair of spaces
