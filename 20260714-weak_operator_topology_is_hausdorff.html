weak/strong/uniform operator topology is hausdorff

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be normed vector spaces and and $\mathrm{BL}(X,Y)$ be equipped with the weak operator / strong operator / uniform operator topology. Then each of the three topologies is hausdorff.

Proof

a)

we show that the weak operator topology is a separating family of seminorms (cf. locally convex topology is hausdorff for a separating family): So let $T \in \mathrm{BL}(X,Y)$ be a bounded linear operator with $T \neq 0$, then we need to find an $x$ and linear functional $l: Y \rightarrow \mathbb{K}$ with $l(T(x)) \neq 0$. Then there exists an $x \in X$ such that $T(x) \neq 0$ and now choose a linear functional $l: Y \rightarrow \mathbb{K}$ with $l(T(x)) = 1$ (cf. K is cogenerator in functional analysis).

b)

feinere Topologie und Hausdorff-Raum:

  • weak operator topology is coarser than the strong operator topology
  • strong operator topology is coarser than the uninform operator topology
  • weak operator topology is coarser than the uniform operator topology
