induced map into the completion of a ring

Definition

Let \(A\) be a ring, $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ an ideal and $\widehat{A}$ the completion with respect to $\mathfrak{a}$. Then there exists a canonical ring-homomorphism 

\begin{align*}
A \rightarrow \widehat{A}
\end{align}*

induced via the universal property and the projection
