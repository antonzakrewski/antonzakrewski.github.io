whitehead theorem for spectra

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a map of spectra TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an equivalence
  2. $f$ is an equivalence on each homotopy group

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

  • equivalence in an infinity category under functors

2) $\implies$ 1)

using:

  • natural equivalence between infinity functors and objectwise equivalence
