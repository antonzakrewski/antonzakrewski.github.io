pushout product

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}_1,\mathcal{C}_2, \mathcal{D}$ are categories, $\otimes: \mathcal{C}_1 \times \mathcal{C}_2 \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a bifunctor Suppose

\begin{align*} g: A \rightarrow& B \\ f: C \rightarrow& D \end{align*}

are morphisms in $\mathcal{C}_1$ resp. $\mathcal{C}_2$

Then the pushout product morphism - if it exists - is defined as morphism

\begin{align*} f \boxtimes g: A \otimes D \coprod_{A \otimes C} C \otimes B \rightarrow B \otimes D \end{align*}

induced by the universal property
