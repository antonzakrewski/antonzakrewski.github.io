cohomology of SO(4)

Proposition

The cohomology of $\mathrm{SO}(4)$ is given by

\begin{align*} H^*(\mathrm{SO}(4)) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } * = 0,6 \\ \mathbb{Z} \oplus \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } * = 3 \\ \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } * = 2,5 \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Furthermore the multiplicative structure is given by generators $\alpha \in H_2(\mathrm{SO}(4)), \beta \in H_3(\mathrm{SO}(4)), x \in H_3(\mathrm{SO}(4))$ together with the relations $x^2 = \alpha^2 = \beta^2 = \alpha \beta = 0$
