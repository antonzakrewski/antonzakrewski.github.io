image of a noetherian module as noetherian module

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $M$ a noetherian module over $R$ and $N$ another \(R\)-module. Suppose $\varphi: M \rightarrow N$ is a homomorphism, then $\varphi[M]$ is also noetherian

Proof

Let $S \subseteq \varphi[M]$ a submodule, then $\varphi^{-1}[S]$ is also a submodule (cf. preimage of a submodule as submodule). Therefore, by assumption $\varphi^{-1}[S]$ is finitely generated and so also $S$. Hence $\varphi[M]$ is noetherian
