restriction of a sheaf to an open set

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space, $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Op}(X)^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{V}$ a sheaf. Then for an open space $O \subseteq X$ the restriction is defined as inverse image sheaf for the open subspace inclusion

\begin{align*} \iota: O \hookrightarrow X \end{align*}

Explicite

Note that for $U \subseteq O$ we have

\begin{align*} \iota_*(\mathcal{F})(U)) =& \mathrm{colim}_{V \supseteq U} \mathcal{F}(V) \\ =& \mathcal{F}(U) \end{align*}
