pi_0 of pointed and unpointed maps for anima

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be anima, then there exists an exact sequence

\begin{align*} \pi_1(Y) \xhookrightarrow{} \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(X,Y)) \rightarrow \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,Y)) \end{align*}

where the first map is a split mono.

Proof

follows from:

  • fiber sequence of mapping anima, pointed mapping anima and target
  • fiber sequence of mapping anima, pointed anima and target splits for a group
