homotopy fibre weakly homotopy equivalent to the fibre for a serre fibration

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a serre fibration, $F_y$ the fibre and $\mathrm{hofib}_y$ the homotopy fibre then the induced map

\begin{align*} \iota: F_y \rightarrow \mathrm{hofib}_y \end{align*}

is a weak homotopy equivalence

Proof
