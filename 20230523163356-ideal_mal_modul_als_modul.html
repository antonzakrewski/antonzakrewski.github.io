product of a module with the ideal

Definition / Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $M$ an \(R\)-module and $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq R$ an ideal. Then the <modulideal> $\mathfrak{a}M \subseteq$ (besserer name bitte ?) is defined as submodule

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a}M \coloneqq \{a \cdot m \vert a \in \mathfrak{a}, m \in M\} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from <Kriterium für ein Untermodul> and definition of an ideal
