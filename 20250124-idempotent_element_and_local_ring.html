idempotent element and local ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a local ring. Then $0,1$ are the only idempotent elements

Proof

Let $e \in A$ be idempotent. Then by passing to the Residue field it follows that

\begin{align*} \overline{e} = \begin{cases} 1 0 \end{cases} \end{align*}

w.l.o.g. assume that $\overline{e} = 1$ (by setting $\overline{e}' = 1 - \overline{e}$). Then it follows that $e \not\in \mathfrak{m}$. Hence $e$ is a unit (cf. local ring and non-units as maximal ideal) By idempotent and trivial unit we may conclude that $e = 1$
