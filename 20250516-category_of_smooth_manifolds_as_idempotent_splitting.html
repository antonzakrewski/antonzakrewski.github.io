category of smooth manifolds is the idempotent completion

Proposition

The category of smooth manifolds is the idempotent completion of the category of

  1. open euclidean subspaces
  2. smooth maps as morphisms

Proof

corollary of:

  • category of smooth manifolds is idempotent complete
  • idempotent completion functor preserves and detects fully faithful functors
  • smooth retract of an open euclidean space is a smooth manifold
