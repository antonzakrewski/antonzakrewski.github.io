chain homotopy in the product category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}_i$ be abelian categories, $\prod \mathcal{A}_i$ the product category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\prod \mathcal{A}_i)$ the category of chain complexes. Suppose $(f^i), (g^i): B_*^i \rightarrow C_*^i$ are chain maps.

TFAE:

  1. $(f^i), (g^i)$ are chain homotopic
  2. $f^i,g^i$ are chain homotopic for each $i$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from the additive forgetful functor

2) $\implies$ 1)
