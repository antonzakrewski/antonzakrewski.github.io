graded ring

Definition

Let $M$ be a monoid and $R$ a ring. Then $R$ is said to be a \(M\)-graded ring, if there exists a decomoposition

\begin{align*} R = \bigoplus_{m \in M} R_m \end{align*}

into abelian groups $R_m$, such that for $m,n \in M$ it follows that

\begin{align*} R_m \cdot R_n \subseteq R_{m + n} \end{align*}
