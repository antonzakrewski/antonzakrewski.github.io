left homotopy between weak equivalences

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}, \mathrm{Cof}, \mathrm{Fib})$ be a model category, $f,g: X \rightarrow Y$ be morphisms Assume that $H: f \sim_l g$ is a left homotopy.

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a weak equivalence
  2. $g$ is a weak equivalence

Proof

Assume that $f$ is a weak equivalence, then since

\begin{align*} f = H \circ \iota_0 \end{align*}

we conclude by 2 out of three that $H$ is also a weak equivalence.

Then so is

\begin{align*} g = H \circ \iota_1 \end{align*}

cf. inclusion into a cylinder object as weak equivalence
