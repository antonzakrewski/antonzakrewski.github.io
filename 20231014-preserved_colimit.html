preserved colimit

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $J: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a diagram and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor. Suppose $CL \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ together with morphisms $\iota_j$ is a colimit, then $L$ is preserved under the functor, if the induced morphism between colimits and functor is an isomorphism 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
&  \mathcal{F}(\mathrm{colim}_I)  & \\
& \mathrm{colim}_{\mathcal{F} \circ I} \arrow[dashed]{u}{\rotatebox{90}{\sim}}  & \\
\mathcal{F}(C_i) \arrow[bend left]{uur}{} \arrow[]{ru}{} \arrow[]{rr}{}& & \mathcal{F}(C_j) \arrow[bend right]{uul}{} \arrow[]{lu}{} \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Note that $\mathcal{F}(\mathrm{colim}_I)$ with $\mathcal{F}(\iota_j)$ is a limit.
