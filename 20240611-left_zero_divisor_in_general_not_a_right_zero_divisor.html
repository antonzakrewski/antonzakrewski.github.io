Proposition

Proof

Consider the \(A\)-module

\begin{align*} A[X] \end{align*}

Then consider the endomorphism ring.

Let

\begin{align*} \partial: A[X] \rightarrow& A[X] \\ x^n \mapsto& x^{n-1} \mathrm{pr}_0: A[X] \rightarrow& A[X] \\ x^n \mapsto \begin{cases} 0 & \mbox{if } n \geq 1 \\ x^n & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} \partial \circ \mathrm{pr}_0 = 0 \end{align*}

But the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{inc}: A[X] \rightarrow& A[X] \\ x^n \mapsto& x^{n+1} \end{align*}

is a left-inverse of $\partial$.
