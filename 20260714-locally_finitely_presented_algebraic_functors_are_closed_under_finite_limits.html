locally finitely presented algebraic functors are closed under finite limits

Proposition

Let $I \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k, \mathrm{Set})$ be a finite diagram consisting of algebraic functor locally of finite presentation. Then the limit is also an algebraic functor locally of finite presentation.

Proof

follows as

  1. filtered colimits commute with finite limits in Set
  2. pointwise calculation of limits & pointwise calculation of colimits
