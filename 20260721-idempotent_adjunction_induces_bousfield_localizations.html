idempotent adjunction induces bousfield localizations

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ functors and $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$ an adjunction. Assume that this adjunction is an idempotent adjunction, then the inclusion $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{fix}} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is a right adjoint and the inclusion $\mathcal{D}_0 \hookrightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is a left adjoint with right adjoint $\mathcal{G} \circ \mathcal{F}$ and left adjoint $\mathcal{F} \circ \mathcal{G}$.

In other words, $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{fix}} \simeq \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{fix}}$ is a left Bousfield localization of $\mathcal{C}$ and a right bousfield localization of $\mathcal{D}$

Proof

$\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{fix}} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$

Let $c \in \mathcal{C}$ and $c_0 \simeq \mathcal{G}(d_0)$ be an element in $\mathcal{C}_0$. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(c, c_0) \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(c, \mathcal{R}(d_0)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(c), d_0) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}_0}(\mathcal{F}(c), d_0) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_0}(\mathcal{G}\mathcal{F}(c), \mathcal{G}(d_0)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_0}(\mathcal{G}\mathcal{F}(c), c_0) \end{align*}

where we have used that $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}$ are equivalence when restricted to $\mathcal{C}_0$ and $\mathcal{D}_0$. pointwise construction of left adjoints & pointwise construction of right adjoints use:
