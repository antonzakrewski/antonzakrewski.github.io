cotangent module of the symmetric algebra over an algebraic functor

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor and $M$ a quasi-coherent module over $X$. Then the map $f: \mathbb{A}(M) \rightarrow X$ admits a cotangent module, given by $\Omega_{f} = f^*(M) \in \mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{A}(M)}$.
