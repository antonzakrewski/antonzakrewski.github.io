arbitrary coproduct of moore spectra is a moore spectrum

Proposition

Let $A_i$ be abelian groups, $\mathbb{S}_{A_i}$ the moore spectra Then $\coprod_{i} \mathbb{S}_{A_i}$ is also a moore spectrum

Proof

follows as

  • homotopy functor is an additive functor from spectra
  • E-homology of spectra is additive functor from spectra
