zeroth homology ring structure of an anima valued monoid

Proposition

Let $M$ be an anima valued monoid, then there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} H_0(M, \mathbb{Z}) \simeq \mathbb{Z}[\pi_0(M)] \end{align*}

Proof

observe that $\tau_{\leq 0}: \mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ is symmetric monoidal (cf. n-truncation of anima preserves products). Therefore we get a map of groups

\begin{align*} M \rightarrow \pi_0(M) \end{align*}

which then becomes an isomorphism on $H_0(-)$.

But $H_0(\pi_0(M), \mathbb{Z})$ can be identified with the group ring, since the inclusion $\mathrm{FreeAb} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{D}(\mathbb{Z})$ is symmetric monoidal.
