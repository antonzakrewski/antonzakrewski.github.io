affine space as frechet-topology

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{A}_K^n$ the affine space with the zariski topology. Then $\mathbb{A}_K^n$ is a Frechet-topology

Proof

Äquivalente Charakterisierung eines T1-Raums: Let $(a_1,...,a_n) \in \mathbb{A}_K^n$. Then

\begin{align*} \{(a_1,...,a_n)\} = V( \langle x_i - a_i\rangle) \end{align*}

Thus $\{(a_1,...,a_n\}$ is closed and hence $\mathbb{A}_K^n$ T1
