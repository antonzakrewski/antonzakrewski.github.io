homology and cohomology of a closed, connected surface

Proposition

Let $M$ be a surface of genus $g$. Then

  1. if $M$ is orientable, then
\begin{align*} H_*(M) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } * = 0 \\ \mathbb{Z}^{2g} & \mbox{if } * = 1 \\ \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } * = 2 \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

and the same cohomology

  1. if $M$ is not orientable
\begin{align*} H_*(M) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } * = 0 \\ \mathbb{F}_2 \oplus \mathbb{Z}^{g-1} & \mbox{if } * = 1 \\ 0 & \mbox{if } * = 2 \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
\begin{align*} H^*(M) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } * = 0 \\ \mathbb{Z}^{g-1} & \mbox{if } * = 1 \\ \mathbb{F}_2 & \mbox{if } * = 2 \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

use classification of closed two dimensional manifolds

  • pi_1 of a closed surface
  • first homology of a non-orientable surface
  • universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology / Poincare Dualität
