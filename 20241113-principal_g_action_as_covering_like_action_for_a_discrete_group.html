principal g-action as covering like action for a discrete group

Proposition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a principal action for a discrete topological group. Then this corresponds precisely a covering-like group action

\begin{align*} \cdot: G \times E \rightarrow E \end{align*}

after choosing an open cover of $B$ with $p^{-1}[U_i] \cong U_i \times G$

\begin{align*} g,e \mapsto g \cdot e \end{align*}

Proof

principal ~> covering like
