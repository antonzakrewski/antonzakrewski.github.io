joyal equivalence between simplicial sets

Definition

Let $\mathrm{sSet}$ be the category sSet and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a morphism of simplicial sets Then $f$ is said to be a joyal equivalence, if for all infinity categories $\mathcal{C}$ the induced map

\begin{align*} f^{ \leftarrow}: \mathrm{Fun}(Y, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is a joyal equivalence
