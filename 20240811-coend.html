1-Coend

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor Then an coend of $\mathcal{F}$, $\int^{c: \mathcal{C}} \mathcal{F}(c,c)$ is defined as cowedge, which is universal, i.e. for any other cowedge $w$

there exists a unique induced morphism in $\mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{D})$ such that

\begin{align*} \theta_{c} = f \circ \iota_c \end{align*}
