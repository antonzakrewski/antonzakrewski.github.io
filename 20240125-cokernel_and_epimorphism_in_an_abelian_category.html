cokernel and epimorphism in an ab-enriched, pointed category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a pointed and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a morphism

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an epimorphism
  2. the cokernel is the zero object: $\mathrm{coker}(f) = 0$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

corollary of epimorphism with zero as cokernel

2) $\implies$ 1)

Suppose there exists $g,h: Y \rightarrow Z$ such that

\begin{align*} g \circ f = h \circ f \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} g \circ f - h \circ f =& (g - h) \circ f \end{align*}

and hence by universal property we get

Thus it follows, that

\begin{align*} g - h =& 0 \\ g =& h \end{align*}
