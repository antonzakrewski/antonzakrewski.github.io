monoidal functor induces functor on category of bialgebras

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}}), (\mathcal{W}, \otimes_{\mathcal{W}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{W}})$ be monoidal categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{V} \rightarrow \mathcal{W}$ be a monoidal functor Then $\mathcal{F}$ induces a functor on the category of bialgebras.
