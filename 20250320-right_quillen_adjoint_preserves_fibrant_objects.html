right quillen adjoint preserves fibrant objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a right quillen adjoint. Then $\mathcal{F}$ preserves fibrant objects

Proof

follows from:

  • right adjoint preserves limits / terminal object as empty limit
  • right quillen adjoint preserves (trivial) fibrations

where

\begin{align*} X \rightarrow * \end{align*}

is a fibration
