compact hausdorff space in general not weakly locally connected

Proposition

Let $X$ be a compact hausdorff topological space. Then $X$ is in general not weakly locally connected.

Proof

consider

\begin{align*} \{\frac{1}{n} \} \coprod \{0\} \subseteq \mathbb{R} \end{align*}

It is a closed subset of $[0,1]$, hence compact. It is a subspace.
