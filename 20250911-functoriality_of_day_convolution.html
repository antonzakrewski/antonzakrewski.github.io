functoriality of day convolution with respect to lax monoidal functors

Proposition

Let $p: \mathcal{C}' \rightarrow \mathcal{C}, q: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}'$ be (lax) monoidal functors where the symmetric monoidal categories satisfy the conditions in the definition of day convolution. Then precomposition resp. postcomposition induce (lax) monoidal functors

\begin{align*} q_*: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}') \\ p^*: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}', \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

Proof
