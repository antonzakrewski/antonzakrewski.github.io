functor category of additive functors for a cocomplete codomain as cocomplete category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be Ab-enriched categories, where $\mathcal{B}$ is a cocomplete Then the functor category of additive functors

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}) \end{align*}

is also codcomplete

Proof

  • colimit of additive functors as additive functor
  • functor category for a cocomplete codomain as cocomplete category
