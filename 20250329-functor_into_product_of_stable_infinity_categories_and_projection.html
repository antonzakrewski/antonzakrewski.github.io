functor into product of stable infinity categories and projection

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_{\alpha}, \mathcal{D}$ be stable infinity categories and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \prod \mathcal{C}_{\alpha} \end{align*}

an infinity functor

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is an exact infinity functor
  2. $\pi_{\alpha} \circ \mathcal{F}$ is an exact functor for all $\alpha$
