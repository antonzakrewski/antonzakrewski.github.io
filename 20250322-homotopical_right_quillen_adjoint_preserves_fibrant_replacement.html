homotopical right quillen adjoint preserves fibrant replacement

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}_{\mathcal{C}}, \mathrm{Cof}_{\mathcal{C}}, \mathrm{Fib}_{\mathcal{C}}), (\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{W}_{\mathcal{D}}, \mathrm{Cof}_{\mathcal{D}}, \mathrm{Fib}_{\mathcal{D}})$ be model categories. Suppose $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is a homotopical right-quillen functor.

Then a fibrant replacement

\begin{align*} X \rightarrow \tilde{X} \end{align*}

gets mapped to a fibrant replacement

\begin{align*} \mathcal{R}(X) \rightarrow \mathcal{R}(\tilde{X}) \end{align*}

Proof

  1. the weak equivalence $X \simeq \tilde{X}$ gets preserved by assumption
  2. right quillen adjoint preserves fibrant objects # the fibration $X \rightarrow *$ gets preserved:
