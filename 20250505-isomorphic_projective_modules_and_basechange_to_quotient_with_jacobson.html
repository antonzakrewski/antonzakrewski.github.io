Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring, $P,Q$ be finitely generated projective modules and $J \subseteq \mathrm{Jac}(R)$ be a 2-sided ideal TFAE:

  1. $P,Q$ are isomorphic
  2. $P \otimes_{R} R/J, Q \otimes_{R} R/J$ are isomorphic
