multidegree

Definition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $f \in R[X_1,...,X_n]$ a polynomial ring and $>$ a monomial order Then the multidegree of $f$ is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{multideg}(f) \coloneqq \mathrm{max} \setminus\{\alpha \in \mathbb{N}_0^{n} \vert \alpha_{\alpha} \neq 0\}\} \end{align*}
