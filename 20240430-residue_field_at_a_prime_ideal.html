residue field at a prime ideal

Definition

Let \(A\) be a ring, $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ the zariski spectrum and $\mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ a prime ideal. Thne the residue field at $\mathfrak{p}$ is defined as

\begin{align*} k(\mathfrak{p}) \coloneqq& Q(A/\mathfrak{p}) \\ \coloneqq& A_{\mathfrak{p}} / \mathfrak{p}} A \end{align*}
