torsion free, divisible abelian group is a Q-Vector space

Proposition

Let $A$ be a torsion-free, divisible abelian group. Then $A$ is a \(\mathbb{Q}\)-vector space

Proof

it suffices to show that it is uniquely divisible (cf. Q-Vector space is uniquely divisible abelian group) So let $a$ be an element and $a_{\frac{1}{m}}, a_{\frac{1}{m}}'$ be two elements with $m \cdot a_{\frac{1}{m}} = a = a_{\frac{1}{m}}'$. Then $m \cdot (a_{\frac{1}{m}} - a_{\frac{1}{m}}') = 0$ and hence - as $A$ is torsion free - $a_{\frac{1}{m}} - a_{\frac{1}{m}}' = 0$ or $a_{\frac{1}{m}} = a_{\frac{1}{m}}'.
