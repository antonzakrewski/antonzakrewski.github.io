exactness condition of a sheaf on a topological space

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Op}(X)^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{A} \end{align*}

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is a sheaf
  2. for any open $U$ and any covering $U_i$ the sequence

is exact
