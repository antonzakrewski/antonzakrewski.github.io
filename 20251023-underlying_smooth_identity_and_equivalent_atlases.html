underlying smooth identity and equivalent atlases

Proposition

Let $M$ be a Manifold and $\mathcal{A}_1, \mathcal{A}_2$ be smooth structures on $M$ TFAE:

  1. the identity on the underlying set $\mathrm{id}: (M, \mathcal{A}_1) \rightarrow (\mathcal{A}_2,M)$ is a smooth map
  2. the atlases are equivalent

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
