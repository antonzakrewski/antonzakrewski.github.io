classification of pointed oriented spherical fibrations

Proposition

Let $B$ be an anima, then pointed spherical fibrations are classified by maps

\begin{align*} B \rightarrow \mathrm{BAut}_*^+(S^d) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{BAut}_*^{+}(S^d)$ denotes the (non-full) infinity subcategory of $\mathrm{BAut}(S^d)$ containing autoequivalences which induce the identity on top homology

Proof

should follow by arguments similar to:

  • classification of pointed fibrations with conected typical fiber F
  • classification of oriented spherical fibrations
