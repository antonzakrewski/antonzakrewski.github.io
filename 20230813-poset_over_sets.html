poset over sets

Definition / Proposition

Let $M$ be a family of sets, then $\mathcal{C}$ with

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C}) =& M \\ \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(M_i,M_j) =& \begin{cases} \{\iota: M_i \rightarrow M_j \} & \mbox{if } M_i \subseteq M_j\\ \emptyset & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

is a welldefined category

Proof

$\mathrm{id}$, transitivity of subset
