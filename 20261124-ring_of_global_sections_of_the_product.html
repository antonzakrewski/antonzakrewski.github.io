ring of global sections of the product

Proposition

Let $X_i$ be algebraic functors, then $\Gamma(\prod X_i) \simeq \bigotimes_{A} X_i$.

Proof

follows as taking globla sections is a right adjoint $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}_{A}, \mathrm{Set}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Aff}_{A} \simeq (\mathrm{CAlg}_{A})^{\mathrm{op}}$ and hence products get mapped to coproducts in $\mathrm{CAlg}_{A}^{\mathrm{op}}$.
