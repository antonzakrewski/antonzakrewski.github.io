adjoint to left kan extension from anima

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a cocomplete category and $X\in \mathcal{C}$ an object. Let

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_(-) X: \mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

be the left kan extension.

Then its adjoint is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,-): \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{An} Y \mapsto \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y) \end{align*}

Proof

pointwise construction of right adjoints shows for any object $C \in \mathcal{C}$

\begin{align*} \mathcal{R}(C) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(*, \mathcal{R}(C)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}( \mathrm{colim}_* X, C) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, C) \end{align*}

where each equivalence is a natural equivalence
