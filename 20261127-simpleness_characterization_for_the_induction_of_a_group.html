simpleness criterion for the induction of a group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a finite group and $H$ a subgroup Let $W$ be a representation of $H$. For $s \in G$, write $H_{s}$ for $s H s^{-1} \cap H$.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathrm{Ind}_{H}^{G}(W)$ is a simple representation
  2. $W$ is a simple representation and for $s \in G \setminus H$, $\mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{s^{-1}H s} {\mathrm{conj}_{s}(W)$ and $\mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{H}(W)$ dont contain any isomorphic summand
  3. $W$ is a simple representation and for $s \in G \setminus H$, $\langle \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{s^{-1}H s} {\mathrm{conj}_{s}(W), \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{H}(W) \rangle = 0$

Proof

2) $\implies$ 3), 3) $\implies$ 2)

orthogonal characters and no isomorphic summand

other part

By abuse of notation, we omit the $\chi$ and write the representation for the character

using frobenius reciprocity over C & mackey decomposition of representations & additivity of characters we can write

\begin{align*} \langle \mathrm{Ind}_{H}^{G}(W), \mathrm{Ind}_{H}^{G}(W) \rangle =& \langle \mathrm{Res}_{H}^{G} \mathrm{Ind}_{H}^{G} W, W \rangle \\ =& \langle \bigoplus_{s \in H \backslash G / H} \mathrm{Ind}_{H_s}^{H} \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{s H s^{-1}} \mathrm{conj}_{s}(W), W\rangle \\ =& \sum_{s \in H \backslash G / H} \langle \mathrm{Ind}_{H_s}^{H} \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{s H s^{-1}} \mathrm{conj}_{s}(W), W\rangle \\ =& \sum_{s \in H \backslash G / H} \langle \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{s H s^{-1}} \mathrm{conj}_{s}(W), \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{H} W\rangle \\ =& \sum_{s \in H \backslash G / H, s \not\in H} \langle \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{s H s^{-1}} \mathrm{conj}_{s}(W), \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{H} W\rangle + \mathrm{Res}_{H}^{H} (W), \mathrm{Res}_{H}^{H} W\rangle \\ =& \sum_{s \in H \backslash G / H, s \not\in H} \langle \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{s H s^{-1}} \mathrm{conj}_{s}(W), \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{H} W\rangle + \langle W, W\rangle \end{align*}

where $H_s = s H s^{-1} \cap H$.

Here each term is $\geq 0$, since it inner product of characters is dimension of Hom.

1) $\implies$ 3)

Suppose $\mathrm{Ind}_{H}^{G}(W)$ is simple. Then by orthogonality of characters $\langle \mathrm{Ind}_{H}^{G}(W), \mathrm{Ind}_{H}^{G}(W) \rangle = 1$ and hence

\begin{align*} 1 =& \sum_{s \in H \backslash G / H, s \not\in H} \langle \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{s H s^{-1}} \mathrm{conj}_{s}(W), \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{H} W\rangle + \langle W, W\rangle \end{align*}

where $\langle W, W\rangle \geq 1$, therefore $\langle W,W\rangle = 1$ and $W$ is simple and $\sum_{s \in H \backslash G / H, s \not\in H} \langle \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{s H s^{-1}} \mathrm{conj}_{s}(W), \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{H} W\rangle = 0$ or each summand is zero (since it is nonnegative).

3) $\implies$ 1)

Suppose $W$ is a simple representation and for $s \in G \setminus H$, $\langle \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{s^{-1}H s} {\mathrm{conj}_{s}(W), \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{H}(W)\rangle = 0$. Then

\begin{align*} \sum_{s \in H \backslash G / H, s \not\in H} \langle \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{s H s^{-1}} \mathrm{conj}_{s}(W), \mathrm{Res}_{H_s}^{H} W\rangle + \langle W, W\rangle =& 0 + 1 \\ =& 1 \end{align*}

which imples that $\langle \mathrm{Ind}_{H}^{G}(W), \mathrm{Ind}_{H}^{G}(W) \rangle = 1$ or that $\mathrm{Ind}_{H}^{G}(W)$ is simple.
