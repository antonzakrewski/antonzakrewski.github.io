augmented tensor singular chain complex bifunctor

Definition

Given category Top and an abelian group $M$, the augmented singular chain complex tensor bifunctor is defined as functor to the category of augmented chain complexes

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(- \times -;M): \mathrm{Top} \times \mathrm{Top} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ch_*}^M(\mathcal{A}) \\ (X,Y) \mapsto& \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(X;M) \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(Y;M) \\ (f_1,f_2: (X,Y) \rightarrow (X',Y')) \mapsto& \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(f_1) \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(f_2): \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(X;M) \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(Y;M) \rightarrow \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(X';M) \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathrm{Sing}^{\mathrm{augmented}}(Y';M) \end{align*}

as functor composition of

  1. tensor product of chain complexes functor using augmented chain complexes as full subcategory of normal chain complexes
  2. augmented singular chain complex functor
