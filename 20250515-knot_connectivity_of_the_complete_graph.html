knot connectivity of the complete graph

Proposition

Let $K_n$ be the complete graph with $n \geq 2$. Then it has $n-1$ as knot connectivity of a graph

Proof

it is smaller that $n$ since $\vert V \vert = n$. It is at least $n-1$ since removing $d \leq n-1$ knots gives $K_{n-d}$ which is still connected
