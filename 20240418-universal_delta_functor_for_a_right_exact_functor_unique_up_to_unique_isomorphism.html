universal delta functor for a right exact functor unique up to unique isomorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories and $\mathcal{F}_0: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ a right exact functor Then a universal delta functor is, if it exists, unique up to unique natural isomorphism

Proof
