S-local objects of a presentable category as presentable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category, $S$ a small collection of mophisms. Let $\overline{S}$ denote the strongly saturated closure and $\mathcal{C}'$ the subcategory of W-local objects

Then $\mathcal{C}'$ is also presentable.
