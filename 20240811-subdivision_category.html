subdivision category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category Then the subdivision category $\mathcal{C}^{§}$ is defined as free category containing

  1. $\mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C}^{§}) \coloneqq \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C}) \coprod \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$
  2. for each morphism $f: A \rightarrow B \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$ there exists morphism
\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
A^{§} \arrow[]{r}{f_{A}^§} & f^{§} & \arrow[]{l}{f_B^§} B^{§}
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Note that there don't exist non trivial compositions
