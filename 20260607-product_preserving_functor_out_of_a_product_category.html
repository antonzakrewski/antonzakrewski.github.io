product preserving functor out of a product category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_1, \mathcal{C}_2, \mathcal{D}$ be a categories admitting finite products. Then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C}_1 \times \mathcal{C}_2, \mathcal{D}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C}_1, \mathcal{D}) \times \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C}_2, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

Proof
