compact objects in the functor category out of a compact anima into a module category

Proposition

Let $X$ be a compact anima and $\mathrm{Mod}(R)$ the infinity module category of a ring spectrum. Then the compact objects in $\mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{Mod}(R))$ are characterized as closure of objects $(\iota_{\pi_0(X)})_!(R)$ under compact colimits.
