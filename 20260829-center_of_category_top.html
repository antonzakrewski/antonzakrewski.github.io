center of category Top

Proposition

The center of category Top is trivial.

Proof

use that the identity functor $\mathrm{id}: \mathrm{Top} \rightarrow \mathrm{Top}$ postcomposed with the faithful forgetful functor $\mathrm{Top} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ is representable by $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Top}}(*,-)$. But $\mathrm{End}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Top}, \mathrm{Set})}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Top}}(*,-)) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Top}}(*,*) = \{\mathrm{id}\}$.

Now $\mathrm{id}$ is of course a natural transformation before and since the forgetful functor is faithful, <it induces a monomorphism $\mathrm{End}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Top}, \mathrm{Top})}(\mathrm{id}) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{End}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Top}, \mathrm{Set})}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Top}}(*,-))$.
