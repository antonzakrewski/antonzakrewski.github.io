stalkwise characterization of isomorphisms

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space and $\eta: \mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ a natural transformation

TFAE:

  1. $\varphi_{x}: \mathcal{F}_{x} \rightarrow \mathcal{G}_{x}$ is an isomorphism on all stalks
  2. $\varphi_{U}: \mathcal{F}(U) \rightarrow \mathcal{G}(U)$ is an isomorphism for all $U \in \mathrm{Open}(X)^{\mathrm{op}}$.
  3. $\mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ is an natural isomorphism

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

using stalkwise characterization of monomorphisms it suffices to show that $\varphi_{U}$ is surjective. By stalkwise characterization of epimorphisms we may assume that for each $t \in \mathcal{G}(U)$, there exists an open cover $U = \bigcup U_i$ and $s_i \in \mathcal{F}(U_i)$ such that $\varphi_{\vert U_i} = t_{\vert U_i}$. We claim that $(s_{i})_{\vert U_i \cap U_j} = (s_{j})_{\vert U_i \cap U_j}$: Here by naturality, we have that $\varphi((s_{i})_{\vert U_i \cap U_j}) = t_{\vert U_i \cap U_j} = \varphi((s_{i})_{\vert U_i \cap U_j})$ and since $\varphi_{\vert -}$ is injective by the stalkwise criterion before, we get $(s_{i})_{\vert U_i \cap U_j} = (s_{j})_{\vert U_i \cap U_j}$.

By the equalizer-sheaf condition, we get a section $s$ which restricts to $(s_{j})$ and this maps to a section $\varphi(s)$ with the property that $\varphi(s)_{\vert U_i} = t_{\vert U_i}$. Now the sections $\varphi(s)$ and $t$ agree on an open cover in $U$, hence by the sheaf condition, they are the same elements and we have shown injectivity.

2) $\implies$ 3)

natural equivalence between functors and pointwise equivalence

3) $\implies$ 1)

follows as taking the colimit is a functor (cf. colimit functor).
