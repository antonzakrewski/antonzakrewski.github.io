category PSet

Definition

The category of Pset is defined as category containing

  1. sets as objects
  2. nonempty sets of maps as morphisms: $F \subseteq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Set}}(A,B)$
  3. composition defined as
\begin{align*} F \circ F' = \{f \circ f' \vert f \in F, f' \in F'\} \end{align*}
