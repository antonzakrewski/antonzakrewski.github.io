Proposition

Let $G$ be a finite group. Then the representation ring $R_{\mathbb{C}}(G) \subseteq \mathrm{CL}(G, \mathbb{C})$ is the subring spanned by integer combinations

Here $\mathrm{Cl}(G, \mathbb{C})$ is equipped with the pointwise multiplication, not the convolution
