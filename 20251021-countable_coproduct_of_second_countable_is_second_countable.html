countable coproduct of second countable is second countable

Proposition

Let $U_n$ be secondcountable spaces for $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then $\coprod U_n$ is also second countable

Proof

definitions: Take $\mathcal{B} = \bigcup_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathcal{B}_n$ as basis if $\mathcal{B}_n$ are countable bases.
