restriction of a group homomorphism to the image as group homomorphism

Proposition

Let \(G,H\) be groups, $\varphi: M \rightarrow N$ a group-homomorphism and $L \subseteq N$ a subgroup such that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{im}(\varphi) \subseteq L \end{align*}

Then the map

\begin{align*} \varphi': M \rightarrow L \\ m \mapsto& \varphi(m) \end{align*}

is a welldefined group homomorphism

Proof

Properties of the group homomorphism are inherited by $\varphi'$, hence it follows from the definitions
