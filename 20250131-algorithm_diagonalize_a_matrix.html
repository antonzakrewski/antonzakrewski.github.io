algorithm: diagonalize a matrix

Problem

Let $A \in K^{n \times n}$ be a matrix. Suppose $A$ is diagonalizable (cf. criterias: diagonalizable Matrix).

Then to diagonalize it, one has to find an invertible matrix $S \in K^{n \times n}$ and a diagonal matrix $D$ such that

\begin{align*} A = S \cdot D \cdot S^{-1} \end{align*}
