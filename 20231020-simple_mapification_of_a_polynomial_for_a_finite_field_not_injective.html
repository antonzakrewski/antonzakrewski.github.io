simple mapification of a polynomial for a finite field not injective

Proposition

Let $F$ be a finite field, $F[X_1,...,X_n]$ the polynomial ring. Then the mapification $\mathrm{map}$ is not injective

Proof

consequence of the Pigeonhole principle, as

\begin{align*} \vert F[X_1,...,X_n] \vert = \infty \vert \mathrm{Maps}(F^n, F) \vert < \infty \end{align*}

alternatively:

  • Kleiner Satz von Fermat
\begin{align*} P(x_1,...,x_n) \coloneqq (x_1^q - x_1) \end{align*}
