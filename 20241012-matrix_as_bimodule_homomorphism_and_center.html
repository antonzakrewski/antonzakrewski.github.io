matrix as bimodule homomorphism and center

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $A: R^n \rightarrow R^m$ a left module-homomorphism represented by a matrix $A = (a_{i,j})$.

TFAE:

  1. $A$ is a bimodule homomorphism
  2. each $a_{i,j} \in C(R)$ is an element in the center

Proof

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows as for $v = (b_1,...,b_n) \in R^n$

\begin{align*} A(v) =& (\sum_{j} b_{j,1} a_{k,j))_{1,k} \\ =& (\sum_{j} a_{k,j} b_{j,1})_{1,k} \\ =& A^{\mathrm{right}}(v) \end{align*}

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows as the projections and coprojections $\iota: R \rightarrow R^n, \pi: R^m \rightarrow R$ are bimodule homomorphisms (see above) and the composition $R \rightarrow R$ is given by the element $a_{i,j}$ Thus it follows as the composition is also a bimodule homomorphism, that

\begin{align*} (a_{i,j})(x) =& a_{i,j}(1 \cdot x) \\ =& a_{i,j}(1) \cdot x \\ =& a_{i,j} \cdot x \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} (a_{i,j})(x) =& a_{i,j}(x \cdot 1) \\ =& x \cdot a_{i,j}(1) \\ =& x \cdot a_{i,j} \end{align*}

for arbitrary $x \in R$ showing $a_{i,j} \in C(R)$
