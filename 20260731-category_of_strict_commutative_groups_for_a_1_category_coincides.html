category of strict commutative groups for a 1-category coincides with E_infinity groups

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a (1,1)-Category with finite products. Then both the category of strict commutative groups in $\mathcal{C}$ and the category of category of cartesian commutative groups coincide.

Proof

recall that the $\mathrm{Grp} \simeq \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathrm{Grp}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}, \mathrm{op}}}(\mathrm{Set}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathrm{Grp}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}, \mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Set})$. Now consider the yoneda embedding $よ: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$, which is fully faithful and preserves limits. It induces a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sGrp}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{sGrp}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})) = \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathrm{Grp}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}, \mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Set})) \end{align*}

but currying of functors preserving limits / colimits shows that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathrm{Grp}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}, \mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Set})) \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}},\mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathrm{Grp}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}, \mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Set})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}},\mathrm{Grp}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}},\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{segal}, \mathrm{grplike}}(\mathrm{Fin}_*, \mathrm{Set})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{segal}, \mathrm{grplike}}(\mathrm{Fin}_*, \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}},\mathrm{Set})) \\ \end{align*}
