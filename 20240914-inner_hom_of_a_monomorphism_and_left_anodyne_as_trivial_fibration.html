inner hom of a monomorphism and left anodyne as trivial fibration

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ an left fibration $\iota: A \rightarrow B$ be a left anodyne map Then the map

\begin{align*} \langle f,\iota\rangle: X^B \rightarrow X^A \times_{Y^A} Y^B \end{align*}

is a trivial fibration
