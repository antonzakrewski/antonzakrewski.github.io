infinite loop functor out of spectrum objects

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a finitely cocomplete infinity category which admits terminal objects Then the infinity loop functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \Omega^{\infty}: \mathrm{Sp}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

given by evaluation at the nontrivial point of

\begin{align*} S^0_* \in \mathcal{S}_*^{\mathrm{fin}} \end{align*}
