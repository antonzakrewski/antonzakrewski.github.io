coimage

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an additive category and $f: M \rightarrow N \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{A})$ be a morphism. Then the coimage is defined as kernel / cokernel

\begin{align*} \mathrm{coim}(f) =& \mathrm{coker}(\iota: \mathrm{ker}(f) \hookrightarrow M) \\ =& M/\mathrm{ker}(f) \end{align*}
