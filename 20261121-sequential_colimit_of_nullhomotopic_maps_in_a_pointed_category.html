sequential colimit of nullhomotopic maps in a pointed category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a pointed category and $\mathrm{colim}_{\mathbb{N}} X$ a sequential colimit where for each $n$, there exists a $n'$ such that $X_n \rightarrow X_{n'}$ is nullhomotopic. Then $\mathrm{colim}_{\mathbb{N}} X \cong *$

Proof
