left exact functors out of an exact category into connective spectra are prestable

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{E}$ be an exact category and $\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{lex}}(\mathcal{E}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Sp}_{\geq 0}))$ be the full subcategory of the functor category containing left exact functors. Then this is a prestable category.
