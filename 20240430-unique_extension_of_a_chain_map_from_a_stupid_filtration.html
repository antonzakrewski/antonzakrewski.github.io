unique Extension of a chain map from a stupid filtration

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes. Suppose

are chain complexes $\sigma_{\leq n}(A), \sigma_{\leq n}(B)$ the stupid filtrations and $f_*^{\sigma} \sigma_{\leq n}(A) \rightarrow \sigma_{\leq n}(B)$ a chain map

Suppose furthermore that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}(A_{k}, H_k(B)) = 0 \mathrm{Hom}(A_{k+1}, H_k(B)) = 0 \end{align*}

for $k \geq n$.

Then there exists a chain map $f_*: A_* \rightarrow B_*$ extending $f_*^{\sigma}$, which is unique up to chain homotopy
