cofinal infinity functor as composition of right-anodyne with trivial fibration

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a morphism of simplicial sets

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ factors as right anodyne map followed by a trivial fibration
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ is coinitial

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

  • trivial fibration as cofinal right fibration
  • right anodyne and cofinal
  • infinity cofinal and composition

2) $\implies$ 1)

use the factorization of right anodyne followed by right fibration. Then

  • infinity cofinal right fibration as trivial fibration
  • infinity cofinal and composition
