homology of a noetherian module as noetherian module

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $M_2$ a Noetherian module and given a chain complex

Then the homology of

\begin{align*} H_n(M_3 \rightarrow M_2 \rightarrow M_1) \end{align*}

is also a Noetherian module

Proof

follows from

  • quotient of a noetherian module as noetherian module / submodule of a noetherian module as noetherian module

where

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ker}(M_2 \rightarrow M_1) \subseteq M \end{align*}

is a noetherian submodules, hence also the quotient.
