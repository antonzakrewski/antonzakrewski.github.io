pullback of a covering map as covering map

Proposition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a covering map and $f: X \rightarrow B$ a continuous map , then for the pullback

the map

\begin{align*} f: X \times_B E \rightarrow X \end{align*}

is also a covering map

Proof

see:

  • pullback of a fiber bundle
  • where a covering map is precisely a fiber bundle with discrete fibres
  • by pullback of a fibre bundle the fibres of the pullback bundle stay the same, i.e. discrete
