flat module and trivial tor functor

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $M$ an \(A\)-module.

TFAE:

  1. $M$ is flat
  2. for each $i > 0$ and \(A\)-module $N$, it holds for the Tor Functor
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tor}_i^A(M,N) = 0 \end{align*}
  1. for each \(A\)-module $N$, it holds for the Tor Functor
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tor}_1^A(M,N) = 0 \end{align*}
  1. for each ideal $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ and the quotient ring $A/\mathfrak{a}$ the term
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tor}_1^A(A/\mathfrak{a}, N) = 0 \end{align*}

is zero

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

left derived functor of an exact functor

2) $\implies$ 3)

special case

3) $\implies$ 4)

speical case

4) $\implies$ 1)

note that by flat module and A-flat it suffices to check, if for each ideal $\mathfrak{a}$ the exact sequence

is preserved

This holds if and only if $\mathrm{Tor}_{1}^A(A/\mathfrak{a}, M) = 0$ in the long exact sequence of a derived functor.
