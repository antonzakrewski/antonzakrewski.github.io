cotensor product with a simplicial set

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $K$ a simplicial set and $X \in \mathcal{C}$ an object. Then the cotensor product

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}^X \end{align*}

is defined as limit of the constant functor

\begin{align*} p: K \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}
