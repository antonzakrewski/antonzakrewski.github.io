chain cone functor

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes and $\mathrm{Arrow}(\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}))$ the arrow category Then the chain cone functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{cone}: \mathrm{Arrow}(\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \\ (\varphi: A_* \rightarrow B_*) \mapsto& \mathrm{cone}(\varphi) \\ (\alpha, \beta: \varphi \rightarrow \psi) \mapsto& (\alpha \oplus \beta): \mathrm{cone}(\varphi) \rightarrow \mathrm{cone}(\psi) \end{align*}
