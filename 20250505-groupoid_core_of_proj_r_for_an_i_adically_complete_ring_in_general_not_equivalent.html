groupoid core of Proj(R) for an I-adically complete ring in general not equivalent

Proposition

the functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Proj}(R)^{\simeq} \rightarrow \mathrm{Proj}(R/I)^{\simeq} \end{align*}

induced by $R \rightarrow R/I$ is not an equivalence for $I \neq 0$.

Proof

Let $i \in I$ with $i \neq 0$. Then consider the map

\begin{align*} R^{\times} \simeq \mathrm{Aut}(R,R) \rightarrow \mathrm{Aut}(R/I,R/I) \simeq (R/I)^{\times} \end{align*}

it follows that $1 + i$ lies in the kernel (considered as group homomorphism)
