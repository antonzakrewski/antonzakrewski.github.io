anima

Definition

Let $X$ be an category. Then $X$ is said to be an anima if every morphism is an equivalence

see:

  • infinity category of Anima

Name

You start with sets - empty husks with no life and only cardinality as invariants You then animate those husks to get Anima and if you stabilize an anima, you kill the nonlinear part, leaving spectra behind to haunt you.
