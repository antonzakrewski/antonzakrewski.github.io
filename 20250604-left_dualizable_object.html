left dualizable object

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a monoidal infinity category and $X$ an object. We say that $X$ is left dualizable if there exists another object $X^{\vee}$ (called the left dual of $X$) and a pair of maps

  1. $c: 1 \to X^{\vee} \otimes X$ (coevaluation)
  2. $e: X \otimes X^{\vee} \to 1$ (evaluation)
