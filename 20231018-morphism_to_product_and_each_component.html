Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\prod_{i \in I} C_i$ a categorical product and $\varphi, \psi: S \rightarrow \prod_{i \in I} C_i$ morphisms

TFAE:

  1. for each $i \in I$, we conclude that $\pi_i \circ \varphi = \pi_j \circ \psi$
  2. $\varphi = \psi$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

By assumption and universal property, there exists a unique morphism

Thus $\epsilon = \varphi = \psi$

2) $\implies$ 1)
