free module functor

Definition

Given category set, a ring $A$ and category AMod, the free module functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{FreeMod}: \mathrm{Set} \rightarrow \mathrm{AMod} X \mapsto& A^{X} \\ f \mapsto& f^{\vert X} \end{align*}

where $A^{X}$ is the free module generated by $X$ without any relations.
