triangulated category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an additive category Then $\mathcal{A}$ is said to be a triangulated category, if it is equipped with

  1. translation functor
  2. a family of exact triangles satisfiyng following axioms

a) TR1 of a triangulated category b) rotation of a triangulated category c) TR3 of a triangulated category d) [[id:ae32f52e-5a94-407c-a820-8ba017702f2b][octahedral axiom of a triangulated triangulated functorcategory]]
