preimage of a maximal ideal in general not a maximal ideal

Proposition

let $A,B$ commutative rings, $f: A \rightarrow B$ a homomorphism and $\mathfrak{b} \subseteq B$ a maximal ideal Then $f^{-1}[\mathfrak{b}]$ is in general not maximal

Proof

the inclusion

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{Q} \end{align*}

is a homomorphism and $\{0\} \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ is maximal (Nullideal als maximale Ideal und Körper). But $\iota^{-1}[\{0\}] = \{0\} \subseteq \mathbb{Z}$ is not maximal
