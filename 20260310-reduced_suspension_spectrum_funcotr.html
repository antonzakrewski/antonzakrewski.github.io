reduced suspension spectrum functor

Definition

The reduced suspension spectrum functor is defined as unique functor

\begin{align*} \tilde{\mathbb{S}}: \mathrm{An}_* \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp} \end{align*}

corresponding to $\mathbb{S}$ under the equivalences

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{red}}(\mathrm{An}_*, \mathrm{Sp}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{An}, \mathrm{Sp}) \supseteq \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}}(\mathrm{An}, \mathrm{Sp}) \simeq \mathrm{Sp} \end{align*}
