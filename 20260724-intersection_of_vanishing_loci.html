intersection of vanishing loci

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor and $F_i$ subsets of $\mathcal{O}(X)$. Then

\begin{align*} \bigcap_{i \in I} V(F_i) = V(\bigcup_{i \in I} F_i) \end{align*}

where the intersection is either the categorical limit or the pointwise intersection
