classification of effective epimorphism in anima

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a map of Anima TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an effective epimorphism
  2. $\pi_0(f)$ is surjective / an epimorphism

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

suppose $f$ is an effective epimorphism, i.e.

is a colimit diagram. Then applying $\pi_0$ gives a colimit diagram in category set (cf. pi_0 preserves colimits) and in particular, $Y/\sim \rightarrow X$ is an isomorphism This implies thta $Y \twoheadrightarrow Y/\sim \simeq X$ is an epimorphism or surjection.

2) $\implies$ 1)
