irreducible spec and nilradical

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ the affine scheme TFAE:

  1. the nilradical $\mathcal{N}_A$ is a prime ideal
  2. $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ is an irreducible topological space

Proof

corollary of:

  • irreducible space and ideal as prime ideal
  • Nilradical as intersection of prime ideals
