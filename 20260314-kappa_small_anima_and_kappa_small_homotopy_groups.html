kappa small anima and kappa small homotopy groups

Proposition

Let $\kappa > \omega_0$ be a regular cardinal and $X$ an anima TFAE:

  1. $X$ is kappa small anima
  2. $\pi_*(X)$ is $\kappa$ small for each $\kappa$.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

We show that countable anima have $\kappa$ small homotopy groups: If $X$ is a simply connected anima, then this follows from fundamental theorem of serre class theory for kappa small abelian groups is a derived serre ring.

So let $X$ be arbitrary. Then universal cover of a kappa small anima is kappa small.

2) $\implies$ 1)

we have shown that countable anima have countable homotopy groups. Then an argument akin to CW-Approximation of Anima suffices, where each step only uses countably many cells - hence each skeleton is still a countable cw complex.
