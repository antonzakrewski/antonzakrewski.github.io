union of nonvanishing loci in general not an equality

Proposition

Proof

consider $A = \mathbb{Z}$ and $F_1 = (2), F_2 = (3)$. Then $F_1 \cup F_2$ generates the unit ideal, so $D(F_1 \cup F_2) = \mathbb{Z}$. But $\mathrm{id} \in \mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})(\mathbb{Z})$ does not lie in $D(F_1)(\mathbb{Z})$ or $D(F_2)(\mathbb{Z})$
