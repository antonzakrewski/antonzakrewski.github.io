completion functor of filtered spectra

Proposition

The inclusion of complete filtered $\widehat{\mathrm{FilSp}} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{FilSp}$ admits a left adjoint, called the completion, which is objectwise given by

\begin{align*} \widehat{-}: \mathrm{FilSp} \rightarrow& \widehat{\mathrm{FilSp}} \\ X \mapsto& \mathrm{cofib}(X^{\infty} \rightarrow X^{\bullet}) \end{align*}

Proof
