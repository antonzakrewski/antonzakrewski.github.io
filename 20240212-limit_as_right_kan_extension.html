limit as right kan extension

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $J: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a diagram. Then a limit is precisely a right kan extension

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_{\mathcal{J}} = \mathrm{Ran}_{\mathrm{E}}(\mathcal{J}) \end{align*}

where

  1. $\eta(j) = \pi_j$
  2. $\mathcal{E}$ is the constant functor
