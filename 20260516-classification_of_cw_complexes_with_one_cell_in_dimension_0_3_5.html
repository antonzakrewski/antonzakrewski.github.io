classification of cw complexes with one cell in dimension 0,3,5

Proposition

Let $X$ be a CW-Complex with exactly one cell in dimension 0,3,5. Then

\begin{align*} X \cong& \begin{cases} \Sigma \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^2 & \mbox{if } \\ S^2 \wedge S^3 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

recall $\pi_2(*) \cong \{*\}, \pi_4(S^3) \cong \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}$ (cf. fourth homotopy group of the 3 sphere). hence there exist at most $2$ equivalence classes of cw complexes.

But we can write $\Sigma \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^2, S^3 \wedge S^5$ which have the desired cell structure (cf. suspension of a complex projective spectrum is not a wege of spheres).
