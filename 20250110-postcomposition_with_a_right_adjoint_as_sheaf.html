postcomposition with a right adjoint as sheaf

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a site, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a sheaf and $\mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ a right adjoint Then so is

\begin{align*} \mathcal{G} \circ \mathcal{F} \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of:

  • right adjoint preserves limits
  • postcomposition with a functor preserving limits as sheaf
