universal property of the torsion quotient

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod, $M$ an \(R\)-module. Let $M^{\mathrm{tf}}$ the torsion quotient, then for each module-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: M \rightarrow N \end{align*}

to a torsion-free module $N$

Proof
