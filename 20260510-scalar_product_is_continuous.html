scalar product is continuous

Proposition

Let $V$ be a Prähilbertraum, then the scalar product

\begin{align*} \langle -,-\rangle \colon V \times V \rightarrow \mathbb{K} \end{align*}

is a continuous map

Proof

observe that $V$ is a metric space, so we show that $\langle -,-\rangle$ is sequentially continuous. Let $(x_n,y_n) \xrightarrow{n \rightarrow \infty} x,y$ be a convergent sequence, i.e., $\| x - x_n \|, \| y - y_n \| \xrightarrow{n \rightarrow \infty} 0$. Then we may estimate

\begin{align*} \vert \langle x_n,y_n\rangle - \langle x,y\rangle \vert =& \vert \langle x_n,y_n\rangle - \langle x,y\rangle + \langle x_n,y\rangle - \langle x_n,y\rangle \vert \\ =& \langle x_n,y - y_n\rangle - \langle x-x_n,y\rangle \\ \leq& \vert \langle x_n,y - y_n\rangle \vert + \vert \langle x-x_n,y\rangle \vert \end{align*}

so it suffices to show that $\langle x_n,y - y_n\rangle, \langle x-x_n,y\rangle \xrightarrow{n \rightarrow \infty} 0$. This follows from the Cauchy-Schwarz Ungleichung:

\begin{align*} \vert \langle x_n, y - y_n \rangle \vert \leq& \| x_n \| \cdot \| y - y_n \| \end{align*}

where $\| x_n \|$ is constant and $\| y - y_n \| \xrightarrow{n \rightarrow \infty} 0$ by assumption (and similarly for $\langle x-x_n,y\rangle$).
