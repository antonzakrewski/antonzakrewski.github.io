n-cover adjunction of anima

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathrm{An}_{\geq n}$ be the full subcategory of category of Anima containing anima $X$ with

\begin{align*} \pi_{i}(X) = 0 \end{align*}

for $i \in \{0,...,n-1\}$

Then the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An}_{\geq n} \rightarrow \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

admits a right adjoint and $X_{\geq n} := \mathcal{R}(X)$ is characterized by the property that the counit $X \rightarrow X_{\geq n}$ is (pointwise) an isomorphism on $\pi_{l}(-)$ for $l \geq l$

Proof

Consider the n-truncation adjunction for anima. Then define

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An} \rightarrow& \mathrm{An} \\ X \mapsto& \mathrm{fib}(X \rightarrow X_{\leq n}) \end{align*}

then it follows for any $Y_{\geq n} \in \mathrm{An}_{\geq n}$ that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}(Y_{\geq n}, X_{\geq n}) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}(Y_{\geq n}, X) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}(Y_{\geq n},X_{\leq n}) \end{align*}

is a fiber sequence.

But $\mathrm{map}(Y_{\geq n},X_{\leq n}) \cong *$.
