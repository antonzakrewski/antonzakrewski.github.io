representation of a group is a functor from BG

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group and $k$ a field Then there exists an equivalence of categories

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{BG}, \mathrm{Vect}_{k}) \simeq& \mathrm{Rep}_{G} \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Mod}_{k[G]} \end{align*}

In particular, any map $f: V \rightarrow W$ of objects in $\mathrm{Mod}_{k[G]}$ is a morphism if it is \(k\)-linear and $G$ equivariant.

Proof

note that one of these equivalences is true by definition of $\mathrm{Rep}_{G}$ so it suffices to show that $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{BG}, \mathrm{Vect}_k) \simeq \mathrm{Mod}_{k[G]}$.
