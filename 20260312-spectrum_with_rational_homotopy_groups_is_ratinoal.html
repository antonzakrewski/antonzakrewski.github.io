spectrum with R-local homotopy groups is R-local

Proposition

Let $R = \mathbb{Z}[p^{-1} \vert p \in P] \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ be a subring and $X$ be a spectrum with $\pi_*(X)$ \(R\)-local abelian groups

Proof

follows from:

  • R-local spectrum and multiplication by p
  • homotopy functor is an additive functor from spectra
  • whitehead theorem for spectra
