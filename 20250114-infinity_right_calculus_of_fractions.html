infinity right calculus of fractions

Definition

Let $x \in \mathcal{C}$, $\mathcal{C}, W(x)$ infinity categories. Then a functor

\begin{align*} \pi: W(x) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

is said to be a right calculus of fractions, iff

  1. $W(x)$ has a terminal object $1$
  2. All morhpism in $W(x)$ with codomain $1$ get mapped to $W$
  3. $\pi$ is W-colocal functor
