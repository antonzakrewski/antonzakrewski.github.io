covering of a hausdorff space as hausdorff space

Proposition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ a covering map of topological spaces $E,B$, where $B$ is hausdorff. Then so is $E$.

Proof

Let $e,e' \in E$ be different points. Suppose $p(e) = p(e')$, then choose a trivializing cover of $B$. Then it follows, that $p(e) \in U_i$ and furthermore

\begin{align*} \coprod U_i \cong p^{-1}[U_i] \end{align*}

Note that $p$ is injective on each $U_i$, hence $p(e), p(e')$ are elements of different $U_i$'s. Thus $U_i,U_i'$ suffice.

Suppose $p(e) \neq p(e')$, then choose disjoint neighbourhoods $U_e, U_e'$ of $p(e),p(e')$. Then the preimage is open and disjoint.
