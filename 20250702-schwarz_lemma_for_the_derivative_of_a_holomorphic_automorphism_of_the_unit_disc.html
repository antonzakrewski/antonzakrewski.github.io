schwarz lemma for the derivative of a holomorphic automorphism of the unit disc

Proposition

Let $f: \mathbb{D} \rightarrow \mathbb{D}$ be a holomorphic function of the open unit ball with $f(0) = 0$. Then $\vert f'(-) \vert \leq 1$ for $z \in \mathbb{D}$ with equality for $z_0 \in \mathbb{D}\setminus \{0\}$ if and only if $f(-) = \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i} \theta} \cdot -$ for some $\theta$

Proof

using schwarz lemma for holomorphic automorphisms of the unit disc

reminder

Let $g \coloneqq \frac{f(z)}{z}$. Then this function extends via holomorphic function divided by zero as holomorphic function.

inequality

Let $R \leq 1$, then for $z \in C_R$ we get

\begin{align*} \vert g(z) \vert = \frac{\vert f(z) \vert}{\vert z \vert} \leq \frac{1}{R} \end{align*}

since $f(z) \in \mathbb{D}$ or $\vert f(z) \vert \leq 1$

Thus by the maximum modulus principle $g(z) \leq \frac{1}{R}$ on $z \in \overline{D}_{R}$. Now let $R \rightarrow 1$ to get

\begin{align*} \vert g(z) \vert \leq 1 \end{align*}

which shows the inequality

\begin{align*} g(0) =& \mathrm{lim}_{z \rightarrow 0} g(z) \\ =& \mathrm{lim}_{z \rightarrow 0} \frac{f(z) - f(0)}{z} \\ \end{align*}

and the proof shows $g(0) \leq 1$
