amount of edges of the hypercube graph

Proposition

Let $Q_d \coloneqq [0,1]^d$ denote the hypercube of dimension $d$ as Graph Then $Q_d$ contains precisely $d \cdot 2^{d-1}$ edges

Proof

Let $f_d$ denote the amount of edges of $Q_d$.

recursion formula

Note that $Q_d$ contains 2 copies of $Q_{d-1}$ where the opposite edges are connected via a single edge. Hence

\begin{align*} f_d = 2 f_{d-1} + 2^{d-1} \end{align*}

since $Q_{d-1}$ contains $2^{d-1}$ edges.

induction formula

Proof by induction

induction basis

clear for $d = 0$

induction step

Then by the recursion formula

\begin{align*} f_{d} =& 2 \cdot f_{d-1} + 2^{d-1} \\ =& 2 \cdot ((d-1) \cdot 2^{d-2}) + 2^{d-1} \\ =& d \cdot 2^{d-1} \end{align*}
