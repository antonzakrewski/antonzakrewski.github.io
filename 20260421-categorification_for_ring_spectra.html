categorification for ring spectra

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Sp}) \rightarrow \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\kappa})$ be the categorification. Then the essential image consists of

  1. stable, presentable categories
  2. where the tensor product $- \otimes -: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ preserves colimits in both variables (follows as lies in $\mathrm{Pr}^L$
  3. where the unit object is compact and generates $\mathcal{C}$ in the following sense: $\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(c,-): \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}$ is a conservative functor
