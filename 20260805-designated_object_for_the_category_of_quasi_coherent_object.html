designated object for the category of quasi-coherent object

Remark

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{CAlg} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{op}}$ be a functor. Assume that there exists a natural transformation from $\mathcal{F}$ to the constant funcor $\mathrm{cons}(*)$, which becomes a natural transformation $*(-) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(-)$ in $\mathrm{Cat}$. Then passing to functor of quasi-coherent objects induces a designated object in $\mathcal{F}(X)$ for $X \in \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}, \mathrm{Set})$.
