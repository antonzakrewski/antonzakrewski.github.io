functor mapping commutative rings to line bundles via extension of scalars

Proposition

The

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Line}_{-}: \mathrm{CRing} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat} \\ R \mapsto& \mathrm{Line}_R \\ (f: R \rightarrow S) \mapsto& - \otimes_{R} S: \mathrm{Line}_R \rightarrow \mathrm{Line}_S \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • invertible module as algebraic line bundle
  • extension of scalars is symmetric monoidal
  • monoidal functor induces functor on invertible objects
