Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a locally small category, $\mathcal{F} \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,-): \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ a covariant (or contravariant representable functor) Then $\mathcal{F}$ is a projective object in the functor category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Set}) \end{align*}
