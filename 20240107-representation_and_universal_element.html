representation and universal element

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a locally small category and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} \end{align*}

a functor, $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and $a \in \mathcal{F}(A)$

TFAE:

  1. $(A,a)$ is a representation of $\mathcal{F}$
  2. $(A,a)$ is a universal element of $\mathcal{F}$

Proof
