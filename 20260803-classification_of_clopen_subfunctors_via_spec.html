classification of clopen subfunctors via spec

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor, then there exists a Bijection

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}(X, \mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z})) \simeq \{\textrm{clopen subfuntcors of } X\} \\ \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • classification of clopen subfunctors via idempotents
  • coordinate ring and spec adjunction
