cartesian Monoid

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category which admits finite products Then a cartesian monoid is an infinity simplicial object $X: \Delta^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ such that

  1. $X_0 \simeq *$ is a terminal object
  2. $X$ satisfies the segal condition, i.e.
\begin{align*} X_n \rightarrow \prod X_1 \end{align*}

(note that $X_0 \cong *$ shows that $X_1 \times_{X_0} X_1 \cong X_1 \times X_1$ - todo)
