universal cover of a nice space as connected cover

Proposition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a universal cover, where $B$ is locally and globally path-connected. Then $E$ is connected

Proof

Let $e \in p^{-1}(b)$ and let $E_e$ be the connected component. Then $E_e$ is open, since $E$ is locally homeomorphic to a locally connected space

Furthermore,

\begin{align*} p_{\vert E_e}: E_e \rightarrow B \end{align*}

is a covering map

Then we get a commutative diagram

preserving $e$. By uniqueness of connected factorization through a covering map, it is the identity

Furthermore

again preserves the basepoint. Hence by universal property of a universal cover it again the identity morphism. therefore, $E_e \cong E$ with $\iota$ as homeomorphism, thus $E = E_e$ and hence $E$ is connected
