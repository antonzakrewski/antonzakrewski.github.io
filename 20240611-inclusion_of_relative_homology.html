Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ be open. Then the induced map

\begin{align*} H_n(\mathbb{R}^n, U;M) \rightarrow \prod_{x \in U} H_n(\mathbb{R}^n, \mathbb{R}^n \setminus \{x\};M) \end{align*}
