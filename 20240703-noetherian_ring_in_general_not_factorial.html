noetherian ring in general not factorial

Proposition

Let $A$ be a noetherian ring. Then $A$ is in general not factorial

Proof

consider

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-5}] \end{align*}

then it is not factorial (to link) but it is noetherian as it the quotient ring

\begin{align*} \varphi: \mathbb{Z}[X] \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-5}] \end{align*}
