objectwise kappa compact functor as kappa compact object

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal, $S$ a kappa-small simplicial set and $\mathcal{C}$ an infinity category Let $\mathcal{F}: S \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be an infinity functor such that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(s) \end{align*}

is a kappa compact objects for $s \in S$

Then $\mathcal{F}$ is also a \(kappa\)-compact object.

Proof
