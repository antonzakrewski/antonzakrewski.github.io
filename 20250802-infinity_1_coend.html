coend

Definition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor Then the coend is defined as colimit of the composition

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tw}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}
