descent adjunction

Proposition

Let $T$ be a category with pullbacks and \(I\)-indexed colimits and let $X = \mathrm{colim}_{i} X_i$ be a colimit in $T$. Then the functor $T_{/X} \rightarrow \lim_{i} T_{/X_i}$ admits a left adjoint

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}: \lim_{i} T_{/X_i} \rightarrow T_{/X} \end{align*}
