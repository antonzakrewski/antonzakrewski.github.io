ring of global sections of the projective space

Proposition

Let $S = \mathrm{Spec}(A)$. Then the ring of global sections of the projective space is given by $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$.

Proof

Using the sheaf condition of the ring of global sections and the standard affine cover of $\mathbb{P}^n$ by $\mathbb{A}^n$ as well as the embedding of the intersection into $\mathrm{Spec}(A[t_0^{\pm 1}, ..., t_n^{\pm 1}]$: Here $\Gamma(\mathbb{P}^n)$ consists of pairs $(f_0,f_1,...,f_n)$ for $f_i \in A[\frac{t_0}{t_i}] \subseteq A[t_0^{\pm 1},...,t_n^{\pm 1}]$ such that $f_i$ and $f_j$ coincide in $A[t_0^{\pm 1},...,t_n^{\pm 1}]$.

Now if $f_i \in A_i$, then this implies that for each monomial, $t_i$ has no degree $\geq 1$. This implies that the image of $f_i$ has no monomial with any $t_i$ of degree $\geq 1$. As $A[\frac{t_0}{t_i}]$ lies in the 0 graded part of $A[t_0^{\pm 1},...,t_n^{\pm 1}]$, this implies that each monomial of $f$ has total degree $0$, hence $f$ is a constant term.
