intersection of normal subgroups with finite index as normal subgroup with finite index

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group and $N_i$ be finitely many normal subgroups with finite index. Then $\bigcap N_i$ is also a normal subgroup of finite index

Proof

w.l.o.g. assume that $i \in \{1,2\}$. Then we get a kernel sequence

\begin{align*} HK/K \simeq H/H \cap K \rightarrow G/(H \cap K) \rightarrow G/H \end{align*}

where we have used <some isomorphism theorem>

Since we have groups, each preimage has the same cardinality.

Thus

\begin{align*} \vert G/(H \cap K) \vert = \vert HK/K \vert \cdot \vert G/H \vert < \infty \end{align*}

where $\vert HK/H \vert < G/K \vert < \infty$
