isomorphism preserves saturations

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor, which is an isomorphism Let $S \subseteq \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$ be a subcollection of morphisms.

Then the saturation

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(\overline{S}) = \overline{\mathcal{F}(S)} \end{align*}
