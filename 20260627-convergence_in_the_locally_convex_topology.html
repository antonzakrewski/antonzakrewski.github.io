convergence in the locally convex topology

Proposition

Let $X$ be a vector space equipped with the locally convex topology for a family of seminorms $p_i$. Then a sequence $x_n \in X$ converges to $x$ with respect to the locally convex topology if and only if $p_i(x_n) \xrightarrow{n \rightarrow \infty} p_i(x)$ for all $i \in I$.

Proof

Recall that the locally convex topology is the coarsest topology such that $p_i: X \rightarrow \mathbb{K}$ is continuous. In other words, a map $f: Y \rightarrow X$ is continuous if and only if each map $p_i \circ f$ is continuous.
