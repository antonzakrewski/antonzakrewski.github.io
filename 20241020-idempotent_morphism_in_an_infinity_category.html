idempotent morphism in an infinity category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category Then an idempotent morphism in $\mathcal{C}$ is given by a morphism of simplicial sets

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Idem} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Idem}$ is the idempotent classifier nerve
