stability under pullbacks

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. Some type of morphisms is said to be stable under pullbacks, if for 1 objects $A,B,C \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$

  1. a morphism $f: A \rightarrow C$ of the certain type
  2. any morphism $g: B \rightarrow C$
