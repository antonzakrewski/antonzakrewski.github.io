category of augmented chain complexes

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an additive category, $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{A})$ an object. Then the the category of \(A\)-augmented chain complexes is defined as category containing

  1. augmented chain complexes as objects
  2. morphisms $(f_*, f_*')$ of commuting diagrams of chain maps
