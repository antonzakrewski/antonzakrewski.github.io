cohomology ring of the loop space of the sphere

Proposition

Let $n \geq 2$, then the cohomology ring of $\Omega S^n$ is given by

  1. the divided power algebra for $n-1$ if $n$ is odd

Proof

use cohomology serre spectral sequence for

\begin{align*} \Omega S^n \rar & * \rar & S^n \end{align*}

which gives
