Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{O}_{X}$ be a ringed space TFAE:

  3. there exists an affine open covering $X = \bigcup_{i} U_i$ for $U_i = \mathrm{Spec}(A_i)$ and \(A_i\)-modules $M_i$ with $\mathcal{F}_{\vert U_i} \cong M_i$

\begin{align*} A_f \otimes_{A} \Gamma(U, \mathcal{f}) \rightarrow \Gamma(D(f), \mathcal{F}) \end{align*}
