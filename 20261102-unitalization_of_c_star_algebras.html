unitalization of c star algebras

Proposition

the unitalization functor of star algebras restricts to C star algebra.

In particular, the adjunction restricts to the subcategories and htere exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} C^*-\mathrm{Alg}^*{nu} \simeq mC^*-\mathrm{Alg}_{/\mathbb{C}} \end{align*}

Proof

a)

\begin{align*} \ (a, \lambda) \|_{\mathrm{max}, A^u} \leq \| a \|_{\mathrm{max}, A} + \vert \lambda \vert \leq 3 \| (a, \lambda) \|_{\mathrm{max}, A}$ \end{align*}

where the middle part is the sum norm on the banach space $A \oplus $\mathbb{C}$, which induces a complete metric space. The inequality shows that both norms are equivalent (c.f äquivalent norm) and therefore $A^{u}$ is also complete metric space.

b)

the kernel is a closed subspace, hence also a $C^*$ Algebra.
