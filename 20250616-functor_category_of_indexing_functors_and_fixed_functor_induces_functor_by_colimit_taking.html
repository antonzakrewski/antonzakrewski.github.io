functor category between indexing categories and functor induces functor via colimit

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be infinity categories and $\mathcal{C}$ another infinity category with sufficiently many colimits, then taking colimits is an infinity functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(X,Y) \times \mathrm{Fun}(Y, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ (f, \mathcal{F}) \mapsto& \mathrm{colim} (\mathcal{F} \circ f) \end{align*}
