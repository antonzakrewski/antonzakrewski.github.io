dimension of a quotient for a prime ideal and a finitely generated integral domain

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $B$ an Integral domain and finitely generated K-Algebra. Then for a prime ideal $\mathfrak{p}$, it holds that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{height}(\mathfrak{p}) + \mathrm{dim}(B/\mathfrak{p}) = \mathrm{dim}(B) \end{align*}

Proof
