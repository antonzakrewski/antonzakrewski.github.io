lie quotient requires closed subgroup

Proposition

Let $N \trianglelefteq G$ be a normal subgroup of a lie group (or topological group which is T1). Suppose

\begin{align*} \pi: G \rightarrow G/N \end{align*}

is the projection to the quotient group where $G/N$ is also a lie group (or again T1).

Then $N$ must be closed

Proof
