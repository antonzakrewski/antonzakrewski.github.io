Definition

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold Then $M$ is almost complex, if there exists an smooth isomorphism of the real tangential bundle $J: \mathrm{TM} \rightarrow \mathrm{TM}$ which satisfies $J^2 = - \mathrm{id}$
