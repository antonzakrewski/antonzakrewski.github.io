simplicial n-cube as nerve

Proposition

The simplicial n-cube is a nerve

\begin{align*} \square^n =& N(\prod_{i=1}^n [1]) \\ =& N(P_{0,n}) \end{align*}

where $[1] = \{0,1\}$ is the partially ordered set as category and $\prod [1]$ is the product category and $P_{0,n}$ is the cube poset

Proof

follows from:

  • nerve functor preserves limits
  • inspection of the cube poset
