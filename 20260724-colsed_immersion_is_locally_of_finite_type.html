closed immersion is locally of finite type

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a closed immersion. Then $f$ is a morphisms of locally finite type.

Proof

Let $\mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow Y$ be part of an affine open covering. Then the pullback is of the form $V(F) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(R)$ and hence $R/F$ (cf. vanishing locus of spec is affine). In particular, $R \twoheadrightarrow R/F$ is a surjection, hence $R/F$ is locally of finite type.
