1-presheaf category arises by freely adding colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a cocomplete 1-category and $\mathcal{C}$ any 1-category. Then there exists an equivalence of categories

\begin{align*} \mathcal{Y}^*: \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}}(\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

see also:

  • realization / sing adjunction

Proof

use the left kan extension

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \\ \mathrm{Lan}_{\mathcal{Y}}: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{C}, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

and left kan extension along a fully faithful functor as fully faithful functor so the left adjoint is fully faithful. Now we want to invoke the statement fully faithful left adjoint and conservative right adjoint as equivalence.

For that we use that precomposition with yoneda embedding is conservative on colimit preserving functors and adjunctions restrict to full subcategories.

Hence it suffices to show that the left kan extension of any functor is colimit preserving.

This follows follows from realization / sing adjunction which shows that the left kan extension is even a left adjoint
