rational numbers as sequential colimit of Z

Proposition

The rational numbers are a sequential colimit of $\mathbb{Z}$ given by

Proof

Consider

\begin{align*} \iota_j: \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow& \mathbb{Q}\\ 1 \mapsto& \prod_{i=1}^j \frac{1}{i} \end{align*}

which gives a cocone.

Let $A$ be any abelian group and given a natural transformation to $A$. Then define a morphism $\mathbb{Q} \rightarrow A$ by the following way: For $\frac{a}{b}$ choose a sufficiently late $\mathbb{Z}$ in the diagram such that $\frac{a}{b}$ lies in the image of $\iota_j$. Then trace the preimage of $\iota_j$ and map it into $A$.

It can be checked that this is welldefined and defines a group homomorphism
