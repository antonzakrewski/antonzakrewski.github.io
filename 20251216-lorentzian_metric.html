lorentzian metric

Definition

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold with A riemannian metric is a <pairing>

\begin{align*} g: (TM \times TM)_{\Delta \subseteq M \times M} \rightarrow \mathbb{R} \end{align*}

which is

  1. smooth
  2. bilinear
  3. symmetric bilinear form
  4. indefinite, but non degenerate ?
