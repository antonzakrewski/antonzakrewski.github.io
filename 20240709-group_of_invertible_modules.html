picard group for a commutative ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, then the Picard ring is defined as set of invertible modules up to iso with the tensor product as addition

Proof

neutral element

follows from $A$

inverse element

follows from assumption, that each module $M \in \mathrm{Picard}(A)$ is invertible

abelian

follows from commutativity of the tensor product for a commutative ring

closed

  • invertible modules closed under tensor product
