infinite dimensional schurs lemma / diximier lemma

Proposition

Let $A$ be an Algebra over $\mathbb{C}$ and let $V$ be an simple representation with a countable basis Then any morphism $\varphi: V \rightarrow V$ is a scalar multiplication.

Proof

Observe that $\mathrm{End}_A(V)$ is a a countably dimensional vector space and a skew field. Now suppose that $\varphi$ is not a scalar, then $\mathbb{C} \subseteq \mathbb{C}(\varphi)$ is a nontrivial field extension (since it is generated by a single element) generated by one element. Here $\mathbb{C}$ is an algebraically closed field, hence it must be a transcendental.
