projection of affine spaces is smooth

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, then any map $\mathbb{A}_R^n \rightarrow \mathbb{A}_R^{n-k}$ for $k \leq n$ is a smooth morphism

Proof

follows from

\begin{align*} \mathbb{A}_R^n \cong V(\emptyset) \rightarrow (\mathbb{A}_R^{n-k})^k \rightarrow \mathbb{A}_R^{n-k} \end{align*}
