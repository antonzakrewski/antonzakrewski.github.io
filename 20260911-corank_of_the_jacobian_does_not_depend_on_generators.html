corank of the jacobian does not depend on choice of generators

Proposition

Let $A \cong k[x_1,...,x_m]/(f_1,...,f_r)$ be a finitely generated k-Algebra over a field $k$. Then for $g \in (f_1,...,f_r)$, we have that the jacobian matrices of $(f_1,...,f_r)$ and $(f_1,...,f_r,g)$ have the same corank

Proof

By assumption there exists an equation

\begin{align*} \sum_{i=1}^r \alpha_i f_i = g \end{align*}

and then applying the derivative - which is \(R\)-linear - derivatives gives

\begin{align*} \sum_{i=1}^r \alpha_i \frac{\partial}{ \partial x} f_i = \frac{\partial}{\partial x} g \end{align*}

which shows that one column/ row can be written as sum of the other
