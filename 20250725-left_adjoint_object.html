1-categorical left adjoint object

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a functor and $c \in \mathcal{C}$. Then a left adjoint object for is given by an object $\mathcal{F}(c) \in \mathcal{D}$ together with a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(c,\mathcal{G}(-)) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(c),-) \end{align*}
