arbitrary union of algebraic sets in general not algebrai

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space. Then the arbitrary union of algebraic sets is in general not algebraic.

Proof

consider the affine line $\mathbb{C}[X]$ It is endowed with the cofinite topology (cf. simple affine line has cofinite topology).

Then it follows that for example $\mathbb{R} \subeteq \mathbb{C}$ is not algebraic
