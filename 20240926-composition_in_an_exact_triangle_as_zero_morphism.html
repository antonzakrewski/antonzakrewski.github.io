composition in an exact triangle as zero morphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a triangulated category and

an exact triangle

Then the composition

\begin{align*} g \circ f = 0 \end{align*}

is the zero morphism

Proof

consider 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
X \arrow[]{d}{\mathrm{id}} \arrow[]{r}{\mathrm{id} } & X \arrow[]{d}{\mathrm{id}} \arrow[]{r}{} & 0 \arrow[]{r}{}& X[1] \arrow[]{d}{\mathrm{id}}
X \arrow[]{r}{} & Y \arrow[]{r}{} & Z \arrow[]{r}{}& X[1]
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

then by TR3 of a triangulated category there exists a map $0 \rightarrow Z$ making the diagram commute. 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
X \arrow[]{d}{\mathrm{id}} \arrow[]{r}{\mathrm{id} } & X \arrow[]{d}{\mathrm{id}} \arrow[]{r}{} & 0 \arrow[dashed]{d}{} \arrow[]{r}{}& X[1] \arrow[]{d}{\mathrm{id}}
X \arrow[]{r}{} & Y \arrow[]{r}{} & Z \arrow[]{r}{}& X[1]
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

This has to be the zero morphism.

Hence we especially have the commuting diagram
