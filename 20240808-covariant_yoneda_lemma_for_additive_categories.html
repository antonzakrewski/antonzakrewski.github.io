covariant yoneda lemma for ab-enriched categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an Ab-enriched category, $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(A,-): $ the enriched hom functor and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab}$ a functor. Then there exists a bijection

\begin{align*} \mathrm{NatTrans}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(A,-), \mathcal{F}(-)) \cong \mathrm{Set}(\mathcal{F}(A)) \end{align*}
