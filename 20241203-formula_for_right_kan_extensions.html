formula for right kan extensions

Proposition

Let $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an infinity functor between infinity categories $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$. Then for a complete category $\mathcal{X}$ and a functor $\mathcal{G}$ the right kan extension

\begin{align*} f_* \mathcal{G}(x) \cong \mathrm{lim}_{} f(c) \end{align*}

Proof
