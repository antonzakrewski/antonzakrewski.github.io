segal mapping anima and associated category

Proposition

Let $X$ be a segal anima, $\mathrm{asscat}(X) \in \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}$ the associated category. Then the segal mapping anima and the mapping anima are equivalent, ie..

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{asscat}(X)}(x,x') \simeq \mathrm{Map}_{X}(x,x') \end{align*}

Proof
