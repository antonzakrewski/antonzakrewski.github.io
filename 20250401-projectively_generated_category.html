projectively generated category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a cocomplete infinity category and $S$ be a collection of objects. Then $S$ is a set of compact projective generators for $\mathcal{C}$ if

  1. $S$ consists of compact projective objects
  2. the full sub infinity category spanned by $S$ is small
  3. $S$ generates $\mathcal{C}$ under small colimits
