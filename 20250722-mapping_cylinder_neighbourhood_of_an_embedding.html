mapping cylinder neighbourhood of an embedding

Definition

Let $\iota: M \hookrightarrow W$ be an locally flat embedding.

Then a mapping cylinder neighbourhood consists of a codimension $0$ embedding $K \hookrightarrow W$ with $\mathrm{im}(M \hookrightarrow W) \subseteq K$ such that $K$ is homeomorphic to the mapping cylinder of a map

\begin{align*} r: \partial K \rightarrow M \end{align*}

with homotopy fibres equivalent to $S^{w-m-1}$
