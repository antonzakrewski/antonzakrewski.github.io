some low cohomology of K(Z/2,4)

Proposition

\begin{align*} \tilde{H}^k(K(\mathbb{F}_2,4), \mathbb{Z}) = \begin{cases} 0 & \mbox{if } k = 0,1,2,3,4,6 \\ \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } k = 5,7,8 \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

using integral cohomology of a space with finite homotopy groups and homology & Hurewicz gives $H^5(K(\mathbb{F}_2,4)) = \mathbb{F}_2, H^6(K(\mathbb{F}_2,4)) = 0$. Now consider some low cohomology of K(Z/2,3) and write down the cohomological serre spectral sequence
