representable functors closed under limits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category with (enough) limits (or colimits). Then the representable functors are closed under limits.

Proof

use that

\begin{align*} \lim \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,c_i) \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,\lim c_i) \end{align*}

where the RHS is representable

use some fully faithful yoneda argument.
