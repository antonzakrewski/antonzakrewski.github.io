flat module and factorization

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $F$ an \(R\)-module TFAE:

  1. $F$ is a flat module
  2. for any \(R\)-module $M$ and homomorphism $\varphi: F \rightarrow M$ there exists a free module $G$ and homomorphisms $g: F \rightarrow G$ and $h: G \rightarrow M$ such that following diagram commutes 
\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{ker}(\varphi) \arrow[hookrightarrow, ]{r}{\iota} M \arrow[]{}{}& 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

with

\begin{align*} g[K] = 0 \end{align*}

where $K \subseteq \mathrm{ker}(\varphi)$ is a finitely generated module

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
