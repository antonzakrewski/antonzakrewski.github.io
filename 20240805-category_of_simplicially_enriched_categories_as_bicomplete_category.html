category of simplicially enriched categories as bicomplete category

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Cat}_{\Delta}$ be the category of simplicially enriched categories. Then $\mathrm{Cat}_{\Delta}$ is a bicomplete category

Proof

using:

  • fully faithful functor reflects colimits
  • fully faithful functor reflects limits
  • functor category for a bicomplete codomain as bicomplete category
  • category cat as bicomplete category
  • category of simplicially enriched categories as full subcategory of simplicial categories
