Simpson theorem for presentability

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ is an presentable
  2. $\mathcal{C}$ is accessible and for each regular cardinal $\kappa$ the full infinity subcategory $\mathcal{C}^{\kappa}$ admits kappa-small colimits
  3. there exists a regular cardinal $\kappa$ such that $\mathcal{C}^{\kappa}$ is kappa accessible and admits $\kappa$ small
  5. there exists a small infinity category $\mathcal{D}$ such that $\mathcal{C}$ is an accessible localization
