orthogonal complement of the closure of the generated subspace

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{H}$ be a prehilbert space and $M \subseteq \mathcal{H}$. Then $M^{\bot} = \left(\overline{\mathrm{span}(M)}\right)^{\bot}$.

Proof

$\subseteq$

Let $n \in M^{\bot}$ and $m \in \overline{\mathrm{span}(M)}$ be arbitrary. Then we need to show that $\langle m,n \rangle = 0$. But $\mathrm{ker} \langle n,-\rangle$ is a closed subspace and contains $M$, so in particular $\mathrm{ker} \langle n,-\rangle \supseteq \overline{\mathrm{span}(M)}$ or $\langle m,n\rangle = 0$.
