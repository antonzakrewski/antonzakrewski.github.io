morphism of pair of ideals induces morphism on pullbacks

Proposition

Let $\varphi: R \rightarrow S$ be a ring-homomorphism, $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq R, \mathfrak{b} \subseteq S$ ideals such that $\varphi[\mathfrak{a}] \subseteq \mathfrak{b}$. Then there exists a canonical induced morphism

\begin{align*} R \times_{R/\mathfrak{a}} R \rightarrow S \times_{S/\mathfrak{b}} S \end{align*}

Proof

follows from lim functor and the evident natural transformation
