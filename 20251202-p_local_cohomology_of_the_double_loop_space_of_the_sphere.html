some p-local cohomology of the double loop space of the odd sphere

Proposition

Let $n \geq 3$, then

\begin{align*} H^k(\Omega^{2} S^{n+2}; \mathbb{Z}_{(p)}) \cong \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z}_{(p)} & \mbox{if } k = 0,n \\ \mathbb{F}_p & \mbox{if } k = p(n+1) - 1 \\ 0 & \mbox{if } k < p(n+1) - 1 \textrm{ and} k \neq 0,n \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

consider the cohomological serre spectral sequence for

\begin{align*} \Omega^2 S^{n+2} \rightarrow * \rightarrow \Omega S^{n+2} \end{align*}

Here using cohomology ring of the loop space of the sphere we geta $H^*(\Omega S^{n+2}, \mathbb{Z}) = \Gamma_{\mathbb{Z}}[x_{n+1}]$ as divided power algebra.

Now using the Hurewicz theorem we get some low cohomologies

Here we get that the first map has to be an isomorphism (since both terms must die and this is the only possible way) so after appropriately choosing generators $x,y$ this gives multiplication with $1$ resp. the map $x \mapsto y$. Then using multiplicativity and the divided power algebra structure we get $y \cdot y = 2z$ for a generator $z$ in $E_2^{4,0}$.

But multiplicativity yielsd

\begin{align*} d(xy) =& d(x) y + xd(y) \\ =& y^2 \\ =& 2z \end{align*}

which shows that $xy \mapsto 2z$.

Analogous arguments show that the map $E_{2}^{(k-1)n,2} \rightarrow E_2^{kn,0}$ is given by multiplication with $k$ (after choosing appropriate generators). In particular these maps are \(p\)-locally isomorphisms up until the \(p\)-th map.

Therefore the first term which can and has to be nonzero of $H^*(\Omega^2 S^{n+2}, \mathbb{Z}_{(p)})$ is given by $H^{p(n+1)-1}(\Omega^2 S^{n+2}, \mathbb{Z}_{(p)})$. For degree reasons this has to be $\mathbb{F}_p$ so that it both dies in the $E_{\infty}$ page and that it kills $E_{?}^{p(n+1),0}$
