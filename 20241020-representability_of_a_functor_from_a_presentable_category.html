representability of a presheaf from a presentable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable category, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ a presheaf

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is a contravariant representable functor
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ dualizes colimits to limits

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
