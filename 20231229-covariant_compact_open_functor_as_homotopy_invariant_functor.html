covariant compact open functor as homotopy invariant functor_

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space, then the covariant based compact open functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}(-,X): \mathrm{Top} \rightarrow \mathrm{Top} \end{align*}

is homotopy invariant

Proof

corollary of postcomposition with homotopic maps for the mapping spaces_
