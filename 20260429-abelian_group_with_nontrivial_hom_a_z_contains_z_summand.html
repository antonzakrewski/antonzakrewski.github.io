abelian group with nontrivial Hom(A,Z) contains Z summand

Proposition

Let $A$ be an abelian group.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(A,\mathbb{Z})$ is nontrivial
  2. $A$ contains $\mathbb{Z}$ as direct summand

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

assume that there exists a nonzero map $f: A \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}$. Then the image is a (nontrivial) submodule, i.e. given by $(n)$ for some $n$. In particluar, it is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}$, so we may map

\begin{align*} A \xrightarrow{f} (n) \xrightarrow{\cdot \frac{1}{n}} \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

Hence $f$ is surjective.

Therefore, we may split

2) $\implies$ 1)

use that $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(-, \mathbb{Z})$ is an additive functor
