binary product functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category containing finite coproduct.

Then the binary product functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \prod: \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ A,B \mapsto& A \prod B \\ (f: A \rightarrow A', g: B \rightarrow B') \mapsto& \left((f \prod g): (A \prod A') \rightarrow (B \prod B')\right) \end{align*}
