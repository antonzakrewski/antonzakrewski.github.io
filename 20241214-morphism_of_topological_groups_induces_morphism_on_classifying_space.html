morphism of topological groups induces morphism on classifying space

Proposition

Let $\varphi: G \rightarrow G'$ be a morphism of group objects. Then this induces a continuous map

\begin{align*} B \varphi: BG \rightarrow BG' \end{align*}

Proof

follows from

  • morphism of topological groups induces a natural transformation on principal g-bundles
