homotopy relative to a subset

Definition

Let $(X,\mathcal{T}_X)$ and $(Y,\mathcal{T}_Y)$ be topological spaces, $A \subseteq X$ and $f,g: X \rightarrow Y$ continuous maps such that

\begin{align*} f_{\vert A} = g_{\vert A} \end{align*}

Then a homotopy relative to $A$ is a homotopy relative to $f_{\vert A}$
