localization and hom-functor commute for finitely generated Modules over a Noetherian Ring

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{AM_{fg}}$ be the category of finitely generated modules for a noetherian ring $A$. Then it holds

\begin{align*} \eta: S^{-1}(\mathrm{Hom}_{A}(-,-)) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{S^{-1}A}(S^{-1}(-), S^{-1}(-)) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism see: localization and hom-functor

Proof

corollary:

  • finitely presented module commutes with hom-functor and localization
  • finitely generated module over a noetherian ring as finitely presented module
