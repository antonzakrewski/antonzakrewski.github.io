height of a prime ideal and quotient

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{p}$ a prime ideal. Then for the dimension and height

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{p}) + \mathrm{dim}(A/\mathfrak{p}) \leq \mathrm{dim}(A) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from the correspondence theorem for prime ideals and quotient ring and glueing the chains
