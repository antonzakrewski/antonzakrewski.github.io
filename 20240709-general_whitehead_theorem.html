general whitehead theorem

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrowz Y$ be a d-connected map and $A$ a CW-Complex Then the postcomposition on the mapping space

\begin{align*} f_*: \pi_0(\mathrm{Map}(A,X)) \rightarrow \pi_0(\mathrm{Map}(A,Y)) \end{align*}
  1. if $\mathrm{dim}(A) \leq d$, then $f_*$ is surjective
  2. if $A < d$, then $f_*$ is bijective
