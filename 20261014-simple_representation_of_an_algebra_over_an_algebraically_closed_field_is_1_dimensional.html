simple representation of a commutative algebra over an algebraically closed field is 1-dimensional

Proposition

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field and $A$ a commutative K-Algebra. Then every0 finite dimensional simple representation $V$ of $A$ is \(1\)-dimensional.

Proof

Observe that multiplication with $a \in A$ is a morphism of $A$ modules. Hence multiplication with $a$ is multiplication with a scalar $\lambda \in k$ by schur lemma over algebraically closed fields. This holds for all $a \in A$. But any subvector space is a subrepresentation of $A$, so $V$ must be 1 dimensional.
