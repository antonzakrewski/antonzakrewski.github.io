multivariate holomorphic map between affine subsets

Definition

Let $U \subseteq \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n, V \subseteq \mathbb{C}^m $ be an open subset . Then a map $f: U \rightarrow V$ is said to be holomorphic, if for each point $x \in U$ and each i \leq m$ there exists an open neighbourhood $V$ and a power series $g \in \mathbb{C}[ [X_1,...,X_n]]$ such that

\begin{align*} \pi_i f_{\vert V} = g \end{align*}
