n-connected map and cohomology

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be an n-connected map and $M$ a module . Then

\begin{align*} H^i(f): H_i(X, M) \rightarrow H_i(Y,M) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism for $i < n$ and an injection for $i = n$

Proof

use:

  • universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology
  • Four-Lemma of modules of monomorphisms for the monomorphism of the short exact sequence to show that $H^n(Y, M) \rightarrow H^n(X,M)$ is a monomorphism
