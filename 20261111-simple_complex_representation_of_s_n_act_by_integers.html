simple, complex representation of S_n act by integers

Proposition

Since $V_{\lambda} = \mathbb{C}[S_n] c_{\lambda}$ and $c_{\lambda} \in \mathbb{Q}[S_n]$ so $V_{\lambda} = (\mathbb{C} \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} \mathbb{C}[S_n]) c_{\lambda}$ Let $p: G \rightarrow \mathrm{Gl}(W)$ for $G$ finite and $W \in \mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb{Q}}$. Then pick a basis $\{w_i\}$ of $W$. Consider the lattice $L = \sum_{g \in G} \sum \mathbb{Z}p(g) w \subseteq W$.

Now $L$ contains the $w_i$ and is $G$ invariant. Now $L$ contains $\bigoplus_{i \in I}^d \mathbb{Z} w_i \subseteq L \subseteq \bigoplus_{i \in I}^d \mathbb{Z} \frac{w_i}{d}$ where $D$ clears the denominators of all the $v_i$.

But $L$ is then a submodule of a free module of at least dimension $d$, hence L \cong \bigoplus_{i = 0}^d $\mathbb{Z}$ and we can pick a $\mathbb{Q}$ basis of $W$. But now $G$ acts by integer matrices in $\{v_i\}$.

Apply this to $\mathbb{Q}[S_n] c_{\lambda}$ which is acted upon $S_-$.

Proof
