map of principal g-bundles as pullback

Proposition

Then any map of principal g-bundles

is a pullback

Proof

Note that a pullback induces a morphism

which in particular is a morphism of principal bundles.

Here the map

\begin{align*} E \rightarrow E' \times_{B'} B \\ e \mapsto (f(e), p(e)) \end{align*}

is a \(G\)-equivariant map, since

\begin{align*} g \cdot e \mapsto& (f(g \cdot e), p(g \cdot e)) =& (g \cdot f(e), p(e)) \\ =& g \cdot (f(e), p(e)) \end{align*}

(?)
