p-completion of a spectrum is p-complete

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum, then $X_{p}^{\wedge}$ is p-complete spectrum.

In particular, the canonical map $X_{p}^{\wedge} \rightarrow (X_{p}^{\wedge})_{p}^{\wedge}$ is an equivalence

Proof

We show $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], X_{p}^{\wedge}) \simeq 0$ (cf. p-complete spectrum and mapping out of an R-local spectrum) Here

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], X_{p}^{\wedge}) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], \lim ( \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X/p^n)) \\ \lim ( \xrightarrow{\cdot p} \mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], X/p^n)) \end{align*}

where we have used that $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(-,-)$ is additive in both variables. Now we it suffices to show that $\mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], X/p^n) \simeq 0$ since any limit of terminal objects (zero object) is the terminal object (cf. infinity limit of terminal objects as terminal object). Here $\mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], X/p^n) = \mathrm{cofib}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], X) \xrightarrow{\cdot p^n} \mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], X))$ where we have used that $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(-,-)$ is additive in both variables. But this map also identifies with precomposition with $\cdot p^n$, which is an equivalence on $\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}]$, hence also induces an equivalence on $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], X)$. This shows that the cofiber is trivial.
