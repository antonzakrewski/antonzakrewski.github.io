hom functor and exact sequence in RMod

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring, $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod and $K$ an \(R\)-module. Then the covariant hom functor is a covariant left exact functor

Proof

covariant left exact functor and exact sequence

using:

  • covariant left exact functor and exact sequence
  • Category Rmod as abelian category

Suppose there exists a short exact sequence

$\varphi^{ \rightarrow}$ injective

follows from:

  • injective map as monomorphism
  • postcomposition with a morphism as injection on hom sets and monomorphism

$\mathrm{im}(\otimes_K(\varphi)) \subseteq \mathrm{ker}(\otimes_K(\psi))$

Suppose $f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{RMod}}(K,M)$, then for arbitrary $k \in K$, we get

\begin{align*} \psi^{ \leftarrow} \circ \varphi^{ \leftarrow}(f)(k) =& (\psi \circ \varphi)(f(k)) \\ =& 0(f(k)) \\ =& 0 \end{align*}

since $\psi \circ \varphi$ is the zero map

$\mathrm{ker}(\otimes_K(\psi)) \subseteq \mathrm{im}(\otimes_K(\varphi))$

Suppose $f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{RMod}}(K,N)$ with $f \in \mathrm{ker}(\psi^{ \leftarrow})$. Then for arbitrary $k \in K$, we get $\psi(f(k)) = 0$, hence $f(k) \in \mathrm{ker}(\psi)$. Since $\varphi$ is injective, $(M, \varphi)$ is a submodule, hence by restriction of a module homomorphism to the image as module homomorphism
