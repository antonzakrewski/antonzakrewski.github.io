subdivision of the standard simplex as nerve of the twisted arrow category

Proposition

The edgewise subdivision of $\Delta^n = \mathrm{Hom}_{\Delta}(-,[n]): \Delta \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{An}$ is given by the twisted arrow category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sd}^*(\Delta^n) \simeq \mathrm{Tw}(\Delta^n) \end{align*}

Proof
