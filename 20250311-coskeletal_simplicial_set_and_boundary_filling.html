coskeletal simplicial set and unique boundary filling

Proposition

Let $X$ be a simplicial set.

TFAE:

  1. $X$ is n-coskeletal simplicial set
  2. each lifting problem

admits a unique solution

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from

  • bijection of extension problem solutions via adjoint

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from

  • skeleton coskeleton adjunction in sSet

where a morphism

\begin{align*} Y \rightarrow \mathrm{cosk}_n(X) \end{align*}

corresponds to

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sk}_n(Y) \rightarrow X \end{align*}

Here $\mathrm{sk}_n$ is a left adjoint: hence it preserves the cw structure of a simplicial set

Hence if we may solve an extension problem

uniquely we may (uniquely) lift inductively $\mathrm{sk}_n(X),...,\mathrm{sk}_k(X),...$ and hence get a bijection

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(Y,X) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\mathrm{sk}_n(Y),X) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(Y,\mathrm{cosk}_n(X)) \end{align*}
