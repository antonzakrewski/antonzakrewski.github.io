projective space

Definition

Let $F$ be a field, then the projective space $\mathbb{P}_F^n$ is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathbb{P}_F^n \coloneqq F^{n+1} \setminus \{0\}/ \sim \end{align*}

with

\begin{align*} (p_0,...,p_n) \cong (q_0,...,q_n) :\Leftrightarrow \exists \lambda \in F : p_i = \lambda q_i \forall i \in \{1,..,n\} \end{align*}
