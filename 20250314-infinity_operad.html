infinity operad

Definition

An infinity operad is an infinity functor

\begin{align*} p: \mathcal{O}^{\otimes} \rightarrow N(\mathrm{Fin}_*) \end{align*}

between categories (cf. category Fin*) such that

  1. the pullback
\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}' \dar \rar & \mathcal{O}^{\otimes} \dar \\
N(\mathrm{Fin}_{*,\mathrm{inert}}) \rar & N(\mathrm{Fin}_*)
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

is a cocartesian fibration, i.e. we may lift inert morphisms to cocartesian morphisms

  1. (under possibly straightening-unstraightening of cocartesian fibrations ) this $\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}' \rightarrow N(\mathrm{Fin}_{*,\mathrm{inert}})$ satisfies the segal condition
