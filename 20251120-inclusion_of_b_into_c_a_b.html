inclusion of B into C_A,B

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{B} \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ a abelian full subcategory. Then there exists a fully faithful inclusion

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathcal{B} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}} B \mapsto& (B \rightarrow 0) \end{align*}

Proof

Note that morphisms $(B,A) \rightarrow (B',A')$ are given by morphisms $B \rightarrow B', A \rightarrow A'$ such that extra conditions hold. But in the case $A = A' = 0$ those are fullfilled for trivial reasons.
