category PrL

Definition

Let $\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}$ be the infinity category of infinity categories. Then $\mathcal{Pr}^{R}$ is defined as infinity subcategory of infinity category of infinity categories containing

  1. presentable infinity categories
  2. infinity left adjoint as morphisms (resp. infinity functor preserving colimits of a shape, see: infinity right adjoint functor theorem)
