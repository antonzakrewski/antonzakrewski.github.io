local epimorphism for a grothendieck topology

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \tau)$ be a site and $f: \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ a natural transformation of set-valued presheaves. Then $f$ is said to be a \(\tau\)-local epimorphism, if for every $X \in \mathcal{C}$ and every $x \in \mathcal{G}(x)$, the set of maps $u: Y \rightarrow X$ such that there exists a factorization

is a covering sieve.
