low homotopy group of S^n wedge S^1

Proposition

Let $k \leq 2n-2$ (???) be in the stable range.. Then there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \pi_{k}(S^1 \wedge S^n) \cong \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \pi_k(S^n) \end{align*}

Proof

use:

  • universal cover and higher homotopy groups, i.e. $\bigvee S^n \rightarrow S^1 \vee S^n$ induces an isomorphism $\pi_n(\bigvee S^n) \rightarrow \pi_n(S^1 \vee S^n)$
  • homotopy groups of highly connected wedges
