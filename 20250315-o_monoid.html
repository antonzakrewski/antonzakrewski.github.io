O-monoid

Definition

Let $\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}$ be an infinity operad and $\mathcal{C}$ an infinity category Then an \(\mathcal{O}\)-monoid is a functor

\begin{align*} M: \mathcal{O}^{\otimes} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

where for $X \in \mathcal{C}^{\otimes}_n$ with

\begin{align*} X \simeq (X_1,...,X_n) \in \mathcal{C}^n \end{align*}

the induced map

\begin{align*} M(X) \rightarrow \prod_{i\in i}^n M(X_i) \end{align*}

is an equivalence

i.e. $M(-)$ preserves some form of products
