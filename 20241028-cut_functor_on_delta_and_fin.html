Cut functor on Delta and Fin partial

Definition

Let $\Gamma$ the category Fin partial and $\Delta$ the simplex category Then for the opposite categories the cut functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cut}: \Delta^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \Gamma^{\mathrm{op}} \\ [n] \mapsto& \{1,...,n\} \\ \end{align*}

where a morphism $\alpha: [n] \rightarrow [m]$ resp. the opposite morphism $\alpha^{\mathrm{op}}$ gets send to

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cut}(\alpha^{\mathrm{op}}): \{1,...,m\} \rightarrow \{1,...,n\} \\ i \mapsto& \begin{cases} j & \mbox{if } \alpha(j - 1) < i \leq \alpha(j) \\ \mathrm{undef} & \mbox{if } i \leq \alpha(0) \\ \mathrm{undef} & \mbox{if } \alpha(n) < i \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

here

  • $\{1,...,n\}$ are the elements representing the dedekind cuts of $[n] = \{0,...,n\}$ via.
\begin{align*} i \sim \{1,...,i-1\} \coprod \{i,...,n\} \end{align*}

and a morphism $\alpha: [n] \rightarrow [m]$ in $\Delta$ and a dedekind cut $\{1,...,i-1\} \coprod \{i,...,m\}$ gets send to $\alpha^{-1}[ \{i,...,i-1\}] \coprod \alpha^{-1}[\{i,...,m\}]$ if it is a welldefined dedekind cut
