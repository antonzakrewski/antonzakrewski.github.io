Proposition

Let $X$ be a vector space over a field $K$. Let $f_1,...,f_n,f$ be linear functional.

TFAE:

  1. $f \in \langle f_1,...,f_n\rangle$
  2. $\bigcap \mathrm{ker}(f_i) \subseteq \mathrm{ker}(f)$

2) $\implies$ 1)
