schur lemma for finite complex representations

Proposition

Let $V_1,V_2$ be complex representations of a group where $V_1$ is finite dimensional and $V_1,V_2$ are irreducible representation. Suppose there exists a nonzero G-linear map $\varphi: V_1 \rightarrow V_2$ Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbb{C}[G]}(V_1,V_2) \cong \mathbb{C} \end{align*}

as \(\mathbb{C}\)-vector space after restriction of scalars for $\mathbb{C} \rightarrow \mathbb{C}[G]$

Proof

note that $\varphi$ is an isomorphism (cf. hom of simple objects in an abelian category).

So let $\varphi^{-1}$ be the inverse.

We want to show that any $\varphi' \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbb{C}[G]}(V_1,V_2)$ is given by $\alpha \cdot \varphi$ for some suitable $\alpha \in \mathbb{C}$.

So consider $\varphi' \circ \varphi^{-1}: V_2 \rightarrow V_2$ which is still \(\mathbb{C}\)-linear. by <Jordan-Normalform> type arguments this map has to contain an Eigenwert $\alpha$

Now this shows

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ker}(\alpha \mathrm{id} - \varphi' \circ \varphi^{-1}) \neq 0 \end{align*}

hence by hom of simple objects in an abelian category we get that $\alpha \mathrm{id} - \varphi' \circ \varphi^{-1} =0$ or $\alpha \mathrm{id} = \varphi' \circ \varphi^{-1}$.

Thus

\begin{align*} \alpha \varphi = \varphi' \end{align*}
