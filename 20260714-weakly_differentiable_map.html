weakly differentiable map

Proposition

Let $D \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be a region and $X$ a $\mathbb{C}$-valued banach space. Let $f: D \rightarrow X$ be a continuous map, then $f$ is said to be weakly differentiable in $z_0 \in D$ if for any bounded linear functional $l \in X^*$, the map $l \circ f$ is complex differentiable
