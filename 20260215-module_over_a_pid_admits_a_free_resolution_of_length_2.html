Proposition

Let $R$ be a principal ideal domain, $M$ an \(R\)-module. Then $M$ admits a free resolution of length $2$

Proof

follows from submodule of a free module over a pid as free module so choosing a surjection

\begin{align*} \coprod R \twoheadrightarrow M \end{align*}

and taking the kernel suffices
