cap product and inclusion for a regalur hausdorff space

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a regular hausdorff space and $U \hookrightarrow X$ an open subset and $s \in H_n^{\mathrm{lf}}(X;R)$ an element in the locally finite homology Then there exists a diagram 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
H_c^{p}(U;M) \arrow[]{r}{\iota_{#}} \arrow[]{d}{\cap \iota^{#}(s)} & H_c*^p(X;R) \arrow[]{d}{\cap s}\\
H_{n-p}(U) \arrow[]{r}{\iota_{#}} & H_{n-p}(X;R)
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

commutes.

Proof
