coloring of pseudo d-regular circle

Proposition

Let $G = ([n -1],E)$ be a graph and $k \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $\frac{n}{k+1} \in \mathbb{N}$ and $n \geq k+2$ Suppose each knot $l$ is connected to the \(k\)-previous and \(k\)-next vertices, i.e.

\begin{align*} N_{G}(l) = \{l + d \equiv{n} : d \in \{1,...,k\}\} \end{align*}

Then the chromatic number is given by

\begin{align*} \chi(G) = k+1 \end{align*}

Proof

$\chi(G) \leq k+1$

consider the map

\begin{align*} i \mapsto i + \mathbb{Z}/(k+1) \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

It is a \(k+1\)-coloring: Suppose different $l,j \in [n-1]$ have the same coloring, then

\begin{align*} l - j \in (k+1) \mathbb{Z} \setminus \{0\} \end{align*}

in particular they are more than $k$ vertices between $l$ and $j$.

$\chi(G) \geq k+1$

using chromatic number and clique number: Then consider the clique

\begin{align*} \{1,...,k,k+1\} \end{align*}

which contains $k+1$ many elements.
