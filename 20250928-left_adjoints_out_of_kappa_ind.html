left adjoints out of kappa ind into a presentable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a presentable category and $\mathcal{C}$ a small category with \(\kappa\)-small colimits. Then the restriction along the yoneda embedding induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{L}_{\kappa}(\mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\kappa-\mathrm{colim}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}^{\kappa}) \end{align*}

here

  1. $\mathrm{Fun}^L_{\kappa}$ is the full subcategory of $\mathrm{Fun}(-,-)$ containing left adjoints and kappa compact object preserving functors
  2. $\mathrm{Fun}^{\kappa-\mathrm{colim}}(-,-)$ is the full subcategory of $\mathrm{Fun}(-,-)$ containing functors which preserve \(\kappa\)-small colimit preserving functors.
