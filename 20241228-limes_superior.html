limes superior

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $A_n$ be objects for $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then the limes inferior is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}^{\mathrm{op}}} \mathrm{colim}_{m \geq n} A_m =& \mathrm{lim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}^{\mathrm{op}}} \coprod_{m \geq n} A_m \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} \coprod_{m \geq n+1} A_m \rightarrow \coprod_{m \geq n} A_m \end{align*}

is the induced map by the universal property - "including into the \(n\)-th component" and $\mathbb{N}^{\mathrm{op}}$ is regarded as opposite category of $\mathbb{N}$ as partially ordered set
