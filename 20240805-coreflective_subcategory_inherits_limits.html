coreflective subcategory inherits limits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories such that $\mathcal{R}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is a coreflective subcategory Suppose $J: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is a diagram and $\mathrm{lim}_{\mathcal{F} \circ J}$ the limit in $\mathcal{D}$. Then $\mathrm{R}(\mathrm{lim}_{\mathcal{F} \circ J}$ is a colimit of $J$ in $\mathcal{C}$
