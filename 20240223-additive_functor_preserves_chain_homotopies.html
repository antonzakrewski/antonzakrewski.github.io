additive functor preserves chain homotopies

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ an additive functor and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}), \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{B})$ the categories of chain complexes Let $\mathcal{F}^*: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{B})$ be the induced functor on chain complexes.

Then $\mathcal{F}^*$ preserves chain homotopies

Proof

Let $f,g: A_* \rightarrow B_*$ be chain maps

with

\begin{align*} d_{n+1} h_n + h_{n-1} d_n = f_n - g_n \end{align*}

and

Then by applying additivity of the functor, we get

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(d_{n+1} h_n + h_{n-1} d_n) =& \mathcal{F}(f_n - g_n) \\ \mathcal{F}(d_{n+1} h_n) + \mathcal{F}(h_{n-1} d_n) =& \mathcal{F}(f_n) - \mathcal{F}(g_n) \\ \end{align*}
