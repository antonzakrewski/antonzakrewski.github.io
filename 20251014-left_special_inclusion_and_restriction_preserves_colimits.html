left special inclusion and restriction for P_lex preserves colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{U} \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ be an extension closed subcategory of an exact category TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{U} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is left-special
  2. the restriction $j^*: \mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{lex}}(\mathcal{U}) \rightarrow \mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{lex}}(\mathcal{U})$ preserves colimits
