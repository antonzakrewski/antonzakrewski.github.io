inclusion in ind is exact for a small abelian category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a small abelian category Then the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{A}) \end{align*}

is an exact functor

Proof

left exact functor

  • follows the yoneda embedding preserves limits
  • forgetful functor from Ab to Set preserves limits

right exact functor

using product preserving functor out of an additive category lifts to commutative groups, so we may identify $\mathrm{Ind}$ as the smallest subcategory of $\mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Ab}$ which is closed under filtered colimits

  • we claim that we may pointwise calculate the colimit in $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Ab})$ and see that it lands in $\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{A})$.

for that use filtered colimits commute with finite limits in RMod (sloppy)
