union of a chain of injective maps as injective map

Proposition

Let $X_i,Y$ be sets where $X_0 \subseteq ...$ is a chain with compatible injective maps

\begin{align*} f_i: X_i \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

Then the union

\begin{align*} \bigcup f_i: \bigcup X_i \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

is also an injective map

Proof
