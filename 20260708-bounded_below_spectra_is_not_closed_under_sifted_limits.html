Proposition

The category of bounded below spectra is not closed under $\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}$ indexed limits

Proof

use some:

  • spectra is cotensored over $\mathrm{An}$

Let $S = \bigvee_{n \in \mathbb{N}} S^n$. Then $S \simeq \mathrm{colim}_{\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}} (\coprod *)$ (cf. anima is geometric realization). Now let $X$ be any nontrivial bounded below spectrum, e.g. $\mathbb{S}$. Then it follows for the mapping spectrum

\begin{align*} \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{S}[-n] \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{S}[n],\mathbb{S}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\widehat{\mathbb{S}}[S],\mathbb{S}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\widehat{\mathbb{S}}[S], \mathbb{S}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\widehat{\mathbb{S}}[\mathrm{colim}_{\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}} \coprod *], \mathbb{S}) \\ \simeq& \lim_{\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\coprod \mathbb{S}, \mathbb{S}) \\ \simeq& \lim_{\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}} \prod \mathbb{S} \end{align*}
