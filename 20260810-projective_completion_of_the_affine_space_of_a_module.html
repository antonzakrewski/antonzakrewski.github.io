projective completion of the affine space of a module

Proposition

Let $k$ be a commutative ring, $M$ a \(k\)-module. Then the canonical embedding

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathbb{A}(M) \hookrightarrow& \mathbb{P}(M \oplus k) (a: M \otimes_{k} R \rightarrow R) \mapsto& ((a, \mathrm{id}_R): ((M \otimes_{k} R) \oplus R) \twoheadrightarrow R) \end{align*}

is an open immersion.

Proof

Let $\mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow \mathbb{P}(M \oplus k)$ be a map classifying a quotient line $(M \otimes_{k} R) \oplus R \twoheadrightarrow L$. Then the pullback

is given by the isomorphism locus, which is an open subfunctor.
