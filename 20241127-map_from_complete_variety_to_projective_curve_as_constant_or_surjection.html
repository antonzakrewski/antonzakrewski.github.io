map from complete variety to projective curve as constant or surjection

Proposition

Let $X$ be a complete quasi-projective variety, $Y$ a quasi-projective variety of dimension $1$. Then any regular map

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

is either constant or surjective

Proof

corollary of:

  • image of a complete variety as closed complete variety
  • dimension of a proper closed subspace of a finite dimensional, irreducible subspace strictly smaller
