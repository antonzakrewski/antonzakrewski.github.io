additive functor and biproduct

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be additive categories.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is an additive functor
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ preserves finite biproducts (including the zero object)

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

zero object

Let $0 \in \mathcal{C}$ be the zero object, then

\begin{align*} \vert \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(0,0) \vert = 1 \end{align*}

Then by functorality, it follows, that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(\mathrm{id}) = \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{F}(0)} \end{align*}

Furthermore it follows, that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(0,0) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(\mathcal{F}(0), \mathcal{F}(0)) \end{align*}

is a group-homomorphism, thus

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(0) = 0 \end{align*}

Therefore since $\mathrm{id}_{0} = 0$ we conclude that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{F}(0)} =& 0 \end{align*}
