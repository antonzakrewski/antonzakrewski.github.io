infinity category of R-Module objects

Definition

Let $R$ be a ring, $\mathcal{C}$ an infinity category and $\mathrm{FreeRMod} \subseteq \mathrm{R-Mod}$ the full subcategory containing free modules. Then the category of \(R\)-module objects is defined as full subcategory of finite product preserving functors

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(N(\mathrm{FreeRmod})^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
