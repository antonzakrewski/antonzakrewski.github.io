colimit over an anima as colimit over universal cover modulo pi_1 action

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: X \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

an infinity diagram for a connected anima.

Assume $\mathcal{C}$ admits enough colimits

Then the colimit is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{X} \mathcal{F} =& \mathrm{colim}_{\tau_{\leq 1}(X)} \mathrm{colim}_{\tilde{X}} \tilde{\mathcal{F}} =& \mathrm{colim}_{B \pi_1(X)} \mathrm{colim}_{\tilde{X}} \tilde{\mathcal{F}} \end{align*}

where we use the universal cover functor

Proof

using infinity colimit and left kan extension we may consider

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_X \mathcal{F} = (r_{X})_!(\mathcal{F}) \end{align*}

where $r_{(-)}: (-) \rightarrow *$ denotes the terminal morphism.

but also associativity of infinity kan extensions we may consider the factorization

where we get

\begin{align*} r_{\tau_{\leq 1}(X)} \circ \tau_{\leq 1} = r \end{align*}

or

\begin{align*} (r_{X})_! =& r_{\tau_{\leq 1}(X)}_! \circ (\tau_{\leq 1})_! \end{align*}

Here using the infinity left kan extension formula & pullback diagram of 1-truncation and universal cover via forgetful map from slice for a connceted anima we get

\begin{align*} (r_{X})_! =& r_{\tau_{\leq 1}(X)}_! \circ \mathrm{colim}_{\tilde{X}} \tilde{\mathcal{F}} \\ =& \mathrm{colim}_{\tau_{\leq 1}(X)} \mathrm{colim}_{\tilde{X}} \tilde{\mathcal{F}} \\ \end{align*}
