Proposition

There exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ind}(\mathrm{An}_{\mathrm{fin}}) \simeq \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ind}(\iota): \mathrm{Ind}(\mathrm{An}_{\mathrm{fin}}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathrm{An}^{\omega}) \end{align*}

which is induced by the fully faithful inclusion $\mathrm{An}_{\mathrm{fin}} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}^{\omega}$.
