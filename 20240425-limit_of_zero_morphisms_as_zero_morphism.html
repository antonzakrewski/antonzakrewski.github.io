limit of zero morphisms as zero morphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a pointed category, $C_i \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ objects and $\mathrm{lim}(C_i)$ the limit Let $0$ be the zero morphism, then the limit

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}(0) = 0 \end{align*}

is also the zero morphism.

Proof

follows dually from colimit of zero morphisms as zero morphism
