universal property of a quotient map

Proposition

Let $X,Y,Z$ be topological spaces and $\pi: X \twoheadrightarrow Y$ a quotient map and $f: Y \rightarrow Z$ a map

TFAE:

  1. $f \circ \pi$ is continuous
  2. $f$ is continuous

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Suppose $O \subseteq Z$ is open. Then by continuity $\pi^{-1}[f^{-1}[O]]$ is open. hence by surjective image of a preimage and definition of a quotient map, $f^{-1}[O]$ is open

2) $\implies$ 1)

corollary of composition of continuous maps
