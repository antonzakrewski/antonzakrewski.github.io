correspondence theorem for normal subgroups

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group $N$ a normal subgroup and $H \supseteq N$ a subgroup TFAE:

  1. $H/N$ is a normal subgroup of $G/N$
  2. $H$ is a normal subgroup of $G$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Suppose $g \in G$ and $h \in H$. Then since $H/N \trianglelefteq G/N$, we conclude that

\begin{align*} gN \cdot hN \cdot \left(gN \right)^{-1} = h'N \end{align*}

for some $h'N \in H/N$ resp. $h' \in H$

Furthermore

\begin{align*} h'N =& gN \cdot hN \cdot \left(gN \right)^{-1} \\ =& \left(gh g^{-1}\right)N \end{align*}

Thus there exists an $n \in N$ such that

\begin{align*} h' \cdot n = ghg^{-1} \end{align*}

since $N \subseteq H$ and $H$ is closed, it follows, that

\begin{align*} h' \cdot n \in H \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)
