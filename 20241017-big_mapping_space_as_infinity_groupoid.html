big mapping space as infinity-groupoid

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category. Then for $y \in \mathcal{C}_0$ the big mapping space

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{F},y) \end{align*}

is an infinity-groupoid
