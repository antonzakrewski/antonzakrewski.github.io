functor preserving chain homotopies

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be additive categories, $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}), \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{B})$ the category of chain complexes and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{B}) \end{align*}

be a functor

Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to preserve chain homotopies, if for chain complexes with a chain homotopy $h$

the resulting chain complexes

are chain homtopic
