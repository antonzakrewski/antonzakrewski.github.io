colimit functor as right exact functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category admitting small colimits of a shape $\mathcal{J}$ and $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{J}, \mathcal{A})$ the functor category. Then the colim functor is right exact

Proof

corollary of:

  • colimits commute with colimits
