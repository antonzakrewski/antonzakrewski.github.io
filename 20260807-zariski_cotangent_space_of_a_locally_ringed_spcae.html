zariski cotangent space of a locally ringed spcae

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{O}_{X})$ be a locally ringed space and $x \in X$. Then the zariski cotangent space is defined as the $k(x)$-module $T_x^*(X) = \mathfrak{m}_{x}/\mathfrak{m}_{x}^2$

Remark

$\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$ is isomorphic to $\mathfrak{m} \otimes_{R} R/\mathfrak{m}$.
