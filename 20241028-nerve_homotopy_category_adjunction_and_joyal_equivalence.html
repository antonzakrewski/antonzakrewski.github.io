nerve homotopy category adjunction and naturally equivalent functors

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathcal{D}$ a \(1\)-category and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}: h(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

a \(1\)-categorical functor out of the the homotopy category

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F} \cong \mathcal{G}$ are natural isomorphic functors
  2. $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ under the nerve functor homotopy category adjunction are naturally equivalent

Proof

follows from

  • natural isomorphism as functor out of the product with J

where

\begin{align*} J \times h(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

corresponds by homotopy category functor preserves finite products and homotopy category of a nerve canonically isomorphic to a functor

\begin{align*} h(N(J) \times \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

which by the homotopy category Nerve adjunction corresponds to

\begin{align*} N(J) \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow N(\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

which by inner hom of simplicial Sets corresponds to

\begin{align*} N(J) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

which shows that they are precisely naturally equivalent (todo)
