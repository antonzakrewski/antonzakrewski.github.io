compact coproduct and compact components in a pointed category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a pointed category, $c_i$ be objects and $\kappa$ a regular cardinal. Suppose $\coprod_{i \in I} c_i$ is kappa compact.

Then each $c_i$ is kappa compact for each $i\in I$.

Proof

follows as $c_i$ is a retract of $\coprod c_i$ and compact object closed under retracts.
