symmetric monoidal equivalence between filtered abelian groups and tau graded abelian groups

Proposition

There exists a symmetric monoidal equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{FilAb} \simeq \mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{Z}[\tau]}(\mathrm{grAb}) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{grAb}$ is equipped with the <tensor product without sign>

Proof
