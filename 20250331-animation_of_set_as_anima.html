Animation of set as Anima

Proposition

The animation of category set is the infinity category of Anima

Proof

Note that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An}(\mathrm{Set}) \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\coprod}((\mathrm{Set}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}})^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\coprod}(\mathrm{FinSet}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An}) \end{align*}

(cf. finite sets as 1-sfp object, animation as functors preserving finite coproducts)

But finite set as subcategory of Anima generated by a point under finite infinity coproducts, hence coproduct preserving functors out of $\mathrm{FinSet}$ are given by arbitrary functors

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(*,\mathrm{An}) \simeq \mathrm{An} \end{align*}
