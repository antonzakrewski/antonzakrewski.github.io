element criterion for a closed subfunctor via the quasi-coherent ideal

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor, $A \subseteq X$ an closed subfunctor classified by a classified by a qcideal $I$ and $x \in X$ an element. TFAE:

  1. $x$ lies in $U$
  2. for $x: \mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow X$, the induce map reads as $x^{-1}[I] = I$.

Proof

follows from the pullback along representables criterion for elements in subfunctors
