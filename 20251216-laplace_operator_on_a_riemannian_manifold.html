laplace operator on a riemannian manifold

Definition

Let $M$ be a riemannian manifold. Then the laplace operator is defined as

\begin{align*} \Delta: \Omega^k(M) \rightarrow \Omega^k(M) \\ \omega \mapsto& (\mathrm{d}^\dagger \mathrm{d} + \mathrm{d} \mathrm{d}^{\dagger})(\omega) \end{align*}
