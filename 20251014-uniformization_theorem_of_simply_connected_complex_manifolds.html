uniformization theorem of simply connected, complex 1-dimensional manifolds

Proposition

Let $X$ be a simply connected, \(1\)-dimensional complex manifold Then

\begin{align*} X \simeq \begin{cases} \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^1 & \mbox{if } \\ \mathbb{C} & \mbox{if } \\ \{z \in \mathbb{C} \vert \vert \vert < 1 \} & \mbox{if } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
