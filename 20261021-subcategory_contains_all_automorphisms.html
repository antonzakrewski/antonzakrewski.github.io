subcategory contains all automorphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $\mathcal{C}' \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ a subcategory. Then for any object $x \in \mathcal{C}'$, any automorphism in $\mathcal{C}$ also lies in $\mathcal{C}'$

Proof

follows from the inclusion of path components $(\mathcal{C}')^{\simeq} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\simeq}$.
