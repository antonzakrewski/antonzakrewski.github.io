generator of a ring as module as units

Proposition

Let \(A\) be a ring, then the generators of $A$ as left \(A\)-module are precisely the left units

Proof

unit ~> generator

follows from

\begin{align*} r = (r \cdot g^{-1}) \cdot g \end{align*}

generator ~> unit

follows from taking looking at how the $1$ gets generated

\begin{align*} r \cdot g = 1 \end{align*}
