constant colimit in spectra is tensor product

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{const}_E: B \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}$ be a constant functor. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{const}_E \simeq \mathbb{S}[B] \otimes E \end{align*}

Proof

follows as $\mathrm{const}_E = \mathrm{const}_{E \otimes \mathbb{S}}$ and the colimit preserves colimits in both variables, hence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_B (E \otimes \mathbb{S}) \simeq& E \otimes (\mathrm{colim}_B \mathbb{S}) \\ \simeq& E\otimes \mathbb{S}[B] \end{align*}
