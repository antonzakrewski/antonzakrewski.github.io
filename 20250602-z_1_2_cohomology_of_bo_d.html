Z[1/2] cohomology of BO(d)

Proposition

The $\mathbb{Z}[\frac{1}{2}]$ singular cohomology of BO is given by

\begin{align*} H^*(\mathrm{BO}(d), \mathbb{Z}[\frac{1}{2}]) \simeq \mathbb{Z}[\frac{1}{2}](p_1,...,p_{\lfloor \frac{d}{2} \rfloor}) \end{align*}
