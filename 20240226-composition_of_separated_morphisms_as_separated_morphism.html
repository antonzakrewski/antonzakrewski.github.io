composition of separated morphisms is separated

Proposition

Let $Z \xrightarrow{g} Y \xrightarrow{f} X$ be separated morphisms of algebraic functors Then so is the composition

Proof

follows from pullback square of relative diagonals, which shows that the right square is a pullback In particular, $\Delta_f, \Delta_g$ are closed immersions by assumption and hence so is $\iota: Z \times_{Y} Z \rightarrow Z \times_{X} Z$ (cf. closed immersions are closed under pullbacks).

Therefore the composite $\Delta_{f \circ g} = \iota \circ \Delta_{g}$ is a closed immersion (cf. composition of closed immersions).
