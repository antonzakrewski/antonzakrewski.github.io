noether Normalization

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $A$ a finite type K-Algebra with dimesnion $\mathrm{dim}(A) = n > 0$. Then there exists a maximal algebraically independent subset

\begin{align*} \{c_1,...,c_n\} \subseteq A \end{align*}

such that

\begin{align*} K[c_1,...,c_n] \hookrightarrow A \end{align*}

is an integral extension

Proof
