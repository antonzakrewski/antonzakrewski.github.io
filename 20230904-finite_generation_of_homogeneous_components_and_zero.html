finite generation of homogeneous components for finitely generated Algebra and Module

Proposition

Let (R = \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0} R_n) be a graded ring and $M = \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0}$ a graded module. Suppose $R$ is a finitely generated \(R_0\)-Algebra and $M$ a finitely generated \(R\)-module. Then $M_n$ for $n \in \mathbb{N}_0$ is a finitely generated module

Proof
