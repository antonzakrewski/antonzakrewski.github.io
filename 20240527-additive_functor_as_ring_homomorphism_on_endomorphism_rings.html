additive functor as ring homomorphism on endomorphism rings

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be additive categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ be an additive functor. Then $\mathcal{F}$ induces a ring-homomorphism on the endomorphism ring
