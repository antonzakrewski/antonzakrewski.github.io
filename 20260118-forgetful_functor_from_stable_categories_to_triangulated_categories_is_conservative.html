forgetful functor from stable categories to triangulated categories is conservative

Proposition

The forgetful functor from stable categories to triangulated categories is a conservative functor

Proof

  • projection to the homotopy category preserves products / projection to the homotopy category preserves coproducts
  • $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(a,b)$ is a group
  • additive functor is a morphism of groups
  • suffices to show that this map is an pi_0 equivalence and an equivalence on the zero component

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an exact functor. Note that we may detect whether a functor is essentially surjective on the level of homotopy categories

So it remains to show that it is fully faithful, so let $a,b \in \mathcal{C}$ and we need to show that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(a,b) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(a),\mathcal{F}(b)) \end{align*}

is an equivalence. So using whitehead theorem for anima it suffices to show this for $\pi_n$

where $\Sigma^n$ denotes the shift functor on $\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}$ and $h \mathcal{C}, h \mathcal{D}$ resp.
