product of exact infinity category as exact infinity category

Proposition

let $\mathcal{E}, \mathcal{E}'$ be exact infinity categories. Then the product category $\mathcal{E} \times \mathcal{E}'$ is also an exact infinity category with the structure

\begin{align*} \mathrm{in}(\mathcal{E} \times \mathcal{E}') =& \mathrm{in}(\mathcal{E}) \times \mathrm{in}(\mathcal{E}') \\ \mathrm{pr}(\mathcal{E} \times \mathcal{E}') =&\mathrm{pr}(\mathcal{E}) \times \mathrm{pr}(\mathcal{E}') \end{align*}

and furthermore exhibits $\mathcal{E} \times \mathcal{E}'$ as product in $\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{Ex}}$

Proof
