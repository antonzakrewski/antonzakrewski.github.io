chern class of the complex conjugate

Proposition

Let $E$ be a complex vector bundle and $\overline{E}$ the conjugation Then

\begin{align*} c_d(\overline{E}) = (-1)^d c_{d}(E) \end{align*}

Proof

complex conjugation of a vector bundle and orientation reversing

\begin{align*} e(\overline{E}) = (-1)^d e(E) \end{align*}
