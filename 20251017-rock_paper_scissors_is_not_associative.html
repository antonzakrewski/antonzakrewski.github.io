rock paper scissors is not associative

Proposition

Let $R,P,S$ be rock, paper and scissors. Then the map

\begin{align*} \cdot: \{R,P,S\} \times \{R,P,S\} \rightarrow \{R,P,S\} \\ (X,Y) \mapsto& \begin{cases} X & \mbox{if } X \neq Y, X \mathrm{ beats } Y\\ Y & \mbox{if } X \neq Y, Y \mathrm{ beats } X\\ X & \mbox{if } X = Y \end{cases} \end{align*}

is not associative

\begin{align*} (RP)S=PS=S R(PS)=RS=R \end{align*}

Proof
