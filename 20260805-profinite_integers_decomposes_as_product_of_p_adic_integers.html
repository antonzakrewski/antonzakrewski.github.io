profinite integers decomposes as product of p-adic integers

Proposition

There exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \widehat{\mathbb{Z}} \simeq \prod_{p} \mathbb{Z}_p \end{align*}

Proof

Observe that the general chinese remainder theorem shows that there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z} \simeq \prod_{p} \mathbb{Z}/p^{n_p} \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

and under the CRT, any quotient map $\mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}/n' \mathbb{Z}$ corresponds to the quotient maps $\prod_{p} \mathbb{Z}/p^{n_p} \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \prod_{p} \mathbb{Z}/p^{n_p'} \mathbb{Z}$ in each factor. Hence $\lim_{n} \mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z}$ is given - under this isomorphism - by $\lim_{n} \prod_{p} \mathbb{Z}/p^{n_p} \mathbb{Z}$ and therefore we may use that limits commute with limits to get $\prod_{p} \lim_{n} \mathbb{Z}/ p^{n_p} \mathbb{Z}$. Now we have a lot of $0$ in this: Either we may ignore these (+ some limit computation) or we can observe that this diagram is right kan extended from the classical diagram (by staring at the poset) and use that limit of a right kan extended diagram is canonically equivalent to the limit of the original digaram.
