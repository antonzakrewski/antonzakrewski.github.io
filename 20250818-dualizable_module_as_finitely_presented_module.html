dualizable module as finitely generetad, projective module

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a commutative ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod, $M$ a module TFAE:

  1. $M$ is a dualizable object
  2. $M$ is a finitely generated projective module

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

By assumption we get maps

\begin{align*} \mathrm{coev}: R \rightarrow& M \otimes \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(M,R) \\ 1 \mapsto& \sum_{i=1}^n m_i \otimes f_i \\ \mathrm{ev}: M \otimes \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(M,R) \rightarrow& R \\ m \otimes f \mapsto& f(m) \end{align*}

which satisfies some triangle equality, in particular

\begin{align*} M \rightarrow M \otimes \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(M,R) \otimes M \rightarrow M \\ m \mapsto (\sum_{i=1}^n m_i \otimes f_i) \otimes m \mapsto \sum_{i=1}^n m_i \cdot f_i(m) = m % \\ \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(M,R) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(M,R) \otimes M \otimes \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(M,R) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(M,R) \\ % g \mapsto g \otimes (\sum_{i=1}^n m_i \otimes f_i) \mapsto \sum_{i=1}^n g(m_i) \otimes f_i = g \end{align*}

This in particular shows that $M$ is finitely generated by $m_i$ since $f_i(m) \in R$ are always scalars

Now consider the maps

\begin{align*} p: R^I \rightarrow& M \\ (r_i) \mapsto& \sum r_i m_i \iota: M \rightarrow& R^I \\ m \mapsto (f_i(m)) \end{align*}

where the latter map is obtained by $M \rightarrow M \otimes \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(M,R) \xrightarrow{\mathrm{ev}}$

Then we get

\begin{align*} M \rightarrow R^I \rightarrow M \\ m \mapsto (f_i(m)) \mapsto \sum f_i(m) m_i = m \end{align*}

which shows that $M$ is a retract of a $R^I$ (cf. retract of a projective object as projective object)

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from:
