Proposition

Let $G$ be a group such that each element $g \in G \setminus \{1\}$ has group order $2$. Then $G$ is abelian

Proof

Let $\alpha,\beta \in G$, then since $\alpha = \alpha^{-1}$ it follows, that

\begin{align*} \alpha \beta =& \alpha^{-1} \beta^{-1} \\ =& \left(\beta \alpha \right)^{-1} \\ =& \beta \cdot \alpha \end{align*}
