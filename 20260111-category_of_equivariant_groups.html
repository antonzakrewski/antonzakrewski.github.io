category of equivariant groups

Definition

The category of equivariant groups is defined as category containing

  1. pairs $(G,H)$ together with a group-homomorphism $G \rightarrow \mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{Grp}}(H)$
  2. morphisms are given by pairs $(G,H) \xrightarrow{\psi,\varphi} (G',H')$ which are \(G\)-equivariant
\begin{align*} \varphi(g \cdot h) = \psi(g) \cdot \varphi(h) \end{align*}
