suspension of a perfect category

Definition

Let $\kappa$ be an uncountable regular cardinal. The suspension functor of category Cat Perf is defined as

\begin{align*} \Sigma_{\kappa} -: \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{perf}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{perf}} \\ \mathcal{A} \mapsto& \mathrm{cofib}(\mathcal{A} \rightarrow (\mathrm{Ind}_{\omega}(\mathcal{A}))^{\kappa}) \end{align*}
