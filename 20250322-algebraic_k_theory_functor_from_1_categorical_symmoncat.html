direct sum algebraic k-theory functor from 1-categorical SymMonCat

Definition

The algebraic k-theory functor from \(1\)-categorical Symmetric monoidal categories is defined as infinity functor

\begin{align*} K: \mathrm{SymMonCat}_{1} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp} \end{align*}

given by the composition

of the inclusion of symmetric monoidal 1 categories into symmetric monoidal infinity categories and algebraic k-theory functor from infinity SymMonCat
