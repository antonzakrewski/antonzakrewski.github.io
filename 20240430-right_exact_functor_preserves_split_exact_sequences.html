right exact functor preserves split exact sequences

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ a right exact functor Then $\mathcal{F}$ preserves split exact sequences

Proof

corollary of:

  • right exact functor as additive functor
  • additive functor preserves split exact sequences
