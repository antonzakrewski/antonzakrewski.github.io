functor to the sing functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be categories, where $\mathcal{A}$ is a small category and $\mathcal{B}$ is cocomplete. Then for the sing functor we get a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sing}_{-}: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{A},\mathcal{B}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{B}, \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Set}))^{\mathrm{op}} \\ \mathcal{F} \mapsto& \mathrm{Sing}_{\mathcal{F}}(-) \\ (\eta: \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{G}) \mapsto& \eta^*(X) \end{align*}
