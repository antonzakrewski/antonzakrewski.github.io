Proposition

\begin{align*} f: (B,A) \rightarrow (Y,X) \end{align*}

Then there exists a relative cellular map

\begin{align*} g: (B,A) \rightarrow (Y,X) \end{align*}

and a homotopy relative to $A$

\begin{align*} g \simeq_{A} f \end{align*}
