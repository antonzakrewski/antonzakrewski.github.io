coordinate ring of a reduced scheme is reduced

Proposition

Let $X$ be a reduced scheme, then the coordinate ring $\mathcal{O}(X)$ is a reduced ring

Proof

use:

  • scheme is given as a coequalizer of affine schemes
  • the category of schemes is a full subcategory of $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}, \mathrm{Set})$
  • product of reduced rings & subring of a reduced ring as reduced ring
