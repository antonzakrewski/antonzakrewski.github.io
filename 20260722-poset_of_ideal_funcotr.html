poset of ideals functor

Definition

The poset of ideals functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ideals}:\mathrm{CAlg} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat} \\ R \mapsto& \{I \hookrightarrow R\} \\ (f: R \rightarrow S ) \mapsto& ((I \hookrightarrow R) \mapsto& (I \otimes_{R} S \rightarrow R \otimes_{R} S)) \end{align*}

Remark

here we identify an ideal as submodule $I \hookrightarrow R$. Then we take the tensor with $S$ and get a map $I \otimes_R S \rightarrow R \otimes_{R} S \simeq R$ where we identify this map with the image, i.e. some factorization $I \otimes S \rightarrow IS \hookrightarrow S$
