geometric realization of a simplicial set as cw complex

Proposition

Let $X$ be a simplicial set. Then the geometric realization of $X$ is canonically a CW-Complex

Proof

follows from

  • skeleton of a simplicial set as pushout
  • simplicial set as colimit over skeleta
  • cw structure of a simplicial set
  • left adjoint preserves colimits
  • geometric realization as left adjoint
  • geometric realization preserves monomorphisms

where

  • geometric realization of a boundary as n-sphere

and

\begin{align*} \vert \Delta^n \vert =& \Delta^n_{\mathrm{top}} \\ \cong D^n \end{align*}

after choosing homeomorphisms $\varphi: \Delta^n_{\mathrm{top}} \rightarrow D^n$
