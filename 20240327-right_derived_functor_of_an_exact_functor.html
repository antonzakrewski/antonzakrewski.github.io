right derived functor of an exact functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ a left exact functor Suppose the right derived functor exists.

TFAE:

  1. the right derived functor is given by the zero functor $\mathcal{RF}_n(-) = 0$ for $n \geq 1$
  2. the right derived functor is given by the zero functor $\mathcal{RF}_n(-) = 0$ for $n = 1$
  3. $\mathcal{F}$ is an exact functor

Proof

follows dual to left derived functor of an exact functor
