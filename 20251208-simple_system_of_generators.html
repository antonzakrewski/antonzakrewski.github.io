simple system of generators of a commutative graded F2-Algebra

Definition

Let $R$ be a graded-commutative F2-Algebra. Then a subset $I \subseteq R$ consisting of homogeneous elements is said to be a simple system of generators, if the set

\begin{align*} \{X_{J} = \prod_{x \in J} x \vert \vert J \subseteq I \textrm{ finite}\} \end{align*}

forms a basis
