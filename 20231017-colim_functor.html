colim functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{J}$ be categories and let $\mathrm{FunCoLim}(\mathcal{J}, \mathcal{C})$ be the Category FunColimit Then the colimit functor is defined as covariant functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}: \mathrm{FunCoLim}(\mathcal{J}, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow& \mathcal{C} \\ (\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}) \mapsto& \mathrm{colim}_{\mathcal{F}} \\ (\eta: \mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \mathcal{G}) \mapsto& \left(\eta': \mathrm{colim}_{\mathcal{F}} \rightarrow \mathrm{colim}_{\mathcal{G}} \right) \end{align*}

where $\eta'$ is the morphism induced by the universal property
