group of order 56 not simple

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group of group order $56$. Then $G$ is not simple

Proof

Since $56$ factorizes as $56 = 8 \cdot 7 = 2^3 \cdot 7$, by number of sylow groups we get the possibilities of

\begin{align*} s_7 \in& \{1,8\} \\ s_8 \in& \{1,7\} \end{align*}

Suppose $s_7,s_8 \neq 1$, because otherwise by sole sylow group as normal subgroup, there exists an normal subgroup

Then by group of order p as cyclic group, $U_7 \cong \mathbb{Z}/ 7 \mathbb{Z}$ and different cyclic subgroups as disjoint groups it follows, that there exist $8 \cdot \varphi(7) = 8 \cdot 6 = 48$ different elements of group order $7$ (see: euler's phi function). Furthermore, there exists at least $8$ elements of order $2^n, n \in \mathbb{N}^+$, as there are more than one different subgroups of order $8$. Together with the neutral element, there exists at least $57$ elements, contradiction
