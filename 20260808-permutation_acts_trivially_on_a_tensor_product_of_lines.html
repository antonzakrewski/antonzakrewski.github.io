permutation acts trivially on a tensor product of lines

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $L$ a Line. Then the $\Sigma_n$ action on $L^{\otimes n}$ is trivial.

Proof

Let $f_1,...,f_n$ generate $R$ such that $L_{f} \cong R_{f}$ if free over $R_{f}$ (cf. finitely generated projective module as finitely locally free module). Then the action on $R^{\otimes d}$ is trivial as $R$ is the monoidal unit. Furthermore, zariski descent for modules implies that the $\Sigma_n$ action on $R_{f}$ is trivial, hence trivial on $L^{\otimes n}$.
