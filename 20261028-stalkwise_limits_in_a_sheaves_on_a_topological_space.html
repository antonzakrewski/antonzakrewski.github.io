stalkwise finite limits in a sheaves on a topological space

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable 1-category and $X$ a topological space. Let $I \rightarrow \mathrm{Shv}^{\mathrm{hyp}}(X,\mathcal{C})$ be a finite diagram of sheaves
