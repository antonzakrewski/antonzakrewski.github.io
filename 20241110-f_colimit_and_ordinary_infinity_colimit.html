f-colimit and ordinary infinity colimit

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \Delta^0$ the terminal morphism and $p: K^{\triangleright} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a diagram out of the left cone

TFAE:

  1. $p$ is an colimit of $p_{\vert K}$
  2. $p$ is a 1-colimit

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
