preadditive infinity-category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a pointed infinity category Then $\mathcal{C}$ is said to be preadditive, if

  1. products
  2. coproducts

exist and

  1. <the canonical morphism>
\begin{align*} C_1 \coprod C_2 \rightarrow C_1 \times C_2 \end{align*}

is an equivalence

also called semiadditive
