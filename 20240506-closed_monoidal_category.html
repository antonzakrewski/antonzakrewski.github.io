closed monoidal category

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a monoidal category. Then $\mathcal{V}$ is said to be closed, if there exists an internal hom

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{V}}(X, \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(Y,Z)) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X \otimes_{\mathcal{V}} Y, Z) \end{align*}
