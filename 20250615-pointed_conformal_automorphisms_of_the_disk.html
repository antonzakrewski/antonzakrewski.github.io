pointed conformal automorphisms of the disk

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{D}$ be the open unit ball, then the conformal automorphism group is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{D}) = \{ e^{\mathrm{i} \theta} \psi_{\alpha} : \theta \in \mathbb{R}, \alpha \in \mathbb{D}\} \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} \psi_{\alpha}(z) = \frac{\alpha - z }{1 - \overline{a}z} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from

  • conformal automorphism group of the disk where any conformal map $\varphi$ is given as
\begin{align*} \varphi(z) = e^{\mathrm{i} \theta} \psi_{\alpha}(z) \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} \varphi(0) = 0 \end{align*}

if and only if $\psi_{\alpha}(0) = 0$ which is equivalent to $\alpha = 0$ resp. $\psi_{\alpha} = \mathrm{id}$

\begin{align*} \psi_{\alpha}(z) = \frac{\alpha - z }{1 - \overline{a}z} \end{align*}
