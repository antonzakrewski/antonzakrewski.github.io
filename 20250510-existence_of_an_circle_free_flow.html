existence of an circle free flow

Proposition

Let $N$ be a network and $f$ a flow. Then there exists a flow $g$ with

  1. $\mathrm{val}(g) = \mathrm{val}(f)$ (cf. value of a flow)
  2. $g$ is circlefree

Proof

Suppose $f$ has a circle indexed by $v_1,...,v_k$. Let $c \coloneqq \mathrm{min}(f(v_1,v_2))$. Then define

\begin{align*} g_1(v_i,v_j) \coloneqq \begin{cases} f(v_i,v_j) & \mbox{if } v_i,v_j \mathrm{ not in circle} \\ f(v_i,v_j) - c & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

and iterate until you get a circle free flow:

note that $g_1$ satisfies the capacity restrictions, since $g_1 < f$. Furthermore flow conservation also follows from flow conservation of $f$: Let $v$ be any vertex, then we claim that the difference between input and output does not change, since any vertex $w$ which appears in the circle $n$ times gets $n \cdot c$ less as total input and $n \cdot c$ less as total output.

algorithm ends
