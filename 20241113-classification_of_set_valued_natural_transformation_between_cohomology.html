classification of cohomology operations

Proposition

Let $A,B$ be abelian groups, then the set-valued natural transformation are classified by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{NatTrans}_{\mathrm{Set}}(H^k(-,A), H^n(-,B)) \cong& H^n(K(A,k),B) \\ \cong& \end{align*}

where $K(A,k)$ is an Eilenberg-MacLane space

Proof

follows from:

  • representability of singular cohomology
  • natural transformation from covariant hom functor as postcomposition
