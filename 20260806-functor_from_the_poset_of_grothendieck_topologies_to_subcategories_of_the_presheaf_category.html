functor from the poset of grothendieck topologies to subcategories of the presheaf category via sheaves

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. Then there exists a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sh}_{-}: \{\textrm{topologies on } \mathcal{C}\}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \{\textrm{subcategories of } \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})\} \\ \tau \mapsto& \mathrm{Sh}_{\tau}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

between partially ordered sets regarded as category

Proof

Let $\tau \leq \tau'$ be grothendieck topologies, where $\tau$ is coarser. Then being a sheaf on $\tau$ is a weaker condition, hence $\mathrm{Sh}_{\tau'} \subseteq \mathrm{Sh}_{\tau}$.
