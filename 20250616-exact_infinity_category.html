exact infinity category

Definition

An exact infinity category is defined as additive infinity category $\mathcal{E}$ together with subcategories

  1. $\mathrm{in}(\mathcal{E})$
  2. $\mathrm{pr}(\mathcal{E})$

such that

  1. the initial morphism $\emptyset: 0 \rightarrow X\in \mathrm{in}(\mathcal{E})$
  2. the terminal morphism $\emptyset: X \rightarrow 0 \in \mathrm{pr}(\mathcal{E})$
  3. $\mathrm{in}$ is closed under pushouts, $\mathrm{pr}$ is closed under pullbacks
