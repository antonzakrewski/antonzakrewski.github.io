direct summand of a finitely generated module as finitely generated module

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $M$ a finitely generated module over $R$. Suppose $M = N \oplus N'$ is a direct sum. Then $N$ is also finitely generated.

Proof

Follows from

\begin{align*} R^n \arrow[twoheadrightarrow, ]{r}{} & N \olpus N' \arrow[twoheadrightarrow, ]{r}{} & N \end{align*}
