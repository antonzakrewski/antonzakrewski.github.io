forgetful functor from quasi-coherent modules for a zariski-local epimorphism is conservative

Proposition

Let $(Y_i \rightarrow X)$ be a zariski-local epimorphism, then the forgetful functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mod}_{X} \rightarrow \prod_{i \in I} \mathrm{Mod}_{Y_i} \end{align*}

from quasi-coherent modules is conservative.
