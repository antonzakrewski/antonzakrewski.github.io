category of infinity cosheaves on a topological space

Definition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $\mathcal{C}$ be an bicomplete infinity category. Then the category of cosheaves is defined as infinity category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{coSh}_{\mathcal{C}}(X) \coloneqq \mathrm{Sh}_{\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}}(X)^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}
