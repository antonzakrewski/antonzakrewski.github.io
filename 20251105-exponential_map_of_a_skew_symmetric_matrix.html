exponential map from skew symmetric matrices to the orthogonal group

Definition / Proposition

The exponential restricts from the skew symmetric matrices to the orthogonal group

\begin{align*} \mathrm{exp}: \mathfrak{o}(n) \rightarrow O(n) \end{align*}

Proof

Let $A \in \mathfrak{o}$. Then in particular $A^t \cdot A = -A \cdot A = A \cdot A^t$. Therefore we may apply the functional equation of the matrix exponential & exponential map and transpose to get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{e}^{0} =& \mathrm{e}^{A + A^t} \\ =& \mathrm{e}^{A} \cdot \mathrm{e}^{A^t) \\ =& \mathrm{e}^A \cdot (\mathrm{e}^{A})^t \end{align*}
