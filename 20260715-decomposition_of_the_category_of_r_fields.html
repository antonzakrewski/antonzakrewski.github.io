decomposition of the category of R-fields

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, then the category of R-fields is equivalent to

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Field}_{R} \simeq \coprod_{\mathfrak{p} \subseteq R} \mathrm{Field}_{R_{\mathfrak{p}}/\mathfrak{p}R_{\mathfrak{p}}} \end{align*}

Proof

Let $K$ be an R-field, then the kernel of $R \rightarrow K$ is a prime ideal (cf. kernel of a ring epimorphism to an integral domain as prime ideal). Moreover, $K$ contains $R_{\mathfrak{p}}/\mathfrak{p} R_{\mathfrak{p}}$. hence each object lies in $\mathrm{Field}_{R_{\mathfrak{p}}/\mathfrak{p}R_{\mathfrak{p}}}$.

So now let $K,K'$ be a R-fields, then any map $f: K \rightarrow K'$ of R-Algebras / R-fields induces a commutative diagram

moreover, $f$ is injective (cf. morphism between skew fields is injective) and hence the map 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
& R \arrow[]{rdd}{} \arrow[]{ldd}{}& \\
R_{\mathfrak{p}}/\mathfrak{p}R_{\mathfrak{p}} \arrow[d]{}{} \arrow[dashed]{rd}{}&  & \\
K \arrow[]{rr}{}& & K'
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

this shows that $K'$ is also a $R_{\mathfrak{p}}/\mathfrak{p} R_{\mathfrak{p}}$ field and not a field over another field of fractions:
