k-invariant of an object for a t-structure

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category equipped with a t-structure and $X$ an object in $\mathcal{C}$. Then the \(n\)-th k-invariant is defined as map $k_n: \tau_{\leq n-1} X \rightarrow \pi_n(X)[n+1]$ or equivalently the element $k_n \in \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{C}}^{n+1}(\pi_n(X), \tau_{\leq n-1} X)$

Here the map $k_n$ is obtained by rotating the fiber sequence

\begin{align*} \tau_{\leq n} X \rightarrow \tau_{\leq n-1} X \rightarrow \pi_n(X)[n+1] \end{align*}
