evaluation out of the mapping space for a sphere as serre fibration

Proposition

The evaluation map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ev}: \mathrm{Map}(S^n, X) \rightarrow X \end{align*}

is a serre fibration

Proof

Take

\begin{align*} p: X \rightarrow * \end{align*}

as serre fibration

\begin{align*} \iota: * \rightarrow S^n \end{align*}

as locally finite CW Pair

Then the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}(S^n, X) \rightarrow \mathrm{Map}(*, X) \times_{\mathrm{Map}(*,*} \mathrm{Map}(S^n,*) \simeq \mathrm{Map}(*,X) \simeq X \end{align*}

is a serre fibration by precomposition with a cw inclusion and postcomposition with a serre fibration as serre fibration
