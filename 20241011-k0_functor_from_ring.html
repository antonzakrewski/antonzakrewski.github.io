K0 functor from Ring

Definition

Let $\mathrm{Ring}$ be the category Ring. Then the $K_0$ functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} K_0: \mathrm{Ring} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ab} R \mapsto& K_0(R) \\ (f: R \rightarrow S) \mapsto& \begin{cases} \mathrm{Proj}(f)^#: K_0(R) \rightarrow& K_0(S) \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
  • extension of scalars preserves projective modules
  • restriction of scalars as right adjoint / extension of scalars as left adjoint
