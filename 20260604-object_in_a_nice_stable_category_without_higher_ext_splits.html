Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category equipped with a complete t-structure compatible with products and coproducts. Assume that $\mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{C}}^{n}(A,B)$ vanishes for objects $A,B \in \mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}$ and $n \geq 2$.

Then each object $X \in \mathcal{C}$ is equivalent to

\begin{align*} X \simeq \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \pi_n(X)[n] \end{align*}

Proof
