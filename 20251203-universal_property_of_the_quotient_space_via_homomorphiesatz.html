universal property of the quotient space via homomorphiesatz

Proposition

Let $U \subseteq V,W$ be vector spaces and $\pi_U: V \twoheadrightarrow V/U$ the kanonical projection. Then precomposition induces a vectorspace-isomorphism

\begin{align*} (\pi_{U})^*: \mathrm{Hom}_{K}(V/U,W) \rightarrow \{f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{K}(V,W) \vert f_{\vert U} = 0 \} \end{align*}

Warning

the proof consists purely of diagram chasing. I think the proof injectivity is a bit absurd (albeit interesting) There exist less abstract proofs :)
