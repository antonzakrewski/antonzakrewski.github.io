pointwise construction of infinity left adjoints

Proposition

Let $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor between infinity categories. TFAE:

  1. $f \dashv g$ is part of an infinity adjunction
  2. the infinity functor
\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(f(-),d) \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,\overline{d}) \end{align*}

is representable for each $d \in \mathcal{D}$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from

\begin{align*} \overline{d} = g(d) \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)

Consider the functor (implicitly currying)

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(-), -): \mathcal{D} \rightarrow& \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) \\ d \mapsto& (c \mapsto \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(c),d) \end{align*}

Then by assumption this functor lands in the essential image of the infinity yoneda functor $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$.
