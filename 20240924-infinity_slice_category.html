infinity slice category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $x \in \mathcal{C}_0$ an object. Then the infinity slice category is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}_{/x} \end{align*}

left slice for $\Delta^0 \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ corresponding to $x$

Explicite

\begin{align*} n \mapsto \mathrm{Hom}_{x/\mathrm{sSet}}(\Delta^n \star \Delta^0, \mathcal{C}) = \{y \in X_{n+1} \vert y(n+1) = x\} \end{align*}

Proof
