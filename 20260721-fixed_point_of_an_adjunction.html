fixed point of an adjunction

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ functors and $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$ an adjunction Then an object $c \in \mathcal{C}$ ($d \in \mathcal{D}$) is a fixed point if the unit (counit) is an equivalence when evaluated at $c$ ($d$)
