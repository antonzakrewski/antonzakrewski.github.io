weak coverage on a category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category Then a coverage on $\mathcal{C}$ is a collection of covering families for each object $c \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ such that

  1. for a covering family $\{f_i: U_i \rightarrow U\}$ and a morphism $g: V \rightarrow U$ there exists a covering family $\{h_j: V_j \rightarrow V \}$ suhc that each composite $gh_j$ factors through some $f_i$

here $k$ is a suitable morphism in $\mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$
