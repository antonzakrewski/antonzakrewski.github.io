natural transformation between left exact and morphism of right derived functors

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be an abelian categories, $\mathcal{F},\mathcal{G}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ left exact functor and $\eta: \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ a natural transformation Suppose $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}$ admit right derived functors $\{\mathcal{LF}_n\}, \{\mathcal{LG}_n\}$

Then this induces a unique morphism of delta functors

\begin{align*} \mathcal{R}(\eta): \mathcal{RF}_n \rightarrow \mathcal{RG}_n \end{align*}

Proof

follows dually to natural transformation between right exact functors and morphism of left derived functors
