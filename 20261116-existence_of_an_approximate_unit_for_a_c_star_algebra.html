existence of an approximate unit for a c star algebra

Proposition

Every C star algebra has an approximate unit

Proof

if $A$ is unital, then the constant net suffices Now assume that $A$ is not unital, then let

\begin{align*} N = \{u \in A^+ : \|a \| < 1\} \end{align*}

Where we take $a < b$ as relation: we claim that it is a filtered poset:

Let $u,v \in N$ then we need to show that there exists an $w \}n N$ with $v \leq w, u \leq w$. Let $s = u (1 - u)^{-1}$ and $t = v (1 - v)^{-1}$.

approximate unit

Fix an $a \in A$ and let $\delta > 0$. Let $b = a a^*$ and $v \coloneqq b ( b + \delta)^{-1} \in N$.
