existence of non equivalent spectra with equivalent truncations

Proposition

There exist non equivalent spectra $X,X'$ such that for each $n$, there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \tau_{\leq n} X \simeq \tau_{\leq n} X' \end{align*}

Proof

Recall that the sphere spectrum has infinitely many, nontrivial homotopy groups (cf. finite, nontrivial spectrum has infinitely many nontrivial homotopy groups). We claim that $X = \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \tau_{\leq n} \mathbb{S}$ and $X' = X \oplus \mathbb{S}$ suffice:

equivalent truncations

Recall that the truncation functor $\tau_{\leq n}$ preserves colimits, which shows

\begin{align*} \tau_{\leq n} X \simeq& \bigoplus_{m \leq n} \tau_{\leq m} \mathbb{S} \oplus \bigoplus_{m > n} \tau_{\leq n} \mathbb{S} \\ \tau_{\leq n} (X \oplus \mathbb{S}) \simeq& \bigoplus_{m \leq n} \tau_{\leq m} \mathbb{S} \oplus \bigoplus_{m > n} \tau_{\leq n} \mathbb{S} \oplus \tau_{\leq n} \mathbb{S} \end{align*}

where some indexing shift of $\bigoplus_{m > n} \tau_{\leq n} \mathbb{S}$ shows that $\bigoplus_{m > n} \tau_{\leq n} \mathbb{S}$ and $\bigoplus_{m > n} \tau_{\leq n} \mathbb{S} \oplus \tau_{\leq n} \mathbb{S}$ are equivalent.

nonequivalent spectra

observe that $\mathbb{S}$ is a retract of $X \oplus \mathbb{S}$. But we claim that $\mathbb{S}$ is not the retract of $\bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \tau_{\leq n} \mathbb{S}$:

Here we can write $\bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \tau_{\leq n} \mathbb{S} \simeq \mathrm{colim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \bigoplus_{m \leq n} \tau_{\leq m} \mathbb{S}$ so any map $\mathbb{S} \rightarrow X$ factors through some finite sum $\bigoplus_{m \leq n} \tau_{\leq m} \mathbb{S}$ as $\mathbb{S}$ is a compact spectrum (cf. equivalence of finite / dualizable / compact spectra). Suppose there exists a split mono $f: \mathbb{S} \rightarrow X$, which factors through some finite sum $\bigoplus_{m \leq n} \tau_{\leq m} \mathbb{S}$. Moreover, the map $f: \mathbb{S} \rightarrow \bigoplus_{m \leq n} \tau_{\leq m} \mathbb{S}$ is still a split mono, since the composition $\mathbb{S} \rightarrow \bigoplus_{m \leq n} \tau_{\leq m} \mathbb{S} \rightarrow X \rightarrow \mathbb{S}$ would be the identity.

This is a contradiction to the statement, that finite, nontrivial spectrum has infinitely many nontrivial homotopy groups (or homotopy group of the sphere spectrum nonzero in infinitely many degrees)
