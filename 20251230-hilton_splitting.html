Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be anima (?), then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \Omega ( \Sigma X \vee \Sigma Y) \simeq \Omega(\Sigma X) \times \Omega (\Sigma Y) \times \Omega \Sigma (\bigvee_{i,j \geq 1} X^{\wedge i} \wedge Y^{\wedge j}) \end{align*}
