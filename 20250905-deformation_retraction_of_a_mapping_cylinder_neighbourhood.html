deformation retraction of a mapping cylinder neighbourhood

Proposition

Let $M,W$ be Manifolds and let $\iota: M \rightarrow W$ be an embedding. Suppose we have given a mapping cylinder neighbourhood $M \hookrightarrow K \hookrightarrow W$.

Thent here exists a canonical deformation retraction

\begin{align*} \overline{r}: K \rightarrow M \end{align*}

Proof
