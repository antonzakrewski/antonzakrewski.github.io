morphism of multiplicative spectral sequences

Definition

Let $(E_{*,*}^r, M_n), (E_{*,*}^r, M_n')$ be multiplicative spectral sequences for $\mathrm{RMod}$. Then a morphism between those spectral sequences is given by a morphism ($\varphi: (E_{*,*}^r, M_n) \rightarrow (E_{*,*}^r, M_n')$ such that

\begin{align*} \varphi^r(x \cdot y) = \varphi^r(x) \cdot \varphi^r(y) \end{align*}
