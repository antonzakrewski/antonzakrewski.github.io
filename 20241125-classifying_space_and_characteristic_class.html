classifying space and characteristic class

Proposition

Let $G$ be a topological group and $A$ an abelian group. Then a characteristic class of $G$ and $A$ determines a unique element

\begin{align*} \theta \in H^n(BG, A) \end{align*}

see:

  • classifying space of a topological group

Proof

follows from the

  • classification theorem of principal g-bundles
  • contravariant Yoneda Lemma

after restricting from $\mathrm{hTop}$ to $\mathrm{hCWC}$ (cf. category hCWC as full subcategory of hTop)
