serre quotient

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, $\mathcal{B}$ a serre subcategory. Then the serre quotient is defined as abelian category $\mathcal{A}/\mathcal{B}$ with an exact functor $p: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{A}/\mathcal{B}$ such that precomposition with it induces a fully faithful functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{ex}}(\mathcal{A}/\mathcal{B},-) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{A},-) \end{align*}

with essential image of functors vanishing on $\mathcal{B} \subseteq \mathcal{A}$
