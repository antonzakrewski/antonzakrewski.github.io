affine space as subspace of Spec

Proposition

Let $K$ be a (algebraically closed) field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space. Then $\mathbb{A}_K^n$ is a subspace of the zariski spectrum $\mathrm{Spec}(K[X_1,...,X_n])$

Proof

bijection for point

  • weak Nullstellensatz

topology

Let $X = V(\mathfrak{a}) \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^n$ a closed subset. Then $\mathcal{V}(\mathfrak{a}) \cap \mathbb{A}_K^n \subseteq \mathrm{Spec}(K[X_1,...,X_n])$ is also closed.
