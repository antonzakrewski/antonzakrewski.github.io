kappa filtered colimits and kappa small limits commute in Anima

Proposition

Let $I$ be an infinity category, $\kappa$ a regular cardinal

TFAE:

  1. $I$ is a kappa filtered infinity category
  2. the infinity limit functor
\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}: \mathrm{Fun}(I, \mathrm{An}) \rightarrow \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

preserves kappa-small colimit
