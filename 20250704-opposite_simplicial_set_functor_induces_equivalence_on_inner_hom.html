opposite simplicial set functor induces equivalence on inner hom

Proposition

Let $(-)^{\mathrm{op}}$ be the opposite simplicial set functor and $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(-,-)$ the inner hom of simplicial Sets. Then there exists a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}((-),(-))} \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}((-)^{\mathrm{op}},(-)^{\mathrm{op}})} \end{align*}
