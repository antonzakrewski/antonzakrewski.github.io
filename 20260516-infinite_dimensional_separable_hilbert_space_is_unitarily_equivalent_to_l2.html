infinite dimensional, separable hilbert space is unitarily equivalent to l2

Proposition

Let $X$ be a infinite dimensional, separable Hilbert space. Then $X$ is unitarily equivalent to $l^2$

Proof

Fix a countable orthonormal basis $\{e_n\}$ (cf. hilbert space admits an orthonormal basis, separable hilbert space and countable orthonormal basis) and consider the map

\begin{align*} U: X \rightarrow& l^2 \\ x \mapsto& ( \langle e_n, x\rangle) \end{align*}

This map -- if well-defined -- is linear as product of linear maps. Moreover, $U(x)$ is well-defined by bessels inequality and also an isometric embedding between metric spaces (hence injective) by parsevals equality. Furthermore, it is surjective by countable orthonormal basis is a schauder basis (i.e., in particular, $\mathrm{span}(U(e_n))$ is dense and we may pull convergent sequnences in $\mathrm{span}(U(e_n))$ back to $X$ and take the limit, since $X$ is complete.).
