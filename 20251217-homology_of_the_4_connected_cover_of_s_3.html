homology of the 4-connected cover of S^3

Proposition

Let $F_4$ be the 4-cover of $S^3$. Then its integral homology is given by

\begin{align*} \tilde{H}_*(F_4, \mathbb{Z}) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z}/k \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } * = 2k-1 \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • fundamental theorem of serre class theory for finite abelian groups is a derived serre ring
  • universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology
  • cohomology of the 4-connected cover of S^3
