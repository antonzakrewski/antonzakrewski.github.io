right kan extension formula of the forgetful functor for the slice category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category with pullbacks along $u$ for a morphism $u: Y \rightarrow X$ and $u^*: \mathcal{C}_{/Y} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{/X}$ be the forgetful functor Then the right kan extension along $u^*$ is pointwise given by

\begin{align*} u_{*}(\mathcal{F})(U \rightarrow X) \simeq& \mathcal{F}(U \times_{X} Y \rightarrow Y) \end{align*}

Proof
