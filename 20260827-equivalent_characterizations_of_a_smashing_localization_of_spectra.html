equivalent characterizations of a smashing localization of spectra

Proposition

Let $E$ be a spectrum, $T = \mathcal{L}_E(\mathbb{S})$ the E-localization of the sphere spectrum. TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{L}_{E}$ preserves filtered colimits
  2. E-local spectra are closed under filtered colimits
  3. \(E\)-local spectra form a tensor ideal in a symmetric monoidal category
  4. there exists a natural equivalence $\mathcal{L}_{E} \simeq T \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} -$
  5. $T$ is an idempotent ring spectrum
  6. the bousfield classes $\langle E\rangle = \langle T\rangle$ coincide

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from some limits in a bousfield localization / colimits in a left bousfield localization argument

2) $\implies$ 3)

3) $\implies$ 4)

4) $\implies$ 5)

5) $\implies$ 6)

6) $\implies$ 1)
