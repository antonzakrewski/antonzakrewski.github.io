pi_1 of a closed surface

Proposition

Let $M$ be a closed surface.

  1. if $M$ is orientable, then
\begin{align*} \pi_1(M) = \mathrm{Free}(a_1,b_1,...,a_g,b_g)/<[a_1,b_1] \cdot .... [a_g,b_g]> \end{align*}

where $[a_i,b_i]$ is the commutator.

  1. if $M$ is not orientable, then
\begin{align*} \pi_1(M) = \mathrm{Free}(a_1,...,a_g)/(\prod_{i=1}^g a_i^2) \end{align*}

Proof
