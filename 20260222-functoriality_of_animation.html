2-functoriality of animation

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be cocomplete categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ be 1-sifted colimit preserving functors. Then there exists a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An}(\mathcal{G}) \circ \mathrm{An}(\mathcal{F}) \Rightarrow \mathrm{An}(\mathcal{G} \circ \mathcal{F}) \end{align*}
