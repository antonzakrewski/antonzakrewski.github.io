Graphs of maps to Hausdorff spaces are closed

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T}_X)$ be a topological space and $(Y,\mathcal{T}_Y)$ be a Hausdorff-Space. Suppose $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a continuous map, then the Graph $\Gamma_f$ is closed in $X \times Y$

Proof

We want to show that $X \times Y \setminus \Gamma_f$ is closed Let $(x,y) \in X \times Y\Gamma_f$, then by assumption $f(x) \neq y$. Choose two disjoint neighbourhoods $f(x) \in U, y\in V$.

Now since $f$ is continuous it follows that $W = f^{-1}(U)$ is also open.

Now consider $W \times V \subseteq X \times Y$ as open set. It does not intersect the graph, since $f(W) \subseteq U$ is disjoint to $V$
