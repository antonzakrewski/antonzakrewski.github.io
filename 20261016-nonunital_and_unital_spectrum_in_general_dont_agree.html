nonunital and unital spectrum in general dont agree

Proposition

Let $A$ be a unital K-Algebra. Then the unital spectrum for a unital algebra & nonunital spectrum for a nonunital algebra in general don't agree.

Proof

Let $1 \in A$, then $0$ lies in the untial spectrum, but $(1,0)$ is not invertible in the unitalization of the (unital) algebra $A$. Hence $0$ does not lie in the nonunital spectrum.
