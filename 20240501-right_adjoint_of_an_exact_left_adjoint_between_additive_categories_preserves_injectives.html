right adjoint of an exact left adjoint between additive categories preserves injective objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be additive categories, $\mathcal{L} \dashv \mathcal{R}$ adjoint functors where $\mathcal{L}$ is left exact functor (resp. exact). Then $\mathcal{R}$ preserves injective objects.

Proof
