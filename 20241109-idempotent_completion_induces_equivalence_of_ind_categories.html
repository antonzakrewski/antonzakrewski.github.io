idempotent completion induces equivalence of ind categories

Proposition

Let $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor which induces an equivalence on idempotent completion and let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(f): \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

is also an equivalence
