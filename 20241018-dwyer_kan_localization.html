dwyer-kan localization

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{C}[S^{-1}]$ be infinity categories, $S \subseteq \mathcal{C}_1$ a subcollection of morphisms. An (S-local) infinity functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}[S^{-1}] \end{align*}

is called a dwyer-kan localization of $\mathcal{C}$ along $S$, if for every infinity category $\mathcal{D}$ the restriction functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}[S^{-1}], \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

is

  1. fully faithful
  2. with essential image of a functor $\mathcal{F}^{S}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ (cf. infinity functor category with groupoid morphisms)
