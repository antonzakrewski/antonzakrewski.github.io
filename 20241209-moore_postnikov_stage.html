Moore-Postnikov stage

Definition

Let $X,Y$ be path connected spaces, $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a continuous map and $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then the \(n\)-th Moore-Postnikov stage of $f$ is defined as a topological space $Z_n$ with maps $a_n: X \rightarrow Z_n$ and $p_n: Z_n \rightarrow Y$ such that

  1. $p_n \alpha_n = f$
  2. $a_n$ is an isomorphism on $\pi_k$ for $k < n$ and surjective on $\pi_n$
  3. $p_n$ is an isomorphism on $\pi_k$ for $k > n$ and injective on $\pi_n$
