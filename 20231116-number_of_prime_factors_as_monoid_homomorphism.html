number of prime factors as monoid homomorphism

Proposition

Let $R$ be a unique factorization domain. Then for $R \setminus \{0\}$, the map

\begin{align*} \varphi: R \rightarrow& \mathbb{N}_0 \\ r = \varepsilon \cdot p_1 \cdot ... \cdot p_n \mapsto& n \end{align*}

where $\varepsilon \in R^{\times}$ is a unit and $p_1,...,p_n$ prime, i.e. a prime decomposition.
