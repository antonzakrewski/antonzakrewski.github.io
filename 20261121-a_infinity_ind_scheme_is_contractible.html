A infinity ind scheme is A^1 contractible

Proposition

The ind scheme $\mathbb{A}^{\infty} \setminus \{0\} = \mathrm{colim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{A}^n \setminus \{0\}$ is $\mathbb{A}^1$ contractible.

Proof

We claim that the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathbb{A}^{n} \setminus \{0\} \hookrightarrow \mathbb{A}^{n+1} \setminus \{0\} \end{align*}

is nullhomotopic.

In fact, consider the standard inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathbb{A}^{n} \setminus \{0\} \times \mathbb{A}^1 \hookrightarrow \mathbb{A}^{n+1} \setminus \{0\} \end{align*}

which is precisely a homotopy from the inclusion (at $t = 0$) to a map, which restricts to $\mathbb{A}^n \times \{0\} \simeq \mathbb{A}^n$. Hence this map is null homotopic.
