precomposition with an equivalence in the functor category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{C}', \mathcal{E}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}'$ an equivalence. Then precomposition on the functor categories

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}^*: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}', \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

is also an equivalence

Proof

choose a weak inverse $\mathcal{F}^{-1}$ with natural isomorphisms $\mathcal{F}^{-1} \mathcal{F} \cong \mathrm{id}, \mathcal{F} \mathcal{F}^{-1} \cong \mathrm{id}$. Then by whiskering

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}^{-1}^* \mathcal{F}^*(-) \cong& \mathrm{id}(-) \\ \mathcal{F}^* \mathcal{F}^{-1}^*(-) \cong& \mathrm{id}(-) \\ \end{align*}
