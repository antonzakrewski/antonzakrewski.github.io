cotorsion module

Definition

Let $R$ be a ring and $M$ a right \(R\)-module. Then $M$ is said to be cotorsion, if $\mathrm{Ext}_R^1(F,M) = 0$ for all flat right \(R\)-modules $F$.

Remark

this is equivalent to the statement that for any flat module $F$, any short exact sequence

is trivial. see: Ext1 and extensions in a stable category
