splitting of cohomology into reduced homology and base

Proposition

Let $X$ be a pointed anima and $E$ any spectrum, then there exists an additive splitting

\begin{align*} E^*[X] \simeq \tilde{E}^*[X] \oplus E^*[*] \end{align*}

note that this is rarely multiplicative

Proof

follows as

  • splitting of a suspension spectrum into reduced and base
  • homotopy group functor from spectra maps preserves arbitrary sums
