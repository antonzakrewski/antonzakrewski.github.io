Image Factorization System

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. An image factorization system for $\mathcal{C}$ is a pair $(\mathcal{E}, \mathcal{M})$ such that:

  • $\mathcal{E}$ is a nonempty class of epimorphisms, closed under composition
  • $\mathcal{M}$ is a nonempty class of monomorphisms, closed under composition
  • Any isomorphism belongs both to $\mathcal{E}$ and $\mathcal{M}$
  • Every morphism $f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,B)$ can be factored as $f = m \circ e$ where $m \in \mathcal{M}, e \in \mathcal{E}$, which is unique up to isomorphism, i.e.
