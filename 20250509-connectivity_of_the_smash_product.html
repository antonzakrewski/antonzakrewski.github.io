connectivity of the smash product of anima

Proposition

Let $X,Y \in \mathrm{An}_*$ be \(n\) resp. \(m\) connected pointed anima. Then the smash product $X \smash Y$ is \(n+m+1\)-connected.

Proof

non conceptual

choose cw structures $\mathrm{sk}_n(X), \mathrm{sk}_n(Y)$ (cf. cw structure of an anima). Then w.l.o.g. we get $\mathrm{sk}_i(X) = *, \mathrm{sk}_j(Y) = *$ for $i \in \{1,...,n\}, j \in \{1,...,m\}$ (cf. n-connected anima equivalent to anima with trivial n-1 skeleton)

It follows that

\begin{align*} X \wedge Y \end{align*}

admits a cw structure with

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sk}_{i} = \bigcup_{l + k = i} \mathrm{sk}_{l}(X) \wedge \mathrm{sk}_{k}(Y) \end{align*}

or

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sk}_{0}(X \wedge Y), ..., \mathrm{sk}_{n+m+1}(X \wedge Y) \simeq * \end{align*}

by cellular approximation for anima / cellular approximation we get

\begin{align*} \pi_{n+m+1}(\mathrm{sk}_{n+m+1}(X \wedge Y)) \twoheadrightarrow \pi_{n+m+1}(X \wedge Y) \end{align*}
