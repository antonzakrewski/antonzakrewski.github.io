inclusion of a finite wedge of n spheres as 2n-1 connected map

Proposition

Let $I$ be a finite set

\begin{align*} \iota: \bigvee_{I} S^n \rightarrow \prod_{I} S^n \end{align*}

be the map obtained by the universal property of the wedge product as coproduct of based spaces and the product as categorical product

Then $\iota$ is a (2n-1)-connected map

Proof

corollary of:

  • cw complex and product with a locally compact cw complex
  • n skeleton inclusion as n-1 connected map

where $\prod I S^n$ is a \(2n\)-dimensional cw complex
