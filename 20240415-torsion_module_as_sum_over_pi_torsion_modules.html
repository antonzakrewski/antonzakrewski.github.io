torsion module as sum over pi torsion modules for a finitely generated module over a PID

Proposition

Let $R$ be a principal ideal domain, $M$ a finitely generated module and $T(M)$ the torsion submodule. Then

\begin{align*} T(M) \cong \bigoplus_{p \in \mathbb{P}} T_p(M) \end{align*}

where $\mathbb{P}$ is the set of irreducibles and $T_p(M)$ the pi-torsion submodule.

Proof
