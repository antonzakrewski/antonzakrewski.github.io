heart is an abelian category

Proposition

The heart is an abelian category

Proof

additive category

note that $0 \in \mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}$. Furthermore for $X,Y \in \mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}$ we get that

\begin{align*} X \oplus Y \in \mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit} \end{align*}

since

  1. $X \times Y \in \mathcal{C}_{\leq 0}$ (cf. lower t-subcategory closed under limits)
  2. $X \coprod Y \in \mathcal{C}_{\geq 0}$ (cf. upper t-subcategory closed under colimits)

kernel / cokernel

We claim that for $f: X \rightarrow Y$ the kernel resp. cokernel is given by

\begin{align*} \tau_{0}(\mathrm{fib}(f)) = \pi_0(\mathrm{fib}) \rightarrow X \xrightarrow{f} Y \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} X \xrightarrow{f} Y \rightarrow \pi_0(\mathrm{cofib}) = \tau_{0}(\mathrm{cofib}(f)) \end{align*}
