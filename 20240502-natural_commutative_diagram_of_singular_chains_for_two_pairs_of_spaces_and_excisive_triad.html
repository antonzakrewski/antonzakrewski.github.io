natural diagram of singular chains for two pairs of spaces and excisive triad

Proposition

Let $(X,A), (Y,B)$ be pairs of spaces, such that $(X \times Y, X \times B, A \times Y)$ is an excisive triad. Then for the singular chain complex and the natural diagram of singular chains for two pairs of spaces 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
0 \arrow[]{r}{} & C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X) \otimes C^{\mathrm{sing}}(B) + C^{\mathrm{sing}}(A) \otimes C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{\mathrm{EZ}_{X,B} + \mathrm{EZ}_{A,Y}} & C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X) \otimes C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{\mathrm{EZ}_{X,Y}} & C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X,A) \otimes C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y,B) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{}& 0 \\
0 \arrow[]{r}{} & C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times B) + C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y \times A) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{\mathrm{id}} & C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times Y) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times Y)/(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times B) \oplus C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y \times A)) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & 0 \\
0 \arrow[]{r}{} & C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times B \cup A \times Y) \arrow[]{r}{} & C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times Y)  \arrow[]{r}{} & C^{\mathrm{sing}((X,A) \times_p (Y,B)) \arrow[]{r}{}& 0
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

the lower vertical maps are chain homotopy equivalences

Proof

left

follows from the definition of an excisive triad

midlde

Here

\begin{align*} \mathrm{id}: C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times Y) \rightarrow C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times Y) \end{align*}

is trivially a chain homotopy equivalence

right

Since both $C^{\mathrm{sing}((X,A) \times_p (Y,B)), C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times Y)/(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times B) \oplus C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y \times A))$ are projective objects, this exact sequence splits (cf. natural diagram of singular chains for two pairs of spaces with exact rows, Splitting Lemma) By applying $H_n$ for arbitrary $n$ we get an actually commutative diagram 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
0 \arrow[]{r}{} & H_n(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times B) + C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y \times A)) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{\cong} & H_n(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times Y)) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{\cong} & H_n(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times Y)/(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times B) \oplus C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y \times A))) \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & 0 \\
0 \arrow[]{r}{} & H_n(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times B \cup A \times Y)) \arrow[]{r}{} & H_n(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times Y))  \arrow[]{r}{} & H_n(C^{\mathrm{sing}((X,A) \times_p (Y,B))) \arrow[]{r}{}& 0
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

using homology functor preserves cokernels for split exact sequences it follows that

\begin{align*} H_n(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times Y)/(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times B) \oplus C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y \times A))) \cong& \mathrm{coker}(H_n(... \rightarrow ...)) \\ H_n(C^{\mathrm{sing}((X,A) \times_p (Y,B))) \cong& \mathrm{coker}(H_n(... \rightarrow ...)) \end{align*}

Hence using the colim functor (plus two out of three for isomorphisms) it follows that 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
 H_n(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times Y)/(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(X \times B) \oplus C^{\mathrm{sing}}(Y \times A))) \arrow[]{d}{} \\
H_n(C^{\mathrm{sing}((X,A) \times_p (Y,B))) 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

is an isomorphism

Therefore the map is a quasi-isomorphism. By quasi isomorphism between chain complexes of projectives and homotopy equivalence this already is a homotopy equivalence
