monoid action as monoid homomorphism

Proposition

Let $M$ be a monoid, $X$ a set and $\varphi: M \times X \rightarrow X$ a map

TFAE:

  1. $\varphi$ induces a monoid-homomorphism
\begin{align*} m \mapsto& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Set}}(X,X) \end{align*}
  1. $\varphi$ is a monoid action

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
