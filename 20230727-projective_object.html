projective object (simplicial colimits)

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $P \in \mathcal{C}$. Then $P$ is said to be projective, if $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(P,-)$ preserves simplicial colimits

Warning

might also be:

  • projective object (epimorphism preserving)
  • two different definitions of projective objects
