cartesian product of subvarietys as subvariety

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n, \mathbb{A}_K^m$ affine spaces and $V \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n, W \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^m$ affine subvarieties Then $V \tiems W \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^{m + n}$ is also an affine subvariety

Proof

Suppose $V = V(\mathfrak{a})$ and $W = V(\mathfrak{b})$ for $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq K[X_1,...,X_n]$ and $\mathfrak{b} \subseteq K[X_{n+1},...,X_{n+m}]$ Then consider the canonical monomorphisms

Let $\mathfrak{a}_n \coloneqq \iota_n[\mathfrak{a}], \mathfrak{b}_m \coloneqq \iota_{m}[\mathfrak{b}]$ Then $V \times W = V( \mathfrak{a}_n + \mathfrak{b}_m)$, since by the intersection of affine subvarieties we get

\begin{align*} V(\mathfrak{a}_n + \mathfrak{b}_m) =& V(\mathfrak{a}_n) \cap V(\mathfrak{b}_m) \\ =& V \times K^m \cap K^n \times W \\ =& V \times W \end{align*}
