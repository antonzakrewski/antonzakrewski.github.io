sheaf hom on a topological space

Definition / proposition

Let $\mathcal{V}$ be a category, $X$ a topological space $\mathrm{Sh}_{\mathcal{V}}(X)$ the category of sheaves on a topological space Then for sheaves $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}$ the sheaf hom is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}(\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}): \mathrm{Op}(X)^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Set} \\ O \mapsto \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sh}(O)}(\mathcal{F}_{\vert O}(-), \mathcal{G}_{\vert O}(-)) \end{align*}

where $\mathcal{F}_{\vert O}(-), \mathcal{G}_{\vert O}(-)$ restriction of a sheaf to an open set
