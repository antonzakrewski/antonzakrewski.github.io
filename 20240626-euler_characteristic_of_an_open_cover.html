euler characteristic of an open cover

Proposition

Let $X$ be a homologically finite topological space and $U_1,U_2$ an open cover Then the homological euler characteristic is given by

\begin{align*} \chi(X) = \chi(U_1) + \chi(U_2) - \chi(U_1 \cap U_2) \end{align*}
