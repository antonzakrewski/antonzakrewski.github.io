quasi coherent sheaf of modules and affine part as associated sheaf

Proposition

Let $X$ be scheme and $\mathcal{F}$ an $\mathcal{O}_X$ sheaf of modules

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is quasicoherent
  2. for eahc open subset $O \subseteq X$ with $X_{O} \cong \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ there exists an \(A\)-module $M$ such that for the associated sheaf of modules functor from Mod and the restriction of a sheaf to an open set
\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_{\vert O} \cong \tilde{M} \end{align*}

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
