arbitrary product of exact functors between stable categories is exact

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}_i$ be stable categories and $\mathcal{F}_i: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}_i$ exact functors. Then the functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \prod \mathcal{D}_i \end{align*}

is also exact.
