degree of an element (graded group)

Definition

Let $G \cong \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z} } C_n$ be a graded abelian group and $g \in C_n \setminus \{0\}$. Then $g$ has degree $n$

Welldefined

Suppose there exists an $g \neq 0$ such that $g = g_n \in C_n, g = g_m \in C_m$ for $n \neq m$. Then

\begin{align*} \varphi(,...0,g_n,...,-g_m,0,...) =& g_n - g_m \\ =& 0 \end{align*}

Therefore $\mathrm{ker}(\varphi) \neq 0$ and $\varphi$ is not injective resp. an isomorphism
