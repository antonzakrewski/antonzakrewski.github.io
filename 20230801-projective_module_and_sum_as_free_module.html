projective module and sum as free module

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $P$ a \(R\)-module. TFAE:

  1. $P$ is a projective module
  2. there exists an module $Q$ such that $P \oplus Q$ is free

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Take a sufficiently large free module $F$, e.g. over $\mathrm{Set}(P)$ as basis. Consider the epimorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: \mathrm{Free}(P) \rightarrow P p \mapsto p \end{align*}

By epimorphism onto a projective splits we get a split exact sequence

and thus also by splitting lemma for modules

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Free}(P) \cong \mathrm{ker}(\varphi) \oplus P \end{align*}
