brown representability theorem for spectra

Proposition

Let $H: \mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab}$ be a cohomological functor, i.e. a functor which

  1. preserves arbitrary products
  2. sends cofiber sequences $X \rightarrow Y \rightarrow Z$ to an exact sequence $H(Z) \rightarrow H(Y) \rightarrow H(X)$.

Then there exists a spectrum $X$ and an equivalence

\begin{align*} \pi_0(\mathrm{map}(-,X)) \cong H(-) \end{align*}

Proof
