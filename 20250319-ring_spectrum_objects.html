category of O-ring spectra

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable closed symmetric monoidal infinity category and $\mathcal{O}$ an infinity operad Then the category of $\mathcal{O}$ ring spectra is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathcal{O}}(\mathrm{Sp}^{\mathcal{O}^{\otimes}}(\mathcal{C})) \end{align*}

where unique extension of a closed symmetric monoidal structure on spectra $\mathrm{Sp}$
