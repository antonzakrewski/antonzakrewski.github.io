Group modulo center isomorphic to inner automorphism group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a group, $Z(G)$ its center. Then the quotient group $G/Z(G)$ is isomorphic to the inner automorphism group $\mathrm{Inn}(G)$

Proof

conjugation as group automorphism

consider the group-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: G \rightarrow& \mathrm{Aut}(G) \\ g \mapsto& (g \cdot x \cdot g^{-1}) \end{align*}

its kernel is $Z$, as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{id}_{G}(x) =& \varphi(g)(x) \\ x = g \cdot x \cdot g^{-1} && \vert \cdot g \\ x \cdot g =& g \cdot x \end{align*}

for all $x \in G$

hence by fundamental group theorem,

\begin{align*} G/Z \cong \mathrm{im}(\varphi) = \mathrm{Inn}(G) \end{align*}
