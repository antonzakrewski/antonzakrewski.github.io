product of holomorphic functions as holomorphic function

Proposition

Let $\Sigma \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be open and $f,g: \Sigma \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ be holomorphic functions. Then the product

\begin{align*} f \cdot g \end{align*}

is also holomorphic with derivative

\begin{align*} (f \cdot g)'(z_0) = f'(z_0) \cdot g(z_0) + f(z_0) g'(z_0) \end{align*}

Proof
