presentable category, Ind and compact generator

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal, $\mathcal{D}$ a locally small infinity category which admits $\kappa$ small coproducts

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{D}$ is presentable
  2. $\mathcal{D}$ is accessible
  3. $\mathcal{D}$ admits kappa filtered colimits and has a set $S$ of kappa compact generator

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 3)

3) $\implies$ 1)

object as colimit of $S$

Let $\overline{S}$ be the smallest full infinity subcategory containing $S$ and \(\kappa\)-small colimits of $S$. Then $\overline{S}$ is small, as we have an epi on objects given by the functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim} \coprod_{\mu < \kappa} \mathrm{Fun}(\kappa, S) \rightarrow \overline{S} \end{align*}

We want to show that $\mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\overline{S}) \cong \mathcal{D}$

There exists a factorization

since $\mathcal{D}$ is cocomplete (cf. universal property of the presheaf category)

Then $\mathcal{L}$ is a left adjoint with right adjoint given by restriction $\mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{P}(D) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\overline{S}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An})$, i.e.

\begin{align*} \mathcal{D} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Fun}(\overline{S}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An}) \\ d \mapsto \mathrm{map}(-,d) \end{align*}

Here $d \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\overline{S}}(-,d)$ preserves limits (cf. mapping space functor preserves limits), hence restricts

(cf. ind object and preserving filtered limits)

This provides an adjunction

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}: \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\overline{S}) \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

Then as $\overline{S}$ consists only of \(\kappa\)-compact objects (cf. kappa small colimit of kappa compact objects), we may conclude that

\begin{align*} c \mapsto \mathrm{map}_{\overline{S}}(-,c) \end{align*}

preserves \(\kappa\)-filtered colimits.
