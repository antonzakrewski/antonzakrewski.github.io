forgetful functor chain complexes to graded objects

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes. Then the forgetful functor from $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ to the category of Z-graded objects is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{G}-\mathcal{A} \\ \end{align*}
