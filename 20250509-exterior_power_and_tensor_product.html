exterior power and tensor product

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a commutative ring and $P,Q$ finite projective modules of rank $n$ (?). Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{det}( P \oplus Q) \cong& \mathrm{det}(P) \otimes_{R} \mathrm{det}(Q) \end{align*}
