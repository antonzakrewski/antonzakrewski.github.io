gabriel-ulmer duality

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Rex}_{\kappa}$ be the category which has

  1. kappa right exact functors
  2. admits kappa small colimits
  3. is idempotent complete (necessary in the case $\kappa = \omega$)

Then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Rex}_{\kappa} \xrightarrow{\simeq} \mathrm{Pr}_{\kappa}^L \end{align*}

Proof
