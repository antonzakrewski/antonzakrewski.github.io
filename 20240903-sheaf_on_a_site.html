set valued sheaf on a site

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a site and $\mathcal{X}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ a functor Then $\mathcal{X}$ si said ot be a set valued sheaf on the site, if for any family of elements $x_i \in X(U_i)$ satisfying

commutes and

\begin{align*} X(g)(x_i) = X(h)(x_j) \end{align*}

Then there exists a unique $x \in \mathcal{X}(U)$ such that

\begin{align*} X(f_i)(x) = x_i \end{align*}

for all $i$
