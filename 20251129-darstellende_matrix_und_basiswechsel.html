Darstellende Matrix und Basiswechsel

Algorithm

Let $\varphi: V \rightarrow W$ be a vectorspace-homomorphism between vector space. Suppose $v_1,...,v_n$ is an ordered basis of $V$, $w_1,...,w_m$ an ordered basis of $W$. Suppose $A_{v_i,w_j}$ is a representing matrix of $\varphi$ with respect to $v_i$ and $w_j$

Then the representing matrix of $\varphi$ with respect to $\overline{v_i}$ and $\overline{w_j}$ is given by

\begin{align*} A_{\overline{v_i}, \overline{w_j}} = T_{w_j \leadsto \overline{w_j}} \cdot A_{v_i,w_j} \cdot T_{\overline{v_i} \leadsto \overline{v_i}} \end{align*}

where

  1. $T_{\overline{v_i} \leadsto v_i}$ is the basis change from $\overline{v_i}$ to $v_i$
  2. $T_{w_i \leadsto \overline{w_i}}$ is the basis change from $w_j$ to $\overline{w_j}$
