join bifunctor of simplicial sets

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{sSet}$ be the category sSet, then the join is a bifunctor

\begin{align*} -\star -: \mathrm{sSet} \times \mathrm{sSet} \rightarrow \mathrm{sSet} (A,B) \mapsto& A \star B (f,g) \mapsto& (f \star B) \circ (A \star g) \end{align*}

Proof

It remains to show that

\begin{align*} (\mathrm{id} \star f) \circ (g \star \mathrm{id} =& (g \star \mathrm{id} \circ (\mathrm{id} \star f) \end{align*}

Then this follows from the definition of the left induced morphism between joins and right induced morphism between joins: As the morphism is induced by the universal property of the coproduct, it suffices to show that this is a bifunctor on the level.
