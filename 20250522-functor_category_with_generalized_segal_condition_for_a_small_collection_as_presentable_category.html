functor category with generalized segal condition for a small collection as presentable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category, $\mathcal{D}$ any small category. Suppose $I_j: \mathcal{I}_j \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ are diagrams, $\underline{a}_j: \mathrm{const}_{a} \rightarrow I$ are natural transformations for some small $j \in J$. Let $\mathrm{Fun}^{I, \underline{a},J}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ denote the full sub infinity category containing functors $M$ such that

\begin{align*} M(\underline{a}): M(a_j) \rightarrow \mathrm{lim}(M(I_j)) \end{align*}

is an equivalence for all $j$

Then $\mathrm{Fun}^{I_j, \underline{a}_j}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ is presentable
