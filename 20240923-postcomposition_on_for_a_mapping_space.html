postcomposition on a mapping space

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $x,y,z \in \mathcal{C}_0$ objects and $f: y \rightarrow z$ a \(1\)-simplex. Then postcomposition is defined as morphism

\begin{align*} f^{*}: \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,z) \end{align*}
