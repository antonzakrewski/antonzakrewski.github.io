homotopy group functor with coefficients is a group/abelian group

Proposition

Let $G$ be an abelian group Then the homotopy group functor with coefficients in a group lifts as

  1. $\pi_n(-,G): \mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathrm{Grp}$ for $n \geq 2$
  2. $\pi_n(-,G): \mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab}$ for $n \geq 3$

Proof

recall that

  • Eckman-Hilton duality for suspension / loop space

$n \geq 2$

  • $\Sigma M(G,1) \simeq M(G,2)$

$n \geq 3$

  • $\Sigma^2 M(G,1) \simeq M(G,3)$
