Stetigkeit der Skalarmultiplikation

Proposition

Let $X$ be a normed vector space over $\mathbb{K}$. Then scalar multiplication

\begin{align*} \cdot \colon \mathbb{K} \times X \rightarrow& X \\ (\lambda,x) \mapsto \lambda \cdot x \end{align*}

is a continuous map

Proof

we show that it is sequentially continuous. So let $x_n \rightarrow x, \lambda_n \rightarrow \lambda$ be convergent sequences. Then $\| x_n\|$ is bounded by some $k$. Then we get

\begin{align*} \| \lambda \cdot x - \lambda_n x_n\| =& \| \lambda \cdot x - \lambda \cdot x_n + \lambda \cdot x_n - \lambda_n \cdot x_n \| \leq& \| \lambda (x - x_n) \| + \| (\lambda - \lambda_n) x_n \| \\ \leq& \vert \lambda \vert \| x - x_n \| + \vert \lambda - \lambda_n \vert \cdot \| x_n \| \\ \leq& \vert \lambda \vert \| x - x_n \| + \vert \lambda - \lambda_n \vert \cdot \| x_n \| \\ \xrightarrow{n \rightarrow \infty}& \lambda \cdot 0 + 0 \cdot k \\ =& 0 \end{align*}

where we have used Stetigkeit der Norm
