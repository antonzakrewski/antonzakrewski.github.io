singular homology as module over the singular cohomology via the cap product

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $R$ a ring. Then the singular homology group is a graded module over the graded singular cohomology ring using the cap product as scalar multiplication $H^*(X;R) \times H_*(X;R) \rightarrow H_*(X;R)$
