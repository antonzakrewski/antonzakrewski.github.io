ext 1 of two finitely generated abelian groups

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{Z}/k, \mathbb{Z}/l \mathbb{Z}$ be two cyclic groups Then Ext is given

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathbb{Z}}^1(\mathbb{Z}/k \mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Z}/ l \mathbb{Z}) \cong \begin{cases} 0 & \mbox{if } k = 0 \\ \mathbb{Z}/\mathrm{gcd}(k,l) & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

$k = 0$

follows as $\mathbb{Z}$ is free and hence projective (cf. free module as projective module)

  • covariant ext for a fixed projective module

else

follows from covariant ext 1 for an integral domain and quotient for a principal ideal as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathbb{Z}}^{1}(\mathbb{Z}/k \mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Z}/l \mathbb{Z}) \cong& (\mathbb{Z}/l \mathbb{Z})/(l \cdot \mathbb{Z}/k) \\ \cong& \mathbb{Z}/(\mathrm{gcd}(k,l)) \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}
