axiom of choice implies dependent choice

Proposition

The Axiom of choice implies the Axiom of dependent choice

Proof

Given a set $A$, an element $x_0 \in A$ and a totale Relation $R$. For each finite list $(x_0,...,x_n)$ with $(x_i,x_{i+1}) \in R$ define

\begin{align*} M_{(x_0,...,x_n)} = \{(x_0,...,x_n,y) \vert y \in R, (x_n,y) \in R\} \end{align*}

Then $M_{(x_0,...,x_n)}$ is inhabited set, as $R$ is a total relation. Then use the Axiom of choice to get a choice function

\begin{align*} \{M_{(x_0,...,x_n)}\} \rightarrow \coprod M_{(x_0,...,x_n)} \end{align*}

Now define a function

\begin{align*} f: \mathbb{N}_0 \rightarrow& A \\ 0 \mapsto& x_0 \\ n+1 \mapsto& \{\mathrm{last element of the chosen list } (x_0...,x_n,y)} \end{align*}
