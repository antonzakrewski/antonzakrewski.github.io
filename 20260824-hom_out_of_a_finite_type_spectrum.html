Hom out of a finite type spectrum preserves filtered colimits of n coconnective spectra

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X,-): \mathrm{Sp} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp} \end{align*}
