Lazard's Theorem for ring spectra

Proposition

Let $R$ be a connective E_1-ring spectrum and $N$ a connective module spectrum over $R$. TFAE:

  1. $N$ is a filtered colimit of finitely generated, free module spectra
  2. $N$ is a filtered colimit of projective module spectra
  3. $N$ is a flat module spectrum
  4. the functor $- \otimes_{R} N$ is a t-exact functor
  5. if $M$ is a discrete module spectrum, then $M \otimes_{R} N$ is also discrete

Proof

HA 7.2.2.15

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 3)

3) $\implies$ 4)

4) $\implies$ 5)

5) $\implies$ 1)
