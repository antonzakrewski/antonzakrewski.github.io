Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable category, $W \subseteq $ Suppose $I$ is a small set of morphisms $C$ Suppose

  3. the weak saturation $\tilde{\mathrm{sat}(I)} \cap W$ is closed under pushouts and sequential colimits

Then $\mathcal{C}$ is a combinatorial model category with

  1. weak equivalences $W$
  2. cofibrations $\tilde{\mathrm{sat}(I)}
