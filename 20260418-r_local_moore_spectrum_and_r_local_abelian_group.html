R-local moore spectrum and R-local abelian group

Proposition

Let $R = \mathbb{Z}[p^{-1} \vert p \in P] \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ be a subring, $A$ be an abelian group and $\mathbb{S}_{A}$ the moore spectrum. TFAE:

  1. $\mathbb{S}_{A}$ is an R-local spectrum
  2. $p$ acts invertibly on $A$ as abelian group

Proof

follows from:

  • stable hurewicz theorem
  • homology is an additive functor
