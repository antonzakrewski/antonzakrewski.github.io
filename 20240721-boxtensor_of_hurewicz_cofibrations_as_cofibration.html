boxtensor of hurewicz cofibrations as cofibration

Proposition

Let $\iota: A \rightarrow B, f: X \rightarrow Y$ be cofibrations, where $\iota A \rightarrow B$ is a closed cofibration Then so is the boxtensor

\begin{align*} \iota \boxtimes f: B \times X \coprod_{} A \times Y \rightarrow B \times Y \end{align*}
