lambda compact object functor from PrL kappa stable preserves bifiber sequences

Proposition

Let $\kappa < \lambda$ be regular cardinals, then the functor

\begin{align*} ()^{\lambda} \colon \mathrm{Pr^L}_{\kappa, \mathrm{st}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{perf}_{\lambda- \mathrm{ex}} \end{align*}

preserves bifiber sequences.

Proof

Observe that this functor is the composition 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{Pr^L}_{\kappa, \mathrm{st}} \rar & \mathrm{Pr^L}_{\lambda, \mathrm{st}} \rar{\simeq} & \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{perf}_{\lambda-\mathrm{ex}}
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

where the first functor preserves finite limits and colimits (cf. inclusion of PrL kappa into PrL preserves kappa small limits + forgetful functor from PrL kappa to PrL preserves colimits) and the second functor is an equivalence of categories.
