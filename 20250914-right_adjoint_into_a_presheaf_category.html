right adjoint into a presheaf category

Proposition

let $\mathcal{C}$ be a cocomplete category and $\mathcal{D}$ a small category, then there exists a canonical equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{R}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C})^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • opposite FunR equivalent to FunL
  • left adjoints out of a presheaf category
