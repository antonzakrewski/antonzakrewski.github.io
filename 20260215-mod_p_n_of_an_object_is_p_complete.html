Proposition

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a stable category and $X$ an object. Then $X/p^n = \mathrm{cofib}(X \xrightarrow{\cdot p^n} X)$ is a p-complete object.

Proof

Recall that it suffices to show that $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], X/p^n) \simeq 0$. Moreover, $X/p^n$ is the cofiber of $X \xrightarrow{\cdot p^n} X$ and $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], -)$ is an exact functor, so $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], X/p)$ is the cofiber of $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], X) \xrightarrow{(\cdot p^n)_*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], X)$ But postcomposition with $\cdot p^n$ identifies with precomposition with $\cdot p^n$ as $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(-,-)$ is additive in both variables. Hence it is an equivalence, which shows that the cofiber - namely $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], X/p^n)$ - vanishes
