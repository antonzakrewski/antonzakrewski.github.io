pi_0 induces adjunction on anima valued monoids and set valued monoids

Proposition

The functor pi_0 of anima induces an adjunction $\mathrm{Mon}(\mathrm{An}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Mon}(\mathrm{Set})$ see: category of cartesian monoids

Proof

follows from:

  • symmetric monoidal left adjoint induces adjunction on Algebras
  • pi_0 of Anima preserves products
