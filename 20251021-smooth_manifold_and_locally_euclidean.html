smooth manifold and locally euclidean

Proposition

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold (candidate). Then it is equivalent to either assume that it is

  1. locally of the form $\mathbb{R}^n$
  2. locally of the form $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from setting $U = \mathbb{R}^n$

2) $\implies$ 1)

Now assume that for each point $x \in M$ and assume that $x \in V_m \simeq U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ is a neighbourhood which is locally homeomorphic to an open of $\mathbb{R}^n$. Then we may use the Maximumsnorm and find a neighbourhoood $[- \varepsilon, \varepsilon]^n \subseteq U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ (w.l.o.g. assuming that $x \mapsto 0 \in U$ after translation). Now use epsilon ball homeomorphic to the euclidean space to get a diffeomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathbb{R}^n \simeq [- \varepsilon, \varepsilon]^n \subseteq U \subseteq M \end{align*}

We want to show that this forms a smooth atlas. So let $\varphi_1: \mathbb{R}^n \hookrightarrow M, \varphi_2: \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow M$ be two different charts which are constructed as above using $\mathrm{im}(\varphi_i)\subseteq U_i \xrightarrow{f_i} M$. Let $W \coloneqq \mathrm{im}(\varphi_1) \cap \mathrm{im}(\varphi_2)$, then we can factor

Here we use that $\varphi^{-1}_1[W] \rightarrow f^{-1}[U_1 \cap U_2]$ is by construction of a diffeo onto its image so after restricting $f^{-1}[U_1 \cap U_2]$ to the image we find that we may factor the transition map

\begin{align*} \varphi_1^{-1}[W] \rightarrow \mathrm{im}(f^{-1}[U_1 \cap U_2]) \rightarrow \mathrm{im}(U_1 \cap U_2) \rightarrow f^{-1}[U_1 \cap U_2] \rightarrow \varphi_2^{-1}[W] \end{align*}

where the first, last and middle composite is smooth
