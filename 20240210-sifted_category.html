1-sifted category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a category. Then $\mathcal{D}$ is said to be sifted, if every colimit of $\mathcal{D}$ shape commutes with any finite product $\mathrm{J}$ in category set

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{D} \mathrm{lim}_J \cong \mathrm{lim}_J \mathrm{colim}_D \end{align*}
