fiberwise criterion for Zariski local epimorphisms

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a map of algebraic functors TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a Zariski-local epimorphism
  2. for every $\mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow Y$, the pullback $\mathrm{Spec}(R) \times_{Y} X \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(R)$ is a zariski local epimorphism.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

zariski-local epimorphisms are closed under pullbacks

2) $\implies$ 1)

use lifting against the identity.
