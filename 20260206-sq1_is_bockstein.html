Sq1 is bockstein modulo 2

Proposition

The Bockstein operator modulo $2$ for $\mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\cdot 2} \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}$ and coefficient change

agrees with the steenrod square $\mathrm{Sq}^1$

Proof

using classification of cohomology operations we get 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
[-,K(\mathbb{F}_2,1)] \rar & [-,K(\mathbb{F}_2,2)] \\
H^1(-,\mathbb{F}_2) \rar \uar{\cong} & H^2(-,\mathbb{F}_2) \uar{\cong}\\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

so it suffices to compute $H^2(K(\mathbb{F}_2,1),\mathbb{F}_2) = \mathbb{F}_2$.

Therefore it suffices to show that both cohomology operations are nontrivial. So we may take $\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}$

  1. here we use $\mathrm{Sq}^1()$ - steenrod operation in RP infinity
  2. tangent bundle of a quotient of lie groups
