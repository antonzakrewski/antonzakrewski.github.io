dual object functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a symmetric monoidal category, then the dual object functor is defined as

\begin{align*} (-)^{\vee}: \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{dbl}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{dbl}}^{\mathrm{op} \\ \end{align*}

where full subcategory of dualizable objects $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{dbl}}$.
