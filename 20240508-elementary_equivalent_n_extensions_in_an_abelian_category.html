elementary equivalent n extensions in an abelian category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, $N,M \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{A})$ objects and

two n extensions

Then $E,E'$ are said to be elementary equivalent, if there exists a natural transformation

\begin{align*} E \rightarrow E' \end{align*}

or $E' \rightarrow E$
