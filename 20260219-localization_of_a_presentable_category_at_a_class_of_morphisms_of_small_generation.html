localization of a presentable category at a class of morphisms of small generation

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable category and $S$ a class of morphisms of small generation Then there localization $S^{-1} \mathcal{C}$ is

  1. a left Bousfield localization
  2. a presentable category
