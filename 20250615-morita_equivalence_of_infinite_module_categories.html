morita theory of functor categories of module spectra

Proposition

Let $X$ be an anima, $R \in \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Sp})$ a commutative ring spectrum. Then the functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{Mod}(R)) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}}(\mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{Mod}(R)), \mathrm{Mod}(R)) \\ \mathcal{F} \mapsto& (\mathcal{G} \mapsto \mathrm{colim}(\mathcal{G} \otimes \mathcal{F})) \end{align*}

is an equivalence

Proof

welldefined

follows as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_I((\mathrm{colim}_X \mathcal{G}) \otimes \mathcal{F}) \simeq \mathrm{colim}_I(\mathrm{colim}_X (\mathcal{G} \otimes \mathcal{F})) \\ \simeq \mathrm{colim}_X(\mathrm{colim}_I (\mathcal{G} \otimes \mathcal{F})) \\ \end{align*}

where

  1. infinity tensor product of modules preserves colimits in boht variables
  2. infinity colimits commute with infinity colimits
