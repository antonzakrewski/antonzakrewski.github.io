infinity limits in Mon

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category, $\mathrm{Mon}_{\mathrm{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{C})$ the category of cartesian commutative monoids. Then limits are computed pointwise.

Proof

follows from:

  • monoid category functor as smashing localization, hence the inclusion
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathrm{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(N(\mathrm{Fin}_*), \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is a right adjoint.

hence apply right adjoint preserves limits

since

  1. infinity functor category into a presentable category as presentable category
  2. category of cartesian commutative monoids as presentable category
