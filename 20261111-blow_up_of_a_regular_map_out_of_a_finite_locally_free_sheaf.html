Proposition

Let $u: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{O}_{X}$ be a regular map where $\mathcal{E}$ is finitely locally free. Let $Z$ be the <vanishing locus> of $Z = v(u) \hookrightarrow X$.

Then $\mathrm{BL}_{z}(X)$ classifies factorizations of $u$ through a quotient line bundle, i.e.

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}(\mathrm{Spec}(R), \mathrm{Bl}_z(X)) \simeq& \mathrm{BL}_z(X)(R) \\ \simeq& \{\varphi: \mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow X \text{ and} factorization \} \end{align*}
