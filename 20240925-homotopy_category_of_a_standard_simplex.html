homotopy category of a standard simplex

Proposition

The homotopy category of the represented simplicial set is given by

\begin{align*} h(\Delta^n) = [n] \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of:

  • homotopy category of a nerve canonically isomorphic,
\begin{align*} [n] \cong N(h(\Delta^n)) \end{align*}
