sInd of an infinity category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category Then the category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sInd}(\mathcal{C}) \coloneqq \mathcal{P}_{\emptyset}^{\mathrm{sifted sSets}} \end{align*}

is defined as simplified generalized presheaf construction, i.e. freely adjoining infinity sifted colimits to $\mathcal{C}$
