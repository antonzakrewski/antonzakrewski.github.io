filtered category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. Then $\mathcal{C}$ is said to be filtered, if for any two objects $j_1,j_2$

  1. and parallel morphisms $f,g: j_1 \rightarrow j_2$ there exists a cofork # an object $k' \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and a morphism $h: j_2 rightarrow k$ such that $h \circ f = h \circ g$
  2. for any two objects $j',j'$ there exists an object $k$ and morphisms $j \rightarrow k, j' \rightarrow k$
  3. and $\mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ is nonempty
