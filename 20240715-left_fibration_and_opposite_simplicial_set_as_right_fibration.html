left fibration and opposite map as right fibration

Proposition

Let $\iota: A \rightarrow B$ be a morphism of simplicial sets

TFAE:

  1. $\iota$ is a left fibration
  2. the opposite morphism $\iota^{\mathrm{op}}$ is a right fibration

Proof

  • lifting problem and isomorphism
  • opposite of a left horn as right horn
