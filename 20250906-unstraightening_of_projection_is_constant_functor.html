unstraightening of projection is constant functor

Proposition

The (cocartesian) unstraightening of the projection $p: \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is given by the constant functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{const}_{\mathcal{D}}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}\\ c \mapsto& \mathcal{D} (f: c \rightarrow c') \mapsto& \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{D}} \end{align*}

Proof
