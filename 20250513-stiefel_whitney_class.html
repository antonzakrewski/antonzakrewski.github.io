stiefel whitney class

Definition

Let $\pi: E \rightarrow B$ be a spherical fibration. Then the \(n\)-th stiefel-whitney class is defined as unique class $w_n(\pi) \in H^n(B;\mathbb{F}_2)$ satisfying

\begin{align*} w_n(\pi) \cdot u = \mathrm{Sq}^n(u) \in H^{n+d}(B; \mathbb{F}_2) \end{align*}
