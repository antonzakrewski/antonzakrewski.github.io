n-th cohomotopy of a closed connected n-manifold

Proposition

Let $M$ be a closed connected n-dimensional manifold. Let $\iota: S^n \rightarrow K(\mathbb{Z},n)$ be an inclusion of an $n+1$-skeleton ($S^n$) into the Eilenberg-MacLane space.

Then the map

\begin{align*} [M,S^n] \rightarrow& [M,K(\mathbb{Z},n)] \cong H^n(M, \mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

is a Bijection and the map $M \rightarrow M/(M \setminus D^n)$ is a generator for an embedding $D^n \rightarrow M$.

Here

\begin{align*} H^n(M, \mathbb{Z}) \cong \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } M \textrm{ orientable} \\ \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

(cf. top cohomology of a closed manifold).
