basification of a product as smash product

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be topological spaces and $()_+$ the basification Functor from Top to based spaces. Then there exists a canonical homeomorphism

\begin{align*} (X \times Y)_{+} \rightarrow X_+ \wedge Y_+ \end{align*}

Proof

Consider

\begin{align*} \varphi: (X \times Y)_+ \rightarrow& X_+ \wedge Y_+ \\ (x,y) \mapsto& (x,y) \\ (*) \mapsto& (*,*) \varphi^{-1}: X_+ \wedge Y_+ \rightarrow& (X \times Y)_+ \\ (x,y) \mapsto& \begin{cases} (x,y) & \mbox{if } x \neq *, y \neq * \\ (*) & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
