boxtensor as inner anodyne and component as inner-anodyne

Proposition

Let $A,B,S,T$ be simplicial sets, $\iota: A \rightarrow B, g: S \rightarrow T$ be morphism of simplicial sets, where $g, \iota$ are monomorphisms Suppose $\iota$ or $g$ is (left/ right / inner) anodyne map

Then so is

\begin{align*} \iota \boxtimes g \end{align*}
