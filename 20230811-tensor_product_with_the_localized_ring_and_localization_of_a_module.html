tensor product with the localized ring and localization of a module

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $S \subseteq R$ multiplicatively closeda and $M$ an \(R\)-module Then the map

\begin{align*} f: S^{-1}R \otimes_R M \rightarrow& S^{-1}M \\ \frac{a}{s} \otimes m \mapsto& \frac{am}{s} \end{align*}

is a natural with respect to module-homomorphismdand an isomorphism of \(S^{-1}R\)-modules (see: elementary tensor)

Proof

welldefined

Let

\begin{align*} \varphi: S^{-1}R \times M \rightarrow& S^{-1}M \\ \left(\frac{a}{s}, m\right) \mapsto& \frac{am}{s} \end{align*}

Then by definition of the tensor product, there exists a factorization

surjective

for $\frac{m}{s} \in S^{-1}M$, we conclude that $f(\frac{1}{s} \otimes_R m) = \frac{m}{s}$, hence $f$ is surjective

injective

elementary tensors generate tensor product Let $I$ be a finite set and

\begin{align*} \sum_{i \in I} \frac{a_i}{s_i} \otimes m_i \in S^{-1}R \otimes_R M \end{align*}

Suppose $s \coloneqq \prod_{i \in I} s_i \in S$ and $t_i = \prod_{j \neq i} s_j$. Then

\begin{align*} \sum_{i \in I} \frac{a_i}{s_i} \otimes m_i =& \sum_{i \in I} \frac{a_i t_i}{s_i} \otimes m_i \\ =& \sum_{i \in I} \frac{1}{s} \otimes a_i t_i m_i \end{align*}

By distributivity of the tensor product, we conclude

\begin{align*} \sum_{i \in I} \frac{1}{s} \otimes a_i t_i m_i =& \frac{1}{s} \otimes \left(\sum_{i \in I} a_i t_i m_i \right) \end{align*}

Hence every element in $S^{-1}R \otimes M$ is of the form $\frac{1}{s} \otimes m$

Assume

\begin{align*} 0 =& f(\frac{1}{s} \otimes m) \\ =& \frac{m}{s} \end{align*}

or $s' \cdot m = 0$ for some $s' \in S$ and thus

\begin{align*} \frac{1}{s} \otimes m =& \frac{s'}{s \cdot s'} \otimes m \\ =& \frac{1}{s \cdot s'} \otimes s' m \\ =& \frac{1}{s \cdot s'} \otimes 0 \\ =& 0 \end{align*}
