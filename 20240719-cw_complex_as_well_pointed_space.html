cw complex as well pointed space

Proposition

Let $(X, \emptyset, \mathrm{SK}_n(X))$ be a CW-Complex Then $X$ is well pointed space for an arbitrary point $x \in X$

Proof

  • composition of cofibrations

Note that $x \in X$ is the interior of a \(d\)-cell. Then it suffices to show that

\begin{align*} x \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Sk}_d(X) \hookrightarrow X \end{align*}

are cofibrations.
