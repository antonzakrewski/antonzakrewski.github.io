unit in the center plus nilpotent element as unit

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $\alpha \in R^{\times} \cap C(R)$ a unit and in the center and $n \in R$ a nilpotent element, then

\begin{align*} \alpha + n \end{align*}

is a unit

Proof

follows from

\begin{align*} (\alpha + n) ( \alpha - n) ... \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} (\alpha + n)(\alpha - n) =& \alpha + n \cdot \alpha - \alpha \cdot n - n^2 \\ =& \alpha + \alpha \cdot n - \alpha \cdot n - n^2 \\ =& \alpha - n^2 \end{align*}
