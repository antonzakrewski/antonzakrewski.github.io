first and second homotopy group of the S^2 wedge S^1

Proposition

Let $S^2 \vee S^1$ be the pushout

Then its homotopy groups are given by

\begin{align*} \pi_1(S^1 \vee S^2) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } \\ \bigoplus_{i\in \mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z} \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

a) Seifert-Van Kampen Theorem for groups

and use

  1. square with parallel equivalences as infinity pushout
  2. $\pi_1(S^1) =& \mathbb{Z}$
  3. $\pi_1(S^2) =& \mathbb{Z}$

b)

now consider the universal cover of $S^1 \vee S^2$. Then it is given (up to homotopy) by $\bigvee_{\mathbb{Z}} S^2$ or $ Now using Hurewicz this shows that

\begin{align*} \pi_2(\bigvee S^2) \cong& H_2(\bigvee_{\mathbb{Z}} S^2) \\ \cong& \bigoplus_{\mathbb{Z}} H_2(S^2) \\ \cong \bigoplus_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}
