mackey decomposition of representations

Proposition

Let $H, K \subseteq G$ be subgroups with finite index. Then $\mathrm{Res}_{K}^{G} \mathrm{Ind}_{H}^{G} W \cong \bigoplus_{ K \ G / H} \mathrm{Ind}_{K_{x}}^{K} \mathrm{Res}_{K_x}^{x H x^{-1}} \mathrm{conj}_{x}W}$ where

  1. $K \ G / H$ are the double cosets $KxH$, that is the set of equivalence classs for the equivalence relation generated by $g \simeq k g h$ for all $k \in K,h \in H$ and $g \in G$
  2. $K_x = K \cap x H x^{-1}$
  3. $W_x$ is the $K_x$ representation given by $k v = (x^{-1} k x) \cdot v)$

Proof

Recall that $\mathrm{Ind}_{H}^G W = k[G] \otimes_{k[H]} W$ so the restriction to $K$ is given by $k[G] \otimes_{k[H]} W$ where we act on the left via $k[K] \subseteq k[G]$. Here we use that $k[G]$ is a $(k[K], k[H])$ module and it suffices to give a decomposition of $k[G]$ into as

\begin{align*} k[G] \overset{!}{=} \bigoplus_{x \in K \ G / H} k[K xH] \end{align*}

as $(k[K], k[H])$ bimodules. We have a \(k\)-linear isomorphism, which is compatible with the $(k[K], k[H])$ module structures.

So

\begin{align*} k[G] \otimes_{k[H]} V = \bigoplus_{x \in K \ G / H} k[K x H] \otimes_{k[H]} V \end{align*}

Therefore it remains to identify the RHS with $\bigoplus_{ K \ G / H} \mathrm{Ind}_{K_{x}}^{K} W_{x}$

\begin{align*} k[K] \otimes_{k[K_x]} V \rightarrow& k[KxH] \otimes_{k[H]} V \\ k \otimes v \mapsto kx \otimes v \end{align*}

which is welldefined and an isomorphism.

well-defined

balanced

we have for $u \in K_x$ that $x^{-1} u x \in K$

\begin{align*} k u \otimes v \mapsto kux \otimes v &= kx (x^{-1} u x) \otimes v \\ &= kx \otimes (x^{-1} u x) \cdot v \\ &= kx \otimes u \cdot_{x} v \end{align*}

where $u$ acts with respect to $\cdot_{x}$ excatly by $x^{-1} u x$.

$K$ equivariant

We have

\begin{align*} k'(k \otimes v) &= k' k \otimes v \\ k'(k x \otimes v) &= k'k x \otimes v \end{align*}

isomorphism

Note that picking representatives $u_i$ of $K/K_x$ and a basis $\{v_j\}$ of $V$, the $\{u_i \otimes v_j\}$ are a basis of $k[K] \otimes_{k[K_x]} v$ Thus

\begin{align*} \{u_i \otimes v_j\} \mapsto \{u_i x \otimes v_j\} \end{align*}

which is a basis of $k[K \times H] \otimes_{k[H]} V$ since $K/K_x \xrightarrow{\cdot x} KxH/H$ is an isomorphism.

Here

\begin{align*} K/K_x \xrightarrow{\simeq} KxH/H \end{align*}

is an isomorphism: It is well-defined: $k'x = k xhx^{-1} x = kxh$ where $kxh \sim kx$

it is surjective, since any equivalence class $[kxh] = [kx]$ has $k$ as a preimage. Let $k,k'$ such that $kx \sim k'x$ i.e. $kx = k'xh$ for some $h \in H$. here $kx = k'xh = k' (x hx^{-1})x$ so $k = k'(xhx^{-1})$ where $xhx^{-1} \in K_x$, hence $x$ is injective.
