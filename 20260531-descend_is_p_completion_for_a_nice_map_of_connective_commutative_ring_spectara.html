descend is p-completion for a nice map of connective commutative ring spectara

Proposition

Let $\varphi: R \rightarrow S$ be a map of $\mathbb{E}_{\infty}$-ring spectra, assume that $\pi_0(R)$ is bounded p-torsion, $\varphi$ is a surjection on $\pi_0(-)$ and $\pi_0(\varphi)/p: \pi_0(S)/p \rightarrow \pi_0(S)/p$ is an isomorphism Then for any connective \(S\)-module spectrum $M$, $\lim_{\Delta} (R^{\otimes_{S} \bullet +1} \otimes_{S} M)$ is p-completion and the canonical map

\begin{align*} M_p^{\wedge} \rightarrow \lim_{\Delta} (R^{\otimes_{S} \bullet +1} \otimes_{S} M) \end{align*}

is an equivalence

Proof

a) RHS is p-complete

follows as

  • R-module spectrum over a ring with pi_0 p-torsion is p-complete
  • p-complete spectra are closed under limits

b) equivalent

recall that a map of p-complete spectrum is an equivalence if and only if it is a mod-p equivalence. Now $(M_{p}^{\wedge})/p$ is equivalent to $M/p$ (cf. p-completion mod p) and $\lim_{\Delta} (R^{\otimes_{S} \bullet +1} \otimes_{S} -)$ is an exact functor, so we may identify

\begin{align*} \lim_{\Delta} (R^{\otimes_{S} \bullet +1} \otimes_{S} M)/p \simeq \lim_{\Delta} (R^{\otimes_{S} \bullet +1} \otimes_{S} M/p) \end{align*}

Hence we are left to show that the canonical map

\begin{align*} M/p \rightarrow \lim_{\Delta} (R^{\otimes_{S} \bullet +1} \otimes_{S} M/p) \end{align*}

is an equivalence - resp. that $M/p$ descends along $\varphi: R \rightarrow S$.

Fortunately, we have copied the assumptions on $\varphi: R \rightarrow S$ from the criterion: descend along a map between connective ring spectra of a bounded below spectrum and X/p has homotopy groups given by Z/p^2
