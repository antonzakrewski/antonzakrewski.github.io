free commutative algebra functor

Definition / Proposition

let $R$ be a commutative ring. The free commutative algebra functor $\mathrm{freeCAlg}_R$ is defined

\begin{align*} \mathrm{freeAlg}_R: \mathrm{Set} \rightarrow& \mathrm{CAlg}_R\\ X \mapsto& \mathrm{FreeAlg}_R(X) \\ (f: X \rightarrow Y) \mapsto& (f': \mathrm{FreeAlg}_R(X) \rightarrow \mathrm{FreeAlg}_R(Y)) \end{align*}

where $f'$ is the algebra-homomorphism induced by

\begin{align*} f'(x) = f(x) \end{align*}
