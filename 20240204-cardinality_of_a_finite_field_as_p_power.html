cardinality of a finite field as p power

Proposition

Let $K$ be a finite field of $\mathrm{char}(K) = p$. Then the cardinality is

\begin{align*} \vert K \vert = p^n \end{align*}

for an $n \in \mathbb{N}$

Proof

By construction, $K$ is a vector space over the prime field $\mathbb{F}_p$. (cf. ring as module over a subring) Since $K$ is finite, there exists a finite basis $\alpha_1,...,\alpha_n$ (cf. Existenz einer Basis in einem Vektorraum)

Then there exists a canonical bijection

\begin{align*} \{0,1,...,p\}^n \siemq K \end{align*}
