quasi-compactness for schemes

Proposition

Let $X$ be a scheme and $(U_i \subseteq X)$ an open covering by affine schemes TFAE:

  1. $X$ is quasi-compact,
  2. the underlying space is compact,
  3. there exists a finite subset $J \subseteq I$ such that $(U_j \subseteq X)_{j \in J}$ is an open covering.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 3)

3) $\implies$ 1)

follows from:

  • an open covering is precisely a map
  • the induced map $\bigcup_{i \in I} U_i \rightarrow X$ is a zariski-local epimorphism
