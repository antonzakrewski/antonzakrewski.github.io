Proposition

Any open immersion is etale

Proof

Let $Y \hookrightarrow X$ be an open immersion, $y \in \vert Y \vert$ a point. Then choose an affine open $\mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow X$ with $f(x) \in \mathrm{Spec}(R)$.

Now the identity morphism $\mathrm{Spec}(R_f) \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(R_f)$ is etale. As $y \in \vert Y \vert$ was arbitrary and being an etale morphism is a local property, this implies that an open immersion is etale.
