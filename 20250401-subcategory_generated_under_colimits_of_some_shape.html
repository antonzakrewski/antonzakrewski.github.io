subcategory generated under colimits of some shape

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category (usually cocomplete) and $\mathcal{C}_0 \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ be a collection of objects and $I$ a collection of simplicial sets. Then the subcategory generated under \(I\)-shaped colimits by $\mathcal{C}_0$ is defined as smallest full sub infinity category

  1. containing $\mathcal{C}_0$
  2. closed under \(I\)-shaped colimits in $\mathcal{C}$
