hahn-bach theorem

Proposition

Let $X$ be a vector space and $p: X \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be convex, i.e., for every $x,x' \in X$ and $\alpha \in [0,1]$

\begin{align*} p(\alpha x + (1 - \alpha)x') \leq \alpha p(x) + (1 - \alpha) p(x') \end{align*}

Let $Y \subseteq X$ be a linear subspace and $\lambda: Y \rightarrow \mathbb{K}$ be linear such that $\mathrm{Re}(\lambda(y)) \leq p(y)$ for every $y \in Y$. Then there exists a linear $\Lambda: X \rightarrow \mathbb{K}$ such that

  1. $\Lambda_{\vert Y} = \lambda$
  2. $\mathrm{Re}(\Lambda(x)) \leq p(x)$ for every $x$ in $X$.

If in adidtion, $p$ also satisfies

\begin{align*} p(\alpha x) \leq p(x) \end{align*}

for all $x \in X$ and all $\alpha \in \mathbb{K}$ with $\vert \alpha \vert = 1$, then we have

\begin{align*} \vert \Lambda(x) \vert \leq p(x) \end{align*}

Proof
