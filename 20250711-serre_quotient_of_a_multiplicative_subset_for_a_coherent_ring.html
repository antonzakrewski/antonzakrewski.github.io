serre quotient of a multiplicative subset for a coherent ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be a left coherent ring and $S$ a multiplicatively closed subset Then the map

\begin{align*} \otimes_{R} R[S^{-1}]: \mathrm{Mod}^{\mathrm{fp}}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}^{\mathrm{fp}}(R[S^{-1}]) \end{align*}

is a serre quotient.
