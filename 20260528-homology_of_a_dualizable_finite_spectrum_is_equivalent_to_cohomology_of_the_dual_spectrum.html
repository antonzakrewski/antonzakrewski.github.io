homology of a dualizable/finite spectrum is equivalent to cohomology of the dual spectrum

Proposition

Let $E$ be a spectrum, then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X,E) \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X, \mathbb{S}) \otimes E \end{align*}

In particular, there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} E^n(X) \cong& E_n(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X, \mathbb{S})) \end{align*}

(for consistent homological grading).

Proof

special case of hom out of a dualizable object.
