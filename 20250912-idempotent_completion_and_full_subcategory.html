idempotent completion and full subcategory

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{idem}}$ the idempotent completion and $\mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{idem}}$ a full subcategory containing $\mathcal{C}$. Then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{idem}} \simeq \mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{idem}} \end{align*}

are canonically equivalent

Proof

argue retract

  • then argue: the of the functors is fully faithful
  • fuly faithful retract is equivalence
