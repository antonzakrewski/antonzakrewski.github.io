sInd object as geometric realization of ind objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an small infinity category with finite coproducts and $X \in \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}})$ an infinity presheaf

TFAE:

  1. $X \in \mathcal{P}_{\Sigma}(\mathcal{C})$ in the sense that $X: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ preserves finite products
  2. $X$ is equivalent to a geometric realization of ind object
\begin{align*} U_*: N(\Delta)^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

i.e. $\mathrm{colim} U_* \simeq X$

Proof
