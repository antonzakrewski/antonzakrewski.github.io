pre-triangulated subcategory

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a pre-triangulated category. Then a pre-triangulated subcategory is given by a pretriangulated category $\mathcal{D}$ and an faithful, object-injective triangulated functor

\begin{align*} (\mathcal{F}, \eta): \mathcal{D} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{A} \end{align*}

such that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(-[1]) = \mathcal{F}(-)[1] \end{align*}
