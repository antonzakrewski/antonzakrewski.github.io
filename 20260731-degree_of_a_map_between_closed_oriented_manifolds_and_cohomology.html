degree of a map between closed oriented manifolds is determined by cohomology

Proposition

Let $M,N$ be closed oriented n-manifolds and $f: M \rightarrow N$. Then the degree is determined by $f^*: H^n(N) \rightarrow H^n(M)$

Proof

Recall that poincare duality shows that $H^n(N) \simeq H_0(N), H^n(M) \simeq H_0(M)$ are the integers. In particular, universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology shows that $H^n(N) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(H_n(N),\mathbb{Z})$ and similarly for $M$. Now the degree $d$ is precisely the map
