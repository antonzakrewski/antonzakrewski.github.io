finite union of closed subschemes and quasi-coherent ideal

Proposition

Let $X$ be a scheme and $Z_1,Z_2 \subseteq X$ closed subschemes classified by quasi-coherent ideals $I_1,I_2$. Then the union $Z_1 \cup Z_2$ is classified by the quasi-coherent ideal $I_1 \cap I_2$
