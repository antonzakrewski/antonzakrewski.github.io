functor category of an Ab5 opp category as Ab5 opp category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an Ab5 opp category, $\mathcal{I}$ an indexing category. Then the functor category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{I}, \mathcal{A}) \end{align*}

is also an Ab5 opp category

Proof

follows from:

  • limit of functors for a complete domain
