explicit description of the tangent bundle of a presentable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category Then the tangent bundle is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Exc}(\mathrm{An}^{\mathrm{fin}}, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^{1}, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \\ \mathcal{F} \mapsto (\mathcal{F}(S^0) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(*)) \mapsto \mathcal{F}(*) \end{align*}

of excisive functors (in general not reduced)
