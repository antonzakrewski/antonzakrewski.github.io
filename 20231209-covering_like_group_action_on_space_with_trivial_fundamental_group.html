covering like group action on space with trivial pi_1

Proposition

Let $G$ be a topological group, $X$ a topological space and $\varphi: G \times X \rightarrow X$ a covering-like topological group action. Suppose the fundamental group is $\pi_1(X,x) = 1$.

Then for the orbital collapse $X/G$, it follows, that

\begin{align*} \pi_1(X/G, [x]) \cong G \end{align*}
