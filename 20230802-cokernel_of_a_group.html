cokernel in Grp

Definition / Proposition

Given category Group, groups $G,G' \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathrm{Grp})$ and $\varphi: G \rightarrow G'$ a homomorphism. Then the cokernel of $G'$ is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{coker}(\varphi) = G/\overline{\mathrm{im}(\varphi)} \end{align*}

where $\overline{\mathrm{im}(\varphi)}$ is the normal closure

Proof

Suppose there exists a group-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \psi: G' \rightarrow C \end{align*}

such that

commutes.

Then define

\begin{align*} \varphi': G'/ \overline{G} \rightarrow C [g] \mapsto& \psi(g) \end{align*}

note that it is welldefined, as $\mathrm{im}(G) \subseteq \mathrm{ker}(\psi)$ with kernel as normal subgroup
