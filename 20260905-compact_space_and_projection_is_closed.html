compact space and projection is closed

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space TFAE:

  1. $X$ is a compact space
  2. for every topological space $Y$, the projection $Y \times X \rightarrow Y$ is a closed map

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $A \subseteq Y \times X$ be closed. Then we need to show that $\pi[A] \subseteq Y$ is closed. Now let $y \not\in \pi[A]$, then use the tube lemma to find an open neighbourhood $U_x \times U_y \subseteq X \times Y \setminus A$ with $y \in U_y$. This shows that $U_y \in Y \setminus \pi[A]$ or that $y$ lies in the interior of the complement, i.e. $\pi[A]$ is closed.

2) $\implies$ 1)
