contraction and sum in general do not commute

Proposition

Let $A,B$ be a commutative ring, $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ be a ring-homomorphism and $\mathfrak{b}_1,\mathfrak{b}_2 \subseteq B$ be ideals

Then in general, the contraction and the sum do not commute, i.e

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{b}_1^c + \mathfrak{b}_2^c \neq (\mathfrak{b}_1 + \mathfrak{b}_2)^c \end{align*}

Proof

Consider

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow& \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z} \\ x \mapsto& (x,x) \end{align*}

and the generated Ideals

\begin{align*} (1,0), (0,1) \subseteq \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} (1,0) + (0,1) = (1,1) = \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

where $\varphi^{-1}[(1,0)], \varphi^{-1}[(0,1)] = \{0\}$.
