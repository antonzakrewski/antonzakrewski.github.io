sign automorphism and restriction of scalars

Proposition

Restriction of scalars along the sign automorphism of the S_n group ring induces a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{C}[S_n]} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{C}[S_n]} V \mapsto V \otimes_{\mathbb{C}} \mathbb{C}^{-} \end{align*}

Proof

a)

for any $V$ and $v \in V$ we have

\begin{align*} \tau \cdot_{\mathrm{sgn}} v =& \mathrm{sgn}(\tau) v \\ =& (-1)^{\mathrm{sgn}(\tau)} \tau v \end{align*}

and furthermore for the tensor product

\begin{align*} \tau \cdot (1 \otimes_{\mathbb{C}} v) =& (1 \cdot \tau \otimes \tau \cdot v) \\ =& (-1)^{\mathrm{sgn}(\tau)} \otimes \tau v \\ =& 1 \otimes (-1)^{\mathrm{sgn}(\tau)} \tau v \\ \end{align*}
