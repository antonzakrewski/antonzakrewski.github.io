pullback preserves colimits in Anima

Proposition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a map, then for any $f: I \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ and natural transformation $\eta: f \Rightarrow \mathrm{const}_B$ we get

\begin{align*} (\mathrm{colim} f(-)) \times_B E \simeq \mathrm{colim} (f(-) \times_B E) \end{align*}

where for $b \in B$ we define $(f(b) \times_{B} E)$ as pullback

Proof

note that colimits in the slice category $\mathrm{An}_{/-}$ are computed underlying in anima (cf. colimit in the slice category). So in particular it suffices to compute the colimit $\mathrm{An}_{/B}$ resp. $\mathrm{An}_{/E}$. using Anima slice as Functors into Anima & precomposition and unstraightening we obtain that any $(f \times E)(b)$ is given by

\begin{align*} (f(b) \times_B E) = \mathrm{Un}(\mathrm{Str}(f(b) \rightarrow B) \circ p) \end{align*}

and the colimit of the LHS is given by $\mathrm{Un}(\mathrm{colim} (\mathrm{Str}(f(b) \rightarrow B) \circ p))$. But precomposition with a functor preserves colimits, so in particular $(\mathrm{colim} \mathrm{Str}(f(b) \rightarrow B) \circ p) \simeq (\mathrm{colim} \mathrm{Str}(f(b) \rightarrow B)) \circ p$. Then we go back using that the unstraightenig with the precomposition is the pullback and hence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Un}((\mathrm{colim} \mathrm{Str}(f(b) \rightarrow B)) \circ p) \simeq& \mathrm{Un}(\mathrm{colim} \mathrm{Str}(f(b) \rightarrow B)) \times_B} E \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{Un}(\mathrm{Str}(f(b) \rightarrow B) ) \times_{B} E \\ \end{align*}

now we again use implicitly that we may compute pullbacks (do we need that ? but it is certainly true) and colimits in $\mathrm{An}_{/E}$ underlying (and abuse notation) to get

\begin{align*} (\mathrm{colim} \mathrm{Un}(\mathrm{Str}(f(b) \rightarrow B) )) \times_{B} E \simeq (\mathrm{colim} f(b)) \times_{B} E \end{align*}

so we have shown that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim} (f(-) \times_B E) \simeq (\mathrm{colim} f(-)) \times_B E \end{align*}

as desired
