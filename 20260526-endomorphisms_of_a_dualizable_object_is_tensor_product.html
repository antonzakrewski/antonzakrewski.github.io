endomorphisms of a dualizable object is tensor product

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a closed symmetric monoidal category and $X$ a dualizable object. Then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,X) \simeq \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(X,\mathbbm{1}) \otimes X \end{align*}

Proof
