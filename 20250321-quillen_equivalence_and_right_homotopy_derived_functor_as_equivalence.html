quillen equivalence and right homotopy derived functor as equivalence

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$ be a quillen adjunction between model categories $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}$ is a quillen equivalence
  2. the homotopy right derived functor $R(\mathcal{G}): \mathrm{Ho}(\mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ho}(\mathcal{C})$ is an equivalence

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
