maximal seminorm of a star algebra is in general not non infinity

Proposition

Proof

consider $\rho_{r}: \mathbb{C}[t] \rightarrow B(\mathbb{C})$ with $t \mapsto r$ for $r \in \mathbb{R}$. then this is a representation with $\|\rho_r(t) \| = \vert r \vert$ and passing to suprema shows $\| t \|_{\mathrm{max}} = \infty$.
