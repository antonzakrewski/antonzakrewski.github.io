self homotopies in an anima valued group

Proposition

Let $X$ be an anima, $G$ an anima valued group and $f: X \rightarrow G$ a map. Then a self homotopy of $f$ is equivalently given by a map

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow \Omega_{e} G \end{align*}

Proof

a) reduction to constant map

note that $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,G)$ is a group, since $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,-)$ is monoidal functor Hence all components are (as animas) equivalent (cf. connected components of an anima valued group equivalent as anima).

So in particular $\Omega_{f} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,G) \simeq\Omega_{\mathrm{const}_e} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,G) $

b) constant map

follows from mapping anima functor preserves limits: A self homotopy is precisely an element in $\Omega_{\mathrm{const}_e} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,G)$ but this is canonically equivalent to

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X, \Omega_{e} G) \end{align*}
