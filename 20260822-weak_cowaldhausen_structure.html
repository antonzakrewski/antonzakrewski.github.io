weak co-Waldhausen structure

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, then a weak co-Waldhausen structure on $\mathcal{C}$ is a wide subcategory $\mathcal{C}^{\dag}$, such that for each span

where $g$ is a morphism in $\mathcal{C}^{\dag}$, the pullback

exists and $g'$ lies in $\mathcal{C}^{\dag}$.
