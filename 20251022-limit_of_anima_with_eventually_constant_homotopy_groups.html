sequential limit of anima with eventually constant homotopy groups

Proposition

Let $X: \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ be a functor such that for each $n \in \mathbb{N}$ there exists an $z_n \in \mathbb{Z}$ with the property that the induced map

\begin{align*} \pi_{n}(X_{l-1}) \rightarrow \pi_{n}(X_{l}) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism for $l \leq z_n$

Then the homotopy groups are (up to isomorphism) given by $\pi_{n}(X_{z_n})$

Proof

use some lim-lim1 sequence for anima argument:

  • lim-lim1 vanishes for surjective maps
  • lim-lim1 sequence for homotopy groups in anima
