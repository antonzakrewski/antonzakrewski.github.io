equivalent characterization of E-local spectrum

Proposition

Let $X,E$ be spectra. TFAE:

  1. $X$ is \(E\)-local, that is the Hom anima $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,A)$ is contractible for each \(E\)-local spectrum $A$
  2. $\pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X,A)) \simeq *$ is the point for each \(E\)-local spectrum $A$
  3. $\pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X,A)) \simeq *$ is the point for each \(E\)-local spectrum $A$
  4. $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X,A) \simeq *$ is the point for each \(E\)-local spectrum $A$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

special case

2) $\implies$ 3)

follows as the mapping spectrum lifts the nonnegative homotopy groups/sets

3) $\implies$ 4)

follows from bousfield class is a localizing subcategory, which shows that $A[n]$ is an \(E\)-local spectrum for each $n \in \mathbb{Z}$. Hence

\begin{align*} \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X,A[n])) \cong \pi_{-n}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X,A)) \end{align*}

4) $\implies$ 1)

follows as the mapping spectrum lifts the nonnegative homotopy groups/sets
