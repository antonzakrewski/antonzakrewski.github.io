simple representations of the subalgebra of upper triangular representations

Proposition

Let $A \subseteq \mathrm{Mat}_n(k)$ be the k-subalgebra of upper triangular representations. Then it is easy to show that the simple representations of $A$ are just $V_i$ and where $A$ acts on $V_i$ through $x_{i,i}$.

Furthermore $\mathrm{Rad}(A) = \{strictly upper triangular matrices\}$ and $A/\mathrm{Rad}(A) = \bigoplus_{n} k$.
