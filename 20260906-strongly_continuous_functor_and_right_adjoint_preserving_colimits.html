strongly continuous functor and right adjoint preserving colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor between presentable categories. TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is a strongly continuous functor
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ admits a right adjoint $\mathcal{G}$, which preserves colimits

Proof

follows from:

  • left adjoint preserves colimits
  • infinity left adjoint functor theorem for presentable categories
