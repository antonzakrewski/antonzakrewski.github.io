exact subcategory and Ab4

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, $\mathcal{A}' \hookrightarrow \mathcal{A}$ an exact abelian subcategory which inherits coproducts Suppose $\mathcal{A}$ is Ab4.

Then so is $\mathcal{A}'$

Proof

corollary of:

  • faithfully exact functor as exact and faithful functor
