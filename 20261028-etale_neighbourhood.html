etale neighbourhood

Definition

Let $Z \hookrightarrow X$ be a closed immersion Then an etale neighbourhood of $Z$ in $X$ is an etale morphism $V \rightarrow X$ such that

\begin{align*} V \times_{X} Z \rightarrow Z \end{align*}

is an isomorphism

remark

some special case of an etale neighbourhood is an open subscheme $V \subseteq X$ which contains $Z$
