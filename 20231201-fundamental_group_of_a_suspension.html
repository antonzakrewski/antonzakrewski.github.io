pi_1 of a suspension of a path connected space

Proposition

Let $X$ be a pathconnected topological space and $\Sigma X$ the suspension. Then it holds for the fundamental group

\begin{align*} \Sigma X = 0 \end{align*}

where $0$ is the zero group

Proof

Take the open cover

\begin{align*} \Sigma X \setminus \{N\}, \Sigma X \setminus \{S\} \end{align*}

Note that $\Sigma X \setminus \{N\} \simeq \mathrm{C}(X)$ to the cone (cf. cone as pointed contractible space, suspension without pole as cone)

Then using the Seifert-Van Kampen Theorem for groups we get a pushout
