sInd_1 as full subcategory of sInd

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small \(1\) category. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sInd}_1(\mathcal{C}) \substeq \mathrm{sInd}_{\infty}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is a full sub infinity category.

proof

note that by 1-projectively generated category and sInd_1 as product preserving functors

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sInd}_{1}(\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}}) = \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Set}) \simeq \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

Hence we may view the inclusion as postcomposition with the inclusion of Set to Anima

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Set}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An}) \end{align*}

Note that the inclusion is for example representable (cf. inclusion of Set to Anima as representable functor, or right adjoint) hence preserves limits

Furthermore 1-Presheaves are a full subcategory of infinity-Presheaves, hence the image of the full subactegory $\mathrm{sInd}_1$ is a full sub infinity category.

Since $\mathrm{Set} \subseteq \mathrm{An}$ is closed under products (even limits) since the inclusion is an infinity right adjoint, a functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} \end{align*}

preserves products if and only if it preserves products as functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}} \rightarrow \mathrm{An} \end{align*}
