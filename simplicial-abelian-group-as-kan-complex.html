simplicial group as kan complex

Proposition

A simplicial group $G: \Delta^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Grp}$ is a Kan complex

Proof

Let $\Lambda_S^n$ be the S-horn. Write

\begin{align*} G_{<S>} \coloneqq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{sSet}}(\Lambda_S^n, G) \end{align*}

for the set of $\Lambda_S^n$-simplices in $G$.

Note that $G$ is a group object in category set, hence by hom into a group object as group valued functor restriction along $\Lambda_S^n \rightarrow \Delta^n$ induces a group-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \iota^*: G_{\emptyset} \rightarrow G_{<S>} \end{align*}

we want to show, that this homomorphism is an group-epimorphism. We will proceed by induction on $\vert [n] \setminus S \vert$

induction basis

use $S = [n] \setminus \{i\}$, then choose a degeneration.

induction step

w.l.o.g. $S \neq \emptyset$. Then choose an $j \not\in S$ such that $j - 1 \in S$ or $j + 1 \in S$. We will proceed to fill the \(j\pm 1\)-th face

correction term

Consider

\begin{align*} \theta: \Lambda_{S \cup j}^n \rightarrow G \end{align*}

given by $e$.

Then by induction hypothesis, there exists an \(n\)-simplex which restricts to $\theta$. Alternatively $y = s_j \theta_j$ if $j + 1 \in S$ or $y = s_{j-1} \theta_j$ if $j-1 \in S$

actual filling

Now consider an element $\sigma: \Lambda_{S}^n \rightarrow G$ which we wish to fill. Then we can restrict to $\Lambda_{S \cup j}^n$ and fill this simplex by some $y: \Delta^n \rightarrow G$.

Now $y_S \coloneqq y_{\Lambda_S^n}$ and $\sigma$ only differ in their \(j\)-th face.

Then consider $y_S^{-1} \cdot \sigma$. It follows that $y_S^{-1} \cdot \sigma$ evaluate to $e$ on each face aside from the \(j\)-th face.

Hence by the correction term, there exists a $\theta$ which restricts to $y_S^{-1} \cdot \sigma$

Now $y \cdot \theta$ is a lift of $\sigma$

idea

  • lift in 2 steps:
    • forget one face
    • lift by induction
    • then everything aside from this one face is fine
    • lift some correction term, then multiply with it:
      • correction term everywhere aside from the wrong face as identity
      • use some degeneration lift (?)
