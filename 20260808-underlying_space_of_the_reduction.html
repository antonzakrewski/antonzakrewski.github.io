underlying space of the reduction

Proposition

Let $X$ be a scheme, then the counit $X^{\mathrm{red}} \rightarrow X$ induces a homeomorphism on underlying spaces

Proof

Recall that the underlying space of a closed subfunctor is the total underlying space minus the open complement (cf. underlying space of a closed subfunctor and open complement). Here the open complement of $V(\sqrt{0})$ is given by $D(\sqrt{0})$ which is the empty scheme (cf. underlying space of the empty scheme)
