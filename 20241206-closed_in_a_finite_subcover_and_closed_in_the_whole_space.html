closed in a locally finite open cover and closed in the whole space

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space, $U_i$ be a locally finite open cover and $A \subesteq X$ a subset TFAE:

  1. $A$ is closed in $X$
  2. $A$ is is closed in each $U_i$ with respect to the subspace topology

Proof

follows from

  • topological space as colimit of a locally finite covering
