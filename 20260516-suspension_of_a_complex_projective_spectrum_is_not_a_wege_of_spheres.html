suspension of a complex projective spectrum is not a wege of spheres

Proposition

Let $n,m \in \mathbb{N}$, then $\Sigma^m \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n$ and $\bigvee S^{2n + m}$ are not equivalent in the category of Anima

Proof

use:

  • steenrod operation
  • stability of steenrod operations
  • singular cohomology ring of the infinite complex projective space / reduce to $\mathbb{F}_2$
