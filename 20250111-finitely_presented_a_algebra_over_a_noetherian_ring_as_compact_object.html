finitely presented A-Algebra over a noetherian ring as compact object

Proposition

Let $A$ be a noetherian ring, $B$ an A-Algebra

TFAE:

  1. $B$ is a finitely presented commutative algebra
  2. $B$ is a compact object in category CAlg_A

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows (essentially) from:

  • universal property of a quotient algebra
  • universal property of a polynomial ring

Given a map

\begin{align*} \varphi: A[X_1,...,X_n]/(\alpha_1,...,\alpha_n) \rightarrow \mathrm{colim}_i B_i \end{align*}

we may find a $B_i$ such that

  1. a representative of $\varphi[X_i]$ lies in $B_$
  2. a representative of $\varphi(\alpha_i)$ is $0$ in $B_i$

Then we may lift the map to $B_i$, hence it is surjective (cf. surjectivity of hom colim and lift)

Suppose there exist two different equivalence classes $f_1,f_2 \in \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{Hom}(A[X_1,...,X_n]/(\alpha_1,...,\alpha_n),B_i)$. w.l.o.g. we may assume that $f_1,f_2$ map to the same codomain. Then these maps are defined by mapping $X_1,...,X_n \rightarrow B_i$ (modulo some restriction). Then it follows that there exists some $X_i$ such that $\varphi_j(f_1(X_i)) \neq \varphi_j(f_2(X_i)) \in B_j$. Otherwise we could pass (after finitely many steps) to some $B_l$ with $\varphi_l(f_1(-)) = \varphi_l(f_2(-))$.

But this shows that $f_1(X_i) \neq f_2(X_i) \in \mathrm{colim} B_i$ by explicit computation.

2) $\implies$ 1)

By A-Algebra as filtered colimit of finitely presented A-Algebras for a noetherian ring we may write

\begin{align*} B \cong \mathrm{colim} C_i \end{align*}

where $C_i$ is finitely presented.

Then by compact object as filtered colimit and retract we may conclude that $B$ is a retract of some $C_i$, in particular there exists a surjection

\begin{align*} C_i \twoheadrightarrow B \end{align*}

hence it is a quotient of quotient of a finitely generated algebra as finitely generated algebra and finitely generated algebra over a noetherian ring as finitely presented algebra
