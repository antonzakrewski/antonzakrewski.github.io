adjunction descends onto slice categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ functors and $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$ an adjunction. Let $y \in \mathcal{D}$ and $\mathcal{D}_{y/}, \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{G}(y)}$ be the slice categories

Suppose that for each morphism $\mathcal{R}(y) \rightarrow x$ in $\mathcal{C}$ the pushout

exists (see: counit).

Then the functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{G}_y: \mathcal{D}_{y/} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{G}(y)/} \end{align*}

admits a left adjoint which is given by

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_y(\mathcal{G}(y) \rightarrow x) \simeq (y \rightarrow y \coprod_{\mathcal{FG}(y)} \mathcal{F}(x)) \end{align*}
