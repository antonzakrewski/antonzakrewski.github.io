length of a finite dimensional representation

Definition

Let $V$ be a finite dimensional representation Then the length $\mathrm{length}(V)$ is defined as lenght of a filtration

\begin{align*} V_0 \subsetneq V_1 \subsetneq .... \subsetneq V_n \end{align*}
