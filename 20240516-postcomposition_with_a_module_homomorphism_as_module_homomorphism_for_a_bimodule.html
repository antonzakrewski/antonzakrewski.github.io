postcomposition with a module homomorphism as module homomorphism for a bimodule

Proposition

Let $R,S$ be rings, $M,N_1,N_2B$ left \(R\)-modules such that $M$ is an $(R,S)$ bimodule. Suppose $\varphi: N_1 $\rightarrow N_2$ is a \(R\)- module-homomorphism, then for the hom-set out of a bimodule postcomposition

\begin{align*} f^{ \leftarrow}: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{R-Mod}}(M,N_1) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{R-Mod}}(M,N_2) g \mapsto& f \circ g \end{align*}

is a \(S\) module homomorphism

Proof

group-homomorphism

follows from postcomposition with a group homomorphism as group hommorphism

module

Let $s \in S$. Then we get for $g \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(M,N_1)$

\begin{align*} s \cdot \varphi^{ \leftarrow}(g(-)) =& s \cdot (\varphi \circ g(-)) \\ =& \varphi \circ g(s \cdot -) \\ =& \varphi^{ \leftarrow}(g(s \cdot -)) \\ =& \varphi^{ \leftarrow}(s \cdot g(-)) \end{align*}
