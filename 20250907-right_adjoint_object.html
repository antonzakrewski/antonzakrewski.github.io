right adjoint object

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor. Let $y \in \mathcal{C}$.

Then $x$ is said to be a right adjoint object to $y$ under $\mathcal{F}$ if there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, x) \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(-),y) \end{align*}
