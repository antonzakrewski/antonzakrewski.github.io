universal property of the cotangent module, but not the conormal module

Proposition

Proof

Argument: the cotangent complex lives in $\mathcal{D}(R)_{\geq 0}$, and $\pi_0$ coincides with $\tau_{\leq 0}$ (since it is already connected). But $\pi_1$ is the composition of a left adjoint and right adjoint, hence does not carry a universal property.
