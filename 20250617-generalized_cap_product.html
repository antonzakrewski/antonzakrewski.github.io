generalized cap product

Definition / Proposition

Let $X$ be an anima and $\mathfrak{M}, \mathfrak{L} \in \mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{Mod}(R))$ be local systems. Then the generalized cap product is defined as adjoint morphism

\begin{align*} r_*(\mathfrak{M}) \otimes_{R} r_{!}(\mathcal{L}) \rightarrow r_{!}(\mathfrak{M} \otimes_{R} \mathcal{L}) \end{align*}

to

\begin{align*} r_*(\mathfrak{M}) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(R)}(r_{!}(\mathcal{L}), r_{!}(\mathfrak{M} \otimes \mathcal{L})) \end{align*}
