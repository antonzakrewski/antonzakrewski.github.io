quasi isomorphism between trivial boundary complexes as isomorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes Suppose $C_*,D_*$ are trivial boundary complexes and $f: C_* \rightarrow D_*$ a quasi-isomorphism

Then $f$ is also an isomorphism

Proof

follows from:

  • homology functor for trivial boundary complexes

where

\begin{align*} H_*(f) = f \end{align*}

and the LHS is an isomorphism
