pretopology on CAlg^op by E

Proposition

Let $E$ be a class of ring-homomorphisms which contains isomorphisms, is closed under composition and pushouts Then there is a Grothendieck pretopology on $\mathrm{CAlg}^{\mathrm{op}}$ whose covers are families $R \rightarrow R_i$ such that

  1. $I$ is finite
  2. $\coprod_{i \in I} \mathrm{Spec}(R_i) \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(R)$ is surjective
  3. each map $R \rightarrow R_i$ belongs to $E$
