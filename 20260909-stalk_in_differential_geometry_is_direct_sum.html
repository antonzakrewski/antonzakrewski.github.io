stalk in differential geometry is direct sum

Proposition

Let $M$ be smooth manifold / complex manifold and $m \in M$ a point. Then the stalk is the direct sum

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O}_{M,m} \cong \mathfrak{m} \oplus \mathbb{K} \end{align*}

consisting of the maximal ideal and $\mathbb{K}$

Proof

follows from the evaluation map $\mathcal{O}_{M,m} \rightarrow \mathbb{K}$, which is surjective hence split Hence the kernel must be a maximal ideal and as $\mathcal{O}_{M,m}$ is a local ring, it has to be the unique maximal ideal.
