translation map of a free action

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category such that the underlying-set functor preserves products. Suppose $G$ is a group object and

\begin{align*} \varphi: G \times C \rightarrow C \end{align*}

a free action Group Action

Then the translation map is defined as map

\begin{align*} t_{G}(C) \rightarrow& G \\ (c,g \cdot c) \mapsto& g \end{align*}

where $t_{G}(C) = \{c, g \cdot c \vert g \in G\} \subseteq C \times C
