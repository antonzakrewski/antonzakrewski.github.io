contracted functor

Definition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{CRing} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab}$ a functor Then denote

\begin{align*} i_{+}: R \rightarrow& R[t] \\ i_{-}: R \rightarrow& R[t^{-1}] \\ j_{+}: R[t] \rightarrow& R[t,t^{-1}] \\ j_{-}: R[t^{-1}] \rightarrow& R[t,t^{-1}] \\ \mathrm{ev}_1: R[t] \rightarrow& R \\ t \mapsto& 1 \end{align*}

and define

\begin{align*} \nabla_{+,-}: (\mathcal{F}(i_+), \mathcal{F}(\iota_-): \mathcal{F}(-) \rightarrow& \mathcal{F}([]-) \oplus \mathcal{F}([]t^{-1]) \\ \pm: \mathcal{F}(i_+) - \mathcal{F}(i_-): \mathcal{F}(R[t]) \oplus \mathcal{F}(R[t^{-1}]) \rightarrow& \mathcal{F}(R[t,t^{-1}]) \end{align*}

Define

\begin{align*} N \mathcal{F}(R) \coloneqq \mathrm{ker}(\mathrm{ev}_1: \mathcal{F}(R[t]) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(R)) L \mathcal{F}(R) \coloneqq \mathrm{coker}(\pm) \end{align*}
