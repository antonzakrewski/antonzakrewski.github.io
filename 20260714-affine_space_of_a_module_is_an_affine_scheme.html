affine space of a module is an affine scheme

Proposition

The affine space of a module is an affine scheme represented by the symmetric algebra on a module $\mathrm{Sym}_k(M)$

Proof

follows from the

  • affine scheme via adjunction
  • and the <free commutative algebra> $\mathrm{Mod}_k \rightarrow \mathrm{CAlg}_k$
  • explicit description of the free En Monoid
