algebra isomorphism of class functions and center of the group algebra

Proposition

Let $G$ be a finite group, $\mathbb{C}[G]$ the group ring and $\mathrm{Fun}(G, \mathbb{C})$ the complex valued functions. Then there exists an algebra isomorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: \mathrm{CL}(G, \mathbb{C}) \rightarrow Z(\mathbb{C}[G]) f \mapsto \sum_{ g \in G} f(g) g^{-1} \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{CL}(G, \mathbb{C}) \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(G, \mathbb{C})$.

for an $f \in \mathrm{Fun}(G,\mathbb{C})$ we have that $\varphi(f)$ lies in the center if and only if it is a fixed point under conjugation. Then we may compute

\begin{align*} g_1 \varphi(f) g_1^{-1} =& g_1 \sum_{g \in G} f(g) g^{-1} g_1^{-1} \\ =& \sum_{g \in G} g_1 f(g) g^{-1} g_1^{-1} \end{align*}

if $f$ is a class function, then

\begin{align*} \sum_{g \in G} g_1 f(g) g^{-1} g_1^{-1} =& \sum_{g \in G} f(g) g_1 g^{-1} g_1^{-1} \\ =& \sum_{g \in G} f(g_1 g g_1^{-1}) g_1 g^{-1} g_1^{-1} \\ =& \sum_{h = g_1 g g_1^{-1} \in G} f(h) h^{-1} \end{align*}

If $f$ is not a class function, then there exist $g_1,g \in G$ with $f(g_1 g g_1^{-1}) \neq f(g)$. In particular, the $g_1 g g_1^{-1}$ compoonents of

\begin{align*} g_1 \varphi(f) g_1^{-1} &= \sum_{g \in G} f(g) g_1 g^{-1} g_1^{-1} \\ \varphi(f) &= \sum_{g_1 g g_1^{-1} \in G} f(g_1 g g_1^{-1}) g_1 g^{-1} g_1^{-1} \end{align*}
