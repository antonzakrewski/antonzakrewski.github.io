Category RMod isomorphic to R-Z-Mod

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring, then there exists a canonical isomorphism of

\begin{align*} \mathrm{RMod} \simeq \mathrm{R-\mathbb{Z}-Mod} \end{align*}

where

  1. $\mathrm{RMod}$ is category RMod
  2. $\mathrm{R-\mathbb{Z}-Mod}$ is the category of R-Z bimodules

Proof

corollary of:

  • module as bimodule for the integers
  • module homomorphism as bimodule homomorphism over the integers
