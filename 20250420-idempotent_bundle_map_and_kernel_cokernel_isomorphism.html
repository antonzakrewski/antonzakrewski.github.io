idempotent bundle map and kernel cokernel isomorphism

Proposition

Let $f: E \rightarrow E$ be a bundle map of locally constant rank which is an idempotent morphism. Then there exists a canonical isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ker}(f) \simeq& \mathrm{im}(\mathrm{id} - f) \\ \mathrm{ker}(\mathrm{id} - f) \simeq& \mathrm{im}(f) \\ \end{align*}

see:

  • kernel of a map of vector bundles
  • image of a map of vector bundles

Proof

a)

using isomorphism of vector bundles and fibrewise isomorphism it suffices to show that the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ker}(f) \hookrightarrow E \rightarrow \mathrm{im}(\mathrm{id} - f) \end{align*}

is an iso on each fibre.

This follows from

  • <idempotent> for modules

b)

follows from setting

\begin{align*} f' \coloneqq \mathrm{id} - f \end{align*}

which is also idempotent
