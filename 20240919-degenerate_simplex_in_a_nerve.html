degenerate simplex in a nerve

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $N(\mathcal{C})$ the nerve and $\mathcal{F}: [n] \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ an \(n\)-simplex

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is a degenerate simplex
  2. $\mathcal{F}(m \rightarrow m + 1) = \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{F}(m)}$ for some $m \in \{0,...,n-1\}$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows as a degenerate simplex is given by some

\begin{align*} [n] \rightarrow [n-1] \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

where some morphism $m \rightarrow m+1$ has to map to $\mathrm{id}$.

2) $\implies$ 1)

Then it follows that $\mathcal{F}$ is the degeneration of

\begin{align*} [n] \rightarrow [n-1] \rightarrow \mathcal{c} \end{align*}

where $[n - 1]$ is given by the sequence of $\mathcal{F}$ minus the identity.
