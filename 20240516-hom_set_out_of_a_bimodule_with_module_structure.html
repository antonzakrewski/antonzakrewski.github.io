hom-set out of a bimodule with module structure

Proposition

Let $R,S$ be rings, $M,N$ left \(R\)-module and $M$ a right \(S\)-module. Then the hom-set

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{R-Mod}}(M,N) \end{align*}

admits a natural right-\(S\) module structure with

\begin{align*} f \cdot s = f(\cdot -s) \end{align*}

or

\begin{align*} \cdot: S \rightarrow& \mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{R-Mod}}(S,M)) \\ s \mapsto& (f(-) \mapsto f(- \cdot s)) \end{align*}

Proof

$1$

follows as

\begin{align*} f(-) = f(1 \cdot -) \end{align*}

additive

follows as

\begin{align*} \cdot(s_1 + s_2) (f \mapsto f(- \cdot (s_1 + s_2))) =& (f \mapsto f(- \cdot s_1 \cdot + - \cdot s_2 )) \\ =& (f \mapsto f(- \cdot s_1) + f(- \cdot s_2)) \\ =& (f \mapsto f(- \cdot s_1)) + (f \mapsto f(- \cdot s_2)) \\ =& f \cdot s_1 + f \cdot s_2 \end{align*}

multiplicative

\begin{align*} f \cdot (s_1 \cdot s_2) =& (f(- \cdot (s_1 \cdot s_2))) \\ =& f(- \cdot s_1 \cdot s_2) \\ =& f((- \cdot s_1) \cdot s_2) \\ =& f(- \cdot s_1) \cdot s_2 \\ =& (f(-) \cdot s_1) \cdot s_2) \end{align*}

follows as

\begin{align*} \cdot(s_1 \cdot s_2) =& (f \mapsto f(s_1 \cdot s_2 \cdot -)) \\ =& (f \mapsto f(s_1 \cdot -) \circ (f \mapsto f(s_2 \cdot -)) \\ =& \cdot(s_1) \cdot \cdot(s_2) \end{align*}
