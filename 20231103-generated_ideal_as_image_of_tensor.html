generated ideal as image of tensor

Proposition

Let \(A,B\) be rings For an ideal $I \subseteq A$ and a ring-homomorphism $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$, the generated Ideal by $\varphi[I]$ is the image of the canonical ring-homomorphism

\begin{align*} I \times_A B \rightarrow B \end{align*}

induced by the universal property of the tensor product for the map

\begin{align*} \varphi: I \times B \rightarrow& B \\ (a,b) \mapsto& \varphi(a) \cdot b \end{align*}

Proof
