coslice category of finitely generated free modules

Definition

Let \(A\) be a ring and $M$ be \(A\)-Modul. Then the coslice category of finitely generated free modules, $E(M)$ is defined as full subcategory of the coslice category $\mathrm{R-Mod} \downarrow M$ containing finitely generated free modules

\begin{align*} \alpha: R^k \rightarrow M \end{align*}

as objects
