coverings of a path connected space and isomorphism classes of coverings

Proposition

let $B$ be a locally and globally path-connected topological space and $\mathrm{Cov}(B)$ be the category of covering maps. Suppose $p: E \rightarrow B, p': E' \rightarrow B$ are pathconnected covering map

TFAE:

  1. $p,p'$ are isomorphic
  2. for $b \in B$ and a fibre $e \in p^{-1}(b), e' \in p^{-1}(b)$, the fundamental groups $\pi_1(E,e), \pi_1(E',e')$ regarded as subgroup of $\pi_1(B,b)$ for the canonical inclusion are conjugates

Proof

corollary of:

  • iomorphic quotients as G-Set and conjugation
  • functor from category of coverings of a path connected space to fundamentalgroup-sets as fully faithful

resp.

  • fully faithful functor as conservative functor
  • isomorphisms under functors
