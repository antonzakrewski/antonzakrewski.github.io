projection from \delta slice to \Delta is coinitial

Proposition

The functor $\Delta_{/[m]} \rightarrow \Delta$ is a coinitial functor.

Proof

$m = 1$, but also works for arbitrary $1$

use quillens theorem A for coinitial functors:

$\pi^{\mathrm{op}}$ is coinitial if and only if

\begin{align*} \vert (\Delta_{/[1]})^{\mathrm{op}} \times_{\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}} (\Delta_{/[n]})^{\mathrm{op}} \vert = * \end{align*}

for all $n$.

but rewritign this gives

\begin{align*} \vert ( \Delta \times_{\Delta \times \Delta} (\Delta \times \Delta)_{/([1], [n])})^{\mathrm{op}} \vert \end{align*}

where $\Delta$ is a sifted category (cf. ), hence the diagonal is coinitial
