fundamental groupoid to homology functor

Definition

Let \(M\) be an \(n\)-dimensional manifold and $A$ an abelian group Then there exists a functor

\begin{align*} \nu_X: \pi_{\leq 1}(X) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab} x \mapsto& H_n(X,X \setminus x;A) (\lambda: x \leadsto x') \mapsto \alpha_{\lambda}: H_n(X,X \setminus x, A) \rightarrow H_n(X,X \setminus x', A) \end{align*}
