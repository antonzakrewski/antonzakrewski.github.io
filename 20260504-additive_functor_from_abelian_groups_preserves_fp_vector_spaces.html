additive functor from abelian groups preserves Fp vector spaces

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Ab} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab}$ be an additive functor Then $\mathcal{F}$ preserves \(\mathbb{F}_p\)-vector spaces

Proof

follows from F_p vector space is p-torsion abelian group, i.e. an abelian group is an $\mathbb{F}_p$ vector space if and only if the map $p \cdot: M \rightarrow M$ is the zero map. But an additive functor preserves multiplication and the zero morphism.
