radical of a finite intersection of ideals as intersection of the radicals

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a}_i \subseteq A$ ideals for $i \in \{1,...,n\}$. Then for the Intersection of ideals and radical, it holds that

\begin{align*} \sqrt{\bigcap_{i=1}^n \mathfrak{a}_i} =& \bigcap_{i=1}^n \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}_i} \end{align*}

Proof

$\sqrt{\bigcap_{i=1}^n \mathfrak{a}_i} \subseteq \bigcap_{i=1}^n \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}_i}$

Suppose $a \in \sqrt{\bigcap\mathfrak{a}_i}$, then there exists an $m \in \mathbb{N}$ and $b \in \bigcap_{i=1}^m \mathfrak{a}_i$ such that $a^m = b$. Therefore, $a \in \mathfrak{a}_i$ for each $i \in \{1,...,n\}$

$\sqrt{\bigcap_{i=1}^n \mathfrak{a}_i} \supseteq \bigcap_{i=1}^n \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}_i}$

Suppose there exists an $a \in \bigcup_{i=1}^n \sqrt{\mathfrak{a}_i}$, then for each $i$ there exists an $m_i$ and a $b_i \in \mathfrak{a}_i$ such that $a^{m_i} = b_i$. Furthermore, $a^{\sum_{i=1}^{n} m_i} =& \prod_{i=1}^n b_i \in \mathfrak{a}_i$ for $i \in \{1,...,n\}$, hence $a \in \sqrt{\bigcap_{i=1}^n \mathfrak{a}_i}$
