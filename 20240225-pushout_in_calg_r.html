Pushout in CAlg_R

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $\mathrm{CAlg}_R$ the category CAlg_R Then $\mathrm{CAlg}_R$ admits pushouts

Proof

Let 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
A \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & C
B 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

be a diagram.

Then consider the tensor product

\begin{align*} B \otimes_A C \end{align*}
