pontryagin class under the forgetful map

Proposition

Let

\begin{align*} u: \mathrm{BU}(d) \rightarrow \mathrm{BO}(2d) \end{align*}

be the forgetful map.

Then the pontryagin class maps to

\begin{align*} p_d \mapsto& u^*(p_d) = (-1)^d c_{d}^2 \in H^{4d}(\mathrm{BU}; \mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

Proof

\begin{align*} u^*(p_d) =& & u^*(c^*((-1)^d c_{2d}) \\ =& (-1)^d c_{2d}(\gamma \otimes \overline{\gamma}) \\ =& (-1)^d \sum_{i=0}^{2d} c_i c_{2d-1} (-1)^i \\ =& (-1)^{2d} c_d^{2} \\ =& c_d^2 \end{align*}
