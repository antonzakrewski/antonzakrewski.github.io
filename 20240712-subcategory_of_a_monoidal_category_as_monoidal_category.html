subcategory of a monoidal category as monoidal category

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a monoidal category. Suppose $\mathcal{U} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{V}$ is a subcategory such that

  1. $\mathbb{1} \in \mathcal{U}$
  2. $\otimes$ restricts to $\mathcal{U} \times \mathcal{U} \rightarrow \mathcal{U}$
  3. the associator and left unitor / right unitor restrict to $\mathcal{U}$

Then the monoidal structure also restricts to $\mathcal{U}$
