cut functor

Definition

Let be $\mathrm{TotOrd}$ the category of linearly ordered sets. Then the cut functor is defined as contravariant functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cut}: \mathrm{TotOrd} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Set} \\ J \mapsto& \{((J_1,J_2) \in \mathrm{Cut}(J)\} \\ \varphi: J \rightarrow J' \mapsto& (\varphi^{-1}: (J_1',J_2') \mapsto (\varphi^{-1}(J_1'), \varphi^{-1}(J_2')) \end{align*}
