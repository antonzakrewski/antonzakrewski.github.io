cross product for an absolutely flat ring as map between Eilenberg MacLane spaces

Proposition

Let $R$ be an absolutely flat ring, then the cross product of singular cohomology corresponds to a map

\begin{align*} K(R,n) \times K(R,n) \rightarrow K(R, 2n) \end{align*}

Proof

use kunneth formula for singular cohomology and that Tor vanishes

then brown representability map for singular cohomology
