homotopy coherent nerve of a simplicial set

Definition

Let $\mathrm{sSet}$ be the category sSet Then the homotopy coherent nerve functor is defined as <colimit extension>

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{C}[-]: \mathrm{sSet} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{sSet}} \\ X \mapsto& \mathfrak{C}[X] (f: X \rightarrow Y) \mapsto& \mathrm{induced map on colimits} \end{align*}
