dimension of an affine variety and hypersurface

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be an affine variety. TFAE:

  1. $\mathrm{dim}(X) = n -1$
  2. $X = Z(F) \subseteq \mathbb{A}_K^n$ is a hypersurface (algebraic geometry)

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
