morphism of marked groups

Definition

Let $(G,S), (G',S')$ be marked group. Then a group-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: G \rightarrow G' \end{align*}

is a morphism of marked groups if

\begin{align*} \varphi[S] \coloneqq (\varphi(s_i)) = (s_i') = S' \end{align*}

Note that

\begin{align*} \vert S \vert = \vert S' \vert \end{align*}

is required (by assumption ?)
