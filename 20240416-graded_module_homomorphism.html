graded module homomorphism

Definition

Let $M$ be a monoid and $R = \bigoplus_{m \in M} R_m$ a graded ring. Suppose $N = \bigoplus_{m \in M} N_m, N' = \bigoplus_{m \in M} N_m'$ are graded modules, then a graded module homomorphism is a module-homomorphism

\begin{align*} f: N \rightarrow N' \end{align*}

such that it factors through the canonical inclusion
