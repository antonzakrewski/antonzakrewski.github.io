localization and zero morphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an additive category, $S$ a multiplicative system, $X \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{A})$ an object and $S^{-1}\mathcal{A}$ the category of fractions

TFAE:

  1. $Q(X) = 0 \in \mathrm{Ob}(S^{-1}\mathcal{A})$
  2. there exists an object $Y$ such that $0: X \rightarrow Y \in S$
  3. there exists an object $Z$ such that $0: Y \rightarrow X \in S$

Proof
