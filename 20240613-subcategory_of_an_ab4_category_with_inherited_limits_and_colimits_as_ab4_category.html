subcategory with inherited colimits and limits and commuting

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{B}$ a subcategory such that colimits / limits are inherited. Suppose $\mathrm{colim}_i( \mathrm{lim}_j B_{i,j})$ exists in $\mathcal{B}$ and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_i( \mathrm{lim}_j B_{i,j}) \cong \mathrm{lim}_i ( \mathrm{colim}_j(B_{i,j})) \end{align*}

commutes in $\mathcal{C}$.

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_i( \mathrm{lim}_j B_{i,j}) \cong \mathrm{lim}_i ( \mathrm{colim}_j(B_{i,j})) \end{align*}

also holds in $\mathcal{B}$
