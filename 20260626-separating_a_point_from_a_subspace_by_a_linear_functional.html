separating a point from a subspace by a linear functional

Proposition

Let $X$ be a normed vector space and $Z \subseteq X$ a subspace. Let $x_0 \in X \setminus Z$ with $\mathrm{dist}(x,Z) > 0$, then there exists some $f \in X^*$ such that

  1. $f_{\vert Z} = 0$
  2. $f(x_0) = d$
  3. $\| f \| = 1$

Proof

Let $\overline{Z}$ be the closure of $X$, which is a closed subspace. Then $x_0 \not\in \overline{Z}$, as $\mathrm{dist}(x_0,Z)$ and hence $\overline{Z} \neq X$.

Now by linear functional taking norm and operatornorm 1 there exists a linear functional $X/\overline{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{K}$ which maps $x_0 + \overline{Z} \mapsto \| x + \overline{Z}\| = d$. then precompose with the quotient map $X \rightarrow X/Z$ and obtain a map $f: X \rightarrow \mathbb{K}$ which satsfies $f(x_0) = d$ and $f(Z) = 0$.

So it remains to show $\| f \| = 1$.

$\|f \| \leq 1$

this follows from Submultiplikativität der Operatornorm

$\|f \| \geq 1$

choose a sequence $z_n$ such that $\|x - z_n \| \rightarrow d$ (with $\| x - z_n \| > 0$). Then

\begin{align*} \lim_{n \rightarrow \infty} \frac{\| f(x - z_n) \|}{\| x - z_n \|} = \frac{d}{\lim_{n \rightarrow \infty} \| x - z_n \|} = \frac{d}{d} = 1 \end{align*}

This shows $\| f \| = \mathrm{sup}_{x \in X} \frac{\| f(x) \|}{\| x \|} \geq 1$.
