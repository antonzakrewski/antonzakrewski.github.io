left kan extension along a fully faithful functor commutes up to natural equivalence

Proposition

Let $\iota: \mathcal{C}_0 \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a fully faithful functor and Then the left kan extension

commutes up to natural equivalence

Proof

use left kan extension formula and observe that for $x\in \mathcal{C}_0$ the slice category

\begin{align*} (\mathcal{C}_{0})_{/x} \end{align*}

contains a terminal object, namely $x \rightarrow x$.
