currying of commutative monoids in commutative monoids

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fin}_* \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

be a cartesian commutative Monoid.

Then under the precomposition of the smash product

and currying the composite becomes an object in

\begin{align*} \mathrm{CMon}(\mathrm{CMon}(\mathcal{C})) \end{align*}

Proof
