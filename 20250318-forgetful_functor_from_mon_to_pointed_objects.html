forgetful functor from commutative Mon to pointed objects

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category with terminal object. Then the forgetful functor from the category of cartesian commutative monoids to the infinity category of pointed objects is given by <restricting>

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(N(\mathrm{Fin}_*),\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1,\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

for $\Delta^1 \rightarrow N(\mathrm{Fin}_*)$ classifying $* \rightarrow \langle 1\rangle$
