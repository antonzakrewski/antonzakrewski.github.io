two different definitions of projective objects

Proposition

The two different notions of projective objects in general do not coincide:

  • projective object (simplicial colimits)
  • projective object (epimorphism preserving)

Proof

a) projective object (simplicial colimits)

  • projective object in a stable category

b) projective object (epimorphism preserving)

  • projective object (epimorphism preserving)
