natural transformation for cartesian monoidal categories as oplax monoidal

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}$ be cartesian monoidal categories and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

functors and $\tau: \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ a natural transformation

Then $\tau$ is canonically oplax monoidal transformation with respect to the canonical oplax monoidal structure

Proof
