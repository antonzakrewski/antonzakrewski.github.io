central character of an indecomposable, finite dimensional representation

Definition / Proposition

Let $V$ be a finite dimensional indecomposable representation representation of a K-Algebra $A$ over an algebraically closed field $k$. Then the central character is defined as \(k\)-algebra homomomorphism

\begin{align*} f: Z(A) \rightarrow& k \\ a \mapsto \lambda_a \end{align*}

where each $a$ has $\lambda_a$ as eigenvalue

Remark: the eigenspace can have dimension $\geq 2$.

Proof

Observe that any element $a \in Z(A)$ acts by a matrix, which has at least one eigenvector $v$ for some eigenvalue $\lambda$.
