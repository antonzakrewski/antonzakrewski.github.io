realization functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be categories, where $\mathcal{A}$ is a small category and $\mathcal{B}$ is cocomplete. Then for a functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ the realization functor $\| \cdot \|_{\mathcal{F}}$ is defined as

\begin{align*} \| \cdot \|_{\mathcal{F}}: \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow \mathcal{B} X \mapsto& \mathrm{colim}_{a \in A/X} \mathcal{F}(a) \end{align*}
