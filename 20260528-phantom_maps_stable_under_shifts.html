phantom maps stable under shifts

Proposition

Let $E,E'$ be spectra and $f: E \rightarrow E'$ a map of spectra. TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a phantom map of spectra
  2. $f[n]$ is a phantom map
  3. $f[-n]$ is a phantom map

Proof

follows as $\Sigma^n, \Sigma^{-n}$ are equivalences of categories, which preserve finite spectra etc.
