Proof

follows from the correspondence theorem for normal subgroups: first take a series of normal series $G_i'$ of $G'$ which correspond to a normal series $H_i'$ of $H$ Note that each $H_i' \trianglelefteq H$ is a normal subgroup, as it is the preimage of the normal subgroup $G_i \subseteq G$ (cf. preimage of normal subgroups as normal subgroup)

Furthermore we may choose a normal series $G_i$ of $G$ with abelian quotient and it follows that $\mathrm{im}(G_i) \subseteq \mathrm{im}(G) \subseteq C(G)$ showing that each $\mathrm{im}(G_i) \subseteq C(H)$ is a normal subgroup (cf. center as normal subgroup)
