classification of topological line bundles

Proposition

Let $X$ be a CW-Complex, then the topological line bundles over $X$ are classified by

  1. $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{hTop}}(X,\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty})$
  2. $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{hTop}}(X,\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty})$
  3. $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{hTop}}(X, \mathbb{H}\mathbb{P}^{\infty})$

Proof

follows from

\begin{align*} BC_2 =& \mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{\infty} \\ BS^1 =& \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty} \\ BS^3 =& \mathbb{H}\mathbb{P}^{\infty} \\ \end{align*}
  • infinite complex projective space as classifying space
  • infinite real projective space as classifying space
  • infinite quaternionic projective space as classifying space
