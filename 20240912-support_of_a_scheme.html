support of a scheme at an element of an open

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{O}_X, O_i)$ be a scheme, $U \subseteq X$ an open subset and $f \in \mathcal{O}_X(U)$ an element. Then the <> is defined as

\begin{align*} U_{f} \coloneqq \{x \in U \vert f_{x} \neq 0 \} \subseteq U \end{align*}

where $f_x$ is the image of $f$ under

\begin{align*} U \rightarrow \mathrm{colim}_{U_i \subseteq U} \mathcal{O}_X(U_i) \end{align*}
