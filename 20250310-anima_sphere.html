anima n-sphere

Definition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then (a model for) the \(n\)-sphere in Anima is defined as \(n\)-fold suspension functor

\begin{align*} \Sigma^n(* \coprod *) \end{align*}

note that for the unpointed case it is convenient to define

\begin{align*} S^{-1} = \emptyset \end{align*}
