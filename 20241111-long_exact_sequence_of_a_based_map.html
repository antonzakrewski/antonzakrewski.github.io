long exact sequence of a based map

Proposition

Let $(X,x), (Y,y)$ be based spaces, where $(X,x)$ is well pointed and $f: (X,x) \rightarrow (Y,y)$ a based map Then there exists a long exact sequence 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
[S^1 \times X, Y]_f \arrow[]{r}{\partial} & \pi_1(Y,y) \arrow[]{r}{} & [X,Y]_* \arrow[]{r}{} & [X,Y] \arrow[]{r}{\mathrm{ev}_0} & \pi_0(Y)
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

here $\partial$ is defined as restriction

\begin{align*} S^1 \times \{x\} \hookrightarrow (S^1 \times X) \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

and for $\gamma \in

\begin{align*} \gamma: [0,1] \rightarrow Y \end{align*}
\begin{align*} \pi_1(Y,y) \rightarrow& [X,Y] \\ (\gamma: S^1 \rightarrow Y) \mapsto& \gamma \cdot f \end{align*}

where $\gamma \cdot f$ is the action on $f$
