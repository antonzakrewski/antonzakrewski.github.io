covariant ab-hom functor as exact functor and projective object

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, $P$ an object and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}^{\mathrm{Ab}}(P,-): \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab} \end{align*}

the ab-hom functor

TFAE:

  1. $P$ is a projective object
  2. $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}^{\mathrm{Ab}}(P,-)$ is exact functor
  3. $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}^{\mathrm{Ab}}(P,-)$ is right exact functor

Proof

corollary of:

  • projective object and covariant hom functor preserving epimorphisms
  • covariant ab-hom-functor as left-exact functor
  • left exact functor as half exact functor
  • half exact functor preserving epimorphisms as right exact functor
