cohomology with local coefficients

Definition

Let $\mathcal{F}: X \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}$ be a local system for an anima. Then cohomology of $X$ with local coefficients is defined via the spectrum

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_X \mathcal{F} \in \mathrm{Sp} \end{align*}

with degree convention

\begin{align*} # H^k(X;\mathfrak{M}) = \pi_{-k}(\mathrm{lim}_{X} \mathfrak{M}) \end{align*}
