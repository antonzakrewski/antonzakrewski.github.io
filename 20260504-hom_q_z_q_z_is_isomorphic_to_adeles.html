Hom(Q/Z,Q/Z) is isomorphic to profinite integers

Proposition

The ring $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})$ is isomorphic to the profinite integers.

Proof

Recall that $\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z} \cong \bigoplus_{p \in \mathbb{P}} \mathbb{Z}[\frac{1}{p}]/\mathbb{Z}$. Let $p,q$ be coprime. Then observe that $\mathrm{Hom}_{}(\mathbb{Z}[\frac{1}{p}]/\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Z}[\frac{1}{q}]/\mathbb{Z}) \cong 0$ is trivial, since each element in $\mathbb{Z}[\frac{1}{p}]/\mathbb{Z}$ is \(p\)-primary torsion and $\mathbb{Z}[\frac{1}{q}]/\mathbb{Z}$ is \(p\)-torsion free. Therefore $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\bigoplus_{p \in \mathbb{P}} \mathbb{Z}[\frac{1}{p}]/\mathbb{Z}, \bigoplus_{p \in \mathbb{P}} \mathbb{Z}[\frac{1}{p}]/\mathbb{Z}) \cong \prod_{p \in \mathbb{P}} \bigoplus_{p \in \mathbb{P}} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\mathbb{Z}[\frac{1}{p}]/\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Z}[\frac{1}{p}]/\mathbb{Z})$. todo
