coordinate ring functor

Definition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor over a commutative ring $k$, then the coordinate ring functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O} = \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k^{\mathrm{op}})}(-, \mathbb{A}^1): \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k, \mathrm{Set})^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{CRing} \end{align*}
