Proposition

The category of nonunital star algebras is compactly generated.

Proof

We claim that finitely presented star algebras, i.e. algebras of the form $\mathcal{F}(S)/ \langle R\rangle$ is a compact object for $S,R$ finite.

We first show that $\mathrm{Hom}(\mathcal{F}(S) / \langle R\rangle, \mathrm{colim}_{i \in I} A(i)) \overset{!}{\cong} \mathrm{Hom}(\mathcal{F}(S) / \langle R\rangle, \mathrm{colim}_{i \in I} A(i))$ are canonically isomorphic. Here we have a comparison map and it is both surjective and injective: For this one can show that filtered colimits of star algebras are computed underlying and then argue that this map is bijective.
