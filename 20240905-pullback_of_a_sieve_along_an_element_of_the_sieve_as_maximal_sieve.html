pullback of a sieve along an element of the sieve as maximal sieve

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $U \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ an object and $S$ a sieve on $U$. Then $f: V \rightarrow U \in S(V)$, then $S \times_U V = h_V$ is a maximal sieve

Proof

Observe that $f^*(S) = \{g: W \rightarrow V \vert W \rightarrow V \rightarrow U \in S\}$ but $S$ is a subpresheaf, so closed under precomposition.
