fully faithful right adjoint and conservative left adjoint as equivalence

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be adjoints.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{R}$ is fully faithful and $\mathcal{L}$ is conservative
  2. $\mathcal{R}$ is fully faithful and essentially surjective
  3. $\mathcal{R}, \mathcal{L}$ is an adjoint equivalence

Proof

using: adjoint equivalence of categories and fully faithful and essentially surjective functor

1) $\implies$ 2)

it suffices to show that $\mathcal{R}$ is essentially surjective

using Adjunction and triangle identity there exists a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \eta: \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{C}} \rightarrow \mathcal{R} \mathcal{L} \end{align*}

which becomes as isomorphism after application of $\mathcal{L}$

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L} \eta: \mathcal{L}(-) \rightarrow \mathcal{L} \mathcal{R} \mathcal{L}(-) \end{align*}
