factorization of a covering through the deck collapse

Proposition

Let $B$ be a topological space, $p: E \rightarrow B$ a connected covering and

\begin{align*} \pi: E/\mathrm{deck}(p) \rightarrow B \end{align*}

the continuous map given by the factorization of a covering through the deck collapse.

Then $\pi: E/\mathrm{deck}(p) \rightarrow B$ is also a covering map
