right adjoint of a symmetric monoidal functor inherits lax symmetric monoidal structure

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be symmetric monoidal infinity categories and $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a symmetric monoidal functor. Then the right adjoint inherits a canonical lax symmetric monoidal structure.

Proof
