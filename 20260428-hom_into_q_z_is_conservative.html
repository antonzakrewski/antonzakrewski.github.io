hom into Q/Z is conservative

Proposition

The functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}(- \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}): \mathrm{Ab}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab} \end{align*}

is a conservative functor.

Proof

follows from:

  • exact functor between abelian categories reflecting zero objects and conservative functor
  • rational circle group is an injective group
  • trivial Hom into Q/Z in abelian groups
