restriction of a sheaf

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space and $\mathcal{V}$ a category and $\mathcal{F}$ a V-valued sheaf. Then for $U \subseteq X$ the restriction of $\mathcal{F}$ - if it exists - is defined as inverse image sheaf for the inclusion $\iota: U \rightarrowtail X$.
