Hom vector space of a countably dimensional vector spaces

Proposition

Let $V,W$ be countably dimensional $k$ vector spaces. Then so is $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Vect}_k}(V,W)$

Proof

let $V \cong \bigoplus_{A} k, W \cong \bigoplus_{B} k$. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Vect}_k}(V,W) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Vect}_k}(\bigoplus_A k, \bigoplus_B k) \\ \cong& \prod_{A} \bigoplus_B k \\ \subseteq& \prod_{A} \prod_{B} k \cong& \prod_{A \times B} k \end{align*}

where $A \times B$ is countable (cf. Abzählbarkeit des endlichen kartesischen Produkts einer abzählbaren Menge).
