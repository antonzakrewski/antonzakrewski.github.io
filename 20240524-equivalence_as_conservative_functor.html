equivalence as conservative functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ part of an equivalence Then $\mathcal{F}$ is conservative

Proof

corollary of

  • adjoint equivalence of categories and fully faithful and essentially surjective functor
  • adjointification of an equivalence
