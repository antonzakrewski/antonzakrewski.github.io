kappa calkin category functor

Definition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal Then the $\kappa$ calkin category functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Calk}(-): \mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}, \textrm{stable}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}, \textrm{stable}} \rightarrow \mathcal{A} \mapsto (\mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{A})/\mathcal{A})^{\sharp} \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}(\mathcal{A})/\mathcal{A}$ is the idempotent completion of the verdier quotient of $\mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{A})$.

Remark

  • observe that it is a functor by the same argument as in calkin category functor
  • the $\kappa$ compact objects are closed under countable coproducts, hence there exists an eilenberg's swindle
