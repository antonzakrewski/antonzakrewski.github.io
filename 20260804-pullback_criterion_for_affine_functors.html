pullback criterion for affine functors

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be algebraic functors and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a morphism. TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an affine morphism
  2. for every ring $R$ and every map $\mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow X$, the pullback $Y \times_{X} \mathrm{Spec}(R)$ is an affine scheme

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
