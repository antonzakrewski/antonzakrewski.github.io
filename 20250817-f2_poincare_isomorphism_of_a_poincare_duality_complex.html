F2-poincare isomorphism of a poincare duality complex

Proposition

Let $X$ be a connected poincare duality complex of dimension $d$. Then the unit

\begin{align*} \mathbb{S} \rightarrow r_*r^*(\mathbb{S}) \simeq r_!(D_X) \end{align*}

determines via the stable hurewicz homomorphism a class $[X] \in H_d(X; \omega_X)$ where the generalized cap product induces an isomorphism

\begin{align*} H^*(X; \mathbb{F}_2) \cong H_{d-*}(X; \mathbb{F}_2) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from

  • poincare isomorphism of a poincare duality complex

where we get

\begin{align*} H^*(X; \mathbb{F}_2) \cong H_{d-*}(X; \mathbb{F}_2 \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \omega_{X}) \end{align*}

where $\omega_X \simeq r^* \mathbb{F}_2$ (since $\mathbb{F}_2 \in \mathrm{Ab} = \mathrm{Sp}^{\heart} \subseteq \mathrm{Sp}$ contains no nontrivial automorphisms, see: heart of spectra as Ab) which shows that

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{M} \simeq \mathfrak{M} \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \omega_X \end{align*}
