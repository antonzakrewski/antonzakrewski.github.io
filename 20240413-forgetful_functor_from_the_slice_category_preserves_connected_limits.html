forgetful functor from the slice category preserves connected limits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $c \in \mathcal{C}$ an object and $\mathcal{C} \downarrow c$ the slice category. Then the forgetful functor from the slice category preserves connected limits
