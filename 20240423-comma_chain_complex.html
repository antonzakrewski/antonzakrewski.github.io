comma chain complex functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{A}', \mathcal{B}$ be additive categories, $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}), \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}')$ the category of chain complexes and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \times \mathcal{A}' \rightarrow \mathcal{B} \end{align*}

an additive functor.

Then the comma chain complex functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}) \times \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A}') \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{B}) \\ (C,D) \mapsto& \mathcal{F}(C,D) \\ (f,g) \mapsto& \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(C,D) = \bigoplus_{p + q = n} \mathcal{F}(C_p,D_q) \end{align*}

with differentials

\begin{align*} d^{\mathcal{F}(C,D)}: \mathcal{F}(C_p,D_q) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(C_{p-1}, D_q) \otimes \mathcal{F}(C_p,D_{q-1}) \end{align*}
