vector bundle functor from CRing

Definition

the vector bundle functor is defiend as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Vect}: \mathrm{CRing} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat} \\ R \mapsto& \mathrm{Vect}_R \\ (f: R \rightarrow S) \mapsto& (S \otimes_{R} -: \mathrm{Vect}_R \rightarrow \mathrm{Vect}_S) \end{align*}

use:

  • equivalent characterizations of vector bundles over a ring
  • extension of scalars is symmetric monoidal
  • monoidal functor preserves dualizable objects
