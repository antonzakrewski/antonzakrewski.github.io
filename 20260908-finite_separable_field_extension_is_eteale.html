finite separable field extension is etale / smooth

Proposition

Let $k$ be a field and $k \subseteq k'$ a finite field extension. TFAE:

  1. $k \subseteq k'$ is a separable field extension.
  2. the morphism $\mathrm{Spec}(k') \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(k)$ is an etale morphism
  3. the morphsim $\mathrm{Spec}(k') \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(k)$ is a smooth map
