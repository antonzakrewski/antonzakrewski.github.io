p-completion of a coconnective spectrum is truncated

Proposition

Let $E \in \mathrm{Sp}_{\leq k}$ be $k$ coconnective. Then $E^{^}_p$ is \(k+1\)-coconnective

Proof

follows from

  • stable p-completion is sequential limit
  • coconnective spectra closed under limits

where $E//p$ is at worst $k+1$ coconnective, since

\begin{align*} E//p \simeq \Sigma (\mathrm{fib}(E \xrightarrow{p^n} E) \end{align*}
