crossed product of a group action on a pre-c-star algebra

Definition / Proposition

Let $G$ be a group and $F \in \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{BG}, \mathrm{pre-C-*-Alg}$ a pre C star algebra with an action of a group $G$. Thne the crossed product $A \rtimes^{\mathrm{alg}} G$ is defined by

  1. the underlying vector space is $\bigoplus_{g \in g} A$ and we write $(a,g)$ for $a$ in the summande indexed by $g$
  2. the product is defined as $(a,g)(b,h) \coloneqq (a g(b), gh)$
  3. the involution is defined as $(a,g)^* = (g^{-1}(a^*), g^{-1})$

Proof

nonunital star algebra

star involution

observe

\begin{align*} ((a,g)^*)^* =& (g^{-1}(a^*),g^{-1})^{*} \\ =& (g \cdot (g^{-1}(a^*))^*,g) \\ =& (g \cdot g^{-1} \cdot (a^*)^*, g) \\ =& (a,g) \end{align*}

multiplication

pre-c-* algebra

Observe that

\begin{align*} A \rightarrow& A \rtimes G \\ a \mapsto& (a,1) \end{align*}

is a morphism of star algebras and therefore also contractive. Hence $\| (a,1) \|_{\mathrm{max}} \leq \|a \|$.

Now let $(a,g)$ be arbitrary, then we have

\begin{align*} \| (a,g) \|^2_{\mathrm{max}} =& \| (a,g) \cdot (a,g)^* \|_{\mathrm{max}} \\ =& \| (a a^*,1) \|_{\mathrm{max}} \end{align*}

which is bounded by the previous argument.
