widehat yoneda of a compactly assembled category restricts to Ind

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a compactly assembled category, then the widehat yoneda $\widehat{よ}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C})$ restricts to the full subcategory $\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\omega_1})$ of $\mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C})$.
