linear polynomial defines vector space

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be an affine variety. Assume that $f \in K[X_1,...,X_n]$ is a polynomial with $\mathrm{deg}(f) = 1$ and w.l.o.g. after shifting coordinates $f(0) = 0$.

Then $Z(f)$ is canonically a vector space

Proof

Let $f = \sum_{i=1}^n \alpha_i X_i$. Then for $(a_i),(b_i) \in Z(f)$ and $\lambda \in K$ we get

\begin{align*} (\sum_{i=1}^n \alpha_i X_i)(a_i + \lambda b_i) =& ... \\ =& 0 \end{align*}
