complex conjugation not holomorphic

Proposition

The complex conjugation is not holomorphic

Proof

Note that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_{h \rightarrow 0} \frac{\overline{z + h} - \overline{z}}{h} = \mathrm{lim}_{h \rightarrow 0} \frac{\overline{h}}{h} \end{align*}

which for example evaluates as

\begin{align*} \frac{\overline{h}}{h} = \begin{cases} 1 & \mbox{if } h \in \mathbb{R} \\ -1 & \mbox{if } h = i \cdot r, r \in \mathbb{R} \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
