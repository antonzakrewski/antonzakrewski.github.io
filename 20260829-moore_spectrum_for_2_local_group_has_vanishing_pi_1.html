moore spectrum for abelian groups away from 2 has vanishing pi_1

Proposition

Let $A$ be an abelian group such that $\cdot 2: A \rightarrow A$ is an isomorphism Then $\pi_1(\mathbb{S}_{A}) = 0$.

Proof

Observe that $\cdot 2: \mathbb{S}_A \rightarrow \mathbb{S}_{A}$ is an equivalence on moore spectrum by the stable hurewicz theorem. Hence $\cdot 2: \pi_1(\mathbb{S}_A) \rightarrow \pi_1(\mathbb{S}_A)$ is an isomorphism on a group, which is 2 torsion (cf. pi_1 of a moore spectrum is 2-torsion). Therefore $\pi_1(\mathbb{S}_A)$ must vanish.
