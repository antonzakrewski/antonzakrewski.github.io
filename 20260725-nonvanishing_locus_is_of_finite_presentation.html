nonvanishing locus is of finite presentation

Proposition

Let $k$ be a commutative ring and $D(F)$ a nonvanishing locus. Then $D(F)$ is an algebraic functor locally of finite presentation.

Proof

Let $\mathrm{colim}_{I} R_i$ be a filtered colimit. Then an element in $D(F)(R_i)$ is precisely an element $x \in k$ such that $f_i(k)$ generates the unit ideal $R$. But this implies $1 \in (f_i(k))$ and a finite equation - i.e. finitely many generators and finitely many relations - which behave well with colimits
