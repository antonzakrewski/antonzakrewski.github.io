filtered colimit of stable categories and exact functors is stable

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_i$ be filtered diagram of stable categories and exact functors Then the colimit is also a stable category and in particular also the colimit in $\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}}$.

Proof

Let $I$ be a finite category, hence a compact object in category Cat. Let $I \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} \mathcal{C}_i$ be a diagram in the colimit, then it lifts to some $\mathcal{C}_i$ by compactness of $I$. We can take the limit / colimit there and we claim that it its image is the limit / colimit in the colimit category.

we show the case of the limit, so let $y \in \mathrm{colim}_i \mathcal{C}_i$ be an object, which lifts to some $\mathcal{C}_j$. Now we may coinitially replace $I$ by the full subcategory containing objects $l \in I$ with $\mathrm{Hom}_{I}(l,i), \mathrm{Hom}_{I}(l,j) \neq \emptyset$.

Then we have using Hom in a filtered colimit of categories

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{colim} \mathcal{C}_i}(y, \lim_I x_i) \simeq& \mathrm{colim} \lim_I \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_i}(y, x_i) \\ \simeq& \lim_I \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_i}(y, x_i) \\ \simeq& \lim_I \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{colim} \mathcal{C}_i}(y, x_i) \end{align*}

where we have used that kappa filtered colimits and kappa small limits commute in Anima

Now as being stable is a property about limits and colimits, so $\mathrm{colim} \mathcal{C}_i$ is stable and by construction $\mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathrm{colim}_i \mathcal{C}_i$ is also exact.

For the last claim about being the filtered colimit in $\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}}$, it suffices to observe that $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Cat}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ is the collection of connected components containing exact functors. Here any functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathrm{colim}_i \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is an exact functor, if it preserves finite limits (/colimits) and there it suffices to see that for any finite diagram $I \rightarrow \mathrm{colim} \mathcal{C}_i$ we can lift it to some $\mathcal{C}_i$ and then $\mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ preserves the limit (/colimit).
