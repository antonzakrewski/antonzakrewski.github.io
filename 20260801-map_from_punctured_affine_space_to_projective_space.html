map from punctured affine space to projective space

Proposition

There exists a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \iota_{i}: \mathbb{A}_k^{n}(R) \setminus \{0\} \hookrightarrow& \mathbb{P}_k^{n-1}(R) \\ (a_1,...,a_n) \mapsto& [a_1 : ... : a_n] \end{align*}

here we have used that $\mathbb{A}_k^n \setminus \{0\}$ consists of the tuples $(a_1,...,a_n)$ such that $(a_i) = R$.
