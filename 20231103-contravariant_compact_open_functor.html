contravariant compact open functor

Definition

given category Top and a topological space $(X, \mathcal{T})$ the contravariant compact open functor is defined

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_X: \mathrm{Top} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Top} \\ (Y) \mapsto& \mathcal{C}(Y,X) \\ (f: Y \rightarrow Z) \mapsto& (f^{\rightarrow}: \mathcal{C}(Z,X) \rightarrow \mathcal{C}(Y,X)) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from compact open bifunctor
