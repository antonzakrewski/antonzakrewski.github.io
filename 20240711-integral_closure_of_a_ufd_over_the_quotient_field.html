integral closure of a ufd over the quotient field

Proposition

Let $A$ be a unique factorization domain, $Q(A)$ the quotient field and $\frac{\alpha}{\beta} \in Q(A)$, w.l.o.g. with $\mathrm{gcd}(\alpha,\beta) = 1$.

TFAE:

  1. $\frac{\alpha}{\beta}$ is integral over $A$
  2. $\beta \in A^{\times}$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

By assumption there exists an equation

\begin{align*} (\frac{\alpha}{\beta})^n + \sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i (\frac{\alpha}{\beta})^{n-i} = 0 \end{align*}

Multiplication by $\beta^n$ results in

\begin{align*} \alpha^n = - \beta ( \sum_{i=1}^n a_i \end{align*}

hence $\beta \mid \alpha$

2) $\implies$ 1)
