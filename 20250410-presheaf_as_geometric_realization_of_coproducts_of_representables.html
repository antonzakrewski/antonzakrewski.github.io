presheaf as geometric realization of coproducts of representables

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small infinity category, and $\mathcal{F} \in \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ a infinity presheaf Then there exists a infinity simplicial object

\begin{align*} Y_*: N(\Delta)^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

with

  1. $\mathrm{colim} Y_* \simeq \mathcal{F}$
  2. $Y_n \in \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ is equivalent to a small coproduct of representables

Proof
