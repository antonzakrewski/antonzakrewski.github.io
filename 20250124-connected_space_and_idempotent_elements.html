connected locally ringed space and idempotent elements

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{O}_X$ be a locally ringed space. TFAE:

  1. $\mathrm{Idem}(\mathcal{O}_{X}(X)) = \{0,1\}$
  2. $X$ is connected

Proof

corollary of

  • bijection between idempotent sections and clopen sets of a locally ringed space
