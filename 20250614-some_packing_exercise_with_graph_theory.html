some packing exercise with graph theory

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{R}^2, \| - \|_{2}$ be the normed space and $x_1,...,x_n\in \mathbb{R}^2$ with

\begin{align*} \| x_i - x_j \| \leq 1 \end{align*}

Then there exist at most

\begin{align*} \lfloor \frac{n^2}{3}\rfloor \end{align*}

pairs with

\begin{align*} \| x_i - x_j \| > \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \end{align*}

Proof

consider the graph

\begin{align*} G = ([n],E) \end{align*}

where $ij \in E$ if and only if $\| x_i - x_j \| > \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$

Then we have to give an upper bound of edges. By turan's theorem for $K_4$ free graphs we get the upper bound

\begin{align*} (1 - \frac{1}{3}) \frac{n^2}{2} = \frac{n^2}{3} \end{align*}

if we can show that $G$ is $K_4$ free.
