frobenius reciprocity over C

Proposition

Let $H \subseteq G$ be finite groups Then

\begin{align*} \langle \chi_{\mathrm{Ind}_{H}^G (V), \chi_{W} \rangle_{G} =& \langle \chi_{V}, \chi_{\mathrm{Res}_{H}^{G}(W)} \rangle_{H} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from

\begin{align*} \langle \chi_{\mathrm{Ind}_{H}^{G}}(V), \chi_{W}\rangle =& \mathrm{dim}( \mathrm{Hom}_{G}(W, \mathrm{Ind}_{H}^G V)) \\ =& \mathrm{dim}( \mathrm{Hom}_{H}(\mathrm{Res} W, V)) \\ =& \langle \chi_{V}, \chi_{\mathrm{Res}} \rangle \end{align*}

where we have used:

  • ambidexterity theorem for induction and coinduction for finite groups
  • <some dimension argument>
