isofibration induces equivalence of 2-pullback and pullback in Cat

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_0, \mathcal{C}_1,\mathcal{C}_2$ be categories with functors $\mathcal{F}_1,\mathcal{F}_2$

where $\mathcal{F}_1$ is an isofibration.

Then the inclusion from the pullback and 2-categorical pullback

is an adjoint equivalence

Proof

adjoint equivalence of categories and fully faithful and essentially surjective functor

fully faithful functor

follows from construction, as $\mathcal{C}_1 \times_{\mathcal{C}_0} \mathcal{C}_2$ is a full subcategory

essentially surjective

Let $(X_1,X_2,\alpha)$ be an object, then by construction

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_1(X_1) \cong \mathcal{F}_2(X_2) \end{align*}

Since $\mathcal{F}_1$ is an isofibration, there exists an isomorphism $\overline{\alpha}: X_1 \rightarrow X_1' \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C}_1)$ with

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_1(\overline{\alpha}) = \alpha \end{align*}

In particular,

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_1(X_1') = \mathcal{F}_2(X_2) \end{align*}

Hence there exists a commutative diagram

therfore we have an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_1(\overline{\alpha}), \mathcal{F}_2(\mathrm{id}): (X_1,X_2, \alpha) \rightarrow (X_1', X_2, \mathrm{id}) \end{align*}

where by construction $(X_1',X_2,\mathrm{id}) \in \mathcal{C}_1 \times_{\mathcal{C}_0} \mathcal{C}_2$.
