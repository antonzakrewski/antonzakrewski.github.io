simplicial cocartesian fibration as left fibration and p-cocartesian fibration

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $p: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a cocartesian fibration TFAE:

  1. $p$ is a left fibration
  2. every morphism in $\mathcal{E}$ is p-cocartesian

Proof

follows dually to cartesian fibration as right fibration and p-cartesian morphism
