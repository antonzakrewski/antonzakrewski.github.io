real numbers modulo rational numbers topology

Definition

LEt $\mathbb{R}$ be the real numbers with the standard topology, then define

\begin{align*} \mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Q} = \mathbb{R}/\sim \end{align*}

as quotient topology for x \sim y :\Leftrightarrow \begin{cases} x - y \in \mathbb{Q} \end{cases} \end{align*}
