continuous kappa-calkin functor preserves kappa filtered colimits

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be an uncountable regular cardinal Then the functor $\mathrm{Calk}_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{cont}}: \mathrm{Cat^{dual}_{st}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat^{Perf}}$ preserves $\kappa$ filtered colimits

Proof

The functor $(-)^{\kappa}: \mathrm{Pr}^{L}_{\mathrm{st},\kappa}$ preserves kappa filtered colimits (cf. kappa compact object functor from PrL kappa preserves kappa filtered colimits). Hence so does the composition

\begin{align*} (-)^{\kappa}: \mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}}^{\mathrm{dual}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Pr}_{\mathrm{st}, \kappa}^{\mathrm{perf}} \end{align*}
