image of a composition and subset

Proposition

Let $X,Y,Z$ be sets and

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow& Y \\ g: Y \rightarrow& Z \end{align*}

maps. then it holds, that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{im}(g \circ f) \subseteq \mathrm{im}(f) \end{align*}
