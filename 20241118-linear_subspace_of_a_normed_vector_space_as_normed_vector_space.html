linear subspace of a normed vector space as normed vector space

Proposition

Let $V$ be a normed vector space and $U \subseteq V$ a linear subspace Then $U$ is also a normed vector space with the restriction

\begin{align*} \langle \rangle_{\vert U \times U}: U \times U \rightarrow \mathbb{K} \end{align*}

Proof
