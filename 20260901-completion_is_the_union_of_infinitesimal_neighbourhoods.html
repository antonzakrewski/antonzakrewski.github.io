completion is the union of infinitesimal neighborhoods for a finitely presented morphism

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor and $Z \subseteq X$ a closed subfunctor Then there is an inclusion $\mathrm{colim}_{n \rightarrow \infty} Z^{(n)} \subseteq X_{Z}^{\vee}$ which is an isomorphism if $Z \hookrightarrow X$ is a morphism of finite presentation.

Proof
