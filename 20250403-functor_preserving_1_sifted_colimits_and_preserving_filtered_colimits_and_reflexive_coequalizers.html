1-functor preserving 1-sifted colimits and preserving filtered colimits and reflexive coequalizers

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor Suppose $\mathcal{C}$ is finitely cocomplete.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ preserves 1-sifted colimits
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ preserves reflexive coequalizer and filtered colimits

Here the assumption, that $\mathcal{C}$ is finitely cocomplete is essential
