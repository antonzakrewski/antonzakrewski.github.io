decomposition of a module over the fraction field and decomposition over the original ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be an Integral domain and $M$ a module over the fraction field $Q(A)$. TFAE:

  1. $M$ is indecomposable module over $A$
  2. $M$ is indecomposable over $Q(A)$.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Suppose there exists a decomposition $M \cong M_1 \oplus M_2$. Then any element $a \in A$ acts on an element $(0,m_2)$ by $(0, a m_2)$. So in particular, if $a$ preserves the sum and hence so must $a^{-1}$.

Hence there exists a unique $Q(A)$ module sturcture on $M$ by the universal property of the localization.

2) $\implies$ 1)

use restriction of scalars.
