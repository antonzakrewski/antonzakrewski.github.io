direct image sheaf functor as right adjoint

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be topological spaces, $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a continuous map Then the direct image sheaf functor is a right adjoint to the inverse image sheaf functor

Proof

Let

\begin{align*} \psi: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sh}(Y)}(\mathcal{G}, f_* \mathcal{F}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Sh}(X)}(f^{-1} \mathcal{G}, \mathcal{F}) \varphi \mapsto& \overline{\varphi} \end{align*}

where it suffices to construct

\begin{align*} \overline{\varphi}: f^{-1}_{\mathrm{pre}}(\mathcal{G}) \rightarrow \mathcal{F} \end{align*}

where $f^{-1}_{\mathrm{pre}}(-)$ is the right kan extension since we have sheafification

Let $U \subseteq X$ be nonempty and open and $s \in f^{-1}_{\mathrm{pre}}(U)$. We define an element $\overline{\varphi}(s) \in \mathcal{F}(U)$.

Since

\begin{align*} f^{-1}_{\mathrm{pre}} \mathcal{G}(U) = \mathrm{colim} \mathcal{G}(V) \end{align*}

we may find a representative $s_v \in \mathcal{G}(V)$ for some $V \subseteq Y$.

Here define

\begin{align*} \overline{\varphi}(U): \mathcal{G}(V) \rightarrow f^{-1}_{\mathrm{pre}}(U) s_v \mapsto s \\ \end{align*}

\begin{align*} U \subseteq f^{-1}[f[U]] \subseteq f^{-1}[V] \end{align*}
