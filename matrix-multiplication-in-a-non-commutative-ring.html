matrix multiplication in a non commutative ring

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring, $A \in R^{p \times m}, B \in R^{n \times p}$ be matrices corresponding to maps.

Then matrix multiplication is given by

  1. when regarded as left modules
\begin{align*} (A \cdot B)_{i,k} = (\sum_{j} b_{j,k} a_{i,j}) \end{align*}
  1. when regarded as right modules
\begin{align*} (A \cdot B)_{i,k} = (\sum_{j} a_{i,j} b_{j,k} ) \end{align*}
