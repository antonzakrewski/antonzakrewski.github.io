strict picard spectrum

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentably symmetric monoidal category. then the strict picard spectru mof $\mathcal{C}$ is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{pic}^{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathcal{C}) \coloneqq& \mathbb{G}_{\mathrm{pic}}(\mathcal{C}) \\ =& \tau_{\geq 0} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{Z}, \mathrm{pic}(\mathcal{C})) \end{align*}

of the mapping spectrum
