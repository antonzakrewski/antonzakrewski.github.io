conjugation of an E_1 group is \pi_1 action on higher homotopy groups

Proposition

Let $G$ be an E_1 group, and $g \in G$. Then the conjugation map $\mathrm{conj}(g): G \rightarrow G$ coincides with the map from the pi_1 action on higher homotopy groups of an anima.

In particular, both maps induce the same map on homotopy groups (since they are both the same map).

Proof

this is the same construction.
