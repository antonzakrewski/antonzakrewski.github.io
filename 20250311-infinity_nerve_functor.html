infinity nerve functor

Definition

The infinity nerve functor is defined as infinity functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{1-Cat} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty} \end{align*}

from the

  • infinity category of 1-categories
  • infinity category of infinity categories

via the homotopy coherent nerve of

\begin{align*} \mathrm{1-Cat} \hookrightarrow& \mathrm{QCat} \end{align*}
  • kan enriched category of 1-categories
  • kan enriched category of infinity categories

where

\begin{align*} N: \mathrm{1-Cat} \hookrightarrow& \mathrm{QCat} \\ C \mapsto& N(\mathcal{C}) \\ \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(C,D) \mapsto& \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(C,D) \end{align*}

is the nerve functor
