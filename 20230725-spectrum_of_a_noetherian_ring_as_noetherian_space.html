Spectrum of a noetherian ring as noetherian space

Proposition

Let $A$ be a noetherian ring, then the zariski spectrum $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ with the Zariski topology is a Noetherian topological space.

Proof

Suppose $V_1 \supseteq V_2 \supseteq V_3 \supseteq ...$ is a Chain of closed subsets. By assumption, each $V_i$ is generated by an ideal $\mathfrak{a}_i$. Furthermore, $\mathfrak{a}_1 \subseteq \mathfrak{a}_2 \subseteq ... $ (see: Vanishing set and order reversing). Since $A$ is noetherian, $\mathfrak{a}_i$ stabilizes and therefore $V(\mathfrak{a}_i)$ stabilizes.
