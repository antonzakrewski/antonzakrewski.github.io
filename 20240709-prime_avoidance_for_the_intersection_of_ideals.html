prime avoidance for the intersection of ideals

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{q}_i$ ideals and $\mathfrak{p}$ a prime ideal such that

\begin{align*} \bigcap_{i=1}^r \mathfrak{q}_i \subseteq \mathfrak{p} \end{align*}

Then there exists an $j \in \{1,...,r\}$ such that

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{q}_j \subseteq \mathfrak{p} \end{align*}

Proof

Follows by induction and

  • product of ideals as subset of the intersection
  • prime ideal and product

showing

\begin{align*} \prod \mathfrak{p}_i \subseteq \bicgap \mathfrak{q}_i \subseteq \mathfrak{p} \end{align*}

and hence by repeated induction $\mathfrak{q}_i \subseteq \mathfrak{p}$
