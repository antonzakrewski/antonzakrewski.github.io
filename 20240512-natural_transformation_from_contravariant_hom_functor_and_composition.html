natural transformation from contravariant hom functor and composition

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $A,B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,A)$ the hom-functor and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ another contravariant functor. Suppose there exists a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \epsilon: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,A) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(-) \end{align*}

Then for composable morphism $g,f$ it follows that

\begin{align*} \epsilon(f \circ g) = \mathcal{F}(g) \circ \epsilon(f) \end{align*}
