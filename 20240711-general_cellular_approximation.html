cellular stacking of homotopies

Proposition

Let $(X,A)$ be a CW Pair. Suppose

\begin{align*} \{h_n: X \times [0,1] \rightarrow Y\}_{n \geq 1} \end{align*}

is a sequence of homotopies relative $A$ such that

  1. $h_n(-,1) = H_{n+1}(-,0)$ for all $n$
  2. there exists a sequnece of natural numbers $k_n \rightarrow \infty$ such that
\begin{align*} h_{n} \end{align*}

is a homotopy relative to to $\mathrm{Sk}_{k_n}(X)$

Then there exists a homotopy

\begin{align*} H: X \times [0,1] \end{align*}

such that

\begin{align*} h(-,0) = (h(-,0)) \begin{align*} and \end{align*}

h(x,1) = \mathrm{lim}_{n \rightarrow \infty}(h_n(x,1)) \end{align*}

Proof

Define $H$ by homotopy stacking.

Then by construction

\begin{align*} H(-,0) = h_1(-,0) \end{align*}

and furthermore

\begin{align*} h(x,1) = \lim_{n \to \infty}\left(h_-(x,1)\right) \end{align*}

as

\begin{align*} h_l(x,1) \end{align*}

is eventually constant.

To show that $H$ i, continuous, it is enough to show that $H$ restricted to

\begin{align*} H_{\vert \mathrm{Sk}_k(X)}: \mathrm{Sk}_k(X) \times [0,1, \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

is continuous because

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sk}_k(X \times [0,1]) \subseteq \mathrm{Sk}(X) \times [0,1] \subseteq \mathrm{Sk}_{n+1}(X \times [0,1]) \end{align*}

as cw complex and product with a locally compact cw complex

Then assumption 2 implies that

\begin{align*} h_{\vert \mathrm{Sk}_k(X) \times [0,1]} \end{align*}

arises from stacking finite hmootopies and one constant homotopy
