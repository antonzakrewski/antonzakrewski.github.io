quillen equivalence induces quillen equivalence on functor categories

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}_{\mathcal{C}}, \mathrm{Cof}_{\mathcal{C}}, \mathrm{Fib}_{\mathcal{C}}), (\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{W}_{\mathcal{D}}, \mathrm{Cof}_{\mathcal{D}}, \mathrm{Fib}_{\mathcal{D}})$ be model categories. Suppose $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{W}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{W} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is a quillen equivalence.

Then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_*: \mathrm{Fun}(I, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(I,\mathcal{W})\\ \mathcal{G}_*: \mathrm{Fun}(I, \mathcal{W}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(I,\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

form also a quillen equivalence.
