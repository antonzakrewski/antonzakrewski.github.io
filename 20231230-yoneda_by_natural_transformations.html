Yoneda by natural transformations from Hom-Sets to a presheaf

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a locally small category and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ a contravariant functor Then for $A \in \mathcal{C}$ there exists a Bijection from natural transformations

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Set})}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, A), F) \cong \mathcal{F}A \end{align*}

Proof

For a natural transformation $\theta$, we define

\begin{align*} \theta \mapsto \theta_A(\mathrm{id}_{A}) \in \mathcal{F}(A) \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} \theta_{A}^A: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,A) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(A) \end{align*}

is given by the natural transformation

for an object $u \in \mathcal{F}A$ we define

\begin{align*} u \mapsto (\theta^{u}(X): \alpha \mapsto \mathcal{F}(\alpha)(u)) \end{align*}

Here we get

naturality

bijection
