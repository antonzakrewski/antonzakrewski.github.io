simple representations of a p-group in characteristic p

Proposition

Let $G$ be a \(p\)-group. Then the only simple representation of $G$ in $\mathrm{char}(k) = p$ is the trivial representation.

Proof

Assume that $V$ is a simple representation. Then pick a $v \in V \setminus \{0\}$ and consider $S = \mathrm{Span}_{\mathbb{F}_p} \{gv\}_{g \}n G}$. Then $S$ is a finite set with cardinality $p^n$ with an action of $G$.
