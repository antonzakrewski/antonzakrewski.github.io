left adjoints out of Anima into a category - to merge

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}}(\mathrm{An}, \mathcal{C}) \simeq \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of:

  • left adjoints out of a presheaf category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An} \simeq& \mathcal{P}(*) \\ \mathrm{Fun}(*,\mathcal{C}) \simeq& \mathcal{C} \end{align*}
