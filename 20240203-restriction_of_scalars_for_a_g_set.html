restriction of scalars for a g-set

Definition

Let $G,H$ be groups and $\varphi: G \rightarrow H$ a group-homomorphism. Suppose $X$ is a H-Set, then $X$ is also a G-Set for

\begin{align*} \cdot: G \times X \rightarrow& X \\ (g,x) \mapsto& (\varphi(g),x) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from group action as group homomorphism, where our construction only precomposes, see: composition of group homomorphisms as group homomorphism
