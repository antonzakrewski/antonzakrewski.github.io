intersection of dense sets in general not dense

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $A_1,A_2 \subseteq X$ be dense subspaces. Then the intersection $A_1 \cap A_2$ is in general not dense

Proof

consider

  1. $X = \mathbb{R}$
  2. $A_1 = \mathbb{Q}$
  3. $A_2 = \mathbb{R} \setminus \mathbb{Q}$
