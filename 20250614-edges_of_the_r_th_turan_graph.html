Proposition

Let $n,r,R \in \mathbb{N}$ with $n = r \cdot \lfloor \frac{n}{r} \rfloor + R$. Then

\begin{align*} e(K^{(r)}_n) = \left(1 - \frac{1}{r} \right) \frac{n^2}{2} - R \frac{r - R}{2r} \leq \left(1 - \frac{1}{r} \right) \frac{n^2}{2} \end{align*}
