intersection of p-primary ideals as p-primary ideal

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{q}_i$ be p-primary ideals for $\mathfrak{p}$. Then the intersection

\begin{align*} \bigcap_{i=1}^r \mathfrak{q}_i \end{align*}

is \(p\)-primary

Proof

$r=2$

By radical of a finite intersection of ideals as intersection of the radicals we conclude that

\begin{align*} \sqrt{\mathfrak{q}_1 \cap \mathfrak{q}_2} =& \sqrt{\mathfrak{q}_1} \cap \sqrt{\mathfrak{q}_2} \\ =& \mathfrak{p} \cap \mathfrak{p} =& \mathfrak{p} \end{align*}

Let $xy hin \mathfrak{q}_1 \cap \mathfrak{q}_2$, w.l.o.g. we may assume that $x \not \in \mathfrak{q}_10\cap \mathfrak{q}_2$. Then we may assume $x \not \in \mathfrak{q}_i$

As $\mathfrak{q}_i$ is primary ideal, we conclude htat $y^n \in \mathfrak{q}_1$ for some $n > 0$. Therefore

\begin{align*} y \in \mathfrak{p} =& \sqrt{\mathfrak{q}_i} \end{align*}

or $y^m \in \mathfrak{q}_2$ for some $m$

general case

follows from induction
