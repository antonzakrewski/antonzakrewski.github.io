twisted arrow category of [n] naturally isomorphic to opposite category

Proposition

The cosimplicial simplicial set

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tw}(-): \Delta \rightarrow& \mathrm{sSet} \\ n \mapsto& \mathrm{Tw}(\Delta^n) \end{align*}

is naturally isomorphic to postcomposition with the opposite simplicial set functor, i.e. there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tw}(-) \simeq \mathrm{Tw}(-)^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}
