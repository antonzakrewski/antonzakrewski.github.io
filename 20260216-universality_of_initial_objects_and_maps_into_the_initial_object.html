Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\emptyset$ the initial object. Assume for a morphism $f: X \rightarrow \emptyset$ the base change functor $\mathcal{C}_{/\emptyset} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{/X}$ preserves initial objects.

Then $f$ is an equivalence.

In particular, if colimits are universal, then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,\emptyset) \simeq \begin{cases} \mbox{} * & \mbox{if } X \simeq \emptyset \\ \emptyset & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
