precomposition with a functor induces map on colimits

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be indexing infinity category, $f: Y \rightarrow X$ an infinity functor and $\mathcal{C}$ an infinity category with (sufficiently many) limits Suppose $\mathcal{F}: X \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is another infinity functor

Then there exists an induced map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{Y} (\mathcal{F} \circ f) \rightarrow \mathrm{colim}_Y \mathcal{F} \end{align*}

Proof

follows as precomposition with $f$ is a functor on functor categories, in particalur we get a map

where we have implicitly used that $\mathrm{const} \circ f = \mathrm{const}$

Then covariant infinity yoneda lemma tells us that this is induced by a map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_X \mathcal{F} \circ f \rightarrow \mathrm{colim}_Y \mathcal{F} \end{align*}
