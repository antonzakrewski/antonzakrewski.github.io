R-local anima and pi_0 condition on mapping anima

Proposition

Let $X$ be an anima and $R \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ a subring TFAE:

  1. $X$ is an R-local Anima
  2. for each R-local equivalence $f: A \rightarrow B$ the induced map on the mapping anima is an isomorphism on $\pi_0$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

special case

2) $\implies$ 1)

by definition we may detect equivalences in the homotopy category, so it suffices to show that $\mathrm{map}(B,X) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}(A,X)$ is an isomorphism in the But by weak yoneda lemma it suffices to show that for every anima $T$, the inducde map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{hAn}}(T,\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(B,X)) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{hAn}}(T, \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(A,X)) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism resp. induces a bijection on $\pi_0$

But this map is under currying given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{hAn}}(T \times B,X) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{hAn}}(T \times A,X) \end{align*}

which is precisely precomposition with $T \times A \rightarrow T \times B$.

But this map is an R-local equivalence (cf. kunneth formula)
