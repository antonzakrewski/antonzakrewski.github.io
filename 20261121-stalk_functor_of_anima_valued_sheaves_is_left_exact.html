stalk functor of anima valued sheaves is left exact

Proposition

The stalk functor $\mathrm{Shv}(X,\mathrm{An}) \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ is left exact

Proof

observe that there exists a limit perseving inclusion $\mathrm{Shv}(X, \mathrm{An}) \hookrightarrow \mathcal{P}(\mathrm{Open}(X))$ so it suffices to show that $\mathcal{P}(\mathrm{Open}(X)) \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ is left exact. But this follows from kappa filtered colimits and kappa small limits commute in Anima.
