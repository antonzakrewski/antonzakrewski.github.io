explicit description of an infinity groupoid core

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category. Then the infinity groupoid core is precisely given by the simplicial set consisting

  1. all objects of $\mathcal{C}$, i.e. $\mathcal{C}^{\cong}_0 = \mathcal{C}_0$
  2. all \(n\)-simplices whose a) spine is given by equivalences as morphisms or equivalently b) whose edges is given by equivalences

Proof

We show that the simplicial subset $\mathcal{C}^{\cong}$ defined this way is a pullback by limit of simplicial sets and standard simplex it suffices to consider the case $\Delta^n$.

for $n = 0$ we have $\mathrm{Pull}_0 = \mathcal{C}_0 = \mathcal{C}_0^{\cong}$

for $n \geq 1$, a morphism

picks a) an \(n\)-simplex in $\mathcal{C}$ b) an \(n\)-simplex in $N((h \mathcal{C})^{\cong})$, i.e. \(n\)-composable morphisms in $h \mathcal{C}$, which happen to be isomorphism.

This ensures, that the spine of the \(n\)-simplex consists of equivalences.

By spine consisting of equivalences and edges as equivalence we conclude that each edge is an equivalence.
