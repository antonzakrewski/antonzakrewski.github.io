stalk of a locally noetherian ring as noetherian ring

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{O}_X, O_i)$ be a locally noetherian scheme. Then each stalk is a local ring

Proof

w.l.o.g. we may assume that $X = \mathrm{Spec}(A)$

Then it follows from stalk of a spectrum and localization and localization of a noetherian ring as noetherian ring
