epimorphism in set as surjective map

Proposition

Given category set $\mathrm{Set}$, sets $A,B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathrm{Set})$ and a morphism $f: A \rightarrow B$.

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is surjective
  2. $f$ is an epimorphism

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

corollary of:

  • surjective map as epimorphism

2) $\implies$ 1)

Proof by contraposition

Suppose $f$ is not surjective, then there exists an $b_0 \in B$ with $b_0 \not\in \mathrm{im}(f)$. Thus for the set $\{1,2}$ and the maps

\begin{align*} g: B \rightarrow& \{1,2\} \\ b_0 \mapsto& 2 \\ b \mapsto& 1 & \mbox{else } \\ h: B \rightarrow& \{1,2\} \\ b \mapsto 1 \end{align*}

it follows, that

commutes, since

\begin{align*} (g \circ f)(a) =& 1 \\ =& (h \circ f)(a) \end{align*}

Nonetheless, by construction it holds, that

\begin{align*} g \neq h \end{align*}
