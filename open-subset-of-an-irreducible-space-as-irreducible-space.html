open subset of an irreducible space as irreducible space

Proposition

Let $X$ be an irreducible space and $U \subseteq X$ an open subspace. Then $U$ is also irreducible

Proof

follows from

  • irreducible space and dense open set

Given an open $O \subseteq U$, then $O$ is also open in $X$ (cf. Übertragung der Offenheit von der Unterraumtopologie) Then any closed subset $A$ in $U$ containing $O$ is given by $U \cap A'$ for some closed $A'$. But $A'$ contains $O$, hence $A' = X$ as $O$ is dense. Thus $A = U \cap X = U$ or $O$ is dense in $U$
