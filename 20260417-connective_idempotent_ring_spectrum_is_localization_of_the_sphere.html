connective idempotent ring spectrum is localization of the sphere

Proposition

Let $R$ be a connective idempotent ring spectrum. Then $R \simeq \mathbb{S}[p^{-1} \vert p \in P]$ for some $P \subseteq \mathbb{Z}$.

Proof

follows from:

  • existence and uniqueness of a moore spectrum
  • homology of an idempotent ring spectrum is concentrated in degree zero
