R-module spectrum descends along a map to R

Proposition

Let $\varphi: S \rightarrow R$ be a map of $\mathbb{E}_{\infty}$-ring spectra. Let $\tilde{M}$ be an \(R\)- module spectrum and $M$ the restriction of scalars along $\varphi$.

Then $M$ descends along $\varphi$.
