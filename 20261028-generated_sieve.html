generated sieve

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $\mathcal{U} = (U_i \rightarrow X)_{i \in I}$ a set of maps. Then the generated sieve

\begin{align*} \langle \mathcal{U}\rangle \subesteq よ(x) \end{align*}

is the subpresheaf which is given by the maps $f: Y \rightarrow X$ which factor through one of the $U_i$

i.e. $\langle \mathcal{U}\rangle$ is the colimit

\begin{align*} \langle \mathcal{U}\rangle = \mathrm{colim}_{\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}} \widececk{C}_*(\coprod_{i \in I} よ(U_i) \rightarrow よ(x))$. \end{align*}
