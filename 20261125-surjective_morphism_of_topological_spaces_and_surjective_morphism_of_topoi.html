surjective morphism of topological spaces and surjective morphism of topoi

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a map of topological spaces TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a surjective map of toplogical spaces
  2. $f^*: \mathrm{Shv}(X) \rightarrow \mathrm{Shv}(Y)$ is a surjective geometric morphism of topoi
