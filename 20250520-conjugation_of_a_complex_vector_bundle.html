conjugation of a complex vector bundle

Definition

The conjugation of a complex vector bundle - under the classification of complex vector bundles - is defined as composition

\begin{align*} X \rightarrow \mathrm{B}U(n) \rightarrow \mathrm{B}U(n) \end{align*}

where the second map is given by complex conjugation on $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n$
