surjective map between connective module spectra and connective fiber

Proposition

Let $f: M \rightarrow N$ be map of connective module spectra over a ring spectrum $R$. TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a surjective map
  2. the fiber in $\mathrm{Mod}_{R}(\mathrm{Sp})$ is also connective

Proof

follows from the long exact sequence of homotopy objects.
