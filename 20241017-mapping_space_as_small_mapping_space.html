mapping space as small mapping space

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $x,y \in \mathcal{C}_0$ objects Then there exists a canonical isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \cong \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from the definition and join of represented simplical sets

\begin{align*} \Delta^0 \star \Delta^0 \cong \Delta^1 \end{align*}
