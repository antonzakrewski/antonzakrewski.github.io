limit of compact hausdorff spaces is compact hausdorff

Proposition

Let

\begin{align*} I: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathrm{CH} \subseteq \mathrm{Top} \end{align*}

be a diagram taking values in compact hausdorff spaces.

Then the limit is again compact hausdorff

Proof

follows as

  1. category CompHaus as reflective subcategory of Top
  2. limits in a bousfield localization, in particular $\mathrm{CH} \subseteq \mathrm{Top}$ is closed under limits
