p-cocartesian morphism and trivial fibration

Proposition

Let $p: X \rightarrow Y$ be an inner fibration and $f: \Delta^1 \rightarrow X$

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is p-cocartesian
  2. the map
\begin{align*} X_{f/} \rightarrow X_{x/} \times_{Y/p(x)} Y_{pf/} \end{align*}

is a trivial fibration

Proof

follows dually to
