coslice category of a filtered category is a filtered category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a kappa filtered category and $x \in \mathcal{C}$. Then the infinity coslice category $\mathcal{C}_{x/}$ is also kappa filtered

Proof

note that $\mathcal{C}_{x/} \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{C})$ is the subcategory containing

  1. functors mapping $0$ to $x$
  2. keeping the natural transformation fixed

Now let $f: F \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{x/}$ be any finite simplicial set. Then we may lift

in particular we get a cone point $f^{\triangleright}(*)$. Then taking any composite from $x$ to $f^{\triangleright}(*)$ gives an element $x \rightarrow f^{\triangleright}(*)$ which induces a functor

\begin{align*} f^{\triangleright}: F^{\triangleright} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{x/} \end{align*}

here we use that $f^{\triangleright}: F^{\triangleleft} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ contains an initial object, namely $x$.
