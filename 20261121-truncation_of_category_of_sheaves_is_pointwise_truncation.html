truncation of category of sheaves is pointwise truncation

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category with a grothendieck topology. Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a presentable category

Then

\begin{align*} \tau_{\leq n} \mathrm{Shv}(\mathcal{C}; \mathcal{D}) \simeq \mathrm{Shv}(\mathcal{C}; \tau_{\leq n}\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

Proof

follows as:

  • n-truncation adjunction for anima
  • surely true for anima:
  • hopefully: true for functor categories (todo)
