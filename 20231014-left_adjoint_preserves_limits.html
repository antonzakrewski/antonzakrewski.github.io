Alternative short version

Let $\mathrm{colim}_{I}(X_i)$ be a colimit. Then we get by definition of a left adjoint

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(\mathrm{colim}_I(X_i), -) \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathrm{colim}_{I}(X(i)), \mathcal{R}(-)) % \cong& \mathrm{lim} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X(i), \mathcal{R}(-)) \end{align*}

Using that contravariant hom-functor dualizes small colimits to limits we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathrm{colim}_{I}(X(i)), \mathcal{R}(-)) \cong& \mathrm{lim}_I(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X_i, \mathcal{R}(-))) \end{align*}

Again applying the adjunction and dualizing colimits gives

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_I(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X_i, \mathcal{R}(-))) \cong& \mathrm{lim}_I(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(X_i), -)) \\ \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathrm{colim}_I(\mathcal{L}(X_i)), -) \end{align*}
