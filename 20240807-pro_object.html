pro-object

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category, $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Set})^{\mathrm{op}}$ the opposite category of the functor category. Then an object $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ is said to be a pro-object, if it is the cofiltered limit of covariant representable functors
