kappa small anima has kappa small pi_1

Proposition

Let $\kappa > \omega_0$ be a regular cardinal and $X$ a kappa small anima. Then $\pi_1(X)$ is also \(\kappa\)-small.

Proof

Let $X_n$ be a cw structure of an anima, then there exists an epimorphism

\begin{align*} \pi_1(X_1) \twoheadrightarrow \pi_1(X_2) \cong& \pi_1(X) \end{align*}

so it suffices to show this for $X_1$, i.e. \(1\)-dimensional CW-Complexes But $X_1 \simeq \bigvee_{< \kappa} S^1$ (cf. classification of one dimensional CW Complexes up to homotopy ) and therefore there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \pi_1(X_1) \cong \bigoplus_{i < \kappa} \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}
