ab-hom functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an Ab-enriched category.

Then the Ab-hom functor is defined as bifunctor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}^{\mathrm{Ab}}(-,-): \mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab} \\ A,B \mapsto& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}^{\mathrm{Ab}}(A,B) \\ (f,g) \mapsto& (f^{ \rightarrow} \circ g^{ \leftarrow}) \end{align*}
