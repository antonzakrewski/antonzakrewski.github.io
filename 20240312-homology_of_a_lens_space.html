homology of a 3 dimensional lens space

Proposition

Let $L(p,q)$ be the lens space. Then the homology is given by

\begin{align*} H_k(L(p,q), \mathbb{Z}) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } k = 0,3 \\ \mathbb{Z}/p \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } k = 1 \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
