zero dimensional ring in general not noetherian

Proposition

Let $A$ be a zero dimensional ring Then $A$ is in general not noetherian

Proof

dimension Let $K$ be a field and consider the dual numbers in infinitely many variables

\begin{align*} K[X_1,X_2...]/(X_1^2,X_2^2,...) \end{align*}

Then as prime ideals are radical ideals and $X_i$ is a nilpotent element, we conclude that any prime ideal contains

\begin{align*} (X_1,X_2...) \end{align*}

But then each element with degree $0$ is a unit, hence not in the prime ideal.

Thus the only chain is given by

\begin{align*} (X_1,...,X_2) \subsetneq A \end{align*}
