irreducible polynomial and affine variety in general not irreducible

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $f \in K[X_1,...,X_n]$ an irreducible polynomial. Then the Vanishing set

\begin{align*} \mathcal{V}(f) \end{align*}

is in general not irreducible

Proof

Let $K = \mathbb{Q}$, i.e. not an algebraically closed field Then consider

\begin{align*} X^2 + 1 \end{align*}

it is clearly an irreducible polynomial.

But $\mathcal{V}(X^2 + 1) = \emptyset$ showing
