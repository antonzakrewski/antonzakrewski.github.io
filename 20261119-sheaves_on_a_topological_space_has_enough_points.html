anima valued sheaves on a topological space has enough points

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space. Then $\mathrm{Shv}(X)$ has enough points.

Proof

Let $f: x \rightarrow y$ be a morphism, which is stalkwise an effective epimorphism. Then we need to show that $f$ is an effective epimorphism, i.e. $\tau_{\leq 0} f$ is an epimorphism (cf. effective epimorphism in a topos and truncation).

We have that truncation and stalk commute for anima valued sheaves and that sheaves of sets on a topological space have enough points, hence $\tau_{\leq 0} f$ is an epimorphism or $f$ is an effective epi.
