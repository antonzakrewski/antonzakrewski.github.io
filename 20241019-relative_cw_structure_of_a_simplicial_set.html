relative cw structure of a simplicial set

Proposition

Let $X$ be a simplicial set and $A \hookrightarrow X$ a simplicial subset Then there exists a filtration $\mathrm{Sk}_n \cup A \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Sk}_{n+1} \cup A$ with

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim} (\mathrm{Sk}_n \cup A) \cong X \end{align*}

where each $\mathrm{Sk}_n \cup A$ is obtained by a pushout
