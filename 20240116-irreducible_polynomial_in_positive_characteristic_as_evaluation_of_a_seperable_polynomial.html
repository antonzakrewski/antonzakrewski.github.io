irreducible polynomial in positive characteristic as evaluation of a irreducible separable polynomial

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field with characteristic $\mathrm{char}(K) = p > 0$ and $f \in K[X]$ an irreducible polynomial. Then there exists a separable polynomial $g \in K[X]$ and an $n \geq 0$ such that

\begin{align*} f = g(X^{p^n}) \end{align*}

Proof

$f$ separable

then it follows from

\begin{align*} f = f(X^{p^0}) \end{align*}

$f$ not separable

then by formal derivation in characteristic p as zero there exists an irreducible $g_i \in K[X]$ such that

\begin{align*} f = g_i(X^p) \end{align*}

Note that for the degree it holds, that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{deg}(g_i) = \mathrm{deg}(f)/p \end{align*}

and hence $\mathrm{deg}(g_i) < \mathrm{deg}(f)$.

Since $\mathbb{N}$ is well-ordered, this can be only iterated finitely many times
