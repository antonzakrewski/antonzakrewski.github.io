epsilon ball homeomorphic to the euclidean space

Proposition

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\varepsilon > 0$, it follows for the euclidean space that the epsilon-Umgebung

\begin{align*} B_{\varepsilon}(x) \cong \mathbb{R}^n \end{align*}

Proofsketch

e.g. consider

\begin{align*} x \mapsto x \cdot \tan(\frac{\pi}{2} \cdot \frac{\| x\|}{\varepsilon}) \end{align*}
