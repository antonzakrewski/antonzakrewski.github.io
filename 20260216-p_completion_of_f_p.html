p-completion of F_p

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{F}_p$ be the finite field, then the p-adic completion is given by $\mathbb{F}_p$

Proof

follows as $\mathbb{F}_p/p^n \mathbb{F}_p \cong \mathbb{F}_p$ for $n \geq 1$ and furthermore the map

\begin{align*} \mathbb{F}_p/p^{n+1} \mathbb{F}_p \rightarrow \mathbb{F}_p/p^n \mathbb{F}_p \end{align*}
