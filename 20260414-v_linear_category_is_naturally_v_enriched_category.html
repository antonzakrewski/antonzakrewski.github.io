Motivation

Let $\mathcal{V}$ be a <commutative algebra> in PrL and $\mathcal{D}$ a module over $\mathcal{V}$.

Then $\mathcal{D}$ is a $\mathcal{V}$ enriched category in the following sense:

There exists an action of $\mathcal{V}$ on $\mathcal{D}$, i.e., a functor $\mathcal{V} \otimes \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$. Moreover, by the bilinear functor from product to lurie tensor product this induces a functor $\mathcal{V} \times \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ by precomposition with $\mathcal{V} \times \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ which preserves colimits in both variables.

Hence for any object $d \in \mathcal{D}$ there exists a morphism

\begin{align*} - \otimes d: \mathcal{V} \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

Moreover, it preserves colimits, i.e. by left adjoint functor theorem for presentable categories admits a right adjoint

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(d,-): \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{V} \end{align*}

This will induce a $\mathcal{V}$ enrichment (but as of the time of writing, I dont know how).
