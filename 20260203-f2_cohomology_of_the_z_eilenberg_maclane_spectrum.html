F2 cohomology of the Z Eilenberg MacLane Spectrum

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{Z}$ be the Eilenberg-MacLane spectrum, then $\pi_*(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{F}_2)) = \mathcal{A}^*/\mathrm{Sq}^1$ is given by the F2 Steenrod Algebra while quotienting all admissible monoimials $\mathrm{Sq}^I$, where $1 \not\in I$.
