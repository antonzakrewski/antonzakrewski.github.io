weak serre finiteness theorem of homotopy groups of spheres

Proposition

Let $S^n$ be the n-sphere for $n \geq 1$. Then the homotopy groups of $S^n$ are finitely generated abelian groups

Proof

follows from the fundamental theorem of serre class theory where

  1. $S^1$ is a simple anima, since higher homotopy groups vanish and $\pi_1(S^1)$ is abelian (cf. simple anima has abelian fundamental group style argument)
  2. $S^n$ are simply connected anima, hence in particular simple
  3. $\tilde{H}^m(S^n, \mathbb{Z}) \in \{0, \mathbb{Z}\}$ which are finitely generated
