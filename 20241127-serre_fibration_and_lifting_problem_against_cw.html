serre fibration and lifting problem against product with CW

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a continuous map.

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a serre fibration
  2. for each CW-Complex $B$, $f$ has the right lifting property against
\begin{align*} B \times \{0\} \rightarrow B \times [0,1] \end{align*}

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

serre fibration and lifting problem of a relative cw complex Note that $f$ has the Right Lifting property against

\begin{align*} A \times [0,1] \coprod_{A \times \{0\}} B \times \{0\} \rightarrow B \times [0,1] \end{align*}

Now set $A = \emptyset$, then this is precisely

\begin{align*} B \times \{0\} \rightarrow B \times [0,1] \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows for $B = D^{n}$
