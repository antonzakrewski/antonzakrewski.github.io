topological manifold

Definition

An \(n\)-dimensional topological manifold is a topological space $(M, \mathcal{T})$ such that

  1. $M$ is locally euclidean - sometimes with fixed dimension $n$
  2. $M$ is depending on the definition secondcountable or admits a compact exhaustion or is paracompact
  3. $M$ is Hausdorff

it may also mean a topological manifold with boundary
