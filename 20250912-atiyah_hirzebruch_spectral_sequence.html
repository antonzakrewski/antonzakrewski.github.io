atiyah-hirzebruch spectral sequence for cohomology

Proposition

Let $E$ be a spectrum, $X$ an anima Then there exists a <convergent> spectral sequence

\begin{align*} E_{i,j}^2 = H^{-i}(X, \pi_j(E)) \Rightarrow E^{-(i+j)}(X) \end{align*}

analog:

\begin{align*} E_{i,j}^2 = H_i(X, \pi_j(E)) \Rightarrow E_{i+j}(X) \end{align*}

Proof

consider

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[X], \tau_{\leq *} E) \end{align*}

with

  1. $\mathrm{lim} \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathbb{S}}(\mathbb{S}[X], E)$, since mapping anima preserves limits
  2. $\mathrm{colim} \simeq 0$: connectivity argument: homotopy groups vanish in more and more degrees

use

\begin{align*} \pi_i \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[X], \tau_{\leq n} E) \simeq& \pi_0 \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[X], \Omega^i \tau_{\leq n} E) \\ \simeq& \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\tau_{\leq n-i} \mathbb{S}[X], \Omega^i \tau_{\leq n} E) \\ \end{align*}

where the sphere spectrum vasnihes for $i > n$. hence for $n \rightarrow - \infty$ we get $0$

  • check R1Lim condition

Now we get

\begin{align*} E_{i,j}^2 = \pi_{i+j} \mathrm{gr}^j(\mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}[X], \tau_{\leq *} E)) \Rightarrow \pi_{i+j} \mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}[X], E) = E^{-(i+j)}(X) \end{align*}

and the LHS is given by

\begin{align*} \pi_{i+j} \mathrm{gr}^j(\mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}[X], \tau_{\leq *} E)) \simeq& \pi_{i+j} \mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}[X], \mathrm{gr}^j(\tau_{\leq *} E)) \\ \simeq& \pi_{i+j}(\mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}[X], \Sigma^j \pi_j(E)) \\ \simeq& \pi_i(\mathrm{map}(\mathbb{S}[X], \pi_j(E)) \\ \simeq H^{-i}(X, \pi_j(E)) \end{align*}
