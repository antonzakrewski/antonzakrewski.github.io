category of enriched categories

Definition

Let $\mathcal{V}$ be a monoidal category. Then $\mathrm{Cat}_{\mathcal{V}}$ is defined as category of enriched categories containing

  1. \(\mathcal{V}\) enriched categories as objects
  2. \(\mathcal{V}\) enriched functors as morphisms

and usual composition

note:

  • identity functor as enriched functor
  • composition of enriched functors as enriched functors
