weak phantom maps and cohomology theories

Proposition

Let $\varphi: X \rightarrow Y$ be a map of spectra, which induces the zero map on cohomology theories. Then $\varphi$ is a weak phantom map of spectra.

Proof

Observe that the map $\pi_n(\mathrm{map}(V,\varphi)): \pi_n(\mathrm{map}(V,X)) \rightarrow \pi_n(\mathrm{map}(V,X))$ is the zero map for each finite spectrum $V$ and each $n$. This shows that $\mathrm{map}(V,\varphi)$ is a weak phantom map of spectra. In particular, we may specify to $V = \mathbb{S}$ and conclude that $\varphi$ is a weak phantom map.
