monoidal unit of day convolution

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})^{\otimes}$ be the functor category equipped with the day convolution Then the monoidal unit is given by

\begin{align*} \mathbbm{1}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})}(x) \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{\mathbbm{1} \rightarrow x} \mathbbm{1}_{\mathcal{D}} \end{align*}

Proof
