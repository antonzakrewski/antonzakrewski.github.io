waldhausen 1-category

Definition

A waldhausen category $\mathcal{C}$ is a pointed category equipped with two collections of morphisms $\mathrm{Cofib}, \mathrm{W}$.

  1. $\mathrm{Cofib}, \mathrm{W}$ contain all isomorphism s
  2. the zero morphism is cofibration
  3. cofibrations are preserved under pushouts
  4. weak equivalences are closed under composition
  5. a natural transformation between two cospans consisting of weak equivalences induces a weak equivalence on their pushouts
