Proposition

Let $R \rightarrow S$ be a ring-homomorphism and let $P,Q$ be polynomial \(R\)-algebras with surjections $\pi_P: P \twoheadrightarrow S, \pi_Q: Q \twoheadrightarrow S$. Then each diagram

induces an isomorphism $\mathcal{N}_{S/R}(\pi_P) \simeq \mathcal{N}_{S/R}(\pi_Q)$.

Moreover, for each pair of polynomial algebras $P,Q$ with a surjection onto $S$, there exists a commutative square as above and hence an isomorphism.
