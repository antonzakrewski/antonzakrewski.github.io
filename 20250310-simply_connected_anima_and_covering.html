simply connected anima and covering

Proposition

Let $X$ be a connected anima.

TFAE:

  1. $X$ is simply connected anima
  2. each covering $p: X' \rightarrow X$ admits a section
  3. each covering $p: X' \rightarrow X$ splits into a disjoint union of copies of $X$
