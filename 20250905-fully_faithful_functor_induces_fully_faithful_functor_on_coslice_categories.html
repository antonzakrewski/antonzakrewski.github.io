fully faithful functor induces fully faithful functor on coslice categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a fully faithful functor. Then for $x\in \mathcal{C}$ the induced functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}_{/x} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}_{/\mathcal{F}(x)} \end{align*}

is also fully faithful
