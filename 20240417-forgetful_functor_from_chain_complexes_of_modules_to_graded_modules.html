forgetful functor from chain complexes of modules to graded modules

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod})$ the category of chain complexes. Then for $R$ as ring with the trivial gradation there exists a forgetful functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Forget}: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod}) \rightarrow \mathrm{G-Mod} \\ (C_*) \mapsto& \bigoplus_n C_n \\ (f_*: C_* \rightarrow D_*) \mapsto& \bigoplus f_n: \bigoplus C_n \rightarrow \bigoplus D_n) \end{align*}

Proof
