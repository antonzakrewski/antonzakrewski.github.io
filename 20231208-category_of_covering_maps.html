category of covering maps

Definition

Let $B$ a topological space. The category of covering maps $\mathrm{Cov}(B)$ is the full subcategory of the slice category containing covering maps $p: E \rightarrow B$ as objects

Explicite

The category $\mathrm{Cov}(B)$ contains

  1. covering maps $p: E \rightarrow B$ as objects
