low stable homotopy groups of an Eilenberg-MacLane space

Proposition

Let $A$ be an abelian group, then the first $2n$ stable homotopy groups of $K(A,n)$ are given by

\begin{align*} \pi_*(K(A,n)) = \begin{cases} A & \mbox{if } * = n \\ 0 & \mbox{if } * \leq 2n, * \neq n \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Indices

The space $K(A,n)$ is $n-1$ connected, so the unit $K(A,n) \rightarrow \Omega \Sigma K(A,n)$ is $2n-1$ connected. In particular $\pi_{2n-1}(K(A,n)) \rightarrow \pi_{2n-1}(\Omega \Sigma K(A,n))$ is an epimorphism, i.e. the degrees hold up to $\pi_{2n}(\Sigma K(A,n)) \cong \pi_{2n-1}(\Omega \Sigma K(A,n))$.
