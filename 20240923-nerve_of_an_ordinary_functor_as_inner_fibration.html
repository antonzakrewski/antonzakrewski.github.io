morphism between nerves of categories as inner fibration

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $N(\mathcal{C}), N(\mathcal{D})$ be the nerve and

\begin{align*} f: N(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow N(\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

a morphism of simplicial sets Then $f$ is an inner fibration

Proof

using:

  • nerve functor as fully faithful functor

Given a lifting problem

we may reduce it (cf. reduction of lifting problems for a fully faithful right adjoint, nerve functor as right adjoint, nerve functor as fully faithful functor)

and by

  • homotopy category of an inner horn
  • homotopy category of a standard simplex

this reduces to the identity $[n] \rightarrow [n]$ which can be solved trivially
