lower central series of a group

Definition

Let $G$ be a group Then the lower central series is inductively defined as

\begin{align*} \Gamma^1(G) \coloneqq& G \\ \Gamma^n(G) \coloneqq [\Gamma^{n-1}(G),G] \trianglelefteq G \end{align*}

Note that

\begin{align*} \Gamma^n(G) / \Gamma^{n+1}(G) \subseteq C(G/\Gamma^{n+1}(G)) \end{align*}

lies in the center
