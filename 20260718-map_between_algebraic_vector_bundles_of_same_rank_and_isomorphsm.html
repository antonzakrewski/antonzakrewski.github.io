map between algebraic vector bundles of same rank and isomorphism

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $V,W$ vector bundles over $R$ with $\mathrm{rk}_{K}(V) = \mathrm{rk}_K(W)$ for all R-fields $K$. Let $f: V \rightarrow W$ be a module-homomorphism TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an isomorphism
  2. $f$ is an injection
  3. $f$ is a surjection

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2),3)

clear

2) $\implies$ 1), 3) $\implies$ 1)

follows from map between algebraic vector bundles and tensor product with fields
