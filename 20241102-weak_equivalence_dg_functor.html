weak equivalence dg functor

Definition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be dg categories. Then an enriched functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is said to be a weak equivalence, if

  1. it is an equivalence on the homotopy categories
  2. it is objectwise a quasi-isomorphism
\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y) \rightarrow \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{F}(X), \mathcal{F}(Y)) \end{align*}
