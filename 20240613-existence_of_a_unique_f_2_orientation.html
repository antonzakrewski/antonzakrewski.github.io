existence of a unique F_2 orientation

Proposition

Let \(M\) be an \(n\)-dimensional manifold, $\mathbb{F}_2$ the finite field. Then there exists a unique \(\mathbb{F}_2\)-orientation

Proof

Note that $H_n(X, X \setminus \{x\}; \mathbb{F}_2 \cong \mathbb{F}_2$ and hence there exists a unique isomorphism

\begin{align*} H_n(M, M \setminus \{m\}; \mathbb{F}_2) \cong \mathbb{F}_2 \end{align*}

as $\mathbb{F}_2$ does not have any automorphisms.

Therefore $\nu_x^{\mathbb{F}_2}$ is uniquely orientable as the maximal subgroupoid is a thin category (cf. commuting in a thin diagram)
