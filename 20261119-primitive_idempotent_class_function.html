primitive idempotent class function in C[G]

Proposition

Let $G$ be a finite group

Let $\chi_i$ be the character of a simple representation and .

\begin{align*} \tilde{\chi}_i = \frac{\mathrm{dim} V_i }{\vert G \vert} \chi_i \end{align*}

Then

  1. the primitive idempotents in $\mathbb{C}[G]$ are precisely $\tilde{\chi}_i$ for $V_i$ a simple representation
  2. $\tilde{\chi}_i(e) > 0$

3)

\begin{align*} \chi_i(g) = \sqrt{ \frac{ \vert G \vert}{\tilde{\chi}_i(e)} \tilde{\chi}_i(g) \end{align*}

where the positive square root is taken
