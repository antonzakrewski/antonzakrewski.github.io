counit of an adjunction

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be functors such that $\mathcal{L} \dashv \mathcal{R}$ are adjoint. Then the counit of the adjunction is defined as natural transformation

\begin{align*} \epsilon: L \circ R \rightarrow \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{D}} \end{align*}

with

\begin{align*} \epsilon(A) = \beta(\mathrm{id}_{R(A)}) \end{align*}

for the natural transformation

\begin{align*} \beta: \mathrm{Hom}_{}(-,R(-)) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{}(L(-),-) \end{align*}

of the adjunction
