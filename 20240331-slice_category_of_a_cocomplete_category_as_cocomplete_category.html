slice category of a cocomplete category as cocomplete category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a cocomplete category, $X \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ an object and $\mathcal{C} / X$ the slice category Then $\mathcal{C} / X$ is also cocomplete
