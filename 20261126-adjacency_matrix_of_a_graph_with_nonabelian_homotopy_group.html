adjacency matrix of a graph with nonabelian automorphism group has multiple eigenvalues

Proposition

Let $\Gamma$ be a Graph with non abelian automorphism group. Then the adjacency matrix has an eigenvalue with algebraic multiplicity $\geq 2$.

Proof

Observe that $\mathbb{C}^{\vert \Gamma \vert}$ admits an action from $\mathrm{Aut}(\Gamma) \subseteq \mathrm{Aut}_{\mathrm{Set}}(\vert \Gamma)$. We claim that the adjacency matrix is an endomorphism of the $\mathrm{Aut}(\Gamma)$ representation.
