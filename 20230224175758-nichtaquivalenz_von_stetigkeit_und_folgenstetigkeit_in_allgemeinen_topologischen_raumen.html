Nichtäquivalenz von Stetigkeit und Folgenstetigkeit in allgemeinen Topologischen Räumen

Satz

In allgemeinen topologische Räumen sind Stetigkeit und Folgenstetigkeit nicht äquivalent zu einander

Beweis

Let $M$ be an uncountable set and consider the cocountable topology. Then consider the map

\begin{align*} \iota: (M, \mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{cocount}}) \rightarrow (M, \mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{disc}}) \end{align*}

which is the set theoretic identity.

It is clearly not continuous, as for example $[0, \infty)$ is open in $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{disc}}$ but not in $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{cocount}}$.

But it is sequentially continuous: Let $x_n \rightarrow x$ be a convergent sequence in $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{disc}}$. Then we claim that $x_n$ is eventually constant:

Assume otherwise, then consider $M \setminus \{x_n \vert x_n \neq x\}$ which by definition of the cocountable topology is an open neighbourhood of $x$. Moreover, if $x_n$ is not eventually constant, then it follows that $x_n$ can't converge to $x$, contradiction.

In particular, it follows that $x_n$ convergens against $x$ in the discrete topology.
