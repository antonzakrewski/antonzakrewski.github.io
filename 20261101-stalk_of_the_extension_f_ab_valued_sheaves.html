stalk of the extension of zero of Ab valued sheaves

Proposition

Let $j_{!} \mathcal{F}$ be the presheaf. Then

\begin{align*} (j_{!} \mathcal{F})_* \begin{cases} \mathcal{F}_{x} & \mbox{if } x \in V \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

both for the presheaf and the sheafification. for the natural map $j_! j^{-1} \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{F}$

Proof

use coinitial morphism of directed posets is coinitial for the map

\begin{align*} \{V \vert x \in V \subseteq U\} \rightarrow \{V \vert x \in V\} \end{align*}

since we can intersect.

For $x \not\in V$, we are taking the filtered colimit $\mathrm{colim}_{x \in U} \mathcal{F}(U) \cong \mathrm{colim}_{x \in U} 0$ where we can use that $\mathrm{colim}$ is always right exact, hence $\mathrm{colim} 0 \cong 0$.
