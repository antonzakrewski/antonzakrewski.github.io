weak convergence in a hilbert space

Proposition

Let $X$ be a Hilbert space. Then a sequence $x_n \xrightarrow{n \rightarrow \infty} x$ is weakly convergent if and only if for each $y \in X$, the sequence

\begin{align*} \langle y,x_n\rangle \xrightarrow{n \rightarrow \infty} \langle y,x\rangle \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • weak convergence and linear functionals
  • Riesz-representation for hilbert spaces
