presheaf category of commutative algebras and slice category

Proposition

Let $k$ be a commutative ring and let $\varphi: \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow k$ be the initial morphism. Then the functor <> induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}_{k}, \mathrm{Set}) \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}, \mathrm{Set})_{/ \mathrm{Spec}(k)} \end{align*}
