smooth morphism between schemes

Definition

Let $f: Y \rightarrow X$ be a morphism of schemes. Then $f$ is smooth if, for every point $y \in \vert Y \vert$ (cf. underlying space functor), there are affine open subschemes $V \subseteq Y, U \subseteq X$ with $y \in \vert V \vert$ and $f[V] \subseteq U$ and a factorization

for some $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and some subset $H \subseteq \mathcal{O}(\mathbb{A}_U^n) = \mathcal{O}(U)[x_1,...,x_n]$ wiht $\vert H \vert \leq n$ such that, for every $v \in \vert V \vert$, the jacobian matrix

\begin{align*} \frac{\partial h}{\partial x_j}(v) \in \mathrm{Mat}_{H \times n}(k(v)) \end{align*}

has maximal rank.
