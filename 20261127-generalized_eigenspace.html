generalized eigenspace

Definition

Let $M$ be an \(R\)-module and $\varphi: M \rightarrow M$ an endomorphism and $r$ a scalar. Then the generalized eigenspace of rank $k \in [1,\infty)$ is the kernel

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ker}((\varphi - r)^k) \end{align*}

where the case $k = \infty$ is defined as $\mathrm{colim}_{k} \mathrm{ker}((\varphi - r)^k)$.
