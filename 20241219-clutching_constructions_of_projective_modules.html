clutching construction of projective modules

Proposition

Let $f: R \rightarrow S$ be a ring-homomorphism, $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq R$ an ideal such that

  1. $f_{\vert \mathfrak{a}}$ is injective
  2. $f[\mathfrak{a}] \subseteq S$ is an ideal

Let $M_1 \in R/\mathfrak{a}-\mathrm{Mod}$, $M_2 \in S-\mathrm{Mod} and

\begin{align*} g: S/\mathfrak{a} \otimes_{R/\mathfrak{a}} M_1 \rightarrow S/\mathfrak{a} \otimes_{S} M_2 \end{align*}

an isomorphism of \(S/\mathfrak{a}\)-modules.

Then define

\begin{align*} (M_1,M_2,g) \coloneqq \{(m_1,m_2) \in M_1 \times M_2 \vert (g \overline{f})_*(m_1) = q_*(m_2) \} \end{align*}

where

  1. $(g \circ \overline{f})_*, q_*$ are the unit of extension of scalars adjunction

Proof
