endomorphism ring in an additive category

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an additive category and $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{A})$ an object. Then the hom-set

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{A}}(A,A) = \mathrm{End}_{\mathcal{A}}(A) \end{align*}

carries a natural ring structure given by

  1. addition as addition
  2. composition as multiplication

Proof
