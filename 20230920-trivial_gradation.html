trivial gradation

Definition / Proposition

Let \(A\) be a ring, then the trivial gradation is defined as:

  • $R_0 = R$
  • $R_i = 0$ for $i \neq 0$

Proof

direct sum

follows from $R_i \cap R_j = 0$ for $i \neq j$ and $r \in R \Leftrightarrow r = (r,0,0,...) \in \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0} R_n$

multiplicativity

Let $R_i, R_j \subseteq R$. Then

\begin{align*} R_i \cdot R_j = \begin{cases} R \subseteq R_{i+j} = R_{0}& \mbox{if } i = j = 0 \\ 0 \subseteq R_{i+j} & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
