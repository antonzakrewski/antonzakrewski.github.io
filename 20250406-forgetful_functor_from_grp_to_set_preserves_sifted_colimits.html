forgetful functor from Grp to Set preserves sifted colimits

Proposition

The forgetful functor from Grp to Set preserves 1-sifted colimits.

Proof

using:

  • 1-functor preserving 1-sifted colimits and preserving filtered colimits and reflexive coequalizers
  • reflexive coequalizer

fitlered colimits

  • forgetful functor from Grp to Set preserves filtered colimits

reflexive coequalizer

Let 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
A \arrow[shift right=1, swap]{r}{f_2} \arrow[shift left=1]{r}{f_1}& \rar B & B/\sim
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displymath}
be a reflexive coequalizer.

Using the description of [[id:34d24f31-a0d5-41bc-9075-f7b2a863c18f][colimit in set]], it remains to show that $B/\sim$ is equipped with a canonical group structure via
\begin{align*}
[b] \cdot [b'] \coloneqq [b \cdot b'] 
\end{align*}
It remains to show that this is welldefined:
Assume that $b_1 \sim b_2$, then we have to show that
\begin{align*}
[b_1 \cdot b'] = [b_2 \cdot b']
\end{align*}
and 
\begin{align*}
[b' \cdot b_1] = [b \cdot b_2]
\end{align*}

The second case can be done analogously (or apply probably apply the [[id:220d333b-7315-484a-878f-a3b75dd8a4e5][opposite group functor]]) 


Note that $b_1 \sim b_2$ [[id:a1245369-4879-458c-aec3-e2905bfa6306][if and only if]] there exist $a_1,...,a_n$ such that
\begin{align*}
f_1(a_1) =& b_1 \\
f_1(a_i) =& f_2(a_{i-1}) \\
f_2(a_n) =& b_{2}
\end{align*}

[[id:e58125c9-5f34-4043-bf52-23ecb4c20f2e][w.l.o.g.]] assume that $n = 1$, the general case follows by induction over the zigzag.
Choose a common section $s$
Then 
\begin{align*}
b_1 \cdot b' =& f_1(a_1) \cdot f_1(s(b')) \\
=& f_1(a_1 \cdot s(b')) 
b_2 \cdot b' =& f_2(a_1) \cdot f_2(s(b')) \\
=& f_2(a_1 + s(b'))
\end{align*}

Hence $b_1 \cdot b' \sim b_2 \cdot b'$
