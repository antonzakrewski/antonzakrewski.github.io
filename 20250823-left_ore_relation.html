left ore relation

Definition

Let \(R\) be a ring, $S \subseteq R$ a subset satisfying the left ore condition. Then define the equivalence relation on $S \times M$ via

\begin{align*} (s,x) \sim (s',x') \end{align*}

if and only if there exists $a,a' \in R$ such that

  1. $as = a's' \in S$
  2. $ax = a'x'$

Proof

reflexive relation

follows from $a = a' = 1$

symmetric relation

follows from symmetry of $=$

transitive relation

Let $(s,x) \sim (s',x') \sim (s'',x'')$ with $a,a'$ and $b',b''$ witnessing the relations. Then

  1. $as = a's' \in S, b's' = b''s'' \in S$
  2. $ax = a'x', b'x' = b''x''$

and using the second condition of the left ore condition we can find elements $u \in S, c \in R$ such that

\begin{align*} u (a' sm) = c (b's') \end{align*}

Hence $(ua' - cb') \cdot s = 0$ so using the third condition we can find some element $t \in S$ with $t \cdot (ua' - cb') = 0$ or

\begin{align*} tuas = tua's' = tcb's' = tcb''s'' \end{align*}

where we have used $as = a's', b's' = b''s''$ Now $t,u,as \in S$ and $S$ is closed under multiplication, hence we may set $\overline{a} = tua, \overline{a}' = tcb''$ to get the first condition of our relation and

\begin{align*} tuax = tcb''x'' \end{align*}

since

\begin{align*} tuax = tua'x' = tcb'x' = tcb''x'' \end{align*}

since $tua' = tcb'$
