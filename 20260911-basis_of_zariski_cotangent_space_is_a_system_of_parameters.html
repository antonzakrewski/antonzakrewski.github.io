basis of zariski cotangent space for a noetherian local ring is a system of parameters

Proposition

Let $(A, \mathfrak{m})$ be noetherian local ring and $f_1,...,f_d \in \mathfrak{m}$ such that their images in the vector space $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$ forms a basis. Then they are a system of parameters.

Proof

observe that $\mathfrak{m}$ is a finitely generated module since $A$ is a noetherian ring. Then use finitely generated Module over a local ring and fibre as generators / Nakayama Lemma to conclude that $f_1,...,f_d$ generate the k-algebra $\mathfrak{m}$.
