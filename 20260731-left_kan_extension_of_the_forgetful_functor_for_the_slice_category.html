left kan extension formula of the forgetful functor for the slice category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $u: Y \rightarrow X$ a morphism and $u^*: \mathcal{C}_{/Y} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{/X}$ be the forgetful functor Then the left kan extension along $u^*$ is pointwise given by

\begin{align*} u_{\sharp}(\mathcal{F})(U \rightarrow X) \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}_{/X}}(U,Y)} \mathcal{F}(U \rightarrow Y) \end{align*}

Proof
