full subcategory of a monoidal category as monidal category

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a monoidal category and $\mathcal{U} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{V}$ a full subcategory such that

  1. $U \otimes U' \in \mathcal{U}$
  2. $\mathbbm{1} \in \mathcal{U}$

Then $\mathcal{U}$ also carries the monoidal structure
