circles in a k-connected graph

Proposition

Let $G = (V,E)$ be a k knot-connected graph. Then there exist at least

\begin{align*} \binom{k}{2} \end{align*}

many circles.

Proof

Let $u,v \in V$. Then by menger theorem about knot connectivity of a graph there exist at least $k$ many vertex disjoint paths $u - v$ Then combining two paths yields a circel, in particular there exists

\begin{align*} \binom{k}{2} \end{align*}

many circles
