covariant compact open functor as right adjoint for a locally compact space

Proposition

Let $Y$ be a locally regular compact topological space. Then the covariant compact open

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}(Y,-): \mathrm{Top} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Top} \\ X \rightarrow& \mathrm{Map}(Y,X) \end{align*}

is a right adjoint to the product functor

\begin{align*} Y \times -: \mathrm{Top} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Top} \\ \end{align*}
