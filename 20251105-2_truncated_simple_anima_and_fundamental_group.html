2-truncated simple anima and abelian pi_1

Proposition

Let $X$ be an n-truncated anima TFAE:

  1. $X$ is a simple anima
  2. $\pi_1(X)$ is abelian for each point

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

simple anima has abelian fundamental group

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows f5om: pi_1 action on pi_1 as conjugation
