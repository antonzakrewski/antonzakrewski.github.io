weak colimit

Definition

A weak colimit is a weak limit in the opposite category

Explizit

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $F: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a diagram. A weak limit of $F$ is a cocone $(L, \phi)$ such that for every other cocone $(N, \psi)$ there exists a (not necessarily unique) morphism $u: N \rightarrow L$ such that following diagram commutes: 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
F(X) \arrow[]{rd}{\varphi_X} \arrow[bend left]{rd}{\psi_X} \arrow[]{rr}{F(f)}& & \arrow[]{ld}{\phi_Y} \arrow[bend right]{rd}{\psi_Y} F(Y) \\
&  L \arrow[dashed]{d}{\exists u}&  \\
& N & \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
