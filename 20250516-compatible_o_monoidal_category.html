o-monoidal category compatible with k-colimits

Definition

Let $\mathcal{K}$ be a class of infinity categories, $\mathcal{O}$ a <cartpattern>. An \(\mathcal{O}\)-<monoidal category> $\mathcal{C}^{\otimes} \rightarrow \mathcal{O}$ is said to be compatible with $\mathcal{K}$ colimits if

  1. the <underlying objects> $\mathcal{C}_{E}$ has $\mathcal{K}$ indexed colimits for $E \in \mathcal{O}^{\mathrm{el}}$
  2. for very active morphism $\varphi: O \leadsto E$ with $E \in \mathcal{O}^{\mathrm{el}}$
\begin{align*} \varphi_i: \prod \mathcal{C}_{O_i} \simeq \mathcal{C}_0 \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_E \end{align*}

preserves $\mathcal{K}$ shaped colimits in each variable
