p-complete spectrum and mapping out of an R-local spectrum

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum. TFAE:

  1. $X$ is p-complete spectrum
  2. $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(Y[p^{-1}], X) \simeq 0$ for all $Y \in \mathrm{Sp}$
  3. $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], X) \simeq 0$

Proof

we use p-complete spectrum and sequential limit, i.e. $X$ is p-complete if and only if $\lim (X \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X)$ vanishes.

1) $\implies$ 2)

Recall that $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(-,-)$ is additive in both variables. Hence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(Y[p^{-1}], X) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathrm{colim} (Y \xrightarrow{\cdot p} Y), X) \\ \simeq& \lim (\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(Y, X) \xrightarrow{\cdot p}\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(Y, X)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(Y, \lim (X \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(Y, 0) \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 3)

special case

3) $\implies$ 1)

Recall that $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(-,-)$ is additive in both variables. Hence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}[p^{-1}], X) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathrm{colim} (\mathbb{S} \xrightarrow{\cdot p} \mathbb{S}), X) \\ \simeq& \lim (\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}, X) \xrightarrow{\cdot p}\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathbb{S}, X)) \\ \simeq& \lim (X \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X) \end{align*}
