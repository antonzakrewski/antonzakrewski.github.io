group of a representation of a ring as module

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring, $\varphi: R \rightarrow \mathrm{End}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M)$ a representation for an abelian group Then $M$ is an \(R\)-module with

\begin{align*} \sigma: R \times M \rightarrow& M \\ (r, m) \mapsto& (\varphi(r))(m) \end{align*}

Proof
