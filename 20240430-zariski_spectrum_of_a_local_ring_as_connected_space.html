zariski spectrum of a local ring as connected space

Proposition

Let $R$ be a local ring, $\mathrm{Spec}(R)$ the zariski spectrum with the Zariski topology. Then $\mathrm{Spec}(R)$ is a connected space.

Proof

Suppose there exist disjoint closed subsets

\begin{align*} V(\mathfrak{a}) \sqcup V(\mathfrak{b}) \end{align*}

Then it follows that $\mathfrak{m} \in V(\mathfrak{a})$. But then we conclude that $V(\mathfrak{b}) = \emptyset$, as otherwise $\mathfrak{m} \in V(\mathfrak{b})$

Therefore $V(\mathfrak{a}) = \mathrm{Spec}(R)$.
