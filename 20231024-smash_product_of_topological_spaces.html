smash product of topological spaces

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T}_X, x_0)$ and $(Y, \mathcal{T}_Y, y_0)$ be based spaces. Then the smash product $X \ Y$ is defined as collapse

\begin{align*} X \wedge Y \clooneqq \left(X \times Y \right) / \left(X \vee Y \right) \end{align*}

for the wedge product $X \vee Y$, which is identified with the subspace $\{(x,y_0)\} \cup \{x_0,y\}$
