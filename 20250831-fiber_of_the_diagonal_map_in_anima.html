fiber of the diagonal map in anima

Proposition

Let $X \rightarrow X\times X$ be the diagonal morphism in Anima. Then for $(x,y) \in X$ the fiber is given by $\mathrm{map}(x,y)$

In particular for $(x,x)$ the fiber is given by the

\begin{align*} \mathrm{fib}(\Delta)_{x} = \Omega_x X \end{align*}
