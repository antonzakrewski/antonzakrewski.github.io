bifunctor preserving monomorphisms and componentwise preserving monomorphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_1,\mathcal{C}_2, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}_1 \times \mathcal{C}_1 \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

be a bifunctor

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ preserves monomorphisms
  2. $\mathcal{F}(A,-)$ and $\mathcal{F}(-,B)$ preserve monomorphisms for arbitrary objects $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C}_1), B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C}_2)$

Proof

dual to bifunctor preserving epimorphisms and preserving componentwise epimorphisms
