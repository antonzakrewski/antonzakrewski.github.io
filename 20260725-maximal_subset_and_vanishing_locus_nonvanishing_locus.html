maximal subset and vanishing locus / nonvanishing locus

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor and $F \subseteq \mathcal{O}(X)$ such that $(F) = \mathcal{O}(X)$. TFAE:

  1. $V(F) = \emptyset_X$, which is the functor given by $\emptyset_X(0) = X(0)$ and $\emptyset_X(R) = \emptyset$ else.
  2. $D(F) = X$.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
