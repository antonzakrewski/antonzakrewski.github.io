left exact functors between exact categories

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{E}$ be a exact categories, then a functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{E} \end{align*}

is said to be left exact, if it sends exact squares to pullbacks and zero object to zero objects.

I dont like the notion because it is, but useful for stabilization of an exact category via left exact functors and Spanier-Whitehead.
