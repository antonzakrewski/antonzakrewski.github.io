transitivity of flatness

Proposition

Let $A,B$ be rings, $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ a flat ring morphism and $M$ a flat \(B\)-module.

Then $M$ regarded as \(A\)-module via restriction of scalars is flat

Proof

follows from associativity of the tensor product for bimodules where

\begin{align*} (- \otimes_{A} M) \cong& (- \otimes_A (B \otimes_B M)) \\ \cong& ((- \otimes_A B) \otimes_B M) \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} ((- \otimes_A B) \otimes_B M) \end{align*}

is a composition of exact functors (cf. composition of exact functors as exact functor)
