affine coordinate ring as affine algebra

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be an algebraic set Then the coordinate ring is an affine k-algebra

Proof

finitely generated

follows as quotient algebra $K[X_1,...,X_n]/I(X)$

reduced

follows from vanishing ideal as radical ideal
