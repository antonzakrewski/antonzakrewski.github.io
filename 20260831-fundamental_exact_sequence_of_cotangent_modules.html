fundamental exact sequence of cotangent modules of ring homomorphisms

Proposition

Let $R \rightarrow S \rightarrow T$ be ring-homomorphisms Then the induecs equence of $T$ modules

\begin{align*} \Omega_{S/R} \otimes_{S} T \rightarrow \Omega_{T/R} \rightarrow \Omega_{T/S} \rightarrow 0 \end{align*}

is exact.

Here the first (second) map comes from the square on the left (right)

Proof

Let $\varphi: \Omega_{S/R} \otimes_{S} T \rightarrow \Omega_{T/R}$ be the map as above, then we need to show that $\mathrm{coker}(\varphi)$ is isomorphic to $\Omega_{T/S}$. Let $M \in \mathrm{Mod}_T$, then we hvae

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{T}(\mathrm{coker}(\varphi), M) \cong& \{T \textrm{ linear maps} \Omega_{T/R} \rightarrow M \vert \} \\ \cong& \{\(R\) \textrm{ derivations} \partial: T \rightarrow M \textrm{ such that } \partial_{\vert S} = 0\} \\ \cong& \mathrm{Der}_{S}(T,M)d \end{align*}

where R-derivation is R_0-derivation with kernel shows the last claim.
