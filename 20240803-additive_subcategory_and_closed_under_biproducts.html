additive subcategory and closed under biproducts

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, $C \hookrightarrow A$ a full subcategory TFAE:

  1. $C$ is an additive category
  2. the zero object $0 \in \mathcal{C}$ and the biproducts of $\mathcal{A}$ are inherited
  3. the zero object $0 \in \mathcal{C}$ and there exists biproducts

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

note that biproducts are characterized by some morphisms, which are inherited: biproduct in an ab-enriched category determined by morphisms

2) $\implies$ 3)

special case

3) $\implies$ 1)
