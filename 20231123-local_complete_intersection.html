local complete intersection

Definition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a morphism of schemes. Then $f$ is said to be a local complete intersection, if for every geometric point $y \in \vert Y \vert$, there are affine open subschemes $V \subseteq Y$ and $U \subseteq X$ with $v \in \vert Y \vert$ and $f(V) \subseteq U$ such that $f_{\vert V}$ factors as $V \xhookrightarrow{\iota} \mathbb{A}^n_{U} \rightarrow U$ where $\iota$ is a regular closed immersion.
