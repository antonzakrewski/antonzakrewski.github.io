o.B.d.A. für $\angle AMB$ und $\angle BMC$: 

\begin{tikzpicture}

\node[right] (A) at (a) {A};
\node[above] (B) at (b) {B};
\node[left] (C) at (c) {C};

\node[above] (M) at (m) {M};
\foreach \n in {a,b,c,i}
    \node at (\n)[circle, fill, inner sep=1pt]{};
\draw (m) -- (a);

\end{tikzpicture}

Aufgrund des Gleichseitigen Dreieckes $\triangle AMB$ folgt: $m\angle BAM = m\angle MBA$

\begin{align*} 180^\circ =& m\angle BMC + m\angle AMB \\ 180^\circ =& 2 \cdot m\angle BAM + m\angle AMB \\ \Rightarrow m\angle BMC =& 2 \cdot m\angle BAM \end{align*}
