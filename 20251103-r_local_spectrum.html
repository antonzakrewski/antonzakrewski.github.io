R-local Anima

Definition

An anima $X$ is said to be \(R\)-local for a spectrum $R$ if for all maps $f: A \rightarrow B$ which become equivalences

\begin{align*} R(f)_*: R_*(A) \rightarrow R_*(B) \end{align*}

then the map

\begin{align*} f^*: \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(B, X) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(A,X) \end{align*}

is an equivalence
