ideal quotient as superset

Proposition

Let \(A\) be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ an ideal and $S \subseteq A$ a subset Then the ideal relative to a subset is a superset

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a}_S \supseteq \mathfrak{a} \end{align*}

Proof

For $\alpha \in \mathfrak{a}$ and $s \in S$ it follows by multiplicative closure that

\begin{align*} \alpha \cdot s \in \mathfrak{a} \end{align*}
