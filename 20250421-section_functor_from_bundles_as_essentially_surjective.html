section functor from bundles as essentially surjective

Proposition

The section functor from Bundles restricted to category of finitely generated projective modules on a compact hausdorff space is essentially surjective

Proof

Let $P$ be a projective module over the ring of continuous functions. Then there exists an $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $P \oplus Q \simeq R^n$ for $R = C(X)$.

thus there exists an idempotent matrix $A \in M_n(C(X))$ finitely generated projective modules induces idempotent matrix with $\mathrm{im}(A) \simeq P$ under using section functor from Bundles as fully faithful we get a morphism of trivial bundles

\begin{align*} f: \mathbb{K}^n \times X \rightarrow \mathbb{K}^n \times X \end{align*}

(see: section functor on trivial bundles)

one can then show, that $f$ is a bundle map of locally constant rank with $\mathrm{im}(f) = E$
