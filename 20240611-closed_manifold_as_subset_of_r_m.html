closed topological manifold as subset of R^m

Proposition

Let $X$ be a closed topological manifold, then there exists a topological embedding

\begin{align*} X \hookrightarrow \mathbb{R}^m \end{align*}

for some $m \in \mathbb{N}$

Proof

Choose coordinate charts $U_i$. By compactness finitely many $U_1,...,U_n$ suffice such that $X = \bigcup U_i$.

Then let

\begin{align*} \varphi_i: X \rightarrow X/(X \setminus U_i) \cong U_+ \end{align*}

be the map to the collapse where $U_+$ is the one point compactification (cf. one point compactification of a subspace as collapse)
