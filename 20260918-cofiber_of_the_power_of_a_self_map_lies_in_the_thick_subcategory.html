cofiber of the power of a self-map lies in the thick subcategory

Proposition

Let $f: \Sigma^d X \rightarrow X$ be a self-map of degree $d$. then for each $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\mathrm{cofib}(f^n)$ lies in $\mathrm{Thick}(\mathrm{cofib}(f))$

Proof

Proof by induction

induction basis

for $n = 1$, this follows by assumption.

induction step

Assume that $\mathrm{cofib}(f^n)$ lies in $\mathrm{Thick}(\mathrm{cofib}(f))$. Then recall that we have a sequence $\Sigma^d X \xrightarrow{f} X \xrightarrow{f^n} \Sigma^{-d n} X$. Then cofiber sequence of cofibers of a composite shows that there exists a cofiber sequence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{cofib}(f) \rightarrow \mathrm{cofib}(f^{n+1}) \rightarrow \mathrm{cofib}(f^n) \end{align*}

and stable categories are closed under extension, hence $\mathrm{cofib}(f^{n+1})$ lies in $\mathrm{Thick}(\mathrm{cofib}(f))$.
