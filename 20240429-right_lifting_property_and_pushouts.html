right lifting property and pushouts

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a countably cocomplete $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a morphism and $X,Y,Z \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ objects. If $f$ has the right lifting property with respect to $g: W \rightarrow Z, h: W \rightarrow V$$ then $f$ has the right lifting property with respect to the pushout

\begin{align*} V \rightarrow V \coprod_W Z \end{align*}

Proof
