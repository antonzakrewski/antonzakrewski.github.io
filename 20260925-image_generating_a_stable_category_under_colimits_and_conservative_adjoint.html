image generating a presentable category under colimits and conservative adjoint

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a left adjoint between presentable categories TFAE:

  1. the right adjoint $\mathcal{G}$ is a conservative functor
  2. the smallest subcategory of $\mathcal{D}$ closed under colimits and containing the essential image of $\mathcal{F}$ is $\mathcal{D}$.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $\mathcal{D}' \subseteq \mathcal{D}$ be the smallest full subcategory containing the essential image and which is closed under colimits. Then there exists a regular cardinal $\kappa$ such that $\mathcal{F}$ preserves $\kappa$ compact objects and that $\mathcal{C}$ is generated by $\mathcal{C}^{\kappa}$. Hence $\mathcal{D}'$ is generated by the set $\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{C}^{\kappa})$. Note that $\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{C}^{\kappa})$ consists of $\kappa$ compact objects in $\mathcal{D}'$ and not only $\mathcal{D}$, since colimits in $\mathcal{D}'$ are computed in $\mathcal{D}$.

This shows that $\mathcal{D}$ is a presentable category and the inclusion $\mathcal{D}' \hookrightarrow \mathcal{D}$ admits a right adjoint and left adjoints compose, so we have a diagram

Here the functor $\mathcal{D}' \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is fully faithful and $\mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}'$ is conservative since the composition $\mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}' \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is conservative by assumption.

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $f: x \rightarrow y$ be a morphism in $\mathcal{D}$ such that $\mathcal{G}(f)$ is an equivalence Let $\mathcal{D}'$ be the full subcategory containing objects $z \in \mathcal{D}$ such that $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(z,x) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(z,y)$ is an equivalence. Then $\mathcal{D}'$ is closed under colimits and contains the essential image of $\mathcal{F}$, which implies $\mathcal{D}' = \mathcal{D}$. Therefore, a yoneda argument implies that $f$ is already an equivalence, which was to show.
