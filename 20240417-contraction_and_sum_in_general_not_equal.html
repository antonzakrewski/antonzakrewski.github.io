contraction and sum dont commute in general

Proposition

Let $A,B$ be a commutative ring, $\varphi: A \rightarrow B$ be a ring-homomorphism and $\mathfrak{b}_1,\mathfrak{b}_2 \subseteq B$ be ideals. Then the sum and contraction dont commute in general

Proof

Consider

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathbb{Z} \hookrightarrow Z[X] \end{align*}

Then $(x), (x + 1)$ are clearly coprime, thus

\begin{align*} ((x) + (x + 1))^{c} =& (\mathbb{Z}[X])^{c} \\ =& \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

But

\begin{align*} (x)^{c} =& (0) \\ (x+1)^{c} =& (0) \end{align*}
