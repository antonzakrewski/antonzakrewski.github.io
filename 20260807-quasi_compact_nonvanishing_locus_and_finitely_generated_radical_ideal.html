quasi-compact nonvanishing locus and finitely generated radical ideal

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $I$ a radical ideal TFAE:

  1. $I$ is a finitely generated radical ideal
  2. the nonvanishing locus $D(I)$ is quasi-compact

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from the standard zariski local epimorphism

2) $\implies$ 1)

Consider the Zariski-local epimorphism $\coprod_{\textrm{ finite subsets } G \subseteq F} D(G) \rightarrow D(F)$. Then we can find a finite zariski local epimorphism $\coprod_{i=1}^n D(G_i) \rightarrow D(F)$ which factors through $D(\bigcup G_i) \rightarrow D(F)$. Now this implies that $D(\bigcup G_i) \rightarrow D(F)$ is a Zariski-local epimorphism (cf. composition is a zariski local epimorphism implies left morphism is zariski local) , where $\bigcup G_i$ is a finite union of finite sets, hence also finite.
