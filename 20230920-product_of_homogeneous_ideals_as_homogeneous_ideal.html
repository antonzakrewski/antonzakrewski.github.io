product of homogeneous ideals as homogeneous ideal

Proposition

Let $M$ be a monoid and $R = \bigoplus_{m \in m} R_m$ a graded ring. Then for two homogeneous ideals $\mathfrak{a}, \mathfrak{b}$, the product of ideals

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a} \cdot \mathfrak{b} \end{align*}

is also homogeneous

Proof

follows from:

  • homogenous ideal and generation by homogeneous elements
  • product of homogeneous elements as homogeneous element
