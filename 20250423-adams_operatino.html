adams operation

Definition / Proposition

The adams operation on complex k-theory are defined as natural transformation

\begin{align*} \psi^k: K_{ \mathbb{C}}^0(-) \rightarrow K_{\mathbb{C}}^0(-) \end{align*}

for $k \in \mathbb{N}$ with following properties

  1. it is objectwise a ring-homomorphism
  2. $\psi^k \cdot \psi^l = \psi^{k \cdot l}$
  3. for a complex line bundle $L$ it holds $\psi^l[L] = L^l = \bigotimes L$
  4. $\psi^p(\alpha) \equiv \alpha^p \mod{p}$
  5. $\psi^k: K_{\mathbb{C}}^0(S^{2n}) \rightarrow K_{ \mathbb{C}}^0(S^{2n})$ is defined as multiplication with $\mathbb{C}^n$
