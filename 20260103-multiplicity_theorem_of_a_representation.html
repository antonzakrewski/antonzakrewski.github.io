multiplicity theorem of a representation

Proposition

Let $V$ be a finite dimensional representation of a finite group and $V_1$ an irreducible representation. Then

\begin{align*} n_1 =& (\Chi_{V_1}, \Chi_{V_2}) \\ =& \frac{1}{\vert G \vert } \sum_{g \in G} \overline{\Chi_{V_1}(g)} \Chi_{V_2}(g) \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of the first orthogonality theorem
