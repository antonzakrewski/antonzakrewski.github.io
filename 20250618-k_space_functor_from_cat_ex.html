connective K space functor from Cat Ex

Definition

The \(K\)-space functor is defined as as functor

\begin{align*} K: \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}^{\mathrm{Ex}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Sp}_{\geq 0} \simeq \mathrm{CGrp}(\mathrm{An}) \\ \mathcal{E} \mapsto& \Omega( \vert \mathrm{Span}(\mathcal{E}) \vert) \end{align*}
