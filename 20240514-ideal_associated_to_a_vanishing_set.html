ideal associated to a vanishing set

Definition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ the zariski spectrum with the Zariski topology. Suppose $T \subseteq \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ is a subset, then the ideal associated to $T$ is defined as

\begin{align*} I(T) =& \{f \in A \vert \forall \mathfrak{p} \in T \vert f \in \mathfrak{p}\} \\ =& \bigcap_{\mathfrak{p} \in T} \mathfrak{p} \end{align*}
