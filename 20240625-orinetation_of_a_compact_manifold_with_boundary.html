orientation of a compact manifold with boundary

Proposition

Let $W$ be a \(n+1\)-dimensional compact topological manifold with boundary. Then an R-Orientation for a ring \(R\) is canonically given by an element

\begin{align*} H_{n+1}(W, \partial W;R) \end{align*}

Proof

Note that the poset of open collar complements is cofinal (cf. cofinal poset of open collar complements for a compact manifold) showing

\begin{align*} H_p^{\mathrm{lf}}(\mathrm{int}(W);R) \coloneqq& \mathrm{lim}_{K \in \mathcal{K}^{\mathrm{op}}_{\mathrm{int}(W)}}(H_p(\mathrm{int}(W), \mathrm{int}(W \setminus K);R) \\ \cong& \mathrm{lim}_{K \in \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}_W} H_p(\mathrm{int}(W), \mathrm{int}(W \setminus K);R) \\ \end{align*}

using relative singular homology and excision theorem we get a natural transformation

\begin{align*} H_p( \mathrm{int}(W), \mathrm{int}(W \setminus K);R) \rightarrow H_p(W, W \setminus K;R) \end{align*}

Therefore

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_{K \in \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}_W} H_p(\mathrm{int}(W), \mathrm{int}(W \setminus K);R) \cong& \lim_{K \in \mathcal{C}_W^{\mathrm{op}}}(H_p(W, W \setminus K);R) \\ \cong& H_p(W, \mathrm{int}(W);R) \end{align*}
