cut as upper bound of a flow

Proposition

Let $N$ be a network, $f$ a flow and $S$ a cut of a network Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{val}(f) \leq c(S) \end{align*}

Proof

Note that

\begin{align*} c(S) =& \sum_{i \in E(S, V \setminus S)} c(e) \\ \geq& \sum_{i \in E(S, V \setminus S)} f(e) - \sum_{E(V \setminus S, S)} f(e) + \sum_{e \in E(S,S)} f(e) - \sum_{e \in E(S,S)} f(e) \\ =& \end{align*}
