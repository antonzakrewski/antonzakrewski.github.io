induced ringhomomorphism preserves roots

Proposition

Let \(R,S\) be rings and $\varphi: R \rightarrow S$ a ring-homomorphism. Suppose $f \in R[X]$ is a polynomial, $\alpha \in R$ a root and $\varphi': R[X] \rightarrow S[X]$ the induced ring homomorphism for the polynomial rings)

Then $\varphi(\alpha)$ is a root of $\varphi'(f)$

Proof

By Ausklammern von Nullstellen von Polynomen there we get

\begin{align*} f = (X - \alpha) g \end{align*}

Thus applying $\varphi$ we get

\begin{align*} \varphi(f) =& \varphi(X - \alpha) \varphi(g) \\ =& (X - \varphi(\alpha)) \cdot \varphi(g) \end{align*}

and evaluating at $\varphi(\alpha)$ gives

\begin{align*} \varphi(f)(\varphi(\alpha)) =& (\varphi(\alpha) - \varphi(\alpha)) \cdot \varphi(g)(\varphi(\alpha)) \\ =& 0 \cdot \varphi(g)(\varphi(\alpha)) \\ =& 0 \end{align*}
