excisive triad

Definition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $A,B \subseteq X$ subspaces. Then $(X,A,B)$ is said to be an excisive triad, if the canonical map

is a chain homotopy equivalence

where

\begin{align*} C^{\mathrm{sing}}(A) + \mathrm{C}^{\mathrm{sing}}(B) \coloneqq \mathrm{im}(C^{\mathrm{sing}}(A) \oplus \mathrm{C}^{\mathrm{sing}}(B) \rightarrow C^{\mathrm{sing}}(A \cup B)) \end{align*}
