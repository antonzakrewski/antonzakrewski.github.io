classification of open subfunctors of an algebraic functor

Proposition

Let $X$ be an algebraic functor, then there exists an isomorphism of partially ordered sets

\begin{align*} V: \{\texrm{ quasi-coherent radical ideals over } X\}^{\mathrm{op}} \simeq \{\textrm{ open subfunctors of } X\} \end{align*}

Proof

use that

  • closed immersions are closed under pullbacks

affine case

follows from classification of open subfunctors of affine schemes

general case

using the equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}}(\mathcal{P}(\mathrm{Aff}), \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{op}}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Aff}, \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{op}}) \end{align*}

(cf. colimit preserving functors out of a presheaf category). it suffices to show that both functors preserve colimit:

  1. the fuctor to poset of quasi-coherent ideals is the left kan extension from a presheaf category, hence admits a right adjoint
  2. the other functor is given by mapping $X$ to the full subcategory $\mathrm{AlgFun}_{/X}$ containing open immersions. Now open immersions are closed under pullbacks and hence they satisfy descent (cf. class of morphism stable under pullback in a topos satisfies descent). in other words, it is also colimit presreving
