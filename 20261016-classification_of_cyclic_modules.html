classification of cyclic modules

Proposition

Let $A$ be a ring and $M$ a cyclic module. Then there exists a left ideal $I$ such that $M \cong A/I$. Furthermore any module of the form $A/I$ is cyclic.

Proof

choose a generator $v$, then consider the map $A \rightarrow M$ which maps $1$ to $v$. It is surjective and the kernel is a submodule of $A$, i.e., a left ideal. Now the fundamental theorem for modules implies $A/I \cong M$.

Here it suffices to notice that $1$ is a generator.
