non degenerate, skew symmetric bilinear form between finite dimensional vector spaces

Proposition

Let $I: V, \otimes_K V \rightarrow K$ be a non degenerate, antisymetric Bilinearform on a finite dimensional vector space $V$ over a field $K$. Then $V$ is even dimensional

Proof

Then

\begin{align*} I(x,y) = x^t \cdot A \cdot y \end{align*}

for some quadratic matrix $A$

Then

\begin{align*} x^t \cdot A \cdot y =& - y^t A \cdot x \\ =& - x^t A^t y \end{align*}

showing $A = - A^t$.

Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{det}(A) =& \mathrm{det}(-A^t) \\ =& (-1)^{\mathrm{dim}(V)} \mathrm{det}(A) \end{align*}

showing that $\mathrm{dim}(V)$ is even
