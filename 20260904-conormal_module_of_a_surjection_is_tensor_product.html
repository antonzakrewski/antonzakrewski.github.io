conormal module of a surjection is tensor product

Proposition

Let $S \twoheadrightarrow S'$ be a surjection of commutative rings Then $I^2/I$ is isomorphic to $S' \otimes_{S} I$ as module.

Proof

use the short exact sequence $I \rightarrow S \rightarrow S /I$ and tensor it with $I$ to get the cokernel
