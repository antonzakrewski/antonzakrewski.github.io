colimit of a connected constant diagram

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a connected category, $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and

\begin{align*} \underline{A}: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

a constant diagram. Then

\begin{align*} \colim_{\underline{A}} = A \end{align*}

Proof
