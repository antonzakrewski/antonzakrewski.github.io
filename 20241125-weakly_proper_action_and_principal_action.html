weakly proper action and principal action

Proposition

Let $G$ be a topological group, $E$ a topological space and $E \times G \rightarrow E$ a weakly proper action.

TFAE:

  1. the action is a principal action
  2. there exists an open cover $U_i$ of $E/G$, such that $p^{-1}[U_i] \rightarrow U_i$ admits sections
