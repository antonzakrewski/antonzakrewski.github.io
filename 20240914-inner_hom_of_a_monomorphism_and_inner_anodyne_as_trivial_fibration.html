inner hom of a monomorphism and inner anodyne as trivial fibration

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ an inner fibration $\iota: A \rightarrow B$ be an inner anodyne map. Then the map

\begin{align*} \langle f,\iota\rangle: X^B \rightarrow X^A \times_{Y^A} Y^B \end{align*}

is a trivial fibration

Proof

follows analogously to

using:

  • inner hom of a monomorphism and inner fibration
