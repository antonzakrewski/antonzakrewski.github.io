relative concrete category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories. Then $\mathcal{C}$ with a functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is said to be relative concrete to $\mathcal{D}$, if

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

is a faithful functor
