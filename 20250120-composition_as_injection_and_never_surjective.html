composition as injection and never surjective

Proposition

Let

be maps between sets

Assume that

  1. $g \circ f$ is injective
  2. $g$ is not injective.

Then $f$ is not surjective

Proof

By assumption 2) there exist different elements $b_1,b_2 \in B$ such that

\begin{align*} g(b_1) = g(b_2) \end{align*}

Suppose $f$ is surjective: Then choose preimages $a_1,a_2$ with $f(a_i) = b_i$. Note that $a_1 \neq a_2$ since $f(a_1) \neq f(a_2)$

This shows a contradiction to $g \circ f$ being injective, as

\begin{align*} (g \circ f)(a_1) = (g \circ f)(a_2) \end{align*}
