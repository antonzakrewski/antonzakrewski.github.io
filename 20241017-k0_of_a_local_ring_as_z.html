k0 of a local ring as Z

Proposition

Let $R$ be a local ring, then K0 is given by

\begin{align*} K_0(R) = \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • finitely generated projective module over a local ring as free module
  • local ring satisfies the IBP

alternatively

  • isomorphic projective modules and basechange to quotient with jacobson
