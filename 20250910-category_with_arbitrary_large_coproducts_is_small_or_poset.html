category with arbitrary large coproducts is poset

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, where we assume that there exists a cardinal $\kappa$ such that each mapping anima is \(\kappa\)-small. Suppose $\mathcal{C}$ admits coproducts, which are $\kappa$ large.

Then each mapping anima is either contractible or empty

Proof

Assume that there exist $x,y\in \mathcal{C}$ with

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \not\simeq \begin{cases} * \\ \emptyset \end{cases} \end{align*}

Then consider $\coprod_{\kappa} x$ which by assumption exists. Furthermore

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\coprod_{\kappa} x, y) \simeq \prod_{\kappa} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x, y) \end{align*}

where the LHS is by assumption $\kappa$ small and the RHS is (e.g. by simplicial set analysis) larger than $\kappa$.
