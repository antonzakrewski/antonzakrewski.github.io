zero sheaf

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $X$ a topological space. Then the zero sheaf is defined as sheaf

\begin{align*} 0: \mathrm{Op}(X)^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathcal{A} \\ O \mapsto& 0 \\ (\mathrm{res}: O \rightarrow U) \mapsto& 0: 0 \rightarrow 0 \end{align*}
