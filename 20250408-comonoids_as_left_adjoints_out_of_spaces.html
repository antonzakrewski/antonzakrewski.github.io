Comonoids as left adjoints out of Anima-valued Monoids

Proposition

There exists a natural equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}}(\mathrm{Mon}_{E_{\infty}}(\mathrm{An}), \mathcal{D}) \simeq \mathrm{coMon}_{\mathrm{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

see:

  • infinity category of cocartesian infinity comonoids

Proof

corollary of:

  • adjunction of left adjoints and Monoids
  • left adjoints out of Anima

where

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}}(\mathrm{Mon}_{E_{\infty}}(\mathrm{An}), \mathcal{D}) \simeq& \mathrm{coMon}(\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}}(\mathrm{An}, \mathcal{D})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{coMon}(\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}
