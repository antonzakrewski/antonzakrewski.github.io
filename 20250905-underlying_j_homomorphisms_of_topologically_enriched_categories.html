underlying j-homomorphisms of topologically enriched categories

Definition

The underlying j-homomorphisms are defined as forgetful functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb{H}}^{\mathrm{fin}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb{C}}^{\mathrm{fin}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb{R}}^{\mathrm{fin}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Euc} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sph} \end{align*}

note that the last map is given on hom objects by the forgetful functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{BTop}(n) \rightarrow \mathrm{BG}(n) \end{align*}
