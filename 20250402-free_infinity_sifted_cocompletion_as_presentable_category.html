free infinity sifted cocompletion as presentable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category, then the free infinity sifted cocompletion

\begin{align*} \mathcal{P}_{\Sigma}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is a presentable category.

Proof

corollary of:

  • limit preserving functors to Anima as S-local objects
  • S-local objects of a presentable category as presentable category
