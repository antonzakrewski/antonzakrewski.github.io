radical of a homogeneous ideal as homogeneous ideal

Proposition

Let \(R = \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} R_n\) be a Z-graded ring. Let $I$ be a homogeneous ideal of $S$ and $J$ the radical

Then $J$ is also a homogeneous ideal

Proof

Let $x^n \in I$. Then consider the element $x_k \in R_k$ with the highest grading in $x \in \bigoplus R_n$. Then it follows that $x_k^n \in I$, as each other summand of $x = (x_k + ... )^n$ has degree less than $k$ and $I$ is homogoneous, hence $x_k^ \in I$. Then $x - x_k \in J$ so we may repeat this for the highest grading, which is smaller than $k$. Thus we terminate after finitely many steps and have shown, that $x = (x_k + ...)$ with each summand in $J$
