Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be small categories and

be a diagram.

Then the equalizer is given by the category containing

  1. objects $C \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ with $\mathcal{F}(C) = \mathcal{G}(C)$
  2. morphisms $f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Eq}}(C_1,C_2)$ with $\mathcal{F}(f) = \mathcal{G}(C)$

Proof

welldefined category

Associativity is inherited. Let $C,C' \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathrm{Eq})$

identity

Follows from

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(\mathrm{id}_{C}) =& \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{F}(C)} \\ =& \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{G}(C)} \\ =& \mathcal{G}(\mathrm{id}_{C}) \end{align*}

composition

Suppose $f,g \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathrm{Eq})$ with compatible domain/codomain. Then we get

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(f \circ g) =& \mathcal{F}(f) \circ \mathcal{F}(g) \\ =& \mathcal{G}(f) \circ \mathcal{G}(g) \\ =& \mathcal{G}(f \circ g) \\ \end{align*}

universal property

Suppose there exists a category $\mathcal{D}$ a fork

Then we define

\begin{align*} \mathcal{H}': \mathcal{D} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Eq} \\ A \mapsto& \mathcal{H}(A) \\ f \mapsto& \mathcal{H}(f) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Eq}$ is interpreted as subcategory.
