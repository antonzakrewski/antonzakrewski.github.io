ko spectrum

Definition

The $\mathrm{ku}$ spectrum is defined as commutative group completion of symmetric monoidal structure of Vect_K^fin (resp. their groupoid core)

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ko} =& (\coprod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathrm{BO}(n))^{\simeq} \\ =& (\coprod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathrm{BGL}(n))^{\simeq} \\ \end{align*}
