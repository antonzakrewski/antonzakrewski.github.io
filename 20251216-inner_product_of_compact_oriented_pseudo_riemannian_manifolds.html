inner product of compact, oriented pseudo-riemannian manifolds

Definition

Let $M$ be a compact, oriented pseudo riemannian manifold Then the inner product is defined as

\begin{align*} (-,-): \Omega^k \times \Omega^k \rightarrow& \mathbb{R} \\ \alpha, \beta \mapsto (\alpha, \beta) = \int_{M} \alpha \wedge * \beta \end{align*}
