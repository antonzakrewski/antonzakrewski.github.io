tensor product of chain complexes preserves trivial boundary complexes

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod})$ be the category of chain complexes Then the tensor product of chain complexes functor preserves trivial boundary chain complexes in $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod}) \times \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod})$

Proof

follows from the definition, where the differential is given by the sum of zero morphisms
