affine nonvanishing locus is covered by affines

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $D(F) \subseteq \mathrm{Spec}(R)$ an open subfunctor. Then $D(F)$ is covered by $(D(f) \subseteq D(F) )_{f \in F}$

Proof

follows from characterizations of an open covering of algebraic functors using e.g. $\mathrm{Rad}_{R}$
