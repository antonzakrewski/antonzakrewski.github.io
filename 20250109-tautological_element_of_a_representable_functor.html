tautological element of a represented functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ be a represented functor The tautological element of a represented functor is the element $c \in \mathcal{F}(c)$ corresponding to $\mathrm{id} \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(c,c)$
