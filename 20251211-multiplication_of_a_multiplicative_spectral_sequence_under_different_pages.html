multiplication of a multiplicative spectral sequence under different pages

Proposition

Assume that we have a multiplicative spectral sequence in $\mathrm{RMod}$. Then assume we have two elements $x,y$ in an \(n\)-paeg and their product of the $E_n$ page $x \cdot_{E_n} y$.

Suppose that $x,y$ survive to the $E_m$ page for $m > n$ in the sense that they lie in the kernel of the differentials and $x \neq_{E_m} 0, y \neq_{E_m} 0$.

Then $x \cdot_{E_n} y$ also lies in the kernel of the differentials and $x \cdot_{E_n} y = x \cdot_{E_m} y$ in the $E_m$ page ?

Proof
