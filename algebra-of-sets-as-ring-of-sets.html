algebra of sets as ring of sets

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{R} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)$ be an algebra of sets. Then $\mathcal{R}$ is also a ring of sets

Proof

note that $\emptyset = X^{c} \in \mathcal{R}$ where $X \in \mathcal{R}$ and $\mathcal{R}$ is closed under absolute complements. By assumption it is also closed under finite unions.
