proj localization at a prime ideal

Definition

Let \(R = \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0} R_n\) be a N-graded ring, $\mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm{Proj}(R)$ a homogeneous ideal. Then the proj localization $R_{(\mathfrak{p})}$ is localization at the contraction of a prime ideal $\mathfrak{p}' \subseteq R_0$
