colimit of a corepresentable functor as contractible Anima

Proposition

Let $F \in \mathrm{Fun}(K,\mathrm{Ani}), F \simeq \mathrm{map}_{K}(k,-)$ be a corepresentable infinity functor Then $\mathrm{colim} F$ is contractible

Proof

corollary of:

  • Lurie theorem about colimits in Anima:
  • unstraightening of a corepresentable functor admits initial object-
  • infinity category with initial object as weakly contractible category
