free infinity sifted cocompletion as generalized presheaf constructions for all simplicial sets relative to finite sets

Proposition

Let small infinity category with finite infinity coproducts

Then the free infinity sifted cocompletion is given as the simplified generalized presheaf construction $\mathcal{P}_{K}^{K'}(\mathcal{C})$ (Notation e.g. HTT Chapetr 5.3.6) for

  1. $K' = \emptyset$, $K = \{\mathrm{small filtered categories}\} \cup \{N(\Delta)^{\mathrm{op}}\}$
  2. $K' = \emptyset$, $K = \{\mathrm{small sifted categories}\}$
  3. $K' = \{\mathrm{finite set}\}$ (cf. discrete simplicial set) and $K = \{\mathrm{small simplicial sets}\}$

Proof
