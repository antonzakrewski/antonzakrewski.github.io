weak semiorthogonal decomposition

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an exact category and $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be full subcategories (not necessarily stable). Then $\mathcal{A} \perp \mathcal{B}$ is said to be a semiorthogonal decomposition if

  1. for every pair of objects $a \in \mathcal{A}, b\in \mathcal{B}$ we get $\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(a,b) = 0$
  2. for every object $x \in \mathcal{C}$ there exists a bifiber sequence

with $a \in \mathcal{A}, b \in \mathcal{B}$
