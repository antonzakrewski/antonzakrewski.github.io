infinity functor creating limits of a shape

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an infinity functor, $K$ be a collection of simplicial sets Then $f$ is said to create K-shaped limits if it

  1. preserves K-shaped limits
  2. reflects K-shaped limits
