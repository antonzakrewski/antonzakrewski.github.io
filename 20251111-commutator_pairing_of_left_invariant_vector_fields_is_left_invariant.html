commutator pairing of left invariant vector fields is left invariant

Proposition

Let $G$ be a lie group and $X,Y\in \mathfrak{X}(G)$ two left invariant vector fields. Then so is their commutator pairing

Proof

We need to show

\begin{align*} L_{g*}[X,Y] =& [L_{g*} X, L_{g*} Y] \\ =& [X,Y] \end{align*}
