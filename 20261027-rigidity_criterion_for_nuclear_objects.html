rigidity criterion for nuclear objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C} \in \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}L_{\mathrm{st}})$ be a compactly generated category with compact monoidal unit. Suppose that any trace class morphism between compact objects is the composiiton of two trace-class maps.

Then $\mathrm{Nuc}(\mathcal{C})$ is a rigid symmetric monoidal category.

Proof
