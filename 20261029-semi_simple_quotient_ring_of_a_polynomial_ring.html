semi-simple quotient ring of a polynomial ring

Proposition

Let $k$ be a field and $f \in k[x]$ a polynomial of the form $f = c \prod p_i^{e_i}$ for irreducible $p_i \in k[x]$, w.l.o.g. monic polynomial.

TFAE:

  1. $A$ is a semi-simple algebra
  2. $e_i = 1$ for all $i$

Proof

follows from:

  • characterization of semi-simple algebras & [[id:9e5ab4c7-a64b-4466-baf9-525412dd40c4][Rad of k[x]/f]]where $\mathrm{Rad}(A) = (\prod p_i)$ is zero if and only if $\prod p_i) = 0 \in A/f$ or $e_1 = 1$.
