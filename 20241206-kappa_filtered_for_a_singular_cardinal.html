kappa filtered for a singular cardinal

Proposition

Let $\lambda$ be a singular cardinal, $\mathcal{C}$ an infinity category. Assume the Axiom of choice

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ is \(\lambda\)-filtered
  2. $\mathcal{C}$ is \(\mathrm{succ}(\lambda)\)-filtered

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
