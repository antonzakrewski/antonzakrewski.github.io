product and equalizer imply existence of a limit

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $J: \mathcal{J} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a diagram and

  1. $\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{disc}}$ the discrete subcategory of $\mathcal{J}$ with the induced functor $J_{\mathrm{disc}}: \mathcal{J}' \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$
  2. $\mathcal{J}_{ \mathrm{morp}}$ the discrete category containing morphisms $f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{J}}(A,B)$ as objects for arbitrary $A,B \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{J})$
  3. the functor $J_{\mathrm{morp}: \mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{morp}}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ with $J_{\mathrm{morp}}: (f: A \rightarrow B) \rightarrow \mathcal{J}(B)$

Suppose that the categorical products for $J_{\mathrm{disc}}, J_{\mathrm{morp}}$ and equalizers exist Then the limit for $J$ exists.
