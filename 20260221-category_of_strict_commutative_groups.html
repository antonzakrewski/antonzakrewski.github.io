category of strict commutative groups

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category (with finite products). Then the category of strict commutative groups is defined as full subcategory of the functor category containing product preserving functors

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}(\mathrm{Ab}^{\mathrm{fin}, \mathrm{free}}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
