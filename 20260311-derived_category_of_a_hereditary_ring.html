object in the derived category of a hereditary ring splits

Proposition

Let $R$ be a hereditary ring and $\mathcal{D}(R)$ the derived category. Then each object $M \in \mathcal{D}(R)$ is given by

\begin{align*} \prod_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} H_n(M) \simeq \coprod{n \in \mathbb{Z}} H_n(M) \end{align*}

Proof

old proof

  • quasi isomorphism to homology for a chain complex over a hereditary ring
  • product and coproduct of shifted uniformly bounded module spectra

new and improved proof

  • object in a nice stable category without higher ext splits
