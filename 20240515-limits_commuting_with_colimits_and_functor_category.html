limits commuting with colimits and functor category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $I,J$ indexing categories, such that limits of the shape $I$ commute with colimits of the shape $J$. Then for another indexing category $L$ and the functor category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(L,\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

it follosw that \(I\)-shaped limits commute with \(J\)-shaped colimits.

Proof

corollary of

  • limit of functors for a complete domain
  • colimit of functors for a cocomplete codomain
