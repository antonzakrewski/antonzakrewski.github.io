projective space as noetherian space

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space with the zariski topology on the projective space Then $\mathbb{P}_k^n$ is a noetherian topological space

Proof

corollary of:

  • bijection from relevant, homogeneous ideals and projective algebraic sets
  • Hilbert's basis theorem for $K[X_0,X_1,...,X_n]$
