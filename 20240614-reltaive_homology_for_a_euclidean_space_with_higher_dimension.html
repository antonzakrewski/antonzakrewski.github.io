relative homology for a euclidean space with higher dimension

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{R}^n$ be the euclidean space, $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ a subspace Then the singular homology for $k > n$ is given by

\begin{align*} H_n(\mathbb{R}^n, K) = 0 \end{align*}

Proof

$k = n+1$
