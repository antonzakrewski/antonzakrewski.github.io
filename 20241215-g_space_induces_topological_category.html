G-Space induces topological category

Proposition

Let $G$ be a topological group, $X$ a G-Space. Then this defines a small topological category $G \curvearrowright X$ with

  1. $\mathrm{Ob}(G \curvearrowright X) = X$
  2. $\mathrm{Mor}(G \curvearrowright X) = G \times X$

and

  1. $\mathrm{id}: x \mapsto (1,x)$
  2. $\mathrm{domain}: (g,x) \mapsto (x)$
  3. $\mathrm{domain}: (g,x) \mapsto (g \cdot x)$
  4. $\circ: (h,gx) \circ (g,x) \mapsto (hg,x)$
