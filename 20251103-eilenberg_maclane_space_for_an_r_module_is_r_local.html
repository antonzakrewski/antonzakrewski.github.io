eilenberg maclane space for an R-module is R-local

Proposition

Let $R \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ be a subring and $V$ an \(R\)-module. Then the Eilenberg-MacLane space $K(V,n)$ is an R-local Anima.

Proof

follows as

  1. it is a simple anima (cf. 2-truncated simple anima and fundamental group
  2. recognition principle for R-local, simple anima
