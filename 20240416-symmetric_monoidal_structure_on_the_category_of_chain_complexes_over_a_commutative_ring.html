closed symmetric monoidal structure on the category of nonnegatively graded chain complexes of Modules over a commutative ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod and $\mathrm{Ch_*}_{\geq 0}(\mathrm{RMod})$ the category of nonnegatively graded chain complexes Then $\mathrm{Ch}_*(\mathrm{RMod})$ has a closed symmetric monoidal structure given by

  1. the tensor product of chain complexes functor as tensor functor of a monoidal category
  2. $R[0]$ as tensor unit
  3. the induced isomorphism by $M_n \otimes N_l \cong N_= \otimes M_n$ and the coproduct functor and further as isomorphism on chain complexes (cf.isomorphism of chain complexes and isomorphism of points)
  4. the hom chain complex functor as right adjoint
