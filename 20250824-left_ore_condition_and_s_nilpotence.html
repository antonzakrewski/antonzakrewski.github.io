left ore condition and S-nilpotence

Proposition

Let $R$ be an (\mathbb{E}_1)-ring spectrum and $S \subseteq \pi_*(R)$ be a homogeneous subset.

TFAE:

  1. $S$ satisfies the left ore condition
  2. for each $s \in S$ the R/Rs is a S-nilpotent module spectrum

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from R/Rs is S-nilpotent

2) $\implies$ 1)
