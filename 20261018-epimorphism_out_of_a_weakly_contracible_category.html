epimorphism out of a weakly contracible category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an epimorphism in category Cat If $\mathcal{C}$ is weakly contractible category, then so is $\mathcal{D}$.

Proof

use that $\vert \mathcal{F} \vert$ is an epimorphism and acyclic maps out of the point are trivial + classification of epimorphism in anima.
