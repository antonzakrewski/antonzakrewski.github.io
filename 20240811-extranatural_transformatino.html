extranatural transformation

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}, \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories,

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \times \mathcal{B} \times \mathcal{B}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathcal{D} \\ \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{A} \times \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathcal{D} \\ \end{align*}

be functors

an extranatural transformation $\alpha: \mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ consists of a family of natural transformation

\begin{align*} \alpha_{-,b,c}: \mathcal{F}(-,b,b) \rightarrow \mathcal{G}(-,c,c) \end{align*}

for $b \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{B}),c \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ such that

  1. it is extranatural in $b$, i.e. for $g: b_1 \rightarrow b_2$

commutes for $c \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ inside $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{D})$

  1. it is extranatural in $c$, i.e. for $g: c_1 \rightarrow c_2$

commutes for $b \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ inside $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{D})$
