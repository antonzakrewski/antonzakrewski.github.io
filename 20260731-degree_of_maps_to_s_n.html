degree of maps to S^n from a closed orientable manifold

Proposition

Let $M$ be a closed connected orientable manifold, then the degree of $D(M,S^n) \simeq \mathbb{Z}$

Proof

observe that there exists a map $[M,S^n] \rightarrow D(M,S^n)$ where we claim that $[M,S^n] \simeq [M,K(\mathbb{Z},n)]$. Here $\pi_n(S^n) \simeq \mathbb{Z}$ and $S^n$ is \(n-1\)-connceted, so we can attach $n+2$ cells and higher to kill $\pi_{n+1}(S^n)$ and higher. In particular, something like cellular approximation tells that $[M,S^n] \simeq [M,K(\mathbb{Z},n)]$.
