map out of cone as nullhomotopy

Proposition

Let $(X,\mathcal{T}_X)$ and $(Y,\mathcal{T}_Y)$ be topological spaces and $C(X)$ the Cone. Then a map

\begin{align*} f: C(X) \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

corresponds to a map

\begin{align*} \overline{f}: X \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

together with a nullhomotopy

\begin{align*} H: [0,1] \times X \rightarrow Y \end{align*}
