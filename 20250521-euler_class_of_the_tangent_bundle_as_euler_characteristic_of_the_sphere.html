euler class of the tangent bundle as euler characteristic of the sphere

Proposition

Let $S^n$ be the n-sphere Then the euler class of the tangent bundle is given by the euler characteristic of a sphere

\begin{align*} e(TS^n) =& \chi(S^n) \\ =& \begin{cases} 0 & \mbox{if } n = \mathrm{odd} \\ 2 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof
