abelian galois field extension of a polynomial and simple extension

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $f \in K[X]$ an irreducible separable polynomial, such that the splitting field

\begin{align*} L = K(f) \end{align*}

is an abelian field extension.

Then for a root $\alpha \in L$ of $f$ it holds, that

\begin{align*} L = K(\alpha) \end{align*}

is a simple field extension

Proof

By definition, $\mathrm{Gal}(L/K)$ is abelian and hence each subgroup is a normal subgroup. By Galoiskorrespondenz, since $\mathrm{Gal}(K(\alpha)/K)$ is a normal subgroup, it follows, that $K(\alpha)$ is a normal field extension. Hence by definition, $f$ splits over $K(f)$.
