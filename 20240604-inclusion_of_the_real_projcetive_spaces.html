standard inclusion of the real projective spaces

Definition

Let $k \leq n \in \mathbb{N}$ be numbers, then the standard inclusion of real projective spaces is defined as

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^k \hookrightarrow& \mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^n \\ [x_0:,...,x_k] \mapsto& [x_0,...,x_k,0,...,0] \end{align*}
