integral homology of BZ/nZ

Proposition

The integral homology of $B \mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z}$ is given by

\begin{align*} H_*(B \mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Z}) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } * = 0 \\ \mathbb{Z}/n \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } n \text{ is odd} \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
