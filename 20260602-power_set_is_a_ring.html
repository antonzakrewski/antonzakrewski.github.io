power set is a ring

Proposition

Let $X$ be a set, then the powerset $\mathcal{P}(X)$ is a commutative ring with $\Delta, \cap$ as adddition and multiplication respectively.

Proof

lawvere theory

Recall that the category of commutative $\mathbb{F}_2$ algebras $\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{LMod}_{\mathbb{F}_2})$ is equivalent to $\mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}((\mathbb{F}_2-\mathrm{CAlg}^{\mathrm{fin}, \mathrm{free}})^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Set})$. Now the power set is isomorphic to $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Set}}(X,\mathbb{F}_2)$ and $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Set}}(X,-)$ among others preserves finite product (cf. covariant representable functor preserves limits). Hence postcomposition with $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Set}}(X,-)$ induces a functor 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}((\mathbb{F}_2-\mathrm{CAlg}^{\mathrm{fin}, \mathrm{free}})^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Set}) \rar{\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Set}}(X,-)_*} & \mathrm{Fun}^{\prod}((\mathbb{F}_2-\mathrm{CAlg}^{\mathrm{fin}, \mathrm{free}})^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Set})  \\
 \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{LMod}_{\mathbb{F}_2}) \uar{\yo} \rar & \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{LMod}_{\mathbb{F}_2}) \uar{\yo}
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

In particular, the bottom horizontal functor maps $\mathbb{F}_2$ to $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Set}}(X,\mathbb{F}_2)$ and the addition/multiplication is given by the pointwise addition/multiplication. But these operations correspond to $\Delta$ and $\cap$ under the isomorphism $\mathcal{P}(X) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Set}}(X,\mathbb{F}_2)$.

monoidal

Let $\mathrm{LMod}_{\mathbb{F}_2}^{\otimes}$ be the Let $X$ be a set or equivalently the groupoid $\coprod_{x \in X} *$. Then the category $\mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{LMod}_{\mathbb{F}_2})$ admits a pointwise monoidal structure (cf. functor category inherits pointwise monoidal structure). Moreover, the constant diagram functor $\mathrm{LMod}_{\mathbb{F}_2} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{LMod}_{\mathbb{F}_2})$ is strong monoidal with respect to these monoidal structures (cf. constant diagram functor is strong monoidal functor, ). Then the limit functor is lax monoidal so we have a lax monoidal functor

\begin{align*} \prod_{x \in X} -: \mathrm{LMod}_{\mathbb{F}_2} \rightarrow& \mathrm{LMod}_{\mathbb{F}_2} \\ V \mapsto& \prod_{x \in X} V \end{align*}

Thus we get a functor

\begin{align*} \prod_{x \in X}: \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{LMod}_{\mathbb{F}_2}) \rightarrow \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{LMod}_{\mathbb{F}_2}) \end{align*}

which maps the commutative $\mathbb{F}_2$ algebra $\mathbb{F}_2$ to $\prod_{x \in X} \mathbb{F}_2 \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Set}}(X, \mathbb{F}_2) \simeq \mathcal{P}(X)$

The addition/multiplication is then given by the pointwise addition/multiplication. But these operations correspond to $\Delta$ and $\cap$ under the isomorphism $\mathcal{P}(X) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Set}}(X,\mathbb{F}_2)$.
