p-completion of the p-prüfer Eilenberg-MacLane spectrum

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{Z}[p^{-1}]/\mathbb{Z}$ be the p-prüfer group, regarded as spectrum. Then the derived p-completion is given by shifted p-adic integers $\mathbb{Z}_p^{\wedge}[1]$

Proof

consider the fiber sequence

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}[p^{-1}] \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}[p^{-1}]/\mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

then p-completion of spectra is exact, so this results in a cofiber sequence

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Z}_p^{\wedge} \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}[p^{-1}]_{p}^{\wedge} \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}[p^{-1}]/\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{\wedge} \end{align*}

where the middle term vanishes (cf. R-local spectrum and p-completion). Hence $\mathbb{Z}[p^{-1}]/\mathbb{Z}_{p}^{\wedge} \simeq \mathbb{Z}_p^{\wedge}[1]$.
