inclusion for a chain cone naturally nullhomotopic

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes, $C_*,D_*$ chain complexes and $\varphi: C_* \rightarrow D_*$ a chain map. Then for the cone and the inclusion for a chain cone $\iota: D_* \rightarrow \mathrm{cone}(\varphi)$ the composition

\begin{align*} \iota \circ \varphi: C_* \rightarrow \mathrm{cone}(\varphi) \end{align*}

is naturally nullhomotopic
