open subgroup of a topological group is also closed

Proposition

Let $G$ be a topological group and $H$ an open subgroup Then $H$ is also closed.

Proof

Let $g \not \in H$, then we want to show that there exists an open neighborhood of $g$ disjoint from $H$. For this consider $g \cdot H = \{g \cdot h \vert h \in H\}$, which is open as multiplication with $g$ is a homeomorphism. Moreover, it is disjoint: if $g \cdot h = h'$ for $h,h' \in H$, then this would imply g = h' \cdot h^{-1} \in H$, which is a contradiction to our assumption.
