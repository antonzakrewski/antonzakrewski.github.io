explicit formula for the n-th catalan number

Proposition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, then the \(n\)-th catalan number is given by

\begin{align*} B_n = \frac{1}{n+1} \binom{2n}{n} \end{align*}

Proof

Let $B = (B_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \in \mathbb{N}[ [X]]$. Then since $B_0 = 1$ we get

\begin{align*} B =& 1 \cdot X^0 + \sum_{n \geq 1} B_n X^n \\ =& 1 + \sum_{n\geq 1} ( \sum_{k=1}^n B_{k-1} B_{n-k}) X^n \end{align*}

where the second step follows from the recursion formula for the catalan number.

Then by index shift we get

\begin{align*} \sum_{n\geq 1} ( \sum_{k=1}^n B_{k-1} B_{n-k}) X^n =& \sum_{n\geq 1} ( \sum_{k=0}^n B_{k} B_{(n-1)-k}) X^n \end{align*}

or

\begin{align*} 1 + \sum_{n\geq 1} ( \sum_{k=1}^n B_{k-1} B_{n-k}) X^n =& 1 + \sum_{n\geq 1} ( \sum_{k=0}^n B_{k} B_{(n-1)-k}) X^n \\ =& 1 + \sum_{n \geq 1} (B^2)_{n-1} \cdot X^n \end{align*}

where we use $\sum_{k=0}^n B_{k} B_{(n-1)-k} = (B^2)_{n-1}$ or

Now by index shifting this gives

\begin{align*} 1 + \sum_{n \geq 1} (B^2)_{n-1} \cdot X^n = 1 + \sum_{n \geq 0} B^2_n X^n+1 =& 1 + X \cdot \sum_{n \geq 1} (B^2)_{n-1} \cdot X^n = 1 + \sum_{n \geq 0} B^2_n X^n \\ =& 1 + X \cdot B^2 \end{align*}

Now let

\begin{align*} C = X \cdot B = (0,B_0,B_1,...) \end{align*}

Then we get in particular $C_{n+1} = B_n$

Now using $C = X \cdot B$ and $B = 1 + X B^2$ we get

\begin{align*} B =& 1 + B^2 && \vert \cdot X \\ C =& X + C^2 C^2 - C =& - X \\ \end{align*}

and do <quadratische ergänzung> to get

\begin{align*} 4C^2 - 4C =& -4X \\ 4C^2 - 4C + 1 =& 1 - 4X \\ (2C - 1)^2 =& 1 - 4X \\ \end{align*}

where we use that the binomial formulas hold in any ring Now use

\begin{align*} (2C - 1)^2 =& 1 - 4X \\ \end{align*}

which shows that

\begin{align*} (2C - 1) = \pm (1 - 4X)^{\frac{1}{2}} \end{align*}

using the formal binomial coefficient theorem for power series ring we get

\begin{align*} ((1 - 4X)^{\frac{1}{2}})_n =& \binom{\frac{1}{2}}{n} \cdot (-4)^n \\ =& \frac{\frac{1}{2}^{\overline{n}}{n!} (-4)^n \\ =& \frac{1}{n!} \prod_{i=0}^n-1} (\frac{1}{2} - i) \cdot (-4)^n \\ =& \frac{1}{n!} \prod_{i=0}^n-1} (\frac{1}{2} - i) \cdot (-4)^n \\ =& \frac{1}{n!} \prod_{i=0}^n-1} (\frac{1}{2} - i) \cdot (-2)^n \cdot (2)^n \\ =& \frac{1}{n!} \prod_{i=0}^n-1} (2i - 1) \cdot (2)^n \\ =& \frac{1}{n!} \prod_{i=0}^n-1} (2i - 1) \cdot (2)^n \cdot \frac{(n-1)!}{(n-1)!}\\ =& - \frac{2}{n!} \frac{(2n-2)!}{(n-1)!} \\ =& - \frac{2}{n} \binom{2n-2}{n-1} \end{align*}

Hence we get

\begin{align*} 2C - 1 = -\sqrt{(1 - 4X)} \end{align*}

therefore we get

\begin{align*} C = \frac{1}{2} (1 - \sqrt{(1 - 4X)}) \end{align*}

or (for $n \geq 1$)

\begin{align*} C_n = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \binom{2n-2}{n-1} \end{align*}

or

\begin{align*} C_{n+1} = B_n = \frac{1}{n+1} \binom{2n}{n} \end{align*}
