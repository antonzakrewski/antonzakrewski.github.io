semiorthogonal decomposition criterion

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A} \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ be a stable subcategory.

a)

TFAE:

  1. the inclusion $\mathcal{A} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$ admits a left adjoint
  2. $(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{A}^{\perp}$ forms a semiorthogonal decomposition see: right orthogonal for a stable subcategory

b)

Proof

a)

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{A}$ denote the right adjoint We have to show that $\mathrm{Fun}_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{A}^{\perp}}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{C})$ consisting of arrows $a \rightarrow a^{\perp}$ is equivalent to $\mathcal{C}$ via the fiber functor. Here we have that this functor is fully faithful (cf. left orthogonal subcategory and fully faithful functor). So it remains to show that this is essentially surjective (cf. fully faithful essentially surjective functor is an equivalence).

Now use the unit and its cofiber to get a bifiber sequence

\begin{align*} c \rightarrow \iota(\mathcal{R}(c)) \rightarrow \mathrm{cofib}(\eta(c)) \end{align*}

By left bousfield localization of a stable category induces a orthogonal category the cofiber lies in $\mathcal{A}^{\perp}$.

Hence we may take $\iota(\mathcal{R}(c)) \rightarrow \mathrm{cofib}(\eta(c)) \in \mathrm{Fun}_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{A}^{\perp}}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{C})$

2) $\implies$ 1)

By assumption $\mathcal{C} \simeq \mathrm{Fun}_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{A}^{\perp}}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{C})$ is an equivalence and the inclusion $\iota: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is given by

\begin{align*} \mathcal{A} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Ar}_{\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{A}^{\perp}}(\mathcal{C}) a \mapsto& (a \rightarrow 0) \end{align*}

Thus a left adjoint is given by projection to the first factor.
