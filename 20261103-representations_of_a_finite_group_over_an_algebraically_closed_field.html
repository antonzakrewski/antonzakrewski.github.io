amount of representations of a finite group over an algebraically closed field

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{char}(k) \mid \vert G \vert$ and $k = \overline{k}$ algebraically closed. Then the number of isomorphism classes of simple representations of $G$ is equivalent to the number of conjugation classes.

Proof

follows from:

  • $\mathrm{dim}(CK(G,k)) = # \{\mathrm{conj classes of} G \}$
