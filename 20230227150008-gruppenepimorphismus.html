group epimorphism

Definition

Ein Gruppenepimorphismus ist ein surjektiver Gruppenhomomorphismus

see also:

  • epimorphism of groups as surjective

Legacy Notes

group epimorphism can also be interpreted as categorical epimorphism in category Group. It is clear, that a surjective groupmorphism is an epimorphism in $\mathrm{Grp}$. It turns out, that the converse is also true (cf. epimorphism in Group as surjection)
