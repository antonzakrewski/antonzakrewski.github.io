delta functor as half exact functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, $\{\mathcal{F}_n: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}\}$ a delta functor. Then each $\mathcal{F}_n$ is half exact

Proof

follows from the definition, as a short exact sequence

gets sent to an exact sequence
