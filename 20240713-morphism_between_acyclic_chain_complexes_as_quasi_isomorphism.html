morphism between acyclic chain complexes as quasi isomorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes Suppose $C_*,D_*$ are acyclic chain complexes, then each morphism $f: C_* \rightarrow D_*$ is a quasi-isomorphism

Proof

follows from the fact, that the only morphism between the zero objects is an isomorphism
