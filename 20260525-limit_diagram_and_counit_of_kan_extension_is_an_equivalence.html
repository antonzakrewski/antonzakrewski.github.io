limit diagram and unit of right kan extension is an equivalence

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category with enough/$I$ shaped limits and $I^{\triangleright} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a diagram TFAE:

  1. $I^{\triangleright} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is a limit diagram
  2. the unit $I^{\triangleright} \rightarrow \mathrm{lim} I$ is an equivalence
