p-group fixed point lemma

Proposition

Let $G$ be a p-group acting on a finite set $X$. Then $\vert X \vert \equiv \vert X^{G} \vert \mod{p}$.

Proof

decompose $X$ into orbits (cf. g-set as coproduct of orbits). Then mod $p$, any orbit of a non-fixed point has order divisible by $p$ (cf. orbit isomorphic to group modulo stabilizer).
