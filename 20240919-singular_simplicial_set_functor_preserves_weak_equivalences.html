singular simplicial set functor preserves weak equivalences

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a weak homotopy equivalence, then $\mathrm{Sing}(f)$ is a weak equivalence between simplicial sets - even a homotopy equivalence

Proof

By covariant weak yoneda it suffices to show that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sing}(f): [Z, \mathrm{Sing}(X)] \rightarrow [Z, \mathrm{Sing}(Y)] \end{align*}

is an isomorphism

Note that by

  1. simplicial maps up to homotopy as coequalizer
  2. continuous maps up to homotopy as coequalizer

and the sing adjunction we get

By geometric realization of a simplicial set as cw complex and the general whitehead theorem we conclude that

\begin{align*} f^*: [\vert Z \vert, X] \rightarrow [ \vert Z \vert, Y] \end{align*}

is an isomorphism

But the adjunction gives a natural isomorphism of the coequalizer diagrams, hence we have

\begin{align*} [Z, \mathrm{Sing}(X)] \cong [\vert Z \vert, X] \cong [ \vert Z \vert, \mathrm{Sing}(Y)] \cong [Z, \mathrm{Sing}(Y)] \end{align*}
