regular local and quotient

Proposition

Let $(A, \mathfrak{m})$ be a regular local ring of dimension $n > 0$ and $f \in A$. TFAE:

  1. $A/(f)$ is a regular local ring of dimension $n-1$
  2. $f \in \mathfrak{m} \setminus \mathfrak{m}^2$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

observe that we may assume that $f \not\in A^{\times}$ or equivalently $f \in \mathfrak{m}$ since the zero ring has dimension $- \infty$. Now suppose that $f \in \mathfrak{m}^2$, then $(\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2)/(f)$ is isomorphic to $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$ and hence has dimension $n$, i.e. $A/(f)$ cannot be a regular local ring.
