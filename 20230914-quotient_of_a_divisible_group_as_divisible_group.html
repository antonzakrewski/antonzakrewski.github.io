quotient of a divisible group is a divisible group

Proposition

Let $(G,\cdot)$ be a divisible group and $N \trianglelefteq G$ a normal subgroup. Then the quotient group $G/N$ is also a divisible group

Proof

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}^+$ and $g + N \in G/N$. Then by assumption, there exists a $h \in G$ such that $h^n = g$ Thus

\begin{align*} (h + N)^n =& h^n + N \\ =& g + N \end{align*}
