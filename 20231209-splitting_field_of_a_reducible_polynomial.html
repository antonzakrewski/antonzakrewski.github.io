splitting field of a product of polynomials

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $f \in K[X]$ a polynomial with $f = g_1 \cdot g_2$. Then for the splitting fields, it holds

\begin{align*} K(f) =& K(g_1)(g_2) \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of:

  • splitting of a polynomial and splitting of a product
  • and minimality of $K(f), K(g_1)(g_2)$
