compact object

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $C \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$. Then $C$ is said to be a compact object, if the induced morphism for a small filtered diagram

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim} \mathrm{Hom}(C,-) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}(C,\mathrm{colim}(-)) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism resp. Bijection
