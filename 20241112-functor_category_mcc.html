functor category of marked cocartesian fibrations

Definition

Let $p: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a cocartesian fibration and $K$ a marked simplicial set Then the functor category of marked cocartesian fibrations is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{mmc}}(K, \mathcal{E}) \end{align*}

containing morphisms which send a \(1\)-simplex of $K$ to a p-cocartesian morphism in $\mathcal{E}$
