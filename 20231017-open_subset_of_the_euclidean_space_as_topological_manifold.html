open subset of the euclidean space is a smooth manifold

Proposition

Let $O \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ be open, then $O$ is a \(n\)-dimensional Manifold

Proof

follows from $\iota: O \rightarrowtail \ioto[O] = O \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ as chart
