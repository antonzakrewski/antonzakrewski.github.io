pi_n of a map of sphere spectra

Proposition

Let $A: \bigoplus \mathbb{S} \rightarrow \bigoplus \mathbb{S}$ be a map of spectra Then $\pi_n(A)$ identifies with

\begin{align*} A: \bigoplus \pi_n(A) \rightarrow \bigoplus \pi_n(A) \end{align*}

Proof

follows as homotopy functor is an additive functor from spectra.
