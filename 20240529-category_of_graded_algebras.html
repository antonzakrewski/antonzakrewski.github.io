category of graded algebras

Definition

Let $M$ be a monoid and $R = \bigoplus_{m \in m} R_m$ a graded ring. Then the category of graded-commutative Algebras is defined as coslice category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{GCRing}_M \downarrow R \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{GRing}_M$ is the category of graded rings
