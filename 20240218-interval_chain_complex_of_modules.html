interval chain complex of modules

Definition

Let $R$ be a ring, $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod})$ be the category of chain complexes Then the interval chain complex $I$ is defined as

where

\begin{align*} I_n =& \begin{cases} 0 & \mbox{if } n \geq 2 \\ R & \mbox{if } n = 1 \\ R \otimes R & \mbox{if } n = 0 \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} d_1: R \rightarrow& R \otimes R \\ r \mapsto (r, -r) \end{align*}
