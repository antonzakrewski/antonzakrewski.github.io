epimorphisms in functor categories

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a category with pushouts and $I$ a small category and $\eta: \mathcal{F}_1 \Rightarrow \mathcal{F}_2$ a natural transformation. TFAE:

  1. $\eta$ is an epi in the functor category
  2. $\eta(i)$ is an epimorphism for all objects $i \in I$

Proof

pointwise calculation of colimits
