boundary map of cellular chain complexes

Proposition

Let $(X, \emptyset, \mathrm{SK}_n(X))$ be a CW-Complex and $n \geq 2$. Then the differential of the cellular chain complexes is given by

\begin{align*} (\varphi_n)_*: \bigoplus_{J_n} \mathbb{Z} \cong H_{n-1}(\coprod_{J_n} S^{n-1}) \rightarrow H_{n-1}(\bigvee_{J_{n-1}} S^{n-1}) \cong \bigoplus_{J_{n-1}) \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

Proof
