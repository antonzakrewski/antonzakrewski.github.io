infinite free commutative monoid functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a \(\kappa\)-presentable infinity category The forgetful functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathrm{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

admits a left adjoint, the free commutative monoid functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_\infty}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of:

  • limit in the category of commutative monoids
  • colimits in the category of commutative monoids for a presentable category
  • left adjoint functor theorem for presentable categories
