intersection of radical/prime ideals as radical ideal

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $\mathfrak{p}_i \subseteq R$ prime ideals/radical. Then the intersection

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{r} \coloneqq \bigcap_{i \in I} \mathfrak{p}_i \end{align*}

is a radical ideal

Proof

Suppose there exists an $p \in \mathfrak{r}$ and a $a \in R$ such that $a^n = p$ for $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then for any $\mathfrak{p}_i$ we conclude, that $a^{n-1} \cdot a \in \mathfrak{p}$ and hence by induction $a \in \mathfrak{p}_i \forall i \in I$ and thus $a \in \mathfrak{r}$
