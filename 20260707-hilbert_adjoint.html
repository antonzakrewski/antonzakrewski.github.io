hilbert adjoint

Definition

Let $\mathcal{H}_1, \mathcal{H}_2$ be Hilbert spaces and $T \in \mathrm{BL}(\mathcal{H}_1, \mathcal{H}_2)$. Then the hilbert adjoint $T^{*}$ is defined as composition

Remark

for any $x_2 \in \mathcal{H}_2$, the image $T^*(x_2)$ is the unique $x_1 \in \mathcal{H}_1$ such that

\begin{align*} \langle x_2, T(y_1)\rangle = \langle x_1, y_1\rangle \end{align*}
