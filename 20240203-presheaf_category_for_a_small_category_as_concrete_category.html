presheaf category for a small category as concrete category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category and $\mathrm{Psh}(\mathcal{C}) = \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Set})$ the category of presheaves (functor category). Then there exists a faithful functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{Set}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Set} \\ (\mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}) \mapsto& \coprod_{c \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})} \mathcal{G}(c) \\ (\eta: \mathcal{G}_1 \rightarrow \mathcal{G}_2) \mapsto& (\coprod \eta(c): \coprod_{c \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})} \mathcal{G}_1(c) \rightarrow \coprod_{c \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})} \mathcal{G}_2(c) ) \end{align*}
