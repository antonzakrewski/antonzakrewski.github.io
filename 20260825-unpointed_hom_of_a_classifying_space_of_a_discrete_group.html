unpointed Hom of a classifying space of a discrete group

Proposition

Let $A$ be a disrete group, then

\begin{align*} \pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}}(\mathrm{B}A,\mathrm{B}A)) \simeq& \mathrm{End}_{\mathrm{Grp}}(A)/\mathrm{innerAut}\\ \pi_1(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}}(\mathrm{B}A,\mathrm{B}A)) \simeq& C(A) \end{align*}

are equivalent to the set of endomorphisms up to inner automorphism and to the center.

Proof

follows from the fiber sequence of mapping anima, pointed mapping anima and target

and the long exact sequence of homotopy groups + recognition principle for anima valued E_n groups
