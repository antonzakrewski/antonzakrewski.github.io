functor from modules to vector spaces for the residue field is a right exact functor

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and $\mathfrak{m} \subseteq A$ a maximal ideal. Then the functor

\begin{align*} A/\mathfrak{m}: \mathrm{RMod} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Vec}_{A/\mathfrak{m}} \\ M \mapsto M/\mathfrak{m}M \end{align*}

is right exact

Proof

follows from the natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} A/\mathfrak{m}(-) \cong A/\mathfrak{m} \otimes_{A} - \end{align*}
