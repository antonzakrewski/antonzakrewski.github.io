homotopy category functor of a dg category

Definition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $\mathcal{C}$ a dg category. Then the homotopy category of $\mathcal{C}$ is defined as induced functor on category of of enriched functors for

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{X,Y) \rightarrow H_0(\mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y)_*) = \mathrm{coker}(d: \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y)_1 \rightarrow \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y)_0 = \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y)) \end{align*}
