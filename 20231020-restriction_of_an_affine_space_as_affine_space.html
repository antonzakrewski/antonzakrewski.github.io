Restriction of an affine space as affine space

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{A}_K^n$ the affine space with the Zariski topology. Then for $m < n$, it follows, that

\begin{align*} \mathbb{A}^m \cong \mathbb{A}^n \cap K^n \end{align*}

with respect to the subspace topology
