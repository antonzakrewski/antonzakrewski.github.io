infinity limit of terminal objects as terminal object

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $1$ the terminal object and $I \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a diagram which restricts objectwise to $1$. Then the limit is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_{I} 1 \simeq 1 \end{align*}

Proof

note that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,\mathrm{lim}_I 1) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,\mathrm{lim}_I 1) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{lim}_I \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, 1) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{const}_I * \end{align*}

where one can use quillen formula for infinity limits in Anima and observe that the constant diagram to the point suffices, where the limit in the \(1\)-category is given by the point.
