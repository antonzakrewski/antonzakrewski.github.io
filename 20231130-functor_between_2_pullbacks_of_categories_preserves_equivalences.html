functor between 2-pullbacks of categories preserves equivalences

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_{1,2,3}, \mathcal{D}_{1,2,3}$ be categories, with functors $\mathcal{F}_{1}, \mathcal{F}_{2} \mathcal{G}_{1}, \mathcal{G}_2$

Suppose there exist adjoint equivalences

\begin{align*} L_i: \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{D}_i \end{align*}

for $i \in \{1,2\}$ and $\mathcal{L}_0$ as fully faithful functor

Then the 2-categorical pullbacks

are canonically equivalent

Proof

corollary of:

  • adjoint equivalence of categories and fully faithful and essentially surjective functor
  • functor between 2-pullbacks of categories preserves essentially surjectivity under fully faithfullness
  • functor between 2-pullbacks of categories preserves fully faithfulness
