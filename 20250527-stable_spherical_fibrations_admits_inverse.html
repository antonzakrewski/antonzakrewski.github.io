stable spherical fibrations admits inverse

Proposition

Let $\pi: B \rightarrow \mathrm{BG}$ be a stable spherical fibration. Then there exists an inverse

\begin{align*} \pi^{-1}: B \rightarrow \mathrm{BG} \end{align*}

i.e. with the property that

\begin{align*} \pi \oplus \pi^{-1}: B \rightarrow \mathrm{BG} \end{align*}

is nullhomotopic.

Furthermore this $\pi^{-1}$ is unique up to stabilization.
