K1 as first integral homology of the classifying space

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring. Then there exists a canonical isomorphism

\begin{align*} K_1(R) \cong H_1(B \mathrm{GL}(R), \mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

where $B \mathrm{GL}(R)$ is the classifying space of $\mathrm{GL}(R)$ regarded as discrete group

Proof

using first homology functor as abelianization of the first homotopy functor

\begin{align*} \pi_1(B \mathrm{GL}(R)) \end{align*}
