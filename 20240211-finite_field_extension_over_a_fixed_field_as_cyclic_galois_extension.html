finite field extension over a fixed field as cyclic galois extension

Proposition

Let $L$ be a field, $\sigma \in \mathrm{Aut}(L)$ a field-automorphism and $K = L^{\sigma}$ the fixed field. Suppose $E \subseteq L$ is an intermediate extension, such that $E/K$ is a finite field extension. Then

\begin{align*} E/K \end{align*}

is a cyclic galois extension

Proof
