heart of a t structure as 1-category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category equipped with a t-structure. Then the heart is a 1-category.

Proof

follows as

\begin{align*} \pi_k(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y)) \simeq& \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(X[+k],Y)) \\ \simeq& 0 \end{align*}
