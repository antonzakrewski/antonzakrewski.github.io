contractible cofiber for a map between nilpotent anmia implies equivalence

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a map of anima where both $X,Y$ are nilpotent anima. TFAE:

  1. the cofiber is contractible
  2. $f$ is an equivalence

Proof

follows from:

  • homology whitehead for nilpotent connected spaces
  • square with parallel equivalences as infinity pushout
  • long exact sequence of homology for a mapping cone
