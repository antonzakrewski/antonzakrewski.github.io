projective object in an abelian category and splitting sequence

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $P \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{A})$.

TFAE:

  1. $P$ is a projective object
  2. each epimorphism $f: M \rightarrow P$ gives a split exact
\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
M \arrow[twoheadrightarrow, ]{r}{f} & \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[bend left]{l}{}P 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from

and thus

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ker}(f) \arrow[]{r}{\iota} & M \arrow[]{r}{f} & P \arrow[]{r}{} & 0 \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)
