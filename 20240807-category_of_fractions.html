category of fractions

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $W \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ a left multiplicative system or right multiplicative system Then the category of fractions $\mathcal{C}[W^{-1}]$ is defined as left calculus of fractions (resp. right calculus of fractions)
