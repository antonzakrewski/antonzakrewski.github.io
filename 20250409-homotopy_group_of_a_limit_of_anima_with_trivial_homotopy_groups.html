homotopy group of a limit of anima with trivial homotopy groups

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{I}$ be a \(1\)-category, $I: \mathcal{I} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}_*$ a diagram. Then suppose $I$ restricts to anima where $\pi_n(I) = \{*\}$ is trivial.

Then the limit of $I$ has

\begin{align*} \pi_n(\mathrm{lim}_{I}) = \{*\} \end{align*}

also trivial homotopy group
