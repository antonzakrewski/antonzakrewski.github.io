Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be the fully faithful right adjoint. Let $p: K \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a diagram.

Consider $\mathrm{lim}_p d_i \in \mathcal{D}$. using the characterization as left bousfield localization (cf. left bousfield localization as fully faithful or localization) and infinity fully faithful functor reflects limits we have to show, that $\mathrm{lim} p \in \mathcal{C}$, where $\mathcal{C}$ consists of \(S\)-local objects for some $S$

This holds if and only if

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(-,\mathrm{lim}_p d_i) \end{align*}

is S-local functor.

But then since mapping space functor preserves limits

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(-,\mathrm{lim}_p d_i) \simeq& \mathrm{lim}_p \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(-,d_i) \\ \end{align*}

where precomposition with a morphism of $S$ is pointwise an equivalence, hence stays an equivalence after applying the infinity limit functor
