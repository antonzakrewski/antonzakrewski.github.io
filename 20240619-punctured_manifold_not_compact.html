punctured manifold not compact

Proposition

Let \(M\) be an \(n\)-dimensional manifold for $n > 1$ and $N \coloneqq M \setminus \{m_i\}$ a puncture Then $N$ is not compact

Proof

Proof by contraposition Suppose $N$ is compact. Then it is also sequentially compact (cf. first countable, compact space as sequentially compact space)

Choose a chart $U_i$ of $m_i$ in $M$ and let $U_I \cap N$. Choose a sequence $\alpha_i$ in $U_i \cap N$ such that $\alpha_i \rightarrow m_i$ in $M$

Then $\alpha_i$ has exactly one limit point in $M$ (cf. Existenz und Eindeutigkeit eines Häufungspunkts einer Konvergenten Folge), namely $m_i$ But $\alpha_i$ has no limit point in $N$ - if it had one, then in particular it would also be a limit point in $M$ (since the inclusion is continouos)
