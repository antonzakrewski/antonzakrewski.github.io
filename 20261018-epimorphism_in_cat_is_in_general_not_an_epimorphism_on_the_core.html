Proposition

The groupoid core functor in general does not detect nor preserve epimorphisms

Proof

preserve

\begin{align*} B \mathbb{N} \rightarrow B \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

which becomes $* \rightarrow S^1$ in category of Anima, which is not an epimorphism (cf. classification of epimorphism in anima).

or alternatively $\mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{Z}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}_{\mathbb{Q}}$ for $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{Z}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{Q})$.

  • which is not a conservative functor

detect

consider the epimorphism $\Delta^1 \rightarrow \Delta^0$, which is obtained from fully faithfulness of the inclusion $\mathcal{C}^{\simeq} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ar}(\mathcal{C})^{\simeq}$ or using that $\Delta^0 \simeq \Delta^1[ \rightarrow^{-1}]$ It becomes $S^0 \rightarrow \Delta^0$, which is not an epimorphism, since $S^0$ is not acyclic $(\Sigma S^0) \cong S^1$ is not contractible.
