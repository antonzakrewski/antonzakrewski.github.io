existence of a minimal primary decomposition for a noetherian ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a noetherian ring, $\mathfrak{a}$ a strict ideal. Then $\mathfrak{a}$ admits a minimal primary decomposition.

Proof

note that $\mathfrak{a}$ admits a primary decomposition (cf. primary decomposition) $\mathfrak{q}_1,...,\mathfrak{q}_n$

w.l.o.g. we may assume that no ideal $\mathfrak{q}_i$ is redundant, i.e.

\begin{align*} \bigcap_{j \neq i} \mathfrak{q}_i \subsetneq \mathfrak{q}_i \end{align*}

for all $i$

By intersection of p-primary ideals as p-primary ideal we may replace $\mathfrak{q}_i$ with primary ideals with $\sqrt{\mathfrak{p}}$
