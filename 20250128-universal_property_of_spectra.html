universal property of spectrum objects for an infinity category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a stable infinity category Then

\begin{align*} \Omega^{\infty}: \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{Lex}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathrm{Sp}(\mathcal{C})) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{Lex}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is an equivalence

Proof

Adjunction gives an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Exc}_*(-, \mathrm{Exc}_*(-,-)) \cong \mathrm{Exc}_*(- \times -, -) \end{align*}
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Exc}_{*}(\mathcal{D}, \mathrm{Sp}(\mathcal{C})) \simeq& \mathrm{Sp}(\mathrm{Exc}_*(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C})) \\ \end{align*}

Note that $\Omega^{\infty}_{\mathrm{Exc}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C})$ is an equivalence, since $\mathrm{Exc}_{*}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ is a stable infinity category. Under the adjunction above, $\Omega^{\infty}$ corresponds to $\Omega_{\mathcal{C}}^{\infty}$

This gives

Thus

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{Lex}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathrm{Sp}(\mathcal{C})) \simeq& \mathrm{Exc}_*(\mathcal{D}, \mathrm{Sp}(\mathcal{C})) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Exc}_*(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{Lex}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
