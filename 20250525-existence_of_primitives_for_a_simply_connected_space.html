existence of primitives for a simply connected space

Proposition

Let $\Omega \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be open and simply connected and $f: \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ be holomorphic. Then $f$ has a primitive Function in $\Omega$

Proof

using existence of primitives and integral of triangles it suffices to show that the integral

\begin{align*} \int_{T} f(-) \mathrm{d}z \overset{\mathrm{!}}{=} 0 \end{align*}

But by homotopy invariance and by the assumption of simply connectedness we get that $T$ is nullhomotopic hence apply cauchy's theorem for the disc
