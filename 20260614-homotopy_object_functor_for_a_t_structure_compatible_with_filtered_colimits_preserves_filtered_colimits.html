Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category equipped with a t-structure compatible with kappa filtered colimits. Then the functors $\pi_n: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}^{\heartsuit}$ preserve $\kappa$ filtered colimits

Proof

Recall that $\pi_0$ is the composition $\tau_{\geq 0} \tau_{\leq 0}$ and $\tau_{\geq 0}$ is equivalently given by the fiber $\mathrm{id}(-) \Rightarrow \tau_{\leq 1}$. Now the fiber functor of colimit preserving functors preserves colimits in a stable category, so it suffices to show that both $\mathrm{id}(-)$ and $\tau_{\leq 1}$ preserve filtered colimits. But $\mathrm{id}(-)$ always preserves colimits and $\tau_{\leq 1}$ is the compoistion $\mathcal{C} \xrightarrow{\tau_{\leq 1}} \mathcal{C}_{\leq 1} \xrightarrow{\iota} \mathcal{C}$ where $\tau_{\leq 1}$ is a left adjoint (cf. left adjoint preserves colimits) and the inclusion preserves $\kappa$ filtered colimits by assumption.
