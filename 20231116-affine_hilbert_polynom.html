affine Hilbert-polynomial

Definition

Let $K$ be a field, $K[X_1,...,X_n]$ the polynomial ring, $I \subseteq K[X_1,...,X_n]$ an ideal.

Then a polynomial $\mathrm{HP} \in \mathbb{Q}(t)$ is said to be the affine Hilbert-polynomial of $I$, if for almost all $t$ and the affine hilbert-function it holds, that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{HF}_{I} = \mathrm{HP} \end{align*}
