truncated objects stable under limits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category Then the full subcategory $\tau_{\leq n}(\mathcal{C})$ of n-truncated objects is stable under limits which exist in $\mathcal{C}$

Proof

Consider the infinity yoneda embedding. Then the image of $\tau_{\leq n}(\mathcal{C})$ consists precisely of the

  1. pointwise n-truncated anima

which are

  1. corepresentable

But both collections are stable under limits, which exist in $\mathcal{C}$:

  1. limit of truncated anima
  2. one as infinity yoneda functor preserves small limits
