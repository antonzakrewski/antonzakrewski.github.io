weak semiorthogonal decomposition closed under sums

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category and $\mathcal{A} \perp \mathcal{B}$ a weak semiorthogonal decomposition. Then both $\mathcal{A}$ and $\mathcal{B}$ are closed under sum.

Proof

follows by weak semiorthogonal decomposition is determined by one half where $\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,-)$ preserves sums in both variables.
