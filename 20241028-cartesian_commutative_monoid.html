cartesian commutative Monoid

Definition

Let $\mathrm{Fin}$ be the infinity category Fin, then a cartesian commutative Monoid in an infinity category $\mathcal{C}$ is an infinity functor

\begin{align*} M: \mathrm{Fin} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

such that

  1. $M_0 \cong *$ is a terminal object
  2. the Segal map out of category Fin induce an equivalence
\begin{align*} M_n \rightarrow \prod M_1 \end{align*}
