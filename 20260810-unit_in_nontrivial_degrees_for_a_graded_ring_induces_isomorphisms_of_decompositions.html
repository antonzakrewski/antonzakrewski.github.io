unit in nontrivial degrees for a graded ring induces isomorphisms of decompositions

Proposition

Let $A$ be a $\Gamma$ graded ring and $f \in A$ a unit with $\delta = \vert f \vert \neq 0$. Then multiplication with $f$ induces an isomorphism

\begin{align*} A_{\gamma} \xrightarrow{\cong} A_{\delta + \gamma} \end{align*}

In particular, this is a stronger statement if $\delta$ generates the additive group of $\Gamma$.
