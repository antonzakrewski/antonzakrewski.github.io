cofiber sequence of mod m,n and mn in a stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category, $X$ an object and $n,m \in \mathbb{N}$. Then there exists a cofiber sequence

\begin{align*} X/n \xrightarrow{\cdot m} X/nm \xrightarrow{} X/m \end{align*}

Proof

spectra case

sphere

follows as moore spectra are unique and looking at the long exact sequence of homology shows that $\mathrm{cofib}(\mathbb{S}/n \xrightarrow{\cdot m} \mathbb{S}/nm)$ is the moore spectrum $\mathbb{S}/m$

general case

follows as mod n in spectra is tensor product with sphere and $- \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} X$ is an exact functor.

general case

follows as stable category is tensored over finite spectra
