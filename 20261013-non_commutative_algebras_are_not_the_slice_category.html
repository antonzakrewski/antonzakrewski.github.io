non commutative algebras are not the slice category

Proposition

The forgetful functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Alg}_{R} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ring}_{R/} \end{align*}

is not essentially surjective.

Proof

use:

  • requirement that the map $R \rightarrow A$ has to lie in the center
  • here just take $R = k[x]$, $A = k\{x,y\}$ the free noncommutative $k$ algebra.

Then the map $k[x] \rightarrow k \{x,y\}$ does not lie in the center, but is a valid element in $\mathrm{Ring}_{R/}$.
