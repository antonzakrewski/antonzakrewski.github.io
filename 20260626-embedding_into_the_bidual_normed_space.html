embedding into the bidual normed space

Definition / Proposition

Let $X$ be a normed vector space, then there exists an isometric embedding

\begin{align*} J: X \rightarrow& (X^*)^* \\ x \mapsto& (f \mapsto f(x)) \end{align*}

Proof

recall that $J: X \rightarrow (X^*)^*$ is an \(\mathbb{K}\)-linear map by lineare algebra (cf. morphism to the double dual).

Moreover, $J$ is an isometric embedding:

\begin{align*} \| J x \|_{**} =& \mathrm{sup}_{f \in X^*} \frac{\vert (Jx)(f) \vert}{\| f \|_*} \\ =& \frac{\vert f(x) \vert}{\| f \|_*} \\ =& \| x \| \end{align*}

where we have used norm is supremum over linear functionals.
