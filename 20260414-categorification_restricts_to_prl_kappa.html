categorification restricts to PrL kappa

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C} \in \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\kappa})$ be kappa presentable, Then the categorification functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Mod}_{C}(\mathrm{Pr}^L_{\kappa})) \\ A \mapsto& \mathrm{Mod}_{A}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

restricts to a morphism in $\mathrm{CAlg}(\mathrm{Pr^L}_{\kappa})$
