Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. TFAE:

  1. $R$ is a coherent ring
  2. every finitely generated ideal in $R$ is finitely presented

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $(f_1,...,f_n)$ be a finitely generad ideal. Then consider the map $R^n \xrightarrow{(f_1,...,f_n)} R$ and the cokernel is $R/(f_1,...,f_n)$ which lies in $\mathrm{Mod}_{R}^{\mathrm{fp}}$. Hence the kernel of $R \rightarrow R/(f_1,...,f_n)$ - which is given by $(f_1,...,f_n)$ - must lie in $\mathrm{Mod}_{R}^{\mathrm{fp}}$ since it is an abelian subcategory of $\mathrm{Mod}_{R}^{\mathrm{fp}}$. This implies that $(f_1,...,f_n)$ is finitely presented.

2) $\implies$ 1)
