localization at the total ring of quotients

Definition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $S \subseteq A$ a multiplicatively closed subset Then the localization as subset of the total ring of quotients is defined as localization

\begin{align*} (S \cap \mathrm{reg})^{-1} A \end{align*}

at the intersection of multiplicatively closed sets
