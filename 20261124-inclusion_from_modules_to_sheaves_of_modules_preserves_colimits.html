inclusion from modules to sheaves of modules preserves colimits

Proposition

Let $S = \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ for a commutative ring $A$. Then the functor from modules over a ring to modules over the ringed space

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mod}_{A} \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}_{\mathcal{O}_{X}} \end{align*}

preserves colimits

Proof

follows from right bousfield localization and global sections adjunction for sheaves of modules
