locally homeomorphic

Definition

Let $(X,\mathcal{T})$ and $(Y,\mathcal{T}_Y)$ be topological spaces. Then $X$ is said to be locally homeomorphic to $Y$, if for every point $x \in X$ there exists an open neighbourhood $O \subseteq X$ and $V \subseteq Y$ and a homeomorphism

\begin{align*} f: O \rightarrow V \end{align*}
