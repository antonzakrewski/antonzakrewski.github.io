geometric morphism of topoi

Definition

Let $\mathcal{X}, \mathcal{Y}$ be topoi, then a geometric morphism is a functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{X} \rightarrow \mathcal{Y}$ which both

  1. preserves colimits (equivalently: admits a right adjoint)
  2. preserves finite limits
