Proposition

Let $Z$ be a spectrum with finitely generated abelian groups and $X$ another spectrum. TFAE:

  1. $X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} I_{\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}}(Z) \simeq 0$
  2. $\mathrm{Phantom}(V \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} X, Z) = \pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbb{S}}(V \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} X,Z))$ for all $V$, i.e. every map $V \otimes X \rightarrow Z$ is a phantom map
  3. $\mathrm{Phantom}(V \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} X, Z) = \pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbb{S}}(V \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} X,Z))$ for all finite spectra $V$
  4. $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathbb{S}}(X,Z)$ is rational
