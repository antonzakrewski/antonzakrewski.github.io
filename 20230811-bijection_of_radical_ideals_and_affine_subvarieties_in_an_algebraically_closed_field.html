Bijection of radical ideals and affine algebraic sets in an algebraically closed field

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space $\mathcal{A}$ the set of algebraic sets and $K[X_1,...,X_n]$ the polynomial ring and $\mathcal{R}$ the set of radical ideals Then the maps

\begin{align*} \mathcal{I}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow& \mathcal{R} \\ A \mapsto& \mathcal{I}(A) \\ Z: \mathcal{R} \rightarrow& \mathcal{A} \\ \mathfrak{a} \mapsto& Z(\mathfrak{a}) \end{align*}

are inverse, order reversing bijections
