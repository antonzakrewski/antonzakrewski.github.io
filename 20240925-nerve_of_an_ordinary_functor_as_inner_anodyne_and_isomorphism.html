nerve of an ordinary functor as inner-anodyne and isomorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor

TFAE:

  1. $N(\mathcal{F})$ is an isomorphism
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ is an isomorphism
  3. $N(\mathcal{F})$ is an inner anodyne map

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

  • fully faithful functor as conservative functor
  • nerve functor as fully faithful functor

2) $\implies$ 3)

  • isomorphisms under functors

3) $\implies$ 1)

  • isomorphism and lifting against itself
  • nerve of an ordinary functor as inner fibration
