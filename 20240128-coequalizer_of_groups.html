coequalizer of Groups

Proposition

Let $G,H$ be groups and

be group-homomorphisms

Then the coequalizer is given as quotient group

\begin{align*} H/N \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} N = \overline{\langle \varphi(g)^{-1} \cdot \psi(g) \vert g \in G \rangle} \end{align*}

is the normal closure of the generated subgroup

Proof

cofork

Note that

\begin{align*} \pi(\varphi(g)) =& \varphi(g) N \\ =& \varphi(g) \cdot \varphi(g)^{-1} \cdot \psi(g) N \\ =& \psi(g) N \\ =& \pi(\psi(g)) \end{align*}

universal property

Suppose $f: H \rightarrow M

makes this a cofork, then for $\varphi(g) \cdot \psi(g)^{-1} \in N$, it follows, that

\begin{align*} f(\varphi(g) \cdot \psi(g))^{-1} =& f(\varphi(g)) \cdot f(\psi(g))^{-1} \\ =& f(\varphi(g)) \cdot f(\varphi(g))^{-1} \\ =& 1 \end{align*}

by Kern eines Gruppenhomomorphismus als Normalteiler, it follows by minimality of $\overline{N}$ that

\begin{align*} N \subseteq \mathrm{ker}(f) \end{align*}

hence by the fundamental theorem of group homomorphisms there exists a unique group homomorphism

\begin{align*} f': H/N \rightarrow M \end{align*}
