inner hom of a monomorphism and isofibration as isofibration

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $p: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an isofibration and $\iota: K \rightarrow L$ a monomorphism of simplicial sets. Then the induced functor

\begin{align*} (\mathcal{p}, i):\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(L, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(K, \mathcal{C}) \times_{\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathcal{D}, K)} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{L}) \end{align*}

is an isofibration

Proof
