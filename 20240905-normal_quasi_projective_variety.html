normal quasi-projective variety

Definition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ a quasi-projective variety Then $X$ is said to be a normal quasi-projective variety, if for each $P \in X$, the ring of regular functions $\mathcal{O}_{X,P}$ is a normal integral domain, i.e. $\mathcal{O}_{X,P}$ is integrally closed in its quotient field
