complex-orientable cohomology theory

Definition

Let $E$ be a ring spectrum. Then $E$ is said to be complex-orientable, if the map

\begin{align*} E^2(\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}) \rightarrow E^2(S^2) \cong E^0(*) \cong \pi_0(E) \end{align*}

is surjective where we use:

  1. $S^2 \cong \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^1 \rightarrow \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}$
  2. the suspension isomorphism.
