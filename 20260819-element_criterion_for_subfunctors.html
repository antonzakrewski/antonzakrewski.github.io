pullback along representables criterion for elements in subfunctors

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a (1,1)-Category, $U \hookrightarrow X$ a Subfunctor of a presheaf, $c \in \mathcal{C}$ an object and $x \in X(c)$ an element. TFAE:

  1. $x \in U(c)$
  2. there exists a pullback diagram
\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
よ(c) \rar{\mathrm{id}} \dar & よ(c) \dar{x} \\
U \arrow[hookrightarrow, ]{r}{}& X \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
P \arrow[hookrightarrow, ]{r}{} \dar & よ(c) \dar{x} \\
U \arrow[hookrightarrow, ]{r}{}& X \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

be the pullback, in particular $P \hookrightarrow よ(c)$ is a Subfunctor (cf. pullback of a monomorphism is a monomorphism). Using pointwise calculation of limits, we may conclude that $(x,\mathrm{id}(c))$ lies in $P \subseteq よ(c) \times U(c)$ and hence $\mathrm{id}$ lies in $P$ after identification as subfunctor of よ(c)$. But subfunctor of a representable presheaf containing the identity shows that $P $\rightarrow よ(c)$ is a natural isomorphism.

2) $\implies$ 1)

trace $\mathrm{id}(c)$
