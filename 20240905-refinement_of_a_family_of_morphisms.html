refinement of a family of morphisms

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{U} = \{f_i: U_i \rightarrow U\}, \mathcal{V} = \{g_i: V_i \rightarrow V\}$ be families of morphisms. Then a morphism of families of morphisms with a fixed target

\begin{align*} \mathcal{U} \rightarrow \mathcal{V} \end{align*}

is said to be a refinement ($\mathcal{U}$ is a refinement of $\mathcal{V}$), if the morphism $U \rightarrow V$ is the identity

\begin{align*} \mathrm{id} : U \rightarrow U = V \end{align*}

Morally a) still a covering b) each open $U_i$ is a subset of some $V_j$
