union of a chain of groups is a group

Proposition

Let $G_1 \subseteq G_2 \subseteq G_3 ... $ be a chain of groups. Then $G \coloneqq \bigcup_{n \in \mathbb{N}} G_n$ is also a group

Proof

monoid

cf. union of a chain of monoids as monoid

inverse element

Let $g_1 \in G$, then by assumption there exists a $G_i$ such that $g_1 \in G_i$ and hence $g_1^{-1} \in G_i \subseteq G$
