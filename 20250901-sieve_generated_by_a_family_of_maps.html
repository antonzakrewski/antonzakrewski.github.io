sieve generated by a family of maps

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category, $\mathcal{X} = \{X_i \xrightarrow{p_i} X\}_{i \in I}$ be a family of maps with target $X$. The sieve $S(\mathcal{X})$ on $X$ generated by the family $\mathcal{X}$ is given by

\begin{align*} S(\mathcal{X})(Y) = \{Y \xrightarrow{\alpha} Y \vert \exists i \in I : \alpha = p_i \alpha_i \mathrm{ for some } \alpha_i: Y \rightarrow X_i\} \end{align*}

Proof
