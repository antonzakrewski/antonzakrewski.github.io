coproduct of modules is retract of product of modules

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $\mathrm{Mod}(R)$ the category RMod, $M_i$ \(R\)-modules Then $\coprod_i M_i$ is a retract of $\prod_{i} M_i$.

Proof

Consider the map

\begin{align*} p: \prod_{i} M_i \rightarrow& \coprod_i M_i \\ (m_i) \mapsto \begin{cases} (m_i) & \mbox{if } (m_i) \in \coprod_i M_i \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

which is well-defined no ! and evidently exhibits $\coprod_i M_i$ as retract.
