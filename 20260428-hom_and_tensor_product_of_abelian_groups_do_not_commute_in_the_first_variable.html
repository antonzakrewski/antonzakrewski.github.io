Hom and tensor product of abelian groups do not commute in the first variable

Proposition

Proof

consider e.g. $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{Z})$. Then $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{Z}) \simeq 0$ and hence $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{Z}) \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Q}$.

But $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{Q})$ is nontrivial (or even $\mathbb{Q}$).
