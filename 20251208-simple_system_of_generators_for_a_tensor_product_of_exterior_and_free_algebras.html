simple system of generators for a tensor product of exterior and free algebras

Proposition

Let $y_i, x_i$ be elements, then the tensor product of graded-commutative algebras

\begin{align*} \Lambda_{\mathbb{F}_2}[y_i] \otimes_{\mathbb{F}_2} \mathbb{F}_2[x_1] \end{align*}

has $\{y_i \otimes x_{k}^{2^i}\}$ as one simple system of generators.

Proof
