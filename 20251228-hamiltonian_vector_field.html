hamiltonian vector field

Definition

Let $f: T^* M \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be any function and $\omega$ a symplectic form. Then the hamiltonian $X_f$ is defined implicitely by

\begin{align*} \omega(X_f,-) = \mathrm{d} f \end{align*}

where

  1. $\omega$ is of course a map on $(T(T^*M) \times T(T^*M))_{- \setminus \Delta} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$
  2. $\mathrm{d}f: T(T^*M) \rightarrow T(\mathbb{R}) \cong \mathbb{R}$ is a map (with preferred isomorphis)
