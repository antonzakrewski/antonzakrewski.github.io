graded abelian group

Definition

Let \( (G,+)\) be an abelian group and $(C_n)_{n \in \mathbb{Z}}$ a family of subgroups. Then $G$ is said to be graded, if the induced inclusion map

\begin{align*} \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} C_n \rightarrow& C (c_n) \mapsto& \sum_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} c_n \end{align*}

is a isomorphism
