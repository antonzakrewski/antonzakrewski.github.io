Grp as free additive infinity category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be presentable infinity categories, where $\mathcal{D}$ is an additive infinity category. Then precomposition with the infinite free group functor $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Grp}(\mathcal{C})$ induces an equivalence of FunL

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^L(\mathrm{Grp}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}^L(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

Proof
