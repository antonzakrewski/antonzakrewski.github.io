subcategory of a stable category closed under kappa small coproducts is closed under kappa small colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category, $\mathcal{D}$ any full subcategory and $\kappa$ a regular cardinal. Suppose $\mathcal{C}$ is closed under $\kappa$ small coproducts and cofibers

Then $\mathcal{C}$ is closed under $\kappa$ small colimits.

Proof

follows from:

  • bifiber sequence and pushout/pullback in a stable category (mayer vietoris)
  • kappa small coproducts and pushouts imply kappa small colimits
