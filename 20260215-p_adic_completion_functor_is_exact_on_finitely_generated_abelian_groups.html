p-adic completion functor is exact on finitely generated abelian groups

Proposition

The p-adic completion functor is an exact functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{FgAb} \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}(\mathbb{Z}_p) \end{align*}

when restricted to finitely generated abelian groups

Proof

use:

  • Artin-Rees Lemma
