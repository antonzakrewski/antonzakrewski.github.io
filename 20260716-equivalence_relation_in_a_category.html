equivalence relation in a 1-category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a (1,1)-Category Then an equivalence relation in $\mathcal{C}$ is a diagram

such that for every object $Y \in \mathcal{C}$, the induced map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y,R) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y,X) \times \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y,X) \end{align*}

is injective and defines an equivalence relation on $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(Y,X)$
