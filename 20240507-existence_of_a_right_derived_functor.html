existence of a right derived functor for a category with enough injectives

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, where $\mathcal{A}$ admits enough injectives

Proof

follows dually from existence of a left derived functor for a category with enough projectives

\begin{align*} \mathcal{R}F^n \coloneqq (\mathcal{L}( \mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{op}})_n)^{\mathrm{op}}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B} \end{align*}
