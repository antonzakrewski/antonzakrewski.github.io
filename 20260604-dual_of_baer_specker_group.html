baer-specker group and free group are dual

Proposition

There exist isomorphisms

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\prod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Z}) \simeq& \coprod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{Z} \\ \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\coprod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Z}) \simeq& \prod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

Remark

neither group is dualizable.

Proof

observe that the second isomorphism follows from hom preserves limits.

Now any homomorphism $f: \prod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}$ is determined by $f(e_n)$ (cf. abelian group homomorphism from baer-specker group to a finitely generated group is determined by restriction).

This shows that the morphism

\begin{align*} \coprod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\prod_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Z}) \\ e_n \mapsto& \mathrm{pr}_n \end{align*}
