relative cellular map

Definition

Let $(B,A), (Y,X)$ CW Pairs and

\begin{align*} f: (B,A) \rightarrow (Y,X) \end{align*}

a map of pairs.

Then $f$ is said to be a relative cellular map, if

\begin{align*} f: B \rightarrow X \end{align*}

is cellular for

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sk}_{-1}(B) =& A \\ \mathrm{Sk}_{-1}(Y) =& Y \end{align*}
