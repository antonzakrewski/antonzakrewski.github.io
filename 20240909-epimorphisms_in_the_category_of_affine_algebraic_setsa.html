epimorphisms in the category of affine algebraic sets as dominant regular morphisms

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X,Y \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be algebraic sets and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a morphism of affine algebraic sets

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is dominant, i.e. $f[X]$ is dense in $Y$
  2. $f^*: \mathcal{A}(Y) \rightarrow \mathcal{A}(X)$ is injective
  3. $f^*: \mathcal{A}(Y) \rightarrow \mathcal{A}(X)$ is a monomorphism
  4. $f$ is an epimorphism in category of affine varieties

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 3)

injective map as monomorphism

3) $\implies$ 4)

  • equivalence of categories preserves and reflects epimorphisms
  • equivalence between algebraic sets and reduced k-algebras

4) $\implies$ 1)
