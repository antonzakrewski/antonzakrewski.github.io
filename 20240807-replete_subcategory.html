replete subcategory

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $\mathcal{D} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a subcategory Then $\mathcal{D}$ is said to be replete, if for $d \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{D})$ and an isomorphism $f: d \cong c$ it follows that

  1. $c \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{D})$
  2. $f \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{D})$
