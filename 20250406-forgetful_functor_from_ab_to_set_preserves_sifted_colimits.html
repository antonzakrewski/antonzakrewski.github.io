Let

be a reflexive coequalizer.

Using the description of colimit in set, it remains to show that $B/\sim$ is equipped with a canonical group structure via

\begin{align*} [b] + [b'] \coloneqq [b + b'] \end{align*}

It remains to show that this is welldefined: Assume that $b_1 \sim b_2$, then we have to show that

\begin{align*} [b_1 + b'] = [b_2 + b'] \end{align*}

Note that $b_1 \sim b_2$ if and only if there exist $a_1,...,a_n$ such that

\begin{align*} f_1(a_1) =& b_1 \\ f_1(a_i) =& f_2(a_{i-1}) \\ f_2(a_n) =& b_{2} \end{align*}

w.l.o.g. assume that $n = 1$, the general case follows by induction over the zigzag. Choose a common section $s$ Then

\begin{align*} b_1 + b' =& f_1(a_1) + f_1(s(b')) \\ =& f_1(a_1 + s(b')) b_2 + b' =& f_2(a_1) + f_2(s(b')) \\ =& f_2(a_1 + s(b')) \end{align*}

Hence $b_1 + b' \sim b_2 + b'$
