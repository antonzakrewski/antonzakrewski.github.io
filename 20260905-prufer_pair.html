prüfer pair

Definition

Let $j: X' \hookrightarrow X$ be a monomorphism of schemes. Then $(X,X')$ is a prüfer pair if

  1. $j$ is a quasi-compact morphism
  2. $X'$ is the intersection of open neighborhoods in $X$
