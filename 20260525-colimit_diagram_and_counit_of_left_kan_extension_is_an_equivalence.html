colimit diagram and counit of left kan extension is an equivalence

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category with enough/$I$ shaped colimit and $I^{\triangleleft} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a diagram TFAE:

  1. $I^{\triangleleft} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is a colimit diagram
  2. the counit $\mathrm{colim} i \rightarrow I^{\triangleleft}$ is an equivalence
