brown comenetz dual of Q is Q

Proposition

The brown comenetz dual of $\mathbb{Q}$ (as abelian group) is given by $\mathbb{Q}$ (as eilenberg maclane spectrum)

Proof

We need to show that there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \pi_n(\mathrm{map}(X, \mathbb{Q})) \simeq (\mathrm{map}(\pi_{-n}(X), \mathbb{Q})) \simeq \end{align*}

but

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}(X, \mathbb{Q}) \simeq& \mathrm{map}(X_{\mathbb{Q}}, \mathbb{Q}) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}(\bigoplus_{n} \pi_n(X_{\mathbb{Q}})[n], \mathbb{Q} ) \\ \simeq& \bigoplus_{n} \mathrm{Hom}(\pi_n(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}, \mathbb{Q})[-n] \\ \end{align*}
