reduced scheme, reduced stalks and reduced coordinate rings

Proposition

Let $X$ be a scheme. TFAE:

  1. $X$ is a reduced scheme, i.e. there exists an affine covering by reduced rings,
  2. the stalks are reduced rings,
  3. for each open $U$, the coordinate ring $\mathcal{O}_{X}(U)$ is a reduced ring

Proof

Functor of points

reduced is a point local property

boomer proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

w.l.o.g. we may assume that $X$ is an affine scheme with reduced ring $R$. Then a point is also equivalently given by a prime ideal $\mathfrak{p} \subseteq R$ and the stalk is $R_{\mathfrak{p}}$, which is the localization of a reduced ring, hence itself reduced: localization of a reduced ring as reduced ring

2) $\implies$ 3)

There exists a monomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O}_{X}(U) \rightarrow \prod_{x \in U} \mathcal{O}_{X,x} \end{align*}

into the presheaf of discontinuous sections.

Now the RHS is by assumption a product of reduced rings, hence also reduced. Moreover, reduced rings are closed under limits & subring of a reduced ring as reduced ring.

3) $\implies$ 1)
