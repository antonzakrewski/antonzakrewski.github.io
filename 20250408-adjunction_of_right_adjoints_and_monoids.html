Adjunction of right adjoints and Monoids

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be presentable infinity categories. Then there exists a canonical equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}_{E_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{RAdj}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{RAdj}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Mon}_{E_{\infty}}\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

Proof

using:

  • infinity left adjoint functor theorem for presentable categories
  • category of cartesian commutative monoids as presentable category

we note that the right adjoints are precisely the accessible and limit preserving functors.

But by

  • limits in a left bousfield localization
  • filtered colimits in a left bousfield localization

we may work within

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{FinSet}_*,-) \supseteq \mathrm{Mon}_{E_{\infty}}(-) \end{align*}

using pointwise calculation of limits and adjoint functor theorem we note that a right adjoint $\mathcal{F} \in \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{RAdj}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{Mon}_{E_{\infty}}\mathcal{D})$ is precisely a functor

\begin{align*} F: \mathrm{FinSet}_* \times \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

which

  1. satisfies the segal condition in the first variable
  2. preserves limits / filtered colimits in the second variable (see discussion about limits / filtered colimits)

see: currying of functors preserving limits / colimits

But similarly the segal condition for $\mathrm{Mon}_{E_{\infty}}(\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{RAdj}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}))$ can be checked in $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D})$ (by limits in a left bousfield localization)

Hence the currying in cat infinity restricts appropriately. Then the functors are fully faithful functor since both subcategories are full.
