image of a relatively compact set from a first countable to a metrizable space is relatively compact

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a continuous map where $Y$ is a metrizable space and $X$ is first-countable. Then the image of a relatively compact subset is also relatively compact.

Proof

Let $A$ be relatively compact, in particular, $\overline{A}$ is also sequentially compact (cf. first countable, compact space as sequentially compact space) Then $f[\overline{A}]$ is also sequentially compact and hence also compact. Moreover, $f[\overline{A}]$ is compact (cf. image of compact space is compact) and hence closed (cf. <compacts are closed in hausdorff spaces>.
