Proposition

Let $M \in \mathrm{Mon}(\mathrm{An})$ be an anima valued monoid.

TFAE:

  1. $M$ is an anima valued group
  2. for each $m \in M$ the multiplication
\begin{align*} \{m\} \times M \rightarrow M \times M \xrightarrow{\simeq} M \end{align*}

is an equivalence

  1. $\pi_0(M)$ is a group
