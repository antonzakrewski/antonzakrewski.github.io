rational cohomology of BG is Q

Proposition

The rational cohomology of $\mathrm{BG}$ (cf. BG) is given by

\begin{align*} H^*(\mathrm{BG},\mathbb{Q}) \simeq \mathbb{Q} \end{align*}

Proof

identify

  • homotopy groups of BG as stable homotopy groups
  • rational cohomology of a connected space with finite homotopy groups
