functorial weak factorization system

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{L},\mathcal{R} \subseteq \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$ be subcollections of morphisms. Then $\mathcal{L}, \mathcal{R}$ is said to be a functorial weak factorization system, if there exists a factorization functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{fact}: \mathcal{C}^{[1]} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C}^{[2]} \\ \end{align*}

with

\begin{align*} \mathrm{fact}(f)[0 \rightarrow 1] \in& \mathcal{L} \\ \mathrm{fact}(f)[0 \rightarrow 2] \in& \mathcal{R} \end{align*}
