spectrum object of a functor category as functor category

Proposition

Let $X$ be a simplicial set and $\mathcal{C}$ be an finitely complete infinity category. Then for the category of spectrum objects there exists an isomorphism of simplicial sets

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sp}(\mathrm{Fun}(K,\mathcal{C})) \simeq& \mathrm{Fun}(K, \mathrm{Sp}(\mathcal{C})) \end{align*}
