support criterion for vanishing derived tensor products

Proposition

Let $R,S$ be objects in $\mathcal{D}(\mathbb{Z})$. TFAE:

  1. $R \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} S \simeq 0$
  2. $\mathrm{supp}(R) \cap \mathrm{supp}(S) = \emptyset$

here

\begin{align*} \mathrm{supp}(R) = \{\mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z}) \vert R \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}^{\heartsuit} k(\mathfrak{p}) \neq 0\} \end{align*}

Proof

follows as

  • derived tensor product with Q and F_p are jointly conservative
  • infinite extension of scalars is symmetric monoidal
  • derived tensor product over a field

Remark

we have

\begin{align*} \mathrm{supp}(R) = \bigcap_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \mathrm{supp}(\pi_n(R)) \end{align*}
