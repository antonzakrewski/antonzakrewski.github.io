homogenity of a endomap of a ring and additivity

Proposition

Let $R$ be rings, such that

\begin{align*} f(\lambda \cdot x) = \lambda \cdot f(x) \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} f(z + y) = f(z) + f(y) \end{align*}

Proof

Let $\lambda = (z + y)$ and $x = 1$, then

\begin{align*} f((z + y) \cdot 1) =& (z + y) \cdot f(1) \\ =& z \cdot f(1) + y \cdot f(1) \\ =& f(z) + f(y) \end{align*}
