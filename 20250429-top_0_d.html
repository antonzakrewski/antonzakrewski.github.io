Top_0(d)

Definition

The space $\mathrm{Top}_*(d)$ is defined as subspace

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Top}_*(d) \coloneqq \mathrm{Homeo}_*((\mathbb{R}^d,0), (\mathbb{R}^d,0)) \subseteq \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{Top}}((\mathbb{R}^d,0), (\mathbb{R}^d,0)) \end{align*}

consisting of based homeomorphisms.
