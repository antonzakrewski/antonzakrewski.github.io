functorality and postcomposition with a faithful functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be categories, $\mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor-candidate and $\mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ a faithful functor.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{G}$ is a functor
  2. $\mathcal{F}\circ \mathcal{G}$ is a functor

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from composition of functors as functor

identity

by assumption, $\mathcal{F} \circ \mathcal{G}(\mathrm{id}_{A}) = \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{FG}(A)}$. Here since $\mathcal{F}$ is faithful, there exists a unique morphism $f: A \rightarrow A$ such that $\mathcal{F}(f) = \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{GF}(A)}$. Since $\mathcal{F}$ is a functor, it follows, that $f = \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{G}(A)}$ and hence it follows, that $\mathcal{G}(\mathrm{id}_{A}) = \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{G}(A)}$
