characterization of quasi-separated schemes

Proposition

Let $X$ be a scheme and $(U_i \subseteq X)_{i \in I}$ an open covering by affine schemes TFAE:

  1. $X$ is quasi-separated
  2. the intersection of two quasi-compact open subsets of $\vert X \vert$ is quasi-compact
  3. for every $i \in I$, $f^{-1}[U_i]$ is quasi-compact

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 3)

3) $\implies$ 1)
