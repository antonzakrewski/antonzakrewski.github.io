forgetful functor from real vector bundles to spherical fibrations

Definition

The forgetful functor from the <oriented real vector bundles> to <oriented spherical fibrations> is given by postcomposition

\begin{align*} [X,\mathrm{BSO}(d)] \rightarrow [X,\mathrm{SG}(d)] \end{align*}
