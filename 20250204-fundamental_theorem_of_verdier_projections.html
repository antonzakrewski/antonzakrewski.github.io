fundamental theorem of verdier projections

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{C}'$ be stable infinity categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}'$ be an exact infinity functor

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is a verdier projection
  2. $\mathcal{F}$ is a right bousfield localization
  3. $\mathcal{F}$ is a projection

Proof
