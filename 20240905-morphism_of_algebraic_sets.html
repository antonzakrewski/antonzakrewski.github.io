morphism of affine algebraic sets

Definition

Let $K$ be a field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X,Y$ algebraic sets. Then a morphism $f: X \rightarrow Y$ is a map

\begin{align*} f = (f_1,...,f_n): X \rightarrow& Y \\ P \mapsto& f(P) = (f_1(P),...,f_n(P)) \end{align*}

where $f_i \in K[X_1,...,X_n]$ are polynomials
