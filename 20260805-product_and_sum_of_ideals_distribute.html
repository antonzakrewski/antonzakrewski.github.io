product and sum of ideals distribute

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring and $\mathfrak{a}, \mathfrak{b}_i$ be ideals. Then

\begin{align*} \mathfrak{a} (\sum \mathfrak{b}_i) = \sum (\mathfrak{a} \cdot \mathfrak{b}_i) \end{align*}

Proof

Let $a + (\sum_{j =1}^n b_j) \in \mathfrak{a} (\sum \mathfrak{b}_i)$, then it is given by $\sum_{j=1}^n a b_j$. let $\sum_{j=1}^n a_j b_j \in \sum (\mathfrak{a} \cdot \mathfrak{b}_i)$, then each $a_j b_j$ lies in $\mathfrak{a} (\sum \mathfrak{b}_i)$ and hence so does the sum.
