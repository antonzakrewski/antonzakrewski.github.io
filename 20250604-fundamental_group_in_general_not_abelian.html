pi_1 of a commutative monoid in general not abelian

Proposition

The fundamental group of a anima valued commutative monoid is in general not an abelian group

Proof

consider $(\mathrm{Fin}, \coprod)$ as cocartesian monoidal category (resp. ) It is clearly a monoid. Then $\pi_1(\{3\}) = S_3$

reasons

  • $\pi_1$ only sees one connected component
  • each
  • no eckmann hilton trick:
    • since pi_1 requires pointed anima, we only have a group object in unpointed anima
    • components are in general not equivalent
