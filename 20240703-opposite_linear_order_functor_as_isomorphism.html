opposite linear order endofunctor as isomorphism

Proposition

The opposite linear order endofunctor is an isomorphism with itself as inverse

Proof

Let $f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\Delta}([m],[n])$.

objects

note that it is the identity on the objects

morphisms

Then

\begin{align*} f^{\mathrm{op}}: i \mapsto& n - f(m - i) \end{align*}

and therefore

\begin{align*} (f^{\mathrm{op}})^{\mathrm{op}}: i \mapsto& n - f^{\mathrm{op}}(m - i) \\ =& n - (n - f(m - (m - i))) \\ =& (n - n) + f((m - m) + i) \\ =& f(i) \end{align*}
