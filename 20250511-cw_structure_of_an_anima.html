cw structure of an anima

Proposition

Let $X$ be an anima. Then there exist anima $\mathrm{sk}_n(X)$ for $n \in \mathbb{N}_{0} \cup \{-1\}$ with morphisms $\mathrm{sk}_n(X) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{sk}_{n+1}(X)$

  1. $\mathrm{colim}_{\mathbb{N}} \mathrm{sk}_n(X) = X$
  2. we have pushout diagrams

Proof

see:

  • cw structure of a simplicial set
  • quillen formula for infinity colimits in Anima

where we replace $\coprod S^n \rightarrow \coprod *$ with $\coprod S^n \rightarrow \coprod D^{n+1}$
