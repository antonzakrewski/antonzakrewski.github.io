excision for local homology

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a Frechet topological space and $x \in X$ a point. Then for an open neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ and the inclusion

\begin{align*} (U, U \setminus \{x\}) \rightarrow (X, X \setminus \{x\}) \end{align*}

the induced morphism

\begin{align*} H_n(X,X \setminus \{x\}) \cong H_n(U, U \setminus \{x\}) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism

Proof

follows from excision theorem for an open cover for

\begin{align*} X =& X \\ X_1 =& U \\ X_2 =& X \setminus \{x\} \end{align*}

or

\begin{align*} H_n(U, U \cap X \setminus \{x\}) \cong H_n(X, X \setminus \{x\}) \end{align*}
