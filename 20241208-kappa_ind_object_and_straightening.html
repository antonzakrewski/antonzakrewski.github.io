kappa ind object and straightening

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small infinity category, $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ the infinity presheaf category, $\kappa$ a regular cardinal and $F \in \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ an infinity presheaf.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F} \in \mathrm{Ind}_{\kappa}$
  2. the unstraightening yields a right fibration $\mathrm{Un}(\mathcal{F}): \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ where $\mathcal{E}$ is \(\kappa\)-kappa filtered infinity category
