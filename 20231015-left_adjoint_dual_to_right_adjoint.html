left adjoint dual to right adjoint

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{L} \vdash \mathcal{R}$ adjoints, where $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is a left adjoint, $\mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ is a right adjoint. Then for the opposite functors

\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}^{\mathrm{op}}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{op}} \\ \mathcal{R}^{\mathrm{op}}: \mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \\ \end{align*}

$\mathcal{L}^{\mathrm{op}}$ becomes a right adjoint and $\mathcal{R}^{\mathrm{op}}$ a right adjoint
