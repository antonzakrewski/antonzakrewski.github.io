hilton-milnor splitting for anima

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be anima, then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \Omega( X \vee Y) \simeq& \Omega X \times \Omega Y \times \Omega(\Sigma(\Omega X \wedge \Omega Y)) \end{align*}
