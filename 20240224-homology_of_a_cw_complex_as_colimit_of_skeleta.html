homology of a cw complex as colimit of skeletons

Proposition

Let $(X, \emptyset, \mathrm{SK}_n(X))$ be a CW-Complex, then the canonical map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{n \geq 0} H_{k}(\mathrm{sk}_n(X)) \rightarrow H_k(X) = H^{k}(\mathrm{colim}_{n \geq 0}(X)) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism

  • induced morphism between colimits and functor

Proof

follows (somewhat ?) from strong Fernbahnhof theorem for exact functors

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{n \geq 0} H_k(\mathrm{sk}_n(X)) \cong H_k(\mathrm{colim}_{n \geq 0} \mathrm{sk}_n(X)) \\ =& H_k(X) \end{align*}
