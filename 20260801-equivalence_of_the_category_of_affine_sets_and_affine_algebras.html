equivalence of the category of affine sets and affine algebras

Proposition

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field. Then the map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{AffSet}_k \rightarrow& (\mathrm{CAlg}_{k}^{\mathrm{affine}})^{\mathrm{op}} (n,X) \mapsto& k[x_1,...,x_n]/I(X) \end{align*}

is an equivalence of categories

Proof

essentially surjective

follows from strong nullstellensatz

fully faithful
