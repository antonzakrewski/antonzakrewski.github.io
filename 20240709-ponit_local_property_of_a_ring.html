point local property of a ring

Definition

Let $\Pi$ be a property of a ring $A$ Then $\Pi$ is said to be a point local property, if

TFAE:

  1. $A$ has the property $\Pi$
  2. the property is point local
  3. for each maximal ideal $\mathfrak{p}$, the localization at a prime ideal $A_{\mathfrak{p}}$ has the property $\Pi$
