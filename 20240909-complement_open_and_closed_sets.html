complement open and closed sets

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space, $A \subseteq X$ a closed and $O \subseteq X$ an open subspace. Then

  1. $O \setminus A$ is open and
  2. $A \setminus O$ is closed
