Proposition

Let $G = (V,E)$ be a graph. TFAE:

  1. $G$ is a tree
  2. $G$ is connected graph and $\vert E \vert = \vert V \vert - 1$

Proof

2) $\implies$ 1)

note that a tree is by definition connected.

Proof by induction, number of vertices

induction basis

for $\vert V \vert = 1$ this is clear.

induction step

use tree contains at least two leafs and tree minus leaf as tree. (todo)

1) $\implies$ 2)

Let $G$ be a connected graph with $\vert E(G) \vert = \vert V(G) \vert - 1$. Then using Äquivalente Charakterisierungen eines Baums choose a minimally connected subgraph $T$ of $G$ with $V_T = V_G$. Then $T$ is a tree and has precisely as many edges as $G$, in particular

\begin{align*} T = G \end{align*}
