strongly continuous condition for mapping into semiorthogonal gluings

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{E} \in \mathrm{Pr}^L_{\mathrm{st}}$ $\mathcal{E} = \langle \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}\rangle$. Then a functor $\mathcal{F}: T \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ is strongly continuous if and only if $\pi_1 \mathcal{F}, \pi_2 \mathcal{F}$ are strongly continuous.
