morphism of ringed spaces

Definition

Let $(X,\mathcal{O}_X), (Y, \mathcal{O}_Y)$ be \(k\) ringed spaces over a commutative ring $k$. A morphism $f: (X,\mathcal{O}_X) \rightarrow (Y,\mathcal{O}_Y)$ of ringed spaces is a pair $(f^*,f^#)$, where

  1. $f^*': X \rightarrow Y$ is a continuous map
  2. $f^#: \mathcal{O}_Y \rightarrow f_*\mathcal{O}_X$ is a morphism of sheaves in the category Ring, see: direct image sheaf functor

Remark

the direct image sheaf functor is a right adjoint, so any morphism $f^#: \mathcal{O}_{Y} \rightarrow f_*(\mathcal{O}_X)$ corresponds to a morphism $f^*(\mathcal{O}_Y) \rightarrow \mathcal{O}_X$
