additive category

Observe that each Hom anima is canonically a group: this follows as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \simeq& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\Sigma^n x,\Sigma^n y) \\ \simeq& \Omega^n \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x, \Sigma^n y) \end{align*}

where $\Omega^n \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,\Sigma^n y)$ is a loop space, hence an \(\mathbb{E}_n\) group.
