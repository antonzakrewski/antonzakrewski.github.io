descent along the empty sieve

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}$ be a presheaf on a category. Then $\mathcal{F}$ satisfies descent along the empty sieve $\emptyset \subseteq よ(X)$ if and only if $\mathcal{F}(X) \simeq *$

Proof

follows from the definition:

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(X) \overset{!}{\simeq} \mathrm{Hom}(\emptyset, \mathcal{F}) \simeq * \end{align*}
