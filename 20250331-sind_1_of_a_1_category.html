sInd_1 of a 1-category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sInd}_1(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathcal{P}_1(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is defined as full subcategory

  1. containing the image of $\mathcal{C}$ under the \(1\)-categorical Yoneda Lemma by yoneda embedding
  2. which is closed under 1-sifted colimits
