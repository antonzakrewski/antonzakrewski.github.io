localization and quotient for an ideal

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $S \subseteq A$ a multiplicatively closed subset and $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ an ideal. Then there exists a ring-isomorphism

\begin{align*} \overline{S}^{-1}(A/\mathfrak{a}) \simeq (S^{-1}A)/(S^{-1}\mathfrak{a}) \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} \overline{S} \coloneqq \pi_{\mathfrak{a}}[S] \subseteq A/\mathfrak{a} \end{align*}

\begin{align*} \overline{S}^{-1}(A) \rightarrow S^{-1}(A)/S^{-1}(\mathfrak{a}) \end{align*}

\begin{align*} a \mapsto \frac{a}{1} \mapsto \frac{0}{0} \end{align*}

\begin{align*} s \in S \end{align*}
