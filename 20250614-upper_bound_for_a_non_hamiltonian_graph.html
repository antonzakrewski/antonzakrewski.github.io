upper bound of vertices for a non-hamiltonian graph

Proposition

Let $G = ([n],E)$ be a non hamiltonian graph. Then

\begin{align*} E(G) \leq& \binom{n-1}{2} + 1 = \binom{n}{2} - (n-2) \end{align*}

Furthermore this bound is optimal
