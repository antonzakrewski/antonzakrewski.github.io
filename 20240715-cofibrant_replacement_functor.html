cofibrant replacement functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a model category with functorial factorization. Then the cofbrint replacement functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{c}} \end{align*}

together with a natural transformation

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \mathrm{id} \end{align*}

which chooses for each $f: x \rightarrow y \in \mathcal{C}$ a factorization

where $\tilde{x}, \tilde{y}$ are cofibrant objects
