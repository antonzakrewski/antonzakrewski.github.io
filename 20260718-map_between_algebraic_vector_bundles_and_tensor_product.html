map between algebraic vector bundles and tensor product with fields

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $V,W$ vector bundles over $R$. Let $f: V \rightarrow W$ be a module-homomorphism TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an isomorphism
  2. $f \otimes_{R} K$ is an isomorphism for every R-field

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

clear

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows from:

  • characterization of subbundles of vector bundles over a ring & characterization of a quotient bundle of a vector bundle over a ring
