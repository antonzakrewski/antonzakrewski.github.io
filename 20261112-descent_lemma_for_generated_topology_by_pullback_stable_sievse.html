descent lemma for generated topology by pullback stable sieves

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $T$ a collection of sieves in $\mathcal{C}$. Assume that $T$ is closed under pullbacks

Let $\tau$ be the topology generated by $T$, then $F \in \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ is a sheaf of $\tau$ if and only if it satisfies descent for all sieves in $T$

Proof

By abuse of notation write $\mathrm{Shv}_{T}(\mathcal{C})$ for the collection of sheaves, which satisfy descent for $T$. Then $T \subseteq \tau$, i.e.$\mathrm{Shv}_{\tau}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathrm{Shv}_{T}(\mathcal{C})$.

Now it remains to show the other inclusion.

Let $\rho$ be the collection of sieves

Then by definition $\mathrm{Shv}_{T} \subseteq \mathrm{Shv}_{\rho}$.
