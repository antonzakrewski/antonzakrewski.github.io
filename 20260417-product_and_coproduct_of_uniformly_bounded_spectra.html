product and coproduct of shifted uniformly bounded spectra

Proposition

Let $X_n$ be a collection of spectra such that there exists an $n_{\mathrm{min}}$ and an $n_{\mathrm{max}}$ such that

  1. $\tau_{\leq n_{\mathrm{min}}}(X_n) \simeq 0$
  2. $\tau_{\geq n_{\mathrm{max}}}(X_n) \simeq 0$

Then the canonical map $\coprod_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \Sigma^n X_n \rightarrow \prod_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \Sigma^n X_n$ is an equivalence

Proof

we use the whitehead theorem for spectra, so it suffices to show that this map is an equivalence on homotopy. So let $n$ be arbitrary, then there exist constants $m_{\leq},m_{\geq}$ such that $\pi_n(\Sigma^m X_m) = 0$ for $m \leq m_{\leq}$ or $m \geq m_{\geq}$.
