phantom map of spectra and homotopy groups of tensored map

Proposition

Let $\varphi: X \rightarrow Y$ be a map of spectra TFAE:

  1. $\varphi$ is a phantom map
  2. $\pi_*(\varphi \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} V)$ is the zero morphism for every spectrum $V$
  3. $\pi_*(\varphi \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} \tilde{\mathbb{S}}[X])$ is the zero morphism for every anima $X$.
  4. $\pi_*(\varphi \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} V)$ is the zero morphism for every finite spectrum $V$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 3)

3) $\implies$ 4)

special case

4) $\implies$ 1)
