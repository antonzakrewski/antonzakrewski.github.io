uniqueness of analytic functions on open connected subsets

Proposition

Let $\Sigma \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be open and $\tilde{\Sigma} \supseteq \Sigma$ be connected and open. Suppose $f: \Sigma \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ is a function. Then there exists at most one analytic function $F: \tilde{\Sigma} \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ which coincides with $f$ on $\Sigma \subseteq \tilde{\Sigma}$

Proof

follows from uniqueness of analytic functions via convergent sequence where any convergent sequence in $\Sigma$ will do the job.
