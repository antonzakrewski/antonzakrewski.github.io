Eilenberg-MacLane spectrum is not finite

Proposition

Let $A \neq 0$ be an abelian group. Then the Eilenberg-MacLane spectrum $A$ is not a finite spectrum

Proof

recall that a finite spectrum has finitely many, finitely generated homology

non finitely generated abelian group

using the stable hurewicz theorem we may conclude that $H_0(A) \cong \pi_0(A) \cong A$.

finitely generated abelian group

using the classification of finitely generated abelian groups and homology is additive it suffices to show this statement for $\mathbb{Z}$ or $\mathbb{F}_p$.

  1. Z homology of Z is unbounded
  2. F2 cohomology of the F2 Eilenberg MacLane Space (+ generalization to $\mathbb{F}_p$ should work)
