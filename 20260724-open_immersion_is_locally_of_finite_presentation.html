open immersion is locally of finite presentation

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be an open immersion. Then $f$ is a morphisms of locally finite presentation.

Proof

Let $\mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow Y$ be part of an affine open covering, then the pullback $\mathrm{Spec}(R) \times_{Y} X$ is of the form $D(F)$. Now use the standard affine zariski local epimorphism into a nonvanishing locus of an affine scheme so we have $\mathrm{Spec}(R_f) \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(R)$. In particular, $R_{f}$ is finitely generated R-Algebra generated by $\frac{1}{f}$.
