projection to the quotient sheaf is a surjection on stalks

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F} \subseteq \mathcal{G}$ be a subobject in the category $\mathrm{Shv}(X, \mathrm{Ab})$ of $\mathrm{Ab}$ (or $\mathrm{Mod}_{R}$) valued sheaves. Then the projection $\mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{F}/\mathcal{G}$ is a surjection on stalks.

Proof
