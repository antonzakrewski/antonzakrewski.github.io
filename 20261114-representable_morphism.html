representable morphism

Definition

Let $\eta: \mathcal{F} \rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ be a natural transformation between presheaves on a category $\mathcal{C}$. Then $\eta$ is said to be representable, if, for every $c \in \mathcal{C}$ and every map $よ(c) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}$, the pullback $\mathcal{G} \times_{\mathcal{F}} よ(c)$ is a representable functor.
