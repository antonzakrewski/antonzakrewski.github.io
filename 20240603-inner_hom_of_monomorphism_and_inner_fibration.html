inner hom of a monomorphism and inner fibration

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be an inner fibration and $\iota: A \rightarrow B$ a monomorphism Then

\begin{align*} <f,\iota>: \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(B,X) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(A,X) \times_{\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(A,Y)} \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(B,Y) \end{align*}

is also an inner fibration

Proof

By boxtensor as inner anodyne and component as inner-anodyne $\iota \boxtimes g$ is an inner fibration. Hence there exists a lift against the inner fibration $f$
