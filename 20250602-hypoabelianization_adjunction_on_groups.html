hypoabelianization adjunction on groups

Proposition

the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Grp}^{\mathrm{hypo}} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Grp} \end{align*}

admits a left adjoint, namely

\begin{align*} G \mapsto G/P \end{align*}

where $P$ is the maximal perfect subgroup.
