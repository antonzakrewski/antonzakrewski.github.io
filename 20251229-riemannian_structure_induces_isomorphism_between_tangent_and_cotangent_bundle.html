riemannian structure induces isomorphism between tangent and cotangent bundle

Proposition

Let $(M,g)$ be a riemannian manifold, then this induces an isomorphism

\begin{align*} T M \rightarrow T^*M \end{align*}

which is fiberwise given by

\begin{align*} f_p: TM_p \rightarrow& T^*M_p \\ v \mapsto& g(v,-) \end{align*}
