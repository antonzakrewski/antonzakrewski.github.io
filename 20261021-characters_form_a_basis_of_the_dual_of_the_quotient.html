characters form a basis of the dual of the quotient modulo the commutator

Proposition

Let $\{V_i\}$ be non-isomorphic simple A-representations over a finite dimensional K-Algebra $A$. Then $\chi_{V_i}: A/[A,A] \rightarrow k$ form a basis in the vector space $(A/[A,A])^*$.

Here $[A,A]$ is the span generated by the usual commutator bracket.
