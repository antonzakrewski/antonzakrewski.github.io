category with weak equivalences

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $\mathcal{W} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a wide subcategory Then $\mathcal{C}$ is said to be a category with weak equivlances, if

  1. $\mathcal{W}$ contains all isomorphism in $\mathcal{C}$
  2. $\mathcal{W}$ is closed under two out of three
