category of elementary objects over an object for an algebraic pattern

Definition

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be an algebraic pattern. Then we define

\begin{align*} \mathcal{O}_{O/}^{\mathrm{el}} \coloneqq \mathcal{O}^{\mathrm{el}} \times_{\mathcal{O}^{\mathrm{int}}} \mathcal{O}_{O/}^{\mathrm{int}} \end{align*}
