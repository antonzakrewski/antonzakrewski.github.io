vertex, edge connectivity and minimal degree in general not equal

Proposition

There exists a Graph $G$ with

\begin{align*} K(G) < \lambda(G) < \delta(G) \end{align*}

Proof

consider the undirected graph

where both copies of the complete graph $K_n$ together with their respective $L/R$ form $K_{n+1}$, i.e. the left $L$ is connected to each edge of the left $K_n$ and vice versa.

Then it is connected, hence at least $1$ vertex/knot connected.

Assume that $n > 3$

It is

  1. precisely $1$ knot connected, since we may remove $L$ and get
  2. precisely $2$ edge connected, since we may remove the edges from $L$ going to the right, i.e. both $*$
  3. has minimal degree $3$, since $*$ has the least degree (L,R and each vertex in $K_n$ have degree $n+1$)
