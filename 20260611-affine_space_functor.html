affine space algebraic functor is an affine scheme

Definition

Let $I$ be a set and $k$ a ring . Then the affine space algebraic functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathbb{A}^I \coloneqq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{CAlg}_k}(k[x_i \vert i \in I],-): \mathrm{CAlg}_k \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} \end{align*}

Remark

The universal property gives an equivalence $\mathbb{A}^I(R) \simeq R^I$
