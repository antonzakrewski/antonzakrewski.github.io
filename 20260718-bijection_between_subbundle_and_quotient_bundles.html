bijection between subbundle and quotient bundles

Proposition

Let $V$ be a vector bundle over a commutative ring $R$. Then the dual object functor induces bijections

\begin{align*} (-)^{\vee} \colon \{\textrm{subspaces of } V\}/~ \cong& \{\texrm{quotient spaces of } V^{\vee}\} \\ (U \hookrightarrow V) \mapsto& (V^{\vee} \twoheadrightarrow U^{\vee}) \\ (-)^{\vee} \colon \{\texrm{quotient spaces of } V\} \cong& \{\textrm{subspaces of } V^{\vee}\}/~ \\ (V \twoheadrightarrow W) \mapsto& (W^{\vee} \twoheadrightarrow V^{\vee}) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • characterization of subbundles of vector bundles over a ring & dual of a surjective module homomorphism into a vector bundle is universally injective
  • characterization of a quotient bundle of a vector bundle over a ring & universally injective map between vector bundles and surjective dual

where

  • subcategory of dualizable objects is equivalent to the opposite category
