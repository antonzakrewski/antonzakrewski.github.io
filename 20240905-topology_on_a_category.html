topology on a category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. A topology $\tau$ on $\mathcal{C}$ is given by a collection of covering sieves $\mathrm{Cov}_{\tau}(X)$ such that

  1. each representable functor $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,X) \in \mathrm{Cov}_{\tau}(X)$
  2. for each $f: X \rightarrow Y$ and each sieve $S \in \mathrm{Cov}_{\tau}(Y)$ the pullback $f^{*}(S) \in \mathrm{Cov}_{\tau}(Y)$
  3. Let $S \in \mathrm{Cov}_{\tau}(X)$ and $R \in \mathrm{Sieve}(X)$ be an arbitarry sieve. Suppose that for each $Y \in \mathcal{C}$ and each $f \in S(Y)$ we have that $f^*(R) \in \mathrm{Cov}_{\tau}(Y)$. Then $R \in \mathrm{Cov}_{\tau}(X)$
