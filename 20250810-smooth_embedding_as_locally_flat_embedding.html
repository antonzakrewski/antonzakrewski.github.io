smooth embedding as locally flat embedding

Proposition

Let $\varphi: M_m \rightarrow N_n$ be a smooth embedding. Then $\varphi$ is locally flat.

see:

  • embedding of topological manifolds

Proof

Let $m \in M$, then choose charts $U \subseteq N$ of $\varphi(m)$ and a neighbourhood $V \subseteq M$ of $m$ w.l.o.g. with $V \cap \varphi^{-1}[U] = V$. Then

Then since $\varphi$ is an smooth embedding, we have that $J_{\varphi}(m)$ is a vectorspace-monomorphism. So extend the differential of $\varphi: V \rightarrow U \hookrightarrow \mathbb{R}^n$ to a basis $X_{m+1},...,X_n$ and consider the map

\begin{align*} f: U \times \mathbb{R}^{n - m} \rightarrow& \mathbb{R}^n \\ (u,t_{m+1},...,t_n) \mapsto& \varphi(u) + \sum_{i=m+1}^n t_i X_i \end{align*}

it has a differential given by a vectorspace-isomorphism at $p$ so we may use the inverse function theorem to get an inverse map

\begin{align*} h: U' \rightarrow \tilde{U} \end{align*}

around neighbourhoods of $p, \varphi(p)$.

In particular $U' \rightarrow \tilde{U}$ determines a chart which is then locally flat.
