choice of generator of H_n(S^n)

Definition

Assume we have fixed generators $[S^n] \in H_n(S^n, \mathbb{Z})$, namely inductively by the suspension isomorphism of singular homology and once in life a generator of $\tilde{H_0}(S^0, \mathbb{Z})$.

Note that the suspension isomorphisms also requires a choice, so do it once in life (cf. choice of suspension isomorphism of singular homology)
