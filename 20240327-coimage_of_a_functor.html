coimage of a functor

Definition

(injective ?) Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor. Then the coimage of $\mathcal{F}$ is defined as category containing

  1. equivalence classes of objects $[A]$ for $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ with $A \sim B \Leftrightarrow \mathcal{F}(A) = \mathcal{F}(B)$
  2. equivalence classes of morhpisms $[f]$ for $f \sim g \Leftrightarrow \mathcal{F}(f) = \mathcal{F}(g)$

where composition is defined on representatives

Proof

composition independent on relatives

Suppose $[f_1] = [f_2]$, $[g_1] \sim [g_2]$ with suitable domain resp. codomain in $\mathrm{coim}(\mathcal{F})$ then we get

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(f_1 \circ g_1 ) =& \mathcal{F}(f_1) \circ \mathcal{F}(g_1) \\ =& \mathcal{F}(f_2) \circ \mathcal{F}(g_2) \\ =& \mathcal{F}(f_2 \circ g_2) \end{align*}

showing

\begin{align*} [f_1 \circ g_1] = [f_2 \circ g_2] \end{align*}
