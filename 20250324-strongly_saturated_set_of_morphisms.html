strongly saturated set of morphisms

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category which admits small infinity colimit. Suppose $S \subseteq \mathcal{C}_1$ is a collection of morphisms.

Then $S$ is said to be strongly saturated, if

  1. $S$ is closed under pushouts
  2. $S \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(\Delta^1, \mathcal{C})$ is closed under colimits
  3. $S$ satisfies infinity two out of three
