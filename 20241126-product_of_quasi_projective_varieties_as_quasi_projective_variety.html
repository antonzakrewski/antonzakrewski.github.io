product of quasi projective varieties

Proposition

Let $F$ be an algebraically closed field, $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}_k^m, Y \subseteq \mathbb{P}_k^n$ quasi-projective varieties. Then the categorical product is given as $X \times Y \subseteq \mathbb{P}_k^N$ for $N = (m + 1) (n + 1) - 1$ where

\begin{align*} X \times Y = \{[a_0b_0 : a_0 b_1 : ... : a_1 b_0 : ... : a_m b_0 : ... : a_m b_n] \vert [a_0 : ... : a_m] \in X, [b_0 : ... : b_n] \in Y \} \end{align*}

where the projection $\pi: X \times Y \rightarrow X$ is given by choosing for each ponit $Y = [b_0 : ... : b_m]$ an $b_i \neq 0$ and projecting

\begin{align*} \pi: [a_0 b_0 : ... : a_m b_n] \mapsto [a_0 b_i : ... : a_m b_i] \end{align*}
