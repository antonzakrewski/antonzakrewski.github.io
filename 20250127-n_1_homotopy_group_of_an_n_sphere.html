n+1 homotopy group of an n-sphere

Proposition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then

\begin{align*} \pi_{n+1}(S^n) =& \begin{cases} 0 & \mbox{if } n=0, n =1 \\ \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } n = 2 \\ \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
