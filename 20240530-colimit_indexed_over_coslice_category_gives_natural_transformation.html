colimit indexed over coslice category gives natural morphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $C \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ an object and $\mathcal{C} \downarrow C$ the coslice category Suppose $\mathcal{I}$ is a subcategory of the coslice category and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_I(\mathrm{Forget}(C_i)) \end{align*}

a colimit where $\mathrm{Forget}(-)$ denotes the forgetful functor from the coslice category, then there exists a natural morphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_I(\mathrm{Forget}(C_i)) \rightarrow C \end{align*}
