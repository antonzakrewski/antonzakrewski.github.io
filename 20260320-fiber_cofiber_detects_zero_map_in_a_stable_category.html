fiber / cofiber detects zero map in a stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category and $f: x \rightarrow y$ a map.

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is the zero morphism
  2. the cofiber of $f$ is given by $y \xrightarrow{\mathrm{id} \oplus 0} y \oplus \Sigma y$
  3. the fiber of $f$ is given by $\Omega y \oplus x \xrightarrow{0 \oplus \mathrm{id} } x$.

1) $\implies$ 2)

clear
