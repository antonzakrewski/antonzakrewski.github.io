functor category functor

Definition

The functor category functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}^{\mathrm{op}} \times \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty} (\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \mapsto \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}
