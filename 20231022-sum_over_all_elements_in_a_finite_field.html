sum over all elements in a finite field

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{F}_q$ be a finite field and $0 < i < \mathrm{char}(\mathbb{F}_q) - 1$. Then

\begin{align*} \sum_{x \in \mathbb{F}_q} x^i = 0 \end{align*}

where

Proof

By assumption, $\mathrm{char}(\mathbb{F}_q) \neq 0$. Thus we may assume, that for $\alpha \in \mathbb{F}_q \setminus \{0\}$ it holds

\begin{align*} \alpha \neq -\alpha \end{align*}

(cf. additiv selbstinverses Element eines Körpers und charakteristik 2)

Thus, we have $p^n - 1$, or an even amount (cf. Produkt ungerader Zahlen) of nontrivial elements (ignoring the $0$)
