right ore condition

Definition

Let $R$ be a ring and $S$ a subset. Then $S$ satisfies the right ore condition if

  1. the set $S$ contains the units of $R$ and is multiplicatively closed
  2. for every $x \in R, s \in S$, there exist elements $y \in R, t \in S$ such that $xt = sy$
  3. for every $x \in R, s \in S$ with $sx = 0$, there exists an element $t \in S$ such that $xt = 0$
