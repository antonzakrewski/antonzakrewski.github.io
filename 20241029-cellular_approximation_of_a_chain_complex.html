cellular approximation of a chain complex of modules with degreewise free modules

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $C_* \in \mathrm{Ch_{\geq}(\mathcal{A})$ be a chain complex with bounded below homology Then there exists a degreewise chain complex of free modules $P_*$ and a quasi-isomorphism

\begin{align*} P_* \rightarrow C_* \end{align*}

Proof

wlog assume that $H_{-1}(C_*)$ and below are $0$. Then choose a free module and $R^{\kappa} \twoheadrightarrow M$
