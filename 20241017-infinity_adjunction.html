adjunction

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be functors Then an adjunction is an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(\mathcal{L}(-), -) \cong \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-, \mathcal{R}(-)) \end{align*}
