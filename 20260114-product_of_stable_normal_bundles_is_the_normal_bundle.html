product of stable normal bundles is the normal bundle

Proposition

Let $M,M'$ be (smooth) Manifolds and $\nu_M, \nu_{M'}$ be the (smooth) stable normal bundles. Then the product

\begin{align*} \nu_{M} \times \nu_M: M \times M \rightarrow \mathrm{BO} \times \mathrm{BO} \rightarrow \mathrm{BO} \end{align*}

resp.

\begin{align*} # \nu_{M} \times \nu_M: M \times M \rightarrow \mathrm{BTop} \times \mathrm{BTop} \rightarrow \mathrm{BTop} \end{align*}

identifies with the stable normal bundle of $M \times M$
