strongly cartesian morphism

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $p: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a functor A strongly cartesian morphism is a morphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: y \rightarrow x \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

such that for each $z \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ the map

\begin{align*} \Phi: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(z,y) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(z,x) \times_{ \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(p(z), p(x))} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(p(z), p(y)) \psi \mapsto& (\varphi \circ \psi, p(\psi)) \end{align*}

is bijective
