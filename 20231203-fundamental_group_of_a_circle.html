pi_1 of a circle

Proposition

Given the 1-sphere $S^1$ and the fundamental group

\begin{align*} \pi_1(S^1) \cong \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

Proof

using Seifert van Kampen about groupoids.

Cover $S^1$ by two open intervals, such that the intersection is given by two disjoint intervals.

Then there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\tau_{\leq 1}(S^1), \mathcal{G}) \cong \mathrm{Fun}(\tau_{\leq 1}(0,1), \mathcal{G}) \times_{\mathrm{Fun}(\tau_{\leq 1}(0,1), \coprod \tau_{\leq 1}(0,1), \mathcal{G})} \mathrm{Fun}(\tau_{\leq 1} (0,1), \mathcal{G}) \end{align*}

Since $(0,1)$ is contractible, we conclude, that $\tau_{\leq 1}(0,1) \cong *$. Therefore

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\tau_{\leq 1}(S^1), \mathcal{G}) \cong& \mathrm{Fun}(*, \mathcal{G}) \times_{\mathrm{Fun}(*, \coprod *, \mathcal{G})} \mathrm{Fun}(*, \mathcal{G}) \\ \cong& \mathcal{G} \times^2_{\mathcal{G} \times \mathcal{G}} \mathcal{G} \end{align*}

for the diagonal functor

Here $\mathcal{G} \times^2_{\mathcal{G} \times \mathcal{G}} \mathcal{G}$ is the 2-categorical pullback of a functor diagram.

by natural equivalence between groupoids of functor category and 2-pullback, we conclude , that
