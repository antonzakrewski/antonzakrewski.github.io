power of a principal prime ideal as primary ideal

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $(p)$ a principal prime ideal. Then the power $(p^n)$ is a primary ideal.

Proof

Let $x,y \in A$ such that $x \cdot y \in (p^n)$. Then $p^n \mid (x \cdot y)$. Suppose $p^n \not\mid y$ then $p^n \mid x$ and hence $p^n \in (p^n)$. Otherwise $p \mid y$ or $p^n \mid y^n$
