Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a $\kappa$ small category, $\mathcal{D}$ a kappa cocomplete category. Then the yoneda embedding induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}}(\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D}) \cong \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}}(\mathrm{PSh}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D})$ is the full subcategory of colimit preserving functors
