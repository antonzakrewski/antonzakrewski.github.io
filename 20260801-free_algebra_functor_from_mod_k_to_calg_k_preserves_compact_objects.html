free algebra functor from Mod_k to CAlg_k preserves compact objects

Proposition

the free algebra functor $\mathrm{Mod}_k \rightarrow \mathrm{CAlg}_k$ preserevs compact objects.

Proof

follows from:

  • left adjoint and kappa compact objects
  • forgetful functor from CAlg_k to Mod_k preserves filtered colimits
