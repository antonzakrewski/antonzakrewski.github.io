estimates for the maximal norm of the unitalization

Proposition

Let $a \in A$ for a nonunital star algebra $A$. Then 1)

\begin{align*} \| \eta(a) \|_{\mathrm{max}, A^u} = \| a \|_{\mathrm{max}, A} \end{align*}
  1. $\vert \lambda \vert \leq \| (a, \lambda) \|_{\mathrm{max}, A^u} \leq \| a \|_{\mathrm{max}, A} + \vert \lambda \vert$
  2. $\| a \|_{\mathrm{max}, A} \leq 2 \| (a,\lambda) \|_{\mathrm{max}, A^u}$

Proof

1)

use that we can extend any representation

2)

$\vert \lambda \vert = \| (0, \lambda) \|$

use that $(0,1)$ is a projection, hence has norm $0$ or $1$. now $\| \lambda \cdot (0,1) \| = \vert \lambda \vert \cdot \| (0,1) \|$.

first

use the augmentation map $\epsilon: A^u \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$

second

use the triangle inequality and the claim above

3)

use

\begin{align*} \| a \|_{\mathrm{max}, A} =& \| (a,0) \_{\mathrm{max}, A^n} \\ \leq& \|(a, \lambda) \| + \| - \lambda \|_{\mathrm{max}} \\ \leq& 2 \cdot \| (a, \lambda) \| \end{align*}
