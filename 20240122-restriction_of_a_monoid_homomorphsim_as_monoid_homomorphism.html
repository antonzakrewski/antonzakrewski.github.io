restriction of a monoid homomorphism as monoid homomorphism

Proposition

Let \(M,N\) be monoids, $\varphi: M \rightarrow N$ a monoid-homomorphism and $L \subseteq N$ a submonoid such that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{im}(\varphi) \subseteq L \end{align*}

Then the map

\begin{align*} \varphi': M \rightarrow L \\ m \mapsto& \varphi(m) \end{align*}

is a welldefined monoid homomorphism

Proof

Properties of the monoid homomorphism are inherited by $\varphi'$, hence it follows from the definitions
