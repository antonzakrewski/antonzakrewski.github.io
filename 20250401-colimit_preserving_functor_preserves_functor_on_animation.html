coproduct preserving functor induces colimit preserving functor out of animation

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category which admits finite coproducts and $\mathcal{D}$ a sifted cocomplete category. Then the universal property of animation

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{sifted}}(\mathrm{Ani}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{A}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}}, \mathcal{A}) \end{align*}

restricts to an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}}(\mathrm{Ani}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{A}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\coprod}(\mathcal{C}^{1-\mathrm{sfp}}, \mathcal{A}) \end{align*}
