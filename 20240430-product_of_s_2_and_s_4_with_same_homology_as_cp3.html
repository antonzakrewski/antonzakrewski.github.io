homology of the product of S^2 and S^4

Proposition

Let $S^2, S^4$ be the n-spheres, then their product $S^2 \times S^4$ has homology

Proof

using kunneth formula for singular homology for

gives

as $H_*(S^4) \times H_*(S^2)$

Then it remains to calculate the Tor part.

But it consists of direct sums of tensored flat components. Hence by flat module tensored with another flat module and direct sum of flat modules as flat module both components are flat.

Therefore

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Tor}^{\mathbb{Z}}(H(X), H(Y)) = ... \rightarrow 0 \rightarrow 0 \rightarrow ... \end{align*}

and hence the map

\begin{align*} H_*(X) \otimes_\mathbb{Z} H_*(Y) \rightarrow H(X \times Y) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism (cf. sandwhiched by zero object and isomorphism)
