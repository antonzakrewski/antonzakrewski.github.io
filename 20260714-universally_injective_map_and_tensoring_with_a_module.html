universally injective map and tensoring with a module

Proposition

Let $\varphi: M \rightarrow M'$ be a map of \(R\)-modules. TFAE:

  1. $\varphi$ is universally injective map
  2. for every \(R\)-module $N$, the map $\varphi \otimes_{R} N: M \otimes_{R} N \rightarrow M' \otimes_{R} N$ is injective .
