cohomotopy of RP^n

Proposition

The cohomotopy of $\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^n$ is given by

\begin{align*} [\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^n, S^n] = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } n= \textrm{ odd} \\ \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } n \textrm{ even} \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

Consider the Eilenberg-MacLane space $K(\mathbb{Z},n)$. Then $K(\mathbb{Z},n)$ admits a CW-structure, where we attach cells of dimension $n+2$ and above. then by cellular approximation shows that

\begin{align*} [\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^n, K(\mathbb{Z},n)] \cong& [\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^n, \mathrm{Sk}_{n+1}(K(\mathbb{Z},n))] \\ \cong& [\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^n, S^n] \end{align*}

so by representability of cohomology this yields

\begin{align*} [\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^n, S^n] = H^*(\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^n, \mathbb{Z}) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } n = \textrm{odd} \\ \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } n = \textrm{even} \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}
