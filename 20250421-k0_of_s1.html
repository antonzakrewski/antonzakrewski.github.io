K0 of S1

Proposition

The topological k0 of the 1-sphere is given as

\begin{align*} K^0_{\mathbb{K}}(S^1) =& \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} \oplus \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } \mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R} \\ \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } \mathbb{K} = \mathbb{C} \\ \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } \mathbb{K} = \mathbb{H} \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • k0 of a cw complex and dimension

where classification of topological line bundles are given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{hTop}}(S^1,\mathbb{K}^{\infty}) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } \mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R} \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

which generate the nontrivial part.

Furthermore by direct sum of two moebius bundles as trivial bundle we conclude that the element representing the moebius strip is \(2\)-torsion
