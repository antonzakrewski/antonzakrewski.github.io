1-categorical yoneda is natural in both variables

Proposition

The natural isomorphism of the yoneda lemma is natural in both variables, i.e.

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(よ(x), \mathcal{F}) \simeq \mathcal{F} \end{align*}

is natural in the first variable with respect to maps in $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}$ and natural in the second with respect to natural transformation $\mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \mathcal{G}$

Proof

Let $f: Y \rightarrow X$ be a morphsim in $\mathcal{C}$ and $\eta: \mathcal{F} \Rightarrow \mathcal{G}$ a natural transformation

Now the top square commutes, since $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})}(-,-)$ is a bifunctor and precomposition with a map is a natural transformation, i.e. a morphism in the presheaf category, which follows from currying. Now the bottom square commutes since $\eta$ is a natural transformation of presheaves.

So it suffices to prove that the sides commute. we prove that the left side and the back side commute, the front and right side follow from the same argument by replacing $よ(x)$ with $よ(y)$ and $\mathcal{F}$ with $\mathcal{G}$ resp.
