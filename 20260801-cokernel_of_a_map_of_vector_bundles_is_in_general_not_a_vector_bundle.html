cokernel of a map of vector bundles is in general not a vector bundle

Proposition

Let $\varphi: V \rightarrow W$ be a map between vector bundles. Then the cokernel is in general not a vector bundle.

Proof

consider $\mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\cdot 2} \mathbb{Z}$. where projective modules are torsion free module.
