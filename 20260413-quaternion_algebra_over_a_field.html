quaternion algebra over a field

Definition

Let $k$ be a field with $\mathrm{char}(k) \neq 2$. Then for $a,b \in k^{\times}$ let us denote $(a,b) = (a,b)_k = \begin{pNiceMatrix} a,b \\ k\end{pNiceMatrix}$ the \(4\)-dimensional \(k\)-vector space with the basis $1,\mathrm{i}, \mathrm{j}, \mathrm{ij}$ and \(k\)-linear associative multiplication determined by $ji = -ij, i^2 = a, j^2 = b$
