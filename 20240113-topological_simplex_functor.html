topological simplex functor

Definition / Proposition

Let $\Delta$ be the simplex category, then the topological simplex functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \Delta_{\mathrm{Top}}: \Delta \rightarrow& \mathrm{Top} \\ \Delta^n \mapsto& \Delta_{\mathrm{Top}}^n \sigma_i \mapsto& \sigma_i \\ \delta_i \mapsto& \delta_i \\ \mathrm{id} \mapsto& \mathrm{id} \end{align*}

where

  1. $\Delta_{\mathrm{Top}}^n$ is the topological simplex
  2. $\sigma_i$ is the degeneracy map of a topological simplex
  3. $\delta_i$ is the face map of a topological simplex
