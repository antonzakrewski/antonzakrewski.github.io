colimit of transition functors and right adjoints in PrL

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_i$ be a filtered diagram of presentable categories and $\mathcal{C}$ be the colimit in $\mathrm{Pr^L}$. Let $\mathcal{F}_{i,j}: \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_j$ be the transition functors and $\mathcal{F}_i: \mathcal{C}_i \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ the functor to the colimit. Let $\mathcal{G}_{i,j}, \mathcal{G}_i$ be the corresponding right adjoints.

Then there exists an natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim} \mathcal{F}_i \mathcal{G}_i \simeq \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{C}} \end{align*}

where the LHS is an endomorphism in the functor category.

Proof

Recall that $\mathcal{C} \simeq \lim_{i} \mathcal{C}_i$ after passing to the right adjoints Now let $x,y \in \mathcal{C}$, then we have

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathrm{colim}_{i} \mathcal{F}_i \mathcal{G}_i(x),y) \simeq \lim_i \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{F}_i \mathcal{G}_i(x), y) \\ \simeq \lim_i \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}_i}(\mathcal{G}_i(x), \mathcal{G}_i(y)) \\ \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \end{align*}

where the last step uses Hom in the limit category.
