infinity derived category of a grothendieck category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a grothendieck category Then the derived infinity category of $\mathcal{A}$ is defined as dg nerve

\begin{align*} N_{\mathrm{dg}}(\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})^{\circ}) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})^{\circ}$ is the category of fibrant chain complexes
