R-dualizing spectrum

Definition / Proposition

Let $X$ be an anima, $R$ ring spectrum. Then there exists a unique object $D_{X}^R \in \mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{Mod}(R))$ such that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_X (-) \simeq \mathrm{colim} (- \otimes_R D_X^R) \in \mathrm{Mod}(R) \end{align*}

Proof

existence

follows from morita theory of infinite module categories

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{Mod}(R)) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}^L(\mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{Mod}(R)), \mathrm{Mod}(R)) \mathcal{F} \mapsto r_!(- \otimes_R \mathcal{F}) = \mathrm{colim} (- \otimes_{R} \mathcal{F}) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{lim}_X$ is a colimit preserving functor (cf. compact limit in a stable infinity category commutes with colimits), thus by the morita equivalence yields a functor $D_X^R$ with

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}_X (-) = r_*(-): \mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{Mod}(R)) \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}(R) \end{align*}
