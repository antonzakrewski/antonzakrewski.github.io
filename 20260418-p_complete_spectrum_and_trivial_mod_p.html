p-complete spectrum and trivial mod p implies trivial spectrum

Proposition

Let $X$ be a p-complete spectrum such that $X/p \simeq 0$. Then $X \simeq 0$.

Proof

If $X/p \simeq 0$, then multiplication with $p$ is an equivalence. In other words, $X \in \mathrm{Sp}[p^{-1}]$ and p-complete spectrum and mapping out of an R-local spectrum shows that $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X,X) \simeq 0$ or equivalently $X \simeq 0$.
