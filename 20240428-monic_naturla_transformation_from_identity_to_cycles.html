monic natural transformation from cycles to identity

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes. Then there exists a monic natural transformation

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathrm{Cyl} \Rightarrow& \mathrm{id}_{\mathcal{A}} \\ (A_n) \mapsto& (\iota_n: \mathrm{ker}(\partial_n) \hookrightarrow A_n) \end{align*}

for the cycle functor of chain complexes

Proof

natural transformation

follows from the naturality of the kernel 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{ker}(\partial_n) \arrow[]{d}{} \arrow[]{r}{}& A_n \arrow[]{d}{}\\
\mathrm{ker}(\partial_{n-1}) \arrow[]{r}{} & A_{n-1} \arrow[]{d}{}\\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

monic

follows from:

  • map out of kernel as monomorphism
  • natural transformation and morphisms as monomorphism
