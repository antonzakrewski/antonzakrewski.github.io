quotient of a covering like, free group action from a finite group on a manifold is a topological manifold

Proposition

Let $M$ be a Manifold and $G \curvearrowright M$ a free covering-like group action for a finite group $G$. Then the quotient $M/\sim$ is also a Manifold

Proof

a) locally euclidean

  • orbital projection of a free action as covering map and covering-like

so $M/\sim$ is locally like $M$ and hence locally euclidean

b) hausdorff

  • follows from covering map from a hausdorff space and finite fibre

c) second countable

  • follows from:
    • manifold with compact exhaustion implies second countability & second countable, locally compact space admits compact exhaustion
    • where any compact exhaustion of $M$ induces a compact exhaustion of $M/\sim$ via the projection (cf. image of compact space is compact)
