tensor product of tor independent modules

Proposition

Let $R \rightarrow S$ be a ring-homomorphism and $M$ an \(R\)-module and $N$ an \(S\)-module. Suppose that

  1. $M$ and $S$ are tor independent over $R$
  2. $M \otimes_{R}^{\heartsuit} S$ and $N$ are tor independent over $S$

Then $M$ and $N$ are tor independent over $R$

Proof

follows from associativity of the derived tensor product:

\begin{align*} (M \otimes_{R} S) \otimes_{S} N \simeq M \otimes_{R} (S \otimes_{S} N) \simeq M \otimes_{R} N \end{align*}
