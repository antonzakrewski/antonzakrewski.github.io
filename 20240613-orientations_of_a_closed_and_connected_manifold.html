orientationability of a closed and connected manifold

Proposition

Let \(M\) be a closed connected manifold and $R$ a ring TFAE:

  1. $M$ is R-orientable
  2. the natural transformation
\begin{align*} \rho: \underline{H_n(X;R)} \Rightarrow \nu_x \end{align*}

for the orientation functor construction is a natural isomorphism

  1. $H_n(X;R) \cong R$

Proof
