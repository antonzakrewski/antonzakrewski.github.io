saturation of a set with respect to a multiplicatively closed set as superset

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $A \subseteq R$ a subset and $S \subseteq R$ a multiplicatively closed set. Then

\begin{align*} A \subseteq A^S \end{align*}

Proof

Let $a \in A$, then by assumption $1 \in S$ and hence

\begin{align*} a = 1 \cdot a \in A^S \end{align*}
