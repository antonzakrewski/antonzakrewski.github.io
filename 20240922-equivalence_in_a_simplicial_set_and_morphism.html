equivalence in a simplicial set and morphism

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be simplicial sets, $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a morphism of simplicial sets and $\sigma: \Delta^1 \rightarrow X$ an equivalence. Then so is $f(\sigma) \in Y_1$

Proof

follows from:

  • homotopy category functor of sSet
  • isomorphisms under functors
