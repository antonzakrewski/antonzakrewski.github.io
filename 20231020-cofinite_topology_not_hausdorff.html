cofinite topology not hausdorff for an infinite set

Proposition

Let $X$ be an infinite set, i.e. $\vert X \vert = \infty$. Then the cofinite topology on $X$ is not Hausdorff

Proof

Suppose there exist two nonempty disjoint open sets $O_1,O_2$ Then

\begin{align*} O_1 \cap O_2 = \emptyset \end{align*}

by de morgan, we conclude

\begin{align*} X =& X \setminus \empytset \\ =& X \setminus (O_1 \cap O_2) \\ =& X \setminus O_1 \cup X \setminus O_2 \end{align*}

which is a contradiction to $X \setminus O_1, X \setminus O_2$ finite
