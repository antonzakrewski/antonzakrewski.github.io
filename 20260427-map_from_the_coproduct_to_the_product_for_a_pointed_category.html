map from the coproduct to the product for a pointed category

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a pointed category and $X_i$ a collection of objects. Suppose that the product and coproduct exists, then there exists a canonical, natural map

\begin{align*} \coprod_{i \in I} X_i \rightarrow \prod_{i \in I} X_i \end{align*}

induced by $(\mathrm{id}_j, 0): X_j \rightarrow X_j \times (\prod_{i \neq j} X_j) \simeq \prod_{i \in I} X_i$
