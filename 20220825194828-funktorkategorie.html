functor category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories. then the functor category $\mathcal{D}^{\mathcal{C}}$ is defined as category containing

  1. functors $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ as objects
  2. natural transformations as morphisms
