unit constraint of a strong monoidal functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be monoidal category, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a monoidal functor.

Then the unit constraint is a designated isomorphism

\begin{align*} \upsilon: \mathcal{F}(1) \rightarrow 1 \end{align*}
