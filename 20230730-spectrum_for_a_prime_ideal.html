spectrum for the localization at a prime ideal

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ a prime ideal and $A_{\mathfrak{p}}$ the localization at $\mathfrak{p}$. Then the zariski spectrum with the Zariski topology is given as subspace topology

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Spec}(A_{\mathfrak{p}}) = \{\mathfrak{q} \in \mathrm{Spec}(A) \vert \mathfrak{q} \cap \mathfrak{p} = \{0\}\} \end{align*}

Proof

correspondence theorem for prime ideals and localization
