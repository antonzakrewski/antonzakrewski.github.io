generalized presheaf construction

Proposition

Let $K$ be a collection of simplicial sets (small in some universe), $\mathcal{C}$ an infinity category and $\mathcal{R} = \{\overline{p}_{\alpha}: K^{\triangle}_{\alpha} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}\}$ a collection of infinity diagrams in $\mathcal{C}$. Assume that $K_{\alpha} \in K$.

Then there exists a new infinity category

\begin{align*} \mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{R}}^{K}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

and an infinity functor

\begin{align*} \iota: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{R}}^{K}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

such that

  1. $\mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{R}}^{K}$ admits \(K\)-indexed colimits
  2. for each infinity category $\mathcal{D}$ admitting \(K\)-indexed colimits, composition
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}_{K}(\mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{R}}^K(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}_{\mathcal{R}}(\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

of \(K\)- colimits preserving functors resp. functors which send diagrams in $\mathcal{R}$ to colimits.

informal

$\mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{R}}^K$ is freely generated by $\mathcal{C}$ under \(K\)-indexed colimits and the relation $\mathcal{R}$
