multiplicative spectral sequence and ring isomorphism between graded part

Proposition

Let $X\in \mathrm{CAlg}\mathrm{Fil}(\mathrm{Sp})$ be a E_infinity Algebra in category of filtered spectra. Let $E_{*,*}^*$ be the multiplicative spectral sequences associated to it.

Then we get a multiplicative isomorphism between

  1. the $E^{\infty}$ page
  2. the associated grading of $\pi_*(X)$

Proof
