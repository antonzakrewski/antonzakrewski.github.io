categorical issues when left kan extending n-categorical yoneda into m-categories

Remark

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, where $\mathcal{C}$ is an \((n,1)\)-category and $\mathcal{D}$ is a \((m,1)\)-category. Suppose $\mathcal{D}$ is also cocomplete / complete.

Let $\mathcal{P}^l(\mathcal{C}) = \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An}_{\leq n})$. Then for $l \geq n$, the yoneda embedding $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ restricts to an embedding $\mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})^l$ to the \((l,1)\)-categorical yoneda embedding.

Moreover, we may left kan extension / right kan extension any functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ along the \(l\)-categorical yoneda embedding (pointwise left kan extension / pointwise right kan extension). Furthermore for $l,l' \in [n,\infty]$, the left kan extensions of the \(l'\) and \(l\)-categorical yoneda embedding agree on the category $\mathcal{P}^l(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathcal{P}^{l'}(\mathcal{C})$ (cf. left kan extension along a fully faithful functor commutes up to natural equivalence / right kan extension and commutative square commutes up to natural transformation).

In both cases, the colimit should be taken over the unstraightening

But for $m > l$, $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})^l$ is not the free, \((m,1)\)-categorical cocompletion of $\mathcal{C}$, hence there does not exist (in general ?) an equivalence $\mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{colim}}(\mathcal{P}^l(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$, we can only identify $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ with a full subcategory of $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{P}^l(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D})$.

I think that for any functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}_{\leq n}$, the unstraightening should be an \(n\)-category (where we use the $n$ in $\mathrm{An}_{\leq n}$ and $\mathcal{C}$ as \(n,1\)-category).

Summary

  1. don't have the presheaf category arises as free cocompletion
  2. but equivalent functors kan extend to equivalent functors
