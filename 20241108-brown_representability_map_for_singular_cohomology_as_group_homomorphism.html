brown representability map for singular cohomology as group homomorphism

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $A$ an abelian group and $n \geq 1$. Then the brown representability

\begin{align*} \chi_n^A: [X,K(A,n)] \rightarrow H^n(X,A) \end{align*}

is a group-homomorphism with respect to the canonical group structure of $H^n(X,A)$ and the group structure of [X,K(A,n)]$ defined hom into an eilenberg maclane space as abelian group

\begin{align*} m: K(A,n) \times K(A,n) \rightarrow K(A,n) \end{align*}
