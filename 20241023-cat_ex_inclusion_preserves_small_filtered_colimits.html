Cat Ex inclusion preserves small filtered colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}^{\mathrm{Ex}}$ be Cat Ex, then the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}^\mathrm{Ex} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty} \end{align*}

into the infinity category of infinity categories preserves small filtered infinity colimit

Proof
