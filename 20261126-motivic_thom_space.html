motivic thom space

Definition

Let $X \in \mathrm{Sm}_{/S}$ and $\mathcal{E}$ a <finite locally free sheaf> on $X$. Then hte thom space of $\mathcal{E}$ is

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Th}_{X}(\mathcal{E}) = \mathbb{P}(\mathcal{E} \oplus \mathcal{O}) / \mathbb{P}(\mathcal{E}) \in \mathcal{P}(\mathrm{Sm}_{/S})_* \end{align*}
