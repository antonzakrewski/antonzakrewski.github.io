idempotent completion of idem is idem plus

Proposition

the idempotent completion of the category $\mathrm{Idem}$ is given by Idem plus.

Proof

using: idempotent completion of a 1-category is a 1-category

So let $\mathcal{C}$ be any idempotent complete category. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Idem}^+, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Idem}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is an equivalence of categories
