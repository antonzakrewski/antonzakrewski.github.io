bredon cohomology

Definition

Let $G$ be a (discrete) group, $M: \mathrm{Or}_G^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}$ a contravariant coefficient system. Then bredon cohomologoy with coefficients in $M$ is defined as right kan extension

along the yoneda embedding

In particular it is the unique colimit preserving functor out of $\mathrm{An}_G$ mapping $\mathrm{Orb}_G$ to $M$
