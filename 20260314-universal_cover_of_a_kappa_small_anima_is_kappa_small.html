universal cover of a kappa small anima is kappa small

Proposition

Let $\kappa > \omega_0$ be a regular cardinal and $X$ a kappa small anima. Then the universal cover is also $\kappa$ small.

Proof

Observe that $\pi_1(X)$ is kappa small (cf. kappa small anima has kappa small pi_1). Hence the covering map of a cw complex induces CW structure on the domain shows the claim.
