bilinear functor from product to lurie tensor product

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be presentable categories. Then there exists a canonical functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \otimes \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

which commutes with small colimits in both variables.
