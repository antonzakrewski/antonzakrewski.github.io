quotient group of a p-group as p-group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a p-group and $N \subseteq G$ a normal subgroup. Then the quotient group

\begin{align*} G/N \end{align*}

is also a p-group

Proof

Let $[g] \in G/N$. Then choose a representative $g \in G$ and an $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with

\begin{align*} g^{p^n} = 1 \end{align*}

Hence

\begin{align*} [g]^{p^n} =& [g^{p^n}] \\ =& [1] \\ \end{align*}
