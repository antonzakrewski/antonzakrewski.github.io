preadditive infinity category and homotopy category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category with finite coproducts and finite products.

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ is a preadditive infinity-category
  2. the homotopy category is a preadditive 1-category

Proof

definitions +

  • projection to the homotopy category preserves products
  • projection to the homotopy category preserves coproducts
