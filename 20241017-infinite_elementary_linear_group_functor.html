infinite elementary linear group functor

Definition

the infinite elementary linear group functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{E}: \mathrm{Ring} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Grp} \\ R \mapsto& \mathrm{E}(R) \\ (f: R \rightarrow S) \mapsto& (\mathrm{E}(f): \mathrm{E}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{E}(S)) \end{align*}
