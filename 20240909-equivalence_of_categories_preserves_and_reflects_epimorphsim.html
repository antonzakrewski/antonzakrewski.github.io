equivalence of categories preserves and reflects epimorphisms

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be part of an equivalence of categories Then $\mathcal{F}$

  1. preserves epimorphisms
  2. reflects epimorphisms

Proof

using fully faithful and essentially surjective functor with Axiom of Choice implies an adjoint equivalence

  1. faithful functor reflects epimorphisms
  2. left adjoint preserves epimorphisms
