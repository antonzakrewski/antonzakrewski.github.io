fp homology and cohomology of M(Fp,n)

Proposition

Let $n > 0$ and $p$ prime. Then the $\mathbb{F}_p$ homology / cohomology of the moore spectrum $M(\mathbb{F}_p,n)$ is given by

\begin{align*} H_*(M(\mathbb{F}_p,n), \mathbb{F}_p) =& \begin{cases} \mathbb{F}_p & \mbox{if } * = 0,n,n+1 \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \\ H^*(M(\mathbb{F}_p,n), \mathbb{F}_p) =& \begin{cases} \mathbb{F}_p & \mbox{if } * = 0,n,n+1 \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from universal coefficient Theorem for cohomology & universal coefficient theorem for singular homology
