join of anima spheres as sphere

Proposition

there exists an equivalence

\begin{align*} S^n \star S^m \simeq S^{n+m+1} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from join of pointed anima as suspension of the smash product

\begin{align*} S^n \star S^m \simeq& \Sigma( S^n \wedge S^m) \\ \simeq& \Sigma(S^{n+m}) \\ \simeq& S^{n+m+1} \end{align*}
