epimorphisms mirrored by pullbacks in a regular category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a regular category, $A,B,C \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and

a pullback where

\begin{align*} f: A \twoheadrightarrow C \end{align*}

is an epimorphism

Then so is

\begin{align*} \pi_B: A \times_C B \twoheadrightarrow B \end{align*}

Proof
