specialization and open subset

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $x,x' \in X$.

TFAE:

  1. $x$ is a specialization of $x'$
  2. each neighbourhood of $x$ also contains $x'$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Suppose there exists a neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ not containing $x'$. Then $X \setminus U$ is a closed subset containing $x'$ and hence $\overline{ \{x'\}} \subseteq X \setminus U$ or

\begin{align*} x \not \in \overline{\{x'\}} \end{align*}

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $A$ be a closed subset containing $x'$. Then it follows that $x \in A$ as otherwise $X \setminus A$ would be an open neighbourhood of $x$ without $x'$.

But then the closure is the intersection of those closed subsets $A$. But all $A$ contain $x$, hence $x \in \overline{ \{x'\}} = \bigcap A$
