antilinear map and restriction of scalars

Proposition

Let $V_1,V_2$ be $\mathbb{C}$-vector spaces. Then an antilinear map $\varphi: V_1 \rightarrow V_2$ is precisely given by a map

  1. $\varphi: \overline{V_1} \rightarrow V_2$ or
  2. $\varphi: V_1 \rightarrow \overline{V_2}$

where $\overline{-}$ denotes the conjugate complex structure, that it the object under the restriction of scalars $\overline{-}: \mathbb{C} \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$.

Remark

immediately shows that the composition of two antilinear maps is antilinear
