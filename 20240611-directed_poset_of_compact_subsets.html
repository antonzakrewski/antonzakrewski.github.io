directed poset of compact subspaces

Definition / Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space. Then the upward directed poset of compact subspaces is defined as set

\begin{align*} \mathcal{K}_{X} \coloneqq \{C \subseteq X \vert C \textrm{ compact}\} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • finite union of compact sets
