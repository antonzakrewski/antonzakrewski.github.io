inclusion of the image of a strongly continuous functor out of a dualizable category is strongly continuous

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be stable presentable categories, where $\mathcal{C}$ is a dualizable category. Suppose $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is a strongly continuous functor. Then the inclusion $\mathrm{Im}(\mathcal{F}) \hookrightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is a strongly continuous functor.
