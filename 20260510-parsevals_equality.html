parsevals equality

Proposition

Let $X$ be a Hilbert space and $\{e_\alpha\}_{\alpha \in J}$ a orthonormal basis. Then $x = \sum_{\alpha \in J} \langle e_{\alpha}, x\rangle e_{\alpha}$, where at most countably many terms are $\neq 0$ and

\begin{align*} \| x \|^2 = \sum_{\alpha \in J} \vert \langle e_{\alpha}, x\rangle \vert \end{align*}
