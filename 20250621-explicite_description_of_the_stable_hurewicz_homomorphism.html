explicit description of the stable hurewicz homomorphism for a suspension spectrum

Proposition

Let $X$ be a suspension spectrum. Then the stable hurewicz homomorphism is given by

\begin{align*} \pi_n^\mathrm{st}(X) \cong \mathrm{colim}_{*+n} \pi_*(\Sigma^* X) \rightarrow H_n(X, \mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

induced via the classical hurewicz homomorphism which is compatible with Hurewicz Homomorphism and suspension.

Proof
