graded ring homomorphism as additive functor

Proposition

Let $M$ be a monoid and $R = \bigoplus_{m \in m} R_m, S = \bigoplus_{m \in M} S_m$ graded rings.

Then a graded ring homomorphism is equivalently described by an additive functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: BR \rightarrow BS \end{align*}

between the categorifications of graded ring such that for $l \in M$ 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{Fun}([0,1], BR) \arrow[]{r}{\mathcal{F}}& \mathrm{Fun}([0,1], BS)
\mathrm{Fun}([0,1], BR) \arrow[]{r}{\mathcal{F}} \arrow[]{u}{\mathcal{L}_l^{BR}}& \mathrm{Fun}([0,1], BS) \arrow[]{u}{\mathcal{L}_l^{BS}}
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

commutes for the arrow categories

Proof

ring homomorphism

unit

Note that $1_R = \mathrm{id}_{BR} \in \mathrm{Hom}_{BR}(1,1)$ hence $\mathcal{F}(\mathrm{id}_{BR}) = \mathrm{id}_{BS} = 1_S$

additive

follows from the assumption, that $\mathcal{F}$ is additive on each component

multiplicative

Using distributivity bla bla it suffices to show this for homogeneous elements.

Given $r_i, r_j$ this follows from

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(r_i \cdot r_j) =& \mathcal{F}(\mathcal{L}_j^{BR}(r_i) \circ r_j) \\ =& \mathcal{F}(\mathcal{L}^{BR}_j(r_i)) \circ \mathcal{F}(r_j) \\ =& \mathcal{L}_j^{BS}\mathcal{F}(r_i) \circ \mathcal{F}(r_j) \\ =& \mathcal{F}(r_i) \cdot \mathcal{F}(r_j) \end{align*}

graded compatibility

follows from

\begin{align*} R_m = \mathrm{Hom}_{BR}(1,m) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{BS}(1,m) \subseteq BS \end{align*}
