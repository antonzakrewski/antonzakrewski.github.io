infinity ind object

Definition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal, $\mathcal{C}$ be a small infinity category, $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ the infinity presheaf category and $\mathcal{F} \in \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ a infinity presheaf. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be a \(\kappa\)-ind object, if it is a kappa filtered infinity colimit of representables
