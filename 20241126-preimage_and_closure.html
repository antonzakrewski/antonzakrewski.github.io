preimage and closure

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be topological spaces and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a map

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is continuous
  2. for any subset $A \subseteq Y$ it holds that
\begin{align*} \overline{f^{-1}[A]} \subseteq f^{-1}[\overline{A}] \end{align*}

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

By preimage of a closed set and continuous map $f^{-1}[\overline{A}]$ is closed and contains $f^{-1}[A]$. Hence it follows by Abschluss als kleinste abgeschlossene Menge

2) $\implies$ 1)

Then $f$ is continuous if and only if $f^{-1}[A]$ is closed for any closed subset $A$. We also have

\begin{align*} f^{-1}[A] \subseteq \overline{\overline{f}^{-1}[A]}\subseteq f^{-1}[\overline{A}] = f^{-1}[A] \end{align*}

showing that $f^{-1}[A] = \overline{f^{-1}[A]}$ where the RHS is closed
