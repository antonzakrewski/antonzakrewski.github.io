symmetric monoidal localization

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}^{\otimes}$ be a symmetric monoidal infinity category Then a localization compatible with the symmetric monoidal structure is defined as right bousfield localization such that for each local equivalence

\begin{align*} X \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

and for each object $Z \in \mathcal{C}$, the induced morphism

\begin{align*} X \otimes Z \rightarrow Y \otimes Z \end{align*}

is also a local equivalence
