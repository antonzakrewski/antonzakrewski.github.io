left derived functor as half exact functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, $\mathcal{F}_n: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ a right exact functor. Suppose the left derived functor $\{\mathcal{LF}_n\}$ exists, then for each $n \in \mathbb{N}$

\begin{align*} \mathcal{LF}_n: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B} \end{align*}

is half exact
