finite cw complex has infinitely many stable homotopy groups or is acyclic

Proposition

Let $X$ be a finite CW-Complex. Then $X$ either has trivial stable homotopy groups or infinitely many nontrivial.

Proof

follows from:

  • finite, nontrivial spectrum has infinitely many nontrivial homotopy groups
  • suspension spectrum of a finite cw complex is finite
