bijection of coextension problems for a fully faithful right adjoint

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be adjoints $\mathcal{F} \dashv \mathcal{G}$, where $\mathcal{G}$ is fully faithful

Then there exists a bijection between coextension problems

Proof

follows from:

  • reduction of a coextension problem via adjoint
  • reduction of coextension problems for a full right adjoint

where both are mutually inverse (note that the choice is unique)
