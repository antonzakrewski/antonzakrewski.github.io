functor from the spanier whitehead category to spectra

Definition

Let $\mathrm{Sp} = \mathrm{colim}_{\mathbb{Z}}^{\mathrm{Pr}^L} \mathrm{An}_*$ and $\mathrm{SW} = \mathrm{colim}_{\mathbb{Z}}^{\mathrm{Cat}} \mathrm{An}_*$. Then the functor from the spanier whitehead category is defined as induced map $\mathrm{SW} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}$ for the forgetful functor $\mathrm{Pr}^L \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}$
