fiberwise regularity implies flatness

Proposition

Let $A \rightarrow B$ be a local ring map of local rings, essentially of finite presentation. Let $P$ be a finitely generated projective module $B$ (or equivalently, $B^n$).

Let $u: P \rightarrow B$ be \(B\)-linear map.

Suppose $B$ is flat over $A$ and $\overline{u}: P/mP \rightarrow B/mB$ is regular. Then $B/u(P)$ is flat over $A$.

Proof
