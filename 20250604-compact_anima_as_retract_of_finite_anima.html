compact anima as retract of finite anima

Proposition

Any compact anima is precisely a retract of a finite anima

Proof

finite anima is compact

observe that pointed anima n-sphere is compact. Moreover, small colimit of compact objects are compact again

retract of finite anima is compact

follows from compact object closed under retracts

compact object is retract of finite

Let $X$ be a compact anima and $\mathrm{An}^{\mathrm{fin}}$ be the full subcategory of finite anim. Observe that anima generated by cofibers, coproducts and sequential colimits and point, in other words:

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ind}_{\omega}(\mathrm{An}^{\mathrm{fin}}) \simeq \mathrm{An} \end{align*}

But any object in $\mathrm{Ind}_{\omega}(\mathrm{An}^{\mathrm{fin}})$ -- in particular $X$ -- is a filtered colimit of representables or finite anima. So let $X \simeq \mathrm{colim}_{I} X_i$ for finite $X_i$. Then compact object as filtered colimit and retract shows that $X$ is a retract of some $X_i$ and hence a retract of finite anima.
