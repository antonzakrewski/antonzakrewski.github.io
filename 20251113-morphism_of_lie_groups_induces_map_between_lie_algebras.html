morphism of lie groups induces morphism between lie algebras

Proposition

Let $G,H$ be a lie groups and $\varphi: G \rightarrow H$ be a smooth group-homomorphism. Then the differential

\begin{align*} Df_{e}: T_{e} G \rightarrow T_{e}H \end{align*}

is a lie algebra homomorphism

Proof

Let $X$ be a left invariant vector field on $G$. Then $D(f_e)(X_{e})$ extends to a left invariant vector field $Y$ on $H$ (cf. correspondence between left invariant vector fields on a lie group and the tangent space).

Then $X$ is f-related to $Y$ so

\begin{align*} (Df)_{g}(X_g) =& \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}_{\vert t = 0} f(g \cdot \gamma_{e}^X(t)) \\ =& f(g) \cdot \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}_{\vert t = 0} f(\gamma^X \mathrm{e}^{t})\\ =& f(g) \cdot (Df)_{e} (X_e) \\ =& Y_{f(g)} \end{align*}
