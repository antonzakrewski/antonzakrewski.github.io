multiplication with an element is symmetric monoidal for a ring

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring (even don't need unit element), then for each $r \in R$, the map

\begin{align*} \cdot r: (R,+) \rightarrow& (R,+) \\ s \mapsto& s \cdot r \end{align*}

is a symmetric monoidal functor on monoid regarded as monoidal category.

Proof

follows as the unit is $0$ and multiplication $r \cdot 0 = 0$. Furthermore, distributivity shows $r(s \otimes s') = r s \otimes r s'$.
