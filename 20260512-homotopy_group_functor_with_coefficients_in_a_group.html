homotopy group functor with coefficients in a group

Definition

Let $G$ be a abelian group and $n \geq 2$ then we define

\begin{align*} \pi_n(-,G) = \pi_0(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(M(G,n),-)): \mathrm{An}_* \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} \end{align*}

where $M(G,n)$ is a simply connected Moore space. see:

  • homotopy group functor with coefficients is a group/abelian group
  • nilpotent moore space is unique up to non unique equivalence / existence of a moore space
