tensor product of functors for a monoidal category

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a monoidal category, $\mathcal{C}$ another category and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathcal{V} \\ \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathcal{V} \end{align*}

be functors. Then the tensor product of $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}$ - if it exists - is defined as Coend

\begin{align*} \int^{c: \mathcal{C}} \mathcal{F} \otimes_{\mathcal{V}} \mathcal{G} \end{align*}
