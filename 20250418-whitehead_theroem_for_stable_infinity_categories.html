Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a morphism TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an equivalence
  2. the induced map
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{C}}^k(Y,-) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{C}}^k(X,-) \end{align*}

is an equivalence

  1. the induced map
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{C}}^k(-,X) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{C}}^k(-,Y) \end{align*}

is an equivalence

  1. the induced map
\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{C}}^k(Y,Y) \rightarrow \mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{C}}^k(X,Y) \end{align*}
