base change for a ring map

Definition

Let $\varphi: k \rightarrow k'$ be a ring-homomorphism. Then base change is defined as precomposition with the restriction of scalars functor

\begin{align*} \varphi^*: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k,\mathrm{Set}) \rightarrow : \mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{CAlg}_k',\mathrm{Set}) \end{align*}
