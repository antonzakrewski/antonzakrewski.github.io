rank of a module is multiplicative

Proposition

Let $M,M'$ be an \(R\)-module over a commutative ring. Then for any R-field $K$, we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}_K(M \otimes_{R} M' \otimes K) =& \mathrm{dim}_K((M \otimes_{R} K)) \cdot \mathrm{dim}_K(M' \otimes_{R} K)) \end{align*}

in $(\mathrm{Set}, \times)$.

Proof

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}_K(M \otimes_{R} M' \otimes K) =& \mathrm{dim}_K((M \otimes_{R} K) \otimes_{K} (M' \otimes_{R} K)) \\ =& \mathrm{dim}_{K}((M \otimes_{R} K) \otimes_{K} (M' \otimes_{R} K)) \\ =& \mathrm{dim}_{K}((M \otimes_{R} K) \cdot \mathrm{dim}_K(M' \otimes_{R} K)) \\ \end{align*}
