represented functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set}$ a functor. Then $\mathcal{C}$ is said to be represented with representing object $c$, if it comes with a natural isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F} \cong \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(c,-) \end{align*}
