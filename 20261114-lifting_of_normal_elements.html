lifting of self-adjoint elements

Proposition

Let $f: A \rightarrow B$ be a map of C star algebras Let $b \in B$ be a self-adjoint element and $a \in A$ a lift. Then there exists self-adjoint lift $\tilde{a}$.

Proof

Choose any lift $b$ and write

\begin{align*} b =& b_1 - \mathrm{i} b_2 \\ \end{align*}

where $b_1 = \frac{1}{2} b + b^*$ and $b_2 = \mathrm{i} \frac{1}{2 \mathrm{i}} (b - b^*)$. then $B_1$ is self-adjoint $b_2^* = - b_2$.

we claim that $f(b_1) = f(b)$ or equivalently $f(b_2) = 0$. Here it suffices to observe that $f(b_2) = f(b) - f(b_1)$ is a sum of self-adjoint elements, hence also self adjoint and satisfies $f(b_2) = f(b_2)^* = f(b_2^*) = - f(b_2)$.
