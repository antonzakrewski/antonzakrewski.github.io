schur lemma for representation of algebras

Proposition

Let $V_1,V_2$ be $A$-Modules over an K-Algebra $A$ and $f: V_1 \rightarrow V_2$ a nonzero module-homomorphism Then if

  1. $V_1$ is simple representation, then $f$ is a monomorphism
  2. $V_2$ is a simple representation, then $f$ is an epimorphism
  3. if both are simple, then $f$ is an isomorphism

Proof
