equality of two subgroups and short exact sequence

Proposition

Let Suppose

be a short exact sequence of groups

Assume that $M_1,M_2 \subseteq B$ are subgroups such that

\begin{align*} \iota^{-1}[M_1] =& \iota^{-1}[M_2} \\ \pi[M_1] =& \pi[M_2} \\ \end{align*}

and $M_2 \subseteq M_1$

Then $M_1 = M_2$

Proof

Let $m_1 \in M_1$. Then $[m_1] \in \pi[M_2]$ as $[m_1] \in \pi[M_1] \subseteq \pi[M_2]$ Hence there exists an $m_2 \in M_2$ and an $a \in A = \mathrm{im}(\iota)$ (sloppy notation) such that

\begin{align*} m_1 =& m_2 \cdot a \\ m_2^{-1} \cdot m_1 =& a \\ \end{align*}

This shows that $m_2^{-1} \cdot m_1 \in M_2$ as $m_2^{-1} \in M_2 \subseteq M_1$ and $M_1$ is closed under multiplication.

But then $a \in A, a \in M_2$ showing that $a \in \iota^{-1}[M_1] = \iota^{-1}[M_2]$ or $a \in M_2$. Then

\begin{align*} m_2 \cdot a =& m_2 \cdot m_2^{-1} \cdot m_1 =& m_1 \in M_2 \end{align*}

and hence $M_1 = M_2$
