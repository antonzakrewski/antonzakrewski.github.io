inclusion of wedge into product is pushout product in pointed objects

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a finitely bicomplete category and $X,Y$ objects. Then the inclusion

\begin{align*} X \vee Y \rightarrow X \times Y \end{align*}

is the pushout product in the cartesian monoidal $\mathcal{C}_{*/}$ for $X \rightarrow *, Y \rightarrow *$
