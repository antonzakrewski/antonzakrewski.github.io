cyclotomic polynomial

Definition

Let $F$ be an algebraically closed field, $n \in \mathbb{N}^+$ with $\mathrm{char}(F) \not\mid n$ and $\overline{F}$ an algebraic closure Then the cyclotomic polynomial is defined as polynomial

\begin{align*} \Phi_n(x) = \prod_{i=1}^{r = \varphi(n)} (X - \zeta_i) \end{align*}

where $\varphi(n)$ is the euler's phi function and $\zeta_i$ are the primitive root of unity
