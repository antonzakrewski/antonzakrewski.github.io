product preserving functors out of a preadditive infinity category is preadditive

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories where

  1. both admit finite infinity coproducts & finite infinity products
  2. one of them is an additive infinity category

Then so is the product preserving functor category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\Pi}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

Proof

$\mathcal{C}$ preadditive

using the pointwise calculation

  • infinity limit in a functor category
  • infinity colimit in a functor category

suffices:

Note that the product of product preserving functors is again product preserving, since infinity limits commute with infinity limits

$\mathcal{D}$ preadditive
