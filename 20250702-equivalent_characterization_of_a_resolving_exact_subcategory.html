Let $\iota: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$ be an exact infinity subcategory.

TFAE:

  1. $\iota$ is a resolving exact subcategory, i.e. it satisfies a) for every $x \hookrightarrow y \twoheadrightarrow z$ in $\mathcal{E}$ with $y \in \mathcal{A}$ it follows that $x \in \mathcal{A}$ b) for every $z \in \mathcal{E}$ there exists a projection $y \twoheadrightarrow z$ with $y \in \mathcal{A}$
