right adjoint of a functor preserving monomorphisms preserves injective objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories, $\mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a functor which is a right adjoint $\mathcal{L} \dashv \mathcal{R}$. Suppose $\mathcal{L}$ preserves monomorphisms Then $\mathcal{R}$ preserves injective objects
