minimal primary decomposition

Definition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ an ideal, then a primary decomposition is said to be minimal, if

  1. $\bigcap_{j \neq i} \mathfrak{q}_i \not\subsetneq \mathfrak{q}_i$ for all $i$
  2. $\sqrt{\mathfrak{q}_i} \neq \sqrt{\mathfrak{q}_j}$ for $i \neq j$
