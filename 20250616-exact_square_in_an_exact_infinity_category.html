exact square in an exact infinity category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathrm{inj}(\mathcal{C}), \mathrm{proj}(\mathcal{C})$ be an exact infinity category. Then a bicartesian square

is called exact, where

  1. horizontal maps $\hookrightarrow$ are injections
  2. vertical maps $\twoheadrightarrow$ are projections
