sequential limit with eventually constant homotopy objects for a complete t-structure

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category equipped with a complete t-structure. Let $... \rightarrow X_{2} \rightarrow X_1 \rightarrow X_0$ be a sequential diagram, such that there exists for each $n$ an $m >> 0$ such that $... \rightarrow \pi_n(X_{m+1}) \rightarrow \pi_n(X_m)$ consists only of isomorphism.

Then the canonical map $\lim_{\mathbb{N}} X \rightarrow X_m$ induces an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \pi_m(\lim_{\mathbb{N}} X) \rightarrow \pi_*(X_m) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from lim-lim1 sequence of homotopy objects in a stable category and object with trivial homotopy objects in a complete t-structure
