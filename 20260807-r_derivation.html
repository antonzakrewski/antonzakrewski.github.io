R-derivation

Definition

Let $R \rightarrow S$ be a ring-homomorphism and $M$ an \(S\)-module. Then an \(R\)-derivation of $S$ into $M$ is an \(R\)-linear map $\delta: S \rightarrow M$ such that $\delta$ satisfies the leibniz rule, i.e.,

\begin{align*} \delta(a \cdot b) = a \delta(b) + b \delta(a) \end{align*}
