frobenius functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{G}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ functors. Then $\mathcal{F}$ (and $\mathcal{G}$) is said to be a frobenius functor, if $\mathcal{F}$ is both left and right adjoint to $\mathcal{G}$
