cartesian commutative Group

Definition

An cartesian commutative Monoid is said to be a cartesian commutative group, if the underlying Monoid of a cartesian commutative Monoid is a cartesian group, i.e. equivalently the shear map

\begin{align*} :\mathcal{F}(<1>) \times \mathcal{F}(<1>) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(<1>) \times \mathcal{F}(<1>) \end{align*}

is an equivalence
