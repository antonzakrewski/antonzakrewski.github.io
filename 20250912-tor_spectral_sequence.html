tor spectral sequence for ring spectra

Proposition

Let $R\in \mathrm{RingSp}$$ be an \(\mathbb{E}_1\)-ring spectrum. Let $M$ be a <left module>, $N$ a <right module>.

Then there exists a <convergent> spectral sequence

\begin{align*} E_{i,j}^2 = \mathrm{Tor}^{\pi_* R}_i(\pi_* M, \pi_* N)_j \Rightarrow \pi_{i+j}(M \otimes_{R} N) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{Tor}_i^{\pi_* R}(\pi_* M, \pi_* N) \in \mathrm{Mod}(R)$ is again graded

Proof

consider the filtered spectrum

\begin{align*} \tau_{\geq *} M \otimes_{\tau_{\geq *} R } \tau_{\geq *} N \end{align*}

as tensor product in filtered spectra (<restricts to it>)

Then the colimit is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim} \simeq M \otimes_{R} N \end{align*}

since $\mathrm{colim}: \mathrm{Fil}(\mathrm{Sp}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}$ is a symmetric monoidal functor

Furthermore the limit is given by $0$. (since each spectrum has bounded homotopy groups - todo).

for each spectrum in $\mathrm{fil}_{\geq *}(\mathrm{Sp})$ the limit is given by $0$ because more mand moer homotopy groups will be $0$.

Associated graded is symmetric monoidal:

\begin{align*} \mathrm{gr}^* \simeq& \mathrm{gr}^*(\tau_{\geq *} M) \otimes_{\mathrm{gr}^*(\tau_{\geq *} R)} \mathrm{gr}^*(\tau_{\geq *} N) \simeq& \Simga^* \pi_* M \otimes_{\Sigma^ \pi_* R} \Sigma^* \pi_* N \\ \simeq& \Simga^* (\pi_*(M ) \otimes_{\pi_* R} \pi_*N) \end{align*}

where we use thta <$\mathrm{gr}^*$> is symmetric monoidal. and that $\Sigma^*$ is at least \(\mathbb{E}_1\)-monoidal.

or

\begin{align*} E^2_{i,j} =& \pi_{i+j} \mathrm{gr}^j =& \pi_*{i+j} (\Sigma^j (\pi_{*}M \otimes_{\pi_* R} \pi_*(N)) \\ \simeq& \pi_i((\pi_* M \otimes_{\pi_* R} \pi_* N)_j) \\ \simeq& H_i(\pi_* M \otimes_{\pi_* R} \pi_* N) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{Tor}_{i}^{\pi_* R}(\pi_* M, \pi_* N)_j \end{align*}
