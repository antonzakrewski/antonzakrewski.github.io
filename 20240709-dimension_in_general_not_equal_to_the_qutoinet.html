dimension in general not equal to the quotient

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ an ideal. Then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{a}) + \mathrm{dim}(A/\mathfrak{a}) \neq \mathrm{dim}(A) \end{align*}

Proof

consider

\begin{align*} A \coloneqq \mathbb{Z}_{(p)}[X] \end{align*}

and $(pX - 1) \subseteq Z_{(p})$

Then

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Z}_{(p})/(pX - 1) \simeq& \{\frac{1}{q}, \frac{1}{1}\}^{-1} \mathbb{Z} \\ \mathbb{Q} \end{align*}

and hence $(pX - 1)$ is a maximal ideal. Furthermore we get

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}(pX - 1) = 1 \end{align*}

by

  1. height theorem
  2. the chain $(0) \subseteq (pX-1)$ where $(0)$ is a prime ideal, since $\mathbb{Z}_{(p)} \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ is an Integral domain

But the dimension

\begin{align*} \mathrm{dim}( \mathbb{Z}_{(p)}[X]) \geq 2 \end{align*}

(even equal), for example as

\begin{align*} (0) \subseteq (p) \subseteq (p,X) \end{align*}

where $(p,X)$ is a prime ideal, as

\begin{align*} \mathbb{Z}_{(p)}[X]/(p,X) \cong& \mathbb{Z}_{(p)}/X \\ \cong& \mathbb{F}_p \end{align*}

this shows

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ht}(\mathfrak{a}) + \mathrm{dim}(A/\mathfrak{a}) \neq& \mathrm{dim}(A) \\ 1 + 0 \neq& 2 \end{align*}
