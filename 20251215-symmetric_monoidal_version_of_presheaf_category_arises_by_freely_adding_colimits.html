symmetric monoidal version of presheaf category arises by freely adding colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a prepresentably symmetric monoidal category, $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})$ the presheaf category with the day convolution with respect to some small symmetric monoidal category $\mathcal{D}$. Then restricting along the yoneda embedding induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\textrm{colim}, \textrm{ symmetric monoidal}}(\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C}), \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}^{\textrm{symmetric monoidal}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

Proof
