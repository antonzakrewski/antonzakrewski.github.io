weak equivalence between simplicial sets

Definition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a morphism of simplicial sets. Then $f$ is said to be a weak equivalence, if it induces a Bijection

\begin{align*} f^*: \pi_0 \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}()(Y,K) \rightarrow \pi_0 \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}()(X,K) \end{align*}

for every Kan complex $K$
