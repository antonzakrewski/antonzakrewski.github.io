morphism from a scheme to affine scheme as ring morphism

Proposition

Let $X$ be a scheme, $Y = \mathrm{Spec}(A)$ an affine scheme. Then there exists a Bijection natural with respect to morphism of schemes

\begin{align*} \theta: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Scheme}}(X,Y) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{CRing}}(A, \mathcal{O}_X(X)) \\ (f^*,f^#) \mapsto& f^#(Y): A = \mathcal{O}_Y(Y) \rightarrow f_*\mathcal{O}_X(X) \end{align*}

Proof

Let $U_i = \mathrm{Spec}(A_i)$ be an open cover by afine schemes. Let $U_{i,j} \coloneqq U_i \cap U_j$ be the open subschemes

Then consider the diagram

0 & \mathrm{Hom}(A, \mathcal{O}_X(X)) & \prod_i \mathrm{Hom}(A, \mathcal{O}_X(U_i)) & \prod_{i,j} \mathrm{Hom}(A, \mathcal{O}X(U{i,j})) \arrow[]{d}{\prod \theta_{i}} 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
0 \arrow[]{r}{} & \mathrm{Hom}(X, Y) \arrow[]{d}{\theta} & \prod \mathrm{Hom}(U_i, Y) & \prod_{i,j} \mathrm{Hom}(U_{i,j}, Y)  \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
