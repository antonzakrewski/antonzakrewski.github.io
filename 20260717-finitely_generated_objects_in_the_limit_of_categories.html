finitely generated objects in the limit of categories

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be a regular cardinal, $I \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}$ be a diagram, such that each morphism is kappa accessible and each functor preserves monomorphism. Let $\lim_{I} \mathcal{C}_i$ be the limit and let $(x_i)$ be an object in $\lim_{I} \mathcal{C}_i$, such that each object $x_i$ is finitely generated in $\mathcal{C}_i$. Then $(x_i)$ is also $\kappa$ kappa finitely generated object

Proof

follows from:

  • colimits in a limit category
  • Hom in the limit category
