F2 euler class of a spherical fibration

Definition

Let $\pi: E \rightarrow B$ be a spherical fibration and $B \rightarrow \mathrm{Th}(\pi)$ the induced map of the Thom Space Then the $\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}$ euler class is defined as pullback of the F2 thom class

\begin{align*} \overline{e}(\pi) = z^*(\overline{u}(\pi)) \in \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} z: B \rightarrow \mathrm{Th}_*(\pi) \end{align*}

is the induced map
