TR1 of a triangulated category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a triangulated category. Then for an object $A$ and a morphism $u: A \rightarrow B$:

  1. there exists an exact triangle
  1. the sequence $(\mathrm{id}_{A}, 0,0)$ is an exact triangle
  2. given two isomorphic sequences

one is an exact triangle if and only if the other is also an exact triangle
