homology of a trivial boundary complex

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathcal{A})$ be the category of chain complexes and $A_*$ a trivial boundary complex. Then the \(n\)-th homology of $A_*$ is given by

\begin{align*} H_n(A_*) =& A_n \end{align*}

Proof

follows from

\begin{align*} H_n(A_*) =& \mathrm{ker}(0)/ \mathrm{im}(0) \\ =& A_n/0 =& A_n \\ \end{align*}
