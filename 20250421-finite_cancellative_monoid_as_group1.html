finite cancellative monoid as group

Proposition

Let $M$ be a finite cancellative semigroup. Then $M$ is a group

Proof

consider

\begin{align*} \cdot m: M \rightarrow& M \\ n \mapsto& n \cdot m \end{align*}

which is injective

by Äquivalenz von Bijektivität, Surjektivität und Injektivität bei gleich mächtigen, endlichen Mengen this has to be surjective, hence there exists a preimage $n = m^{-1}$ of $e$.
