weak cw structure of an anima

Definition

Let $X$ be an anima Then a weak cw structure on $X$ is given by a sequence

\begin{align*} ... \rightarrow X_{n-1} \rightarrow X_{n} \rightarrow X_{n+1} \rightarrow ... \end{align*}

together with a natural transformation $(X_n) \rightarrow \mathrm{const}(X)$ such that

  1. for each $n \in \mathbb{N}$ the map $X_{n} \rightarrow X_{n+1}$ is n-connected map
  2. the natural transformation exhibits $\mathrm{colim}_n(X_n) \simeq X$
