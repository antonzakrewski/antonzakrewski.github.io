1-categorical polynomial functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{F}: A \rightarrow B$ be a functor between additive idempotent complete categories Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be polynomial of degree

  1. $0$, if $f$ is constant functor
  2. $n + 1$, if for all $x$ the derivative
\begin{align*} D_x \mathcal{F}: A \rightarrow B \\ Y \mapsto \mathrm{coker}(\mathcal{F}(X \oplus Y) - \mathcal{F}(x)) \end{align*}
