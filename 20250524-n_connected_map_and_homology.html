n-connected map and homology

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be an n-connected map and $M$ a module . Then

\begin{align*} H_i(f): H_i(X, M) \rightarrow H_i(Y,M) \end{align*}

is an isomorphism for $i < n$ and a surjection for $i = n$

Proof

follows from

  1. relative hurewicz theorem for the homotopy fiber
  2. universal coefficient theorem for singular homology for any arbitrary module
  3. long exact sequence of homology of a pair of spaces
