continuous kappa-calkin category is compact objects of the fiber of the colimit functor

Proposition

Let $\kappa$ be an uncountable regular cardinal, then the continuous kappa calkin category is the full subcategory of compact object of the fiber

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}: \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\kappa}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • colimits in PrL via PrR
  • some verdier argument
