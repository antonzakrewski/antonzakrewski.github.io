Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category, $X \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ an object and $1 \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ the terminal object. Then the categorical product is cannonically isomorphic

\begin{align*} X \times 1 \cong X \end{align*}
