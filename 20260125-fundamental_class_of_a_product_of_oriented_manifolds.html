fundamental class of a product of oriented manifolds

Proposition

Let $M,N$ be \(R\)-orientable closed topological manifolds of dimension $m$ and $n$. Then under the kunneth formula for singular homology (+ closed orientable manifold and top homology) the fundamental class corresponds to

\begin{align*} H_{m+n}(M \times N,R) \cong& H_m(M,R) \otimes H_n(N,R) \\ [M \times N] \mapsto [M] \otimes [N] \end{align*}

Proof
