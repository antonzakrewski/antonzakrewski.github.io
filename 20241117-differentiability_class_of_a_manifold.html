differentiability class of a Manifold

Definition

Let $M$ be a differentiable manifold. Then for $k \in \mathbb{N}_0 \cup \{\infty\}$ is defined as set of functions

\begin{align*} f: M \rightarrow \mathbb{R} \end{align*}

which are at least \(k\)-times differentiable

for $k = 0$ those are the continuous maps, for $k = \infty$ those are the smooth maps
