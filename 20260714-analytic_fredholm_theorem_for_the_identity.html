analytic fredholm theorem for the identity

Proposition

Let $T \in \mathrm{BL}(\mathcal{H})$ be a compact operator and $z \in \mathbb{C} \setminus \{0\}$. Then either

  1. $T \psi = z \psi$ has a solution $0 \neq \psi$
  2. $(T - z)^{-1} \in \mathrm{BL}(\mathcal{H})$ exists

Proof

special case of

  • analytic fredholm theorem for $D = \mathbb{C} \setminus \{0\}$ and $f(z) \coloneqq \frac{T}{z}$

now A1 does not hold, because $(\frac{T}{z} - \mathbbm{1})^{-1}$ exsits for large enough $z$ by the neumann series of a bounded linear operator.

Hence $A_2$ holds
