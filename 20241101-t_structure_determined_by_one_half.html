t-structure determined by one half

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category equipped with a t-structure. Then

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C}_{\geq n} =& \{x \in \mathcal{C} \vert \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \forall y \in \mathcal{C}_{\leq n-1}\} \\ \mathcal{C}_{\leq n} =& \{x \in \mathcal{C} \vert \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(y,x) \forall y \in \mathcal{C}_{\geq n+1}\} \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • weak semiorthogonal decomposition is determined by one half
