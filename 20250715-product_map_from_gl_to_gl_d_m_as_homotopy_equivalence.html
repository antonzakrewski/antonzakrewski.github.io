product map from GL to GL(d,m) as homotopy equivalence

Proposition

The product map from GL to GL(d,m) is a homotopy equivalence

Proof

Let

\begin{align*} \mathrm{GL}(d-m) \times \mathrm{GL}(m) \rightarrow \mathrm{GL}(d,m) \rightarrow \mathrm{Map}(\mathbb{R}^m, \mathbb{R}^d) \times \mathrm{GL}(d-m) \end{align*}

Then deform the bad part of $\mathrm{Map}(\mathbb{R}^m, \mathbb{R}^d)$ to get a homotopy equivalenc between the image of $\mathrm{GL}(d,m) \rightarrow \mathrm{Map}(\mathbb{R}^m, \mathbb{R}^d)$ and $\mathrm{GL}(m)$
