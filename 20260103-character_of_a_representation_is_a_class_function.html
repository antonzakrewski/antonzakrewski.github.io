character of a representation is a class function

Proposition

Let $V$ be a $\mathbb{C}$ finite dimensional representation of a finite group Then the character $\chi_V$ is a class function.

Proof

follows as

\begin{align*} \chi_V(h g h^{-1}) =& \mathrm{tr}_V(\rho_h \rho_g \rho_{h^{-1}}) \\ =& \mathrm{tr}_V(\rho_{h^{-1}} \rho_h \rho_g) =& \mathrm{tr}_V(\rho_g) \\ =& \chi_V(g) \end{align*}

where we use that the trace commutes with cyclic permutations
