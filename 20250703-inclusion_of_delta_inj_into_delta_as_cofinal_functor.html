inclusion of simplex inj category into simplex category is coinitial

Proposition

The inclusion

\begin{align*} \Delta_{\mathrm{inj}} \hookrightarrow \Delta \end{align*}

is coinitial

Proof

using quillen theorem A for coinitial functors we need to show that the category

\begin{align*} (\Delta_\mathrm{inj}){/[n]} \end{align*}

this is given as wide subcategory of the ordinary slice category $\Delta_{/[n]}$ where the morphisms between slices are injections.

But $\mathrm{id}: [n] \rightarrow [n]$ as terminal object lies inside $(\Delta_{\mathrm{inj}})_{/[n]}$, hence the category is weakly contractible (as infinity category with terminal object as weakly contractible simplicial set)
