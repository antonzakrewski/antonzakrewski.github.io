conservative functor between stable categories and acyclic objects

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an exact functor. TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is conservative
  2. the only F-acyclic object is the zero object

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

If $X$ is an acyclic object, then the zero morphisms $\mathcal{F}(X) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(0)$ is an equivalence and therefore $X \rightarrow 0$

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a morphism such that $\mathcal{F}(f)$ is an equivalence. Let $\mathrm{fib}(f)$ be the fiber of $f$, then in particular $\mathcal{F}(\mathrm{fib}(f)) \simeq \mathrm{fib}(\mathcal{F}(f)) \simeq 0$ which shows that $\mathrm{fib}(f) \simeq 0$ or in particular $f$ is an equivalence.
