S-localization of a module spectrum

Definition

Let $R$ be an (\mathbb{E}_1)-ring spectrum and $S \subseteq \pi_*(R)$ be a homogeneous subset satisfying the left ore condition. Then the \(S\)-localization functor is defined as right adjoint

\begin{align*} \mathrm{LMod}_R \rightarrow \mathrm{LMod}_R^{S-\mathrm{Loc}} \end{align*}

given by upper truncation of a t-structure
