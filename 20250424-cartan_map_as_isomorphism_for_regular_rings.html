cartan map as isomorphism for regular rings

Proposition

Let $R$ be a regular ring. Then the cartan map from K to G is an isomorphism

Proof

consider the Horshoe lemma and Schanuels lemma

We want to construct an inverse. Then define

\begin{align*} D: G_0(R) \rightarrow& K_0(R) \\ [M] \mapsto& \sum_{j} (-1)^{j} [P_j] \end{align*}

where $P_* \rightarrow M$ is a projective resolution of finite length consisting of finitely generated modules.

arguments welldefined

Note that such a projective resolution exists.

Furthermore this map is welldefined by Schanuels Lemma for projective resolutions.

given a short exact sequence

we have to show that

\begin{align*} D([M]) = D([M']) + D([M'']) \end{align*}

Then choose projective resolutions $P_* \rightarrow M', Q_* \rightarrow M''$. then applying the Horshoe lemma gives a projective resolution

\begin{align*} (P \oplus Q)_* \rightarrow M \end{align*}

which shows that

\begin{align*} D([M]) = D([M']) + D([M'']) \end{align*}
