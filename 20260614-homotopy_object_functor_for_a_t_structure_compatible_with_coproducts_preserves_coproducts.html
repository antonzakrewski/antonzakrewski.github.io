homotopy object functor for a t-structure compatible with coproducts preserves coproducts

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category equipped with a t-structure compatible with coproducts. Then the product functor

\begin{align*} \pi_n: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

preserves coproducts.
