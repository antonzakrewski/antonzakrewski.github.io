dimension shifting of a left derived functor via cokernel

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, where $\mathcal{A}$ admits enough projectives. Suppose $\mathcal{F}$ is a right exact functor, $\mathcal{LF}_n(-)$ the left derived functor, then for $n \geq 1$, $k < n$ and $A \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{A})$ there exists an object $A' \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{A})$ such that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{LF}_n(A) = \mathcal{LF}_{n-k}(A) \end{align*}

Proof / Construction

take a projective resolution

Then consider

Let $A \coloneqq \mathrm{coker}(P_{k+1} \rightarrow P_k)$

Then it follows that 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
... \arrow[]{r}{} & P_{k+2} \arrow[]{r}{} & P_{k+1} \arrow[]{r}{} & P_{k} \arrow[twoheadrightarrow]{r}{} & \mathrm{coker}(P_{k+1} \rightarrow P_k) \arrow[]{r}{} & 0
\end{tikzcd}
suffices as [[id:0d6124f2-59f2-4f96-838a-e8b023821bc6][projective resolution]]

Furthermore by construction
\begin{align*}
H_n(\mathcal{F}(P_*^A)) = H_{n-k}(\mathcal{F}(P_*^A'))
\end{align*}

therefore the identity suffices
