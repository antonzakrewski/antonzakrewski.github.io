stalk functor from sheaves of modules is symmetric monoidal

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{O}_X$ be a ringed space and Then the stalk functor from sheaves of modules is a symmetric monoidal functor

Proof

Let $x \in X$, then we want to show that the canonical map

\begin{align*} (\mathcal{F} \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_X} \mathcal{G})_{x} \rightarrow (\mathcal{F}_x \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X,x}} \mathcal{G}_{x}) \end{align*}

is an equivalence

for this we recall that the (pointwise) tensor product is the coequalizer

In this case, we may use that $- \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} -$ preserves colimits, and the diagonal $I \rightarrow I^{J}$ is a coinitial functor for finite set $J$ and a filtered category (cf. filtered category is sifted). Moreover, colimits commute with colimits, so this shows the claim.
