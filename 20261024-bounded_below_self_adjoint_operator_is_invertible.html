bounded below, self adjoint operator is invertible

Proposition

Let $T$ be a self-adjoint operator with $\langle T x\rangle \geq c \| x \|$ for some $c > 0$. Then $T$ is an isomorphism

Proof

we show that it is a Bijection, then the inverse is also bounded since $T$ is bounded below. Observe that it is injective by the bounded below part and $\mathrm{im}(T)^{\bot} = \mathrm{ker}(T^*) = \mathrm{ker}(T^*) = 0$ implies that the image is dense (cf. kernel and image of an adjoint)

Now we want to show that the image is closed: Let $w_n$ be a Cauchy sequence in the image, then we can choose preimages $v_n$ and $v_n$ is still cauchy, since $T$ is bounded below. In particular, $v_n$ converges to some $v$ and since $T$ is bounded, $T(v)$ is the limit of $w_n$, hence the image is closed.
