tangent space of a point of an affine algebraic set as vector space

Proposition

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field, $\mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ the affine space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}_{K}^n$ be an algebraic set Then the tangent space of a point is canonically endowed with a vector space structure over $K$.
