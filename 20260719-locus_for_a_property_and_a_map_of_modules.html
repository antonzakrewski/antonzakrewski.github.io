locus for a property and a map of modules

Definition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $f: M \rightarrow N$ an morphism of \(A\)-modules, $P$ a property, which is preserved by extension of scalars. Then the locus for the property $P$ and module $M$ is defined as Subfunctor of $\mathrm{Spec}(A)$ (cf. Spec) given by

\begin{align*} R \mapsto \{\varphi: A \rightarrow R \vert R \otimes_{A} f \texrm{ has property P}\} \end{align*}
