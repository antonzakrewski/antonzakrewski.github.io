center of the weyl algebra in positive characteristic

Proposition

Let $k$ be a field of positive characteristic. Then the center of the Weyl algebra is the generated submodule by $x^p, y^p$.

Proof

$x^p,y^p$ is in the center

It suffices to compute on generators. But here we have $x^p \cdot y = y \cdot x^p \pm p = y \cdot x^p$ and $x^p$ commutes with $x$ for trivial reasons. $x^p y = x^{p-1} x y = x^{p-1} yx \pm x^{p-1}$ and hence by induction $x^p y = yx^p + p x^{n-1} = yx^p$ for $\mathrm{char}(k) = p$. This implies that both elements lie in the center.
