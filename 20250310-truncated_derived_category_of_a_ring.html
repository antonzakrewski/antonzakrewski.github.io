truncated derived category of a ring

Definition

Let \(R\) be a ring. Then the truncated derived infinity category

\begin{align*} \mathcal{D}(R)_{\geq n} = \mathrm{Ch}(\mathrm{R-Mod})_{\geq n}[\mathrm{quasi-iso}^{-1}] \end{align*}

is defined as dwyer-kan localization along the quasi-isomorphism
