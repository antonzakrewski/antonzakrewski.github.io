semiorthogonal decomposition of an exact category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{E}$ be an exact category, then a semiorthogonal decomposition is given by two exact subcategory $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ such that

  1. $\mathcal{A} \perp \mathcal{B}$, i.e. for $a \in \mathcal{A}, b \in \mathcal{B}: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(a,b) = 0$
  2. for each $x \in \mathcal{C}$ there exists an exact sequence

with $a \in \mathcal{A}, b \in \mathcal{B}$
