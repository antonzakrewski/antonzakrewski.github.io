p-complete, bounded below spectrum and derived p-complete homolgoy

Proposition

Let $X$ be a bounded below spectrum spectrum. TFAE:

  1. $X$ is p-complete
  2. $H_*(X, \mathbb{Z})$ is derived p-complete

Proof

follows from:

  • fundamental theorem of serre class theory for spectra
  • derived p-complete abelian groups form a serre class
  • p-complete spectrum and derived p-complete homotopy groups
