pullback of a cartesian transformation is a cartesian transformation

Proposition

Let $I$ be an infinity category and

an pullback in $\mathrm{Fun}(I, \mathrm{An})$, where $\tau$ is a cartesian transformation.

Then so is $\tau'$

Proof

Let $i \rightarrow j$ be any morphism. Then consider the cube

Then apply the three out of four lemma for pullbacks where

  1. the front side is a pullback by pointwise limit construction
  2. the back side by the same reason
  3. the right side is a pullback since $\tau$ is a cartesian transformation
