Let $p: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a cocartesian fibration, $f: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ any infinity functor and $e \in \mathcal{E}$ an terminal object Then evaluation at $e$ induces a trivial fibration

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}_{f}^{\mathrm{mc}}(\mathcal{E}^#, \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow p{-1}(f(e)) \end{align*}

Note that 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{Fun}_{f}(\mathcal{E}^{#}, \mathcal{C}) \rar \dar & \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{mc}}(\mathcal{E}^#, \mathcal{C}) \dar \\
\Delta^0 \rar & \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{E}, \mathcal{D})
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

where

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{mc}}(\mathcal{E}^#, \mathcal{C}) = \mathrm{Fun}^{?}(\mathcal{E}^#, \mathcal{C}^{?}) \end{align*}

If $p:$ is a right fibration, then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}_f^{\mathrm{mc}}(\mathcal{E}^#, \mathcal{C}) = \mathrm{Fun}_{f}(\mathcal{E}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
