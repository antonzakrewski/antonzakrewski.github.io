unit in a filtered colimit of commutative rings

Proposition

Let $R = \mathrm{colim} R_i$ be a filtered colimit and $(r_i) \in R$. w.l.o.g. we may assume that $(r_i) = r_j$ for some $r_j \in R_j$ (cf. element in a filtered colimit of commutative rings)

TFAE:

  1. $\varphi_k(r_j)$ is a unit in $R_k$ for some $k$
  2. $r_j$ is a unit in $R$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
