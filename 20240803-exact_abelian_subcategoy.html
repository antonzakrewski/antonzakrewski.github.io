exact abelian subcategory

Definition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{A}$ a subcategory Then $\mathcal{C}$ is said to be an exact abelian subcategory if

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ is an abelian category
  2. the inclusion $\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{A}$ is an exact functor
