compact set and finite intersection with disjoint open sets

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $U_i$ a disjoint covering of $X$ and $K \subseteq X$ compact. Then the set

\begin{align*} \{i \in I \vert U_i \cap K \neq \emptyset\} \end{align*}

is finite

Proof

Suppose not, then $U_i$ is an open covering, without finite subcover
