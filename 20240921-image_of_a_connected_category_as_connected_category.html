image of a connected category as connected category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a connected category and $\mathcal{D}$ another category Then for a functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ the full image (even the Image up to definition) is connected.

Proof

Given $a,b \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathrm{Im}(\mathcal{F}))$ we may choose $a', b' \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C})$ and a morphism between $a'$ and $b'$. After applying $\mathcal{F}$ this becomes a morphism between $a$ and $b$
