quotient ring of an integral domain as integral domain

Proposition

Let $A$ be an Integral domain and $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq A$ an Integral domain. Then $A/\mathfrak{a}$ is also an integral domain.

Proof

Let

\begin{align*} \iota: A/\mathfrak{a} \rightarrowtail A \end{align*}

be the canonical monomorphism. Then apply the lemma: monomorphism and integral domain
