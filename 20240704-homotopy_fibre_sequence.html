homotopy fiber sequence

Definition

A homotopy fibre sequence is a sequence of composable continuous maps

together with a nullhomotopy of $fi$ to a constant map with value $y$ such that the induced map

\begin{align*} F \rightarrow \mathrm{hofib}_y(f) \end{align*}

is a weak homotopy equivalence
