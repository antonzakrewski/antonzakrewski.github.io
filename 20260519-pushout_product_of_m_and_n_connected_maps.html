pushout product of m and n connected maps is m+n+2 connected

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{X}$ be a topos and $u: A \rightarrow B, v: C \rightarrow D$ be maps which are $m$ and $n$ connected. Then the canoncial map

\begin{align*} B \times C \coprod_{A \times C} A \times D \rightarrow B \times D \end{align*}

is $m+n+2$ connected.

Proof
