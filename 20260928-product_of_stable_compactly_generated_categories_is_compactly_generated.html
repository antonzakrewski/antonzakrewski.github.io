product of stable compactly generated categories is compactly generated

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}_i$ be stable, compactly generated categories. Then so is the product category.

Proof

We have $\mathcal{C}_i \simeq \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}_{i}^{\omega}) \simeq \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{lex}}(\mathcal{C}_{i}^{\omega, \mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An})$
