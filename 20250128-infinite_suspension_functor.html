infinite suspension functor for a presentable category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category. Then the infinite suspension functor is defined as infinity left adjoint

\begin{align*} \mathbb{S}[-]: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}(\mathcal{C}_*) \\ \mathbb{S}[-]: \mathcal{C}_* \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}(\mathcal{C}_*) \\ \end{align*}

to the infinite loop functor out of spectra

remark

one is defined as adjoint to forgetting to pointed objects; the other to forgetting to just $\mathcal{C}$
