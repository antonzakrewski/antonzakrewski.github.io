rank map of a finitely generated projective module

Proposition

Let $P$ be a projective module over a commutative ring $R$. Then the rank map is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{rk}_{(-)}(P): \mathrm{Spec}(R) \rightarrow& \mathbb{N}_0 \\ \mathfrak{p} \mapsto& \mathrm{rk}_{\mathfrak{p}}(P) \end{align*}
