projective graded module and degreewise projective

Proposition

Let \(R = \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0} R_n\) be a graded ring and $M = \bigoplus_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0} M_n$ a graded module. TFAE:

  1. $M$ is a projective graded module
  2. $M$ is degreewise a projective module

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

corollary of direct summand of a projective object as projective object

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $\bigoplus M_i$ projective module and sum as free module
