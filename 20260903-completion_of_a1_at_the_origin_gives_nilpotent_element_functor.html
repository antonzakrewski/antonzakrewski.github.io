completion of affine line at the origin gives nilpotent element functor

Proposition

The completion of the affine line at the origin gives the nilpotent element functor.

Proof

Let $k$ be an arbitrary commutative ring and $\mathbb{A}^1_k$ the affine line. Recall that the inclusion of the origin is given by

\begin{align*} V(x) \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(k[x]) \end{align*}

and it is a morphism of finite presentation (cf. closed immersion locally of finite presentation and finitely generated ideal).

So $(\mathbb{A}^1_k)_{0}^{\wedge}$ is the union of $V(x^n)$ for $n \in \mathbb{N}$. But $V(x^n) \cong \mathrm{Spec}(k[x]/x^n)$ classifies the elements, which are n-nilpotent (correct name ?). Thus the union consists of all the nilpotent elements.
