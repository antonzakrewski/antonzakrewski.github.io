di-homomorphism

Definition

Let $R,S$ be rings, $M$ an \(R\)-module and $L$ \(S\)-module. A di-homomorphism consists of an ring-homomorphism $\varphi: R \rightarrow S$ and a group-homomorphism $\psi: M \rightarrow L$ such that

\begin{align*} \psi(s \cdot m) = \varphi(s) \cdot \psi(m) \end{align*}

for $s \in S, m \in M$
