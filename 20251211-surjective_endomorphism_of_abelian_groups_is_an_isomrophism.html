surjective endomorphism of abelian groups is an isomorphism

Proposition

Let $M$ be a finitely generated abelian group and $\varphi: M \rightarrow M$ an epimorphism. Then $\varphi$ is an isomorphism

Proof

a)

  • surjective endomorphism for a noetherian module as isomorphism
  • integers are noetherian

b)

  • surjective endomorphism for a finitely generated module as isomorphism
