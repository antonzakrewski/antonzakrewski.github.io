right kan extension along a fully faithful functor commutes up to natural equivalence

Proposition

Let $\iota: \mathcal{C}_0 \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a fully faithful functor and $\mathcal{D}$ a category with enough limits for the right kan extension formula Then the right kan extension

commutes up to natural equivalence

Proof

use the right kan extension formula and observe that for $x \in \mathcal{C}_0$ the slice category

\begin{align*} (\mathcal{C}_{0})_{x/} \end{align*}

contains an initial object, namely $\mathrm{id}: x \rightarrow x$.

Furthermore inclusion of initial object is cofinal.

we have implicitly used:

  • fully faithful functor induces fully faithful functor on slice categories
