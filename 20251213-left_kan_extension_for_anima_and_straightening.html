left kan extension for functors into anima and unstraightening

Proposition

Let $f: B \rightarrow B'$ be a map of anima Then under unstraightening the left kan extension

\begin{align*} f_{!}: \mathrm{Fun}(B, \mathrm{An}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(B',\mathrm{An}) \end{align*}

corresponds to the functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An}_{/B} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}_{/B'} (A \rightarrow B) \mapsto (A \rightarrow B \rightarrow B') \end{align*}
