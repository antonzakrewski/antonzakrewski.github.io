finite product commute with colimits in sSet

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{sSet}$ be the category sSet, $X_1,...,X_n,Y_i \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathrm{sSet})$ be simplicial set. Then finite products commute with colimits.

\begin{align*} (\prod X_j) \times \mathrm{colim} Y_i \simeq \mathrm{colim} \prod X_j \times Y_i \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of:

  • finite product commutes with colimits in set
  • limits commuting with colimits and functor category
