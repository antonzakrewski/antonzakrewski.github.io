relative K1-Group as kernel

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $\mathfrak{a} \subseteq R$ an ideal Then the relative K1-Group is given by

\begin{align*} K_1(R,\mathfrak{a}) \cong \mathrm{ker}(\pi_*: K_1(R \times_{R/\mathfrak{a}} R) \rightarrow K_1(R)) \end{align*}
