let $G$ be a groupoid, then the pushout of Seifert van Kampen about groupoids induces an adjoint equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\pi_{\leq 1}(X), \mathcal{G}) \cong \mathrm{Fun}(\pi_{\leq 1}(B), \mathcal{G}) \times^{2}_{\mathrm{Fun}(\pi_{\leq 1}(A \cap B), \mathcal{G})} \mathrm{Fun}(\pi_{\leq 1}(A), \mathcal{G}) \end{align*}

natural in $\mathcal{G}$
