unstraightening of a representable functor admits terminal object

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F} \in \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An})$ be a representable infinity functor Then the domain of the unstraightened functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Un}(F) \end{align*}

admits a terminal object

Proof

Let $F \simeq \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,c)$. Then consider $\mathrm{id}_c$ over $c$.
