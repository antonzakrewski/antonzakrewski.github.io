presentation of sieves

Proposition

Let $R$ be a sieve on $X$ and let $(Y_i \rightarrow X)_{i \in I}$ be a family of maps generating a sieve $R$ Then the maps $Y_i \rightarrow R$ induce an isomorphism of presheaves

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim} (\coprod_{i,j \in I} よ(Y_i) \times_{よ(X)} よ(Y_j) \rightrightarrows \coprod_{i \in I} よ(Y_i)) \end{align*}
