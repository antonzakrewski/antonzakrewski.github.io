kappa-calkin functor preserves kappa filtered colimits

Proposition

The kappa calkin category functor $\mathrm{Calk}_{\kappa}: \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{Perf}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{Perf}}$ preserves filtered colimits.

Proof

The functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Cat}_{\mathrm{st}}^{\mathrm{dual}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat}^{\mathrm{cg}}_{\mathrm{st}} \\ \mathcal{C} \mapsto& \mathrm{Ind}(\mathcal{C}^{\kappa}) / \widehat{よ}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

commutes with kappa filtered colimits.
