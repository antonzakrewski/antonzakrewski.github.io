isomorphism as point local property

Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring and $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod and $f: M \rightarrow N$.

Then the property of $f$ being an isomorphism is a point local property

Proof

corollary of:

  • surjectivity as point local property
  • injectivity as point local property
  • abelian category as balanced category
