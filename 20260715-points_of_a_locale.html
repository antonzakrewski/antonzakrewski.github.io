points of a locale

Definition

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be a locale. Then the topological space $\mathrm{Pt}(\mathcal{O})$ is defined as follows:

  1. the elements of $\mathrm{Pt}(\mathcal{O})$ aer the morphisms $\mathrm{Open}(*) = 1 \rightarrow \mathcal{O}$
  2. the open subsets of $\mathrm{Pt}(\mathcal{O})$ are the subsets $\mathrm{Pt}(u) \{p: 1 \rightarrow \mathcal{O} \vert p^*(u) = *\}$ for all $u \in cal♥O$
