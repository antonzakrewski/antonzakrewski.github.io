steenrod square

Definition

Let $i \in \mathbb{N}, n \in \mathbb{N}, x \in H^{n}(X,\mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z})$ for a topological space $X$. Then define the \(i\)-th steenrod square as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq}^i(x) \in H^{n+1}(X, \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

as the coefficient of $t^{n-i}$ of the power operation $P_t(x)$.
