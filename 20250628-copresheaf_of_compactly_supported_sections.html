copresheaf of compactly supported sections

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a pointed infinity category admitting (sufficiently many) colimits and fiber sequences. Let $X$ be a hausdorff space and $\mathcal{F} \in \mathrm{PSh}_{\mathcal{C}}(X)$ be a infinity presheaf.

Then the associated copresheaf is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_c: U \mapsto& \mathrm{colim}_{K \subseteq U} (\mathrm{fib}(\mathcal{F}(X) \rightarrow \mathcal{F}(X \setminus K))) \end{align*}

where $U \subseteq V$ uses the inclusion of

\begin{align*} \{K \subseteq V\} \hookrightarrow \{K\subseteq U\} \end{align*}
