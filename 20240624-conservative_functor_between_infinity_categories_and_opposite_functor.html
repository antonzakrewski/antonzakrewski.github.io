conservative functor between infinity categories and opposite functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ a infinity functor

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{F}$ is a conservative functor
  2. $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{op}}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{op}}$ is conservative

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

equivalence in an infinity category and 2 simplices equivalence in an infinity category

2) $\implies$ 1)

follows dually
