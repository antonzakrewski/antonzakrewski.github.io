functor category inherits pointwise monoidal structure

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a monoidal category Then the functor category also inherits an \(\mathbb{E}_1\) monoidal structure

Proof

  • functor category functor preserves limits
  • monoidal infinity category as monoid in Cat infinity
  • product preserving functor induces functor on cartesian monoids
