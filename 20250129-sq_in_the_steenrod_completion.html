Sq in the steenrod completion

Definition

In the steenrod completion we define

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sq} \coloneqq 1 + \sum\mathrm{Sq}^1 + \mathrm{Sq}^2 + ... =& \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} \mathrm{Sq}^i \end{align*}

resp.

\begin{align*} (1, 1 + \mathrm{Sq}^1, 1 + \mathrm{Sq}^1 + \mathrm{Sq}^2 , ...) \end{align*}
