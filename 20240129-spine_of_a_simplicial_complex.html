spine of a simplicial complex

Definition

Let $\Delta$ be the simplex category and $[n]$ a simplex. Then the spine $I^n$ is defined a simplicial set

\begin{align*} I^n: \Delta \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} \\ [m] \mapsto& \{f \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\Delta}([m],[n]) \vert \mathrm{im}(f) \subseteq \{j, j+1\}, j \in [n] \} \\ (g: [m] \rightarrow [m']) \mapsto g^{ \rightarrow} \end{align*}

Welldefined

Note that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{im}(f \circ g) \subseteq \mathrm{im}(f) \subseteq \{j, j+1\} \end{align*}
