infinity subcategory closed under extensions

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category, $\mathcal{C}' \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ a full sub infinity category. Then $\mathcal{C}'$ is said to be closed under extensions, if for every triangle

\begin{align*} X \rar & Y \rar & Z \end{align*}

in $\mathcal{C}$, where $X,Z \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{C}')$, the object $Y$ also belongs to $\mathcal{C}'$
