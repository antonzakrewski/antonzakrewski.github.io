groupoid core functor

Definition

Given Category Cat and the category of groupoids the maximal subgroupoid functor is defined as functor

\begin{align*} ()^{\cong}: \mathrm{Cat} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Grpd} \\ \mathcal{C} \mapsto& \mathcal{C}^{\cong} \\ \mathcal{F} \mapsto \mathcal{F}^{\cong} \end{align*}

where

  1. $\mathcal{C}^{\cong}$ is the maximal subgroupoid
  2. $\mathcal{F}^{\cong}$ is the restriction

Proof

note that $\mathcal{F}^{\cong}$ is welldefined by isomorphisms under functors
