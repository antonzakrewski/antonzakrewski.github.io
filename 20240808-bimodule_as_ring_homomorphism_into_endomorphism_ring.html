bimodule as ring homomorphism into endomorphism ring

Proposition

Let $R,S$ be rings, $M$ an abelian group. Then an \(R,S\)-bimodule structure is equivalently given by right \(S\)-module structure on $M$ and a ring-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: R \rightarrow \mathrm{End}_{\mathrm{ModS}}(M) \end{align*}

resp.

\begin{align*} \varphi: S^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{End}_{\mathrm{RMod}}(M) \end{align*}

Proof
