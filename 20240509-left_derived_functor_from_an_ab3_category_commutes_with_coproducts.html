left derived functor commuting with coproducts commutes with coproducts

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{B}$ a right exact functor which preserves coproducts Then there exists isomorphism of delta functors for the left derived functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{LF}_n(\coprod -) \cong \coprod \mathcal{LF}_n(-) \end{align*}

Proof

Here $\coprod \mathcal{F}(-)$ is right exact as $\coprod -$ commutes with colimits (cf. colimits commute with colimits, composition of right exact functors as right exact functor) Hence this follows from natural transformation between right exact functors and morphism of left derived functors
