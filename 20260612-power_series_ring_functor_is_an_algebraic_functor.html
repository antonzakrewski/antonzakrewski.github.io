underlying Power series ring functor is an affine scheme

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, then the underlying Power series ring functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{CAlg}_{R} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Set} \\ K \mapsto& K[ [X] ] \end{align*}

is an affine scheme

Proof

follows as the underlying set is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\coprod_{n \in \mathbb{N}}R, -) \end{align*}

hence use the symmetric algebra functor
