enriched category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{V}$ be a monoidal category. Then $\mathcal{C}$ is said to be an enriched category over $\mathcal{V}$, if $\mathcal{C}$ contains

  1. a class of objects
  2. for each pair $(a,b)$ of objects a hom object $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}^{\mathcal{V}}()(a,b) \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{V})$
  1. a composition functor
\begin{align*} \circ: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}^{\mathcal{V}}(X,Y) \otimes \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}^{\mathcal{V}}(Y,Z) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}^{\mathcal{V}}(X,Z) \end{align*}
