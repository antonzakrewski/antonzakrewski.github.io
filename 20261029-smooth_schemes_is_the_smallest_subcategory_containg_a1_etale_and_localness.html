smooth schemes is the smallest subcategory containg affine line, etale and localness

Proposition

Let $X$ be a scheme. Then the category of smooth schemes over $X$ $\mathrm{Sm}_{X}$ is the smallest full subcategory $\mathcal{C}$ containing $X$ such that

  1. it is closed under open subschemes and if a scheme $Y \in \mathrm{Sch}_{X}$ is covered by open subschemes $V_i \in \mathcal{C}$, then $Y$ also lies in $\mathrm{Sch}_{X}$
  2. $\mathcal{C}$ contains all etale morphisms
  3. $\mathcal{C}$ is closed under products with the affine line

Proof
