inclusion from a coproduct to the product with the interval as cofibration

Proposition

let $X$ be a topological space. Then the inclusion

\begin{align*} \iota: X \times \{0,1\}^{\delta} \rightarrow& X \times [0,1] \end{align*}

is a cofibration

Proof

using cofibration and retraction Let

\begin{align*} \varphi: X \times [0,1] \times [0,1] \rightarrow X \times \{0,1\} \times [0,1] \coprod_{A} X \times [0,1] \times \{0\} (x,a,b) \mapsto& (x,\varphi(a,b)) \end{align*}

where

\begin{align*} \varphi: [0,1] \times [0,1] \rightarrow& \{0,1\} \times [0,1] \coprod [0,1] \times \{0\} \\ (a,b) \mapsto& … \end{align*}

is given by taking the point $(2,0)$, taking the straight line $(a,b)$ and the intersection with $\{0,1\} \times [0,1] \coprod [0,1] \times \{0\}$
