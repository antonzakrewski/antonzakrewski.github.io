fiber sequence of the inclusion of CP^n into CP infinity

Proposition

The inclusion of $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}$ lives in a fiber sequence

\begin{align*} S^{2n+1} \rightarrow \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty} \end{align*}

Proof

a)

Let $\mathrm{Fib}$ be the fiber of the inclusion. Note that the skeleton inclusion $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n = \mathrm{Sk}_{2n+1}(\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n) \rightarrow \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty}$ is $2n+1$ connected. Furthermore $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n$ is simply connected. Furthermore $\mathrm{cofib}$ of $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^{\infty} \rightarrow \mathrm{cofib}$ has homology in degrees $2n+2$ and above.

So relative Hurewicz theorem tells us that the homotopy of the fiber

\begin{align*} \pi_{2n+1}(\mathrm{Fib}) \cong H_{2n+2}(\mathrm{cofib}) \cong \mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

and it has to be zero before that.
