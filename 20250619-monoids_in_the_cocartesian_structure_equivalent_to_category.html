monoids in the cocartesian structure equivalent to category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category with finite infinity coproducts Then the infinity functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ev}_1: \mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

is an equivalence

Proof

essentially surjective functor

follows from the monoid defined by

\begin{align*} \nabla: C \coprod C \rightarrow C \end{align*}

by the codiagonal morphism.
