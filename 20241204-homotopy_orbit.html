homotopy orbit

Definition

Let $X$ be a topological space, $G$ a topological group and $X \times G \rightarrow X$ a left topological group action. Then the homotopy orbit is defined - up to homotopy equivalence - as

\begin{align*} X//G \cong EG \times_{G} X \end{align*}

where $EG \times_{G} X \rightarrow X$ is the associated bundle

Idea

Take $X = X \times * \simeq X \times EG$ and then coequalize both actions - once for $EG$ as principal g-bundle and once for $X$ as G-Object
