tensor product of algebraic line bundles

Proposition

Let $M,N$ be algebraic line bundles over a common commutative ring $A$. Then

\begin{align*} M \otimes_A N \end{align*}

is also an algebraic line bundle

Proof

proj

  • tensor product of projective modules as projective module

finitely generated

  • tensor product of finitely generated modules

constant rank

follows from rank map and tensor product, as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{rk}_{\mathfrak{p}}( M \otimes_R N) = \mathrm{rk}_{\mathfrak{p}}(M) \cdot \mathrm{rk}_{\mathfrak{p}}(N) \end{align*}
