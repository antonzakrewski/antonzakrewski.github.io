right kan extension along a fully faithful functor is fully faithful

Proposition

Let $\iota: \mathcal{C}_0 \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be a fully faithful functor. Suppose $\mathcal{D}$ be a category with enough limits for the right kan extension formula.

Then the right kan extension functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Ran}_{\iota}: \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}_0,\mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

is also fully faithful

Proof

corollary of:

  • fully faithful infinity right adjoint and counit as equivalence
  • right kan extension along a fully faithful functor commutes up to natural equivalence
