morphism of ring spectra induces a morphism on cohomology

Proposition

Let $R \rightarrow S$ be a map of ring spectra. This then induces a natural transformation

\begin{align*} R^*[X] \rightarrow S^*[X] \end{align*}

of ring spectra for any anima $X$

Proof

Then cohomology is given as $\mathrm{lim}_X R$. But of course we have a map

\begin{align*} \mathrm{const} R \rightarrow \mathrm{const} S \end{align*}

given by composition with $R \rightarrow S$

But in particular taking limit is functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(X, \mathrm{RingSp}) \rightarrow \mathrm{RingSp} \end{align*}

so we get a desired map
