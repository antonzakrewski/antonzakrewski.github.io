amount of matching on a bipartite graph

Proposition

Let $n,m \in \mathbb{N}$ and $K_{n,m}$ the complete bipartite graph and $l \leq m,n$. Then there exist

\begin{align*} l! \cdot \binom{m}{l} \cdot \binom{n}{l} \end{align*}

matchings with $l$ many edges.

Proof

note that

\begin{align*} l! \cdot \binom{m}{l} \cdot \binom{n}{l} =& \binom{m}{l} \cdot \frac{n!}{(n-l)!} \end{align*}
