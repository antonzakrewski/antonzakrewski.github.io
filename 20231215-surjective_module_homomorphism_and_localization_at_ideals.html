surjective module homomorphism and localization at ideals

Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring / commutative ring, $M,N$ \(R\)-module and $\varphi: M \rightarrow N$ a module-homomorphism.

TFAE:

  1. $\varphi$ is surjective
  2. for each prime ideal $\mathfrak{p} \in \mathrm{Spec}(R)$, the localization at the ideal $\varphi_{\mathfrak{p}}: M_{\mathfrak{p}} \rightarrow N_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is surjective
  3. for each maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m} \in \mathrm{Spec}(R)$, the localization at the ideal $\varphi_{\mathfrak{m}}: M_{\mathfrak{m}} \rightarrow N_{\mathfrak{m}}$ is surjective

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows from localization functor preserves surjections

2) $\implies$ 3)

special case

3) $\implies$ 1)
