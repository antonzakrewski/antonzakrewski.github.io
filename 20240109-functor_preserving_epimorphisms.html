functor preserving epimorphisms

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a functor. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be preserve epimorphisms, if for an epimorphism $f \in \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(f) \end{align*}

is also an epimorphism
