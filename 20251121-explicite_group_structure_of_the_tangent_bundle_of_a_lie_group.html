explicit group structure of the tangent bundle of a lie group

Proposition

Let $G$ be a lie group. Then the group structure on $TG$ is under the canonical isomorphism $TG \simeq G \times \mathbb{R}^n$ given by

\begin{align*} (G \times \mathbb{R}^n)\times (G \times \mathbb{R}^n) \rightarrow& G \times \mathbb{R}^n \\ ((g,v), (g',v')) \mapsto& (g \cdot g', v + v') \end{align*}

Proof
