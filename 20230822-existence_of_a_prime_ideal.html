existence of a prime ideal

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. TFAE:

  1. there exists a prime ideal
  2. $R \neq 0$ (cf. zero ring)

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

Proof by contraposition: Since $0$ doesn't contain strict ideals, there can't exist a prime ideal

2) $\implies$ 1)

corollary of:

  • existence of a maximal ideal for a strict ideal
  • existence of a minimal prime ideal for a strict ideal

for the zero ideal as strict ideal
