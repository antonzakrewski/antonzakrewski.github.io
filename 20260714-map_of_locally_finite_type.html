morphisms of locally finite type

Definition

Let $f: X \rightarrow S$ be a morphism of algebraic functors. Then $f$ is said to be of locally finite presentation if, for every ring $k$ and every k-point $\mathrm{Spec}(k) \rightarrow S$, the pullback

is locally of finite type when regarded as algebraic k-functor, i.e. $X \times_{S} \mathrm{Spec}(k) \rightarrow \mathrm{Spec}(k)$.
