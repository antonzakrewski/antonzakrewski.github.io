infinity groupoid core and 1-categorical pullback

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be infinity categories. Then the infinity groupoid core commutes with the \(1\)-categorical pullback

l l

\begin{align*} (E \times_{\mathcal{D}} \mathcal{C})^{\simeq} \cong E^{\simeq} \times_{\mathcal{D}^{\simeq}} \mathcal{C}^{\simeq} \end{align*}

Proof

  • infinity groupoid core functor as right adjoint
  • right adjoint preserves limits
