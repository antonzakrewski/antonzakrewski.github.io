smash product of spheres

Proposition

For the sphere and the smash product, it holds that

\begin{align*} S^n \wedge S^m \cong S^{m + n} \end{align*}

Proof

Since smash products commute with one point compactification, we conclude by one point compactification of the euclidean space as n-sphere we conclude

\begin{align*} S^n \wedge S^m \cong& (\mathbb{R}^n)^+ \wedge (\mathbb{R}^m)^+ \\ \cong& ( \mathbb{R}^n \times \mathbb{R}^m)^+ \\ \cong& (\mathbb{R}^{n + m})^+ \\ \cong& S^{n + m} \end{align*}
