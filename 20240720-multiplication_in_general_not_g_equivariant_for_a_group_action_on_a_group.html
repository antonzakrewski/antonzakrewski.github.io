multiplication in general not g-equivariant for a group action on a group

Proposition

Let $G,H$ be groups, $\varphi: G \rightarrow H$ a group action. Then

Proof

consider

  1. $H$ any (reasonable) abelian group, e.g. $\mathbb{Z}$
  2. $G = C_2$.

Then

\begin{align*} \varphi: C_2 \rightarrow& \mathrm{Aut}(H,H) 1 \mapsto (-)^{-1} \end{align*}

suffices as group action inverse map as group homomorphism for an abelian group
