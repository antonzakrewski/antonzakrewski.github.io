isolated prime ideal as minimal prime ideal

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a}$ a strict ideal and $\mathfrak{p} \supseteq \mathfrak{a}$ a prime ideal TFAE:

  1. $\mathfrak{p}$ is minimal prime ideal over an ideal
  2. $\mathfrak{p}$ is an isolated prime ideal to $\mathfrak{a}$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)
