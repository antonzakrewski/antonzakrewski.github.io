internal hom out of a dualizable object preserves colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \otimes, \mathbbm{1}$ be a closed symmetric monoidal category and $x \in \mathcal{C}$ an object. Then $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,-)$ preserves colimits

Proof

Recall that $- \otimes x \dashv \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(x,-)$ is an adjunction, but as $x$ is dualizable, we also have an adjunction $- \otimes x \dashv - \otimes x^{\vee}$ and $- \otimes x^{\vee} \dashv - \otimes x$. uniqueness of a right adjoint shows that $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,-) \simeq - \otimes x^{\vee}$ are equivalent and $- \otimes x^{\vee}$ is also a left adjoint with right adjoint $- \otimes x$. Therefore left adjoint preserves colimits applies.
