singular homology is a homotopy invariant functor

Proposition

The singular homology functor is a homotopy invariant functor

Proof

Using large cardinal yoga (?) we will pretend that $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Top}, \mathrm{Mod}(\mathbb{Z}))$ si an abelian category

Then consider the chain complex

\begin{align*} n \mapsto (X \mapsto C_n^{\mathrm{sing}}(X)) \end{align*}

induced by the identification of a functor category of chain complexes as chain complexes of functors

Then it is by definition levelwise projective (by reasons of (enriched ?) yoneda, similar to representable functor as projective object in the functor category and left adjoint of a functor preserving epimorphisms preserves projective objects, epimorphism in Ab as surjection) Consider

\begin{align*} C_*^{\mathrm{sing}}(-), C_{*}^{\mathrm{sing}}(- \times [0,1]) \end{align*}

viewed as objects in $\mathrm{Ch}(\mathrm{Fun}(\mathrm{Top}, \mathrm{Ab})$.

For $n = 0$ define the map

\begin{align*} h_0: C_0^{\mathrm{sing}}(-) \rightarrow \mathrm{C}_1(- \times [0,1]) \end{align*}

which under the free group adjunction and yoneda corresponds to

\begin{align*} \mathrm{id}: \mathrm{Hom}(\Delta^0,-) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}(\Delta^1, - \times [0,1]) \end{align*}

Then the composite

\begin{align*} C_0^{\mathrm{sing}}(-) \rightarrow C_{1}^{\mathrm{sing}}(- \times [0,1]) \rightarrow C_0^{\mathrm{sing}}(- \times [0,1]) \end{align*}

with $d_1$ is given by

\begin{align*} C_0^{\mathrm{sing}}(\iota_0) - C_{0}^{\mathrm{sing}}(\iota_1) \end{align*}

using the fundamental tautology of homological algebra it suffices to show that certain maps

\begin{align*} C_n^\mathrm{sing}(-) \rightarrow H_n(- \times [0,1]) \end{align*}

vanish.

By yoneda and adjunction those maps are given by elements

\begin{align*} H_n(\Delta^n_{\mathrm{top}} \times [0,1]) \end{align*}

which is a convex set resp. starshaped set.

Thus by homology of a starshaped subset as zero group there exists only one map.
