segal mapping anima

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a segal anima. Then for $x,x' \in X_0$ the mapping anima is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{X}(x,x') = \mathrm{fib}(X_1 \rightarrow X_0 \times X_0) \end{align*}

or

where $p_1$ is part of the segal map is interpreted as projection to the start resp. endpoint of the morphism.
