commutative simple ring as field

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative simple ring. Then $R$ is a field

Proof

By assumption the zero ideal $0$ is the maximal ideal. Therefore $R/0$ is a field (see: maximales Ideal eines kommutativen Rings und Faktorring als Körper) Furthermore

\begin{align*} R/0 \cong R \end{align*}

Hence $R$ is a field
