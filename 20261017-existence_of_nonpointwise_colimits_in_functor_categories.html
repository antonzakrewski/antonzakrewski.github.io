existence of nonpointwise colimits in functor categories

Proposition

There exist small categories $\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}$ and a colimit in $\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})$ which is not preserved by $\mathrm{ev}_c$ for some $c$.

Proof

dual to existence of nonpointwise limits in functor categories.
