unique lift of orientations for a covering map

Proposition

Let $B,E$ be topological spaces and $p: E \rightarrow B$ a covering map Then for an R-Orientation $\tau of $B$ there exists a unique orientation $\sigma$ of $E$ such that

\begin{align*} p: E \rightarrow B \end{align*}

is an orientation preserving map

Proof

Choose a trivializing open cover $U_i$. Then for each copy of $U_i$ in $E$ let

\begin{align*} \sigma_{U_i} \coloneqq \tau_{U_i} \end{align*}

using glueing of compatible families of R-orientations for an open cover it remains to show that this is a compatible family of orientations

Given some point $x \in U_i \cap U_j$ it follows that 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\sigma_{U_i,x} \arrow[]{d}{=} \arrow[dashed]{r}{=}& & \sigma_{U_j,x} \arrow[]{d}{=}\\
\tau_{U_i,x} \arrow[]{d}{} & & \tau_{U_j,x} \arrow[]{d}{}\\
\tau_{U_i \cap U_j,x} \arrow[]{r}{=} & \tau_{U_j \cap U_i, x} \\
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

hence $\sigma_{U_i}$ is a compatible family
