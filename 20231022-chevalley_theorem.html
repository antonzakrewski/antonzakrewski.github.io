Chevalley theorem

Proposition

Let $\mathbb{F}_q$ be a finite field of $\mathrm{char}(\mathbb{F}_q) = p$ and $f \in \mathbb{F}_q[X_1,...,X_n]$ a polynomial with $\mathrm{deg}(f) < n$. Suppose

\begin{align*} f(0) = 0 \end{align*}

then there exists another root
