functor preserving finite limits preserves compact limits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an infinity functor. Suppose $\mathcal{F}$ preserves finite limits.

Then $\mathcal{F}$ also preserves compact limits
