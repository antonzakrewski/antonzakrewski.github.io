Lurie injection from the grothendieck topologies and left exact topologies on a small category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category and $\mathcal{L}_{\tau}$ the sheafification The assignment $\tau \mapsto \mathcal{L}_{\tau}$ gives an injection

\begin{align*} \{\texrm{ Grothendieck topologies} on \mathcal{C} \} \hookrightarrow \{\textrm{ left exact localizaitons of } \mathcal{P}(\mathcal{C})\} \end{align*}

Proof
