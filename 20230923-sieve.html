1-sieve

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a small category. A sieve $S$ on an object $X$ is a subfunctor of the hom-functor

\begin{align*} S \subseteq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,X) \end{align*}

in particular: $S: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow X$ is a functor equipped with a natural transformation $S \rightarrow X$ which is objectwise an injection

Note

Suppose $\alpha \in S(A) \subseteq \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(A,X)$, then for a morphism $g \in \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(B, A)$ it follows, that

\begin{align*} \alpha \circ g: B \rightarrow X \in S(B) \end{align*}
