p-complete spectrum and derived p-complete homotopy groups

Proposition

Let $X$ be a spectrum. TFAE:

  1. $X$ is p-complete
  2. $\pi_*(X)$ is derived p-complete

Proof

recall that $X$ is p-complete if and only if $\lim_{n \in \mathbb{N}} (... \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X )$ vanishes (cf. p-complete spectrum and vinishing sequential limit of multiplication). Moreover an abelian group is derived p-complete if and only if the sequential limit of $... \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X$ in Category Ab and its lim-lim1 vanishes (cf. derived p-complete abelian group and vanishing ordinary sequential limit of multiplications and vanishing limlim1).

Now recall the lim-lim1 sequence of homotopy objects in a stable category.

\begin{align*} 0 \rightarrow \mathrm{lim} \mathrm{lim}^1_{n \geq 0} \pi_{k+1}(X_n) \rightarrow \pi_k(\mathrm{lim}_{n \geq 0} X_n) \rightarrow \mathrm{lim}_{n \geq 0} \pi_k(X_n) \rightarrow 0 \end{align*}

1) $\implies$ 2)

If $X$ is p-complete, then $\mathrm{lim}_{n \geq 0} X_n$ and hence $\pi_k(\mathrm{lim}_{n \geq 0} X_n)$ vanishes. This shows that $\mathrm{lim} \mathrm{lim}^1_{n \geq 0} \pi_{k+1}(X_n), \mathrm{lim}_{n \geq 0} \pi_k(X_n)$ vanish or equivalently that $\pi_*(X)$ is derived p-complete.

2) $\implies$ 1)

If $\pi_{k}(X)$ is derived p-complete for all $k$, then both outer terms vanish. Hence $\pi_k(\mathrm{lim}_{n \geq 0} X_n)$ or equivalently $\mathrm{lim}_{n \geq 0} X_n)$ vanishes.
