postcomposition with product preserving symmetric monoidal and accessible functor on Grp as lax symmetric monoidal functor

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C},\mathcal{D}$ be closed symmetric monoidal presentable infinity categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a product preserving, symmetric monoidal, accessible functor. Then postcomposition with $\mathcal{F}$

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_*: \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathrm{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathrm{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

inherits a lax symmetric monoidal structure

Proof
