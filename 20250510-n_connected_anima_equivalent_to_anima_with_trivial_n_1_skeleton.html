n-connected anima equivalent to anima with trivial n skeleton

Proposition

Let $X$ be an n-connected anima. then it is equivalent to an anima with trivial n skeleton

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sk}_{0}(X),...,\mathrm{sk}_{n}(X) \simeq * \end{align*}

Furthermore if $\pi_{n+1}(X) \neq 0$, then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{sk}_{n+1}(X) \simeq \bigvee S^{n+1} \end{align*}

Proof

use a CW-Approximation type argument
