right t-exact functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be stable infinity categories equipped with t-structures. Then an exact functor $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ is said to be right \(t\)-exact if

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(\mathcal{C}_{\geq 0}) \subseteq \mathcal{D}_{\geq 0} \end{align*}
