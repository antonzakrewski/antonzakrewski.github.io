category of E-local spectra is a stable subcategory

Proposition

The category of E-local spectra is a stable subcategory

Proof

follows from the equivalent characterization of E-local spectrum: The category $\mathrm{Sp}_{E}$ is given by the fiber of

\begin{align*} \prod_{A \textrm{ acyclic}} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(-,A): \mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \prod_{A \textrm{ acyclic}}\mathrm{Sp} \end{align*}

where arbitrary product of exact functors between stable categories is exact.

hence it is the stable as fiber of an exact functor.
