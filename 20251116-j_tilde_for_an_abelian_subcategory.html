j for an abelian subcategory via kernel

Proposition

Let $\iota: \mathcal{B} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{A}$ be an abelian full subcategory. Then consider the unit

\begin{align*} \epsilon: \mathrm{id}_{\mathrm{Pro}(\mathcal{A})} \rightarrow \iota(\iota^L(-)) \end{align*}

Then define the functor

\begin{align*} j: \mathrm{Pro}(\mathcal{A}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Pro}(\mathcal{A}) X \mapsto \mathrm{ker}(X \xrightarrow{\epsilon(X)} \iota(\iota^L(X)) ) \end{align*}

Proof
