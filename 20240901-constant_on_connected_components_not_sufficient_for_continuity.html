constant on connected components not sufficient for continuity

Proposition

Let $(X,\mathcal{T}_X)$ and $(Y,\mathcal{T}_Y)$ be topological spaces and $f: X \rightarrow Y$ a continuous map which is constant on each connected component

Proof

Consider the converging sequence space, which is totally disconnected (cf. converging sequence space is totally disconnected), hence each map is constant on each connected component. But by universal property of the converging sequence space not every map is connected.
