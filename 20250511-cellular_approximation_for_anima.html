cellular approximation for anima

Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be anima and $\mathrm{sk}_n(X), \mathrm{sk}_n(Y)$ cw structures. Then there

  1. exist an isomorphism $\pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}}(\mathrm{sk}_n(X),\mathrm{sk}_{n+1}(Y))) \rightarrow \pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}}(\mathrm{sk}_n(X),Y))$
  2. a surjection $\pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}}(\mathrm{sk}_n(X),\mathrm{sk}_{n}(Y))) \rightarrow \pi_0(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{An}}(\mathrm{sk}_n(X),Y))$

Proof

Let $\mathrm{fib}$ be the fiber of $\mathrm{sk}_n(X) \rightarrow X$, which is <so and so> connected (cf. n skeleton inclusion as n-1 connected map). follows from Hom and n-connected map
