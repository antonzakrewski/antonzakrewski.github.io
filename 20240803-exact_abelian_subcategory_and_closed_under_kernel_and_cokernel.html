exact abelian subcategory and closed under kernel, cokernel and biproducts

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $\mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{A}$ a full subcategory

TFAE:

  1. $\mathcal{C}$ is an exact abelian subcategory
  2. $\mathcal{C}$ is closed under a) biproducts b) kernels c) cokernels

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows as the inclusion functor preserves kernels / cokernels / biproducts (and those exist, as $\mathcal{C}$ is an abelian category)

2) $\implies$ 1)

abelian

follows from image coimage isomorphism

exact

group structure, (co)limits are inherited
