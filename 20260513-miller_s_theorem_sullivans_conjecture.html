miller's theorem (sullivans conjecture)

Proposition

Let $G$ be a finite group and $BG$ the classifying space. Let $X$ be a finite anima, then $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(BG, \Omega^n X)$ is contractible.

Proof

reduction to the classical case

observe that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(BG, \Omega^n X) \simeq \Omega^n \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}_*}(BG, X) \end{align*}

so it suffices to prove this for $n = 0$.
