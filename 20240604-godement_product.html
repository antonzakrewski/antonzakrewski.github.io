godement product

Definition / Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}$ be categories and

be functors with natural transformations

\begin{align*} \alpha: \mathcal{F}_1 \rightarrow \mathcal{F}_2 \\ \beta: \mathcal{G}_1 \rightarrow \mathcal{G}_2 \\ \end{align*}

Then the godement product is defined as natural transformation

\begin{align*} (\beta \circ \alpha): \mathcal{F}_{2} \circ \mathcal{F}_1 \rightarrow \mathcal{G}_2 \circ \mathcal{G}_1 \end{align*}

which is given objectwise
