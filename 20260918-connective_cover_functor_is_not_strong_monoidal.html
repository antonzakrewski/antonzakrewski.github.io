connective cover functor is not strong monoidal

Proposition

The cover functor $\tau_{\geq 0}: \mathrm{Sp} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}$ of spectra does not admit a strong monoidal structure.

Proof

Let $X = \mathbb{S}[1], Y = \mathbb{S}[-1]$. then $X \otimes Y \simeq \mathbb{S}$ and $\tau_{\geq 0}(X \otimes Y) \simeq \mathbb{S}$ but

\begin{align*} \tau_{\geq 0}(\mathbb{S}[1]) \otimes \tau_{\geq 0}(\mathbb{S}[-1]) \simeq& \mathbb{S}[1] \otimes (\tau_{\geq 1} \mathbb{S})[-1] \\ \simeq& \tau_{\geq 1 } \mathbb{S} \end{align*}

Remark

This also shows $X \otimes \tau_{\geq 0} Y \simeq \tau_{\geq 0} X \otimes \tau_{\geq 0} Y \not\simeq \tau_{\geq 0}(X \otimes Y)
