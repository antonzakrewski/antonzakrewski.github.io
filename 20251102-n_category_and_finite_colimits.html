n-category and finite colimits

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an (n,1)-category which admits m-finite colimit for some $m \in [n,\infty]$. Then $\mathcal{C}$ also admits l-finite colimits for all $l \in [n,\infty]$.

Proof

follows from:

  • n-category admitting n-finite colimits and admitting finite coproducts and finite pushouts
