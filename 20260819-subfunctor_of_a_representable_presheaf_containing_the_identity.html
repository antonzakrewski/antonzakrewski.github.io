subfunctor of a representable presheaf containing the identity

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a (1,1)-Category and $U \subseteq よ(X)$ a Subfunctor TFAE:

  1. the inclusion $U \hookrightarrow よ(X)$ is an isomorphism
  2. $\mathrm{id} \in U(X)$ (after the identification)

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

clear

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $f: Y \rightarrow X$ be a morphism, then we get 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
U(X) \dar{f^*} \arrow[hookrightarrow, ]{r}{} & よ(X) \dar \\
U(Y) \arrow[hookrightarrow, ]{r}{} & よ(Y)
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}
