equivalent characterizations of an alternating multilinear form

Proposition

Let $V$ be a vector space over $K$ and $w: \prod_{i=1}^k V \rightarrow K$ be a multilineare map. TFAE:

  1. $w$ is alternating form
  2. $w(v_1,...,v_k) = 0$ if the multiset $\{v_1,...,v_k\}$ is linearly independent
  3. $w(v_1,...,v_k) = 0$ if there existse
  4. $w(v_1,..,.v_j,v_{j+1},...,v_n) = w(v_1,..,.v_{j+1},v_{j},...,v_n)$
