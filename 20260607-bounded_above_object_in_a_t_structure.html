trivial bounded below object in a left-complete t-structure

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable infinity category equipped with a left complete t-structure and $x \in \mathcal{C}_{\geq n}$ for some $n \in \mathbb{Z}$. TFAE:

  1. $x$ is the zero object
  2. $\pi_n(x) = 0$ for all $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

clear

2) $\implies$ 1)
