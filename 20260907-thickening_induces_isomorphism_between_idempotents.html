thickening induces isomorphism between idempotents

Proposition

Let $\varphi: R \rightarrow \overline{R}$ be a thickening of a ring. Then this induces a Bijection

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Idem}(R) \rightarrow \mathrm{Idem}(\overline{R}) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from:

  • clopen subfunctors and clopen subsets in the underlying space
  • classification of clopen subfunctors via idempotents
  • thickening of schemes induces isomorphism on underlying spaces
