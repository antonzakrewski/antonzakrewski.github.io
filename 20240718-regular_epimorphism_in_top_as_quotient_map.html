regular epimorphism in top as quotient map

Proposition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a continuous map in category Top

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is a regular epimorphism
  2. $f$ is a quotient map

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

2) $\implies$ 1)

Let $X^{\delta}$ be the free topology. Then choose for each point $y$ a fibre $x_y \in f^{-1}{y}$

Then consider the map

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow& X \\ x \mapsto& x_{f(x)} \end{align*}
