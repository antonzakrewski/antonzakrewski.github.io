top cohomology of a closed manifold_

Proposition

Let $M$ be a closed connected n-dimensional manifold. Then

\begin{align*} H^n(M, \mathbb{Z}) \cong \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{if } M \textrm{ orientable} \\ \mathbb{Z}/2 \mathbb{Z} & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

top cohomology of a non orientable, closed manifold & for the other statement use poincare duality
