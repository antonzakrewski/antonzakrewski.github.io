vector space endomorphism with kernel as image and dimension

Proposition

Let $\varphi: V \rightarrow V$ be a vectorspace-endomorphism between finite dimensional vector spaces with

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ker}(\varphi) = \mathrm{im}(\varphi) =: U \end{align*}

Then

\begin{align*} 2\cdot \mathrm{dim}(U) = \mathrm{dim}(V) \end{align*}

Proof

choose a basis $u_1,...,u_n$ of $U$. Then since $u_1,...,u_n \in \mathrm{im}(\varphi)$ we may choose preimages $w_1,...,w_n$, i.e. $\varphi(w_i) = u_i$. We claim that $\{u_1,...,u_n,w_1,...,w_n\}$ forms a basis. If we can show this, then we are done as this shows $\mathrm{dim}(U) = n, \mathrm{dim}(V) = 2n$.

linearly independent

Suppose we have a linear combination $\sum_{i=1}^{n} \lambda_i u_i + \sum_{j=1}^n \lambda'_j w_j = 0$. Then we may apply $\varphi$ and get

\begin{align*} 0 =& \varphi(0) \\ =& \varphi(\sum_{i=1}^{n} \lambda_i u_i + \sum_{j=1}^n \lambda'_j w_j)\\ =& ... \\ =& \sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i \varphi(u_i) + \sum_{j=1}^n \lambda_j' \varphi(w_j) =& \sum_{j=1}^n \lambda_j' u_j \end{align*}

since $u_i \in U =\mathrm{ker}(\varphi)$.

Now we use that $u_j$ is linearly independent to conclude that $\lambda'_j = 0$ for $j \in \{1,...,n\}$. Hence we get $\sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i u_i = 0$ and again (since $u_i$ is linearly independent) $\lambda_i = 0$.

generates $V$

Let $v \in V$ be arbitrary. Then $\varphi(v) \in \mathrm{im}(\varphi)$, so in particular $\varphi(v) = \sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i u_i = \sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i \varphi(w_i) = \varphi(\sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i w_i)$. Now consider $v - \sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i w_i$. It lies in the kernel since $\varphi(v - \sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i w_i) = \varphi(v) - \varphi(\sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i w_i) = 0$ and thus $v - \sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i w_i = \sum_{j=1}^n \lambda_j' u_i$. Then putting both results together yields

\begin{align*} v =& v - \sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i w_i + \sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i w_i \\ =& \sum_{j=1}^n \lambda_j' u_i + \sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i w_i \end{align*}

which shows that $v \in \langle u_1,...,u_n,w_1,...,w_n\rangle$
