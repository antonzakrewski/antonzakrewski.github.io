cauchy integral formula

Proposition

Let $\Omega \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be open and $f: \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ be holomorphic function Then $f$ has infinitely many complex derivatives and if $D$ is a disk with $\overline{D} \subseteq \Omega$ and $C = \partial D$, then for all $z \in D$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}_0$

\begin{align*} f^{(n)}(z) = \frac{n!}{2\pi \mathrm{i}} \int_{C} \frac{f(\zeta)}{(\zeta - z)^{n+1}} \mathrm{d} \zeta \end{align*}

Proof
