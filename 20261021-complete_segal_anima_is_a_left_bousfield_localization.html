complete segal anima is a left bousfield localization of the presheaf category

Proposition

The inclusions $\mathrm{cSAn} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Fun}_{\mathrm{Segal}}(\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An}) \hookrightarrow \mathcal{P}(\Delta^{\mathrm{op}})$ both admit left adjoints

Proof

use right adjoint functor theorem for presentable infinity categories

a)

The left adjoint to the inclusion $\mathrm{cSAn} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Fun}_{\mathrm{Segal}}(\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An})$ is the dwyer-kan localization at the fully faithful and essentially surjective maps. Here a map $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ of segal anima is fully faithful if for all $x,y \in \mathcal{C}_0$, the map $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(f(x),f(y))$ is an equivalence, where

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,y) = * \times_{\mathcal{C}_0} \mathcal{C}_1 \times_{\mathcal{C}_0} * \end{align*}

for where the maps to $\mathcal{C}_0$ are given by $x$, source, target, $y$.

A map is essentially surjective, if $f: \mathcal{C}_0 \rightarrow \mathcal{D}_0$ is essentially surjective on components.
