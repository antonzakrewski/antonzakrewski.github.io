brown representability theorem and natural transformations

Proposition

Let $H_1,H_2: \mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ab}$ be a cohomological functor and $\eta: H_1 \Rightarrow H_2$ a natural transformation Suppose $H_1 \simeq \pi_0(\mathrm{map}(-,X_1)), H_2 \simeq \pi_0(\mathrm{map}(-,X_2))$.

Then there exists a (in general non unique!) map of spectra $X_1 \rightarrow X_2$
