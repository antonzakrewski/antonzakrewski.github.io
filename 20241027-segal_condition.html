segal condition

Definition

Let $X: \Delta^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be an infinity simplicial object (or simplicial object) Then the segal condition states, that the segal map

\begin{align*} X_n \rightarrow X_1 \times_{X_0} X_1 \times_{X_0} ... \times_{X_0} X_1 \end{align*}

is an equivalence (or isomorphism in the 1 categorical case)
