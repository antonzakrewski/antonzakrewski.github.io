weak extension closure of an abelian category is abelian

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{B}\subseteq \mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category, such that $\mathcal{B}$ is closed under subobjects and quotients. Let $\overline{\mathcal{B}}$ be the full subcategory which contains extensions of objects from $\mathcal{B}$

Then $\overline{\mathcal{B}}$ is an abelian subcateogry

Proof

We want to show that $\overline{\mathcal{B}}$ is closed under sums, quotients and subobjects.

  1. sum: sum of short exact sequences is short exact
  2. quotient objects and subobjects

The first case follows as sums of short exact sequences is short exact.

Now suppose that $A \rightarrow B \rightarrow C$ is an exact sequence and $B' \subseteq B$ is a subobject. Then consider

where $A'$ is the pullback and $C'$ is the cokernel of $A' \rightarrow B'$.

Then as monomorphisms are preserved under pullbacks, we get that the top sequence is short exact and $A' \subseteq A$ is a subobject, so in particular $A \in \mathcal{B}$. So it remains to show that $C' \in \mathcal{B}$

Now apply the snake lemma, which shows that $0 \rightarrow \mathrm{ker}(C' \rightarrow C) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{coker}(A' \rightarrow A)$ is exact resp. $\mathrm{ker}(C' \rightarrow C) \in \mathcal{B}$ since it is a subobject. Now consider the short exact sequence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ker}(C' \rightarrow C) \rightarrow C' \rightarrow \mathrm{im}(C' \rightarrow C) \end{align*}

here $\mathrm{im}(C' \rightarrow C)$ is a subobject of $C$, so in particular in $\mathcal{B}$.
