category of finitely filtrated objects

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a pointed category Then the category of finitely filtrated objects is defined as full subcategory of $\mathrm{Fun}((\mathbb{Z}, \leq), \mathcal{C}$ containing $X$ such that

  1. $X(n) = 0$ for $n >> 0$
  2. $X(n) \cong X$ for some fixed $X \in \mathcal{C}$ and $n << 0$
  3. $X(n) \hookrightarrow X(n-1)$ is a monomorphism
