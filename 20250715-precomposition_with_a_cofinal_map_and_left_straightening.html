precomposition with a cofinal map and left straightening

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathrm{An}$ be an infinity functor, $\mathrm{Un}(\mathcal{F}) = p: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ the left unstraightening where $\mathcal{E}$ is an anima Let $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a cofinal map.

Then for the pullback

the top morphism is a weak equivalence

Proof

Consider $\mathcal{F} = \mathrm{Str}_{\mathrm{cocart}}(p) \circ f$. then it follows that the pullback $\mathcal{E}\times_{\mathcal{D}} \mathcal{C}$ in Cat infinity is given by the left unstraightening of $\mathcal{F}$ (cf. precomposition and cocartesian straightening). Note that by Lurie theorem about colimits in Cat infinity

\begin{align*} \vert \mathrm{Un}(\mathcal{F}) \vert \simeq \mathrm{colim}_{\mathcal{C}} \mathcal{F} \end{align*}

where by cofinality we get

\begin{align*} \vert \mathcal{E} \times_{\mathcal{D}} \mathcal{C} \vert \simeq& \vert \mathrm{Un}(\mathcal{F}) \vert \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{\mathcal{C}} \mathcal{F} \circ f \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{\mathcal{D}} \mathcal{F} \\ \simeq& \vert \mathrm{Un}(\mathcal{F}) \vert \\ \simeq& \vert \mathcal{E} \vert \end{align*}
