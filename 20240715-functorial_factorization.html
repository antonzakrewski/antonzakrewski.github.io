functorial factorization

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category. Then a functorial factorization is a functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{fact}: \mathcal{C}^{[1]} \rightarrow& \mathcal{C}^{[2]} \\ \end{align*}

such that for a diagram 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
A_1 \arrow[]{r}{} \arrow[]{d}{} & & C_1 \arrow[]{d}{}\\
A_2 \arrow[]{r}{}& & C_2
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

there exists an induced, functorial, commuting diagram
