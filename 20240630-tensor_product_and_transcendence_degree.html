tensor product and transcendence degree

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field, $A,B$ K-Algebras with $A,B \neq 0$ and $A \otimes_K B$ the tensor product of K-algebras. Then the transcendence degree is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{tr}(A \otimes_K B) =& \mathrm{tr}(A) + \mathrm{tr}(B) \end{align*}

Proof

finite case

  • transcendence degree as cardinality of the transcendence basis

Let $a_1,...,a_n$ and $b_1,...,b_m$ be transcendence basis of $A$ resp. $B$. Then $a_i \otimes 1, 1 \otimes b_j$ is a transcendence basis of $A \otimes_K B$

algebraically independent

Let

generating set

Let

\begin{align*} \alpha \in A \otimes_K B \end{align*}

then it is a finite sum of elements of the form

\begin{align*} \alpha \otimes_K \beta = (\alpha \otimes_K 1) \cdot (1 \otimes_K \beta) \end{align*}

Thus it suffices to generate $(\alpha \otimes_K 1)$ resp $(\beta \otimes_K 1)$.

taking a generator of $\beta$ in $B$ and then mapping into $A \otimes B$ suffices, as

\begin{align*} \alpha \mapsto& \alpha \otimes_K 1 \beta \mapsto& 1 \otimes_K \beta \end{align*}

is a ring homomorphism.
