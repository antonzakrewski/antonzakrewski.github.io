trivial in mathematics

Some thougths

  • avoid using trivial:
    • disencouraging for people who don't know why something is "trivial"
    • unnecessary information for people who know why something is true

I like instead

  • special case
  • by definition/ by construction: plugging in the definition/construction
  • by lemma X
  • by induction

because:

  • it is not longer
  • If you cannot summarize the triviality in like one sentence, it most likely is not so trivial at all...
