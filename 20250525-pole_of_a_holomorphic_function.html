pole of a holomorphic function

Definition

Let $\Omega \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be open, $z_0 \in \Omega$ and $f: \Omega \setminus \{z_0\} \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ be a holomorphic function Then $z_0$ is a pole if

  1. there exists a neighbourhood $U$ of $z_0$ such that $0\not\in f[U \setminus \{z_0\}]$
  2. $\frac{1}{f}$ extended as $\frac{1}{f}(z_0) = 0$ is holomorphic
