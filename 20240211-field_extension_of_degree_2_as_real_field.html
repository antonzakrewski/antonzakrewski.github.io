field extension of degree 2 as real field

Proposition

Let $K/\mathbb{Q}$ be an algebraic field extension of degree $2$ Then $K \subseteq \mathbb{R}$

Proof

Suppose $\alpha \in \mathbb{C}$, then

\begin{align*} \alpha = r \cdot \mathrm{e}^{x \mathrm{i}} \end{align*}
