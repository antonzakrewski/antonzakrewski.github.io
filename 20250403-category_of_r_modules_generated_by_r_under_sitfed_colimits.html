Proposition

Let \(R\) be a ring, then category RMod is generated under 1-sifted colimits by finitely generated free modules

Proof

using module as filtered colimit of finitely generated submodules it suffices to show that each finitely generated module is generated under sifted colimits. Here we can induct on the number of relations $a = 0$ and consider the reflexive coequalizer

\begin{align*} (a,\mathrm{id}), (0,\mathrm{id}): R \oplus M \rightarrow M \end{align*}

where both $R \oplus M,M$ are generated by the induction hypothesis
