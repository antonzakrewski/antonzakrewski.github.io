enriched opposite category functor

Definition

Let $\mathcal{V}$ be a symmetric monoidal category, $\mathrm{Cat}_\mathcal{V}$ the category of enriched categories Then the enriched opposite category functor is defined as

\begin{align*} (-)^\mathrm{op}: \mathrm{Cat}_\mathcal{V} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Cat}_\mathcal{V} \\ \mathcal{C} \mapsto& \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \\ \mathcal{F} \mapsto& \mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}
