Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $\mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ a full subcategory Suppose $\mathcal{C}$ admits sufficiently many colimits (e.g. $\mathcal{D}$ is small and $\mathcal{C}$ is cocomplete).

Denote for $c \in \mathcal{C}$ with $\mathcal{D}/c$ the infinity comma category and with

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim} \mathcal{D}/c \end{align*}

the colimit of $\mathcal{D}/y$ after applying the forgetful functor forgetting $y$ (note that we assume that this colimit exists for $c \in \mathcal{C}$.

Denote $\overline{\mathcal{D}} \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ the full subcategory of $\mathcal{C}$ containing objects $d$ with $\mathrm{colim} \mathcal{D}/c \simeq d$ for some $c \in \mathcal{C}$.

Then the inclusion

\begin{align*} \overline{\mathcal{D}} \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

is a left adjoint

Proof

using pointwise construction of an infinity adjunction
