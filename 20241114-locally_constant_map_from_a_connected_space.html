locally constant map from a connected space as constant map

Proposition

Let $X$ be a connected topological space, $Y$ another topological space and

\begin{align*} f: X \rightarrow Y \end{align*}

a locally constant map

Then $f$ is constant

Proof

Suppose w.l.o.g. $X \neq \emptyset$. Then choose $x \in X$. It follows that

\begin{align*} f^{-1}[ f(x)] \sqcup f^{-1}[Y \setminus f(x)] \end{align*}

is a cover. By assumption, for each point $x' \in f^{-1}[y]$ there exists an open neighbourhood $U \subseteq f^{-1}[y]$. By openness and neighbourhood for all points we may conclude, that arbitrary preimages $f^{-1}$ are open. Here $x \in f^{-1}[f(x)] \neq \emptyset$, hence by connectedness we may assume that $f^{-1}[Y \setminus f(x)] = \emptyset$.
