n-truncation from anima valued commutative groups

Definition

The n-truncation functor

\begin{align*} \tau_{\leq n}\mathrm{CGrp}(\mathrm{An}) \rightarrow \mathrm{CGrp}(\mathrm{Set}) = \mathrm{Ab} \end{align*}

is defined via postcompisition with $\tau_{\leq n}$

  • n-truncation preserves finite products
  • postcomposition with product preserving left adjoint induces adjunction on category of cartesian groups
