map into pontryagin bidual is injective

Proposition

Let $G$ be an abelian group Then the map

\begin{align*} G \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(G,\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}), \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}) \end{align*}

is an injection

Proof

let $g \in G$ be an element. Then there exists a map $f: G \rightarrow \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$ which satisfies $f(g) \neq 0$ (cf. rational circle group is an injective group, rational circle group is a cogenerator ), hence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{ev}_g: \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(G,\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}) \rightarrow \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z} \end{align*}

is nontrivial.
