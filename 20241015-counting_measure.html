counting measure

Definition

Let $X$ be a set, $\mathcal{P}(X)$ the powerset as sigma-algebra. Then the counting measure is defined as

\begin{align*} \mu: \sigma \rightarrow& [0, \infty] \\ A \mapsto& \vert A \vert \end{align*}
