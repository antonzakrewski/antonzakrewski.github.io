infinity category of C-valued presheaves

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories Then the infinity category of $\mathcal{C}$ valued infinity presheaves on $\mathcal{D}$ is defined as infinity functor category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
