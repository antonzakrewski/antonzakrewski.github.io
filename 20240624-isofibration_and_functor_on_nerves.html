isofibration and functor on nerve of homotopy

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be infinity categories and $f: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an inner fibration

TFAE:

  1. $f$ is an isofibration between infinity categories
  2. the induced functor
\begin{align*} \mathcal{N}(h \mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathcal{N}(h \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

is an isofibration

  • homotopy category functor
