Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a closed symmetric monoidal infinity category and $\mathcal{L}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{R}: \mathcal{D} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a symmetric monoidal localization. Then for each pair of objects $C,D \in \mathcal{C}$, where $D$ is a local object, the unit of the localization

\begin{align*} C \rightarrow \mathcal{RL}(C) \end{align*}

induces an equivalence

\begin{align*} \underline{\mathrm{map}}(\mathcal{RL}(C), D) \rightarrow \underline{\mathrm{map}}(C,D) \end{align*}
