filtered category is weakly contractible

Proposition

Let $X$ be a kappa filtered simplicial set. Then $X$ is weakly contractible.

Proof

HTT 5.3.1.18

using whitehead theorem for anima & colimit of a point in Anima & kan replacement as dwyer-kan localization consider

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{X} * \simeq \vert X \vert \end{align*}

Then any map

\begin{align*} S^n \rightarrow \mathrm{colim}_X * \end{align*}

factors through some $*$, since $S^n$ is compact.

Hence any map $S^n \rightarrow \vert X \vert$ is nullhomotopic.
