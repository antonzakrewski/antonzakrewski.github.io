every object is projective (epimorphism preserving) in a stable category

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category and $x \in \mathcal{C}$ an object. Then $x$ is projective in the sense that $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(x,-)$ preserves epimorphisms

Proof

follows from:

  • epimorphisms are equivalences in a stable category
  • and functors trivially preserve equivalences + equivalences are epimorphisms.
