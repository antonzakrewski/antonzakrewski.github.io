p-completion of spectra is a left adjoint

Proposition

the p-completion of spectra

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sp} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp}_p^{\wedge} \end{align*}

is a left adjoint for the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Sp}_p^{\wedge} \rightarrow \mathrm{Sp} \end{align*}

In particular, it is a well-defined functor. cf: pointwise construction of left adjoints

Proof

Let $Y$ be a p-complete spectrum and $X$ any spectrum.. then there exists a fiber sequence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{lim}(... \rightarrow X \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X) \rightarrow X_p^{\wedge} \end{align*}

(cf. fiber of p-completion of spectra). Then $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(-,Y)$ is an exact functor so we get a fiber sequence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X_p^{\wedge},Y) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(X,Y) \rightarrow \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathrm{lim}(... \rightarrow X \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X), Y) \end{align*}

we need to show that the first map is an equivalence or equivalently, that the cofiber $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathrm{lim}(... \rightarrow X \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X), Y)$ vanishes. Here $\mathrm{lim}(... \rightarrow X \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X)$ is \(R\)-local for $R = \mathbb{Z}[\frac{1}{p}]$ (cf. sequential limit of a morphism inverts the morphism) and hence $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Sp}}(\mathrm{lim}(... \rightarrow X \xrightarrow{\cdot p} X), Y) \simeq 0$. (cf. p-complete spectrum and mapping out of an R-local spectrum)
