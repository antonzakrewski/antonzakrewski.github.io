endomorphism representation of a simple representation is semi-simple

Proposition

Let $V$ be a simple representation and consider $\mathrm{End}_{k}(V)$ with the action by $A$ via left multiplication. Then $\mathrm{End}_{k}(V)$ is semi-simple.

Proof

consider the map $\mathrm{End}_{k}(V) \rightarrow \bigoplus_{n} V$ where the map is given by

\begin{align*} \mathrm{End}_{k}(V) \rightarrow \bigoplus_{n} V A \mapsto& (Av_1,...,Av_n) \end{align*}

after choosing a basis $v_1,...,v_n$.

This is a map of representations (todo) and an isomorphism on the underlying vector space.
