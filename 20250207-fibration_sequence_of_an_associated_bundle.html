fibration sequence of an associated bundle

Proposition

Let $p: E \rightarrow B$ be a principal g-bundle and $F$ a G-Object. Then the associated bundle gives a homotopy fibration sequence 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
F \rar & E \times_{G} F & \rar  B
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

Proof

  • associated bundle aamouns fibre bundle
  • fibre bundle as serre fibration
  • homotopy fibre weakly homotopy equivalent to the fibre for a serre fibration
