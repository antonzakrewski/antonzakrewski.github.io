nonvanishing locus is open complement of the vanishing locus

Proposition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $I \subesteq R$ an ideal. Then the open complement of the vanishing locus $V(I)$ is the nonvanishing locus $D(I) = D(\sqrt{I})$.

Proof

follows from the

  1. classification of open subfunctors of affine schemes
  2. classification of closed subfunctors of affine schemes
