additive structure of the representation ring in good characteristic

Proposition

Let $G$ be a finite group and $\mathrm{char}(K) \not\mid \vert G \vert$. Pick a maximal set of pairwise non isomorphic simples.

Then the map

\begin{align*} \bigoplus_{[S_i]} \mathbb{Z} \rightarrow R_k(G) a s_i \mapsto a [S_i] \end{align*}

is an isomorphism of abelian groups

Proof

By maschkes theorem, each finite dimensional $V$ or finitely generated $k[G]$ module is isomorphic to a direct sum of $\bigoplus S_i^{n_i}$. So the monoid of isomorphism classes is generated by $S_i$ and the monoid satisfies has no further relations as a commutative monoid by Jordan-Hölder theorem (?).

Now the group completion of $\bigoplus \mathbb{N}$ is then given by $\bigoplus \mathbb{Z}$.
