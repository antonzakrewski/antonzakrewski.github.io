dual algebraic line bundle

Definition / Proposition

Let $L$ be an algebraic line bundle over a commutative ring $R$. Then the dual algebraic line bundle is defined as

\begin{align*} L^* \coloneqq \mathrm{Hom}(L,R) \end{align*}

Proof

projective

  • contravariant hom functor of modules preserves projective modules

finitely generated

  • follosw as summand of $R^n$, projective

constant rank

Let $\mathfrak{p} \subseteq R$ be a prime ideal Then we have

\begin{align*} (L^*)_{\mathfrak{p}} =& (\mathrm{Hom}_{R}(L,R))_{\mathfrak{p}} \\ \cong& \mathrm{Hom}_{R_{\mathfrak{p}}}(L_{\mathfrak{p}}, R_{\mathfrak{p}}) \end{align*}

(cf. localization and hom-functor commute for finitely generated Modules over a Noetherian Ring)
