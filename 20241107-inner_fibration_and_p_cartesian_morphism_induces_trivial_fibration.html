inner fibration and p-cartesian morphism induces trivial fibration

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{E}, \mathcal{C}$ be infinity categories, $p: \mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ an inner fibration and $f: \Delta^1 \rightarrow \mathcal{E}$, $f: x \rightarrow y$ a p-cartesian morphism. Then for all $z \in \mathcal{E}$ the induced map

\begin{align*} \mathcal{E}^{/f} \times_{\mathcal{E}} \{z\} \rightarrow ( \mathcal{E}^{/y} \times_{\mathcal{C}^{/p(y)}} \mathcal{C}^{/pf}) \times_{\mathcal{E}} \{z\} \end{align*}

is a trivial fibration

Proof

Consider the commutative diagram

where by construction the big outer and lower squares are pullbacks.

Thus the upper square is also a pullback (cf. Window Lemma for pullbacks)

Here the map

\begin{align*} \mathcal{E}^{/f} \rightarrow \mathcal{E}^{/y} \times_{\mathcal{C}^{/p(y)}} \mathcal{C}^{/pf} \end{align*}

is by assumption a trivial fibration (cf. p-cartesian morphism and trivial fibration)

Then by right fibrations mirrored under pullbacks it follows that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{E}^{/f} \times_{\mathcal{E}} \{z\} \rightarrow ( \mathcal{E}^{/y} \times_{\mathcal{C}^{/p(y)}} \mathcal{C}^{/pf}) \times_{\mathcal{E}} \{z\} \end{align*}

is a trivial fibration
