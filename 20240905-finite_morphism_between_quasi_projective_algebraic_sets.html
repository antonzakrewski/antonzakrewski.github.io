finite morphism between quasi-projective algebraic sets

Definition

Let $f: X \rightarrow Y$ be a regular map between quasi-projective algebraic sets Then $f$ is said to be finite, if for each point $Q \in Y$ there exists an open affine neighbourhood $V$ such that

\begin{align*} U = f^{-1}[V] \subseteq X \end{align*}

is

  1. open affine
  2. $f_{\vert U}: U \rightarrow V$ induces a finite morphism of rings on affine coordinate rings
