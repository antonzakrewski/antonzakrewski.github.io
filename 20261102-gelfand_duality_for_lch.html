gelfand duality for LCH_+

Proposition

There exists an equivalence of categories

\begin{align*} C_0(-) :(\mathrm{LCH}_+)^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow C_{\mathrm{comm}}^*-\mathrm{Alg}^{nu} \end{align*}

where $C_0(X) = \mathrm{ker}(\mathrm{ev}_{*}: C(X^+) \rightarrow \mathbb{C})$.

Proof

follows from:

  • unitalization of c star algebras
  • LCH_+ is pointed CH
  • Gelfand duality for CH
