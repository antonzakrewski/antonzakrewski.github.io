perfect core

Definition / Proposition

Let $G$ be a group. Then there exists a unique maximal perfect subgroup

Proof

Consider the subgroup $H$ generated by all perfect subgroups $G_i$. We need to show that $H$ is also perfect. using perfect group and commutator it suffices to show that $[H,H] = H$. So let $h = g_1 \cdot ... g_n$ be an arbitrary element in $H$ for $g_i \in G_i$.

But $[H,H]$ is a subgroup (cf. commutator subgroup), so $h \in [H,H]$ follows from $g_i \in [G_i,G_i]$
