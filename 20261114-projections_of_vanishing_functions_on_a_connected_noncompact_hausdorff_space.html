projections of vanishing functions on a connected, noncompact hausdorff space

Proposition

Let $X$ be a noncompact, locally compact, hausdorff space and $B$ a C star algebra Then $C_{0}(X,B)$ has only the zero element as projection

Proof

Let $p \in C_0(X,B)$ be an element. Then we have a continuous map

which restricts to $\{0,1\}$ since the norm of a projection is contained there. But we also know that it must be locally constant (cf. continuous map into a discrete space as locally constant map) and hence constant since $X$ is connected (cf. locally constant map from a connected space as constant map).

We also know that we can extend $p: X \rightarrow B$ to a continuous map $p^+: X^+ \rightarrow B$ with $p(*) = 0$ out of the connected $X^+$. This implies that the composite must be the constant zero map, i.e. pointwise given by $0$.
