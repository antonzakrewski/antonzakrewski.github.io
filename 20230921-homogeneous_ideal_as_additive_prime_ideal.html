homogeneous ideal as additive prime ideal

Proposition

Let $M$ be a monoid, $R = \bigoplus_{m \in M} R_m$ a graded ring and $\mathfrak{h}$ an ideal

TFAE:

  1. $\mathfrak{h}$ is a homogeneous ideal
  2. for $r_{m_i} \in R_{m_i)$, $m_i \neq m_j \Leftrightarrow i \neq j$, $i \in \{1,...,n\}$ and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} r_{m_i} \in \mathfrak{h}$ it follows that $r_{m_i} \in \mathfrak{h}$ for all $i$
  3. for $r_{m_i} \in R_{m_i)$, $m_i \neq m_j \Leftrightarrow i \neq j$, $i \in \{1,...,n\}$ and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} r_{m_i} \in \mathfrak{h}$ it follows that $r_{m_i} \in \mathfrak{h}$ for some $i$

i.e. if the sum from different homogeneous components is an element, so is at least one summand

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

By assumption, $\sum r_{m_i} \in R \cong \bigoplus h_m$. Here by assumption each $r_{m_i}$ lies in different $h_m$, so none cancel.

Hence tracing $\pi_m: \bigoplus R_m \rightarrow R_m$ shows, that each $r_{m_i} \in h_{m_i}$ or $r_{m_i} \in h$ for all $i \in I$

2) $\implies$ 3)

special case

3) $\implies$ 1)

We claim, that $h = \bigoplus \pi_m(h)$. In particular, we want to show that $h \supseteq \bigoplus \pi_m(h)$. Let $r = \sum_{m \in M} r_m \in h$ be an element Then we may induct on the number of summands. Here the assumption shows, that at least one element $r_n$ lies in $h$. Thus

\begin{align*} \sum_{m \in M} r_m - r_n = \sum_{m \neq n} r_m \in h \end{align*}
