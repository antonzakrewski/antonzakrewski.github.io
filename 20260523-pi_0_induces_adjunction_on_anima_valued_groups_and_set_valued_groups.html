pi_0 induces adjunction on anima valued groups and set valued groups

Proposition

The functor pi_0 of anima induces an adjunction $\mathrm{Grp}(\mathrm{An}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Grp}(\mathrm{Set})$ see: category of cartesian groups pi_0 of Anima preserves products

Proof

follows from:

  • pi_0 induces adjunction on anima valued monoids and set valued monoids
  • infinity adjunctions restrict to full subcategories
  • anima valued group and pi_0 is a group
