homotopy category of a horn

Proposition

Let $\Lambda_j^n$ be the j-horn. Then the homotopy category is given by

\begin{align*} h \Lambda_j^n =& \begin{cases} [n] & \mbox{if } n \geq 3 \\ [2] & \mbox{if } n = 2, i = 1 \\ * & \mbox{if } n = 2 \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

and 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
& * & 
* \arrow[]{ru}{} \arrow[]{rr}{}& *  
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

for $\Lambda_0^2$

resp 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
& * \arrow[]{rd}{} & 
* \arrow[]{rr}{}& *  
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

for $\Lambda_2^2$

Proof

$n \geq 3$

  • isomorphism on 2 skeleton and isomorphism on homotopy category
  • homotopy category of a nerve canonically isomorphic

$n = 1$

  • follows from homotopy category functor as left adjoint and
\begin{align*} \Lambda_0^1 =& \Delta^1 \end{align*}

$n = 2$

follows similarly from realizing the horn as pushout, e.g. $\Lambda_2^2$ as
