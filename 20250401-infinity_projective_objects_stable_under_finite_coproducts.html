infinity projective objects stable under finite coproducts

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category. Then the full subcategory of projective objects is closed under finite coproducts

Proof

follows from:

  • geometric realization as infinity-sifted colimit

where

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\coprod_{i = 1}^n x_i, -) \simeq \prod_{i = 1}^n \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x_i, -) \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} \prod_{i = 1}^n \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x_i, \mathrm{colim}_{N(\Delta)^{\mathrm{op}}} y_j) \simeq& \prod_{i = 1}^n \mathrm{colim}_{N(\Delta)^{\mathrm{op}}} \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x_i, y_j) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{colim}_{N(\Delta)^{\mathrm{op}}} \prod_{i = 1}^n \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(x_i, y_j) \end{align*}
