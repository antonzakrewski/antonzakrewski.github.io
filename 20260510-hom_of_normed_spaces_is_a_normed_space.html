bounded hom of normed spaces is a normed space

Proposition

Let $X, Y$ be normed vector spaces, then

\begin{align*} \mathrm{BL}(X,Y) \coloneqq \{T: X \rightarrow Y \vert \text{linear and bounded}\} \end{align*}

is also a normed vector space with respect to the Operator norm

Proof

well-defined

follows from boundedness of $T$

$\| T \| \geq 0$

Follows as the operator norm is the supremum of nonnegative numbers

$\| T \| = 0$ if and only if $T = 0$

observe that $\mathrm{sup}_{\| x \| = 1} \frac{\| T(x) \|}{\| x \|}$ is zero if and only if $\| T(x) \| = 0$ for all $x$ and as $\| - \|$ is a norm on $Y$ we may conclude that $\| T(x) \| = 0$ if and only if $T(x) = 0$.

triangle inequality

Let $T_1,T_2 \in \mathrm{BL}(X,Y)$ and (for now) assume that $T_1 + T_2 \in \mathrm{BL}(X,Y)$. Then

\begin{align*} \| T_1 + T_2 \| \coloneqq& \mathrm{sup}_{\| x \| = 1} \frac{\| T_1(x) + T_2(x) \|}{\| x \|} \\ \leq& \mathrm{sup}_{\| x_1 \| = 1} \frac{\| T_1(x_1) \|}{\| x_1 \|} + \mathrm{sup}_{\| x_2 \| = 1} \frac{\| T_2(x_2) \|}{\| x_2 \|} \\ =&\| T_1 \| + \| T_2\| \end{align*}

scalar

Let $T_1 \in \mathrm{BL}(X,Y), \lambda \in \mathbb{K}$ and (for now) assume that $\lambda T_1 \in \mathrm{BL}(X,Y)$. Then

\begin{align*} \| \lambda T \| \coloneqq& \mathrm{sup}_{\| x \| = 1} \frac{\| \lambda T(x) \|}{\| x \|} \\ =& \lambda \cdot \mathrm{sup}_{\| x \| = 1} \frac{\| T(x) \|}{\| x \|} \\ =& \lambda \cdot \| T \| \end{align*}

well-defined vector space

Observe that it is a subset of $\mathrm{Hom}{\mathrm{Vect}{\mathbb{K}}}() now this follows immediately from the triangle inequality and the scalar multiplication: the (pointwies) addition / scalar multiplication of bounded linear operators is closed

Alternatively we use equivalent characterization of a bounded/continuous operators and get $T_1 + \lambda T_2$ as composition

where we use that scalar multiplication & addition is continuous on $Y$.
