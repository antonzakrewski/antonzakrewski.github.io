kan enriched category of infinity categories

Definition

The kan enriched category of infinity categories is defined as category containing

  1. infinity categories as objects
  2. $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D})^{\simeq}$ as hom objects where $\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(-,-)$ is the inner hom of simplicial Sets and $(-)^{\simeq}$ is the infinity groupoid core

see also:

  • infinity-groupoid and Kan complex

Proof

Note that there exists the simplicial category of infinity categories. Then a lax monoidal functor induces a a functor on category of of enriched functors. Here the infinity groupoid core functor is a monoidal functor, as infinity groupoid core functor preserves limits
