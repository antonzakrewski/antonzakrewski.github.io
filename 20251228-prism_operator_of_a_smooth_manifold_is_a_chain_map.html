prism operator of a smooth manifold is a chain homotopy

Proposition

The prism operator $P: \Omega^k([0,1] \times M) \rightarrow \Omega^{k-1}(M)$ is a chain homotopy between $\iota_0,\iota_1: \Omega^*([0,1] \times M) \rightarrow \Omega(M)$ (where the chains have nontrivial entries in the negative degrees).
