heart structure of a stable category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a stable category Then a heart structure is given by a pair of full subcategories $\mathcal{C}_{\geq 0}, \mathcal{C}_{\leq 0}$ satisfying the following properties

  1. $\mathcal{C}_{\geq 0}$ is closed under extensions and finite colimits
  2. $\mathcal{C}_{\leq 0}$ is also closed under extensions and finite limits
  3. for every $x\in \mathcal{C}$ there exsits a cofiber sequence

with $x_{\leq 0} \in \mathcal{C}_{\leq 0}$ and $x_{\geq 0} \in \mathcal{C}_{\geq 0}$
