n-th infinitesimal neighbourhood of a closed subfunctor

Proposition

Let $Z \subseteq X$ be a closed subfunctor of an algebraic functor $X$, classified by a quasi-coherent ideal $I$. Then the $n$-th infinitesimal neighbourhood $Z^{(n)}$ of $X$ is the closed subfunctor of $X$ defined by $I^{n+1}$

Proof
