nullhomotopic map in module spectra and nullhomotopic in spectra

Proposition

Let $R,S$ be ring spectra, $R \rightarrow S$ a map of ring spectra and $f: M \rightarrow N$ be a map of \(R\)-module spectra. TFAE:

  1. $f$ is nullhomotopic as a map of \(R\)-module spectra
  2. $f$ is nullhomotopic as map of \(S\)-module spectra for the restriction of scalars
  3. $f$ is nullhomotopic as a map of spectra

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

follows as restriction of scalars is additive (even exact)

2) $\implies$ 3)

follows as restriction of scalars is additive (even exact)

3) $\implies$

Recall that the restriction of scalars functor is conservative and preserves colimits (restriction of scalars preserves colimits). Hence it reflects colimits (cf. conservative functor reflects preserved colimits).

In particular, $\Sigma M \wedge N$ is the cofiber of $f$. But by cofiber and shift functor is an equivalence for a stable category this shows that $f$ has to be nullhomotopic.
