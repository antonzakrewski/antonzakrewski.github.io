existence of a minimal decomposition of a projective algebraic set into projective varieties

Proposition

Let $K$ be a field and $\mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ the projective space and $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}_{K}^n$ a projective algebraic set Then there exists projective varieties $V_i \subseteq \mathbb{P}_K^n$ such that

\begin{align*} \bigcup V_i = X \end{align*}

Proof

corollary of:
