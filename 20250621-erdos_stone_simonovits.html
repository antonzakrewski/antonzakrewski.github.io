Proposition

Let $\varepsilon > 0$ and $H$ a Graph. Then there exists an $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that for each H-free Graph $G$ with $V(G) = n > n_0$

\begin{align*} (1 - \frac{1}{\chi(H)-1} - \varepsilon) \frac{n^2}{2} \leq e(G) \leq (1 - \frac{1}{\chi(H)-1} + \varepsilon) \frac{n^2}{2} \end{align*}
