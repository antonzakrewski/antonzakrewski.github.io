multiplicative spectral sequence and y-axis

Proposition

Let $E_{*,*}^r$ be a multiplicative spectral sequence which is concentrated in the first quadrant. Suppose the internal multiplication

\begin{align*} E_{0,q}^r \otimes E_{p,0}^r \rightarrow E_{p,q}^r \end{align*}

is an epimorphism

Suppose we want understand the differentials of the \(r\)-th page.

Then it suffices to understand the differentials from the \(y\)-axis.

Proof

  • where one part vanishes for degree reasons
