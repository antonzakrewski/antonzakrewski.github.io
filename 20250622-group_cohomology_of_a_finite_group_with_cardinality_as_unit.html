group cohomology of a finite group with cardinality as unit

Proposition

Let $R$ be ring, $G$ a finite group with $\vert G \vert \in R^{\times}$ as unit. Then the group cohomology is

\begin{align*} H^n(\mathrm{B}G, R) \simeq \mathrm{Ext}_{R[G]}^n(R,R) = \begin{cases} R & \mbox{if } n = 0 \\ 0 & \mbox{else } \\ \end{cases} \end{align*}

Proof

$n = 0$

follows from

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{R[G]}(R,R) \simeq \mathrm{Hom}_R(R,R) \simeq R \end{align*}

or

\begin{align*} H^0(BG,R) = R \end{align*}

$n > 0$

follows from:

  • right derived functor of an injective object
  • ring as projective module for a group ring with cardinality as unit, hence injective in the opposite category.
