R-Orientation of a manifold

Definition

Let \(M\) be an \(n\)-dimensional manifold and $R$ a ring. Then an \(R\)-orientation is an element

\begin{align*} \sigma_X = (\sigma_{X,K})_{K} \in& \mathrm{lim}_{K \in \mathcal{K}_{X}^{\mathrm{op}}} H_n(X,X \setminus K;R) \in& H_n^{\mathrm{lf}} \end{align*}

such that each $\sigma_{X,K}$ is a R-Orientation at $K$
