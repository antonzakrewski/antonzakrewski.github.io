category of cartesian monoids

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category admitting finite infinity products Then the category of cartesian monoids is defined as full sub infinity category

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Mon}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(N(\Delta)^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}
