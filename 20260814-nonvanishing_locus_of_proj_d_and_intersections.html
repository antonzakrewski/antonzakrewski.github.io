nonvanishing locus of Proj_d and intersections

Proposition

Let $A$ be an N-graded ring and $f_1,...,f_k \in A$ be homogeneous elements of positive degrees. Then there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} D(f_1 \cdot ... f_k) = D(f_1) \cap ... \cap D(f_1) \end{align*}

Proof

follows from the isomorphism $D(f) \cong \mathrm{Spec}(A_{f})$ established in nonvanishing locus of Proj_d is affine and finite intersection of nonvanishing loci and nonvanishing locus of Spec for a single element is affine.
