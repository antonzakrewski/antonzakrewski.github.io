correspondence theorem for prime ideals and quotient ring

Proposition

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak{a}$ an ideal and $\mathfrak{p} \supseteq \mathfrak{a}$ another ideal. TFAE:

  1. $\mathfrak{p} \subseteq A$ is prime
  2. $[\mathfrak{p}] \subseteq A/\mathfrak{a}$ is a prime ideal

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

corollary of Image of an epimorphism and prime ideal

2) $\implies$ 1)

corollary of: Preimage of a prime ideal as prime ideal
