infinity colimit cone

Definition

Let $K$ be a simplicial set, $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category and $\mathcal{F}: K \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ a morphism of simplicial sets Then a cone $\overline{\mathcal{F}}: K \star \Delta^0 \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ if for all objects $x \in \mathcal{C}_0$ the canonical morphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\overline{\mathcal{F}}, x) \rightarrow \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathcal{F}, x) \end{align*}

is a homotopy equivalence
