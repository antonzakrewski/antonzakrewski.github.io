twisted poset of compact subspaces

Definition

Let $X$ be a topological space. Then the twisted poset of compact subspaces $M$ is defined as 1 category containing

  1. $(S,0)$ for $S$ compact, $(S,2)$ for $X \setminus S$ compact or $(S,1)$ for arbitrary $i$
  2. $(S,i) \leq (T,j)$ if one of those cases holds a) $S \subseteq T$ and $i \leq j$ b) $i = 0, j = 2$

Note

Let $M[i]$ denote the full subcategories containing $(S,i)$ for fixed $i$.

Then note that

\begin{align*} M[0] =& \mathcal{K}(X) \\ M[2] \simeq& \mathcal{K}(X)^{\mathrm{op}} \\ \end{align*}

are isomorphic to the directed poset of compact subspaces resp. the opposite category

Here the second uses the functor

\begin{align*} S \mapsto (X \setminus S) \end{align*}
