straightening functor of right fibrations

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category Then straightening functor of right fibrations is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{straight}_{\mathrm{RFib}}: \mathrm{RFib}(\mathcal{C}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathrm{An}) \end{align*}

by restricting to $\mathrm{RFib}(\mathcal{C}) \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Cart}(\mathcal{C})$

Proof
