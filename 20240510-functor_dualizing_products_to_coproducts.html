functor dualizing products to coproducts_

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be a contravariant functor. Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be dualizing products to products if $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ as covariant functor preserves coproducts
