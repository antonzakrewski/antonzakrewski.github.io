adjoint to cohomology

Proposition

Let $R$ be a spectrum and

\begin{align*} \mathrm{An} \rightarrow& \mathrm{Mod}(R)^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}

be the cohomology functor.

Then it is a left adjoint with right adjoint given by

\begin{align*} \Omega^{\infty} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(R)}(-,R): \mathrm{Mod}(R)^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathrm{An} \\ \end{align*}

Proof

pointwise construction of right adjoints Let $T$ be any spectrum in $\mathrm{Mod}(R)$ and $X$ any anima. Then we need to show that

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X,\Omega^{\infty} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(R)}(T,R)) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(R)^{\mathrm{op}}}(\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(R)}(R[X],R), T) \end{align*}

We write

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(R)^{\mathrm{op}}}(\mathrm{map}(R[X],R), T) \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(R)}(T,\mathrm{map}(R[X],R)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(R)}(T \times R[X], R) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(R)}(R[X], \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(R)}(T,R)) \\ \simeq& \mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(X, \Omega^{\infty} (\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{Mod}(R)}(T,R))) \end{align*}

which shows the adjoint.
