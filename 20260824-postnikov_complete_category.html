postnikov complete category

Definition

let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable category. Then $\mathcal{C}$ is said to be postnikov complete, if the canonical functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \lim_{n \in \mathbb{N}, \geq} (... \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{\leq n +1} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{\leq n} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}_{\leq n-1}) \end{align*}

is an equivalence.
