homogeneous prime spectrum

Definition

Let $A$ be an N-graded ring Then the homogeneous prime spectrum of $A$ is the topological space $\mathrm{hPrim}(A)$ defined as follows:

  1. the elements of $\mathrm{hPrim}(A)$ are the saturated homogeneous ideals of $A$ or equivalently the homogenous prime ideals of $A$ that do not contain $A_{+}$ (cf. criterion for saturated, homogenous prime ideals).
  2. The open sets of $\mathrm{hPrim}(A)$ are the the subsets $\mathrm{hPrim}(I) = \{\mathfrak{p} \vert I \subsetneq \mathfrak{p}\}$ for all homogeneous subsets $I \subseteq A$
