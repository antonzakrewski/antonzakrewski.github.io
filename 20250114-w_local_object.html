S-local object

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $x \in \mathcal{C}$ an object and $S \subseteq \mathrm{Mor}(\mathcal{C})$. Then $x$ is said to be \(S\)-local, if the mapping space functor is a S-local functor

In other words, precomposition with a morphism $f \in S$ on $\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{C}}(-,x)$ induces an equivalence.
