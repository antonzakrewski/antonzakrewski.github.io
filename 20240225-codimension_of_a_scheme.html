codimension of an irreducible closed subset of a scheme

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{O}_X, O_i)$ be a scheme and $Z \subseteq X$ an irreducible closed subset of $X$. Then the codimension is defined as supremum of integers $n \in \mathbb{N}$ such that there exists a chain

\begin{align*} Z = Z_0 \subsetneq Z_0 ... \subsetneq Z_n \end{align*}

of closed irreducible subsets.
