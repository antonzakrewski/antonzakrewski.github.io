composition in hsSet

Definition / Propositios

Let $X,Y,Z$ be simplicial sets. Then there exists a welldefined composition map

\begin{align*} [Y,Z] \times [X,Y] \rightarrow& [X,Z] \\ ([f],[g]) \mapsto& [f \circ g] \end{align*}

where $[-,-]$ is the hom-set in category hsSet

Proof

using simplicial maps up to homotopy as coequalizer 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathrm{Hom}_{}(Y \times \Delta^1, Z) \arrow[shift right=1, swap]{r}{\pi_1^*} \arrow[shift left=1]{r}{\pi_0^*} \dar{(f^* \times \mathrm{id})} & \mathrm{Hom}_{}(Y, Z) \dar{f^*} & [Y,Z] \\
\mathrm{Hom}_{}(X \times \Delta^1, Z) \arrow[shift right=1, swap]{r}{\pi_1^*} \arrow[shift left=1]{r}{\pi_0^*}& \mathrm{Hom}_{}(X, Z) & [X,Z]
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

which commutes, i.e. induces a welldefined map.

identity

follows from

\begin{align*} \mathrm{id} = [\mathrm{id}] \end{align*}

and

\begin{align*} [f] \circ [\mathrm{id}] =& [f \circ \mathrm{id} ] \\ =& [f] \end{align*}

analagously for the other side
