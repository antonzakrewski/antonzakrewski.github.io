quotient of smooth covering like group action from a finite group on a smooth manifold is a smooth manifold

Proposition

Let $G$ be a finite group and $G \curvearrowright M$ a free and smooth group action on a smooth manifold $M$. Then the quotient topology $M/\sim$ is canonically a smooth manifold where the smooth structure is uniquely determined by the property that the map

\begin{align*} \pi: M \rightarrow M/\sim \end{align*}

is a local diffeomorphism

Proof

a)

  • quotient of a covering like group action from a finite group on a manifold is a topological manifold

b) smooth structure

note that $\pi: M \rightarrow M/\sim$ is a covering map orbital projection of a free action as covering map and covering-like For $[x] \in M/\sim$ we may find an open neighbourhood $U_i \subseteq M/\sim$ with $\pi^{-1}[U_i] \simeq \coprod U_i$. So choose smooth charts $\mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow U_i \hookrightarrow M$ (w.l.o.g. restricting to $U_i$) and define charts

\begin{align*} \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow U_i \rightarrow M \rightarrow M/\sim \end{align*}

Those are local homeomorphisms since $\mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow U_i$ and $U_i \rightarrow M/\sim$ are local homeomorphisms.

Now let $\varphi_1: \mathbb{R}^n \hookrightarrow M/\sim, \varphi_2: \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow M/\sim$ be two charts around a point $m \in M$.
