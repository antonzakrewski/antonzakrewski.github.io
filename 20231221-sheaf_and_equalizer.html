sheaf on a complete category

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category containing (sufficiently many) products, $X$ a topological space and

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Op}(X) \rightarrow \mathcal{C} \end{align*}

a presheaf

Then $\mathcal{F}$ is said to be a sheaf, if for an open cover $U_i, i \in I$, the products and the morphisms

form an equalizer, where the morphisms are defined by the universal property using

monomorphism
