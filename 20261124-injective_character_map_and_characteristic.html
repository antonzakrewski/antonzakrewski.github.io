injective character map and characteristic 0

Proposition

Let $G$ be a finite group and $k$ a field Let $\chi: R_k(G) \rightarrow \mathrm{Cl}(G,k)$ be the character map

TFAE:

  1. the character map is injective
  2. $\mathrm{char}(k) = 0$

Proof

1) $\implies$ 2)

equal characters imply isomorphic representations for a semi-simple algebra in characteristic zero equal characters imply isomorphic representations for finite dimensional representations for characteristic zero

2) $\implies$ 1)
