infinity diagonal morphism

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category, $X \in \mathcal{C}$ an object such that $X \times X$ exists. Then the diagonal morphism is defined as

\begin{align*} \Delta: X \rightarrow X \times X \end{align*}

given by $(\mathrm{id}, \mathrm{id})$
