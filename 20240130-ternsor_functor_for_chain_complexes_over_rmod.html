ternsor functor for chain complexes over RMod

Definition / Proposition

Let $R$ be a ring, $\mathrm{RMod}$ the category RMod and $M$ an \(R\)-module. Then for the category of chain complexes $\mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod})$ there exists an induced functor

\begin{align*} (\otimes_{R} M)_*: \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod}) \rightarrow& \mathrm{Ch_*}(\mathrm{RMod}) \\ N_* \mapsto& (M \otimes_R N)_{*} \\ (f: N_* \rightarrow N_*') \mapsto& (f: (M \otimes_R N)_{*} \rightarrow (M \otimes_R N')_{*}) \end{align*}
