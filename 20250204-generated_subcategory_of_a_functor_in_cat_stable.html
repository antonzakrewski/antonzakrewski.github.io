generated subcategory under retracts of a functor in cat stable

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{C}'$ be stable categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ an exact functor Then define

\begin{align*} \langle \mathcal{F} \rangle \subseteq \mathcal{D} \end{align*}

as smallest full sub infinity category containing $\mathrm{im}(\mathcal{F}) \subseteq \mathcal{D}$ and which is closed under retracts
