endomorphism of exact triangles with outer zeros and composition

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a pre-triangulated category, $(X,Y,Z)$ an exact triangle and $(0,b,0), (0,b',0)$ be morphism of triangles. Then the composition

\begin{align*} (0,b,0) \circ (0,b',0) = (0,0,0) \end{align*}

is the zero morphism.
