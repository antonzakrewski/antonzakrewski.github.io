initial object as cogroup

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a category and $I$ the initial object Then there exists a unique cogroup structure on $I$ via the initial morphisms

\begin{align*} \mu: I \rightarrow& I \coprod I \\ \eta: I \rightarrow& I \\ \end{align*}

Proof

note that $I \coprod I$ exists (cf. coproduct with the initial object canonically iso) then everything commutes by uniqueness of the initial morphism.
