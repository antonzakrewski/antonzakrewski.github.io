connectedness as equivalence relation

Proposition

Let $(X, \mathcal{T})$ be a topological space. Then the Relation

\begin{align*} x \sim y \Leftrightarrow x \in C(y) \end{align*}

where $C(y)$ is the connected component is an equivalence relation
