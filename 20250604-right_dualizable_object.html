right dualizable object

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a monoidal infinity category and $X$ an object. We say that $X$ is right dualizable if there exists another object $X^{\vee}$ (called the right dual of $X$) and a pair of maps

  1. $c: 1 \to X \otimes X^{\vee}$ (coevaluation)
  2. $e: X^{\vee} \otimes X \to 1$ (evaluation)

(where 1 denotes the unit object of C) for which the composite maps 1)

\begin{align*} X \stackrel{\text{c}\otimes \text{id}}{\longrightarrow} X \otimes X^{\vee} \otimes X \stackrel{\text{id}\otimes e}{\longrightarrow} X \end{align*}
\begin{align*} X^\vee\stackrel{\mathrm{id}\otimes c}{\longrightarrow} X^\vee\otimes X\otimes X^\vee\stackrel{e\otimes\mathrm{id}}{\longrightarrow} X^\vee \end{align*}

are the identity on X and $X^{\vee}$, respectively.
