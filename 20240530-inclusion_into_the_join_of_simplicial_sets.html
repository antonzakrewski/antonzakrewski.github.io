left inclusion into the join of simplicial sets

Definition / Proposition

Let $X,Y$ be simplicial sets, $- \star -$ the join

Then there exists a canonical morphism

\begin{align*} \iota: X \rightarrow& X \star Y \\ [n]: \mapsto& (\iota_n: X([n]) \cong X([n]) \times Y(\emptyset) \hookrightarrow \coprod_{(J_1,J_2) \in \mathrm{Cut}([n])} X(J_1) \times Y(J_2) = (X \star Y)([n])) \end{align*}
