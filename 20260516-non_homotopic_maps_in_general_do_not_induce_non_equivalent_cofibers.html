non homotopic maps in general do not induce non-equivalent cofibers in anima

Proposition

Let $f,g: X \rightarrow Y$ be nonequivalent maps of anima. Then $\mathrm{cofib}(f), \mathrm{cofib}(g)$ are - in general not nonequivalent.

Proof

consider $-1,1: S^n \rightarrow S^n$. Then these maps are not equivalent - neither in the pointed nor in the unpointed case (this follows from $\mathrm{map}_{\mathrm{An}}(S^n,S^n) \simeq \mathrm{End}_{\mathrm{Ab}}(H_n(S^n)) \cong \mathbb{Z}$. but both maps are an equivalence, so $\mathrm{cofib}(f) \simeq * \simeq \mathrm{cofib}(g)$ are equivalent (cf. square with parallel equivalences as infinity pushout).
