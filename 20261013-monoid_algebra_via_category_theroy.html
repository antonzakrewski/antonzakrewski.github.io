monoid algebra via category theory

Remark

The monoid algebra functor uses that the free R-module functor $\mathrm{An} \rightarrow \mathrm{Mod}_{R}$ is a symmetric monoidal left adjoint (here use that this functor on anima is in the 1-categorical case given by applying $\pi_0$). Therefore it induces a left adjoint (cf. symmetric monoidal left adjoint induces adjunction on Algebras).

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbb{E}_n}(\mathrm{An}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbb{E}_n}(\mathrm{Mod}_{R}) \end{align*}

and for the group case we can use that the inclusion $\mathrm{Grp}_{\mathbb{E}_n}(\mathrm{An}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Mon}_{\mathbb{E}_n}(\mathrm{An}) \simeq \mathrm{Alg}_{\mathbb{E}_n}(\mathrm{An})$ is a left adjoint.
