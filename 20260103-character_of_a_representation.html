character of a representation

Definition

Let $(V,\rho)$ be a finite dimensional representation of a finite group $G$. Then the character-map is defined as

\begin{align*} \chi_V: G \rightarrow& \mathbb{C} \\ g \mapsto& \mathrm{tr}(\rho_g) \end{align*}
