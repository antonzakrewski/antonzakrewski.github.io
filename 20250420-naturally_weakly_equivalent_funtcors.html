naturally weakly equivalent functors

Definition

Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be homotopical categories and $f_1,f_2: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be homotopical functors. Then $f_1,f_2$ are said to be naturally weakly equivalent, if there exists a zigzag of natural transformations

\begin{align*} f_1 \leftarrow g_1 \rightarrow g_2 ... \leftarrow g_m \rightarrow f_2 \end{align*}
