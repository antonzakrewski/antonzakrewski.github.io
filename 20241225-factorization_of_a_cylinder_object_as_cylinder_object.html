factorization of a cylinder object as cylinder object

Proposition

Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{W}, \mathrm{Cof}, \mathrm{Fib})$ be a model category, $A \coprod A \rightarrow A \wedge I \rightarrow A$ a cylinder object Then any factorization

\begin{align*} A \wedge I \rightarrow A \end{align*}

as trivial cofibration followed by fibration resp. cofibration followed by trivial fibration induces a cylinder object

Proof

follows from 2 out of 3 for weak equivalences and 

\begin{displaymath}
\begin{tikzcd}
A \wedge I \arrow[]{rr}{\simeq} \arrow[dashed]{rd}{}& & A \\
& A \wedge I' \arrow[dashed]{rd}{}& 
\end{tikzcd}
\end{displaymath}

where one of the dashed arrows is a weak equivalence
