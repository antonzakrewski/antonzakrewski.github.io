symmetric monoidal left adjoints inherit local object isomorphism

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{L}: \mathrm{Pr}^{L} \rightarrow \mathrm{Pr}^L$ be a symmetric monoidal localization. Let $\mathcal{C} \in \mathrm{Pr}^{L}$ be a closed symmetric monoidal infinity functor. Consider the symmetric monoidal functor (cf. symmetric monoidal localization induces monoidal structure)

\begin{align*} \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{L}(\mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

This induces an equivalence for FunLAdj, symmetric monoidal and a \(L\)-local object $\mathcal{D}$d

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}, \otimes}(\mathcal{L}\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{LAdj}, \otimes}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}) \end{align*}

Proof
