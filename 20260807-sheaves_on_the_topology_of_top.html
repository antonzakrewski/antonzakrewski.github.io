sheaves on the topology of Top

Proposition

Let $\mathrm{Top}$ be the category Top with the pretopology on the category Top. Then a presheaf is a sheaf with respect to the associated topology if and only if $\mathcal{F}_{\vert \mathrm{Open}(T)}$ is a sheaf

Proof

follows from:

  • descent condition for the grothendieck topology associated to a pretopology
