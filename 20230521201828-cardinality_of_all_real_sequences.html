Cardinality of all real sequences

Proposition

\begin{align*} \vert \mathbb{R} \vert = \vert \mathbb{R}^\mathbb{N} \vert \end{align*}

Proofsketch

\begin{align*} \vert \mathbb{R}\vert =& \vert \{0,1\}^{\mathbb{N}} \vert \\ \vert \mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{N}} \vert =& \vert (\{0,1\}^{\mathbb{N}})^{\mathbb{N}} \vert \\ \vert \mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{N}} \vert =& \vert \{0,1\}^{\mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N}} \\ \vert \{0,1\}^{\mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N}} \vert =& \vert \{0,1\}^{\mathbb{N}} \vert \\ =& \vert \mathbb{R} \vert \end{align*}
