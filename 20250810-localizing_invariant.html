karoubi-localizing invariant

Definition

A localizing invariant is an functor

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}: \mathrm{Cat}_{\infty}^{\mathrm{perf}} \rightarrow \mathcal{E} \end{align*}

into a stable category such that karoubi-verdier sequences are sent to fiber sequences.
