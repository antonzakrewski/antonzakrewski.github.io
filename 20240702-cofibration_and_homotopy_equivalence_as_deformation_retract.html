cofibration and homotopy equivalence as deformation retract

Proposition

Let $\iota: A \rightarrow X$ be a cofibration and Homotopy equivalence Then $\iota$ is a deformation retract

Proof

Choose a homotopy inverse $r: B \rightarrow A$, which is a homotopy equivalence. Then choose a homotopy

\begin{align*} H: A \times [0,1] \rightarrow& A \\ \end{align*}

from $\iota \circ r$ to $\mathrm{id}$

Then using the cofibration we get an extension

define

\begin{align*} r_1: X \rightarrow& A \\ x \mapsto& \overline{H}(x,1) \end{align*}

Then by definition

\begin{align*} r_1 \iota = \mathrm{id}_A \end{align*}

Furthermore $r_0$ is a homotopy inverse of $\iota$, as

\begin{align*} \iota r_1 =& \iota r_1 \circ \mathrm{id} \\ \simeq& \iota r_0 \iota r \\ =& \iota \mathrm{id}_A r \\ =& \iota r \\ \simeq& \mathrm{id}_X \end{align*}

Hence

is a diagram with $\iota, \mathrm{id}$ as cofibrations and $r_1$ as homotopy equivalence

Thus we are done by diagram of cofibrations and homotopy equivalences
