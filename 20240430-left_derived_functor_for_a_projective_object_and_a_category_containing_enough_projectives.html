projective object as coacyclic for enough projectives

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}$ be abelian categories, where $\mathcal{A}$ admits enough projectives Let $P \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{A})$ be a projective object and $\mathcal{LF}_n$ a left derived functor Then for $n \geq 1$ it follows that

\begin{align*} \mathcal{LF}_n(P) = 0 \end{align*}

Proof

follows from the construction as witnessed in the existence of a left derived functor for a category with enough projectives where

suffices as projective resolution and

\begin{align*} H_n(\rightarrow \mathcal{F}(0) \rightarrow ) =& H_n( \rightarrow 0 \rightarrow) \\ =& 0 \end{align*}
