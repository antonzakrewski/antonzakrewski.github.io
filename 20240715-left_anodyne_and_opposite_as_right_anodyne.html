left anodyne and opposite as right anodyne

Proposition

Let $\iota: A \rightarrow B$ be a morphism of simplicial sets

TFAE:

  1. $\iota$ is a left anodyne map
  2. the opposite morphism $\iota^{\mathrm{op}}$ is a right anodyne map

Proof

a)

  • left anodyne as saturation of left horn inclusions / right anodyne as saturation of right horn inclusions
  • isomorphism preserves saturations
  • opposite of a left horn as right horn

b) alternative

  • lifting problem and isomorphism
