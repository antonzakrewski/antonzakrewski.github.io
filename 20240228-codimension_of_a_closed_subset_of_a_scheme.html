codimension of a closed subset of a scheme

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{O}_X, O_i)$ be a scheme and $Y \subseteq X$ an closed subset of $X$. Then the codimension is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathrm{inf}(\mathrm{codim}(Z,X) \end{align*}

where $Z$ is a closed irreducible subset of $Y$ see:
