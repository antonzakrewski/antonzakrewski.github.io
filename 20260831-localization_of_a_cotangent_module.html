localization isomorphism of a cotangent module

Proposition

Let $S \rightarrow S'$ be a localization of R-Algebras. Then the \(S'\)-linear map $\Omega_{S/R} \otimes_{S} S' \rightarrow \Omega_{S'/R}$ is an isomorphism

Proof

Follows from the universal property of localization
