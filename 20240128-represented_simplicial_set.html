standard n-simplex

Definition

Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ Then the simplicial set

\begin{align*} \Delta^n: \Delta^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} \end{align*}

is defined as contravariant hom-functor represented by $[n] \in \mathrm{Ob}(\Delta)$, i.e. the contravariant hom-functor

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Hom}_{\Delta}(-, [n]): \Delta \rightarrow \mathrm{Set} \end{align*}
