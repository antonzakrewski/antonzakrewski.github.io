injective object and ext

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an abelian category and $I \in \mathrm{Ob}(\mathcal{A})$ an object.

TFAE:

  1. $I$ is exact
  2. $\mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{A}}^{n}(-,I)$ is the zero functor
  3. $\mathrm{Ext}_{\mathcal{A}}^{1}(-,I)$ is the zero functor

Proof

follows from:

  • contravariant ab-hom functor as exact functor and injective object
  • right derived functor of an exact functor
