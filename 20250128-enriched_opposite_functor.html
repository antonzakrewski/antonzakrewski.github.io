enriched opposite functor

Definition

Let $(\mathcal{V}, \otimes_{\mathcal{V}}, \mathds{1}_{\mathcal{V}})$ be a symmetric monoidal category and $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ (\mathcal{V})-enriched categories and $\mathcal{F}: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}$ be an enriched functor. Then the enriched opposite functor is defined as

\begin{align*} \mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{op}}: \mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}} \rightarrow& \mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{op}} c \mapsto& \mathcal{F}(c) \\ f^{\mathrm{op}} \mapsto& \mathcal{F}(f)^{\mathrm{op}} \end{align*}
