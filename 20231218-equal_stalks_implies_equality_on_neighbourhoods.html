equal germs imply equality on neighbourhoods

Proposition

Let $X$ be a topological space and $\mathcal{F}$ be a presheaf on category set. Supopse $U$ is an open neighbourhood and $s,t \in \mathcal{F}(U)$ are sections, such that the germ

\begin{align*} s_{x} = t_x \end{align*}

are equal.

Then for an arbitrary neighbourhood $V \subseteq X$ of $x$, there exists an open neighbourhood $W \subseteq U \cap V$ such that

\begin{align*} s_{\vert W} = t_{\vert W} \end{align*}
