R-derivation is Z-derivation with kernel

Proposition

Let $R_0 \rightarrow R \rightarrow S$ be ring-homomorphism Then an \(R\)-derivation is equivalently an \(R_0\)-derivation such that $\delta_{ \vert R} = 0$

Proof

Observe that it still satisfies the leibniz rule (by assumption). So it suffices to show that it is \(R\)-linear. But $\delta(r \cdot s) = r \delta(s) + s \delta(r) = r \delta(s) + 0 = r \delta(s)$ shows this.
