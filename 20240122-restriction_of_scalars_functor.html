restriction of scalars functor

Definition / Proposition

Let $R,S$ be rings, $\varphi: R \rightarrow S$ a ring-homomorphism. Then the restriction of scalars functor is defined as functor from category SMod to category RMod

\begin{align*} \varphi^{*}: \mathrm{SMod} \rightarrow& \mathrm{RMod} \\ M \mapsto& M \\ (f: M \rightarrow N) \mapsto& (f: M \rightarrow N) \end{align*}
