field of rational functions not of finite type

Proposition

Let $A$ be an Integral domain and $A(X)$ the field of rational functions. Then $A(X)$ is not of finite type

Proof

Let $X_1,...,X_n$ be generators and

\begin{align*} n \coloneqq \mathrm{min}(\mathrm{inv}(X_i)) \end{align*}

where $\mathrm{inv}$ is the smallest degree of a monomial

Then it follows that $X^{n+1}$ has no inverse
