colimit of p cohomology of a compact subset for an oriented cconnected closed manifold

Proposition

Let \(M\) be a closed connected orientable manifold and $K \subseteq X$ a semi-locally contractible compact subset . Then there exists an isomorphism

\begin{align*} \mathrm{colim}_{U \supseteq K, \mathrm{ open nbhd}} H^(p)(U) \simeq H_{n-p}(X, X \setminus K) \end{align*}

Proof
