infinity filtered colimits in Grp

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a presentable infinity category, $\mathrm{Grp}_{\mathrm{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{C})$ the category of cartesian commutative groups. Then filtered colimits are computed pointwise.

Proof

follows from:

  • group category functor as smashing localization

hence the inclusion

\begin{align*} \mathrm{Grp}_{\mathrm{E}_{\infty}}(\mathcal{C}) \subseteq \mathrm{Fun}(N(\mathrm{Fin}_*), \mathcal{C}) \end{align*}

is a right adjoint.
