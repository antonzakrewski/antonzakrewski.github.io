infinity colimits in the coslice category for a weakly contractible diagram

Proposition

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an infinity category and $c \in \mathcal{C}$. Then the forgetful functor $\mathcal{C}_{c/} \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ creates colimits indexed by weakly contractible simplicial sets.

Proof

follows from colimit in a coslice category pushout lemma for infinity colimits in the coslice category
