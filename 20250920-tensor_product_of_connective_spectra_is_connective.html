tensor product of connective spectra is connective

Proposition

Let $X \in \mathrm{Sp}_{\geq n}$ and $Y \in \mathrm{Sp}_{\geq m}$, then the tensor product$ X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} Y$ lies in $\mathrm{Sp}_{\geq m + n}$.

Proof

Recall that $X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} Y$ lies in $\mathrm{Sp}_{\geq m + n}$ if for each $Z \in \mathrm{Sp}_{\leq n +m -1}$, the spectrum $\mathrm{map}(X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} Y, Z)$ lies in $\mathrm{Sp}_{\leq -1}$ (cf. t-structure of spectra and mapping spectrum). Now we have equivalence

\begin{align*} \mathrm{map}(X \otimes_{\mathbb{S}} Y, Z) \simeq \mathrm{map}(X, \mathrm{map}(Y,Z)) \end{align*}

where repeated application.
