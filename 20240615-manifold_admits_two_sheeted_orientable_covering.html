manifold admits two-sheeted orientable covering

Proposition

Let \(M\) be an \(n\)-dimensional manifold. Then $M$ admits a two-sheeted orientable covering map

\begin{align*} p: Y \rightarrow M \end{align*}

Proof

using orientation construction induces group homomorphism from fundamental group to unit group we get a group-homomorphism

\begin{align*} \varphi: \pi_{\leq 1}(M,m) \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}/2 \end{align*}

using the fundamental theorem of covering theory and quotient of subgroup as G-Set we get a two sheeted covering map.
