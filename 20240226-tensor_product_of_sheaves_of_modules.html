pointwise definition of the tensor product of sheaves of modules

Definition

Let $(X, \mathcal{O}_{X})$ be a ringed space and $\mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}$ sheaves of modules. Then the tensor product is pointwise defined as

\begin{align*} (\mathcal{F} \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X}} \mathcal{G})(U) \simeq& \mathcal{F}(U) \otimes_{\mathcal{O}_{X}(U)} \mathcal{G}(U) \end{align*}
