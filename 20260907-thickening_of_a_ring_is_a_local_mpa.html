thickening of a ring is a local map

Proposition

Let $\varphi: R \rightarrow S$ be a thickening of a ring. Then $\varphi$ is a local ring homomorphism.

Proof

Let $r \in R$ be an element such that $\varphi(r)$ is a unit. There exists an inverse $s$ and we can lift $s$ to some $r'$.

In particular, $1 - r \cdot r'$ is nilpotent, hence $1 + (1 - r \cdot r') = r \cdot r'$ is a unit: the LHS is a unit plus a nilpotent element, so a unit.
